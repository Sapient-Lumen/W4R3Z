# Reading paths

Begin with a route into original passages, then explore neighboring questions. These connections do not claim that the works agree, share one doctrine, or belong to only one subject. Follow an interesting turn; the [alphabetical catalog](CATALOG.md) remains the complete way to browse.

## What do we owe each other?

### From an act of care to a reason for acting

This route begins with a story, then asks questions a story can disclose without settling: what makes another's need a reason for me, how institutions should respond, and what remains owed when acting rightly costs someone dearly. Read the linked originals first; the project introductions offer wider context afterward.

1. **Notice what equal shares leave out.** In [The Hill Where the Sheaves Met](OG%20510P/Parables/versions/snapshot-1771735262/files/Parables.txt), each brother secretly carries grain to the other. One sees a family to feed; the other sees an old age without family care. The movement is not simply from selfishness to generosity. Two opposed descriptions of need each turn the giver's attention away from his own apparent entitlement. Ask what each brother sees correctly, and what neither knows until they meet.

2. **Let generosity reach its recipient.** Read [The Grapes That Would Not Stay](OG%20510P/Parables/versions/snapshot-1771735262/files/Parables.txt) beside the sheaves. Every recipient passes the grapes to someone thought needier; finally the elder divides them. The ending matters: care does not remain an indefinitely circulating intention. The story can be enjoyed as a story while also prompting a question about when deference serves another and when someone needs to accept or distribute the gift.

3. **Distinguish goodwill from attachment.** The [Ithkuil translation notes](OG%20510P/Conlang%20Rosetta%20Stone/versions/Ithkuil-108/files/ithkuil_project/ithkuil_notes.md) separate the essay's ethical use of love as goodwill from affective love, and retain explicit carriers where a technical concept has not yet earned a translation. Read the opening style decisions and root-correction note. Choosing a familiar word for “love” would hide the difference; choosing a precise-looking symbol without a defensible referent would hide a different problem. Translation here exposes commitments in the original rather than merely dressing them in new notation.

4. **Follow a principle to the price of using it.** [Procedural affordability](OG%20510P/The-Good/versions/rev0098/files/The-Good-rev0098-2026.03.25.23.52-proceduralaffordability-feewaiver-nopaytoprove/archive/109-procedural-affordability-fee-waivers-and-no-pay-to-prove-or-be-heard.md) asks what a right amounts to when the necessary copy, correction or first appeal costs more than its holder can pay. Its distinction is not simply free versus paid services: it asks whether a price falls on an optional extra or on the route by which a person can establish standing and be heard. Compare formally equal fees with the brothers' equal shares. The comparison opens a question about unequal burdens; it does not make a parable a policy proof.

5. **Keep the loss after the decision.** [Collisions, tragic remainder and repair](OG%20510P/The-Good/versions/rev0098/files/The-Good-rev0098-2026.03.25.23.52-proceduralaffordability-feewaiver-nopaytoprove/archive/013-kernel-collisions-triage-tragic-remainder-and-repair.md) separates optional improvements, threats to protected conditions, genuine collisions and dilemmas manufactured by institutions. Before choosing a loser, it asks whether options can be widened or burdens shared. When a loss cannot be prevented, the argument carries acknowledgment and repair beyond the decision. Ask whether the proposed protections are justified, whether they resolve the particular collision, and who would otherwise disappear into the word “necessary.”

6. **Return to lives worth inhabiting.** [Affirmative flourishing](OG%20510P/The-Good/versions/rev0098/files/The-Good-rev0098-2026.03.25.23.52-proceduralaffordability-feewaiver-nopaytoprove/archive/019-affirmative-flourishing-play-beauty-learning-and-celebration.md) insists that play, beauty, learning and celebration are substantive goods, not ornamental leftovers or fuel for productivity. It also refuses to let cultural richness excuse domination. Read it after the collision chapter so that two questions remain distinct: what makes life good, and what justifies an action toward someone else. A flourishing life does not by itself establish right conduct; a justified choice need not leave its agent better off.

Continue through [The Good](OG%20510P/The-Good/README.md), [Righteousness](OG%20510P/Righteousness/README.md) and [Goldenrule](OG%20510P/Goldenrule/README.md) to compare their approaches rather than merge them. [LivingChristFigures](OG%20510P/LivingChristFigures/README.md) and [DeathVocab](OG%20510P/DeathVocab/README.md) offer a neighboring inquiry into care at thresholds and the language surrounding it.

The connections above are reading questions, not a claim that these works establish one shared moral theory. In particular, moving from an account of flourishing to an obligation requires an argument, not just a change of vocabulary.


### Care, witness and the power to describe

Follow this shorter route from recognition to evidence and back to practical responsibility. It asks what care enables without making the person cared for belong to the helper's account.

1. **Separate recognition from a completed story.** Read **Recognition after a loss** in [Threshold Offices][care-offices], then the earlier [Name After Death Is Not Ownership][care-name]. Identification, burial, accessible records and family authority accomplish different things. Ask which good a practice actually supplies, and which claim remains unresolved. Rev0105 also resists automatic obscurity: public witness may be chosen and necessary. Refusing appropriation does not give an editor unlimited power to silence the people whose authority it means to protect.

2. **Keep an observation from becoming an unsupported interior life.** DeathVocab's [witnessed-moment method][care-moment] begins with a scene rather than a term. Read its proposed fields for witness role, raw observation, interpretation and uncertainty beside the [inside-view evidence verbs][care-verbs]. “Reported,” “witnessed” and “inferred” make different claims; bodily stillness does not by itself establish experienced peace. The [setting note][care-setting] adds another question: who was allowed to witness, speak, touch or decide in that room? Then inspect the [belongings record][care-belongings]: its drawer, shoes and unchanged room are a public-source scaffold, not a contributor's testimony. The record explicitly marks the precise belongings layer as under-sourced and quarantined. Ask which sentences are broad context, editorial interpretation or an unresolved invitation for evidence. Vividness cannot supply the missing witness.

3. **Return from careful description to useful help.** In [Threshold Offices][care-offices], read **Moving the door**, **Staying while the answer is delayed** and **What travels between offices**. Moving a service can reduce an access burden while increasing the institution's knowledge and power. A meal can matter today without making the arrangement that necessitates it just. Ask what material possibility has improved, what additional control is being claimed, and who can contest it. Careful language should help make those decisions intelligible, not substitute for assistance or prove its effects.

The works are neighbors, not one method. LivingChristFigures compares recurring practices and their burdens; DeathVocab organizes descriptions around scenes, evidence roles and settings. Their shared resistance to turning a person into an illustration still leaves different questions to answer.

## What deserves to survive an interruption?

### What does it mean to continue the same work?

A conversation can continue while its files disappear. A machine can persist while the selected conversation changes. A daemon can receive a revision that has not reached the working directory. This route asks what must remain connected for each kind of continuation to be faithful. It compares mechanisms rather than proposing a stack to install.

1. **Separate the archive from the entrance and the test.** Start with DelayBasin's [memory stores, re-entry packets and admission packets](OG%20510P/DelayBasin/versions/rev0052/contents/docs/10-method/memory-stores-vs-regime-reentry-packets.md). Retaining a fact, helping a successor recover the current direction, and deciding whether an update counts are different jobs. Read its countermodels too: perhaps better summarization explains the effect, or the checks do the useful work while the packet adds ceremony. Ask which material deserves retention and which deserves scarce opening attention. The [wider DelayBasin account](OG%20510P/DelayBasin/README.md) follows that question through revisions and self-criticism.

2. **Keep the instruction stable while the subject changes.** Read qomrade's [captured-prompt contract](Maintained%20Tools/qomrade.user.js#L185-L210). The browser controller repeats the operator's captured instruction; it does not silently replace it with instructions found in files or page prose. Yet an instruction to continue a project can lead to a different useful action each turn because the project and its working materials have changed. Distinguish the controller's recurrence from the model's judgment and the project's carried state. Follow the [Send implementation](Maintained%20Tools/qomrade.user.js#L8078-L8275) to see how that recurrence is tied to the current composer and turn. The [qomrade explanation](guides/qomrade.md) connects those roles to its own source-carrying development workshop.

3. **Ask where the work can act.** Sandcodex's [authority postures](Maintained%20Tools/sandcodex#L285-L325) distinguish a selected writable project from the surrounding host, trusted frontend state, narrower delegated commands and an explicitly host-uncontained mode. A capable browser/desktop environment is useful because work often crosses more interfaces than a compiler, but capability must still have a location and scope. Read the [data map](Maintained%20Tools/sandcodex#L327-L366) and [loop/campaign distinction](Maintained%20Tools/sandcodex#L429-L464), then ask what belongs in the working directory, what survives the process, and what a separate worker's result still needs before adoption. Continue with the [environment and campaign account](guides/Sandcodex.md).

4. **Do not mistake one kind of persistence for another.** Sandpi's [native session account](Maintained%20Tools/sandpi#L4905-L4955) puts conversation ownership in Pi, while Sandpi supplies the persistent guest. The final paragraph gives a sharp example: choosing an older conversation branch does not revert current files. The [cold-checkpoint contract](Maintained%20Tools/sandpi#L5188-L5206) also puts the live host project outside guest-disk rollback. Read the [three persistence layers](guides/Sandpi.md) and ask which operation restores a conversation, which restores guest state, and which protects or revises the actual project.

5. **Make acceptance refer to the thing that was tried.** Monsternix's [first loop](Maintained%20Tools/configuration.nix#L62-L88) carries a complete candidate between collaborators. Its [promotion implementation](Maintained%20Tools/configuration.nix#L14395-L14443) checks the exact source, receipt, tested running system and health under the transaction boundary. The question is not just whether somebody once saw a passing result, but whether that result still describes the system now proposed for acceptance. Read the [full account](guides/Monsternix.md) for the predecessor/candidate judges and the difference between source-level exit, closure restoration and mutable-state recovery.

6. **Preserve the history available where an edit was made.** IoTox's [read-write reconciliation](Maintained%20Tools/IoTox/src/sync_multiwriter_reconcile.cpp#L188-L414) loads the signed workspace's active history separately from newer branches already in storage. It publishes local edits against that installed history before merging the newer knowledge. A remote edit that arrived but was not yet projected into this working directory must not falsely count as something the local edit superseded. This is filesystem history, not a measure of human awareness. The [IoTox explanation](Maintained%20Tools/IoTox/W4R3Z.md) follows the distinction into conflicts, sparse custody and durable command evidence.

Return to the opening question: did the next participant receive the relevant materials, the working context, and the authority to make the next change, or only an object that looks complete? These works give different answers at different boundaries. Their relationship does not establish common provenance, one implementation, or an automatic progression from the older OG research cubes to newer binary Datacubes.

For neighboring inquiries, read [MissingKnowledgeHalf](OG%20510P/MissingKnowledgeHalf/README.md) on forgotten failures and negative knowledge, [EvidenceVault](OG%20510P/EvidenceVault/README.md) on incomplete recovery, and [Lacuna](OG%20510P/Lacuna/README.md) on what happened, what was said, what is believed and what might still happen. Those works also have purposes beyond illustrating software continuity.

## What can language make expressible?

[Conlang Rosetta Stone](OG%20510P/Conlang%20Rosetta%20Stone/README.md) works through translation rather than treating meaning as a word-for-word substitution. [Ministry of Many Worlds](OG%20510P/Ministry-of-Many-Worlds/README.md) imagines a formal language for resources, authority and possible worlds. [Metaphysics](OG%20510P/Metaphysics/README.md) asks what our conceptual distinctions commit us to.

In [LLMPoetry](OG%20510P/LLMPoetry/README.md), begin with [Hard Water](OG%20510P/LLMPoetry/versions/rev0008/files/Claude-F5P/01_hard_water.md), then [Subtraction](OG%20510P/LLMPoetry/versions/rev0008/files/Claude-F5P/13_subtraction.md). One keeps its vocabulary to single syllables; the other removes the final word at each step. In the first, practical instruction gives way to a retrospective uncertainty; in the second, the loss of words changes what the remaining grammar can hold. Read what those constraints let the poems do before turning to their process notes. Ask where the rule makes a loss palpable, and where it risks becoming predictable.

## What makes a claim trustworthy?

### Carry the conditions, not just the conclusion

A claim can become more polished while losing the very conditions that made it defensible. This route moves from a speculative mechanism to a missing research denominator, an assessed time interval, an observation model and a proposed physical theory. Each asks a different question about the connection between a claim and what could support it. The point is not to make them pass one universal test, but to notice exactly which inference each work refuses to give away.

1. **Give a possibility something that could count against it.** Start with Salient Speculations' [charter][trust-charter], then its historical [listing-permission thesis][trust-listing]. The note proposes that short-term-rental enforcement moves upstream from pursuing completed stays to controlling whether a listing can enter a booking flow. Read the mechanism, predictions and weakening conditions together: would widespread routing around the platforms weaken the thesis? Would a rule that exists but cannot be enforced support it? The source's “canon” label is an internal editorial status, not external consensus. This is a March 2026 speculation to examine, not an updated guide to housing law. Ask whether its watchpoints distinguish the proposed change from ordinary paperwork or merely let any new regulation count as confirmation.

2. **Ask what would make missing evidence visible.** MissingKnowledgeHalf's [audit-pairing method][trust-audit] compares a record of studies or protocols that existed with the published literature readers can find. Nonpublication, delayed publication, an unlinked paper and omitted outcomes are different discrepancies. A search that finds only published successes cannot tell you how many attempts disappeared. Read the [registration distinctions][trust-denominator] next: registration is not completion, and a preregistration is not proof of adherence. Ask which evidence establishes the attempted population in a particular comparison, and what remains outside it. This is a way of investigating missingness, not permission to presume hidden failures wherever support is thin.

3. **Do not let agreement manufacture independence or freshness.** Read TimeSync's early [decision memo][trust-time-memo], especially its separation of source authenticity from the quality of a time estimate. Then compare the [rev0132 change][trust-time-change] and its [carried fallback example][trust-time-example]. The example contains a narrow combined interval while retaining same-root common-mode risk, a large staleness value and a fallback to coarse logging. Its lesson is not that the interval has no value, but that overlap does not improve every other property of the evidence. Ask which inputs support the intersection, whether they share a dependency, and which permitted use survives the weakest admitted condition. This is the supplied local-state adjudicator's example, not a live-clock measurement.

4. **Specify what an observer sees, not merely how often it sees something.** Anonymity's [rev0900 correction][trust-anonymity] shows why equal reveal rates do not determine a secret-independent erasure channel. Read its conditional-observation counterexample before the proof-carrying section. The same entrance also contrasts observations that conceal information separately with their revealing joint observation. Ask what the output distribution depends on, what a selector or contact history exposes, and which independence assumptions the calculation requires. The repaired verifier's boundary is “valid under the identified model,” not proof that a running system realizes that model. The affected papers remain on Hold in this snapshot; reading the correction does not promote them. Compare TimeSync carefully: shared clock ancestry and joint privacy leakage are different mathematical problems, though both defeat an unjustified improvement from combining summaries.

5. **Ask which part of an explanation the theory actually supplies.** Theory of Everything's [observer/record minimum][trust-record] asks where an evidence-bearing distinction lives, how it persists and how a later interaction can recover it. Its [witness-borrowing ladder][trust-borrowing] then asks how much of that account comes from the candidate theory and how much is supplied by ordinary detector, laboratory or boundary descriptions. Read its [bounded Ryu–Takayanagi audit][trust-rt] as a worked case: the archive grants a specific subregion-entanglement/geometric-area connection in a restricted holographic setting while leaving the origin of persistent records on the boundary or laboratory side. A more detailed interface may be real progress without deriving the record-making structure it relies on. Borrowing is not inherently illegitimate, and the ladder is this program's proposed assessment, not an established universal ranking of physics. Ask what extra explanatory claim a candidate makes, what it still imports, and what would constitute genuine progress rather than renaming the imported machinery. The documents explicitly leave the measurement problem unresolved.

6. **Follow the correction beyond the archive.** Return to MissingKnowledgeHalf's [correction-memory synthesis][trust-uptake]. Its chain runs from capture and discoverability through interpretation, authority, incorporation into practice, follow-through and eventual decay. A correction can be stored accurately and still never reach the decision that needs it. Ask what evidence would show that a procedure changed, that the change helped, and that the lesson has not become empty ritual. Neither an excellent archive nor a successful structural checker supplies that evidence by itself.

A concrete computational companion is CloudtainerML's [step-boundary memory probe](OG%20510P/CloudtainerML/versions/rev0016/files/CloudtainerML-rev0016-2026.06.10.22.11-nativehpohardening-vericache-steprewrite-sablelynx/experiments/periodic_cache_rewrite/step_rewrite_probe.cpp) and its [supplied result table](OG%20510P/CloudtainerML/versions/rev0016/files/CloudtainerML-rev0016-2026.06.10.22.11-nativehpohardening-vericache-steprewrite-sablelynx/artifacts/probe-results/REV0016_PERIODIC_CACHE_REWRITE_SMOKE.json). Follow the information available to each policy before reading the winner counts: the reconsolidation policy consults the generated set of needed facts, even though its name lets it enter the tally excluding “oracle” methods. The experiment can still explore a memory-retention tradeoff, but that tally does not establish the same advantage for a deployable selector lacking that information. Ask what observable signal could replace the privileged input, and which comparison would then need rerunning. The [full introduction](OG%20510P/CloudtainerML/README.md) follows other probes into sparse attention and model-trace capture.

These stops preserve different kinds of uncertainty: whether a mechanism will spread, what attempts are missing, which use a time assessment warrants, whether a channel model fits, what a theory explains, and whether a correction changes practice. None can settle the others merely by sharing words such as “evidence,” “model” or “record.” Continue through the fuller introductions to [Salient Speculations](OG%20510P/Salient-Speculations/README.md), [MissingKnowledgeHalf](OG%20510P/MissingKnowledgeHalf/README.md), [TimeSync](OG%20510P/TimeSync/README.md), [Anonymity](OG%20510P/Anonymity/README.md) and [Theory of Everything](OG%20510P/Theory-of-Everything/README.md).

## What must survive when a system changes?

### When an operation stops, what has actually happened?

Read these three works through an ordinary predicament: an operation’s reported outcome does not by itself establish what happened. A failure can leave useful effects behind; an apparent success can describe the wrong executed object. Neither label alone decides what to discard, repeat or trust. Each stop offers a different boundary to inspect, not a universal recovery mechanism.

1. **Keep a rejected call separate from the bytes it left.** BrowserRT's [valid-block rollback slice][change-block] starts with a content-addressed write that can reject after the final bytes already match their hash. Its fake-OPFS cases include a throwing observer and an abort after close. Read the [rollback implementation][change-block-code]: permission to clean up a path is checked before a read-only integrity check decides whether valid bytes should survive. Ask why deleting everything associated with a failed call could destroy something useful, and why keeping those bytes still does not mean the application committed its save. The reported probe is not evidence of power-loss durability or a cross-tab transaction. The [full BrowserRT explanation](OG%20510P/BrowserRT/README.md) follows admission, staging, shared coordination and recovery guidance around this distinction.

2. **Find the boundary between two durable accounts.** AnonSync's [regular-file rename account][change-rename] describes a destination File and causally linked source Tombstone, inferred conservatively when content and path conditions identify one eligible source. Follow the [publication sequence][change-rename-code]: the replica pair can commit before the folder catalog pair. If execution stops between them, a later scan can adopt the retained destination operation and covering source tombstone, provided current bytes, absence and visible-head checks still agree. Ask what each database records, which evidence lets the next scan adopt the existing operation, and why atomicity inside each database is not an atomic remote filesystem move. The [AnonSync introduction](OG%20510P/AnonSync/README.md) connects this to ordinary synchronization and explains why matching content alone cannot resolve ambiguous duplicates.

3. **Bind checking to the program that actually runs.** EvidenceVault's [execution-snapshot correction][change-execution] examines a different failure of continuity. In its synthetic regression using reconstructed parent gate code, legitimate validator bytes are checked, a replacement runs, and the originals return in time for the final hash check. Read the [snapshot capture][change-execution-code] alongside that sequence. The corrected gate checks and copies the inventory, then runs validators from the captured tree. A swap followed by restoration in the original tree can still pass: the relevant achievement is that the substituted source bytes did not execute, not proof that nothing changed anywhere. Ask which object the result describes and where trust in the interpreter, bootstrap and host still comes from. The [EvidenceVault introduction](OG%20510P/EvidenceVault/README.md) relates this narrow execution-integrity repair to its broader recovery and proof experiments.

Return to the differences. BrowserRT may retain valid bytes without acknowledging the caller's operation. AnonSync may need to complete one account of a change already recorded in another. EvidenceVault must prevent the checked identity from being exchanged for a different executable object. None licenses treating all errors as success, blindly replaying every failed operation, or equating self-consistent hashes with independent authenticity. The useful reading habit is to ask: what remains, what exactly does it establish, and which next action follows from that evidence?

Continue with [DeriveBSD](OG%20510P/DeriveBSD/README.md), which asks how intent becomes a derived system with inspectable transitions, and the [nicotine+](OG%20510P/nicotine%2B/README.md) research shelf's narrower boundaries between a model, an admitted record and an effect.

For a smaller formal world, [MTG](OG%20510P/MTG/README.md) distinguishes faithfully representing a game action from drawing a justified conclusion about strategy. [bzip4](OG%20510P/bzip4/README.md) offers another bounded comparison: compatibility and a measured performance advantage have conditions that should travel with the claim.
