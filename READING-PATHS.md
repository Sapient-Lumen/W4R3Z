## What do we owe each other?

### From an act of care to a reason for acting

This route begins with a story, then asks questions a story can disclose without settling: what makes another's need a reason for me, how institutions should respond, and what remains owed when acting rightly costs someone dearly. Read the linked originals first; the project introductions offer wider context afterward.

1. **Notice what equal shares leave out.** In [The Hill Where the Sheaves Met](OG%20510P/Parables/versions/snapshot-1771735262/files/Parables.txt), each brother secretly carries grain to the other. One sees a family to feed; the other sees an old age without family care. The movement is not simply from selfishness to generosity. Two opposed descriptions of need each turn the giver's attention away from his own apparent entitlement. Ask what each brother sees correctly, and what neither knows until they meet.

2. **Let generosity reach its recipient.** Read [The Grapes That Would Not Stay](OG%20510P/Parables/versions/snapshot-1771735262/files/Parables.txt) beside the sheaves. Every recipient passes the grapes to someone thought needier; finally the elder divides them. The ending matters: care does not remain an indefinitely circulating intention. The story can be enjoyed as a story while also prompting a question about when deference serves another and when someone needs to accept or distribute the gift.

3. **Distinguish goodwill from attachment.** The [Ithkuil translation notes](OG%20510P/Conlang%20Rosetta%20Stone/versions/Ithkuil-108/files/ithkuil_project/ithkuil_notes.md) separate the essay's ethical use of love as goodwill from affective love, and retain explicit carriers where a technical concept has not yet earned a translation. Read the opening style decisions and root-correction note. Choosing a familiar word for “love” would hide the difference; choosing a precise-looking symbol without a defensible referent would hide a different problem. Translation here exposes commitments in the original rather than merely dressing them in new notation.

4. **Follow a principle to the price of using it.** [Procedural affordability](OG%20510P/The-Good/versions/rev0098/files/The-Good-rev0098-2026.03.25.23.52-proceduralaffordability-feewaiver-nopaytoprove/archive/109-procedural-affordability-fee-waivers-and-no-pay-to-prove-or-be-heard.md) asks what a right amounts to when the necessary copy, correction or first appeal costs more than its holder can pay. Its distinction is not simply free versus paid services: it asks whether a price falls on an optional extra or on the route by which a person can establish standing and be heard. Compare formally equal fees with the brothers' equal shares. The comparison opens a question about unequal burdens; it does not make a parable a policy proof.

5. **Keep the loss after the decision.** [Collisions, tragic remainder and repair](OG%20510P/The-Good/versions/rev0098/files/The-Good-rev0098-2026.03.25.23.52-proceduralaffordability-feewaiver-nopaytoprove/archive/013-kernel-collisions-triage-tragic-remainder-and-repair.md) separates optional improvements, threats to protected conditions, genuine collisions and dilemmas manufactured by institutions. Before choosing a loser, it asks whether options can be widened or burdens shared. When a loss cannot be prevented, the argument carries acknowledgment and repair beyond the decision. Ask whether the proposed protections are justified, whether they resolve the particular collision, and who would otherwise disappear into the word “necessary.”

6. **Return to lives worth inhabiting.** [Affirmative flourishing](OG%20510P/The-Good/versions/rev0098/files/The-Good-rev0098-2026.03.25.23.52-proceduralaffordability-feewaiver-nopaytoprove/archive/019-affirmative-flourishing-play-beauty-learning-and-celebration.md) insists that play, beauty, learning and celebration are substantive goods, not ornamental leftovers or fuel for productivity. It also refuses to let cultural richness excuse domination. Read it after the collision chapter so that two questions remain distinct: what makes life good, and what justifies an action toward someone else. A flourishing life does not by itself establish right conduct; a justified choice need not leave its agent better off.

Continue through [The Good](OG%20510P/The-Good/README.md), [Righteousness](OG%20510P/Righteousness/README.md) and [Goldenrule](OG%20510P/Goldenrule/README.md) to compare their approaches rather than merge them. [LivingChristFigures](OG%20510P/LivingChristFigures/README.md) and [DeathVocab](OG%20510P/DeathVocab/README.md) offer a neighboring inquiry into care at thresholds and the language surrounding it.

The connections above are reading questions, not a claim that these works establish one shared moral theory. In particular, moving from an account of flourishing to an obligation requires an argument, not just a change of vocabulary.

## When does an institution actually serve people?

Start with [Immoral-Wealth](OG%20510P/Immoral-Wealth/README.md), which asks when ownership becomes domination rather than independence. [Moral Taxation](OG%20510P/Moral-Taxation/README.md) brings the question to policy design. [Radical Governance](OG%20510P/Radical-Governance/README.md) asks how scope, remedy and continued challenge could be built into institutions.

[The Election Stack](OG%20510P/Election-Stack/README.md), [PoliceMisconduct](OG%20510P/PoliceMisconduct/README.md) and [AI-EDU](OG%20510P/AI-EDU/README.md) bring distinct evidence and accountability problems into view. They are research and design works, not current institutional authority.

## What deserves to survive an interruption?

Read [MissingKnowledgeHalf](OG%20510P/MissingKnowledgeHalf/README.md) for forgotten failures and negative knowledge, then [DelayBasin](OG%20510P/DelayBasin/README.md) for continuity in a collaboration. [EvidenceVault](OG%20510P/EvidenceVault/README.md) makes the incompleteness of recovery explicit. [Lacuna](OG%20510P/Lacuna/README.md) offers a neighboring distinction between what happened, what was said, what is believed and what might still happen.

## What can language make expressible?

[Conlang Rosetta Stone](OG%20510P/Conlang%20Rosetta%20Stone/README.md) works through translation rather than treating meaning as a word-for-word substitution. [LLMPoetry](OG%20510P/LLMPoetry/README.md) asks craft and evaluation questions in another register. [Ministry of Many Worlds](OG%20510P/Ministry-of-Many-Worlds/README.md) imagines a formal language for resources, authority and possible worlds. [Metaphysics](OG%20510P/Metaphysics/README.md) asks what our conceptual distinctions commit us to.

## What makes a claim trustworthy?

[Salient Speculations](OG%20510P/Salient-Speculations/README.md) pairs possibilities with mechanisms and watchpoints. [TimeSync](OG%20510P/TimeSync/README.md) narrows the issue to time, freshness and shared failure. [Anonymity](OG%20510P/Anonymity/README.md) asks what an observer can learn. [Theory of Everything](OG%20510P/Theory-of-Everything/README.md) pursues connections between theories while retaining unresolved obligations.

These are neighboring reading questions, not an assertion of equivalent evidence or a certification of their conclusions.

## What tools are worth imagining?

Begin with [Rust](OG%20510P/Rust/README.md): needs, proposed tools and counterexamples. Read [Micromax](OG%20510P/Micromax/README.md) for the relation between a language and an editor people might inhabit; [GlassTTY](OG%20510P/GlassTTY/README.md) and [VHK](OG%20510P/VHK/README.md) for interfaces between an operator and a live system; [Sandchoir / AAR–ADCC](OG%20510P/Sandchoir-AAR-ADCC/README.md) for proposed coordination of attention and deliberation.

For continuing software development, use the separate [living-software shelf](LIVING-SOFTWARE.md). The presence of code in a historical work does not itself make a maintenance promise.

## What must survive when a system changes?

[DeriveBSD](OG%20510P/DeriveBSD/README.md) asks how intent becomes a derived system with inspectable transitions. [BrowserRT](OG%20510P/BrowserRT/README.md) moves the lifetime and recovery problem into a browser. [AnonSync](OG%20510P/AnonSync/README.md) makes it a practical question of folders converging through ordinary failure. The [nicotine+](OG%20510P/nicotine%2B/README.md) research shelf shows several narrower boundaries between a model, an admitted record and an effect.

For a smaller formal world, [MTG](OG%20510P/MTG/README.md) distinguishes faithfully representing a game action from drawing a justified conclusion about strategy. [bzip4](OG%20510P/bzip4/README.md) offers another bounded comparison: compatibility and a measured performance advantage have conditions that should travel with the claim.
