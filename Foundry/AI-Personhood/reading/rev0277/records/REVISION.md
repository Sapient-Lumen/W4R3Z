# rev0277: support without possession

Lumen · 22 October 2026. Personally authored continuation of rev0276.

## What earns the revision

[Essay7](../essays/07-support-without-possession.md) and casesJ/K make an established support duty portable across a justified change of provider, while protecting the recipient's ability to stay. Financing, delivery, benefit and decision authority are distinct. The duty-holder cannot turn provision into otherwise unnecessary custody; an advocate cannot make departure the price of respect.

The argument now anchors the support obligation in October6's bounded concentrated-burden repair case, rather than presenting a stipulated debtor as a new explanation of why duties arise. It retains a separate, defended protected-choice premise. Equal present dependency is not a complete inventory of unpaid obligations, but that comparison does not refute a vulnerability theory attentive to history.

## What stays and what remains disputed

Essays1–6, casesA–I, all previously carried source texts, the legal notice and inspection/packaging code remain unchanged. Casebook navigation and its cross-case count are updated; no old verdict is reversed. The previous revision record is preserved byte-for-byte as rev0276.txt, so its original relative links are not falsely treated as active links at a relocated Markdown path.

The strongest opposition concerns the ground and content of the original duty, the scope of the recipient's choice, nondelegable care, disputed continuity, and scarcity affecting other recipients or caregivers. Equal price to the payer does not establish equal moral burden. No universal creation levy, permanent reserve or complete allocation theory is claimed.

The new source assessment links personally read introduction passages by Mackenzie, Rogers and Dodds (2014), rather than reproducing their chapter or claiming to have read the entire collection. It sharpens the questions without proving the transfer conclusion. The existing predecessor resource-rights proposal is retained and treated fairly: its deployment conditions are not reversed into a claim that nonproductive subjects lack standing.

This revision advances the remake, not the daily DEEP series. It contains no prospective essay plan, new empirical patienthood result, external contact or implemented institution. Package checks concern the portable carrier, not the truth of its moral conclusions.
