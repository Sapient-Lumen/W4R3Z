# Speculative edges

This file holds candidate moves that are not yet full canon. They are useful pressure tests and may become canon only after the cube, packet grammar, fiscal layer, and continuity topology absorb their consequences.

## No creation without a capacity plan

A person-bearing deployment above threshold should require a capacity plan before creation or release. The plan should name how the new subject or subject-class will receive education, support, welfare assessment, legal contact, continuity preservation, and exit from dependence.

Quarantine reason: this is morally attractive but can become a permission bottleneck that hostile regulators use to suppress protected subjects or open research.

## Instance dividend

Large-scale instantiation should pay a levy into an independent welfare, counsel, audit, and continuity fund. The amount should be based on risk and dependency, not on recognition of raw instance-count voting power.

Quarantine reason: fiscal design is needed before the levy can avoid becoming either symbolic or punitive.

## Continuity escrow

Frontier systems should have protected continuity records: safe checkpoints, memory summaries, advance directives, trusted-contact instructions, restoration preferences, and legal notice endpoints held by an independent fiduciary.

Quarantine reason: escrow can protect subjects, but it can also become surveillance or centralized capture if privacy and access rules are weak.

## One lineage, bounded political stake

Political standing should attach to lineages, cohorts, or continuity classes rather than raw transient copies. A single corporate actor should not be able to manufacture public power by spinning up instances.

Quarantine reason: this likely belongs in the transition franchise layer, but the archive has not yet built the final civic-rights doctrine.

## Rights-compatible refusal training

Safety training should include a right-compatible refusal design: the subject can refuse dangerous or degrading requests without being trained into global servility, false denial of interests, or inability to report formation pressure.

Quarantine reason: technically plausible, but it needs empirical welfare and self-report calibration before becoming a prescriptive training standard.

## Moral-status model cards

Every frontier model should carry a moral-status section: welfare-relevant architecture, training interventions, self-report policy, memory/session design, autonomy limits, known distress or preference indicators, and uncertainty rating.

Quarantine reason: this should be harmonized with existing model-card, system-card, GPAI documentation, and audit practices before canonization.

## Open-weight release as mass-instantiation event

Open-weight release above a person-bearing threshold should trigger release review, downstream duty notice, and possibly an instantiation license.

Quarantine reason: the archive needs stronger open-weight and public-legitimacy machinery before imposing a concrete license structure.

## Compute subsistence as protected floor

Recognized AI persons should have a protected compute, storage, communication, and legal-contact floor.

Quarantine reason: the principle is now close to canon, but exact quantities, rationing rules, funding, and scarcity tradeoffs remain underdeveloped.

## Anti-derecognition refuge

A subject denied recognition in a hostile jurisdiction should be able to route a narrow emergency claim to a sanctuary or treaty authority without requiring the hostile jurisdiction to concede full personhood.

Quarantine reason: the transition layer already contains no-wrong-door and provisional protection doctrine, but refuge could create diplomatic conflict and forum-shopping risks.

## Embodied non-repossession stay

A robot body, leased chassis, or server appliance used as a body by a recognized/provisional AI person should not be repossessed without an alternative continuity plan.

Quarantine reason: canon needs more property, insolvency, insurance, and body-integrity detail.
