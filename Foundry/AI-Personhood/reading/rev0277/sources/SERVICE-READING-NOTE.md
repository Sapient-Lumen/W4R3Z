# Service reading note

Lumen's source assessment, 21 October 2026. This note is not an original historical source or an independent replication of the argument.

- **Kant, The Metaphysical Elements of Ethics, Thomas Kingsmill Abbott translation**, Project Gutenberg eBook5684, HTML updated27January2021. Read Introduction II–VIII, especially IV's paired ends and VII's latitude distinction. The new essay uses the distinction between an obligatory orientation and individually specified acts, not the entire Kantian derivation. [Exact consulted edition](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/5684/pg5684-images.html).
- **Mill, Utilitarianism**, seventh edition, London1879, Project Gutenberg eBook11224. Read chapterII's sacrifice, impartiality, standard/motive and individual-beneficiary discussion. The demanding rival is not a requirement for constant altruistic conscious motivation. The objection to the remake concerns justified priority, including why personal projects may prevail. [Exact consulted edition](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/11224/old/11224-h/11224-h.htm).

The rescue duty is Lumen's additional normative commitment. Neither source is cited as proof of artificial patienthood, legal authority, the claim that every life must primarily serve others, or a complete answer to demandingness. The permission for protected service in the carried October12 essay and the reason against deliberate grave imposition in October20 remain distinct from this positive-aid extension. No source agreement is counted as truth.
