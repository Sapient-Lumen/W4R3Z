# W4R3Z

**Tools, stories, philosophy, translations and research, each worth encountering on its own terms.**

W4R3Z brings together software for working with agents, systems and devices with an extensive collection of literary and intellectual work. There are stories that make care visible in a gesture, arguments that follow a moral claim into the details of ordinary life, translations that expose what a familiar word leaves unsettled, and designs for systems whose behavior can be understood rather than merely accepted.

Some of these works are meant to be used. Others are meant to be read, questioned, inhabited or carried into a different project. You do not need to turn a work into a product to find something valuable in it.

## Three ways in

- **Read a work.** [OG 510P][og] holds stories, essays, translations, philosophical inquiries and research. Start with a particular voice, scene or argument, and follow it at its own pace.
- **Follow an inquiry.** The [reading paths][paths] connect works through questions about care, language, institutions, knowledge, possibility and continuity. They offer sequences and contrasts rather than a single theory of the collection.
- **Work with the tools.** [Maintained Tools][tools] brings together Sandcodex, Sandpi, qomrade, Monsternix and IoTox: different approaches to agent-assisted work, Linux systems and connections between devices.

[Browse every work in the catalog][catalog].

## Care, in a scene and in a system

In [Parables][parables-source], two brothers each carry grain to the other because each sees a need his own life does not contain. Equal division is only the beginning of the story. Mirrored journeys, repeated words and a meeting on a hill let the reader experience a change of attention before reducing it to a rule.

[The Good][good010] approaches related questions through an extended philosophical argument. It asks which conditions deserve strong protection, how different forms of flourishing can coexist, and what happens when a right is present on paper but its first usable step is too expensive. Its [account of play, beauty, learning and celebration][good019] also refuses to make a protected life merely an administered one.

## Language that makes a choice visible

[Conlang Rosetta Stone][conlang-guide] works through passages about love in Ithkuil and Lojban. Translation becomes an inquiry into meaning: is love being described as goodwill, attachment, intention, a relation, or something a language must express through several coordinated choices? Drafts and notes expose those choices rather than hiding them behind a finished-looking line. The work offers a way of reading familiar language more attentively, even to someone who will never speak either constructed language.

## Worlds that can remain open without forgetting

[Lacuna][lacuna-source] separates what a character says, what someone believes, what might explain an event and what a fictional world has accepted as true. Several explanations can remain possible; changing one premise brings its commitments and consequences into view. It is a Python/SQLite kernel, but its design also asks an artistic question: how can a story keep inventing without losing its obligations to the reader or player?

[BrowserRT][browser-source] poses a different design question about serious local browser software. Its runtime architecture makes workers, resource ownership, bounded queues and recovery explicit. [Object references][browser-objects] let the design reason about where data lives and how it moves, rather than hiding those costs inside an apparently simple call.

## Work that survives the next session

[DelayBasin](OG%20510P/DelayBasin/README.md) asks how a long-running project can resume after its working context disappears. It distinguishes the larger memory store, the compact packet that lets a new session find its footing, and the checks that determine whether a revision counts as faithful continuation. Its experiments and self-criticism reach a practical question: what must travel with a work so another person or model can genuinely continue it, rather than merely imitate its surface?

## Capacity to keep making

The maintained tools give practical form to another part of the collection: working on projects that can be inspected, changed and continued. [Sandcodex](guides/Sandcodex.md) gives Codex a selected Linux project, development tools, and browser/desktop access; [Sandpi](guides/Sandpi.md) gives the Pi coding agent a persistent NixOS virtual machine. [qomrade](guides/qomrade.md) keeps a ChatGPT project moving through successive rounds of research, writing or construction without a person sending every continuation. [Monsternix](guides/Monsternix.md) makes a NixOS configuration and its process of revision travel together. [IoTox](Maintained%20Tools/IoTox/W4R3Z.md) develops commands, synchronization and messaging among a person’s own devices.

Their differences matter. Follow the [tool introductions][tools] for the working model, source and operator requirements of each. They are separate tools, not a mandatory stack.

The broader invitation is to become more capable of making and understanding things, rather than remaining only their consumer. That includes a system one can alter, a research question one can carry further, and a story or argument that changes what one can notice.
