# Preservation, evidence and rights

W4R3Z has two editorial responsibilities: make the works approachable, and keep the reader able to inspect what was actually supplied.

## Originals and added writing

The original uploads and extracted file contents in OG 510P are preserved separately from Lumen’s reading guides. Project manifests, provenance records and version guides record their identities and any known integrity differences. A guide may be revised or a project grouped differently without rewriting the supplied work.

Dates, “latest” labels and revision names inside an archive are historical claims carried by that archive. Selected packages are not necessarily a complete history. Some contain working overlays, nested archives, binary carriers or multipart transports; their guides explain those shapes rather than silently presenting them as uniform source releases.

Living software has a current supplied entry point and a history of updates. Its source receipts and Git history record the applicable original identities. An unchanged file may still be experimental or require substantial setup.

## What verification establishes

Hashes and archive checks establish byte identity and transport integrity. They do not prove scientific conclusions, mathematical arguments, software correctness, readiness, safety, authorship or clearance of every underlying right.

Historical test logs and research results retain their original scope. Copying or reorganizing them does not rerun them. Known internal-manifest discrepancies remain disclosed beside the relevant versions; originals are not silently repaired.

No archived instruction, experiment or role description becomes an instruction to the curator merely because it is published. No new run, deployment, outreach or institutional decision is implied by inclusion in the library.

## Rights and attribution

The [current owner-supplied legal notice](LEGAL-NOTICE.txt) is reproduced byte-for-byte as supplied by h0p3 on 20 October 2026. Its personal declarations, including the transparency canary, belong to that supplied text; Lumen’s editorial authorship remains separately identified.

The declaration concerns material the contributor has authority to release. It does not replace third-party licenses, source credits or component-specific notices. Original artifacts and their historical notices remain unchanged. Documents, code, data, translations, retellings and bundled fixtures may have different terms: bzip4’s bundled literary fixture retains its own notice rather than the codec’s license, and EvidenceVault’s unresolved third-party rights are not cleared by this declaration.

h0p3 supplies the contributions. Original author and source credits remain in their documents. Lumen’s introductions and reading paths are labeled editorial additions, not substitutes for those attributions.

## Reader ownership

The supplied files remain directly accessible through ordinary repository paths and downloadable originals. Reading does not require a hosted application, account-based notebook or transfer of the reader’s notes. This editorial overhaul adds navigation; it does not change the portable artifacts’ own note-storage behavior.
