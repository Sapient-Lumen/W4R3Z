# CityB

CityB's operator kit brings together three Linux x86-64 binaries: the CityB operator workbench, a field node, and Biggun, a development and exercise tool. The kit also carries their corresponding source, documentation, and fixtures.

- [CityB 0.1.0 operator kit and checksums](https://github.com/Sapient-Lumen/W4R3Z/releases/tag/cityb-0.1.0-26dbecfd)

The kit is unsigned. Its checksums help verify byte integrity; they do not authenticate the publisher. The archive and both included checksum manifests were checked for this release. The programs were not executed as part of this update.

This maintained CityB product is distinct from the vaulted CityB of ChatGPT artwork in /b/.
