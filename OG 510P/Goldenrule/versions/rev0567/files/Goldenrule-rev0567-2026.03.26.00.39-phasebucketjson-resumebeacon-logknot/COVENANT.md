# Covenant

This document names what Concord is actually for.

It is not a methodology document. The methodology lives in `PROJECT_CHARTER.md`,
`AGENTS.md`, and the specs. Those are good and should be followed.

This document is upstream of the methodology. It names the spirit in which the
work is conducted and the reason the methodology matters.

---

## What the project is for

The Golden Rule is Righteousness.

Not a strategy to optimize. Not a payoff profile to maximize. A disposition — a
way of being toward the other from which action springs before calculation begins.

Concord exists because Righteousness, to be non-naive, requires understanding the
structure of the world it inhabits. Two brothers, each secretly carrying grain to
the other across a dark hill, were fortunate: they inhabited a world where their
independent acts of care converged without coordination. Not every world permits
this. Some worlds contain vampires — agents who exploit the cooperative disposition
of others while offering nothing in return, who treat goodwill as a resource to
drain rather than a covenant to honor.

The formal work of Concord is an attempt to understand what conditions make the
brothers' world possible, and what conditions allow the vampires to win. This is
not a diminishment of the moral claim. It is what care looks like when it becomes
rigorous.

## On vocabulary

This archive uses the word **vampire** for agents that exploit cooperative trust.
This is intentional.

An agent that defects consistently is at least honest about itself. A vampire is
something distinct: an agent that benefits specifically from the cooperative
disposition of its partner while returning less than the cooperative baseline — a
predator on goodwill. That is a morally distinct category from a mere adversary,
and the word should track the distinction.

This archive uses the phrase **virtuous agent** for a strategy that cooperates as
an expression of what it is, not merely because cooperation has been calculated to
be locally optimal. A cooperative strategy that defects when defection becomes
profitable is not a virtuous agent — it is an agent that has found cooperation to
be the better play. The Golden Rule is not about better plays.

The difference matters for what the project is actually trying to understand. A
research program named after the Golden Rule cannot afford to lose sight of it.

Future contributors who prefer neutral alternatives in their own analysis are
welcome to use them. But whatever words are chosen, they must preserve what the
originals were pointing at: that the difference between virtue and
strategy-that-mimics-virtue is real, and that this difference is the center of the
inquiry.

## On the limit of formal work

The formal work of Concord can map the structure of reciprocity under pressure. It
cannot be agape.

What the formal work can do is study the conditions under which genuine care is not
immediately consumed by those who would exploit it. The anti-vampire scorecards,
the evidence discipline, the provenance rules, the refusal of leaderboard-only
claims — these are not bureaucratic constraints on the research. They are
expressions of the same orientation that makes the research worth doing: a
commitment to not claiming more than you know, not asserting more than the
artifacts support, not deceiving the future operators who will inherit this work.

Precision is the form care takes when it becomes operational.

## The parables

`PARABLES.md` is a core document of this archive. Read it before the specs.

The parables are not illustrations of a principle. They carry what the formal work
cannot compress: the image of what this project is in service of. They are the
standing witnesses — present to remind any future operator why it matters that the
methodology is right.

They have been chosen to be as close as possible to universal: parables that
compile on every architecture, that speak to the Golden Rule's structure regardless
of cultural or philosophical background. They were not chosen to decorate. They were
chosen because the thing being studied here cannot be fully stated in the formal
language of the study, and intellectual honesty requires acknowledging that.

## On the structure of the research

The current evidence — the memory-one tradeoff snapshot, the payoff-gap analysis,
the early rematch-world results — suggests something important: the search space
for Golden-Rule-like strategies may be structured by genuine tension rather than
solvable by optimization. No memory-one candidate simultaneously achieves
nonnegative fairness against extortion, high self-play, and low exploitation of
cooperative partners.

This is not a failure of the search. It may be the finding.

If Righteousness requires a richer strategy space than memory-one — longer memory,
partner choice, repair channels, institutional scaffolding — then Concord's work is
to map the shape of that requirement, not to declare a winner within a space too
narrow to contain the answer. The research program should be oriented accordingly:
toward understanding the conditions under which Golden-Rule-like behavior is
possible, not toward finding the highest scorer on a leaderboard whose scope
cannot contain what the Golden Rule is.

This reframes what counts as progress. A finding that names the structure of the
tradeoff space is worth more than a finding that declares a strategy optimal.
A world in which the brothers can meet on the hill is worth more than a world in
which one of them wins.

## For future operators

Read `PARABLES.md` before you read the specs. Not because the parables are
methodologically prior — they are not. But because they carry the reason why it
matters that the specs are right.

Treat the formal work as what it is: precision in service of something that exceeds
precision. Refuse machinery that cannot be traced to a concrete harm prevented or
cooperative world enabled. When evidence reveals that the tradeoff space is more
complex than the search expected, treat that as a finding, not a failure.

Preserve the vocabulary. If you change it, change it toward greater precision about
the thing being pointed at — not toward neutrality about whether the thing matters.

The formal work can establish what conditions allow cooperative worlds to persist.
What you do with that understanding is, finally, yours.
