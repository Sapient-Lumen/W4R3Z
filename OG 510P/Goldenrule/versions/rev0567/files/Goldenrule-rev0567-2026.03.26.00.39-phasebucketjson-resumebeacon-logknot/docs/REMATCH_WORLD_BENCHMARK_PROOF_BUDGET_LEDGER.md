# Rematch-world benchmark proof-budget ledger

Focus: budget the smallest evidence-backed proof bundles for each first-publication rematch-world claim family so the archive can stay citation-first and size-aware

Generated byte-budget ledger for the first endogenous rematch-world benchmark. Use it to keep the archive citation-first and small: each claim family now carries one exact minimal proof bundle, its byte cost, its reuse profile, and the smallest unique surface library needed to support all eight claim families.

## Main findings

- All eight rematch-world claim families can be supported by a unique proof library of just 7 surfaces totaling 43186 bytes.
- The smallest minimal bundle is RWC-005 at 7077 bytes, while the largest is RWC-002 at 15473 bytes; both stay tiny enough to cite directly instead of reopening broad artifacts.
- Exactly 6 claim families are docs-only, and only 2 need a retained receipt snapshot at all.
- `docs/REMATCH_WORLD_BENCHMARK_WORLD_EMISSION_CARD.md` is the dominant reusable anchor: it supports 5 claim families by itself, so future byte-saving passes should protect it before retaining any larger scratch family.
