# Indirect Reciprocity, Reputation, and Institutions

Why this matters:
- Dyadic reciprocity doesn’t scale cleanly; institutions and reputation systems do.
- “Golden rule” behaviors in the wild often depend on third-party enforcement and shared norms.

What Concord should build (concrete):
- World modules where:
  - agents have reputations (public or private),
  - observers have biased/noisy reports,
  - gossip can be adversarial,
  - institutions exist (courts/moderators/escrow/restorative channels).
- Strategy families that reason about reputation (not just last-move memory).
