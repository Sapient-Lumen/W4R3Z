# The first retained rematch-world benchmark should carry a turnover-tempo interpretation handoff

The archive already has a compact publication target for `SQ-015`: every rematch-world benchmark should publish one turnover or persistence field beside fixed rematch delay.

The problem is that the first retained benchmark seed still made the inheritor remember **why** that field matters from the proxy turnover report.

Without one compact **benchmark-facing handoff**, a future implementor still has to reopen `artifacts/reports/rematch_proxy_turnover_tempo_snapshot_20260306.{md,json}` just to recover the standing interpretation:

> fixed rematch delay behaves like a tempo-scaled tax because short-lived matches pay the same dead-time more often.

A retained benchmark seed should therefore carry one tiny copied turnover-tempo interpretation handoff instead of forcing the next session to reconstruct that rule from scratch.

## What the copied handoff should keep

Only the smallest durable payload is needed:

1. the source-report digest and path,
2. the tested policy / extortion / delay support,
3. the tempo-scaling headline from the proxy report,
4. the highest-churn and lowest-churn delay-2 anchor cells,
5. the `4.877836x` loss-ratio headline,
6. and one minimal field rule saying benchmark turnover rows must pair delay with `avg_match_length` (or an equivalent turnover metric).

That is enough for the inheritor to cite the current interpretation without copying the wider scenario table.

## Why this is worth retaining

This handoff does **not** replace the native world contract.
It simply prevents the archive from losing the current implementor lesson while the world sections remain template-backed.

It also clarifies the division of labor inside the seed:

- `matching_state_interpretation_handoff` says delay sweeps are delay-tax diagnostics rather than full efficiency claims,
- `turnover_tempo_interpretation_handoff` says the size of that delay tax is strongly governed by partnership tempo,
- and the eventual `turnover_tempo_contract` will carry world-native per-policy turnover rows once the engine emits them.

## Size discipline

Treat the copied handoff as a frozen citation surface inside the benchmark seed until the engine can emit a real world-native turnover-tempo section.
Keep the wide scenario table in the cited proxy snapshot rather than copying it into retained benchmark artifacts.
