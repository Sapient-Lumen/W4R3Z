# Cooperation benchmark compact-card scope and taxonomy reports should be citable governance handles

The compact-card archive already treats `cooperation_benchmark_card_scope_surface` as the subsystem-boundary witness and `cooperation_benchmark_card_taxonomy` as the token/governance witness.
Those two report families are not disposable scratch in the same way as ad hoc snapshots: they are compact governance handles for what belongs to the subsystem and what its stable identifiers mean.

## Archive consequence

When archive-size pressure rises, future inheritors should cite this topic together with `docs/BENCHMARK_PROGRAM.md` instead of assuming the retained JSON reports themselves must remain the only durable explanation of compact-card boundary and taxonomy semantics.
