# Overlap, precedence, and composite-case routing

Use this card when several rows in the founding-answer files apply at once. **Do not average them. Apply them in order.** The archive's answer to composite cases is a priority rule: first block category mistakes, then protect floors, then locate the real controller and base, then decide scale, incidence, timing, and rates.[S4][S5][S8][S10][S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S31][S83][S101]

## Precedence ladder

| Order | Question | Default consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Is the supposed direct taxpayer the wrong kind of subject?** Current LLMs, copied model instances, nonhuman animals, and ecological commons are not direct civic remitters now; future artificial persons would be direct subjects only after a real rights / standing / non-ownership switch.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44][S101] | Route liability upstream to controllers, owners, beneficiaries, extractors, polluters, or other accountable institutions rather than taxing the protected side or a current non-person machine. |
| 2 | **Would collection breach the protected human floor or ignore care loads?** Subsistence, disability-sensitive floors, dependency, and heavy care load outrank ordinary revenue collection.[S4][S31][S32][S33][S64][S65][S66] | Move the person-side result toward negative to zero net through transfers, services, automaticity, or bounded deferral before collecting routine tax. |
| 3 | **Is the activity a catastrophic or non-compensable risk that should be gated before priced?**[S15][S17][S53][S54] | Use approval, caps, bonding, reserves, insurance, decommissioning, or refusal first; any tax is only a sidecar. |
| 4 | **Who is the real controller, beneficial owner, or grouped beneficiary?** Paper labels, white-label branding, thin resale, delegated operation, or fragmented entities do not settle liability.[S10][S16][S20][S21][S34][S35][S83] | Assign primary liability to the actor or real controller group with durable governance, continuation power, and residual upside; assign only bounded co-controller duties for genuinely governed sub-layers. |
| 5 | **What is the morally best base?** Prefer harms, rents, land, inheritance, monopoly, extraction, and concentrated windfalls before routine labor or gross throughput.[S5][S8][S21][S73][S93] | Use the stronger base first and treat ordinary labor or generic user activity as the fallback, not the first grab-handle. |
| 6 | **Which scale has the first claim?** Situs and local burden lead for immovable assets and place-bound harms; national systems usually lead for person-level floors and broad revenue; club coordination is needed for mobile rents and cross-border profit.[S3][S5][S9][S15][S23][S28] | Split claims by real object rather than letting one jurisdiction erase the others or letting every level pile onto the same base. |
| 7 | **Who actually bears the burden after pass-through?**[S4][S21][S83] | Repair or redesign any instrument that pushes the burden onto protected workers, low-income consumers, tenants, or weak suppliers contrary to the moral target. |
| 8 | **Do timing, liquidity, measurement, and real-value honesty still fit?**[S23][S25][S27][S50][S55][S56][S57] | Only after the first seven steps should the archive choose withholding, instalments, deferral, valuation proxies, indexation, thresholds, and review cadence. |

## Composite-case defaults

| Composite case | Result |
|---|---|
| **Low-income human using ordinary hosted AI tools** | The person stays an ordinary taxpayer with floor protection; there is no special prompt tax merely because AI is involved.[S4][S10][S19][S31] |
| **Wealthy disabled heir or land-rich caregiver** | Disability or care load protects the floor and delivery design, but it does not cancel inheritance, land-rent, or other concentrated-base claims above that floor.[S5][S8][S31][S64][S73] |
| **Open or public-interest model project that still causes large local utility or water burden** | Preserve the lighter innovation track for the project as such, but still price the place-bound burden at the facility or host layer and use ordinary safety rules where risk is real.[S10][S15][S21] |
| **DAO-like or committee-governed frontier deployment with no single visible owner** | Do not treat shared governance as a controller vacuum. Group the real coalition, name a lead remitter if needed, and keep temporary step-in command visible when it occurs.[S20][S21][S34][S35][S83] |
| **Future rights-bearing artificial person created inside a profit-seeking stack** | Once rights-bearing personhood is genuinely recognized, ownership must end and taxation switches to ordinary person-side logic; until then, controller-side liability remains primary.[S18][S19][S20][S42][S43][S44][S101] |

## Compression rule

When a new edge case can be resolved by this ladder plus one existing routing file, **do not add another framework note**. The archive should grow by genuine new routing rules or calibration memos, not by multiplying named composites.
