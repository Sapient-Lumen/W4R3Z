# Wealth-transfer, dynastic-concentration, and liquidity routing

## Question in one sentence

When inheritance, estate, gift, trust, exit, carried unrealized gains, billionaire minimum tax, family office, or dynastic wealth concentration is at issue, how should the archive tax unearned transfer and top-end accumulation without creating modest-estate or liquidity traps?[S8][S73][S496]

Route: dynastic transfer and top-end wealth concentration are priority bases after floor protection; use thresholds, lifetime aggregation, recipient sensitivity, liquidity relief, anti-fragmentation, and cross-border coordination.[S8][S73]

## Core rule

Inheritance is not ordinary labor. Large unearned transfers and billionaire-scale accumulation can entrench political and market power. But modest family security, disability trusts, family homes, small businesses, farms, and illiquid assets need thresholds, deferral, installment, and hardship rules rather than forced-sale design.

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| modest inheritance or vulnerable beneficiary | floor gate | exemption, deferral, special-needs protection, or low rate. |
| large lifetime gifts and inheritance | aggregation gate | recipient or estate tax with lifetime aggregation and anti-fragmentation. |
| trust/entity slicing | beneficial-ownership gate | look-through, related-party grouping, valuation rule, or anti-avoidance. |
| ultra-high-net-worth low effective tax | minimum-tax gate | top-end minimum tax, mark-to-market proxy, or exit collector. |
| illiquid business/farm/home | liquidity gate | installment, lien, deferral, or business-continuity rule. |
| cross-border wealth mobility | coordination gate | exit tax, information sharing, collector-of-last-resort, and treaty coordination. |

## Anti-patterns

- **dynastic immunity** — trusts, valuation discounts, unrealized gains, and migration erase large transfer claims.
- **liquidity trap** — tax design forces sale of modest illiquid assets while liquid dynastic wealth escapes.
- **charity laundering** — charitable vehicles preserve donor control while avoiding transfer tax.
- **basis step-up amnesia** — accrued gains disappear at death without explicit justification.
