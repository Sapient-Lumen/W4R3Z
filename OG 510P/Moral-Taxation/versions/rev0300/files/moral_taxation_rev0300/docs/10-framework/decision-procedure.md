## 0. What job belongs to tax, and what belongs to another instrument?

Name the job first: **revenue, rent capture, priceable harm, floor protection, capacity-building, prefunding, or gating**. Use tax mainly for revenue, rents, and priceable harms; use transfers, services, or rebates for floors; explicit subsidy or public provision for capacity; bonds, reserves, or insurance for prefunding; and approval, caps, or refusal for catastrophic or non-compensable risk. Route: [`instrument-choice-and-non-tax-alternatives-routing.md`](instrument-choice-and-non-tax-alternatives-routing.md).[S1][S2][S4][S15][S18][S21][S29][S53][S54][S64]


## 0A. What policy action is actually needed?

Before accepting the proposal's label, classify the institutional move: tax, fee, mandate, public option, compensation, no-go rule, penalty, reporting rule, or release block. A user fee needs a special-benefit or cost/value nexus; a tax needs legislative revenue/rent/harm authority and incidence review; a mandate is required when price is not enough; a no-go rule is required where harm is non-compensable; and a public fallback is required when a private rail controls access. Route: [`policy-action-tax-fee-mandate-ban-public-option-and-compensation-routing.md`](policy-action-tax-fee-mandate-ban-public-option-and-compensation-routing.md).

## 1. What is the moral target?

Choose one or more:

- revenue for public goods
- correction of external harms
- rent capture
- anti-domination / anti-oligarchy
- reciprocity for large system beneficiaries
- emergency stabilization


## 2. What is the thing being taxed?

- person
- household
- firm
- land parcel
- resource flow
- emissions ton
- transaction
- profit stream
- compute or deployment event


## 2A. What is the assessment or filing unit?

Before moving on, name the operative human unit:

- individual person,
- joint household or couple,
- dependent-aware transfer unit,
- or beneficial-ownership / related-party group for anti-avoidance.

Default to the **individual person** for ordinary earnings and direct civic liability. Use household facts only where support targeting or anti-avoidance really requires them, then test for second-earner penalties, hidden intra-household inequality, or unsafe pooling assumptions. Route: [`assessment-units-care-and-dependency-routing.md`](assessment-units-care-and-dependency-routing.md).[S31][S32][S33]

## 2B. What beneficial-ownership or related-party group really controls the base?

Before trusting paper entity boundaries, ask whether the same real controller, family, trust structure, holding group, or coordinated affiliate network sits behind multiple taxpayers, facilities, or transactions.

- name the natural persons or genuinely independent collective that ultimately controls or benefits,
- ask whether subsidiaries, trusts, nominees, or SPVs are fragmenting one moral target into many legal shells,
- aggregate where needed to stop evasion or threshold gaming,
- but avoid over-aggregation where control and benefit are genuinely separate.[S34][S35]

Route: [`beneficial-ownership-and-anti-fragmentation-routing.md`](beneficial-ownership-and-anti-fragmentation-routing.md).

## 3. Is this base more morally attractive than ordinary labor?

If a less distortionary or more justice-sensitive base exists — land, rent, inheritance, monopoly, pollution — prefer that first.

## 3A. If this is an annual net-wealth tax, is it really a backstop and are visibility conditions strong enough?

Before leaning on annual net wealth, ask:

- have land, inheritance, anti-deferral capital-income, monopoly-rent, and similar front-line instruments already been used seriously,
- is the proposal aimed at large concentrated command wealth rather than ordinary precautionary saving,
- can beneficial ownership, related-party grouping, and major asset classes actually be seen well enough to administer the backstop honestly,
- and if illiquidity is real, does the design solve it through instalments, deferral, liens, or realization-linked settlement rather than blanket exemption?

If the answer is mostly no, strengthen the front-line concentrated-wealth taxes first or wait until visibility improves. Route: [`annual-wealth-tax-backstop-routing.md`](annual-wealth-tax-backstop-routing.md).[S5][S8][S18][S21][S23][S24][S34][S35]

## 4. Who controls the relevant behavior?

For AI especially, ask who can actually change the system:

- model developer
- cloud / compute provider
- deployer
- integrator
- downstream commercial beneficiary

Attach liability where control and benefit are real.

## 4A. Is this really a taxable harm, or a catastrophic / non-compensable risk that needs gating first?

Before pricing a harm, ask whether the activity can be governed effectively **after** it occurs. If the main danger is catastrophic, irreversible, or not realistically remediable after the fact, taxation is usually not the first instrument. Prefer some mix of:

- approval, conditional approval, or disapproval,
- licensing or permitting where ex post control is too late,
- safety cases, monitoring, and decommissioning powers,
- hard caps or tiered deployment conditions,
- and ex ante bonds, reserves, or insurance where operation is still allowed.[S15][S17][S53][S54]

Do not let a levy become a moral permission slip for a risk that should instead be constrained, tiered, or refused. Route: [`precaution-and-catastrophic-risk-routing.md`](precaution-and-catastrophic-risk-routing.md).[S15][S17][S53][S54]

## 4B. If this is a special AI levy, what exceptional trigger actually justifies it?

Before singling out AI for bespoke taxation, ask which controller-side trigger does the moral work:

- place-bound burden such as grid, water, congestion, or local-service stress,
- scarce-capacity appropriation in frontier training or large deployment,
- concentrated bottleneck rent,
- automation-linked windfall or bargaining-power displacement,
- or disproportionate draw on publicly financed inputs that may justify a bounded reciprocity contribution.

If none of these can be named clearly, default back to ordinary taxation and governance rather than inventing an AI-specific surcharge. Route: [`ai-exceptional-levy-trigger-routing.md`](ai-exceptional-levy-trigger-routing.md).[S10][S11][S13][S15][S16][S17][S22][S67]

## 4C. Is a necessary private rail doing the real fiscal work?

Before incidence and delivery are treated as ordinary implementation, ask whether the burden, remedy, proof, or payment must pass through a bank, wallet, payroll processor, tax software vendor, insurer, telecom carrier, app store, cloud provider, identity vendor, certification body, landlord, utility, or platform that affected people cannot realistically avoid.

If yes, identify:

- who controls access to the rail,
- who can pass through cost, delay, denial, or service degradation,
- who lacks exit because the rail is necessary for filing, payment, refund, benefit receipt, work, housing, connectivity, or appeal,
- whether a public, offline, assisted, or interoperable fallback exists,
- whether the channel is using legal classification to avoid the duty it performs,
- and whether the remedy should be price, rebate, duty, public option, fallback channel, or prohibition.

A private rail can be a good remittance or delivery anchor. It fails when it becomes the only practical constitution of the public tax claim. Route: [`necessary-private-rail-and-channel-incidence-routing.md`](necessary-private-rail-and-channel-incidence-routing.md).[S21][S27][S41][S64][S65][S66][S443][S444][S648]

## 5. Who is likely to bear the real burden?

Do not stop at the legal remitter. Ask which people or institutions are likely to absorb, pass through, or escape the burden:

- protected workers,
- low-income consumers,
- tenants,
- suppliers with weak bargaining power,
- monopolists with rents to spare,
- or owners of scarce and inelastic assets.

If the apparent target and the real bearer diverge, redesign the instrument or add rebates, dividends, deferral, or competition safeguards. Route: [`incidence-and-protected-burden-routing.md`](incidence-and-protected-burden-routing.md).[S4][S21]

## 5A. What remedy follows if the incidence or channel story is true?

Before rates, thresholds, or proceeds are finalized, translate the incidence finding into a remedy profile:

- the default corrective move,
- the superficially plausible move that must be blocked,
- the guardrails needed for notice, correction, timing, fallback, and review,
- the trigger for escalation to public option, injunction, no-go rule, refund reissue, release block, or route retirement,
- and the proceeds-integrity rule if money is collected in the name of repair.

A remedy word is not enough. The remedy must reach the real burden bearer through a workable channel and with authority to change the result. Route: [`remedy-traceability-and-escalation-routing.md`](remedy-traceability-and-escalation-routing.md).[S117][S118][S141][S196]

## 6. When is liability due, and against what cash flow?

Ask whether the payment schedule matches the economic reality of the base:

- frequent flow income may suit withholding or pay-as-you-go,
- recurring rents and recurrent property taxes may suit annual or periodic collection,
- illiquid but justified bases may require instalments, deferral, or liens,
- high-risk deployment may require ex ante bonds, reserves, or insurance,
- and emergency taxes require explicit start and stop conditions.

[S5][S15][S16][S17][S23][S24]

## 6B. How does the design treat real losses, negative years, and downside?

Before moving on, ask whether the proposal taxes upside and recognizes downside on terms that are at least morally intelligible:

- are genuine losses carried forward in a way that preserves enough real value to matter,
- is a bounded carry-back, refund, or refundable credit needed where liquidity or policy neutrality matters,
- are loss-making but publicly valuable or high-uncertainty activities being excluded from the intended relief because they have insufficient tax liability,
- and are anti-abuse rules aimed at artificial losses, duplicate deductions, wash-sale style timing games, or loss trafficking rather than written so broadly that genuine downside no longer counts?

A tax that seizes upside now but only grudgingly recognizes downside later becomes a one-way state call option rather than a reciprocal fiscal rule. Route: [`downside-symmetry-and-loss-routing.md`](downside-symmetry-and-loss-routing.md).[S50][S51][S52]

## 6C. Is the design taxing nominal rather than real gains, thresholds, or relief?

Before fixing rates or thresholds, ask whether inflation or nominal drift is doing hidden work:

- are brackets, allowances, credits, filing thresholds, and benefit phase-outs indexed or openly re-set,
- are capital gains, depreciation allowances, carry-forwards, basis rules, or reserve amounts being taxed or valued in ways that mistake inflation for real gain,
- are bond amounts, AI registry fees, compute thresholds, or safe harbors drifting into overreach or irrelevance because they are frozen in nominal terms,
- and if the design intentionally declines to index, is that being defended as a visible real tightening rather than left to happen by stealth?

A tax that quietly uses inflation to raise burdens or erode relief is not simpler morality; it is hidden policy through money illusion. Route: [`real-value-and-indexation-routing.md`](real-value-and-indexation-routing.md).[S50][S55][S56][S57][S58]

## 6A. What notice, transition, and retroactivity rule applies?

Before locking the design, ask how people learn about it and how they move into it:

- default to a published future effective date for major new liability,
- use phase-ins, instalments, or bounded safe harbours where legitimate reliance or system readiness really matters,
- close announcement-driven avoidance windows quickly where pure prospectivity would invite gaming,
- and keep grandfathering finite so certainty does not fossilize unjust privilege.

A tax that is attractive in substance but sprung without fair notice, or one that protects incumbent rent forever in the name of reliance, still fails moral fit. Route: [`notice-transition-and-retroactivity-routing.md`](notice-transition-and-retroactivity-routing.md).[S23][S27][S36][S37][S38]

## 7. How is the base measured or valued?

Ask whether the proposal depends on a measurement regime that is honest enough to justify coercion:

- is the base directly observable or only approximated,
- are valuations re-run on a credible cadence,
- are proxies disclosed and contestable,
- are simplified regimes limited to small taxpayers with graduation rules,
- and does the design avoid fake precision when the underlying moral target is noisy?

If the base can only be administered through stale assessments, hidden vendor models, or easy-to-game proxies, redesign the instrument before moving on. Route: [`measurement-valuation-and-proxy-routing.md`](measurement-valuation-and-proxy-routing.md).[S16][S23][S25][S26][S27]

## 7A. Can the liability be explained, corrected, and appealed without black-box dependence?

Before trusting the administrative side, ask whether the proposal can name:

- the operative rule version and effective date,
- the measured base, rate, threshold, and jurisdictional basis,
- the data sources, proxy variables, or model-assisted estimates that mattered,
- the correction path and deadlines,
- and the review or appeal lane with authority to reverse the result.

If the basis of the claim can only be discovered by reverse-engineering a vendor model or navigating an authority-starved appeal loop, the design is not ready. Machine-readable rules still need correction paths. For closure, use [`bounded-contest-issue-scoped-correction-and-period-finality-routing.md`](bounded-contest-issue-scoped-correction-and-period-finality-routing.md). Route: [`administration-explanation-and-appeal-routing.md`](administration-explanation-and-appeal-routing.md).[S1][S16][S17][S23][S25][S27][S44]

## 7B. Does the design discriminate directly or indirectly by protected status or status proxy?

Ask whether the rule:

- explicitly differentiates by sex, ethnicity, nationality, disability, family status, residence status, caste-like status, or other protected ground,
- uses apparently neutral proxies such as household defaults, documentation filters, neighborhood coding, or platform risk scores that predictably disadvantage protected groups,
- has disaggregated evidence and a public justification tied to need, harm, control, or benefit rather than administrative convenience alone,
- and provides accessible filing, refund, notice, translation, and appeal paths for groups likely to be excluded.

If a cleaner base exists, redesign. Equality is not defeated merely because the proxy is indirect, geographic, or automated. Route: [`anti-discrimination-and-status-proxy-routing.md`](anti-discrimination-and-status-proxy-routing.md).[S32][S33][S44][S45][S46]

## 8. Is there a category mistake about personhood?

If the proposal taxes a current machine system *as if it were already a civic person*, stop and ask whether the real target is instead its controller, owner, deployer, or beneficiary.[S19][S20]

## 8A. Does the direct subject have standing, remedy, and representation?

Even if the base is otherwise attractive, ask whether the supposed direct taxpayer can actually bear civic tax liability without domination:

- can it be informed, assisted, and heard,
- can it access appeal or review,
- can it act through a guardian, fiduciary, or other non-conflicted representative if it cannot act alone,
- and does it stand inside a legal order that offers some real channel of voice or proxy voice?

If not, move liability back to a controller, guardian, estate representative, owner, or beneficiary rather than pretending that a rightless or voiceless subject can carry civic tax duties on its own.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44]

## 8B. If a future artificial subject may be crossing the threshold, does the regime actually switch from property logic to person logic?

Ask whether the proposal really changes category or merely cherry-picks obligations:

- has ownership or owner-equivalent command actually ended,
- do floor protection, ordinary anti-discrimination, explanation, appeal, and non-conflicted representation arrive together with direct liability,
- is the base now person-like ability-to-pay and reciprocity rather than substrate or equipment logic,
- does the design avoid species surcharges once personhood is recognized,
- and if the threshold is still genuinely uncertain, does controller-side liability remain primary until the rights bundle is actually in place?

If the answer is no, keep the system inside controller-routed taxation rather than imposing direct civic duties first and debating rights later. Route: [`artificial-personhood-and-regime-switch-routing.md`](artificial-personhood-and-regime-switch-routing.md).[S18][S19][S20][S42][S43][S44]

## 9. Does it invite classification arbitrage?

Ask whether the proposal creates a reward for relabeling:

- human labor as contractor income or machine service,
- household income as firm profit,
- owned models as supposedly independent tax persons,
- or risky deployment as low-risk commodity inference.

A tax that rewards relabeling, nominee ownership, or shell multiplication is unstable. Route: [`classification-arbitrage-and-substance-over-form-routing.md`](classification-arbitrage-and-substance-over-form-routing.md).[S9][S19][S20][S34][S35]

## 10. What scale of government and which jurisdiction have the strongest claim?

- local
- state / province
- national
- treaty / club / transnational

Then ask which anchor is strongest for the base:

- situs for land, extractive sites, and fixed facilities,
- burdened locality for congestion, grid strain, cooling-water stress, and similar place-bound harms,
- destination for final consumption,
- market access for part of large cross-border residual profits,
- residence or organisational control for many personal and firm obligations,
- and club co-ordination where mobile rents outrun any single state.

Wrong scale or jurisdiction usually means failure or evasion. Routes: [`jurisdiction-and-scale-routing.md`](jurisdiction-and-scale-routing.md) and [`international-coordination-and-apportionment-routing.md`](international-coordination-and-apportionment-routing.md).[S1][S2][S3][S9][S15][S28][S69][S70]

## 11. What is the net fiscal stack after abatements, credits, and subsidies?

Do not evaluate the proposed tax in isolation. Ask what sits beside it:

- sectoral tax holidays or negotiated abatements,
- accelerated depreciation or special deductions,
- cheap public power, land, water, or infrastructure access,
- direct subsidies or off-budget support,
- and household-side rebates, credits, or child support that may be morally part of floor protection.

If strong incumbents are quietly reimbursed, the moral object is the **net stack**, not the rate. Distinguish floor protection from rentier carve-outs, and use [`net-fiscal-stack-and-hidden-negative-tax-routing.md`](net-fiscal-stack-and-hidden-negative-tax-routing.md).[S29][S30][S31]

## 11A. What happens if the taxpayer fails, abandons the site, or enters insolvency?

Before trusting the proposed tax, ask what happens in the failure state:

- do ordinary tax claims outrank unpaid wages, estate administration, or serious public-cleanup and victim-repair claims,
- are withheld or trust-like taxes being distinguished from ordinary assessed taxes,
- should the design have required ex ante bonds, reserves, insurance, escrow, or controller guarantees instead of relying on ex post tax priority,
- and is limited liability being used after genuine productive risk or after deliberate undercapitalization, affiliate stripping, or loss dumping?

Route: [`failure-ordering-and-limited-liability-routing.md`](failure-ordering-and-limited-liability-routing.md).[S47][S48][S49]

A tax that depends on unjust failure ordering to be collectible was designed too late.[S47][S48][S49]

## 12. How is regressivity corrected?

Use floor protection that arrives in time and through the right channel. Prefer visible correction over exemption jungles, and treat paper-only offsets as failures. See [`regressivity-correction-and-floor-protection-routing.md`](regressivity-correction-and-floor-protection-routing.md).[S3][S4][S64][S65][S66]

## 12A. Will the intended relief, rebate, or refund actually be received?

Before trusting the distributive story, ask how the offset is delivered:

- does the state already hold enough information to prefill, auto-enrol, or default-pay the relief,
- is a fresh application being required mainly out of administrative habit rather than moral necessity,
- are human-help, translation, disability, offline, and correction channels real,
- does digital identity or banking infrastructure create a choke point for the intended recipients,
- and is non-take-up measured as a design failure rather than treated as proof that support was unnecessary?

Route: [`automaticity-and-claim-friction-routing.md`](automaticity-and-claim-friction-routing.md).[S64][S65][S66][S68]

Prefer automaticity, prefill, one-step confirmation, and low-friction correction where the state already knows enough to act.[S64][S65][S66]

## 12B. Is relief as easy as compliance, and is heavy proof kept upstream?

Ask whether the design:

- keeps households and weak firms out of reconstructing facts that stronger remitters, platforms, employers, or controller groups already hold,
- avoids turning routine compliance or relief into paid-preparer, proprietary-software, or repeat-document dependence,
- and keeps richer reporting with concentrated record-holders, not downstream users or local victims.

If hidden-fact proof is the live dispute, use [prf](record-asymmetry-burden-shifting-and-adverse-inference-routing.md).

If not, redesign. Route: [par](compliance-burden-and-filing-parity-routing.md).[S27][S38][S64][S65][S66][S68]

## 13. Where do the proceeds go?

Ask whether revenue is routed toward one or more of the following:

- floor protection or equalisation,
- repair or adaptation for taxed harms,
- transition support where tech or ecological shocks are involved,
- local repair where local burdens are concentrated,
- or public capacity that reduces future dependence on bottlenecks.

If proceeds mainly disappear into unrelated patronage, incumbent subsidy loops, opaque sinkholes, or baseline-replacement theater, downgrade the proposal even if the tax base itself is attractive.[S1][S7][S22][S29]

## 14. What is the appeal, audit, and recovery path?

Ask not only whether the tax can be assessed, but how the state will pursue it once disagreement, error, arrears, or concealment appears:

- are notices intelligible before coercion accelerates,
- do correction, instalment, hardship, and offset routes come before harsher recovery,
- are strong tools aimed first at strategic evasion and concentrated control rather than weak visible debtors,
- are data matching, model-assisted triage, and automation reviewable and bounded by purpose,
- and does the system avoid floor-stripping garnishment, sweeping freezes, or surveillance creep when narrower tools would work?

Route: [`enforcement-proportionality-and-recovery-routing.md`](enforcement-proportionality-and-recovery-routing.md).[S16][S17][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44]

## 15. What review cadence, triggers, and exit path apply?

Before final approval, ask how the system learns and changes:

- what evidence would count as success, drift, capture, or failure,
- who performs the review and whether affected outsiders can challenge the official story,
- what routine cadence applies for monitoring and deeper evaluation,
- what trigger events reopen the rule early: pass-through surprises, inflation, concentration, local burden, capability jumps, avoidance, rights impacts, or failure to meet the stated goal,
- and whether the policy has a credible path to recalibration, conversion, sunset, or repeal rather than permanent inertia.

Emergency taxes especially should name an expiry event in advance, but experimental, AI-linked, or negotiated tax regimes also need explicit review architecture when uncertainty or concentrated power is high.[S15][S54][S59][S60][S61][S62][S63]

## Case-contract pass

After selecting candidate routes and remedy profiles, check whether a golden case contract matches the facts. If it does, carry forward the expected route IDs, route-backed flags, raw-axis obligations, required remedy profiles, sources, and must-not answers before writing the final answer. A response that names a nearby route but loses the contract obligations is a regression.

## Actor-accountability pass

Before finalizing liability, remedy, or policy-action classification, identify the accountability chain:

1. primary accountable actor;
2. secondary accountable actors;
3. non-responsible adjacent actors;
4. real burden bearers;
5. beneficiary or rent recipient;
6. bottleneck/channel actor;
7. default liability or duty move;
8. public fallback-duty owner; and
9. evidence required to avoid title-based guilt or conduit punishment.

Use [`actor-accountability-controller-beneficiary-bottleneck-and-fallback-duty-routing.md`](actor-accountability-controller-beneficiary-bottleneck-and-fallback-duty-routing.md) and [`actor-accountability-responsibility-chain-beneficiary-bottleneck-and-fallback-duty-ladder.md`](../20-calibration/actor-accountability-responsibility-chain-beneficiary-bottleneck-and-fallback-duty-ladder.md). The answer must not compensate the wrong actor, punish a mere conduit, erase a beneficiary, or outsource a protected floor without a public fallback.
