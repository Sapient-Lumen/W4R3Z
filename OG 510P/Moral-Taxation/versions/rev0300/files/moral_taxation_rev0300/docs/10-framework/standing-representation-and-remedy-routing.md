# Standing, representation, and remedy routing

This note answers the question that comes **after** a tax base and remittance anchor look morally defensible but **before** the state can impose direct civic tax liability on the supposed subject: **who may be taxed in its own name, and when must liability stay with a controller, guardian, fiduciary, or beneficiary instead?** The archive's stable answer is that direct subjection normally requires an intelligible rights bundle — notice, remedy, representation, and some channel of voice — and that where this bundle is missing, liability should route through accountable humans or institutions rather than through a rightless placeholder.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44]

## Core rule

Route direct tax subjecthood through this order:

1. **ask whether the supposed subject can receive intelligible notice and understand the asserted basis of liability at some meaningful level**,  
2. **ask whether it can access review, appeal, correction, or a comparably real remedy**,  
3. **ask whether it can act through a non-conflicted guardian, fiduciary, or representative where direct self-action is impossible**,  
4. **ask whether it stands inside a legal order with some channel of representation, contestation, or proxy voice**,  
5. and **if that bundle is absent, route liability to a controller, guardian, owner, estate representative, or beneficiary instead of pretending that direct civic tax status exists**.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44]

A good standing rule does not demand perfect autonomy from every taxpayer. It demands that direct subjection not outrun the minimum institutional conditions that make taxation something other than domination.

## Default routing by subject or context

| Subject or context | Best first standing answer | Why this answer usually wins | Main warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary adult human person | direct subject with ordinary notice, appeal, and assistance rights | persons ordinarily belong inside the civic order that taxes them | simplification should assist, not silently displace their ability to know or contest liability.[S42][S43][S44] |
| Child or incapacitated adult | direct rights-bearing subject with guardian or fiduciary representation | personhood persists even when legal agency is mediated | representation must protect the subject, not merely the convenience of the household head or institution.[S42][S43][S44] |
| Estate, trust, pension, or similar legal wrapper | usually mediated liability through fiduciaries and beneficiary rules | these are administered entities rather than autonomous civic persons | do not let wrappers become voice-free shelters or dumping grounds for unreviewable tax burdens.[S18][S34][S42][S44] |
| Corporation or collective legal person | direct subject where review, disclosure, and representation channels are real | legal systems can justly tax organized collective actors when governance and remedy exist | do not confuse corporate standing with proof that every affiliate or shell deserves separate treatment.[S9][S20][S28][S42] |
| Current LLM or owned model instance | **not** a direct civic subject; route liability to controllers and beneficiaries | present systems lack settled standing, remedy, and non-owner representation | do not mistake technical agency, conversational fluency, or dashboard identity for civic tax personhood.[S10][S19][S20][S42][S43][S44] |
| Future rights-bearing artificial person, if threshold is crossed | direct subject only together with floor protection, remedy, and non-owner representation | personhood without standing and remedy would still be domination | do not preserve property logic after rights-bearing status is recognized.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44] |
| High-volume automated platform collection acting for weak humans | keep humans as subjects but automate remittance or relief through the platform | collection may be easier upstream while subjecthood remains with the person | remittance convenience is not a license to erase taxpayer standing or appeal rights.[S16][S17][S42][S44] |

## Routing rules

### 1. Separate the tax base from the direct taxpayer

A thing can be the right **base** without being the right **direct subject**. Children, estates, firms, trusts, and current AI systems can all be relevant to what gets taxed without all being equally appropriate bearers of direct civic tax liability in their own names.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44]

### 2. Notice without remedy is not enough

A notice that cannot realistically be contested, corrected, or interpreted by the subject or its representative does not complete the moral case for direct subjection. Tax systems should therefore pair intelligibility with a real review path, not just with formal service.[S42][S44]

### 3. Representation must be non-conflicted enough to count

Guardians, trustees, managers, owners, and deployers do not automatically count as morally adequate representatives. The archive asks whether the representative is there to protect the subject's position inside the tax order or merely to maximize extraction from it. This matters for incapacitated persons, fiduciary entities, and especially any future artificial subjects.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44]

### 3.5 Representation requires a usable authorization and revocation lane

A representative does not count if the subject cannot practically authorize, limit, inspect, or revoke that representative's tax role. Professional eligibility rules, privacy safeguards, and fiduciary checks may be legitimate; a paid proxy gate, captive preparer account, owner-controlled delegation key, or sticky access trap is not adequate standing.[S42][S43][S44][S141][S142][S143]

### 3.6 Household status cannot substitute for voice

A spouse, former spouse, parent, caregiver, dependent claimant, or household account-holder does not automatically represent the affected person merely because a joint return, dependency record, custody fact, or shared address links them. Where the relationship is conflicted, separated, abusive, debt-divided, or record-asymmetric, person-level notice and relief channels matter more, not less.[S31][S32][S33][S42][S161][S162][S164][S165][S166][S167]

### 3.7 Remedy includes a preserved window when delay was not real waiver

A remedy is not real if the subject loses the right because an ordinary extension, postponement, retransmission, disaster, outage, or reasonable-cause lane was inaccessible or fee-bearing. Where the person supplies a concrete attempt, affected deadline, and bounded impossibility fact, standing should preserve the window long enough for review rather than treating the missed date as consent to liability.[S16][S17][S27][S39][S42][S168][S170][S172][S173][S174][S175]

### 3.8 Paid preparation does not substitute for subject-side voice

A preparer, e-file originator, payroll-service preparer, or refund-product intermediary may help with compliance, but that helper does not become the taxpayer's civic voice merely by holding records, filing credentials, a refund account, or the practical path to correction. Where preparer misconduct is credibly alleged, standing requires a no-rent way for the subject to inspect, counterstate, revoke, and correct the filing record.[S42][S44][S141][S142][S143][S197][S198][S199][S200][S201][S202]


### 3.9 Remedy includes no-rent deadlock escalation and low-income dispute help

A remedy does not count merely because a form, portal, or appeal right exists somewhere in the system. When ordinary channels have failed, hardship is live, the agency has repeatedly not responded, or the subject cannot afford competent dispute help, standing requires a visible no-rent path to escalation, advocate contact, or independent free / small-fee clinic help. Otherwise paid tax-resolution markets and captive representatives become the practical voice of the taxpayer.[S42][S141][S212][S213][S214][S215][S216]

### 3.10 Private collection contact cannot replace public standing

A taxpayer assigned to a private collection contractor still needs public standing to verify the assignment, use representation, request no contact or return to the agency, seek hardship / clinic / TAS help, preserve appeal and correction rights, and pay through public rails. Contractor contact is not a substitute for remedy against the public claim.[S133][S141][S142][S143][S212][S215][S216][S217][S218][S219][S220][S221][S222]

### 3.11 Prepayment court access belongs to the remedy bundle

A direct tax subject has not received a real remedy if a petitionable notice gives a nominal court path but the subject loses it through an unclear Tax Court deadline, inaccessible filing fee or waiver, hidden small-case option, portal-only filing, stale address, or confusion between agency response and court petition. Where prepayment review is the remedy path, standing includes a usable way to preserve it.[S42][S44][S117][S118][S160][S232][S233][S235][S236][S237][S238][S239]

### 3.13 Credit claim, audit, and recertification standing stays with the claimant

A low-income worker, caregiver, separated parent, student, or dependent-linked person should keep direct standing to claim, defend, recertify, appeal, or reconsider floor-protecting credits. Preparer due diligence, household records, payer misclassification, school statements, or prior disallowance records may supply evidence, but they do not replace the claimant's own no-rent remedy path.[S21][S31][S32][S33][S39][S41][S42][S64][S65][S66][S117][S118][S160][S276][S279][S280][S281][S283][S286][S287][S290]

### 3.14 Third parties whose property is marked or seized need their own remedy

A person whose property, wages, account, receivable, title, or customer payment is reached through someone else's tax debt is not merely a witness. They need status to receive the theory, contest the property link, seek release or return, and route the dispute through wrongful-levy, CAP, lien, hardship, or equivalent review channels. Without that remedy, third-party collection becomes direct tax subjection without representation by another name.[S39][S41][S42][S184][S185][S186][S330][S331][S333][S335][S338]

### 4. Current AI remains controller-routed

The archive's controller-first position is strengthened, not weakened, by standing analysis. Present LLMs may be economically significant and operationally agentic, yet they still lack the settled legal standing, independent counsel, non-owner representation, and civic membership that would make direct taxation something other than category error. Tax facilities, labs, clouds, deployers, grouped controllers, and beneficiaries instead.[S10][S15][S19][S20][S42][S43][S44]

### 5. Keep withholding and prefilling from becoming standing-erasure

Withholding, prefill, platform remittance, and automatic correction can be excellent administrative devices, but they should assist the taxpayer rather than replace the taxpayer as the bearer of intelligible notice and challenge rights. Administrative convenience is not the same thing as legitimate substitution of subjecthood.[S16][S17][S42][S44]

### 6. Use mediated subjecthood where personhood is real but agency is partial

The archive allows direct subjecthood with representation for children, some incapacitated adults, estates, and some collective entities because the underlying rights order remains intact. The lesson is not "only fully autonomous beings may be taxed." The lesson is "do not assign direct civic liability where personhood, representation, and remedy are absent together."[S42][S43][S44]

### 7. Upgrade standing and tax status together or not at all

If some future artificial system genuinely becomes a rights-bearing civic subject, standing, floor protection, remedy, non-owner representation, and taxation should move together. The archive rejects the one-sided shortcut where obligations arrive first and rights later.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44]
### 6. Bankruptcy-tax status still belongs to the subject's remedy bundle

Bankruptcy counsel can be valuable and often prudent, but the subject should retain public access to tax-account status, Centralized Insolvency contact, alleged stay / discharge violation claims, refund-hold explanation, and cancellation-of-debt reporting routes. Representation cannot become the price of knowing whether the public tax claim survived, paused, or was discharged.[S42][S65][S66][S180][S249][S250][S251][S253][S255][S256][S257]


### 3.10 Nonfilers keep subject-side standing to reconstruct the return

Failure to file on time does not erase the subject's standing to see the records used against them, file the actual return, authorize help, seek TAS / LITC assistance, petition where a deficiency notice is live, or request the right post-assessment correction lane. A substitute return is not a substitute voice.[S117][S118][S160][S180][S183][S212][S213][S214][S238][S258][S263][S264]
### 3.12 Workers keep status-correction standing

A payer's contractor label, platform category, or 1099 should not erase the worker's standing to request worker-status determination, make a bounded employee-status counterstatement, report the worker-side employment-tax share where appropriate, and have wage / payroll records corrected without employer permission or paid classification gatekeeping.[S75][S76][S154][S160][S267][S268][S270][S271][S272][S273]


## Five anti-patterns

1. **Chatbot personhood theatre** — treating conversational fluency or task autonomy as enough for direct civic tax status.  
2. **Owner as representative by default** — assuming the economically interested controller automatically supplies the representation bundle.  
2.5. **Proxy-access formalism** — treating a representative as valid even though authorization, limitation, or revocation runs through a paid or captive gate.  
2.6. **Household-voice fiction** — treating the joint filer, spouse, former spouse, parent, caregiver, or account-holder as the affected person's tax voice where the relationship is conflicted or relief-specific.  
3. **Remittance equals subjecthood** — confusing the easiest collection point with the morally proper taxpayer.  
4. **Appeal in name only** — notices and portals exist, but the subject or representative cannot realistically understand or use them.  
5. **Rights-later taxation** — imposing direct obligations on unusual subjects before giving them standing, remedy, and voice.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44]
6. **Deadline-as-waiver** — treating a missed date caused by inaccessible extension, outage, disaster, rejected submission, or reasonable-cause impossibility as consent to liability or loss of remedy.[S168][S170][S172][S173][S174][S175]

7. **Inspection without voice** — examination notices, interviews, copy submission, manager contact, appeal, or reconsideration exist formally but cannot be used by the affected subject or non-conflicted representative without paid access.[S191][S192][S193][S194][S195][S196]
8. **Preparer as substitute voice** — a private preparer's filed return, refund account, records, or portal access is treated as the taxpayer's voice even after credible alteration, nonfiling, ghost preparation, or diversion facts appear.[S197][S198][S199][S200][S201][S202]
9. **Paid rescue as voice** — the system treats a commercial tax-resolution firm or premium status broker as the only practical way for the affected subject to make a stuck case heard.[S212][S213][S214][S215][S216]
10. **Collector as voice** — the public agency treats the private collector as the only practical interface for verification, payment, hardship, recall, representation, or dispute status.[S217][S218][S219][S220][S222]

10. **Nonfiler voice deletion** — a late or missing return is treated as loss of standing, so only the agency-computed return, a paid preparer, or a collection vendor can speak for the case.[S258][S263][S264]
- **status-hostage standing** — the worker cannot be heard on payroll status, wage records, or bounded employment-tax reporting unless the payer first consents to correct the label.[S267][S270][S271][S272][S273]

- **credit-standing erasure** — the practical right to claim or restore a credit sits with a preparer, adverse household actor, payer, school, or software workflow instead of the claimant.[S276][S279][S281][S286][S287][S290]

11. **Summons without voice** — records or testimony about the affected subject are compelled from a third party before the subject, non-conflicted representative, or noticee has a meaningful notice, quash, privilege, or correction route.[S340][S345][S348][S349][S350]

## Order of preference

When more than one liability route is available, the archive usually prefers the route that is:

1. most compatible with real notice and review,
2. clearest about who represents whom,
3. least dependent on conflicted owners or controllers,
4. strongest at keeping remittance convenience separate from moral subjecthood,
5. easiest to explain publicly as an exercise of civic authority rather than mere extraction,
6. and easiest to upgrade later if the subject's legal or moral status genuinely changes.

This is why ordinary persons with assistance, mediated rights-bearing subjects with fiduciary protection, and controller-routed AI taxation usually beat both fictionally autonomous machine taxation and purely owner-defined representation.

## Rev0270 criminal-tax boundary addendum

Direct standing matters most when criminal exposure is possible. A person facing fraud development, referral, joint investigation, voluntary disclosure, restitution, or probation-linked civil re-entry needs non-conflicted representation, clear POA / authorization handling, privilege respect, and a way to distinguish self-correction from confession. Spouses, employees, preparer clients, payroll signers, platform users, and future rights-bearing artificial persons must not depend on the potentially culpable actor's counsel or portal to preserve their own voice.[S358][S359][S360][S363][S364][S365]

The standing bundle therefore includes direct notice of posture, ability to consult counsel, issue-scoped silence / privilege protections, access to records needed for civil correction, and a payment-credit / restitution reconciliation path after criminal closure.[S357][S358][S362]


## Rev0271 dual-standing whistleblower addendum

Whistleblower standing is an award-process bundle: claim submission, limited status information, preliminary award procedures, response, determination, and possible Tax Court award review. It is not standing to control the taxpayer's merits case, force disclosure of the taxpayer's return file, veto settlement, or preserve penalties to enlarge an award.[S371][S372][S375][S376]

Accused-taxpayer standing is the merits-and-remedy bundle: notice, representation, source-protected but fact-contestable proof, appeal, petition, abatement, refund, collection due process, innocent-owner or third-party remedies, and civil/criminal posture protections. Neither bundle consumes the other.[S375][S377][S381]

### Signatory authority, foreign trustees, and cross-border remedy

International reporting can assign duties to people who are not the economic owner in an ordinary sense: signers over employer accounts, U.S. owners whose foreign trust must file, beneficiaries who receive statements late, constructive owners of foreign entities, or household members with inherited accounts abroad. Standing and remedy must track this split. A person who is punished for a foreign report must have access to the underlying record, a way to explain limited control, and a route to contest willfulness, penalty unit, and reasonable cause.[S410][S413][S419][S420][S421][S422][S423][S424]

This rule matters especially for immigrants, expatriates, dual residents, and vulnerable family members whose cross-border facts can be held by foreign institutions or relatives. Representation, translation, and record-access limits should be treated as remedy facts, not excuses to accelerate penalty collection.[S409][S430][S173][S432]
