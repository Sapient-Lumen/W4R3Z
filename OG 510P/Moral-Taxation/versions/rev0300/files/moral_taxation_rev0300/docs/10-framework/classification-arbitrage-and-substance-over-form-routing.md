# Classification arbitrage and substance-over-form routing

This note answers the question that comes **after** a morally attractive base has been chosen and **before** category labels are allowed to dissolve it: **when should tax law ignore the name of an arrangement and follow its real substance instead?** The archive's stable answer is that category choice is not morally innocent. A tax fails when it is easy to relabel labor as capital service, household income as firm flow, firm profit as fee flow, owned AI systems as fake independent persons, visible users or thin resellers as fake upstream controllers, high-risk deployment as low-risk commodity inference, or white-label packaging or vendor-hosting stories as proof of independent control in order to escape the burden that the design was actually trying to place.[S9][S10][S15][S16][S17][S19][S20][S21][S28][S34][S35][S44][S83]

## Core rule

Route classification disputes through this order:

1. **name the real moral variable first** — labor, rent, control, benefit, harm, congestion, inheritance, or ordinary consumption,
2. **ask who actually works, controls, bears downside, captures residual upside, and can stop, continue, update, or redesign the activity**,  
3. **treat labels, contracts, wrappers, and interface stories as rebuttable rather than decisive**,  
4. **preserve genuinely different arrangements when substance really differs, rather than collapsing everything into one category for convenience**,  
5. **keep reclassification legible, evidence-based, and reviewable**,  
6. refuse to manufacture personhood, independence, or low-risk status merely because a stronger actor benefits from the relabeling, and
7. **do not let version resets, paper successors, or affiliate transfers erase materially continuous duties**, and
8. **do not let temporary step-in command hide inside an "outside review" label once the actor is actually running the live system**,
9. **do not let mission-setting buyers hide inside a "mere customer" label once they govern a dedicated deployment through procurement or command rights**, and
10. **do not let committee, consortium, foundation, or DAO-like governance hide inside a "nobody controls this" label when a real coalition still governs the live stack**.[S9][S16][S17][S19][S20][S21][S27][S28][S34][S35][S41][S44][S83]

A good anti-arbitrage rule follows substance far enough to keep the chosen base intact, but no farther than necessary to avoid erasing real pluralism.

## Default routing by classification problem

| Classification problem | Best first substance test | Why this test usually wins | Main warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Worker labeled as independent contractor, platform seller, or vendor | behavioral control, financial control, relationship facts, dependence, and who controls market access / pricing / reporting | ordinary labor can be stripped of withholding, payroll-tax sharing, wage records, and floor protection by contract language alone | do not let payroll avoidance or platform-label theater masquerade as entrepreneurial freedom when the worker's real dependence has not changed.[S21][S31][S32][S33][S44][S75][S76][S267][S268][S269][S273] |
| Household income routed through shells, family firms, or nominee vehicles | beneficial owner, control map, and who really uses or enjoys the income | household domination and tax avoidance can both hide behind formal entity lines | do not let person-based civic liability quietly turn into household shadowing or shell-mediated income splitting.[S31][S32][S33][S34][S35] |
| Corporate profit relabeled as royalties, management fees, cost-sharing, or affiliate service revenue | residual-profit and control analysis across the related-party group | paper invoice paths often tell a thinner story than who controls intangibles, financing, and market power | do not let accounting choreography defeat rent taxation or minimum-tax design.[S9][S21][S28][S34][S35] |
| Owned AI system presented as an independent vendor or proto-person | ownership, training / deployment control, shutdown power, beneficiary chain, and standing threshold | current AI systems remain tools or controlled stacks unless real rights-bearing personhood is crossed | do not let commercial actors manufacture fake machine independence to escape controller-side liability.[S10][S15][S16][S17][S19][S20] |
| High-risk deployment labeled as ordinary low-risk inference or generic software service | real capability, deployment context, externality map, and catastrophic-risk profile | risk labels can be gamed downward more easily than the underlying danger changes | do not let cosmetic service descriptions launder a system past precaution, bonding, or stronger oversight.[S15][S16][S17][S53][S54] |
| Renamed model line, checkpoint migration, affiliate transfer, or asset sale presented as a wholly fresh taxpayer story | continuity of governance, users, update channels, beneficiary chain, and inherited duties | version labels and transaction form often change faster than the real controller or deployment does | do not let paper resets wipe threshold history, reserve duties, or incident-linked obligations that still belong to a materially continuous stack.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S41][S83] |
| White-label embedding, OEM packaging, or franchise branding presented as proof that control sits entirely with the visible seller or entirely with the invisible supplier | who governs updates, safety posture, workflow, customer promises, incident handling, and continuation for each layer | branding often shifts faster than durable governance, so logo maps can hide either upstream control or downstream co-controller duties | do not let labels or customer frontage erase the actor that actually governs the live layer in dispute.[S10][S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S83] |
| Receiver, incident manager, or emergency step-in operator labeled as a mere outside adviser while holding live command powers | who can currently pause, continue, restart, roll back, or reconfigure the deployment from inside the loop | emergency titles are easier to relabel than the fact of live operational command | do not let temporary control disappear behind advisory or custodial language once the actor is really governing the live layer.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S41][S44][S83] |
| Commissioning buyer or principal labeled as a passive customer while governing a dedicated vendor-hosted deployment | who sets the standing mission, service levels, approvals, escalation rights, retrieval or tool scope, and stop / continue authority for the deployment | contract form and vendor hosting can hide the fact that mission control sits with the buyer | do not let procurement or managed-service language erase a principal that is really governing the live layer in dispute.[S10][S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S41][S44][S83] |
| Consortium, foundation, steering committee, or DAO-like stack labeled as uncontrolled because no one actor dominates it | who can jointly amend rules, control treasury, gate updates, appoint operators, or pause / continue the live deployment | split governance can hide real command behind a fiction of ownerlessness or mere community process | do not let a controller group disappear just because its power is distributed across several linked actors.[S20][S21][S34][S35][S41][S83] |
| Local burden recast as remote or cloud-only activity | facility, energy, water, congestion, labor, and site dependence | place-bound burdens still exist even when revenue is booked elsewhere or the service is branded as global | do not let digital rhetoric erase local taxing claims for real infrastructure strain.[S3][S5][S15][S28][S69][S70] |

## Routing rules

### 1. Choose the category that best matches control, benefit, and risk

When category labels diverge from the real activity, prefer the classification that tracks who directs the conduct, captures the upside, and can internalize the downside. Tax morality cares about those facts more than about whichever label a stronger party can cheaply print into a contract.[S9][S16][S17][S20][S21][S28]

Worker status uses this rule in its most ordinary form. A payer's 1099, app category, invoice template, or independent-contractor recital is not enough where behavioral control, financial control, relationship facts, or platform dependence point toward employment-tax treatment; status correction needs a public determination / bounded reporting lane rather than a paid classification market.[S75][S76][S267][S268][S269][S270][S271][S272][S273]

### 2. Keep anti-fragmentation and anti-relabeling distinct but coordinated

Fragmentation asks whether one real actor has been split into many paper actors. Relabeling asks whether the same activity has been moved into a softer category without any real change in substance. Both matter, but they are not the same failure mode. A proposal can group shells correctly and still fail if it rewards relabeling labor, rent, or AI control into a cheaper tax story.[S21][S28][S34][S35]

### 3. Do not manufacture machine personhood for tax convenience

Present LLMs and deployed AI systems may be technically autonomous in narrow senses while still lacking the standing bundle that would justify direct civic tax subjecthood. Until that threshold is actually crossed, owned or controlled systems should not be reclassified into independent taxpayers merely because doing so would reduce controller liability.[S10][S19][S20][S42][S43][S44]

### 4. Substance-over-form should not erase real difference

Not every contractor is a disguised employee, not every small firm is a payroll dodge, and not every automated system is an owned lab stack. The archive prefers the narrowest anti-relabeling rule that protects the chosen base without collapsing genuinely distinct arrangements into one compliance bucket.[S21][S26][S27][S31][S32][S33]

### 5. Risk tiers should follow real capability and deployment facts

The same discipline applies to corrective taxes, reserves, and frontier-AI levies. If the real system, workload, or deployment pattern creates the externality or catastrophic-risk profile, then cosmetic descriptions like "general compute," "ordinary inference," or "decision support only" do not settle the moral category.[S15][S16][S17][S53][S54]

### 6. Step-in control beats outside-review theater

A receiver, incident manager, or emergency step-in operator does not stay a mere outsider once it can actually pause, continue, roll back, restart, or reconfigure the live deployment. Substance-over-form should follow the current command facts for the governed layer and period rather than the softer title on the engagement letter or emergency order.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S41][S44][S83]

### 7. Commissioned mission control beats passive-customer theater

A buyer, principal, or public body does not stay a mere customer once it sets the dedicated system's mission, service levels, approvals, escalation path, or stop / continue authority while a vendor mainly hosts or maintains the machinery. Substance-over-form should follow the actor governing the dedicated deployment rather than whichever party owns the generic platform name or operating contract.[S10][S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S41][S44][S83]

### 8. Successor continuity beats paper reset theater

A new version label, checkpoint family name, affiliate owner, or asset-sale document does not by itself create a fresh fiscal story. When governance, users, revenue, update channels, and unresolved duties remain materially continuous, substance-over-form should treat the stack as successor-continuous for controller-side tax purposes unless a visible rule chooses otherwise.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S41][S83]

### 9. Reclassification needs notice, explanation, and review

Substance-over-form cannot become a permission slip for arbitrary recharacterization by the state. If the administration reclassifies a worker, a flow of income, a controller chain, or an AI deployment category, it should name the operative test, the decisive facts, and the route for challenge or correction.[S16][S17][S23][S27][S44]

### 8. Distributed governance does not equal nobody-governs

A consortium, foundation, protocol council, or DAO-like stack does not become tax-subjectless merely because no one seat dominates alone. If a coalition can jointly change rules, control treasury, appoint operators, gate releases, or pause and continue the live system, substance-over-form should follow that controller group rather than the rhetoric of ownerlessness or community process.[S20][S21][S34][S35][S41][S83]

## Five anti-patterns

1. **Contract worship** — assuming the printed label decides the tax category even when control, risk, and dependency tell a different story.  
2. **Household laundering** — hiding person-based income or domination inside family entities, nominee accounts, or dependent-status defaults.  
3. **Fee-flow alchemy** — turning profit into deductible service or royalty payments through related-party choreography.  
4. **Fake machine independence** — presenting owned or tightly controlled AI systems as though they were separate civic persons or external vendors.  
5. **Risk downgrading by description** — calling a high-impact deployment a low-risk commodity service without changing the real capability or externality map.  
6. **outside-review camouflage** — calling a step-in receiver or incident manager a mere overseer after live command has already moved.  
7. **procurement laundering** — calling a mission-setting principal a mere customer because a vendor still hosts or operates the stack.[S9][S10][S15][S16][S17][S19][S20][S21][S27][S28][S31][S32][S33][S34][S35][S41][S44]

## Order of preference

When more than one classification rule is available, the archive usually prefers the one that:

1. follows real control, benefit, and risk rather than the strongest party's chosen label,
2. preserves person-based treatment of ordinary human earnings unless genuine independence is present,
3. prevents profit, rent, or AI-control laundering through related-party or wrapper structures,
4. refuses to treat current AI systems as direct civic tax persons merely for convenience,
5. and remains legible enough that both taxpayers and administrators can say why the classification was chosen.

This is why classification integrity belongs in the narrow waist rather than living only as an open question or one step in the decision procedure.

### Foreign entity labels are visibility labels, not automatic substance findings

Foreign corporation, partnership, disregarded-entity, branch, related-party, trust, and account labels are sometimes used to conceal income or shift value. They are also common in ordinary cross-border commerce and family life. Form 5471, Form 5472, Form 8865, Form 8858, Form 3520, Form 3520-A, Form 8938, and FBAR labels should therefore trigger substance review only after the file identifies the actual control, benefit, income, transaction, and record-holder facts.[S416][S419][S421][S423][S424][S425][S426][S427]

The anti-arbitrage move is to follow real control and income, not to treat the word foreign as a trump card. If the foreign form shows only visibility, the proper response is correction or additional proof. If it shows a sham, nominee, related-party distortion, or willful concealment, escalation can proceed on those facts.[S407][S408][S410][S413][S432]
