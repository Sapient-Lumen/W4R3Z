# Gambling, prediction-market, event-contract, and addiction routing

## Question in one sentence

When wagering, sports betting, prediction markets, or event contracts blur gambling, financial trading, hedging, and information aggregation, how should fiscal design protect public revenue without monetizing addiction or laundering jurisdiction?[S634][S635][S644]

## Core rule

Event markets can price information and hedge real exposures, but the label “derivative” cannot erase gambling-like behavioral risk, state-law conflict, insider information, match-fixing, or youth/addiction exposure. Tax and fee design should charge real externalities and integrity costs while banning or gating contracts that sell harm rather than risk transfer.[S634][S635][S644]

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| contract references sports, elections, war, assassination, terrorism, or gaming | public-interest gate | route through CFTC/event-contract and state-law conflict review before treating it as ordinary finance. |
| platform claims hedging but users mostly wager retail outcomes | function gate | test hedgeable exposure, market purpose, marketing, and loss concentration. |
| sports or political insiders can trade | integrity gate | require identity, restricted-person controls, surveillance, and void/restitution rules. |
| tax revenue depends on high-volume addictive play | addiction-rent gate | fund treatment and self-exclusion before counting revenue as clean. |
| federal derivative status conflicts with state gambling limits | jurisdiction gate | do not let classification arbitrage nullify floor protections. |
