# Data extraction, privacy, and ad-market rent routing

## Question in one sentence

When platforms, data brokers, ad-tech intermediaries, or AI systems collect, combine, infer, target, or monetize personal and behavioral data, when is taxation justified as rent or harm repair rather than a crude data toll?[S459][S460][S461]

Route: data is not taxable merely because it exists. Tax or charge extraction rents, privacy harms, gatekeeper bottlenecks, behavioral-ad surplus, or public repair needs only after data minimization, consent quality, competition, and non-tax rules are considered.[S459][S460]

## Core rule

The protected subject is the person whose data, attention, inference, or vulnerability is monetized. The tax base should not be a head tax on being observed. It should track the extractor's surplus, market power, harmful processing, or use of public/legal infrastructure to monetize private life.

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| ordinary data processing with real consent and low risk | data-minimization route | no special tax merely for processing. |
| behavioral advertising or auction rent | ad-market rent gate | levy rent or excess surplus; consider competition remedy first. |
| gatekeeper combines cross-service data | gatekeeper/evidence gate | regulatory restriction, data-separation duty, or rent charge. |
| sensitive inference or protected-status targeting | harm gate | prohibition or strong regulation before revenue collection. |
| data broker monetizes non-transparent profiles | transparency/remedy gate | registry, access/correction rights, harm fee, or ban for non-compensable uses. |
| public data or legal compulsion feeds private monetization | public-input route | commons repair, access limits, or public-capacity proceeds. |

## Anti-patterns

- **consent theater** — formal clickwrap is treated as moral permission for extraction.
- **privacy sale** — a tax is used to sell permission for non-compensable surveillance harm.
- **data head tax** — people are effectively taxed for being observed while extractor rents remain hidden.
- **gatekeeper laundering** — competition and privacy problems are reframed as ordinary advertising revenue.
