# Implementation audit, tax expenditure evaluation, and program-integrity routing

## Question in one sentence

When a tax expenditure, credit, subsidy, enforcement program, registry, or automated tax-administration tool is adopted, what audit and evidence duties are needed so the rule does not become performance theater or a black-box subsidy?[S532][S533][S534][S535][S536]

Route: every material tax preference, credit, emergency program, compliance regime, or administrative AI tool needs an evidence plan: named objective, baseline, beneficiary/incidence data, cost, take-up, error/fraud rate, protected-floor effects, public reporting, independent audit, and sunset/repair trigger.[S534][S535]

## Core rule

A moral tax rule is incomplete until it can be audited. The archive should reject rules whose purpose, cost, distribution, error burden, and review trigger are hidden or non-falsifiable, especially when the instrument is a tax expenditure rather than a direct appropriation.

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| tax credit or deduction substitutes for spending | tax-expenditure gate | cost, benchmark, beneficiary, and comparable direct-spending review. |
| program claims job, climate, housing, or innovation gains | metric gate | ex ante target and counterfactual. |
| compliance program uses risk scoring or automation | explainability gate | audit log, appeal, bias test, and human correction. |
| improper payments or false claims rise | integrity gate | root-cause review, targeted controls, and floor-sensitive recovery.[S532][S533] |
| data unavailable | sunset gate | temporary authority until reporting exists. |

## Anti-patterns

- **performance theater** — claimed purpose is not measurable or not measured.
- **black-box subsidy** — tax expenditure beneficiaries and costs are hidden from ordinary budget review.
- **auditless automation** — risk scores impose burdens without explanation or correction.
- **integrity overcorrection** — fraud controls cut off eligible protected recipients.
