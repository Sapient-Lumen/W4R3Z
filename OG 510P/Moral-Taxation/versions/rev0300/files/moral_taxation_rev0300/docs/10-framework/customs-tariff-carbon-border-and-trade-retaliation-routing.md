# Customs, tariff, carbon-border, and trade-retaliation routing

## Question in one sentence

When a jurisdiction uses tariffs, customs duties, carbon border adjustments, de minimis rules, platform collection, import fees, or trade retaliation, how should the archive separate legitimate border-base protection from hidden consumer taxes, administrative traps, and trade-rent theater?[S462][S463][S464][S465][S466]

Route: border instruments are tax instruments with trade consequences. They must state their base, incidence, administrative proof path, exemption logic, retaliation risk, and proceeds repair; carbon border measures must track embedded emissions and domestic carbon-cost equivalence rather than become industrial-policy camouflage.[S462][S463]

## Core rule

A tariff is not automatically illegitimate and not automatically moral. It can price carbon leakage, protect a real domestic base, collect VAT/GST on imports, or defend against evasion; it can also hide consumer taxes, protect incumbents, invite retaliation, or punish low-capacity exporters through proof demands.

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| carbon leakage / domestic carbon-price equivalence | CBAM gate | embedded-emissions proof, credit for foreign carbon price, transition/equalization review. |
| low-value e-commerce revenue leakage | de minimis/platform gate | platform/intermediary collection before consumer trap where administrable.[S465][S466] |
| tariff for ordinary revenue | incidence gate | disclose consumer/pass-through burden and rebate if regressive. |
| national-security or retaliation claim | non-tax alternatives and review gate | narrow scope, sunset, retaliation-risk review, and legislative visibility. |
| proof burden on low-capacity exporters | capacity gate | default factors, technical assistance, safe harbor, or exemption rather than exclusion. |
| trade measure shelters domestic rent | rent/protection gate | reject or replace with direct subsidy/transition instrument if public purpose is honest. |

## Anti-patterns

- **hidden consumer tax** — tariff costs are politically described as paid by foreigners while ordinary consumers bear them.
- **carbon border camouflage** — climate language hides sectoral protection without equivalent domestic carbon cost.[S462]
- **de minimis whiplash** — low-value import rules change abruptly and make consumers or small sellers the compliance shock absorber.
- **retaliation theater** — border taxes are used as symbolic punishment without review of domestic incidence and countermeasures.
