# Tax morale, state capacity, and political-economy routing

## Question in one sentence

Before adopting a tax, penalty, filing channel, exemption, abatement, AI enforcement tool, or simplification proxy, how should the archive test whether the rule can survive real administration, public trust, capture pressure, and weak-state capacity without becoming extraction from the floor?[S450][S451][S452]

## Core rule

A morally elegant tax can fail if it requires capacity the state does not have, invites capture the archive ignores, or teaches taxpayers that compliance is arbitrary. Tax morale and state capacity are not soft context; they are part of incidence and legitimacy.[S450][S451][S452]

## Political-economy screen

| Screen | Question | Warning sign |
|---|---|---|
| trust | will taxpayers see a connection between burden, public service, and fair treatment? | high enforcement fear with low service visibility.[S450][S452] |
| capacity | can the administration measure, collect, refund, review, and appeal this honestly? | complex rule administered as a blunt penalty.[S451] |
| capture | who can lobby, restructure, or buy advice to avoid the burden? | exemption forest for insiders, fixed penalties for small actors. |
| compliance market | who sells the tools needed to comply? | public duty becomes private software or advisory rent. |
| floor protection | does the rule simplify by taxing subsistence, informality, or small vendors first? | capacity shortcut becomes regressive extraction. |
| credibility | are proceeds visible enough to sustain cooperation? | tax increase with opaque substitution or no service repair. |
| reversibility | can the state sunset, audit, or adjust after evidence arrives? | emergency proxy becomes permanent normal law. |

## Weak-capacity default

When capacity is low, the archive may allow simple bases, presumptions, safe harbors, withholding anchors, standard deductions, short forms, and de minimis rules. But the simplification must move upward toward rents, harms, imports, land, natural resources, concentrated firms, high-value property, and administrable withholding before it moves downward onto subsistence traders or ordinary labor.[S451]

## Rev0295 accountability map

The capacity question now has an actor map rather than a generic public-body answer. The accountable actor is the legislature, budget office, and revenue agency that choose the service floor, penalty intensity, filing channel, and administrative budget. The likely beneficiary is not always the treasury; it can be the high-capacity actor that retains an undercollected advantage, the private compliance market that sells access to a required channel, or the enforcement program that converts low service capacity into penalty revenue. The protected burden bearer is the ordinary taxpayer, compliant small business, or low-capacity household that faces complexity, delay, or coercion without a workable compliance path.

The route should therefore ask for four records before approving a capacity design: service-level and staffing evidence; filing and notice accessibility evidence; penalty, abatement, and cure evidence; and a distributional trace of who benefits from undercollection or overreach. That turns tax morale from an exhortation into an assignment of repair duties.
