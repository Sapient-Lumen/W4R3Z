## Core rule

Long-term care finance should treat dependency risk as predictable social risk, not as a private family failure. Means tests, insurance, caregiver credits, and provider payments are legitimate only if they preserve dignity, home/community choice, disability access, and caregiver labor rather than requiring destitution or unpaid family substitution.[S628][S629][S623]

## Anti-patterns

- **spenddown ransom** — care arrives only after near-destitution.
- **family-care extraction** — public budgets assume unpaid relatives can absorb the burden.
- **institutional bias** — financing favors facilities over home/community choice.
- **longevity blame** — older or disabled people are treated as fiscal failures rather than rights holders.
