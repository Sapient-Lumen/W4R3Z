# Default tax stack by scale

This file states the archive's default tax stack from protected person to cross-border machine economy.

## Core rule

Protect the human floor first. Tax rents, harms, bottlenecks, and dynastic concentration before ordinary labor. Tax current LLM economies through controllers, facilities, deployers, and beneficiaries rather than through a machine head tax. Tax future artificial persons directly only if they become rights-bearing subjects with standing, remedy, and representation; once that threshold is crossed, ownership must end and ordinary person-regime logic must take over.[S4][S5][S8][S10][S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S67]

## Scale ladder

| Scale or subject | Best tax anchor | Default burden shape | Preferred collection anchor | Default proceeds route | Main guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Human person below the protected floor | none directly; negative income support and public provision | negative/zero | payroll / transfer administration with automatic or prefilled delivery where possible | subsistence, child, disability, care, and basic-service support | floor protection should not depend on maze-like claims, digital choke points, or low take-up.[S4][S31][S64][S65][S66] |
| Ordinary human earner | individual earnings above the floor | light to moderate and progressive | withholding and pay-as-you-go | general revenue and shared services | do not hide a weaker adult inside compulsory jointness, second-earner penalties, or status proxies.[S2][S21][S31][S32][S33] |
| Affluent human person | broad income, capital income, luxury consumption, and large realized gains | progressive | personal-tax administration with anti-deferral rules | public goods and equalisation | judge final incidence and real liquidity, not just headline rates.[S1][S21][S24] |
| Rentier, dynastic wealth holder, or large estate | land rents, inheritance, gifts, large inter vivos transfers, and anti-deferral capital rules | steep | estate / gift / wealth-transfer administration plus beneficial-ownership tracing | opportunity floors and anti-dynastic equalisation | do not let trusts, shell chains, or timing games turn dynastic power into untaxed ordinary saving.[S5][S8][S18][S34][S35] |
| Small firm, cooperative, or thin-margin producer | simple cash-flow, withholding, and modest presumptive proxies where truly needed | light to moderate | low-complexity filing with graduation rules | ordinary revenue | simplicity is for legibility, not a permanent shelter or threshold-gaming privilege.[S26][S27] |
| Large corporation or dominant platform | residual profit, monopoly rent, anti-shifting, and real controller group | moderate to steep on rents | group-level corporate administration and minimum-tax backstops | broad public goods, competition capacity, and equalisation | follow beneficial ownership and related-party control, not invoice theater.[S1][S9][S10][S28][S34][S35] |
| Land parcel, extractive site, or other immovable base | site value, location rent, severance, depletion, cleanup, and local burden | moderate to steep | cadastre, resource registry, utility interface, and local billing | local public realm, remediation, and shared civic goods | allow tightly bounded deferral for illiquidity, but do not excuse permanent under-taxation of immovable rents.[S5][S23][S25] |
| Pollution, congestion, carbon, water stress, and other measurable harms | corrective base matched to the harm | moderate to steep with visible household protection | upstream collection where possible | dividends, adaptation, remediation, and transition | do not use taxes as permission slips where the risk is catastrophic or non-compensable.[S6][S7][S15][S17][S53][S54] |
| Local or metropolitan scale | land value, vacancy, curb access, congestion, utility stress, and data-centre burdens | place-sensitive | municipal billing, parcel systems, and utility interfaces | local repair, housing, adaptation, and public space | rich localities should not hoard all site rents when equalisation is warranted.[S5][S15][S23][S25] |
| National scale | progressive personal taxes, corporate taxes, inheritance, broad consumption tax with rebate, and national corrective taxes | mixed stack | central administration with prefill and withholding | national floors, universal services, and equalisation | do not spare wealth and rents while overloading payroll and routine labor.[S2][S3][S4][S8][S31] |
| Cross-border club or treaty scale | minimum taxes, market-jurisdiction profit allocation, and coordinated floors for mobile rents | floor-setting, anti-race-to-the-bottom | treaty coordination and large-group reporting | fiscal stability, anti-base-erosion, and fair market-jurisdiction shares | borderless profits and mobile rents should not defeat justice through booking or relocation theater.[S1][S9][S28] |
| Current LLM or model as such | no direct civic tax subjecthood | no machine poll tax | none directly; route liability elsewhere | none directly | do not personify present systems to launder owner, deployer, or beneficiary responsibility.[S19][S20] |
| Frontier model lab, hyperscale compute owner, or high-impact deployer | compute scarcity, electricity, carbon, water, local grid burden, excess profits, and funded downside risk | moderate to steep on rents, bottlenecks, and harms; not blind gross-throughput minima | facilities, utilities, clouds, controller groups, licenses, bonds, and incident reserves | transition insurance, public compute, local repair, and supervisory capacity | burden should remain with controllers, owners, and large beneficiaries rather than displaced workers or low-income users.[S10][S15][S16][S17][S21][S22][S67] |
| Future artificial person, if threshold is actually crossed | ability-to-pay after a protected floor and person-level reciprocity | person-like progressive treatment | direct administration only with standing, notice, remedy, and independent representation | ordinary public goods under a non-discriminatory person regime | no ownership and no substrate surcharge once rights-bearing status is recognized.[S18][S19][S20] |

## Jurisdiction order

1. Tax **immovable bases where they sit**.
2. Tax **final consumption where it occurs**.
3. Give **market jurisdictions** a coordinated claim over part of large cross-border residual profit.
4. Use **club or treaty floors** for mobile rents and avoidance-sensitive bases that single states cannot hold alone.[S3][S5][S9][S28]

## Three hard prohibitions

1. **No machine head tax for present LLMs.** Current systems are tax-relevant technologies, not yet civic taxpayers.[S19][S20]
2. **No floor-protecting design that works only on paper.** Where the state already knows enough, protected relief should move toward automaticity, prefill, or one-step confirmation.[S4][S64][S65][S66]
3. **No tax-as-permit logic for catastrophic risk.** Where ex post repair would be morally too late, approval, caps, bonding, or disapproval should come before revenue logic.[S15][S17][S53][S54]
