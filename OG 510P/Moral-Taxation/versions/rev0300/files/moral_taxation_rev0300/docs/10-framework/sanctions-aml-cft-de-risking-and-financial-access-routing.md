# Sanctions, AML/CFT, de-risking, and financial-access routing

## Question in one sentence

When sanctions, AML/CFT duties, suspicious-activity controls, correspondent banking risk, or de-risking affect tax collection and financial rails, how should the archive target culpable risk without creating account death or humanitarian toll traps?[S507][S508][S510][S511]

Route: sanctions and AML/CFT are conduct-risk regimes, not permission to exclude whole populations, remittance corridors, charities, or mistaken-name taxpayers. Financial access is part of tax administration because filing, refunds, withholding, payroll, remittance, and proof often run through accounts.[S507][S508]

## Core rule

Risk-based compliance must remain risk-based. A bank, platform, agency, or tax authority may block or report where the legal and factual basis is specific enough, but account closure, frozen refunds, denied filing channels, blocked humanitarian payments, and name-mismatch penalties require notice, correction, review, and proportionate alternatives.

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| sanctions-name false positive | correction gate | notice, identity resolution, bounded hold, and release if unresolved suspicion fails. |
| charity or humanitarian corridor blocked | humanitarian gate | general-license clarity, safe harbor, or public payment channel. |
| bank exits category of customers | de-risking gate | regulator review and inclusion remedy.[S508] |
| AML/CFT rule blocks low-risk accounts | tiered-KYC gate | simplified due diligence and transaction caps. |
| enforcement penalty sought | culpability gate | apply OFAC/AML factors; distinguish voluntary disclosure, recklessness, and willfulness.[S510] |

## Anti-patterns

- **account death** — a person or group loses ordinary financial life without actionable reason or correction.
- **humanitarian toll** — charities or aid corridors must pay avoidable compliance rents to reach protected beneficiaries.
- **category de-risking** — banks exit migrants, NGOs, money transmitters, or regions instead of applying proportionate controls.
- **name-match liability** — weak identity signals become tax, payment, or sanctions consequences without review.
