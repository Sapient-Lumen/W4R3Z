# Procurement, subsidy, industrial policy, and public-upside routing

## Question in one sentence

When public procurement, grants, tax credits, guarantees, local abatements, or industrial-policy subsidies are at issue, how should the archive distinguish public capability from corporate welfare and captive-winner rents?[S512][S513][S514]

Route: public spending and tax subsidies are moral tax facts because they change the net fiscal stack. Procurement and industrial policy may build public capability, but must disclose the subsidy, compare alternatives, set conditions, preserve competition, protect workers and localities, and reclaim public upside when private value is created.[S512][S513]

## Core rule

A subsidy is not illegitimate merely because a firm receives it. It is illegitimate when public risk, public inputs, or public purchasing power are converted into private upside without transparent purpose, competitive access, performance conditions, clawbacks, open standards, local repair, worker protections, or public return.

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| sole-source procurement or single bidder | competition gate | justification, benchmark, audit, and renegotiation trigger. |
| tax credit or grant without performance terms | condition gate | clawback, milestone, wage/access/local condition. |
| public R&D or infrastructure creates private asset | upside gate | royalties, warrants, public equity, price condition, or open-access duty. |
| local abatement paired with infrastructure burden | net-stack gate | net subsidy disclosure and host-repair priority. |
| strategic industry claim | purpose gate | capacity/security rationale plus sunset and spillover review.[S513] |

## Anti-patterns

- **corporate welfare** — public money or tax expenditure funds ordinary private investment without public return.
- **captive winner rent** — procurement locks the state into a vendor, standard, or platform.
- **subsidy lock-in** — initial support becomes permanent because withdrawal is politically costly.
- **net-stack concealment** — credits, abatements, grants, cheap land, infrastructure, and procurement are not counted together.
