## Core rule

The archive distinguishes tax-transparency evidence from tax liability. Public CbCR, tax-strategy disclosure, and sustainability reporting can reveal profit-location, tax-payment, and substance mismatches, but they should not replace audits, create mob taxation, or expose genuinely sensitive data beyond the public purpose.

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| MNE reports profits and tax privately only | public-accountability gate | test whether public CbCR or tax-strategy disclosure is needed for civic legitimacy. |
| public CbCR appears but is too aggregated | granularity gate | separate jurisdictions, activities, employees, revenues, profit, tax accrued, and tax paid where required. |
| firm claims confidentiality for all tax data | shield gate | separate genuine commercial sensitivity from public-accountability avoidance. |
| public disclosure is used as substitute for audit | audit gate | treat disclosure as risk signal, not final liability. |
| small entities face transparency burden | scale gate | thresholds, simplified reporting, and no small-entity penalty mill. |

## Anti-patterns

- **publicity theater** — disclosure format looks transparent while hiding the substance.
- **confidentiality shield** — secrecy protects aggressive tax planning rather than legitimate privacy or trade secrets.
- **risk-signal finality** — CbCR mismatch becomes liability without audit.
- **small-entity transparency mill** — rules written for MNEs spill onto small firms.
