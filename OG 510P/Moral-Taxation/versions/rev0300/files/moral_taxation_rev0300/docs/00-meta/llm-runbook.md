# LLM runbook

Future editors should preserve the archive's narrow waist.

## Reopen in clusters

1. **Gate and scope** — read `docs/10-framework/waist-closure-and-calibration-frontier.md`, `docs/00-meta/calibration-frontier-map.md`, and `docs/00-meta/calibration-memo-format.md` first. Add a new framework note only when a real routing rule is missing or a compression win is large enough to justify new waist surface.
2. **Founding answer** — reopen `docs/10-framework/ideal-taxation-by-class-context-and-species.md`, `docs/10-framework/class-context-species-matrix.md`, `docs/10-framework/default-tax-stack-by-scale.md`, and `docs/10-framework/overlap-precedence-and-composite-case-routing.md` for the archive's direct answer to the human / LLM / species question, especially when one subject fits several classes at once.
   Keep the founding-answer note top-light when editing it: one compact direct answer above the schedule is enough; spend extra bytes in the schedule, rules, or routes instead of a duplicate bullet summary.
3. **Constitution and routing** — start with `docs/10-framework/constitutional-principles.md`, then use `ARCHIVE_INDEX.md` and the `docs/10-framework/*` family map to reach only the routing file you need. Typical branches: instrument choice; subjecthood and regime switch; overlap, creditability, uncertainty; incidence and regressivity; proceeds; timing, measurement, real value; jurisdiction; collection, administration, filing parity, minimization / verification, channel access, record asymmetry, bounded contest / finality, interim liability, sanctions, capacity, non-waiver, enforcement; assessment units, standing, anti-discrimination; and AI-trigger, wealth-backstop, downside, precaution, or net-stack guards.
4. **Calibration** — use `docs/00-meta/calibration-frontier-map.md` to choose the memo family, then open the matching ladder. Main families are AI-trigger ladders; cross-border and stack discipline; subject, boundary, controller-ranking, proof, controller settings, controller-map standards, controller-map signer authority / delegation, controller-map confidence / unknown / imputation, controller-map severability / issue-scoped correction, controller-map visibility tiers, controller-map integrity, controller-map retention / preservation, controller-map verification / review intensity, controller-map contest / finality, controller-map reuse / portability, controller-map packet identity / supersession, provisional controller filing / escrow, and representation; administration, measurement, and enforcement; incidence, proceeds, timing, and thresholds; and loss, real value, reliance, failure, and review.
5. **Backstops and archive notes** — use `docs/10-framework/proposal-scorecard.md` for evaluation and the compact archive notes when a routing rule needs its thesis spine, especially `archive/101`, `108`, `110`, and `112–131`.

## Editing posture

- Keep `docs/10-framework/open-questions.md` in frontier-prompt form: short unresolved question, first calibration memo, then source cues — not repeated narrow-waist boilerplate on every line.
- Keep `docs/10-framework/decision-procedure.md` checklist-first: keep one short companion-routes block, not a long repeated route preamble.
- Keep `docs/10-framework/ideal-taxation-by-class-context-and-species.md` answer-first: one-screen schedule, short section intros, and compact `Route:` callouts rather than repeated framing prose.
- Keep `docs/10-framework/ai-exceptional-levy-trigger-routing.md` trigger-first and route-cluster-first.
- Keep `docs/00-meta/calibration-frontier-map.md` cluster-first and table-first: it should route to memo families, not regrow into nine long repeated mini-memos.
- Keep `docs/20-calibration/timing-cashflow-liquidity-deferral-and-prefunding-ladder.md` ladder-first: use companion routes, a compact option scan, and the five timing lanes instead of the older long calibration scaffold.
- Keep `docs/20-calibration/proceeds-visibility-local-share-and-earmarking-ladder.md` ladder-first: use companion routes, a compact option scan, and the five destination lanes instead of the older long calibration scaffold.
- Keep `docs/20-calibration/collection-anchor-choice-and-remittance-chain-ladder.md` ladder-first: use companion routes, a compact option scan, and the five anchor lanes instead of the older long calibration scaffold.
- Keep the central distinction stable: **current AI changes the tax-base map more than the tax-subject map**.
- Add new taxes only after naming the moral target: rent, harm, reciprocity, congestion, inheritance, windfall, or emergency finance, and after checking whether the real answer is transfer, service provision, prefunding, or governance instead.
- Prefer operational additions that sharpen class, context, and species treatment over commentary that merely restates settled doctrine.
- Before adding an edge-case note for missing facts, try `docs/10-framework/uncertainty-defaults-and-conservative-routing.md`; many unknowns should collapse there rather than spawn a new doctrine file.
- Use the strongest available collection anchor before pushing compliance onto weak downstream actors.
- If the dispute is really about contracts, clickwrap, indemnities, or lease language trying to undo the public routing answer, route first through `docs/10-framework/private-ordering-non-waiver-and-anti-indemnification-routing.md` rather than drafting a sector note.
- Treat ordinary compliance cost, preparer dependence, and controller-side opacity dumped onto weak users as design defects, not background friction.
- If the design seems to need full raw files, repeated proofing, or routine sensitive attributes, route first through `docs/10-framework/data-minimization-and-purpose-bounded-verification-routing.md` before inventing a narrower doctrinal note.
- If the design only works through one bank, one app, one vendor, one device, or one identity rail, route first through `docs/10-framework/channel-pluralism-and-access-independence-routing.md` rather than treating private gatekeeper dependence as mere implementation detail.
- If the live dispute is who must prove hidden or concentrated facts, route first through `docs/10-framework/record-asymmetry-burden-shifting-and-adverse-inference-routing.md` rather than reopening the same proof dispute separately in controller, beneficial-ownership, or filing notes.
- If the live dispute is how long a period stays open, whether a narrow error should reopen the whole case, or what later facts justify reopening, route first through `docs/10-framework/bounded-contest-issue-scoped-correction-and-period-finality-routing.md` rather than drafting a sector-specific finality note.
- If the live dispute is who must float the cash-flow burden of uncertainty while review is live, route first through `docs/10-framework/interim-liability-stays-escrow-and-hardship-relief-routing.md` rather than rearguing pay-first review separately inside collection, timing, controller, or appeal notes.
- If the live dispute is a third-party reporting error the recipient did not author, route first through `docs/10-framework/third-party-reporting-correction-and-bounded-recipient-shelter-routing.md` rather than drafting a sector-specific mismatch note.
- If spouse relief, injured-spouse allocation, dependency, custody / separation, or joint-return records become captive household power, route first through `docs/10-framework/assessment-units-care-and-dependency-routing.md` and archive 173.
- If the live dispute is whether a settled packet or claim should travel by same-facts reliance, bounded deltas, and local supplements instead of clean-sheet refiling, route first through `docs/10-framework/same-facts-reuse-portability-and-delta-update-routing.md` rather than reopening portability separately inside filing, privacy, or controller-map notes.
- If the live dispute is whether a model may help administer a tax without becoming the real decider, route first through `docs/10-framework/model-assisted-tax-administration-and-accountable-review-routing.md` rather than drafting a narrower automation note.
- If the live dispute is whether a burden or support may stay ambient, when hiddenness is doing the moral work, or when line-item / summary / registry visibility is required, route first through `docs/10-framework/burden-salience-disclosure-and-anti-concealment-routing.md` rather than treating concealment as a cosmetic communication issue.
- If the live dispute is what response fits non-compliance after liability is already clear, route first through `docs/10-framework/sanctions-culpability-and-disclosure-routing.md` rather than letting interest, punishment, disclosure relief, and criminal shadow blur together.
- Check final incidence, not just the remitting entity.
- Protect floors with visible rebates, services, transfers, or automaticity rather than exemption jungles and heroic claiming effort.
- Treat timing, liquidity, downside symmetry, and real-value honesty as constitutional substance rather than implementation detail.
- Treat catastrophic or non-compensable risk as a governance problem first; do not let tax become a permission slip where approval, caps, bonding, or refusal should come earlier.
- Judge proposals on the **net fiscal stack**: abatements, tax expenditures, discounted public inputs, and direct subsidies can cancel the headline levy.
- When several levies touch one activity, separate moral objects first; allow distinct-object stacking, but credit or consolidate true duplicates rather than relying on "already taxed" rhetoric.
- Prefer person-based human liability; do not silently turn the tax subject into the household when the real moral unit is the individual.
- Do not impose direct civic tax liability on current LLMs; route through controllers, owners, deployers, bottlenecks, beneficiaries, and externalities unless a real rights-bearing regime switch has occurred.
- If future artificial persons ever exist, no species surcharge: ownership must end and ordinary person-regime protections must arrive as a bundle.
- Before adding a new source, merge duplicates by URL or title and repoint citations rather than forking the registry.
- Keep `SOURCES.md` as a thin citation index and put any explanatory note in `SOURCES.json` rather than duplicating a blurb across both surfaces.
- Keep `archive/*.md` source cues thin; do not carry footnote tails for IDs cited only there.
- Keep markdown citation clusters, source-footnote blocks, the source registry, and machine-readable JSON surfaces compact and deduped.
- When a note uses `[S..]` citations, keep the matching local `[S..]:` link definitions at the bottom of that note; `SOURCES.md` is the registry target, not a substitute for local definitions.
- For high-traffic reopening links, prefer explicit markdown anchors over heading-slug luck so route surfaces survive title edits.
- Keep `CHANGELOG.md`, `START_HERE.md`, `ARCHIVE_INDEX.md`, the matrix, `proposal-scorecard.md`, and this runbook lean: route, table, checklist, and family map first; `CHANGELOG.md` should normally keep only the current detailed entry above a compact ledger, and `MANIFEST.json` should carry the exhaustive inventory.
