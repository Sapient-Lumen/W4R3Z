# Wealth-transfer, dynastic-concentration, and liquidity ladder

## Question in one sentence

What ladder should choose between inheritance tax, estate tax, gift tax, capital-gains realization, wealth-tax backstop, minimum effective tax, exit tax, or liquidity relief in large-wealth cases?[S8][S73][S496]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — no transfer tax | rely on income tax alone. | Insufficient where accrued gains and dynastic transfers escape. |
| B — estate tax | tax donor estate at death. | Useful, but needs anti-fragmentation and liquidity relief. |
| C — recipient inheritance tax | tax cumulative receipts by beneficiary. | Often best for equality and floor-sensitive design. |
| D — capital-gains realization at death | tax accrued gains or deny step-up. | Strong backstop where valuation is feasible. |
| E — annual/top-end wealth backstop | minimum effective tax on ultra-high-net-worth persons. | Use for persistent low effective taxation and mobility. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **floor gate** — protect spouse/partner support, modest inheritance, disability/special-needs trusts, dependent minors, and vulnerable beneficiaries.
2. **aggregation gate** — aggregate lifetime gifts, inheritances, trusts, related-party transfers, foundations, and entity interests by donor and recipient.[S8]
3. **asset gate** — classify liquid assets, family business, farm, home, art, private company, carried interest, crypto, trust assets, and foreign holdings.
4. **accrued-gain gate** — decide whether death, gift, migration, trust transfer, or entity change realizes gain, carries basis, or triggers minimum tax.[S73]
5. **valuation gate** — use appraisals, safe harbors, valuation bands, look-throughs, or reserve discounts; sanction engineered discounts.
6. **liquidity gate** — allow installment, deferral, lien, or business-continuity relief where genuine illiquidity exists, with interest and anti-abuse.
7. **avoidance gate** — test dynasty trusts, GRAT-like transfer, valuation freeze, insurance wrapper, foundation/DAF, expatriation, and low-tax migration.
8. **minimum-tax gate** — for ultra-high-net-worth taxpayers with persistent low effective rates, apply coordinated minimum tax or domestic backstop.[S496]
9. **proceeds gate** — route revenue to human floor, opportunity, housing, education, transition, or wealth-floor repair.
10. **review gate** — recalibrate after avoidance growth, asset-price booms, concentration, liquidity hardship, or cross-border evasion.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| modest estate or vulnerable heir | exemption, low rate, or deferral | protect family security. |
| large lifetime transfer | progressive inheritance/estate/gift tax | aggregate and prevent fragmentation. |
| accrued gains at death | realization or carryover basis | no silent step-up unless explicit. |
| ultra-high-net-worth low effective tax | wealth or minimum-tax backstop | coordinate cross-border. |
| illiquid but valuable asset | installment/deferral with lien | no forced sale, no dynastic immunity. |
