## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — physical stockpile | public inventory of countermeasures and supplies. | Strong for surge items with clear rotation. |
| B — warm-base contract | pay for standby domestic or allied capacity. | Use with open-book costs and surge obligations. |
| C — advance purchase commitment | precommit demand for scarce countermeasures. | Use when innovation or scale needs demand certainty. |
| D — allocation rule | predefined priority and distribution protocol. | Required before crisis scarcity. |
| E — public production option | state-backed or public-private manufacturing. | Use for severe market failure or strategic dependence. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **hazard gate** — define threat, item class, shelf life, and surge quantity.
2. **role gate** — assign federal, state, tribal, local, and provider responsibilities.
3. **inventory gate** — publish enough visibility for accountability without security overexposure.
4. **rotation gate** — avoid expiration through replenishment, donation, use, or replacement rules.
5. **procurement gate** — use competition, open-book pricing, and clawbacks where urgency weakens bargaining.
6. **allocation gate** — rank medical need, exposure, disability, geography, tribal access, and continuity.
7. **logistics gate** — test last-mile cold-chain, rural, language, and accessibility delivery.
8. **fraud gate** — separate good-faith emergency error from culpable diversion.
9. **equity gate** — monitor distribution by place and protected floor.
10. **review gate** — reopen on stockout, expiration loss, allocation disparity, or supplier windfall.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| ordinary preparedness | physical stockpile and warm base | rotation and audit. |
| innovation gap | advance purchase | public access and price conditions. |
| crisis scarcity | allocation rule | need, exposure, and floor protection. |
| supplier windfall | clawback or price rule | open-book cost evidence. |
