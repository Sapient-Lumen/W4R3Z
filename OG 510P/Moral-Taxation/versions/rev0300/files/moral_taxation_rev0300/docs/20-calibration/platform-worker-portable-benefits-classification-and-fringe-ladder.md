# Platform-worker, portable-benefits, classification, and fringe ladder

## Question in one sentence

When portable benefits are proposed for platform work, how should contribution rates, worker status, wage records, and non-waiver rules be set so the account adds security instead of laundering misclassification?[S214][S641][S642][S643][S498]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — employee-status first | classify under economic reality/control and attach payroll/social-insurance duties | Required when control, dependence, and integration support employment. |
| B — supplemental portable account | benefits follow worker across platforms without changing status law | Useful only if platform-funded, adequate, portable, and non-waiving. |
| C — sectoral benefit fund | platforms contribute to pooled benefits by hours/pay/orders/revenue | Can fit fragmented work if records are accurate and worker voice exists. |
| D — worker-funded fringe account | deduct from worker pay while platform gets legal certainty | Reject where it reduces wages or substitutes for employer duties. |
| E — status safe harbor | participation in benefits immunizes platform from employment classification | Reject as a category mistake unless legislature openly changes worker law and preserves floors. |

## Parameter ladder

1. **classification first** — Apply employee/contractor test before portable-benefit design; do not let account labels decide status.
2. **control and dependence metrics** — Score price setting, dispatch, rating, deactivation, customer control, exclusivity, and algorithmic supervision.
3. **contribution base** — Set platform contribution on gross labor receipts, active time, engaged time, or orders with anti-avoidance rules.
4. **adequacy floor** — Compare account value to UI, workers compensation, paid leave, health, retirement, and expense reimbursement gaps.
5. **non-waiver clause** — State that benefits do not waive wage, payroll, employee, compensation, antidiscrimination, or organizing rights.
6. **worker record portability** — Create a portable wage/time/contribution record across platforms and tax agencies.
7. **multi-platform aggregation** — Aggregate work across apps for thresholds, benefits, and contribution caps.
8. **worker voice and governance** — Give workers representation in fund governance and dispute rules.
9. **deduction and fee bar** — Do not fund employer-side duties through worker deductions, opaque fees, or lower net pay.
10. **review trigger** — Reopen on DOL/federal/state classification change, deactivation spike, contribution shortfall, benefit inadequacy, or platform work-rule redesign.

## Default settings

| Parameter | Default | Redesign trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Status rule | Classification first; benefit participation non-waiving | Safe harbor or evidentiary bar against employee status. |
| Funding | Platform-side contribution tied to control/value capture | Worker deductions or fare cuts fund benefits. |
| Records | Portable wage/time/contribution ledger | Workers reconstruct platform data alone. |
| Adequacy | Benchmarked to real social-insurance gaps | Symbolic account too thin to replace protections. |
| Review cadence | Rulemaking/litigation-triggered plus annual adequacy review | Static account despite changed worker-control facts. |

## Anti-pattern definitions

- **misclassification safe harbor** — benefits are offered in exchange for immunity from employment, payroll, or compensation duties.
- **benefit theater** — a visible account is too small or narrow to repair the security gap it invokes.
- **worker funded fringe** — the worker pays for the benefit through deductions, lower pay, or platform fees.
- **algorithmic control denial** — platforms deny employment duties while retaining price, dispatch, rating, and deactivation control.
- **portable account lock in** — benefit access becomes dependent on one platform wallet, dashboard, or proprietary record.

## Rev0297 accountability map

- **Primary actor:** the labor platform or portable-benefit fund controller with dispatch, pricing, rating, deactivation, contribution-base, worker-data, or benefit-ledger power.
- **Beneficiary to trace:** platforms or fund vendors that use portable accounts to buy legal certainty, avoid employment/payroll duties, or charge workers for employer-side protections.
- **Required packet:** platform control and deactivation records, worker earnings/time/order/expense files, contribution and benefit ledgers, non-waiver/safe-harbor terms, and classification-review records.
- **Default move:** classification first; platform-funded contributions and portable records may supplement rights but cannot waive wage, payroll, compensation, antidiscrimination, or organizing protections.
