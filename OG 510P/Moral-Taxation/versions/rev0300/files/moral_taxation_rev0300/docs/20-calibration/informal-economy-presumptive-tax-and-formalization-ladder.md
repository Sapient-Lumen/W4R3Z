# Informal-economy presumptive-tax and formalization ladder

## Question in one sentence

What ladder should decide whether an informal-sector tax should be exemption, simplified registration, presumptive income tax, turnover tax, license fee, VAT threshold, ordinary audit, service-first formalization, or no tax?[S451][S467][S468]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — exemption below floor | no tax or rebate below subsistence threshold. | Adopt when activity is survival-level. |
| B — simple registration only | ID/record access without revenue charge. | Use as service-first formalization. |
| C — presumptive/turnover tax | low-rate proxy for micro/small firms. | Use only with threshold, taper, contest, and graduation. |
| D — license fee | flat market-access charge. | Reject if it becomes a toll; permit only for costed service. |
| E — ordinary audit | full income/VAT enforcement. | Use for capacity-rich evasion or post-graduation firms. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **activity gate** — classify subsistence vendor, microenterprise, household enterprise, seasonal worker, professional cash business, or disguised larger firm.
2. **floor gate** — estimate household floor risk before revenue interest; protect food, housing, health, transport, caregiving, and schooling capacity.
3. **record gate** — identify available sales, mobile-money, inventory, supplier, platform, license, and location records; do not demand records the state has not made feasible.
4. **threshold gate** — set exemption, presumptive, turnover, VAT, and ordinary-regime thresholds with taper rather than cliffs.[S467]
5. **rate gate** — keep proxy rates modest and sector-sensitive; avoid gross-receipts rates that exceed realistic margins.
6. **benefit gate** — pair formalization with visible services: legal identity, market access, social insurance, credit records, dispute help, and infrastructure.[S451]
7. **contest gate** — allow correction when proxy overstates income, shocks occur, disability/care burdens arise, or records are wrong.
8. **graduation gate** — define when firms move to ordinary records and taxes; avoid permanent micro-regime shelters for larger firms.[S468]
9. **anti-abuse gate** — prevent fragmentation, false micro status, and platform/supplier pressure shifting liability to tiny sellers.
10. **review gate** — recalibrate when prices, margins, inflation, digital records, enforcement capacity, or trust measures change.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| below floor | exemption, rebate, or no-rent registration | no subsistence toll. |
| micro but above floor | low-rate presumptive or turnover tax | taper, contest, and service benefit. |
| small growing firm | simplified regime with graduation | no cliff to full complexity. |
| capacity-rich evader | ordinary audit/proxy | do not hide behind micro status. |
| state capacity weak | simpler base | protect floor and trust first. |
