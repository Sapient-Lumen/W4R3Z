# Implementation audit, tax-expenditure evidence, and program-integrity ladder

## Question in one sentence

What ladder should decide whether a tax expenditure, credit, registry, automated program, or enforcement initiative has enough evidence and auditability to remain legitimate?[S532][S533][S534][S535][S536]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — ex ante evidence plan | objective, metric, baseline. | Required for material rules. |
| B — annual public reporting | cost, take-up, beneficiaries, errors. | Required for tax expenditures. |
| C — independent audit | IG/SAI/GAO-style review. | Required for high fiscal or rights risk. |
| D — sunset / trigger | automatic review or repeal. | Required under uncertainty. |
| E — black-box continuation | no metric, no audit. | Reject. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **objective gate** — state the measurable purpose, target population, and baseline.
2. **instrument gate** — identify tax expenditure, direct spending, credit, enforcement, registry, or AI tool.
3. **cost gate** — estimate revenue loss, administrative cost, compliance burden, and fiscal stack.[S534]
4. **beneficiary gate** — measure statutory beneficiary, real incidence, intermediaries, and protected floors.
5. **data gate** — collect take-up, denial, error, fraud, appeal, non-take-up, and distribution data.[S536]
6. **comparison gate** — compare tax expenditure with direct spending or simpler alternative.
7. **integrity gate** — measure improper payments, overpayments, underpayments, and root causes.[S532][S533]
8. **audit gate** — assign independent reviewer, publication cadence, and response duty.
9. **inventory gate** — ensure program inventory and tax-expenditure surfaces are complete and searchable.[S535]
10. **trigger gate** — sunset, narrow, expand, redesign, or repeal when targets, errors, or harms cross thresholds.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| new tax credit | evidence plan | cost and incidence. |
| mature tax expenditure | public evaluation | comparable alternatives. |
| automated enforcement | audit log | explanation and appeal. |
| high improper payment | root-cause control | floor-sensitive recovery. |
| missing data | temporary authority | reporting condition. |
