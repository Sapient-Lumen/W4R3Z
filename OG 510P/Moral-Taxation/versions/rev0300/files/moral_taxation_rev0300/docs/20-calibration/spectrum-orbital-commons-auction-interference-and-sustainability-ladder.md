## Ten-gate ladder

1. **resource gate** — define band, orbit, interference contour, geography, and time.
2. **public-use gate** — reserve safety, science, emergency, defense, unlicensed, or community needs.
3. **competition gate** — test concentration, interoperability, roaming, and entry effects.
4. **auction gate** — price scarcity only after non-revenue public duties are set.
5. **sharing gate** — prefer sharing where technical coordination can preserve value.
6. **buildout gate** — attach service, coverage, milestone, and release duties.
7. **filing gate** — prevent paper satellites, warehousing, and speculative filings.
8. **interference gate** — assign monitoring and mitigation to avoidable risk creators.
9. **sustainability gate** — require debris, deorbit, tracking, insurance, or bond rules.
10. **review gate** — reopen on hoarding, interference disputes, unmet deployment, or access loss.

## Default settings
