# Beneficiary composite netting / residual-ordering ladder

## Question in one sentence

Given the archive's closed rule that **customer diffusion, supplier pass-through, creditor distress, insurance benefit, site-rent absorption, charitable transfer, and ring-fenced prefunding can each narrow a downstream beneficiary-side windfall claim only for the real outside slice, and only without double-counting or outranking harder duties**, **what is the smallest workable ladder for ordering several such slices in the same case and taxing only the retained exceptional residual?**[S10][S11][S15][S18][S21][S22][S28][S29][S34][S35][S47][S48][S49][S67][S69][S71][S83]

## Minimal workable ladder

| Rung | Facts on the ground | Fiscal answer |
|---|---|---|
| 0. stacked-theater bundle | several diffusion defenses are asserted at once, but affiliate grouping is unresolved, the same dollar seems to appear in several stories, or exceptional residual margins clearly continue | ignore the bundle as a full-immunity claim; group first, demand decomposition, and keep the beneficiary-side lane live.[S10][S11][S18][S21][S22][S34][S35][S67][S83] |
| 1. dominant single-lane case | one outside lane plainly explains the case and the others are minor or derivative | use the matching single-lane memo; do not add a composite discount just because several labels were spoken.[S18][S21][S22][S28][S67][S83] |
| 2. duty-first composite | worker transition, cleanup, compensation, local repair, or genuinely locked prefunding for outside/public obligations are materially real | honor those harder-duty slices first; do not let softer customer, creditor, charity, or halo stories outrank them.[S15][S18][S21][S22][S47][S48][S49][S67][S69][S71][S83] |
| 3. bounded multi-slice split | several outside slices are real, separable, and outside the group after dependency or recapture screens | net each real outside slice once in a bounded period, then tax only the retained exceptional residual; do not tax gross activity.[S10][S11][S18][S21][S22][S28][S34][S35][S47][S48][S49][S67][S69][S71][S83] |
| 4. near-fully socialized case | after bounded one-time netting, exceptional residual is no longer the main story in that period | stand down or defer the beneficiary-side charge for that period, but keep ordinary taxation, review triggers, and recapture checks alive.[S18][S21][S22][S29][S47][S48][S49][S67][S69][S71][S83] |
| 5. contested mixed case | several slices may be real but evidence is incomplete, overlapping, or strategically timed | allow provisional relief only for the well-evidenced slice, keep the rest on the retained-surplus side for now, and true-up later rather than granting blanket immunity.[S15][S18][S21][S22][S34][S35][S39][S41][S67][S83] |

## Settings that matter most

### 1. Group first, then split

Do not run multi-lane netting until related parties, captive channels, donor-controlled vehicles, related landlords, affiliate debt, affiliate reinsurance, and other recapture paths have been screened. Composite relief is for **real outside slices**, not for the same coordinated group speaking through several shells.[S18][S21][S22][S34][S35][S47][S48][S49][S67][S71][S83]

### 2. One dollar gets one outside story

The same incremental dollar cannot be counted once as customer pass-through, again as supplier benefit, again as creditor cure, and again as charity. Choose the best-fitting outside story for each slice and net it once. Composite calibration exists to prevent both gross over-taxation and multi-label immunity theater.[S10][S11][S18][S21][S22][S28][S34][S35][S67][S83]

### 3. Order harder duties before softer diffusion claims

The archive's default order inside a genuine composite case is: **(a)** direct worker, compensation, cleanup, local-repair, and locked prefunding duties, **(b)** governed service or worker-reciprocity lanes, **(c)** broad market diffusion to customers or outside suppliers, **(d)** fixed-claim, risk-pool, and site-rent absorption, **(e)** outside charitable transfer, and only then **(f)** the retained residual. That ordering can be rebutted by better facts, but soft virtue stories should not crowd out harder prior claims.[S15][S18][S21][S22][S29][S47][S48][S49][S67][S69][S71][S83]

### 4. Mixed cases should residualize, not moralize

Where several lanes are partly real, the answer is usually not all-or-nothing. Net the credibly outside slice, reject the overlapping or theatrical slice, and tax the remainder. The archive's composite answer is a **residualized** base, not a rhetorical victory for whichever lane sounds noblest.[S10][S11][S18][S21][S22][S28][S34][S35][S67][S83]

### 5. Use bounded windows and re-check recapture

Composite cases are especially vulnerable to timing games. Use a bounded period, require the outside slice to be real in that period, and re-check whether dividends, executive extraction, affiliate fees, donor control, related-party lease reset, or other recapture has re-entered before turning one temporary outward flow into durable immunity.[S18][S21][S22][S29][S34][S35][S47][S48][S49][S67][S69][S71][S83]

## Three anti-patterns

1. **layer-cake immunity** — stacking many small defenses until the whole retained-surplus question disappears,[S10][S11][S18][S21][S22][S67][S83]
2. **same-dollar double-netting** — letting the same incremental gain be counted under several outward-flow stories at once,[S10][S11][S18][S21][S22][S28][S34][S35][S67][S83]
3. **soft-story inversion** — allowing charity, halo, ordinary leverage, or optional growth rhetoric to outrank worker, cleanup, compensation, local-repair, or locked-duty claims.[S15][S18][S21][S22][S29][S47][S48][S49][S67][S69][S71][S83]
