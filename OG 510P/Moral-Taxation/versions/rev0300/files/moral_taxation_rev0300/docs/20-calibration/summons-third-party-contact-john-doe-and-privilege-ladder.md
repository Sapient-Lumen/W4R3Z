# Summons, third-party contact, John Doe, and privilege ladder

Use this calibration memo when tax administration seeks records, testimony, platform data, bank records, employer or customer information, advisor files, software/source-code material, or unknown-taxpayer class data through **voluntary request, third-party contact, administrative summons, third-party summons, John Doe summons, privilege dispute, or summons enforcement**.

## Option scan

| Option | Description | Default judgment |
|---|---|---|
| A — voluntary-only rule | never compel records or testimony, even when relevant facts are withheld, dissipating, or controlled by a strategic record holder.[S39][S41][S343] | Reject: makes hidden income, sham fragmentation, and strategic opacity too easy. |
| B — full record-dragnet rule | treat any tax inquiry as authority to demand broad raw files, contact third parties, ingest platform logs, and enforce first.[S339][S343][S345] | Reject: turns information power into surveillance rent. |
| C — tiered information-power ladder | start with narrow request and already-held records, then use third-party contact, taxpayer summons, third-party summons, John Doe summons, privilege/sealed review, and enforcement only as the facts require.[S339][S340][S343][S345][S346][S348][S349][S350] | Adopt. |
| D — court-first for every compulsory record request | require judicial approval before any summons or compelled testimony.[S339][S340][S348] | Reject as universal rule; court gating is crucial for John Doe and enforcement disputes but too rigid for ordinary issue-scoped summons use. |

## Adopted ladder

1. **Already-held / voluntary-production lane** — use when the live issue can be resolved by existing transcripts, filed records, reusable credentials, targeted document requests, or willing witness production.[S39][S41][S89][S343][S344]
2. **Named third-party-contact lane** — use when an employer, customer, vendor, bank, platform, payroll provider, advisor, or other third party may hold the needed fact, but give advance notice and keep a contact record unless a genuine statutory exception applies.[S339][S349]
3. **Taxpayer-records summons lane** — use when the taxpayer or taxpayer-controlled actor has relevant/material records or testimony and lighter channels have failed; keep the summons period, issue, and appearance demand specific.[S339][S342][S343][S344]
4. **Third-party summons / notice-and-quash lane** — use when a non-taxpayer record holder has the relevant evidence; preserve noticee identification, waiting period, petition-to-quash, intervention, and record-location specificity.[S340][S345][S348]
5. **John Doe class-demand lane** — use only when identities are unknown but the group is ascertainable and a real tax-law basis exists; require Counsel / DOJ routing, court approval, class minimization, and post-use retention limits.[S339][S340][S341][S346]
6. **Privilege / sealed-review lane** — use when attorney, federally authorized tax-practitioner, constitutional, work-product, source-code, trade-secret, or comparable claims are asserted; require logs, Counsel review, separated nonprivileged production, and the narrowest private review that can resolve the claim.[S344][S348][S350]
7. **Summons-enforcement lane** — use only after service, notice, timing, possession/control, privilege, repeated-examination, reasonable-extension, and abuse-of-process checks are clean; enforcement should not repair a defective or overbroad information demand.[S340][S342][S345][S348]

## Evidence packet before escalation

A summons or third-party-contact packet should show:

- the taxpayer, person, class, period, tax type, and issue being tested;
- why already-held records, voluntary production, or a narrower cross-check cannot answer the issue;
- the exact records, testimony, source-code, software, transaction set, account, or platform data sought;
- the relevance/materiality bridge between each requested category and the tax purpose;
- notice, Letter 3164 / third-party-contact posture, third-party summons waiting period, quash/intervention route, or John Doe court approval posture as applicable;
- privilege, confidentiality, sealed-review, and representative handling;
- witness-fee, search, reproduction, transport, and engineering-cost handling;
- retention, deletion, and nonresponsive-record minimization after production.[S339][S340][S342][S343][S345][S346][S347][S348][S349][S350]

## Defaults

- **Ordinary missing statement or small mismatch:** request the specific document or use already-held records first; do not summon the full account history by reflex.[S39][S89][S343]
- **Employer, bank, platform, or vendor holds a narrow fact:** use third-party contact or summons only after notice rules and issue scope are ready; do not use reputational pressure as proof.[S340][S345][S349]
- **Platform-scale unknown-user inquiry:** route to John Doe only if the class is bounded and court approval, minimization, and nonresponsive-record deletion are explicit.[S340][S346]
- **Advisor or representative file:** separate return-preparation facts, nonprivileged records, and privileged tax advice before drawing adverse inference.[S344][S348][S350]
- **Witness says records need more time or are not in its possession:** consider extension, narrowing, or possession/control proof before enforcement.[S348]
- **Summoned third party incurs real production costs:** provide the reimbursement path where available rather than shifting public investigation costs into private hidden fees.[S342][S347]

## Red flags requiring supervisory or Counsel review

- request language says all records, all communications, all users, all logs, or all source artifacts without a record-category bridge;
- a third-party contact is planned before the taxpayer has a meaningful chance to provide the same information;
- third-party records will be examined before notice and quash windows are practically usable;
- John Doe facts are really a bulk data desire, not an ascertainable class with a reasonable tax-law basis;
- a privilege claim is treated as obstruction before a log, Counsel review, or narrowed nonprivileged production;
- enforcement is referred after defective service, impossible timing, repeated examination, reasonable-extension request, or possession/control denial;
- cost reimbursement is invisible to a small third-party witness or technically burdened platform;
- rich AI, platform, identity, or payment telemetry is being treated as a substitute for issue-scoped relevance.[S339][S340][S345][S346][S347][S348][S349][S350]

## Rev0296 accountability map

- **Primary accountable actor:** the summons official, supervisor, court, or disclosure-control owner deciding third-party compulsion, John Doe scope, notice exceptions, privilege treatment, and adverse use.
- **Beneficiary / rent recipient:** the requesting authority that gains low-friction third-party access if notice, narrowing, privilege, or minimization is bypassed.
- **Protected burden bearer:** taxpayers, privilege holders, representatives, third-party record holders, and privacy-exposed nonparties.
- **Required packet:** summons authority, relevance and period scope, third-party contact notice or exception, John Doe approval, production chain, privilege log, taint review, return / nonuse record, minimization, and taxpayer contest record.
- **Default move:** narrow, quash, pause, return, or taint-review records before using third-party material adversely where notice, privilege, scope, or minimization is defective.
