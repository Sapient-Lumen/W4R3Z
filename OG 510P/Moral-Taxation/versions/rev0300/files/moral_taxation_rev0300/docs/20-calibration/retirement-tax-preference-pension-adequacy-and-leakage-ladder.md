## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — uncapped deferral | large tax-preferred saving without adequacy or distribution test. | Weak when benefits skew upward or shelter dynastic wealth. |
| B — floor-focused credit or match | refundable or matched support for low/moderate savers. | Strong for adequacy and gender/care gaps. |
| C — automatic enrollment with opt-out | increase participation while preserving liquidity safeguards. | Strong if fees and leakage are controlled. |
| D — leakage guardrail | limit hardship traps, fees, loans, and early-withdrawal spirals. | Required when liquidity stress defeats adequacy. |
| E — pension/gender care credit | credit unpaid care and interrupted work. | Strong where pension gaps reflect care burdens. |

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| low/moderate saver | refundable match or credit | do not concentrate subsidy at top. |
| care-interrupted worker | care credit / pension credit | repair gender and caregiver pension gaps. |
| high-balance shelter | cap or integration rule | prevent tax-preferred dynastic lockup. |

## Rev0297 accountability map

- **Primary actor:** the retirement-tax-preference designer, plan sponsor, asset manager, recordkeeper, or employer controlling plan access, fees, contribution rules, leakage, or distribution limits.
- **Beneficiary to trace:** high-balance savers, plan sponsors, asset managers, or fee recipients that capture public retirement subsidy without delivering retirement adequacy or caregiver/low-income repair.
- **Required packet:** tax-expenditure incidence estimates, account-balance and contribution records, plan-fee and asset-manager compensation records, leakage/hardship files, and caregiver/gender/low-income pension-gap data.
- **Default move:** convert upside shelters into refundable saver support, fee caps, high-balance limits, care credits, or leakage guardrails tied to retirement-floor evidence.
