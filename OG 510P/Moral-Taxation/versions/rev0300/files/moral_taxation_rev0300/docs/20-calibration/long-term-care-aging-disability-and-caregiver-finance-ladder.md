## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — family-only obligation | treat unpaid family care as free fiscal capacity. | Weak; it hides gender, disability, and poverty costs. |
| B — social-insurance LTC contribution | spread predictable dependency risk broadly. | Strong if benefits are adequate and not means-test ransom. |
| C — means-tested Medicaid-style support | protect floor after income/asset tests. | Needed but risky if spenddown becomes destitution precondition. |
| D — caregiver credit / respite support | recognize unpaid care and prevent labor-market penalty. | Strong where family care is relied upon. |
| E — quality and workforce fee | finance inspection, wages, and home/community capacity. | Use when provider underpayment or extraction drives poor care. |

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| predictable aging care risk | social-insurance or broad public finance | avoid household bankruptcy first. |
| current low-income care need | means-tested floor with asset protections | spenddown cannot be ransom. |
| family caregiver burden | credit/respite/wage support | do not hide fiscal savings in unpaid care. |

## Rev0297 accountability map

- **Primary actor:** the long-term-care financing agency, Medicaid/care office, provider, insurer, estate-recovery office, or caregiver-support program controlling assessment, spenddown, placement, home-care access, or recovery.
- **Beneficiary to trace:** institutional providers, insurers, estate-recovery actors, or public budgets that capture spenddown, family-care extraction, or coverage denial while older or disabled people lose home/community choice.
- **Required packet:** care-need and disability assessments, asset/spenddown/estate-recovery records, provider quality and staffing files, home-care waitlist/capacity data, and caregiver hours/respite/exit evidence.
- **Default move:** protect home/community care, spousal resources, caregiver respite, hardship relief, and independent review before allowing asset-test or provider-gate loss to become irreversible.
