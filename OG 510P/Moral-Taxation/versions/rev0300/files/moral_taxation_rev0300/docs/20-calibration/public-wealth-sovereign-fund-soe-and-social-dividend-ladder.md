## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — general fund spend-down | windfall spent immediately. | Allowed only after stabilization and repair tests. |
| B — stabilization fund | buffer volatile revenue. | Useful for resource or cyclical rents. |
| C — savings/future fund | intergenerational asset. | Use for exhaustible or exceptional windfalls. |
| D — strategic investment fund | public capital deployed for missions. | Use with governance, valuation, and performance rules. |
| E — social dividend | visible per-capita or floor-linked payout. | Use only with stable funding and no substitution. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **objective gate** — choose stabilization, savings, development, public upside, or dividend purpose.
2. **deposit gate** — define which rents, royalties, taxes, equity proceeds, or SOE returns enter the fund.
3. **withdrawal gate** — set rule-based spending, emergency, dividend, and stabilization conditions.
4. **governance gate** — separate ownership, regulation, policy, and day-to-day investment decisions.
5. **transparency gate** — publish accounts, portfolio, risk, fees, benchmarks, and related-party exposure.
6. **valuation gate** — price public equity, royalties, guarantees, and asset sales before claiming proceeds.
7. **mission gate** — for strategic funds, disclose crowd-in goal, loss tolerance, and performance metrics.
8. **beneficiary gate** — state whether benefits flow by floor repair, universal dividend, local share, or future savings.
9. **anti-capture gate** — block patronage, incumbent subsidy, and off-budget spending.
10. **review gate** — audit returns, distribution, substitution, and long-run balance-sheet effect.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| resource windfall | stabilization plus savings | avoid procyclical spending. |
| public equity in subsidy recipient | upside rule | valuation and clawback. |
| SOE dividend | ownership policy | separate regulator and owner. |
| strategic fund | mission governance | performance and risk disclosure. |
| asset sale | long-run value test | no deficit camouflage. |
