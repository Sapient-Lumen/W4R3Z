# Defense, security procurement, secrecy, and industrial-base ladder

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — competitive fixed-price procurement | ordinary competition with defined deliverables. | Prefer where requirements are stable. |
| B — cost-type development contract | government bears more uncertainty for immature technology. | Use only with visibility and learning gates. |
| C — performance-based financing | recoverable financing tied to milestones. | Use where it lowers price or expands capacity. |
| D — industrial-base subsidy | support for capacity, workforce, supply chain, or surge. | Use with public-upside and anti-capture conditions. |
| E — classified urgent pathway | fast or secret procurement. | Use narrowly with cleared audit and sunset. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **mission gate** — state the threat, capability, users, and time horizon.
2. **maturity gate** — match contract type to technology and requirements maturity.
3. **competition gate** — test alternatives, modularity, open architecture, and second sources.
4. **secrecy gate** — limit classified withholding and preserve cleared oversight.
5. **financing gate** — make advances recoverable and tied to objective outcomes.
6. **supply-chain gate** — require visibility for critical origin, foreign dependency, and resilience.
7. **testing gate** — do not treat deployment pressure as evidence of capability.
8. **public-upside gate** — use pricing, data rights, clawbacks, or equity-like return for public capacity finance.
9. **worker gate** — count industrial-base labor and local transition without captive-contractor theater.
10. **review gate** — reopen on cost growth, schedule slip, test failure, sole-source drift, or audit weakness.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| mature requirement | fixed-price competition | avoid risk dumping onto weak suppliers. |
| immature technology | cost-type with gates | learning milestones and off-ramps. |
| capacity bottleneck | conditioned industrial-base support | public upside and supply-chain visibility. |
| classified urgency | cleared audit route | sunset secrecy and preserve whistleblower lanes. |
