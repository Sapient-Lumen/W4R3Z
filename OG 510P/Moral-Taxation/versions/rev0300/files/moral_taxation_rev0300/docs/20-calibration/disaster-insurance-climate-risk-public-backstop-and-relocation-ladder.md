## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — underpriced universal premiums | broad affordability through hidden subsidy. | Reject as climate-risk denial and debt transfer. |
| B — pure actuarial pricing | fully risk-based premiums. | Too harsh without legacy/floor protection. |
| C — risk-based plus means-tested aid | honest price plus targeted affordability. | Default for legacy households. |
| D — mitigation-conditioned subsidy | premium relief tied to elevation, hardening, drainage, or fire defense. | Adopt where adaptation is viable. |
| E — buyout/relocation/no-build rule | move people or bar rebuilding. | Required for repeated loss or non-adaptable risk. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **risk-map gate** — identify flood, wildfire, wind, heat, drought, sea-level, landslide, or compound risk; test map age, uncertainty, and disclosure.
2. **legacy gate** — distinguish long-settled resident, recent buyer with disclosure, landlord, developer, second home, critical facility, and new construction.
3. **affordability gate** — measure premium plus deductible plus property-tax plus mortgage burden for low- and moderate-income households.[S493]
4. **actuarial gate** — separate true expected loss, administrative cost, cross-subsidy, debt service, and public-policy discount.[S491]
5. **mitigation gate** — test whether elevation, hardening, drainage, defensible space, codes, relocation, or nature-based protection can reduce loss.
6. **repeat-loss gate** — identify repeated claims, severe repetitive loss, post-map rebuilding, and new development in known risk areas.[S492]
7. **backstop gate** — route public insurer, reinsurance, guarantee, or disaster aid through public-backstop pricing and conditions.
8. **relocation gate** — provide voluntary buyout, replacement housing, community continuity, renters' protection, and anti-speculation guardrails where retreat is needed.
9. **proceeds gate** — route surcharges or savings to mitigation, mapping, buyouts, local resilience, and floor aid.
10. **review gate** — trigger review after map updates, insurer withdrawal, debt growth, repeated disasters, or adaptation failure.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| legacy low-income household in high risk | targeted voucher plus mitigation | no abandonment pricing. |
| new luxury/risky development | full risk price, bond, or no-build rule | no public subsidy. |
| repeated-loss property | buyout/elevation/relocation before renewed subsidy | avoid endless rebuild subsidy. |
| insurer withdrawal | public backstop with conditions | no unpriced private exit externality. |
| stale/inaccurate map | correction and transition | no map-shock forfeiture. |

## Rev0298 accountability map

- **Primary actor:** the insurer, regulator, land-use authority, developer, or public backstop sponsor controlling risk price, disclosure, rebuild permission, mitigation, or relocation support.
- **Beneficiary to trace:** the insurer, developer, new high-risk property owner, or rebuild beneficiary receiving underpriced backstop, hidden climate-risk transfer, or repeated-loss subsidy.
- **Required packet:** hazard-map age and uncertainty, premium and nonrenewal records, legacy/new-build status, mitigation feasibility, repeated-loss files, buyout/relocation ledgers, and taxpayer-backstop accounts.
- **Default move:** pair honest risk pricing with targeted floor aid, mitigation, buyout, relocation, or no-build rules rather than hiding risk in taxpayer or household backstops.
