## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — pure disclosure | require incident reporting only. | Incomplete unless resilience, victim repair, and cost allocation are also addressed. |
| B — resilience standard | require risk governance, supplier oversight, backup, and recovery controls. | Strong baseline for critical or data-rich actors. |
| C — breach-cost charge | allocate avoidable breach costs to negligent controllers. | Use with causation and safe harbors. |
| D — public cyber fund | finance shared threat intelligence, local capacity, and victim repair. | Use where spillovers exceed firm boundaries. |
| E — punitive penalty | large sanctions for concealment or reckless disregard. | Reserve for culpability, not ordinary incident occurrence. |

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| critical infrastructure | resilience standard plus incident report | avoid disclosure without repair capacity. |
| avoidable breach from neglected controls | breach-cost allocation | safe harbor for reasonable controls and prompt repair. |
| victim identity or account recovery | public/firm repair lane | no victim-paid recovery toll. |

## Rev0296 accountability map

- **Primary accountable actor:** the system, data, critical-infrastructure, supplier, cloud, or managed-service controller that controls security architecture, privileged logs, patch channels, reporting, ransom decisions, and victim repair.
- **Beneficiary / rent recipient:** the controller, supplier, or ransom recipient that benefits from underinvestment, delayed reporting, externalized recovery cost, or normalized extortion.
- **Protected burden bearer:** breach victims, patients, consumers, small businesses, public bodies, and taxpayers asked to finance avoidable private resilience failures.
- **Required packet:** asset and data inventory, control-failure / root-cause record, supplier dependency log, incident timeline, reporting materiality decision, ransom-payment record, victim notification and repair ledger, and post-incident audit.
- **Default move:** allocate reporting, repair, resilience, and supplier cure costs to the actor with control; reserve public funding for shared spillover capacity and victim recovery that private actors cannot perform alone.
