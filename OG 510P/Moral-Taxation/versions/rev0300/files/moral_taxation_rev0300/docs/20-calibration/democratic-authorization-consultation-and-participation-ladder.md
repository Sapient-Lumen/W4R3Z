# Democratic authorization, consultation, and participation ladder

## Question in one sentence

How much democratic authorization, public explanation, consultation, and affected-party voice is required before a tax-like rule can harden?[S562][S563][S564]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — bare legality | statute or delegated rule exists. | Insufficient for major or floor-sensitive burdens. |
| B — notice and reasons | plain public explanation plus legal authority. | Minimum for ordinary changes. |
| C — meaningful consultation | stakeholders can affect design before closure. | Required for concentrated or novel burdens. |
| D — deliberative/participatory design | representative deliberation, participatory budgeting, or affected-community process. | Use for fundamental, durable, or trust-sensitive choices. |
| E — emergency delegation | temporary action with later ratification. | Only with sunset and public review. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **authority gate** — name the legislative, regulatory, or contractual authority and its limits.
2. **purpose gate** — state the public problem, non-tax alternatives considered, and why a fiscal instrument is used.
3. **affected-party map** — identify who pays, who benefits, who is heard, and who may be missed.
4. **timing gate** — consultation must occur before material design closure unless emergency facts justify provisionality.
5. **access gate** — provide language, disability, digital, and nondigital participation routes.
6. **evidence gate** — publish distributional, fiscal, and administrative evidence in usable form.
7. **response gate** — answer material objections and explain accepted and rejected changes.
8. **delegation gate** — require named accountability where agencies, utilities, vendors, or emergency actors set charges.
9. **sunset gate** — emergency or experimental fiscal powers need expiry and conversion conditions.
10. **repair gate** — defective participation triggers reopening, phase-in, holdback, or post-enactment correction.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| ordinary rate/indexing update | notice plus reasons | publish formula and incidence. |
| major new base | consultation | respond to objections. |
| concentrated local burden | affected-voice process | local hearing and repair route. |
| fundamental long-run burden | deliberative design | representative participation plus legislative vote. |
| emergency charge | temporary authority | sunset and ratification. |
