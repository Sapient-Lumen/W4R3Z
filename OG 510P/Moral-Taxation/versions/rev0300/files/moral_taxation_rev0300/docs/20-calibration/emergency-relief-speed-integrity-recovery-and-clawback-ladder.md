## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — prepayment control | verify before paying. | Use where it does not defeat floor speed. |
| B — provisional payment | pay now, true up later. | Preferred for subsistence crises. |
| C — postpayment sampling | audit after disbursement. | Required when speed relaxes proof. |
| D — good-faith safe harbor | waive recovery for eligible floor cases. | Required to avoid clawback trap. |
| E — fraud recovery | recover from culpable actors. | Required for integrity and trust. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **emergency gate** — define shock, affected population, deadline, and harm of delay.
2. **floor gate** — identify food, rent, wages, health, utility, disaster, or business-survival need.
3. **identity gate** — use resilient identity controls and victim-repair paths.[S531]
4. **eligibility gate** — prefer observable data, attestation, and later true-up where documents are disrupted.
5. **speed gate** — decide which controls must be prepayment and which can be postpayment.
6. **risk gate** — add targeted controls for high-risk channels, duplicates, stolen identities, and organized false claims.[S533]
7. **notice gate** — tell recipients what is provisional, final, appealable, or recoverable.
8. **recovery gate** — distinguish agency error, good-faith recipient, reckless claimant, and organized fraud.
9. **floor-recovery gate** — waive or cap clawback where recovery would invade subsistence.
10. **review gate** — report improper payments, root causes, recovery rates, and eligible non-take-up.[S532]

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| urgent floor aid | provisional payment | later true-up. |
| unavailable documents | attestation | sample review. |
| stolen identity | victim repair | no future block. |
| good-faith overpayment | waiver/cap | no hardship collection. |
| false certification | recovery/penalty | culpability first. |
