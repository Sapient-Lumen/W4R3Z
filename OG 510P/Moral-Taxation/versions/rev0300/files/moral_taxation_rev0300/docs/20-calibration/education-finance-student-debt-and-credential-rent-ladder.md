# Education finance, student debt, and credential-rent ladder

## Question in one sentence

What ladder should decide when education finance is a capability investment, when student repayment is fair, and when a school, lender, or credential gatekeeper is extracting rent?[S503][S504]

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **capability gate** — identify the public capability: literacy, vocational skill, civic capacity, research, care work, transition, or local labour-market need.
2. **access gate** — ask whether low-income, first-generation, rural, disabled, migrant, caregiver, and adult learners can realistically enter and complete.
3. **finance gate** — compare grants, public provision, income-contingent repayment, debt cancellation, tax credits, employer training, and institutional support.[S503]
4. **price gate** — test whether aid capitalizes into tuition, fees, housing, mandatory materials, or credential gatekeeping.
5. **quality gate** — test completion, employment, earnings, licensing, student satisfaction, and transferability.
6. **debt gate** — protect borrowers against negative amortization, servicing error, default spirals, nondischargeability traps, and no-income periods.
7. **public-finance gate** — do not let shrinking public aid be laundered into private household debt or regressive tax expenditure.[S504]
8. **institutional-rent gate** — distinguish surplus spent on instruction/access from executive pay, marketing, endowment hoarding, or low-value programs.
9. **worker-portability gate** — training subsidies must not bind workers through repayment traps or non-compete-like devices.
10. **review gate** — revisit when debt distress, completion failure, tuition inflation, aid decline, or labor-market mismatch crosses threshold.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| floor access | grant/public provision | automatic and simple. |
| uncertain earnings return | income-contingent repayment | low-income floor and discharge. |
| high price/weak value | institutional condition | clawback or eligibility loss. |
| tax benefit skewed upward | retarget or convert | protect access and completion. |
| employer training | credit/subsidy | portable skill and no worker lock-in. |

## Rev0297 accountability map

- **Primary actor:** the program-quality controller, lender, servicer, accreditor, credential gatekeeper, or employer screen that controls price, access, servicing, or credential value.
- **Beneficiary to trace:** low-value programs, servicers, accreditors, or credential screens that capture tuition, fees, repayment spread, or labor-market gatekeeping benefits while borrowers carry the downside.
- **Required packet:** tuition and aid records, completion/earnings outcomes, servicing-error and collection records, accreditation or employment-placement claims, and borrower discharge/appeal records.
- **Default move:** route repair to price-quality review, servicing correction, debt relief, or public pathway capacity before blaming the student for relying on an officially eligible credential.
