# Whistleblower, relator reward, and revenue-integrity ladder

## Question in one sentence

What ladder should decide whether a whistleblower, informant, or relator regime improves public integrity rather than monetizing accusation, retaliation, or confidential-tax-information leakage?[S557][S558][S559][S560]

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **jurisdiction gate** — identify whether the claim concerns tax, securities, procurement, benefits, or other public funds.[S557][S560]
2. **specificity gate** — require original, specific, timely, credible information rather than generalized suspicion.
3. **confidentiality gate** — protect taxpayer and whistleblower information; disclose only as authorized and necessary.[S558]
4. **retaliation gate** — provide secure channels and anti-retaliation referral or remedy where applicable.[S561]
5. **causation gate** — link award to actual collected proceeds, sanctions, or public recovery.
6. **public-control gate** — preserve government control over intervention, settlement, dismissal, and litigation posture in relator cases.[S559]
7. **culpability gate** — reduce, deny, or separately pursue awards where the whistleblower planned, led, or profited from misconduct.
8. **proportionality gate** — cap or scale awards to avoid over-incentivizing speculative or coercive claims.
9. **timeliness gate** — publish claim aging, award delay, and communication standards.[S558]
10. **repair gate** — feed confirmed schemes into systemic compliance redesign, not only one-off recoveries.

## Rev0296 accountability map

- **Primary accountable actor:** the program-integrity office, whistleblower office, inspector-general function, court, or award decisionmaker controlling tip intake, corroboration, recovery, confidentiality, and award calculation.
- **Beneficiary / rent recipient:** the award claimant or relator and the public recovery program, but only after the information is separated from retaliation risk and translated into contestable proof.
- **Protected burden bearer:** the accused taxpayer or grantee, the source exposed to retaliation, and the public fisc if award incentives steer enforcement posture.
- **Required packet:** source basis, conflict screen, corroboration, action classification, confidentiality safeguards, accused-party notice / contest file, collected-proceeds ledger, and award-factor record.
- **Default move:** treat the tip as a lead until corroborated and firewall the award file from merits, penalty, collection, settlement, and criminal-referral posture.
