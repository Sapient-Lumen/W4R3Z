## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — fuel standard | declining lifecycle emissions intensity rule. | Strong when traceable and technology-neutral enough. |
| B — emissions levy | charge per verified emissions unit. | Use with proceeds repair and mobility safeguards. |
| C — eligible offsets | limited crediting for residual emissions. | Weak unless high-integrity and transitional. |
| D — transition fund | uses revenue for fuel, port, vessel, or route transition. | Strong with public-upside and equity rules. |
| E — mobility rebate | protects essential passenger or island access. | Use for floor cases, not general discounting. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **coverage gate** — define routes, ships, flights, fuels, and responsible operators.
2. **lifecycle gate** — include production, feedstock, land-use, and combustion effects.
3. **offset gate** — test additionality, permanence, leakage, double counting, and registry integrity.
4. **incidence gate** — map passenger, freight, consumer, port, and island burdens.
5. **local-health gate** — address port-adjacent air pollution separately from global carbon.
6. **proceeds gate** — dedicate funds to transition, climate repair, or protected mobility.
7. **competition gate** — prevent fuel-standard rents and incumbent carrier capture.
8. **data gate** — verify emissions without excessive trip surveillance.
9. **transition gate** — set phase-in, exemptions, and sunset for hard floor cases.
10. **review gate** — reopen on offset failure, fuel price spike, port burden, or compliance-data gap.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| high-integrity fuel path | fuel standard or credit | lifecycle evidence and feedstock guard. |
| residual emissions | narrow offset use | independent eligibility and no double credit. |
| port burden | local repair charge | health repair, not general fund absorption. |
| essential access | mobility rebate | means- or route-sensitive floor rule. |

## Rev0298 accountability map

- **Primary actor:** the airline, ship operator, fuel supplier, offset registry, verifier, port authority, or regulator controlling fuel choice, emissions reporting, credit eligibility, green premiums, or port-burden repair.
- **Beneficiary to trace:** the carrier, fuel supplier, credit broker, registry, or offset project capturing green premium, low-quality credit, bunker-fuel loophole, or transition-fund value.
- **Required packet:** lifecycle fuel and bunkering records, emissions verification, offset additionality/permanence/leakage/no-double-counting proof, port pollution records, mobility-floor files, and transition-fund ledgers.
- **Default move:** use verified fuel standards or emissions levies, limit offsets to high-integrity residual use, and route proceeds to fuel transition, port repair, and floor-preserving mobility rebates.
