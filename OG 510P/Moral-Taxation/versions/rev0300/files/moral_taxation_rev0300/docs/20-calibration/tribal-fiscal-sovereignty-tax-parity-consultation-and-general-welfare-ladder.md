## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — state/local parity | Tribal governments receive comparable tax treatment. | Floor default where government function is similar. |
| B — Tribal-specific rule | rule reflects treaty, trust, land, membership, or sovereignty facts. | Use where parity is insufficient. |
| C — general-welfare exclusion | member benefits excluded under program rules. | Strong with Tribal deference and anti-compensation limits. |
| D — consultation compact | rulemaking or project consultation. | Valid only with early, resourced, responsive process. |
| E — revenue-sharing/local repair | project proceeds support Tribal public goods. | Use with consent and non-substitution. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **status gate** — identify Tribal government, member, wholly owned entity, enterprise, or outside contractor.
2. **treaty/trust gate** — name treaty, trust, land, resource, or federal obligation.
3. **parity gate** — compare treatment with state, local, territorial, and nonprofit analogues.
4. **self-determination gate** — defer to Tribal program purpose and community need absent abuse.
5. **general-welfare gate** — separate welfare benefits from compensation, private benefit, or lavish transfer.
6. **consultation gate** — require early notice, resources, meaningful agenda control, and response-to-reasons.
7. **confidentiality gate** — protect culturally sensitive, personal, and commercial information.
8. **proceeds gate** — route resource, land, energy, data, or gaming claims to Tribal public goods first.
9. **administration gate** — avoid duplicated reporting, remittance, or penalty traps.
10. **review gate** — reopen on parity gap, consultation failure, sovereignty bypass, or member-floor harm.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| government function | state/local parity | improve where treaty/trust facts require. |
| member benefit | general-welfare exclusion | defer to Tribal need determination. |
| project on Tribal land | consent plus revenue share | no substitution for federal obligations. |
| federal rulemaking | consultation compact | response-to-reasons record. |

## Rev0299 accountability map

Rev0299 centers sovereignty, parity, and consultation records rather than treating tribal fiscal questions as ordinary public-benefit administration.

| Question | Default accountability answer |
|---|---|
| Who controls the decision? | Tribal governments, federal and state tax authorities, compact counterparties, benefit administrators, and consultation decisionmakers. |
| Who benefits? | State, federal, local, or private counterparties that capture parity gaps, jurisdictional bypass, revenue-share value, or reclassification benefits without meaningful tribal consent. |
| Who should not be blamed by default? | Tribal members, tribal governments bearing service load, and indigenous communities bypassed by consultation failure. |
| What evidence is decisive? | Government-to-government consultation, tribal fiscal-capacity records, compact and revenue-share files, general-welfare design, member access, and counterparty value capture. |
| Default move | Restore consultation, parity, self-governance, and general-welfare nonreclassification before extracting revenue or denying benefits. |
