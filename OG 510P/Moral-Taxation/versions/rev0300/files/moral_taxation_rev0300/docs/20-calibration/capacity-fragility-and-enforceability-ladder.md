# Capacity, fragility, and enforceability ladder

## Question in one sentence

Given the archive's closed rule that **administrative fit is part of tax justice, weak capacity should simplify toward observable bases and strong upstream anchors rather than weak-side paperwork, and simplification must still preserve rent-first ordering, floor protection, and review-bound graduation**, **what is the smallest workable ladder for deciding when ordinary administration is enough, when anchor-first simplification is justified, when bounded presumptive proxies may replace finer measurement, when emergency short-form collection is tolerable, and what review / sunset conditions must return the system to a richer rule set as capacity improves?**[S5][S9][S10][S15][S16][S17][S21][S23][S25][S26][S27][S39][S41][S64][S65][S66][S68][S69][S70]

Route: simplify only as far as real capacity requires, keep relief at least as workable as extraction, keep simplification tied to the right moral object, and bind low-capacity shortcuts to explicit review and graduation rather than letting fragility become a permanent excuse for regressive convenience.[S5][S15][S21][S23][S26][S27][S39][S41][S64][S65][S66][S68][S69]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — high-information ideal regardless of capacity | keep individualized valuation, broad filing, and dense review even where the records, staffing, or trust structure do not exist.[S21][S26][S27][S39][S41] | Reject: elegant on paper, unjust in operation. |
| B — convenience-first squeezing | tax whatever is easiest to bill or confiscate, even if that means staples, payroll, petty trade, or user-side fees while concentrated rents stay lightly touched.[S5][S21][S23][S25][S26][S27][S39] | Reject: fragility is not permission for convenience regressivity. |
| C — anchor-first simplification with review-bound graduation | move to ordinary records, withholding, customs, utilities, cadastre, controller groups, and bounded proxies while keeping floor protection and a path back to richer administration.[S9][S10][S15][S16][S17][S21][S23][S25][S26][S27][S39][S41][S64][S65][S66][S68][S69][S70] | Adopt. |
| D — emergency exceptionalism without sunset | use crisis or weak capacity to justify indefinite special levies, opaque bargains, or one-way extraction tools.[S21][S27][S39][S41][S69] | Reject: too easy to turn temporary incapacity into permanent lower-standard rule. |

## Five-rung ladder

1. **ordinary-record lane** — where payroll, billing, cadastre, customs, registry, or routine account records already see the base, keep the ordinary design rather than inventing a fragile-state exception too early.[S21][S23][S26][S27][S39][S41]
2. **upstream-anchor lane** — if diffuse downstream filing would fail, shift collection or reporting toward the strongest existing chokepoint such as withholding agents, utilities, merchants of record, customs points, large platforms, controller groups, or other actors that already see the flow.[S9][S10][S15][S16][S17][S21][S26][S27][S39][S41][S68]
3. **bounded-proxy lane** — where exact valuation or individualized measurement is not yet credible, use simple presumptive bands, safe harbors, cadence limits, and repairable proxies that still point at the right moral object.[S21][S23][S25][S26][S27][S39][S41][S66]
4. **crisis-short-form lane** — in fiscal collapse, conflict-adjacent governance, or comparable breakdown, use a narrow temporary code at the strongest visible anchors, paired with explicit floor protection and an announced review / sunset trigger.[S21][S27][S39][S41][S64][S65][S66][S69]
5. **graduation lane** — as cadastres improve, payroll or benefit coverage deepens, appeal capacity stabilizes, or controller-side packets become reliable, retire emergency shortcuts and move back toward richer base measurement, narrower proxies, and more ordinary review.[S21][S26][S27][S39][S41][S65][S66]

## Provisional recommendation

Adopt **Option C — anchor-first simplification with review-bound graduation** as the archive's default calibration for low-capacity or fragile settings.[S9][S10][S15][S16][S17][S21][S23][S25][S26][S27][S39][S41][S64][S65][S66][S68][S69][S70]

Presumption:

- use the ordinary design where ordinary records already exist,
- otherwise move first to **upstream anchors**, not weak-side refiling,
- rely on **bounded proxies** only where finer measurement is not yet honestly supportable,
- reserve **crisis short forms** for real breakdown rather than routine administrative laziness,
- and attach every simplification path to visible **review and graduation triggers**.

This is the archive's smallest workable setting because it preserves moral ordering under fragility without pretending weak institutions can sustain high-information elegance or letting low capacity excuse arbitrary extraction.

## Default placement matrix

| Context | Default rung | Why it usually fits | Archive warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| ordinary payroll, utility, customs, or cadastre administration with tolerable records | ordinary-record lane | existing ordinary anchors already carry most of the work.[S21][S23][S26][S27][S39][S41] | do not downgrade to a crisis code just because a simpler story is politically attractive. |
| weak filing capacity but strong employer, platform, customs, utility, or payment chokepoints | upstream-anchor lane | strong intermediaries see the flow more reliably than households or tiny firms do.[S9][S10][S15][S16][S17][S21][S26][S27][S39][S41][S68] | anchor-first is not a license to make users or workers prove the same facts again downstream. |
| sparse valuation capacity, incomplete registries, or informal practice that defeats fine measurement | bounded-proxy lane | honest presumptions are often better than fake precision.[S21][S23][S25][S26][S27][S39][S41][S66] | keep proxies tied to the right object; do not let convenience silently change the tax. |
| local extraction or data-centre host burden with weak bargaining or low administrative depth | upstream-anchor lane, sometimes bounded-proxy lane | concentrated immovable burdens can usually be reached through site, utility, service-burden, or host-charge anchors.[S15][S23][S25][S29][S30][S69][S70] | do not swap durable claims for abatements, cheap inputs, or opaque siting bargains. |
| emergency, collapse, or conflict-adjacent governance | crisis-short-form lane | a short workable code can be more just than a comprehensive one that cannot run.[S21][S27][S39][S41][S69] | crisis simplification must carry sunset and floor-protection rules from the start. |
| low-capacity AI or cross-border digital governance | upstream-anchor lane, with bounded proxies if needed | controller groups, compute, utilities, merchants of record, and payment rails usually see more than end users do.[S9][S10][S15][S16][S17][S21][S27][S39][S41][S68] | do not answer weak capacity with user-side machine head taxes or fantasy direct model liability. |

## Anti-patterns

- **fragile-state perfectionism** — writing a morally elegant code that cannot actually be seen, billed, reviewed, or repaired.[S21][S26][S27][S39][S41]
- **convenience regressivity** — sparing concentrated rents while squeezing the easiest visible weak-side flows.[S5][S21][S23][S25][S26][S27][S39]
- **relief lag under simplification** — letting collection ride the strong rail while rebates, corrections, or hardship support still depend on separate maze-like claims.[S21][S27][S39][S41][S64][S65][S66][S68]
- **digital theater** — mistaking vendor dashboards, thin software, or imported compliance suites for real authority, record integrity, and appeal capacity.[S21][S27][S39][S41][S68]
- **emergency permanence** — keeping crisis shortcuts after the trigger is gone or after institutions have recovered enough to graduate.[S21][S27][S39][S41][S69]

## What would change the recommendation

Tighten, loosen, or partly replace this ladder if:

- evidence shows some low-information settings can support richer review or finer measurement without shifting burden back onto weak actors,
- reliable public registries, benefits delivery, open credentials, or controller-side packets make graduation faster than this memo assumes,
- or fragility proves so deep that even the proposed anchors routinely fail to carry floor protection, correction, or anti-corruption discipline without stronger club support or non-tax instruments.[S10][S15][S16][S17][S21][S23][S26][S27][S39][S41][S64][S65][S66][S68][S69][S70]
