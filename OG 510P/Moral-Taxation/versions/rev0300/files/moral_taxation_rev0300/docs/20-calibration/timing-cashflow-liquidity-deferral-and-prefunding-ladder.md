# Timing, cash-flow, liquidity, deferral, and prefunding ladder

## Question in one sentence

Given the archive's closed rule that tax timing should sit **as close as justified to cash generation, recurring rent, measurable harm, or risk creation**, **what is the smallest workable ladder for deciding when to withhold near receipt, when to bill on a periodic cadence, when to use adjustable prepayment, when instalments or lien-backed deferral are enough, and when ex ante prefunding should replace ordinary after-the-fact payment entirely?**[S21][S23][S24][S39][S41][S50][S51][S64][S65][S66][S86]

Route: match timing to real cash flow, recurring rent, or risk creation as closely as possible, but do not let timing convenience become hardship-blind extraction, permanent deferral privilege, or a fantasy that post-collapse collection will do the work of earlier prefunding.[S21][S23][S39][S41][S50][S51][S64][S65][S66][S86]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — annual settlement default | use ordinary year-end or period-end settlement as the general answer, with only narrow withholding or estimated-payment exceptions.[S21][S39] | Reject: delays known collection points and creates avoidable arrears on regular flows. |
| B — realization or transfer timing for most non-wage bases | collect mainly on sale, inheritance, formal transfer, or other large visible legal events, and avoid recurring billing except where law already makes it unavoidable.[S8][S23][S24] | Reject: overprotects durable rents and easily deferred appreciation. |
| C — timing-and-liquidity ladder | sort bases into near-receipt, periodic-billing or adjustable-prepayment, instalment-and-true-up, bounded-deferral, or ex-ante-prefunding lanes.[S15][S17][S21][S23][S24][S39][S41][S50][S51][S64][S65][S66][S86] | Adopt. |
| D — maximum prefunding and deposit by default | require broad advance deposits, reserves, or high-frequency prepayment across most tax bases whenever technology can support it.[S39][S41] | Reject: flattens real differences among wages, rents, illiquid holdings, and catastrophic-risk activity. |

## Five-rung ladder

1. **near-receipt lane** — use for wages, salaries, routine platform earnings, and other highly regular visible flows where withholding or pay-as-you-go collection best tracks cash generation,[S21][S39][S65][S66]
2. **periodic-billing or adjustable-prepayment lane** — use for recurring rents, utility-like burdens, or business flows where quarterly or annual billing works only if it can adjust quickly to downturns,[S23][S24][S39][S50][S51]
3. **instalment-and-true-up lane** — use where the base is right but the cash arrival is lumpy, so staged payment and rapid correction are better than blunt immediate collection,[S23][S24][S39][S50][S64][S65][S66]
4. **lien-backed or tightly bounded deferral lane** — use for illiquid but durable bases, especially land or asset-rich cash-poor cases, where the claim should survive but current cash pressure makes immediate full payment crude,[S5][S23][S24][S39][S86]
5. **ex ante prefunding lane** — use for catastrophic or thin-shelled downside, where reserves, bonds, insurance, escrow, or supervised prefunding should arrive before failure rather than after it.[S15][S17][S39][S41]

## Provisional recommendation

Adopt **Option C — the timing-and-liquidity ladder** as the archive's default calibration.[S15][S17][S21][S23][S24][S39][S41][S50][S51][S64][S65][S66][S86]

Presumption:

- stay near receipt where flows are regular and already observed,
- bill or prepay on cadence where rents or business flows recur but can still be re-estimated honestly,
- use staged payment and rapid true-up where the base is correct but timing mismatch is real,
- allow only bounded, visible deferral where current illiquidity is genuine and the claim can be secured without disappearing,
- and move to reserves, bonds, insurance, or escrow first where ordinary payment would predictably arrive after insolvency, cleanup need, or public loss.

That is the narrowest workable setting because it blocks both hardship-blind acceleration and sympathy-driven permanent escape.

For AI-era taxes, the timing implication is practical: ordinary controller-side receipts or routine facility charges can sit in withholding, billing, or adjustable-prepayment lanes, but catastrophic compute, environmental, grid, or cleanup downside belongs in the **ex ante prefunding lane**. Thin shells plus ex post collection are not a timing design; they are a forecast of non-payment.[S15][S17][S39][S41]

## Default timing table

| Base or context | Default timing rung | Why this rung usually fits | Archive warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| ordinary wage and salary income | near-receipt lane | employer withholding tracks regular cash wages closely and performs strongly in practice compared with looser payment channels.[S21][S39][S65][S66] | withholding convenience must not outrun floor protection, correction rights, or payroll syncing. |
| broad consumption taxes or utility-like corrective charges | near-activity remittance plus fast offset overlay | the taxable event is visible at purchase or billing, but floor protection must arrive comparably fast.[S3][S4][S6][S7][S64][S65][S66] | do not let rebates or credits land much later than the burden they are supposed to neutralize. |
| land and recurring immovable rents | periodic-billing lane, with instalments if needed | durable records and recurring rent support scheduled billing better than sale-only timing.[S5][S23][S25][S39] | illiquidity may justify instalments or secured deferral, not erasure of the site-rent claim. |
| inheritance, gifts, and large realized gains | event-time settlement with staged payment where liquidity is genuinely mismatched | legal transfer is a salient moment, but staged settlement may be needed where assets are not instantly liquid.[S8][S23][S24] | do not let staging quietly become indefinite untaxed appreciation or dynastic shelter. |
| small firms and volatile-income taxpayers | adjustable-prepayment lane | estimated payments can work if they adjust quickly to downturns and overcollection is refunded or credited rapidly.[S26][S39][S50][S51] | rigid presumptive schedules should not keep taxing the memory of last year's income. |
| concentrated fortunes tied up in land or thinly traded assets | lien-backed or bounded deferral lane after visibility is established | the backstop claim can survive current cash mismatch if visibility, valuation, and security are strong enough.[S23][S24][S39][S73][S86] | do not smuggle a standing privilege into the system under the name of liquidity relief. |
| frontier AI, hazardous extraction, or other high-downside stacks | ex ante prefunding lane | post-incident or post-insolvency collection arrives too late where the real problem is failure risk and cleanup finance.[S15][S17][S39][S41] | thin shells plus ex post collection are not a timing design; they are a forecast of non-payment. |

## Anti-patterns

- **fast taking, slow giving** — near-real-time withholding or billing paired with delayed refunds, rebates, or loss recognition.[S50][S51][S64][S65][S66]
- **sale-event fetish** — refusing recurring billing on durable rents even when realization merely measures patience and avoidance capacity.[S5][S8][S23][S24]
- **defer-by-status** — turning visible liquidity relief into a long-lived privilege for politically protected asset holders.[S23][S24][S39][S86]
- **estimated-payment inertia** — continuing prepayment schedules through downturns as if current income were unchanged.[S39][S50][S51]
- **post-failure make-believe** — pretending that tax claims after collapse can substitute for bonds, reserves, insurance, or escrow before collapse.[S15][S17][S39][S41]

## What would change the recommendation

Tighten, loosen, or replace this ladder if:

- real-time data and prefilled systems begin reliably improving both collection timing and speedy correction without expanding coercion or surveillance,[S65][S66]
- better evidence shows that some recurring taxes can be billed less frequently without materially increasing arrears, hardship, or avoidance,[S39]
- secured deferral proves either more abuse-prone or more humane than current evidence suggests for asset-rich cash-poor cases,[S23][S24][S86]
- or high-downside sectors demonstrate genuinely robust prefunding, making some ex post recovery tools less central than they are now.[S15][S17][S39][S41]
