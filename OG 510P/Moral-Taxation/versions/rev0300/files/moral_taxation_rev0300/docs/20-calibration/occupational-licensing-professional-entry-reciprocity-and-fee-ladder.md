## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — full license monopoly | exclusive right to practice plus fees and local exams. | Strong only for real health/safety risk with least-restrictive proof. |
| B — registration / title protection | reserve a title but allow lower-risk work. | Often better for consumer information without entry rent. |
| C — reciprocity / compact | recognize comparable licenses across jurisdictions. | Strong where mobility burden is the main problem. |
| D — supervised provisional practice | allow entry while completing missing local requirements. | Strong for military spouses, migrants, and low-income entrants. |
| E — fee waiver / record cure | remove unpaid-fee or paperwork traps. | Required when fees become work-permission tolls. |
