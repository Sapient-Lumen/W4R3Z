# Same-facts reuse, portability, and delta-update ladder

## Question in one sentence

Given the archive's closed rule that **the same actor should not have to prove the same facts again when the live object, period logic, and legal test are materially the same**, **what is the smallest workable ladder for deciding when a prior packet should simply travel, when a short no-change or changed-fact delta is enough, when a local supplement is required, and when full refiling is justified because integrity, legal difference, or material change really broke the earlier answer?**[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S68][S89][S91][S92]

Route: portability should usually reduce repeated proof, not turn one old packet into a universal passport. Reuse should move through an operative prior answer plus bounded deltas, local supplements, and integrity-triggered reopening when the earlier packet no longer matches the live question.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S68][S89][S91][S92]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — clean-sheet refiling by default | require a new full return, packet, or claim each period or authority interaction even when nothing material changed.[S27][S39][S41][S68][S89] | Reject: multiplies burden, recollects the same facts, and makes weaker actors restate hidden records they do not control. |
| B — universal passport lane | let one accepted packet bind every later authority, period, and levy until someone proves fraud or gross error.[S20][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S91][S92] | Reject: portability cannot erase legal difference, audience rights, or material change. |
| C — graduated reuse lane | reuse the settled core when the live question is materially the same, use no-change or changed-fact deltas for bounded updates, require local supplements for genuinely different tests, and reserve full refiling for integrity failure or real mismatch.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S68][S89][S91][S92] | Adopt. |
| D — silent backend reuse only | let officials reuse prior answers internally without visible lineage, subject notice, or issue-bounded contest rights.[S21][S39][S40][S41][S44][S91][S92] | Reject: hidden portability defeats contestability and turns reuse into unreviewable administrative memory. |

## Five-rung ladder

1. **live-core reuse lane** — when the object, period logic, and legal test are materially unchanged, rely on the operative prior packet or determination as the starting answer rather than demanding a new full submission.[S27][S39][S41][S68][S89]
2. **no-change attestation lane** — when continuity matters but nothing material changed, accept a short attribution plus no-change statement instead of recollecting the whole file.[S27][S39][S41][S68][S89]
3. **changed-fact delta lane** — when one bounded fact changed, require only the changed field, the effective date, and any directly dependent recalculation.[S21][S27][S39][S41][S68][S89]
4. **local-supplement lane** — when a later authority, payment rail, audience tier, or levy asks a genuinely narrower or different question, reuse the common core and add a short local supplement instead of discarding the core.[S20][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S91][S92]
5. **fresh-file lane** — require full refiling only when integrity failure, material legal difference, supersession confusion, unresolved contradiction, or material-change accumulation means the prior packet no longer answers the live question reliably.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S68][S89][S91][S92]

## Provisional recommendation

Adopt **Option C — graduated reuse lane** as the archive's default calibration for ordinary portability outside the controller-map special family.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S68][S89][S91][S92]

Presumption:

- same object plus same test should usually stop at **live-core reuse**,
- continuity checks should usually be satisfied by a **no-change attestation**,
- bounded updates should usually move by **changed-fact delta**,
- genuinely different local requirements should travel as a **local supplement** layered on the reusable core,
- and **fresh-file refiling** should be reserved for integrity break, material legal difference, or accumulated change that makes the old answer misleading.

This is the archive's smallest workable general setting because it preserves lineage and contestability while preventing both passport absolutism and refiling theater.

## Default portability matrix

| Live posture | Minimum portability answer | Why it usually fits | Archive warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| same filer, same asset or claim, nearby period, same test | live-core reuse | repeated full proof is usually busywork rather than truth-seeking.[S27][S39][S41][S68][S89] | do not quietly carry stale packets across a material period break. |
| same question, no material change | no-change attestation | continuity matters, but recollecting the whole file adds little.[S27][S39][S41][S68][S89] | keep the attestation attributable and period-bounded. |
| one changed amount, address, threshold fact, status, or issuer correction | changed-fact delta | the archive usually needs the new fact, not a rebuilt dossier.[S21][S27][S39][S41][S68][S89] | do not hide foundational shifts inside a supposed small delta. |
| different authority or levy with a narrower or partly different test | local supplement | portability should reduce duplication without flattening difference.[S20][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S91][S92] | do not export one regime's closure point as automatic closure everywhere else. |
| stale, contradictory, strategically partial, or lineage-broken prior packet | fresh file or issue-bounded reopen | bad portability spreads bad answers fast.[S16][S17][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S89][S91][S92] | suspend reuse first, not contest rights. |

## Anti-patterns

- **refile theater** — demanding a clean-sheet packet because the authority can, not because the live issue changed.[S27][S39][S41][S68]
- **passport absolutism** — pretending one prior answer settles every later period, audience, or levy.[S20][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S91][S92]
- **delta laundering** — burying a foundational shift inside what is labeled a tiny update.[S16][S17][S21][S27][S39][S41][S89]
- **hidden reuse against strangers** — silently carrying a prior packet into a later dispute without giving affected parties the audience-tier access needed to contest it.[S40][S41][S44][S91][S92]
- **lineage fog** — making portability depend on a long guesswork chain of versions, supersessions, or partial corrections.[S21][S27][S39][S41][S68][S89]

## What would change the recommendation

Tighten, loosen, or partly replace this ladder if:

- verifiable credentials or privacy-preserving proofs make no-change reuse more reliable across authorities,
- packet lineage and supersession standards become robust enough that wider reuse carries less contestability risk,
- evidence shows some recurring claims still need more frequent fresh-file resets than this ladder assumes,
- or subject-side notice and portability rights prove too weak to keep backend reuse visible and challengeable.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S68][S89][S91][S92]
