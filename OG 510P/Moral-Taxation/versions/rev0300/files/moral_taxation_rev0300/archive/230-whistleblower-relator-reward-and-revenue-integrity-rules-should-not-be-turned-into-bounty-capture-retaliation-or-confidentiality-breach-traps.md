# Whistleblower, relator reward, and revenue-integrity rules should not be turned into bounty capture, retaliation, or confidentiality-breach traps

## Claim

Whistleblower and relator systems are legitimate integrity tools when they reveal hidden evasion or public-fund fraud, but they require specificity, causation, public control, confidentiality, anti-retaliation, and culpability screens.[S557][S559][S560]

## Why it matters

Public agencies need insiders and affected parties to surface schemes that audits miss. But a reward system can also monetize accusation, leak taxpayer information, chill internal compliance, or expose vulnerable whistleblowers unless the channel is tightly governed.[S558][S561]

## Route

Use [`../docs/10-framework/whistleblower-relator-reward-and-revenue-integrity-routing.md`](../docs/10-framework/whistleblower-relator-reward-and-revenue-integrity-routing.md) and [`../docs/20-calibration/whistleblower-relator-reward-and-revenue-integrity-ladder.md`](../docs/20-calibration/whistleblower-relator-reward-and-revenue-integrity-ladder.md). The compact rule is: reward verified public recovery, not suspicion; protect informants, accused taxpayers, and the public process.

## Anti-patterns

- **bounty capture** — accusation becomes a private revenue strategy.
- **informer extortion** — threat of disclosure replaces lawful process.
- **retaliation chill** — no safe channel exists for credible insiders.
- **confidentiality breach** — tax or case data escape the authorized proceeding.
