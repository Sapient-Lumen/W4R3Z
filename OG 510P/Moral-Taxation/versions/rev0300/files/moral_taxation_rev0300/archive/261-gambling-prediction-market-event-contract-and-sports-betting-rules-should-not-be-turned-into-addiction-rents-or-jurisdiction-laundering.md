# Gambling prediction market event contract and sports betting rules should not be turned into addiction rents or jurisdiction laundering

## Claim

Event markets can price information and hedge real exposures, but the label “derivative” cannot erase gambling-like behavioral risk, state-law conflict, insider information, match-fixing, or youth/addiction exposure. Tax and fee design should charge real externalities and integrity costs while banning or gating contracts that sell harm rather than risk transfer.[S634][S635][S644]

## Why it matters

Classification seams are where public systems often leak moral clarity: an insurance surcharge looks actuarial, a wager looks like finance, a telecom contribution looks like a small bill line, a digital-ad tax looks like rent capture, a portable benefit looks like worker protection, an environmental payment looks like mitigation, or a release checklist looks like bureaucracy. The archive should ask who controls the burden, who bears it, and what floor or public claim is at stake before accepting the label.[S634][S635][S644]

## Anti-patterns

- **derivative label laundering** — federal finance form is used to escape gambling, consumer, tribal, or public-integrity safeguards.
- **addiction rent** — revenue depends on high-frequency repeat loss, inducements, or vulnerable retail participation.
- **inside information bet** — actors with privileged event access or influence trade against the public.
- **state law bypass** — market location or contract form is chosen mainly to defeat local gambling policy.
- **sports integrity theater** — integrity is promised without enforceable insider bans, monitoring, or data-sharing.
