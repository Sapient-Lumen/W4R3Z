# IP, data, intangible, royalty, and transfer-pricing rules should not be turned into paper-ownership rents or source-state erasure traps

## Claim

Intangible income should not follow legal title alone. Patents, software, brands, algorithms, data assets, and licenses must be routed through real development, enhancement, maintenance, protection, exploitation, funding control, risk control, market/user contribution, and administrability.[S456][S457][S458]

## Why it matters

Paper ownership can move faster than factories, workers, users, public infrastructure, and markets. If the tax system treats shell title as value creation, it converts administrative complexity into a rent and makes weaker source states prove facts that the group alone controls.

## Route

Use [`../docs/10-framework/intangible-ip-data-and-royalty-shifting-routing.md`](../docs/10-framework/intangible-ip-data-and-royalty-shifting-routing.md) and [`../docs/20-calibration/intangible-transfer-pricing-dempe-and-royalty-routing-ladder.md`](../docs/20-calibration/intangible-transfer-pricing-dempe-and-royalty-routing-ladder.md). The compact rule is: legal IP ownership starts the record, but real control, risk, people, exploitation, and market/source contribution decide the moral claim.

## Anti-patterns

- **paper-title rent** — a low-substance entity claims the residual because it owns the document.
- **royalty base drain** — deductions shift local earnings to a conduit without commensurate value.
- **proof asymmetry trap** — the taxpayer controls all group records while the source state is told it lacks comparables.
- **routine-distributor laundering** — a simplified return meant for low-risk distribution shelters unique intangible returns.
