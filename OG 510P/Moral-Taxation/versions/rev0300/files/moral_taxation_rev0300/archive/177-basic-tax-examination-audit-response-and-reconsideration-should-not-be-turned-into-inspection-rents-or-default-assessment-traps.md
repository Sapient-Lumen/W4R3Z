# 177 — Basic tax examination, audit response, and reconsideration should not be turned into inspection rents or default-assessment traps

## One-line thesis

When the tax administration examines a return, requests records, schedules an interview, changes an assessment after audit, or reopens an audit through reconsideration, the verification lane should be issue-scoped, intelligible, reschedulable where reasonable, document-copy friendly, and no-rent. Audit is a legitimate integrity tool; it should not become a paid inspection market, an impossible-availability trap, or a default-assessment machine that converts one missed letter, one inconvenient appointment, or one inaccessible proof channel into practical liability finality.[S16][S17][S27][S39][S41][S42][S44][S89][S191][S192][S193][S194][S195][S196]

## Why this matters

The archive now blocks many procedural rents: review paywalls, filing-access gates, hardship rents, payout skims, record rents, comprehension tolls, remittance tolls, certificate rents, submission tolls, proxy-access gates, stale-coordinate traps, victim-paid identity recovery, reporter-hostage mismatch cleanup, captive-household allocation, time-trap forfeiture, ledger suspense, and collateral collection-release ransom. Those rules cover the ordinary edges of tax administration. But an examination sits at the coercive center: it can ask for records, hold refunds, change liability, move the case toward assessment, and make later appeal harder if the subject cannot practically answer.[S16][S27][S39][S41][S42][S117][S118][S168][S174][S191][S192][S193][S194][S195][S196]

Current public materials show the right nonextractive shape. IRS audit guidance describes an audit as a review or examination of books, accounts, and financial records to verify reported information and the correct amount of tax; Publication 556 explains examination rules, appeal rights, and refund claims; TAS correspondence-audit guidance describes reading the letter, comparing proposed changes, sending copies rather than originals, requesting more time, and using manager or appeal routes; TAS in-person audit guidance explains notice, exam location, interview scope, representation, and accommodation where time or place is inconvenient; and TAS audit-reconsideration guidance recognizes reopened review where the taxpayer has new information, disagrees, missed an appointment, or moved and never received the report.[S191][S192][S193][S194][S195][S196]

The archive generalizes from those anchors. A tax authority may verify; it may not price access to verification, demand needless original-document surrender, convert correspondence delays into silent admissions, or require ordinary subjects to buy representation merely to keep an examination from hardening into liability. The question is not whether audits may exist. It is whether the audit design preserves standing, proportionality, and bounded proof.[S16][S17][S27][S39][S41][S42][S89][S191][S192][S193][S194][S195]

## Archive rule

1. **Keep audit scope named and bounded.** The opening notice should identify the return, period, issue or line items under examination, requested records, response channel, deadline, and what happens if the subject agrees, disagrees, needs help, or needs more time.[S16][S27][S39][S41][S191][S192][S193][S194]
2. **Match intensity to the disputed fact.** Correspondence, narrow document review, targeted interview, field visit, summons-like compulsion, and escalated review should sit on a ladder. The administration should not start with the most intrusive lane where a narrower record check can test the issue.[S16][S17][S27][S40][S41][S89][S191][S192][S193][S194]
3. **Do not sell the answer channel.** Baseline audit response, document-copy submission, ordinary status inquiries, extra-time requests, manager contact, and first appeal should not require paid representatives, proprietary portals, premium upload tools, or paid transcript bundles.[S27][S39][S41][S42][S117][S118][S138][S139][S140][S193][S194][S196]
4. **Do not turn scheduling into liability.** Reasonable rescheduling, location accommodation, representative attendance, and remote or written alternatives should be available when issue scope permits. A person should not lose on the merits merely because daytime appearance, travel, disability, caregiving, confinement, disaster, digital outage, or address failure made one audit setting unusable.[S27][S39][S41][S42][S64][S65][S168][S170][S193][S194][S195]
5. **Use copies and targeted records before raw-record domination.** Ordinary examinations should request copies, extracts, or bounded attestations where sufficient. Original documents, full-account credentials, complete inboxes, broad device access, or unrelated logs require a stronger issue-specific justification.[S16][S17][S27][S40][S41][S89][S91][S92][S193]
6. **Preserve disagreement paths before coercive assessment.** A proposed audit change should come with a visible path to agree, disagree in part, provide additional evidence, seek supervisory review, seek appeal where available, or pay and claim refund where that is the chosen route.[S16][S27][S39][S41][S42][S117][S118][S186][S192][S193][S196]
7. **Keep audit reconsideration from becoming a premium rescue lane.** Where the taxpayer missed an appointment, never received the report, moved, later finds material evidence, or plausibly shows that the audit default does not reflect the merits, a bounded reconsideration path should be available without private rescue fees or total whole-case relitigation when a narrower correction can do the work.[S27][S39][S41][S42][S174][S195][S196]
8. **Current AI gets no direct subject-side analogue because current AI is not yet a tax person.** For present systems, audits run against controllers, remitters, firms, fiduciaries, and accountable humans or entities. If a future rights-bearing artificial person ever exists, examination notice, response, interview accommodation, representation, and reconsideration must be person-side, not owner-console mediated.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44][S191][S194][S195]

## Pattern pack

### 1. Audit is verification, not punishment by appointment

The fact that a return is selected for audit should not itself be treated as culpability. Selection can be random, risk-based, mismatch-based, or issue-based; the moral duty is to give the subject a real chance to answer the named issue before the process becomes sanction, collection, or public stigma.[S16][S17][S27][S39][S40][S41][S191][S192]

### 2. Correspondence audit should stay correspondence-sized

A mail audit for a bounded credit, dependency claim, wage item, withholding fact, or other narrow issue should not grow into a whole-life proof demand unless the response reveals a real broader contradiction. The state may ask for support; it should not require ordinary people to recreate their entire financial archive to answer one line item.[S27][S39][S41][S89][S193][S196]

### 3. In-person audit requires accommodation logic

An in-person interview can be justified when the issue genuinely turns on business records, financial history, unavailable facts, or live explanation. But time, place, representation, disability, caregiving, distance, custody, detention, language, or disaster constraints should shape the channel. Scheduling inconvenience should be managed as administration, not exploited as waiver.[S27][S39][S42][S64][S65][S168][S194]

### 4. Copies first; originals and full credentials last

The ordinary audit response should preserve the subject's own records. Requests to send originals, surrender devices, expose full account credentials, or provide broad communications should require clear necessity and bounded handling. The archive's proof principle is targeted verification, not raw access because raw access is easier.[S16][S17][S27][S40][S41][S89][S91][S92][S193]

### 5. Manager contact and appeal are not luxuries

If the examiner does not accept the documentation or proposes changes, the next step should be visible: explanation, manager conference where available, written disagreement, appeal, or refund-claim path. A subject may still choose a professional representative, but baseline disagreement should not be a product sold by the private market.[S27][S39][S41][S42][S117][S118][S186][S192][S193][S196]

### 6. Default assessment should be reopenable for real missed-process facts

Audit finality matters, but default finality is morally weak where the subject never received the report, moved, missed an appointment for a nonculpable reason, or later obtains material evidence. Reconsideration should be issue-scoped and time-disciplined, yet reachable without paying the alleged balance merely to be heard.[S27][S39][S41][S42][S174][S195][S196]

### 7. Future artificial persons need person-side examination response

If a future artificial person becomes a real tax subject, the tax authority cannot audit it only through a former owner, hosting provider, custodian, or wallet console. Examination notice, response, records, interview substitutes, representation, and reconsideration must be available to the recognized subject in its own standing bundle.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44][S191][S194][S195]

## Guardrails

- no paid representative, paid portal, paid transcript broker, premium upload tool, or proprietary software account as the ordinary path to answer a basic examination
- no whole-case default assessment where the examination was framed as a bounded issue and the subject raised a concrete response, rescheduling, notice, disability, language, disaster, or address problem
- no treating missed appointment, late response, or failed upload as merits admission without a no-rent cure path where the failure was plausibly nonculpable
- no routine demand for original documents, full credentials, complete communications archives, or unrelated logs where copies, bounded attestations, or narrower records can test the issue
- no audit-generated collection escalation that ignores live disagreement, audit reconsideration, hardship, deadline-preservation, ledger, identity, household, or reporter-mismatch facts
- no owner-side monopoly over examination response or reconsideration if future artificial personhood ever makes the taxed subject real

## Failure modes

- **inspection rent** — the subject must buy professional help, software, account access, or private record-reconstruction merely to answer a routine examination.
- **default-assessment trap** — missed mail, address error, digital failure, inconvenient appointment, or absent original record becomes practical liability finality.
- **scope creep audit** — a line-item or correspondence audit expands into broad financial discovery without a named issue-specific reason.
- **original-document hostage** — the subject is pressured to surrender irreplaceable records or credentials when copies or bounded evidence would suffice.
- **scheduling as waiver** — travel, work, caregiving, disability, confinement, disaster, or language barriers are treated as consent to the proposed change.
- **reconsideration paywall** — the practical way to reopen an audit default is to hire a professional, pay in full, or buy a special proof package.
- **owner-exam tax person** — a future artificial person's audit can be answered only through the former owner's dashboard, records, or representative key.

## Practical tests

1. Does the audit notice identify the period, issue, requested records, response channel, deadline, and disagreement path?
2. Is the chosen audit channel proportional to the live issue, or is the administration using in-person or broad-document demands by default?
3. Can the subject send copies, explanations, or bounded evidence without surrendering originals, credentials, or unrelated records?
4. Can the subject request more time, a different practical setting, manager contact, or appeal through a no-rent channel?
5. If the audit default was caused by nonreceipt, move, missed appointment, inaccessible channel, or later material evidence, is reconsideration issue-scoped and reachable?
6. If the subject were a future rights-bearing artificial person, could it receive notice, answer, be represented, and seek reconsideration without former-owner permission?
