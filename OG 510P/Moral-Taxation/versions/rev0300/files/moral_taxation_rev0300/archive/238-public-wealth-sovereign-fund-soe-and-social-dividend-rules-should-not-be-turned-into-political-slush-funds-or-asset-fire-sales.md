# Public wealth, sovereign fund, SOE, and social-dividend rules should not be turned into political slush funds or asset fire sales

## Claim

Public ownership and sovereign funds are not automatic justice. They can preserve rents for future generations, stabilize budgets, and fund social dividends, but only with clear objectives, professional governance, public accounts, withdrawal rules, and anti-capture safeguards.[S579][S580][S581]

## Why it matters

A tax archive focused only on annual flows misses public balance sheets. Resource rents, AI windfalls, public equity, SOE earnings, and strategic funds can repair common claims across time. They can also hide subsidies, patronage, risky investments, or asset stripping. The same incidence discipline must follow the asset.[S579][S580][S581]

The compact rule is: do not accept fiscal power that hides authority, burden, beneficiary, proof limits, floor risk, or public repair behind a more convenient label.

## Anti-patterns

- **political slush fund** — assets finance patronage or off-budget spending.
- **asset fire sale** — public balance-sheet value is liquidated to hide current deficits.
- **SOE rent shield** — state ownership protects inefficiency, monopoly, or corruption from scrutiny.
- **dividend theater** — social dividend is promised without stable funding or beneficiary rule.
