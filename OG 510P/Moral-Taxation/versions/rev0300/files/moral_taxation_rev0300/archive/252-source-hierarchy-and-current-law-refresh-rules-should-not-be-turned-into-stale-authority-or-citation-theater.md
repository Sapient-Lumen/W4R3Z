# Source hierarchy and current-law refresh rules should not be turned into stale authority or citation theater

## Claim

The archive needs citations to stay trustworthy, but citations are not magic. A stale source, proposed rule, news summary, or advocacy report should not harden into current law merely because it has an ID.[S59][S594]

## Why it matters

A datacube grows brittle when source accumulation substitutes for source judgment. This note gives the cube a source-hierarchy valve: primary law and official guidance settle legal status; authoritative data grounds incidence; news flags live events; speculation stays labeled.[S59][S594]

## Route

Use [`../docs/10-framework/source-hierarchy-conflict-refresh-and-current-law-routing.md`](../docs/10-framework/source-hierarchy-conflict-refresh-and-current-law-routing.md) and [`../docs/20-calibration/source-hierarchy-conflict-refresh-and-current-law-ladder.md`](../docs/20-calibration/source-hierarchy-conflict-refresh-and-current-law-ladder.md). The compact rule is: use the strongest current source for the claim, and prune sources that do no routing work.

## Anti-patterns

- **stale authority** — a past proposal or old rule is treated as current.
- **citation theater** — citations decorate rather than support the route.
- **news as law** — event coverage substitutes for operative legal text.
- **source pileup** — the registry grows while the cube does not become clearer.
