# Personal data, behavioral advertising, and platform gatekeeper rents should not be turned into consent theater or privacy-extraction traps

## Claim

A tax system should not treat personal data as a natural resource owned by extractors or as a taxable head count on users. It should route by extraction rent, gatekeeper power, privacy harm, behavioral-ad surplus, public-input dependence, and whether a non-tax rule is morally superior.[S459][S460][S461]

## Why it matters

Data extraction often hides behind formal consent while the real bargain is lock-in, opacity, behavioral manipulation, or market dependence. Taxing the data flow without fixing the extraction can turn surveillance into a licensed revenue stream.

## Route

Use [`../docs/10-framework/data-extraction-privacy-and-ad-market-rent-routing.md`](../docs/10-framework/data-extraction-privacy-and-ad-market-rent-routing.md) and [`../docs/20-calibration/data-extraction-privacy-harm-and-ad-market-rent-ladder.md`](../docs/20-calibration/data-extraction-privacy-harm-and-ad-market-rent-ladder.md). The compact rule is: do not tax being observed; charge or prohibit extraction, bottleneck, and harm.

## Anti-patterns

- **consent theater** — clickwrap is treated as substantive permission.
- **privacy-as-revenue** — harmful processing is allowed because it pays a fee.
- **user-count proxy tax** — the base falls on people rather than extractor surplus.
- **ad-market fog** — auction opacity hides who captures the rent and who bears the burden.
