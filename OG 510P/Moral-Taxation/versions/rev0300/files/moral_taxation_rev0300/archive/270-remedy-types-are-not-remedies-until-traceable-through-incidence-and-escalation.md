# 270. Remedy types are not remedies until traceable through incidence and escalation

A cube axis can say `rebate`, `waiver`, `audit`, `fallback_channel`, or `clawback` and still fail morally. The label only tells us the shape of a possible correction. The remedy exists only when the archive can identify the real burden bearer, the delivery channel, the timing needed to avoid floor harm, the authority that can correct the result, the blocked superficial answer, and the trigger for escalation.

This matters because tax design often offers relief through the same channel that created the injury: a bankless taxpayer must use an online bank-linked workflow to unfreeze a refund; a worker must claim benefits through the platform that misclassified them; a ratepayer receives a bill credit while the surcharge base keeps shrinking; an overburdened community receives mitigation money while cumulative exposure remains non-compensable.

Rev0288 therefore adds a remedy spine. Each cube route now needs a remedy profile with a default move, blocked move, required guardrails, escalation trigger, and proceeds-integrity posture. The profile is not new moral prose for its own sake. It is a regression target: if future edits preserve the route label but lose the corrective move, the checker should fail.
