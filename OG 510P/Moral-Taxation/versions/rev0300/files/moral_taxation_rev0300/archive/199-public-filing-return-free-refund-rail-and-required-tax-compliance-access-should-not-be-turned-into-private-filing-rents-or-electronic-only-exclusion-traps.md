# Public filing, return-free, refund-rail, and required tax-compliance access should not be turned into private filing rents or electronic-only exclusion traps

## Claim

When the state requires filing, payment, correction, information reporting, identity proof, or refund claiming, it must preserve a no-rent public lane. Private tax software, paid preparers, banks, card issuers, payroll intermediaries, identity vendors, and refund products may help, but none should become the practical owner of baseline compliance or owed refund receipt.[S119][S441][S443][S444]

## Why it matters

A taxpayer can be legally required to file even when the only usable path is private, eligibility-limited, device-dependent, bank-account-dependent, language-thin, or paid. That converts public tax compliance into a compliance toll. A digital-first refund policy can similarly become a hidden burden when a rejected direct deposit freezes a refund instead of triggering timely assisted reissue.[S442][S443][S444]

## Route

Use [`../docs/10-framework/public-filing-floor-and-refund-rail-fallback-routing.md`](../docs/10-framework/public-filing-floor-and-refund-rail-fallback-routing.md) and [`../docs/20-calibration/public-filing-floor-refund-rail-and-channel-fallback-ladder.md`](../docs/20-calibration/public-filing-floor-refund-rail-and-channel-fallback-ladder.md). The compact rule is: legal duty implies public channel; public money owed implies public payout fallback.

## Anti-patterns

- **private filing rent** — the taxpayer must buy software or a preparer to perform a simple required duty.
- **eligibility maze as access** — the official free path is a list of partner conditions, upsells, or state-return gaps rather than a public floor.[S119][S441]
- **bank-account hostage** — a refund is delayed until the taxpayer can receive ACH, card, wallet, or account-based payment safely.[S443][S444]
- **electronic-only exclusion** — disabled, elderly, rural, low-income, incarcerated, recently unhoused, language-access, or identity-compromised taxpayers are treated as edge cases after the channel is closed.
- **public-data resale** — the state has wage and withholding data but makes taxpayers purchase private reconstruction of that same record.
