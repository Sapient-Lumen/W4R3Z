# Aviation, shipping, and hard-to-abate transport carbon rules should not be turned into offset theater or mobility-floor harm

## Claim

Aviation and shipping climate rules are legitimate when they reduce lifecycle emissions, fund transition, and repair climate or port burdens. They become suspect when offset credits, fuel labels, or levies hide weak additionality, incumbent rents, or protected mobility burdens.[S602][S603][S604]

## Why it matters

Hard-to-abate transport connects global emissions, local air burdens, freight prices, and essential mobility. A moral fiscal rule must therefore test fuel evidence, offset integrity, pass-through, and proceeds before treating a carbon label as sufficient.[S602][S603][S604]

Use [`../docs/10-framework/hard-to-abate-transport-aviation-shipping-fuels-and-offset-integrity-routing.md`](../docs/10-framework/hard-to-abate-transport-aviation-shipping-fuels-and-offset-integrity-routing.md) and [`../docs/20-calibration/hard-to-abate-transport-aviation-shipping-fuels-and-offset-integrity-ladder.md`](../docs/20-calibration/hard-to-abate-transport-aviation-shipping-fuels-and-offset-integrity-ladder.md). The compact rule is: price and reduce real transport emissions while protecting essential mobility and port-adjacent communities.

## Anti-patterns

- **offset theater** — credits substitute for cuts without integrity.
- **green-premium rent** — fuel labels or mandates enrich incumbents without additionality.
- **port burden externalization** — global compliance ignores local air harm.
- **mobility-floor harm** — island, rural, disabled, or low-income access is priced as luxury.
