# Emergency relief, automatic payments, recovery, and clawback rules should not be turned into fraud gateways or clawback traps

## Claim

Emergency relief should move quickly enough to protect floors, but it must be designed with identity resilience, targeted controls, postpayment review, and good-faith recipient protection. Speed without integrity creates fraud gateways; integrity without floor protection creates clawback traps.[S531][S532][S533]

## Why it matters

Crises destroy records, liquidity, housing, and work at the same time that fraud opportunities rise. A fair system plans for both realities before the emergency, instead of overcorrecting against victims after the fact.

## Route

Use [`../docs/10-framework/emergency-relief-speed-integrity-and-clawback-routing.md`](../docs/10-framework/emergency-relief-speed-integrity-and-clawback-routing.md) and [`../docs/20-calibration/emergency-relief-speed-integrity-recovery-and-clawback-ladder.md`](../docs/20-calibration/emergency-relief-speed-integrity-recovery-and-clawback-ladder.md). The compact rule is: provisional aid, smart controls, postpayment sampling, identity-victim repair, culpable recovery, and hardship waivers.

## Anti-patterns

- **fraud gateway** — foreseeable high-risk channels lack controls.
- **clawback trap** — good-faith floor recipients are pursued for design error.
- **identity-victim rent** — stolen identity blocks future tax or benefit access.
- **documentation impossibility** — displaced households lose aid for missing records.
