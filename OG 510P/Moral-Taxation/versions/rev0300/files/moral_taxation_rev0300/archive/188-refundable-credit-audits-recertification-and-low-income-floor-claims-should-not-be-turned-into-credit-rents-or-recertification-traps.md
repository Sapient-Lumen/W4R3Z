# 188 — Refundable-credit audits, recertification, and low-income floor claims should not be turned into credit rents or recertification traps

## One-line thesis

Refundable and floor-protecting credits can be verified, but the verification lane may not become a paid proof market. EITC, CTC / ACTC / ODC, AOTC, head-of-household, CP75-family audits, CP79-family recertification, Form 8862, Form 8867, PATH Act refund holds, and 2-year / 10-year credit bans should remain issue-scoped, document-plural, status-visible, and no-rent, or else a credit meant to protect low-income households becomes a credit rent, a recertification trap, or a poverty-audit toll.[S21][S27][S31][S32][S33][S39][S41][S42][S64][S65][S66][S68][S117][S118][S160][S173][S177][S182][S276][S277][S278][S279][S280][S281][S282][S283][S284][S285][S286][S287][S288][S289][S290]

## Why this matters

The archive already protects basic filing access, language access, account records, refund receipt, submissions, representation, stale-account updates, identity-theft recovery, third-party reporting correction, household-status correction, deadline preservation, ledger reconciliation, audit response, preparer misconduct recovery, self-correction, deadlock escalation, automated-notice response, Tax Court petition access, collection alternatives, nonfiler reconstruction, and worker-classification correction. But refundable-credit administration is a distinct seam: the same dollar can be a household floor, a dependency recognition device, an income-work supplement, and an integrity target at once.[S21][S31][S32][S33][S64][S65][S66][S68][S173][S177][S182][S276][S277][S278]

IRS materials show the operational pattern. EITC and ACTC can delay an entire refund until the statutory PATH Act period has passed; CP75 / CP75A-style credit audits ask for supporting documents and may hold the refundable-credit portion; CP79-family notices tell taxpayers that future claims may require Form 8862, and CP79A / CP79B identify 2-year or 10-year bans; Form 8862 applies after certain disallowances other than math or clerical error; and paid preparers have a separate due-diligence checklist regime under Form 8867.[S279][S280][S281][S282][S283][S284][S285][S286][S287][S288][S289]

Those tools can be morally defensible where they screen false claims, repeated reckless claiming, or preparer-driven abuse. The wrong is turning them into a one-way proof ladder for the weakest claimant: holding the whole refund when only a credit slice is disputed, requiring documents only affluent households can easily produce, making Form 8862 a hidden gate, applying bans without appeal clarity, or letting preparer due diligence become client exclusion rather than preparer accountability.[S21][S27][S39][S41][S42][S64][S65][S66][S117][S118][S160][S173][S177][S178][S279][S280][S281][S282][S283][S284][S285][S286][S287][S288]

## Archive rule

1. **Floor-protecting credits get a no-rent proof lane.** EITC, CTC / ACTC / ODC, AOTC, and comparable floor-linked credits may require eligibility facts, but the ordinary path to supply them must include public, plain-language, accessible, document-plural routes rather than paid preparer dependence, proprietary software, or a single digital upload choke point.[S21][S31][S32][S33][S64][S65][S66][S91][S92][S276][S277][S278][S286][S290]
2. **Credit audits should be itemized and issue-scoped.** A CP75-family or comparable audit should name the credit, child / student / household / income fact at issue, requested documents, response deadline, and no-rent response channels; it should not convert a whole return or whole refund into suspense when only a bounded credit fact is under review.[S39][S41][S42][S64][S65][S66][S177][S281][S282][S286]
3. **PATH Act timing is not a discretionary float license.** A statutory EITC / ACTC refund delay may coordinate integrity review, but it should be explained as a timing rule, tracked to a status surface, and followed by prompt release of undisputed amounts once the hold period and bounded review predicate no longer justify retention.[S21][S39][S41][S64][S65][S66][S175][S176][S180][S276][S289]
4. **Form 8862 is a recertification bridge, not a poverty toll.** Where Form 8862 is required after a disallowance other than math or clerical error, the next eligible claim should have a clear form, instructions, and assistance path. Failure to know the gate should trigger correction-first treatment before punishment when the taxpayer's eligibility is otherwise concrete.[S27][S39][S41][S42][S65][S66][S117][S118][S160][S279][S280][S283]
5. **Credit bans need culpability, notice, and appeal clarity.** Two-year reckless / intentional-disregard and ten-year fraud consequences should not arise from ordinary poverty proof gaps, stale household facts, inaccessible records, preparer misconduct, identity theft, or payer misclassification without independent culpability findings and a visible appeal or reconsideration route.[S27][S39][S41][S42][S98][S99][S160][S173][S178][S284][S285]
6. **Document plurality matters.** Residency, relationship, income, tuition, disability, separated-household, or care facts often exist in schools, clinics, landlords, shelters, agencies, employers, payers, or informal caregivers rather than in a perfect household file. Accept credible third-party statements, substitute records, prior records, corrected payer data, and assisted affidavits when they are fit for the fact being tested.[S31][S32][S33][S39][S41][S42][S64][S65][S66][S154][S155][S158][S161][S164][S165][S286][S290]
7. **Preparer due diligence is not a claimant exclusion device.** Form 8867-style preparer obligations should discipline paid preparers and reduce false claims, but they should not make preparers avoid eligible low-income, separated-household, disabled, student, or informally housed claimants, nor should a preparer's failure become the taxpayer's admission.[S27][S39][S41][S42][S178][S197][S198][S199][S200][S201][S202][S287][S288]
8. **Future artificial persons get no direct analogue yet.** Current AI systems do not claim EITC, CTC, ACTC, ODC, AOTC, or head-of-household status. If a future rights-bearing artificial subject ever received floor protection, the same no-rent claim, hold, audit, and recertification architecture would have to attach directly to the subject rather than to an owner-rented dashboard.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44]

## Pattern pack

### A. A credit hold is not a moral verdict

A refund hold or credit audit says an eligibility fact needs verification. It does not itself prove fraud, recklessness, or non-entitlement. The administration should distinguish ordinary proof incompleteness from intentional falsehood before penalty, ban, or public suspicion hardens.[S27][S39][S41][S42][S98][S99][S281][S282][S284][S285]

### B. The stronger record-holder should help carry the record burden

A worker may not have payroll correction records; a caregiver may not control school enrollment paperwork; a separated parent may not control lease or medical files; a student may depend on institutional tuition statements. After a concrete claimant counterstatement, the proof lane should seek records from the actor that actually sees the fact rather than charging the claimant for reconstruction.[S27][S31][S32][S33][S39][S41][S42][S154][S155][S158][S267][S270][S272][S286][S290]

### C. Recertification should restore eligibility, not keep suspicion alive

Form 8862 should function as a way to show that the taxpayer now qualifies, not as an indefinite stigma. Once the claimant supplies the required recertification packet and the current-year facts satisfy the credit rule, future delivery should return to ordinary claim and correction treatment.[S279][S280][S283]

### D. Bans are sanction lanes, not ordinary claim-friction lanes

A two-year or ten-year credit ban is not just a paperwork rule. It is a severe sanction against a floor-linked claim. It therefore belongs with notice, culpability, appeal, and reconsideration discipline rather than with silent math-error or low-friction screening.[S27][S39][S41][S42][S98][S99][S117][S118][S160][S284][S285]

### E. Preparer caution should not become low-income redlining

Due-diligence rules can make paid preparers ask better questions and keep better records. But if the only safe commercial answer is to reject poor, mobile, informal-care, language-vulnerable, or separated-household taxpayers, the design has shifted public credit administration into private eligibility rationing.[S64][S65][S66][S91][S92][S197][S198][S287][S288]

### F. Refund timing should be transparent enough to budget around

Where a statutory refund delay applies, the taxpayer should see whether the issue is the statutory mid-February delay, an audit, an identity hold, a mismatch, a ledger problem, or a true disallowance. Different statuses require different next steps; merging them creates avoidable hardship and private rescue markets.[S21][S39][S41][S64][S65][S66][S175][S180][S212][S213][S276][S281][S289]

## Guardrails

- no paid preparer, paid software, premium portal, or proprietary transcript product as the ordinary gateway to claiming, documenting, recertifying, or restoring a floor-protecting credit;
- no whole-refund hold when only a bounded EITC / ACTC / CTC / ODC / AOTC / household-status slice is disputed and the remainder is clear;
- no Form 8862 hidden-gate denial without a visible recertification path, correction-first notice, and assistance route;
- no two-year or ten-year credit ban without independent culpability findings, clear notice, and appeal / reconsideration cues;
- no treating missing poverty paperwork, school / medical / lease instability, informal caregiving, separation, identity theft, preparer misconduct, or payer misclassification as fraud by itself;
- no preparer due-diligence regime that disciplines low-income claimants while letting preparer noncompliance or commercial avoidance set the practical eligibility boundary;
- no owner-rented claim, audit, or recertification dashboard if future artificial personhood ever makes direct floor protection real.

## Failure modes

- **credit rent** — a claimant must buy help, software, records, or preparer access to claim or defend a credit meant to protect the floor.
- **recertification trap** — a prior disallowance silently blocks a later valid claim because Form 8862 or equivalent restoration is hidden, inaccessible, or treated as stigma.
- **poverty-audit toll** — a low-income household must reconstruct residency, care, school, disability, or income records at a cost that wealthier taxpayers do not face.
- **PATH float** — a statutory EITC / ACTC timing delay becomes an indefinite refund hold or budgeting shock without clear status or release logic.
- **ban-as-paperwork shortcut** — a two-year or ten-year credit ban follows from missing or confusing documents rather than a culpability finding.
- **preparer redlining** — due-diligence penalties make commercial preparers avoid eligible claimants with complex but legitimate low-income facts.
- **whole-refund hostage** — the entire refund is frozen for a bounded credit fact even where wages, withholding, or other credits are not disputed.
- **owner-held credit standing** — a future rights-bearing artificial subject's claim or recertification is mediated through a former owner or controller rather than held by the subject.

## Practical tests

1. Does the notice say which credit and which eligibility fact is disputed?
2. Can the claimant answer without a paid preparer, paid software, or a single inaccessible upload path?
3. Are alternative documents or third-party statements accepted for the fact being tested?
4. Is the refund hold limited to the disputed slice and statutory timing rule, with status visible to the claimant?
5. If Form 8862 is needed, did the administration make the recertification gate clear and correctable?
6. If a ban is imposed, does the record show culpability, notice, and appeal / reconsideration cues rather than mere documentation failure?
7. Are preparer due-diligence obligations aimed at preparers rather than converted into client exclusion or taxpayer admission?
8. If the claimant were a future rights-bearing artificial person with direct floor protection, would the claim, audit, and recertification path belong to the subject rather than an owner account?
