# Withholding tax, treaty relief, MAP, and double-tax protection rules should not be turned into reclaim rents or double-tax traps

## Claim

Withholding is legitimate as a collection anchor only if treaty relief, domestic credits, refunds, MAP, and competent-authority assistance are real channels rather than slow, expensive, paper-bound promises.[S548][S550][S551]

## Why it matters

Cross-border taxpayers can be overcollected by source-state withholding and then trapped by residence-state timing, documentation, or competent-authority delay. Small investors and ordinary migrants experience this as a hidden toll; multinationals experience it as double-tax uncertainty.[S549]

## Route

Use [`../docs/10-framework/withholding-tax-treaty-relief-map-and-double-tax-protection-routing.md`](../docs/10-framework/withholding-tax-treaty-relief-map-and-double-tax-protection-routing.md) and [`../docs/20-calibration/withholding-tax-treaty-relief-map-and-double-tax-protection-ladder.md`](../docs/20-calibration/withholding-tax-treaty-relief-map-and-double-tax-protection-ladder.md). The compact rule is: source collection must carry a functioning relief-at-source, reclaim, credit, and MAP escape hatch.

## Anti-patterns

- **reclaim rent** — treaty rights require costly intermediaries.
- **double-tax trap** — two states tax the same income without timely relief.
- **treaty-relief paywall** — relief depends on paperwork inaccessible to ordinary claimants.
- **settlement hostage** — domestic closure sacrifices foreign relief without warning.
