# Digital gatekeeper cloud app store and payment rail rules should not be turned into access rents or private tolls

## Claim

Digital gatekeeper rules are legitimate when they preserve access, interoperability, data portability, and public fallback while charging real bottleneck rent. They fail when private rails sell the only path to pay, file, switch, enter a market, or receive what is owed.[S460][S614]

## Why it matters

Modern tax and benefit systems increasingly touch app stores, wallets, payment processors, data aggregators, and cloud dashboards. These anchors can reduce friction, but they can also transform civic access and market entry into privately priced chokepoints.[S460][S614]

## Route

Use [`../docs/10-framework/digital-gatekeeper-cloud-app-store-payment-rail-routing.md`](../docs/10-framework/digital-gatekeeper-cloud-app-store-payment-rail-routing.md) and [`../docs/20-calibration/digital-gatekeeper-cloud-app-store-payment-rail-ladder.md`](../docs/20-calibration/digital-gatekeeper-cloud-app-store-payment-rail-ladder.md). The compact rule is: use the rail as evidence or anchor, but do not let the rail become the right.

## Anti-patterns

- **private toll constitution** — a dominant rail sells access to what the public system should provide directly.
- **portability theater** — data access exists only through costly or privacy-invasive intermediaries.
- **compliance console lock-in** — filing, remittance, or proof works only through one vendor dashboard.
- **self-preference rent** — gatekeeper fees finance exclusion of rivals or public alternatives.
