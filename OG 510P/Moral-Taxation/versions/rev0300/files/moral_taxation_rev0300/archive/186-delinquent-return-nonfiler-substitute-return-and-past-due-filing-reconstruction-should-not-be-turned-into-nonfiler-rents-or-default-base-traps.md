# 186 — Delinquent return, nonfiler, substitute-return, and past-due filing reconstruction should not be turned into nonfiler rents or default-base traps

## One-line thesis

A missing or late return may justify notice, reconstruction, assessment, and collection discipline, but it may not turn third-party wage-and-income data into a practically final tax base or force the subject to buy back the public records, forms, filing channels, assistance, and petition-preservation information needed to file the real return.[S27][S39][S41][S42][S65][S66][S238][S258][S259][S260][S261][S262][S263][S264][S265][S266]

## Why this matters

The archive already blocks answer rents, record rents, deadline traps, automated-adjustment default, petition-access loss, amendment confession traps, preparer captivity, and rescue-market escalation. A nonfiler or past-due filer can still fall through the seam between them. The agency may compute from W-2, 1099, platform, bank, or other third-party reports, but that record is usually one-sided: it may omit basis, filing status, dependents, deductions, refundable credits, self-employment offsets, corrected payer records, disaster facts, or reasonable-cause history.[S154][S156][S158][S172][S174][S175][S223][S224][S258][S261][S262][S263]

That one-sided computation is useful as a revenue-protection proposal. It becomes unjust when it is treated as moral finality merely because the taxpayer missed the first return deadline, could not retrieve old records, lacked a preparer, moved, had identity disruption, or did not understand that a CP3219N deficiency notice also starts a prepayment court clock.[S117][S118][S160][S183][S238][S258][S260][S261][S263][S264][S265]

## Archive rule

For delinquent-return, nonfiler, substitute-return, and past-due filing cases, route the case through these constraints:

1. **Notice ladder before default base.** CP59, CP2566, CP2566R, CP3219N, and comparable notices should say the return year, filing status assumed, third-party records used, proposed penalties / interest, response path, and what changes if the taxpayer files the actual return.[S258][S259][S260][S261][S262]
2. **Substitute return is provisional evidence, not person-side admission.** A return prepared from third-party records may start assessment machinery, but it should not be treated as the taxpayer's confession, waiver, or complete liability picture.[S258][S261][S263][S264][S265]
3. **No paid reconstruction gate.** Wage-and-income transcripts, prior forms, instructions, mailing addresses, public filing help, accessible channels, and basic assistance should be available without forcing the subject into paid preparer, tax-resolution, transcript-broker, or portal-rent markets.[S65][S66][S127][S128][S212][S213][S258][S263][S264]
4. **Petition clock must remain distinct.** Where a deficiency notice is issued, the court petition date must be visible and must not be blurred by ordinary IRS-response instructions. Filing a late return, asking for reconsideration, or calling the agency may be relevant, but it should not be presented as extending a petition deadline unless law actually does that.[S117][S118][S160][S183][S232][S238][S258][S261]
5. **Correct tax beats default tax.** Once the taxpayer submits a credible past-due original return, corrected payer record, counterstatement, or bounded reconstruction, the case should move toward the correct liability rather than preserve an overstated substitute-return amount as leverage.[S154][S156][S158][S172][S174][S175][S258][S263][S264][S266]
6. **Refund limitation is not liability inflation.** Expired refund windows may limit payout, but they do not morally authorize collecting tax that the corrected record shows was never owed. Separate refund expiration, credit reduction, penalty abatement, and correct-tax computation.[S176][S179][S204][S208][S258][S260][S263][S266]
7. **Assessment-limit status must be visible.** If a substitute return does not start the ordinary assessment limitation period and a filed original return changes limitation posture, that status belongs in the account explanation rather than in specialist-only tax procedure.[S160][S238][S258][S265]
8. **Escalation stays no-rent.** When ordinary channels fail, TAS, LITC, hardship, penalty-abatement, audit-reconsideration, refund-claim, CDP, OIC-doubt-as-to-liability, and similar public routes should be visible according to the live posture, not displaced by settlement mills or rescue vendors.[S180][S184][S212][S213][S214][S215][S216][S240][S241][S244][S263][S264]

## Pattern pack

### A. Early nonfiler notice

The baseline response should be to file, explain why no return is due, or send a signed copy if the return was already filed. The notice should also warn that refund and credit claims can expire and that address or identity errors may have caused misrouting.[S144][S145][S146][S148][S149][S260]

### B. Substitute-return proposal

When the agency proposes a return from third-party records, it should expose the records used and the assumptions made. The taxpayer should not need a paid transcript product or private preparer gate to find the wage, income, withholding, or payer records that drove the proposal.[S127][S128][S154][S156][S158][S258][S261][S262]

### C. CP3219N / deficiency posture

The notice-of-deficiency lane is not merely another correspondence letter. It is the bridge from administrative response to prepayment court review. The archive therefore routes CP3219N through both the nonfiler lane and the docket-rent rule.[S117][S118][S160][S183][S232][S238][S258][S261]

### D. Late original return after SFR

A late original return should be treated as a bounded reconstruction of the real tax, not as an all-or-nothing plea for mercy. The agency may verify it, but the filed return should narrow the case to disputed items, penalties, limitation effects, and collection status.[S39][S41][S42][S160][S191][S204][S258][S263][S264][S265][S266]

### E. Penalties and reasonable cause

Late filing and late payment can justify penalty analysis, but penalty relief belongs on a reasoned culpability lane. Missing records, disaster, illness, identity theft, nonreceipt, inaccessible channels, or preparer misconduct should be sorted before penalties are treated as ordinary revenue.[S41][S42][S99][S148][S149][S168][S172][S174][S197][S198][S263]

### F. Collection after default assessment

If a substitute-return assessment moves into collection, the account should still identify which portion is undisputed, which portion is being reconstructed, which offsets or credits are limited by statute, and which relief lane is live. Lien, levy, passport, private collection, or compromise routing should not harden an inflated default base.[S176][S181][S183][S184][S212][S218][S219][S240][S241][S244][S258][S263][S264]

## Guardrails

This note does not create a free option to ignore filing indefinitely. Repeated nonfiling, concealment, false documents, identity misuse, or willful obstruction can still trigger assessment, collection, penalties, and, where justified, criminal or fraud lanes. The constraint is narrower: the public system should not convert missing return posture into a paid access market or into automatic moral truth about the tax base.[S39][S41][S99][S258][S262][S263]

Nor does this note erase statutory refund limits. It requires the archive to distinguish three questions that are often blurred: what tax was correctly owed, what refund or credit can still be paid out, and what penalties or interest remain after reasoned relief review.[S176][S179][S204][S208][S258][S260][S263][S266]

## Practical tests

A nonfiler or SFR design fails this note if:

- the taxpayer cannot see the third-party records and assumptions behind the proposed return;
- the notice tells the taxpayer to call or file with the IRS without separately flagging a Tax Court petition deadline;
- wage-and-income reconstruction requires a paid preparer, paid transcript broker, or captive account product;
- an expired refund period is used rhetorically to preserve an overstated balance due;
- a late original return is ignored because a substitute return has already become administratively convenient;
- penalty relief, TAS / LITC help, or audit reconsideration is invisible until after collection has escalated;
- the account does not say whether filing the taxpayer's own return changes assessment-limit posture.[S117][S118][S127][S128][S160][S183][S191][S212][S213][S258][S263][S264][S265]

## Relations

This note binds together the record-access, third-party mismatch, automated-notice, petition, amendment, sanction, collection, and rescue-rent notes. Use it when the live wrong is not simply a bad W-2 / 1099, an audit, or a math-error notice, but the broader move from **missing return** to **government-computed default tax base** without a no-rent route back to the real return.[S127][S128][S154][S156][S160][S172][S174][S175][S179][S180][S183][S184][S204][S208][S223][S224][S258][S263][S264]
