# Standards certification accreditation and audit rules should not be turned into certified rents or conformity theater

## Claim

Standards and certification can make complex risks legible, but only if evidence is clear, assessors are independent, alternatives are accepted, and small entities can comply without buying a private badge as the price of legality.[S621][S622]

## Why it matters

As the archive adds AI, cyber, climate, procurement, and data-center routes, certification pressure will grow. Without safeguards, proof of compliance becomes a market in rented legitimacy.[S621][S622]

## Route

Use [`../docs/10-framework/standards-certification-accreditation-and-audit-gatekeeping-routing.md`](../docs/10-framework/standards-certification-accreditation-and-audit-gatekeeping-routing.md) and [`../docs/20-calibration/standards-certification-accreditation-and-audit-gatekeeping-ladder.md`](../docs/20-calibration/standards-certification-accreditation-and-audit-gatekeeping-ladder.md). The compact rule is: make the requirement public, the evidence portable, and the assessor contestable.

## Anti-patterns

- **certified rent** — a paid badge becomes a private tax on legality.
- **conformity theater** — formal compliance masks missing performance.
- **auditor capture** — the party paid by the firm sells the public signal.
- **standard paywall** — the binding rule is hidden behind proprietary access.
