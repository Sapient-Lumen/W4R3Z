# 114 — Incidence, pass-through, and protected burden-bearing

## One-line thesis

A morally justified tax is not identified only by **who remits it**. It must also survive the harder question: **who actually bears the burden after prices, wages, rents, and bargaining power adjust?**

## Why this matters

Many tax arguments stop too early. They point to a corporation, platform, landlord, cloud provider, or model lab as the statutory payer and assume the moral work is done. But taxes can be shifted. Workers can receive lower wages, tenants can face higher rents, consumers can face higher prices, and weak suppliers can absorb margin squeezes. Moral taxation therefore requires a second lens: final incidence.[S4][S21]

## Archive rule

Prefer taxes whose likely real burden falls on one or more of the following:

- holders of land and other inelastic rents,
- monopoly or bottleneck owners,
- resource extractors capturing common inheritance,
- high-margin beneficiaries of automation,
- and actors creating harms that can be corrected only if they face the cost.

Be suspicious of taxes whose likely real burden falls on:

- subsistence workers,
- low-income households,
- tenants in tight housing markets,
- weak suppliers or dependent contractors,
- or open and public-interest research that lacks bottleneck power.

## Human-side implications

### 1. Wage taxation is morally second-rank

Ordinary labor is often the least defended and most easily exposed base in people's daily lives. That is one reason the archive prefers floors, payroll relief, and negative-income support over labor-heavy extraction.[S2][S21]

### 2. Broad consumption taxes need visible correction

A broad VAT or GST can be administratively strong, but it is not morally self-justifying. It needs rebates, transfers, or equivalent public-service offsets so protected households do not end up carrying a disproportionate burden.[S3][S4]

### 3. Land and inheritance remain attractive partly because incidence is cleaner

Land cannot leave the jurisdiction, and dynastic transfers are not routine work effort. These are among the reasons land and inheritance taxation remain unusually attractive bases for moral taxation.[S5][S8][S21]

## AI-side implications

### 1. Controller-first is necessary but not sufficient

It is correct to route present-day AI taxation through facilities, labs, deployers, and beneficiaries. But that is only a first pass. A good AI tax still fails if firms can predictably push the burden onto workers, dependent developers, or low-income users.

### 2. Token taxes usually fail the incidence test

A universal per-token levy is attractive because it is visible. But visibility is not morality. Such taxes risk hitting downstream usage without clearly targeting rents, harms, or concentrated beneficiaries.[S10][S15][S20]

### 3. Better AI instruments are harder to shift cleanly downward

More promising instruments include:

- site-specific electricity, water-stress, and carbon charges for major facilities,
- excess-profit or windfall capture where AI bottlenecks generate outsized rents,
- high-risk deployment bonds and insurance,
- and beneficiary-side contributions tied to large productivity windfalls.

These instruments are not perfect, but they are closer to the real levers of control, rent, and harm.[S9][S10][S15][S16][S17]

## Design moves when pass-through risk is high

- add household rebates or dividends,
- add payroll relief or worker-side credits,
- allow deferral for cash-poor asset-rich cases,
- strengthen competition policy where market power drives pass-through,
- narrow the tax to above-normal rents rather than normal returns,
- or reject the tax as badly targeted.

## Fast diagnostic

Ask five questions:

1. Who remits the tax?
2. Who can change the taxed behaviour?
3. Who has enough market power to pass the tax on?
4. Who has the weakest bargaining position in the chain?
5. After all adjustments, who is predictably poorer?

If the answer to the last question is mainly "protected humans," the proposal is misdesigned even if the legal remitter is a corporation or AI operator.
