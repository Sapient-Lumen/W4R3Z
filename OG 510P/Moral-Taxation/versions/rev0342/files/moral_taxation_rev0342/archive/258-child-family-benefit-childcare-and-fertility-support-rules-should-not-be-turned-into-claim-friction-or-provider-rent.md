# Child family benefit childcare and fertility support rules should not be turned into claim friction or provider rent

## Claim

Child and family fiscal support is legitimate when it reaches children, supports caregivers, and preserves work-care choice. It fails when nonrefundability, co-pays, documentation, waitlists, or provider scarcity turn the promised benefit into friction or rent.[S277][S625][S626][S627]

## Why it matters

Family supports are often defended by poverty reduction, labor supply, fertility, or child development. Those goals diverge in design. The archive routes by the live problem, not by family rhetoric.[S277][S625][S626][S627]

The compact rule is: deliver the child floor before optimizing the household label.

## Anti-patterns

- **claim-friction child policy** — a benefit exists legally but not practically.
- **provider rent** — subsidies inflate price or finance low-quality scarcity.
- **care cliff** — work or marriage triggers loss larger than earnings.
- **pronatalist extraction** — tax rules pressure births while underfunding children and caregivers.
