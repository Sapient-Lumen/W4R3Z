# Occupational licensing professional entry and reciprocity rules should not be turned into entry rents or mobility traps

## Claim

Occupational licensing is a public-safety tool only when it is necessary, proportionate, portable, and cost-based. It becomes a fiscal and moral problem when work permission is sold through fees, duplicate exams, or incumbent-controlled scarcity.[S619][S620]

## Why it matters

Licensing can protect patients, clients, and consumers. It can also block low-income workers, migrants, military spouses, and people with old records from lawful work while raising prices for everyone else.[S619][S620]

The compact rule is: license the risk, not the worker class.

## Anti-patterns

- **entry rent** — fees, hours, or exams protect incumbents rather than consumers.
- **mobility trap** — valid skills must be repurchased jurisdiction by jurisdiction.
- **debt gate** — unrelated fines or fees become work-permission ransom.
- **scope creep** — low-risk tasks are pulled into protected monopoly.
