# Immigration status, asylum, work authorization, and benefit-request fees should not be turned into status ransom or humanitarian-access tolls

## Claim

Immigration filing and status fees may recover bounded administrative cost, but they become impermissible tolls when they condition asylum, work authorization, family unity, legal identity, appeal, or status maintenance on cash beyond protected capacity.[S523][S524][S525]

## Why it matters

The state controls the legal gate. When the gate is necessary for safety, work, family, or personhood recognition, fee design is not ordinary user pricing; it is a fiscal condition on legal existence.

## Route

Use [`../docs/10-framework/immigration-status-asylum-and-benefit-fee-floor-routing.md`](../docs/10-framework/immigration-status-asylum-and-benefit-fee-floor-routing.md) and [`../docs/20-calibration/immigration-status-asylum-benefit-fee-and-waiver-ladder.md`](../docs/20-calibration/immigration-status-asylum-benefit-fee-and-waiver-ladder.md). The compact rule is: humanitarian and floor-sensitive claims get exemptions, waivers, fallback channels, wrong-fee cure, and no agency-delay rent.

## Anti-patterns

- **status ransom** — lawful recognition or work access is unaffordable.
- **humanitarian-access toll** — protection depends on ability to pay.
- **agency-delay rent** — recurring charges attach to pending cases the state has not decided.
- **wrong-fee forfeiture** — opaque schedules cause rejection and priority loss.
