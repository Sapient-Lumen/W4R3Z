# Taxpayer-side AI preparer, agent, reliance, and liability should not be turned into ghost-preparer or refund-diversion rents

## Claim

AI assistance in tax filing is a tool, not a tax person and not an accountability vacuum; return preparation must preserve accountable identity, source traceability, taxpayer review, refund control, correction, and victim relief.[S197][S198][S201][S654][S655][S656][S657][S658][S659]

## Why it matters

Taxpayers can be harmed by wrong advice, fake authority, unsigned preparation, refund diversion, or black-box software while still being formally responsible for filed returns. The archive routes the duty to the actor with control, sale, signature, submission, or refund-rail power.[S197][S198][S201]

## Anti-patterns

- **AI ghost preparer** — paid preparation happens with no accountable signer or professional trail.
- **prompt opacity** — facts, assumptions, authority, and model output cannot be reconstructed.
- **refund rail capture** — the tool or preparer directs proceeds to an account the taxpayer does not control.
- **liability mirror trick** — automation errors are shifted entirely to the taxpayer despite vendor/preparer control.
- **official-looking hallucination** — fake or stale authority is presented as tax law.
