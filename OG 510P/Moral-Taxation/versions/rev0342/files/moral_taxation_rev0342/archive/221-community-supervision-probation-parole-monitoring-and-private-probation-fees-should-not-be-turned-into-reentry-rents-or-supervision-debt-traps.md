# Community supervision, probation, parole, monitoring, and private-probation fees should not be turned into reentry rents or supervision-debt traps

## Claim

Probation, parole, monitoring, testing, treatment, and supervision fees are suspect when they finance the apparatus controlling the person, enrich captive vendors, lengthen supervision, or expose inability to pay to reincarceration.[S516][S517][S537]

## Why it matters

Reentry requires work, housing, transportation, treatment, and family stability. Charging people for mandatory supervision competes directly with those goods and can turn a sentence into a fee subscription.

## Route

Use [`../docs/10-framework/community-supervision-private-probation-and-monitoring-fee-routing.md`](../docs/10-framework/community-supervision-private-probation-and-monitoring-fee-routing.md) and [`../docs/20-calibration/community-supervision-monitoring-private-probation-and-reentry-fee-ladder.md`](../docs/20-calibration/community-supervision-monitoring-private-probation-and-reentry-fee-ladder.md). The compact rule is: fund core supervision publicly, waive or cancel floor-burdening fees, and prohibit poverty-based extension or revocation.[S518]

## Anti-patterns

- **reentry rent** — mandatory civic return is monetized.
- **supervision debt trap** — inability to pay extends state control.
- **private-probation toll** — a vendor profits from court coercion.
- **mandatory-service markup** — required monitoring/testing is sold at captive prices.
