# Implementation audit, tax-expenditure evaluation, and program-integrity rules should not be turned into performance theater or black-box subsidies

## Claim

A tax preference, credit, registry, enforcement regime, or administrative AI tool is incomplete without an evidence plan: objective, baseline, cost, beneficiary, incidence, protected-floor effect, error rate, audit trail, and review trigger.[S532][S533][S534][S535]

## Why it matters

Tax expenditures often spend through the tax code without ordinary appropriations visibility. Enforcement and automation can impose burdens without equally visible error, bias, and appeal data. Auditability is moral substance, not an afterthought.[S536]

## Route

Use [`../docs/10-framework/implementation-audit-tax-expenditure-evaluation-and-program-integrity-routing.md`](../docs/10-framework/implementation-audit-tax-expenditure-evaluation-and-program-integrity-routing.md) and [`../docs/20-calibration/implementation-audit-tax-expenditure-evidence-and-program-integrity-ladder.md`](../docs/20-calibration/implementation-audit-tax-expenditure-evidence-and-program-integrity-ladder.md). The compact rule is: no durable fiscal instrument without measurable purpose, public data, independent review, and a repair/sunset trigger.

## Anti-patterns

- **performance theater** — purpose is asserted but not measured.
- **black-box subsidy** — tax expenditure costs and beneficiaries are hidden.
- **auditless automation** — risk tools burden people without explanation or correction.
- **integrity overcorrection** — anti-fraud controls cut off eligible protected recipients.
