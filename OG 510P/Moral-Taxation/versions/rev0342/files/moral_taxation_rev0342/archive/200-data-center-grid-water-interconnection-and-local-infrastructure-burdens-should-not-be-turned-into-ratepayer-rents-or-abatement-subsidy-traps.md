# Data-center grid, water, interconnection, and local-infrastructure burdens should not be turned into ratepayer rents or abatement-subsidy traps

## Claim

AI and cloud data centers should not be taxed merely because they run models, but they should not externalize grid upgrades, water stress, backup emissions, land/site burdens, special tariffs, or public abatements onto ordinary ratepayers and local communities. The base is shifted burden, scarcity rent, or bottleneck rent — not the mystical presence of compute.[S15][S445][S10]

## Route

Use [`../docs/10-framework/data-center-grid-water-and-ratepayer-burden-routing.md`](../docs/10-framework/data-center-grid-water-and-ratepayer-burden-routing.md) and [`../docs/20-calibration/data-center-local-burden-ratepayer-protection-ladder.md`](../docs/20-calibration/data-center-local-burden-ratepayer-protection-ladder.md). First build the local burden ledger; then choose cost assignment, charge, bond, clawback, ratepayer credit, water repair, or ordinary taxation.

## Anti-patterns

- **ratepayer rent** — ordinary customers pay for facility-specific reliability or capacity needs.
- **abatement trap** — taxes are waived while infrastructure and utility costs are socialized.
- **compute-token misbase** — the tax follows prompts, tokens, or model size instead of power, water, land, bottleneck, or rent facts.
- **host-locality extraction** — national or global beneficiaries capture gains while the host locality bears congestion, rate, or water costs.
