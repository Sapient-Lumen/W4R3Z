# Informal economy, presumptive tax, and small-vendor simplification should not be turned into subsistence tolls or formalization ransom

## Claim

Presumptive and simplified taxes can be legitimate capacity tools, but only when they lower compliance burden, protect the subsistence floor, include contest and graduation, and pair formalization with real services. They should not tax the poor because the poor are easier to see.[S451][S467][S468]

## Why it matters

A flat license fee or presumptive tax can be administratively simple and morally crude. If it falls on stall access, street vending, household enterprise, or survival trade before rent, land, monopoly, pollution, or elite evasion, it converts state weakness into extraction from the floor.

## Route

Use [`../docs/10-framework/informal-economy-presumptive-tax-and-subsistence-floor-routing.md`](../docs/10-framework/informal-economy-presumptive-tax-and-subsistence-floor-routing.md) and [`../docs/20-calibration/informal-economy-presumptive-tax-and-formalization-ladder.md`](../docs/20-calibration/informal-economy-presumptive-tax-and-formalization-ladder.md). The compact rule is: simplification may graduate enterprise; it may not ransom subsistence.

## Anti-patterns

- **subsistence toll** — a low-income trader pays because enforcement is easy.
- **recognition ransom** — legal identity, stall access, or registration is made contingent on unaffordable fees.
- **margin blindness** — gross receipts are taxed as if all sectors have the same profit.
- **permanent micro-shelter** — larger firms fragment to stay inside simplified treatment.
