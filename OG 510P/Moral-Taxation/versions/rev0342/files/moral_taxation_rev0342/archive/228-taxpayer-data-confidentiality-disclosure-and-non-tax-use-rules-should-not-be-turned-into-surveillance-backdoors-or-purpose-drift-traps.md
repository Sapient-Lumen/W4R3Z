# Taxpayer data confidentiality, disclosure, and non-tax-use rules should not be turned into surveillance backdoors or purpose-drift traps

## Claim

Tax information is compelled for tax administration and must not become a general-purpose database for unrelated enforcement, benefits policing, immigration control, commercial scoring, or political monitoring without exact authority, necessity, minimization, logging, and remedy.[S378][S545]

## Why it matters

Confidentiality is a condition of voluntary compliance. If taxpayers believe addresses, family facts, income records, or identity data will be reused outside the tax purpose, compliance becomes dangerous for protected groups and the fiscal system consumes its own trust.[S546][S547]

## Route

Use [`../docs/10-framework/taxpayer-data-confidentiality-non-tax-use-and-purpose-wall-routing.md`](../docs/10-framework/taxpayer-data-confidentiality-non-tax-use-and-purpose-wall-routing.md) and [`../docs/20-calibration/taxpayer-data-confidentiality-non-tax-use-and-purpose-wall-ladder.md`](../docs/20-calibration/taxpayer-data-confidentiality-non-tax-use-and-purpose-wall-ladder.md). The compact rule is: tax data may move only through an explicit purpose wall with minimum necessary disclosure, verified identity, recipient controls, and remedy.

## Anti-patterns

- **confidentiality backdoor** — tax records become all-purpose state data.
- **non-tax-use dragnet** — broad policy goals overwhelm narrow disclosure law.
- **data-match policing** — weak matches trigger coercive action.
- **trust liquidation** — immediate enforcement destroys long-term compliance.
