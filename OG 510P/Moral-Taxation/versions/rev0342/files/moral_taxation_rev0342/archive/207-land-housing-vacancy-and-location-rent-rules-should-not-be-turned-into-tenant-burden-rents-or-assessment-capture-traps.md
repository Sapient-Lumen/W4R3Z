# Land, housing, vacancy, and location-rent rules should not be turned into tenant-burden rents or assessment-capture traps

## Claim

Land and location value are priority tax bases because they often reflect public infrastructure, community growth, scarcity, and legal exclusion rather than owner labor. But housing is shelter, so land-rent tools must protect tenants, modest homeowners, and asset-rich/income-poor households.[S23][S473][S474]

## Why it matters

A nominal property or land tax can become morally inverted if assessments are stale, elite parcels are shielded, transaction taxes lock households in place, or landlords pass the legal charge through to tenants who have no exit.

## Route

Use [`../docs/10-framework/land-housing-vacancy-and-speculation-rent-routing.md`](../docs/10-framework/land-housing-vacancy-and-speculation-rent-routing.md) and [`../docs/20-calibration/land-housing-vacancy-speculation-and-assessment-ladder.md`](../docs/20-calibration/land-housing-vacancy-speculation-and-assessment-ladder.md). The compact rule is: tax land rent and speculative withholding before labor, but only with current assessment, tenant-incidence review, liquidity relief, transparency, and visible housing repair.

## Anti-patterns

- **tenant-burden rent** — a legal landowner tax lands on renters in a shortage market.
- **assessment capture** — stale or unequal values protect powerful parcels.
- **transaction lock-in** — mobility is taxed while holding scarcity is rewarded.
- **forced-sale morality** — shelter tax is collected without liquidity relief.
