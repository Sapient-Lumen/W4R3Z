## Question in one sentence

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **jurisdiction gate** — identify whether the claim concerns tax, securities, procurement, benefits, or other public funds.[S557][S560]
2. **specificity gate** — require original, specific, timely, credible information rather than generalized suspicion.
3. **confidentiality gate** — protect taxpayer and whistleblower information; disclose only as authorized and necessary.[S558]
4. **retaliation gate** — provide secure channels and anti-retaliation referral or remedy where applicable.[S561]
5. **causation gate** — link award to actual collected proceeds, sanctions, or public recovery.
6. **public-control gate** — preserve government control over intervention, settlement, dismissal, and litigation posture in relator cases.[S559]
7. **culpability gate** — reduce, deny, or separately pursue awards where the whistleblower planned, led, or profited from misconduct.
8. **proportionality gate** — cap or scale awards to avoid over-incentivizing speculative or coercive claims.
9. **timeliness gate** — publish claim aging, award delay, and communication standards.[S558]
10. **repair gate** — feed confirmed schemes into systemic compliance redesign, not only one-off recoveries.
