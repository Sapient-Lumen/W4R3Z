## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — private toll preservation | permit dominant rail fees, exclusivity, or anti-steering as ordinary private price. | Weak unless the fee reflects real cost and exit/portability is preserved. |
| B — interoperability / portability condition | require open interfaces, data transfer, alternative channels, or steering rights. | Strong where gatekeeper power blocks access or switching. |
| C — access-fee or rent charge | charge scarcity/platform rent or route proceeds to public access. | Use only after incidence and bypass checks. |
| D — public fallback rail | provide public or neutral channel for payments, filing, notices, or essential transactions. | Strong for rights-bearing and essential services. |
| E — structural remedy / nondiscrimination | separate gatekeeping power from downstream self-preference. | Use for repeated exclusion or tying. |

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| essential access through dominant rail | interoperability plus public fallback | no compulsory private toll for civic or subsistence access. |
| private fee for optional premium service | cost-based fee review | separate luxury convenience from basic access. |
