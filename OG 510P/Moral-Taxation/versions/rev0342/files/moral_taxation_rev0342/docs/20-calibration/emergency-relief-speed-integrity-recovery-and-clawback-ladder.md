# Emergency relief, speed, integrity, recovery, and clawback ladder

## Question in one sentence

How should rapid emergency aid preserve speed, identity integrity, fraud control, postpayment review, and floor-sensitive recovery?[S531][S532][S533]

## Option scan

| Option | Use | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| prepayment control | high-risk or nonurgent cases | do not defeat floor speed. |
| provisional payment | subsistence crisis | clear true-up rules. |
| postpayment sampling | speed relaxes proof | root-cause reporting. |
| good-faith safe harbor | eligible floor cases | no hardship clawback. |
| fraud recovery | culpable actors | culpability and identity repair first. |

## Priority gates

1. **emergency/floor gate** — define shock, deadline, affected population, and food, rent, wage, health, utility, disaster, or business-survival need.
2. **identity gate** — use resilient identity checks and victim-repair paths.[S531]
3. **eligibility gate** — prefer observable data, attestation, and later true-up when documents are disrupted.
4. **speed/risk gate** — decide which controls must happen prepayment and which can move to postpayment sampling.
5. **notice gate** — label what is provisional, final, appealable, recoverable, or fraud-sensitive.
6. **recovery gate** — separate agency error, good-faith recipient, reckless claimant, identity thief, and organized false-claim actor.[S533]
7. **hardship gate** — waive or cap recovery before collection invades subsistence.
8. **review gate** — report improper payments, recovery rates, root causes, and eligible non-take-up.[S532]

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
