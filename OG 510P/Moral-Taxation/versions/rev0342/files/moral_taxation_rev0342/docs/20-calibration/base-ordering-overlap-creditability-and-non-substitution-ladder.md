# Base-ordering, overlap, creditability, and non-substitution ladder

## Question in one sentence

Which compact ladder should decide when several taxes may justly stack on the same activity, when near-duplicate levies should credit or consolidate, and when an “already taxed” objection is really just a wrong-base attempt to erase a distinct claim?[S3][S4][S5][S8][S9][S10][S15][S21][S28][S29][S30][S73][S83][S87][S88][S93]

## Closed rules invoked

Routes: [`../10-framework/tax-base-ordering.md`](../10-framework/tax-base-ordering.md), [`../10-framework/default-tax-stack-by-scale.md`](../10-framework/default-tax-stack-by-scale.md), [`../10-framework/net-fiscal-stack-and-hidden-negative-tax-routing.md`](../10-framework/net-fiscal-stack-and-hidden-negative-tax-routing.md), [`../10-framework/incidence-and-protected-burden-routing.md`](../10-framework/incidence-and-protected-burden-routing.md), [`../10-framework/proceeds-routing-and-fiscal-reciprocity.md`](../10-framework/proceeds-routing-and-fiscal-reciprocity.md), and [`../10-framework/annual-wealth-tax-backstop-routing.md`](../10-framework/annual-wealth-tax-backstop-routing.md).

Closed rule: reach land, resource rents, monopoly, pollution, inheritance, and other concentrated claims before routine labor; treat broad taxes as non-substitutes for prior rent or harm pricing; and use annual wealth taxation as a backstop rather than a first-line replacement for cleaner concentrated bases.[S3][S4][S5][S8][S73][S87][S93]

## Why calibration is still needed

A usable overlap rule still has to avoid five failures:

1. **single-base substitution**, where one familiar tax is treated as if it erases every other moral claim,[S5][S8][S21][S73]
2. **same-base pile-on**, where two levies charge the same harm, stock, or rent slice without any duplication screen,[S15][S21][S83]
3. **wrong-base crediting**, where a local harm charge is offered as a reason not to tax monopoly rent, or ordinary profit tax is offered as a reason not to price emissions or land rent,[S5][S9][S15][S21][S28]
4. **hidden substitution**, where abatements, cheap inputs, or tax expenditures quietly reverse a nominal burden while officials still say the activity is already taxed enough,[S29][S30]
5. **omnibus-super-levy temptation**, where several distinct objects are collapsed into one bespoke tax and the archive loses sight of what is being priced, redistributed, or repaired.[S10][S15][S21][S28][S83]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — one-master-tax substitution | Pick one dominant tax for an activity or taxpayer and treat the rest as largely redundant — for example ordinary corporate tax instead of land and carbon charges, or annual wealth tax instead of inheritance, land, and anti-deferral reform.[S5][S8][S9][S73] | Reject: hides distinct moral objects inside one familiar base and turns “already taxed” into a rhetorical eraser. |
| B — stack everything that can be named | Layer every plausible claim and let lobbying, deductions, or later bargaining sort out overlap.[S21][S29][S83] | Reject: invites same-base pile-ons and obscures real incidence. |
| C — distinct-object stacking with duplicate screening | Identify moral objects separately, stack taxes that reach genuinely different objects, credit only when two levies really charge the same object, and keep backstops for last.[S3][S4][S5][S8][S9][S10][S15][S21][S28][S73][S83][S87][S93] | Adopt. |
| D — bespoke omnibus levy | Replace several ordinary instruments with one custom sector levy and promise later earmarking or rebates will handle overlap.[S21][S29][S30] | Reject: blurs whether the state is pricing harm, capturing rent, financing repair, or just building a politically saleable label. |

## Five-rung ladder

1. **separate the moral objects first** — land/location rent, direct harm or scarcity burden, ordinary broad income or consumption, cross-border residual profit, dynastic transfer, and wealth-stock backstop should not be collapsed by default,[S3][S5][S8][S9][S15][S73][S93]
2. **stack genuinely distinct objects** — especially when one levy is place-bound or harm-based and another is a broader profit or transfer claim,[S5][S8][S9][S15][S28]
3. **credit or consolidate only true duplicates** — the same emissions, the same measured stock, the same narrow rent slice, or the same transaction base under two names,[S15][S21][S83]
4. **treat ordinary broad taxes as non-substitutes for prior rent or harm charges** — interaction may occur through ordinary deduction or profit measurement, not through moral cancellation,[S3][S4][S5][S9][S21][S87][S93]
5. **hold backstops for last** — especially annual wealth taxation and bespoke AI windfall layers, after land, inheritance, anti-deferral, harm-pricing, and ordinary profit rules have been taken seriously.[S8][S10][S22][S73]

## Provisional recommendation

Adopt **Option C — distinct-object stacking with duplicate screening and narrow creditability** as the archive's default calibration for overlapping bases.[S3][S4][S5][S8][S9][S10][S15][S21][S28][S29][S30][S73][S83][S87][S88][S93]

Presumption:

- **remove fake offsets first** — disclose abatements, cheap public inputs, tax expenditures, and negotiated support before judging whether the activity is already burdened at all,[S29][S30]
- **price place-bound rents and direct harms first** — land, extraction, congestion, local utility stress, water draw, carbon, and cleanup-security claims attach where they arise and are not ordinarily creditable against later profit or transfer taxes merely because the same actor pays both,[S5][S15][S28][S88][S93]
- **run ordinary broad taxes next** — broad VAT with explicit repair, ordinary personal income tax, and ordinary corporate-profit tax remain part of the stack, but they do not erase earlier harm or rent claims simply by being broad,[S3][S4][S9][S87]
- **add residual-rent layers only on what remains distinct** — monopoly-rent, windfall, market-jurisdiction residual profit, or narrow AI-exceptional layers should be screened against earlier direct charges so the same rent slice is not claimed twice under two narratives,[S9][S10][S22][S28][S83]
- **hold wealth-stock backstops for last-resort concentration repair** — annual wealth taxation belongs only after land, inheritance, capital-income, and anti-deferral tools still leave concentrated fortunes materially undertaxed.[S5][S8][S73]

That is the narrowest workable setting because the archive can say both **“yes, several taxes may justly apply to one activity”** and **“no, not every overlap is a new moral claim.”**

## Default stack table

| Context | What usually stacks | What usually should not substitute away | Where credit or consolidation is most plausible |
|---|---|---|---|
| land-rich or housing-rich wealth | recurrent property / land tax, inheritance or lifetime-receipts tax, capital-income or gains rules, then wealth backstop if still needed | annual wealth tax should not replace land and inheritance reform by convenience.[S5][S8][S23][S73][S93] | only where two stock-based taxes are plainly charging the same narrow asset base in the same period. |
| data-centre or frontier-AI campus | site-value or local burden charge, carbon or electricity-stress charge, ordinary profit tax, market-profit allocation where applicable, then narrow AI-exceptional layer only if a distinct bottleneck or public-input claim remains | local grid or water-burden charges should not cancel controller-side profit or market-jurisdiction claims, and ordinary corporate tax should not stand in for direct local burden pricing.[S9][S10][S15][S22][S28] | where two levies are both measuring the same kWh, the same emissions stream, or the same narrow residual-rent slice. |
| fossil or extractive activity | resource-rent or severance, carbon pricing, ordinary profit tax, cleanup bonding or reserves | cleanup-security and carbon charges are not substitutes for one another, and support measures must be netted before claiming the sector is already taxed enough.[S6][S7][S29][S30][S88] | where two instruments are charging the same emissions ton or the same extraction event under two labels. |
| broad household consumption | broad VAT plus explicit cash / transfer repair where needed | reduced rates or exemptions should not replace targeted floor protection when the real goal is distribution, and broad VAT should not be asked to price sector-specific harms it does not measure.[S3][S4][S87] | where multiple consumption taxes are simply overlapping on the same general base without a distinct externality claim. |
| dominant digital or platform rent | ordinary profit tax, minimum-tax floor, market-jurisdiction share, and where justified a narrow excess-profit or bottleneck-rent layer | local sales or payroll exposure alone should not excuse residual-rent capture where market access and concentration are doing separate work.[S9][S10][S21][S28][S83] | where two rent-focused instruments are both claiming the same residual-profit base under different statutory language. |
