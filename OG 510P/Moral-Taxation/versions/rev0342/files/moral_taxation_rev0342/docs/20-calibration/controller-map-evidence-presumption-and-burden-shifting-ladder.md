# Controller-map evidence, rebuttable presumptions, and burden-shifting ladder

## Question in one sentence

Given the archive's closed rule that controller status follows **real governance, continuation power, and residual upside** rather than mere visibility or paper titles, **what is the smallest workable ladder for deciding when outside-interface actors get a rebuttable non-controller presumption, when a named controller map is enough, when proof burdens should shift toward actors with superior access to the governance facts, and when opacity or fragmentation justifies adverse inference or provisional controller-group treatment?**[S10][S15][S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S39][S41][S83]

Route: first rank the actors by real governance, then decide who should carry the proof burden. Do not demand impossible outsider proof when the decisive facts sit inside the affiliate chain, committee room, safety gate, or deployment console.[S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S39][S41][S83]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — outsider-proves-everything lane | keep the full proof burden on the challenger unless it can already show the whole governance chain from outside.[S20][S21][S27][S39] | Reject: rewards opacity and makes controller status easiest to escape where records are most concentrated. |
| B — opacity-means-everyone-controls lane | whenever records are incomplete, presume every visible participant is a joint controller.[S21][S27][S41][S83] | Reject: overcorrects by collapsing support, oversight, remittance, and real governance into one blob. |
| C — staged presumptions and burden shifting | start with role-sensitive presumptions, require a short controller map from the best-informed actor, then shift burdens and use adverse inference only when concrete governance signals or strategic opacity appear.[S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S39][S41][S83] | Adopt. |
| D — maximal logging and discovery by default | solve proof problems by retaining broad logs, communications, and identity-linked records across the stack.[S16][S17][S39][S40][S41] | Reject: too privacy-costly and too easily repurposed into generalized surveillance. |

## Five-rung ladder

1. **ordinary outside-interface presumption** — prompt users, thin resellers, auditors, certifiers, insurers, payment processors, passive hosts, ordinary tool providers, and ordinary data / benchmark suppliers begin with a rebuttable **non-controller** presumption unless concrete inside-the-loop governance facts appear.[S10][S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S41][S83]
2. **named controller-map lane** — the best-informed visible actor should provide a short controller map naming who can continue, halt, update, approve, allocate capacity, define mission scope, or capture residual upside.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S41]
3. **superior-access burden shift** — once a challenger points to specific governance signals such as mission-setting procurement, persistent retrieval or tool control, successor continuity, group veto power, or step-in command, the burden shifts toward the actor with superior access to the real governance facts.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S34][S35][S41][S83]
4. **adverse-inference / provisional group lane** — if relevant records are withheld, affiliate boundaries are used opportunistically, or several actors offer inconsistent controller stories, the state may provisionally infer controller-group status, successor continuity, or timed controller sequence status for collection and filing purposes, subject to review.[S21][S27][S34][S35][S39][S41][S83]
5. **timed correction lane** — once fuller records arrive, refine the provisional answer through apportionment, credits, refunds, or corrected group allocation without letting perfect proof become a veto on timely collection or interim floor protection.[S21][S27][S39][S41][S83]

## Provisional recommendation

Adopt **Option C — staged presumptions and burden shifting** as the archive's default proof discipline for controller disputes.[S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S39][S41][S83]

Presumption:

- keep ordinary outside-interface actors in the **non-controller lane** until concrete governance facts appear,
- require a **short controller map** from the best-informed actor before demanding heavy discovery from weaker downstream parties,
- shift burdens when a challenger shows specific evidence of durable mission control, continuation power, residual upside, or inside-the-loop governance,
- allow **adverse inference or provisional controller-group treatment** when opacity looks strategic rather than accidental,
- and preserve a real correction path so provisional filing or collection does not harden into unreviewable fiction.

This is the archive's narrowest workable setting because it blocks opacity bonuses without turning every dispute into unlimited discovery or universal joint liability.

## Default presumption table

| Live fact pattern | Starting presumption | What rebuts it |
|---|---|---|
| ordinary prompting, ordinary hosted use, thin resale, outside audit, insurance, billing, or passive hosting | **non-controller** | concrete evidence of persistent approval power, deployment policy control, capacity allocation, or live workflow governance.[S20][S21][S27][S41][S83] |
| durable downstream orchestration, retrieval governance, tool permissioning, or safety-policy control | **bounded co-controller for that layer** | credible proof that the actor only offers optional advice or one-off configuration without durable operational authority.[S16][S17][S20][S21] |
| mission-specific commissioning with dedicated approvals, service-level command, stop / continue rights, or residual upside concentrated in the buyer or principal | **real controller or co-controller** | credible proof that the vendor, not the buyer, actually governs ordinary deployment scope and continuation.[S20][S21][S34][S35][S83] |
| distributed committee, foundation, consortium, or DAO-like command over update, pause, release, or treasury choices | **controller-group** | credible proof that the apparent coalition lacks durable operational authority and functions only as an external forum.[S20][S21][S34][S35][S41] |
| successor transfer, checkpoint migration, rename, affiliate move, or step-in command period | **continuity or timed-controller-sequence** | credible proof of a real governance break rather than relabeling, emergency veneer, or paper transfer theater.[S20][S21][S34][S35][S41] |
