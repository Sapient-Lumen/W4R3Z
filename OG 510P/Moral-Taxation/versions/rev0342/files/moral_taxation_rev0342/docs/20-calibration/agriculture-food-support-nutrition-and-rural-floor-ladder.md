# Agriculture, food support, nutrition, and rural-floor ladder

## Question in one sentence

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — broad producer support | transfers or price support to producers. | Weak unless incidence and floor repair are shown. |
| B — targeted farmer income support | direct, capped, means- or scale-sensitive support. | Prefer where rural floor is the real aim. |
| C — nutrition-side support | rebate, voucher, school meals, or healthy-diet transfer. | Prefer when consumer floor is the problem. |
| D — environmental public-goods payment | pay for verified soil, water, biodiversity, or emissions gains. | Strong when additionality is real. |
| E — transition compact | time-limited support for practice, crop, or regional transition. | Use with sunset and local resilience metrics. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **purpose gate** — name nutrition access, farmer floor, environmental repair, resilience, or transition.
2. **incidence gate** — estimate producer, landowner, worker, consumer, and taxpayer burden.
3. **nutrition gate** — test healthy-diet affordability for low-income households.
4. **producer-cap gate** — cap or graduate support that would otherwise flow to large land or quota holders.
5. **input gate** — disclose fertilizer, fuel, water, feed, insurance, and credit subsidies as a net stack.
6. **environment gate** — separate baseline compliance from additional public-goods payment.
7. **labor gate** — test farmworker conditions and pass-through before counting rural benefit.
8. **trade gate** — test import/export distortion and food-security spillovers.
9. **transition gate** — set practice, crop, regional, or resilience metrics with sunset.
10. **review gate** — trigger redesign on food-price spikes, land-value capture, or poor take-up by small farmers.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| food affordability failure | nutrition-side support | automatic or low-friction delivery. |
| small-farmer distress | capped income support | no land-value windfall. |
| ecological degradation | public-goods payment | verified additionality. |
| input shock | temporary transition compact | sunset and substitution plan. |
