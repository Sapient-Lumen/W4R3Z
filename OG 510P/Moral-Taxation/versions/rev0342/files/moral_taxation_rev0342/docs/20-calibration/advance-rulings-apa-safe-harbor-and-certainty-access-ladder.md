# Advance rulings, APAs, safe harbors, and certainty-access ladder

## Question in one sentence

What ladder decides whether an advance ruling, APA, user-fee, or safe-harbor design expands lawful certainty without creating elite-only certainty or bespoke exception rents?[S552][S553][S554][S555]

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **uncertainty gate** — identify whether uncertainty is factual, legal, valuation, treaty, or transfer-pricing.
2. **public-guidance gate** — ask whether a generally applicable instruction, FAQ, revenue ruling, or form fix would solve repeated cases.
3. **safe-harbor gate** — use a simple option only if it protects low-risk compliance and cannot be scaled into avoidance.
4. **fee gate** — test whether user fees bar low-income taxpayers, small entities, or public-interest claimants.[S555][S556]
5. **access gate** — provide reduced-fee, pro bono, clinic, or simplified request paths where uncertainty threatens floor rights.
6. **APA gate** — use APAs for genuine transfer-pricing certainty, with bilateral/multilateral preference where double tax is likely.[S552][S554]
7. **consistency gate** — compare similarly situated taxpayers and publish redacted reasoning where possible.[S553]
8. **anti-preference gate** — prohibit rulings that create private tax expenditures, disguised subsidies, or nonprecedential arbitrage.
9. **duration gate** — limit reliance to stated facts, years, and conditions; require update when facts change.
10. **conversion gate** — convert recurring private-rule patterns into public guidance or law repair.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| common uncertainty | public guidance | no private toll. |
| low-risk small taxpayer | safe harbor | anti-abuse cap. |
| unique transaction | PLR | fee and publication discipline. |
| cross-border TP | APA | MAP coordination. |
| repeated private rulings | conversion | public rule. |
