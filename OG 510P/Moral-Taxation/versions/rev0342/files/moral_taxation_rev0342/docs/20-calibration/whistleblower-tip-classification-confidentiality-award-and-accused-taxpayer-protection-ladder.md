Route: treat the tip as a lead; classify credibility; protect source and taxpayer confidentiality; convert relied-on material into contestable taxpayer facts; decide award rights separately after collected-proceeds and contribution analysis; never let bounty expectations, retaliation, or hidden source status control audit severity, penalty posture, criminal referral, or collection pressure.[S366][S370][S372][S373][S374][S375][S376][S377][S380][S381][S382]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — ignore tips | refuse or underuse whistleblower claims because source motives are messy. | Reject: insiders can reveal hidden underpayment and organized fraud that ordinary matching cannot see.[S366][S368][S373] |
| B — bounty-first enforcement | let potential proceeds and award expectations drive audit depth, penalties, and settlement resistance. | Reject: turns public enforcement into a private upside market.[S370][S374][S376] |
| C — confidential lead, contestable proof | protect identities and return information while converting any relied-on material into ordinary evidence the taxpayer can answer. | Adopt. |
| D — full source adversarial disclosure | give the accused taxpayer the entire source file by default. | Reject as default: source safety and tax administration matter; the taxpayer needs contestable facts, not automatic source exposure.[S366][S377][S379][S380] |

## Eight-gate ladder

1. **claim-intake gate** — require Form 211-style specificity: named taxpayer, facts, periods, documents, source of information, contact route, and penalty-of-perjury declaration.[S366][S373]
2. **conflict-and-motive gate** — mark divorce, employment, competitor, preparer, payroll, platform, or family conflict; do not disqualify automatically, but do require corroboration before taxpayer burden expands.[S366][S373][S376]
3. **credibility / public-source gate** — distinguish original documents and firsthand knowledge from speculation, public information, press analysis, or pure legal argument.[S370][S373][S376]
4. **classification / action gate** — decide whether the information substantially contributes to an administrative or judicial action and what related actions, taxpayers, periods, and proceeds are actually connected.[S374]
5. **taxpayer-evidence conversion gate** — before adverse action, turn the lead into line items, computations, documents, third-party records, interviews, transaction maps, or legal theories the taxpayer can contest.[S375][S377][S379]
6. **disclosure-necessity gate** — any disclosure of returns or return information to a whistleblower, representative, contractor, or third party needs legal authority, purpose, necessity, minimum scope, safeguards, and a stopping rule.[S377][S378][S380][S381][S382]
7. **award-determination gate** — after final tax determinations and collected-proceeds work, apply contribution, positive/negative factors, planned-and-initiated rules, criminal-role denial, preliminary recommendation, response, determination, and appeal routes.[S370][S372][S375][S376]
8. **posture-firewall gate** — prevent award litigation, source frustration, payment timing, or proceeds targets from controlling taxpayer settlement, penalty abatement, criminal referral, collection alternative, bankruptcy treatment, or civil re-entry.[S371][S375][S376]

## Default routing table

| Signal | Start here | Escalate only if | Stop / narrow when |
|---|---|---|---|
| concrete insider documents | claim-intake and evidence-conversion gates | documents map to tax periods, issues, and actors.[S366][S373] | source wants a broad fishing expedition. |
| anonymous accusation | credibility gate | independent records corroborate specific facts.[S373] | facts are speculative, stale, or revenge-coded. |
| leaked taxpayer records | disclosure-necessity and lawfulness screen | use is authorized and necessary for tax administration.[S377][S379][S380] | origin, scope, or safeguards are defective. |
| public article plus analysis | public-source gate | analysis substantially contributes beyond public material.[S370][S376] | claim merely repackages public information. |
| source helped design scheme | negative-factor / denial lane | information still aids collection or prosecution. | source conduct makes award reduction, denial, or separate sanction appropriate.[S372][S376] |
| taxpayer merits dispute | taxpayer-evidence conversion gate | source facts have been translated into contestable proof.[S375][S377] | administration cannot state the adjustment without relying on untestable accusation. |
| whistleblower status request | Taxpayer First Act status lane | statutory conditions for written notice are met.[S371] | notice would seriously impair tax administration or imply merits conclusions. |
| award appeal | award-determination gate | final determination letter is issued. | preliminary recommendation is not yet appealable.[S375] |
| disclosure to whistleblower for proceedings | disclosure-necessity gate | information is necessary for the proceeding and safeguards apply.[S381][S382] | only broad context or curiosity is requested. |

## Award firewall

A whistleblower's award file should answer award questions, not taxpayer-liability questions. The award file may ask whether the IRS acted based on information, whether proceeds were collected, whether related actions count, what positive and negative factors apply, whether the whistleblower planned or initiated the underpayment, and whether criminal conduct bars payment. The taxpayer merits file must answer a different question: what tax, penalty, interest, period, actor, and legal theory is actually proven.[S370][S372][S374][S376]

## Disclosure ledger

Before sharing return information with a source or other outside recipient, record:

- the statutory or regulatory authority;
- the tax-administration or whistleblower-proceeding purpose;
- the exact records or fields needed;
- why less disclosure would not work;
- confidentiality agreement, safeguard, and use limits;
- breach, redisclosure, and destruction / non-use controls;
- whether the same information can be obtained from a less sensitive source;
- the contestable non-source facts that will appear in any taxpayer notice.[S377][S378][S379][S380][S381][S382]

Axes: `bounty_steering`, `data_or_confidentiality_overreach`, `opacity_or_erasure`, `ghost_proof`. Watch for source incentives or ghost proof driving posture before allegations become contestable evidence.
