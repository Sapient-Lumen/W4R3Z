# Intergovernmental equalization, local-share, and subnational-capacity ladder

## Question in one sentence

What ladder should divide proceeds among host locality, burdened ratepayers, regional pool, national equalization, public-capacity grants, and general revenue when local burden and unequal fiscal capacity coexist?[S469][S470][S471]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — local retention | host keeps all revenue. | Correct only for locally generated burdens/effort after spillover review. |
| B — national sweep | center keeps all revenue. | Reject when host bears unrepaired burden. |
| C — formula equalization | share by need/capacity indicators. | Adopt for location/resource rents and broad fiscal capacity gaps. |
| D — matching transfer | higher level funds mandated service or repair. | Adopt for mandates and under-capacity. |
| E — negotiated benefit deal | project-specific community benefit agreement. | Use only after net fiscal-stack disclosure and enforceability. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **base gate** — identify land rent, natural resource, port, tourism, data center, grid, water, platform, property, sales, or income base.
2. **burden gate** — quantify direct local burdens: infrastructure, water, grid, housing, environmental, administrative, emergency, school, health, or policing costs.
3. **benefit gate** — identify local, regional, national, and global beneficiaries of the activity or tax base.
4. **capacity gate** — compare revenue capacity, expenditure needs, administrative ability, and inherited infrastructure.[S469]
5. **effort gate** — distinguish low capacity from low effort; protect reasonable local autonomy and accountability.
6. **spillover gate** — identify cross-border users, commuters, ratepayers, environmental spillovers, and market beneficiaries.
7. **mandate gate** — match spending duties with revenue authority or transfers; prevent unfunded obligations.[S471]
8. **formula gate** — choose local share, regional pool, national equalization, matching grant, floor grant, or capacity-building transfer.[S470]
9. **net-stack gate** — offset abatements, grants, public infrastructure, debt, and hidden subsidies before declaring a local windfall.
10. **review gate** — recalibrate when tax base moves, burden changes, service need shifts, or capacity indicators update.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Proceeds rule |
|---|---|---|
| direct unrepaired local burden | local repair share | host repair first. |
| geography/resource/location windfall | equalization formula | local share plus redistribution. |
| higher-level mandate | transfer or revenue authority | no unfunded duty. |
| rich locality captures mobile base | shared base or regional pool | prevent fiscal hoarding. |
| poor locality lacks capacity | capacity grant and simple base | do not punish weakness. |
