## Ten-gate ladder

1. **status gate** — identify Tribal government, member, wholly owned entity, enterprise, or outside contractor.
2. **treaty/trust gate** — name treaty, trust, land, resource, or federal obligation.
3. **parity gate** — compare treatment with state, local, territorial, and nonprofit analogues.
4. **self-determination gate** — defer to Tribal program purpose and community need absent abuse.
5. **general-welfare gate** — separate welfare benefits from compensation, private benefit, or lavish transfer.
6. **consultation gate** — require early notice, resources, meaningful agenda control, and response-to-reasons.
7. **confidentiality gate** — protect culturally sensitive, personal, and commercial information.
8. **proceeds gate** — route resource, land, energy, data, or gaming claims to Tribal public goods first.
9. **administration gate** — avoid duplicated reporting, remittance, or penalty traps.
10. **review gate** — reopen on parity gap, consultation failure, sovereignty bypass, or member-floor harm.
