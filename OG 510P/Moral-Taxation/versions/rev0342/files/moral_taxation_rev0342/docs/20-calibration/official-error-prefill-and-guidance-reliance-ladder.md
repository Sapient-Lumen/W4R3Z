# Official-error, prefill, and guidance-reliance ladder

## Question in one sentence

Given the archive's closed rule that **good-faith reliance on official prefill, portal logic, calculators, schema validation, and guidance should usually trigger correction-first treatment rather than retrospective ambush**, **what is the smallest workable ladder for deciding when that reliance buys only no-penalty treatment, when it should narrow the recoverable amount, when accepted official packets should receive stronger shelter, and when industrialized official error should trigger batch correction and system repair instead of case-by-case cleanup?**[S16][S17][S27][S36][S38][S42][S44][S65][S66][S68][S89]

The constitutional question is already settled: **the state should not profit from its own misleading prefill, accepted packet design, stale guidance, or defective portal logic against people who used the official path in good faith; but obvious contradiction, concealment, or strategic exploitation can still narrow or defeat reliance shelter.**[S16][S17][S27][S36][S38][S42][S44][S65][S66][S68][S89]

## Why calibration is still needed

The closed rule settles direction, but not yet the smallest usable operating ladder. A workable calibration still has to avoid six failures at once:

1. **portal non-binding theater** where the administration urges use of official tools but later claims they never carried any protective weight,[S27][S36][S42][S65][S66][S68]
2. **blanket immunity drift** where any contact with an official channel becomes a universal shield even against clear contradiction or withheld facts,[S27][S36][S42]
3. **one-field-becomes-whole-case** error where one bad prefilled or validated field reopens every settled part of a return, packet, or benefit decision,[S17][S21][S42][S89]
4. **official-error liquidity loan** where the state concedes the error but still keeps the cash, time value, or denied payment while the weaker side waits,[S21][S27][S39][S42][S65][S66]
5. **asymmetric reliance markets** where large actors negotiate comfort, rulings, or practical tolerance while ordinary users get only trap-like prefill and stale help text,[S27][S36][S38][S65][S66][S68]
6. and **industrialized bug externalization** where one bad schema, FAQ, portal rule, or accepted validator spreads the same mistake across many people and the state still demands individualized downstream cleanup.[S16][S17][S27][S42][S44][S68][S89]

## Small option set

### Option A — no special reliance effect

Recover the tax normally; perhaps waive only a small fraction of penalties. This is the archive's worst default because it lets official convenience travel one way only: the state gets the benefits of prefill, portals, and accepted schemas without taking meaningful responsibility when those channels mislead.[S27][S36][S42][S65][S66][S68]

### Option B — no-penalty-only shelter

Where the official channel misled, waive civil penalty or fraud rhetoric but still collect the full remainder on ordinary terms. This is better than Option A, but it still treats official error mostly as a sanctions issue rather than a correction and burden-allocation problem.[S27][S36][S42]

### Option C — issue-scoped reliance ladder

Give reliance protection on the **affected field, period, or packet slice**, run correction through the same or successor official channel where possible, recover only the clear remainder that survives the reliance rule, and move quickly to batch repair when the official defect was systematic.[S16][S17][S21][S27][S36][S42][S44][S65][S66][S68][S89]

This is the archive's cleanest default because it protects good-faith use of official channels without turning obvious contradiction into a free option.

### Option D — accepted-official-output estoppel for everything

Treat any accepted prefill, portal output, or schema validation as permanently binding against the state. This can make sense for a narrow accepted-packet lane, but as a universal rule it is too strong: it would freeze correction even where the contradiction was plain, the subject concealed facts, or the official channel was only advisory rather than operative.[S27][S36][S42][S65][S66][S68][S89]

## Provisional recommendation

Adopt a **five-stage reliance ladder**:

| Stage | When it should apply | Reliance answer | Main guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. obvious contradiction or concealment | the subject could plainly see the mismatch, withheld decisive facts, or strategically exploited a known defect | **ordinary correction, with no special reliance shelter beyond basic review rights** | reserve this stage for real contradiction, not hindsight perfectionism |
| 1. advisory-channel mislead | help text, script, calculator, or FAQ materially misled, but the official channel did not itself determine the filed field | **no-penalty treatment plus prospective repair and simplified correction** | do not let "advisory" become a label that drains all protection from mass official guidance |
| 2. operative-field reliance | official prefill, portal logic, or routine validation supplied the disputed figure, classification, or eligibility field | **issue-scoped correction-first treatment, recover only the clear remainder, and keep interest / penalty shelter on the affected slice** | correct the smallest field or period first rather than reopening everything |
| 3. accepted-packet / schema reliance | the state accepted an official packet, validator result, or schema-compliant filing path that later proves defective | **stronger packet-level shelter, bounded true-up only for clear surviving remainder, and prospective schema repair or delta update** | packet acceptance is not a license to conceal contrary facts |
| 4. industrialized official defect | one official bug or stale rule affected many people or many controller packets | **batch correction, automatic restoration where possible, refund / release of clear excess, and system repair before individual blame** | do not force thousands of one-off appeals to repair one official failure |

The archive's provisional setting is therefore **C as the general rule, with Stage 3 for accepted official packet / schema cases and Stage 4 for repeated system-caused error**.[S16][S17][S21][S27][S36][S38][S39][S42][S44][S65][S66][S68][S89]

That is the narrowest workable recommendation because it distinguishes **guidance** from **operative official determination**, distinguishes **field-scoped correction** from **whole-case reopening**, and distinguishes **good-faith reliance** from **opportunistic exploitation**.

## Typical placements

| Context | Default stage | Why |
|---|---|---|
| ordinary wage prefill accepted by a household | Stage 2 | the state invited reliance on its own wage / withholding picture.[S27][S42][S65][S66][S68] |
| official calculator or withholding table used by a small firm | Stage 1 or 2 | treat as Stage 2 when the tool effectively set the filed figure; otherwise Stage 1 with no-penalty shelter.[S27][S36][S65][S66][S68] |
| tax-credit or rebate portal silently suppressing a protected payment | Stage 2, often escalating to Stage 4 | protected-side delivery should not fail harder than collection, and repeated portal suppression is a batch-repair problem.[S21][S27][S42][S64][S65][S66][S68] |
| controller-side packet accepted under an official schema or validation tool | Stage 3 | accepted official packet design should carry strong reliance weight absent contradiction or concealment.[S16][S17][S27][S41][S44][S68][S89] |
| stale official FAQ or script after a rule change | Stage 1 | guidance mattered, but the channel may not have directly authored the filed field.[S27][S36][S38][S42][S65][S68] |
| mass official bug across many similarly situated filers | Stage 4 | batch correction beats individualized cleanup.[S27][S42][S44][S65][S66][S68] |
