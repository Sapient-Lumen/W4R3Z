# Criminal tax referral, civil/criminal boundary, voluntary disclosure, and restitution ladder

## Question in one sentence

When a tax case begins to look like fraud, **what is the smallest workable ladder for deciding whether the case should stay civil, enter fraud development, be referred to Criminal Investigation, be coordinated as a joint or suspended civil matter, be routed through voluntary disclosure, and later return to civil restitution / assessment without double recovery?**[S351][S352][S353][S354][S355][S356][S357][S358][S359][S360][S361][S362][S363][S364][S365]

## Eight-gate ladder

1. **civil correction gate** — use when the live problem is late filing, nonpayment, mismatch, lost records, negligence, reliance, hardship, or explainable error without affirmative deceptive acts.[S354][S364]
2. **indicator documentation gate** — use when fraud badges appear; record facts, avoid guilt language, involve the manager, and decide whether FEA consultation or fraud development is warranted.[S353][S354]
3. **affirmative-act development gate** — use when the case needs targeted development to test whether facts show deception, concealment, false statement, evasion, obstruction, or record falsification.[S354][S363][S365]
4. **referral-theory gate** — use only after firm indications of fraud or willfulness are developed; the referral should identify actor, period, scheme, taxpayer explanation, estimated criminal tax liability, and proof method.[S355]
5. **CI evaluation gate** — CI acceptance, decline, or feedback should be recorded; a declined or discontinued criminal case returns to civil routing without treating the mere referral as proof.[S355][S356]
6. **joint / suspense posture gate** — once criminal predominates, civil activity must be coordinated, statutes protected, civil pressure controlled, and open modules or related returns handled under joint-investigation or suspense rules.[S356][S357][S358]
7. **DOJ / prosecution gate** — prosecution routing should remain Tax Division / DOJ disciplined, offense-specific, evidence-weighted, and proportionate rather than a collection device.[S361][S363][S364][S365]
8. **civil re-entry and restitution gate** — after discontinuance, declination, conviction, restitution, probation, or closure, the civil ledger must resume with payment credits, clear period mapping, and no duplicate recovery.[S357][S362]

## Voluntary disclosure side lane

Voluntary disclosure is not a ninth punitive rung. It is a side lane for taxpayers with potential criminal exposure who come forward truthfully, timely, and completely, cooperate, submit required returns and reports, and make good-faith payment arrangements. Use it only when willful exposure is plausible and ordinary amended, delinquent, information-return, nonfiler, abatement, or correction lanes do not fit.[S359][S360]

Required safeguards:

- plain-language distinction between nonwillful correction and willful voluntary disclosure;
- visible preclearance / Part II timing and extension logic;
- clear statement that preliminary acceptance is not immunity;
- narrow disclosure scope tied to the noncompliance period and issue;
- counsel / representation access without paid gatekeeping as a practical prerequisite;
- no overbroad admissions, waivers, or professional-enabler narratives beyond what the lane actually requires;
- revocation or rejection routed with reasons sufficient to permit safe civil re-entry where criminal action is not pursued.[S359][S360]

## Default routing table

| Case posture | Default lane | Why | Do not do this |
|---|---|---|---|
| missed filing after illness, disaster, confusion, language barrier, or poverty | civil correction / abatement | no affirmative act is shown.[S354][S364] | threaten criminal failure-to-file posture as a payment prod. |
| late payment where liability is visible and taxpayer is seeking a plan | interest / civil collection | the conduct is debt management unless concealment is shown.[S354] | use criminal language to jump the collection queue. |
| false W-2, altered invoices, fake deductions, or destroyed records | indicator then affirmative-act development | the facts may show intentional deception.[S354][S365] | treat every record defect as fraud without development. |
| preparer, promoter, payroll agent, or platform created repeated false filings | enabler / controller-focused development | upstream design may be the high-leverage culpability center.[S352][S355][S365] | prosecute weakest end filers while leaving the scheme architecture untouched. |
| digital assets, privacy coins, mixers, or missing exchange records | targeted development | digital-asset facts can be indicators, not automatic guilt.[S354] | presume criminal intent from technology choice alone. |
| civil audit discovers firm indications of evasion | Form 2797 referral-theory gate | referral needs facts, explanation, liability estimate, and proof method.[S355] | send a vague criminal label without a tested theory. |
| CI opens joint investigation | joint / suspense posture gate | criminal predominates; civil actions need coordination and statute controls.[S356][S357] | keep ordinary civil pressure running as hidden criminal leverage. |
| prosecution ends with restitution or probation conditions | civil re-entry and ledger-credit gate | restitution and civil assessments must reconcile.[S357][S362] | collect the same tax twice through separate labels. |
| taxpayer asks about voluntary disclosure | side lane screen | useful for willful exposure but not guaranteed immunity.[S359][S360] | sell disclosure as automatic amnesty or use it for nonwillful mistakes. |
| future rights-bearing artificial person, if recognized | same ladder plus direct rights bundle | criminal status requires notice, counsel, proof, and remedy.[S351][S361] | leave criminal response under owner or host console control. |

## Failure-mode capsule

Axes: `civil_criminal_boundary_blur`, `duplicate_penalty_stack`, `liability_misassignment`. Watch for criminal shadow, restitution, or disclosure lanes used as collection leverage without actor-specific willfulness.

## Recalibration trigger capsule

Axes: `coercive_escalation_or_criminal_boundary`, `remedy_or_contest_failure`, `protected_floor_or_incidence_shift`. Reopen when civil tools substitute for criminal proof, restitution is not credited, or counsel/privilege protections thin out.
