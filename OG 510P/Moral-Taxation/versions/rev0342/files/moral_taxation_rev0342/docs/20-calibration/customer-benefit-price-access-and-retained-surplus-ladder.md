# Customer benefit / price-access diffusion / retained-surplus ladder

## Question in one sentence

Given the archive's closed rule that **real broad customer or user diffusion can narrow or defeat a downstream beneficiary-side windfall claim, but only when the benefit is durable enough and retained surplus is not still exceptional**, **what is the smallest workable ladder for deciding when AI-linked gains have really been competed away into price, access, or quality, when the case is mixed, and when a beneficiary-side charge should still attach to the retained surplus?**[S3][S4][S10][S11][S18][S21][S22][S28][S67][S69][S83]

The constitutional answer is already closed: **broad customer or user benefit can morally matter, but only real and durable diffusion narrows the beneficiary-side windfall story; promotional pricing, lock-in subsidies, feature theater, or partial pass-through alongside persistent exceptional margins do not dissolve retained concentrated gain.**[S3][S4][S10][S11][S18][S21][S22][S28][S67][S69][S83]

## Why calibration is still needed

Once the archive opens a customer-diffusion lane, five practical failures remain easy:

1. **promo theater** where temporary discounts, growth subsidies, or free tiers are treated as if they proved durable social diffusion,
2. **quality mirage** where firms claim user benefit through speed, convenience, or product polish while still keeping exceptional margins,
3. **false full immunity** where partial pass-through is used to erase the whole retained-surplus question,
4. **regulated-service confusion** where formula-pass-through or rate-review cases are treated as ordinary private competition instead of service-benefit routing,
5. **burden erasure** where customer benefit rhetoric is used to hide worker displacement or burdened-place repair that still needs its own lane.[S3][S4][S10][S11][S18][S21][S22][S28][S29][S67][S69][S83]

## Small option set

### Option A — retained-surplus default

Treat most alleged customer benefit as too weak or too noisy to change the beneficiary lane unless the firm proves otherwise.[S10][S11][S21][S22][S28][S67][S83]

This is strongest when pricing is opaque, margins are persistently supra-normal, or user benefit looks mostly strategic. It is too blunt when competition is real and broad pass-through is actually visible.

### Option B — broad pass-through safe harbor

If price, access, or quality improvement is broad and durable enough, narrow or defeat the beneficiary-side windfall claim without forcing the case into a public-service lane.[S3][S4][S10][S11][S21][S22][S67][S83]

This is strongest in genuinely competitive, thin-margin sectors. It is too permissive when firms can gesture at user benefit while retaining substantial exceptional surplus.

### Option C — split-case retained-surplus design

Treat customer diffusion and retained concentration as separable: recognize real broad pass-through, but still tax the exceptional residual that remains concentrated.[S10][S11][S21][S22][S28][S67][S83]

This handles mixed reality better, but it still needs a practical ladder for deciding when the split is large enough to matter.

### Option D — layered diffusion ladder

Use a layered answer:

1. **screen for real breadth and durability** rather than slogans,
2. **separate price, access, and quality channels** instead of assuming only sticker-price cuts count,
3. **compare user diffusion against retained margin** rather than treating either fact as exclusive,
4. **send regulated or formula-pass-through sectors to the service-benefit lane**, and
5. **keep worker, local, and controller claims alive** where they remain real.[S3][S4][S10][S11][S18][S21][S22][S28][S29][S67][S69][S83]

This is the archive's cleanest default because it preserves real customer reciprocity without letting a little diffusion launder large retained gain.

## Provisional recommendation

Adopt **Option D** as the default calibration ladder once the archive has already decided the case belongs near the customer-benefit / competitive-pass-through lane rather than plainly on an ordinary public-service, worker-benefit, or saver-integration lane.

That means:

1. **require a visible baseline** for price, access, quality, or wait-time claims rather than accepting marketing assertions,[S3][S4][S10][S11][S21][S22][S67][S83]
2. **treat broad and durable user diffusion as morally real** even when it appears through access, reliability, or quality rather than only a sticker-price cut,[S3][S4][S10][S11][S21][S22][S67][S83]
3. **measure retained surplus after diffusion** so mixed cases pay only on the exceptional residual rather than on gross activity,[S10][S11][S21][S22][S28][S67][S83]
4. **reroute formula-pass-through or member-rate cases** to the public / mutual / regulated-service lane instead of double-classifying them as ordinary private windfalls,[S18][S21][S22][S29][S69]
5. **keep other claimant lanes visible** where customer benefit coexists with worker displacement, local burden, monopoly rent, or controller-side responsibility.[S3][S4][S10][S11][S18][S21][S22][S28][S67][S69][S83]

## Default customer-diffusion ladder

| Lane | When it should apply | Default answer | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. ordinary uncertain-benefit lane | the gain is modest, evidence is weak, or user benefit is too diffuse to verify | keep ordinary taxation and the current beneficiary-side screen; do not add a ceremonial customer-benefit adjustment | do not litigate tiny or speculative user gains as if they settled windfall justice |
| 1. promo / lock-in lane | user benefit appears mainly as temporary discounts, free tiers, growth subsidies, or below-cost expansion | no customer-diffusion concession; keep the beneficiary-side lane live | customer acquisition theater is not durable social diffusion.[S10][S11][S21][S22][S28][S67][S83] |
| 2. broad price-pass-through lane | prices, fees, or effective user cost fall broadly enough that residual margins are ordinary | narrow or defeat the beneficiary-side windfall claim and leave the case mostly on ordinary tax plus incidence review | do not pretend a competitive market still has a concentrated windfall just because AI is present.[S3][S4][S10][S11][S21][S22][S67][S83] |
| 3. broad access / quality lane | the main user gain appears through access, reliability, wait-time reduction, or quality improvement and is broad, durable, and not strongly exclusionary | treat the diffusion as morally real, but still check retained margins and protected burdens | quality counts only when it is broad enough to function like real user benefit rather than premium segmentation or feature theater.[S3][S4][S10][S11][S21][S22][S67][S83] |
| 4. mixed retained-surplus lane | some gain diffuses to users but meaningful exceptional surplus remains concentrated | tax only the retained exceptional residual through a narrower beneficiary-side charge | do not force an all-or-nothing label when both diffusion and concentration are materially real.[S10][S11][S21][S22][S28][S67][S83] |
| 5. regulated / formula-pass-through lane | rates, fees, or charges are already governed by member, rate, or service-benefit rules | route first through service / member-relief / anti-supplantation logic | do not duplicate a public-service lane as if it were just market competition.[S18][S21][S22][S29][S69] |
| 6. extraction override | user benefit is real at the margin but persistent monopoly power, lock-in, or other retained rent still dominates | keep or restore the beneficiary-side lane for the retained concentrated surplus and keep monopoly or local-burden review alive | modest user benefit does not launder exceptional retained surplus.[S10][S11][S21][S22][S28][S67][S83] |

## Four calibration rules that matter most

### 1. Durability beats announcement

A durable price cut, reliable access expansion, or stable quality gain matters more than launch-period subsidy, couponing, or short-run freemium growth. The archive wants visible diffusion, not marketing cycles.[S10][S11][S21][S22][S28][S67][S83]

### 2. Quality and access count, but only when they are broad enough

The archive does not require every benefit to appear as a posted price cut. Faster service, shorter waits, wider availability, or higher quality can count as real user diffusion, but only when those gains are broad and durable rather than narrowly premium, exclusionary, or merely cosmetic.[S3][S4][S10][S11][S21][S22][S67][S83]

### 3. Mixed cases should tax the residual, not the whole activity

Where customer diffusion is real but incomplete, the clean answer is to leave the broad part to ordinary taxation and incidence review while taxing only the retained exceptional residual. This preserves reciprocity without abandoning windfall discipline.[S10][S11][S21][S22][S28][S67][S83]

### 4. Customer benefit does not erase other direct claims

Even strong user benefit does not wipe out worker-transition duties, local repair, controller-side responsibility, or monopoly-rent scrutiny. Customer diffusion changes one lane; it does not abolish the rest of the archive.[S3][S4][S10][S11][S18][S21][S22][S28][S67][S69][S83]
