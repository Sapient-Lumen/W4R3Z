# Land, housing, vacancy, speculation, and assessment ladder

## Question in one sentence

What ladder should distinguish good land/location-rent taxation from tenant pass-through, forced-sale risk, stale assessments, transaction-lock-in, and speculative-hoarding loopholes?[S23][S473][S474][S475]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — outdated recurrent property tax | low rates on stale values. | Reject or repair: hides inequity and capture. |
| B — transaction tax | charge purchase/sale. | Use sparingly; often locks in households and depresses mobility. |
| C — current-value recurrent tax | updated property/land value with relief. | Accept if assessment and floor protections are real. |
| D — land value or split-rate tax | higher charge on land than improvements. | Preferred when administrable and politically transparent. |
| E — vacancy/hoarding charge | escalating charge on vacant, underused, or derelict property. | Adopt where shortage and speculation are shown. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **shelter gate** — identify whether the burdened party is tenant, modest homeowner, asset-rich/income-poor owner, landlord, developer, investor, opaque owner, or institutional owner.
2. **land/improvement gate** — separate land/location value from buildings, maintenance, accessibility improvements, energy upgrades, and ordinary homeowner effort where administrable.[S23]
3. **assessment-integrity gate** — test valuation currency, appeal access, ratio studies, regressivity, neighborhood bias, and political exemption patterns.[S473]
4. **liquidity gate** — provide deferral, circuit breaker, hardship installment, or lien-limited collection for asset-rich/income-poor households.
5. **tenant-incidence gate** — check rent regulation, market tightness, lease terms, pass-through clauses, shortage, and tenant bargaining power before assuming landowner incidence.
6. **mobility gate** — compare transaction taxes with recurrent charges; reject instruments that trap households while undercharging holding rents.[S23]
7. **vacancy/hoarding gate** — identify deliberate vacancy, land banking, dereliction, speculation, STR conversion, or withholding from use.
8. **transparency gate** — require beneficial-owner, foreign-owner, trust/entity, and cross-border real-estate information when opacity blocks claim allocation.[S475]
9. **proceeds gate** — route visible proceeds to tenant relief, housing supply, infrastructure, local repair, or equalization rather than generic capture.
10. **review gate** — recalibrate after reassessment cycles, rent spikes, displacement, appeals data, and affordability metrics.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| public-created location gain | land value/split-rate tax or betterment charge | disclose net fiscal stack and local repair. |
| scarce housing plus vacancy | vacancy or dereliction charge | protect genuine renovation, probate, disaster, and hardship cases. |
| modest illiquid homeowner | deferral/circuit breaker | no forced-sale tax design. |
| opaque/cross-border property | reporting, withholding, beneficial ownership | do not treat foreignness alone as guilt. |
| stale assessment base | reassessment plus appeals | avoid assessment shock without phase-in. |
