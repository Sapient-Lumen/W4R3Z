# Controller-map confidence, unknowns, and bounded-imputation ladder

## Question in one sentence

Once the archive already knows **how to rank controllers**, **what a controller map must contain**, **who may sign it**, **how it is reviewed**, and **how provisional filing works**, **what is the smallest workable rule for marking which control facts are known, inferred, unknown, or temporarily imputed without either freezing administration until perfect certainty or laundering guesswork into false confidence?**[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83][S89][S91][S92]

Route: controller maps should separate **observed facts**, **supported inferences**, **live unknowns**, and **temporary imputations**. Unknowns should stay visible, and imputation should stay as narrow and reversible as the filing or protective decision allows.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83][S89][S91][S92]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — certainty-only lane | do not file, rank, or allocate until every decisive control fact is fully proven.[S16][S17][S20][S27][S39][S41] | Reject: lets opacity and delay become a tax shelter. |
| B — guess-and-file lane | allow signers or reviewers to collapse inference, uncertainty, and assumption into one confident packet.[S20][S21][S27][S39][S41][S89] | Reject: hides what is actually known. |
| C — confidence / unknown / bounded-imputation lane | require packets to label direct evidence, supported inference, unresolved unknowns, and any temporary imputation; keep imputation narrow, rebuttable, and tied to the smallest administrative need.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83][S89][S91][S92] | Adopt. |
| D — hard adverse-inference lane | whenever facts are incomplete, impute against the filer at the most revenue-maximizing or sanction-maximizing setting.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83] | Reject: too blunt for ordinary uncertainty; reserve harder inferences for strategic opacity and integrity failure. |

## Five-rung ladder

1. **fact-state lane** — each decisive controller-map field should be marked as directly evidenced, responsibly inferred, unknown, or temporarily imputed; do not let prose blur those states together.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S41][S89]
2. **confidence lane** — use simple confidence bands only for inferred or imputed facts, and tie the band to stated support rather than to the signer's tone or convenience.[S16][S17][S20][S27][S39][S40][S41][S89][S91][S92]
3. **unknowns-stay-visible lane** — if a decisive fact is genuinely unresolved, name the gap, identify who is best placed to cure it, and keep filing or review moving on the facts that are already settled.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41]
4. **bounded-imputation lane** — impute only when a filing, escrow, safety, or grouping decision cannot wait, and then choose the narrowest rebuttable assumption that preserves collection or protection without pretending the fact is settled.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83]
5. **hardening lane** — move from bounded imputation toward stronger presumptions only when the uncertainty is strategic, repeated, or tied to stale, false, selectively incomplete, or spoliated packets.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83]

## Provisional recommendation

Adopt **Option C — confidence / unknown / bounded-imputation lane** as the archive's default uncertainty rule for controller maps.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83][S89][S91][S92]

Presumption:

- every decisive map field should disclose whether it is **observed**, **inferred**, **unknown**, or **temporarily imputed**,
- confidence labels belong to inferences and imputations, not to settled facts,
- genuine unknowns should remain explicit rather than being quietly converted into certainty,
- imputation should be the **narrowest reversible bridge** needed to keep filing, escrow, or protective action moving,
- and only strategic opacity or integrity failure should harden ordinary uncertainty into stronger adverse presumptions.

This is the archive's narrowest workable setting because it keeps packets honest about uncertainty while still allowing administration to proceed when delay itself would create arbitrage.

## Default handling matrix

| Live posture | Default handling | Why it usually fits | Archive warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| decisive field is backed by direct documents, logs, or reachable attestation | mark as observed | settled facts should not be dressed up as opinion.[S20][S21][S39][S41][S89] | do not add fake confidence bands to facts that are already directly shown. |
| governance fact is not directly logged but is strongly supported by surrounding control evidence | mark as inferred with support note | responsible inference can be more honest than forced false precision.[S16][S17][S20][S27][S39][S40][S41] | say what supports the inference. |
| decisive field is unresolved but not strategically hidden | mark as unknown and keep the cure path visible | uncertainty itself is sometimes the truest report.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41] | do not halt the whole packet if other fields are settled. |
| filing or protective action cannot wait for missing facts | use bounded temporary imputation | some decisions need a bridge rather than a pause.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83] | imputation is not final truth and should be easy to displace with better facts. |
| missing facts follow opacity, stale packets, failed updates, or post-notice deletion | escalate to stronger presumptions | repeated non-cure changes the moral posture of uncertainty.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83] | reserve harder treatment for strategic failure, not ordinary incompleteness. |
