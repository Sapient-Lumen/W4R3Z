## Option scan

| Option | Use | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| public access condition | grant-funded outputs | no needless embargo. |
| nonexclusive license | broad use feasible | preserve follow-on research. |

| march-in/licensing review | serious access or nonuse failure | evidence and due process. |
| publication-cost rule | grant-paid dissemination | cap rent in open access. |

## Priority gates

1. **funding-trace gate** — identify grant, facility, data, procurement, or public-research support.
2. **publication/data gate** — require public access and metadata while preserving real privacy/security limits.
3. **patent/license gate** — verify disclosure, title, government rights, utilization plan, and necessity of exclusivity.
4. **price/access gate** — test affordability, reasonable availability, public-program burden, disability access, and clinical need.
5. **cost gate** — stop publication, platform, or license fees from consuming the grant's public purpose.
6. **competition gate** — preserve follow-on research, small-firm access, and noncommercial use where possible.
7. **remedy gate** — use reporting, license terms, government-use, reasonable-pricing, or march-in review before blunt tax.
8. **review gate** — reopen on access failure, unreasonable price signal, publication breach, unused patent, or public-program double payment.

Cube axes: anti-pattern `taxpayer_funded_paywall`, `double_payment`; burden mechanic `taxpayer_funded_paywall_or_exclusive_license`, `double_payment_paywall_burden`. Keep exceptions in the ladder; do not rebuild taxonomy here.
