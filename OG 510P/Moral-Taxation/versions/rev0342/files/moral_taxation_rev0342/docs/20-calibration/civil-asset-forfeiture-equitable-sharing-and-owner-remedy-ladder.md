# Civil asset forfeiture, equitable sharing, and owner-remedy ladder

## Question in one sentence

What ladder should decide when seizure and forfeiture are valid public remedies and when they become suspicion taxes, self-funding policing, or owner-hostage traps?[S519][S520][S521][S522]

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **property gate** — identify cash, vehicle, home, account, real estate, weapon, contraband, or business asset.
2. **connection gate** — distinguish proceeds, instrumentality, substitute asset, and unrelated property.
3. **charge/conviction gate** — require stronger process where no person has been convicted.
4. **owner gate** — identify innocent, joint, lienholder, family, or third-party owners.[S520]
5. **value/cost gate** — compare property value with claim cost and counsel access.
6. **proportionality gate** — test excessive relation to offense and owner culpability.
7. **proceeds-conflict gate** — remove seizing-agency budget upside.[S519][S521]
8. **federalism gate** — block equitable-sharing end runs around state protections.
9. **return gate** — set prompt return, interest, storage-cost, and damage rules.
10. **review gate** — publish seizure, claim, default, return, proceeds, and demographic data.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| crime proceeds after conviction | forfeiture | victim/public restoration. |
| innocent owner | return | no owner-burden trap. |
| small cash seizure | simple contest | no cost-barrier default. |
| agency keeps proceeds | neutral fund | remove self-interest. |
| state-law bypass | anti-end-run rule | respect stronger protections. |

## Failure-mode capsule

Axes: `equitable_sharing_end_run`, `property_owner_hostage`, `self_funding_policing`, `suspicion_tax`. Watch for seizure/default/proceeds incentives that turn property into a self-funding enforcement base.

Axes: `property_or_proceeds_contest_failure`, `proceeds_or_surplus_trace_failure`, `remedy_or_contest_failure`. Reopen when claim cost, default, custody, or equitable-sharing facts defeat owner remedy.
