# Controller-map contest window, counter-map, and finality ladder

## Question in one sentence

Once the archive already knows **who owes a controller map**, **what it must contain**, **who may see it**, **how integrity, preservation, and verification work**, and **how provisional filing continues while disputes remain open**, **what is the smallest workable ladder for deciding who may contest the map, how a short counter-map should be made, when the interim answer hardens into an operative final answer, and when later reopening is still justified without letting controller disputes drag on forever?**[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S83]

Route: keep controller-map contests **short, reviewable, and materially grounded**. The archive should not force weaker parties to litigate forever, but it also should not let a first packet become unchallengeable merely because it was filed early.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S83]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — open-ended contest lane | allow controller maps to stay indefinitely provisional, with no real closure point.[S21][S27][S39][S41] | Reject: rewards drip-disclosure, delay, and perpetual uncertainty. |
| B — first-packet finality lane | let the first signed map harden quickly unless an outsider proves fraud from scratch.[S20][S21][S27][S41] | Reject: gives timing and information asymmetry too much weight. |
| C — bounded contest, short counter-map, material-change reopening | provide a short contest window, allow a compact counter-map or pointed challenge, harden the result after proportionate review, and reopen only for material new facts, correction-triggering error, or successor / step-in changes.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S83] | Adopt. |
| D — full-adjudication-before-finality lane | refuse any operative closure until every controller dispute is finally litigated.[S17][S21][S27][S39][S41] | Reject: collapses provisional filing into endless interim administration. |

## Five-rung ladder

1. **notice-and-contest lane** — once a controller map is visible to the right audience tier, open a short, clear contest window that names the filing period, the issues fit for challenge, and the channel for a pointed objection or counter-map.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S40][S41]
2. **pointed-objection lane** — ordinary challengers should be able to identify the disputed governance claim, missing actor, stale event, or sham redaction without rebuilding the whole packet themselves.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S41]
3. **short counter-map lane** — when the challenger is well placed to know the facts, allow a bounded counter-map naming the rival controller story, decisive governance levers, and the material events that would change interim filing or grouping.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S41][S83]
4. **operative-finality lane** — after the contest window and the proportionate verification ladder run, harden the result into the operative controller answer for that period so filing, escrow release, crediting, and enforcement do not remain perpetually provisional.[S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S41][S83]
5. **material-change reopening lane** — reopen only for genuinely new governance facts, corrected identity or chain errors, successor continuity events, step-in command, integrity-triggering falsity, or comparable changes that could realistically alter controller ranking or allocation.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83]

## Provisional recommendation

Adopt **Option C — bounded contest, short counter-map, material-change reopening** as the archive's default finality rule for controller-map disputes.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S83]

Presumption:

- every controller map that can affect filing, escrow, grouping, or sanctions should face a **clear but short contest window**,
- ordinary challengers should usually be able to file a **pointed objection** before they are forced into a full rival packet,
- better-informed challengers may submit a **short counter-map** rather than sprawling discovery demands,
- once the archive has run the ranking, proof, visibility, integrity, preservation, and verification ladders, the result should become **operative and period-bounded** rather than endlessly provisional,
- and later reopening should require a **material change or material error**, not mere dissatisfaction with the first resolved outcome.

This is the archive's narrowest workable setting because it preserves contestability without letting controller disputes metastasize into permanent uncertainty or making early paperwork dispositive by default.

## Default finality matrix

| Live posture | Default action | Why it usually fits | Archive warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| routine filing, current map, no named contradiction | short contest window only | most periods need closure more than repeated rehearing.[S17][S20][S21][S27][S41] | do not reopen just because the map names several actors. |
| named dispute over omitted actor, stale period fact, or sham redaction | pointed objection plus targeted review | the issue is bounded and should stay bounded.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41] | do not force a weak challenger to submit a whole rival packet first. |
| rival well-informed actor contests mission control, successor continuity, or co-controller split | short counter-map | the challenger can name a concrete alternative governance story.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S39][S41][S83] | keep counter-maps compact and issue-specific. |
| contest window closed and verification complete | operative finality for the period | filing, escrow, and enforcement need a settled working answer.[S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S41] | finality is period-bounded, not eternal immunity. |
| later material change, new event trail, or integrity failure | reopening and true-up | closure should yield to facts that could really change controller ranking or shares.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83] | do not use reopening as a disguised second appeal on unchanged facts. |
