## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — no shelter disclosure | wait for ordinary audit to find marketed avoidance one taxpayer at a time. | Reject: replicated products and adviser networks need visibility and upstream intervention.[S383][S389][S390] |
| B — disclosure equals confession | treat Form 8886 / 8918 or a list entry as proof of abuse. | Reject: this punishes transparency and drives risk back into concealment.[S384][S386][S393] |
| C — category-specific visibility with role separation | disclose reportable structures, identify material advisers and advisees, then prove merits, penalties, and injunctions separately. | Adopt. |
| D — promoter dragnet | use shelter labels to chill ordinary advice, broad opinions, return preparation, and defensive correction. | Reject: ordinary advice and privilege-sensitive correction are anti-abuse infrastructure.[S350][S394][S395] |

## Nine-gate ladder

1. **transaction-category gate** — identify listed transaction, transaction of interest, loss transaction, confidential transaction, contractual protection, tax-exempt prohibited shelter, or another reportable category; state the public anchor and effective period.[S383][S388][S393]
2. **substantial-similarity gate** — map factual elements and tax consequences to the anchor; do not rely on labels, marketing names, or superficial resemblance alone.[S393]
3. **participant-disclosure gate** — decide whether the taxpayer or tax-exempt entity had a Form 8886 / 8886-T duty for the year, return, and transaction; preserve disclosure as transparency, not admission.[S384][S385][S393]
4. **material-advisor gate** — test material aid, tax statement, role, fee threshold, timing, and exceptions before treating an adviser as a material adviser.[S386][S387][S394][S395]
5. **reportable-transaction-number gate** — ensure any number is furnished to relevant taxpayers and advisers, while recording that receipt does not mean IRS approval or transaction completeness.[S386][S387][S388]
6. **advisee-list gate** — verify section 6112 list duty, written request, response deadline, list components, privacy scope, good-cause extension, and reasonable-cause exception.[S396][S397][S400]
7. **penalty-posture gate** — separate 6707, 6707A, 6708, 6662A, preparer/promoter, fraud, and ordinary accuracy penalties; record separate predicates, amounts, approvals, defenses, and appeal routes.[S389][S398][S399][S400][S401][S402][S403]
8. **promoter / injunction gate** — escalate to lead development, promoter/material-advisor investigation, summons, or section 7408 injunction only for recurring specified conduct or material network risk.[S389][S390][S404][S405]
9. **reliance / cure gate** — if the transaction is later identified or guidance changes, test disclosure windows, penalty waivers, rescission, reasonable cause, and good-faith reliance before penalties stick.[S398][S402][S406]

## Default routing table

| Signal | Start here | Escalate only if | Stop / narrow when |
|---|---|---|---|
| taxpayer voluntarily files Form 8886 | participant-disclosure gate | facts show tax-avoidance category and underpayment risk.[S384][S393] | disclosure itself is the only adverse fact. |
| tax-exempt entity appears in structure | tax-exempt disclosure and public-benefit screen | the entity controlled or materially benefited from prohibited shelter use.[S385] | entity was an incidental public-benefit participant or victim of adviser design. |
| adviser gives implementation memo | material-advisor gate | memo is a tax statement tied to material aid and thresholded compensation.[S394][S395] | memo is defensive, corrective, generic, or ordinary-return advice. |
| reportable-transaction number appears | RTN gate | number connects actual participant and transaction records.[S386][S388] | number is treated as IRS approval or admission. |
| IRS seeks advisee list | advisee-list gate | written transaction-specific request is issued and scope matches section 6112.[S396][S397][S400] | request becomes a profession-wide client list demand. |
| penalty proposed | penalty-posture gate | predicate, amount, approval, and defense are separately recorded.[S389][S398][S399][S400][S401][S402][S403] | one ambiguity supports multiple penalties without a ledger. |
| ongoing marketed scheme | promoter / injunction gate | recurrence, specified conduct, and upstream role are documented.[S390][S404][S405] | ordinary disagreement or one-off advice is being chilled. |
| later-listed or later-interest transaction | reliance / cure gate | public guidance sets clear deadline and participant had realistic notice.[S388][S406] | penalties harvest a timing mismatch or reasonable reliance. |
| privilege claim | privilege screen | tax-shelter exception is issue-specific and necessary to the predicate.[S350][S393][S395] | privilege is treated as globally waived. |

## Same-facts penalty ledger

Before stacking or preserving parallel penalties, record:

- the taxpayer, adviser, promoter, entity, period, return, and transaction slice;
- the public reportable or listed anchor and effective date;
- the disclosure obligation, filing deadline, and whether disclosure was made;
- who had a participant duty, tax-exempt duty, material-advisor duty, or list duty;
- whether any reportable-transaction number was furnished and how it was used;
- the exact facts supporting each penalty or injunction predicate;
- the separate defense lane: reasonable cause, good faith, rescission, waiver, reliance, good-cause extension, appeal, abatement, or Tax Court route;
- and the facts that should be excluded because they merely show transparency, association, ordinary advice, or adviser failure by another actor.[S389][S398][S399][S400][S401][S402][S403]
