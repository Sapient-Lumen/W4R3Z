## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — extraction royalty | public charge on mineral rent. | Strong when valuation and reclamation are credible. |
| B — processing credit | support for refining or midstream capacity. | Use only with anti-bottleneck and public-upside conditions. |
| C — strategic stockpile | public purchase or reserve inventory. | Use for resilience, not price rescue. |
| D — recycling credit | support for recovery, reuse, and secondary supply. | Prefer where additionality and safety are real. |
| E — moratorium / no-go zone | disallow extraction in high-uncertainty or sacred/ecological areas. | Required when harm is non-compensable. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **criticality gate** — show supply risk, economic importance, and lack of cleaner substitutes.
2. **rent gate** — separate mineral scarcity rent from cost recovery and normal return.
3. **community gate** — require benefit agreements, water protection, labor safety, and local remedy.
4. **Indigenous gate** — honor consent, consultation, treaty, and sacred-site claims.
5. **environment gate** — bond reclamation, tailings, biodiversity, and long-run monitoring.
6. **processing gate** — test whether credits create monopoly or single-buyer power.
7. **stockpile gate** — publish purpose, trigger, rotation, disposal, and price discipline.
8. **recycling gate** — favor secondary supply where it reduces primary burden.
9. **security gate** — guard classified or emergency exceptions from becoming permanent opacity.
10. **review gate** — reopen on supply shock, export control, reclamation failure, or community burden concentration.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| supply shock | stockpile release or targeted purchase | no producer bailout. |
| processing gap | conditioned credit | open access and anti-monopoly terms. |
| local burden | royalty share and bond | repair before general revenue. |
| frontier uncertainty | precautionary pause | no ordinary fiscal monetization of irreversible harm. |

## Failure-mode capsule

Axes: `sacrifice_zone`, `processing_monopoly`, `stockpile_price_support`, `opacity_or_erasure`. Transition-mineral laundering: security/climate claims justify extraction or monopoly while communities carry tailings, water, labor, or sacred-site harm.


## Recalibration trigger capsule

Triggers: `controller_or_accountability_drift`, `harm_or_resilience_threshold_breach`, `protected_floor_or_incidence_shift`. Reopen on reclamation failure, monopoly pricing, stockpile opacity, recycling failure, or concentrated burden.
