## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — governmental bond | public issuer, public project. | Strong if debt and service burden are transparent. |
| B — qualified private activity bond | private user with statutory public-benefit channel. | Needs strict public-purpose and private-use review. |
| C — taxable bond | no federal interest exclusion. | Prefer when public benefit is weak or private capture high. |
| D — direct subsidy or credit | explicit federal support. | Often more transparent than hidden investor preference. |
| E — public bank/fund finance | public balance-sheet lending. | Use with governance and risk disclosure. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **purpose gate** — identify essential infrastructure, housing, health, education, transport, utility, or other public benefit.
2. **tax-expenditure gate** — disclose federal revenue cost and investor-side benefit.
3. **issuer gate** — identify issuer, conduit borrower, guarantor, fees, and private control.
4. **private-use gate** — test private business use, payment, security, and management contracts.
5. **arbitrage gate** — track proceeds, yield restriction, rebate, and spend timing.
6. **debt gate** — show maturity, callable risk, refunding, reserves, and debt-service path.
7. **incidence gate** — test local taxpayer, ratepayer, user-fee, and service-access burdens.
8. **disclosure gate** — require official statement and continuing disclosure usability.
9. **post-issuance gate** — name compliance officer, records, training, and remediation path.
10. **review gate** — reopen on private-use drift, arbitrage, disclosure failure, or burden shift.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| core public infrastructure | tax-exempt governmental bond | debt and access transparency. |
| mixed public/private project | qualified private activity bond | strict private-use and benefit tests. |
| weak public benefit | taxable finance | no hidden subsidy. |
| federal policy goal | direct subsidy/credit | compare to exemption distribution. |
