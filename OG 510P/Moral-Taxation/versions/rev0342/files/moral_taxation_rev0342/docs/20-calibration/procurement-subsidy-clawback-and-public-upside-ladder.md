# Procurement, subsidy, clawback, and public-upside ladder

## Question in one sentence

When does procurement, credit, grant, guarantee, or abatement buy public capability, and when is it private rent?[S512][S513][S514]

## Priority gates

1. **public-purpose gate** — name the capacity, resilience, security, affordability, decarbonization, access, job-quality, or local-repair objective.
2. **instrument gate** — compare procurement, direct provision, credit, grant, loan, guarantee, abatement, preference, equity, royalty, or regulation together.
3. **net-stack gate** — count every public contribution: tax, cash, land, utility discount, infrastructure, guarantee, procurement, and depreciation advantage.
4. **competition gate** — test bidder count, lock-in, interoperability, switching cost, small-business access, and concentration.[S512]
5. **performance gate** — set measurable milestones and automatic recovery for missed jobs, access, price, domestic/local capability, resilience, emissions, or service obligations.[S513]
6. **public-upside gate** — use warrants, royalties, profit share, price caps, open licenses, or public equity when public risk creates private asset value.
7. **incidence/equalization gate** — check whether shareholders, executives, landlords, input suppliers, contractors, or rich jurisdictions capture the support.
8. **review gate** — reopen after single-bid awards, missed milestones, cost overruns, market-share shifts, subsidy races, or supplier windfalls.[S514]
