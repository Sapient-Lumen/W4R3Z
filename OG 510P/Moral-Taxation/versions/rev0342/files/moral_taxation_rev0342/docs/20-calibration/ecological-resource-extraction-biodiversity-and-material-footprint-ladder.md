# Ecological resource extraction, biodiversity, and material-footprint ladder

## Question in one sentence

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — ordinary business tax only | tax profits from extraction as usual. | Insufficient where depletion and external harms are unpaid. |
| B — emissions or resource tax | charge measurable externality or extraction unit. | Adopt for measurable, compensable harm with incidence repair. |
| C — royalty/severance plus bond | price public resource and require cleanup security. | Default for extractive rents and reclamation risk. |
| D — cap/prohibition | disallow non-compensable or irreversible harm. | Required when tax would sell permission to destroy. |
| E — offset/credit market | allow substitute repair. | Use only with additionality, permanence, no double counting, and local consent. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **commons gate** — identify affected ecological commons, species, watershed, atmosphere, soil, forest, fishery, frontline community, and future generation.
2. **base gate** — choose emissions, extraction volume, toxicity, water withdrawal/consumption, land disturbance, waste, habitat conversion, biodiversity pressure, or material footprint.[S477][S478]
3. **irreversibility gate** — if harm is catastrophic, non-compensable, or irreversible, use prohibition, cap, or permit denial before price.[S103]
4. **rent gate** — separate resource rent from cleanup cost, ordinary return, labor, capital recovery, and public subsidy.
5. **bond/security gate** — require reclamation bond, insurance, reserve, or escrow where cleanup or long-tail harm may exceed solvency.
6. **frontline-incidence gate** — test whether the burdened community receives repair or is merely hosting extraction for outside benefit.
7. **transition gate** — allow transition-critical materials only with stronger lifecycle proof, recycling, repair, and no sacrifice-zone shortcut.
8. **offset-integrity gate** — test additionality, permanence, leakage, double counting, indigenous/local consent, and monitoring before credit use.
9. **proceeds gate** — route funds to local repair, biodiversity restoration, transition support, health monitoring, and public capacity.
10. **review gate** — recalibrate after new science, threshold breach, cumulative-impact data, offset failure, or community harm evidence.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| measurable carbon harm | carbon tax/ETS with floor repair | protect low-income energy users and trade leakage. |
| public resource extraction | severance/royalty plus bond | local repair and intergenerational fund. |
| biodiversity-critical habitat | cap, ban, no-go zone, or strict mitigation hierarchy | no fee-only permission. |
| uncertain cumulative harm | precautionary charge plus monitoring | revisit with new evidence. |
| weak offsets | reject or discount heavily | no greenwashing settlement. |

## Failure-mode capsule

Axes: `harm_pricing_or_offset_theater`, `sacrifice_zone`, `opacity_or_erasure`. Permission-to-degrade: royalties or offsets monetize extraction while habitat, reclamation, or material footprint remain unfunded.


## Recalibration trigger capsule

Triggers: `harm_or_resilience_threshold_breach`, `record_or_measurement_staleness`. Reopen on threshold breach, offset failure, reclamation gap, or new lifecycle evidence.
