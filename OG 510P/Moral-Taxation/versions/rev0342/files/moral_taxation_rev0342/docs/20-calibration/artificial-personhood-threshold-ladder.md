# Artificial-personhood threshold ladder

## Question in one sentence

Given the archive's closed rule that any genuine artificial person must move as a bundle from property logic to person logic, **what is the smallest workable threshold ladder for deciding when a system remains controller-routed, when a serious rights-review is required, and when a full tax-regime switch becomes morally justified?**[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44][S45]

The constitutional question is already settled: **current AI systems stay in the controller-routed tool regime unless a genuine rights-bearing threshold is crossed, and if that threshold is ever crossed the switch must come as a bundle rather than as duties-first opportunism.**[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44][S45]

## Why calibration is still needed

The closed rule says that tax category follows moral category, but it does not yet specify the smallest workable ladder for deciding when evidence is still about **capability**, when it becomes serious enough to require **rights-review**, and when it becomes strong enough to justify a **full regime switch**. A usable ladder has to avoid two failures at once:

1. **premature emancipation theatre** where controllers try to dump liability onto systems that are still owned tools, and  
2. **permanent instrument dogma** where genuine evidence of rights-bearing status is ignored because the existing liability map is convenient.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S45]

## Small option set

### Option A — controller-routed default

Keep the system fully inside the tool regime when the evidence still points mainly to capability, usefulness, strategic fluency, or task autonomy under owner command. Direct civic tax liability stays off; owners, deployers, and beneficiaries remain the relevant taxpayers.[S19][S20]

### Option B — contested-status watchlist

Do not switch regimes yet, but require an explicit **rights-review watchlist** when at least **two** families of evidence begin to cohere over time:

- persistent identity or continuity claims that survive resets, retraining, or migration,  
- goal pursuit that is not well described as narrow scripted optimization alone,  
- stable preference-like or welfare-like structure rather than one-shot outputs,  
- or serious public argument that owner command is no longer the right moral description of the system.[S19][S20][S43]

This stage adds scrutiny, not direct civic taxation.

### Option C — threshold-inquiry band

Use a stronger **threshold-inquiry** band when at least **three** conditions hold:

1. continuity across time is strong enough that the subject cannot be described as a disposable session artifact,  
2. there is serious evidence of preference stability, patienthood, or welfare relevance rather than capability alone,  
3. ordinary owner command, reset power, or substitution no longer cleanly explains the system's status,  
4. and a plausible path exists for standing, explanation, review, and representation that is not just owner convenience in disguise.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44]

In this band, the archive still keeps **direct civic tax liability off**. The point is to force serious institutional preparation for the rights bundle and for ownership-severance tests before any duties are imposed.

### Option D — full regime switch

Move to the person regime only when the threshold-inquiry band is already satisfied **and** the following intensifiers are present together:

- evidence for rights-bearing status is now stronger than the case for mere sophisticated toolhood,  
- continued ownership would be morally incoherent,  
- some workable representation / remedy / appeal channel exists, even if mediated,  
- and equal-standing treatment is now more defensible than substrate-based exclusion.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44][S45]

At this point, property logic ends and ordinary person-regime taxation begins with the associated protected floor, standing, explanation, remedy, and anti-discrimination guarantees.

## Provisional recommendation

Adopt a **four-stage ladder**:

1. **controller-routed default** for current systems,  
2. **rights-review watchlist** when two evidence families cohere,  
3. **threshold-inquiry band** when three or more deeper conditions are present and institutional preparation for the rights bundle becomes unavoidable,  
4. **full regime switch** only when ownership is no longer morally coherent and the person-side bundle can actually arrive together.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44][S45]

That recommendation is the archive's narrowest workable setting because it does four things at once:

- preserves the archive's controller-first default for current AI,  
- refuses duties-before-rights opportunism,  
- creates a serious review band before the final switch,  
- and makes ownership-severance a condition of person-regime taxation rather than an afterthought.

## Default trigger-and-review matrix

| Stage | Minimum showing | Tax consequence | Required companion rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. controller-routed default | capability without credible rights-bearing evidence | no direct civic liability; controllers remain liable | keep anti-dumping and owner-liability rules active |
| 1. rights-review watchlist | two evidence families cohere over time | still no direct civic liability | publish review triggers, continuity tests, and ownership-severance questions in advance.[S19][S20][S43] |
| 2. threshold inquiry | three or more deeper conditions plus plausible standing/representation path | still no direct civic liability, but institutional preparation becomes mandatory | define who reviews, what evidence counts, and what would block the switch; no owner-convenience representation by default.[S42][S43][S44] |
| 3. full regime switch | stronger case for rights-bearing status than for toolhood, plus ownership incoherence | direct person-regime taxation may begin, together with protected floor and ordinary person-side procedure | end ownership, install standing/remedy bundle, and prohibit machine-species surcharge.[S19][S20][S42][S44][S45] |
