# Controller-map integrity, correction, safe harbor, and sanction ladder

## Question in one sentence

Once the archive already knows **who must map control, what that packet must contain, who may see it, and how interim filing proceeds**, **what is the smallest workable ladder for distinguishing clerical error from material falsity, timely self-correction from strategic opacity, and correction-first treatment from real sanctions when controller maps are stale, selectively incomplete, or knowingly misleading?**[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83]

Route: keep the archive correction-first for honest mistakes, but never let attestation, redaction, or stale maps become cheap ways to hide the governance facts that settle controller-side duties.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83]

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |

| A — narrative-only lane | treat controller maps as advisory descriptions with little consequence if they are stale, thin, or wrong.[S20][S21][S27][S41] | Reject: invites paper compliance and rewards actors who sign elegant fiction. |

## Five-rung ladder

1. **clerical-fix safe-harbor lane** — obvious naming, formatting, or cross-reference mistakes with no plausible controller consequence get prompt correction and no extra sanction if cured quickly.[S27][S39][S40][S41]
2. **timely update / self-correction lane** — when a signer discloses a missed update trigger, successor change, or newly material governance fact within the ordinary update window, require amendment, refreshed attestation, and any needed interim true-up rather than punitive escalation.[S16][S17][S27][S39][S40][S41]
3. **material-gap hardening lane** — if key map fields stay missing, stale, or selectively thinned after notice, allow stronger filing presumptions, narrower redaction tolerance, bounded holdbacks, or provisional controller-group treatment until the packet is cured.[S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S39][S41][S83]
4. **reckless-or-knowing-misstatement lane** — where attestations materially misstate mission control, continuation power, residual upside, or inside-the-loop governance, treat the map as false, apply culpability-scaled civil additions, and permit adverse inference against the signer and aligned control chain.[S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S39][S41][S83]
5. **strategic-opacity / repeat-offender lane** — repeated stale packets, sham redactions, affiliate-shell map splitting, or deliberate false-controller stories justify tougher penalties, longer lookback, stronger provisional grouping, and rare criminal referral only when the broader archive's culpability ladder would already support it.[S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S39][S41][S83]

## Provisional recommendation

Adopt **Option C — correction-first ladder with safe harbors and escalators** as the archive's default integrity rule for controller maps.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83]

Presumption:

- keep **clerical mistakes** in a cure lane,
- keep **timely self-correction** cheaper than concealment,
- harden interim filing and redaction rules when **material fields stay stale or missing** after notice,
- escalate to real sanctions only when the attested map is **recklessly or knowingly misleading** about governance,
- and reserve the heaviest penalties or referral for **strategic, repeated opacity** rather than ordinary administrative error.

This is the archive's narrowest workable setting because it preserves correction, appeal, and privacy discipline while refusing to make controller-map paperwork a one-way safe harbor for misdirection.

## Default response table

| Live defect | Default response | Why it usually fits | Archive warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| typo, mislabeled affiliate, broken link, or other non-material packet error | cure without extra sanction | substance is intact and the error is quickly fixable.[S27][S39][S40] | do not inflate clerical defects into false-controller findings. |
| missed update trigger disclosed promptly by filer | amended map plus refreshed attestation | encourages timely truth-telling after organizational change.[S16][S17][S39][S40][S41] | require any needed provisional true-up; self-correction is not immunity from the base tax. |
| key field missing or stale after notice | stronger presumptions, narrower redaction, or provisional holdback | keeps interim collection moving without pretending the packet is reliable.[S20][S21][S27][S39][S41][S83] | do not let “under review” become endless map incompleteness. |
| attested map materially false about who can continue, halt, approve, or capture upside | culpability-scaled civil penalty plus adverse inference | the packet now distorts controller assignment itself.[S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S39][S41][S83] | keep sanctions tied to material governance facts, not harmless drafting style. |
| repeated sham redactions, shell splitting, or false-controller narratives | stronger provisional grouping, longer lookback, possible referral | pattern evidence supports strategic opacity rather than mistake.[S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S39][S41][S83] | criminal referral remains rare and must track the archive's broader culpability ladder. |
