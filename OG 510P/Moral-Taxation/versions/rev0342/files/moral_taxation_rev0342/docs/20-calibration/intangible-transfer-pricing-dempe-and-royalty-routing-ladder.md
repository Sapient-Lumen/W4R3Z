# Intangible transfer-pricing, DEMPE, and royalty-routing ladder

## Question in one sentence

What ladder should decide whether intangible income, royalty deductions, cost-sharing returns, data-derived value, or hard-to-value intangible upside stays with the legal owner, moves to another group member, is split with source/market jurisdictions, or receives a simplified proxy?[S456][S457][S458]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — legal-owner rule | accept contract and title. | Reject when title diverges from control, risk, people, or exploitation. |
| B — bespoke arm's-length analysis | full transfer-pricing inquiry. | Use when records and capacity are adequate. |
| C — DEMPE/risk-control ladder | allocate by real functions, control, risk, and exploitation. | Adopt as default. |
| D — simplified distributor return | bounded Amount B-like routine return. | Adopt for eligible low-risk routine distribution, not unique intangible owners. |
| E — formulary or withholding proxy | simpler source/market claim. | Use when capacity/comparable gaps would otherwise erase a real claim. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **intangible-definition gate** — identify patent, software, model, brand, algorithm, trade secret, user data, database, customer list, platform access, or bundled know-how.
2. **legal-owner gate** — list registered owner, cost-sharing parties, licensees, funders, royalty recipients, and conduits.
3. **DEMPE gate** — map who develops, enhances, maintains, protects, and exploits the intangible, including people, decision rights, and operational control.[S456]
4. **funding-and-risk gate** — distinguish cash funding from real control over economically significant risks.
5. **market/user/source gate** — identify whether users, local markets, public infrastructure, or source-country activity materially generate the rent.
6. **royalty-strip gate** — test deductions, withholding, treaty claims, conduit entities, hybrid instruments, and related-party circularity.
7. **hard-to-value gate** — require contemporaneous assumptions and later reasonableness review when ex ante valuations are highly uncertain.[S456]
8. **simplification gate** — use routine-distributor simplification only when the entity lacks unique/valuable intangibles and significant risk.[S457]
9. **capacity gate** — protect low-capacity administrations from proof standards that only the multinational can satisfy; use UN-oriented practical tools where needed.[S458]
10. **remedy gate** — select adjustment, deduction denial, withholding, formulary split, safe harbor, documentation sanction, or prospective agreement.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| title and DEMPE align | respect ordinary allocation | preserve documentation and review. |
| title without substance | reallocation or deduction denial | do not let paper ownership carry rent. |
| routine distributor | simplified return | exclude unique intangibles and significant risks. |
| source/market contribution material | claim split or proxy | avoid source-state erasure. |
| evidence controlled by group | burden shift or adverse inference | keep correction issue-scoped and reviewable. |
