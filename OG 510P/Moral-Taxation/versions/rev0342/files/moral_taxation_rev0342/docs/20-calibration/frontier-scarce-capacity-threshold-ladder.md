# Frontier scarce-capacity threshold ladder

## Question in one sentence

Given the archive's closed rule that a special AI levy needs an exceptional controller-side trigger, **when should a frontier-training registry fee, scarce-capacity charge, bond, or reserve activate rather than leaving controller groups inside ordinary taxation and ordinary permitting alone?**[S10][S15][S16][S17][S58]

## Closed rules invoked

This memo does **not** reopen the archive's constitutional waist. It relies on:

The constitutional question is already settled: **ordinary taxation remains the default, and a scarce-capacity levy is justified only where controller groups are drawing unusually large shares of stressed industrial capacity, grid headroom, or strategic accelerators in ways ordinary taxation and ordinary permitting are not already handling well enough.**[S10][S15][S16][S17]

## Why calibration is still needed

The closed rule names **scarce-capacity appropriation** as one exceptional AI trigger, but it does not yet specify the smallest workable activation ladder. A usable ladder has to avoid two failures at once:

1. **false positives** where every visible training run or data-centre build is treated as scarcity-intensive even when the load is ordinary, flexible, or already priced through ordinary infrastructure charges, and  
2. **false negatives** where very large controller-side training events absorb scarce accelerators, electricity, cooling, queue priority, or public attention long before ordinary profit taxation or ordinary utility tariffs catch up.[S10][S15][S16][S17][S58]

## Small option set

### Option A — ordinary-tax and ordinary-permit default

Use ordinary corporate, property, labor, energy, and environmental taxation plus ordinary siting, utility, and safety permitting. Do **not** activate a dedicated scarce-capacity levy unless controller-side use is unusually large relative to stressed capacity or strategic bottlenecks.[S1][S10][S15]

### Option B — registry and disclosure watchlist

Do not levy yet, but require covered controller groups to enter a **frontier-capacity registry** when at least **one scale threshold** and **one opacity-risk condition** are met over a review window:

- unusually large accelerator reservation or accelerator-hour demand,
- unusually large electricity demand or power-reservation request,
- unusually large cooling or water requirement,
- or a training / deployment plan that would materially compress capacity for other users without clear public accounting.[S10][S15][S16][S17]

This option is appropriate where scale is visible but genuine scarcity or public burden is not yet strong enough for immediate fiscal activation.

### Option C — thresholded scarce-capacity contribution

Activate a **bounded controller-side registry fee, reserve, or scarce-capacity charge** when both of these hold:

1. the controller group crosses a named **industrial-scale threshold** keyed mainly to auditable physical inputs such as accelerator-hours, electricity demand, reserved megawatts, or comparable controller-side aggregates, and  
2. at least one **scarcity intensifier** is present: local grid stress, accelerator bottleneck concentration, queue displacement of weaker users, repeated emergency procurement, or other evidence that the capacity draw is not merely large but **scarcity-relevant**.[S10][S15][S16][S17]

The contribution should attach to a reviewable controller-side aggregate or band. It should **not** be keyed mainly to token counts, parameter counts, benchmark scores, or anthropomorphic claims about what the model "is."[S10][S16][S17]

### Option D — stronger bond / reserve band

Use a stronger band only when Option C is already satisfied **and** one or more intensifiers are present:

- the controller group occupies a bottleneck position in compute supply or cloud access,
- local infrastructure stress is recurring rather than episodic,
- the event raises precaution concerns beyond ordinary pricing,
- or repeated threshold-crossing shows that a one-off fee is too weak relative to the scale of scarce-capacity capture.[S10][S15][S16][S17]

This is not a universal compute tax. It is a review-bound response to unusually large and scarcity-relevant controller-side capacity draw.

## Provisional recommendation

Adopt a **two-key activation ladder**:

1. **baseline ordinary-tax / ordinary-permit default**,  
2. **registry and disclosure watchlist** once a controller group crosses a named industrial-scale threshold,  
3. **bounded scarce-capacity contribution** only when that scale threshold is paired with a real scarcity intensifier such as grid stress, queue displacement, or bottleneck concentration.[S10][S15][S16][S17][S58]

That recommendation is the archive's narrowest workable setting because it does four things at once:

- preserves the archive's ordinary-tax default,
- uses auditable controller-side industrial inputs rather than token mysticism,
- requires actual scarcity relevance rather than mere model size theater,
- and leaves room to intensify only when concentrated capacity capture or precaution concerns become clearer than in the baseline case.

## Default trigger-and-review matrix

| Stage | Minimum showing | Fiscal consequence | Required companion rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. ordinary default | no exceptional scale / scarcity pairing yet | no special levy | keep ordinary tax, utility, and siting review active |
| 1. watchlist | industrial-scale threshold crossed | registry, disclosure, earlier review cadence | publish covered entities, physical proxies, error bounds, and review window.[S16][S17] |
| 2. scarce-capacity band | stage 1 plus a named scarcity intensifier | bounded controller-side registry fee, reserve, or scarce-capacity charge | tie proceeds to local repair, grid or water resilience, public-interest compute, or safety capacity rather than incumbent subsidy loops.[S15][S22][S68] |
| 3. strong band / bonding | stage 2 plus recurring bottleneck control or precaution intensifier | stronger reserve, bond, or scarcity band | add explicit sunset / conversion path and stronger governance coupling rather than tax-as-permission theater.[S15][S17][S53][S54][S59][S60][S61][S62][S63] |
