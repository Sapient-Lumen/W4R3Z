## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — pure disclosure | require incident reporting only. | Incomplete unless resilience, victim repair, and cost allocation are also addressed. |
| B — resilience standard | require risk governance, supplier oversight, backup, and recovery controls. | Strong baseline for critical or data-rich actors. |
| C — breach-cost charge | allocate avoidable breach costs to negligent controllers. | Use with causation and safe harbors. |
| D — public cyber fund | finance shared threat intelligence, local capacity, and victim repair. | Use where spillovers exceed firm boundaries. |
| E — punitive penalty | large sanctions for concealment or reckless disregard. | Reserve for culpability, not ordinary incident occurrence. |

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| critical infrastructure | resilience standard plus incident report | avoid disclosure without repair capacity. |
| avoidable breach from neglected controls | breach-cost allocation | safe harbor for reasonable controls and prompt repair. |
| victim identity or account recovery | public/firm repair lane | no victim-paid recovery toll. |

## Failure-mode capsule

Axes: `checkbox_security`, `ransom_normalization`, `public_loss_private_upside`, `silent_externality`. Watch for weak controls or ransom routines shifting repair cost to victims and the public.

## Recalibration trigger capsule

Axes: `harm_or_resilience_threshold_breach`, `victim_repair_or_security_control_failure`, `privacy_or_confidentiality_breach`. Reopen when material incidents, repeat breaches, ransom, control failure, or unfunded victim repair appears.
