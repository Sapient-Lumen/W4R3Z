# Corporate tax transparency, public CbCR, and confidentiality ladder

## Question in one sentence

How should public corporate tax transparency be scaled so it improves accountability without becoming either publicity theater or confidentiality breach?[S576][S577][S578]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — private-only CbCR | tax authority receives data. | Useful for risk assessment but weak civic visibility. |
| B — public CbCR | jurisdictional tax and activity data. | Default for large public-interest groups where law applies. |
| C — tax strategy/governance disclosure | board approach, controls, stakeholder engagement. | Useful complement. |
| D — audit-trigger use | transparency as risk signal. | Allowed but not final liability. |
| E — unrestricted public file | all tax data public. | Reject; overbroad and harmful. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **threshold gate** — apply public reporting mainly to large groups with public-interest scale.
2. **jurisdiction gate** — disclose by tax jurisdiction rather than broad regions where possible.
3. **activity gate** — report revenue, profit/loss, taxes accrued/paid, employees, capital, and nature of activities.
4. **governance gate** — disclose tax strategy, risk controls, board oversight, and stakeholder engagement.
5. **reconciliation gate** — explain material gaps between profit, substance, and tax paid.
6. **confidentiality gate** — protect genuinely sensitive data through narrow deferral or aggregation, not blanket secrecy.
7. **audit gate** — treat disclosure mismatch as risk evidence, not proof of liability.
8. **small-entity gate** — avoid imposing MNE transparency burden on small firms.
9. **public-use gate** — publish in usable, comparable, machine-readable format.
10. **review gate** — test whether disclosure reduces opacity or merely creates compliance theater.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| large MNE public-interest scale | public CbCR plus governance disclosure | machine-readable. |
| private CbCR only | risk-assessment use | not civic substitute. |
| commercial sensitivity claim | narrow redaction | time-limited and explained. |
| disclosure mismatch | audit trigger | not liability by default. |
| small business | simplified/no public CbCR | avoid spillover. |
