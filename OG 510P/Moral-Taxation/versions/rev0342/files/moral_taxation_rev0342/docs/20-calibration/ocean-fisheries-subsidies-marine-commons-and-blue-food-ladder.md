# Ocean fisheries, subsidies, marine commons, and blue-food ladder

## Question in one sentence

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — open producer support | fuel, vessel, gear, or income subsidy. | Weak unless no capacity expansion and a real floor are shown. |
| B — stock-rebuilding compact | time-limited support paired with reduced effort and recovery metrics. | Strong when stock status and transition are transparent. |
| C — access rent / license fee | charges on scarce harvest rights or privileged access. | Use when rent is real and small-scale floors are protected. |
| D — monitoring and IUU levy | funds observer, traceability, port-state, and enforcement capacity. | Strong for legality and commons repair. |
| E — nutrition-side blue-food support | consumer or school-food support for healthy aquatic foods. | Use when food access, not producer income, is the problem. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **stock gate** — start from stock status, harvest pressure, and uncertainty.
2. **capacity gate** — block support that increases effort where stocks or ecosystems are stressed.
3. **legality gate** — remove support for IUU or unregulated activity before calibrating rates.
4. **livelihood gate** — separate small-scale fisher floors from vessel-owner rent.
5. **quota gate** — test whether licenses or quotas have become private scarcity assets.
6. **food gate** — test seafood affordability and nutrition floors separately from producer price.
7. **habitat gate** — count bycatch, gear damage, aquaculture feed, disease, and pollution.
8. **monitoring gate** — dedicate revenue to stock assessment, traceability, and enforcement where needed.
9. **transition gate** — fund exit, gear conversion, retraining, or community resilience without locking in overcapacity.
10. **review gate** — reopen on overfished status, IUU findings, quota concentration, or coastal-food harm.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| overfished stock | remove expansion support | stock-rebuilding and transition metrics. |
| real scarcity rent | license or quota charge | small-scale and Indigenous floor protection. |
| monitoring deficit | IUU/traceability levy | visible marine-repair use. |
| food-access failure | nutrition-side support | avoid producer-price inflation. |

## Failure-mode capsule

Axes: `blue_washing`, `overfishing_subsidy`, `opacity_or_erasure`, `access_exclusion`. Blue-food capture: subsidies, quotas, certificates, or levies preserve overcapacity while stocks and coastal nutrition floors erode.


## Recalibration trigger capsule

Triggers: `IUU_signal`, `overfished_stock`, `protected_floor_or_incidence_shift`. Reopen on overfishing, IUU findings, quota concentration, traceability gaps, or coastal-food burden.
