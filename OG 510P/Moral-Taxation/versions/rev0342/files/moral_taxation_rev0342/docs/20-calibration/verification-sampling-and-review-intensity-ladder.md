# Verification, sampling, and review-intensity ladder

## Question in one sentence

Given the archive's closed rule that **tax verification should test only the contested moral fact at the lightest intensity that can really test it**, **what is the smallest workable ladder for deciding when facial completeness is enough, when targeted cross-checks or bounded samples are justified, when stronger supervisory sign-off belongs, and when sealed or independent review may replace broader inspection without normalizing universal deep audit?**[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S83][S89]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — attestation-first with complaint trigger only | accept ordinary packets unless outsiders reconstruct a contradiction first.[S16][S17][S27][S39][S41] | Reject: dumps too much proof work onto weaker parties. |
| B — universal deep-audit lane | verify serious claims through broad records, matching, or replay by default.[S16][S17][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44] | Reject: too privacy-costly, coercive, and administration-heavy as a standing rule. |
| C — tiered review lane | start with facial completeness, escalate through targeted cross-checks and bounded samples, and reserve sealed review for high-stakes or contradiction-heavy cases.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S83][S89] | Adopt. |
| D — sealed-review first for machine-era complexity | route most material disputes straight into confidential expert or regulator-private review.[S16][S17][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44] | Reject: secrecy is too blunt and too hard to contest as a default lane. |

## Five-rung ladder

1. **facial-completeness lane** — confirm that the filing, packet, or attestation is current, signed or otherwise attributable, internally complete, period-bounded, and not obviously inconsistent with prior accepted records or already-available anchors.[S16][S17][S21][S27][S41][S89]
2. **targeted cross-check lane** — test only the named disputed fact against existing anchors such as payroll or platform reports, registries, meter reads, threshold logs, prior accepted packets, declared payment rails, or other already-held records that can answer the claim without widening the case.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S41][S83][S89]
3. **bounded sample lane** — where the truth depends on real practice rather than formal labels, sample a small number of material events or transactions that bear directly on the disputed claim.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S41][S83]
4. **supervised escalation lane** — if lighter checks expose contradiction, selective omission, sham redaction, repeated mismatch, or large downside, require harder supervisory sign-off, narrower adverse presumptions, or claim-specific additional records rather than pretending the light lane still suffices.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83][S89]
5. **sealed or independent-review lane** — use regulator-private, court-supervised, or third-party review only when the dispute is genuinely high-stakes, security-sensitive, trade-secret exposed, or persistently contradictory and no narrower lane can test it adequately.[S16][S17][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44]

## Provisional recommendation

Adopt **Option C — tiered review lane** as the archive's default calibration for general tax verification.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S83][S89]

Presumption:

- routine low-contradiction cases should usually stop at **facial completeness**,
- named fact disputes should usually move to **targeted cross-checks** rather than whole-file excavation,
- practice-sensitive disputes should use a **bounded sample** of material events or transactions,
- contradiction, strategic opacity, repeated mismatch, or large downside can justify **supervised escalation**,
- and **sealed or independent review** belongs only after narrower lanes have proven inadequate.

This is the archive's smallest workable general setting because it keeps verification real without treating every meaningful tax relationship as grounds for standing deep audit.

## Default review matrix

| Live posture | Minimum review intensity | Why it usually fits | Archive warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| routine filing, current packet, low contradiction | facial completeness | most ordinary tax relationships do not justify more than completeness and obvious-consistency review.[S16][S17][S27][S41][S89] | do not confuse neat paperwork with conclusive truth where hidden practice matters. |
| named mismatch over threshold crossing, reported amount, eligibility fact, or issue-bounded correction | targeted cross-check | specific claims should trigger specific checks using already-held anchors where possible.[S16][S17][S27][S39][S41][S89] | keep the check claim-scoped; do not reopen the whole period by habit. |
| practice-sensitive dispute over real use, burden routing, controller-like command, or recurring transaction pattern | bounded sample | a small sample of material events often tests reality better than either attestation worship or full replay.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S41][S83] | sample decisive events, not whole communications archives or constant telemetry. |
| selective omission, stale packet, contradiction, repeated mismatch, or large downside | supervised escalation | stronger review becomes justified because the lighter lanes have lost credibility.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83][S89] | escalation should still stay issue-bounded and reviewable. |
| high-stakes dispute with real secrecy or system-security concerns | sealed or independent review | some cases need private inspection without turning private inspection into the norm.[S16][S17][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44] | return the shortest contestable conclusion possible rather than a secrecy dump. |
