## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — siloed eligibility | each program protects its own budget. | Reject when households face combined cliffs. |
| B — income phaseout only | single-program taper. | Minimum but incomplete. |
| C — combined EMTR model | taxes, credits, benefits, fees, premiums. | Default for low-income schedules. |
| D — transitional protection | time-limited step-down or earnings disregard. | Use for large cliffs. |
| E — automatic coordination | integrated renewal, prefill, data minimization. | Use when state already has reliable data. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **bundle gate** — identify all taxes, credits, benefits, premiums, fees, and recertification costs.
2. **marginal-rate gate** — estimate the net effect of an extra dollar, hour, or income band.
3. **cliff gate** — flag any discontinuous loss larger than the earnings gain.
4. **household gate** — test marriage, second earner, children, disability, eldercare, and childcare costs.
5. **time gate** — check monthly, annual, and recertification-period mismatches.
6. **formalization gate** — test whether entering payroll or registration creates a net penalty.
7. **administrative gate** — count reporting burden, portal dependence, and document churn.
8. **smoothing gate** — use disregards, tapers, grace periods, and transitional protection.
9. **purpose-wall gate** — coordinate data only with minimization, correction, and confidentiality.
10. **remeasure gate** — publish combined-rate tables and update after wage, rent, health, or benefit changes.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| single credit phaseout | ordinary taper | avoid cliffs. |
| benefit stack | combined EMTR model | include fees and premiums. |
| large cliff | transition protection | step-down or disregard. |
| formalization penalty | graduation lane | do not ransom work. |
| multi-agency data | purpose-bounded coordination | correction rights. |
