# Bounded contest, issue-scoped correction, and period-finality ladder

## Question in one sentence

Given the archive's closed rule that **contest should be visible and bounded, pointed objection should usually precede full rival reconstruction, correction should stay issue-scoped where possible, and settled periods should reopen only for material new facts, material error, or integrity failure**, **what is the smallest workable ladder for deciding how short ordinary contest windows should be, when objection alone is enough, when wider reconstruction is justified, how narrow correction must stay, and what later facts can honestly break closure?**[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S38][S39][S40][S41][S42][S44][S65][S66][S83][S89][S91][S92]

## Why calibration is still needed

The closed rule answers direction, but not the smallest working operating ladder. Calibration is still needed to avoid six recurring failures:

1. **first-filer closure** where the earliest packet or easiest claimant wins because others lacked time, access, or records,[S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S41]
2. **whole-case blast radius** where one wrong field reopens every settled element,[S17][S21][S39][S40][S41][S89][S91][S92]
3. **perpetual provisionality** where review never ends because some hypothetical later objection always remains possible,[S17][S21][S27][S38][S39][S41]
4. **reconstruction by the weak** where ordinary challengers are forced to rebuild the full file just to identify one bad field,[S16][S17][S21][S27][S39][S41][S83]
5. **reopening by regret** where unchanged facts are relitigated because the outcome is unwelcome,[S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S41]
6. and **retention by non-finality** where systems keep everything forever because they refuse to choose a closure rule.[S17][S27][S39][S40][S41][S89][S91][S92]

## Small option set

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — long-window universal reopenability | keep periods loosely open, allow broad objections late, and tolerate whole-case reset whenever a new angle appears.[S17][S21][S27][S38][S39][S41] | Reject: closure and retention discipline collapse together. |
| B — first-packet hardening | the earliest accepted return, statement, map, or assessment hardens quickly unless a full rival reconstruction arrives immediately.[S17][S20][S21][S39][S41][S83] | Reject: procedural speed becomes substantive victory. |
| C — bounded objection and issue-scoped correction ladder | allow pointed objection first, widen only when dependence is shown, and reopen later only for named material change, error, or integrity failure.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S38][S39][S40][S41][S42][S44][S83][S89][S91][S92] | Adopt. |
| D — no finality until perfect certainty | keep provisional filing, escrow, review, or retention alive until every rival possibility is exhausted.[S17][S21][S27][S38][S39][S41] | Reject: turns honest administration into permanent suspense. |

## Five-rung ladder

1. **notice-and-objection lane** — use for ordinary returns, assessments, and reconciliations where the core need is a short visible chance to name the disputed period, field, threshold, or missing fact without reconstructing the whole file,[S16][S17][S21][S27][S38][S39][S41][S44]
2. **issue-bounded correction lane** — use where the objection is concrete and the challenged field can be severed from most of the file; correct the field and its real dependencies only,[S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83][S89][S91][S92]
3. **coupled-subset reconstruction lane** — use where the challenged field affects ranking, grouping, allocation share, or another tightly linked dependency; widen the review only to that coupled subset,[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83]
4. **integrity-reopen lane** — use where new facts, successor / step-in change, sham redaction, spoliation, forged reporting, or similar integrity failure could realistically change the settled answer,[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83][S89][S91][S92]
5. **closure-and-release lane** — once the issue-scoped review path has run, harden the period, release related escrow or suspense, narrow retention to ordinary preservation bounds, and move later disputes onto later periods unless a Rung 4 trigger is shown.[S17][S20][S21][S27][S38][S39][S40][S41][S42][S44][S83]

## Provisional recommendation

Adopt **Option C — the bounded objection and issue-scoped correction ladder** as the archive's default calibration.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S38][S39][S40][S41][S42][S44][S83][S89][S91][S92]

Presumption:

- ordinary filers, claimants, and weak-side challengers usually begin in **Rung 1** rather than with full rival reconstruction,
- most narrow mismatches should stay in **Rung 2**,
- move to **Rung 3** only when real dependence is named,
- reserve **Rung 4** for genuinely new facts, genuine integrity failure, or genuine successor / step-in change,
- and end in **Rung 5** rather than indefinite suspense.

That is the narrowest workable recommendation because it distinguishes **objection**, **field correction**, **coupled dependence**, **integrity-triggered reopening**, and **true closure** instead of letting every later disagreement behave like a universal reopen permit.

## Default placements

| Context | Default rung | Why |
|---|---|---|
| wage, transfer, or household-year reconciliation | Rung 1 or 2 | ordinary people need a pointed correction lane and then closure, not rolling year-by-year exposure.[S21][S27][S38][S39][S41][S65][S66] |
| property-value, situs, or simplified-regime fit dispute | Rung 2 | most such errors are narrow enough for field correction rather than whole-account reset.[S23][S25][S27][S38][S39][S41] |
| grouping, beneficial-ownership, controller, or allocation dispute | Rung 3 | one contested link often changes a subset of shares, not every settled fact in the period.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83] |
| forged filing, sham redaction, spoliation, or late successor fact | Rung 4 | later reopening is justified only because the integrity picture materially changed.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83][S89][S91][S92] |
| model-assisted high-volume assessment after correction window runs | Rung 5 | machine-scaled administration needs final release and bounded retention once issue-scoped review ends.[S16][S17][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S89] |
