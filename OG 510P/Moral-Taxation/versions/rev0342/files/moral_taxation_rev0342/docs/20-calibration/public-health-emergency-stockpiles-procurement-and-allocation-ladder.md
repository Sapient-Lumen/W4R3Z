| public production | severe market failure | sunset or capacity test. |

## Priority gates

1. **hazard/capacity gate** — define threat, item class, shelf life, surge quantity, and public/private production role.
2. **responsibility gate** — assign federal, state, tribal, local, provider, and supplier duties.
3. **inventory/rotation gate** — publish enough visibility for accountability; prevent expiry through replenishment, donation, use, or replacement.
4. **procurement gate** — use competition, open-book pricing, and clawbacks where urgency weakens bargaining.
5. **allocation gate** — rank medical need, exposure, disability, geography, tribal access, and continuity.
6. **logistics/equity gate** — test last-mile cold chain, rural, language, accessibility, and distribution by protected floor.
7. **diversion gate** — separate good-faith emergency error from culpable hoarding, resale, or supplier windfall.
8. **review gate** — reopen on stockout, expiration loss, allocation disparity, confidentiality breach, or windfall.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| ordinary preparedness | stockpile/warm base | rotation and audit. |
| innovation gap | advance purchase | public access and price. |
| crisis scarcity | allocation rule | need, exposure, floor. |
| supplier windfall | clawback or price rule | open-book evidence. |
