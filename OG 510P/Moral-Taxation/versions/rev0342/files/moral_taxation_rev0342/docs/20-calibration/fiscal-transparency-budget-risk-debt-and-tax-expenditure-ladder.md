## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — cash budget only | current receipts and outlays. | Too thin for modern fiscal stacks. |
| B — tax-expenditure report | revenue-loss list. | Required but not enough. |
| C — fiscal-risk statement | guarantees, PPPs, debt, and contingent liabilities. | Required where hidden risk exists. |
| D — citizen budget and open data | plain summary plus machine-readable tables. | Default for legitimacy. |
| E — full balance-sheet and stress test | assets, liabilities, risks, scenarios. | Required for major shocks or debt stress. |

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **coverage gate** — include public revenues, expenditures, financing, assets, liabilities, and fiscal risks.
2. **tax-expenditure gate** — list cost, beneficiaries, purpose, sunset, and direct-spending analogue.
3. **debt gate** — state deficit, interest cost, rollover risk, and intergenerational burden.
4. **guarantee gate** — estimate contingent liabilities and stress scenarios.
5. **off-budget gate** — consolidate public entities, PPPs, and special funds that perform public fiscal work.
6. **resource-revenue gate** — disclose extraction revenue, fund deposits, withdrawals, and stabilization rules.
7. **distribution gate** — show household, firm, regional, and future-cohort effects where feasible.
8. **audit gate** — assign oversight and publish follow-up on findings.
9. **open-data gate** — provide machine-readable budget and tax-expenditure data.
10. **sunset gate** — renew only after cost, incidence, and performance are re-estimated.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| ordinary annual budget | citizen budget plus machine data | show revenues and spending. |
| large tax preference | tax-expenditure evaluation | state beneficiary and cost. |
| major guarantee or PPP | fiscal-risk statement | stress test and cap. |
| debt-financed package | intergenerational statement | show interest and future taxpayers. |
| resource fund | fund governance disclosure | deposit and withdrawal rules. |
