# Taxpayer data confidentiality, non-tax use, and purpose-wall ladder

## Question in one sentence

What ladder governs whether tax return information may be disclosed, matched, reused, or transferred without turning tax compliance into general surveillance?[S378][S545]

## Ten-gate ladder

1. **source gate** — identify whether the data are return, return information, identity information, or derived tax-administration data.[S545]
2. **purpose gate** — classify the recipient's purpose: tax administration, taxpayer assistance, court-ordered law enforcement, benefits administration, or other.
3. **authority gate** — cite the exact statutory disclosure pathway and required approvals.[S378]
4. **necessity gate** — disclose the least data needed for the authorized purpose.
5. **identity-match gate** — require verified identifiers, reject weak or partial matches, and document error rates.
6. **bulk-use gate** — require heightened approval, sampling, and public accountability for programmatic matching.
7. **floor-risk gate** — test chilling effects on immigrants, low-income taxpayers, safety-sensitive households, and voluntary compliance.[S546][S547]
8. **recipient-control gate** — bind downstream use, retention, audit, security, and redisclosure.
9. **remedy gate** — provide correction, suppression, damages, or notice where lawful after unauthorized disclosure.
10. **trust-review gate** — terminate or narrow programs whose trust cost exceeds tax-administration value.

## Default settings

| Posture | Instrument | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| taxpayer-directed help | consent disclosure | narrow scope and expiry. |
| tax administration | disclosure exception | minimum necessary. |
| non-tax enforcement | court/statutory gate | verified target and audit. |
| bulk match | high scrutiny | error sampling and approval. |
| unauthorized disclosure | repair | suppression and remedy. |
