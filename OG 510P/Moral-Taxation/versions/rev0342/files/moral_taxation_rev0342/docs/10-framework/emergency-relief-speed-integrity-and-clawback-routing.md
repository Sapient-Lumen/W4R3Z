# Emergency relief, speed, integrity, and clawback routing

## Question in one sentence

When the state deploys emergency relief, tax credits, disaster payments, wage supports, loans, or automatic benefits at speed, how should the archive preserve rapid floor protection while preventing fraud gateways and later clawback traps?[S531][S532][S533]

Route: emergency design should pre-build controls that are fast enough for crisis and fair enough for later review: identity resilience, prepayment checks where feasible, postpayment sampling, safe harbors for good-faith recipients, and targeted recovery from culpable actors.[S531][S533]

## Core rule

Speed and integrity are not opposites. A crisis program that delays floor aid until perfect proof arrives fails morally; a crisis program that ignores identity, duplicate claims, and high-risk channels invites fraud and later punishes innocent recipients through blunt clawbacks.

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| subsistence relief needed now | floor-speed gate | simple eligibility, provisional payment, and later true-up. |
| high fraud channel or stolen identity | integrity gate | identity controls, duplicate checks, and victim repair. |
| documentation unavailable during crisis | proof gate | attestation plus postpayment sampling. |
| overpayment to good-faith low-income recipient | clawback gate | waiver, cap, installment, or no-recovery. |
| organized fraud or false certification | culpability gate | recovery, penalty, prosecution, and debarment. |

## Anti-patterns

- **fraud gateway** — urgency removes controls from predictable high-risk payments.
- **clawback trap** — innocent floor recipients are pursued years later for state design errors.
- **identity-victim rent** — stolen identity blocks future benefits or tax filings.
- **documentation impossibility** — displaced or disaster-hit households lose aid for missing papers.
