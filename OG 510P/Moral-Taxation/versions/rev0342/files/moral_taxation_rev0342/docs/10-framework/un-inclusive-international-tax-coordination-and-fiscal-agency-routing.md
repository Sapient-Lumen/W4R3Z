# UN-inclusive international tax coordination and fiscal-agency routing

## Question in one sentence

When international tax coordination is justified, how should the archive distinguish useful club coordination from legitimate global settlement, especially where weaker or lower-capacity states have source, market, services, withholding, or administrative claims?[S9][S36][S440][S451]

## Core rule

Club coordination can be administratively useful, but it is not automatically morally complete. A cross-border rule that stabilizes tax positions for mobile capital or residence states must still be tested against source-state fiscal agency, developing-country administrability, dispute capacity, withholding feasibility, services taxation, and whether the rule shifts compliance or revenue away from weaker states.[S440][S451]

## Legitimacy tests

| Test | Ask | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|
| participation | who had agenda-setting and drafting power? | club settlement presented as global consent.[S440] |
| fiscal agency | does each state retain a workable way to fund public goods? | coordination becomes tax-capacity suppression.[S451] |
| administrability | can weaker administrations apply the rule without elite intermediary dependence? | formal rights with unusable machinery. |
| source/market claim | are services, data, digital presence, users, labor, resources, and local burdens visible? | residence-state overclaim. |
| dispute path | can small administrations and taxpayers contest outcomes without excessive cost? | arbitration or MAP becomes a rich-state gate. |
| transition/equalization | are revenue losses, systems costs, and capacity gaps repaired? | global rule with local fiscal damage. |

## Routing posture

Use OECD or other club materials as technical evidence where they improve administrability, anti-fragmentation, and certainty. Use UN-inclusive process evidence when the live moral question is whether the settlement protects weaker states' fiscal agency, not merely whether sophisticated actors can comply with a common rule.[S9][S36][S440]
