# Data-center grid, water, and ratepayer-burden routing

## Question in one sentence

When AI or cloud data centers require scarce power, grid upgrades, water, land, backup generation, public incentives, or special tariffs, how should the archive separate legitimate local development from ratepayer cross-subsidy, abatement rents, bottleneck rents, and unpriced local burdens?[S15][S445][S10]

Route: do not tax compute as such. Route by real burden and real bottleneck: power capacity, interconnection, transmission, water, local emissions, emergency generation, land/site rent, tax abatement, public infrastructure, and ratepayer incidence.[S15][S445]

## Core rule

A data center is not automatically a villain or a public benefactor. It is a local-burden case when ordinary customers, ratepayers, water users, public budgets, or communities absorb costs that are not priced into the facility's own stack. The tax or fee base should track the shifted burden, not the mere fact that AI workloads exist.

## Burden ledger

Record at least:

- contracted load, peak load, curtailment rights, backup-generation plan, and interconnection cost;
- who pays for generation, transmission, distribution, substations, grid studies, queue priority, and reliability upgrades;
- retail-rate treatment, special tariff, interruptible contract, demand charge, and cross-subsidy exposure;
- water withdrawal, consumption, discharge, cooling technology, scarcity period, and local watershed constraints;
- local emissions, noise, land use, emergency diesel, and decommissioning obligations;
- property, sales, income, gross-receipts, utility, and infrastructure taxes paid;
- abatements, tax increment financing, grants, cheap land, subsidized power, accelerated depreciation, and public guarantees;
- local jobs, wage quality, supplier commitments, public-service effects, and data-center-specific community benefits;
- proceeds route: ratepayer credit, local infrastructure, water repair, public capacity, or general fund.

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| ordinary retail tariff covers full marginal cost | normal utility/tax stack | no special AI levy merely because compute occurs. |
| special tariff or abatement shifts costs | net fiscal-stack and ratepayer ledger | clawback, surcharge, cost assignment, or ratepayer credit. |
| grid upgrade primarily benefits the facility | interconnection-cost gate | facility-side contribution before ordinary ratebase allocation. |
| local water scarcity or cooling burden | water-burden gate | scarcity-priced charge, withdrawal cap, recycling duty, or disapproval if non-compensable. |
| hyperscaler bottleneck rent | rent/bottleneck route | tax scarce-capacity rent or excess return, not every server. |
| local benefits are claimed | benefit verification | net jobs, wages, taxes, and infrastructure against subsidies and ratepayer cost. |

## Anti-patterns

- **ratepayer cross-subsidy** — socializing upgrades while privatizing compute rent.
- **abatement without netting** — counting gross investment while ignoring tax concessions, utility discounts, and public infrastructure.
- **AI token theater** — taxing prompts, tokens, or parameters while missing the power, land, and bottleneck rents.
- **local burden without local repair** — sending proceeds to a general fund while the host locality bears water, grid, or rate impacts.
