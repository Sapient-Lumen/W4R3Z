# Nonprofit, charity, foundation, and exempt-status public-benefit routing

## Question in one sentence

When tax exemption, charitable deduction, donor-advised fund, private foundation, nonprofit hospital, university endowment, religious exemption, or public-benefit claim is at issue, when is relief justified and when is it private benefit laundering?[S483][S484][S485][S486]

Route: tax exemption is a public subsidy. It is justified only by public benefit, non-inurement, non-private-benefit operation, access, mission fit, and timely deployment of subsidized resources.[S483][S484]

## Core rule

Nonprofit form is evidence, not moral settlement. Exemption and deductibility must be routed by who benefits, who controls assets, how long resources are warehoused, whether beneficiaries can access the public benefit, and whether subsidy exceeds mission delivery.

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| ordinary public charity with active mission delivery | public-benefit gate | exemption and deduction can stand. |
| hospital exemption with opaque community benefit | hospital gate | CHNA, financial assistance, billing, charge limits, charity care review. |
| donor retains advisory/control benefits | donor-control gate | limit subsidy, require payout, disclose control and related-party flows. |
| endowment or DAF accumulates without deployment | warehousing gate | payout, excise, sunset, or reduced deduction timing benefit. |
| nonprofit sells market services like a firm | unrelated-business gate | UBIT, taxable subsidiary, or subsidy netting. |
| private benefit, inurement, or prestige laundering | integrity gate | denial, excise, recapture, or revocation. |

## Anti-patterns

- **private benefit laundering** — exemption converts private status, influence, or self-dealing into public subsidy.
- **endowment lockup** — deductible contributions are warehoused while urgent public needs go unmet.
- **community-benefit fog** — hospital or institutional charity uses vague reporting to preserve exemption.
- **donor control rent** — tax-subsidized funds remain effectively under donor influence without public accountability.
