# Whistleblower, relator reward, and revenue-integrity routing

## Question in one sentence

When a tax or public-funds system uses whistleblowers, relators, informants, or paid awards, what design preserves integrity without creating bounty capture, retaliation chill, confidentiality breaches, or informer extortion?[S557][S558][S559][S560]

Route: whistleblower incentives are legitimate when they reveal otherwise hidden evasion, fraud, or misuse of public funds; they become suspect when reward design encourages speculative accusations, retaliation, private coercion, or disclosure beyond what the enforcement process requires.[S557][S559]

## Core rule

The archive treats whistleblower systems as integrity instruments, not independent tax bases. The reward follows verified public recovery or sanction value, but the accused taxpayer's confidentiality, due process, and protected information remain bounded.

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| information is specific, timely, and credible | intake gate | preserve claim, protect identity, and screen for jurisdiction.[S557] |
| recovery depends on the tip | award gate | percentage, causation, culpability reduction, and appeal path.[S558][S560] |
| relator litigates on behalf of government | public-control gate | intervention, dismissal/settlement oversight, and proportional share.[S559] |
| whistleblower participated in misconduct | culpability gate | reduction, disqualification, or separate enforcement. |
| retaliation or confidentiality risk arises | protection gate | anti-retaliation, limited disclosure, and secure channels.[S561] |

## Anti-patterns

- **bounty capture** — reward size drives accusations rather than public integrity.
- **informer extortion** — private leverage substitutes for official enforcement.
- **retaliation chill** — insiders stay silent because the system cannot protect them.
- **confidentiality breach** — taxpayer information is exposed beyond the lawful proceeding.
