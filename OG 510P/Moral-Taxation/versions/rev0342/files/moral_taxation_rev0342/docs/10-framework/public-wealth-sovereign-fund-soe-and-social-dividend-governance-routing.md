# Public wealth, sovereign fund, SOE, and social-dividend governance routing

## Question in one sentence

When taxes, rents, royalties, public equity, SOE returns, or windfalls are converted into public wealth, what governance keeps them from becoming slush funds, incumbent subsidies, or one-time asset sales?[S579][S580][S581]

## Core rule

Public wealth is proceeds routing with a balance sheet. The archive supports sovereign funds, public equity, SOE dividends, strategic funds, and social dividends only where objectives, deposit and withdrawal rules, investment policy, accountability, beneficiary claims, and anti-capture safeguards are explicit.

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| windfall or resource rent is saved | fund-purpose gate | choose stabilization, savings, development, or dividend objective before deposits. |
| SOE or public asset returns finance budget | ownership gate | separate policy, ownership, regulation, and political interference. |
| public takes equity or royalty in subsidized project | upside gate | publish valuation, dilution, exit, and public-return rules. |
| fund invests domestically for industrial policy | mission gate | use performance conditions, crowding-in test, and loss tolerance disclosed ex ante. |
| government sells assets for short-term revenue | asset-sale gate | test long-run public value, monopoly risk, labor/community effects, and fiscal illusion. |

## Anti-patterns

- **political slush fund** — assets finance patronage or off-budget spending.
- **asset fire sale** — public balance-sheet value is liquidated to hide current deficits.
- **SOE rent shield** — state ownership protects inefficiency, monopoly, or corruption from scrutiny.
- **dividend theater** — social dividend is promised without stable funding or beneficiary rule.
