# Standards, certification, accreditation, and audit-gatekeeping routing

## Question in one sentence

When a standard, audit, certification, accreditation, or conformity-assessment badge is made a condition for public benefits, procurement, market access, or tax relief, how should the archive preserve trust without creating certified rents?[S621][S622]

## Core rule

Standards and conformity assessment should specify evidence, reduce uncertainty, and protect users. They fail when one paid certifier, proprietary standard, or conflicted auditor becomes the gate to legal status, subsidy, procurement, or market entry.[S621][S622]

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| public rule incorporates private standard | standards-access gate | ensure public availability or equivalent route. |
| certification is mandatory for subsidy or procurement | assessor gate | require independence, price proportionality, appeal, and alternatives. |
| audit firm sells both remediation and certificate | conflict gate | separate advice, testing, and pass/fail authority. |
| small entity cannot afford badge | access gate | use public schema, sampling, or fee support. |
| standard becomes outdated | refresh gate | sunset or update before stale certification hardens. |

## Anti-patterns

- **certified rent** — a paid badge becomes a private tax on legality.
- **conformity theater** — formal compliance masks missing performance.
- **auditor capture** — the party paid by the firm sells the public signal.
- **standard paywall** — the binding rule is hidden behind proprietary access.
