# Land, housing, vacancy, and speculation-rent routing

## Question in one sentence

When housing wealth, vacant land, land banking, short-term holding, foreign or opaque ownership, transaction taxes, mortgage preferences, or outdated assessments are at issue, how should the archive tax land/location rent without creating tenant burdens or assessment-capture traps?[S23][S473][S474][S475]

Route: land/location rent is a priority base, but the instrument must separate land from improvements where possible, update assessments fairly, protect asset-rich/income-poor households with deferral, prevent tenant pass-through, and net hidden subsidies before calling a burden legitimate.[S23][S473]

## Core rule

Land is not labor. Location value often reflects public infrastructure, agglomeration, zoning, scarcity, and community growth. But housing is also shelter. A moral tax design can reach land rent, vacancy, speculative holding, and unearned location gains only after protecting tenants, modest homeowners, disabled and elderly owners, and communities facing displacement.

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| ordinary owner-occupied shelter with low liquidity | homeowner floor gate | defer, circuit-break, rebate, or phase in before forced sale. |
| land value rises from public investment or zoning scarcity | location-rent gate | land value tax, split-rate tax, betterment levy, or proceeds repair. |
| vacancy, land banking, dereliction, or speculative non-use | anti-hoarding gate | vacancy tax, escalating charge, or use-it/repair-it rule. |
| transaction tax is proposed | mobility gate | usually prefer recurrent land/property charge; avoid locking households in place. |
| assessments are stale or regressive | assessment-integrity gate | update valuations, audit assessment ratios, protect appeals, disclose methods. |
| opaque or cross-border real-estate ownership | transparency gate | beneficial ownership, information exchange, withholding, or transfer reporting. |

## Anti-patterns

- **tenant pass-through** — the legal taxpayer is landlord or landowner, but rent-constrained tenants bear the burden.
- **assessment capture** — politically powerful parcels remain undervalued while weaker owners are revalued.
- **transaction lock-in** — transfer taxes suppress mobility while leaving holding rents lightly taxed.
- **shelter squeeze** — land-rent taxation is imposed without floor protection, deferral, or circuit breakers.
