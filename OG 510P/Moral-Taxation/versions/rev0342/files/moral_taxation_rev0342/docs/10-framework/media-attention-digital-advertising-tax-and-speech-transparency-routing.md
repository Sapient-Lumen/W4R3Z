# Media attention, digital-advertising tax, and speech-transparency routing

## Question in one sentence

When states tax digital advertising, attention extraction, or media-platform revenue, how should design reach real data/ad rents without suppressing price speech, taxing small publishers, or creating gross-receipts distortions?[S461][S636][S637]

## Core rule

Digital advertising and attention extraction can contain real rents, but a tax on speech-adjacent revenue needs precise base design, apportionment, transparency, and anti-pass-through honesty. The state may tax market power and data extraction; it should not censor invoice speech or pretend incidence disappears because disclosure is forbidden.[S461][S636][S637]

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| digital-ad gross-revenue tax is proposed | base-definition gate | define taxable advertising, apportionment, thresholds, and global-revenue rate triggers. |
| law bans line-item pass-through or explanation | speech-transparency gate | prefer burden salience over censorship; do not hide incidence from users. |
| small publishers or advertisers are swept in | floor-and-entry gate | use thresholds, credits, or narrower bottleneck bases. |
| platform ad rent overlaps data-extraction route | overlap gate | credit against privacy/data-rent instruments to avoid duplicate taxation. |
| tax funds public media or education | proceeds gate | verify proceeds routing and avoid ordinary-fund substitution. |
