# Platform-worker portable-benefits, classification, and fringe routing

## Question in one sentence

When platforms, agencies, or lawmakers propose portable benefits for nonstandard workers, how should fiscal design add security without ratifying misclassification or shifting payroll/social-insurance costs to workers?[S214][S641][S642][S643]

## Core rule

Portable benefits can repair fragmented work only if they supplement, rather than waive, employee status where control is real. Contributions should follow labor-value capture and platform control, be portable across roles, and preserve wage, unemployment, workers compensation, tax withholding, and appeal rights.[S214][S641][S642][S643]

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| worker is labeled contractor but platform controls price, access, ratings, or dispatch | classification gate | test economic reality before designing a portable-benefit substitute. |
| portable account is funded by worker deductions | incidence gate | charge the controlling platform or beneficiary before taking from low net wages. |
| benefit fund is too thin to replace employment protections | adequacy gate | do not let symbolic accounts waive wage, UI, comp, or leave duties. |
| data on platform work is missing | measurement gate | use CWS/platform-work data and require platform reporting. |
| multi-app worker has fragmented records | portability gate | standardize contribution, identity, and wage-record repair across platforms. |

## Anti-patterns

- **misclassification safe harbor** — the instrument protects its category label while losing the real burden.
- **benefit theater** — the instrument protects its category label while losing the real burden.
- **worker funded fringe** — the instrument protects its category label while losing the real burden.
- **algorithmic control denial** — the instrument protects its category label while losing the real burden.
