# Compliance burden and filing parity routing

This note answers the question that comes **after** a tax looks morally targeted and technically administrable but **before** it can claim legitimacy in ordinary life: **who must do the paperwork, buy the expertise, expose the data, or navigate the interface, and is that burden distributed justly?** The archive's stable answer is to keep compliance work with the **strongest record-holder and highest-capacity actor**, make **relief at least as easy to receive as liability is to impose**, and treat paid-preparer dependence, proprietary-software dependence, or controller-side opacity dumped onto weak users as design failures rather than mere inconveniences.[S26][S27][S38][S64][S65][S66][S68][S77][S89][S91][S92]

The tax state should not make ordinary people, weak firms, or downstream users perform forensic reconstruction of facts that a large employer, platform, lab, cloud, landlord, or tax authority already sees better. Complexity is sometimes unavoidable, but **where it lands** is part of justice.[S27][S38][S65][S66][S68][S77][S89]

## Default lanes

| Situation | Default filing / proof path | Why this path usually wins | Main warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary wage earner, pensioner, or benefit-recipient | withholding, prefill, return-free settlement, or one-step confirmation where the state already knows enough | routine income is usually already visible upstream, so repeated manual filing mainly adds friction and error | do not turn ordinary compliance into an annual scavenger hunt for facts the system already holds.[S27][S38][S65][S66][S68][S77] |
| Low-income household, disabled claimant, heavy caregiver, or person facing banking / identity friction | automatic enrolment, assisted filing, offline and accessible channels, and correction-first proofing | floor protection fails when the most burdened people must clear the highest procedural hurdles | do not let relief depend on fresh paperwork, perfect digital access, or paid help the target group cannot reliably obtain.[S31][S32][S33][S64][S65][S66][S91][S92] |
| Small firm, co-op, or thin-margin producer | simple filing, stable safe harbors, and bounded presumptions with graduation | simplicity can preserve productive activity and reduce arbitrary error when records are modest | do not let simplification harden into sheltering, threshold camping, or a preparer-dependent maze that only looks simple on paper.[S26][S27][S38][S65][S66] |
| Large firm, dominant platform, multinational group, or real controller group | rich digital reporting, beneficial-ownership disclosure, machine-readable ledgers, and versioned evidence packets | concentrated actors can bear higher reporting burdens and usually control the facts most relevant to rent, control, and cross-border claims | do not excuse opacity by calling it innovation, proprietary process, or affiliate complexity.[S1][S9][S27][S34][S35][S65][S68][S77][S89] |
| Broad consumption, excise, or platform-mediated charges | collect at merchant, platform, or other transaction-side remitter and repair regressivity through automatic rebates or transfers | upstream collection is usually cleaner than making final consumers self-report routine transactions | do not make poor households keep receipts and file later for protection that could have been delivered automatically.[S3][S4][S21][S64][S65][S66] |
| AI controller-side taxes, facility burdens, or model-governance duties | controller maps, compute / energy / location disclosures, machine-readable event logs, and registry-quality updates from labs, clouds, deployers, and real controller groups | the actors governing the stack already see the relevant operational facts better than end users, workers, or affected households do | do not push controller opacity onto prompt authors, downstream users, or local victims through user-side reporting demands.[S10][S15][S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S68][S83][S89] |

## Routing rules

### 1. Treat compliance burden as part of incidence

A tax can look progressive on its face and still be unjust if filing, proof, delay, or error risk fall mainly on weaker actors. Hours lost, paid-preparer dependence, inaccessible interfaces, repeated document submission, and delayed relief are part of the burden map, not side issues.[S27][S38][S64][S65][S66]

### 2. Make relief at least as low-friction as extraction

If tax can be withheld automatically but rebate, credit, or floor protection requires a fresh application, extra documents, or specialist help, the design is distributively suspect. The archive therefore prefers automatic enrolment, prefill, or one-step confirmation whenever the state or remitter already holds enough reliable information.[S31][S64][S65][S66][S68][S77]

### 3. Put record work on the actor who already sees the facts

The default reporter is usually the employer for payroll, the merchant or platform for transaction taxes, the land registry or assessor for immovable bases, and the controller group for frontier-AI governance facts. Do not force downstream people to reconstruct upstream control, affiliate structure, or facility-side behavior that concentrated actors already monitor more directly.[S21][S27][S34][S35][S65][S68][S83][S89]

### 4. Reserve heavy complexity for concentrated actors, not ordinary life

Strong firms, controller groups, and cross-border networks can justly be asked for richer reporting, data governance, and audit trails because they capture more upside and can spread fixed compliance cost. Ordinary workers, beneficiaries, and small firms should not need lawyer-grade interpretation or closed software just to remain compliant.[S26][S27][S38][S65][S66][S68]

### 5. Accessibility, privacy, interoperable access, and baseline credentials are not optional extras

Where proof is needed, prefer reusable credentials, bounded attestations, and accessible channels over repeated raw-document submission. A system that is digitally elegant for the administrator but exclusionary, over-collecting, surveillance-hungry, or dependent on paid gateway credentials for baseline filing is not administratively neutral. Basic tax identity, filing-account access, and required ordinary compliance credentials should be part of the baseline channel rather than a revenue-raising doorway.[S27][S42][S65][S66][S68][S89][S91][S92][S119][S120]

Baseline proof that a subject filed, paid, or presently stands in ordinary compliance belongs in that same baseline channel. Ordinary receipts, filing confirmations, and standard good-standing evidence should not be sold back through per-certificate charges, paid broker lanes, or premium clearance portals when the administration already holds the status and can expose it through a bounded proof path.[S27][S39][S42][S65][S68][S89][S134][S135][S136][S137]

Routine account-coordinate maintenance belongs there too. Address, contact-preference, responsible-party, refund, payment, and withholding / prepayment updates should not require paid profile management, proprietary software, or captive preparer / payroll gates merely to keep ordinary tax records current enough for notice, delivery, and compliance.[S27][S39][S65][S66][S68][S124][S128][S144][S145][S146][S147]

Tax-identity compromise belongs on the same parity lane. If an impostor files first, redirects a refund, reports phantom wages, captures an account, or forges authorization, the real subject should not face a higher paid-compliance burden merely to restore the tax account than an ordinary subject faces to use it. Baseline affidavits, protective credentials, account freezes, counterstatements, and recovery status paths should remain no-rent and assisted where digital proofing fails.[S27][S39][S40][S41][S42][S65][S66][S68][S148][S149][S150][S151][S152][S153]

Third-party reporting correction belongs there too. A worker, retiree, small seller, or platform user should not need paid assistance, proprietary dashboards, or indefinite issuer cooperation merely to dispute a missing, duplicated, wrongly attributed, or overstated W-2, 1099, 1099-K, withholding, or comparable information-return field. The reporting advantage sits upstream, so ordinary mismatch repair should begin upstream after a concrete recipient counterstatement.[S27][S39][S41][S42][S65][S66][S68][S154][S155][S156][S157][S158]

Worker-classification correction belongs on the same parity lane. A worker who was labeled as an independent contractor, platform seller, or vendor should not have to buy classification advice or reconstruct the payer's payroll system merely to ask whether the real relationship required wage withholding, employment-tax treatment, Form SS-8 review, or bounded Form 8919 reporting.[S21][S27][S39][S65][S66][S75][S76][S267][S268][S270][S271][S272][S273]

Refundable-credit proof belongs on the parity lane too. A low-income worker, caregiver, separated parent, student, disabled claimant, or informal household should not have to buy a preparer, proprietary software, transcript bundle, or idealized document set merely to claim, defend, or recertify EITC, CTC / ACTC / ODC, AOTC, or head-of-household-linked relief. The public lane should explain Form 8862, accept fit alternative records, and keep preparer due diligence aimed at preparers rather than claimant exclusion.[S31][S32][S33][S39][S41][S42][S64][S65][S66][S276][S277][S279][S280][S281][S282][S286][S287][S288][S290]

Household-status correction also belongs on the parity lane. A spouse, former spouse, separated parent, caregiver, or dependent-linked person should not need paid software, paid representation, adverse-household cooperation, or a proprietary joint account merely to request spouse relief, injured-spouse allocation, dependent correction, custody-status review, or separated-household treatment.[S31][S32][S33][S39][S42][S65][S66][S161][S162][S164][S165][S166][S167]

Ledger reconciliation belongs on the parity lane as well. Ordinary payment confirmations, bank records, withholding statements, corrected information returns, prior-year credits, refund traces, and returned-payment explanations should not require paid preparers, premium account products, or repeated full refiling before the system treats the missing amount as disputed.[S176][S177][S178][S179][S180]

Deadline preservation belongs on the parity lane as well. Extension, postponement, retransmission, disaster, and reasonable-cause procedures should not require paid preparers, premium software, or perfect digital access merely to keep an ordinary filing, payment, response, refund, or appeal right alive after a good-faith attempt or documented impossibility event.[S27][S39][S41][S65][S66][S168][S170][S172][S173][S174][S175]

Past-due filing reconstruction belongs on the parity lane too. If the state can compute a substitute return from third-party wage and income records, the subject should have no-rent access to the records, forms, instructions, filing channels, and assistance needed to submit the real return and reduce the case to the correct tax rather than to a default base.[S238][S258][S260][S261][S263][S264][S265][S266]


Collection-restraint release belongs on the parity lane too. Lien release or withdrawal, levy release, returned levy proceeds, passport decertification, employer or bank hold lifting, and public collection-marker correction should not require paid cleanup products, premium certificates, or captive representatives once the live claim is paid, corrected, stayed, allocated, or narrowed.[S182][S183][S184][S185][S187][S188][S190]


Self-correction belongs on the parity lane too. An amended return, timely superseding return, refund claim, or abatement request should not require paid software, a fresh preparer, or specialist timing advice merely to repair an ordinary error, preserve an overpayment claim, or remove a penalty / fee / interest item. The subject may owe a concrete changed-field packet; the state should not convert repair into a compliance toll.[S204][S205][S206][S207][S208][S209][S210][S211]

Private collection assignment also belongs on the parity lane. A low-cash taxpayer should not have to hire a tax-resolution firm merely to verify a collector, avoid a scam, request return to the agency, pay through public rails, or explain that the account is already protected by identity-theft, appeal, hardship, disability, low-income, disaster, installment, offer, examination, or innocent-spouse status.[S148][S153][S162][S212][S217][S218][S219][S220][S221][S222]

Petition preservation belongs on the parity lane as well. A taxpayer who receives a statutory notice of deficiency or comparable petitionable notice should not need paid representation, premium software, a private filing vendor, or expert timing advice merely to understand that Tax Court filing is distinct from agency response, request fee waiver, elect a simplified / small-case route where available, or use a non-digital filing channel before the prepayment-review window closes.[S117][S118][S160][S232][S233][S234][S235][S236][S237][S238][S239]


Basis reconstruction belongs on the parity lane. Ordinary taxpayers should not need paid tax software, broker archives, exchange subscriptions, chain-analytics products, or private appraisal-style reconstruction merely to show that reported proceeds are not taxable gain. Where basis is missing because an asset was transferred, noncovered, inherited, gifted, migrated between wallets, sold through a closing file, or reported before a platform had full lot history, the administration should accept fit public explanations, substitute records, and upstream correction requests before treating basis as zero.[S27][S39][S41][S42][S65][S66][S154][S155][S291][S292][S293][S294][S295][S298][S299][S301][S302]

### 6. Machine-scale governance should produce machine-legible reporting

Where a lab, cloud, or deployer operates at machine speed, the answer is not to intensify downstream paperwork. The answer is controller-side logs, stable schemas, event-bounded retention, and versioned packets that let authorities and affected parties review the claim without reverse-engineering the system from scraps.[S16][S17][S27][S68][S77][S83][S89]


Audit-response compliance belongs on the parity lane as well. A subject who receives an examination notice, document request, proposed change, interview setting, manager-conference path, appeal route, or audit-reconsideration opening should not have to buy representation, premium software, transcript brokerage, or impossible daytime availability merely to keep the examination from becoming a default assessment.[S191][S192][S193][S194][S195][S196]

Preparer-misconduct recovery belongs on the parity lane too. A taxpayer who used paid help should not need another paid preparer, refund-product intermediary, proprietary account, or private reconstruction service merely to report that a preparer filed without consent, altered the return, withheld records, used ghost credentials, diverted a refund, or failed to file as agreed.[S197][S198][S199][S200][S201][S202][S203]

Deadlock escalation belongs on the parity lane too. If a case is stuck because normal channels failed, the agency has repeatedly not responded, hardship is live, or the subject needs low-income / language-access dispute help, the taxpayer should not need a commercial tax-resolution firm, premium preparer, or paid status broker merely to get the public system to act on the file.[S141][S212][S213][S214][S215][S216]

Collection-alternative access belongs on the parity lane too. Offer-in-compromise, currently-not-collectible, hardship-delay, and rejected-offer appeal may require current filing and financial proof, but a low-cash taxpayer should not face a higher compliance burden merely to ask whether the debt can be collected, compromised, delayed, or appealed than a better-resourced taxpayer faces to pay or litigate. Fee-waiver, low-income certification, mail / online / assisted routes, and cure information are parity devices, not favors.[S39][S42][S65][S66][S162][S180][S212][S240][S241][S242][S244][S246][S247]

Estimated-tax forecasting belongs on the parity lane too. Pay-as-you-go systems may ask taxpayers to update withholding or estimated payments, but irregular earners, small firms, farmers, fishers, seasonal corporations, and late-year gain recipients should not need proprietary software or paid advice merely to find safe harbors, annualized-income methods, seasonal methods, Form 2210 / 2210-F routes, or waiver facts.[S21][S26][S27][S39][S50][S51][S65][S66][S147][S170][S303][S304][S305][S306][S307][S308][S309][S310][S311]


Penalty relief and interest recomputation belong on the parity lane too. Failure-to-file, failure-to-pay, failure-to-deposit, accuracy-related, First Time Abate, reasonable-cause, Form 843, and interest-rate / compounding issues should not require paid tax-resolution help, transcript brokerage, payroll-vendor cooperation, or proprietary account tools merely to separate the principal, penalty family, relief status, and corrected-base computation.[S39][S41][S42][S65][S66][S98][S172][S173][S208][S312][S313][S314][S315][S316][S317][S318][S319][S320]

## Five anti-patterns

1. **Relief by scavenger hunt** — liability is automatic, but protection requires applications, re-documentation, or perfect timing.  
2. **Paid-preparer or gateway-credential taxation by stealth** — ordinary compliance effectively requires buying expert help, proprietary software, or the baseline identifier / account needed just to file.  
3. **Controller opacity dumped downstream** — user, worker, or locality paperwork substitutes for controller-side disclosure.  
4. **Small-firm "simplicity" that only shifts discretion** — the regime is nominally simple but actually unstable, cliff-heavy, or negotiation-dependent.  
5. **Data gluttony in the name of integrity** — repeated raw-document collection where bounded attestations, reusable credentials, or upstream verification would do.  
6. **Certificate-rent compliance** — ordinary proof of filing, payment, or good standing is routed through paid certificates, clearance brokers, or premium portals even though the administration already knows the answer.[S26][S27][S38][S39][S42][S65][S66][S68][S89][S91][S92][S134][S135][S136][S137]
7. **Update-rent compliance** — ordinary account-coordinate, contact-route, refund, payment, or withholding updates require paid profile brokers, captive payroll / preparer systems, or fee-bearing correction paths before basic records can be kept current.[S27][S39][S65][S66][S68][S124][S128][S144][S145][S146][S147]
8. **Victim-rent recovery** — fraud victims must buy identity-restoration help, paid preparer access, proprietary account products, or expensive representation before the administration will treat unauthorized tax-account activity as disputed.[S27][S39][S40][S42][S65][S68][S148][S149][S150][S151][S152][S153]
9. **Mismatch-rent compliance** — a recipient must buy help, pay a platform, or wait on an unreachable issuer before the system will treat a third-party reporting mismatch as live rather than final.[S154][S155][S156][S157][S158]
10. **Captive-household compliance** — spouse relief, injured-spouse allocation, dependency correction, or separated-household treatment is practically available only through paid help or an adverse household member.[S161][S162][S164][S165][S166][S167]
11. **Deadline-rescue compliance market** — ordinary extension, postponement, retransmission, or reasonable-cause preservation is practically available only through paid assistance, proprietary software, or expert proof after a good-faith attempt or impossibility event.[S168][S170][S172][S173][S174][S175]
12. **Ledger-rent compliance** — the subject must buy help, pay a processor, or refile a whole case merely to show that a payment, credit, carryforward, offset, or returned-payment fact already belongs in the account.[S176][S177][S178][S179][S180]
13. **Release-rent compliance** — the subject must buy help, certificates, or private cleanup merely to unwind a lien, levy, passport certification, hold, or public collection marker after the predicate claim has narrowed.[S182][S183][S184][S187][S188][S190]
14. **Inspection-rent compliance** — a routine audit response, rescheduling request, document-copy submission, manager contact, appeal, or reconsideration path is practically available only through paid expertise, proprietary channels, or impossible availability.[S191][S192][S193][S194][S195][S196]
15. **Captive-preparer compliance** — the public system treats a paid preparer's private records, refund product, ghost signature, PTIN / EFIN trail, or portal account as the only practical way to prove what the taxpayer intended.[S197][S198][S199][S200][S201][S202][S203]
16. **Rescue-rent compliance** — a stuck official case can be moved only through a paid tax-resolution intermediary, premium status channel, or commercial advocate rather than a no-rent public escalation lane.[S212][S213][S214][S215][S216]
17. **Compromise-rent compliance** — a taxpayer must buy a settlement-mill package to learn whether OIC, CNC, hardship delay, fee waiver, cure, or rejected-offer appeal is the right public lane.[S240][S241][S242][S244][S246][S247][S248]
17. **Docket-rent compliance** — a petitionable notice can be preserved only through paid filing help, a proprietary digital workflow, or hidden fee-waiver / small-case knowledge.[S232][S233][S234][S235][S236][S237][S238][S239]

18. **Nonfiler reconstruction toll** — a past-due filer must buy transcript reconstruction, late-return preparation, or tax-resolution help merely to show the correct tax after an agency-computed substitute return.[S238][S258][S260][S261][S263][S264][S265][S266]
19. **Credit-proof toll** — an eligible claimant must buy help, perfect documents, or commercial preparer access to claim, defend, or recertify a floor-protecting credit.[S276][S279][S281][S286][S287][S288][S290]



20. **Basis-reconstruction toll** — a taxpayer must buy software, broker archives, chain analytics, or professional reconstruction to avoid tax on gross proceeds where basis can be shown through fit records or upstream correction.[S291][S292][S294][S295][S298][S299][S301][S302]

- **forecast-preparer dependency** — a taxpayer with lumpy or seasonal income must buy projection help merely to avoid or contest an estimated-tax penalty, even though safe-harbor, annualized-income, seasonal, and waiver routes are public rules.[S303][S305][S306][S307][S310][S311]
- **abatement-maze dependency** — a taxpayer must buy tax-resolution help or proprietary account access merely to identify the penalty family, request First Time Abate or reasonable-cause relief, file Form 843, or recompute interest after correction.[S172][S173][S208][S312][S313][S314][S315][S317][S320]
- **responsible-person proof toll** — a TFRP target must buy specialist help merely to see or contest the authority, willfulness, period, payment, provider-failure, or Letter 1153 protest facts.[S321][S322][S323][S324][S325][S326][S327][S328][S329]

### 6.22 Trust-fund recovery proof should not be sold as emergency representation

A person targeted for trust-fund recovery should be able to see the periods, trust-fund portion, authority facts, interview record, proposed-assessment route, Letter 1153 deadline, protest content, payment disputes, and third-party payroll-provider facts without buying a private rescue package. The public burden is to show real responsibility and willfulness before business payroll failure becomes person-level collection.[S39][S41][S42][S65][S66][S318][S321][S322][S323][S324][S325][S326][S327][S328][S329]

## Compression rule

If a new proposal mainly fails because **the paperwork is landing on the wrong actor**, **relief is harder than liability**, **ordinary compliance proof is being sold back as a certificate product**, **a third-party mismatch is being turned into recipient-paid cleanup**, **a household-status correction is being made captive to another person**, **a deadline-preservation act is being sold as rescue compliance**, **payment / credit reconciliation is being sold back as ledger cleanup**, **collection-restraint release is being sold back as private cleanup**, or **audit response is being sold back as inspection compliance**, or **preparer misconduct is being turned into taxpayer-paid rescue**, or **deadlock escalation is being sold back as a rescue market**, or **prepayment-review petition access is being sold back as docket compliance**, or **refundable-credit proof is being sold back as a credit-proof toll**, or **penalty relief / interest recomputation is being sold back as an abatement maze**, or **trust-fund recovery proof is being sold back as responsible-person defense**, route through this card plus the relevant collection, automaticity, administration, refund, assessment-unit, sanctions, or third-party-reporting note rather than adding a new special-case doctrine.

### Cross-border filing parity and the strongest-record-holder rule

Cross-border households and small firms can face a filing stack that no ordinary domestic taxpayer would recognize: FBAR, Form 8938, Form 3520 / 3520-A, Form 5471, Form 5472, Form 8865, Form 8858, amended returns, and reasonable-cause statements. The burden should sit as much as possible with the party that actually holds the records — bank, trustee, entity manager, employer, partnership, corporation, custodian, or prepared-information intermediary — rather than with the weakest downstream person trying to reconstruct foreign data.[S410][S416][S419][S420][S421][S423][S425][S426][S427]

Filing parity also requires duplicate-reporting controls. When the same foreign asset appears in multiple regimes or was substantially visible through one filing, the system should use correction, cross-reference, and safe-harbor treatment before treating every missing line, schedule, or attachment as a separate rent opportunity.[S407][S416][S417][S418][S430][S173][S432]

## Rev0274 route extension

When the live burden is not form length but access to the filing or refund rail itself, route to [`public-filing-floor-and-refund-rail-fallback-routing.md`](public-filing-floor-and-refund-rail-fallback-routing.md) before accepting partner software, paid preparation, banked-only refunds, or electronic-only channels as adequate compliance parity.[S441][S442][S443][S444]
