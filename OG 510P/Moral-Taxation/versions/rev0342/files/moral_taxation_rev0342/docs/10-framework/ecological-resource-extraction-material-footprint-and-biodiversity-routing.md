# Ecological resource extraction, material footprint, and biodiversity routing

## Question in one sentence

When carbon, minerals, biomass, water, forests, fisheries, biodiversity, waste, plastics, or material footprint are at issue, when should tax price harm and extraction rather than sell permission to degrade ecological commons?[S7][S477][S478][S103]

Route: price emissions, extraction, depletion, pollution, and resource rents where harms are measurable and compensable; prohibit or cap activities where harm is irreversible, non-compensable, or violates ecological floors.[S477][S103]

## Core rule

Ecological commons are protected-side subjects. Tax applies upstream to human controllers and beneficiaries of extraction and degradation. It must not convert irreplaceable habitat, species loss, toxic exposure, or water depletion into a purchasable license.

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| carbon emissions with measurable base | carbon-pricing gate | tax/ETS with floor repair and leakage review. |
| material extraction or severance | extraction-rent gate | royalty, severance tax, reclamation bond, and local repair. |
| biodiversity loss or habitat destruction | ecological-floor gate | avoid, cap, prohibit, or require restoration before tax. |
| waste, plastic, pollution, or non-point harm | lifecycle gate | excise, deposit-return, extended-producer-responsibility, or ban. |
| critical minerals for transition | transition-integrity gate | price harm without blocking essential clean transition; recycle proceeds. |
| offsets or credits claimed | greenwashing gate | reject weak credits, double counting, non-additionality, and impermanent repair. |

## Anti-patterns

- **permission to degrade** — a fee is treated as full moral settlement for irreversible loss.
- **greenwashing rent** — weak credits or offsets launder continued harm.
- **frontline dumping** — proceeds bypass communities bearing extraction, pollution, or water stress.
- **transition blind spot** — clean-energy supply chains are exempted from ecological proof because the end use is green.
