# Public filing floor and refund-rail fallback routing

## Question in one sentence

When the state requires tax filing, return correction, refund delivery, or payment remittance, what minimum public channel must remain available so ordinary compliance does not become a private software rent, preparer hostage path, bank-account gate, or electronic-only exclusion trap?[S119][S441][S442][S443][S444][S661]

Rev0286 filing-floor source note: the current IRS Free File page makes the public/private boundary more concrete by distinguishing partner guided software, Free File Fillable Forms, partner eligibility criteria, state-return limitations, and no-upsell/deceptive-practice guardrails.[S661]

Route: if filing is legally required, then baseline filing, correction, refund, and payment access are public obligations. Private software, return preparers, banks, payroll intermediaries, and identity vendors may assist, but the moral floor is a no-rent public lane plus accessible fallback when the preferred channel fails.[S119][S441][S443][S444]

## Core rule

A filing system may be digital-first, but not private-gatekeeper-only. It may prefer electronic refunds, but not bank-account-or-delay as a practical rule for people who are bankless, identity-compromised, disabled, elderly, rural, recently unhoused, incarcerated, language-isolated, or otherwise unable to use the preferred rail.[S443][S444]

## Routing table

| Case | Default route | Forbidden transformation |
|---|---|---|
| simple wage/withholding return where government already holds core data | public filing or prefill-adjacent lane | forcing paid software or preparer purchase to access a routine right.[S441][S442] |
| partner Free File software exists | supplemental private lane | treating partner eligibility screens as a full substitute for a direct public floor.[S119][S441] |
| public direct filing is removed or unavailable | fallback adequacy review | saying free options exist while the only practical route is private gatekeeping.[S442] |
| refund is owed but electronic deposit fails | refund fallback lane | freezing the taxpayer's own money without a timely assisted reissue path.[S443][S444] |
| taxpayer lacks bank account | multi-rail refund delivery | making bank access a condition of refund receipt. |
| taxpayer has language/disability barriers | accessible filing and correction lane | making comprehension or assistive access a paid add-on. |
| identity-theft or wrong-account risk appears | secure reissue and account-protection lane | requiring victim-paid rescue to unlock a public refund. |

## Tests

1. **legal-duty symmetry** — the stronger the legal duty to file, the stronger the public duty to supply a no-rent filing and correction channel.
2. **data-possession test** — where the state already holds wage, withholding, benefit, and third-party information, the taxpayer should not have to buy a private reconstruction of the government's own record.[S441]
3. **vendor-substitution test** — a partner product is useful only if it is accessible, no-cost for the case, state-return compatible where relevant, non-upsell, and not the sole path.
4. **refund-property test** — an owed refund is taxpayer property in the administrative sense; delay, setoff, identity hold, or rail failure requires a named predicate and bounded review.
5. **bankless-fallback test** — electronic payment preference must be paired with a usable alternative for people who cannot safely receive ACH, card, wallet, or online-account payment.[S443][S444]
6. **anti-churn test** — the taxpayer should not be bounced among IRS, partner software, bank, preparer, payroll issuer, or state agency without one accountable public path.

## Policy posture

This route does not require every tax agency to build a universal full-service preparation product on day one. It does require that required compliance and refund receipt not be monetized by scarcity. A government may sequence implementation through simple-return prefill, public e-file, free fillable forms, VITA/TCE expansion, assisted correction, phone/paper fallback, and accessible in-person help, but it should mark which taxpayers are still outside the public floor and why.[S441]
