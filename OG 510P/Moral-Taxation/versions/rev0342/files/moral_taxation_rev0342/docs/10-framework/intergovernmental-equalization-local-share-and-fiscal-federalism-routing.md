# Intergovernmental equalization, local share, and fiscal-federalism routing

## Question in one sentence

When land, natural-resource, data-center, port, infrastructure, tourism, platform, or tax-base rents arise in one locality but fiscal needs and capacities are distributed across regions, how should the archive divide local repair, local autonomy, national equalization, and anti-hoarding claims?[S469][S470][S471]

Route: burdened localities get first repair claim; host communities get a real share when they supply land, water, grid, public services, or consent. But location rents and mobile tax bases should not become permanent fiscal hoards while poorer jurisdictions carry equal public duties.[S469][S470]

## Core rule

Local repair and equalization are not opposites. The right split depends on burden, benefit, capacity, need, autonomy, spillover, and whether a local revenue source is a genuine local effort or an accident of geography, history, infrastructure, or state-granted exclusivity.

## Routing table

| Signal | Start here | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| locality bears direct burden | local-repair gate | first proceeds to water, grid, roads, housing, schools, health, or mitigation. |
| tax base is location rent or natural-resource rent | equalization gate | local share plus national/regional equalization. |
| unfunded mandate imposed by higher government | mandate gate | matching grant, transfer, or authority to raise funds. |
| rich locality hoards mobile/base rents | fiscal-hoarding gate | equalization transfer, shared base, or regional pool. |
| poor locality lacks administrative capacity | capacity gate | transfer plus technical capacity, not punishment. |
| data center or port claims local benefit | net-benefit gate | net subsidies and infrastructure before counting local revenue. |

## Anti-patterns

- **fiscal hoarding** — a rich locality captures a geography or infrastructure windfall while poorer regions fund equivalent public duties.
- **unfunded mandate trap** — higher levels assign public obligations without revenue authority or transfers.
- **local-repair bypass** — national revenue sweeps a burdened community's repair claim.
- **equalization without effort** — transfers erase incentives for reasonable local effort or transparent administration.
