# Session deep audit — rev0295

## Chosen priority

The riskiest unfinished surface was not another schema. It was the large `public_finance_core` family that still let high-level routes pass with generic beneficiary and evidence language. Rev0295 therefore clears the whole family rather than adding a new doctrine layer.

## What changed

- All 44 public-finance-core profiles now name route-specific accountable actors, beneficiaries/rent recipients, bottlenecks, burden bearers, evidence packets, default moves, and public fallback duties.
- The actor-accountability audit now makes public-finance-core placeholder regression fatal.
- Ten high-use public-finance route documents now include Rev0295 accountability maps: tax morale/state capacity, intangible IP royalty shifting, data extraction/ad-market rent, automaticity/take-up, base ordering, incidence evidence, net fiscal stack, proceeds visibility, instrument choice, and remedy traceability.

## Risk reduced

The archive is less likely to answer with a policy label while missing the responsible actor. Instrument-choice, incidence, proceeds, remedy, base overlap, and automaticity routes now require concrete records: formulas, ledgers, models, pass-through data, channel logs, disbursement evidence, transition records, and correction outcomes.

## Remaining risk

Archive-wide generic beneficiary placeholders fell to 54; generic evidence bundles fell to 73. The unresolved families are now smaller but still substantive. The highest-risk remaining candidates are `legal_enforcement_penalty`, `environment_climate_commons`, and `labor_care_benefits`.
