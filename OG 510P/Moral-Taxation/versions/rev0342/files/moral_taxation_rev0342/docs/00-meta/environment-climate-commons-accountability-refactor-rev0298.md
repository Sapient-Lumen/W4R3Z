# Environment/climate/commons accountability refactor — rev0298

Rev0298 clears the `environment_climate_commons` accountability debt. Before this pass, all 10 profiles in the family still used `beneficiary_or_rent_recipient_to_trace` and the generic `benefit_or_rent_trace` evidence bundle. The family also contained one severe cube-template holdover: `failure_waterfall_and_ex_ante_security` still looked like a generic harm-tax file even though the memo is about prefunding, insolvency, thin shells, cleanup, wages, trust-like claims, and controller guarantees.

## What changed

- All 10 environment/climate/commons profiles now identify concrete accountable actors, beneficiaries, burden bearers, bottlenecks, non-responsible actors, default moves, fallback/no-go duties, evidence packets, and review triggers.
- The audit now rejects environment/climate profiles that keep generic beneficiary placeholders, generic `benefit_or_rent_trace`, public-channel-only bottlenecks, or too-thin responsibility bases.
- `failure_waterfall_and_ex_ante_security` received the deepest cube-axis correction: it now exposes prefunding, bonds, reserves, insurance, controller guarantees, limited-liability failure, insolvency waterfalls, cleanup, wages, victims, and public backstop risks rather than `new_calibration_file`, `tax`, and generic harm.
- The rest of the family received targeted market/channel/status corrections so data-center, ecological, disaster-insurance, water, transport, fisheries, critical-minerals, shipping/aviation, and environmental-justice routes no longer all report `not_market_specific` and `not_channel_specific`.

## Accountability clusters

### Infrastructure and local public burdens

Data centers, water systems, road pricing, and transport charges now route responsibility through the actor controlling load, withdrawal, tariff, toll, exemption, location data, proceeds, or service substitution. The visible bill payer or traveler is not the accountable actor when a facility, utility, toll vendor, or public authority controls the burden.

### Commons extraction and non-compensable harm

Ecological extraction, critical minerals, fisheries, and offsets now require the permit, concession, quota, bond, stock, IUU, consent, reclamation, and repair packet. Pricing is acceptable only for measurable compensable harm; irreversible, sacred, non-consensual, or non-compensable harm escalates to no-go or denial.

### Climate-risk backstops and green-premium routes

Disaster insurance and hard-to-abate transport now distinguish risk-pricing, floor protection, fuel transition, low-quality offsets, mobility floors, port communities, and public backstops. Hidden subsidies and green premiums must be traced to beneficiaries rather than passed through to disaster survivors, island communities, low-income travelers, or ordinary taxpayers.

### Cumulative burden and prefunding

Environmental-justice siting now begins with cumulative burden, tool version, monitoring, language/disability access, housing stress, and no-go evidence before fiscal incentives are approved. Failure-waterfall routing now asks whether ex ante security was required before a thin shell externalized cleanup, wage, victim, or public-backstop claims.

## Remaining debt

After rev0298, 22 profiles still have beneficiary placeholders and 41 still use the generic benefit-trace evidence bundle. The next bounded substantive passes should target `social_floor_public_services`, `cross_border_reporting`, `regulated_networks_platforms`, and `release_integrity_currentness` rather than adding new meta layers.
