# Official error, prefill, and guidance reliance routing

Use this card when the live dispute is not only whether a tax rule was published or a notice was legible, but **whether the administration itself created or invited the error through official prefill, portal logic, calculators, tables, schema validation, or guidance that an ordinary subject was told to rely on**. The archive's stable answer is that **good-faith reliance on official tax representations should usually trigger correction-first treatment, bounded shelter from punitive add-ons, and system-side repair rather than retrospective ambush of the weaker party**.[S27][S36][S38][S42][S44][S65][S66][S68][S96]

## Core rule

Route state-created or state-invited tax error through this order:

1. **separate subject-supplied falsehood from official prefill, guidance, or portal-caused error**,
2. **protect good-faith reliance unless the contradiction was obvious or concealed**,
3. **correct the smallest disputed field or period first rather than reopening everything**,
4. **recover only the clear remainder that still survives reliance protection**,
5. and **push process repair upstream into notices, calculators, schemas, models, and help channels so the same trap does not repeat**.[S27][S36][S38][S42][S44][S65][S66][S68][S96]

A reliance rule is good when it does not let people profit from obvious falsehood, but also does not let the state monetize its own misleading prefill, stale tables, or officially induced confusion against those with less power to detect or correct it.

## Default routing by context

| Context | Best first reliance rule | Why this rule usually wins | Main warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary prefilled wage return or refund | correction-first, with interest / penalty shelter absent obvious contradiction or withheld facts | the administration invited reliance on its own wage and withholding picture | do not turn prefill into a trap where the state says “confirm this” and later treats that confirmation as expert reconstruction duty.[S27][S42][S65][S66][S68][S96] |
| Official calculator, withholding table, or portal estimate used by households or small firms | prospective repair plus bounded true-up on the clear remainder | ordinary users are pushed toward official tools precisely because the system is complex | do not punish small actors for using the very official tool they were told would make compliance safe.[S27][S36][S38][S65][S66][S68][S96] |
| Tax-credit, rebate, or floor-protection portal that silently misclassifies or suppresses payment | immediate restoration of the clear protected amount, then issue-bounded correction of the disputed field | protected-side delivery should not fail harder than extraction | do not make the weak side finance official delay or portal error while review drags on.[S21][S27][S42][S64][S65][S66][S68] |
| Controller-side AI registry, filing schema, or official validation tool accepted by the state | accept good-faith schema reliance for the accepted packet, then repair prospectively or by bounded delta unless concealment or contradiction was material | controller-side compliance already depends on official packet design and validation standards | do not let accepted but defective official schemas become a retroactive penalty machine after labs or deployers filed as instructed.[S16][S17][S27][S41][S44][S68][S89] |
| Newly changed rule paired with stale official notice, FAQ, or help-desk script | narrow reliance shelter for the misled period plus rapid notice repair | tax certainty and fair notice matter most when rules have just moved | do not blame the subject for following stale official instructions that the administration left live.[S36][S38][S42][S65][S68][S96] |

## Routing rules

### 1. Good-faith reliance on official tax representations usually cuts against punishment

Where the administration or its required official channel supplied the operative figure, classification, or filing instruction, good-faith reliance normally weighs strongly against civil penalty, fraud rhetoric, or hostile collection on the affected slice. Official convenience cannot be converted into a unilateral option to punish the people who used the official path as directed.[S27][S36][S38][S42][S65][S66][S68][S96]

### 2. Shelter narrows when contradiction was obvious, hidden, or strategically exploited

Reliance protection is not a license to ignore glaring mismatch, conceal contrary records, or game a known defect. The archive protects ordinary reliance, not opportunistic dependence on a mistake the filer could plainly see and exploit.[S27][S36][S42][S96]

### 3. Correction should run through the same official channel where possible

If the state prefilled, calculated, validated, or instructed through one official channel, it should usually repair through that channel too: corrected prefill, revised notice, propagated packet update, or restored credit. The cure for official error is not usually to force the weaker side into a wholly new expert compliance lane.[S27][S42][S65][S66][S68][S89]

### 4. State-created error should not become a hidden liquidity tax

Where official error led to overcollection, benefit suppression, or delayed refund, the archive treats delay itself as part of the wrong. Fast extraction plus slow official correction is not neutral administration; it is a covert burden, especially on workers, households, and thin-margin firms.[S21][S27][S39][S42][S65][S66][S68][S96]

### 5. Industrialized official error is a system-repair problem first

Repeated portal failure, stale guidance, bad prefill logic, or defective official AI triage should trigger notice repair, rule-version cleanup, channel redesign, and bounded relief for those already hit. The archive rejects a pattern where the state scales one mistake across thousands of people and then insists on individualized downstream cleanup.[S16][S17][S27][S42][S44][S65][S66][S68][S96]

## Five anti-patterns

1. **Prefill trap** — the state invites one-click reliance, then treats acceptance as proof the subject vouched for every hidden field.  
2. **Guidance whipsaw** — official help text or scripts remain live, but only the subject bears the cost when they prove stale.  
3. **Portal estoppel asymmetry** — the user is bound by portal output while the state claims the portal bound nothing.  
4. **Official-error liquidity loan** — correction is conceded, but the state keeps the cash and time value while the weak side waits.  
5. **Vendor shield outsourcing** — the administration blames contractors, software, or models while leaving the affected subject without meaningful official shelter.[S27][S39][S42][S44][S65][S66][S68][S96]

## Compression rule

If the live dispute mainly turns on **whether the subject followed an official prefill, calculator, portal, schema, or guidance path in good faith**, use this card with the administration, automaticity, sanctions, third-party reporting, refund, or finality notes rather than drafting a sector-specific reliance memo.
