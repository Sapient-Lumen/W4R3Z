# Verification, sampling, and review-intensity routing

Use this card when the live dispute is not only **what facts matter**, but **how much review is morally justified to test them**. The archive's stable answer is to verify only the contested moral fact at the lightest intensity that can really test it, and to escalate only when stakes, contradiction, opacity, or concentrated power make lighter lanes inadequate.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S83][S89]

This closes a recurrent gap between administration, proof burdens, data minimization, model-assisted review, and enforcement. Without a shared rule here, the archive keeps oscillating between packet-trusting formalism and universal deep-audit logic.[S16][S17][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S83][S89]

## Default lanes

| Situation | Default lane | Why this lane usually wins | Main warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| routine filing, current attestation, low contradiction | facial-completeness review only | most ordinary cases do not justify heavy inspection.[S16][S17][S27][S41] | do not pretend elegance of paperwork settles hidden reality forever. |
| bounded mismatch over a named fact, threshold, or eligibility condition | targeted cross-check against existing anchors | specific claims should trigger specific checks, not whole-case excavation.[S16][S17][S27][S39][S41][S89] | keep the check claim-scoped; do not widen it into open discovery by habit. |
| practice-sensitive dispute where command, control, or real burden turns on what actors actually did | bounded event or transaction sample | a small sample of material events often tests real practice better than broad document hoarding.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S41][S83] | sample decisive events, not whole communications archives. |
| material contradiction, strategic opacity, sham redaction, repeated mismatch, or very high stakes | escalated review with stronger presumptions, targeted demands, or harder supervisory sign-off | stronger review is justified when lighter lanes can no longer separate truth from theater.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83][S89] | escalation should still stay issue-bounded and reviewable. |
| security-sensitive, market-moving, or otherwise exceptional disputes | sealed or independent review as the narrow fallback | some cases need private inspection without turning private inspection into the norm.[S16][S17][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44] | return the shortest reviewable conclusion possible rather than a permanent secrecy dump. |

## Core routing rules

### 1. Review intensity is part of justice, not a backend setting

A tax can fail morally when the administration verifies too little to test a serious hidden claim, but it can also fail when it verifies too much by default and turns routine compliance into surveillance. The archive therefore asks for the **least intrusive review that can still seriously test the live issue**.[S16][S17][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S89]

### 2. Start with the disputed fact, not with the full record universe

Name the contested moral fact first: hidden controller status, income mismatch, threshold crossing, sham fragmentation, eligibility error, proxy failure, or burden-shifting claim. Then verify that fact specifically. Do not default to broad logs, full inbox review, or standing data lakes just because review is legally possible.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S83][S89]

### 3. Escalation should track contradiction, opacity, stakes, and dissipation power

Heavier review is easier to justify when the actor holds the decisive records, when lighter checks already exposed contradiction, when the amount or downside is large, or when assets or evidence can disappear quickly. It is harder to justify where the subject is weak, the issue is routine, or the same assurance could come from a narrower check.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44][S83]

### 4. Sampling beats blanket replay when practice matters

Where truth depends on how a system was actually governed or used, a bounded sample of material events is usually better than either attestation worship or total operational replay. The archive prefers **event-focused sampling** because it tests reality without normalizing permanent forensic intrusion.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S41][S83]

### 5. Secrecy is a last lane, not a standard lane

Some controller, security, or anti-evasion disputes justify sealed inspection or independent review. But secrecy should narrow what is exposed, not expand what is collected. The public-facing result should still return as the shortest intelligible conclusion that supports contest, correction, and later accountability.[S16][S17][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44]

## Four design failures

- **packet worship** — signed or machine-produced packets are treated as dispositive even when the live issue is hidden practice or concealed control.[S20][S21][S27][S41][S83]
- **universal deep audit** — the administration treats every meaningful tax relationship as grounds for broad replay, matching, or retention.[S16][S17][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44]
- **complaints-only integrity** — weak outsiders must reconstruct contradictions themselves before the state tests obvious risk.[S16][S17][S27][S39][S41]
- **escalation without a stopping rule** — once a case enters heavier review, the file never narrows again and provisional scrutiny becomes permanent condition.[S16][S17][S27][S39][S40][S41][S44]
