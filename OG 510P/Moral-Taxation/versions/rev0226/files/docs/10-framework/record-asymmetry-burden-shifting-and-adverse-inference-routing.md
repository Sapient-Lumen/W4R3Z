# Record asymmetry, burden shifting, and adverse-inference routing

Use this card when the live dispute is not only **what the right tax answer is**, but **who must prove the decisive facts when the records, logs, ownership chain, or governance story sit mostly inside the stronger actor's hands**. The archive's stable answer is to place heavy proof with the actor that has **superior access to the morally relevant facts**, require only a concrete trigger from weaker outsiders before burdens shift, use the **narrowest rebuttable presumption** that keeps filing or protection moving, and harden toward adverse inference mainly when opacity, selective incompleteness, or post-notice deletion looks strategic rather than accidental.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S39][S40][S41][S83][S89][S91][S92]

This is a general routing rule, not only a controller-map rule. It applies wherever tax justice would otherwise be defeated by **impossible outsider proof**: beneficial ownership hidden behind shells, platform or payroll intermediaries sitting on the key records, multinationals holding the apportionment facts, facility operators holding burden meters, or AI controller groups keeping the real governance story inside private systems.[S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S39][S41][S83]

## Default lanes

| Situation | Default proof lane | Why this lane usually wins | Main warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Worker, household, tenant, or small supplier needs relief while the decisive earnings, withholding, meter, or contract records sit upstream | require the stronger record-holder or public system to prefill, attest, or verify first; do not demand a full evidentiary dossier from the weak side | the stronger actor usually already sees the decisive facts and can spread proof cost more fairly | do not make protected-floor access depend on documents that the claimant cannot reliably obtain or preserve.[S21][S27][S31][S38][S64][S65][S66][S68][S89][S91][S92] |
| Beneficial ownership, related-party grouping, or anti-fragmentation is contested | shift burden toward the actor controlling the affiliate chain, trust structure, nominee stack, or internal books once a concrete shell or coordination signal is shown | opacity should not be a reward for choosing the most layered legal form | do not force outsiders to prove the whole hidden chain before any grouping presumption can arise.[S21][S27][S34][S35][S39][S41] |
| AI controller status is disputed and decisive governance facts sit inside labs, deployers, clouds, committees, or affiliates | require the best-informed visible actor to produce the shortest workable controller map, then escalate burdens if mission control, continuation power, or residual upside still remain contested | controller duty should not be easiest to escape where the operational facts are most concentrated | do not jump straight from incomplete records to universal joint liability or limitless logging.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83][S89] |
| Local burden, extraction, emissions, congestion, or facility stress is contested | place primary measurement and rebuttal duties on the operator, host-beneficiary, or remittance anchor that already meters the activity | the actor running the burden-generating system usually sees the relevant operational facts best | do not make affected households or localities prove the whole industrial process from outside before repair can start.[S15][S21][S23][S27][S69][S79][S83] |
| Cross-border profit, apportionment, transfer pricing, or bottleneck rent is disputed | shift burden toward the multinational, platform, or controller group with the internal allocation facts once a plausible mismatch is shown | internal ledgers, pricing rules, and benefit splits are not equally visible to all parties | do not let cross-border complexity become an immunity cloak.[S3][S9][S21][S27][S28][S30][S34][S35][S39][S41] |

## Routing rules

### 1. Heavy proof follows superior access, not formal pleading order

When decisive facts sit mainly with one actor, the archive prefers burden shifting toward that actor once the challenger points to a concrete governance, ownership, payment, metering, or mismatch signal. The point is not to reverse every burden automatically; it is to stop **record concentration** from becoming a justice advantage.[S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S39][S41][S83]

### 2. Weaker outsiders usually owe a trigger, not a complete hidden map

Households, workers, small firms, localities, and outside challengers should generally have to show a **specific reason to doubt the paper story** rather than prove the whole interior chain before the better-informed party must answer. A credible trigger may be enough: inconsistent filings, affiliate loops, durable mission-setting, unexplained pass-through, metered burden, or a governance signal that contradicts the nominal story.[S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S39][S41][S83]

### 3. Use narrow rebuttable presumptions before hard adverse inference

The archive prefers a short ladder: ordinary presumption, superior-access shift, provisional grouping or filing bridge where needed, then stronger adverse inference only if the records stay strategically incomplete. This keeps administration moving without pretending uncertainty is already guilt.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83][S89]

### 4. Adverse inference hardens mainly for strategic opacity, sham splitting, or spoliation

Missing records, stale packets, sham redaction, shell fragmentation, inconsistent stories, or post-notice deletion change the moral posture of proof. Once the better-informed party appears to be exploiting evidence control itself, stronger presumptions, escrow hardening, or sanction escalation become more justified.[S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S39][S40][S41][S83]

### 5. The cure for impossible proof is not universal surveillance

A good burden-shifting rule should reduce, not expand, raw-data appetite. Use the smallest packet, narrow credential, bounded sample, or targeted cross-check that can answer the live issue. Do not solve every proof problem by demanding full books, full logs, full communications, or routine sensitive-attribute ingestion.[S16][S17][S27][S39][S40][S41][S89][S91][S92]

## Five anti-patterns

1. **Opacity bonus** — the actor with the best internal records wins by refusing to clarify them.  
2. **Impossible outsider proof** — the weak side must prove the whole hidden chain before burdens shift.  
3. **Everyone-controls maximalism** — messy proof turns every visible participant into the presumptive liable actor.  
4. **Discovery gluttony** — proof disputes justify permanent broad logging or generalized surveillance.  
5. **Sanction-max imputation** — ordinary incompleteness is treated as a warrant for the harshest possible inference without first trying a narrower rebuttable bridge.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83][S89]

## Compression rule

If a case mainly turns on **who has the decisive records and when burdens should shift**, use this card plus the relevant subjecthood, beneficial-ownership, filing-parity, administration, sanctions, or controller-boundary note rather than adding a new sector-specific proof doctrine.
