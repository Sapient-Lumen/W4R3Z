# Class / context / species matrix

This matrix is the archive's tightest single answer to the founding question once you want anchor-level detail. For the shortest subject + scale answer, start with [`ideal-taxation-by-class-context-and-species.md#one-screen-schedule`](ideal-taxation-by-class-context-and-species.md#one-screen-schedule). Read the table left to right: identify the subject, pick the best tax anchor, then apply the override checklist only if the dispute turns on unit choice, controller chains, incidence, timing, measurement, standing, or jurisdiction.

| Subject or class | Moral status now | Best tax anchor | Default treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Child, dependent, or person below subsistence | rights-bearing person | protected floor, not extraction | negative income support, public provision, and automatic or low-friction delivery where possible |
| Single parent or heavy unpaid caregiver | rights-bearing person with dependent load | protected floor plus care recognition | low or negative net burden, refundable support, childcare/care provision, and person-based liability rather than coerced jointness |
| Low-wage or median human worker | rights-bearing person | ability-to-pay after subsistence | light direct burden with rebate-protected consumption taxation where broad consumption taxes exist |
| High-income human person | rights-bearing person | ability-to-pay plus luxury and inheritance | progressive personal taxes and capital-rent capture |
| Rentier or dynastic household | rights-bearing persons holding concentrated advantage | land, inheritance, anti-deferral capital income, luxury | steep treatment of unearned concentration |
| Household consuming in market | rights-bearing unit but not sole moral unit | final consumption | broad consumption tax with rebate or transfer offset, ideally prefilled or automatically delivered where eligibility is already visible |
| Landowner | person or firm holding scarce site value | land/location rent | land-value taxation or equivalent site-rent capture, with deferral or instalments where justified by liquidity |
| Extractive resource holder | person or firm | resource rent | severance or rent taxation, depletion, restoration bonds, and cleanup obligations |
| Small firm or cooperative | legal instrument serving production | simple cash-flow or presumptive base with threshold and graduation | low-complexity compliance, modest burden, and genuine loss recognition rather than incumbent-only relief |
| Large corporation | legal instrument, not natural person | residual profit, rent, anti-shifting, beneficial ownership | minimum tax, residual-profit taxation, anti-base-erosion rules, and related-party anti-fragmentation |
| Dominant digital or AI platform | legal instrument controlling a bottleneck | monopoly rent, destination, interoperability choke points, real group control | excess-profit, competition-linked, destination-based taxation, and group-level anti-fragmentation |
| Public body, mutual, nonprofit, university, hospital, charity, or regulated utility using AI without a real private residual claimant | rights-bearing institution serving users, members, or the public, not a private windfall claimant by default | service capacity, rate or member relief, local repair, and ordinary employer / contractor bases | governance, rate review, anti-supplantation, worker or user benefit, and local-burden repair before beneficiary-side windfall extraction; use substance-over-form where executive, contractor, or affiliate rents are the real gain |
| Worker-owned, employee-owned, or other labor-residual firm using AI with broad worker claims on the gain | rights-bearing workers organized through a firm, not a public body and not an ordinary shareholder-windfall case by default | wages, hours, member dividends, transition buffers, and ordinary profit / payroll / land bases | ask first whether the gain is visibly becoming broad worker benefit rather than narrow elite or outside extraction; use substance-over-form where managers, financiers, landlords, vendors, or thin paper ownership are doing the real work |
| Open or public-interest model project or small static checkpoint release | tool or legal instrument | ordinary organisational taxes and real externalities | differentiated, lighter treatment than frontier commercial bottlenecks |
| Maintained model hub, monetized checkpoint distributor, or release channel with update / access control | legal instrument with ongoing gatekeeping power, but not automatically the sole deployer | distribution rent, hosted access revenue, update governance, and assigned reserve or disclosure duties | bounded upstream duties may attach without displacing downstream deployment liability from actual deployers and major beneficiaries |
| Commercial frontier model lab | tool under concentrated firm control | controller-side profit, bottleneck rent, scarce-capacity draw, local burden, automation windfall, and bounded public-input reciprocity | ordinary corporate tax first; add AI-specific levies only on real exceptional triggers; collect through strong group-level anchors and preserve loss symmetry |
| High-risk AI deployer | human or firm actor | risk creation and externalized downside | licensing fees, ex ante bonding, insurance, incident reserves, and no reliance on post-collapse tax grabs for repair |
| Passive cloud, utility, landlord, lender, or contract host without material AI-stack discretion | ordinary person or firm, but not usually the primary AI controller | ordinary business income, site rent, utility, host-burden, or financing base | can be taxed or used as a remittance anchor without being assigned special controller-side AI liability unless real capacity or risk governance control is shown |
| External overseer, insurer, surety, reserve custodian, commercial channel intermediary, or third-party tool / API provider without operational control | public institution, outside checker, risk-finance firm, marketplace / billing channel, or capability provider, but not usually the primary AI controller | ordinary public, service, premium, surety-fee, custody-income, channel-fee, or tool-fee base rather than deployment profit or risk | approval, audit, cover, bonding, reserve custody, billing, discovery, settlement, or callable capability supply can gate risky activity or execute actions without socializing private controller liability; move into controller treatment only when the actor actually procures, directs, or governs live deployment choices |
| Step-in receiver, emergency manager, conservator, or incident-response operator with live command | rights-bearing person, firm, or public body acting as a temporary controller for the layer it actually governs | temporary deployment-layer revenue, reserve, reporting, and emergency-control obligations tied to the live command period | outside oversight becomes temporary controller treatment once the actor can actually pause, continue, roll back, restart, or reconfigure the live system from inside the loop, without wiping predecessor or successor duties elsewhere |
| Catastrophic or non-compensable risk activity | human or firm actor creating irreparable danger | ex ante gating before price | approval, conditional approval, caps, safety cases, decommissioning powers, and sidecar charges rather than pure tax-as-permit logic |
| Ordinary prompt author, hosted-model customer, thin reseller, or pure white-label seller | rights-bearing person or ordinary firm, but not usually the upstream controller | ordinary earnings, consumption, or business-profit base | no default user-side AI levy; move into controller treatment only if the actor materially chooses deployment scale, model configuration, risk posture, workflow governance, mission-specific commissioning, or durable behavior-shaping customization |
| Large downstream beneficiary capturing extraordinary AI-enabled gains without controlling the model stack | rights-bearing person or ordinary firm, but not necessarily the controller | concentrated beneficiary-side gain, margin expansion, labor-displacing surplus, or other auditable windfall proxy | special beneficiary-side contributions can be justified without faking controller status, but only where concentrated gain is real, ordinary taxation is missing it, the levy stays aggregate rather than user-side or per-token, related affiliates have been grouped enough to rule out split-stack innocence, cross-border claims follow beneficiary home and market logic rather than upstream model geography, real outside site-rent absorption is routed to land / host-burden tools rather than treated as blanket immunity, real outside policyholder benefit can narrow but not erase the lane, discretionary philanthropy matters only for the truly outside and irrevocable slice, and self-directed reinvestment usually stays inside retained-surplus screening unless the prefunding is genuinely locked to outside or public obligations |
| Large downstream beneficiary with equity widely held through pensions, retirement accounts, index funds, or similar passive saver wrappers | rights-bearing person or ordinary firm with diffuse portfolio owners, but not a public-body, member, or worker-benefit case by default | concentrated firm-side gain first, then ordinary person-side retirement / saver fairness | passive broad-saver exposure does not by itself erase concentrated beneficiary-side gain; handle saver fairness mainly through incidence, proceeds, and ordinary pension / person-side rules |
| Large downstream firm using AI where apparent gains are being broadly competed away into lower prices, wider access, or materially better user service | rights-bearing person or ordinary firm with weak residual capture **if** the diffusion is real | ordinary profit plus incidence / customer-benefit routing rather than default windfall treatment | do not call a genuinely diffused gain a concentrated beneficiary windfall, but do not let temporary discounts, lock-in pricing, or persistent high margins fake customer benefit |
| Large downstream firm using AI where apparent gains are being bid away into better terms for suppliers, creators, contractors, licensors, franchisees, or other complementors | rights-bearing person or ordinary firm with weaker residual capture **if** the pass-through is real and outside the group | ordinary profit plus incidence / supplier-benefit routing rather than default windfall treatment | do not call a genuinely diffused gain a concentrated beneficiary windfall, but do not let affiliate routing, captive channels, fee recapture, or dependency theater fake counterpart benefit |
| Large downstream firm using AI where apparent gains are being absorbed by lower premiums, policyholder dividends, outside risk-pool benefit, or claims stabilization | rights-bearing person or ordinary firm with weaker residual capture **if** the insurance story is real and outside the group | ordinary profit plus insurance-benefit / incidence routing rather than default windfall treatment | do not call genuinely outside policyholder benefit a concentrated beneficiary windfall, but do not let captive insurance, self-insurance, or reserve rhetoric fake broad diffusion |
| Large downstream firm using AI where apparent gains are being capitalized into higher commercial rents, lease premia, or scarce-location claims | rights-bearing person or ordinary firm with weaker residual capture **if** the location-rent story is real and outside the group | land / site-rent and local host-burden routing plus ordinary profit review rather than default windfall treatment | do not call a genuinely capitalized outside site rent a concentrated beneficiary windfall, but do not let related landlords, sale-leasebacks, or ordinary lease expense fake land-rent absorption |
| Large downstream firm using AI where apparent gains are being donated away through charity, community grants, foundation transfers, or other public-benefit giving | rights-bearing person or ordinary firm with weaker residual capture **if** the transfer is truly outside, irrevocable, and not privately recaptured | ordinary profit plus beneficiary / proceeds / anti-supplantation review rather than default immunity | do not call a genuinely outside irrevocable public-benefit transfer a concentrated beneficiary windfall, but do not let CSR rhetoric, captive foundations, donor-advised shells, or reputational spend fake broad diffusion |
| Large downstream firm using AI where apparent gains are being plowed into capex, compute, model spend, expansion, or discretionary reserves | rights-bearing person or ordinary firm with concentrated control over the surplus unless the prefunding is genuinely locked away from ordinary private option value | ordinary profit plus timing / prefunding / beneficiary routing rather than default immunity | self-directed reinvestment usually remains retained surplus; only real ring-fenced worker, safety, cleanup, or public-duty prefunding narrows the beneficiary lane |
| Employee, contractor, trustee, nominee, or outsourced operator acting under another actor's standing instructions | rights-bearing person or ordinary firm, but not usually the primary AI controller | ordinary labor, service, fiduciary, or business base | delegated operation or formal frontage does not by itself relocate controller-side AI liability away from the principal or grouped controller that set objectives, budget, scope, and risk posture |
| Downstream fine-tuner, agent scaffold, or retrieval / tool-governance layer | rights-bearing person or ordinary firm acting as a real co-controller for its governed deployment layer | deployment-layer revenue, risk, local burden, reserve, or controller-side obligations tied to the layer it actually governs | persistent behavior-shaping control can create real co-controller duties without erasing upstream duties; when several actors each look partly controller-like, primary status follows durable governance, continuation power, and residual upside |
| Commissioning buyer, principal, or mission owner over a vendor-hosted stack | rights-bearing person or ordinary firm that can become a real controller or co-controller for the dedicated deployment it governs | deployment-specific revenue, benefit, local burden, reserve, and mission-layer controller obligations | procurement, service levels, approval rules, escalation rights, or stop / continue power can make the buyer a real controller even when a vendor still hosts weights or infrastructure |
| White-label integrator, OEM-branded distributor, or franchise-style branded layer | rights-bearing person or ordinary firm whose status depends on which layer it actually governs | customer-facing deployment revenue, integration margin, workflow risk, and any still-separate upstream controller bases above it | branding alone does not settle controller status; keep upstream duties where updates, safety posture, or continuation stay upstream, but assign downstream co-controller duties where the branded layer governs workflow, approvals, support promises, or incident handling |
| Current LLM/model as such | not yet established as person and lacks standing bundle | none directly as a person | do not tax as a civic subject; tax accountable controllers instead |
| Copied, forked, fine-tuned, or swarming model instances under common control | not yet established as persons and not multiplied into separate civic subjects by copy count | controller group, deployment revenue, compute, externalities, and risk | ignore instance count as a person proxy; group by real control unless rights-bearing independence is shown |
| Updated, renamed, migrated, or affiliate-transferred model family with substantial continuity | same underlying person / firm chain unless genuinely new independent controller governance is shown | continuity of deployment, users, update channels, beneficiary chain, and inherited duties | do not let rebranding, checkpoint migration, or asset choreography wipe threshold history, reserve duties, or reporting continuity |
| Nonhuman animals under human control or foreseeable exposure | morally considerable protected being, not civic filer | controller-side harm, confinement, extraction, and pollution | upstream taxes, fees, and bonds on controllers; route proceeds toward welfare, transition, inspection, and remediation rather than animal-side liability |
| Ecosystem, habitat, or watershed | protected commons, not civic filer | pollution, depletion, land conversion, access, or extraction | biodiversity-positive taxes and fees, restoration bonds, and bounded beneficiary charges; no direct “taxpayer” fiction for the commons |
| Autonomous artificial person (conditional, future) | rights-bearing only if threshold is met and ownership ends | ability-to-pay and reciprocity after floor protection | tax like other persons only with standing, remedy, representation, and no machine-species surcharge |

## Context matrix

Context primarily changes **instrument choice, anchor choice, and administrative shape**. It should not silently convert current LLMs into direct taxpayers or erase floor, review, and sunset discipline.

| Context | Preferred instruments | Cautions |
|---|---|---|
| Stable welfare state | land, inheritance, corporate rent, broad consumption with rebates, progressive income | avoid payroll-heavy states that spare wealth and rents |
| High-inequality / rent-heavy economy | land, inheritance, anti-monopoly, excess profits, luxury consumption | do not rely on symbolic millionaire surtaxes alone |
| Automation shock | automation dividend, windfall profit capture, transition insurance, training finance | do not tax displaced workers to fund adjustment; special AI levies need real gain and displacement triggers rather than branding alone |
| Climate emergency or water stress | carbon, methane, congestion, scarcity, resource depletion, adaptation finance, and where needed ex ante caps or permits | protect low-income households with dividends or public-service offsets, and do not rely on taxes alone where harms are irreversible or highly concentrated |
| Biodiversity collapse or habitat stress | biodiversity-positive taxes and fees, pollution and runoff charges, resource-use or access fees for privileged beneficiaries, and restoration bonds | keep animals and commons on the protected side; do not turn damaged ecosystems or nearby weak communities into the payers of last resort |
| High inflation or rapid nominal drift | indexed or openly reset brackets, allowances, credits, carry-forwards, reserve minima, and threshold rules | do not let bracket creep, phantom gains, or decaying bonds substitute for visible policy choice |
| Weak-capacity state | simple withholding, land cadastre, excises, customs, resource rents, and narrow presumptive regimes | complexity and fake precision can destroy compliance legitimacy |
| Fragile or failure-state governance | strongest visible anchors, short emergency schedules, customs, withholding, utility billing, resource rents, and protected-floor transfer corridors where possible | do not let crisis become payroll-first looting, checkpoint extraction, or permanent emergency taxation; simplification must still preserve floor protection, bounded review, and sunset |
| Highly digital transnational economy | destination-based profit rules, market-jurisdiction allocation, minimum tax, cloud/compute coordination, and platform-rent capture | unilateral digital taxes alone are brittle |
| Local or metropolitan government | land value, vacancy, curb access, congestion, and data-centre siting or utility-burden charges | do not assign borderless profit bases to municipalities |

## Species rule

The archive now recognizes four stable routing regimes:

1. **Human persons** — direct tax subjects with rights, notice, appeal, and representative channels.
2. **Current machine systems** — tax-relevant tools, not direct persons; route liability through controllers, owners, deployers, beneficiaries, and externalities.
3. **Nonhuman animals and ecological commons** — protected-side interests, not civic remitters; route liability through controllers, extractors, polluters, and privileged beneficiaries.
4. **Possible future artificial persons** — only if rights-bearing agency is shown **and** standing, remedy, and representation can be supplied without owner domination; once that threshold is crossed, move from property logic to person logic rather than mixing both.

Species or substrate does not itself supply a tax rate class, and infrastructure position, delegated operation, outside oversight, risk finance, commercial channel intermediation, ordinary third-party tool provision, ordinary training-input supply, white-label branding, or outside oversight alone does not settle controller status, though real commissioning command or step-in command can.
