# Tax subjecthood and liability routing

This note answers the question that comes **before** rates: who is the direct civic tax subject, what base is being taxed, who remits first, who is likely to bear the real burden, and who must have standing to contest the result? The archive's stable answer is that current AI changes the **routing map** more than the **person map**.[S19][S20][S21]

## One-line thesis

Tax justice should route direct liability to rights-bearing persons, controller groups, and place-bound assets according to control, benefit, burden-bearing, standing, delegated authority, and the boundary between operational governance and outside oversight, input supply, channel intermediation, branding frontage, or mere vendor hosting. Current LLMs are not yet direct tax persons; they are tax-relevant technologies inside human and firm liability chains, and copy count, delegated operation, external supervision, training-input supply, risk finance, commercial channel intermediation, white-label branding, or mere vendor hosting does not by itself multiply or relocate civic taxpayers before a real person-regime switch. When several actors each look partly controller-like, primary status follows durable governance, continuation power, and residual upside rather than mere visibility, genuinely shared governance still routes through the real controller group, version resets or affiliate transfers do not by themselves erase controller continuity, and genuine commissioning or step-in command can re-route controller duties to the actor actually governing the live layer.[S10][S16][S17][S19][S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S41][S42][S43][S44][S83]

## The five roles

1. **Direct subject** — who is answerable in civic terms.
2. **Tax base** — what is actually being measured and taxed.
3. **Remitter** — which actor is used for first collection.
4. **Final bearer** — who is likely to end up poorer after pass-through and bargaining.
5. **Reviewer / representative** — who can receive notice, contest the claim, and speak for the subject where needed.[S21][S42][S43][S44]

Do not collapse these roles into one box. A tax can be well-aimed in base but misrouted in subject, collection point, or burden-bearing.

| Employee, contractor, trustee, nominee, or outsourced operator acting under delegated authority | the person or firm for ordinary tax or fiduciary purposes, but **not** usually the primary AI controller | ordinary labor, service, fiduciary, or business income; special AI levies only if the actor also has real discretionary governance power | payroll, vendor billing, fiduciary account, or managed-service interface where needed | weaker operators and formal fronts should not become stand-ins for principals that set objectives, budget, deployment scope, or risk posture | delegated operation, custody, or frontage is not enough; follow the actor that could materially redesign or stop the system without asking permission.[S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S42][S44][S83] |

| Payment processor, merchant of record, app store, ad network, third-party tool / API provider, or other channel intermediary without operational governance | the firm for its own ordinary intermediation or tool-fee income, but **not** usually the primary AI controller | channel fees, payment spreads, service income, tool fees, and where deliberately assigned limited remittance or reporting duties | merchant account, app-store billing, settlement interface, execution log, action rail, or marketplace ledger where needed | dependent sellers, workers, end-users, and weak counterparties should not become residual payers merely because a visible channel or tool sits between the controller and the customer | billing, discovery, ranking, traffic acquisition, settlement, or callable execution is not controller status; escalate only when the intermediary can materially choose the live model, standing permissions, safety settings, or ordinary continuation of the deployment.[S10][S20][S21][S27][S41][S68][S83] |
| Downstream customizer, orchestrator, or retrieval / tool-governance layer with durable discretion | the person or firm remains the ordinary legal subject and may also become a real co-controller for its deployment layer | deployment-layer revenue, risk, externality, reserve, or controller-side obligations tied to the layer it actually governs | its own business, deployment, or approval interface | do not let “just a wrapper” language hide the actor that materially shaped what the system could know, do, or trigger | once an actor materially fine-tunes, scaffolds, curates retrieval, controls tools, or sets safety policy for a standing deployment, treat it as a real co-controller for that layer without erasing upstream duties.[S15][S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S83] |
| Possible future artificial person | the artificial person itself, **only if** rights-bearing threshold plus standing are actually present | person-level ability-to-pay after a protected floor | direct administration only with independent representation and remedy | no ownership and no substrate surcharge once personhood is recognized | once the threshold is crossed, move from property logic to person logic rather than mixing both opportunistically.[S18][S19][S20][S42][S43][S44] |

## Hard defaults

### 1. Separate subject from remitter

The strongest collection point is not always the morally primary subject. Wage withholding may sit at the employer; VAT remittance may sit at the seller; controller-side AI levies may sit at a facility or cloud intermediary. That does not settle who is the true bearer or what justice requires.[S3][S21]

### 2. Protect the bearer before celebrating the bill

A tax aimed at a firm, platform, or utility is still badly routed if the predictable final burden lands mainly on people whose floor the archive is committed to protect. Rate design, rebates, dividends, competition checks, or re-routing may all be needed.[S4][S21][S31]

### 3. Household is usually context, not personhood

The archive uses household information for care, dependency, and means-testing only with caution. The default direct human tax subject for ordinary earnings remains the individual person.[S31][S32][S33]

### 4. Current LLMs change controller liability, not civic personhood

Present models can change where rents, harms, and bargaining power concentrate, but that still points the archive toward controllers, deployers, facilities, and beneficiaries rather than toward a machine head tax.[S10][S11][S15][S19][S20][S22][S67]

Replication does not change that by itself. A thousand owned instances or a managed agent swarm can enlarge the taxable activity base, yet still fail to create a thousand civic taxpayers; the archive groups by real control until independent standing exists.[S19][S20][S21][S34][S35][S42][S43][S44]

Ordinary prompting, ordinary hosted use, thin resale, and pure white-label branding do not change it either. Those facts can matter for ordinary income, business-profit, or consumption taxation, but special controller-side AI liability should still follow the actors who can materially choose model configuration, deployment scale, safety posture, and rent extraction.[S3][S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S83]

A separate point matters, however: a downstream actor can still be a proper target of a **beneficiary-side** windfall or automation-dividend contribution without becoming the controller. Keep controller duties and concentrated-beneficiary duties conceptually distinct so outsourcing does not erase extraordinary gain and ordinary use does not become a user tax.[S11][S13][S14][S21][S22][S67][S83]

Delegated operation does not change it by itself either. Employees, contractors, trustees, nominees, and outsourced operators can file, run, monitor, or front the system without becoming the morally primary controller when the real strategic levers remain with a principal, grouped owner, or governing institution.[S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S42][S44][S83]

Material downstream customization can change it, however. A firm that persistently fine-tunes, scaffolds, governs retrieval, grants tool permissions, or sets approval and safety policy for a deployment layer may become a real co-controller for that layer even when upstream labs, hosts, or large beneficiaries still share responsibility.[S15][S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S83]

White-label packaging can split duties without settling them. A branded seller that mainly fronts billing or customer acquisition does not become the sole controller by label alone, and an upstream maintainer does not disappear merely because another firm owns the interface. But a branded layer can become a real co-controller where it governs workflow, approvals, support promises, escalation, or incident handling for the customer-facing deployment.[S10][S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S83]

Passive infrastructure and finance do not change it by themselves either. A cloud, utility, landlord, lender, or contract host can be an appropriate rent target, remittance point, or host-burden payer without becoming the morally primary AI controller unless it can materially allocate scarce capacity, veto or direct deployment, set safety or incident posture, or otherwise govern whether the high-impact activity proceeds.[S5][S10][S15][S16][S17][S20][S21][S23][S25][S27][S34][S35][S83]

Open-weight release, maintained distribution, and third-party tool execution do not settle it either. Static publication is usually not permanent controller status, and outside tools usually execute on general terms, but a maintained hub, gated checkpoint channel, continuing maintainer, or inside-the-loop tool governor can still carry bounded or co-controller duties when it controls updates, access, ranking, monetization, safety defaults, action permissions, or ordinary continuation.[S10][S15][S16][S17][S19][S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S53][S54][S83]

Training-input supply does not settle it either. Outside licensors, dataset curators, benchmark operators, and label vendors can matter greatly to quality or access, but they remain input and evaluation layers unless they can materially govern persistent training policy, release gates, update approval, or ordinary deployment choices for the live system.[S10][S16][S17][S20][S21][S22][S27][S34][S35][S83]

Step-in command can settle it for a period, however. A receiver, incident-response team, public conservator, or emergency manager that can actually pause, continue, roll back, restart, or reconfigure the live deployment is no longer merely an outsider for that layer and period, even though predecessor or successor duties can still survive elsewhere in the chain.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S27][S41][S44][S83]

When several actors each satisfy part of the controller story, break the tie by durable governance rather than by visibility. Ask who sets the standing deployment purpose, who can revise persistent permissions or safety posture, who can continue, halt, or materially update the system without asking elsewhere, who allocates scarce capacity or budget, and who captures the residual upside after ordinary fees are paid. That actor is the primary controller unless the facts instead show genuinely split layer governance, in which case keep one primary controller for the highest strategic layer and assign only narrow co-controller duties below it.[S16][S17][S20][S21][S34][S35][S83]

### 5. No direct tax subjection without standing

Where notice, contestability, representation, and some path of voice are absent, direct civic taxation is misrouted. Move liability to a guardian, estate representative, owner, controller, or beneficiary instead.[S42][S43][S44]

### 6. Protected nonhuman interests belong on the claim side, not the remitter side

Animals, habitats, and ecological commons can matter morally without becoming civic taxpayers. Tax the controllers, extractors, polluters, and privileged users whose actions impose the burden, then make restoration and guardianship legible enough that the protected side of the ledger is not forgotten.[S6][S7][S69][S101][S102][S103]

## Jurisdiction echo

The routing question also helps decide **where** liability belongs:

- situs for land, extractive sites, and other immovable bases,
- burdened locality for congestion, grid strain, cooling-water stress, and similar place-bound harms,
- destination for final consumption,
- market access for some share of large cross-border residual profit,
- and treaty or club coordination for mobile rents that otherwise outrun any one state.[S3][S5][S9][S15][S28]
