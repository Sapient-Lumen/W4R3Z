# Proceeds routing and fiscal reciprocity

This note answers the question that comes **after** a tax is justified in base, subject, rate, and collection: **where should the proceeds go first?** The archive's stable answer is that revenue destination is part of the tax itself. A morally serious regime routes proceeds toward the people, places, and public capacities most entangled with the taxed rent, harm, or civic claim.[S1][S4][S5][S6][S7][S8][S13][S15][S18][S22][S69]

## Core rule

Route proceeds in this order unless a strong reason defeats it:

1. **repair** measurable harms and uncompensated losses,
2. **protect** the floor and other predictably burdened groups,
3. **share** common rents and inherited advantages more broadly,
4. **build** durable public capacity that reduces future domination, fragility, or extraction.[S1][S4][S6][S7][S8][S15][S18][S22][S69]

A tax that reaches the right base but routes proceeds into opaque privilege, incumbent subsidy loops, distant extraction, or fake recycling through baseline replacement still fails the archive's standard.[S29][S71][S78][S79]

## Routing table

| Tax base or context | First moral destination for proceeds | Secondary destination | Main warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carbon, congestion, water stress, and similar corrective charges | repair and cushioning for exposed households and places | adaptation, resilience, and cleaner substitutes | do not treat corrective revenue as a license to backfill unrelated privilege while harmed places wait.[S6][S7][S69] |
| Broad consumption taxes | visible floor protection: rebates, transfers, or equivalent service support | general revenue only after protected households are made whole enough | broad-base consumption can be administratively clean yet morally dirty if recycling is regressive or invisible.[S3][S4][S18] |
| Land value, resource rents, and extractive site charges | local repair, local public realm, and equalisation tied to place-bound burden | wider civic dividend from common inheritance | illiquidity can justify timing relief, not diversion of common-rent proceeds into narrow private privilege.[S5][S7][S18] |
| Inheritance, large gifts, and dynastic transfers | child opportunity floors, equalisation, or broad civic endowment | general revenue after anti-concentration purposes are served | do not let anti-dynastic taxes vanish into fiscal fog with no visible sharing of inherited social advantage.[S8][S18] |
| Residual corporate profit, monopoly rent, and bottleneck charges | competition capacity, public oversight, and broad floor-supporting revenue | public investment or debt reduction where distributively neutral | avoid recycling dominance taxes back into the same incumbents through bespoke subsidies or procurement favors.[S9][S10][S15] |
| Current AI / automation rents, facility charges, and deployment-linked downside funding | worker transition, local burden repair, and public-interest oversight capacity | public compute, public-interest models, and broad social dividends where rents are truly common | do not let AI-tax proceeds become a reinvestment loop for concentrated labs and cloud bottlenecks.[S10][S11][S13][S14][S15][S16][S17][S22] |
| Risk-funded fees, bonds, and reserve contributions | compensation, remediation, and supervised reserve integrity for that risk class | only residual balances should flow to general revenue | money collected for non-compensable or cleanup-heavy risk should not disappear into unrelated budgeting before victims or cleanup are secured.[S16][S17][S53][S54] |
| Future rights-bearing artificial persons | ordinary civic reciprocity after person-level floor protection | the same public goods expected of other persons | no species earmark once a being is actually inside the rights-bearing person class.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44] |

## Five routing rules

### 1. Match destination to the moral reason for the tax

Corrective taxes should first repair or cushion the burdens they price. Rent taxes should first socialize concentrated advantage. Risk-funded charges should first secure remediation and compensation. General-revenue freedom expands only after the tax's own justification has been honored.[S6][S7][S8][S16][S17][S69]

### 2. Protect predictable losers before celebrating net progressivity

A tax can be progressive in aggregate and still create acute hardship for specific households, workers, or localities. Proceeds routing should therefore protect the predictably burdened first, not rely only on ex post claims that the total fiscal system is progressive enough.[S4][S18][S69]

### 3. Keep local shares where burdens are local

Where data centers, extractive sites, congestion zones, or pollution-intensive facilities impose place-bound burdens, a material local share is morally prior. Upward redistribution can still be justified, but not by pretending that local burdens are unreal.[S5][S7][S15][S22]

### 4. Separate true public capacity from incumbent subsidy loops

Building capacity is a valid use of proceeds only when it widens access, oversight, resilience, or public option strength. Routing revenue back into the dominant actors who generated the taxed rents usually defeats the point.[S9][S10][S15][S22]

### 5. Do not fetishize rigid earmarking

The archive does **not** require every dollar to be locked into a single silo forever. The rule is narrower: if a tax is morally defended by repair, cushioning, equalisation, or shared capacity, some visible and credible routing toward that end should be legible **and additive enough to be real**. Otherwise the moral defense becomes decorative.[S1][S18][S29][S69][S71][S78][S79]

## Scale grammar

- **Local scale:** prioritize repair, siting compensation, infrastructure, and service capacity where the burden is place-bound.
- **National scale:** prioritize floor protection, broad equalisation, and public goods that cannot be cleanly localised.
- **Cross-border / club scale:** prioritize anti-race-to-the-bottom coordination, transnational public goods, and transfers where rents or spillovers outrun single jurisdictions.
- **Failure-state or emergency settings:** prioritize minimum floor continuity, emergency logistics, and corruption-resistant visible support before fine-grained targeting.[S1][S7][S9][S15][S22]

## AI-specific default

For current LLM economies, the archive's default routing order is:

1. local repair for energy, water, land-use, and congestion burdens,
2. worker transition and portable income-security measures where displacement risk is real,
3. supervisory and incident-response capacity for high-risk deployment,
4. public-interest compute and public-option capability that reduces private chokepoints,
5. only then wider general revenue or social-dividend sharing.[S10][S11][S13][S14][S15][S16][S17][S22][S67]

This keeps AI taxation from collapsing into either symbolic punishment or incumbent subsidy.

## Anti-patterns

1. **Sinkhole budgeting** — the tax is justified one way and spent another.
2. **Distant extraction** — local burdens are real but all revenue flows away.
3. **Incumbent laundering** — dominance taxes are routed back into dominant firms.
4. **Protection by application maze** — revenue is said to offset burdens, but relief depends on high-friction claiming.
5. **Species earmarking after personhood** — once personhood is recognized, stop using substrate as a reason for special fiscal destination.[S4][S15][S19][S20][S64][S65][S66]
6. **relabelled reciprocity** — promised repair or local sharing exists only because baseline duties were cut elsewhere.[S29][S69][S71][S78][S79]
