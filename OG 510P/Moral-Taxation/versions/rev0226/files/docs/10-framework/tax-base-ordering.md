# Tax-base ordering

This is the archive's current priority order for what should be taxed first.

## Tier 0 — protect or subsidize

- subsistence consumption
- primary caregiving and dependency support
- essential childhood development
- disability accommodation
- civic minimum participation

## Tier 1 — tax hardest first

- land and location rents
- natural-resource extraction rents
- monopoly and platform bottleneck rents
- pollution and congestion externalities
- very large inheritances and gifts
- windfall profits tied to privileged bottlenecks or crisis scarcity
- annual net-wealth taxation only as a **backstop** where these clearer concentrated bases still leave large fortunes materially undertaxed

## Tier 2 — tax broadly with correction

- broad consumption, preferably with uniform or near-uniform base plus rebate/refund mechanics rather than extensive reduced-rate clutter.[S3][S4]
- residual corporate profits, especially where destination and anti-shifting rules can be enforced.[S9]

## Tier 3 — tax progressively but carefully

- high personal labor income
- capital income that is really rent, insider timing advantage, or concentrated appreciation rather than ordinary saving
- luxury positional consumption

## Tier 4 — tax reluctantly

- routine labor at modest incomes
- ordinary household saving aimed at stability rather than domination
- small cooperative or community surplus with clear public-benefit recycling

## Why this order

The order follows a moral intuition and an administrative intuition at once: tax first what is least like earned subsistence effort and most like socially created advantage, exclusion power, or publicly imposed cost. Annual net-wealth taxation therefore sits as a residual check on large concentrated fortunes, not as the master tax that substitutes for land, inheritance, and anti-deferral reform. The order should also be read net of tax expenditures and subsidies: a sector cannot honestly be called heavily taxed if abatements, credits, accelerated depreciation, or cheap public inputs quietly reverse the claim.[S5][S8][S24][S29][S30]

## Corollary for AI

Compute, energy, model deployment, and AI-enabled profit concentration are important **tax bases**. They do not by themselves make the model a tax **person**, and the archive prefers measurable controller-side bases over fake precision about token value or model essence. For the archive's closed overlap rule on when land, local-burden, carbon, profit, windfall, and wealth-backstop claims may stack, credit, or refuse wrong-base substitution, start from [`overlap-creditability-and-non-substitution-routing.md`](overlap-creditability-and-non-substitution-routing.md); use [`../20-calibration/base-ordering-overlap-creditability-and-non-substitution-ladder.md`](../20-calibration/base-ordering-overlap-creditability-and-non-substitution-ladder.md) only for narrower settings work.
