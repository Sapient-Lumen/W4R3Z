# Canonical schedule by subject and scale

## Canonical schedule

| Subject / context | Direct subject now? | First scale with the strongest claim | Best base / anchor | Default burden shape | Must not do |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Person below subsistence, disabled person with exposed floor, child, or dependent.[S4][S31][S64][S65][S66] | yes, but net-protected | national / local delivery systems | negative income support, public provision, and low-friction transfers | negative to zero | do not run head-tax logic, high-friction claiming, or floor-stripping collection |
| Ordinary worker above the floor.[S2][S3][S4][S31][S55] | yes | national person-level liability | earnings above the protected floor, with rebate-protected consumption taxes where used | light to moderate, then progressive | do not overload payroll while sparing rents; do not hide liability inside compulsory household pooling |
| Heavy caregiver, single parent, or care-loaded household.[S31][S32][S33][S64] | yes, person-first | national / local | person-based liability plus transfers, services, and care recognition | low to negative net | do not create second-earner traps or make care recognition depend on unrealistic jointness assumptions |
| Affluent person, rentier, or large heir.[S8][S18][S21][S24][S34][S35][S73] | yes | national, with club support for mobile avoidance-sensitive pieces | progressive income, capital-rent capture, inheritance / receipts taxation, luxury bases, and anti-deferral rules | progressive to steep | do not let trusts, shells, or timing games reclassify dynastic power as ordinary saving |
| Landowner, extractive-right holder, or other immovable-rent claimant.[S5][S23][S25][S79][S86] | yes or entity remitter tied to the asset | local first for site value; national / regional for resource-rent splits | land value, site rent, severance, depletion, cleanup, and restoration bonds | moderate to steep | do not excuse durable under-taxation merely because the base is illiquid in cash terms |
| Small firm, cooperative, or thin-margin producer.[S26][S27][S80] | not a natural person, but a remitting entity | local / national | simple cash-flow, withholding, and modest presumptive proxies only where needed | light to moderate | do not impose cliff-heavy thresholds, gross-turnover punishment, or complexity that only incumbents can survive |
| Large corporation, dominant platform, or real controller group.[S1][S9][S10][S21][S28][S34][S35] | entity remitter, not a civic natural person | national plus cross-border club / market coordination | residual profit, bottleneck rent, minimum-tax floors, destination / market allocation, and anti-fragmentation | moderate to steep on rents | do not follow invoice theater instead of real control, beneficial ownership, and related-party grouping |
| Pollution, congestion, carbon, water stress, and similar measurable harms.[S6][S7][S15][S17][S53][S54][S88] | yes, through the harming actor or remitter | local for place-bound burden; national / global club support where spillovers cross borders | corrective pricing, upstream collection, reserves, bonds, and repair funding | moderate to steep | do not treat catastrophic or non-compensable risk as if a price alone makes it acceptable |
| Current LLM or model as such.[S19][S20][S44] | no | none directly | none directly as a civic person | none directly | do not create a machine poll tax, token head tax, or fake personhood merely because model activity is visible |
| Frontier model lab, hyperscale compute gatekeeper, or high-impact deployer.[S10][S11][S15][S16][S17][S22][S67][S70] | controller-side liability, not model-side personhood | local for facility burdens, national for profits and labor displacement, club floors for mobile rents | controller-side profits, scarce-capacity draw, local burden, automation-linked windfall, public-input reciprocity, and funded downside risk | moderate to steep on rents, bottlenecks, and harms | do not default to token-count, parameter-count, or blind gross-throughput taxes |
| Ordinary prompt author, ordinary hosted-model customer, or thin reseller.[S10][S15][S20][S21] | yes as ordinary person or firm | ordinary local / national tax system | ordinary earnings, consumption, or business-profit bases | ordinary schedule only | do not levy a special AI user tax absent real deployment control, durable customization, or concentrated gain |
| Public body, mutual, nonprofit, university, hospital, charity, or regulated utility using AI without a private residual claimant.[S11][S18][S21][S22][S29][S64][S69][S70][S83] | entity remitter or public institution, not a private windfall claimant by default | local / national service or rate system | service capacity, member or ratepayer relief, local repair, and ordinary tax on any real private extraction | ordinary to light, with visible recycling first | do not tax service savings as if they were distributable shareholder windfall, and do not let mission form hide executive or contractor rents |
| Worker-owned, employee-owned, or other labor-residual firm using AI where gains still flow mainly to a broad worker class.[S11][S13][S14][S18][S21][S22][S26][S27][S67][S83] | yes as ordinary persons and firm remitter, but not an ordinary shareholder-windfall case by default | national first, with ordinary local burdens still separate | ordinary tax first, then worker-benefit routing through wages, hours, member dividends, training, and transition buffers | ordinary to light on broad worker gain, rising only where concentrated private gain remains | do not treat broad worker reciprocity like generic shareholder windfall, and do not let paper worker ownership hide elite or outside extraction |
| Large downstream beneficiary capturing exceptional automation or AI-enabled upside while outsourcing the stack.[S10][S11][S13][S14][S21][S22][S67] | yes as ordinary person or firm, but not necessarily as controller | national first, with ordinary local burdens still separate | ordinary profit and income first, then bounded beneficiary-side windfall contribution if concentrated gain remains undertaxed | ordinary-to-moderate, rising only with real concentrated gain | do not confuse outsourcing with innocence, passive-saver diffusion, debt service, outside policyholder benefit, outside site-rent absorption, or discretionary philanthropy with public benefit, real customer diffusion with retained windfall, or self-directed reinvestment claims with broad diffusion either |
| Maintained model hub, gated distributor, downstream orchestrator, or real co-controller for a governed deployment layer.[S10][S15][S16][S17][S20][S21] | often yes for the layer actually governed | national / market / local depending on the governed layer and burden location | distribution rent, hosted-access revenue, deployment-layer risk, reserve, and disclosure duties | bounded ordinary-to-moderate duties, rising when real control rises | do not erase upstream duties merely because a downstream actor also governs one layer |
| Nonhuman animals and ecological commons under human control or foreseeable exposure.[S7][S15][S17][S69][S79][S101][S102][S103] | no direct civic tax subjecthood | local, national, and sometimes cross-border commons claims | upstream harm, extraction, confinement, pollution, habitat-conversion, privileged access, and restoration-failure charges routed to controllers and beneficiaries | none directly; upstream moderate to steep | do not treat protected beings or ecosystems as remitters instead of protected sides of the ledger |
| Future artificial person, if rights-bearing and non-owned.[S18][S19][S20][S42][S43][S44][S101] | yes, but only after a real regime switch | ordinary person-regime scales | person-level ability-to-pay after a protected floor | zero floor, then ordinary progressive treatment | do not keep ownership or add a substrate surcharge once personhood is recognized |

## Five routing rules that survive scale changes

1. **Protect floors before collecting ordinary revenue.** A nominal rebate that most eligible people fail to receive is not a real floor.[S4][S31][S64][S65][S66]
2. **Tax rents, bottlenecks, inheritances, and harms before routine labor where possible.** That remains the archive's ordering rule from household to multinational scale.[S5][S8][S21][S93]
3. **For current AI, route through controllers, beneficiaries, bottlenecks, facilities, and harms.** Novelty is not a tax subject.[S10][S15][S19][S20][S22]
4. **Let the strongest jurisdictional anchor lead.** Situs for immovable bases, locality for place-bound burdens, destination or market access for final consumption and some borderless profit, and club floors where single states cannot hold mobile rents alone.[S3][S5][S9][S28]
5. **Standing changes liability routing.** If the supposed direct subject lacks standing, remedy, or representation, liability should usually sit with a controller, guardian, owner, or beneficiary instead.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44]
