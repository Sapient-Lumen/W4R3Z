# Uncertainty defaults and conservative routing

Use this card when morally decisive facts remain genuinely uncertain after ordinary fact-finding. **Uncertainty should not migrate burden onto protected people, current non-person machine systems, or the ecological commons.** It should slow direct subjection, keep collection moving at visible powerful anchors, and force reversible, reviewable choices rather than pretend certainty.[S4][S15][S16][S19][S20][S21][S23][S27][S31][S59][S83][S101]

This card is for real residual uncertainty, not for ambiguity strategically created by the actor who controls the records or the stack. When the better-evidenced party withholds or degrades evidence, presumptions should harden against that party rather than against the protected side.[S16][S17][S27][S39][S40][S41][S83]

## Default lanes

| Uncertain issue | Default |
|---|---|
| **Subjecthood / standing** — are we unsure whether the supposed taxpayer is a rights-bearing subject? | Do **not** impose direct civic liability first. Keep current LLMs, copied instances, and other present non-person systems controller-routed until the rights / standing / non-ownership bundle is real.[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44][S101] |
| **Protected-floor or incidence facts** — are we unsure who will really bear the burden? | Presume risk to the floor until shown otherwise. Use rebates, dividends, staged collection, hardship lanes, or redesign before loading the burden onto low-income people, tenants, weak workers, or dependent suppliers.[S4][S21][S31][S64][S65][S66] |
| **Catastrophic or non-compensable risk** — are we unsure whether pricing is morally enough? | Gate before price. Use approval, caps, bonds, reserves, insurance, monitoring, or refusal rather than letting a tax operate as a permission slip.[S15][S17][S53][S54] |
| **Real controller / grouped beneficiary** — are several visible actors plausibly in charge and the map is incomplete? | Keep collection moving through provisional lead-controller filing, designated remittance, group presumptions, and bounded escrow for genuinely disputed shares; do not freeze liability just because the coalition is messy.[S16][S20][S21][S27][S34][S35][S83] |
| **Best base or valuation** — are we unsure how to measure the morally relevant gain or harm? | Prefer the more observable and less gameable base or proxy: rents, land, extraction, bottleneck profit, metered burden, or simple public proxies before fake-precision claims about total value, status, or interior model qualities.[S5][S16][S23][S25][S27] |
| **Jurisdiction / claim split** — are several scales plausibly entitled to tax the same activity? | Use a temporary non-duplicative split with creditability, destination or host-burden anchors, and later true-up rather than letting every level pile on or forcing the weakest jurisdiction to surrender by default.[S3][S9][S15][S23][S28] |
| **Duration and policy confidence** — are novelty, emergency, or rapid technical change still high? | Add review triggers, expiry, conversion paths, and explicit reopening conditions. Under uncertainty, a reversible tax is usually more just than a permanent one with no review architecture.[S59][S60][S61][S62][S63] |

## Cross-cutting rule

When uncertainty remains, the archive's default is:

1. **do not create a new direct taxpayer without the rights bundle,**
2. **do not push unresolved burdens downward onto protected humans,**
3. **do not let unresolved catastrophic risk be priced as though it were ordinary harm,**
4. **do not reward evidence control by the stronger actor,**
5. **and do not let uncertainty stop all collection when a stronger provisional anchor is visible.**

That means uncertainty should usually produce **controller-side provisionality, floor-side protection, simpler observable bases, and tighter review**, not paralysis and not coercion against the weak.[S4][S15][S16][S20][S21][S23][S27][S31][S59][S83]

## Compression rule

If a proposal or edge case can be resolved by this card plus one existing routing file, **do not add another framework note**. The archive should not regrow by converting every factual unknown into a new doctrine file.
