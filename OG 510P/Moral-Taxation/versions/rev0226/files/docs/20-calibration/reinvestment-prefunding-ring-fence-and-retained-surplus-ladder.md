# Reinvestment / prefunding ring-fence / retained-surplus ladder

## Question in one sentence

Given the archive's closed rule that **self-directed reinvestment usually remains retained surplus, while only real ring-fenced prefunding for outside or public obligations can narrow a downstream beneficiary-side windfall claim**, **what is the smallest workable ladder for deciding when claimed capex, model spend, safety spend, reserves, or expansion should still be taxed as retained exceptional surplus, when some locked prefunding should be netted first, and when timing tools rather than base-erasure are the right answer?**[S10][S11][S15][S16][S17][S18][S21][S22][S23][S24][S47][S48][S49][S67][S83]

## Minimal workable ladder

| Rung | Facts on the ground | Fiscal answer |
|---|---|---|
| 0. growth-rhetoric lane | the story mostly rests on ordinary capex, compute, model spend, market expansion, treasury reserves, or discretionary safety budgets while exceptional margins remain | ignore the reinvestment defense; keep the beneficiary-side lane on retained gain.[S10][S11][S18][S21][S22][S67][S83] |
| 1. reversible-reserve lane | the funds sit in internal reserves, board-controlled budgets, or projects that can still be repurposed, delayed, pledged, or distributed | treat the amount as retained surplus; timing relief may still be allowed, but not base erasure.[S18][S21][S23][S24][S47][S48][S49][S67][S83] |
| 2. mixed prefunding case | some amount is genuinely segregated for worker transition, cleanup, safety, or other outside-facing obligations, but meaningful board-controlled option value remains | net only the truly locked prefunding slice first, then tax the retained exceptional residual.[S15][S16][S17][S18][S21][S22][S47][S48][S49][S67][S83] |
| 3. real ring-fenced prefunding lane | funds are legally or operationally segregated, tied to a live obligation or permit condition, non-supplanting, and not realistically reversible into private extraction | stand down the beneficiary-side charge on that locked slice; keep any remaining residual and timing review separate.[S15][S16][S17][S18][S21][S47][S48][S49][S83] |
| 4. timing-not-immunity lane | the claim is morally real but the project is mid-build, mid-retrofit, or cash-constrained | keep the base, but use instalments, staged true-up, capped deferral, or other timing tools instead of zeroing the claim.[S21][S23][S24][S47][S48][S49][S83] |
| 5. extraction override | buybacks, special dividends, affiliate transfers, reserve releases, or management extraction coexist with the alleged prefunding story | collapse any concession back toward the retained-surplus lane unless the locked slice remains truly untouchable.[S18][S21][S34][S35][S47][S48][S49][S67][S83] |

## Settings that matter most

### 1. Net only truly locked prefunding

Count only amounts that are genuinely segregated from ordinary private option value: required safety reserves, cleanup trusts, decommissioning funds, locked worker-transition funds, or similarly bounded vehicles. Ordinary capex plans, internal reserves, and board-approved growth budgets do not count merely because management says they are prudent.[S15][S16][S17][S18][S21][S47][S48][S49][S67][S83]

### 2. Keep the window obligation-linked and reviewable

Prefunding relief is strongest when tied to a visible obligation, permit, or bounded transition episode. Use renewal checks, release rules, and margin re-tests so a one-time commitment does not harden into permanent immunity for later retained surplus.[S15][S16][S17][S18][S21][S47][S48][S49][S67][S83]

### 3. Mixed cases should split, not relabel

The clean answer is usually not to choose between “all reinvestment” and “all retained windfall.” It is to identify the slice that is already locked to outside or public duties, then tax only the still-retained exceptional residual.[S15][S16][S17][S18][S21][S22][S47][S48][S49][S67][S83]

### 4. Timing can soften payment without dissolving liability

Where the retained residual is real but cash timing is awkward because assets are still being built or reserves are being staged, use instalments, capped deferral, staged true-up, or security-backed timing tools. Liquidity can change the schedule without erasing the base.[S21][S23][S24][S47][S48][S49][S83]

## Three anti-patterns

1. **growth-halo laundering** — treating any capex, R&D, expansion, or discretionary safety spend as if it had already socialized the gain,[S10][S11][S18][S21][S22][S67][S83]
2. **reserve cosplay** — calling an internal treasury cushion or reversible project budget a public-duty fund,[S18][S21][S23][S24][S47][S48][S49][S67][S83]
3. **timing-to-zero drift** — turning a cashflow or staging problem into a claim that no liability exists at all.[S21][S23][S24][S47][S48][S49][S83]
