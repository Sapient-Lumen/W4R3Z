## Current judgment in one paragraph

The archive's current answer is **rent-first, harm-first, floor-protecting, incidence-aware, filing-parity-aware, non-waiver-aware, duplicate-screening, capacity-aware, timing-aware, real-value-aware, standing-aware, personhood-sensitive, and substrate-neutral**. Humans remain the default direct tax persons; ordinary human earnings default to the individual, with dependents and care recognized through transfers, services, and bounded means tests rather than compulsory jointness. Current AI should mostly change **what gets taxed** — externalities, bottlenecks, automated windfalls, cross-border shifting, controller-side concentration, and where justified concentrated beneficiary-side gains — not **who counts as a direct civic taxpayer**; direct AI tax personhood begins only after a real rights / standing / non-ownership regime switch, copy count or swarm size does not by itself multiply civic taxpayers before that switch, ordinary prompting, ordinary use, thin resale, delegated operation, outside oversight, risk finance, commercial channel intermediation, third-party tool provision, or ordinary training-input supply does not by itself make the downstream actor, the state, the reviewer, the insurer, the billing intermediary, the execution-rail provider, or the corpus / benchmark supplier the upstream controller, where several actors each look partly controller-like primary status follows durable governance, continuation power, and residual upside rather than mere visibility, collective governance without a single dominant owner still routes through the real controller group, and the controller-boundary synthesis compresses that family into a fast ranking rule; proof architecture should usually ask only for the smallest morally sufficient fact, reuse narrow credentials where possible, keep ordinary compliance and relief reachable through plural rather than captive private channels, shift contested hidden facts toward the actor with superior access to them, and escalate to purpose-bounded matching rather than raw-data defaulting; strategic opacity should harden presumptions more than ordinary uncertainty; controller maps should mark which facts are observed, inferred, unknown, or only temporarily imputed so provisional filing does not launder guesswork into certainty, should usually preserve settled fields while correcting only the disputed issue, version resets, renames, checkpoint migrations, or affiliate transfers do not by themselves erase controller continuity, and temporary step-in command can itself create controller duties for the governed layer and period. Passive infrastructure, finance, channels, and tool layers can be a rent target, remittance point, or safety / host-burden anchor without thereby becoming the primary AI controller, static open-weight publication does not create endless controller liability even though maintained hubs and gated distribution can carry bounded upstream duties, and durable downstream customization, mission-specific commissioning, live operational governance, or public-body deployment can create real co-controller duties when an actor materially governs retrieval, tool permissions, system scaffolds, safety-policy posture, service levels, approvals, or ordinary deployment choices. When controller facts remain unresolved after ranking, proof, mapping, visibility sorting, bounded preservation, and proportionate verification, keep collection moving through provisional lead-controller filing, bounded escrow for genuinely disputed shares, short contest windows, and later true-up rather than freezing remittance or hardening convenience into final liability; treat attested controller maps as correction-first instruments, give them bounded review and bounded reopening, keep controller-map evidence event-focused and retention-bounded, reuse settled maps by versioned packet and delta update when the same governance facts recur, and do not give stale, selectively incomplete, strategically false, or post-notice-spoliated packets any safe harbor against stronger presumptions, sanctions, or grouping.
