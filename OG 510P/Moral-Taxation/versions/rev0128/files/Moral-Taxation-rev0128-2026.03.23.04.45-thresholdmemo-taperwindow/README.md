## Current judgment in one paragraph

The archive's current answer is **rent-first, harm-first, floor-protecting, incidence-aware, timing-aware, real-value-aware, standing-aware, and personhood-sensitive**. Humans remain the default direct tax persons; ordinary human earnings default to the individual, with dependents and care recognized through transfers, services, and bounded means tests rather than compulsory jointness. Current AI should mostly change **what gets taxed** — externalities, bottlenecks, automated windfalls, and cross-border shifting — not **who counts as a direct civic taxpayer**; direct AI tax personhood begins only after a real rights / standing / non-ownership regime switch.
