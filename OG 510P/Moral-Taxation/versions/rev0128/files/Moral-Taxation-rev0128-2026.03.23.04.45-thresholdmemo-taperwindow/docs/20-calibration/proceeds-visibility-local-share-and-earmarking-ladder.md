# Proceeds visibility, local-share, and earmarking ladder

## Question in one sentence

Given the archive's closed rule that **where revenue goes is part of the tax itself**, **what is the smallest workable ladder for deciding when ordinary general-revenue treatment is enough, when visible floor-first recycling or local sharing is required, when a dedicated reciprocity or public-capacity fund is justified, and when ring-fenced reserve treatment is morally required?**[S1][S4][S7][S18][S22][S69][S78][S79]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — general revenue almost always | send nearly all proceeds to general revenue and trust later budgeting to handle repair, floor protection, and reciprocity.[S1][S78] | Reject: too weak where the tax is morally sold as repair, cushioning, or burden-sharing. |
| B — hard earmark everything | lock each stream into a silo with little room for equalisation, substitution, or broader budget trade-offs.[S69][S78] | Reject: mistakes legibility for justice and traps money away from higher-priority floors. |
| C — visible-priority routing ladder | sort proceeds into general-revenue, repair / floor-first, local-share plus equalisation, dedicated reciprocity / capacity, or ring-fenced reserve lanes.[S1][S4][S7][S18][S22][S69][S78][S79] | Adopt. |
| D — negotiated side-payment routing | turn proceeds into siting bargains, incumbent deals, or political hush-money pools built outside ordinary routing rules.[S7][S15][S79] | Reject: launders concentrated rents and weakens equal treatment. |

## Five-rung ladder

1. **general-revenue lane** — use for broad civic taxes whose moral case does not depend on a named burdened group, place, or risk class,[S1][S18][S78]
2. **visible repair / floor-first lane** — use where legitimacy depends on cushioning predictable losers, removing regressivity, or compensating near-term hardship,[S4][S18][S22][S69]
3. **local-share plus equalisation lane** — use where burdens are strongly place-bound and some material local routing is morally prior but should still sit beside national pooling,[S7][S15][S69][S79]
4. **dedicated reciprocity / public-capacity lane** — use where the tax is defended as sharing concentrated rent or repaying common inputs through transition, oversight, public options, or broad capability building,[S11][S13][S14][S22]
5. **ring-fenced reserve or trust lane** — use where the money was collected for cleanup, compensation, incident response, or another risk class that would be morally defeated if pooled away before the claim matures.[S16][S17][S53][S54]

## Provisional recommendation

Adopt **Option C — the visible-priority routing ladder** as the archive's default calibration for proceeds.[S1][S4][S7][S18][S22][S69][S78][S79]

Presumption:

- use **general revenue** only where the tax is already justified as ordinary civic finance rather than by a named repair, burden, or reciprocity story,
- require **visible recycling** where regressivity correction, hardship cushioning, or transition legitimacy is carrying part of the moral defense,
- require **local shares** where burdens are strongly place-bound, but usually pair them with equalisation rather than let them replace it,
- justify **dedicated public-capacity funds** only where the tax is genuinely defended as rent sharing, public-input reciprocity, or transition finance,
- and require **ring-fencing** where the charge was collected for compensation, remediation, reserve integrity, or supervised cleanup rather than ordinary budget support.

That is the narrowest workable setting because it blocks both sinkhole budgeting and silo worship.

For AI-era taxes, the destination implication is practical: ordinary controller-side receipts can still flow through general revenue when their defense is ordinary finance, but automation-rent or public-input reciprocity claims often belong in the **dedicated reciprocity / public-capacity lane**, and catastrophic cleanup or incident-response charges belong in the **ring-fenced reserve lane**. Taxes on concentrated AI rents should not quietly reappear as incumbent subsidy loops.[S11][S13][S14][S16][S17][S22]

## Default destination table

| Base or context | Default routing rung | Why this rung usually fits | Archive warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| broad consumption taxes | visible repair / floor-first lane | the main moral vulnerability is regressivity, so some visible rebate, transfer, or basic-service offset should be legible before the rest disappears into general revenue.[S4][S18][S69] | do not defend a broad-base tax with relief that arrives too late or only to high-capacity claimants. |
| carbon, congestion, water-stress, or similar corrective taxes | visible repair / floor-first lane, often with local-share overlay | legitimacy depends partly on cushioning households and helping burdened places adapt while the price signal works.[S6][S7][S69][S78] | do not call the tax corrective while leaving exposed households or host localities to self-insure. |
| land, extraction, data-centre siting, or other place-bound rent and burden charges | local-share plus equalisation lane | the burden and bargaining asymmetry are local enough that some place-bound routing is morally prior, though the common-rent element still supports wider sharing.[S5][S7][S15][S18][S79] | do not let local shares become pure side-payments that excuse wider inequality or weak national standards. |
| AI automation rents or public-input reciprocity contributions | dedicated reciprocity / public-capacity lane | the moral defense often points toward worker transition, oversight capacity, public options, or broad capability building rather than undifferentiated budget use.[S11][S13][S14][S22] | do not recycle proceeds back into dominant labs, clouds, or incumbent subsidy loops. |
| bonds, reserve fees, incident-response charges, cleanup levies | ring-fenced reserve or trust lane | the point of collection is to ensure future compensation, remediation, or reserve integrity before failure or insolvency.[S16][S17][S53][S54] | pooled budgeting defeats the point if victims or cleanup later compete with ordinary spending. |
| ordinary broad personal or corporate income taxation | general-revenue lane unless a specific repair promise has been made | these taxes usually finance the general civic bundle rather than a named burdened class.[S1][S18][S78] | do not manufacture decorative earmarks where the moral case is already ordinary public finance. |

## Anti-patterns

- **sinkhole budgeting** — the justification names repair or reciprocity, but the money cannot be tracked to any visible destination.[S1][S18][S22]
- **silo worship** — every tax stream is locked away even when pooling would better protect floors or equalisation.[S69][S78]
- **local hush money** — place-bound shares are used to buy acquiescence while national or cross-border burdens go unaddressed.[S7][S15][S79]
- **incumbent recycling** — taxes on concentrated rent quietly reappear as bespoke grants, discounted inputs, or procurement favors to the same actors.[S1][S22][S69]
- **manual reciprocity** — the state routinizes collection but leaves benefits discretionary, delayed, or application-heavy.[S4][S22][S69]

## What would change the recommendation

Tighten, loosen, or replace this ladder if:

- ordinary budget processes in a given system begin preserving visible repair and floor commitments without destination-specific rules,[S1][S18][S22]
- intergovernmental equalisation becomes strong enough that local extraction or siting burdens are being credibly answered without explicit local shares,[S7][S69][S79]
- dedicated reciprocity funds begin consistently outperforming or underperforming ordinary appropriations across sectors,[S22][S69][S78]
- or reserve / trust structures prove overused where ordinary public budgeting could protect victims and cleanup equally well without weakening credibility.[S16][S17][S53][S54][S78]
