# Burden salience, disclosure, and hidden-tax ladder

## Question in one sentence

Given the archive's closed rules that tax liability must remain **intelligible, auditable, and not morally laundered by opacity**, **what is the smallest workable ladder for deciding when ordinary withholding can stay low-friction, when line-item or bill disclosure is required, when annual or periodic burden summaries are required, when tax expenditures and negotiated support must be made publicly visible, and when a proposal should be rejected because it depends on concealment?**[S27][S71][S77][S94][S95]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — seamless opacity by default | let withholding, platform remittance, utility charging, and negotiated tax-side support stay mostly invisible so long as the administration can reconcile the numbers later if challenged.[S27][S77] | Reject: confuses easy collection with justified burden and invites hidden taxation plus hidden subsidy. |
| B — universal line-item visibility for everything | require explicit real-time line items or direct notices for nearly every tax, surcharge, withholding, contribution, and offset regardless of size, cadence, or burden.[S27][S94][S95] | Reject: mistakes maximal visibility for useful legibility and turns routine systems into receipt theater. |
| C — tiered salience and disclosure ladder | sort taxes and tax-side supports into ambient withholding, line-item billing, periodic burden summaries, budget / registry disclosure, or redesign / reject for concealment.[S27][S71][S77][S94][S95] | Adopt. |
| D — strategic concealment for politically difficult taxes | treat low salience as a design virtue whenever a tax is efficient or corrective but unpopular.[S94][S95] | Reject: a tax or subsidy that works only while hidden fails democratic and distributive review. |

## Five-rung ladder

1. **ambient withholding lane** — allow routine payroll, account-side, or platform-side collection where readable pay-period or transaction records plus a clean year-end reconstruction path preserve contestability,[S27][S77]
2. **line-item invoice or bill lane** — require visible bill-time disclosure where the tax signal, burden split, or public-versus-private price distinction matters,[S3][S27][S94][S95]
3. **periodic burden-summary lane** — require annual or periodic summaries where layered charges, multiple jurisdictions, or embedded collection would otherwise hide the total burden,[S27][S77][S94]
4. **budget / registry disclosure lane** — require public visibility for abatements, credits, guarantees, concessions, and negotiated support so hidden negative taxes can be compared with direct spending,[S71]
5. **no-concealment lane** — redesign or bar proposals whose viability depends on workers, consumers, firms, or host localities not being able to identify the tax, the subsidy, or the burdened counterparties.[S27][S71][S94][S95]

## Provisional recommendation

Adopt **Option C — the tiered salience and disclosure ladder** as the archive's default visibility calibration.[S27][S71][S77][S94][S95]

Presumption:

- allow **ambient withholding** for ordinary recurring liabilities only when readable records make later reconstruction practical,
- require **line-item disclosure** where the charge is part of the intended price signal or would otherwise masquerade as a private fee,
- require **periodic burden summaries** where many embedded charges or jurisdictions would otherwise hide the total burden,
- require **budget-side visibility** for tax expenditures, guarantees, abatements, and negotiated support packages,
- and reject **concealment as strategy**: if a proposal works only while payers or affected places cannot identify the burden or offsetting support, redesign it.

That is the narrowest workable setting because it preserves collection practicality without letting either positive taxes or hidden negative taxes survive by stealth.

For AI-era taxes, the visibility implication is practical: controller-side levies can be collected smoothly through cloud, lab, platform, utility, or payroll-adjacent rails, but the trigger, base, and proceeds cannot remain rhetorical fog. If a levy is defended as burden repair, public-input reciprocity, or local host compensation, those claims need readable public packets rather than silent backend extraction.[S10][S15][S22][S69]

## Default salience table

| Context or base | Default rung | Why this usually fits | Archive warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| ordinary wage withholding and social contributions | ambient withholding lane | recurring payroll systems can collect efficiently, but workers still need readable pay-period and year-end records.[S27][S77] | do not let payroll coding turn liability, floor protection, or refund rights into unreadable backend noise. |
| broad VAT / sales tax and corrective consumption taxes | line-item invoice or bill lane | the burden and price signal often matter at the point of sale, and visible tax amounts can improve accountability.[S3][S95] | do not use line items as a substitute for floor protection or for repairing bad incidence. |
| land, property, and local public-finance charges | line-item bill plus visible service / local-share explanation | compliance and legitimacy improve when taxpayers can see the basis of the bill and the public goods or local services linked to it.[S94] | do not rely on visibility alone where valuation, liquidity, or equalisation problems remain unresolved. |
| utility-linked, congestion, extraction, or data-centre host-burden charges | line-item bill or public tariff lane, often with periodic host-burden summary | the charge competes with private price narratives and should remain visible to affected users or places.[S7][S15][S94] | do not bury public-burden recovery inside opaque fees that look like ordinary vendor pricing. |
| tax expenditures, abatements, guarantees, negotiated tax packages | budget / registry disclosure lane | these instruments are otherwise less visible than direct spending and can escape fiscal constraints.[S71] | hidden negative taxes should not be easier to create than openly appropriated spending. |
| controller-side AI exceptional levies or public-input reciprocity contributions | periodic burden summary plus public trigger / proceeds packet | the levy is often justified by concentrated rents, bottlenecks, or public-input claims that need visible trigger logic and visible use of proceeds.[S10][S22][S69] | do not sell a novel levy through rhetoric while hiding its trigger, base, or destination. |

## Anti-patterns

- **seamless taking, silent state** — the administration collects easily but never gives the subject a usable account of what happened.[S27][S77]
- **line-item theater** — a bill shows a tax amount, but the base, valuation date, correction path, or offsetting support remains opaque.[S27][S94]
- **negative-tax invisibility** — subsidies, abatements, guarantees, and concessions stay off-stage while explicit taxes take all the scrutiny.[S71]
- **private-price masquerade** — public charges are bundled into platform, utility, or cloud pricing so public burden and accountability disappear together.[S77][S95]
- **visibility without usability** — the payer technically receives information, but only in codes, formats, or fragmented channels that defeat reconstruction and appeal.[S27][S94]

## What would change the recommendation

Tighten, loosen, or replace this ladder if:

- evidence shows that some currently embedded taxes can stay lower-salience without weakening contestability or democratic review,
- durable user testing shows that lighter summary packets outperform bill-time line items in some contexts without increasing hidden incidence,
- governments begin publishing tax expenditures, guarantees, and negotiated packages with enough budget visibility that hidden-negative-tax concerns materially fall,
- or new collection rails make visibility easier to preserve alongside seamless collection than they do today.[S27][S71][S77][S94][S95]
