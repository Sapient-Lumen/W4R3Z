# Compliance cost, assisted filing, and preparer-dependence ladder

## Question in one sentence

Given the archive's closed rule that **tax administration should stay low-friction, intelligible, and not quietly converted into a private gatekeeper market**, **what is the smallest workable ladder for deciding when ordinary self-service is enough, when prefilling or embedded filing should replace manual reconstruction, when public assisted filing is justified, and when a proposal should be redesigned because honest compliance still depends on paid preparers, proprietary software, or repeated expert mediation?**[S26][S27][S38][S64][S65][S66][S68][S96][S97]

Route: treat compliance cost as part of tax burden; prefer prefilling, embedded collection, public assistance, and machine-readable public interfaces before ordinary civic liability is allowed to drift into a private preparer or software market.[S26][S27][S38][S64][S65][S66][S68][S96][S97]

## Option scan

| Option | Shape | Archive verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — do-it-yourself filing by default | treat ordinary self-service as adequate for nearly all taxes so long as forms, portals, and guidance are technically public.[S27][S38][S65] | Reject: public access to a form does not prove low burden, real intelligibility, or freedom from paid-expert dependence. |
| B — private preparer and software market default | accept that most people and small firms will rely on paid preparers, payroll vendors, accountants, or tax software, and treat state responsibility as satisfied if those intermediaries exist.[S27][S96] | Reject: turns ordinary compliance into a secondary market purchase and creates gatekeeper rents. |
| C — compliance-cost and assistance ladder | sort proposals into ordinary self-service, prefilled / embedded, public assisted-filing, bounded credentialed-intermediary, or redesign / block lanes.[S26][S27][S38][S64][S65][S66][S68][S96][S97] | Adopt. |
| D — mandatory AI or software autopilot | push routine filing, claiming, and correction through proprietary software or AI copilots by default and treat human understanding as optional backup.[S16][S17][S44] | Reject: assistance may be useful, but compulsory machine mediation is not a substitute for legible law, public help, and contestable outcomes. |

## Five-rung ladder

1. **ordinary self-service lane** — use only where the obligation is genuinely short, stable, and intelligible without expert help or heroic time costs,[S26][S38][S65][S97]
2. **prefilled, embedded, or administration-generated lane** — use where withholding data, third-party reporting, registries, property rolls, payroll systems, e-invoicing, or similar natural-system data already make manual reconstruction needless,[S66][S68][S77][S97]
3. **public assisted-filing and multichannel lane** — use where facts are manageable but ordinary taxpayers reasonably need phone, in-person, offline, language-accessible, or guided digital help to comply without private gatekeepers,[S64][S65][S97]
4. **bounded credentialed-intermediary lane** — use for genuinely complex, high-value, cross-border, dispute-heavy, or entity-structure matters where specialist help is proportionate but should not become the hidden default,[S27][S38][S96]
5. **redesign-or-block lane** — use where ordinary lawful compliance still depends on routine paid preparers, proprietary software, or repeated expert mediation.[S26][S38][S65][S96][S97]

## Provisional recommendation

Adopt **Option C — the compliance-cost and assistance ladder** as the archive's default calibration for live filing-burden disputes.[S26][S27][S38][S64][S65][S66][S68][S96][S97]

Presumption:

- stay in the **ordinary self-service lane** only where an ordinary person or genuinely small firm can comply without paid help and without a hidden time-tax,
- move quickly to the **prefilled, embedded, or administration-generated lane** wherever the administration already sees most of the relevant facts,
- use the **public assisted-filing and multichannel lane** when complexity, language, disability, connectivity, or confidence barriers make pure self-service unrealistic,
- tolerate the **bounded credentialed-intermediary lane** only for genuinely complex or exceptional matters,
- and move to **redesign-or-block** whenever ordinary taxpayers still need routine purchase of expertise, software, or platform mediation merely to comply honestly.

That is the narrowest workable setting because it preserves room for genuine complexity while refusing to treat paid gatekeeper dependence as a morally neutral feature of ordinary taxation.

For AI-era administration, the implication is practical: **AI filing assistants, tax copilots, and embedded accounting tools may be useful, but they cannot count as the only lawful path for ordinary compliance, correction, or claiming. Assistance is allowed; compulsory dependence is not.**[S16][S17][S44][S65][S96]

## Default assistance table

| Context or base | Default rung | Why this usually fits | Archive warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| ordinary wage income, routine withholding reconciliation, and simple credits | prefilled, embedded, or administration-generated lane | the administration often already sees wages, withholding, and many routine offsets, so manual re-entry is wasteful.[S65][S66][S68] | do not make ordinary workers buy software or preparer time merely to release a refund or confirm data the state already has. |
| simple local property, land, and recurring public charges | administration-generated bill with public-help fallback | assessments, cadastres, and billing systems often permit direct billing without private reconstruction.[S25][S65][S97] | do not call a tax “simple” if the valuation logic remains too obscure for bill recipients to check or challenge. |
| genuinely small firms in simplified or presumptive regimes | embedded or assisted lane, often with narrow bookkeeping defaults | simplified regimes are justified partly to keep compliance costs proportionate and avoid expert dependence.[S26][S38][S65][S97] | do not let “simplicity” mean permanent threshold camping or dump all complexity downstream once the firm grows slightly. |
| recurring VAT or payroll remittance where system-to-system data already exists | embedded natural-system lane | e-invoicing, payroll systems, and connected business software can reduce duplicate data entry and routine error.[S66][S68][S77] | seamless remittance is not an excuse to hide liability, refund paths, or correction rights. |
| cross-border structures, disputes, large-group reporting, wealth valuation, or controller-chain questions | bounded credentialed-intermediary lane | some matters are complex enough that specialist assistance is realistic and proportionate.[S27][S38][S96] | do not let high-end complexity justify copying the same burden onto ordinary households or small firms. |
| current AI controller-side taxes and reporting stacks | embedded structured reporting first, then bounded specialist help | labs, clouds, and large deployers can bear structured reporting, but even here the archive prefers machine-readable public interfaces over consultant-dependent black boxes.[S10][S15][S16][S17][S65][S68] | do not let novel AI taxes become a compliance moat that only major incumbents and professional service firms can navigate. |

## Anti-patterns

- **DIY absolutism** — assuming public forms prove public accessibility.[S27][S38][S65]
- **preparer tax by stealth** — the statute says one thing, but the real annual burden is the private fee needed to comply safely.[S96]
- **software lock-in** — ordinary compliance depends on a proprietary product, vendor channel, or platform account with no real public alternative.[S65][S68]
- **public help as afterthought** — multichannel assistance, accessibility, and offline correction arrive only after large non-compliance or exclusion appears.[S64][S65][S97]
- **expertized ordinariness** — routine civic obligations are designed as if every filer were a professionally advised business group.[S26][S38][S96]

## What would change the recommendation

Tighten, loosen, or replace this ladder if:

- administrations can move far more ordinary cases into accurate prefilling or administration-generated billing without creating new rights or privacy defects,[S66][S68][S97]
- public assisted-filing and multichannel support become robust enough that digital-only self-service is no longer the hidden bottleneck for ordinary compliance,[S64][S65][S97]
- preparer oversight, competence standards, and liability rules become strong enough that bounded intermediary dependence is less risky than the archive now assumes,[S96]
- or trustworthy AI assistance becomes auditable, optional, affordable, and publicly backstopped enough that it reduces rather than redistributes compliance burden.[S16][S17][S44]
