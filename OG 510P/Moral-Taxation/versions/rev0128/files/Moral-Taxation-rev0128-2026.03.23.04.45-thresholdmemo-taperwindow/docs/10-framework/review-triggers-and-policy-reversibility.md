# Review triggers and policy reversibility

This note answers the question that comes **after** a tax is morally targeted and administrable but **before** it can claim durable legitimacy over time: **what evidence should reopen the rule, who reviews it, when does review happen, and how can the policy tighten, loosen, convert, or end without waiting for inertia or capture to decide?** The archive's stable answer is to pair uncertain, emergency, negotiated, or fast-moving taxes with a visible review architecture: measurable purpose, routine cadence, trigger review, outsider challenge paths, and a credible recalibration / conversion / sunset grammar.[S15][S54][S59][S60][S61][S62][S63]

## Core rule

Route review through five things in this order:

1. **a falsifiable purpose**,
2. **routine monitoring plus deeper periodic review**,
3. **explicit trigger events that force early reconsideration**,
4. **contestable evidence rather than official self-certification alone**,
5. and **a pre-named path to tighten, loosen, convert, sunset, or repeal**.[S59][S60][S61][S62][S63]

A review architecture is good when it learns quickly enough to catch drift, slowly enough to avoid headline whiplash, and concretely enough that a tax cannot survive forever on prestige, panic, or incumbent convenience alone.

## Default review anchors by base or context

| Base or context | Best first review anchor | Why this anchor usually wins | Main warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Broad personal and payroll taxes | bracket drift, floor protection, filing burden, and take-up of offsets | mature taxes still drift through inflation, household effects, and claim friction | do not treat stable collection as proof that the burden is still justly distributed.[S4][S21][S55][S56][S57][S64][S65][S66] |
| Broad consumption taxes with rebates | rebate adequacy, pass-through, non-take-up, and inflation interaction | headline neutrality can hide timing and floor failures | a formally compensated VAT can become regressive if prices move faster than offsets or if households miss the offset entirely.[S3][S4][S21][S64][S65][S66] |
| Property, land, and immovable-base taxes | revaluation cadence, local equalisation, and deferral use | place-bound taxes age mainly through stale values and political carve-outs | do not let old assessments, frozen caps, or special abatements become permanent hidden redistribution upward.[S5][S23][S25][S29][S30] |
| Small-firm presumptive or simplified regimes | threshold camping, graduation, and competitive distortion | simplifications are most vulnerable to becoming shelters | review whether the simple regime still serves genuinely small actors or has become a tax-avoidance habitat.[S26][S27][S34][S35] |
| Large corporate and cross-border profit taxes | shifting responses, minimum-tax integrity, and market-jurisdiction share | mobile profit rules face rapid arbitrage and treaty interaction | do not review receipts alone while ignoring relocation theatre, booking shifts, and incidence leakage.[S9][S21][S28][S34][S35] |
| Corrective taxes on carbon, congestion, utilities, and other harms | harm reduction, household cushioning, and local burden | the tax is justified partly by what it changes, not only what it collects | do not keep a corrective tax unchanged if the harm map, technology, or protection package has materially shifted.[S6][S7][S15][S22][S69][S70] |
| AI-facility, model, or deployment-linked taxes | concentration, incident rates, local utility burden, worker transition outcomes, and capability drift | AI-linked taxes face unusually rapid technical and market movement | a frozen levy can underreact to capability jumps or overreach after the factual predicate weakens.[S15][S17][S54][S61][S62][S67][S70] |
| Temporary, emergency, or negotiated tax measures | sunset date, conversion trigger, and outsider audit | exceptional taxes most need a visible exit path | auto-renewal by administrative fatigue is not a review architecture.[S59][S60][S61][S63] |
| Future rights-bearing artificial person | ordinary person-regime review plus anti-discrimination safeguards | once a machine subject is a person, review should look like person-regime review, not equipment monitoring | do not preserve substrate surcharges or owner-side substitution merely because the old regime was easier to administer.[S18][S19][S20][S42][S44] |

## Routing rules

### 1. Name what success and failure would look like before launch

A tax should say what it is trying to change in observable terms: revenue adequacy, burden protection, emissions, congestion, rent capture, local repair, worker transition, concentration, or public-capacity gains. If the proposal cannot name evidence that would count against itself, later review becomes theatre.[S59][S60][S61]

### 2. Separate routine review from trigger review

Use two clocks: a routine cadence for monitoring and deeper evaluation, and a trigger path that reopens the rule when inflation, local burden, pass-through, concentration, capability jumps, avoidance, or rights impacts cross a named threshold. Calendar-only review is too slow for fast-moving systems; trigger-only review is too noisy for ordinary administration.[S15][S54][S60][S61][S62]

### 3. Review incidence, take-up, and hidden offsets, not just receipts

A morally serious review asks whether the burden actually stayed with the intended target, whether promised rebates or repair reached people in time, and whether abatements, utility discounts, or other hidden negatives quietly canceled the tax. Treasury totals are part of the picture, not the whole picture.[S21][S29][S30][S64][S65][S66]

### 4. Match review intensity to uncertainty and concentrated power

The more novel the base, the more fragile the measurement, the higher the catastrophe downside, and the more concentrated the affected power, the stronger the review architecture should be. This is why negotiated abatements, emergency taxes, and AI-linked charges need tighter trigger discipline than mature wage withholding does.[S15][S17][S59][S60][S61][S63]

### 5. Give outsiders a way to contest the official success story

Review should not belong only to the remitter, regulator, or treasury. Workers, residents, low-income payers, localities, and other materially affected outsiders need a way to challenge the official narrative when the tax is drifting, being captured, or doing hidden distributive work.[S59][S60][S61]

### 6. Name the exit grammar in advance

When a tax is temporary, experimental, or factual-predicate-sensitive, publish what happens next if the evidence changes:

- **tighten** if harms, rents, or concentration are worse than expected,
- **loosen** if burden protection failed or the proxy overshot,
- **convert** if a temporary levy should become a standing instrument,
- **sunset** if the factual predicate disappears,
- or **repeal and replace** if the chosen base was the wrong one.[S59][S60][S61][S63]

### 7. Treat AI review as post-deployment governance, not only tax administration

For AI-linked taxes, review should track incident rates, near-misses, concentration, local grid and water burdens, worker transition outcomes, and capability drift alongside receipts and declared tax bases. NIST's AI RMF playbook treats monitoring, feedback, and decommissioning as live governance tasks; the tax side should not pretend those are someone else's problem.[S54][S62]

## Five anti-patterns

1. **Temporary-by-inertia** — emergency or experimental rules survive because no real exit path was named.
2. **Receipt fetishism** — collections are reviewed while incidence, exclusion, and hidden offsets are ignored.
3. **Trigger vagueness** — officials promise adaptive review but never specify what forces reconsideration.
4. **Incumbent self-audit** — the strongest beneficiaries define success for everyone else.
5. **Frozen novelty** — AI or negotiated-burden rules remain unchanged while the technology, burden map, or local stress profile changes quickly.[S15][S54][S59][S60][S61][S62][S63]

## Order of preference

When more than one review architecture is available, the archive usually prefers the one that is:

1. clearest about what evidence matters,
2. most able to detect incidence drift and hidden negative-tax reversal,
3. least dependent on self-report by concentrated incumbents alone,
4. fastest where risk and novelty are high,
5. and most explicit about how the rule can tighten, loosen, convert, or end.

This is why published metrics, routine monitoring, named trigger events, outsider challenge lanes, and finite sunset / conversion logic usually beat soft promises of "future evaluation" without thresholds, cadence, or exit grammar.
