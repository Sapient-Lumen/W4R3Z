# Classification arbitrage and substance-over-form routing

This note answers the question that comes **after** a morally attractive base has been chosen and **before** category labels are allowed to dissolve it: **when should tax law ignore the name of an arrangement and follow its real substance instead?** The archive's stable answer is that category choice is not morally innocent. A tax fails when it is easy to relabel labor as capital service, household income as firm flow, firm profit as fee flow, owned AI systems as fake independent persons, or high-risk deployment as low-risk commodity inference in order to escape the burden that the design was actually trying to place.[S9][S10][S15][S16][S17][S19][S20][S21][S28][S34][S35][S44]

## Core rule

Route classification disputes through this order:

1. **name the real moral variable first** — labor, rent, control, benefit, harm, congestion, inheritance, or ordinary consumption,
2. **ask who actually works, controls, bears downside, captures residual upside, and can stop or redesign the activity**,  
3. **treat labels, contracts, wrappers, and interface stories as rebuttable rather than decisive**,  
4. **preserve genuinely different arrangements when substance really differs, rather than collapsing everything into one category for convenience**,  
5. **keep reclassification legible, evidence-based, and reviewable**,  
6. and **refuse to manufacture personhood, independence, or low-risk status merely because a stronger actor benefits from the relabeling**.[S9][S19][S20][S21][S28][S34][S35][S44]

A good anti-arbitrage rule follows substance far enough to keep the chosen base intact, but no farther than necessary to avoid erasing real pluralism.

## Default routing by classification problem

| Classification problem | Best first substance test | Why this test usually wins | Main warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Worker labeled as independent contractor or platform seller | degree of control over schedule, pricing, tools, risk, and exit | ordinary labor can be stripped of protections and shifted into weaker tax categories by contract language alone | do not let payroll avoidance masquerade as entrepreneurial freedom when the worker's real dependence has not changed.[S21][S31][S32][S33][S44] |
| Household income routed through shells, family firms, or nominee vehicles | beneficial owner, control map, and who really uses or enjoys the income | household domination and tax avoidance can both hide behind formal entity lines | do not let person-based civic liability quietly turn into household shadowing or shell-mediated income splitting.[S31][S32][S33][S34][S35] |
| Corporate profit relabeled as royalties, management fees, cost-sharing, or affiliate service revenue | residual-profit and control analysis across the related-party group | paper invoice paths often tell a thinner story than who controls intangibles, financing, and market power | do not let accounting choreography defeat rent taxation or minimum-tax design.[S9][S21][S28][S34][S35] |
| Owned AI system presented as an independent vendor or proto-person | ownership, training / deployment control, shutdown power, beneficiary chain, and standing threshold | current AI systems remain tools or controlled stacks unless real rights-bearing personhood is crossed | do not let commercial actors manufacture fake machine independence to escape controller-side liability.[S10][S15][S16][S17][S19][S20] |
| High-risk deployment labeled as ordinary low-risk inference or generic software service | real capability, deployment context, externality map, and catastrophic-risk profile | risk labels can be gamed downward more easily than the underlying danger changes | do not let cosmetic service descriptions launder a system past precaution, bonding, or stronger oversight.[S15][S16][S17][S53][S54] |
| Local burden recast as remote or cloud-only activity | facility, energy, water, congestion, labor, and site dependence | place-bound burdens still exist even when revenue is booked elsewhere or the service is branded as global | do not let digital rhetoric erase local taxing claims for real infrastructure strain.[S3][S5][S15][S28][S69][S70] |

## Routing rules

### 1. Choose the category that best matches control, benefit, and risk

When category labels diverge from the real activity, prefer the classification that tracks who directs the conduct, captures the upside, and can internalize the downside. Tax morality cares about those facts more than about whichever label a stronger party can cheaply print into a contract.[S9][S16][S17][S20][S21][S28]

### 2. Keep anti-fragmentation and anti-relabeling distinct but coordinated

Fragmentation asks whether one real actor has been split into many paper actors. Relabeling asks whether the same activity has been moved into a softer category without any real change in substance. Both matter, but they are not the same failure mode. A proposal can group shells correctly and still fail if it rewards relabeling labor, rent, or AI control into a cheaper tax story.[S21][S28][S34][S35]

### 3. Do not manufacture machine personhood for tax convenience

Present LLMs and deployed AI systems may be technically autonomous in narrow senses while still lacking the standing bundle that would justify direct civic tax subjecthood. Until that threshold is actually crossed, owned or controlled systems should not be reclassified into independent taxpayers merely because doing so would reduce controller liability.[S10][S19][S20][S42][S43][S44]

### 4. Substance-over-form should not erase real difference

Not every contractor is a disguised employee, not every small firm is a payroll dodge, and not every automated system is an owned lab stack. The archive prefers the narrowest anti-relabeling rule that protects the chosen base without collapsing genuinely distinct arrangements into one compliance bucket.[S21][S26][S27][S31][S32][S33]

### 5. Risk tiers should follow real capability and deployment facts

The same discipline applies to corrective taxes, reserves, and frontier-AI levies. If the real system, workload, or deployment pattern creates the externality or catastrophic-risk profile, then cosmetic descriptions like "general compute," "ordinary inference," or "decision support only" do not settle the moral category.[S15][S16][S17][S53][S54]

### 6. Reclassification needs notice, explanation, and review

Substance-over-form cannot become a permission slip for arbitrary recharacterization by the state. If the administration reclassifies a worker, a flow of income, a controller chain, or an AI deployment category, it should name the operative test, the decisive facts, and the route for challenge or correction.[S16][S17][S23][S27][S44]

## Five anti-patterns

1. **Contract worship** — assuming the printed label decides the tax category even when control, risk, and dependency tell a different story.  
2. **Household laundering** — hiding person-based income or domination inside family entities, nominee accounts, or dependent-status defaults.  
3. **Fee-flow alchemy** — turning profit into deductible service or royalty payments through related-party choreography.  
4. **Fake machine independence** — presenting owned or tightly controlled AI systems as though they were separate civic persons or external vendors.  
5. **Risk downgrading by description** — calling a high-impact deployment a low-risk commodity service without changing the real capability or externality map.[S9][S10][S15][S16][S17][S19][S20][S21][S28][S31][S32][S33][S34][S35][S44]

## Order of preference

When more than one classification rule is available, the archive usually prefers the one that:

1. follows real control, benefit, and risk rather than the strongest party's chosen label,
2. preserves person-based treatment of ordinary human earnings unless genuine independence is present,
3. prevents profit, rent, or AI-control laundering through related-party or wrapper structures,
4. refuses to treat current AI systems as direct civic tax persons merely for convenience,
5. and remains legible enough that both taxpayers and administrators can say why the classification was chosen.

This is why classification integrity belongs in the narrow waist rather than living only as an open question or one step in the decision procedure.
