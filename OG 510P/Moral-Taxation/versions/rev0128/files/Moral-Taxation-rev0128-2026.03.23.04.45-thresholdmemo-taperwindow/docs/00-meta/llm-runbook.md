# LLM runbook

Future editors should preserve the archive's narrow waist.

## Reopen in clusters

1. **Gate and scope** — read `docs/10-framework/waist-closure-and-calibration-frontier.md`, `docs/00-meta/calibration-frontier-map.md`, and `docs/00-meta/calibration-memo-format.md` first. Add a new framework note only when a real routing rule is missing or a compression win is large enough to justify new waist surface.
2. **Founding answer** — reopen `docs/10-framework/ideal-taxation-by-class-context-and-species.md`, `docs/10-framework/class-context-species-matrix.md`, and `docs/10-framework/default-tax-stack-by-scale.md` for the archive's direct answer to the human / LLM / species question.
   Keep the founding-answer note top-light when editing it: one compact direct answer above the schedule is enough; spend extra bytes in the schedule, rules, or routes instead of a duplicate bullet summary.
3. **Constitution and routing** — start with `docs/10-framework/constitutional-principles.md`, then use `ARCHIVE_INDEX.md` and the `docs/10-framework/*` family map to reach only the routing file you need. Typical first branches are subjecthood and regime switch; incidence and regressivity; proceeds; timing, measurement, and real value; jurisdiction and international co-ordination; collection, administration, and enforcement; assessment units, standing, and anti-discrimination; and AI-trigger, wealth-backstop, review, downside, precaution, or net-stack guards.
4. **Calibration** — use `docs/00-meta/calibration-frontier-map.md` to choose the memo family, then open the matching ladder. Main families are AI-trigger ladders; cross-border and stack discipline; subject, boundary, and representation; administration, measurement, and enforcement; incidence, proceeds, timing, and thresholds; and loss, real value, reliance, failure, and review.
5. **Backstops and archive notes** — use `docs/10-framework/proposal-scorecard.md` for evaluation and the compact archive notes when a routing rule needs its thesis spine, especially `archive/101`, `108`, `110`, and `112–131`.

## Editing posture

- Keep `docs/10-framework/open-questions.md` in frontier-prompt form: short unresolved question, first calibration memo, then source cues — not repeated narrow-waist boilerplate on every line.
- Keep `docs/10-framework/decision-procedure.md` checklist-first: keep one short companion-routes block, not a long repeated route preamble.
- Keep `docs/10-framework/ideal-taxation-by-class-context-and-species.md` answer-first: one-screen schedule, short section intros, and compact `Route:` callouts rather than repeated framing prose.
- Keep `docs/10-framework/ai-exceptional-levy-trigger-routing.md` trigger-first and route-cluster-first.
- Keep `docs/00-meta/calibration-frontier-map.md` cluster-first and table-first: it should route to memo families, not regrow into nine long repeated mini-memos.
- Keep `docs/20-calibration/timing-cashflow-liquidity-deferral-and-prefunding-ladder.md` ladder-first: use companion routes, a compact option scan, and the five timing lanes instead of the older long calibration scaffold.
- Keep `docs/20-calibration/proceeds-visibility-local-share-and-earmarking-ladder.md` ladder-first: use companion routes, a compact option scan, and the five destination lanes instead of the older long calibration scaffold.
- Keep `docs/20-calibration/collection-anchor-choice-and-remittance-chain-ladder.md` ladder-first: use companion routes, a compact option scan, and the five anchor lanes instead of the older long calibration scaffold.
- Keep the central distinction stable: **current AI changes the tax-base map more than the tax-subject map**.
- Add new taxes only after naming the moral target: rent, harm, reciprocity, congestion, inheritance, windfall, or emergency finance.
- Prefer operational additions that sharpen class, context, and species treatment over commentary that merely restates settled doctrine.
- Use the strongest available collection anchor before pushing compliance onto weak downstream actors.
- Check final incidence, not just the remitting entity.
- Protect floors with visible rebates, services, transfers, or automaticity rather than exemption jungles and heroic claiming effort.
- Treat timing, liquidity, downside symmetry, and real-value honesty as constitutional substance rather than implementation detail.
- Treat catastrophic or non-compensable risk as a governance problem first; do not let tax become a permission slip where approval, caps, bonding, or refusal should come earlier.
- Judge proposals on the **net fiscal stack**: abatements, tax expenditures, discounted public inputs, and direct subsidies can cancel the headline levy.
- Prefer person-based human liability; do not silently turn the tax subject into the household when the real moral unit is the individual.
- Do not impose direct civic tax liability on current LLMs; route through controllers, owners, deployers, bottlenecks, beneficiaries, and externalities unless a real rights-bearing regime switch has occurred.
- If future artificial persons ever exist, no species surcharge: ownership must end and ordinary person-regime protections must arrive as a bundle.
- Before adding a new source, merge duplicates by URL or title and repoint citations rather than forking the registry.
- Keep `SOURCES.md` as a thin citation index and put any explanatory note in `SOURCES.json` rather than duplicating a blurb across both surfaces.
- Keep `archive/*.md` source cues thin; do not carry footnote tails for IDs cited only there.
- Keep markdown citation clusters, source-footnote blocks, the source registry, and machine-readable JSON surfaces compact and deduped.
- When a note uses `[S..]` citations, keep the matching local `[S..]:` link definitions at the bottom of that note; `SOURCES.md` is the registry target, not a substitute for local definitions.
- For high-traffic reopening links, prefer explicit markdown anchors over heading-slug luck so route surfaces survive title edits.
- Keep `CHANGELOG.md`, `START_HERE.md`, `ARCHIVE_INDEX.md`, the matrix, `proposal-scorecard.md`, and this runbook lean: route, table, checklist, and family map first; `CHANGELOG.md` should normally keep only the current detailed entry above a compact ledger, and `MANIFEST.json` should carry the exhaustive inventory.
