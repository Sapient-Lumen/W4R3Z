## Executive answer

### Humans

The ideal human tax regime:

- protects a **tax-free or negatively taxed subsistence floor**,
- defaults to **individual taxation of earnings and personhood claims**, while recognizing children, dependents, and caregivers through transfers and public provision rather than compulsory jointness,
- funds a **universal basic civic bundle** and negative-income support,
- taxes **land, natural-resource rents, monopoly rents, inheritance, and pollution** harder than wages,
- uses a **broad consumption base with visible rebates** rather than exemption jungles,
- keeps progressive personal taxes for genuinely high ability-to-pay,
- treats dynastic wealth as a stronger tax target than ordinary saving,
- checks **final burden-bearing**, not just statutory assignment,
- and routes proceeds toward floor protection, shared civic goods, and repair rather than fiscal sinkholes,
- rejects direct or indirect discrimination by protected status or status proxy, using direct measures of income, care load, harm, or control where possible,
- indexes or openly re-sets brackets, allowances, credits, loss relief, and long-lived thresholds where inflation would otherwise do hidden distributive work,
- while using withholding, instalments, or deferral where timing and liquidity would otherwise make a justified tax coercive, using transparent valuation/proxy rules where direct measurement is not honestly available, assigning taxing rights by situs, destination, market access, and local burden rather than by bookkeeping theater alone, judging the net fiscal stack rather than headline rates alone, defaulting to prospective notice and finite transition rather than surprise retroactivity or permanent grandfathering, refusing to treat catastrophic or irreversible risks as ordinary tax-priced permission slips, and naming in advance what evidence, cadence, and triggers would force the regime to tighten, loosen, convert, or expire rather than drift by inertia.

### Current LLMs and AI systems

The ideal current AI tax regime:

- does **not** impose a direct poll tax on models, parameters, or token streams,
- taxes the **controllers and beneficiaries** of AI systems,
- prices **energy, water, carbon, congestion, and incident risk** from training and deployment,
- taxes **AI-enabled windfalls and monopoly bottlenecks**,
- uses automation dividends to fund **transition insurance, training, and public AI capacity**,
- requires high-risk deployers to hold **bonds, reserves, or insurance**,
- routes AI-linked proceeds toward **worker transition, public capacity, and local repair**,
- indexes or openly resets **compute thresholds, bond amounts, registry fees, and safe harbors** where inflation or nominal drift would otherwise hollow out or silently intensify the regime,
- uses **withholding, instalments, deferral, ex ante bonds, disclosed proxy rules, bounded loss recognition, and low-friction or automatic floor-protecting rebates** where timing, liquidity, noisy measurement, or claim friction would otherwise distort justice,
- rejects stacks that can only be financed by shifting costs onto protected workers or low-income users,
- and gives first claim to siting jurisdictions for local burdens, to market jurisdictions for customer-linked profit, and to coordinated clubs for mobile rents that no single state can hold alone, while netting nominal AI taxes against abatements, utility discounts, and other hidden support that would otherwise cancel them, refusing to use taxes alone as a substitute for approval, tiering, bonding, or shutdown where deployment risk is catastrophic or non-compensable, and publishing review triggers plus conversion or sunset paths where the technology, burden map, or risk profile may shift quickly.

### Future artificial persons

If a machine system becomes a rights-bearing person, the regime changes category:

- it should no longer be treated as ownable equipment,
- it should receive a protected floor and legal standing,
- and it may then be taxed like other persons on **ability-to-pay and civic reciprocity**, not because it is a machine species.
