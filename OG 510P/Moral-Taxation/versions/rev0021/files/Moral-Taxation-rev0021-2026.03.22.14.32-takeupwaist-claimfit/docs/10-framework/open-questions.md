
# Open questions

The archive has not closed these questions.

1. **Automation dividend trigger:** what empirical threshold should trigger a dedicated AI windfall levy — payroll contraction, market concentration, margin expansion, or task displacement?
2. **Frontier compute threshold:** should special frontier-training levies be keyed to electricity, accelerator-hours, capability thresholds, or revenue at deployment?
3. **Public data commons finance:** when models draw disproportionate value from publicly funded text, images, and code, what is the best charging mechanism short of impossible per-token micro-accounting?
4. **AI personhood threshold:** what combination of autonomy, continuity, consciousness evidence, preference stability, and legal capacity would justify moving an artificial system from tool-regime to person-regime?[S19][S20]
5. **International coordination and apportionment:** what club design best links carbon floors, minimum taxes, and compute regulation while also assigning claims cleanly among siting, market, residence, and poorer jurisdictions without trapping weaker states in dependency?
6. **Care-load calibration after individualization:** once the archive defaults to individual liability plus dependent-aware support, what mix of refundable credits, childcare/care services, caregiver allowances, and optional filing adjustments best recognizes care without recreating second-earner traps or hidden dependency?[S31][S32][S33]
7. **Wealth-tax backstop conditions:** under what visibility and anti-avoidance conditions should an annual net-wealth tax supplement land, inheritance, and capital-income reform?
8. **Classification-arbitrage defense:** what are the cleanest anti-avoidance rules for preventing firms from relabeling labor, profits, or owned AI systems across categories to escape the intended tax base?[S9][S19][S20]
9. **Measurement and proxy discipline:** which valuation cadences, proxy hierarchies, and graduation rules best keep land, small-firm, and AI-related taxes measurable without fake precision or threshold camping?[S15][S16][S23][S25][S26][S27]
10. **Jurisdiction assignment:** what is the cleanest compact rule for splitting claims among data-centre host localities, national controller states, and customer-market jurisdictions in frontier AI without opening new no-tax space?[S3][S9][S15][S28]
11. **Net fiscal-stack discipline:** what benchmark rules best distinguish legitimate floor-protecting credits and public-interest support from covert sectoral favors, especially for data centres, fossil fuels, and large capital projects that mix tax breaks with subsidised inputs?[S29][S30][S31]
12. **Beneficial ownership and anti-fragmentation:** what is the narrowest grouping rule that can reliably follow real controllers across trusts, shell chains, SPVs, cloud affiliates, and AI deployment subsidiaries without collapsing genuinely independent actors into one tax unit?[S34][S35]
13. **Reliance versus privilege in transition:** what is the cleanest compact rule for distinguishing legitimate reliance that deserves notice, phase-in, or time-bounded grandfathering from incumbent rent extraction that should not be frozen in place under the banner of certainty?[S23][S27][S36][S37][S38]
14. **Enforcement proportionality and privacy:** what is the narrowest administrable rule for staging recovery, protecting hardship cases, bounding data access, and constraining model-assisted enforcement so that tax collection remains firm against strategic evasion without becoming a standing surveillance or domination system?[S16][S17][S27][S39][S40][S41]
15. **Standing and representation for unusual tax subjects:** what minimum bundle of notice, appeal, fiduciary representation, and institutional voice would have to exist before any artificial system, automated estate wrapper, or heavily mediated subject could justly bear direct civic tax liability rather than only controller-side liability?[S19][S20][S42][S43][S44]
16. **Status-proxy and anti-discrimination discipline:** what is the smallest equality-impact test that can catch direct and indirect discrimination in tax design, filing channels, automated triage, and place-based proxies without turning every reform into a litigation maze?[S32][S33][S44][S45][S46]
17. **Failure ordering and limited-liability discipline:** what is the narrowest rule for ranking ordinary tax claims against unpaid wages, trust-like withheld taxes, tort victims, and cleanup obligations in insolvency, and when should ex ante bonding, reserves, or controller liability replace ex post tax priority altogether?[S47][S48][S49]
18. **Downside symmetry and loss recognition:** what is the narrowest rule for indexed carry-forwards, bounded carry-backs, refundability, and anti-abuse screens that recognizes genuine downside without turning the state into a free insurer or an artificial-loss factory?[S50][S51][S52]
19. **Precaution versus pricing:** what is the cleanest compact rule for deciding when a harmful activity should face ordinary taxation, when it should face tax-plus-governance, and when taxes are the wrong first tool because the risk is catastrophic, irreversible, or not realistically remediable after the fact?[S15][S17][S53][S54]
20. **Real-value and indexation discipline:** what is the smallest workable rule for indexing or honestly approximating brackets, allowances, benefits, capital gains, carry-forwards, depreciation, bond amounts, and AI or infrastructure thresholds so that inflation does not become stealth tax policy or silent relief erosion?[S50][S55][S56][S57][S58]
21. **Review architecture and reversibility:** what is the narrowest rule for naming measurable success conditions, routine review cadence, early trigger events, and credible recalibration / conversion / sunset / repeal paths so that emergency, experimental, and AI-linked taxes remain answerable to evidence rather than inertia?[S15][S54][S59][S60][S61][S62][S63]
22. **Automaticity and take-up discipline:** what is the smallest workable rule for deciding when rebates, refunds, floor protection, or harm-repair payments should be automatic, prefilled, one-click confirmable, or otherwise low-friction, and when a higher-friction application path is still justified?[S64][S65][S66]
