# Moral Taxation

## Public burdens should follow responsibility, not convenience

Moral Taxation asks how a society can finance common life, share socially created advantages and repair harms without making ordinary survival, useful work or access to justice its easiest sources of revenue. Its proposed tax constitution starts with a protected civic minimum and gives priority to rents, concentrated windfalls and measurable harms over modest wages and ordinary security buffers. It then follows the proposal through the institutions that collect, distribute and contest the money. A good intention has to survive that journey.

The inquiry extends beyond tax rates. A levy, a refund, a safety condition and a public service do different jobs. The work asks who controls an activity, who benefits, who ultimately pays, which losses can be repaired, and who can challenge the decision. Its practical ambition is to keep those questions connected rather than let a convenient meter, invoice, legal label or computer-generated result settle them by default.

Start with the original [charter](versions/rev0021/files/Moral-Taxation-rev0021-2026.03.22.14.32-takeupwaist-claimfit/docs/00-meta/charter.md), the [constitutional principles](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/docs/10-framework/constitutional-principles.md) and the later [decision procedure](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/docs/10-framework/decision-procedure.md). The charter supplies the purpose; the principles make substantive commitments; the procedure shows what those commitments demand of a proposed policy.

## What should be taxed first?

The [tax-base ordering](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/docs/10-framework/tax-base-ordering.md) distinguishes subsistence and caregiving from the ability to capture a location's scarcity value, exclude competitors, inherit a commanding fortune or impose costs on others. It favors taxing the latter before leaning heavily on routine labor. Broad consumption and residual corporate profits remain in the proposed mix, but broad consumption taxation is paired with visible distributive repair. The argument is a priority among bases, not a claim that all profit is rent or that every public need can be funded from one instrument.

The [land-rent note](versions/rev0021/files/Moral-Taxation-rev0021-2026.03.22.14.32-takeupwaist-claimfit/archive/104-land-location-and-natural-resource-rent-taxation.md) makes the distinction concrete. A building's construction and upkeep are not the same thing as the site's value arising from surrounding population, infrastructure and public order. Taxing them identically can penalize improvement while leaving passive scarcity gains comparatively protected. But the preferred base still needs credible revaluation, appeal and payment timing: an asset-rich, cash-poor resident may need deferral or instalments, rather than either forced sale or permanent immunity.

The same discipline limits the [annual wealth-tax proposal](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/docs/10-framework/annual-wealth-tax-backstop-routing.md). It is a residual backstop for large fortunes escaping better-targeted land, inheritance, capital-income and rent instruments. It depends on ownership visibility, defensible valuation and related-party grouping. The work does not offer a universal wealth levy as a substitute for those harder institutional tasks.

Headline rates are also incomplete evidence. An industry paying tax while receiving abatements, subsidized infrastructure or cheap public inputs may face a very different net public claim. Floor-protecting rebates have a different purpose from favors that return revenue to a concentrated beneficiary. The relevant comparison has to include both sides.

## Who controls, who remits, and who bears the cost?

A person liable under the law, an intermediary sending money to the state, an actor controlling the activity and a person bearing the final cost can be four different parties. The [accountability framework](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/docs/10-framework/actor-accountability-controller-beneficiary-bottleneck-and-fallback-duty-routing.md) asks for all of them, including adjacent actors who should not be blamed and the public body responsible if a private delivery channel fails.

AI is a demanding example. The archive routes obligations for current systems through accountable people and institutions, while distinguishing energy and water burdens, deployment risks, scarce capacity and concentrated returns. Tokens or model instances do not become civic persons merely because they can be counted. Its separate [artificial-personhood argument](versions/rev0021/files/Moral-Taxation-rev0021-2026.03.22.14.32-takeupwaist-claimfit/archive/110-conditional-artificial-personhood-and-tax-subjecthood.md) is conditional: a genuine transition to personhood would require rights, standing, remedy and an end to ownership together, not duties first and rights later.

“Controller” is not simply another word for the largest company nearby. The [controller-ranking ladder](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/docs/20-calibration/controller-boundary-and-co-controller-ranking-ladder.md) rejects both the most visible brand and the deepest infrastructure provider as automatic answers. Ordinary prompting, passive hosting or payment processing alone does not establish control. Durable authority over a deployment's mission, retrieval material, tool permissions or release decisions can establish bounded responsibility for that layer. Power to continue, stop or materially redesign the system, together with residual benefit, helps identify primary control. A temporary remittance anchor does not settle that moral question.

Consider the archive's [data-center case](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/docs/20-calibration/data-center-local-burden-ratepayer-protection-ladder.md). The task is to follow contracted and peak demand, grid upgrades, water use, public concessions and the costs passed to other utility customers. If the facility already pays its full marginal local burden, the proposed answer can be ordinary taxation without an extra local-burden charge. If costs are shifted to households, a facility-side charge, ratepayer credit or abatement clawback may fit. Severe water constraints may require a cap or denial instead. A general AI surcharge cannot do all of these jobs just because the same computers appear in each story.

## A protected floor has to reach its recipient

The work's most immediate claim is that eligibility on paper is not enough. The early [essay on take-up and claim friction](versions/rev0021/files/Moral-Taxation-rev0021-2026.03.22.14.32-takeupwaist-claimfit/archive/131-automaticity-take-up-claim-friction-and-prefilled-justice.md) treats missed relief as evidence about the design, especially when the state already knows enough to make payment. Collection that happens automatically, paired with refunds that require expert navigation, distributes administrative advantage toward those already equipped to claim it.

The later [delivery ladder](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/docs/20-calibration/automaticity-and-take-up-delivery-ladder.md) makes this more specific. Routine payments with decisive facts already known should tend toward automatic delivery. Recurring circumstances that change can use prefilled confirmation. Complex or disputed cases need assisted claims, including human and offline routes. Full bespoke applications are more defensible for negotiated privileges than for subsistence support. Universal automation is rejected too: stale household records, inaccessible identity checks and uncorrectable classification can create a different exclusion system.

Timing belongs inside the argument. A rebate arriving long after prices rise may not protect a household when the burden falls. A refund paid into an inaccessible channel may not reach the person owed it. The question is therefore what people can actually receive and contest, not only what a budget line promises.

The [threshold ladder](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/docs/20-calibration/threshold-cliff-smoothing-and-graduation-ladder.md) applies similar reasoning to a small raise or a growing business. A little more income should not ordinarily trigger the loss of an entire benefit or retroactively impose a larger schedule. Tapers, marginal bands and graduation windows can avoid that punishment. Yet the work also rejects smoothing everything: a genuinely categorical permit or safety obligation may need a real boundary. The boundary has to answer to its purpose rather than become either a trap or an indefinite shelter.

## When several claims apply at once

The archive does not resolve complicated cases by averaging every attractive consideration. It uses precedence: protect the floor, reject mistaken subjects and impermissible risk, identify control and the appropriate base, then examine incidence, timing and measurement.

Its [composite-netting proposal](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/docs/20-calibration/beneficiary-composite-netting-and-residual-ordering-ladder.md) shows what that means for a beneficiary-side windfall claim. A firm may argue that exceptional gains went to lower prices, workers, suppliers, creditors, insurance, community repair or charity. Before accepting those defenses, the proposal asks whether money actually left the controlling group and whether it was later recaptured. The same dollar cannot reduce the taxable residual several times under different descriptions.

Harder duties, such as worker transition, compensation, cleanup and genuinely secured local repair, come before softer stories of general benefit. Real outside transfers can narrow the exceptional gain remaining with the beneficiary; ordinary taxation and separate harm obligations do not disappear. In a disputed case, the archive favors relief for the evidenced slice with later adjustment, rather than either gross taxation of everything or complete immunity. This is a proposed way of making mixed cases tractable, with real demands on evidence and administration.

## A tax receipt cannot perform every public duty

The [instrument-choice argument](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/docs/10-framework/instrument-choice-and-non-tax-alternatives-routing.md) is deliberately willing to choose something other than a tax. Transfers and services protect a floor; explicit support can build shared capacity; bonds and reserves can secure repair before an operator fails; permits, caps or refusal can address risks that cannot be made acceptable by a price.

This matters especially where compensation would arrive too late or the main harm could not be repaired. Paying a levy does not establish that an activity is safe or permissible. Conversely, uncertainty alone does not justify prohibiting every activity. The work's [precaution essay](versions/rev0021/files/Moral-Taxation-rev0021-2026.03.22.14.32-takeupwaist-claimfit/archive/128-precaution-catastrophic-risk-and-non-compensable-harm.md) asks which kind of harm is present and which institution can actually answer it.

A workable arrangement also needs authority. The [democratic-authorization route](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/docs/10-framework/democratic-authorization-consultation-and-affected-voice-routing.md) insists on intelligible reasons and affected-party participation while choices can still change. Consultation after a decision is locked, or a technical model offered in place of political reasons, does not satisfy that demand.

## How a proposal becomes reviewable

The archive also implements part of this reasoning as a deterministic Python pipeline. Its [answer emitter](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/tools/answer_case.py) ranks candidate routes from case facts and axes, then joins each selected route to policy-action, accountability and remedy profiles. The resulting packet identifies responsible actors, prohibited shortcuts, default moves, guardrails and the sources attached to particular claims. Relevance scoring and decision precedence do different work: finding a related route does not make its instruction the first thing to apply.

The disposition layer carries those obligations into specific demands for current-law, jurisdiction, quantitative, floor-delivery and risk-threshold evidence. This makes a missing premise addressable: a reader can ask which model input, authority source or delivery assumption a conclusion still needs, rather than receive one undifferentiated confidence score. The command-line entrance works with the supplied golden cases; the packet is a structured argument and evidence requirement, not a live tax assessment.

The [review-bundle exporter](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/tools/export_decision_review_bundle.py) then packages evidence payloads, public trust-policy material, attestations, scope bindings and replay results so another reviewer can check what was consumed. Its sandbox status keeps a successful verification exercise from turning into final advice. That is a distinct contribution from the tax constitution itself: a way to carry its questions and unresolved obligations between reviewers without replacing judgment with a clean audit result.

## Where the argument remains demanding

The priorities are substantive proposals, not neutral outputs of a calculator. Calling them the archive's settled constitutional core does not prove them correct. A reader can ask whether a broad tax with rebates will reliably outperform targeted exemptions where relief is weak, whether rents can be distinguished from productive returns well enough to tax them, and whether detailed controller and beneficiary tracing costs more than an administration can sustain.

Incidence can defeat a well-chosen legal target. Anti-fragmentation can become over-aggregation. Automatic delivery can require data practices that threaten the privacy it is meant to respect. Relief, consultation and appeal can be formally available yet practically unusable. The archive's [state-capacity discussion](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/docs/10-framework/tax-morale-state-capacity-and-political-economy-routing.md) and [open questions](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/docs/10-framework/open-questions.md) keep many of these tensions live; they do not supply a tested universal answer.

There is also a deeper political question: who can revise the ordering when reasonable citizens disagree about floors, risk, public benefit or legitimate reliance? The work offers review, standing and affected voice as part of its answer. Those institutions must themselves remain capable of changing decisions, rather than becoming another layer of vocabulary around a predetermined result.

## An argument developed through its own difficulties

The five supplied snapshots allow the reader to follow development without mistaking the highest revision number for a replacement philosophy. The substantive twenty-three constitutional principles are already present in rev0021. Later work adds more precise threshold and delivery choices, controller boundaries, composite claims and concrete accountability across borders.

A particularly revealing [self-examination preserved in rev0342](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/docs/00-meta/evidence-truth-boundary-release-lineage-refactor-rev0337.md) reports that the project's own machinery had confused internally generated test records with evidence from the world. Its correction separates moral routing from empirical evaluation. A consistent record can show what was processed; it cannot establish that the facts are true, that a fiscal model is calibrated or that a policy is justified.

The [latest review bundle](versions/rev0342/files/moral_taxation_rev0342/docs/00-meta/decision-review-bundle-handoff-promotion-refactor-rev0342.md) develops a portable, independently re-checkable sandbox handoff with evidence payloads, attestations and replay information, while keeping its decision output held. That procedural experiment belongs beside the philosophical argument: it tries to make judgments inspectable without allowing inspection machinery to impersonate the grounds of judgment. The archive does not provide current tax or legal advice.

Read beside [Immoral-Wealth](../Immoral-Wealth/README.md) for usable independence and concentrated power, [The Good](../The-Good/README.md) for plural goods and the cost of procedure, and [AI-Personhood](../AI-Personhood/README.md) for an explicitly stipulated alternative institutional premise. These are connected questions, not a requirement to adopt one doctrine across the collection.

## Editions, snapshots and preservation

### A route through the collection

#### [rev0021](versions/rev0021/README.md)

Introduces automaticity and take-up as requirements of morally adequate relief: protected minima can fail when refunds or transfers are trapped behind application friction. The archive frames tax design around harms, rents, final incidence, public floors and the people or firms controlling AI systems.

#### [rev0128](versions/rev0128/README.md)

Compresses the threshold-cliff smoothing and graduation memo into a ladder-first format while retaining the tax boundary table. Read this as a calibration and exposition checkpoint, not evidence that the archive adopted a different tax schedule.

#### [rev0226](versions/rev0226/README.md)

Adds composite netting and residual ordering: when several outward-flow defenses apply, the same dollar should be netted only once, with harder duties ahead of softer justifications. The manifest root label still says mt_rev0225 although the supplied archive and changelog identify rev0226; file hashes match the shipped manifest.

#### [rev0300](versions/rev0300/README.md)

Reworks cross-border accountability around concrete actors, control points, evidence and remedies. The covered routes include coordination, border charges, remittances, immigration/status fees, withholding relief and global-minimum-tax reporting. These are the archive’s analytical classifications, not a verification of current tax law.

#### [rev0342](versions/rev0342/README.md)

Adds an exportable decision-review bundle containing public trust-policy material, payload hashes, attestations, replay records and held-output status. Its own README says the bundle is sandbox-only and does not establish current law, jurisdiction clearance, production trust or final advice.
