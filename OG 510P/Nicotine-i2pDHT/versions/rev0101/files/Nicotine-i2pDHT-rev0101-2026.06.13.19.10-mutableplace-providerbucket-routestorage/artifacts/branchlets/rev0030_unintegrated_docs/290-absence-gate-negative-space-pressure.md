# Absence gate and negative-space pressure

The riskiest DHT answer is sometimes not a forged positive answer but a convenient empty answer. A node can say "not found" quickly, repeatedly, and from a captured route family. rev0030 adds `absencegate.py` to keep negative evidence typed.

The local rule:

```text
empty answer != absence truth
```

The toy assessment accepts only two forms of useful absence:

1. a short soft negative cache after diverse empty provider/head observations; or
2. a durable tombstone-supported absence when diverse empties are joined to diverse tombstone evidence.

Fast empty monoculture, expired observations, target mismatch, and positive-provider/head conflict quarantine the window. Timeout pressure asks for more paths rather than caching absence.

This matters for future mutable torrents, sync heads, garden ledgers, and provider records: stale or captured empty answers should not erase live mutable state.
