# ADR 0125 — key-crisis recovery checkpoint join

Status: accepted for rev0030 baby cube.

A key-crisis recovery or succession record must not erase checkpointed hard-negative evidence. rev0030 joins key-crisis assessment with checkpoint memory and egress pressure before local recovery acceptance.

Rationale: recovery paths are emergency doors. Emergency doors are where rollback, tombstone loss, and revocation loss tend to hide.
