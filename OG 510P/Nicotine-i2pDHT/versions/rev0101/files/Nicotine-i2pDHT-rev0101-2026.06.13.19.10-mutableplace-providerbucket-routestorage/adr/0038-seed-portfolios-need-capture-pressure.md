# ADR 0038 — seed portfolios need capture pressure

## Decision

Seed portfolios must be analyzed for channel, family, garden, and known-attacker concentration before being treated as healthy bootstrap material.

## Rationale

Mutable seed heads are the path away from central entrances. Without portfolio pressure checks, they can become central entrances in disguise.
