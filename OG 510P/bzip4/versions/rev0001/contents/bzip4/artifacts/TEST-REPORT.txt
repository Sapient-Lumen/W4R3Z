FRAMING NOTE
------------
The upstream-compatible in-memory bz3_compress/bz3_decompress frame includes
a 32-bit block count after magic and block size. The CLI stream omits that
field. They share the BZ3v1 magic but are not byte-stream interchangeable.
Both upstream layouts are retained and explicitly documented.
