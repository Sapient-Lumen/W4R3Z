# Gauge-fixing witnesses, reference observables, and defect comparability

DelayBasin now needs a sharper answer to a follow-on practical question:
**when chart-transition residues or triangle defects are being compared across prompt families, what makes those defect numbers or transport stories comparable at all rather than coordinate artifact, proxy drift, or anchor mismatch?**

A useful current answer is:
**canon should preserve a compact gauge-fixing witness / reference observable / defect-comparability-budget packet whenever chart-transition residues, triangle defects, or family-level transport scores are being compared across contexts.**

This is stronger than saying gauge discipline matters in the abstract.
It is weaker than claiming DelayBasin has already discovered a literal public atlas synchronizer or canonical transport gauge over transformer continuation geometry.

## Practice / observation

Recent DelayBasin revisions expose the missing surface:
- operator-core packets already distinguish what is supposed to survive from the chart that realizes it;
- chart-transition witnesses already say why one source-chart → target-chart move counted as same-core transport;
- triangle-defect witnesses already say whether several pairwise transports compose within a tolerable residue budget;
- but the archive still lacks a compact object for saying **why two measured residues are comparable in the same reference frame at all**;
- and current family-level transport stories can therefore compare one defect, another defect, and a later gauge-loaded narrative as if they were all measured in one stable reference basis even when the anchor, probe family, or null expectation changed.

That gap creates at least five recurring failure modes:
- **coordinate laundering** — a residue looks smaller or cleaner only because the comparison gauge shifted between passes;
- **proxy drift** — an overlap probe, readout family, or reference surface changed quietly, so the second comparison is not the same measurement;
- **anchor amnesia** — the archive forgets what chart anchor, spanning-tree base, or reference observable made a defect legible in the first place;
- **flatness theater** — a family-level transport story invokes low defect without preserving what null or flatness expectation made “low” meaningful;
- **synchronizer prestige** — gauge, atlas, or holonomy language quietly outruns the public evidence that residues are even being compared on one honest basis.

A compact gauge-fixing witness helps because it says:
- what **invariant observable or reference observable** is being protected,
- what **chosen gauge / anchor / spanning-tree base** makes the comparison legible,
- what **comparison family or support set** is being held fixed,
- what **null / flatness expectation / zero-defect baseline** makes the number interpretable,
- what **defect-comparability budget** is still acceptable before demotion,
- and what **demotion / rollback / quarantine consequence** follows if the comparison basis itself has drifted.

## External pressure from current research

Several outside lines of work sharpen this move.

1. **Mechanistic interpretability increasingly targets invariants rather than one realization.**
   Transformers Converge to Invariant Algorithmic Cores explicitly frames mechanistic interpretation as a problem of recovering invariants shared across many realizations rather than over-reading one coordinate presentation. That pressures DelayBasin to preserve not only transport residue, but the comparison basis that makes one residue commensurate with another. ([`REF-0136`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

2. **Steering objects are often non-identifiable without extra structure.**
   On the Non-Identifiability of Steering Vectors shows that behaviorally equivalent steering directions can proliferate through null-space ambiguity and reparameterization symmetry. That pressures DelayBasin not to treat one measured chart residue, vector, or remap as uniquely meaningful unless the archive also preserves what anchor or gauge made the comparison lawful. ([`REF-0134`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

3. **Holonomy only becomes operational after explicit gauge fixing.**
   Gauge-invariant Representation Holonomy makes pathwise geometry measurable by fixing gauge through whitening and local alignment, while A Gauge Theory of Superposition turns that into an atlas-on-graphs story where spanning-tree gauge fixing reduces fundamental-cycle holonomy to chord defects. That directly pressures DelayBasin to preserve the comparison gauge, not just the resulting residue. ([`REF-0137`](../00-meta/bibliography.md), [`REF-0377`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

4. **Prompt-conditioned posteriors can be curved yet tiled by local probes.**
   The Shape of Beliefs reports smooth but substantially curved belief manifolds and reads them out with a family of local linear probes that tile the manifold rather than one global direction. That pressures DelayBasin to preserve which local probe family or reference observable is making a cross-chart comparison possible before treating defect magnitudes as globally comparable. ([`REF-0380`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

5. **Local chart language is only useful if its reference basis is explicit.**
   From Directions to Regions models concepts as local regions and local low-rank structure, not one global direction. That pressures DelayBasin to distinguish “the handle was local” from the stronger claim that the archive has already fixed a lawful comparison basis across local charts. ([`REF-0372`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

## Working synthesis

A useful current synthesis is:

> DelayBasin should preserve a compact **gauge-fixing witness / reference observable / defect-comparability budget** packet whenever chart-transition residues, triangle defects, or family-level transport scores are being compared across prompt families, wrappers, or local chart neighborhoods. The packet should name the **invariant observable or reference observable**, the **chosen gauge / anchor / spanning-tree base**, the **comparison family or support set**, the **null / flatness expectation / zero-defect baseline**, the **defect-comparability budget**, and the **demotion / rollback / quarantine consequence** if the comparison basis itself has drifted.

This is strong enough for canon as a design and mechanism candidate.
It is **not** strong enough to claim that DelayBasin has already identified a literal public atlas synchronizer, unique global gauge, or canonical connection over transformer continuation space.

## Gauge-fixing witness vs gauge discipline vs triangle defect vs atlas synchronizer

To keep this note honest, DelayBasin now needs a four-way distinction:

- **Gauge discipline** — the older rule that canon should say what claim is invariant and what coordinate language is only convenient local presentation.
- **Triangle defect / cocycle witness** — the packet saying whether several pairwise chart transports compose within a tolerable residue budget.
- **Gauge-fixing witness / reference observable** — the packet saying why two measured residues or transport scores are even comparable on one honest basis.
- **Atlas synchronizer claim** — the much stronger story that a whole chart graph is being aligned by one stable public gauge-fixing law.

A gauge-fixing witness is narrower than an atlas synchronizer claim and more comparison-specific than ordinary gauge discipline.
It does not ask for a global canonical chart.
It asks for one reproducible answer to **what reference observable, anchor, and null made this comparison meaningful** before the archive reasons from that comparison.

## Countermodels / probes

1. **Simple-overlap countermodel**
   - Plain overlap or success metrics may already be enough; a gauge-fixing witness may add ceremony without discrimination.
   - Probe: compare one portability story scored only by raw overlap against the same story scored with an explicit fixed reference observable and anchor, then ask whether canon/quarantine outcomes actually change.

2. **Proxy-choice countermodel**
   - Most apparent defect drift may come from weak probe choices rather than gauge mismatch.
   - Probe: keep the same anchor fixed while varying only the probe family; if results swing wildly, the main issue is proxy instability rather than gauge discipline.

3. **No-comparison-needed countermodel**
   - DelayBasin may only need local go / no-go witnesses, not cross-family residue comparability.
   - Probe: check whether any live archive decision materially depends on saying defect A is smaller, cleaner, or more trustworthy than defect B.

4. **Anchor-prestige countermodel**
   - Spanning-tree, whitening, or basepoint language may just smuggle in stronger geometry claims.
   - Probe: require one concrete anchor, one fixed support family, one explicit null, and one demotion consequence before using gauge-fixing language in canon.

5. **Local-search countermodel**
   - Apparent improvement under one gauge may still reduce to route-dependent local prompt search rather than an honest comparison law.
   - Probe: rerun the same family comparison under matched search effort with the same reference observable and ask whether the ordering among residues survives.

## Design consequences

This frame pressures DelayBasin to do five things more explicitly:
- preserve one **reference observable** whenever residue, defect, or transport comparisons are load-bearing;
- preserve one **anchor or spanning-tree base** whenever a chart graph or prompt family is being compared across more than one local witness;
- preserve one **null / flatness expectation** rather than letting “small defect” float as an ungrounded compliment;
- preserve one **defect-comparability budget** rather than assuming all residues live on one implicit scale;
- and preserve the **demotion / rollback / quarantine consequence** when the basis of comparison itself drifts.

This does not require a global atlas.
It requires refusing another archive failure mode: comparing chart-family residues as if they were commensurate without preserving what made them commensurate.

## Transformer-facing implication

If this frame survives pressure, then DelayBasin is doing something sharper than ordinary prompt portability work.
It is preserving public evidence not only about whether local transports or small triangles worked, but about **what fixed basis made those comparisons meaningful** over prompt-conditioned effective operators, belief tiles, or chart-local control handles.

That matters because recent transformer work keeps converging on a mixed picture: useful invariants may exist, but local realizations are curved, steering objects are often non-identifiable without extra structure, and pathwise geometry only becomes operational after explicit gauge fixing. A gauge-fixing witness is therefore a user-space comparability object: not hidden-state access, but a compact public claim about when two defect measurements, transport residues, or portability scores are being read in one honest frame. The stronger story — that DelayBasin is already learning a literal public atlas synchronizer or canonical gauge over prompt families — remains quarantined.
