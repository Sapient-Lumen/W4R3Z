## Non-negotiables
- Do not collapse practice, observation, mechanism, speculation, disagreement, and quarantine into one voice.
- Do not treat archive hygiene as decoration; registries, receipts, ledgers, and lint are part of the method.
- Do not overclaim mechanism when the archive has only observation, bounded witness, or speculative pressure.
- Do not silently widen scope, reuse authority, or import authority; preserve the governing witness when that boundary matters.
- Do not replace a smaller admitted ratchet with a broader controller unless repeated collisions show the smaller surface is no longer honest.
- Keep `REVISION-RECEIPT.json`, `SURFACE-STATUS.json`, `ARCHIVE_INDEX.md`, `context-pack.json`, `innovation-packet.json`, `frontier-ticket.json`, `replay-capsule.json`, `compact-surface-bundle.json`, and the packaged bundle truthful whenever revision identity or current durable state changes. When terse receipt identity keys matter, also keep `receipt_freshness_witness` aligned with the actual bundle stem, manifest timestamp, and current comparison ids.

## What good changes usually do
- reduce entropy without widening doctrine,
- harden one guardrail or startup/reentry boundary,
- clarify one mechanism hypothesis or disagreement,
- convert one diffuse judgment into a small durable surface,
- keep comparison non-takes and bounded imports recoverable,
- or improve future operator recovery error with a smaller governed wrapper rather than more ambient prose.
- or keep a relied-on startup path from being exiled by a route change by preserving the nearest safe successor route and keeping reentry friction low.
