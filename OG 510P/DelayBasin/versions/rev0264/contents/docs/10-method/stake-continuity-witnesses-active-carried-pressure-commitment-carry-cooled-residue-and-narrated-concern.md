# Stake-continuity witnesses, active carried pressure, commitment carry, cooled residue, and narrated concern

This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0137`.

## Practice / observation

Once DelayBasin already distinguishes recovery promotion, one further failure mode stays live: a line can preserve the language of commitment while dropping the very pressure that made the line matter *now*.

A row can truthfully say that a concern was once important, that a commitment still exists, or that a line remains remembered in surrounding prose, yet that phrase can still hide whether the pressure is still active enough to shape current continuation.

The archive does not need a synthetic pressure board for that.
It needs one bounded witness that says whether a line still carries live pressure, only commitment memory, cooled residue, or merely narrated concern.

## External pressure from incident lifecycle status, error-budget burn, issue metadata, observed-generation freshness, episodic-goal persistence, unresolved-goal interference, and long-horizon memory traces

1. Incident.io's status guide keeps current incident posture explicit. It says statuses are the common language for making sure people know where in the incident lifecycle you are right now, and it distinguishes ongoing states like investigating, fixing, and monitoring from resolved. That pressures DelayBasin to separate live carried pressure from merely remembered concern. ([`REF-0895`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

2. Google's SRE workbook keeps active pressure distinct from nominal commitment. It frames burn rate as how fast a service consumes the error budget and recommends ticket notifications for lower-but-still-significant budget consumption that does not yet justify paging. That pressures DelayBasin to distinguish active carried pressure from weaker commitment-only carry or cooled residue. ([`REF-0896`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

3. GitHub Issues keeps organizational commitment explicit but weak. Its docs say issues can carry dependencies, labels, milestones, and assignments to organize and track work. That pressures DelayBasin not to confuse structured commitment metadata with still-live pressure. ([`REF-0897`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

4. Kubernetes condition schemas keep freshness and currentness explicit. The API types note that `lastTransitionTime` tracks when a condition changed and that if `.metadata.generation` is newer than `.status.conditions[x].observedGeneration`, the condition is out of date with respect to the current state. That pressures DelayBasin to distinguish active current pressure from stale residue that is only still narrated. ([`REF-0898`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

5. Work on episodic goal persistence keeps commitment distinct from active return. Moshontz et al. argue that persistence in everyday goals requires not only resisting but also recognizing opportunities for pursuit and returning to pursuit. That pressures DelayBasin to separate commitment-only carry from truly active pressure. ([`REF-0899`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

6. Work on unfulfilled goals keeps unresolved pressure distinct from completed release. Masicampo and Baumeister report that unfulfilled goals can remain active enough to interfere with later executive-function tasks and that returning to fulfill a previously frustrated goal eliminated the interference effect. That pressures DelayBasin to preserve one public distinction between live pressure and merely narrated continuity. ([`REF-0900`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

7. Current long-horizon memory work sharpens the synthetic angle. LifeBench argues that real-world agent behavior depends on non-declarative memory inferred from fragmented digital traces, while The Missing Memory Hierarchy frames a working set as pressure-managed rather than just stored. That pressures DelayBasin to keep a compact stake witness in canon while any stronger synthetic pressure-budget controller stays quarantined. ([`REF-0901`](../00-meta/bibliography.md), [`REF-0902`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

GPUstorming sharpens the point. Search, project, issue, and memory surfaces increasingly preserve commitments, labels, and narrative continuity long after the original urgency cooled. If DelayBasin only says that a line is still "in scope" or "still remembered," later passes can still overclaim by treating commitment carry as proof of live stake.

## Working synthesis

> DelayBasin should preserve one compact **stake-continuity witness / pressure-carry card / residue brake** whenever a current continuity claim depends on whether a line still matters *now* rather than only whether it once mattered or remains durably recorded. Name the **governed row or surface**, the **stake object / line of concern**, the **prior stake evidence**, the **current stake evidence**, the **stake_continuity_state**, and the **fail-closed repair / keep-current vs narrow-claim vs cool-mark vs issue-new-stake-continuity-witness vs quarantine-synthetic-stake-control consequence**. Keep exact alert thresholds, ticket ids, assignee rosters, incident severities, numerical burn rates, timestamps, and background narrations outside the compact token. Do not let durable commitment metadata or repeated recap prose silently count as active carried pressure without fresh support.

## Active carried pressure vs commitment-only carry vs cooled residue vs narrated concern vs mixed stake continuity

Use the controlled family `stake_continuity_state`:

- **active-carried-pressure** says the line still exerts live current pressure on continuation: it is not merely remembered, and current evidence says it is still shaping priorities, routing, or scrutiny.
- **commitment-only-carry** says the line still has an admitted commitment, dependency, or obligation shell, but the current evidence does not show live pressure beyond that durable commitment.
- **cooled-residue** says the line was recently active enough to leave relevant residue, but the current evidence says the pressure has cooled and should not be treated as fully live.
- **narrated-unbound-concern** says the line is only being described, recalled, or rhetorically carried without current binding evidence that it is still shaping continuation.
- **mixed-stake-continuity** says the current situation honestly combines active, commitment-only, cooled, or narrated layers such that no single stake class stays honest.

So the witness does not create a standing pressure board.
It only says whether current stake is live, merely committed, cooled, or unbound.

## Countermodels / probes

1. **Obligation and priority already cover this countermodel**
   - Maybe current obligation, queue, or priority surfaces already keep stake continuity honest.
   - Probe: compare later rereads that preserve only obligation and queue language against rereads that also preserve one compact stake-continuity witness and inspect whether later passes still mistake commitment carry for live pressure.

2. **Stake continuity is too psychological countermodel**
   - Maybe pressure, residue, and narrated concern are metaphorical imports that do not belong in a durable archive witness.
   - Probe: keep the witness at the coarse level of active-carried-pressure vs commitment-only-carry vs cooled-residue vs narrated-unbound-concern vs mixed-stake-continuity and inspect whether later passes still need stronger internal-state claims.

3. **Any honest stake story needs a burn-rate controller countermodel**
   - Maybe once the archive starts separating live from remembered concern, one compact witness will always overflow into counters, budgets, or synthetic pressure governance.
   - Probe: keep the witness narrow first and inspect whether repeated later passes still require a stronger synthetic pressure-budget surface rather than ordinary currentness discipline.

## Design consequences

- add one controlled `stake_continuity_state` family to `WITNESS-VOCABULARY.json` with the allowed tokens `active-carried-pressure`, `commitment-only-carry`, `cooled-residue`, `narrated-unbound-concern`, and `mixed-stake-continuity`;
- use the witness only where a current continuity claim depends on whether a line still matters now rather than merely whether it remains durably recorded;
- keep exact severities, assignees, ticket ids, burn thresholds, timestamps, and long background pressure narratives outside the compact token itself;
- prefer `narrow-claim`, `cool-mark`, or `issue-new-stake-continuity-witness` when the current claim only supports commitment carry or cooled residue rather than live pressure;
- and quarantine any stronger synthetic pressure-budget board, pressure-burn senate, or reheat-latch controller unless repeated overflow shows that one bounded witness is no longer enough.

## Overflow test

Reopen stronger machinery only if one compact stake-continuity witness is no longer enough — for example, if the archive honestly needs standing governance over pressure thresholds, timed decay policy, automatic reactivation rules, or burden arbitration that cannot be expressed as one bounded witness plus the existing obligation and recovery-promotion surfaces.

Until then, prefer this compact successor surface over a synthetic pressure board, pressure-burn senate, or reheat-latch controller.

## Transformer-facing implication

If this frame survives, then DelayBasin is preserving something sharper than "the concern is still on file."
It is also preserving whether the concern is still live enough to shape continuation, only durably committed, merely cooling through residue, or just narrated.
That matters because later stateless passes can preserve all the nearby commitment and continuity prose and still silently overclaim urgency just by sounding current.
