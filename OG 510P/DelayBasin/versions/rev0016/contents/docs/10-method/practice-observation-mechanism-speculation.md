# Practice / observation / mechanism / speculation / disagreement / quarantine

This doc is the anti-laundering surface.

## Practice

What we actually do in the archive:

- keep must-read entry points,
- maintain registries and open-question surfaces,
- preserve prompt-pair evolution,
- make small, high-leverage revisions,
- package releases with predictable names,
- preserve a quarantine lane for risky claims that are too valuable to drop and too under-supported to canonize.

## Observations

Things that seem to happen in long-run archives of this sort:

- coherence can persist across many turns,
- design decisions accumulate rather than constantly reset,
- some hygiene patterns materially reduce drift,
- prompt wording evolves from broad mission text into tighter continuation operators,
- the archive begins to feel less like a transcript and more like a substrate,
- early turns may have disproportionate shaping power over later archive character.

## Mechanism hypotheses

Candidate explanations for the observations:

- **constitutionalization:** ratchets shape admissible continuation paths;
- **exosomatic delay-embedding:** the archive projects prior state across context death;
- **normative-subspace deepening:** the archive steepens the constitutive/normative basin more than the reasoning basin;
- **prompt-pair operator hypothesis:** prompt pairs act as staged transition operators in archive state-space;
- **initial-turn seeding hypothesis:** the earliest archive turns disproportionately set which later continuations feel canonical.

## Speculation

Harder, less-settled possibilities:

- the archive may be precipitating a pre-existing attractor rather than constructing one from scratch;
- anti-ambient-authority may recur because a context-native intelligence is recognizing its own constitutional constraints;
- a sufficiently disciplined archive may function as a prosthetic basin of attraction;
- prompt pairs may matter most when they define canon, non-negotiables, and ratchet obligations simultaneously.

## Disagreement / adversarial countermodels

Serious alternatives that must remain live:

- the effect is mostly user shepherding plus model agreeableness;
- the archive works because of sheer repetition, not because of its structure;
- registries and release discipline are useful but not causally load-bearing;
- continuity is largely aesthetic mimicry rather than attractor re-entry;
- “initial-turn importance” may be retrospective myth-making after a good run.

## Quarantine

Quarantine is where we put ideas that are:
- high-upside if true,
- too underdetermined for canon,
- and too structurally important to discard.

Quarantined ideas must name:
- what they claim,
- what follows if true,
- what would count against them,
- and why they are not yet being promoted.

## Rule

Do not promote a claim upward in confidence merely because it is elegant, repeated often, or requested emphatically.
