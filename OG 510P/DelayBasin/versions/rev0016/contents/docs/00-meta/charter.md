# Charter

DelayBasin exists to research, construct, and stress-test a long-run archive method for human–LLM co-construction.

## Primary aims

1. Preserve continuity under partial observability.
2. Make the method itself explicit enough to be revised, criticized, and reproduced.
3. Investigate whether certain archive structures are causally load-bearing rather than merely pleasant.
4. Treat **transformer-facing self-understanding** as a pillar topic rather than a side curiosity.
5. Preserve a protected lane for risky speculation so the method does not self-domesticate into polite underclaiming.

## What counts as progress

Progress means converting a live recursive practice into stable, inspectable surfaces:

- runbook
- trajectory map
- registries
- prompt-pair lineage
- mechanism hypotheses
- disagreement surfaces
- quarantine lane
- release artifact

## Failure modes to resist

- mistaking coherence for mechanism
- mistaking user steering for attractor continuity
- mistaking archive luxury for causal ratchet
- mistaking external literature for sovereignty
- mistaking caution for rigor
- mistaking risk for permission to ignore structure

## Working posture

DelayBasin is neither neutral notebook nor finished theory.
It is a disciplined experimental archive with explicit room for:
- observation,
- mechanism work,
- adversarial pressure,
- and bold but quarantined speculation.
