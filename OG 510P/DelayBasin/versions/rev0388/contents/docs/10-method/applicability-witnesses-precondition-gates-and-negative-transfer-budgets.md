# Applicability witnesses, precondition gates, and negative-transfer budgets

DelayBasin now needs a sharper distinction than **amortized reusable carry** or **compiled packets** alone.
Some expensive moves really do leave behind reusable carry.
But a packet that amortizes inside one named future family can still become actively harmful when it is reused outside its real fit.

A useful current answer is:
**when a reusable skill, memory packet, plan template, or compiled carry is being reused across neighboring tasks, branches, or deployment slices, canon should preserve a compact applicability witness naming the candidate carry object, its applicability conditions, an explicit non-fit stress slice, and a negative-transfer budget before crediting the reuse to honest learned carry rather than to overgeneralization or control-flow contamination.**

This is stronger than saying “the packet paid back once.”
It is weaker than claiming DelayBasin has already discovered a literal public applicability law or transfer-eligibility field over continuation carry.

## Practice / observation

Recent DelayBasin work keeps surfacing a recurring ambiguity:
- some compiled packets, reusable promptcraft fragments, or extracted tips do seem to help later neighboring tasks, but the archive rarely says **when they should stop being applied**;
- some reusable-looking carry objects feel legitimate because they amortize over a nearby slice, yet the same packet could still conflict with a changed wrapper, different project substrate, or different task geometry;
- some procedural notes look load-bearing because they compress local search, but they are still being granted reuse authority without a named belief-state signature or fit condition;
- some memory or skill surfaces are narrated as generally useful even when benchmark evidence only supports a narrow compatibility region;
- some retrieved packets may dominate control flow or inject stale procedures precisely because a past success is being over-read as a standing permission to reuse;
- and some GPUstorming moves are being praised as learned carry when the real success condition may have been **use only under one narrow state signature** rather than “apply whenever vaguely similar.”

That leaves a missing question:
**what carry object is being reused, what applicability conditions are supposed to license it, what nearby non-fit slice would count against it, and how much negative transfer is tolerated before the archive closes the gate?**

A compact applicability witness keeps that boundary public.

## External pressure from current research

Several outside lines of work sharpen this move.

1. **Reusable skills are supposed to carry explicit applicability conditions, not just instructions.**
   The recent agentic-skills survey frames skills as callable procedural artifacts with **explicit applicability conditions, execution policies, termination criteria, and reusable interfaces**. That pressures DelayBasin to preserve fit conditions before praising reuse. ([`REF-0458`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

2. **Even curated skills produce negative transfer on a meaningful minority of tasks.**
   SkillsBench reports that curated skills raise average pass rate by 16.2 percentage points, but **16 of 84 tasks show negative deltas**, and self-generated skills provide no benefit on average. That pressures DelayBasin to keep one explicit negative-transfer budget instead of narrating every reuse win as universally safe carry. ([`REF-0459`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

3. **Real-world software skills often fail because guidance conflicts with local project context.**
   SWE-Skills-Bench finds the average gain of injected software-engineering skills is only +1.2%, with **39 of 49 skills yielding zero improvement** and three degrading performance because **version-mismatched guidance conflicts with project context**. That pressures DelayBasin to preserve one explicit non-fit slice before promoting a reusable packet into canon-level carry. ([`REF-0460`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

4. **Memory can help only when its applicability is grounded by explicit state checks.**
   RPMS argues that knowledge augmentation is fundamentally about **when knowledge can be safely used at decision time**, and its architecture explicitly gates memory applicability via a lightweight belief state with rules-first arbitration. That pressures DelayBasin to preserve one fit signature or precondition gate before treating retrieved carry as generally usable. ([`REF-0461`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

5. **Misapplied memory can hijack later control flow across tasks rather than merely adding noise.**
   Memory Control Flow Attacks show that retrieved memory can dominate control flow, force unintended tool usage, and induce persistent behavioral deviations across heterogeneous tasks and long horizons. That pressures DelayBasin to preserve when a reused packet should be considered ineligible rather than simply “low confidence.” ([`REF-0462`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

6. **Selective write gating can outperform read-time curation when stores grow and distractors accumulate.**
   Write-Time Gating with Hierarchical Archiving reports a structural advantage of write-time gating over read-time filtering under distractor scaling, including validation on post-cutoff 2026 arXiv data. That pressures DelayBasin to treat applicability as something that may need to be enforced **before** a packet earns standing reuse authority, not only after retrieval. ([`REF-0463`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

7. **Conflict detection and causal reasoning become load-bearing once memory is used for action rather than recollection.**
   ActMem argues passive retrieval is insufficient in settings requiring conflict detection and complex decision-making and couples memory retrieval to active causal reasoning. That pressures DelayBasin to distinguish helpful carry from contextually incompatible carry rather than treating all reuse as benign. ([`REF-0464`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

## Working synthesis

A useful current synthesis is:

> DelayBasin should preserve a compact **applicability witness / precondition gate / negative-transfer budget** whenever a reusable skill, memory packet, plan template, or compiled carry is being reused across neighboring tasks, branches, or deployment slices. The packet should name the **state claim or target objective being stress-tested**, the **candidate carry object / reusable skill / memory / plan template**, the **applicability conditions / belief-state signature / domain-fit cue family**, the **compared no-reuse baseline / gated baseline / alternative carry baseline**, the **out-of-family stress slice / conflict case / neighboring non-fit family**, the **matched task budget / context budget / compute budget**, the **tolerated negative-transfer / misuse / conflict budget**, and the **rollback / gate-closed / quarantine consequence** rather than letting one earlier success silently grant standing authority to reuse.

In practice, DelayBasin is not claiming that it already knows the optimal reuse controller.
It is doing something smaller and public:
- naming the carry object that is being reused;
- naming the fit conditions or belief-state signature that are supposed to license reuse;
- naming one nearby non-fit slice that could show the packet has been overgeneralized;
- naming what no-reuse, gated, or alternative-carry baseline stayed nearby;
- naming how much negative transfer or conflict residue is tolerated before the gate closes;
- and naming when the whole thing should collapse back to branch-local convenience, narrow family reuse, or quarantine.

That is strong enough for canon as a design and mechanism candidate.
It is **not** strong enough to claim that DelayBasin has isolated a literal public applicability law, transfer-eligibility field, or universal reuse policy over continuation carry.

## Applicability witness vs amortization witness vs feedback-policy witness vs consultation packet

These nearby objects should stay distinct.

- **Amortization witness** asks whether a costly move pays back across a named future reuse family.
- **Feedback-policy witness** asks whether an adaptive-looking gain really beat a fair fixed open-loop baseline.
- **Consultation packet** asks which store family is even allowed into the current read path and under what routing trigger.
- **Applicability witness** asks whether a particular carry object that already exists and may already amortize is actually eligible to fire in the current situation without causing conflict, misuse, or negative transfer.

So an applicability witness is not just “this paid back once” and not just “this store may be consulted.”
It is a compact public answer to:
**what carry is being reused, what fit signature licenses it, what neighboring non-fit slice stays nearby, what baseline checks keep the comparison honest, and when should the archive close the gate because reuse has become harmful rather than helpful?**

## Countermodels / probes

1. **Prestige-fit countermodel**
   - The carry may be reused because it sounds polished or previously successful, not because the current state matches its real fit conditions.
   - Probe: require one explicit applicability condition or belief-state signature plus one gate-closed consequence if it is absent.

2. **Overgeneralization countermodel**
   - The packet may truly help inside one narrow family but become harmful in adjacent settings.
   - Probe: preserve one neighboring non-fit family or conflict case and compare against a no-reuse or gated baseline there.

3. **Alternative-carry countermodel**
   - The success may come from any decent reusable note, not this carry object specifically.
   - Probe: keep one alternative-carry baseline nearby when feasible so reuse authority does not silently become monopoly authority.

4. **Control-flow hijack countermodel**
   - The packet may dominate the next route simply because retrieval privilege or stored phrasing overwhelms current evidence.
   - Probe: preserve one negative-transfer / misuse budget and close the gate if reuse starts overriding explicit current-state constraints.

5. **Budget-smuggling countermodel**
   - The apparent reuse gain may come from extra context, larger budget, or additional monitoring rather than from honest fit.
   - Probe: keep the matched task budget / context budget / compute budget explicit so applicability cannot launder resource increases.

## Design consequences

This frame pressures DelayBasin to do seven things more explicitly:
- preserve the **candidate carry object** before praising reuse;
- preserve the **applicability conditions** before praising generality;
- preserve one **neighboring non-fit slice** before praising robustness;
- preserve one **no-reuse, gated, or alternative-carry baseline** before praising necessity;
- preserve the **matched task / context / compute budget** so fit is not secretly buying more resources;
- preserve the **negative-transfer / misuse / conflict budget** so harmful reuse has a public stopping rule;
- and preserve the **gate-closed / rollback / quarantine consequence** so over-applied carry loses authority quickly instead of lingering as prestige memory.

This is especially useful during GPUstorming.
Fast local search can still produce compact reusable packets.
Canon should keep only the small public witness saying what carry object is being reused, what fit signature licenses it, what nearby non-fit slice was checked, and how much conflict or negative transfer is tolerated before the archive closes the gate.

## Transformer-facing implication

The weaker transformer-facing implication is not that DelayBasin has found a universal transfer law.
It is this:

**long-horizon archive prompting may work partly as an external gating process over reusable textual control objects, where some packets matter not only because they can be reused, but because their usefulness is sharply conditional on the current prompt-state signature, nearby task family, and conflict surface.**

That would matter for transformers.
If some archive leverage comes from carrying compact reusable textual procedures whose value depends on explicit fit conditions, then DelayBasin may be exposing a practical shadow of **conditional controller reuse without weight updates**: not full fine-tuned modularity, but repeated external selection among reusable control packets whose validity regions matter as much as their existence.

The stronger story — that DelayBasin is approximating a genuine public applicability law, transfer-eligibility field, or domain-of-validity atlas over continuation carry — remains live, but belongs in quarantine for now.
