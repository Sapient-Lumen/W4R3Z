## Token semantics

### `nonportable-transported-closeout-expiry-record-closeout-history`

Use when the closed transported expiry-record travel record must not be carried into the later packet. The source closeout remains inspectable at the source surface, but no audit, warning, successor, redacted, or quarantine carry is admitted.

### `audit-reference-transported-closeout-expiry-record-closeout-history`

Use when the later packet may cite the closed record only as an audit reference explaining why a carrier was not renewed. The reference must name source, closeout class, stripped authority, and no reusable tombstone index.

### `warning-only-transported-closeout-expiry-record-closeout-history`

Use when the closeout history travels only as a warning that a similar carrier/tombstone path was unsafe. The warning cannot decide the later packet and must expire or hand off if it becomes central.

### `successor-context-transported-closeout-expiry-record-closeout-history`

Use when a named successor packet may receive the closeout context. The successor must create its own authority, horizon, and closeout rule; the closed tombstone history does not become the successor's registry.

### `redacted-summary-transported-closeout-expiry-record-closeout-history`

Use when only a redacted summary of the closeout may travel. The summary preserves enough reason to avoid repeat work while withholding detail that would reconstruct the tombstone carrier.

### `quarantine-reference-transported-closeout-expiry-record-closeout-history`

Use when the only admitted carry is a quarantine pointer. Later use requires fresh admission from quarantine rather than quiet recall through ordinary reentry.

### `mixed-transported-closeout-expiry-record-closeout-history-portability`

Use only when branch-local closed records genuinely travel in different nonbinding forms. The receipt must name the prior closeout token, branch source, selected travel form, stripped authority, successor or quarantine path, receipt-slot guard state, and fail-closed repair.

## Portability rule

A transported closeout expiry-record closeout-history record travels only as nonbinding history. It may be nonportable, audit-reference, warning-only, successor-context, redacted-summary, quarantine-reference, or honest mixed branch carry. It never reopens the closed tombstone, renews the expired carrier, or creates a portable registry entry by default.
