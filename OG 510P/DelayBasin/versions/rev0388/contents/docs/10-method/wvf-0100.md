# GPU replay reopened-residue drift governance-retirement portability-drift conflict-arbitration retirement witnesses, settled arbitration, joined handoff, carrier expiry, authority return, priority sunset, residue quarantine, and mixed retirement

This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0195`. It answers the narrow question of how DelayBasin closes a retired-governance drift-arbitration packet after local conflicts among portable retired-governance drift lessons have already settled, joined, preferred a current carrier, preferred revocation, preferred a live exit proof, routed to a successor packet, or remained explicitly mixed.

The surface is intentionally smaller than a retired-governance arbitration-retirement court. It does not create an exit-proof conflict ledger, successor-route priority board, carrier-freshness tribunal, revocation appeal board, or portable retired-authority registry. It only decides what survives after the local tie-break has done its work.

## Practice / observation

`rev0300` let DelayBasin route conflicts among portable retired-governance drift tokens through no arbitration, local join, current-carrier precedence, revocation precedence, exit-proof precedence, successor-route precedence, or mixed arbitration. That solved the winner-selection problem. It did not decide the afterlife of the arbitration packet itself: whether the local tie-break shrinks to a citation, hands off joined guidance, expires with its carrier, returns authority, sunsets priority, quarantines residue, or remains mixed.

Use this surface only when all four facts are public:

1. a scoped residue-drift governance surface was retired through a governance-retirement token;
2. the retired lesson earned post-governance-retirement portability, received a portability-drift token, and then required local conflict arbitration;
3. the local arbitration packet has already selected, joined, revoked, expired, excluded, or successor-routed the competing retired-governance lessons; and
4. the later receipt can name the current packet, arbitration token, closeout evidence, minimal surviving record, excluded live authority, successor surface if any, and fail-closed repair.

## External pressure from cleanup completion, audit retention, carrier expiry, authority return, rule sunsets, sampling windows, and transparency history

Kubernetes-style cleanup, finalizers, owner references, audit retention, admission bindings, and policy windows show that a live interlock can complete, a carrier can stop matching, and ordinary authority can resume while the record remains inspectable. Prometheus tombstones, staleness markers, alert/recording-rule windows, and retention horizons show that old evidence may retain warning value after its live signal expires. OpenTelemetry trace carriers and sampling policies show that a trace basis may hand off, narrow, or stop carrying a prior lesson. Transparency-log checkpoints preserve inclusion history while later revocation, expiry, or policy replacement can change live force.

Those analogies support a compact arbitration-retirement witness: record a settled local tie-break, hand joined guidance into the current packet, retire an expired carrier, return authority to the ordinary current owner, sunset a selected priority, quarantine nonwinning residue, or record a mixed retirement. They do not import platform cleanup controllers, observability stores, transparency logs, or admission systems as DelayBasin governance machinery.

## Settled arbitration vs joined handoff vs carrier expiry vs authority return vs priority sunset vs residue quarantine vs mixed retirement

Select `settled-retired-governance-drift-arbitration-retirement` when the local conflict is over and the record only needs to show what won or why no arbitration was needed. Select `joined-route-handoff-retired-governance-drift-retirement` when joined guidance belongs inside the current packet rather than in a separate arbitration memory. Select `carrier-expiry-retired-governance-drift-arbitration-retirement` when the decisive carrier or proof no longer matches. Select `authority-return-retired-governance-drift-arbitration-retirement` when the authority that borrowed the tie-break has returned to its ordinary lane. Select `priority-sunset-retired-governance-drift-arbitration-retirement` when a selected priority must sunset on a named window. Select `residue-quarantine-retired-governance-drift-arbitration-retirement` when losing or partially winning lessons stay inspectable but nonbinding. Select `mixed-retired-governance-drift-arbitration-retirement` only when the receipt names each branch and the smallest surviving record.

## Countermodels / probes

- A local tie-break chose a winner. A winner is not live retired-governance common law; require a named current packet, carrier, authority lane, or sunset before carrying it.
- A losing lesson still has a strong audit row or transparency proof. Durable evidence is not governing force; classify it as history, counterexample, tombstone warning, revoked record, expired proof, or quarantined residue.
- Two lessons were joined. Joined guidance belongs to the current packet and named carrier; it does not become a reusable template unless a later portability path earns that carry.
- A carrier or exit proof disappeared. Carrier expiry is not deletion of the record; it is a reason to retire the arbitration packet to history or residue.
- A successor route won. Successor routing may still need retirement: do not leave the retired-governance tie-break supervising the successor packet forever.
- The excluded synonyms `retired-governance-arbitration-retirement-court`, `exit-proof-conflict-ledger-by-default`, `successor-route-priority-board`, `winner-persists-after-retirement`, and `retired-governance-arbitration-retirement-ish` remain outside the allowed vocabulary.
