This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0202`. It answers the narrow question of how DelayBasin retires a local post-arbitration-governance-retirement portability-drift conflict-arbitration packet after precedence, join, revocation, exit-proof, or successor-route arbitration has already been stated.

The surface is intentionally smaller than a standing closeout court. It does not create an exit-proof priority ledger, freshness closeout board, carrier-freshness tribunal, reusable winner ladder, or post-retirement governance surface. It only closes the local tie-break so its warning, join, revocation, proof, or successor-route evidence can remain inspectable without becoming reusable priority law.

## Practice / observation

`rev0307` let compact post-arbitration-governance-retirement portability-drift conflicts collapse, locally join, prefer a current carrier, prefer revocation, prefer a live exit proof, prefer a successor route, or remain mixed. That solved local arbitration. It did not say what happens after the winning route is recorded: the tie-break might keep governing future packets as de facto common law, disappear before audit history can be inspected, or keep a successor route open with no retirement window.

Use this surface only when all four facts are public:

1. prior governance-threshold, governance-scope, governance-retirement, governance-retirement-portability, portability-drift, and drift-conflict-arbitration tokens are named;
2. the local arbitration packet has a selected winning route, joined route, revocation route, exit-proof route, successor route, or mixed route;
3. the later packet can name the handoff carrier, expiry window, authority-return note, priority-sunset rule, residue quarantine, successor surface, or fail-closed repair; and
4. the receipt states what survives as warning history, what loses live force, and which authority is explicitly not carried forward.

## External pressure from cleanup completion, audit retention, carrier expiry, authority return, rule sunsets, sampling windows, and transparency history

Cleanup finalizers, owner-reference handoffs, admission rebinding, audit retention, alert rule windows, trace-carrier horizons, sampling windows, transparency checkpoints, and revocation records all separate completion from erasure. A prior decision can remain inspectable while the interlock, lease, binding, priority, or current authority that made it live expires.

Those analogies support a compact arbitration-retirement witness: mark a local tie-break as settled, hand off a joined route, expire a carrier or proof, return authority, sunset the priority relation, quarantine residue, or record an honest mixed closeout. They do not import cleanup controllers, metrics stores, trace processors, transparency logs, admission systems, or standing priority boards into DelayBasin.

## Countermodels / probes

- A tie-break selected a winner. A winner is not a standing priority rule; record the local route and sunset the precedence relation unless a later successor route is opened.
- A joined route looks reusable. It may travel only as warning or audit history unless a separate portability/currentness witness admits it.
- A carrier or exit proof still exists. Existing evidence is not current authority after its lease, proof window, sampling horizon, or policy window expires.
- A revocation or warning remains unresolved. Preserve it as quarantine, not as hidden live governance.
- A successor route is named. The successor route gets its own authority; the arbitration packet does not keep governing after handoff.
- The excluded synonyms `post-arbitration-governance-retirement-arbitration-retirement-court`, `exit-proof-priority-ledger-by-default`, `freshness-closeout-board`, `winner-persists-after-post-arbitration-governance-retirement`, and `post-arbitration-governance-retirement-arbitration-retirement-ish` remain outside the allowed vocabulary.

## Design consequences

Receipts that use this family must name the prior governance-threshold, governance-scope, governance-retirement, governance-retirement-portability, portability-drift, and drift-conflict-arbitration tokens; the retired arbitration packet; the selected arbitration route; handoff carrier if any; expiry or authority-return evidence; selected retirement token; surviving warning or audit use; excluded live authority; successor surface if any; quarantine target if any; and fail-closed repair. The witness may close, hand off, expire, return authority, sunset priority, quarantine residue, or mix branches. It must not become a reusable freshness-priority ladder.
