## Practice / observation

DelayBasin can now decide whether retired tie-break registry residue should remain ignored, inspectable, audit-only, fresh-packet-gated, tombstone-only, or mixed. The remaining risk starts only when a fresh packet is actually opened from that residue: the packet may carry a useful local trace, but without a scope witness it can quietly inherit global operator authority from the old registry it was meant to keep retired.

This surface adds one reopened-residue scope witness. It does not create a reopened-residue scope court, global operator authority, binding revival board, silent-deletion auditor, or telemetry common-law engine. It classifies a fresh-packet reopen as packet-local, audit-bound, fresh-carrier, tombstone-bound, authority-limited, or mixed reopened-residue scope.

## External pressure from audit retention, tombstones, finalizers, admission scope, rule staleness, sampling carriers, and transparency history

Kubernetes TTL-after-finished cleanup distinguishes completed work from objects still under controller management (`REF-1067`). That supports `packet-local-reopened-residue`: a reopen packet must name the current packet and cannot treat a completed registry as live machinery.

Kubernetes garbage collection and finalizers separate ownership, deletion interlocks, and cleanup blockers (`REF-1068`, `REF-1070`). That supports `fresh-carrier-reopened-residue` and `authority-limited-reopened-residue`: a surviving finalizer-like trace may force a current carrier or authority check, but it does not restore old registry authority.

Kubernetes audit policy and audit configuration distinguish retained records from live admission authority (`REF-1071`, `REF-1072`). That supports `audit-bound-reopened-residue`: an audit reopen can reconstruct or verify the old trace without deciding future precedence.

Kubernetes admission policy and webhook matching bind effects to selected requests, bindings, match conditions, validation actions, failure policies, and timeouts (`REF-1073`, `REF-1076`). That supports current-scope boundaries: any reopened effect must name the present request/carrier/authority scope instead of borrowing registry-wide reach.

Prometheus recording/alerting rule windows and staleness semantics tie metric-derived conclusions to active evaluation and available series (`REF-1077`, `REF-1078`, `REF-1090`). That supports `tombstone-bound-reopened-residue`: stale or deleted metric evidence can constrain a reopen as a tombstone or unsupported-status marker, not as global proof.

OpenTelemetry sampling and tail-sampling processors keep trace evidence tied to policy, collector context, and trace grouping (`REF-1080`, `REF-1074`). That supports fresh-carrier and mixed scope: sampled trace residue may justify a current packet only inside the carrier it can still honestly identify.

Sigstore/Rekor transparency-log documentation preserves append-only monitorable history (`REF-1081`). That supports audit-bound and tombstone-bound branches: transparency history can remain monitorable without becoming a reopened operator ledger.

## Packet-local vs audit-bound vs fresh-carrier vs tombstone-bound vs authority-limited vs mixed reopened-residue scope

- `packet-local-reopened-residue`: the fresh packet may use the residue only for the named packet and may not export its conclusion to other cases.
- `audit-bound-reopened-residue`: the reopen is limited to reconstruction, verification, or audit trace repair and may not decide future priority.
- `fresh-carrier-reopened-residue`: the residue can matter only through a current carrier, such as a present digest, admission binding, metric rule, sampled trace, finalizer, or transparency-log entry that is named in the new packet.
- `tombstone-bound-reopened-residue`: the reopened packet may preserve a deletion, expiry, unsupported-status, or no-live-support tombstone that blocks silent revival without rebuilding custody.
- `authority-limited-reopened-residue`: the reopened packet may act only inside a current named authority boundary and must return or expire that authority when the local scope is discharged.
- `mixed-reopened-residue-scope`: more than one scope basis is material, such as audit-bound plus tombstone-bound, fresh-carrier plus authority-limited, or packet-local plus quarantined ambiguity.

## Countermodels / probes

- The old registry decided a similar case. Similarity is not scope; classify packet-local unless a current carrier and authority are named.
- The audit row is decisive for reconstructing what happened. Reconstruction is not precedence; classify audit-bound.
- A finalizer or admission binding still exists. The surviving carrier must be current and named; otherwise the reopen is unsupported.
- A tombstone proves the old case cannot revive. That blocks silent revival; it does not prove a new global rule.
- A transparency log preserves the old entry forever. Monitorability is not operator authority.
- Multiple traces point in different directions. Use mixed scope and quarantine what cannot be bounded instead of inventing a scope court.

## Design consequences

A valid reopened-residue scope packet names:

1. the prior governance-threshold, registry-scope, registry-retirement, and residue/reopen packets;
2. the fresh packet that is being opened from retired registry residue;
3. the selected `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_scope_state` token;
4. the exact residue carrier: digest, audit row, tombstone, finalizer, admission binding, metric rule, sampled trace, transparency-log entry, or mixed carrier;
5. the allowed scope: packet-local use, audit reconstruction, current carrier use, tombstone preservation, authority-limited action, or mixed scope;
6. excluded future cases, telemetry families, carrier scopes, authorities, policy windows, and precedence claims;
7. the successor surface if reopened residue later needs closeout, expiry, handoff, or shrink-back governance.

A compact reopened-residue scope packet may give a fresh packet local use of residue. It must not create a reopened-residue scope court, global operator authority, binding revival board, silent-deletion auditor, permanent audit tribunal, or telemetry common-law engine.

## Overflow test

Promote a reopened-residue scope court, global operator authority, binding revival board, or equivalent standing governance only if later revisions show repeated, public, archive-local failures where:

- `packet-local-reopened-residue`, `audit-bound-reopened-residue`, `fresh-carrier-reopened-residue`, `tombstone-bound-reopened-residue`, `authority-limited-reopened-residue`, and `mixed-reopened-residue-scope` cannot keep fresh-packet reopens bounded;
- audit-only reopens repeatedly become live precedence, tombstones repeatedly erase support or become deletion courts, or carrier/authority ambiguity repeatedly escapes packet-local repair;
- a stronger process names its operator authority, admission or audit boundary, live carrier scope, precedence boundary, reopen trigger, and retirement witness.

Until then, `QWS-0266` stays quarantined.
