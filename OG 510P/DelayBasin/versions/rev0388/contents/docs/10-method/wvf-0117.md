`rev0318` answers the question: how do transported closeout expiry-record travel records close without becoming a tombstone registry? The answer is not a tombstone registry board, expiry-record closeout court, carrier-resurrection archive, permanent expiry tombstone ledger, or closed tombstone authority by default. ## Working pressure

`rev0317` admitted a compact transported closeout expiry-record portability family. An expiry record can remain nonportable, travel as an audit tombstone, warning-only cue, successor-bound context, redacted summary, quarantine reference, or mixed branch.

A later packet may still need to close that travel record. If the travel record never closes, audit tombstones and warnings accumulate into a registry that outlives the packet-local reason for travel. If the record disappears too early, a later pass loses why an expired carrier stayed non-renewed. The missing middle is a closeout token that preserves the non-renewal fact while ending the record's live travel role.

## Token semantics

### `closed-transported-closeout-expiry-record-travel`

Use when the transported expiry-record travel has completed its packet-local purpose and no longer needs to be carried as an active warning, tombstone, successor cue, or quarantine pointer. The source record remains inspectable, but the later packet no longer spends it.

### `tombstone-pruned-transported-closeout-expiry-record-travel`

Use when an audit tombstone remains visible only as a minimal non-renewal marker. The pruned tombstone names the expired carrier and closeout reason class, but strips reusable carrier details, positive authority, and registry-like indexing.

### `warning-sunset-transported-closeout-expiry-record-travel`

Use when a warning-only carry has reached its sunset condition. The sunset record explains that the warning is no longer active in ordinary routing while preserving the reason it once blocked unsafe reuse.

### `successor-handoff-transported-closeout-expiry-record-travel`

Use when the traveled expiry record closes because a named successor packet now carries any remaining context. The successor must provide its own authority; the closed expiry-record travel does not become the successor's carrier.

### `quarantine-expired-transported-closeout-expiry-record-travel`

Use when the only remaining travel form is a quarantined pointer whose inspection window has expired or whose ambiguity is no longer worth active carry. Later use requires a fresh packet, not quiet registry recall.

### `redacted-freeze-transported-closeout-expiry-record-travel`

Use when the closeout preserves a redacted frozen summary and explicitly prevents further expansion without reopening the source. The freeze keeps the non-renewal reason legible while refusing carrier resurrection through detail recovery.

### `mixed-transported-closeout-expiry-record-travel-closeout`

Use only when branch-local transported expiry records genuinely close in different ways. The receipt must name the branch-local prior travel tokens, carriers, closeout form, surviving record, stripped authority, successor or quarantine path, and fail-closed repair.

## Closeout rule

A transported closeout expiry-record travel record closes by ending its active travel role while preserving enough source or successor reference to explain non-renewal. Closeout may be complete closure, tombstone pruning, warning sunset, successor handoff, quarantine expiry, redacted freeze, or honest mixed branch.
