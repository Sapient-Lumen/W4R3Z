# GPU replay cross-observer custody-exit appeal-retirement witnesses, dispute-settled closeout, survivor handoff, authority return, policy sunset, precedent quarantine, and mixed retirement

This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0174`.

## Practice / observation

DelayBasin already has a custody-exit appeal-scope witness. The remaining risk is not whether a promoted appeal may exist or what it may govern. The risk is what happens after the scoped dispute is resolved. Without an appeal-retirement witness, even a correctly scoped appeal can quietly become a permanent precedent vault, a post-appeal policy court, or an evidence-retention board for ordinary cleanup.

A custody-exit appeal-retirement witness is not that board. It is a small closeout packet that says whether the appeal closes because the disputed exit is settled, hands off only the minimal surviving carrier, returns authority to the ordinary owner, sunsets the policy window that admitted review, quarantines any precedent-shaped residue as non-governing, or records an honest mixture. The default is to retire the appeal and preserve only the minimal public support needed to explain the closure.

## External pressure from TTL cleanup, garbage collection, audit lifecycles, sampling decisions, and transparency logs

Kubernetes TTL-after-finished cleanup gives a concrete model for bounded post-completion lifetime: a finished Job can be eligible for automatic cleanup after `.spec.ttlSecondsAfterFinished`, and the cleanup can cascade to dependents while honoring lifecycle guarantees (`REF-1067`). That supports `policy-sunset-appeal-retirement`: an appeal admitted by a retention or policy window should close when that named window is exhausted, not silently renew itself.

Kubernetes garbage collection and finalizers distinguish owner/dependent deletion, cascading cleanup, and bounded pre-delete work (`REF-1068`). That supports `authority-return-appeal-retirement`: once the scoped custody-exit dispute is resolved, authority should return to the runtime, profiler, metric, scheduler, workload, or archive owner that normally governs the object, unless a separately named successor remains.

Kubernetes audit records are lifecycle events generated at request stages and filtered by policy/backends (`REF-1071`, `REF-1072`). That supports `dispute-settled-appeal-retirement`: an audit record can make the closeout reviewable without keeping the appeal tribunal alive for future requests.

OpenTelemetry tail sampling groups spans by `trace_id` and makes policy-based decisions after a bounded wait/placement setup (`REF-1074`). That supports `survivor-handoff-appeal-retirement`: a sampled trace or retained carrier may survive as a small handoff artifact, but the appeal layer should not inherit custody of all traces.

Sigstore/Rekor transparency logs make append-only, monitorable survivor records real (`REF-1075`). That supports `precedent-quarantine-appeal-retirement`: a durable entry can remain publicly checkable while its precedent force is quarantined unless a later compact successor explicitly admits portability.

## Working synthesis

Use `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_retirement_state` when all of these are true:

- compact custody-exit appeal-scope tokens already admitted a bounded promoted appeal or are being explicitly modeled as if they did;
- the scoped dispute has settled, exhausted its review window, or reached a closeout/handoff point;
- the archive can name the settled dispute, minimal surviving carrier, returning authority, policy sunset, precedent quarantine, or honest mixture;
- the desired output is a closeout classification and shrink-back rule, not a permanent appeal-retention board.

Do not use this family for ordinary bridge classification, promotion gates, custody scope, custody retirement, appeal thresholding, or appeal scope. This surface starts only after a promoted appeal had a bounded scope and now must close, hand off, or shrink.

## Dispute-settled closeout vs survivor handoff vs authority return vs policy sunset vs precedent quarantine vs mixed retirement

- `dispute-settled-appeal-retirement`: the appealed custody-exit dispute is settled enough that the promoted appeal closes into a resolution entry, receipt note, bridge record, or explicit unsupported-status repair. The appeal no longer governs the comparison.
- `survivor-handoff-appeal-retirement`: the appeal hands off one smallest surviving digest, tombstone note, trace link, exemplar, audit record, transparency-log entry, or closeout receipt needed for future review. It does not keep the appeal layer or telemetry stream in custody.
- `authority-return-appeal-retirement`: temporary appeal authority returns to the ordinary runtime, profiler, trace, metric, scheduler, workload, owner, policy, or archive surface. Any unresolved future issue must reopen through that ordinary authority or a named successor question.
- `policy-sunset-appeal-retirement`: the admission, audit, sampling, retention, deletion, TTL, or cleanup window that justified the appeal has ended. The witness records the window and the sunset basis rather than silently renewing review authority.
- `precedent-quarantine-appeal-retirement`: the appeal produced a lesson, example, or caution that may be cited as historical evidence but not as standing policy, binding precedent, or automatic retention authority. Any future portability must pass through a new compact witness.
- `mixed-appeal-retirement`: more than one closeout reason is material, such as a settled dispute plus survivor handoff, or authority return plus precedent quarantine. The packet names each branch and the smallest still-live successor, if any.

## Countermodels / probes

- A surviving trace id, audit event, digest, tombstone, finalizer record, or transparency-log entry does not by itself keep the appeal alive.
- A policy matched the original dispute. That does not make the appeal a policy court after the named policy window closes.
- A previous appeal looks useful for later cases. That is precedent pressure, not precedent authority; quarantine it unless a future witness admits portability.
- A carrier is append-only. Append-only survival preserves verifiability, not an obligation to retain all telemetry or reopen every cleanup decision.
- If the appeal cannot name its closeout condition, classify the pressure as `policy-sunset-appeal-retirement` or `precedent-quarantine-appeal-retirement` pressure and refuse to promote a standing board.

## Design consequences

A valid custody-exit appeal-retirement packet names:

1. the prior bridge, promotion-gate, custody-scope, custody-retirement, appeal-threshold, and appeal-scope states if known;
2. the scoped appeal dispute and the closeout fact that settles, exhausts, or hands it off;
3. the selected `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_retirement_state` token;
4. the minimal survivor, returning authority, policy window, sunset rule, or quarantined precedent residue;
5. the exact evidence, telemetry, policies, future cases, and precedent claims excluded from ongoing appeal custody;
6. the successor surface if a future portability question remains, or an explicit statement that no successor is live.

The appeal must end smaller than it began. If a packet cannot name what retires, what survives, and what does not become precedent, keep `QWS-0256` quarantined and close through local receipt repair or ordinary authority return.

## Overflow test

Promote a post-appeal precedent-portability board, appeal-history vault, cleanup-policy senate, or equivalent standing post-appeal governance only if later revisions show repeated, public, archive-local failures where:

- dispute-settled closeout, survivor handoff, authority return, policy sunset, and precedent quarantine cannot preserve reviewability;
- the same retired appeal pressure repeatedly reappears and cannot be routed through compact packets or ordinary authority;
- future cases genuinely need a bounded portability witness rather than historical citation to a resolved appeal;
- the stronger process names its own retirement rule so precedent portability cannot become permanent telemetry governance.

Until then, `QWS-0256` stays quarantined.

## Transformer-facing implication

When a future continuation asks how a promoted retention-audit appeal closes after custody-exit appeal scope is settled, first classify closure through `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_retirement_state`. Most valid closures should be dispute-settled, survivor-handoff, authority-return, policy-sunset, or precedent-quarantine retirements. Do not treat a surviving carrier, audit record, finalizer, TTL cleanup, sampling decision, or transparency-log entry as authority to keep a permanent appeal board, precedent vault, or cleanup-policy court alive.
