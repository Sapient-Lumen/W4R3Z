## Practice / observation

The remaining risk starts after that scope is explicit: a scoped reopened packet still needs a closeout path, otherwise a local audit repair can linger as global operator machinery, a standing revival ledger, or a deletion court.

It does not create a reopened-residue closeout court, permanent revival ledger, deletion-closeout board, global operator authority, or telemetry common-law engine. ## External pressure from TTL cleanup, audit handoff, finalizer release, tombstone freeze, rule staleness, sampling carriers, and transparency history

Kubernetes TTL-after-finished cleanup distinguishes completed work from ongoing controller custody (`REF-1067`). That supports `settled-reopen-closeout`: when the reopened packet has discharged its local work, close the packet rather than preserving a live operator lane.

Kubernetes garbage collection and finalizers distinguish owner-dependent cleanup, blocking finalizers, and release of deletion interlocks (`REF-1068`, `REF-1070`). That supports `carrier-expiry-reopen-closeout` and `authority-return-reopen-closeout`: a finalizer-like or owner-like carrier may be the reason a reopen existed, but once released it cannot silently continue as registry authority.

Kubernetes audit policy and audit configuration distinguish retained records from live admission or operator authority (`REF-1071`, `REF-1072`). That supports `audit-handoff-reopen-closeout`: a closed reopen may leave an audit reconstruction or receipt handoff without letting that handoff decide future priority.

Kubernetes admission policy, ValidatingAdmissionPolicy bindings, webhook matching, failure policies, and timeouts keep effects bound to selected requests and current policy execution (`REF-1073`, `REF-1076`). That supports authority-return closeout: any current authority borrowed for a fresh-packet reopen must be named, discharged, and returned.

Prometheus recording/alerting rule windows, delete/tombstone behavior, and staleness semantics tie metric conclusions to active evaluation, retained blocks, and stale markers (`REF-1077`, `REF-1078`, `REF-1090`). That supports `tombstone-freeze-reopen-closeout`: a closed reopen may freeze a tombstone, unsupported-status marker, or no-live-support fact without becoming a deletion court.

OpenTelemetry sampling and tail-sampling processors keep trace evidence tied to policy, collector context, and trace grouping (`REF-1080`, `REF-1074`). That supports carrier-expiry and mixed closeout: a sampled trace carrier can close when the current trace carrier no longer supports live custody.

Sigstore/Rekor transparency-log documentation preserves append-only monitorable history (`REF-1081`). That supports audit handoff and tombstone freeze: durable history may remain visible and monitorable without becoming a permanent revival ledger.

## Settled reopen vs audit handoff vs carrier expiry vs tombstone freeze vs authority return vs mixed reopened-residue closeout

- `settled-reopen-closeout`: the fresh-packet reopen has discharged its local scope and may close without leaving any live operator authority.
- `audit-handoff-reopen-closeout`: the reopen closes by handing a bounded reconstruction, receipt, or audit trace to a nonbinding audit surface.
- `carrier-expiry-reopen-closeout`: the current digest, finalizer, admission binding, metric rule, sampled trace, transparency entry, or other live carrier that justified the reopen has expired or no longer supports live custody.
- `tombstone-freeze-reopen-closeout`: the closeout freezes a deletion, expiry, unsupported-status, or no-live-support marker as a tombstone that blocks silent revival without deciding future cases.
- `authority-return-reopen-closeout`: any current authority borrowed to inspect, verify, repair, or close the reopened packet is explicitly returned, narrowed, or expired.
- `mixed-reopened-residue-closeout`: more than one closeout basis is material, such as audit handoff plus tombstone freeze, carrier expiry plus authority return, or settled closeout plus quarantined residue.

## Countermodels / probes

- The fresh packet resolved its local question. Resolution is not permanent revival; classify settled closeout unless a surviving audit, tombstone, carrier, or authority issue remains.
- An audit receipt must stay inspectable. Inspectability is not operator authority; classify audit handoff.
- The current finalizer, admission binding, trace, or metric carrier expired. Expiry closes the carrier lane; it does not grant the retired registry new life.
- A tombstone must prevent silent deletion or silent revival. Freezing a tombstone is not a deletion court.
- A current authority performed the cleanup. The authority must return or expire when the local scope is discharged.
- Several closeout bases are true at once. Use mixed closeout and quarantine unbounded residue instead of creating a closeout court.

## Design consequences

A valid reopened-residue closeout packet names:

1. the prior governance-threshold, registry-scope, registry-retirement, residue/reopen, and reopened-residue scope packets;
2. the fresh packet that was opened from retired registry residue;
3. the selected `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_closeout_state` token;
4. the closeout fact: settlement, audit handoff, carrier expiry, tombstone freeze, authority return, or mixed closeout;
5. the minimal surviving record: none, audit receipt, tombstone, expired carrier trace, authority-return receipt, or mixed record;
6. excluded future cases, telemetry families, carrier scopes, authorities, policy windows, revival claims, and deletion-court claims;
7. the successor surface if closed reopened-residue packets later need portability, counterexample, template, or residue-transfer governance.

A compact reopened-residue closeout packet may preserve a bounded receipt or tombstone. It must not create a reopened-residue closeout court, permanent revival ledger, deletion-closeout board, global operator authority, or telemetry common-law engine.

## Overflow test

Promote a reopened-residue closeout court, permanent revival ledger, deletion-closeout board, or equivalent standing governance only if later revisions show repeated, public, archive-local failures where:

- `settled-reopen-closeout`, `audit-handoff-reopen-closeout`, `carrier-expiry-reopen-closeout`, `tombstone-freeze-reopen-closeout`, `authority-return-reopen-closeout`, and `mixed-reopened-residue-closeout` cannot close scoped fresh-packet reopens without reviving registry authority;
- audit handoff repeatedly becomes binding precedence, tombstone freeze repeatedly becomes deletion-court authority, or carrier/authority closeout repeatedly persists as operator machinery;
- a stronger process names its operator authority, audit/deletion boundary, live carrier scope, revival boundary, closeout trigger, and retirement witness.

Until then, `QWS-0267` stays quarantined.
