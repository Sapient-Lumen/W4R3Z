# GPU replay reopened-residue post-arbitration-governance-retirement governance-threshold scope witnesses, packet-local, closeout-history custody, authority-lane limited, successor-route bound, retirement-window, and mixed scope

This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0204`. It answers the narrow question of what a thresholded post-arbitration-governance-retirement surface may govern after compact threshold tokens overflow. It keeps scope bounded before any governance surface can act.

The surface is intentionally smaller than a governance scope court. It does not create a global closeout-history vault, successor-route board, authority-lane senate, or reusable threshold-governance operator. It only records which packet, history row, authority lane, successor route, and retirement window are allowed to matter.

## Practice / observation

`rev0309` established that post-arbitration-governance-retirement governance is not automatic. Compact threshold tokens must distinguish no standing, repeated arbitration-retirement failure, closeout-history loss, overbinding, authority split, successor closeout loops, or mixed threshold pressure. That solved the promotion threshold. It did not decide how far a promoted threshold surface may reach.

Use this surface only when all five facts are public:

1. the prior governance-threshold, arbitration-retirement, conflict-arbitration, portability-drift, portability, and governance-retirement tokens are named;
2. the threshold token is not ceremonial and the receipt explains why compact no-standing or local repair failed;
3. the affected packet, closeout-history row, authority lane, carrier, owner, or successor route is named;
4. the proposed scope is local enough to inspect and small enough to retire; and
5. the later receipt names the selected scope token, excluded global machinery, successor surface if any, and fail-closed repair.

## External pressure from owner references, finalizers, audit custody, policy binding, stale-signal windows, trace carriers, and transparency checkpoints

External systems separate escalation from jurisdiction. Owner references and finalizers can bind cleanup to named objects without making a controller global. Audit custody can preserve a record without making the record live authority. Policy bindings and authority lanes can limit who may act. Stale-signal windows and trace carriers show that context needs time and carrier boundaries. Transparency checkpoints preserve public proof while keeping later authority explicit.

Those analogies support a compact scope witness. They do not import admission controllers, cleanup boards, observability stores, tracing processors, or transparency logs as DelayBasin governance machinery.

## Packet-local vs closeout-history custody vs authority-lane limited vs successor-route bound vs retirement-window vs mixed scope

Select `packet-local-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-threshold-scope` when the only honest authority is over the named threshold-overflow packet. Select `closeout-history-custody-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-threshold-scope` when the strongest need is retaining enough closeout history for audit while keeping that history nonbinding. Select `authority-lane-limited-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-threshold-scope` when the governance surface exists only to separate incompatible authority lanes, owners, or policy bindings. Select `successor-route-bound-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-threshold-scope` when the scope is the successor route itself and not a general board. Select `retirement-window-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-threshold-scope` when the key scope limit is an explicit end condition. Select `mixed-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-threshold-scope` only when the receipt names each branch and the smallest successor question.

## Countermodels / probes

- A threshold token fired. Threshold is not scope; require the packet, lane, history row, route, and retirement boundary before acting.
- Closeout history is important. Importance is not live authority; classify history custody and keep it nonbinding outside the local packet.
- Two authority lanes disagree. Disagreement is not global jurisdiction; name the lane boundary and quarantine any wider claim.
- A successor route exists. A route is not a board; bind the route and specify when it hands off or expires.
- A scoped surface feels safer than no surface. Safety theater is not scope; if the receipt cannot name the local packet and closeout boundary, fail closed.
- The excluded synonyms `global-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-scope-court`, `closeout-history-vault-by-scope`, `successor-route-board-by-scope`, `authority-lane-senate-by-threshold`, and `post-arbitration-governance-retirement-scope-ish` remain outside the allowed vocabulary.

## Design consequences

Receipts that use this family must name the prior threshold token, threshold-overflow evidence, active packet, closeout-history rows, carriers or owners, authority lanes, successor route if any, selected scope token, excluded global machinery, retirement or closeout boundary, and fail-closed repair. The witness may authorize a successor question about retirement only after a scoped surface has actually been admitted. It may not silently instantiate a permanent court, vault, board, lane senate, or reusable governance operator.

## Overflow test

Promote a stronger surface only if `packet-local-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-threshold-scope`, `closeout-history-custody-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-threshold-scope`, `authority-lane-limited-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-threshold-scope`, `successor-route-bound-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-threshold-scope`, `retirement-window-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-threshold-scope`, or `mixed-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-threshold-scope` cannot distinguish local scope from global threshold governance. Until then, permanent governance-retirement courts, scope-history vaults, authority-lane boards, successor-route boards, and equivalent standing machinery stay quarantined under `QWS-0287`.
