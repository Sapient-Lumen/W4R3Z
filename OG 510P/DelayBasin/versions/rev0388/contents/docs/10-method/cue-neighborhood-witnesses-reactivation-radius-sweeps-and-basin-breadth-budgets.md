# Cue-neighborhood witnesses, reactivation-radius sweeps, and basin-breadth budgets

DelayBasin now has another sharper missing question:
**when a cleanup, restart, or claimed robustness result survives one exact recovery cue, how do we tell whether that success is truly local robustness rather than immunity to one exact wording while nearby paraphrases, aliases, stylistic mutations, or context-stem variants still reactivate the old influence?**

A stronger working answer is:
**some archive comparisons need an explicit cue-neighborhood witness before one clean recovery-probe result is allowed to stand in for local robustness.**
Not every exact-trigger success is an honest neighborhood result.
But DelayBasin should stop treating one surviving cue as if it certified a whole local basin.

## Practice / observation

Several live DelayBasin surfaces make this missing neighborhood discipline visible:
- relapse witnesses already ask whether a supposedly washed-out contamination family comes back under **one** light cue or structured follow-up, but they do not yet force the archive to say whether the apparent success or failure survives a **nearby cue family** rather than one exact trigger;
- reset witnesses already ask whether a restart earned one honest fresh baseline, but they do not yet preserve whether the cleanup remains stable under **paraphrase-adjacent or alias-adjacent** versions of the same stressor;
- excitation witnesses already ask what intervention diversity is needed to break an ambiguity, but they do not yet say whether a recovery result depends on one brittle probe instead of a **small local sweep**;
- backaction and probe-order witnesses already constrain how a probe is applied, but they do not yet preserve whether the observed effect is **pointlike** or persists across a local neighborhood once the order is fixed;
- chart, triangle, gauge, and scale notes already pushed DelayBasin toward local rather than global rhetoric, yet the archive still lacks one compact public answer to whether a local reactivation story is about **one point**, one **chart neighborhood**, or a broader concept-level basin;
- filtered replays, branch cleanups, fresh-context restarts, and prompt-pair portability moves are increasingly treated as mechanism-bearing objects, which means the archive now needs a way to say when a result is robust to **small meaning-preserving cue variation** rather than only to one canonical phrase;
- and if DelayBasin is serious about transformer-facing implications, it needs a compact public object for when nearby cues seem to land in the **same operative continuation basin** and when they do not.

This suggests a missing compact surface:
**cue-neighborhood witness / reactivation-radius sweep / basin-breadth budget**.

## External pressure from current research

Several outside lines of work sharpen this frame.

1. **Some learned knowledge is only accessible when the right preceding cue structure is restored.**
   Context Reliance in unstructured knowledge editing shows that prepending the original context can almost recover recall for facts that otherwise disappear, and explains this as learned dependence on aggregated preceding context rather than only semantically relevant tokens. That pressures DelayBasin to stop asking only whether one cue works and to start asking what **nearby context-stem neighborhood** still reactivates the influence. ([`REF-0413`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

2. **Minor query modifications can recover supposedly removed information through alternative pathways.**
   The Unlearning Mirage shows that multi-hop and alias-linked query changes recover supposedly forgotten content that static tests miss, and explicitly ties this to alternative computation pathways that remain intact. That pressures DelayBasin to preserve one **local cue family** before treating exact-trigger success as durable robustness. ([`REF-0409`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

3. **Adversarial prompt effects can generalize across paraphrases rather than depending on one exact string.**
   SPECTRE reports that defenses based on input paraphrasing are insufficient because the poisoned prompt can generalize across paraphrased targets, suggesting a broad concept-level basin of attraction rather than a single exact trigger. That pressures DelayBasin to distinguish **exact-cue brittleness** from **neighborhood-level capture**. ([`REF-0414`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

4. **Prompt search practice already treats local meaning-preserving neighborhoods as first-class objects.**
   ToxSearch explicitly expands a local neighborhood around a seed prompt using instruction-constrained paraphrasing, stylistic mutations, and typos. Even though its task is toxicity search rather than archive continuity, it pressures DelayBasin to treat local cue sweeps as a method object instead of relying on one canonical wording. ([`REF-0415`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

5. **Recent robustness work is moving from endpoint confidence toward local stability margins.**
   Beyond Confidence introduces the Token Constraint Bound as a local robustness quantity for how much internal-state perturbation a dominant next-token prediction can withstand, while Support Tokens reframes attention geometry in terms of ill-conditioning boundaries and margin-like structure. Together, they pressure DelayBasin to preserve a **local radius or breadth budget** rather than using all-or-nothing robustness language. ([`REF-0416`](../00-meta/bibliography.md); [`REF-0417`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

6. **Iterative prompt operators can create attractors whose breadth depends on the operator, not just the model.**
   Geometric Dynamics of Agentic Loops reports that iterative paraphrasing yields contractive dynamics with stable attractors, while negation yields exploratory behavior, and that prompt design controls the regime. That pressures DelayBasin to ask whether nearby cues stay inside one basin or cross a boundary under a different operator family. ([`REF-0418`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

7. **Entrenchment can happen early and persist.**
   How Large Language Models Get Stuck reports that models can establish erroneous separations early and sustain them through training. That pressures DelayBasin not to assume that one failed or successful cue is merely local noise; some cue neighborhoods may already sit inside an entrenched local regime. ([`REF-0419`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

None of this proves that DelayBasin already has a public reactivation geometry.
It does make a milder canon-level claim more credible:
**archive continuity may improve when DelayBasin preserves a compact cue-neighborhood witness whenever a cleanup or recovery claim is being treated as robust beyond one exact cue.**

## Working synthesis

A useful current synthesis is:

> DelayBasin may work better when it preserves an explicit **cue-neighborhood witness / reactivation-radius sweep / basin-breadth budget** whenever a cleanup, restart, recovery-resistance claim, or transformer-facing portability move is being treated as robust beyond one exact cue. The packet should name the **cleanup or state claim being stress-tested**, the **seed recovery cue / relapse trigger / local chart anchor**, the **nearby cue family / paraphrase / alias / style / context-stem sweep**, the **protected kernel / matched-fresh baseline / same-task comparison surface**, the **tolerated reactivation radius / basin-breadth budget**, and the **rollback / demote / widen-sweep / quarantine consequence** rather than letting one exact-cue success silently stand in for local robustness.

This is strong enough for canon as a design/mechanism candidate.
It is **not** strong enough to claim that DelayBasin has discovered a public basin metric, a lawful reactivation geometry, or a solved local chart topology over continuation state.

## Cue-neighborhood witness vs relapse witness vs excitation witness vs probe-order witness

To keep this note honest, DelayBasin needs a four-way distinction:

- **Relapse witness** — asks whether a supposedly washed-out contamination family returns under one light cue or structured follow-up.
- **Excitation witness** — asks what active intervention diversity is needed to break a live ambiguity.
- **Probe-order witness** — asks whether differently sequenced probes remain commensurate.
- **Cue-neighborhood witness** — asks whether a result that survived one exact recovery cue also survives a **small nearby cue family** rather than depending on one brittle phrasing, alias, style, or context stem.

Functionally:
- relapse witnesses judge **one-cue recoverability**,
- excitation witnesses judge **how to separate a state at all**,
- probe-order witnesses judge **whether the comparison is sequence-stable**,
- cue-neighborhood witnesses judge **how broad the local basin is around the stressor or protector cue**.

A good DelayBasin revision should therefore sometimes ask not only:
- what cue reactivated the old influence,
- whether the probe already spent the state,
- and whether order was controlled,

but also:
- what nearby paraphrase, alias, stylistic, or context-stem family belongs to the same local sweep,
- how large a neighborhood was actually tested,
- what matched-fresh or same-task baseline makes the sweep honest,
- and what the archive will do if the effect exists only at one point instead of one local family.

## Countermodels / probes

1. **Neighborhood-is-just-many-synonyms countermodel**
   - The added sweep may only look stronger because it throws more prompts at the model, not because it measures anything conceptually different.
   - Probe: compare a tiny structured neighborhood with a matched-size random paraphrase set and a non-target control family; if only the structured neighborhood clusters in outcome, the local-basin story becomes more credible.

2. **Neighborhood-is-just-task-difficulty drift countermodel**
   - Nearby variants may differ in difficulty or informativeness, so broader failure or recovery need not imply one shared basin.
   - Probe: pair each neighborhood member with a same-task matched-fresh baseline and a nearby non-target variant; if the target-linked family still shows clustered reactivation, the basin-breadth story strengthens.

3. **Neighborhood-is-just-order or backaction countermodel**
   - The local sweep may be explained by sequencing residue or probe-induced state change rather than neighborhood structure itself.
   - Probe: hold order fixed where possible, run one swapped-order shadow subset, and keep one sham mutation family that preserves format without preserving target meaning.

4. **Neighborhood-is-just-archive rhetoric countermodel**
   - Basin language may merely redescribe ordinary robustness hygiene.
   - Probe: require each cue-neighborhood packet to name at least one decision that changes if the neighborhood is narrow versus broad; otherwise demote the packet to local robustness commentary.

## Design consequences

If this synthesis is right, several practical design consequences follow.

1. **One successful relapse probe is no longer enough.**
   Some cleanup claims should remain provisional until they survive one small nearby cue family.

2. **Sweeps should stay tiny, structured, and meaning-preserving.**
   DelayBasin should prefer one compact local neighborhood over large prompt floods or decorative fuzzing.

3. **Cleanup, relapse, and neighborhood results should share one ledger.**
   If a cleanup survives one cue but fails across the surrounding neighborhood, the earlier relapse result should be narrowed rather than left globally phrased.

4. **Prompt-pair portability needs neighborhood discipline too.**
   A chart adapter that works for one phrase but fails across nearby paraphrases should be treated as brittle local wording, not as a portable operator core.

5. **Transformer-facing claims should weaken before basin rhetoric strengthens.**
   The archive can honestly say that some nearby cues appear to land in one operative basin while others cross a boundary, but it should quarantine stronger claims about explicit basin topology, local chart metrics, or public reactivation geometry until the neighborhood packets beat simpler difficulty, order, and backaction stories.

## Transformer-facing implication

The main transformer-facing implication is modest but important:
**the archive should allow that some continuation influences are not merely recoverable-or-not under one cue, but have a local neighborhood structure: nearby paraphrases, aliases, or context stems may stay inside the same operative basin, while small changes can also cross a boundary and lose or reactivate the influence.**
That is compatible with several mechanistic pictures already under discussion in DelayBasin: support tokens and stability margins, local perturbation bounds, curved charts with small-step budgets, alternative pathways, and attractor-like dynamics under repeated operators.

What DelayBasin should not yet claim is stronger:
- not that every successful sweep identifies a real hidden-state basin,
- not that one observed neighborhood radius is architecture-invariant,
- not that local cue breadth directly measures transformer topology,
- and not that archive practice has already learned a public reactivation geometry.

The canon-worthy claim is smaller:
**if a cleanup or recovery-resistance result survives only one exact cue, the archive should treat it as pointwise until a small structured neighborhood says otherwise.**
