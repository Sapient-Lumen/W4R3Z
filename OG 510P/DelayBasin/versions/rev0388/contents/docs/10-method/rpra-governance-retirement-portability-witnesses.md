This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0199`. It answers the narrow question of how a retired post-arbitration governance-retirement lesson may travel after a scoped governance surface has discharged, sunset, returned authority, frozen conflict history, quarantined residue, or handed off a successor. It repairs the rev0304 successor seam by giving `OQ-0199` a real registry-backed proof surface instead of leaving it as a referenced-but-uninstantiated frontier.

The surface is intentionally smaller than a portability court. It lets a later packet cite a retired post-arbitration governance lesson only as nonbinding history, audit reference, carrier-specific template, exit proof, counterexample, successor-packet requirement, or an explicit mixed case. It does not revive the retired governance surface, reopen conflict-history custody, or create a carrier exchange.

## Practice / observation

`rev0304` closed scoped post-arbitration governance with compact governance-retirement tokens. That solved the afterlife of the promoted governance surface itself, but it also left a precise carry question: a later packet may need to learn from the retired governance closeout without treating that retired surface as live authority. A later continuation therefore needs a portability token before spending the old governance lesson.

Use this surface only when all four facts are public:

1. prior governance-retirement, portability, drift, conflict-arbitration, arbitration-retirement, governance-threshold, governance-scope, and governance-retirement tokens are named;
2. the scoped post-arbitration governance surface has actually retired through discharge, scope sunset, authority return, conflict-history freeze, residue quarantine, successor handoff, or mixed closeout;
3. a later packet wants to cite, reuse, compare, template, warn from, or require successor evidence from that retired governance lesson; and
4. the later packet can name the carrier, audit trail, exit proof, counterexample, successor-packet boundary, or expiry basis that prevents revival of the retired governance surface.

## External pressure from audit retention, finalizer release, tombstone portability, trace carrier reuse, transparency inclusion proof, and policy revalidation windows

Kubernetes-style finalizers and owner references show that cleanup history can remain inspectable after the live interlock releases. Kubernetes audit and admission surfaces show that retained evidence and current admission authority are different lanes. Prometheus tombstones, rule windows, alert windows, and staleness markers show that old facts can remain visible while losing current force. OpenTelemetry trace carriers and sampling horizons show that a lesson may travel with a named carrier without generalizing to every future packet. Transparency checkpoints show that inclusion proof can preserve a durable exit fact without turning the log into a policy court.

Those analogies support a compact post-arbitration-governance-retirement portability witness: keep the retired lesson nonportable, cite it as audit reference, reuse it as a carrier-specific template, carry only the proof that governance exited, treat it as a counterexample, require a fresh successor packet for live use, or mark an honest mixed case. They do not import cleanup controllers, observability stores, admission engines, trace processors, transparency logs, or carrier exchanges as DelayBasin governance machinery.

## Countermodels / probes

- If a later packet relies on old governance authority rather than old governance history, this witness must fail closed to `successor-packet-required-post-arbitration-governance-retirement`.
- If an exit proof is being used to smuggle a retired priority ladder into a new carrier, classify the carry as `counterexample-only-post-arbitration-governance-retirement` or quarantine it.
- If a carrier template lacks a named carrier, owner, expiry, or current validation basis, it is not `carrier-template-post-arbitration-governance-retirement`; it is nonportable or successor-required.
- If a packet merely remembers that a court-like surface once existed, use `audit-reference-post-arbitration-governance-retirement`, not a portability court.
- The excluded synonyms `post-arbitration-governance-retirement-portability-court`, `retired-governance-carrier-exchange`, `conflict-history-exit-proof-registry`, `retired-post-arbitration-authority-travels-live`, and `post-arbitration-governance-retirement-portability-ish` remain outside the allowed vocabulary.

## Design consequences

Receipts that use this family must name the prior governance-threshold, governance-scope, and governance-retirement tokens; the retired packet; the later packet; the carrier or audit basis; the minimal lesson carried; the excluded live authorities; any successor-packet requirement; and the fail-closed repair. The witness may authorize a successor question about portability drift only after the retired lesson has actually traveled. It may not silently preserve a board, custody ledger, authority-return court, carrier exchange, exit-proof registry, or reusable governance operator.
