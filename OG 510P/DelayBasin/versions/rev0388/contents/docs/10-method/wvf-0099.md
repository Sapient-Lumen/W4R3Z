# GPU replay reopened-residue drift governance-retirement portability-drift conflict-arbitration witnesses, no arbitration, local join, current carrier precedence, revocation precedence, exit-proof precedence, successor-route precedence, and mixed arbitration

This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0194`. It answers the narrow question of how DelayBasin routes conflicts among portable retired-governance drift tokens after currentness, stale-template status, carrier conflict, revocation, exit-proof expiry, successor-required live effect, or mixed drift has already been made explicit.

The surface is intentionally smaller than a retired-governance drift-arbitration court. It does not create an exit-proof priority ladder, carrier-freshness tribunal, revocation appeal board, or portable retired-authority registry. It only routes a material disagreement that remains after compact drift tokens have already done their currentness and revocation work.

## Practice / observation

`rev0299` let a portable retired residue-drift governance lesson be classified as current, stale-template-only, carrier-conflicted, revoked, expired, successor-required, or mixed drift. That was enough for one lesson. It did not decide what to do when two or more such compact labels survive and disagree about the same later packet: for example, one carrier still looks current, another exit proof has expired, a revocation note withdraws part of the lesson, and a successor route is required for any live effect.

Use this surface only when all four facts are public:

1. a scoped residue-drift governance surface was retired through a governance-retirement token;
2. the retired lesson already earned post-governance-retirement portability and then received a portability-drift token;
3. at least two portable retired-governance lessons, carrier templates, audit references, exit proofs, revocation notes, expiry windows, or successor-required claims materially disagree about the same later packet; and
4. the later packet can name the current carrier, audit basis, exit proof, revocation note, expiry window, successor route, or fail-closed repair that proves the local tie-break.

## External pressure from admission rebinding, audit retention, tombstones, trace carriers, transparency revocation, finalizer release, and policy windows

Kubernetes-style admission and policy rebinding show that a current request, object, binding, match condition, failure policy, and authority boundary can outrank older but similar records. Kubernetes audit retention shows that old records can remain reviewable without governing current action. Cleanup finalizers and garbage-collection release show that live interlocks can be withdrawn while history remains inspectable.

Prometheus tombstones, staleness markers, alert windows, and recording-rule windows show that visible old evidence is not automatically current signal. OpenTelemetry trace carriers and sampling policies show that a carrier can be rebound, narrowed, or made nonmatching. Transparency-log checkpoints preserve inclusion history while later revocation, expiry, or policy replacement can change what is current.

Those analogies support a compact conflict-arbitration witness: collapse to no arbitration when only one current route remains; locally join compatible surviving lessons; prefer the current carrier when it is public and authority-bearing; prefer explicit revocation when it withdraws force; prefer an unexpired exit proof over an expired one; prefer a successor route when any requested live effect exceeds retired history; or record a mixed arbitration. They do not import platform controllers, observability stores, transparency logs, or admission systems as DelayBasin governance machinery.

## Working synthesis

The witness family is `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_retirement_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_state`. It is exact-token only:

- `no-arbitration-needed-retired-governance-drift-conflict` — after currentness, staleness, carrier, revocation, expiry, and successor checks, the apparent conflict collapses to one surviving route or nonbinding history.
- `local-join-retired-governance-drift-conflict` — multiple surviving lessons can be joined inside the same later packet because their carriers, audit bases, exit proofs, revocation notes, expiry windows, and authority boundaries are compatible.
- `current-carrier-precedence-retired-governance-drift` — the lesson tied to the current carrier, selector, object, audit row, tombstone status, trace/sampling basis, policy binding, or authority lane outranks a nonmatching carried lesson.
- `revocation-precedence-retired-governance-drift` — a public authority-return note, audit correction, policy replacement, tombstone release, cleanup decision, sampling change, or explicit revocation defeats the older lesson's governing force while preserving it as history.
- `exit-proof-precedence-retired-governance-drift` — a still-live exit proof, retention horizon, carrier lease, policy window, sampling window, or inherited sunset outranks an expired proof or stale template.
- `successor-route-precedence-retired-governance-drift` — a fresh successor packet is the winning route because the later packet wants live governance, new carrier exchange, broader authority, or cross-packet force that retired portability cannot carry.
- `mixed-retired-governance-drift-arbitration` — two or more branches are material and must be stated separately, such as current-carrier precedence plus partial revocation, local join plus expired exit-proof exclusion, or successor-route precedence plus surviving audit-only history.

## No arbitration vs local join vs current carrier vs revocation vs exit proof vs successor route vs mixed arbitration

Select `no-arbitration-needed-retired-governance-drift-conflict` when the conflict disappears once stale, revoked, expired, carrier-conflicted, and successor-required branches are removed from live effect. Select `local-join-retired-governance-drift-conflict` only when the surviving lessons can be composed without one silently overriding another. Select `current-carrier-precedence-retired-governance-drift` when the present carrier or authority-bearing lane is the decisive current basis. Select `revocation-precedence-retired-governance-drift` when explicit withdrawal defeats an old lesson's force. Select `exit-proof-precedence-retired-governance-drift` when the tie turns on a still-live proof or sunset window. Select `successor-route-precedence-retired-governance-drift` when any live effect must be routed through a new packet. Select `mixed-retired-governance-drift-arbitration` only when the receipt names each branch and the smallest surviving use.

## Countermodels / probes

- Two retired-governance drift tokens disagree. Disagreement is not automatically a tribunal; first ask whether one side is stale, revoked, expired, carrier-conflicted, or successor-required.
- One exit proof is older or more familiar. Familiarity is not priority; current carrier, explicit revocation, live proof, and successor need must be named.
- A revocation exists but the warning is still useful. Revocation defeats governing force, not reviewability; preserve the old lesson as history, stale template, counterexample, or excluded evidence.
- Two lessons look joinable. Use local join only if their carriers, audit bases, exit proofs, revocation notes, expiry windows, and authority boundaries can coexist without hidden override.
- A successor packet feels heavy. If the desired effect is live governance or broader carrier exchange, successor routing wins over convenience.
- The excluded synonyms `retired-governance-drift-arbitration-court`, `exit-proof-priority-ladder-by-default`, `carrier-freshness-tribunal`, `revoked-retired-governance-lesson-reanimated`, and `retired-governance-drift-arbitration-ish` remain outside the allowed vocabulary.

## Design consequences

Receipts that use this family must name the prior governance-retirement token, post-governance-retirement portability token, portability-drift token, retired packet, later packet, competing lessons or carriers, current carrier/audit/exit-proof/revocation/expiry/successor evidence, selected arbitration token, winning route, joined or losing lessons, smallest surviving use, excluded live authorities, successor surface if any, and fail-closed repair. The witness may select, join, shrink, revoke, expire, or route through a successor packet. It must not become a reusable retired-governance priority ladder.

## Overflow test

Promote a stronger surface only if repeated future revisions show that `no-arbitration-needed-retired-governance-drift-conflict, local-join-retired-governance-drift-conflict, current-carrier-precedence-retired-governance-drift, revocation-precedence-retired-governance-drift, exit-proof-precedence-retired-governance-drift, successor-route-precedence-retired-governance-drift, or mixed-retired-governance-drift-arbitration` cannot route retired-governance drift conflicts without either losing necessary warnings or reviving retired governance as live authority. Until then, retired-governance drift-arbitration courts, exit-proof priority ladders, carrier-freshness tribunals, revocation appeal boards, and equivalent standing machinery stay quarantined under `QWS-0277`.

## Transformer-facing implication

For a transformer reopening DelayBasin, the important move is to separate local conflict routing from standing governance. The model should ask: after compact retired-governance drift classification, does the apparent conflict collapse, locally join, prefer the current carrier, prefer revocation, prefer a live exit proof, require a successor route, or mix branches? It should emit one exact token from `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_retirement_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_state` and refuse to infer a standing freshness tribunal from a hard retired-governance drift conflict.
