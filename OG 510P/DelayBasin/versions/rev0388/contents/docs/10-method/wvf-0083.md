# GPU replay cross-observer custody-exit appeal precedent-conflict arbitration retirement witnesses, settled tiebreak, joined handoff, carrier expiry, authority return, priority sunset, residue quarantine, and mixed retirement

This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0178`.

## Practice / observation

DelayBasin now has a compact way to route conflicts among retired GPU replay custody-exit appeal lessons: no arbitration needed, local join, carrier precedence, authority precedence, sunset precedence, revocation precedence, or mixed arbitration. One ambiguity remains after the tie-break succeeds. A local arbitration packet can keep living as if it were a reusable priority ladder unless the archive says how it retires.

This surface adds one closeout witness for the arbitration packet itself. It does not reopen the conflict and does not turn the winning lesson into permanent law. It asks whether the tie-break is settled, whether joined lessons hand off to the current packet, whether the carrier expired, whether authority returned, whether the priority window sunset, whether nonwinning residue should remain quarantined, or whether more than one closeout branch is material.

## External pressure from TTL cleanup, finalizers, alert resolution, sampling decisions, admission scope, and transparency logs

Kubernetes TTL-after-finished cleanup makes completed work eligible for automatic deletion through `.spec.ttlSecondsAfterFinished` while respecting lifecycle cleanup boundaries (`REF-1067`). That supports `carrier-expiry-arbitration-retirement`: a carrier can expire after a settled decision without keeping the arbitration packet alive as governance.

Kubernetes garbage collection, cascading deletion, owner references, and finalizers preserve a distinction between owner/dependent cleanup and custom cleanup interlocks (`REF-1068`). That supports `authority-return-arbitration-retirement` and `residue-quarantine-arbitration-retirement`: a closeout packet can name the returning authority or the residue kept behind a finalizer-like brake without making every old tie-break immortal.

Kubernetes admission webhooks and ValidatingAdmissionPolicy keep policy application tied to request matching, selectors, bindings, validation actions, failure policy, and timeouts (`REF-1073`, `REF-1076`). That supports `settled-tiebreak-arbitration-retirement` and `joined-lesson-handoff-arbitration-retirement`: once the current request path or binding absorbed the local tie-break, the arbitration packet can shrink to a citation rather than stay as a universal admission court.

Prometheus recording and alerting rules separate reusable expressions, rule groups, evaluation intervals, `for` windows, and `keep_firing_for` continuation windows from indefinite rule force (`REF-1077`, `REF-1078`, `REF-1090`). That supports `priority-sunset-arbitration-retirement`: a prior alert or recorded rule may keep firing or remain queryable for a bounded window, but its closeout must name when priority ends.

OpenTelemetry sampling and tail-sampling processors separate configured sampling policies, trace-level criteria, and trace grouping from later retention decisions (`REF-1080`, `REF-1074`). That supports joined handoff and authority return: a winning sampling lesson may guide the present trace packet while the arbitration packet retires after the collector policy or trace decision no longer needs it.

Sigstore/Rekor transparency-log documentation preserves append-only monitorable records (`REF-1081`). That supports residue quarantine rather than priority permanence: the arbitration decision can remain durable history without keeping the winner as live precedent for unrelated future cleanup.

## Working synthesis

Use `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_conflict_arbitration_retirement_state` when all of these are true:

- a compact `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_conflict_arbitration_state` packet has already resolved or routed a material conflict among retired custody-exit appeal lessons;
- the current question is no longer which lesson wins, but how the local tie-break packet closes, hands off, expires, returns authority, sunsets, quarantines residue, or mixes those closeout reasons;
- the desired output is a bounded closeout record, not a permanent tie-break registry, precedent-priority ledger, arbitration-closeout court, global priority ladder, or telemetry common-law engine.

Do not use this family for first-pass replay envelopes, receipts, trace grades, bridge custody, appeal thresholding, appeal scope, appeal retirement, precedent portability, precedent drift, or the original conflict-arbitration decision. It starts only after compact arbitration has already selected, joined, shrunk, or declined the competing lessons.

## Settled tiebreak vs joined handoff vs carrier expiry vs authority return vs priority sunset vs residue quarantine vs mixed retirement

- `settled-tiebreak-arbitration-retirement`: the local conflict is settled for the current cleanup case, the winning or no-arbitration token is recorded, and the arbitration packet shrinks to a citation plus explicit exclusions.
- `joined-lesson-handoff-arbitration-retirement`: two or more compatible lessons were locally joined and their surviving guidance is handed to the current packet, carrier, policy binding, trace, or cleanup rule rather than preserved as a separate arbitration layer.
- `carrier-expiry-arbitration-retirement`: the evidence carrier, trace, metric series, rule group, admission request, transparency-log entry, or retention window that made the tie-break material expired or ceased to be the current carrier, so the arbitration packet retires to history.
- `authority-return-arbitration-retirement`: the authority boundary that needed a tie-break has returned to an ordinary owner, policy binding, cleanup controller, retention rule, or appeal-retirement surface; the arbitration packet no longer carries decision authority.
- `priority-sunset-arbitration-retirement`: the priority chosen by the tie-break remains valid only until a named TTL, review date, alert/recording-rule window, sampling policy window, policy sunset, or retention horizon, after which it becomes nonbinding history unless reopened.
- `residue-quarantine-arbitration-retirement`: nonwinning or partially winning lessons remain useful as counterexamples, stale records, revoked history, transparency-log evidence, or cautionary residue, but their governing force is quarantined.
- `mixed-arbitration-retirement`: more than one closeout reason is material, such as a settled tie-break with residue quarantine, joined handoff plus authority return, or carrier expiry plus priority sunset. The packet names each branch and the smallest surviving record.

## Countermodels / probes

- A tie-break chose a winner. A winner is not a permanent law; require a named current packet, carrier, authority, or priority window before carrying it forward.
- The losing lesson still has evidence. Evidence survival is not governing force; classify it as residue quarantine, history, counterexample, revoked record, or stale template unless a new portability/drift path reopens it.
- The arbitration packet was useful twice. Reuse alone is not a registry; verify carrier match, authority match, and sunset before treating it as portable.
- A transparency log or audit record keeps the decision visible. Visibility is not priority; append-only history can support residue quarantine without keeping a court alive.
- A TTL or rule window expired. Expiry is not erasure of history; it is a reason to retire priority and preserve only the minimal citation or counterexample.
- If closeout cannot be named, use `mixed-arbitration-retirement` with explicit unresolved residue rather than promoting an arbitration-closeout court.

## Design consequences

A valid arbitration-retirement packet names:

1. the prior conflict-arbitration packet and selected arbitration token;
2. the future cleanup case, packet, policy, trace, rule group, carrier, or authority boundary that needed closeout;
3. the selected `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_conflict_arbitration_retirement_state` token;
4. the closeout evidence: settled claim, joined lesson handoff, carrier expiry, authority return, priority sunset, residue quarantine, or mixed branch;
5. the minimal surviving record, if any, and whether it is current guidance, history, counterexample, stale template, revoked record, transparency-log citation, or excluded evidence;
6. future cases, carriers, authorities, policies, trace families, retention windows, or appeal layers explicitly excluded;
7. the successor surface if repeated closeout failures later require a stronger governance proposal.

A compact arbitration-retirement packet may preserve a digest, citation, or counterexample. It must not preserve a standing priority ladder, global appeal court, or precedent registry by default.

## Overflow test

Promote a permanent tie-break registry, precedent-priority ledger, arbitration-closeout court, or equivalent standing governance only if later revisions show repeated, public, archive-local failures where:

- `settled-tiebreak-arbitration-retirement`, `joined-lesson-handoff-arbitration-retirement`, `carrier-expiry-arbitration-retirement`, `authority-return-arbitration-retirement`, `priority-sunset-arbitration-retirement`, `residue-quarantine-arbitration-retirement`, and `mixed-arbitration-retirement` cannot close compact tie-break packets;
- local arbitration packets repeatedly persist as de facto global priority ladders despite named sunset, carrier expiry, authority return, or residue quarantine;
- nonwinning lessons repeatedly regain governing force merely because their evidence remains visible;
- the stronger process names its own scope, retirement rule, nonbinding-history lane, and authority limit.

Until then, `QWS-0261` stays quarantined.
