## Token semantics

### `nonportable-transported-closeout-history-portability-expiry-record`

Use when the portability-expiry record must not travel outside the source packet. The old expiry fact remains inspectable at source but grants no warning, template, or successor authority elsewhere.

### `audit-reference-transported-closeout-history-portability-expiry-record`

Use when a later packet may cite the expiry record only to explain why old closeout-history carry ended. The audit reference cannot reopen the tombstone, renew the carrier, or decide a later packet.

### `warning-only-transported-closeout-history-portability-expiry-record`

Use when the expiry record travels only as a caution that a prior carry failed, expired, or was revoked. The warning is nonbinding and cannot accumulate into a review layer.

### `successor-context-transported-closeout-history-portability-expiry-record`

Use when a named successor packet may receive limited context from the expiry record. The successor must mint its own authority, horizon, and closeout rule rather than inherit the old record as live governance.

### `redacted-summary-transported-closeout-history-portability-expiry-record`

Use when only a stripped summary may travel because source details, carriers, or closed-history evidence should not be revived. The redaction preserves explanation while blocking hidden renewal.

### `quarantine-reference-transported-closeout-history-portability-expiry-record`

Use when the only admissible travel route is a quarantine reference. The record may be inspected for why promotion was denied or cooled, but it cannot become ordinary carry.

### `mixed-transported-closeout-history-portability-expiry-history-portability`

Use only when branch-local expiry records travel in different ways. The receipt must name the prior expiry token, branch source, travel form, successor or quarantine path, landing-surface additions-alignment guard state, and fail-closed repair.
