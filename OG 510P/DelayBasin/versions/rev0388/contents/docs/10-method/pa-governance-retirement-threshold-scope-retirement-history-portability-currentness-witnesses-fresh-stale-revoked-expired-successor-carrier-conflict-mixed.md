# GPU replay reopened-residue post-arbitration-governance-retirement threshold-scope retirement-history portability-currentness witnesses, fresh, stale, revoked, expired, successor-superseded, carrier-conflict, and mixed

This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0207`.

`rev0313` answers the question: what drift or revocation witness, if any, keeps portable retired threshold-scope history current without reviving threshold governance? The answer is not a drift court, revocation board, currentness exchange, or carrier-conflict senate. It is a packet-local currentness witness that says whether an already-portable retired threshold-scope history item is still fresh, stale, revoked, expired, superseded by a successor, carrier-conflicted, or honestly mixed.

## Working pressure

`rev0312` admitted a compact retired threshold-scope history portability family. That family can keep old history nonportable, audit-only, template-only, authority-return-warning-only, successor-required, counterexample-only, or mixed. It deliberately makes the history nonbinding.

A later packet may still ask whether that nonbinding carry is current. If the archive says portable retired history is always current, audit references and closeout templates become quiet precedent. If the archive discards the history whenever a new packet appears, useful authority-return warnings and counterexamples vanish. The missing middle is a small currentness classification that can stale, revoke, expire, supersede, or conflict a carry without reviving threshold governance.

## Token semantics

### `fresh-retired-threshold-scope-history-carry`

Use when the carried retired threshold-scope history remains current for its explicitly nonbinding use. The receipt must name the prior portability token, the later packet, the evidence that the carry has not stale-dated, and the boundary that prevents it from becoming live threshold authority.

### `stale-retired-threshold-scope-history-carry`

Use when the carried retired history may remain inspectable but is no longer a current audit reference, template, warning, successor trigger, or counterexample for the later packet. It can explain why an old packet closed; it cannot guide the current one.

### `revoked-authority-return-retired-threshold-scope-history`

Use when the authority-return warning itself has been revoked, corrected, or narrowed by a public successor surface. The revocation removes or narrows the warning; it does not create a new revocation board or positive authority.

### `expired-closeout-template-retired-threshold-scope-history`

Use when a closeout template or audit reference had an explicit window, freshness horizon, review date, or carrier condition and that condition has expired. The old template may remain historical, but its template force is expired unless a successor packet re-earns it.

### `successor-superseded-retired-threshold-scope-history`

Use when a named successor packet supersedes the old retired-history carry. The old record remains inspectable as predecessor context; the successor packet carries the current nonbinding lesson, if any.

### `carrier-conflict-retired-threshold-scope-history`

Use when two or more carriers, successor packets, audit references, or authority-return warnings make incompatible currentness claims. This token records the conflict and fails closed to nonbinding audit-only use unless a later local arbitration packet is earned.

### `mixed-retired-threshold-scope-history-currentness`

Use only when multiple currentness shapes are genuinely present and cannot be collapsed without hiding evidence. The receipt must name each branch, the branch-local currentness status, and the fail-closed repair.

## Currentness rule

Portable retired threshold-scope history may remain current only as a nonbinding carry. A currentness token may keep a warning fresh, mark a carry stale, revoke or narrow a warning, expire a template, hand the carry to a successor, record carrier conflict, or preserve a mixed case. It must not revive threshold governance.

The currentness witness is admissible only after a prior `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_retirement_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_retirement_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_threshold_governance_scope_retirement_history_portability_state` token made the history portable or explicitly nonportable. It cannot be used to skip portability, create a drift court, create an authority-return revocation board, or treat old history as current by default.

## Status tail guard

`rev0313` also repairs a status-ledger split-brain risk discovered after `rev0312`: top-level head fields in `SURFACE-STATUS.json` were aligned with the current bundle, but legacy tail fields still carried older revision, stamp, summary, and release-surface values. The new status current-key guard requires terse current status fields, release fields, bundle fields, revision fields, timestamp fields, and summary fields to agree with the manifest and receipt unless they are explicitly previous/historical fields.

## Fail-closed rule

If portable retired threshold-scope history currentness is unclear, mark it stale, keep it audit-only, or open the successor `OQ-0208`. Do not promote a `retired-threshold-scope-history-drift-court`, `authority-return-revocation-board`, `scope-history-currentness-exchange`, `carrier-conflict-senate`, or `portable-history-current-by-default` by default.
