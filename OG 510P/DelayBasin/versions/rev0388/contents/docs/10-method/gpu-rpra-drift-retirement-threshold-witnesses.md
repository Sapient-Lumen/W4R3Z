## Practice / observation

DelayBasin can already retire compact post-closeout portability-drift conflict-arbitration packets through settled tiebreak, joined handoff, carrier expiry, authority return, priority sunset, residue quarantine, and mixed retirement. The live question is not how to close one local tie-break. It is what public evidence would justify even considering standing residue-drift retirement governance after those compact closeout tokens are explicit.

The tempting move is to promote a drift-retirement court, revival-history vault, or arbitration-closeout board whenever a local tie-break leaves awkward residue. This surface adds only a threshold witness. It distinguishes no standing governance from repeated compact closeout failure, loss of necessary nonbinding history, overbinding of old history into de facto revival law, authority split, sunset breach, or mixed escalation. It does not create the court, vault, board, standing revival-priority ladder, deletion court, or telemetry common-law engine.

## External pressure from audit retention, transparency history, cleanup ownership, admission scope, rule sunsets, and sampling windows

Kubernetes audit policy and audit backends separate recorded events, policy levels, stages, and log retention from live admission authority (`REF-1071`, `REF-1072`). That supports `history-loss-reopen-drift-retirement-governance`: threshold pressure is real only when compact citations repeatedly fail to preserve necessary nonbinding closeout facts, not when old records are merely interesting.

Sigstore/Rekor transparency-log documentation preserves append-only monitorable history (`REF-1081`). That supports both `no-standing-reopen-drift-retirement-governance` and `history-overbinding-reopen-drift-retirement-governance`: durable visibility can keep a lost tie-break inspectable, but visible history must not silently become governing force.

Kubernetes garbage collection, owner references, and finalizers distinguish cleanup ownership, deletion interlocks, and finalizer release (`REF-1068`, `REF-1070`). That supports `authority-split-reopen-drift-retirement-governance`: stronger governance is only a threshold question when legitimate cleanup, policy, retention, or packet owners repeatedly block one another after compact authority-return and residue-quarantine paths have been tried.

Kubernetes dynamic admission control and ValidatingAdmissionPolicy bind policy effect to request context, match conditions, policy bindings, validation actions, and failure policy (`REF-1073`, `REF-1076`). That supports the no-standing default: most retirement residue should be absorbed by the current packet or policy binding rather than promoted into a permanent closeout layer.

Prometheus recording rules, alerting windows, staleness behavior, tombstone cleanup, and delete-series effects bind metric-derived conclusions to expressions, labels, evaluation intervals, stale markers, and retention horizons (`REF-1069`, `REF-1077`, `REF-1078`, `REF-1090`). That supports `sunset-breach-reopen-drift-retirement-governance`: repeated silent extension after named TTLs, review dates, rule windows, sampling windows, or tombstone lifetimes is a threshold signal.

OpenTelemetry sampling and tail-sampling processors keep trace evidence tied to collector policy, trace grouping, and sampling criteria (`REF-1080`, `REF-1074`). That supports mixed escalation when history loss, overbinding, authority split, and sunset breach are entangled across trace carriers or policy windows.

## No standing governance vs repeated failure vs history loss vs history overbinding vs authority split vs sunset breach vs mixed escalation

- `no-standing-reopen-drift-retirement-governance`: compact retirement tokens still suffice; local tie-breaks remain citations, handoffs, nonbinding history, counterexamples, tombstone warnings, revoked records, expired sunsets, or quarantined residue without standing governance.
- `repeated-closeout-failure-reopen-drift-governance`: multiple public, archive-local attempts to use settled tiebreak, joined handoff, carrier expiry, authority return, priority sunset, residue quarantine, and mixed retirement have failed for the same class of residue-drift closeout problem.
- `history-loss-reopen-drift-retirement-governance`: useful nonbinding history repeatedly disappears so completely that later packets cannot inspect why a closed-reopen lesson won, lost, joined, expired, or was quarantined.
- `history-overbinding-reopen-drift-retirement-governance`: old local retirement history repeatedly regains governing force merely because audit rows, transparency-log entries, tombstones, rules, or trace records remain visible.
- `authority-split-reopen-drift-retirement-governance`: two or more legitimate packet, cleanup, policy, retention, appeal, or ownership authorities repeatedly claim final closeout authority after compact authority-return repair has failed.
- `sunset-breach-reopen-drift-retirement-governance`: named TTLs, review dates, alert windows, sampling-policy windows, tombstone lifetimes, policy sunsets, or retention horizons are repeatedly breached or silently extended so retired drift tie-breaks revive as live priority.
- `mixed-reopen-drift-retirement-governance-escalation`: more than one threshold is material, such as history loss plus authority split, overbinding plus sunset breach, or repeated closeout failure plus nonbinding-history disappearance.

## Countermodels / probes

- A local drift tie-break was cited again. Reuse is not threshold overflow; require repeated public failures after compact retirement tokens were actually tried.
- An audit row or transparency-log entry survived. Durable evidence is not a revival-history vault; classify visibility as nonbinding unless overbinding or history-loss evidence is public and repeated.
- A losing lesson was hard to find. One retrieval miss is not a standing vault; test whether compact citations, quarantine labels, and replay surfaces repeatedly fail.
- A policy owner disagreed with a cleanup owner. One disagreement is not a drift-retirement court; test whether authority-return repair failed repeatedly across the same closeout class.
- A rule window was extended. One extension is not a sunset breach; require named sunsets that repeatedly revive retired priority without a fresh compact witness.
- If the threshold cannot be named, use `no-standing-reopen-drift-retirement-governance` or `mixed-reopen-drift-retirement-governance-escalation` with explicit unresolved evidence rather than promoting a court, vault, or board.

## Design consequences

A valid standing-governance-threshold packet names:

1. the prior governance-threshold, registry-scope, registry-retirement, residue/reopen, reopened-residue scope, reopened-residue closeout, post-closeout portability, portability-drift, drift-arbitration, and drift-arbitration retirement packets;
2. the compact retirement token already tried, plus the current packet or class of packets where retirement failed or still suffices;
3. the public threshold evidence: repeated closeout failure, history loss, history overbinding, authority split, sunset breach, or mixed escalation;
4. the selected `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_threshold_state` token;
5. the smallest stronger surface under consideration, if any, and its nonbinding-history lane, authority limit, and sunset promise;
6. future packets, carriers, authorities, policies, trace families, tombstone windows, retention windows, and revival-precedent claims explicitly excluded;
7. the successor surface if threshold overflow later requires scoped standing governance rather than another compact threshold witness.

A compact governance-threshold packet may preserve a threshold digest, evidence list, no-standing-governance decision, or escalation sketch. It must not create the drift-retirement court, revival-history vault, arbitration-closeout board, registry operator, permanent priority ladder, or binding revival precedent.

## Overflow test

Promote a standing residue-drift retirement court, revival-history vault, arbitration-closeout board, or equivalent governance only if later revisions show repeated, public, archive-local failures where:

- `no-standing-reopen-drift-retirement-governance`, `repeated-closeout-failure-reopen-drift-governance`, `history-loss-reopen-drift-retirement-governance`, `history-overbinding-reopen-drift-retirement-governance`, `authority-split-reopen-drift-retirement-governance`, `sunset-breach-reopen-drift-retirement-governance`, and `mixed-reopen-drift-retirement-governance-escalation` cannot separate ordinary compact closeout from true standing-governance need;
- compact drift-arbitration retirement packets repeatedly vanish too early or persist as de facto revival law despite named threshold tokens;
- a stronger process names its scope, operator authority, nonbinding-history lane, retirement rule, audit/retention boundary, and sunset.

Until then, `QWS-0272` stays quarantined.
