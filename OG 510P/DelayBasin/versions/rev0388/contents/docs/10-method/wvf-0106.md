# GPU replay reopened-residue drift governance-retirement portability-drift conflict-arbitration retirement governance-retirement portability-drift conflict-arbitration witnesses, no arbitration, local join, current carrier precedence, revocation precedence, exit-proof precedence, successor-route precedence, and mixed arbitration

This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0201`. It answers the narrow question of how DelayBasin routes conflicts among post-arbitration-governance-retirement portability-drift or revocation witnesses after currentness, staleness, carrier conflict, revocation, exit-proof expiry, successor-required live effect, or mixed drift has already been made explicit.

The surface is intentionally smaller than a standing freshness court. It does not create an exit-proof priority ladder, carrier-freshness tribunal, revocation appeal board, portability-drift registry, or reusable retired-governance priority ladder. It only routes a material disagreement that remains after compact drift tokens have already done their currentness and revocation work.

## Practice / observation

`rev0306` let a carried retired post-arbitration governance lesson be classified as current, stale-template-only, carrier-conflicted, revoked, expired, successor-required, or mixed drift. That was enough for one carried lesson. It did not decide what to do when two or more such compact labels survive and disagree about the same later packet: for example, one carrier still looks current, another exit proof has expired, a revocation note withdraws part of the lesson, and a successor route is required for any live effect.

Use this surface only when all four facts are public:

1. prior governance-threshold, governance-scope, governance-retirement, governance-retirement-portability, and portability-drift tokens are named;
2. at least two carried retired post-arbitration governance lessons, carrier templates, audit references, exit proofs, revocation notes, expiry windows, or successor-required claims materially disagree about the same later packet;
3. the later packet can name the current carrier, audit basis, exit proof, revocation note, expiry window, successor route, or fail-closed repair that proves the local tie-break; and
4. the receipt states the winning route, any joined or losing lessons, the smallest surviving use, and the explicit authorities that do not survive.

## External pressure from admission rebinding, audit retention, tombstones, trace carriers, transparency revocation, finalizer release, and policy windows

Admission rebinding and policy-window practice show that a current request, object, binding, match condition, failure policy, and authority boundary can outrank older but similar records. Audit retention shows that old records can remain reviewable without governing current action. Cleanup finalizers and garbage-collection release show that live interlocks can be withdrawn while history remains inspectable.

Staleness markers, tombstones, alert windows, recording-rule windows, trace carriers, sampling horizons, and transparency checkpoints all show the same pressure in neighboring forms: visible old evidence is not automatically current signal, and a preserved proof can lose current force when revocation, expiry, carrier rebinding, or policy replacement is public.

Those analogies support a compact conflict-arbitration witness: collapse to no arbitration when only one route remains; locally join compatible surviving lessons; prefer the current carrier when it is public and authority-bearing; prefer explicit revocation when it withdraws force; prefer an unexpired exit proof over an expired one; prefer a successor route when any requested live effect exceeds retired history; or record a mixed arbitration. They do not import platform controllers, observability stores, transparency logs, or admission systems as DelayBasin governance machinery.

## Countermodels / probes

- Two post-arbitration-governance-retirement drift tokens disagree. Disagreement is not automatically a tribunal; first ask whether one side is stale, revoked, expired, carrier-conflicted, or successor-required.
- One exit proof is older or more familiar. Familiarity is not priority; current carrier, explicit revocation, live proof, and successor need must be named.
- A revocation exists but the warning is still useful. Revocation defeats governing force, not reviewability; preserve the old lesson as history, stale template, counterexample, or excluded evidence.
- Two lessons look joinable. Use local join only if their carriers, audit bases, exit proofs, revocation notes, expiry windows, and authority boundaries can coexist without hidden override.
- A successor packet feels heavy. If the desired effect is live governance or broader carrier exchange, successor routing wins over convenience.
- The excluded synonyms `post-arbitration-governance-retirement-drift-arbitration-court`, `exit-proof-priority-ladder-by-default`, `carrier-freshness-tribunal`, `revoked-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-lesson-reanimated`, and `post-arbitration-governance-retirement-drift-arbitration-ish` remain outside the allowed vocabulary.

## Design consequences

Receipts that use this family must name the prior governance-threshold, governance-scope, governance-retirement, governance-retirement-portability, and portability-drift tokens; the retired packet; the later packet; competing lessons or carriers; current carrier/audit/exit-proof/revocation/expiry/successor evidence; selected arbitration token; winning route; joined or losing lessons; smallest surviving use; excluded live authorities; successor surface if any; and fail-closed repair. The witness may select, join, shrink, revoke, expire, or route through a successor packet. It must not become a reusable retired-governance priority ladder.

## Overflow test

Promote a stronger surface only if repeated future revisions show that `no-arbitration-needed-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-drift-conflict, local-join-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-drift-conflict, current-carrier-precedence-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-drift, revocation-precedence-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-drift, exit-proof-precedence-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-drift, successor-route-precedence-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-drift, or mixed-post-arbitration-governance-retirement-drift-arbitration` cannot route post-arbitration-governance-retirement drift conflicts without either losing necessary warnings or reviving retired governance as live authority. Until then, post-arbitration-governance-retirement arbitration-retirement courts, exit-proof priority ledgers, freshness closeout boards, carrier-freshness tribunals, and equivalent standing machinery stay quarantined under `QWS-0284`.
