## Working pressure

## Token semantics

### `unexpired-transported-closeout-expiry-record-closeout-history-portability`

Use when the transported closeout-history portability record still sits within its named packet, horizon, use, source, and quarantine bounds. It remains nonbinding history and cannot decide a later tombstone or renew a carrier.

### `horizon-expired-transported-closeout-expiry-record-closeout-history-portability`

Use when the record's explicit time, revision, packet, or review horizon has passed. The source history remains inspectable, but ordinary carry is closed unless a successor packet creates fresh authority.

### `use-exhausted-transported-closeout-expiry-record-closeout-history-portability`

Use when the record was admitted for a bounded number of uses, packets, or handoffs and that use budget is spent. The expired record may explain past non-reopening but may not keep traveling as a live warning.

### `successor-superseded-transported-closeout-expiry-record-closeout-history-portability`

Use when a named successor packet has superseded the old portability carry. The successor must create its own token, authority, horizon, and closeout rule; the old closeout-history portability record becomes historical background only.

### `source-revoked-transported-closeout-expiry-record-closeout-history-portability`

Use when the source closeout, tombstone, carrier, or authority-return evidence is later revoked or contradicted. The carry fails closed to source history, quarantine, or a fresh successor; it does not become a review court.

### `quarantine-expired-transported-closeout-expiry-record-closeout-history-portability`

Use when the only allowed path for the portability carry was quarantine-reference and that quarantine path expires, is denied, or cools without promotion. Later use requires a fresh admission path rather than quiet recall.

### `mixed-transported-closeout-expiry-record-closeout-history-portability-expiry`

Use only when branch-local portability records expire in different ways. The receipt must name the prior portability token, branch source, expiry branch, horizon or use budget, successor or quarantine path, archive-index table-shape guard state, and fail-closed repair.

## Expiry rule

A transported closeout expiry-record closeout-history portability record may remain unexpired, horizon-expired, use-exhausted, successor-superseded, source-revoked, quarantine-expired, or honestly mixed. Expiry preserves source inspectability while ending ordinary carry. It never reopens the closed tombstone, renews the expired carrier, or creates a standing tombstone-history review layer.
