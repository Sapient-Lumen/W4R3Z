This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0211`.

`rev0317` answers the question: when may transported retired-history currentness closeout expiry records travel without renewing the expired carrier? The answer is not an expiry-record renewal exchange, carrier-resurrection court, portable expiry authority by default, tombstone registry board, or expired-carrier review senate. It is a packet-local expiry-history portability witness that says whether an expiry record stays nonportable, travels as an audit tombstone, travels as warning only, travels bound to a named successor, shrinks to a redacted summary, travels only by quarantine reference, or remains honestly mixed.

## Working pressure

`rev0316` admitted a compact transported closeout-history expiry family. A transported retired-history currentness-closeout record may remain unexpired, expire by horizon, exhaust its use, be superseded by a successor, be revoked by source evidence, expire through quarantine, or remain mixed.

A later packet may still need to explain why that expiry happened. If expiry records never travel, future packets lose the reason a carrier stopped participating in ordinary reentry. If expiry records travel as renewal authority, the expired carrier silently reopens. The missing middle is a small portability record that carries the expiry fact without carrying current-use permission.

## Token semantics

### `nonportable-transported-closeout-expiry-record-travel`

Use when the expiry record should stay in the source packet and later packets must cite only the source closeout, travel, and expiry surfaces. Nonportable expiry evidence may remain inspectable, but it is not carried forward as a warning, template, or successor cue.

### `audit-tombstone-transported-closeout-expiry-record-travel`

Use when a later packet may carry a tombstone that says the prior carrier expired and why. The tombstone is audit evidence only; it cannot renew the expired carrier, rerun carrier review, or authorize current use.

### `warning-only-transported-closeout-expiry-record-travel`

Use when a later packet may carry a warning that a prior carrier expired through a named horizon, exhausted use, source revocation, successor supersession, or quarantine path. The warning blocks unsafe reuse but does not become positive authority.

### `successor-bound-transported-closeout-expiry-record-travel`

Use when an expiry record may travel only as context for a named successor packet. The successor must supply its own authority; the expiry-history record merely explains why the old carrier no longer participates.

### `redacted-summary-transported-closeout-expiry-record-travel`

Use when the later packet needs the reason class but not the full source trail. The summary must name what was stripped, what authority is absent, and where the fuller record remains inspectable.

### `quarantine-reference-transported-closeout-expiry-record-travel`

Use when the only safe travel form is a pointer to a quarantined expiry record. The later packet may inspect the quarantine reference but may not treat it as a renewed carrier, current warning, or reusable template without a fresh packet.

### `mixed-transported-closeout-expiry-record-travel`

Use only when branch-local expiry records genuinely require different travel states. The receipt must name the branch-local prior expiry tokens, carriers, travel form, stripped authority, successor or quarantine path, and fail-closed repair.

## Portability rule

A transported retired-history currentness-closeout expiry record may travel only as nonbinding expiry history: nonportable source history, audit tombstone, warning-only cue, successor-bound context, redacted summary, quarantine reference, or honest mixed branch. Travel preserves why current-use permission ended while refusing to renew the expired carrier.

This portability witness is admissible only after a prior `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_retirement_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_retirement_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_threshold_governance_scope_retirement_history_portability_currentness_closeout_history_portability_expiry_history_portability_state` token. It cannot be used to skip expiry classification, resurrect an expired carrier, or create a standing tombstone registry.

## Fail-closed rule

If expiry-history portability is unclear, keep the expiry record nonportable, shrink it to an audit tombstone, route it through quarantine, or require a fresh successor packet. Do not promote an `expiry-record-renewal-exchange`, `carrier-resurrection-court`, `portable-expiry-authority-by-default`, `tombstone-registry-board`, or `expired-carrier-review-senate` by default.
