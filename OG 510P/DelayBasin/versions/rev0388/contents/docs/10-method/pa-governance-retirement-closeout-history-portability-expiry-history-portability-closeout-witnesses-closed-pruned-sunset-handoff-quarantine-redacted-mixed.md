## Token semantics

### `closed-transported-closeout-history-portability-expiry-history-portability-record`

Use when the transported expiry-history portability record has fully served its packet-local explanatory purpose. The source remains inspectable, but the travel record grants no further warning, review, successor, or renewal role.

### `tombstone-pruned-transported-closeout-history-portability-expiry-history-portability-record`

Use when the transported record closes by pruning the tombstone reference down to a source pointer. The pruned tombstone cannot be searched as a registry or used as precedent.

### `warning-sunset-transported-closeout-history-portability-expiry-history-portability-record`

Use when a warning-only carry must sunset after its named horizon or use. The sunset prevents warnings from accumulating into a standing review layer.

### `successor-handoff-transported-closeout-history-portability-expiry-history-portability-record`

Use when closeout hands the surviving context to a named successor packet. The successor must mint its own authority and cannot inherit the old warning, audit reference, or redacted summary as live governance.

### `quarantine-expired-transported-closeout-history-portability-expiry-history-portability-record`

### `redacted-freeze-transported-closeout-history-portability-expiry-history-portability-record`

## Closeout rule

Transported closeout-history portability-expiry history portability records close only by full closure, tombstone pruning, warning sunset, successor handoff, quarantine expiry, redaction freeze, or honest mixed closeout. Closeout preserves the source explanation while removing travel authority. It never renews the closed tombstone, converts a warning into standing review, or creates a portable expiry-history closeout court.
