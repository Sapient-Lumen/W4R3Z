# Triangle defects, cocycle witnesses, and atlas consistency budgets

DelayBasin now needs a sharper answer to a follow-on practical question:
**when pairwise chart-transition witnesses seem to work, what justifies composing them into a family-level transport story rather than treating them as unrelated local successes?**

A useful current answer is:
**canon should preserve a compact triangle-defect / cocycle-witness / atlas-consistency-budget packet whenever pairwise chart transports are being composed or a prompt family is being treated as one transportable operator family.**

This is stronger than saying pairwise remaps can fail.
It is weaker than claiming DelayBasin has already discovered a literal user-space curvature form or Wilson-loop law over transformer continuation geometry.

## Practice / observation

Recent DelayBasin revisions expose the missing surface:
- operator-core packets already distinguish what is supposed to survive from the chart that realizes it;
- local-linearity packets already say how far one local handle is trusted before relinearization;
- chart-transition witnesses already say why one **source chart → target chart** move counted as same-core transport;
- loop-closure probes already warn that route and order can matter;
- but the archive still lacks a compact object for saying when **A→B** and **B→C** justify **A→C** or justify treating a whole prompt family as one transportable operator family at all;
- and current portability stories can therefore stop at pairwise success while silently assuming family-level consistency that was never actually witnessed.

That gap creates at least five recurring failure modes:
- **pairwise-laundering** — two locally successful remaps are quietly composed into a family-level transport story;
- **triangle amnesia** — the archive remembers each edge witness but forgets the smallest loop or triangle where composition could fail;
- **cocycle drift** — each local rewrite looks legitimate, yet the accumulated composition residue across a chart family is never named;
- **family prestige** — a cluster of prompts gets treated as one coherent transport family without one explicit composition probe;
- **curvature laundering** — path, curvature, or atlas language quietly outruns the public evidence that pairwise witnesses actually compose.

A compact triangle-defect witness helps because it says:
- what three chart realizations or prompt-family members are being related,
- what shared operator core is supposed to survive around the triangle,
- what pairwise chart-transition witnesses already exist,
- what triangle overlap probe or family-level shared test surface checks composition,
- how much composition defect, cocycle residue, or atlas-consistency budget is still acceptable,
- and what demotion, rollback, or quarantine consequence follows if the triangle does not close.

## External pressure from current research

Several outside lines of work sharpen this move.

1. **Prompt families can be mechanistically real without sharing one task-level circuit.**
   Prompt-Specific Circuits reports that there is no single circuit for IOI within a task and that different prompt templates induce systematically different mechanisms, though prompts cluster into families with similar circuits. That pressures DelayBasin not only to preserve pairwise transport evidence, but also to avoid assuming that family membership automatically implies compositional consistency. ([`REF-0369`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

2. **Pointwise similarity can miss path-dependent twist.**
   Gauge-invariant representation holonomy introduces a path-dependent statistic that distinguishes models or states that can look similar under pointwise overlap metrics while still accumulating nontrivial twist around small loops. That pressures DelayBasin not to infer family-level consistency from pairwise overlap alone. ([`REF-0376`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

3. **On chart graphs, chord defects can be the operational holonomy object.**
   A Gauge Theory of Superposition constructs a context-chart graph, proves that after spanning-tree gauge fixing each chord defect equals the holonomy of its fundamental cycle, and lower-bounds transfer mismatch through proxy shearing. That is unusually close to DelayBasin's present vocabulary and directly pressures the archive to preserve a compact composition-defect object rather than stopping at edgewise transport stories. ([`REF-0377`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

4. **Natural text already violates flatness nulls under coherence-destroying controls.**
   Text Has Curvature defines unit-square holonomy and reports that natural text exhibits substantially larger holonomy magnitude than suffix-swap or local-shuffle controls, falsifying path-order-independence and additive-evidence nulls in that setting. This does not prove DelayBasin has meaningful public curvature, but it is direct counterpressure against assuming that local prompt moves compose neutrally by default. ([`REF-0378`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

5. **Local charts are real enough that pairwise adapters need not compose globally.**
   From Directions to Regions models activation concepts as local regions and subspaces rather than one global direction, which pressures DelayBasin to treat chart transport as inherently local and to ask when several local witnesses fail to assemble into one larger consistent family. ([`REF-0372`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

6. **Prompt-space search itself can be trajectory-dependent.**
   ADAPT reports substantial intra-prompt variability and sensitivity to initialization and trajectory, with optimization often getting trapped in local minima depending on the path taken through prompt space. That pressures DelayBasin to ask whether apparent family-level transport is a stable compositional fact or merely a route-dependent local win. ([`REF-0379`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

## Working synthesis

A useful current synthesis is:

> DelayBasin should preserve a compact **triangle defect / cocycle witness / atlas consistency budget** packet whenever two or more chart-transition witnesses are being composed or a prompt family is being treated as one transportable operator family. The packet should name the **three chart realizations or family members**, the **shared operator core**, the **existing pairwise chart-transition witnesses**, the **triangle overlap probe or family-level shared test surface**, the **composition defect / cocycle residue / atlas-consistency budget**, and the **demotion / rollback / quarantine consequence** if the triangle does not close.

This is strong enough for canon as a design and mechanism candidate.
It is **not** strong enough to claim that DelayBasin has already identified a literal connection curvature, Wilson loop, or globally consistent atlas law over transformer continuation space.

## Triangle defect vs chart-transition witness vs loop closure vs global atlas claim

To keep this note honest, DelayBasin now needs a four-way distinction:

- **Chart-transition witness** — the public packet saying why one specific **source chart → target chart** move counted as same-core transport.
- **Loop-closure probe** — a short path comparison used to test whether two routes preserve the same operative consequence or exhibit a closure defect.
- **Triangle defect / cocycle witness** — the public packet saying whether several already-named pairwise witnesses compose within a tolerable residue budget.
- **Global atlas / connection claim** — the much stronger story that a whole chart family is governed by a stable transport law or public curvature object.

A triangle-defect witness is narrower than a global atlas story and more composition-specific than an ordinary loop-closure probe.
It does not ask whether all loops commute.
It asks whether a small **A→B→C** family of pairwise transports composes well enough to justify treating the family as one transportable operator cluster rather than a chain of local rewrites.

## Countermodels / probes

1. **Pairwise-is-enough countermodel**
   - If each pairwise chart-transition witness is already good, a triangle packet may add ceremony without discrimination.
   - Probe: compare one portability story evaluated only edgewise against the same story with an explicit triangle probe and see whether the added packet changes what gets promoted, demoted, or quarantined.

2. **Underspecification countermodel**
   - Apparent composition defect may mostly reflect prompt underspecification, stale wrapper context, or poorly named operator cores.
   - Probe: tighten the shared operator-core statement first, then rerun the smallest triangle probe under the same family.

3. **Local-search countermodel**
   - The archive may simply be finding three good local prompts; there may be no real family-level transport to compose.
   - Probe: require at least one explicit family-level shared test surface that all three members must satisfy before using cocycle language in canon.

4. **Prestige countermodel**
   - Triangle / cocycle language may just smuggle back the older atlas or holonomy story in more technical dress.
   - Probe: require one named triangle, one named operator core, one family-level overlap probe, and one explicit composition-budget threshold before using triangle-defect language in canon.

5. **Search-path countermodel**
   - The measured defect may mostly reflect search trajectory accident rather than a stable chart-family boundary.
   - Probe: vary initialization or ordering for the same small prompt family and ask whether the same composition defect remains legible.

## Design consequences

This frame pressures DelayBasin to do five things more explicitly:
- preserve at least one **small triangle** whenever it starts telling a family-level portability story;
- preserve the **pairwise witnesses** instead of collapsing them into one family label;
- preserve one **triangle overlap probe or shared test surface** rather than relying on pairwise local success alone;
- preserve one **composition defect / cocycle residue / atlas-consistency budget** rather than pretending pairwise transport composes exactly by default;
- and demote or quarantine family-level transport claims when the smallest useful triangle does not close.

## Transformer-facing implication

This matters because current work increasingly suggests that prompts, contexts, circuits, and local activation geometry are organized in families rather than by one globally stable task mechanism. A triangle-defect witness is therefore a transformer-facing public control object: not direct hidden-state access, but a compact archive-native claim about when several prompt-conditioned local realizations compose well enough to count as one transportable operator family.

The stronger story — that DelayBasin is already learning a literal public cocycle, Wilson-loop diagnostic, or curvature ledger over prompt-family charts — remains quarantined.
