# GPU replay reopened-residue post-arbitration-governance-retirement threshold-scope retirement-history portability-currentness closeout witnesses, settled, stale-mark, revocation-frozen, expiry-complete, successor-handoff, conflict-quarantined, and mixed

This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0208`.

`rev0314` answers the question: how do retired threshold-scope history currentness decisions close without becoming permanent drift machinery? The answer is not a closeout court, drift-retirement board, revocation-history vault, standing currentness senate, or closed-currentness authority. It is a packet-local closeout witness that says whether a prior currentness decision has settled, left only a stale mark, frozen a revocation, completed expiry, handed off to a successor, quarantined conflict, or remains honestly mixed.

## Working pressure

`rev0313` admitted a compact retired threshold-scope history portability-currentness family. That family can mark portable retired history fresh, stale, revoked, expired, successor-superseded, carrier-conflicted, or mixed while keeping the carry nonbinding.

A later packet may still ask whether the currentness decision itself remains live. If every currentness decision stays live, old freshness checks become a durable drift layer. If every currentness decision disappears, later packets lose why a carry was stale, revoked, expired, superseded, or carrier-conflicted. The missing middle is a small closeout classification that can settle, freeze, expire, hand off, or quarantine the decision without creating permanent currentness governance.

## Token semantics

### `settled-retired-history-currentness-closeout`

Use when the currentness decision has completed its packet-local job and no further currentness action is needed. The receipt must name the prior portability token, prior currentness token, old packet, later packet, settlement evidence, and the nonbinding surviving record.

### `stale-mark-retired-history-currentness-closeout`

Use when the only surviving function of the currentness decision is to mark a retired-history carry as stale. The stale mark remains inspectable, but it cannot become a reusable stale-precedent rule or a default disqualification of future carries.

### `revocation-frozen-retired-history-currentness-closeout`

Use when a revocation, correction, or narrowing decision has been frozen as history. It records why the old authority-return warning was revoked or narrowed; it does not become a revocation board or positive authority for unrelated packets.

### `expiry-complete-retired-history-currentness-closeout`

Use when an explicit expiry, window, review date, or carrier condition completed and the currentness decision no longer participates in current packet routing. The old decision remains historical evidence only.

### `successor-handoff-retired-history-currentness-closeout`

Use when a named successor packet inherits the only surviving currentness work. The closeout record must name the successor, what was handed off, what did not transfer, and why the predecessor currentness decision is no longer live.

### `conflict-quarantined-retired-history-currentness-closeout`

Use when carrier, successor, audit-reference, or revocation conflicts cannot be resolved locally and the safest closeout is quarantine. The conflict remains inspectable as residue, but the token fails closed against standing currentness machinery.

### `mixed-retired-history-currentness-closeout`

Use only when multiple closeout shapes are genuinely present and cannot be collapsed without hiding evidence. The receipt must name each branch, the branch-local token, and the smallest surviving nonbinding role.

## Closeout rule

A retired threshold-scope history currentness decision may remain live only while it is doing packet-local currentness work. Once the work has settled, become only a stale mark, frozen a revocation, completed expiry, handed off to a successor, or become conflicted residue, it must close or shrink to nonbinding history.

This closeout witness is admissible only after a prior `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_retirement_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_retirement_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_threshold_governance_scope_retirement_history_portability_currentness_state` token. It cannot be used to skip portability, skip currentness, create a currentness closeout court, revive threshold governance, or treat closed currentness decisions as authority by default.
