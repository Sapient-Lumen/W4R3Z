This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0206`.

`rev0312` answers the question: when may retired threshold-scope governance history travel without reviving threshold governance? The answer is not a portability court, carrier exchange, precedent board, or common-law history surface. It is a packet-local portability witness that states the smallest nonbinding way a retired threshold-scope history item may be reused, or says that it must not travel.

## Working pressure

`rev0311` admitted a compact threshold-scope retirement family. That family can settle scoped governance, expire its window, return authority, freeze history, hand off a successor, quarantine residue, or record mixed closeout. It deliberately freezes history as nonbinding rather than live authority.

A later packet may still need to see that frozen history. If the archive says frozen history never travels, later packets lose useful warnings about authority return, closeout evidence, successor handoff, and failed scope patterns. If the archive lets frozen history travel as authority, threshold-scope retirement silently becomes the portability court it was meant to avoid.

## Token semantics

### `nonportable-retired-threshold-scope-history`

Use when the retired threshold-scope history must remain inspectable only in its original packet, receipt, or ledger. It may warn a reader, but it does not become a template, carrier, rule, or later-packet input.

### `audit-reference-retired-threshold-scope-history`

Use when the retired history may be cited only as an audit reference: what was closed, what authority returned, which window expired, or which residue was quarantined. It is admissible as evidence of the old closeout, not as a reason to decide the new packet.

### `closeout-template-retired-threshold-scope-history`

Use when the old closeout shape may supply a template for how to record settlement, window expiry, authority return, history freeze, successor handoff, residue quarantine, or mixed retirement in a later packet. The later packet must still earn its own scope, threshold, and retirement evidence.

### `authority-return-warning-retired-threshold-scope-history`

Use when the portable lesson is a warning that temporary authority previously returned to an ordinary owner or successor and therefore must not be treated as still live. This token carries a negative constraint, not a power to govern.

### `successor-packet-required-retired-threshold-scope-history`

Use when the old retired history is relevant only by opening or pointing to a fresh successor packet. The old history may define the starting warning, but the new packet must decide its own currentness, scope, and authority.

### `counterexample-only-retired-threshold-scope-history`

Use when the retired history travels only as a counterexample to an overbroad claim, such as “threshold governance always persists,” “authority return is optional,” or “history freeze is live precedent.” It cannot become a positive template or live rule.

### `mixed-retired-threshold-scope-history-portability`

Use only when multiple portability shapes are genuinely present and cannot be collapsed without hiding evidence. The receipt must name each branch and the fail-closed repair.

## Portability rule

Retired threshold-scope history may travel only as nonbinding audit evidence, a closeout template, an authority-return warning, a successor-packet trigger, a counterexample, or an explicit mixed carry. It must not travel as live threshold authority.

The portability witness is admissible only after a prior `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_retirement_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_retirement_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_threshold_governance_scope_retirement_state` token has retired the scoped governance surface. It cannot be used to skip retirement, revive a threshold, create a scope-history carrier exchange, or treat frozen history as current authority.

## Startup cue guard

`rev0312` is also a re-entry hygiene repair. A portability token is not safe if the startup surfaces still contain stale “current packaged head” cues from the prior revision. The new startup current-head check requires root startup surfaces to name the same current bundle and forbids duplicate current-head lines that point at older bundles.

## Fail-closed rule

If retired threshold-scope history portability is unclear, keep the history nonportable, cite it only as an audit reference, or open the successor `OQ-0207`. Do not promote a `retired-threshold-scope-portability-court`, `scope-history-carrier-exchange`, `authority-return-precedent-board`, `retirement-history-common-law`, or `portable-governance-by-default` by default.
