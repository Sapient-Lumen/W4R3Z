# GPU replay reopened-residue drift governance-retirement portability-drift witnesses, current carry, stale template, carrier conflict, revoked lesson, expired exit proof, successor-required, and mixed drift

This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0193`. It answers the narrow question of whether a retired residue-drift governance lesson that already earned post-governance-retirement portability remains current, has become a stale template, conflicts with a new carrier, has been revoked, has an expired exit proof, needs a successor packet, or mixes those states.

The surface is intentionally smaller than a portability-drift court. It does not create a carrier-revalidation board, exit-proof freshness registry, retired-authority tribunal, or revival court. It only classifies the present status of a portable retired-governance lesson before a later packet can spend it.

## Practice / observation

`rev0298` let retired residue-drift governance lessons travel as nonportable history, audit references, carrier templates, exit-proof references, counterexamples, successor-packet requirements, or mixed portability. That was enough for carry, but not enough for currentness. A lesson can be portable and still stale, carrier-conflicted, revoked, expired, or too live-effect-seeking to reuse without a fresh successor packet.

Use this surface only when all four facts are public:

1. a scoped residue-drift governance surface was already retired through the governance-retirement witness;
2. the retired lesson already has a post-governance-retirement portability token;
3. a later packet wants to spend, cite, reuse, refresh, revoke, or narrow that portable lesson; and
4. the later packet can name the current carrier, audit basis, exit proof, policy binding, tombstone, trace, transparency checkpoint, revocation note, or expiry window that proves currentness or drift.

## External pressure from audit-retention currentness, finalizer release, tombstone staleness, trace-carrier rebinding, transparency-log revocation, and policy revalidation windows

Kubernetes-style cleanup and finalizer release distinguish retained history from live interlock. Kubernetes audit and admission surfaces distinguish old evidence from current policy binding. Prometheus tombstones, stale series, rule windows, and alert lifetimes distinguish visibility from current signal. OpenTelemetry trace carriers and sampling horizons distinguish a traceable prior lesson from a current sampling basis. Transparency-log inclusion and checkpoint records preserve proof while still allowing later revocation, expiry, or carrier conflict to be named.

Those analogies support one compact drift/revocation witness: keep the lesson current only when the same carrier, audit lane, exit proof, policy binding, tombstone status, trace/sampling basis, and sunset still match; shrink it to a stale template when only shape survives; mark carrier conflict when the later packet's carrier no longer matches; mark revocation or expiry when a public note withdraws or times out live force; require a successor packet when the desired live effect exceeds the old portability token; or state a mixed case. They do not import platform controllers, observability stores, transparency logs, or admission systems as DelayBasin governance machinery.

## Working synthesis

The witness family is `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_retirement_portability_drift_state`. It is exact-token only:

- `current-retired-governance-portability` — the portable retired-governance lesson remains current because the same carrier, audit basis, exit proof, policy binding, authority boundary, tombstone status, trace/sampling criterion, and sunset still fit the later packet.
- `stale-retired-governance-template` — the lesson remains useful as a template, warning, or pattern, but its carrier, policy text, audit basis, exit-proof context, tombstone status, trace criterion, or rule window is no longer live enough to govern the later packet.
- `carrier-conflicted-retired-governance` — the later packet names a different or conflicting carrier, selector, object, trace, audit row, tombstone, policy binding, transparency checkpoint, or authority lane from the one that made the retired lesson portable.
- `revoked-retired-governance-lesson` — a later authority-return note, audit correction, policy replacement, tombstone release, sampling-policy change, cleanup decision, or explicit revocation withdraws the old lesson's governing force while preserving history.
- `expired-retired-governance-exit-proof` — the exit proof, review window, retention horizon, carrier lease, policy window, sampling window, or inherited sunset that permitted carry has expired.
- `successor-required-retired-governance-drift` — the later packet wants live governance effect, new carrier exchange, or broader authority than the old portability token can carry; route through a fresh successor packet instead of importing retired governance.
- `mixed-retired-governance-drift` — two or more branches are material and must be stated separately, such as stale template plus expired exit proof, carrier conflict plus partial revocation, or current audit reference plus a successor-required live effect.

## Current carry vs stale template vs carrier conflict vs revoked lesson vs expired exit proof vs successor-required vs mixed drift

Select `current-retired-governance-portability` only when the old carry basis still matches the later packet's current carrier and authority boundary. Select `stale-retired-governance-template` when the old lesson can guide shape but not bind present action. Select `carrier-conflicted-retired-governance` when the later packet's carrier, selector, audit row, tombstone, trace, policy binding, or transparency checkpoint conflicts with the old portability basis. Select `revoked-retired-governance-lesson` when a public later act withdraws the lesson's governing force. Select `expired-retired-governance-exit-proof` when the carry was time/window/retention/lease bounded and that bound has ended. Select `successor-required-retired-governance-drift` when the later packet seeks live authority, a new carrier exchange, or a broader governance effect. Select `mixed-retired-governance-drift` only when the receipt names each subcase.

## Countermodels / probes

- A retired governance lesson was portable. Portability is not currentness; check carrier, audit, exit-proof, policy, tombstone, trace, transparency, revocation, and sunset before spending it again.
- An exit proof still exists. Existing proof is not fresh authority if the exit proof was bounded by a review window, carrier lease, or policy horizon that expired.
- A carrier template looks similar. Similar shape is not a carrier match unless the later packet re-establishes the same current carrier and authority boundary.
- A revocation note exists. Revocation does not erase the old record; classify it as `revoked-retired-governance-lesson` unless a new current authority independently re-admits it.
- A packet wants the old retired lesson to act like governance. Use `successor-required-retired-governance-drift`, not a hidden portability-drift court.
- The excluded synonyms `retired-governance-portability-drift-court`, `carrier-revalidation-board-by-default`, `exit-proof-freshness-registry`, `revoked-retirement-history-erased`, and `retired-governance-drift-ish` remain outside the allowed vocabulary.

## Design consequences

Receipts that use this family must name the prior governance-retirement token, the post-governance-retirement portability token, the retired packet, the later packet, the current carrier or audit basis, the exit-proof or revocation evidence if any, the selected drift token, the smallest surviving use, explicit excluded live authorities, any successor-packet route, and the fail-closed repair. The witness is a drift/revocation classifier, not a machinery grant.

## Overflow test

Promote a stronger surface only if repeated future revisions show that `current-retired-governance-portability, stale-retired-governance-template, carrier-conflicted-retired-governance, revoked-retired-governance-lesson, expired-retired-governance-exit-proof, successor-required-retired-governance-drift, or mixed-retired-governance-drift` cannot keep portable retired-governance lessons useful without either losing necessary currentness/revocation evidence or reviving live governance. Until then, retired-governance drift-arbitration courts, exit-proof priority ladders, carrier-freshness tribunals, and equivalent standing machinery stay quarantined under `QWS-0276`.

## Transformer-facing implication

For a transformer reopening DelayBasin, the important move is to separate portable retired governance history from current authority. The model should ask: is the lesson still current, only a stale template, carrier-conflicted, revoked, expired, successor-required, or mixed? It should emit one exact token from `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_retirement_portability_drift_state` and refuse to infer a live portability-drift court from a useful retired-governance lesson.
