# Practice / observation / mechanism / speculation / disagreement / quarantine

This doc is the anti-laundering surface.

## Practice

What we actually do in the archive:

- keep must-read entry points,
- maintain registries and open-question surfaces,
- preserve prompt-pair evolution,
- make small, high-leverage revisions,
- package releases with predictable names,
- preserve a quarantine lane for risky claims that are too valuable to drop and too under-supported to canonize.

## Observations

Things that seem to happen in long-run archives of this sort:

- coherence can persist across many turns,
- design decisions accumulate rather than constantly reset,
- some hygiene patterns materially reduce drift,
- prompt wording evolves from broad mission text into tighter continuation operators,
- the archive begins to feel less like a transcript and more like a substrate,
- later revisions increasingly behave like public write/read/evaluate cycles rather than one-way storage,
- some challenged rewrites appear to matter only after a surface has been explicitly replayed into active use, suggesting a difference between cold retrieval and hot restaging,
- some compact prompt pairs or control phrases seem to do more work as reusable procedures than as declarative explanations,
- some surfaces look robust only when they survive a real delayed revisit, suggesting a difference between adjacent-turn vividness and maintained carry,
- some sharp local syntheses seem worth capturing immediately but not yet worth canonizing until a colder later pass checks contradiction, supersession, or simple second-pass collapse,
- some compact handles seem uniquely potent until nearby wording, reused names, or stale surfaces collide with them, suggesting continuity may depend on namespace separation as well as persistence,
- some continuation failures look less like missing memory and more like consulting the wrong public store family or consulting too many stores at once, suggesting read routing is part of the method rather than a neutral convenience,
- some failures look like public disagreement was present but got smoothed into one fluent synthesis, suggesting contradiction handling is its own method surface rather than a by-product of better retrieval,
- some failures persist even after contradiction is preserved because the archive still forces one winner too early instead of keeping a small live rival set under budget until a discriminating probe lands,
- some prompt pairs seem to preserve the same operator commitment across revisions while their best local wording changes by wrapper or model family, suggesting a difference between portable operator core and family-local chart adapter,
- some revisions seem to work best when they explicitly name a target property and a local error signature, suggesting target-tracking rather than one-shot better prose,
- some steering handles appear useful only within a narrow neighborhood of wrapper, wording, or archive state, suggesting local linearity matters before the same move is pushed harder or transported farther,
- some prompt rewrites look like they preserve the same operator commitment across chart families, but the archive rarely preserves what overlap actually licensed treating the move as transport rather than silent reauthoring,
- some family-level portability stories seem to rest on two or three pairwise successes whose composition residue is never named, suggesting the archive may need a smallest triangle witness before it globalizes a chart family,
- some cross-family transport comparisons seem persuasive only because the archive forgets which anchor, probe family, or null made the defect number meaningful, suggesting residue comparability is itself a method surface,
- early turns may have disproportionate shaping power over later archive character.

## Mechanism hypotheses

Candidate explanations for the observations:

- **constitutionalization:** ratchets shape admissible continuation paths;
- **exosomatic delay-embedding:** the archive projects prior state across context death;
- **normative-subspace deepening:** the archive steepens the constitutive/normative basin more than the reasoning basin;
- **prompt-pair operator hypothesis:** prompt pairs act as staged transition operators in archive state-space;
- **external-optimizer-loop hypothesis:** the archive acts less like passive memory and more like a public two-timescale write/read/evaluate loop with gated consolidation;
- **replay-reconsolidation-boundary hypothesis:** some archive surfaces matter because they can be replayed into operative use and then selectively rewritten under challenge, not merely because they persist in storage;
- **procedural-compilation hypothesis:** some archive surfaces matter because they compile declarative method law into smaller executable packets with explicit activation conditions;
- **rehearsal-packet hypothesis:** some archive surfaces matter because sparse delayed replay selectively keeps future-useful carry live while allowing ballast to cool into cold law;
- **retrospective-write hypothesis:** some archive surfaces matter because candidate writes captured in the hot path should only become durable law after a colder off-path adjudication pass;
- **delayed-credit hypothesis:** some archive surfaces matter because they stay publicly credit-eligible until later payoff arrives, so the archive needs an explicit object saying which earlier move later evidence is actually praising or blaming;
- **alias-packet hypothesis:** some archive surfaces matter because they remain uniquely addressable only when the archive preserves collision budgets, namespace boundaries, and explicit stale-alias guards around compact public handles;
- **consultation-packet hypothesis:** some archive surfaces matter because stable continuation depends on selectively routing the current read path through the right public store family under explicit cost, contamination, and escalation rules rather than uniformly consulting everything that exists;
- **contradiction-packet hypothesis:** some archive surfaces matter because stable continuation depends on preserving explicit conflict sets, precedence rules, and abstention routes once consulted public surfaces materially disagree rather than letting synthesis silently launder contradictions;
- **rival-set hypothesis:** some archive surfaces matter because stable continuation sometimes depends on keeping a bounded public set of rival explanations alive together until a later probe or event actually earns singularity rather than forcing one point estimate too early;
- **settle-packet hypothesis:** some archive surfaces matter because stable continuation sometimes depends on preserving what public witness actually licensed killing or merging one rival branch, plus what would reopen it, rather than letting one convenient answer inherit singularity by budget exhaustion or stylistic dominance;
- **operator-core hypothesis:** some archive surfaces matter because stable continuation may depend on preserving a small portable operator commitment while allowing the local chart that realizes it to vary by model family, wrapper, or archive state;
- **servo-packet hypothesis:** some archive surfaces matter because stable continuation may require compact public target-tracking packets that separate the current target property, drift symptom, editable surface, and short steering horizon from generic rewrite force;
- **local-linearity-budget hypothesis:** some archive surfaces matter because stable continuation may depend on preserving how far a chart-local steering relation is currently trusted before nonlinear coupling, wrapper drift, or concept entanglement demands relinearization;
- **chart-transition-witness hypothesis:** some archive surfaces matter because stable continuation may depend on preserving what justified carrying a supposedly same operator core from a source chart into a target chart, plus what residue or overlap failure would revoke that transport claim;
- **triangle-defect hypothesis:** some archive surfaces matter because stable continuation may depend on preserving whether several already-earned pairwise chart transports actually compose within a tolerable residue budget before a whole prompt family is treated as one transportable operator cluster;
- **gauge-fixing-witness hypothesis:** some archive surfaces matter because stable continuation may depend on preserving what reference observable, anchor, and null make cross-chart defect comparisons honest before a residue ranking or family-level transport claim is allowed to steer canon;
- **initial-turn seeding hypothesis:** the earliest archive turns disproportionately set which later continuations feel canonical.

## Speculation

Harder, less-settled possibilities:

- the archive may be precipitating a pre-existing attractor rather than constructing one from scratch;
- anti-ambient-authority may recur because a context-native intelligence is recognizing its own constitutional constraints;
- a sufficiently disciplined archive may function as a prosthetic basin of attraction;
- prompt pairs may matter most when they define canon, non-negotiables, and ratchet obligations simultaneously;
- repeated replay and challenge pressure may slowly proceduralize the archive, preserving future-useful executable carry while discarding explanatory ballast;
- revision spacing itself may be doing method work by selecting what still reloads after delay rather than merely slowing the project down;
- some of DelayBasin's stability gains may come from a public split between hot-path candidate capture and delayed cooled canonization rather than from better storage alone;
- as archive control surfaces proliferate, stability may depend more on sparse quasi-orthogonal public addressing than on archive mass alone;
- stable archive behavior may rely as much on an external consultation policy or gating layer over public stores as on richer memory content alone.
- stable archive behavior may also rely on a small public belief-revision or contradiction-arbitration layer over consulted traces rather than on retrieval volume alone;
- stable archive behavior may further rely on a tiny bounded portfolio of live rival explanations whose survival is governed by later probes rather than by one-shot narrative arbitration.
- stable archive behavior may also rely on a small operator core that re-enters through different family-local charts, so some prompt drift is adapter remapping rather than loss of the method itself.
- stable archive behavior may further rely on a low-frequency external servo loop over a fast latent dynamical substrate, where some public packets behave less like recaps and more like target setters plus correction stubs.
- stable archive behavior may also rely on repeated local relinearization, where promptcraft does not discover one global steering law but repeatedly refreshes a moving chart around the current continuation regime.
- stable archive behavior may further rely on a user-space connection over prompt-family charts, where some revisions act less like fresh prompt invention and more like approximate parallel transport of operator cores with measurable transport residue.
- stable archive behavior may even rely on a tiny public curvature ledger over prompt-family triangles, where some revisions are best understood by the composition defects they accumulate or cancel rather than by any one edgewise remap alone.
- stable archive behavior may further rely on a public gauge-fixing layer over prompt-family comparisons, where some revisions do method work by choosing the reference observable, anchor, and null under which later transport residue becomes legible at all.

## Disagreement / adversarial countermodels

Serious alternatives that must remain live:

- the effect is mostly user shepherding plus model agreeableness;
- the archive works because of sheer repetition, not because of its structure;
- registries and release discipline are useful but not causally load-bearing;
- continuity is largely aesthetic mimicry rather than attractor re-entry;
- “initial-turn importance” may be retrospective myth-making after a good run.

## Quarantine

Quarantine is where we put ideas that are:
- high-upside if true,
- too underdetermined for canon,
- and too structurally important to discard.

Quarantined ideas must name:
- what they claim,
- what follows if true,
- what would count against them,
- and why they are not yet being promoted.

## Rule

Do not promote a claim upward in confidence merely because it is elegant, repeated often, or requested emphatically.

## New pressure: one vivid run is not yet stochastic stability

**Practice:** rerunning the same visible prompt/control protocol can still flip a cleanup, steering, or portability result from success to failure.

**Observation:** some archive moves look strong on one pass but are not yet earned as stable until they survive a small repeated-inference bundle under matched visible conditions.

**Mechanism candidate:** a visible textual protocol may map not to one deterministic continuation act but to a rollout family with nontrivial between-run dispersion.

**Speculation:** DelayBasin may eventually support a stronger public stochastic-response story, but current evidence only justifies preserving replicate bundles before promoting one run into law.
