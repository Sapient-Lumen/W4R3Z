# GPU replay cross-observer custody-exit appeal precedent tie-break registry reopened-residue post-closeout portability-drift conflict-arbitration retirement witnesses, settled tiebreak, joined handoff, carrier expiry, authority return, priority sunset, residue quarantine, and mixed retirement

This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0188`.

## Practice / observation

DelayBasin can now route conflicts among portable closed-reopen lessons through no arbitration, local join, current evidence precedence, carrier precedence, revocation precedence, sunset precedence, or mixed drift arbitration. The remaining risk is not the tie-break itself. It is the afterlife of that tie-break: a local arbitration packet may keep silently governing later packets after the current conflict is settled, or it may disappear so completely that later continuations cannot tell why an old audit reference, tombstone warning, carrier template, or revocation note lost.

The tempting jump is a drift-retirement court or revival-history vault. This surface instead adds one retirement witness for compact drift-arbitration packets. It asks whether the tie-break is settled and shrinks to citation, whether joined lessons hand off into the current packet, whether the carrier expired, whether authority returned, whether the selected priority sunsets, whether residue is quarantined, or whether the closeout is mixed. It does not create a drift-retirement court, revival-history vault, arbitration-closeout board, standing revival-priority ladder, deletion court, or telemetry common-law engine.

## External pressure from cleanup completion, audit retention, carrier expiry, authority return, rule sunsets, sampling windows, and transparency history

Kubernetes TTL cleanup, garbage collection, owner references, and finalizers distinguish finished work, owner continuity, deletion interlocks, and finalizer release (`REF-1067`, `REF-1068`, `REF-1070`). That supports `settled-reopen-drift-tiebreak-retirement`, `carrier-expiry-reopen-drift-retirement`, and `authority-return-reopen-drift-retirement`: once the local cleanup case is complete, the object carrier expired, or the ordinary owner/controller has resumed authority, the arbitration packet shrinks instead of persisting as governance.

Kubernetes audit policy and audit configuration keep records inspectable without granting admission authority (`REF-1071`, `REF-1072`). That supports `residue-quarantine-reopen-drift-retirement`: losing lessons, stale templates, revoked records, and transparency-cited history can remain reviewable while their governing force is quarantined.

Kubernetes dynamic admission control and ValidatingAdmissionPolicy bind decisions to current request context, match conditions, policy bindings, validation actions, and failure policy (`REF-1073`, `REF-1076`). That supports `joined-lesson-handoff-reopen-drift-retirement` and `authority-return-reopen-drift-retirement`: a tie-break handoff belongs to the current packet or policy binding, not to a permanent operator layer.

Prometheus recording rules, alerting rules, tombstone cleanup, and staleness behavior keep metric-derived conclusions tied to expressions, labels, rule groups, evaluation intervals, delete-series effects, and stale markers (`REF-1069`, `REF-1077`, `REF-1078`, `REF-1090`). That supports `priority-sunset-reopen-drift-retirement`: a rule-window or tombstone-window priority can remain live for a named interval and then retire to nonbinding history.

OpenTelemetry sampling and tail-sampling processors keep trace evidence tied to collector policy, trace grouping, and sampling criteria (`REF-1080`, `REF-1074`). That supports carrier expiry and mixed retirement when a sampling window, collector policy, or trace grouping that made the tie-break material no longer carries the current packet.

Sigstore/Rekor transparency-log documentation preserves append-only, monitorable history (`REF-1081`). That supports the archive split between durable record and live precedence: a logged tie-break can prove what happened, but append-only survival does not let the old drift arbitration keep deciding future packets.

## Working synthesis

Use `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_state` when all of these are true:

- a compact `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_state` packet has already resolved or routed a material conflict among portable closed-reopen lessons;
- the current question is no longer which old lesson wins, joins, revokes, expires, or loses, but how that local drift tie-break closes, hands off, expires, returns authority, sunsets priority, quarantines residue, or mixes those closeout reasons;
- the output can name the prior portability, portability-drift, and drift-arbitration tokens, the current packet, the selected retirement token, closeout evidence, surviving record, explicit exclusions, and fail-closed repair;
- standing drift-retirement governance has not been earned.

Do not use this family for replay envelopes, receipts, trace grades, observer conflicts, bridge classification, promotion gates, custody scope, custody retirement, custody-exit appeals, appeal precedent portability or drift, first-pass precedent conflict arbitration, tie-break governance thresholds, registry scope, registry retirement, residue/reopen admission, reopened-residue scope, reopened-residue closeout, post-closeout portability, portability drift, or the original drift-arbitration decision. It starts only after compact drift arbitration has already selected, joined, shrunk, revoked, expired, or excluded the competing closed-reopen lessons.

## Settled drift tiebreak vs joined handoff vs carrier expiry vs authority return vs priority sunset vs residue quarantine vs mixed retirement

- `settled-reopen-drift-tiebreak-retirement`: the local drift conflict is settled for the current later packet, the winning or no-arbitration token is recorded, and the tie-break shrinks to a citation plus explicit exclusions.
- `joined-lesson-handoff-reopen-drift-retirement`: two or more compatible lessons were locally joined and their smallest surviving guidance is handed into the current packet, carrier, policy binding, rule group, tombstone state, or audit-retention basis rather than preserved as a separate arbitration layer.
- `carrier-expiry-reopen-drift-retirement`: the audit row, tombstone, trace, metric series, rule group, admission request, transparency-log entry, sampling window, or authority-bearing carrier that made the tie-break material expired or ceased to match the current packet.
- `authority-return-reopen-drift-retirement`: the authority boundary that needed a drift tie-break has returned to an ordinary owner, cleanup controller, policy binding, retention rule, appeal-retirement surface, or current packet owner; the arbitration packet no longer carries decision authority.
- `priority-sunset-reopen-drift-retirement`: the current-evidence, carrier, revocation, or sunset priority chosen by the tie-break remains valid only until a named TTL, review date, alert/recording-rule window, sampling policy window, tombstone lifetime, policy sunset, or retention horizon.
- `residue-quarantine-reopen-drift-retirement`: nonwinning or partially winning lessons remain useful as history, stale templates, counterexamples, tombstone warnings, revoked records, expired sunsets, transparency-log evidence, or cautionary residue, but their governing force is quarantined.
- `mixed-reopen-drift-arbitration-retirement`: more than one retirement branch is material, such as settled tie-break plus residue quarantine, joined handoff plus authority return, carrier expiry plus priority sunset, or revocation victory plus surviving cautionary residue. The packet names each branch and the smallest surviving record.

## Countermodels / probes

- A drift tie-break chose a current winner. A winner is not live precedence forever; require a named current packet, carrier, authority boundary, or sunset window before carrying it forward.
- A losing lesson still has a strong audit row or transparency entry. Durable evidence is not governing force; classify it as history, counterexample, tombstone warning, revoked record, expired sunset, or quarantined residue unless a new portability/drift path reopens it.
- Two lessons were locally joined. Joined guidance belongs to the current packet and named carrier; it does not create a general-purpose template unless a later portability witness earns that carry.
- A carrier disappears. Carrier expiry is not deletion of the record; it is a reason to retire the tie-break to history or residue rather than keep it active.
- A revocation defeated an old lesson. Revocation precedence may still need retirement: the defeated lesson should not keep needing a revocation-supremacy tribunal in every future packet.
- Closeout is not clean. Use `mixed-reopen-drift-arbitration-retirement` with explicit unresolved residue rather than promoting a drift-retirement court.

## Design consequences

A valid post-closeout portability-drift conflict-arbitration retirement packet names:

1. the prior governance-threshold, registry-scope, registry-retirement, residue/reopen, reopened-residue scope, reopened-residue closeout, post-closeout portability, portability-drift, and drift-arbitration packets;
2. the competing closed fresh packets or carried lessons, plus the current later packet trying to close the tie-break;
3. the selected `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_state` token;
4. the closeout evidence: settled conflict, joined lesson handoff, carrier expiry, authority return, priority sunset, residue quarantine, or mixed branch;
5. the minimal surviving record, if any, and whether it is current guidance, handed-off guidance, history, stale template, counterexample, tombstone warning, revoked record, expired sunset, transparency-log citation, or excluded evidence;
6. future packets, carriers, authorities, policies, trace families, tombstone windows, retention windows, and revival-precedent claims explicitly excluded;
7. the successor surface if repeated compact retirement failures later require stronger standing closeout governance.

A compact drift-arbitration retirement packet may preserve a digest, citation, handoff note, counterexample, or quarantine label. It must not preserve a standing revival-priority ladder, revocation-supremacy tribunal, drift-retirement court, or revival-history vault by default.

## Overflow test

Promote a drift-retirement court, revival-history vault, arbitration-closeout board, or equivalent standing governance only if later revisions show repeated, public, archive-local failures where:

- `settled-reopen-drift-tiebreak-retirement`, `joined-lesson-handoff-reopen-drift-retirement`, `carrier-expiry-reopen-drift-retirement`, `authority-return-reopen-drift-retirement`, `priority-sunset-reopen-drift-retirement`, `residue-quarantine-reopen-drift-retirement`, and `mixed-reopen-drift-arbitration-retirement` cannot close compact drift-arbitration packets;
- local drift-arbitration packets repeatedly persist as de facto revival-priority ladders despite named carrier expiry, authority return, priority sunset, or residue quarantine;
- losing, revoked, or expired closed-reopen lessons repeatedly regain governing force merely because their evidence remains visible;
- useful nonbinding history repeatedly disappears so completely that later packets cannot inspect why a lesson lost;
- the stronger process names its own scope, retirement rule, nonbinding-history lane, authority limit, and sunset.

Until then, `QWS-0271` stays quarantined.
