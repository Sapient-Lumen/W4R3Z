## Token semantics

### `unexpired-transported-retired-history-closeout`

Use when the transported closeout-history record remains inside its named horizon and its allowed nonbinding use is still available. The receipt must still name the prior travel token, carrier, allowed use, and next expiry trigger; unexpired does not mean live authority.

### `horizon-expired-transported-retired-history-closeout`

Use when the transported closeout-history record has passed its named time, revision, packet, or event horizon. After this token, the record may remain inspectable history, but ordinary reentry must not reuse it as current warning, template, or successor context without a fresh packet.

### `use-exhausted-transported-retired-history-closeout`

Use when the transported record has served its named one-shot or bounded use. The same record may not be recycled as a new warning, new template, or new carrier justification unless a later witness reopens it under a fresh question.

### `successor-superseded-transported-retired-history-closeout`

Use when a named successor packet has replaced the transported record. The old record may cite the successor path, but it cannot compete with or override the successor's fresh evidence.

### `source-revoked-transported-retired-history-closeout`

Use when the source closeout, source travel record, or source authority-return evidence is revoked or corrected. The transported record then expires as usable history and must fail closed to quarantine, audit-only tombstone, or successor revalidation.

### `quarantine-expired-transported-retired-history-closeout`

Use when the only surviving form of the transported record is a quarantine reference whose allowed inspection window or safe-use purpose has ended. Later packets may inspect the quarantine trail, but the expired record cannot become a positive carrier.

### `mixed-transported-retired-history-closeout-expiry`

Use only when multiple expiry states are genuinely present and cannot be collapsed without hiding evidence. The receipt must name branch-local prior travel tokens, carriers, expiry evidence, allowed residual uses, stripped authority, and fail-closed repair.

## Expiry rule

A transported retired-history currentness-closeout record expires by named horizon, exhausted use, successor supersession, source revocation, quarantine expiry, or honest mixed branch. Expiry preserves inspectable history while removing ordinary current-use permission. It must not create a carrier-review layer whose job is to continually renew transported closeout history.
