# GPU replay reopened-residue drift governance-retirement portability-drift conflict-arbitration retirement governance-retirement witnesses, discharge, scope sunset, authority return, history freeze, residue quarantine, handoff, and mixed closeout

This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0198`. It answers the narrow question of what retires a scoped post-arbitration governance surface after its packet, conflict-history custody, authority lane, successor route, and retirement window have been made explicit. It keeps closeout local so promoted governance can stop doing live work without erasing the record needed for audit.

The surface is intentionally smaller than a post-arbitration governance retirement board. It does not create a permanent custody ledger, authority-return court, successor-handoff exchange, residue common law, or reusable governance operator. It only decides how the locally promoted governance surface discharges, sunsets, returns authority, freezes history, quarantines residue, or hands off a successor.

## Practice / observation

`rev0303` established that promoted post-arbitration governance must be packet-local, history-custody-bound, authority-lane-limited, successor-route-bound, retirement-window-bounded, or mixed. That solved the scope question. It did not decide how a scoped surface stops being live once the local threshold overflow has been discharged.

Use this surface only when all five facts are public:

1. prior governance-retirement, portability, portability-drift, conflict-arbitration, arbitration-retirement, governance-threshold, and governance-scope tokens are named;
2. the scoped surface names the affected packet, conflict-history row, authority lane, owner, carrier, successor route, or retirement window;
3. the current receipt names discharge, scope sunset, authority return, conflict-history freeze, residue quarantine, successor handoff, or mixed closeout evidence;
4. the surviving record is explicitly nonbinding outside the retired scope; and
5. the later receipt names the selected retirement token, excluded standing machinery, successor surface if any, and fail-closed repair.

## External pressure from cleanup completion, finalizer removal, owner return, audit freeze, stale-signal tombstones, trace horizon expiry, and transparency checkpoints

Kubernetes-style TTL cleanup, garbage collection, finalizers, owner references, admission binding, and audit trails show that scoped control can need an explicit closeout event, authority return, or retained audit row before it stops acting. Prometheus delete/tombstone behavior, recording rules, alert windows, and staleness markers show that old signals can be frozen or expired without becoming live policy. OpenTelemetry trace carriers and sampling horizons show that context may hand off only through named carriers and time-bounded traces. Transparency checkpoints show how inclusion history can remain inspectable while no longer governing future action.

Those analogies support a compact retirement witness: discharged, scope-sunset, authority-return, conflict-history-freeze, residue-quarantine, successor-handoff, or mixed closeout. They do not import cleanup controllers, admission boards, observability stores, sampling processors, or transparency logs as DelayBasin governance machinery.

## Countermodels / probes

- A scoped governance surface feels safer if left available. Availability is not closeout; choose a retirement token or keep the question open.
- Conflict history is important. Importance is not authority; freeze it as nonbinding history unless a successor packet earns live scope.
- An owner or carrier still wants supervision. Supervision is not retirement; return authority or hand off a named successor rather than preserving ambient governance.
- A successor route exists. A route is not a board; hand off the named route and retire the current surface.
- Residue is uncomfortable. Discomfort is not live jurisdiction; quarantine residue with a trigger instead of letting governance linger.
- The excluded synonyms `permanent-post-arbitration-governance-by-scope`, `immortal-conflict-history-ledger`, `authority-return-court-after-closeout`, `scoped-governance-remains-live`, and `post-arbitration-governance-retirement-ish` remain outside the allowed vocabulary.
