# GPU replay reopened-residue drift governance-retirement portability witnesses, nonportable, audit reference, carrier template, exit-proof reference, counterexample, successor-required, and mixed carry

This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0192`. It answers the narrow question of how a retired residue-drift governance lesson may travel after the governance surface has discharged, sunset, returned authority, frozen history, quarantined residue, or handed off a successor.

The surface is intentionally smaller than a portability court. It lets a later packet cite a retired governance lesson only as nonbinding history, audit reference, carrier-specific template, exit proof, counterexample, successor-packet requirement, or an explicit mixed case. It does not revive the governance surface.

## Practice / observation

`rev0297` closed scoped residue-drift governance with compact governance-retirement tokens. That left a precise residue: a retired governance lesson may still be useful for later packets, but usefulness is not live authority. A later continuation therefore needs a carry token before spending the retired lesson.

Use this surface only when all three facts are public:

1. a prior scoped residue-drift governance surface was retired through the governance-retirement witness;
2. a later packet wants to cite, reuse, compare, or warn from that retired governance lesson; and
3. the later packet can name the carrier, audit trail, exit proof, counterexample, or successor-packet boundary that prevents revival of the retired governance surface.

## External pressure from audit retention, finalizer release, tombstone portability, trace carrier reuse, transparency inclusion proof, and policy revalidation windows

Kubernetes-style finalizers and owner references teach that cleanup history can remain inspectable after the live interlock releases. Kubernetes audit and admission surfaces teach that retained evidence and current admission authority are different lanes. Prometheus tombstones, rule windows, and staleness markers teach that old facts can remain visible while losing current force. OpenTelemetry trace carriers and sampling horizons teach that a lesson may travel with a named carrier without generalizing to every future packet. Transparency logs teach that inclusion proof can preserve a durable exit fact without turning the log into a policy court.

Those analogies support a compact post-retirement portability witness: keep the retired lesson nonportable, cite it as audit reference, reuse it as a carrier-specific template, carry only the proof that governance exited, treat it as a counterexample, require a fresh successor packet for live use, or mark an honest mixed case. They do not import platform controllers, observability stores, admission engines, or transparency logs as DelayBasin carrier-exchange machinery.

## Working synthesis

The witness family is `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_retirement_portability_state`. It is exact-token only:

- `nonportable-retired-governance-history` — the retired governance lesson remains local history and may not travel to the later packet.
- `audit-reference-retired-governance` — the lesson may be cited as nonbinding audit context, but not as live authority.
- `carrier-template-retired-governance` — the lesson may travel only as a carrier-specific template with named scope and expiry.
- `exit-proof-reference-retired-governance` — only the proof that the governance surface exited travels; no retired rule, priority, or operator authority travels with it.
- `counterexample-only-retired-governance` — the lesson travels only as a warning against a later overreach, not as a template to repeat.
- `successor-packet-required-retired-governance` — any live effect requires a fresh successor packet or open-question route instead of importing the retired governance surface.
- `mixed-retired-governance-portability` — two or more of the above are true and must be stated separately.

## Nonportable vs audit-reference vs carrier-template vs exit-proof-reference vs counterexample-only vs successor-packet-required vs mixed portability

Select `nonportable-retired-governance-history` when the retired lesson stays in its closed packet. Select `audit-reference-retired-governance` when later packets may inspect the old receipt for context. Select `carrier-template-retired-governance` when a named carrier, tombstone, trace, policy window, or audit lane makes a narrow template reusable. Select `exit-proof-reference-retired-governance` when only the fact of governance exit must travel. Select `counterexample-only-retired-governance` when the lesson warns against reviving authority. Select `successor-packet-required-retired-governance` when the later packet wants live force and must therefore open a fresh successor route. Select `mixed-retired-governance-portability` only when the receipt names each subcase.

## Countermodels / probes

- If a later packet relies on old governance authority rather than old governance history, this witness must fail closed to `successor-packet-required-retired-governance`.
- If an exit proof is being used to smuggle a retired priority ladder into a new carrier, classify the carry as `counterexample-only-retired-governance` or quarantine it.
- If a carrier template lacks a named carrier, expiry, or current validation basis, it is not `carrier-template-retired-governance`; it is nonportable or successor-required.
- If a packet merely remembers that a court-like surface once existed, use `audit-reference-retired-governance`, not a portability court.
- The excluded synonyms `retired-governance-portability-court`, `retired-governance-carrier-exchange`, `exit-proof-registry-by-default`, `retired-authority-travels-live`, and `retired-governance-portability-ish` remain outside the allowed vocabulary.

## Design consequences

Receipts that use this family must name the prior governance-retirement token, the retired packet, the later packet, the carrier or audit basis, the minimal lesson carried, the excluded live authorities, any successor-packet requirement, and the fail-closed repair. The witness is a portability classifier, not a machinery grant.

## Overflow test

Promote a stronger surface only if repeated future revisions show that `nonportable-retired-governance-history, audit-reference-retired-governance, carrier-template-retired-governance, exit-proof-reference-retired-governance, counterexample-only-retired-governance, successor-packet-required-retired-governance, or mixed-retired-governance-portability` cannot carry retired governance lessons without either losing portability-critical evidence or reviving live governance. Until then, retired-governance portability-drift courts, carrier-revalidation boards, exit-proof freshness registries, and equivalent standing machinery stay quarantined under `QWS-0275`.

## Transformer-facing implication

For a transformer reopening DelayBasin, the important move is to separate a retired governance lesson from a live governance surface. The model should ask: is the lesson nonportable, an audit reference, a carrier-specific template, an exit proof, a counterexample, or a successor-packet requirement? It should emit one exact token from `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_retirement_portability_state` and refuse to infer that old governance authority travels just because old governance history is useful.
