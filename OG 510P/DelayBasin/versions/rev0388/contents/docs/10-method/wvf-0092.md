# GPU replay cross-observer custody-exit appeal precedent tie-break registry reopened-residue post-closeout portability-drift conflict-arbitration witnesses, no arbitration, local join, current evidence precedence, carrier precedence, revocation precedence, sunset precedence, and mixed arbitration

This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0187`.

## Practice / observation

DelayBasin can now classify a portable closed-reopen lesson as current, stale-template-only, carrier-conflicted, revoked, expired, or mixed drift. The remaining risk is not ordinary currentness. It is the case where two or more closed-reopen lessons, audit references, tombstone warnings, carrier templates, or revocation notes survive compact drift classification and still disagree about what a later packet may do.

The tempting jump is a residue-drift court. This surface instead adds one local conflict-arbitration witness. It asks whether no arbitration remains, whether surviving lessons can be joined locally, whether current evidence, the present carrier, explicit revocation, or an unexpired sunset should take precedence, or whether the result is a mixed arbitration packet. It does not create a drift-arbitration court, revival-priority ladder, revocation-supremacy tribunal, standing residue-drift registry, permanent revival ledger, or telemetry common-law engine.

## External pressure from admission bindings, audit-retention currentness, tombstones, rule windows, sampling policy changes, cleanup release, and transparency history

Kubernetes dynamic admission control and ValidatingAdmissionPolicy bind decisions to request context, match conditions, policy text, bindings, validation actions, failure policy, and current carrier shape (`REF-1073`, `REF-1076`). That supports `current-evidence-precedence-reopen-drift` and `carrier-precedence-reopen-drift`: the lesson backed by the currently matching request, policy binding, object, selector, or authority-bearing carrier outranks a similar but nonmatching closed-reopen lesson.

Kubernetes audit policy and audit configuration preserve reviewable records without granting live admission authority (`REF-1071`, `REF-1072`). That supports `no-arbitration-needed-reopen-drift-conflict` when only history remains, and `current-evidence-precedence-reopen-drift` when a current audit-retention basis directly rebuts an older stale template.

Kubernetes TTL cleanup, garbage collection, and finalizers distinguish finished work, owner references, deletion interlocks, finalizer release, and authority withdrawal (`REF-1067`, `REF-1068`, `REF-1070`). That supports `revocation-precedence-reopen-drift` when a later cleanup release or authority-return surface withdraws the old lesson's force, and `sunset-precedence-reopen-drift` when a bounded retention or review window has expired.

Prometheus recording rules, alerting rules, tombstones, and staleness behavior keep metric-derived conclusions tied to expressions, labels, rule groups, evaluation intervals, delete-series effects, and stale markers (`REF-1069`, `REF-1077`, `REF-1078`, `REF-1090`). That supports local joins only when rule windows are compatible, and sunset precedence when an older copied rule or tombstone warning is outside its current evaluation window.

OpenTelemetry sampling and tail-sampling processors keep trace evidence tied to current collector policy, trace grouping, and sampling criteria (`REF-1080`, `REF-1074`). That supports revocation precedence or mixed arbitration when a later sampling policy narrows or withdraws the basis that made an old closed-reopen lesson portable.

Sigstore/Rekor transparency-log documentation preserves append-only, monitorable history (`REF-1081`). That supports the archive split between durable record and live precedence: logged history can help prove current evidence, conflict, revocation, or expiry, but append-only survival does not make the oldest portable lesson automatically win.

## Working synthesis

Use `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_state` when all of these are true:

- a compact post-closeout portability-drift packet already classified the candidate closed-reopen lessons as current, stale-template-only, carrier-conflicted, revoked, expired, or mixed drift;
- at least two surviving portable lessons, audit references, tombstone warnings, carrier templates, revocation notes, or sunset claims materially disagree about the same later packet;

- standing drift governance has not been earned.

Do not use this family for replay envelopes, receipts, trace grades, observer conflicts, bridge classification, promotion gates, custody scope, custody retirement, custody-exit appeals, appeal precedent portability or drift, first-pass precedent conflict arbitration, tie-break governance thresholds, registry scope, registry retirement, residue/reopen admission, reopened-residue scope, reopened-residue closeout, first-pass post-closeout portability, or ordinary single-lesson portability drift. It starts only after compact drift classification leaves a material conflict among portable closed-reopen lessons.

## No arbitration vs local join vs current evidence precedence vs carrier precedence vs revocation precedence vs sunset precedence vs mixed arbitration

- `no-arbitration-needed-reopen-drift-conflict`: after currentness, staleness, carrier conflict, revocation, and expiry checks, only one lesson still has current force, or all apparent conflict collapses to history, stale template, tombstone warning, revoked record, expired sunset, or nonmatching carrier.
- `local-join-reopen-drift-conflict`: multiple surviving lessons can be safely composed inside the same later packet, carrier, audit-retention basis, tombstone status, rule window, sampling criterion, or authority boundary without one overriding the other.
- `current-evidence-precedence-reopen-drift`: a lesson backed by current audit retention, live policy text, current tombstone status, current rule evaluation, current sampling criterion, or current transparency evidence outranks an older stale template or inherited lesson.
- `carrier-precedence-reopen-drift`: a lesson tied to the present carrier, selector, object, audit row, tombstone, trace, rule group, policy binding, or authority-bearing carrier outranks a lesson tied to a nonmatching or weaker carrier.
- `revocation-precedence-reopen-drift`: a later authority return, tombstone release, audit correction, policy replacement, cleanup decision, sampling change, or explicit revocation defeats reuse of an older portable lesson while preserving it as history.
- `sunset-precedence-reopen-drift`: a lesson whose TTL, retention horizon, policy window, sampling window, tombstone lifetime, review window, or inherited sunset is still live outranks one whose bounded window has expired.
- `mixed-reopen-drift-arbitration`: more than one arbitration branch is material, such as current-evidence precedence plus partial carrier conflict, revocation precedence plus surviving stale template, or local join plus expired-sunset exclusion. The packet names each branch and the smallest surviving guidance.

## Countermodels / probes

- Two portable closed-reopen lessons disagree. Disagreement is not automatically a court; first check whether one side is stale, revoked, expired, or carrier-conflicted.
- One lesson is older or more familiar. Age and familiarity do not confer priority; current evidence, current carrier, explicit revocation, and live sunset windows must be named.
- A tombstone warning and an audit reference point different ways. A tombstone is not deletion-court authority and an audit row is not live governance; classify the specific current basis.
- Two lessons look joinable. Use local join only if their carriers, rule windows, audit-retention bases, sampling criteria, and authority boundaries can be named without hidden override.
- A revocation exists but history is still useful. Revocation defeats governing force, not reviewability; keep the old lesson as history, stale template, counterexample, or excluded evidence.
- No clean precedence branch fits. Use mixed arbitration with explicit exclusions rather than promoting a drift-arbitration court.

## Design consequences

A valid post-closeout portability-drift conflict-arbitration packet names:

1. the prior governance-threshold, registry-scope, registry-retirement, residue/reopen, reopened-residue scope, reopened-residue closeout, post-closeout portability, and portability-drift packets;
2. the competing closed fresh packets or carried lessons, plus the later packet trying to spend them;
3. the selected `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_state` token;
4. the current evidence, carrier, selector, policy binding, audit-retention basis, tombstone status, rule window, sampling criterion, cleanup release, transparency-log entry, revocation surface, or sunset window that proves the tie-break;
5. which lesson governs, which lessons are joined, and which shrink to history, stale template, counterexample, tombstone warning, revoked record, expired sunset, or excluded evidence;
6. excluded future packets, telemetry streams, deletion-court claims, revival-precedent claims, operator authorities, and policy windows;
7. the successor surface if repeated compact drift-arbitration packets later need closeout or retirement governance.

A compact drift-arbitration packet may select, join, shrink, revoke, expire, or exclude portable closed-reopen lessons. It must not become a reusable revival-priority ladder.

## Overflow test

Promote a drift-arbitration court, revival-priority ladder, revocation-supremacy tribunal, or equivalent standing residue-drift governance only if later revisions show repeated, public, archive-local failures where:

- `no-arbitration-needed-reopen-drift-conflict`, `local-join-reopen-drift-conflict`, `current-evidence-precedence-reopen-drift`, `carrier-precedence-reopen-drift`, `revocation-precedence-reopen-drift`, `sunset-precedence-reopen-drift`, and `mixed-reopen-drift-arbitration` cannot route material conflicts among portable closed-reopen lessons;
- compact arbitration repeatedly over-retires useful lessons or re-admits revoked and expired lessons as current authority;
- audit references, tombstones, carrier templates, recording rules, sampling policies, or transparency-log entries repeatedly produce cross-carrier priority conflicts that packet-local precedence cannot represent;
- the stronger process names its operator authority, conflict scope, revocation rule, sunset rule, retirement witness, nonbinding-history lane, and authority return.

Until then, `QWS-0270` stays quarantined.
