# GPU replay reopened-residue drift governance-retirement portability-drift conflict-arbitration retirement governance-threshold witnesses, no standing governance, repeated retirement failure, history loss, overbinding, authority split, successor-route loop, and mixed escalation

This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0196`. It answers the narrow question of what public threshold, if any, promotes standing post-arbitration governance after compact retired-governance drift-arbitration retirement tokens fail. It keeps the default answer at no standing governance while naming the few failure modes that could justify a bounded successor governance surface.

The surface is intentionally smaller than a post-arbitration governance court. It does not create an exit-proof conflict-history vault, successor-route closeout board, retired-arbitration common law, carrier-freshness tribunal, or live authority registry. It only asks whether the compact retirement witness has repeatedly failed in a way that a later scoped surface must explicitly bound.

## Practice / observation

`rev0301` let DelayBasin close local retired-governance drift-arbitration packets through settled retirement, joined handoff, carrier expiry, authority return, priority sunset, residue quarantine, or mixed retirement. That solved the afterlife problem for the arbitration packet itself. It did not decide when repeated failures of those compact retirement tokens should stop being treated as one-off residue and instead earn a standing post-arbitration governance question.

Use this surface only when all four facts are public:

1. a prior scoped residue-drift governance surface retired, then produced portability, portability-drift, conflict-arbitration, and conflict-arbitration-retirement tokens;
2. the current receipt can name which compact retirement token failed, which packet or carrier was affected, and what repair was tried;
3. the failure repeats or structurally recurs as history loss, overbinding, authority split, successor-route loop, or mixed escalation rather than a single awkward case; and
4. the later receipt can name the selected threshold token, excluded standing machinery, successor surface if any, and fail-closed repair.

## External pressure from incident escalation, audit retention, policy binding, owner return, stale-signal expiry, and transparency checkpoints

Kubernetes-style finalizers, owner references, admission policies, and audit trails show that live interlocks sometimes require escalation when cleanup cannot complete, retention loses necessary history, or an authority boundary splits across owners and policies. Prometheus staleness, recording rules, and alert windows show that repeated stale-signal or rule-window failure can justify a new rule without turning every old signal into live authority. OpenTelemetry sampling and trace-carrier limits show that repeated carrier gaps can require policy adjustment while a single missing trace remains only evidence. Transparency-log checkpoints show that durable inclusion history can matter without becoming a court.

Those analogies support a compact threshold witness: usually no standing governance; escalate only for repeated retirement failure, conflict-history loss, conflict-history overbinding, authority split, successor-route loop, or honest mixed escalation. They do not import incident managers, admission controllers, observability stores, sampling processors, or transparency logs as DelayBasin governance machinery.

## Working synthesis

The witness family is `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_retirement_portability_drift_conflict_arbitration_retirement_governance_threshold_state`. It is exact-token only:

- `no-standing-post-arbitration-governance` — compact arbitration-retirement tokens still close the local case; preserve the record as citation, handoff, sunset, or quarantine and do not promote standing governance.
- `repeated-retirement-failure-post-arbitration-governance` — multiple later packets show the same compact retirement token cannot close the afterlife problem without recurring repair, reopened residue, or broken closeout.
- `conflict-history-loss-post-arbitration-governance` — compact retirement repeatedly erases or hides history needed to audit why a retired-governance drift conflict was settled, joined, revoked, expired, successor-routed, or quarantined.
- `conflict-history-overbinding-post-arbitration-governance` — compact retirement repeatedly lets old conflict history act as live authority, priority common law, or default exit-proof control after the local packet should have closed.
- `authority-split-post-arbitration-governance` — compact retirement repeatedly leaves two or more packets, carriers, owners, policy bindings, or successor routes claiming incompatible authority over the same retired-governance residue.
- `successor-route-loop-post-arbitration-governance` — compact retirement repeatedly routes the same residue into successor surfaces that return to arbitration, retirement, or portability-drift without closing the authority boundary.
- `mixed-post-arbitration-governance-threshold` — more than one threshold branch is material, such as repeated failure plus history loss, overbinding plus authority split, successor-loop plus carrier expiry, or no-standing for one slice and escalation for another.

## No standing governance vs repeated failure vs history loss vs overbinding vs authority split vs successor loop vs mixed threshold

Select `no-standing-post-arbitration-governance` when the rev0301 retirement vocabulary still suffices and the stronger layer would be ceremonial. Select `repeated-retirement-failure-post-arbitration-governance` only when the same retirement failure recurs across packets or later reviews. Select `conflict-history-loss-post-arbitration-governance` when compact closeout destroys necessary audit history. Select `conflict-history-overbinding-post-arbitration-governance` when compact closeout lets old history govern too much. Select `authority-split-post-arbitration-governance` when current owners or carriers cannot agree on authority after retirement. Select `successor-route-loop-post-arbitration-governance` when successor routing keeps cycling. Select `mixed-post-arbitration-governance-threshold` only when the receipt names each branch and the smallest successor question.

## Countermodels / probes

- One arbitration-retirement packet is awkward. Awkwardness is not threshold overflow; try the compact token, name the repair, and quarantine residue.
- A history row is durable. Durable history is not live governance; require repeated history loss, overbinding, authority split, or successor-loop evidence before escalating.
- A successor route exists. A successor surface is not a board by default; require evidence that successor routing loops or fails to close the authority boundary.
- A current packet wants a shortcut. Convenience is not standing governance; keep the old arbitration record nonbinding unless a threshold token is earned.
- A compact retirement token failed once. Single failure reopens the local packet or quarantine; repeated failure may trigger this threshold family.
- The excluded synonyms `post-arbitration-governance-by-default`, `exit-proof-conflict-history-vault`, `successor-route-closeout-board`, `retired-arbitration-common-law`, and `post-arbitration-governance-ish` remain outside the allowed vocabulary.

## Design consequences

Receipts that use this family must name the prior governance-retirement, portability, portability-drift, conflict-arbitration, and arbitration-retirement tokens; the failing retirement token; the affected packets, carriers, owners, exit proofs, successor routes, or authority lanes; the repeated-failure or no-standing evidence; the selected threshold token; excluded standing machinery; successor surface if any; and fail-closed repair. The witness may authorize a successor question about scope only after threshold overflow. It may not silently instantiate a court, vault, board, or priority registry.

## Overflow test

Promote a stronger surface only if repeated future revisions show that `no-standing-post-arbitration-governance, repeated-retirement-failure-post-arbitration-governance, conflict-history-loss-post-arbitration-governance, conflict-history-overbinding-post-arbitration-governance, authority-split-post-arbitration-governance, successor-route-loop-post-arbitration-governance, or mixed-post-arbitration-governance-threshold` cannot distinguish ordinary compact closeout from genuine threshold overflow. Until then, post-arbitration governance scope courts, conflict-history vaults, successor-route boards, and equivalent standing machinery stay quarantined under `QWS-0279`.

## Transformer-facing implication

For a transformer reopening DelayBasin, the important move is to separate arbitration-retirement failure from governance promotion. The model should ask: did compact retirement merely need local repair, or did it repeatedly fail through history loss, overbinding, authority split, successor-route loop, or mixed escalation?
