# GPU replay cross-observer custody-exit appeal precedent-portability witnesses, nonportable history, carrier match, template binding, counterexample only, sunset inheritance, and mixed portability

This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0175`.

## Practice / observation

DelayBasin already has a custody-exit appeal-retirement witness. It can close a promoted appeal, hand off a minimal survivor, return authority, sunset the policy window, and quarantine precedent-shaped residue. The next risk is narrower: a retired appeal may still be genuinely useful for a later case, but a useful lesson can become a standing policy court if portability is not separately bounded.

A custody-exit appeal precedent-portability witness is not a precedent board. It is a small citation-and-reuse packet that says whether the retired appeal is nonportable history, portable only when the same carrier or identity family is matched, portable only as an explicitly bound policy/template, portable only as a counterexample that blocks a repeated mistake, portable only with the original sunset inherited, or honestly mixed. The default is nonportable history unless the later case names the exact bridge between the old appeal and the new cleanup dispute.

## External pressure from validating policy bindings, ordered audit rules, recording rules, selectors, sampling policies, and transparency logs

Kubernetes ValidatingAdmissionPolicy separates policy definition, parameter kind, match constraints, validation expressions, and bindings (`REF-1076`). That supports `template-bound-precedent`: a retired appeal lesson may become a reusable rule template only when the future case names the binding, parameters, and match constraints that make the template applicable.

Kubernetes audit policy rules are ordered and the first matching rule sets the audit level; audit records also depend on policy, stages, and backends (`REF-1071`, `REF-1072`). That supports `carrier-matched-precedent` and `counterexample-only-precedent`: a prior appeal can inform a later case only through a named record, rule match, or known failure mode, not through ambient audit-log authority.

Prometheus recording rules and alerting rules make repeated metric logic reusable through explicit expressions, labels, annotations, groups, and alert/record names (`REF-1077`, `REF-1078`). That supports `template-bound-precedent` while preserving that a reusable expression is not a cleanup-policy senate.

Kubernetes labels and selectors make grouping portable only through explicit key/value requirements, and empty or unspecified selector semantics depend on the API context (`REF-1079`). That supports `carrier-matched-precedent`: portability needs a named identity or selector bridge, not a visual resemblance between two workloads.

OpenTelemetry sampling distinguishes head and tail decisions; tail sampling considers trace data against specific criteria after spans are available (`REF-1080`). That supports `counterexample-only-precedent` and `sunset-inherited-precedent`: a sampled survivor or sampling policy may preserve a lesson, but future reuse must name the criteria and inherit the bounded decision window.

Sigstore/Rekor transparency-log documentation keeps append-only, monitorable survivor records visible (`REF-1081`, `REF-1075`). That supports `nonportable-history-precedent`: durable public memory can preserve evidence and citation value without becoming automatic governance for future telemetry cleanup.

## Working synthesis

Use `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_portability_state` when all of these are true:

- a promoted custody-exit appeal already retired through `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_retirement_state` or is being explicitly modeled as retired;
- a later case wants to cite, reuse, or learn from that retired appeal rather than reopen the original dispute;
- the future case can name the carrier match, policy/template binding, counterexample family, inherited sunset, or mixed bridge that makes the old lesson relevant;
- the desired output is a portability classification and refusal rule, not a standing policy court, appeal-history vault, cleanup senate, or common-law telemetry engine.

Do not use this family for ordinary bridge classification, promotion gates, custody scope, custody retirement, appeal thresholding, appeal scope, or appeal retirement. This surface begins only after a retired appeal exists and a future case asks whether any lesson can travel.

## Nonportable history vs carrier match vs template binding vs counterexample only vs sunset inheritance vs mixed portability

- `nonportable-history-precedent`: the retired appeal remains citable as history, evidence, or a cautionary example, but it carries no direct rule, custody right, retention obligation, or cleanup authority into the future case.
- `carrier-matched-precedent`: portability depends on a named shared survivor carrier, digest, trace id, exemplar, audit event, object UID, selector, transparency-log entry, or bridge record. Without that match, the appeal stays historical.
- `template-bound-precedent`: portability is through an explicit rule, policy, query, admission expression, sampling criterion, or alert/recording template with named bindings and parameters. The template applies only where its match constraints apply.
- `counterexample-only-precedent`: the retired appeal ports only as a negative control: do not repeat the same unsupported deletion, vaulting, retention, authority-contest, or carrier-loss mistake. It does not create affirmative policy for unrelated cases.
- `sunset-inherited-precedent`: the future case may reuse the lesson only while inheriting the old TTL, retention, policy-window, sampling-window, review-window, or closeout horizon. Portability does not renew the clock.
- `mixed-precedent-portability`: more than one portability reason is material, such as a carrier match plus a template binding, or a counterexample plus inherited sunset. The packet names each branch and the smallest still-live future rule.

## Countermodels / probes

- A retired appeal was persuasive. Persuasiveness is not portability unless the later packet names the carrier, template, counterexample, or inherited sunset.
- A transparency-log entry, audit event, or recording rule is durable. Durability preserves reviewability, not a right to govern future cleanup.
- Two GPU replay disputes look similar. Similarity does not port precedent unless the identity family, selector, rule, or failure mode is explicit.
- A template can be copied. Copying a rule body without its binding, parameter, scope, and sunset is policy laundering.
- A counterexample blocked one failure. It blocks that failure mode; it does not become a general retention mandate.
- If the future case cannot name why the old appeal travels, classify it as `nonportable-history-precedent` and keep any stronger governance quarantined.

## Design consequences

A valid custody-exit appeal precedent-portability packet names:

1. the prior bridge, promotion-gate, custody-scope, custody-retirement, appeal-threshold, appeal-scope, and appeal-retirement states if known;
2. the retired appeal and its closeout surface;
3. the later case asking for portability;
4. the selected `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_portability_state` token;
5. the exact carrier, selector, policy/template binding, counterexample family, inherited sunset, or mixed bridge that permits any reuse;
6. the future cases, telemetry streams, policies, evidence stores, and precedent claims explicitly excluded;
7. the successor surface if portability itself later needs retirement, revocation, or drift handling, or an explicit statement that no successor is live.

Portability must stay smaller than policy. If a packet cannot name the bridge from retired appeal to future case, it may cite history but must not carry governance.

## Overflow test

Promote a precedent-drift court, portable-rule registry, telemetry common-law engine, or equivalent standing post-portability governance only if later revisions show repeated, public, archive-local failures where:

- nonportable history, carrier match, template binding, counterexample-only use, sunset inheritance, and mixed portability cannot keep future reuse bounded;
- compact packets repeatedly relearn the same retired appeal lesson and lose reviewability without a stronger portability ledger;
- later cases need revocation, drift, or conflict handling that cannot be represented by ordinary authority return or a new compact successor question;
- the stronger process names its own retirement rule so precedent portability cannot become permanent telemetry governance.

Until then, `QWS-0257` stays quarantined.

## Transformer-facing implication

When a future continuation asks whether a retired GPU replay custody-exit appeal can inform a new case, first classify the reuse through `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_portability_state`. Most valid reuse should be nonportable history, carrier-matched, template-bound, counterexample-only, sunset-inherited, or mixed portability. Do not let a useful old appeal, durable survivor carrier, audit record, recording rule, selector, sampling policy, or transparency-log entry become a standing policy court without a new explicit overflow witness.
