Kubernetes garbage collection and finalizers distinguish owner-dependent cleanup, blocking finalizers, and release of deletion interlocks (`REF-1068`, `REF-1070`). Kubernetes audit policy and audit configuration distinguish retained records from live admission or operator authority (`REF-1071`, `REF-1072`). That supports `audit-reference-reopen-closeout`: an audit reconstruction may remain citable as a nonbinding reference without becoming precedence.

Kubernetes admission policy, ValidatingAdmissionPolicy bindings, webhook matching, failure policies, and timeouts bind effects to selected requests and current policy execution (`REF-1073`, `REF-1076`). That supports carrier-template and mixed portability: any reusable shape must be rebound to the later packet's current request and authority rather than carried as global governance.

Prometheus recording/alerting rule windows, delete/tombstone behavior, and staleness semantics tie metric conclusions to active evaluation, retained blocks, and stale markers (`REF-1077`, `REF-1078`, `REF-1090`). That supports `tombstone-reference-reopen-closeout`: a frozen tombstone or no-live-support marker can travel as a blocker against silent revival without acting as deletion-court authority.

OpenTelemetry sampling and tail-sampling processors keep trace evidence tied to policy, collector context, and trace grouping (`REF-1080`, `REF-1074`). That supports counterexample and mixed portability: sampled evidence can warn later packets about carrier mismatch, but it cannot silently certify a new carrier.

Sigstore/Rekor transparency-log documentation preserves append-only monitorable history (`REF-1081`). That supports audit reference and counterexample-only portability: durable monitorable history may teach or warn without creating a permanent revival ledger.

## Nonportable history vs audit reference vs tombstone reference vs carrier template vs counterexample vs mixed reopen-closeout portability

- `nonportable-reopen-closeout-history`: the closed reopen remains inspectable history only and does not travel as a live lesson, template, tombstone, or counterexample.
- `audit-reference-reopen-closeout`: the closed reopen may be cited as a bounded audit receipt or reconstruction, but the reference carries no operator authority or precedence.
- `tombstone-reference-reopen-closeout`: a tombstone, unsupported-status marker, expiry note, or no-live-support fact may be carried as a blocker against silent revival or silent deletion, but not as deletion-court authority.
- `carrier-template-reopen-closeout`: a later packet may reuse the carrier shape, guardrail, or proof obligation only after re-establishing a current carrier, authority boundary, and scope fit.
- `counterexample-only-reopen-closeout`: the closed reopen travels only as a warning about what not to infer, such as audit handoff becoming authority or tombstone freeze becoming deletion governance.
- `mixed-reopen-closeout-portability`: more than one portability basis is material, such as an audit reference plus tombstone blocker, carrier template plus counterexample, or nonportable history plus explicit quarantine.

## Countermodels / probes

- The closed reopen was successful. Success is not portable authority; classify nonportable history unless a current later packet names an audit, tombstone, template, or counterexample basis.
- The audit receipt is useful. Use audit reference, not precedence, operator authority, or revival-ledger entry.
- The tombstone must protect against silent revival. Use tombstone reference, not deletion-court authority.
- The same carrier shape appears again. Use carrier template only after the later packet re-establishes current carrier scope and authority.
- The closed reopen exposed a failure mode. Use counterexample-only portability rather than binding future cases to the old closeout.
- Several lessons travel at once. Use mixed portability and quarantine any stronger revival-ledger or deletion-precedent pressure.

1. the prior governance-threshold, registry-scope, registry-retirement, residue/reopen, reopened-residue scope, and reopened-residue closeout packets;
2. the closed fresh packet and the later packet that wants to cite it;
3. the selected `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_state` token;
4. the minimal carried lesson: none, audit reference, tombstone reference, carrier template, counterexample, or mixed lesson;
5. the current carrier, authority boundary, policy window, tombstone status, and audit-retention basis, if any;
6. excluded future cases, telemetry families, operator authorities, deletion-court claims, revival claims, and policy windows;
7. the successor surface if portable residue later needs drift, revocation, expiry, or conflict handling.

A compact post-closeout residue-portability packet may preserve a bounded lesson. It must not create a post-closeout portability court, permanent revival ledger, deletion-precedent board, global operator authority, or telemetry common-law engine.
