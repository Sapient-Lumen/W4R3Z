# GPU replay cross-observer custody-exit appeal precedent-conflict arbitration witnesses, no arbitration, local join, carrier precedence, authority precedence, sunset precedence, revocation precedence, and mixed arbitration

This is the compact successor surface for `OQ-0177`.

## Practice / observation

DelayBasin now distinguishes portable retired appeal lessons from stale templates, carrier conflicts, revoked lessons, sunset expiry, and mixed drift. One ambiguity remains: two or more retired appeal lessons can survive drift classification and still point in different directions for a future cleanup case. The tempting jump is a precedent court. This surface instead adds one compact arbitration packet that names the smallest local tie-break before any standing tribunal is even considered.

A precedent-conflict arbitration witness is not a binding appellate system. It asks whether there is no real conflict left, whether the live lessons can be locally joined, whether the carrier, authority, sunset, or revocation surface gives priority, or whether the conflict is genuinely mixed. The default is to shrink the conflict into the current packet, preserve nonwinning lessons as history, and avoid turning old GPU telemetry cleanup appeals into a permanent policy judiciary.

## External pressure from admission bindings, webhook reinvocation and failure policy, recording-rule ordering, sampling policies, and transparency logs

Kubernetes ValidatingAdmissionPolicy separates policy text, parameter resources, bindings, match constraints, and validation actions (`REF-1076`). That supports `carrier-precedence-precedent-conflict` and `authority-precedence-precedent-conflict`: the lesson attached to the currently matching carrier or authority-bearing binding outranks a similar but nonmatching old lesson without creating a global priority ladder.

Kubernetes dynamic admission webhooks also expose ordering pressure through matching, selectors, match conditions, reinvocation policy, warnings, timeouts, and failure policy (`REF-1073`). That supports `local-join-precedent-conflict` when multiple lessons can safely compose inside one request path, and `carrier-precedence-precedent-conflict` when a mutation window, failure policy, or reinvocation boundary means one lesson applies and the other does not.

Prometheus recording rules run in groups with named expressions, labels, and evaluation intervals (`REF-1077`), while alerting rules and staleness behavior make old rule evidence bounded by current evaluation context and absence semantics (`REF-1078`, `REF-1090`). That supports `sunset-precedence-precedent-conflict`: a live bounded window or currently evaluated rule outranks an expired inherited lesson, while the older lesson remains citeable as historical guidance.

OpenTelemetry sampling and the collector tail-sampling processor separate head/tail decisions, configured policies, and trace-level criteria (`REF-1080`, `REF-1074`). That supports `revocation-precedence-precedent-conflict` where a later sampling criterion, kept-survivor reason, or authority return explicitly withdraws an older lesson's present force.

Sigstore/Rekor transparency-log documentation preserves append-only, monitorable records (`REF-1081`). That supports the archive split between durable history and current precedence: a logged appeal remains inspectable, but append-only survival does not make the oldest lesson automatically win.

## Working synthesis

Use `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_conflict_arbitration_state` when all of these are true:

- more than one retired custody-exit appeal lesson is being considered for the same future case;
- first-pass precedent portability and post-portability drift/revocation/expiry have already been classified where relevant;
- at least two lessons still materially disagree about the future cleanup move;
- the desired output is a compact tie-break classification, not a binding precedent appellate court, global priority ladder, inter-case arbitration tribunal, stale-rule registry, or telemetry common-law engine.

Do not use this family for original replay envelopes, receipts, trace grades, observer conflicts, bridges, promotion gates, custody scope, custody retirement, appeal thresholding, appeal scope, appeal retirement, first-pass precedent portability, or ordinary drift. It starts only after drift classification leaves a material conflict among retired appeal lessons.

## No arbitration vs local join vs carrier precedence vs authority precedence vs sunset precedence vs revocation precedence vs mixed arbitration

- `no-arbitration-needed-precedent-conflict`: only one lesson remains current after portability and drift checks, or all apparent conflicts collapse to history, stale template, revocation, expiry, or nonmatching carrier.
- `local-join-precedent-conflict`: multiple current lessons can be composed inside the same carrier, authority, policy window, or cleanup packet without one overriding the other.
- `carrier-precedence-precedent-conflict`: a lesson tied to the present carrier, selector, trace, exemplar, admission request, recording group, transparency-log entry, or evidence object outranks a lesson tied to a nonmatching or weaker carrier.
- `authority-precedence-precedent-conflict`: a lesson backed by the current authority boundary, authority return, binding, policy owner, or explicit closeout authority outranks a lesson from a stale or lower-authority source.
- `sunset-precedence-precedent-conflict`: a lesson whose TTL, retention window, sampling window, policy window, or review sunset is still live outranks one whose bounded window has expired.
- `revocation-precedence-precedent-conflict`: a later revocation, withdrawal, replacement policy, authority return, cleanup decision, or sampling change defeats reuse of an older lesson while preserving it as history.
- `mixed-precedent-conflict-arbitration`: more than one tie-break branch is material, such as carrier precedence plus sunset expiry, authority precedence plus partial revocation, or a local join that leaves one excluded stale lesson. The packet names each branch and the smallest surviving guidance.

## Countermodels / probes

- Two old lessons disagree. Disagreement is not automatically arbitration; check whether drift already retired one side.
- One lesson is older. Age alone does not confer priority; if the newer lesson has explicit revocation or current authority, use `revocation-precedence-precedent-conflict` or `authority-precedence-precedent-conflict`.
- One lesson has richer telemetry. Richer evidence is not precedence unless it is the current carrier, binding, trace, rule group, or authority surface.
- Two lessons appear compatible. Use `local-join-precedent-conflict` only if their carriers, authorities, windows, and exclusions can be named without hidden override.
- A transparency log preserves both lessons. Preservation is not binding force; the winning token controls current reuse, while the losing lesson remains history, template, counterexample, or revoked record.
- If no tie-break can be named, classify as `mixed-precedent-conflict-arbitration` with explicit exclusions rather than promoting a court.

## Design consequences

A valid precedent-conflict arbitration packet names:

1. the competing retired appeal lessons and their prior portability/drift states if known;
2. the future cleanup case trying to reuse them;
3. the selected `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_conflict_arbitration_state` token;
4. the carrier, authority, policy binding, rule group, sampling policy, revocation note, TTL, retention window, transparency-log entry, or closeout surface that proves the tie-break;
5. which lesson, if any, governs the current packet, which lessons are joined, and which shrink to history, template, counterexample, stale record, revoked record, or excluded evidence;
6. the future cases, telemetry streams, policies, appeal layers, or cleanup authorities explicitly excluded;
7. the successor surface if repeated conflict tie-breaks later need retirement or shrink-back after the conflict is settled.

A compact arbitration packet may select, join, shrink, or decline old lessons. It must not become a reusable appellate hierarchy for all GPU replay cleanup disputes.

## Overflow test

Promote a binding precedent appellate court, global priority ladder, inter-case arbitration tribunal, or equivalent standing governance only if later revisions show repeated, public, archive-local failures where:

- `no-arbitration-needed-precedent-conflict`, `local-join-precedent-conflict`, `carrier-precedence-precedent-conflict`, `authority-precedence-precedent-conflict`, `sunset-precedence-precedent-conflict`, `revocation-precedence-precedent-conflict`, and `mixed-precedent-conflict-arbitration` cannot route material retired-appeal conflicts;
- the conflict recurs across carriers or authorities in a way that compact packet-local precedence cannot represent;
- local tie-breaks repeatedly reanimate revoked or expired lessons despite explicit source surfaces;
- the stronger process names its own scope, retirement rule, nonbinding-history lane, and authority limit.

Until then, `QWS-0260` stays quarantined.

## Transformer-facing implication

When a future continuation sees multiple portable retired GPU replay custody-exit appeal lessons that survived drift checks but conflict, first classify the tie-break through `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_conflict_arbitration_state`. Most valid cases should be no-arbitration-needed, local-join, carrier-precedence, authority-precedence, sunset-precedence, revocation-precedence, or mixed arbitration. Do not let old appeal lessons, durable logs, rule texts, sampling policies, admission bindings, or telemetry carriers become a standing policy court unless a new explicit overflow witness earns that promotion.
