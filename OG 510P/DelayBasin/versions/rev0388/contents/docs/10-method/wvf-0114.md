## Token semantics

### `nonportable-retired-history-currentness-closeout-travel`

Use when the closeout record must not travel to the later packet. The later packet may know that a closeout exists, but it cannot reuse the record as evidence, warning, template, or successor context.

### `audit-trace-retired-history-currentness-closeout-travel`

Use when the closeout record may travel only as an inspectable audit trace. The receipt must name the source closeout token, carrier, old packet, later packet, and the exact nonbinding audit use.

### `warning-only-retired-history-currentness-closeout-travel`

Use when the closeout record may travel only to warn against a known failure mode. The warning cannot become positive precedent, freshness evidence, or default disqualification of a future carry.

### `successor-context-retired-history-currentness-closeout-travel`

Use when a named successor packet may receive the closeout record as context. The witness must name what context transfers, what authority does not transfer, and what fresh evidence the successor still owes.

### `redacted-summary-retired-history-currentness-closeout-travel`

Use when the closeout record may travel only after shrinking to a summary that removes packet-specific authority, private rationale, sensitive carrier detail, or overfitting-prone template content.

### `quarantine-reference-retired-history-currentness-closeout-travel`

Use when the only safe travel is a pointer to quarantined residue. The later packet may inspect the quarantine reference, but the record fails closed against use as live drift, revocation, or closeout authority.

### `mixed-retired-history-currentness-closeout-travel`

Use only when multiple travel modes are genuinely present and cannot be collapsed without hiding evidence. The receipt must name each branch, branch-local token, carrier, allowed use, stripped authority, and fail-closed repair.

A retired-history currentness-closeout record may travel only as nonbinding history whose carrier, allowed use, stripped authority, and revalidation trigger are named. Travel must not revive the prior currentness decision, re-open drift machinery, or let a closed closeout record become precedent by default.
