## Practice / observation

The remaining risk starts after portability: a later packet may treat an old closed-reopen lesson as still current after the carrier, tombstone, audit-retention window, policy binding, sampling criterion, or sunset has changed.

This surface adds one post-closeout portability-drift witness. It does not create a revival-precedent court, residue-drift registry, revocation tribunal, permanent revival ledger, deletion-precedent board, or telemetry common-law engine. It classifies whether a previously portable closed-reopen lesson is current, stale-template-only, carrier-conflicted, revoked, expired, or mixed drift before the lesson can be spent again.

## External pressure from TTL expiry, audit retention, tombstones, admission rebinding, rule staleness, sampling policy change, and transparency history

Kubernetes TTL-after-finished cleanup distinguishes finished work from live controller custody (`REF-1067`). That supports `expired-reopen-closeout-sunset`: a closed-reopen lesson may have been useful during a bounded retention or review window but lose current force once the window is over.

Kubernetes garbage collection and finalizers distinguish ownership, deletion interlocks, and explicit finalizer release (`REF-1068`, `REF-1070`). That supports `revoked-reopen-closeout-lesson`: a later release, owner change, or cleanup decision can withdraw the old lesson's governing force without erasing the audit trail.

Prometheus delete-series / clean-tombstone behavior and query staleness separate retained or tombstoned series from current samples (`REF-1069`, `REF-1090`). That supports `stale-reopen-closeout-template` and `expired-reopen-closeout-sunset`: a tombstone or stale marker can remain a warning while no longer certifying present conditions.

Kubernetes audit policy and audit configuration preserve records without granting live admission authority (`REF-1071`, `REF-1072`). That supports `current-reopen-closeout-portability` only when the current packet can still name the same audit-retention basis; otherwise the old record shrinks to history, stale template, revocation evidence, or mixed drift.

Kubernetes dynamic admission control and ValidatingAdmissionPolicy bind decisions to current match conditions, bindings, validation actions, failure policy, and request context (`REF-1073`, `REF-1076`). That supports `conflicting-reopen-closeout-carrier`: a later packet with a different object, selector, policy binding, authority, or request carrier cannot spend the old portability label as if nothing changed.

Prometheus recording and alerting rules tie reusable metric conclusions to rule bodies, labels, groups, evaluation intervals, and active windows (`REF-1077`, `REF-1078`). That supports stale-template and expired-sunset drift when a copied rule or alert lesson survives as a pattern but its evaluation context no longer matches.

OpenTelemetry sampling and tail-sampling processors keep trace evidence tied to policy, collector state, trace grouping, and sampling criteria (`REF-1080`, `REF-1074`). That supports revoked-lesson and mixed drift when a sampling policy or trace-selection rule changes after a closed reopen was first made portable.

Sigstore/Rekor transparency-log documentation preserves monitorable history (`REF-1081`). That supports the split between durable record and live governance: transparency history can help prove currentness, revocation, or conflict, but it cannot by itself create a revival-precedent court.

## Current portability vs stale template vs conflicting carrier vs revoked lesson vs expired sunset vs mixed drift

- `current-reopen-closeout-portability`: the prior portable closed-reopen lesson remains current because the same carrier, audit-retention basis, tombstone status, policy binding, sampling criterion, authority boundary, and sunset still match the later packet.
- `stale-reopen-closeout-template`: the prior lesson remains useful as a template, warning, or pattern, but its rule body, policy text, tombstone status, audit basis, carrier shape, or sampling context is no longer live enough to govern the later packet.
- `conflicting-reopen-closeout-carrier`: the later packet names a carrier, selector, object, audit row, tombstone, trace, rule group, policy binding, or transparency-log reference that conflicts with the one that made the closed reopen portable.
- `revoked-reopen-closeout-lesson`: a later authority-return, tombstone release, audit correction, policy replacement, sampling change, cleanup decision, or explicit revocation withdraws the old lesson's governing force while preserving historical reviewability.
- `expired-reopen-closeout-sunset`: the prior portability basis was limited by TTL, retention horizon, policy window, sampling window, review window, tombstone lifetime, or inherited sunset that has now expired.
- `mixed-reopen-closeout-drift`: more than one drift branch is material, such as a stale carrier template plus an expired sunset, a tombstone conflict plus partial revocation, or a still-current audit reference with a narrowed sampling criterion.

## Countermodels / probes

- A closed reopen was once portable. Once-portable is not still-current; check carrier, audit, tombstone, policy, sampling, authority, and sunset before spending it again.
- A tombstone reference blocks silent revival. A tombstone blocker may itself become stale, released, or contradicted; classify the present state instead of turning it into deletion-court authority.
- A carrier template looks similar. Similar shape is not a carrier match if the later packet cannot re-establish the same current carrier and authority boundary.
- An audit reference is durable. Durable audit history preserves reviewability; it does not stop revocation, expiry, or carrier conflict from shrinking current use.
- A lesson was explicitly revoked. Revocation does not erase the old record; classify it as `revoked-reopen-closeout-lesson` unless a new current authority independently re-admits it.
- Several signs point different ways. Use mixed drift and name the smallest surviving use rather than promoting a residue-drift registry.

## Design consequences

A valid post-closeout portability-drift packet names:

1. the prior governance-threshold, registry-scope, registry-retirement, residue/reopen, reopened-residue scope, reopened-residue closeout, and post-closeout portability packets;
2. the closed fresh packet and the later packet trying to spend the portable lesson;
3. the selected `gpu_replay_cross_observer_custody_exit_appeal_precedent_tiebreak_registry_reopened_residue_post_closeout_portability_drift_state` token;
4. the carrier, selector, policy binding, audit-retention basis, tombstone status, rule window, sampling criterion, transparency-log entry, authority-return note, revocation surface, or sunset window that proves currentness or drift;
5. the exact part of the old lesson that may still be cited as current guidance, stale template, counterexample, audit history, tombstone warning, or no surviving use;
6. excluded future cases, telemetry streams, deletion-court claims, revival-precedent claims, operator authorities, and policy windows;
7. the successor surface if repeated conflicts among compact drift tokens later show that arbitration governance is honestly needed.

A compact post-closeout portability-drift packet may keep a portable lesson current, shrink it to template/history/counterexample, revoke it, expire it, or mark mixed drift. It must not transform maintenance of useful closed-reopen lessons into a standing revival-precedent court.

## Overflow test

Promote a revival-precedent court, residue-drift registry, revocation tribunal, or equivalent standing post-portability governance only if later revisions show repeated, public, archive-local failures where:

- `current-reopen-closeout-portability`, `stale-reopen-closeout-template`, `conflicting-reopen-closeout-carrier`, `revoked-reopen-closeout-lesson`, `expired-reopen-closeout-sunset`, and `mixed-reopen-closeout-drift` cannot preserve currentness, staleness, carrier conflict, revocation, expiry, and mixed-drift boundaries;
- old audit references repeatedly re-enter as current authority after revocation or expiry;
- tombstone references repeatedly become deletion-precedent authority despite current conflict or release;
- carrier templates repeatedly bypass current-carrier proof, policy rebinding, sampling checks, or authority boundaries;
- the stronger process names its operator authority, drift scope, revocation rule, conflict rule, retirement witness, and nonbinding-history lane.

Until then, `QWS-0269` stays quarantined.
