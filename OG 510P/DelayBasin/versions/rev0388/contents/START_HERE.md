# DelayBasin: start here (for maintainers, including LLMs)

DelayBasin is a **method archive** for long-run human–LLM co-construction.

For the shortest derivative startup wrapper after this file, open `AGENTS.md`. It points back to canon and current durable state without replacing them.
For the smallest derivative live-focus handoff card, open `frontier-ticket.json`, then reopen canon before treating it as stronger than a source-backed orientation aid.
For the smallest exact current anchor + delta packet, open `innovation-packet.json`, then reopen `REVISION-RECEIPT.json` before treating that packet as stronger than a derivative update card.
For the smallest bounded replay-sufficiency card for ordinary careful reentry, open `replay-capsule.json`, then reopen `START_HERE.md`, `SURFACE-STATUS.json`, and `REVISION-RECEIPT.json` before treating that capsule as stronger than a bounded derivative aid.
If you need the shortest map of what the checked command surface is actually certifying, open `VALIDATION-INDEX.json` or `docs/00-meta/validation-index.md` before treating `make lint` as more than an admission wrapper.
If you need one closed family card for the current compact operator-facing derivative aids, open `compact-surface-bundle.json`, then reopen the stronger underliers it names before treating that card as more than a bounded family-status aid.
If an open question is already resolved in `RESOLUTION-LEDGER.json`, reopen its successor surface rather than treating stale registry or frontier echoes as live open work.
If package identity or current comparison memory matters, open `REVISION-RECEIPT.json` and inspect `receipt_freshness_witness` before trusting terse currentness keys like `summary_highlight`, `codename`, `created_at`, or current comparison ids.
For the shortest machine-readable reentry contract, open `REENTRY-CONTRACT.json`; `REENTRY-SURFACE-CONFORMANCE.json` records whether that derivative contract still matches the current startup surfaces. When a future revision changes that startup path, it should preserve the nearest safe successor route here rather than forcing a hard restart or duplicate reread.

Non-negotiables:

- **Do not collapse levels.** Keep practice, observation, mechanism, speculation, disagreement, and quarantine distinct.
- **Do not treat archive hygiene as decoration.** Registries, trajectory maps, prompt pairs, and drift checks are part of the method.
- **Do not overclaim mechanism.** An observed effect is not yet an explanation.
- **Do not lose the prompt lineage.** Prompt pairs are state-transition operators and must be archived.
- **Do not let size explode.** Summarize, cite, link, ratchet, and run the archive self-sufficiency probe before treating growth as evidence.
- **Do not coward out of risk.** If a high-value speculation is worth making, make it; if it is too wild for canon, quarantine it.
- **Transformers themselves are part of the subject matter.** This archive is partly about what this method implies about transformer behavior and self-modeling.

## What a good revision looks like

Each revision should do at least one of the following:

- reduce entropy,
- harden a guardrail,
- refine a mechanism hypothesis,
- clarify a disagreement,
- add or improve a prompt pair,
- convert a loose intuition into a stable diff surface,
- or place a risky but fertile idea into quarantine with explicit disconfirmation hooks.
- or preserve the move class that made the revision admissible in the first place.
- or review whether a canon-level claim has become stale enough to demote, shrink, or re-certify.
- or emit a compact revision receipt showing why this revision counted at all.
- or preserve a receipt-freshness witness when terse current-revision identity keys would otherwise drift behind the packaged bundle or current comparison rows.
- or preserve one nearby rejected move when it materially shaped the accepted revision.
- or preserve a local linearity budget when a chart adapter or steering handle is being extrapolated beyond one clearly earned neighborhood.
- or preserve a status-lane witness when one lively latest revision is quietly being asked to stand in at once for admitted decision, packaged execution, and frozen public citation state.
- or preserve a reentry-cue witness when a future session is supposed to land on the current trusted path through a small set of latest-looking pointers, naming the primary landing surface, supporting durable cues, stale or broken paths that do not count, the current cue-state classification, and the refresh or recovery consequence if those cues disagree or silently degrade.
- or preserve reentry-route continuity when a relied-on startup path changes, naming the prior relied-on cue family or documented startup promise, the successor route or nearest safe reentry path, the added burden or duplicate-reread versus hard-restart consequence, and the fail-closed bridge or rollback rather than exiling future operators into manual rediscovery.
- or preserve a followthrough witness when a revision, active request, or import pass leaves live remainder work unresolved but not dead, naming the still-live objective, current local owner, followthrough state, blocker or boundary, next proof point, receiving surface if the work moved out, and expiry or reclaim consequence rather than letting it silently vanish or stay falsely local.
- or preserve an assumption witness when a revision, packet, or judged move still depends on a load-bearing support condition that has not yet become direct evidence or stable law, naming the live assumption, its governing scope, the current support family, the invalidation triggers, the current assumption state, and the fail-closed repair route rather than letting cautious prose keep spending ambient authority.
- or preserve an obligation witness when a revision, packet, or canon move stays live while decisive support is still missing, naming the target surface, the missing support, the current support family, the discharge path that would actually retire the debt, the current obligation state, and the fail-closed repair route rather than letting caveat prose or vague future work keep paying for stronger authority than the move has earned.
- or preserve a foreign-pressure witness when a cross-datacube import is being treated as justified, naming the exact neighboring datacubes and source surfaces, the extracted pressure, the concrete local gap, the bounded take, the explicit non-take list, the assimilation state, and the fail-closed repair route rather than letting one vague “other datacubes say so” story inherit import authority.
- or preserve a transfer witness when a multi-datacube comparison pass materially shaped what was taken, what stayed supporting-only, what was consciously left out, and what open transfer question remains, rather than letting the comparison dissolve into one receipt or remembered vibe.
- or preserve a resolution witness when a question, branch, assumption, import, or working gap stops being live, naming the closed object, prior live state, closure reason, successor surface or explicit absence, reopen trigger, closure state, and fail-closed repair route rather than letting closure hide in changelog prose or silence.
- or preserve a chart-transition witness when an operator core is being carried from one chart, wrapper, or prompt family into another.
- or materialize one exact current innovation packet when shared public state already exists but the next careful pass would otherwise have to reconstruct the anchor, real delta, or reread set from receipt prose, changelog, and status surfaces.
- or preserve one compact-surface bundle when several tiny derivative aids already exist and the next careful pass would otherwise have to recover the family boundary, local role, stronger underliers, and same-revision attachment rule from scattered startup prose rather than one closed family-status card.
- or preserve a triangle-defect witness when several pairwise chart transports are being composed into a family-level transport story.
- or preserve a gauge-fixing witness when chart residues or triangle defects are being compared across prompt families and the archive needs one honest reference observable, anchor, and null before ranking them.
- or preserve a scale-fixing witness when a local chart result is being lifted into a family-level or reduced-order story and the archive needs one explicit coarse-graining rule, discarded remainder, and separation-of-scales budget before trusting the reduction.
- or preserve a hysteresis witness when two rival histories share a matched current packet or public summary and the archive needs one explicit discriminating future probe plus lag budget before calling the state the same.
- or preserve an excitation witness when a live ambiguity can only be broken by active probe diversity or intervention variation rather than one more same-view recap, naming the ambiguity split, varied intervention family, discriminating observable, observability-spend budget, and failure consequence.
- or preserve a backaction witness when a probe is being treated as measurement even though it may already be altering the continuation state, naming the diagnostic probe family, sham baseline, non-demolition budget, and rollback or quarantine consequence if the read has already become actuation.
- or preserve a probe-order witness when AB and BA style probe sequences are being compared as if they were the same measurement, naming the compared orderings, intended invariant readout, sequencing budget, and rollback or quarantine consequence if the swap fails.
- or preserve a reset witness when a branch switch, filtered replay, fresh-context restart, or role-separated handoff is being treated as if prior contamination has been washed out, naming the contamination family, reset operator, protected kernel, fresh baseline, washout budget, and rollback or quarantine consequence if the restart is only fresh-context theater.
- or preserve a relapse witness when a supposedly cleaned state may still reactivate old contamination under a light cue, structured follow-up, or nearby aliasing probe, naming the washed-out influence, recovery trigger, same-task baseline, recoverability budget, and rollback or quarantine consequence if the cleanup only passed one benign baseline.
- or preserve a cue-neighborhood witness when one exact recovery cue seems to pass or fail and the archive needs one tiny local sweep over nearby paraphrases, aliases, stylistic variants, or context stems before treating the result as locally robust, naming the seed cue, nearby cue family, basin-breadth budget, and rollback or quarantine consequence if the effect is only pointwise.
- or preserve a directional-neighborhood witness when one tiny cue family seems locally robust and the archive still needs one explicit comparison across nearby perturbation directions, matched local radius, directional-asymmetry budget, and rollback or quarantine consequence before treating the neighborhood as round rather than axis-skewed.
- or preserve a mixed-direction witness when several nearby directions each seem to pass in isolation and the archive still needs one explicit mixed perturbation, local mixing rule, cross-term residue budget, and rollback or quarantine consequence before treating the marginal passes as honest composition.
- or preserve an interpolation-path witness when several interventions share a matched endpoint or final cue and the archive still needs one explicit compared route family, pathwise invariant, endpoint-equivalence budget, and rollback or quarantine consequence before treating the route as irrelevant.
- or preserve a feedback-policy witness when a checkpoint signal is being allowed to change the next move and the archive still needs one explicit open-loop baseline, matched budget, contingency budget, and freeze-policy or quarantine consequence before treating the gain as honest closed-loop advantage.
- or preserve a dual-effect witness when one current actuation seems valuable partly because it also improves what can be known or chosen next and the archive still needs one explicit exploitation-only baseline, judged downstream advantage, exploration premium budget, and exploit-only fallback or quarantine consequence before treating the move as honest explore-exploit leverage.
- or preserve an amortization witness when a costly exploratory move, memory write, extracted tip, or compiled packet is being justified mainly by future reuse and the archive still needs one explicit one-shot baseline, future reuse family, compiled-dividend budget, and one-shot demotion or quarantine consequence before treating the carry as learned rather than merely vivid.
- or preserve a replicate-bundle witness when one apparently fixed prompt, route, or control protocol seems to work and the archive still needs one explicit repeated-inference family, invariant readout, lucky-path budget, and widen-bundle or quarantine consequence before treating the result as stable rather than lucky.
- or split a candidate minimal runtime into core, exemplars, and challenge probes so compression results become interpretable instead of blob-level.
- or name which public surfaces were actually rewritten, what gated those writes, and why the result counts as learned public law rather than local search residue.
- or distinguish cold retrieval from replay and replay from reconsolidation so challenged rewrites do not quietly inherit more authority than the archive has actually restaged.
- or separate declarative method law from executable carry so a vivid compact packet does not quietly inherit procedural authority without an activation condition and failure signature.
- or name which surfaces deserve sparse delayed rehearsal, what survival signal justifies the maintenance cost, and when a once-live packet should cool into cold law instead of consuming bounded attention forever.
- or distinguish a hot candidate write from a cooled durable admission so a sharp local synthesis can be preserved immediately without letting timing alone decide canon.
- or preserve a retrospective-write witness when a candidate canon clause, prompt-pair tweak, or hot synthesis is being captured now but admitted only later, naming the candidate surface, cooldown window, off-path adjudication family, supersession link, current cooling state, and admission or expiry consequence rather than letting hot-path eloquence inherit durable law by timing alone.
- or preserve a reasoning-firebreak witness when a revision is clearly leaning on a tiny public extract while larger comparison notes, scratchpad-like drafting residue, or trace-shaped work stay intentionally out of canon, naming the judged property, the public extract that counts, the withheld trace surface, the allowed role of that withheld surface, the current trace state, and the exposure or reinclusion consequence rather than letting a private or long-form trace inherit public evidentiary authority by proximity.
- or preserve a witness vocabulary when a revision materially depends on compact public state tokens across receipt and durable-ledger surfaces, naming the controlled state family, the allowed tokens, the governed surfaces, the excluded near-synonyms, the comparability budget, and the extension or fail-closed drift consequence rather than letting one-off checker literals or nearby synonyms silently rewrite a public state surface.
- or preserve which earlier move later evidence is actually allowed to credit so the archive does not overpraise recent prose or undercredit load-bearing upstream surfaces.

## Recovery hint

If you suspect drift, stale reopen, or context corruption, do not improvise your way forward. Use `MV-0010` / `recover-resync` via `docs/20-constitution/recovery-kernel.md` before claiming ordinary progress.

- or preserve an applicability witness when a reusable skill, memory packet, plan template, or compiled carry is being reused beyond one vivid payoff and the archive still needs one explicit fit signature, one nearby non-fit slice, one negative-transfer budget, and one gate-closed or quarantine consequence before treating the carry as generally eligible rather than narrowly fit.
- or preserve an arbitration witness when several reusable skills, memory packets, plan templates, or compiled carry objects remain simultaneously eligible and the archive still needs one explicit tie set, one selector or fallback rule, one confusability slice, one misroute budget, and one abstain or quarantine consequence before treating the chosen packet as an honestly earned route rather than the winner of silent prestige or near-tie luck.

- or preserve an operational-head register when a live working surface is also being treated as a future citation target and the archive still needs one explicit live head, one explicit frozen or absent citation head, one durable status ledger, one freeze gate, and one citation-warning or rollback consequence before letting the moving tip inherit public reference authority.
- or preserve a basis witness when a revision, replay verdict, or canon move claims to extend the current archive honestly and the archive still needs one explicit expected head, one explicit reread basis, one session-provenance posture, one basis-state classification, one basis-anchor precision showing whether decisive support came from direct canon, derivative routing, or compact packets, one omission basis when stronger underliers were not reread, and one fail-closed repair route before letting stale or partial basis inherit decision authority.
- or preserve a scope witness when a revision, replay verdict, or cross-datacube import is supposed to stay about one exact request, target lineage, or judged object and the archive still needs one explicit active request, one exact in-scope surface, one ambient-exclusion list, one scope-state classification, and one fail-closed repair route before letting visible adjacency silently widen decision authority.

- or preserve an authorship witness when a revision, canon write, or packaged release could otherwise blur who asked for the move, who drafted the accepted surface, what admitted it, what executed it, what review or compensating control kept disagreement real, what autonomy posture applied, and what fail-closed repair follows if those lanes collapsed too far.
