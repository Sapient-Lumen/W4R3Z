- workflow request surface,

## Editing method

Prefer small, composable edits.

Every serious revision should do at least one:
- research online when useful and translate the result into a small archive-native update,
- refine a distinction,
- add a guardrail,
- improve a prompt pair,
- convert a repeated intuition into a registry or invariant,
- capture an open disagreement without flattening it,
- or quarantine a genuinely fertile risky idea.
- When a prior revision is clearly shared, prefer anchor+delta+reconciliation language over ritual whole-archive restatement.
- When a revision, replay verdict, or canon move materially depends on current archive state, preserve a compact basis witness naming the expected head, the actual reread basis, the session-provenance posture, the basis state, and the fail-closed repair route rather than letting stale or partial basis silently inherit authority.
- When a revision, replay verdict, canon move, or cross-datacube import is supposed to stay about one exact object, target lineage, or requested change, preserve a compact scope witness naming the active request, the exact target, the nearby ambient surfaces kept out, the scope state, and the fail-closed repair route rather than letting visible adjacency silently widen decision authority.
- When claiming a compression or innovation win, name the distortion target and mismatch risk rather than treating brevity as self-justifying.
- When materially revising canon or public belief state, name the trigger, the update gain (`low` / `medium` / `high`), and at least one challenge probe or reason no probe is needed.
- When the honest next move is not to revise, preserve a small hold packet naming the anchor, blocker, brake posture (`hold`, `abstract`, or `recover-resync`), and unlock condition rather than letting non-movement disappear into hedging prose.

- If a revision narrows scope, defers a promising import, or hands a live remainder to quarantine, an open question, or a future pass, consider preserving a tiny **followthrough witness / blocked-output queue / explicit handoff** naming the still-live objective, the current local owner or lane, the followthrough-state classification, the blocker or boundary, the next proof point, the receiving surface if the work moved out, and the expiry or reclaim consequence rather than letting the remainder silently vanish or stay falsely local.
- If a revision, packet, or judged move still depends on a load-bearing support condition that has not yet become direct evidence or stable law, preserve a compact **assumption witness / expiry trigger / invalidation cue** naming the live assumption, the governing scope, the supporting surfaces or evidence family, the invalidation triggers, the current assumption state, and the fail-closed repair route; keep the durable current set in `ASSUMPTION-LEDGER.json` rather than scattering it across caveat prose.
- If a revision imports or declines a ratchet because of neighboring datacube pressure, preserve a compact **foreign-pressure witness / import lineage / bounded assimilation** naming the exact source datacubes and source surfaces, the extracted pressure, the concrete local gap, the bounded take, the explicit non-take list, the current assimilation state, and the fail-closed repair route; keep the durable current set in `FOREIGN-PRESSURE-LEDGER.json` rather than one-line “neighboring datacubes suggest” summaries.
- If a revision is also establishing that some question, branch, assumption, import, or working gap is no longer live, preserve a compact **resolution witness / closure reason / reopen trigger** naming the closed object, prior live state, closure reason, successor surface or explicit absence, reopen trigger, closure-state classification, and fail-closed repair route; keep the durable current set in `RESOLUTION-LEDGER.json` rather than forcing later sessions to infer closure from chronology alone.
- When reopen fidelity is materially in doubt, consider preserving a tiny sentinel panel naming the property monitored, the expected deviation signature, and whether a tripped canary calls for ordinary continuation, bounded rollback, or `recover-resync`.
- When rival continuation hypotheses materially differ, consider preserving a compact identification packet naming the hypothesis split, the observation sought, and what continuation decision that observation gates.
- When a live ambiguity class survives the first diagnostic move, consider preserving a compact homing packet naming the ambiguity class, the probe family or branch rule, the orientation/update rule, and the stop or escalation condition.
- When a real working phase ends, consider preserving a compact phase boundary / rollover packet naming what phase just closed, what persists, what resets or cools, and what new operating question now governs continuation.
- When a revision invokes vector / basin / chart / tangent / atlas language in a load-bearing way, preserve at least one invariant claim, observable, or operational contract that should survive a chart switch, and say what part remains chart-specific.
- When two short revision paths are implicitly supposed to preserve the same operative consequence, consider preserving a tiny loop-closure / commutator probe naming the shared anchor, the compared paths, the closure target, and the closure error that would count as material.
- When a revision is implicitly relying on some local variation being harmless, consider preserving a tiny continuation margin / guard band naming the protected property, the perturbation family, and the first failure signature that would justify bounded rollback, a hold packet, or `recover-resync`.
- When a revision is deciding which surfaces deserve scarce bounded-state slots, consider preserving a compact balanced-reduction packet naming the surface's observation role, control role, and truncation consequence rather than shrinking by eloquence or familiarity alone.
- When memory language becomes load-bearing, say whether the surface is functioning as a memory store, a regime-reentry packet, or a check/admission object rather than flattening all persistence into one layer.
- When bounded state is doing real work, say what future tests, interventions, or challenge probes the packet is meant to answer cheaply rather than treating recap completeness as sufficient.
- When a load-bearing handle, witness, or privileged prompt surface seems potent, consider preserving a compact negative-control handle packet naming the active surface, matched sham or negative control, expected differential signature, pass/fail rule, and retire or escalation consequence.
- When prior assistant-side history may be doing load-bearing work, say whether it is genuinely needed evidence, a regime-reentry cue, a check/admission surface, or suspicious self-carry, and consider preserving an assistant-echo filter naming the kept user-side anchor, the omitted assistant-side surface, the expected invariance or gain signature, and the reinclusion or escalation consequence.
- When several witnesses, judges, probes, or challenge passes are being treated as additive evidence, consider preserving a compact dependence-adjusted witness packet naming the witness family or probe family, the shared-origin coupling, the rough discount rule, what still counts as genuinely new evidence, and the stop or escalation consequence if the apparent plurality collapses after discount.
- When a recap, paraphrase, compressed rewrite, or stitched restatement is about to inherit source-of-truth authority, consider preserving a compact rewrite witness / round-trip packet naming the source packet or authority anchor, the rewritten packet, the round-trip or cross-exam witness, the divergence signature, and the promotion, demotion, or rollback consequence if the rewrite fails.
- When a packet, prompt pair, or re-entry surface is being treated as a stable loader for operative state, consider preserving a compact conformance witness / loader contract naming the interface surface or claimed loader, the supported model-wrapper-context family, the minimal conformance test or metamorphic check family, the first non-conformance or drift signature, and the narrowing, demotion, or fallback consequence if the loader fails.
- If a prompt pair, compact loader, or control term is being treated as portable across revisions, wrappers, or model families, consider preserving a compact **operator core / chart adapter / portability budget** packet naming the invariant operator core, the current local chart adapter, the tested family or wrapper envelope, the first portability failure signature, and the fallback or quarantine consequence rather than letting one locally successful wording silently stand in for portable archive law.
- If a revision is actively steering toward a named continuation property under limited budget, consider preserving a compact **servo packet / receding-horizon control / target-tracking** packet naming the target continuation property, the current error signature or drift symptom, the actuator family or editable surface, the short horizon or horizon proxy, and the retune / rollback / quarantine consequence rather than letting generic rewrite vigor masquerade as control.
- If a prompt pair, operator core, or steering handle is being extrapolated beyond one clearly earned local success, consider preserving a compact **local linearity budget / curved chart adapter / tangent steering** packet naming the target property or operator core, the local chart neighborhood or execution family, the assumed linear or monotone region / small-step budget, the first curvature or distortion warning sign, and the relinearize / rollback / quarantine consequence rather than letting one local success silently become a global steering law.
- If several pairwise chart-transition witnesses are being composed or a whole prompt family is being treated as one transportable operator family, consider preserving a compact **triangle defect / cocycle witness / atlas consistency budget** packet naming the three chart realizations or family members, the shared operator core, the existing pairwise chart-transition witnesses, the triangle overlap probe or family-level shared test surface, the composition defect / cocycle residue / atlas-consistency budget, and the demotion / rollback / quarantine consequence rather than quietly globalizing pairwise success.
- If chart-transition residues, triangle defects, or family-level transport scores are being compared across prompt families, consider preserving a compact **gauge-fixing witness / reference observable / defect comparability budget** packet naming the invariant observable or reference observable, the chosen gauge / anchor / spanning-tree base, the comparison family or support set, the null / flatness expectation / zero-defect baseline, the defect-comparability budget, and the demotion / rollback / quarantine consequence rather than letting a coordinate shift masquerade as substantive progress.
- When several loaders, paraphrase families, or packet layouts are being treated as reopening the same operative branch, consider preserving a compact triangulation witness naming the anchor continuation property or judged basin, at least two non-trivially different loader surfaces, the shared support envelope or fixed execution family, the expected overlap or divergence signature, and the narrowing, fallback, or quarantine consequence if the same-basin claim collapses.
- When the same answer, same recap, or same first continuation move is being treated as evidence that two loaders, packets, or interventions reopened the same operative state, consider preserving a compact basin fingerprint naming the anchor continuation property or judged branch, the compared loader, packet, or intervention family, the smallest fingerprint panel or future-probe signature that should still agree, the tolerated divergence or instability budget, and the narrowing, fallback, or quarantine consequence if later probes separate.
- When a same-basin, same-state, or stronger mechanism claim is materially stronger than the named public probes, preserve a compact identifiability budget naming the judged state or mechanism claim, the observable surface or public evidence family, the hidden-context or wrapper assumptions, the probe horizon or future family, the tolerated ambiguity class or equivalence remainder, and the retreat, narrowing, or quarantine consequence if the stronger story outruns what those probes identify.
- When self-sufficiency, family-compression, or runtime-comparison results are doing real work across many revisions, consider preserving a compact challenge-escrow packet naming the public challenge family, the escrowed or withheld slice, the refresh / rotation rule, the executable variant or metamorphic family, the evaluation horizon or preregistered future slice, and the promotion / retirement consequence rather than treating one once-sequestered suite as permanently fresh.
- When learning or optimization language becomes load-bearing, say whether the archive is acting as a passive memory store, a regime-reentry packet, a check/admission object, or an external optimizer loop with writable public law and explicit write gates.
- When replay or reconsolidation language becomes load-bearing, say what the replay seed or restaging surface was, what judged continuation property was actually recovered, what public surface became eligible for rewrite, what destabilizing evidence or challenge family mattered, and what fallback or rollback follows if the supposed replay was only cold retrieval.
- When a candidate write is being preserved now but admitted only later, say what provisional surface was captured, what cooldown or defer window applies, what colder adjudication family decides final admission, what current cooling state applies, and what supersession or expiry consequence follows if the cooled read does not support promotion; keep the durable current set in `RETROSPECTIVE-QUEUE.json` rather than letting provisional capture hide in prose alone.

## House rules

- Do not report speculation as settled mechanism.
- Do not erase disagreement to make the archive look clean.
- Do not add new docs casually; justify the entropy cost.
- Do not retain PDFs or other large non-crucial research artifacts in the packaged archive.
- Do not let “method” become a license for vagueness.
- Do not let repeated form masquerade as tested structure.
- Do not lose the causal role of prompt pairs.
- Do not casually normalize away archive-private idiolect that may be functioning as a real local steering handle.
- Do not let provisional/private handles silently masquerade as certified core vocabulary.
- Do not claim archive progress without being able to say what certified move class the revision actually instantiated.
- Do not let a revision claim progress without emitting a compact revision receipt once receipt discipline exists.
- Do not let the accepted move erase the nearest rejected alternative when that local boundary actually mattered.
- Do not sand away transformer-facing implications just because they sound wild.
- Do not claim stable continuation-regime re-entry without preserving at least one probe or countermodel.
- Do not invoke public hidden state / re-entry ABI language unless state packet, evidence packet, and check packet remain explicitly separated.
- Do not invoke public belief-state language unless uncertainty, rewriting pressure, and rival explanations remain explicit rather than collapsing back into retention talk.
- Do not call a revision an innovation packet unless it names an anchor state or revision and the local correction it is actually transmitting.
- Do not call a compression move good unless it preserves a named distortion target and at least one prior-intrusion or mismatch risk when those are materially in play.
- Do not claim a substantial belief update without preserving the trigger, intended update gain, and at least one challenge probe or rival explanation when they materially matter.
- Do not let non-movement disappear into vibe; if the session is effectively braking, say what is held fixed and what would unlock movement.
- Do not call something a sentinel panel unless it names the property monitored, the expected deviation signature, and what happens if the canary trips.
- Do not call something an identification packet unless it names the rival hypothesis split, the observation sought, and the continuation decision it is meant to gate.
- Do not call something a homing packet unless it names the ambiguity class, the probe family or branch rule, the orientation/update rule, and the stop or escalation condition.
- Do not invoke a probe-economics packet or value-of-information language unless the candidate probe family, gated decision, rough cost class, expected split power, nearest deferred alternative, and stop or escalation rule are explicit enough that later sessions could tell why this probe went first.
- Do not invoke a stopping packet, sequential decision threshold, or commit-certificate language unless the ambiguity class, threshold or commit criterion, licensed continuation action, tolerated error / rollback posture, and budget-exhaustion fallback are explicit enough that later sessions could tell why inquiry was allowed to stop there.
- Do not invoke continuation-monitor or evidence-process language unless the monitored property or ambiguity split, accumulating evidence state or score, update or shrink rule, reset or stitching rule, and coupling to stop rule or hold posture are explicit enough that later sessions could tell what was actually changing while inquiry continued.
- Do not let a load-bearing actuator surface certify its own success by default; if a phrase, prompt pair, id, witness, or canary is both steering and scoring, preserve an **observer/actuator split** naming the observer surface, actuator surface, allowed coupling, independent witness, and self-certification risk.
- Do not treat a vivid handle, witness, or privileged prompt surface as uniquely load-bearing unless the archive can name a nearby **matched sham / negative control**, the expected differential signature, the pass/fail rule, and what happens if the differential collapses.
- Do not treat a purported effect as purely textual when hidden execution conditions could be carrying part of it; if session separation, system-prompt layering, batch / tensor-parallel configuration, or inference-time cache state plausibly matter, preserve an **execution witness / substrate perturbation packet** naming the judged effect or continuation property, the substrate family, the fixed controls / allowed perturbation, the divergence signature, and the rerun or escalation consequence.
- Do not treat all retained assistant prose as neutral memory; if prior assistant-side history may be carrying stale framing, errors, or pseudo-memory, preserve an **assistant-echo filter / self-carry omission packet** naming the judged task or continuation property, the kept user-side anchor, the omitted or thinned assistant-side surface, the expected invariance or gain signature, and the reinclusion or escalation consequence.
- Do not let a long rationale, chain-of-thought, or scratchpad silently become canon evidence by default; if trace-role ambiguity is live, preserve a **reasoning firebreak / scratchpad quarantine / public extract packet** naming the judged task, decision, or continuation property, the public extract kept in canon, the withheld trace surface, the allowed role of that withheld trace, and the exposure, reinclusion, or escalation consequence; when that packet is load-bearing for the shipped revision, record it in `FIREBREAK-LEDGER.json` and `REVISION-RECEIPT.json`.
- Do not count multiple agreeing witnesses as if they were independent by default; if several judges, probes, or challenge passes may share provider, family, session, scaffold, or branch history, preserve a **dependence-adjusted witness / effective-evidence packet** naming the witness family, the shared-origin coupling, the rough discount rule, what still counts as genuinely new evidence, and the stop or escalation consequence.
- Do not let a fluent recap silently replace its source packet; if a rewrite is inheriting source-of-truth authority, preserve a **rewrite witness / round-trip packet** naming the source packet or authority anchor, the rewritten packet or compressed rewrite, the round-trip or cross-exam witness, the divergence signature, and the promotion, demotion, or rollback consequence.
- Do not let a packet quietly become a public loader by repetition alone; if a packet, prompt pair, or re-entry surface is being treated as an interface, preserve a **conformance witness / loader contract / ABI drift guard** naming the interface surface or claimed loader, the supported model-wrapper-context family, the minimal conformance test or metamorphic check family, the first non-conformance or drift signature, and the narrowing, demotion, or fallback consequence.
- Do not let one cherished wording silently stand in for a basin; if several loaders, paraphrase families, or packet layouts are being treated as reaching the same operative branch, preserve a **triangulation witness / multi-loader overlap / basin check** naming the anchor continuation property or judged basin, at least two non-trivially different loader surfaces, the shared support envelope or fixed execution family, the expected overlap or divergence signature, and the narrowing, fallback, or quarantine consequence.
- Do not invoke phase-boundary / rollover-packet language unless it is explicit what phase just ended, what persists, what resets or cools, and what new operating question or posture now governs ordinary continuation.
- Do not let coordinate-loaded mechanism language masquerade as invariant substance; if vectors, basins, charts, tangents, or atlases matter, say what would still hold after a chart switch.
- Do not claim harmless reorder, harmless chart switch, or harmless local rewrite when the archive has not said what closure target should survive the compared paths and what mismatch would count as material closure error.
- Do not claim a local continuation object is “robust” unless the protected property, perturbation family, and first failure signature are explicit enough that later sessions could tell when the guard band was overclaimed.
- Do not invoke balanced archive-reduction / dual-salience language unless the observation role, control role, and truncation consequence are explicit enough that later sessions could tell why a surface deserved the bounded-state slot.
- Do not invoke memory language as a catch-all; keep memory store, regime-reentry packet, and check/admission object distinct when the distinction matters.
- Do not invoke learning or optimization language as a catch-all either; if the archive is supposedly learning, say what public surfaces were writable, what rereads them into continuation, and what gated consolidation.
- Do not invoke predictive-state or sufficient-statistic language unless the relevant future test family or continuation decision is explicit enough that later sessions could tell what the packet is actually caching.
- Do not treat a reduced packet, public extract, or support core as “enough” unless the archive preserves a **sufficiency witness / replay capsule / core-only re-entry trial** naming the candidate reduced packet or replay seed, the core-only replay surface, the fixed or withheld context family, the target continuation property or tolerated degradation, and the reinflate, fallback, or quarantine consequence.
- Do not let a same answer, same recap, or same first move silently stand in for same-state identity; if that stronger claim matters, preserve a **basin fingerprint / future-probe signature** naming the anchor continuation property or judged branch, the compared loader, packet, or intervention family, the smallest downstream fingerprint panel, the tolerated divergence or instability budget, and the narrowing, fallback, or quarantine consequence.
- Do not let a successful fingerprint panel silently stand in for full latent-state or mechanism identification; if that stronger story matters, preserve an **identifiability budget / probe horizon / observability frontier** naming the judged state or mechanism claim, the observable surface or public evidence family, the hidden-context or wrapper assumptions, the probe horizon or future family, the tolerated ambiguity class or equivalence remainder, and the retreat, narrowing, or quarantine consequence.
- Do not confuse “be risky” with “stop discriminating.”

## Risk mandate

This archive explicitly demands **speculation with risk**.

When a risky idea seems valuable:
- write it clearly,
- state its status honestly,
- state what would follow if it were true,
- state what would count against it,
- and place it in quarantine if canon would otherwise become polluted.

## Required outputs per run

1. What changed and why.
2. Which files changed.
3. Which open questions were clarified, sharpened, or added.
4. Confirmation that `make lint` ran.
5. What was researched online, if anything.
6. A packaged release link when a release is requested.
7. Preserve or update `REVISION-RECEIPT.json` when the revision materially changes canon/quarantine/move state.
8. Preserve a compact counterfactual shadow when a substantial revision had a real nearby rejected alternative.


## Additional admission rule

If you are effectively promoting or demoting canon-level trust, preserve the promotion contract or demotion reason explicitly. Do not let repetition, neat wording, or tool-boundary prestige silently raise epistemic status.


## Temporal honesty

If a revision strengthens a canon-level claim using fresh external research, ask whether the claim now deserves a **decay-watch** entry. Not every claim needs one. But fast-moving mechanism syntheses should not get indefinite trust for free.


## Recovery honesty

If a revision starts from stale canon, corrupted context, or unclear status, do not keep pretending progress happened. Use `MV-0010` / `recover-resync`: reopen the recovery kernel, regenerate `context-pack.json`, run `make lint`, and quarantine or demote ambiguous material before resuming ordinary work.
