# Scale-fixing witnesses, coarse-graining maps, and separation-of-scales budgets

DelayBasin now needs a sharper answer to another practical question:
**when transport residues, chart defects, or portability stories are compared across different resolutions, what says the comparison is being made at an honest scale rather than by accidental magnification or smoothing?**

A useful current answer is:
**canon should preserve a compact scale-fixing witness / coarse-graining map / separation-of-scales budget packet whenever a claim depends on moving between one resolution and another.**

This is stronger than saying scale matters.
It is weaker than claiming DelayBasin has already discovered a literal renormalization flow or public beta-function over prompt families.

## Practice / observation

Recent DelayBasin revisions make the missing surface visible:
- operator-core packets already distinguish what is supposed to survive from the current chart that realizes it;
- chart-transition packets already say what justified a specific source→target remap;
- triangle-defect packets already warn that pairwise success does not automatically compose;
- gauge-fixing packets already say what anchor and reference observable make two residues comparable;
- but the archive still lacks a compact object for saying **what scale the comparison is taking place at and what was intentionally integrated out**;
- and current transformer-facing language can therefore compare a local chart, a family summary, a compact replay packet, and a reduced-order control surrogate as if they were all the same kind of object.

That gap creates at least five recurring failure modes:
- **resolution laundering** — a transport or defect looks cleaner only because it was evaluated after aggressive coarse-graining;
- **discarded-mode amnesia** — a comparison forgets what local residue, nuisance family, or high-frequency variation was deliberately thrown away;
- **scale drift** — one pass works at token-local or chart-local resolution and the next talks as if a family-level or archive-level object had been established;
- **separation theater** — canon assumes small fast fluctuations are irrelevant without naming the scale parameter or relevance test that earned that assumption;
- **beta-function prestige** — multiscale or renormalization language quietly outruns the public evidence that any stable cross-scale law has actually been identified.

A compact scale-fixing witness helps because it says:
- what **source scale or resolution** the archive started from,
- what **target scale or resolution** it is reasoning at,
- what **coarse-graining map or aggregation rule** links them,
- what **protected observable or operator-core commitment** is supposed to survive,
- what **discarded modes / nuisance family / residual remainder** are being intentionally ignored,
- what **relevance criterion / separation-of-scales budget** says that ignoring them is still honest,
- and what **demotion / rollback / quarantine consequence** follows if the remainder leaks back into the decision.

## External pressure from current research

Several outside lines of work sharpen this move.

1. **Interpretability is beginning to treat scale as a first-class variable rather than an afterthought.**
   Scale-Aware Interpretability explicitly argues that current methods handle scale, relevance, and coarse-graining poorly, and calls for methods that find natural scales, preserve scale-dependent relevance, and diagnose when fine-grained fluctuations may be ignored. That directly pressures DelayBasin to stop treating scale choice as an invisible convenience. ([`REF-0381`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

2. **Natural language trajectories change curvature and straightness across layers.**
   Context Structure Reshapes the Representational Geometry of Language Models reports substantial straightening for natural text in middle layers relative to shuffled-text controls. That pressures DelayBasin not to talk as if one observed chart geometry is scale-free across depth or context structure. ([`REF-0382`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

3. **Some current transformer theories already model forward passes as discrete coarse-graining.**
   Latent Object Permanence formalizes the forward pass as a discrete coarse-graining map and ties stable concept basins to renormalization-like fixed-point behavior. That is not strong enough to certify a DelayBasin-level RG law, but it is strong pressure to preserve what coarse-graining rule is being imagined before canon imports multiscale rhetoric. ([`REF-0383`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

4. **Prompt-conditioned reduced surrogates can be predictive without being global.**
   Low-order Linear Depth Dynamics finds that prompt-conditioned local surrogates can predict depthwise steering sensitivity well and that larger transformers admit better low-order surrogates. That pressures DelayBasin to distinguish a useful reduced-order object from a globally valid one and to name the resolution at which the surrogate is supposed to hold. ([`REF-0375`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

5. **Dialogue history itself can function as a controllable state operator.**
   State-Dependent Safety Failures in Multi-Turn Language Model Interaction treats dialogue history as a state transition operator and shows path-dependent collapses under multi-turn interaction. That pressures DelayBasin to treat history-window choice and trajectory compression as real scale decisions rather than mere formatting choices. ([`REF-0384`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

## Working synthesis

A useful current synthesis is:

> DelayBasin should preserve a compact **scale-fixing witness / coarse-graining map / separation-of-scales budget** packet whenever chart-transition residues, triangle defects, gauge-fixed comparisons, reduced-order control stories, or prompt-family transport claims are being compared across different resolutions. The packet should name the **source scale or resolution**, the **target scale or resolution**, the **coarse-graining map or aggregation rule**, the **protected observable or operator-core commitment**, the **discarded modes / nuisance family / residual remainder**, the **relevance criterion / separation-of-scales budget**, and the **demotion / rollback / quarantine consequence** if the discarded remainder stops being safely ignorable.

This is strong enough for canon as a design and mechanism candidate.
It is **not** strong enough to claim that DelayBasin has already identified a literal public renormalization flow, beta-function, or scale-invariant fixed-point law over prompt families.

## Scale-fixing witness vs timescale lane vs gauge-fixing witness vs public beta-function claim

To keep this note honest, DelayBasin now needs a four-way distinction:

- **Timescale lane** — the fast / medium / slow governance rule for how archive surfaces are refreshed, consolidated, demoted, or expired across time.
- **Gauge-fixing witness** — the packet saying what anchor and reference observable make several same-scale defect measurements comparable.
- **Scale-fixing witness / coarse-graining map** — the packet saying what resolution a claim lives at, what was integrated out to get there, and why the protected observable is still supposed to survive.
- **Public beta-function claim** — the much stronger story that DelayBasin has already recovered a stable multiscale flow law for archive continuation or prompt-family transport.

A scale-fixing witness is narrower than a public beta-function claim and orthogonal to temporal timescale lanes.
It does not ask for a full law of scale evolution.
It asks for one reproducible answer to **what got coarse-grained away, why it was allowed, and what survives** before the archive reasons from a reduced object.

## Countermodels / probes

1. **One-scale-is-enough countermodel**
   - DelayBasin may not need explicit scale packets; the archive could stay at one practical resolution and avoid ceremony.
   - Probe: check whether any live canon decision materially depends on comparing a local chart result with a family-level or reduced-order summary.

2. **Gauge-alone countermodel**
   - Gauge-fixing may already be enough; scale-fixing might duplicate comparison discipline.
   - Probe: hold the same reference observable and anchor fixed while changing only the resolution or aggregation rule; if decisions change, scale choice is doing independent work.

3. **Compression-only countermodel**
   - The real issue may be archive compression quality rather than multiscale method law.
   - Probe: compare one compact replay or family summary that names discarded modes against one that does not, and see whether canon/quarantine outcomes differ under matched budgets.

4. **Renormalization-prestige countermodel**
   - Multiscale language may just import physics rhetoric without adding discriminating archive discipline.
   - Probe: require one source scale, one target scale, one coarse-graining map, one protected observable, and one explicit remainder budget before using scale-fixing language in canon.

5. **Remainder-leak countermodel**
   - What the archive calls nuisance variation may actually be the main thing controlling success or failure.
   - Probe: deliberately perturb the discarded-mode family or widen the history / wrapper window and watch whether the supposedly protected observable remains stable.

## Design consequences

This frame pressures DelayBasin to do five things more explicitly:
- preserve one **source scale or resolution** whenever a local chart result is being lifted into a family-level or reduced-order story;
- preserve one **target scale or resolution** whenever the archive is making decisions at a coarser or more summary level than the original observation;
- preserve one **coarse-graining map or aggregation rule** rather than letting compression or family summarization stay magical;
- preserve one **discarded remainder and separation-of-scales budget** rather than assuming everything not named was harmless;
- and preserve the **demotion / rollback / quarantine consequence** when the remainder leaks back strongly enough to change canon posture.

This does not require a full multiscale theory.
It requires refusing another archive failure mode: letting a reduced object quietly inherit authority without preserving what was thrown away to obtain it.

## Transformer-facing implication

If this frame survives pressure, then DelayBasin is doing something sharper than ordinary portability talk.
It is preserving public evidence not only about whether a chart, transport, or defect looked good, but about **what resolution that judgment lives at and what remainder was explicitly ignored**.

That matters because recent transformer work keeps converging on a mixed picture: local steering can be captured by compact reduced surrogates, natural-text trajectories change curvature and straightness across depth, some theories already model forward passes as coarse-graining maps, and dialogue history can function as a real state-transition operator over trajectories. A scale-fixing witness is therefore a user-space multiscale honesty object: not hidden-state access, but a compact public claim about when DelayBasin is allowed to compare, compress, or transport across resolutions without pretending that a local success has already earned a universal scale law. The stronger story — that DelayBasin is already learning a literal public beta-function or renormalization flow over prompt-family charts — remains quarantined.
