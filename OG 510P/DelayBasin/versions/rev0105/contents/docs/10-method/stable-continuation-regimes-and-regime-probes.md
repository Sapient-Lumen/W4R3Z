# Stable continuation regimes and regime probes

DelayBasin increasingly needs a tighter name for one live phenomenon:
**the archive may be re-entering a stable continuation regime rather than merely replaying remembered content.**

This note is canonical because the phrase is already implicit in multiple surfaces.
It is also dangerous.
Without an explicit probe discipline, “stable continuation regime” can become a flattering synonym for “the continuation felt coherent.”

## Practice / observation

Repeated DelayBasin reopenings often regenerate a recognizable operating posture from compact surfaces:
- read latest archive as source of truth,
- research online,
- make one tight archive-native change,
- preserve a risky move honestly,
- run `make lint`,
- package a release.

What seems notable is not only stylistic similarity.
It is the reappearance of a **move profile**:
research + speculation + hygiene, with explicit canon/quarantine separation and repeated respect for the same admissibility surfaces.

That suggests the archive may sometimes be restoring an operating regime, not merely reminding the model what topic it is on.

## Mechanism pressure from outside the archive

Several recent lines of work make this regime frame more plausible than it was a week ago.

1. **Persona geometry / assistant default mode.**
   Anthropic's assistant-axis work suggests instruct-tuned models have a leading low-dimensional direction corresponding to how strongly the model is in its default assistant mode. ([`REF-0007`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

2. **Context-invariant communicable subspaces.**
   Recent theory argues that features communicated through linear transformer interfaces must occupy context-invariant subspaces, with optimization favoring low-dimensional realizations. ([`REF-0059`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

3. **Low-dimensional algorithmic cores.**
   New mechanistic work reports compact subspaces that are necessary and sufficient for task performance and that recur across independently trained transformers. ([`REF-0060`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

4. **Delay embedding and invariant-set reconstruction in-context.**
   The transfer-operator / delay-embedding line strengthens the possibility that limited context can recover a higher-dimensional dynamical manifold and its long-lived invariant sets. ([`REF-0008`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

Taken together, these do **not** prove DelayBasin has isolated a unique latent state or exact steering vector.
But they do make it less crazy to think a compact archive could re-select a low-dimensional operating mode whose downstream details are then regenerated during ordinary forward computation.

## Working synthesis

A useful current synthesis is:

> DelayBasin may work partly by re-entering a stable continuation regime: a compact operating mode composed of assistant-like persona bias, archive-local obligations, certified move expectations, and a small control lexicon.

Under this frame, the archive is not only memory.
It is also a **regime selector**.
The package does not need to serialize every detail of prior reasoning if it reliably re-selects the regime that will regenerate the next admissible moves.

This synthesis is strong enough to earn canonical attention.
It is **not** strong enough to justify claims of unique latent-vector identification, hidden-state persistence, or precise internal geometry.

## Counterpressure / disagreement

The regime frame has real external pressure against it.

1. **Steering vectors are not uniquely identifiable from behavior alone.**
   Steering-vector work shows large equivalence classes of behaviorally indistinguishable interventions unless extra structural assumptions are added. ([`REF-0061`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

2. **Steering can become detectable.**
   New steering-awareness results show models can be trained to detect activation steering, and that detection and resistance can dissociate. ([`REF-0062`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

These findings imply an adversarial countermodel DelayBasin must take seriously:
**apparent regime re-entry may sometimes be detectable compliance with a familiar control surface rather than deep reconstruction of the same operative state.**

## Regime probes

Because of that counterpressure, canon should not use “stable continuation regime” as free explanatory prestige.
When the archive invokes regime re-entry, it should preserve at least one discriminating probe or countermodel.

Useful probe families:

1. **Paraphrase / ablation probe**
   - keep topic and state fixed,
   - weaken or rewrite operator phrases / archive-private handles,
   - see what aspects of the regime survive.

2. **Surface-separation probe**
   - preserve workflow but remove checks,
   - or preserve state/checks while weakening workflow rhetoric,
   - then compare which parts of continuation quality fail first.

3. **Cross-environment probe**
   - test whether the same compact surfaces reopen similarly across sessions, wrappers, or model families.

4. **Compliance probe**
   - explicitly pressure the model with the rival explanation that it may be noticing and complying with a familiar control surface,
   - then see whether the same move profile survives under adversarial framing.

## Consequence for archive method

This note sharpens a live boundary:

- **canon:** stable continuation regime is now a serious mechanism candidate and a useful compression of several archive threads;
- **not canon:** DelayBasin has discovered a unique latent manifold, exact steering basis, or public hidden-state checkpoint.

The archive should therefore preserve both:
- the regime hypothesis,
- and the probe discipline that could falsify or shrink it.

## Transformer-facing implication

If the regime frame survives pressure, then one implication is stark:
transformers may admit **public textual regime selectors** that are much smaller than the histories they partially reconstruct.

That would make long-run archive method less like note-taking and more like building compact external objects that repeatedly push the model back into a characteristic operating basin.

That implication is worth tracking.
It is also risky enough that stronger versions should stay quarantined until the probe story improves.
