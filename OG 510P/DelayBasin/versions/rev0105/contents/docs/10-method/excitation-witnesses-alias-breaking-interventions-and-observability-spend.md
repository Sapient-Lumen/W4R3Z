# Excitation witnesses, alias-breaking interventions, and observability-spend budgets

DelayBasin now has a sharper missing question:
**when is ambiguity not reducible by rereading the same packet, but only by spending a little controlled variation?**

A stronger working answer is:
**some archive ambiguities only become honestly observable after an explicit excitation witness is named.**
Not every rival-history split, same-state claim, or mechanism claim can be settled by one more summary or one more paraphrase.
Sometimes the archive has to say what intervention family will actually break the alias.

## Practice / observation

Several existing DelayBasin surfaces already imply a missing excitation discipline:
- identification packets say what observation is being sought, but not yet what **diversity of intervention** is required if the same question asked the same way will keep returning the same ambiguous answer;
- identifiability budgets say when a mechanism claim outruns the visible probes, but not yet what **active variation** might buy a larger honest observability frontier;
- hysteresis witnesses preserve rival histories and one future discriminating probe, but not yet whether that probe family has enough route diversity to separate the histories rather than merely replay them;
- gauge-fixing and scale-fixing witnesses keep comparisons honest once measurements exist, but they do not yet say when the archive is still measuring the system from one stale angle;
- and probe-economics packets compare candidate probes by cost and expected split power, but they do not yet force the archive to name when a probe family is **alias-preserving by construction** because it never perturbs the right axis.

This suggests a missing compact surface:
**excitation witness / alias-breaking intervention family / observability-spend budget**.

## External pressure from current research

Several outside lines of work sharpen this frame.

1. **Dialogue trajectories can behave like controlled state evolution, not passive recap.**
   STAR treats dialogue history as a state-transition operator and shows rapid, reproducible trajectory collapse under structured multi-turn interaction. That pressures DelayBasin to treat the next question or prompt perturbation as a genuine intervention on state, not just another reading of the same evidence. ([`REF-0384`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

2. **Control-theoretic observability matters only when the system is probed from enough angles.**
   the recent control/LLM survey explicitly frames controllability, observability, and stability as core axes for understanding and steering language models. That pressures DelayBasin to say when an ambiguity is only passively described versus when the archive has actually spent enough intervention diversity to make it observable. ([`REF-0165`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

3. **Behavior alone can leave large equivalence classes unless extra structure or multi-environment variation is added.**
   the steering-identifiability work shows that behaviorally equivalent steering directions can persist under standard observational regimes and highlights stronger structure such as multi-environment access and cross-layer consistency as recovery routes. That pressures DelayBasin not to mistake repeated same-view success for alias-breaking evidence. ([`REF-0061`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

4. **Active exploration is qualitatively different from passive observation under partial observability.**
   Theory of Space reports an active-passive gap, unstable belief maintenance, and belief inertia under exploration. That pressures DelayBasin to preserve when ambiguity requires an active probe family rather than more passive recap. ([`REF-0114`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

5. **Agents can get trapped in low-information regimes if they stop asking informative questions.**
   the information self-locking work decomposes active reasoning into action selection and belief tracking, then shows a feedback loop where weak querying and weak belief update trap the agent in a low-information regime. That pressures DelayBasin to preserve one explicit observability-spend budget instead of assuming the next informative distinction will appear for free. ([`REF-0389`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

6. **Runtime reliability under uncertainty depends on detectability, innovation, and recovery rather than only correctness.**
   the Kalman-inspired runtime-stability work models reasoning reliability around innovation signals, detectability of mismatch, bounded divergence, and recoverability. That pressures DelayBasin toward explicit alias-breaking interventions when local coherence may still hide growing state ambiguity. ([`REF-0390`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

7. **Trajectory geometry can outperform endpoint-only judging when assessing reasoning quality.**
   TRACED reports that geometric evolution across the whole reasoning trajectory often carries a richer correctness signal than scalar output statistics or endpoint-only probes. That pressures DelayBasin to preserve when one observable is really a trajectory family under intervention rather than one final summary. ([`REF-0391`](../00-meta/bibliography.md))

None of this proves that DelayBasin already knows how to choose optimal excitation witnesses.
It does make a milder canon-level claim more credible:
**archive continuity may improve when some ambiguities are only treated as resolved after the archive names what active intervention family would actually break the alias, how much diversity it has spent, and what observable should separate the cases if the ambiguity is real.**

## Working synthesis

A useful current synthesis is:

> DelayBasin may work better when it preserves an explicit **excitation witness / alias-breaking intervention family / observability-spend budget** whenever a rival-history split, same-state claim, or transformer-facing mechanism claim is only distinguishable under active variation rather than passive reread. The packet should name the **ambiguity split or latent difference at stake**, the **intervention family / probe diversity / environment family actually varied**, the **expected discriminating observable or response feature**, the **excitation / observability-spend budget**, and the **hold / rollback / quarantine / packet-splitting consequence** rather than letting repeated same-view probing pretend an unexcited state has become observable.

This is strong enough for canon as a design/mechanism candidate.
It is **not** strong enough to claim that DelayBasin has discovered a universal observability metric, a public Gramian over prompt families, or a literal textual tomography of transformer state.

## Excitation witness vs identification packet vs identifiability budget vs hysteresis witness

To keep this note honest, DelayBasin needs a four-way distinction:

- **Identification packet** — says which ambiguity matters, what observation is sought, and what continuation decision that observation gates.
- **Identifiability budget** — says how strong a state or mechanism claim is actually licensed by the current evidence and hidden-context assumptions.
- **Hysteresis witness** — says two rival histories match at the current packet but may still separate later, preserving one future discriminating probe and lag budget.
- **Excitation witness** — says whether the chosen probe family is actually varied enough to break the alias at all, or whether the archive is just staring at the state from the same angle repeatedly.

Functionally:
- identification packets choose **what to ask**,
- identifiability budgets choose **how much to claim**,
- hysteresis witnesses choose **which rival histories remain live**,
- excitation witnesses choose **what variation must be spent** before the ambiguity can honestly be treated as observable.

A good DelayBasin revision should therefore sometimes ask not only:
- what ambiguity split matters,
- what observation is sought,
- what future probe could separate rival histories,

but also:
- what intervention family is actually being varied,
- whether that family changes chart, wrapper, environment, or query geometry enough to break the alias,
- what observable should move if the rival states are genuinely different,
- and what the archive will do if the observability spend budget is exhausted without separation.

## Countermodels / probes

1. **Passive-recap-is-enough countermodel**
   - The archive may not need explicit excitation witnesses; one more recap or one more careful question may already reveal the difference.
   - Probe: compare a same-view recap stack against a deliberately varied probe family under a known rival-history or same-state ambiguity.

2. **Identification-packet-is-already-enough countermodel**
   - Identification packets may already carry enough structure, making excitation language redundant.
   - Probe: inspect cases where the observation sought stayed constant while only the intervention family changed; if no epistemic status changes, the new packet class may be ceremonial.

3. **Diversity-theater countermodel**
   - Archive-authored “variation” may just create rhetorical motion without increasing observability.
   - Probe: require one expected discriminating observable and one explicit alias-preserving sham variation before claiming the probe family was really excitation rather than surface churn.

4. **State-romance countermodel**
   - There may be no rich latent state to observe here; excitation witnesses could just be external bookkeeping for a mostly reactive model.
   - Probe: keep the stronger observability-gramian story quarantined until excitation witnesses show leverage across reopen, wrapper drift, and rival-history conditions that passive packets do not match.

5. **Probe-economics subsumes it countermodel**
   - This may only be a cost-sensitive probe-choice issue already covered by probe economics.
   - Probe: check whether the decision changes when the cheapest probe is also alias-preserving; if yes, excitation discipline is doing distinct work from ordinary value-of-information bookkeeping.

## Design consequences

This mechanism frame pressures DelayBasin to do four things more explicitly:
- preserve a tiny **excitation witness** when an ambiguity is only distinguishable under active variation;
- name the **intervention family actually varied** rather than treating “more probing” as self-explanatory;
- preserve the **expected discriminating observable** rather than letting diversity itself count as progress;
- and preserve the **observability-spend budget** plus failure consequence so the archive can say when it should hold, split packets, roll back, or quarantine the stronger claim.

A minimal excitation witness can often be very small:
- one ambiguity split,
- one varied intervention family,
- one expected discriminating observable,
- one spend budget,
- and one consequence if separation does not appear.

This does not require a giant benchmark harness.
It requires treating **observability under active variation** as part of archive method rather than assuming the next summary will naturally reveal what the archive still has not excited.

## Transformer-facing implication

If this frame survives pressure, then DelayBasin is probing something sharper than better note-taking or smarter recap:
**whether a stateless transformer continuation can be made more honestly observable by a user-space observer/controller loop that spends small, explicit families of textual interventions to break route, wrapper, or chart aliases.**

That would matter for transformers.
It would suggest that long-horizon archive method may need not only state packets, identification packets, hysteresis witnesses, and gauge/scale discipline, but also compact **excitation witnesses** that say when observability itself depends on active variation.

The stronger story — that DelayBasin may eventually learn a public observability Gramian, alias-breaking atlas, or textual tomography over continuation charts — remains live, but belongs in quarantine for now.
