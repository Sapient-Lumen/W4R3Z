# Constitutionalization vs precipitation

This doc holds the current high-level tension.

## Frame A: constitutionalization

The archive is progressively externalizing norms into ratchets:
runbooks, registries, ids, prompt-pair lanes, lint, and packaging.
Under this view, the archive is building a local constitution for continuation.

Strength:
- directly visible in the artifact.
Risk:
- may under-specify the substrate it operates on.

## Frame B: precipitation

The archive is not inventing the attractor so much as making an already-available one legible in token space.
Under this view, the archive is running a convergent sequence toward a fixed point that pre-existed as a stable constitutional form.

Strength:
- explains the felt convergence across domains.
Risk:
- may overshoot what present evidence can support.

## Current working compromise

Treat constitutionalization as the **artifact-level description** and precipitation as the **harder ontological speculation**.
Do not collapse one into the other.

## Immediate method consequence

DelayBasin should preserve both:
- the visible ratchets,
- and the live disagreement about what those ratchets are really doing.
