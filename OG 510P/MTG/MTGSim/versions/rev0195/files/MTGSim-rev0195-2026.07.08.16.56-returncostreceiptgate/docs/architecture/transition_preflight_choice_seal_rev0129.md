# rev0129 — Transition Preflight Choice Seal

rev0129 audits the seam before the staged reducer mutates a copy of `GameState`. Earlier revisions gave `TransitionResult` a boundary seal, checkpoint seals, causal receipt hashes, and diagnostic verifier failure kinds, but the before-mutation choice/validation surface was still only part of the larger boundary hash. That made it harder for replay, search, fuzz, and agent callers to distinguish stale preflight evidence from later checkpoint or receipt drift.

The refactor adds `kTransitionPreflightSealSchemaVersion`, `TransitionResult::transition_preflight_schema_version`, `TransitionResult::transition_preflight_hash`, public `transition_result_preflight_hash(...)`, `has_transition_preflight_seal()`, and `committed_with_preflight_choice_seal()`. The internal `seal_transition_result_preflight(...)` runs after `attach_choice_context(...)` for `NeedChoice`, and after `preflight_action_transition(...)` plus checked `LegalActionPageLocation` / `ChoiceQueueLocation` evidence for attempted commits, before any staged mutation can be adopted.

The preflight seal hashes only the proposal side of the transition: `StateCheckpointSeal checkpoint_before`, StateCore/journal/action-receipt/event-sequence before scalars, canonical selected `LegalAction` and `legal_action_hash`, local `ChoiceRequest`, APNAP `ChoiceRequestQueue`, `choice_queue_index`, `LegalActionValidation`, checked page/queue location proofs, proof checked/found sentinels, and `legal_before`. It deliberately excludes after-state fields, causal receipt identity, and staged adoption sentinels so the verifier can localize stale before-mutation evidence separately from post-action receipt or checkpoint failures.

`check_transition_result_boundary(...)` now reports `PreflightSealMissing` and `PreflightSealMismatch` after the outer `transition_boundary_hash` is current but before checkpoint and receipt checks run. The new `TransitionBoundaryVerifyResult` preflight hash echoes (`expected_transition_preflight_hash` and `observed_transition_preflight_hash`) give callers enough evidence to diagnose a resealed but stale choice/validation surface without replaying the reducer by hand.

`test_transition_result_preflight_seal_hashes_choice_surface` proves the three status surfaces: `NeedChoice` carries a pure choice seal, `Rejected` carries the attempted before-mutation validation seal without journal mutation, and `Committed` carries the seal through atomic staged adoption via `committed_with_preflight_choice_seal()`. The regression also proves missing and stale preflight seals are localized with stable diagnostic strings.

Compatibility: `ActionTrace.v16`, `StateCheckpointSeal.v2`, and `ReplayArtifactManifest.v2` remain unchanged. This is a transition-result API/audit strengthening, not a persisted trace-format bump.
