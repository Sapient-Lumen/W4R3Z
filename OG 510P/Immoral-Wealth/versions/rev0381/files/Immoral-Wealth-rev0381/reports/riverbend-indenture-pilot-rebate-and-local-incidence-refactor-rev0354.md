## Audit/refactor

The AI/data-center proof-debt register now distinguishes private creditor/project-counterparty seniority from public hold-harmless proof. A Google/Hut 8 backstop, secured-note covenant, or private pass-through promise may protect a lender or project party without proving that ordinary ratepayers, West Feliciana taxpayers, school/sheriff tax recipients, or water users are protected.

The remaining proof is now executable rather than doctrinal: signed utility-service/tariff terms, switchyard/line cost assignment, ratebase/FAC/FRP treatment, West Feliciana water agreement and permits, PILOT/rebate implementation, LED exemption/Quality Jobs award and clawbacks, Google guarantee instrument, and actual load-versus-forecast evidence.
