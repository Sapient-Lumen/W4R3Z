# rev0355 — River Bend PILOT distribution and public-instrument ladder

## Summary

Rev0355 adds **S533-S537** and no cases or schema fields. The pass moves the River Bend/Hut 8 layer from headline project claims toward an auditable public-instrument ladder: utility capacity/timeline, IDB/PILOT leaseback revenue architecture, distribution schedule, rebate sequencing, and official parish public-record routing.

Current cube counts: **95 case memos / 95 scoreboards**, **537 sources**, **367 registered field keys**, **50 registered_unused fields**, and **6793 evidence edges**.

## What changed

- **S533** binds Entergy as the utility-side River Bend source for the 330 MW utility-capacity / 245 MW critical-IT-load claim, incentive participation, and Q2 2027 operations target.
- **S534** binds the local IDB/PILOT architecture: land donation/leaseback, annual valuation, 30-year agreement, and up-to-$90M/year public-revenue claim.
- **S535** binds the reported IDB distribution schedule so public-upside proof is no longer just a gross revenue number.
- **S536** binds the property-owner rebate and infrastructure-first sequencing caveat.
- **S537** binds the official parish meeting/public-record surface as the retrieval ladder for missing primary instruments.

## Audit/refactor result

The River Bend proof-debt track is now split into five executable instrument buckets:

1. utility service, switchyard/line cost assignment, tariff, and ratebase/FAC/FRP treatment;
2. IDB/PILOT leaseback, land transfer, valuation, and distribution resolution;
3. water construction, transfer, permits, drought/pressure/fire-flow safeguards;
4. tax exemption, Quality Jobs, Act 434 rebate, clawback, and public-upside implementation;
5. actual load versus forecast and operational reporting.

This avoids the previous overclaim risk: private secured-note covenants, a Google-backed lease, or gross PILOT estimates can support the project-risk map, but they do not prove public hold-harmless or public-upside delivery.
