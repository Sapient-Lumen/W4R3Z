Rev0353 stays substance-first. It does not add cases or schema. It adds the evidence needed to separate Louisiana statewide capacity stress, large-load process acceleration, River Bend private counterparty protections, public incentive exposure, and local water-service claims.

That should reduce future waste: private backstop specificity no longer counts as public/ratepayer protection unless the utility, water, and incentive instruments say so.
