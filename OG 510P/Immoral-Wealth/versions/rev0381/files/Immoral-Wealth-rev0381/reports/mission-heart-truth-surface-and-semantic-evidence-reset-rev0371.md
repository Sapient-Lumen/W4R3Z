# Mission heart, truth surface, and semantic evidence reset — rev0371

## Executive judgment

The heart of the mission is this:

> **Wealth should give every person durable ordinary independence, but should not give anyone durable governing power over other people, public institutions, or the future.**

That is stronger than “reduce the Gini,” “raise the bottom share,” or “tax billionaires.” The project is trying to identify an acceptable **wealth order**: one in which ordinary people possess usable and durable claims; work and citizenship are not subordinated to inheritance, landlord, creditor, employer, platform, or donor power; wealth circulates rather than hardens into dynasty; public institutions own enough capacity to govern rather than beg; and private prosperity does not quietly consume public, social, or ecological assets.

The archive’s distinctive insight is therefore **non-domination through balance-sheet design**. It connects private wealth distribution to claim security, control rights, public liabilities, administrative remedies, political power, inheritance, housing, debt, and public/common wealth. Its best phrase remains a **bounded-top, thick-middle, real-floor, open-circulation order**—but this must now be understood as a constitutional family, not a single share tuple.

The datacube should become a **public-interest institutional compiler**. Given a place, institution, or policy, it should produce a bounded diagnosis of dependence and governing power; trace the legal and financial mechanism; show who bears downside and receives upside; identify the nearest implementable corrective package; state contrary evidence and transition risks; and keep a recertification clock.

The archive is not there yet. Its moral architecture is better than its epistemic production system. It currently rewards opening surfaces, adding records, expanding validators, and incrementing revisions faster than it rewards independent review, contradiction, outcome measurement, or completion.

## What the mission actually requires

A useful case should answer five linked questions.

1. **Security:** What claim can an ordinary person use, keep, prove, and carry through a job loss, illness, move, separation, dispute, or death?
2. **Power:** What can an owner, creditor, employer, landlord, platform, donor, fund manager, or public official command, veto, delay, price, exclude, or hide?
3. **Incidence:** Who ultimately bears cost, risk, delay, administrative burden, ecological depletion, and transition loss; who receives appreciation, rents, subsidies, liquidity support, and rule-making leverage?
4. **Instrument:** Which operative law, contract, tariff, budget, ownership rule, remedy, or governance right changes the mechanism rather than merely compensating around it?
5. **Learning:** What observed outcome, contrary case, or failed prediction would reverse the diagnosis or retire the favored instrument?

This is why the archive’s proper causal chain is:

`harm / dependence -> mechanism -> operative instrument -> expected incidence -> observed outcome -> contrary evidence -> verdict -> recertification`

Most cases still contain the first two elements, a substantial catalogue of possible instruments, and a noncertification conclusion. The bridge through implementation and observed outcomes remains thin.

## What is missing

### 1. Independent review and real contradiction

The ledger has **171 locator-bound evidence records across 10 cases**, but no reviewer or reviewed-by metadata, no independently reviewed record, and **zero `contradicts` relationships**. It has 100 `supports`, 60 `qualifies`, 7 `method_only`, and 4 `context_only` records. This is a major confirmation-risk signal, not proof that every record is wrong.

At least **13 locators** are heuristically recognizable as derived, combined, or synthesis boundaries, and **22 bounded claims** use archive/cube/needs/requires/must language. Those can be valuable reasoning records, but they are not the same thing as a direct source observation. The ledger needs explicit record kinds, reviewer identity, review date, disagreement status, and source-target hash before “verified” can bear its ordinary meaning.

### 2. A completion rule

At the rev0370 input boundary, the archive still had **0 certified current cases**. Caution is appropriate, but a system that can only open proof debt and never finish a case has no calibrated definition of “enough.” It needs a minimum viable certification tier: one bounded question, one time horizon, an explicit perimeter, claim-level lineage, the strongest contrary evidence available, a reviewer, and a recertification trigger. Certification should not mean omniscience.

The next substantive goal should be to finish one case end to end—not to open an eleventh verified-edge perimeter. A smaller, honestly bounded certified product would teach more than another broad dossier.

### 3. Normative target provenance

The `20 / 50 / 20 / 10` center, `15-25 / 45-55 / 15-25 / 8-15` bands, and top-0.1-percent threshold are useful provocations. But WID and OECD sources describe observed distributions and measurement methods; they do not mechanically derive these exact moral thresholds. The archive has repeated its own proposal until it looks externally proven.

The target layer must distinguish four things:

- **normative proposal**—what the archive argues justice requires;
- **empirical benchmark**—what observed societies or institutions achieve;
- **measurement convention**—household or person, pensions, housing, trusts, offshore wealth, valuation date, and public wealth treatment;
- **policy scenario**—what a feasible instrument package may produce over time.

A target-provenance review should test rival ethical foundations, sensitivity to public-service provision and public wealth, and cases in which acceptable-looking shares coexist with landlord, employer, donor, media, or political domination.

### 4. Power and control as a first-class observatory

Asset value is not control. The observatory needs separate measures of voting rights, beneficial control, political finance, lobbying and procurement leverage, media/platform agenda power, workplace authority, landlord and creditor discretion, legal-remedy asymmetry, and credible exit threats. A society can have a respectable wealth-share table and still allow concentrated governing power.

### 5. Affected-person and administrative-friction evidence

The 583-source registry is heavily institutional. Official and multilateral evidence is essential, but the archive lacks a structured track for claimant journeys, denial and appeal time, proof burdens, worker/tenant/borrower experience, service interruptions, retaliation risk, and participatory review. Administrative-burden research distinguishes learning, compliance, and psychological costs; HHS guidance shows how lived experience can affect policy and program design. These are mechanism data, not decorative testimony.

### 6. Outcomes, calibration, and failed predictions

The archive is strong at current-law perimeter mapping and weak at post-implementation outcomes. For each instrument it should store the forecast, expected distributional incidence, time horizon, failure modes, and later result. It also needs a negative-results register: policies that underperformed, cases that contradicted the framework, and prior claims withdrawn after review. Without this, the cube cannot calibrate itself.

### 7. Public wealth as governance and ecological solvency

Positive public net worth is not enough. The archive must ask who controls public assets, who receives services or dividends, whether assets can be raided, whether public corporations and contingent liabilities are consolidated, and whether future generations have enforceable claims. IMF public-sector balance-sheet work supports a broad asset-and-liability view; World Bank comprehensive-wealth work makes clear that produced and financial wealth can rise while natural capital is depleted. Public and ecological solvency should be co-conditions, not footnotes.

### 8. Cross-border and historically constituted wealth

The portfolio remains thin on Indigenous and tribal land/sovereignty, colonial and reparative balance sheets, global supply-chain extraction, sovereign debt restructurings, farmland and food-system ownership, intellectual-property rents, and exported pollution or labor harm. A jurisdiction should not earn a good domestic verdict by exporting the costs of its wealth order.

### 9. Decision usefulness and users

The archive does not say who uses a case, what decision it informs, what question it answered, or whether the answer changed practice. Add product-level telemetry: intended user, decision deadline, decision changed, unresolved uncertainty, and later outcome. Otherwise internal completeness can substitute for public usefulness.

## Where something has gone severely wrong or become wasteful

### A. The all-green validator can certify a false operator surface — severe

Before this revision, the live JSON summary reported **8,005 mechanical associations and 171 locator-bound records**, while `MECHANICAL_EVIDENCE_SUMMARY.md` still reported 7,917 and 155. The claim backlog JSON said 139 records; its markdown said 123. The high-risk queue’s second item used an incompatible schema and rendered as:

`unnamed — tax-expenditure-hidden-public-balance-sheet-rev0319 — pending`

Nevertheless, `python tools/validate_archive.py` returned `PASSED: 0 errors, 0 warnings`.

This is the most important concrete defect found in the current cube. The validator checked that files and fields existed but did not check semantic parity across the surfaces an operator actually reads. “Green” meant that the archive satisfied its own partial test, not that the live system told the truth.

**Correction applied here:** normalize the queue schema; regenerate all three markdown surfaces from their JSON sources; add a data-driven live-surface contract; and add generic parity validation.

### B. “Verified” has become a semantic overclaim — severe

The existing quality audit checks only that required fields are nonempty. A synthesis locator such as “Gate 20E synthesis” passes the same structural test as a direct page/table extraction. Field completeness is useful, but it is not independent substantive verification.

**Correction applied here:** preserve the historical filename for compatibility, change the operator label to **locator-bound evidence records**, add a semantic-boundary audit, expose the zero-review and zero-contradiction facts, and flag synthesis/normative candidates without deleting evidence history.

### C. The validator has become an executable release diary — severe and compounding

At rev0356 the archive reported a validator of about 175,470 bytes, 2,995 lines, 74 checks, 45 revision-specific checks, and 49 distinct revision tokens. At rev0370 it is **255,750 bytes, 4,247 lines, 92 checks, 60 revision-specific checks, and 64 revision tokens**. In fourteen release steps it grew roughly 46% by bytes and 42% by lines.

This is not just aesthetic debt. Historical release assertions remain executable forever; each revision expands the surface that can block current truth, and the validator becomes harder to reason about than the corpus it validates.

**Correction applied here:** rev0371 adds no `check_rev0371_*` function. New current-surface rules live in a small generic module and a declarative contract. Over time, historical checks should be converted to immutable receipt fixtures and removed from the live call chain.

### D. Revision velocity has outrun epistemic review

Revisions rev0356 through rev0370—15 numbered releases—were recorded between 08:30:59Z and 15:38:00Z on June 18, 2026, about seven hours. In that interval the archive added many sources, ledgers, audits, edge records, report pairs, and validator checks. There is no independent-review layer capable of matching that production rate.

Fast iterative work is not inherently bad. The problem is that revision count, file count, and edge count can become theater: visible motion that is easier to produce than a closed, challenged, decision-useful conclusion.

### E. Zero closure despite abundant machinery

There are 95 case memos, 95 scoreboards, 583 sources, 317 fields, 8,005 mechanical associations, 171 locator-bound records, 27 active high-risk work items, and 0 certified cases. The ratio is telling. The system is optimized to preserve obligations, not discharge them.

A time-boxed closure sprint should choose one case with bounded stakes and enough primary evidence, define a minimum viable certification question, recruit a skeptical reviewer, and publish either a certified answer or an explicit failed-certification record. Both outcomes would be progress.

### F. Schema entropy remains high

Of 317 registered fields, **208 are used in one case and 266—about 84%—are used in at most two**. Zero fields are formally `registered_unused`, but “used once” is not the same as reusable ontology. Many fields are case-specific facts wearing the costume of shared schema.

Freeze new field creation. Classify low-use fields as core, extension, alias, case-local, or retirement candidate. Case-local details belong in case packets, not the global scoreboard schema.

### G. Historical report provenance is still unreliable

The rev0370 input contained 209 report files. The rev0371 provenance quarantine scans 212 report files—including this report pair and the current validation report—and still finds 60 JSON filename/declaration revision mismatches and 71 markdown mismatches. Eight exact duplicate groups remain across the archive, including six identical “quality audit” JSON files under different revision names. This preserves evidence of past overwrite behavior but also burdens every operator with ambiguous historical artifacts.

Do not keep historical reports in the active routing layer. Move them to a cold, content-addressed history area with immutable metadata. The active archive should expose one current audit per function and a migration map to history.

### H. Source batching and institutional concentration can hide blind spots

The registry contains 107 undated rows, 23 sources unused by a case, 19 exact duplicate-URL groups, and 388 sources with the same access date of May 25, 2026. OECD alone appears under multiple organization labels, and a large share of evidence comes from official or multilateral institutions.

This is not an argument to discard official sources. It is a reason to add source-role quotas and gap checks: primary instrument, administrative data, evaluation, contrary analysis, affected-person evidence, and historical/comparative evidence. A source registry should expose perspective and role, not only authority.

### I. The newest topic repeatedly captures the front door

Rev0356 restored a mission-first entrypoint. By rev0370 the front door again began with the latest Medicare Advantage case and its ledgers. The case is consequential and recent official evidence supports its salience, but no single instrument lab should displace the mission, target constitution, maturity state, and evidence warnings.

**Correction applied here:** the front door now starts with the charter and this audit; Medicare Advantage is retained as the current lab, not the project identity.

## Recommended operating sequence

### Phase 1 — freeze accumulation and finish one thing

For the next substantive sprint, add no new cases and no new global fields. Select one of the 10 cases with locator-bound records. Define a narrow certification question that can be answered within a bounded time and evidence perimeter. Recruit one skeptical reviewer who did not write the case. Require at least one serious contradicting or alternative-explanation record.

A plausible choice is not necessarily the newest case. Choose the case with the best combination of primary instruments, observable outcomes, manageable perimeter, and public usefulness.

### Phase 2 — split the evidence ontology

Adopt record kinds such as:

- `direct_source_observation`
- `source_interpretation`
- `multi_source_calculation`
- `cross_source_synthesis`
- `archive_normative_inference`
- `contrary_evidence`
- `outcome_observation`

Add exact target selectors, source version/hash, extraction method, reviewer, reviewed_at, disagreement, and supersession. W3C Web Annotation and PROV supply useful interoperable patterns; the archive need not copy them literally.

### Phase 3 — build the observatory the mission needs

Create compact, versioned panels for:

- claimable floor and liquidity;
- broad ownership and person-level title/control;
- concentrated decision power;
- inheritance and circulation;
- full public balance sheet and common-asset rights;
- ecological and cross-border externalization;
- administrative burden and remedy performance.

Do not collapse these into a compensatory aggregate score. A disqualifying domination failure should not be canceled by strength elsewhere.

### Phase 4 — prune the cloudtainer

Separate three storage temperatures:

- **active core:** charter, current data contracts, one current ledger per function, current certified products, and open work;
- **generated observatory:** reproducible ledgers, indexes, dashboards, and validation outputs;
- **cold history:** immutable prior releases, superseded reports, receipts, and content-addressed duplicates.

A reasonable target is to make the active human reading path fewer than 20 documents even if cold history remains complete.

## Online research and what it implies

The external research supports the mission while exposing the archive’s missing contracts.

- The Federal Reserve’s Survey of Consumer Finances and Distributional Financial Accounts combine different data systems and require alignment choices; distributional estimates are model- and vintage-dependent. This supports recording measurement concept and revision, not only a source ID.
- WID and OECD provide descriptive inequality series and increasingly sophisticated alignment with national accounts. Their work supports measurement and comparison, but does not derive the archive’s exact normative target tuple.
- IMF public-sector balance sheets integrate public assets, liabilities, public corporations, and fiscal risks. This supports a fuller public-wealth gate than a single net-worth figure.
- World Bank comprehensive-wealth work includes produced, human, and natural capital and publishes reproducibility packages. This supports ecological co-solvency and reproducible methods.
- W3C Web Annotation, PROV, Data on the Web Best Practices, and the Data Quality Vocabulary distinguish exact targets, provenance, versioning, quality, and fitness for purpose. RO-Crate offers a practical packaging model for files, metadata, people, workflows, and provenance.
- Reproducible Builds guidance identifies timestamps and archive metadata as volatile build inputs and standardizes `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH`. The old build tool embedded the clock time on each run; rev0371 removes that avoidable nondeterminism.
- Administrative-burden research and official HHS guidance on lived experience support a dedicated affected-person evidence track rather than assuming institutional records reveal the whole mechanism.
- Recent HHS-OIG and CMS material confirms that Medicare Advantage payment integrity, denial, appeal, and prior-authorization data are real current issues. That validates the lab while not justifying its capture of the project front door.

## Speculation: the strongest future form

The strongest future form is not a bigger encyclopedia. It is a small, inspectable system that can compile a **wealth-order diagnosis** and expose every inferential jump.

A user would ask: “Does this housing, pension, tax, infrastructure, labor, inheritance, or public-asset regime expand ordinary independence without creating private governing power?” The system would return:

1. the bounded question and affected population;
2. the distributional and power baseline with vintages;
3. the mechanism and operative instruments;
4. direct observations separated from calculations, syntheses, and normative judgments;
5. expected incidence and transition risks;
6. observed outcomes and strongest contrary evidence;
7. a verdict with reviewer disagreement and confidence;
8. a recertification date and machine-reproducible package.

That would make the archive useful to policy designers, journalists, advocates, auditors, unions, public fund trustees, and communities without asking them to traverse hundreds of internally generated documents.

## Bottom line

Do not abandon the project. Its heart is unusually coherent. But stop allowing the cloudtainer’s machinery to define success. The next era should be **mission-first, completion-oriented, contradiction-seeking, reviewer-visible, semantically honest, reproducible, and much smaller at the active surface**.
