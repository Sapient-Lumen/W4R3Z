# Rev0349 — project-level water/ratepayer incidence and proof-debt refactor

## What changed

- Added **S502-S505** and no cases/schema fields.
- Bound the AI/data-center grid-water public-backstop case to a concrete Pennsylvania PPL LP-6 rate-case packet, final PUC approval notice, Virginia JLARC data-center audit, and ICPRB Potomac basin water-use analysis.

## Substance-first audit result

The riskiest unfinished gap was not another taxonomy; it was whether the case could name actual ratepayer, water, and subsidy instruments. Rev0349 adds a PPL LP-6 packet with 50 MW / 75 MW thresholds, 10-year ESAs, credit/security, exit fees, CIAC/security treatment, non-critical load segmentation, and $11 million USR assignment; a final PUC approval surface; Virginia audit evidence for cost, stranded-infrastructure, water, and tax-exemption exposure; and Potomac basin water projections.
