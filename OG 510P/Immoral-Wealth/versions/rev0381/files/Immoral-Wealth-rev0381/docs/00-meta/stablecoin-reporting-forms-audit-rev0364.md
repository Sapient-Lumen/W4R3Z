# Stablecoin reporting-forms audit — rev0364

Rev0364 reduces a real risk: the stablecoin case could otherwise keep saying “issuer data needed” without naming the data.

Findings:

- OCC PS-01 identifies weekly reserve, redemption, pricing, concentration, and counterparty data needed for run-risk testing. [S550]
- OCC PS-02 identifies quarterly condition, off-balance-sheet, capital, and operational-backstop data needed for first-loss testing. [S551]
- FDIC proposed terms identify two-business-day redemption and no pass-through insurance treatment for reserve deposits. [S552]
- None of this certifies the case: final forms/rules, actual filings, public disclosure, insolvency priority, liquidity-facility terms, and public-upside recovery remain missing.
