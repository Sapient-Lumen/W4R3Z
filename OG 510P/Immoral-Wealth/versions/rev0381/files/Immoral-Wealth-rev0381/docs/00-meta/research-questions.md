# Research questions

These are the archive's **stable open questions**, not a queue that must all be pursued now.
Use [`research-triage-and-closure-rules.md`](research-triage-and-closure-rules.md) to decide which ones are spine-critical, which are later calibration, and which should stay parked without attracting new archive weight.


## rev0304 active calibration queue

These questions are active because the rev0304 case portfolio shows that they can change a case verdict, opening lane, or proof-debt burden now:

1. **Floor pass trigger:** what combination of liquid buffers, positive net worth, claimability, protected minima, and ordinary-shock survivability is enough for a real-floor pass?
2. **Family-gated threshold trigger:** what share of first housing, business, education, relocation, or repair entry may depend on parental deposits, guarantees, inherited place, or family balance sheets before open circulation fails?
3. **Public/social substitution ceiling:** when can strong public provision legitimately reduce required private buffers, and when does it become claimability-dependent, household-gated, or patronage-routed?
4. **Housing washout typology:** which housing breach is live — scarcity/rent, owner tax advantage, public-housing assetization, land/title closure, lease decay, local ownership concentration, or climate-insurance self-erosion?
5. **Climate/insurance self-erosion:** when do premiums, deductibles, repairs, nonrenewals, and uninsurable risks convert home equity into a false foothold?[S374][S84]
6. **Wealth-to-rule composite:** which observable channels — political finance, lobbying, media, procurement, beneficial ownership, stewardship, rescue favoritism — trigger anti-oligarchy mode?
7. **Opacity penalty:** when should missing top-tail, trust, beneficial-owner, land-entity, DAF/foundation, family-office, or private-asset data block acceptable or prevention-mode certification by itself?[S23][S54][S435][S64][S76]
8. **Case-portfolio promotion rule:** when is a repeated pattern strong enough to become doctrine rather than remaining a case note?
9. **AI/intangible concentration:** when do compute, data, IP, platforms, and private-market allocations become a top-tail formation channel that ordinary share tables miss?[S20][S60][S68][S69]
