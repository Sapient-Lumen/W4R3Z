# Charter

## The heart of the mission

> **Wealth should give every person durable ordinary independence, but should not give anyone durable governing power over other people, public institutions, or the future.**

The project is not chiefly a ranking of wealth-share tables. It asks whether people have claims they can actually use, keep, prove, transfer, and exit with; whether ownership becomes command; whether advantage is dynastically closed; whether private upside pushes risk and repair costs onto the public; and whether public, social, and ecological wealth gives ordinary people a durable counterweight.

A good wealth order is therefore **bounded at the top, thick through the middle, real at the floor, open in circulation, non-dominating in control, and solvent on the public and ecological balance sheet**.

## Questions the archive must answer

1. What dependence, insecurity, extraction, closure, or governing power is present?
2. Through what legal, contractual, financial, administrative, or political mechanism is it reproduced?
3. Which people bear the loss, delay, risk, or loss of voice—and who receives the upside?
4. Which instrument could change the mechanism, with what implementation path, transition incidence, remedy, and capture risk?
5. What evidence would reverse the diagnosis or show that the preferred instrument failed?

## Three layers that must not be conflated

### 1. Normative constitution

This layer contains the moral commitments, disqualifying domination conditions, target family, and public/common-wealth principles. It changes slowly. Exact numerical targets are **normative proposals and sensitivity screens**, not findings mechanically entailed by descriptive inequality sources.

### 2. Empirical observatory

This layer contains versioned measurements of private wealth, control, public assets and liabilities, ecological assets, administrative burden, lived experience, and institutional performance. Every measurement should preserve concept, population, unit, valuation date, source vintage, revision status, uncertainty, and reviewer.

### 3. Instrument laboratories

This layer contains bounded cases. A case is useful only when it links:

`harm / dependence -> mechanism -> operative instrument -> expected incidence -> observed outcome -> contrary evidence -> verdict -> recertification trigger`

The newest case is a laboratory, not the identity or front door of the project.

## Evidence constitution

- A source link is a route, not proof.
- A populated record is structurally complete, not necessarily true.
- Multi-source synthesis and archive-authored normative inference must be labeled separately from direct source observation.
- Every consequential verdict needs contrary evidence, a reversal rule, and identifiable review responsibility.
- “Verified” must eventually mean independently reviewed against an exact source target; until then the operator label is **locator-bound evidence record**.
- Counts of rows, sources, fields, files, or revisions are maintenance telemetry, never mission success metrics.

## Completion and stop rules

- Do not open a new case while all current cases remain uncertified unless the new case closes a demonstrable portfolio blind spot and an existing case is merged, retired, or deprioritized.
- Do not add a source without linking it to a bounded open claim atom or measurement update.
- Do not add a schema field without a cross-case reuse forecast, owner, promotion test, and retirement rule.
- A release should reduce net proof debt, correct a truth-surface failure, or complete an independently reviewable product. Revision number alone is not progress.
- Current work should prioritize completing one end-to-end case over opening another perimeter.

## Non-goals

This archive is not trying to defend strict sameness, collapse wealth justice into charity, treat GDP growth as an adequate moral score, produce a single-country tax memo, hoard source artifacts for their own sake, or generate self-referential compliance machinery faster than substantive knowledge.
