# Tax instrument decision matrix

## What this note is for

This note makes the tax lane more operational. It does not replace [`tax-capital-gains-inheritance-and-wealth.md`](tax-capital-gains-inheritance-and-wealth.md). It helps a case memo decide **which tax instrument fits which breach**, and which rails must exist before the instrument should be treated as credible.[S04][S05][S06][S36][S56]

## Minimum rule

Do not choose tax instruments by slogan. Choose them by breach:

- dynastic closure,
- unrealized-gain lock-in,
- land rent and housing washout,
- top-tail rule conversion,
- public-revenue need,
- hidden wealth-state subsidy,
- cross-border avoidance,
- or capitalization leakage.

Then ask whether the jurisdiction has the legibility, valuation, enforcement, and political-firewall rails needed to make the instrument real.[S23][S54][S56]

## Matrix

| Instrument | Best for | Main weakness | Rail requirement | Avoidance risk | Good first use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| inheritance / estate / gift tax | dynastic closure and late-life concentration | slow timing; high planning pressure | estate/gift registry, trust reporting, valuation | trusts, discounts, lifetime transfers | rich democracies with administrative capacity |
| recurrent net wealth tax | top-tail compression and revenue | valuation, liquidity, political resistance | beneficial ownership, asset valuation, audit capacity | relocation, entity shifting, under-valuation | very high-capacity states or narrow top-tail design |
| minimum tax on ultra-wealth | top-tail regressivity and mobility games | defining tax base and covered taxpayers | global/international information exchange | citizenship/residence planning, offshore structures | coordinated or high-capacity states |
| capital-gains realization reform | buy-borrow-die and realization deferral | timing and liquidity issues | brokerage reporting, private-asset valuation | asset swaps, debt channels, private markets | financialized states with strong reporting |
| mark-to-market / deemed realization | unrealized-gain lock-in | private valuation and cashflow | valuation safe harbors, deferral rules, audit | hard-to-value assets | publicly traded assets first |
| exit tax | mobility blackmail | legal design and treaty conflicts | residence/asset tracing | pre-migration planning | countries facing elite exit threats |
| land-value / property tax reform | land rent, housing washout, local public goods | assessment politics; liquidity for low-income owners | cadastre, assessment, deferral protections | under-assessment, exemptions | housing-constrained places |
| tax-expenditure cleanup | hidden wealth state and top-heavy subsidies | coalition backlash | budget transparency and incidence analysis | relabeling subsidies | broad first mover |
| foundation / DAF / charity reform | charitable shelter and discretionary elite allocation | boundary between public good and donor influence | payout, governance, related-party, investment rules | shifting to other vehicles | high-philanthropy top-tail states |
| trust / family-office reporting | dynastic opacity and private-market control | privacy objections; definitional gaming | beneficial-ownership verification | entity fragmentation | rail before harder taxes |
| anti-offshore enforcement | cross-border avoidance | coordination and capacity | automatic exchange, beneficial ownership, audit teams | haven switching | necessary complement everywhere |

## Sequencing rule

### If rails are weak

Start with tax-expenditure hygiene, registries, reporting, valuation capacity, beneficial ownership, and immobile bases such as land. Do not pretend a broad wealth tax will do work the state cannot administer.

### If housing washout is dominant

Lead with land/property/land-value capture, anti-speculation, social housing finance, and subsidy hygiene before relying on income-tax changes to fix asset-price entry.

### If dynasty is dominant

Lead with estate/gift integration, trust rules, valuation, foundation/DAF governance, and family-office/reporting visibility. A wealth tax without dynastic rails may miss the actual control architecture.[S435][S64]

### If top-tail regressivity is dominant

Use the ultra-wealth minimum-tax family, capital-gains realization reform, and wealth/inheritance integration. The World Inequality Report 2026 taxation chapter is a live source for minimum wealth-tax framing and international coordination questions.[S56]

### If public trust is weak

Pair taxes with visible floors, early footholds, housing repair, or public/common wealth. Revenue that disappears into opaque budgets will not build the counterweight the archive needs.

## Anti-slogan rule

A case memo should not say **wealth tax now** or **inheritance tax instead** without answering:

1. What breach is the instrument closing?
2. What false pass does it avoid?
3. What rail makes it enforceable?
4. What avoidance channel is likely first?
5. What distributional repair will the revenue fund?
6. What would count as failure after one review cycle?
