# Migrant claimant-perimeter and portability scorecard

Public or social wealth cannot be scored without answering: **for whom?** Gate 13 forces the claimant perimeter into the verdict layer.

## Fields

| Field | Pass | Warning | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| labor-law coverage | migrant workers covered in practice | legal coverage with weak enforcement | exclusion or dependency on employer permission |
| social protection | injury, wage, maternity, health, pension, unemployment, and end-of-service claims available | partial claims or contribution without portability | contribution without claim or claim without access |
| residence security | status not tied to a single employer/gatekeeper | transition rules fragile | employer/gatekeeper can interrupt legal stay |
| remedy access | accessible complaint, inspection, legal aid, translation | slow or retaliation-prone | no safe route before deportation or job loss |
| family and care | family unity, school, care, and housing claims scored | temporary or costly | long-term resident families excluded |
| political/voice channel | worker/resident voice exists | consultative only | no voice over rules that allocate claims |
| portability | pensions/benefits transferable or refundable | bilateral gaps | forfeiture at exit |

## Use cases

Apply this scorecard to sovereign funds, resource states, export zones, care-worker regimes, construction booms, guest-worker systems, seasonal migration, and any public-wealth case with a large resident noncitizen workforce.[S215][S216][S217]

## Result

A high public-wealth country may still fail or watch-fail if a large share of the workers who sustain the order are outside the floor, claim, remedy, and voice perimeter.
