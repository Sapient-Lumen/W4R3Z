# Exit-tax and anti-blackmail rail matrix

This matrix prevents two errors: pretending exit threats do not exist, and letting elite exit threats veto every redistributive or transparency reform.

| Rail | Best for | Weakness | Evidence needed | Route |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| deemed disposal at emigration | unrealized capital gains before residence change | valuation/liquidity disputes | asset class, basis, residence history | tax / enforcement |
| trailing residence rule | short-run residence switching | treaty and administrability limits | days, ties, control, asset location | tax / legal durability |
| source-based taxation | domestic-source rents, land, resource, property income | treaty design and enforcement | source asset registry | land / tax |
| exit tax with deferral + security | real relocation with illiquid assets | collection risk | collateral, installment terms | tax / remedy |
| CRS/AEOI + BO linkage | offshore accounts and entities | nonfinancial assets, weak verification | exchange partners, BO quality | visibility |
| anti-treaty-shopping rule | entity/residence arbitrage | litigation | treaty network, beneficial ownership | legal durability |
| minimum-tax / last-resort collector | low-tax relocation of top wealth | international coordination | residence, nationality, source links | top-tail compression |
| targeted CFM/MPM | destabilizing outflows or inflows | overreach, corruption, investment harm | macro and FX stress evidence | macro stability |

## Evidence rule

Do not infer policy failure from departure anecdotes. Require at least one of: administrative migration/taxpayer data, asset-flow data, tax receipts by cohort, high-net-worth registry changes, real-estate/entity ownership shifts, FX outflow indicators, or credible linked panel evidence.[S209][S211][S212][S213]

## Moral rule

A just system may need to make exit possible but not costless. The test is whether the exit rail collects accrued obligations and preserves ordinary claimants' public capacity without trapping people who need to move for safety, family, work, or care.
