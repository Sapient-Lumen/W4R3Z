# Place-public-finance gate and scorecard

Gate 15 asks whether the public floor exists where people actually live. National public spending, aggregate public wealth, or formal municipal autonomy cannot certify a case if local tax bases, school systems, infrastructure, utilities, transportation, or disaster-rebuild capacity leave ordinary households privately exposed.[S243][S245][S246]

## Gate 15 question

Can an ordinary lower-half household in a low-wealth place rely on local public systems for school, water, sewer, transport, safety, utilities, and disaster recovery without buying private substitutes, taking on debt, moving away, or depending on family rescue?

## Scorecard fields

| Field | Pass | Warning | Fail / veto pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| `school_finance_equalization` | need-adjusted progressive recurring funds | partial formula with gaps | poor/high-need districts receive less or tax harder for less |
| `school_facility_capital_gap` | safe and maintained buildings | capital gaps with repair path | property-wealth-based facilities inequality |
| `municipal_fiscal_capacity` | stable recurring revenue and reserves | narrowing margin | structural stress or service cuts |
| `infrastructure_backlog` | funded asset management | known backlog | deferred maintenance shifts costs to households |
| `water_wastewater_need_pressure` | affordable capital plan | rate pressure | unsafe/unreliable water or sewer, unaffordable rates |
| `utility_affordability_burden` | low burden / protected rates | high burden groups | burden forces food/medicine/care tradeoffs |
| `utility_disconnection_protection` | robust reporting and shutoff guardrails | seasonal or partial protections | frequent disconnection, opaque data, weak restoration |
| `transportation_access_cost_burden` | low combined H+T burden | car-dependence watch | transport insecurity blocks work/care/health |
| `disaster_recovery_fiscal_bridge` | operating bridge and fast reimbursement | debt/reimbursement watch | service collapse or forced local borrowing after disaster |
| `intergovernmental_transfer_reliability` | stable, formulaic, need-sensitive | one-time or discretionary | grants cliff or political/patronage allocation |
| `capital_grant_absorption_capacity` | technical/match capacity | uneven access | low-capacity places cannot use available funds |
| `user_fee_special_assessment_regressivity` | low-income protections | fee pressure | capital needs become regressive household liabilities |
| `abatement_pilot_tax_base_leakage` | transparent and capped | weak review | tax base is given away while services underfund |

## Verdict effects

- **Pass** supports Gate 7 public/common counterweight and Gate 2 floor reality.
- **Warning** prevents `near_ideal` unless the case shows durable repair rails.
- **Fail** usually routes to `correction_required` or `emergency_repair` when utilities, schools, or disaster services fail at scale.
- **Veto pressure** applies when place-level public-service gaps are racialized, caste-linked, citizenship-linked, disability-linked, or structurally tied to property wealth.[S238][S241][S242][S244]

## Evidence pack

Use administrative finance data, audited local budgets, school-finance formula data, facilities and asset-condition inventories, utility arrears/disconnection reports, H+T or transport-insecurity measures, grant absorption records, FEMA/disaster reimbursement data, and subgroup incidence evidence. Missing local data is not neutral when the case depends on local public systems.
