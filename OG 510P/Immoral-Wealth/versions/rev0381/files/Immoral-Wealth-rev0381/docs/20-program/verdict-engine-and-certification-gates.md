# Verdict engine and certification gates

rev0318 keeps the engine's core rule: wealth inequality becomes immoral when wealth functions as durable **dependency, closure, and rule**. A case does not pass from one distribution table, one program success, or one attractive public asset. It passes only if the relevant gates clear without hiding false floors, blocked threshold moments, washout, opacity, weak remedies, political capture, exit leakage, workplace lock-in, place failure, market tollgates, score-mediated exclusion, fresh-start failure, intergenerational transfer failure, or public-balance-sheet/backstop failure.

## Gate order

### Gate 1 — share plausibility

Start with the target family: roughly `20 / 50 / 20 / 10` for bottom 50 / middle 40 / next 9 / top 1 private net wealth, with the top 0.1% near or below `3%`, plus a real public/social counterweight.[S02][S03][S13][S142][S55]

This gate fails or hardens when bottom-half wealth is near zero/negative, the top tail is far above target without counterweight, or the distribution claim relies on source families that miss high-end/control wealth.

### Gate 2 — floor reality

The lower half must be able to keep and use modest wealth. Locked accounts, asset-tested traps, expert-only claims, crisis-only benefits, and cliff-punished gains do not count as a secure floor.[S17][S21][S22][S24][S25]

### Gate 3 — threshold moments

Ordinary people must be able to cross deposit, guarantee, repair, care, safe exit, title/control, and first-stake moments without parental rescue, patronage, household gatekeepers, caste/race/citizenship gatekeeping, or opaque scoring.[S18][S19][S52][S73]

### Gate 4 — anti-averaging and group/person vetoes

National averages cannot certify the order if subordinated groups remain on systematically later, thinner, less secure ownership paths. This is a veto gate, not a diversity footnote.[S57][S58][S66][S76][S85][S98][S99][S100]

### Gate 5 — washout and reconcentration

Successful transfers fail if rents, land costs, debt, fees, insurance, monopoly markups, care costs, legal costs, or score systems drain gains back out.[S34][S35][S42][S43][S59][S418][S374][S104][S110]

### Gate 6 — wealth-to-rule and institutional capture

Top-tail ownership becomes immoral earlier when it converts into lawmaking, media control, party finance, procurement, courts, land use, rescue priority, or rule design for itself.[S05][S06][S31][S36][S61][S62][S435][S64]

### Gate 7 — public/common counterweight

Public or social wealth helps only when it creates rule-bound, claimant-level security. A sovereign fund, social housing system, public pension, or land bank is not a pass if access is discretionary, patronage-gated, citizenship-gated, under-maintained, or non-portable.[S77][S78][S369][S81][S93][S95][S96]

### Gate 8 — liability stack and life interruption

A case must separately score medical debt, student debt, criminal-legal debt, disability onset, care interruption, collections, credit-file drag, and other predictable life interruptions.[S104][S105][S106][S379][S108][S109][S110][S111][S112][S113]

### Gate 9 — conversion rails and claim finality

Nominal claims count only when they become usable person-level control. Benefits, tax credits, public dividends, starter accounts, social housing, disaster aid, pensions, legal aid, and inherited property must be findable, claimable, documentable, receivable, keepable, and convertible.[S120][S263][S122][S123][S126][S262][S130][S133][S139][S140]

Use [`conversion-rail-certification-gate.md`](conversion-rail-certification-gate.md) for the detailed test.

### Gate 10 — measurement uncertainty and owner-control visibility

A case cannot pass from false precision. The scoreboard must state source family, top-tail audit status, national-accounts reconciliation, survey-quality warnings, asset-valuation uncertainty, and owner-control visibility.[S141][S142][S146][S147][S148][S150][S152][S431][S155][S159][S160]

Use [`top-tail-audit-and-uncertainty-bounds.md`](top-tail-audit-and-uncertainty-bounds.md) and [`wealth-data-reconciliation-workflow.md`](wealth-data-reconciliation-workflow.md) for the detailed test.

### Gate 11 — enforceability, remedies, and capture resistance

A case cannot pass from unenforced rights, unaudited taxes, unverified registries, inaccessible courts, or public assets exposed to capture. Claimants and public institutions must be able to defend the package against resistance.[S162][S163][S164][S176][S177]

Use [`enforcement-capacity-and-remedy-gate.md`](enforcement-capacity-and-remedy-gate.md) for the detailed test.

### Gate 12 — democratic non-domination and political-power firewalls

A case cannot pass when concentrated wealth can repeatedly dominate political finance, dark money, lobbying, sponsored expertise, media agenda-setting, local entry decisions, strategic litigation, or post-enactment countermobilization.[S184][S185][S186][S188][S189][S190][S193][S194][S195][S198]

Use [`democratic-power-gate-and-firewall-scorecard.md`](democratic-power-gate-and-firewall-scorecard.md) for the detailed test.

### Gate 13 — jurisdictional mobility, exit blackmail, and claimant perimeter

A case cannot pass when wealth can escape the domestic order while ordinary claimants remain bound by it. Tax residence, legal status, capital mobility, property ownership, public claims, and remedies must align across borders.[S203][S204][S213][S214]

Use [`jurisdictional-mobility-gate-and-perimeter-test.md`](jurisdictional-mobility-gate-and-perimeter-test.md) for the detailed test.

### Gate 14 — workplace power, pre-distribution, and productive ownership

A case cannot pass when ordinary people are expected to build wealth from work but the productive economy blocks saveable wages, bargaining, mobility, classification security, responsibility that follows control, or broad claims on enterprise upside.[S220][S222][S230][S231][S234]

Use [`workplace-power-gate-and-predistribution-scorecard.md`](workplace-power-gate-and-predistribution-scorecard.md) for the detailed test.

### Gate 15 — place public finance, service wealth, and local fiscal capacity

A case cannot pass when public wealth exists nationally but schools, facilities, water, sewer, roads, transit, utilities, disaster recovery, or municipal finance fail where lower-wealth people actually live.[S238][S245][S246][S248][S249]

Use [`place-public-finance-gate-and-scorecard.md`](place-public-finance-gate-and-scorecard.md) for the detailed test.

### Gate 16 — household market extraction, care cost, and cost-of-life tollgates

A case cannot pass when ordinary households are expected to build wealth while consumer credit, banking/nonbank fees, auto finance, app/payroll credit, junk fees, subscription friction, childcare, eldercare, or long-term care drain the first buffer.[S260][S262][S263][S266][S273][S277]

Use [`household-market-extraction-gate-and-scorecard.md`](household-market-extraction-gate-and-scorecard.md) for the detailed test.

### Gate 17 — score-mediated exclusion, risk pricing, and algorithmic denial

A case cannot pass when reports, scores, models, or fraud flags convert prior hardship into blocked access or higher prices. Credit files, specialty consumer reports, tenant screens, employment dossiers, insurance scores, surveillance prices, identity-proofing systems, and public-benefit algorithms can make threshold moments depend on a clean data shadow rather than real capacity, need, or entitlement.[S281][S283][S286][S288][S290][S291][S295][S296]

Gate 17 blocks or hardens the verdict when:

- credit or specialty-report errors are corrected only after the loan, account, job, apartment, insurance, benefit, or utility connection is lost;
- medical debt, collections, old hardship, eviction filings, non-conviction records, or identity-theft traces travel into unrelated decisions;
- tenant screening or employment screening uses proprietary scores without usable explanation, dispute, or individualized review;
- credit-based insurance scores or external consumer data make necessary coverage unavailable or unaffordable;
- surveillance pricing uses personal data to charge different effective prices without disclosure, contestability, or exit;
- identity verification or fraud flags block benefits, refunds, accounts, or payments without offline alternatives, human review, and payment bridges;
- public-benefit algorithms deny, terminate, or delay subsistence claims without reasoned notice, audit, appeal, and emergency continuation;
- vendors or agencies cannot supply subgroup false-positive, correction, adverse-action, or model-audit evidence.[S282][S285][S287][S289][S292][S293][S294][S297][S298][S299][S300]

### Gate 18 — fresh-start capacity, asset protection, and post-shock recovery

A case cannot pass when an ordinary shock creates a long legal or data afterlife that prevents recovery. Bankruptcy, exemptions, wage garnishment, bank-account levy, judgment liens, collection courts, foreclosure/loss mitigation, eviction and foreclosure record cleanup, criminal-record clearance, and collateral-consequence repair must protect the tools needed to rebuild.[S301][S304][S305][S306][S307][S309][S315][S316]

Gate 18 blocks or hardens the verdict when:

- bankruptcy exists formally but filing access, debt exclusions, or thin exemptions leave households unable to restart;
- wages, bank balances, cars, work tools, household goods, or modest home equity can be seized below recovery needs;
- exemptions require fast, technical debtor action rather than self-executing protection;
- collection lawsuits and default judgments convert disputed or stale debts into garnishment, levy, liens, and renewed score shadows;
- foreclosure or eviction generates a durable record penalty after relief, dismissal, satisfaction, time, or changed circumstances;
- criminal legal records continue to block work, housing, licenses, education, benefits, or civic claims after sentence completion;
- clean-slate or sealing rules do not reach private vendors, specialty reports, or decision systems quickly enough.

### Gate 19 — intergenerational transfer, family-law allocation, and estate finality

A case cannot pass open circulation when private family transfer machinery decides who crosses early threshold moments, who keeps title, who loses home equity to care recovery, who controls assets during incapacity, who receives child support, who converts divorce rights into retirement assets, and who can shelter wealth in trusts or tax-optimized entities.[S321][S322][S324][S332][S335][S338][S343]

Gate 19 blocks or hardens the verdict when:

- deposits, tuition, guarantees, emergency liquidity, or first business stakes depend on parental/family wealth rather than public or market-neutral substitutes;
- bequest analysis ignores lifetime gifts and early transfers;
- probate, intestacy, beneficiary errors, or heirs-property title clouds stop inherited assets from becoming marketable, insurable, financeable, or defensible;
- Medicaid estate recovery or long-term-care spenddown consumes modest home equity while high-end households avoid similar exposure through planning;
- guardianship, conservatorship, powers of attorney, trusteeship, or representative payeeship remove person-level asset control without counsel, accounting, review, and restoration rights;
- elder financial exploitation drains late-life savings faster than protective institutions can freeze, report, and restore funds;
- divorce, QDROs, child support, caregiver claims, or survivor benefits fail to convert household wealth into person-level security;
- estate/gift/GST exemptions, dynasty trusts, valuation discounts, and trust opacity preserve top-tail family-line control beyond ordinary circulation.

### Gate 20 — public balance sheet, sovereign backstop, and fiscal-risk incidence

A case cannot pass when public balance-sheet capacity is counted as a counterweight while debt service, social-insurance depletion, public-pension underfunding, contingent liabilities, guarantees, central-bank quasi-fiscal effects, or crisis backstops quietly shift cost onto ordinary claimants or future cohorts.[S345][S346][S350][S351][S357][S360][S363][S369]

Gate 20 blocks or hardens the verdict when:

- public debt and net-interest pressure threaten the floors, services, enforcement, and public investment that lower-wealth people rely on;
- social-insurance or public-pension promises are counted as wealth substitutes despite likely claim haircuts or adjustment burdens;
- guarantees, credit programs, deposit insurance, housing-finance support, disaster aid, or emergency facilities are not disclosed, priced, stress-tested, and incidence-scored;
- private owners, creditors, managers, or intermediaries receive backstop value without public upside recovery, loss-sharing, fees, warrants, equity, clawbacks, or conditions;
- central-bank losses, deferred assets, or remittance effects are ignored because they are off-budget;
- sovereign wealth or public assets are praised as common wealth without withdrawal rules, professional governance, risk reporting, and claimant-linked benefits.

## Verdict rules

- `near_ideal`: clears all gates with direct evidence and durable rails.
- `acceptable`: minor warnings only; no veto gate.
- `acceptable_but_vulnerable`: real achievements, but one or more gates depend on maintenance, fiscal durability, protected institutional capacity, or source refresh.
- `correction_required`: one or more core gates fail, but credible non-revolutionary packages and functioning rails exist.
- `emergency_repair`: floor, shock survival, claim conversion, or life-interruption survival fails at scale.
- `captured_immoral`: closure/rule is structurally dominant and ordinary correction pathways are weak or captured.
- `provisional`: evidence is too incomplete for certification; missing evidence itself may become an opacity penalty.

## Evidence penalty

Missing evidence is not neutral. It has three levels:

1. **ordinary evidence debt** — record and refresh;
2. **hard evidence debt** — blocks soft certification;
3. **opacity penalty** — counts as a warning or fail when missingness is produced by ownership secrecy, top-tail undercoverage, administrative exclusion, vendor opacity, algorithmic opacity, or household-level masking.

rev0319 addendum: Gate 20 is now blocked by default when a case lacks a public-balance-sheet register, Gate 20 subgate findings, or seniority waterfall. Public wealth is not a counterweight until claimant seniority and public-upside recovery are visible.
