# Foreclosure, eviction recovery, and record-sealing router

Use this router when housing loss creates a durable afterlife.

## Router questions

- Was loss mitigation offered before foreclosure or lockout?
- Were fees, dual tracking, documentation churn, or language barriers decisive?
- Are dismissed, satisfied, old, or no-fault eviction filings sealed automatically?
- Are consumer-reporting and tenant-screening vendors required to delete sealed records quickly?
- Can applicants see and contest records before denial?
- Are foreclosure and eviction records time-limited and contextualized in later decisions?

## Preferred reforms

Pre-filing diversion, emergency rent/mortgage bridges, servicer loss-mitigation review, filing-stage privacy, automatic sealing by disposition/age, vendor deletion duties, damages for noncompliance, pre-denial record disclosure, and transaction-preserving correction.[S309][S310][S311][S312][S313][S260]
