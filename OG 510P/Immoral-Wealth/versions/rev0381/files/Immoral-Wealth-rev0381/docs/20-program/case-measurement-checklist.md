# Case measurement checklist

Before a case can use wealth shares for certification, answer these questions.

1. What is the source family: survey-only, oversampled survey, survey plus rich-list, tax capitalization, DINA, national-accounts-consistent, registry reconstruction, or mixed?
2. What is the wealth perimeter: private net wealth, pension-inclusive wealth, public claims, household net worth, person-level wealth, or control claims?
3. Are assets and liabilities measured in the same year and population?
4. Does the estimate capture the top 1%, 0.1%, and 0.01%?
5. Does a top-tail adjustment materially change the verdict?
6. Are survey quality, response, imputation, and accreditation warnings disclosed?
7. Are private companies, private markets, trusts, offshore entities, and legal arrangements visible?
8. Are land/property title holders linked to beneficial controllers?
9. Are lower-half assets usable, liquid, person-controlled, and title-final?
10. Does the case publish a low/central/high moral interval?

If the answer to 4, 5, 6, 7, or 8 is unknown and the case seeks a comfort verdict, the scoreboard must register proof debt or a blocked Gate 10.
