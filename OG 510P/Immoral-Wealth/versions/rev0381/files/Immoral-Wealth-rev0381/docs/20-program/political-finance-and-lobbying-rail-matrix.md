# Political-finance and lobbying rail matrix

Use this matrix when a case shows wealth-to-rule conversion through donations, independent expenditures, party access, lobbying, sponsored expertise, or foreign/cross-border political finance.

| Breach | Minimum rail | Hardening rail | Evidence source family |
|---|---|---|---|
| Large-donor dependence | timely donor disclosure, low thresholds, searchable records | public matching, caps, small-donor vouchers, strict source checks | election authority data, political-finance database, party accounts |
| Independent-expenditure exposure | real-time spending reports, sponsor and transfer disclosure | anti-coordination enforcement, shell/nonprofit look-through, digital ad libraries | FEC/electoral body reports, third-party ad data |
| Dark money | donor-obscuring vehicle disclosure for election influence | beneficial-owner and transfer-chain disclosure, penalties, anti-evasion rules | nonprofit/shell filings, campaign reports, investigative datasets |
| Lobbying opacity | register of lobbyists, clients, subjects, meetings | advisory, think-tank, foreign-state-interest, grassroots, digital influence disclosure | OECD/public-integrity indicators, registers, meeting logs |
| Party access market | publication of donations, loans, gifts, hospitality, secondments | source-of-funds checks, foreign-link screening, enforcement powers | electoral commission data, parliamentary reports |
| Enforcement weakness | independent electoral/lobbying regulator | investigatory powers, bank/tax/crypto information access, meaningful sanctions | regulator reports, audit reports, court/sanction records |

## U.S. calibration note

In the U.S. federal 2023-2024 cycle, FEC summary data report large campaign and committee flows and $4.4 billion in independent-expenditure disbursements, while FEC guidance states independent expenditures are not contributions and are not subject to limits. The U.S. case therefore requires a separate independent-spending and dark-money screen rather than relying on candidate contribution limits alone.[S184][S185]

## UK calibration note

UK cases should now separately test donation permissibility, overseas donor resources, party compliance capacity, Electoral Commission investigatory powers, and enforcement after the 2026 political-finance and foreign-influence reviews.[S192][S193][S194]
