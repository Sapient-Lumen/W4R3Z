# Post-shock recovery evidence pack

Minimum evidence for Gate 18:

1. bankruptcy filings, chapter mix, discharge, dismissal, and plan-completion rates;
2. state exemption tables for wages, bank accounts, cars, tools, household goods, and home equity;
3. whether exemptions are self-executing;
4. garnishment, levy, lien, and default-judgment volumes;
5. debt-collection complaint and enforcement outcomes;
6. eviction and foreclosure filing, judgment, sealing, and vendor-deletion data;
7. loss-mitigation timing, language access, and fee evidence;
8. record-clearance eligibility, implementation backlog, and vendor compliance;
9. collateral-consequence inventory by employment, licensing, housing, education, benefits, voting, and family claims;
10. subgroup outcomes by race, gender, disability, age, language, place, and immigration/status where lawful and safe.[S301][S304][S305][S307][S309][S315][S316]

If this evidence is missing, do not issue a comfort verdict. Route to proof debt and repair the highest-severity recovery rail first.
