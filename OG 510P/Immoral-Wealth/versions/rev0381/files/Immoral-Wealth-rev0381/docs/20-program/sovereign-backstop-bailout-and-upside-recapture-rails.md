# Sovereign backstop, bailout, and upside-recapture rails

A crisis backstop can be necessary. It becomes wealth-order distortion when it rescues private balance sheets without public upside, conditions, or loss discipline.

## Rail requirements

- define eligible claimants and public purpose;
- preserve critical household, payroll, deposit, and payment functions first;
- impose management, dividend, buyback, executive-compensation, and affiliate restrictions where relevant;
- require private loss absorption before public capital where feasible;
- take warrants, equity, fees, or repayment rights when public risk preserves private upside;
- publish facility terms, borrowers or counterparties where legally possible, collateral, rates, maturities, and losses;
- sunset automatically unless renewed after public findings;
- audit distributional effects after stress.[S361][S362][S368]

The operator question is not “was rescue legal?” but “did public downside create public upside and protect ordinary claimants first?”
