# Media pluralism and agenda-power screen

Use this screen when a case has enough media or expertise concentration to affect whether wealth-order reforms can be understood, contested, or defended.

## Minimum variables

- media ownership transparency;
- market plurality / concentration;
- political independence of media governance;
- state advertising and subsidy allocation;
- public-service media independence;
- sponsored expertise and think-tank funding transparency;
- digital political advertising transparency;
- cross-linkage between media owners, donors, public contractors, land owners, and regulated sectors.

The Media Pluralism Monitor's four dimensions are an acceptable starting template: fundamental protection, market plurality, political independence, and social inclusiveness.[S190][S191]

## Gate logic

A case cannot use free public debate as a comfort factor when the information environment is itself wealth-shaped. If outlet ownership, policy sponsorship, or state advertising cannot be traced, the case carries proof debt. If concentrated owners repeatedly define the wealth-policy agenda while holding interests in land, finance, procurement, platforms, or regulated industries, the case shifts to veto pressure.

## Repair package

- ownership-transparency registry for media and major policy sponsors;
- concentration and cross-ownership review;
- fair state-advertising rules;
- public-service media independence protections;
- funding disclosure for think tanks, expert testimony, and third-party policy campaigns;
- digital political ad archives;
- anti-SLAPP, journalist-safety, and whistleblower protections where intimidation is material.
