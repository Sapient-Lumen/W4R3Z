# Family-law, child-support, and caregiver-wealth router

Use this router when household assets must become person-level security after divorce, separation, widowhood, custody changes, or caregiving.

## Diagnostic questions

1. Can each adult identify, value, and claim marital/community assets without expert counsel?
2. Do retirement assets convert through QDRO or equivalent orders without tax/fee traps?[S329]
3. Are pension survivor benefits, Social Security, housing rights, and beneficiary designations protected for caregivers and spouses?
4. Are unpaid care contributions recognized in property division and support?
5. Do child-support collections reach families, or are they retained to reimburse public assistance?[S330][S331]
6. Are arrears and enforcement calibrated to ability to pay, child benefit, and wealth formation?
7. Is legal aid available for low-asset but high-stakes family-property cases?

## Routing choices

- automatic retirement-division checklists and model QDROs;
- required asset disclosure and valuation help;
- full pass-through/disregard of child support to families where possible;
- arrears compromise for government-owed debt where payment is unrealistic;
- license/incarceration sanctions limited to willful nonpayment;
- caregiver credits, pension-sharing, and survivor-benefit protections;
- post-separation housing and bank-account access.

## Certification rule

Household wealth cannot certify person-level freedom if family transitions routinely move one caregiver, custodial parent, or lower-earning spouse from asset access into dependency.
