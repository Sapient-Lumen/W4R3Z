# Dynastic opacity and charitable-vehicle router — rev0357

Route a case here when family wealth, tax planning, charitable form, or trust law may hide control, extend duration, or convert a public subsidy into private discretion.

## Required register

For each vehicle, record:

- legal form and governing jurisdiction;
- settlor/donor, trustee/sponsor, protector/advisor, beneficiary or class, and investment/distribution control;
- duration and rule-against-perpetuities status;
- tax preference, deduction, exemption, or other public subsidy;
- payout, recapture, reporting, and enforcement mechanism;
- current-law snapshot and refresh trigger.

## Certification rule

No comfort certification when vehicle control, public subsidy, payout timing, or beneficial ownership cannot be seen and refreshed.
