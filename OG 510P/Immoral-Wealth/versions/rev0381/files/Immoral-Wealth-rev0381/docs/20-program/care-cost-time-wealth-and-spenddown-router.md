# Care-cost, time-wealth, and spenddown router

Use this router when childcare, elder care, disability care, or long-term care determines whether a household can work, save, retain assets, or avoid dependence on family rescue.[S273][S275][S277][S278]

## Childcare route

1. Score net cost as a share of income for relevant family types.
2. Score availability, waiting lists, hours fit, transport access, quality, and safety.
3. Score subsidy cliffs, copays, documentation, and provider payment timing.
4. Score labor-force interruption: reduced hours, quits, declined training, missed shifts, and career penalties.
5. Score person-level power: whether a caregiver can exit a bad job, bad relationship, or unsafe housing without losing care.

## Long-term-care route

1. Score out-of-pocket cost relative to income and assets.
2. Score eligibility before spenddown and whether home/community care is available.
3. Score informal-care burden on family members' earnings, pensions, health, and migration.
4. Score quality, wait time, geographic access, and portability.
5. Score whether care financing preserves modest assets or requires poverty first.

## Package menu

Universal or near-universal care provision, sliding-scale caps, supply-side provider payment, worker wage/quality floors, caregiver credits, respite, home/community-based services, asset-protection rules, claim-conversion simplification, and disability/eldercare portability.
