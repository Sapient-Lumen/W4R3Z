# Democratic-power gate and firewall scorecard

Gate 12 asks whether the wealth-order program can survive a fair democratic contest, or whether concentrated wealth can repeatedly dominate the agenda, buy access, obscure sponsorship, veto entry, or exhaust remedies.

## Gate result

- **passed:** political finance, lobbying, media, local-land-use, advisory, litigation, and counter-mobilization channels are visible, contestable, and enforced.
- **watch:** one channel is materially weak but the reform package has credible short-term repairs.
- **blocked:** two or more channels are weak enough to let concentrated wealth hollow or reverse the package.
- **missing:** the case lacks basic political-finance, lobbying, media, or local-veto evidence.

## Scorecard

| Surface | Pass evidence | Warning | Fail / veto pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Political finance | timely donor/spending disclosure, real source checks, limits/public funding where needed, active sanctions | high spending but visible and contestable | dark money, unlimited independent spending, weak source-of-funds checks, late disclosure |
| Lobbying/advisory access | comprehensive register, clients/subjects/meetings, think-tank and third-party campaign disclosure | register exists but misses informal or digital influence | low-quality rules, no sanctions, hidden clients, revolving-door advisory capture |
| Media/agenda power | ownership transparency, concentration review, independent public media, fair state advertising | ownership known but cross-linkage weak | opaque owners, concentrated agenda channels, state-ad patronage, sponsored expertise hidden |
| Local-land-use democracy | entrant voice, regional/state review, by-right rules, anti-displacement | local process legal but outsider voice thin | incumbent homeowner veto blocks entry and mobility |
| Litigation and judicial power | standing symmetrical enough for claimants, timely remedies, anti-SLAPP/protective rules | litigation costly but bounded | reform can be exhausted by strategic litigation before remedies preserve claims |
| Countermobilization survival | package has monitoring, funding, institutional independence, and ratchets | survival depends on one cycle | capture, defunding, loophole lobbying, or media reversal likely after enactment |

## Evidence debt

A case cannot clear Gate 12 without at least a proxy reading for:

1. campaign/party receipts and donor concentration;
2. independent expenditure or third-party spending exposure;
3. dark-money or nontransparent sponsorship channels;
4. lobbying-register quality and sanctions;
5. media ownership and political independence;
6. local-land-use veto risk where housing/place is a dominant breach;
7. enforcement body independence and powers;
8. post-enactment countermobilization risk.

Missingness is severe when it is produced by donor secrecy, shell entities, nonprofits, unincorporated associations, foreign-linked financing, opaque media ownership, or fragmented local records.[S186][S188][S190][S193][S194]
