# Credit-program, guarantee, and subsidy transparency router

Use this router for student loans, disaster loans, mortgage insurance, GSE support, small-business credit, farm credit, export credit, and any public guarantee or direct-loan program.

## Required distinction

- budget subsidy under statutory accounting;
- fair-value or market-risk cost;
- borrower benefit by income/wealth/place/group;
- lender/intermediary benefit;
- default/loss expectations under stress;
- whether support lowers threshold barriers or inflates asset prices;
- whether public risk captures upside or merely cheapens private leverage.[S363][S364][S365][S366][S367]

Credit support passes only when it expands real entry without creating hidden taxpayer downside, asset-price inflation, or intermediary capture.
