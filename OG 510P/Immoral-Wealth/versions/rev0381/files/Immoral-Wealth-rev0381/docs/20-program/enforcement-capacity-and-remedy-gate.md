# Enforcement-capacity and remedy gate

rev0309 adds Gate 11 to the verdict engine.

## Gate 11 — enforceability, remedies, and capture resistance

A case must show that the institutions supposed to prevent dependency, closure, and rule can actually act. The gate covers **claimant remedies** and **state enforcement**.

### Pass

Gate 11 passes only when:

- claimants have standing, notice, evidence access, and timely remedies;
- enforcement agencies have staff, skills, data, audit tools, and collection authority;
- registries are verified and usable by authorized actors;
- beneficial ownership, trust/entity chains, and offshore accounts can be traced where relevant;
- public/common assets are protected by transparent governance and conflict controls;
- legal attack surfaces have been designed around rather than ignored;
- capture and defunding risks are monitored.

### Warning

Gate 11 is `watch` or `warning` when the formal law exists but the case depends on:

- underfunded enforcement;
- expert-only claiming;
- weak legal aid or inaccessible civil justice;
- long court or appeal delays;
- registry access limited so tightly that enforcement, journalism, research, procurement, or civil-society oversight cannot use it;
- volatile appropriations or political interference;
- reliance on one fragile instrument.

### Fail

Gate 11 fails when:

- no actor has standing to challenge the breach;
- remedies arrive only after irreversible loss;
- high-wealth enforcement is structurally weaker than ordinary enforcement;
- beneficial-owner or controller visibility collapses;
- public procurement converts common wealth into private insider fortunes;
- a reform is predictably struck down or disabled because legal constraints were ignored;
- asset recovery is unavailable after capture or corruption.

## Relationship to other gates

- Gate 9 asks whether claims convert.
- Gate 10 asks whether the cube can see wealth/control.
- Gate 11 asks whether the law can **defend** conversion and visibility against resistance.

This gate can independently block certification. It can also harden proof debt in Gates 1, 5, 6, 7, 9, and 10.
