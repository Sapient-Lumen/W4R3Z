# Case application protocol

## What this note is for

This note gives the archive a **standard thin workflow for real cases**.
Its purpose is to stop future country, city, sector, or cohort memos from becoming mini-archives of their own.
A serious case memo should **apply** the archive's target, verdict rule, scoreboard, mode rule, and implementation screen rather than re-explaining them every time.[S02][S13][S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33]

Before drafting, use [`case-portfolio-triage-and-memo-selection.md`](case-portfolio-triage-and-memo-selection.md) if several candidate cases are competing for archive attention and use [`case-portfolio-caps-and-retirement.md`](case-portfolio-caps-and-retirement.md) if the archive already has a crowded live case portfolio. Then use [`case-work-decision-path.md`](case-work-decision-path.md) if you want the archive's shortest operator bridge from evidence to verdict to package to watch posture. If several live or exemplar cases are being set beside one another, route that work through [`cross-case-decision-path.md`](cross-case-decision-path.md) and retain any actual comparison memo in the shape given by [`cross-case-application-protocol.md`](cross-case-application-protocol.md) so the archive keeps one stable comparison order and one stable memo form instead of rebuilding the whole stack inside each memo preface. Then gather the archive's minimum case evidence using [`minimum-evidence-pack-for-case-work.md`](minimum-evidence-pack-for-case-work.md) and sort any thin or conflicting evidence with [`source-order-and-conflict-resolution.md`](source-order-and-conflict-resolution.md). That keeps future case memos thin **without** letting thinness become guesswork, fake precision, or soft certification.[S01][S13][S15][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33]

## Minimum operating rule

Every real-case memo in this archive should answer **ten questions in order**:

1. **What is the verdict?**
2. **How far is the case from the archive's target family?**
3. **Which one or two breaches are doing the most constitutional damage right now?**
4. **Which washout channel will undo visible gains fastest if left open?**
5. **Which governing mode fits the case: prevention, correction, or anti-oligarchy response?**
6. **What opening lane mix is nearest-real rather than merely rhetorically satisfying?**
7. **Which parts can begin now, which must build rails while moving, and which remain gated?**
8. **How confident should the memo be in the verdict, the package, the rails, and any softer-certification claim?**
9. **What evidence would change the verdict over the next durability window?**
10. **How often should the case be reviewed again, when should continued miss force reroute, and what ordinary pressure must any softer reading still survive?**

That sequence is meant to keep case work short, comparable, and decision-useful.
It prevents a recurring failure mode in inequality writing: jumping from atmospheric description to favorite policy instrument without ever stating the verdict rule, the main breach, the washout channel, or the actual governing posture.[S13][S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33]

## The required memo shape

### 1. One-sentence verdict block

Start with one sentence that uses the archive's verdict language directly:
**structurally failing**, **correction-required**, **provisional near-pass**, or **near-ideal / constitutionally acceptable**.
Do not make the reader infer the verdict from twenty paragraphs of scene-setting.
Say the verdict first and defend it second.[S02][S13][S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23]

### 2. Target-distance block

Report the case against the archive's preferred family in a compact block:
- bottom 50 / middle 40 / next 9 / top 1 if available,
- the top 0.1% where visibility permits,
- public/social wealth or the nearest usable counterweight proxy,
- and one sentence on whether the case sits **inside the narrow acceptable family**, merely outside the worst zone, or clearly in warning territory.[S02][S03][S142][S20][S23]

Do **not** let this block become the whole memo.
The archive's target numbers matter, but they are only the first screen.
A case can still fail near the numbers when the floor is thin, entry is too inherited, control remains too concentrated, or apparent progress is being washed out upward.[S13][S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33]

### 3. Dominant-breach block

Name the **one or two breaches** doing the most constitutional damage right now.
Use the archive's existing surfaces rather than inventing fresh categories each time.
In most cases the dominant breach will be some combination of:
- floor failure,
- late footholds / dynastic closure,
- control concentration,
- rent-washout and debt fragility,
- thin public/common counterweight,
- or wealth-to-rule conversion.[S13][S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33]

This block is the memo's hinge.
If the breach is misidentified, the whole policy section usually becomes either too diffuse or too theatrical. When several plausible candidates compete, use [`dominant-breach-and-fastest-washout-selection.md`](dominant-breach-and-fastest-washout-selection.md) to choose one dominant breach and, at most, one genuine secondary breach rather than letting every failure pretend to be equally first.

### 4. Fastest-washout block

State explicitly which channel will undo visible gains fastest if left open.
In most real cases that means **housing and land**, **debt and carrying costs**, **monopoly or fee extraction**, **opacity and controller invisibility**, **public downside absorption**, or some combination of them.[S07][S10][S17][S20][S23][S31][S32][S33][S36][S42]

This block matters because the archive does not treat every improvement as equally vulnerable.
A society can adopt broadening measures and still move backward quickly when the live washout channel remains untouched. When several channels look live, use [`dominant-breach-and-fastest-washout-selection.md`](dominant-breach-and-fastest-washout-selection.md) to choose the one that would reabsorb visible gains fastest rather than the one that is merely most vivid rhetorically.

### 5. Mode block

Place the case in **prevention**, **correction**, or **anti-oligarchy response** and say why.
The key question is not only how unequal the case looks.
It is whether ordinary corrective government is still materially available or whether capture, opacity, and blocked governability already require a harder posture.[S15][S23][S31][S32][S33]

### 6. Opening-package block

Only after the verdict, target distance, dominant breach, washout channel, and mode are stated should the memo name the opening package.
That package should be described as a **lane mix**, not as one magical instrument.
Use the archive's seven-lane direct-answer stack and the four-slot composer in [`opening-package-router.md`](opening-package-router.md), then—if more than one serious composition survives—use [`package-choice-and-tie-break-rules.md`](package-choice-and-tie-break-rules.md) to say why one package wins. State which lanes lead first, which lane must close the fastest washout channel, which extra lane hardens durability, and what stays gated.[S04][S07][S09][S12][S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33][S36]

The standing shortcut is:
- floor failure -> usually lead with lanes **2, 3, and 5**, while opening **4** early,
- late entry / dynastic closure -> usually lead with **4 and 5**, often backed by **2**,
- rent-washout -> usually push **5** with support from **2, 3, and 7**,
- top-tail rule -> usually harden **6 and 7** earlier,
- thin public/common counterweight -> usually strengthen **7** together with **5** and whichever of **2-4** most directly reduces forced dependence.[S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33][S34][S35][S36][S39][S139][S45][S50][S52][S197][S54]

### 7. Rail-and-gating block

Every case memo should say which parts of the proposed package:
- can **begin now**,
- should **build rails while moving**,
- or remain **gated** until visibility, delivery, valuation, integrity, or enforcement become real enough to carry them.[S04][S15][S23][S31]

This is where case work should stay honest.
A package counts less when it quietly assumes capacities the state does not yet have, and it also counts less when weak capacity is used as an excuse to postpone the easiest floor, tenant, labor, or entry-opening moves.[S04][S07][S09][S12][S21][S22][S23][S24][S25][S31][S39]

### 8. Confidence-label block

Before the memo closes, add one short confidence block using [`confidence-labels-and-action-under-proof-debt.md`](confidence-labels-and-action-under-proof-debt.md).
That block should label separately:
1. **verdict confidence**,
2. **package confidence**,
3. **rail confidence**,
4. and **pass / de-escalation confidence**.

The archive should not let confidence leak across those questions.
A memo can be quite confident that the case is still failing while only moderately confident that one package cleanly beats the next-best option, and it can be less confident still that current rails are ready to deliver or that softer certification is warranted.

### 9. Next-scoreboard and proof-debt block

End the memo with two short lists:
1. the **next scoreboard** that should be watched over the next durability window,
2. and the **evidence debt** that still blocks a cleaner pass or a cleaner de-escalation.

Use [`next-scoreboard-and-evidence-debt-selection.md`](next-scoreboard-and-evidence-debt-selection.md) to keep those lists thin: the next scoreboard should usually be only three to six live watch lines, and the evidence debt should usually be only the missing proof that could still move the verdict, package, rails, or softer-certification claim. For provisional near-passes, proposed de-escalations, or cases that look close enough to certify, use [`provisional-passes-and-reopening-triggers.md`](provisional-passes-and-reopening-triggers.md) to say which surface would reopen correction and which stronger trigger would justify hardening beyond correction again. If the package is already underway and missing, use [`package-monitoring-and-reroute-triggers.md`](package-monitoring-and-reroute-triggers.md) to say which live failure would justify fast reroute before the whole verdict shifts. If the package is already underway and landing, use [`hardening-gains-into-ratchets.md`](hardening-gains-into-ratchets.md) to say which gain is now real enough to defend and what hardening move is due before softer certification is spoken too easily.

### 10. Timing block

After the scoreboard and the evidence debt are chosen, add three timing lines using [`review-cadence-and-durability-window-selection.md`](review-cadence-and-durability-window-selection.md):
1. **review cadence**,
2. **reroute horizon**,
3. **durability window**.

Keep this block narrow.
It should say when the case will be looked at again, how long continued miss is allowed before the package is revised, and what ordinary turnover or pressure still has to be survived before softer language deserves more weight.
If the memo will later be reused rather than freshly rewritten, apply [`case-staleness-and-recertification.md`](case-staleness-and-recertification.md) before carrying forward anything softer than the still-live part of its verdict.

The archive should not let missingness operate as permission to stay comfortable.
If top-tail ownership, transfer pathways, controller visibility, group disaggregation, or person-level access remain unclear, the memo should say so plainly and treat that uncertainty as part of the verdict discipline rather than as a reason to go soft.[S15][S23][S31][S32][S33]

## Minimal reusable template

A future case memo can often stay narrow by using only these headings:

- **Case and date**
- **Verdict**
- **Target distance**
- **Dominant breach**
- **Fastest washout**
- **Mode**
- **Opening package**
- **Rail / gating note**
- **Confidence labels**
- **Next scoreboard**
- **Evidence debt**
- **Review cadence / reroute horizon / durability window**

If those twelve lines cannot be filled cleanly, the archive usually does **not** need more rhetoric yet.
It needs better evidence, cleaner diagnosis, or a more honest account of rail limits. If the memo seems to teach a wider recurring rule, first use [`cross-case-decision-path.md`](cross-case-decision-path.md) and, if a retained comparison is warranted, shape it with [`cross-case-application-protocol.md`](cross-case-application-protocol.md) before routing any wider lesson through [`../00-meta/case-to-doctrine-promotion-and-quarantine.md`](../00-meta/case-to-doctrine-promotion-and-quarantine.md) rather than letting the memo rewrite archive doctrine by drift. If repeated memos or stronger evidence instead seem to strain an already-live archive rule, route that repair question through [`../00-meta/doctrine-revision-and-demotion-under-case-pressure.md`](../00-meta/doctrine-revision-and-demotion-under-case-pressure.md) rather than normalizing the strain as an unwritten exception.

## The main false passes

### 1. The memo rewrites the archive instead of applying it

A case memo fails compression discipline when it spends most of its space re-describing the whole theory rather than stating what is special about the case in front of it.
The archive should keep permanent theory in the stable notes and keep case work mostly to **application, diagnosis, and next moves**.

### 2. The memo starts with favorite instruments rather than the live breach

Case work fails when it begins from a preferred tax, fund, or ownership vehicle before saying what the case is actually failing on.
That invites ideological repetition rather than diagnosis.[S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23]

### 3. The memo mistakes a flattering aggregate for a passable floor

A case memo should count less seriousness when home values, pension claims, household averages, or a softened national share series are allowed to stand in for resilient, person-usable footholds at the lower half.[S13][S17][S18][S19][S21][S22][S27][S28][S29][S30][S41]

### 4. The memo treats an announced package as a governed package

Case work should distinguish legislation, aspiration, and implementation.
If delivery, visibility, anti-capture, or recovery rails are missing, the memo should say so rather than crediting the case for a paper package it cannot yet carry.[S04][S15][S23][S31][S32][S33]

### 5. The memo lets uncertainty soften the verdict

Where ownership is hidden, transfers are obscured, beneficial control is unclear, or excluded-group access is weakly measured, the archive should usually become **less** willing to certify the case, not more willing.[S15][S23][S31][S32][S33]

### 6. The memo lists too many priorities to govern

A case memo should usually leave the reader with **one verdict, one or two dominant breaches, one fastest washout channel, one mode, and one opening package**.
When everything is equally urgent on paper, nothing is actually sequenced.

## Fast use rule

Use this note in seven steps:

1. **Write the verdict first.**  
   Do not make the reader infer the archive's constitutional judgment.
2. **Keep the target block compact.**  
   Shares and public/common counterweight belong near the top, but they are not the whole memo.
3. **Name the dominant breach and the fastest washout separately.**  
   They are often related, but they are not always the same thing.
4. **Choose the mode before naming the package.**  
   The same instruments change meaning across prevention, correction, and anti-oligarchy response.
5. **State the rail and gating limits plainly.**  
   A serious memo says what can begin now, what must build rails while moving, and what still cannot honestly be sold as ready.
6. **Add the confidence labels.**  
   Separate verdict confidence, package confidence, rail confidence, and pass / de-escalation confidence rather than letting them blur together.
7. **End with the scoreboard and the proof debt.**  
   That keeps the memo tethered to what would actually upgrade or downgrade the verdict later.
8. **Add the timing burden.**  
   Say how often the case will be reviewed, when continued miss forces reroute, and what ordinary pressure still blocks softer language.

## Bottom line

The archive should now be able to produce much thinner real-case memos without becoming vague.
A good case memo is **not** a miniature manifesto.
It is a short application of the archive's standing target, verdict rule, washout logic, mode rule, and implementation screen to one concrete case.
That is how the archive stays cumulative instead of turning every new case into another full rewrite.[S02][S13][S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33]
