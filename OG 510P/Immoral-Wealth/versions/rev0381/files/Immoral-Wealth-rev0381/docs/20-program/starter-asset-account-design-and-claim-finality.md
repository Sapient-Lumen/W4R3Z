# Starter asset account design and claim finality

Starter accounts can be valuable because they place assets at a threshold moment. But they can also become a false pass if accounts are lost, regressive, too small, too locked-up, captured by better-off families’ contributions, or inaccessible to the people they were supposed to help.

The UK Child Trust Fund experience is the archive’s warning case: millions of accounts were opened, but substantial matured balances remained unclaimed and the tracking system itself became a policy problem.[S133][S134] The current U.S. Trump Accounts pilot creates a different design surface: a federal seed contribution for eligible children born in 2025-2028, with citizenship/SSN and election rules that require conversion scrutiny.[S137][S138]

## Pass conditions

A starter account counts as a real foothold only when:

1. the account is opened automatically or with near-zero friction;
2. the account is in the child/person’s name, not merely controlled by household gatekeepers;
3. the public seed is large enough or progressive enough to matter;
4. private contributions do not turn the account into a subsidy amplifier for already-wealthy families;
5. default investment is low-cost, diversified, and protected from predatory fees;
6. the account can be found at maturity;
7. the claimant receives repeated notice in usable channels;
8. claims are no-fee and do not require paid intermediaries;
9. the asset is protected from inappropriate seizure or household capture;
10. permitted uses match real threshold moments: education, training, housing deposit, business stake, relocation, disability adaptation, safe exit, or long-term retirement security.

## Design warnings

### Lost-account warning

An account that exists but cannot be located is not a delivered asset. Claim finality requires account tracing, address updates, provider continuity, clear public lookup tools, school/community outreach, and automatic matured-account reunification where possible.[S133][S134]

### Regressive contribution warning

Universal seed accounts can become regressive if high-income families can contribute large amounts while low-income families cannot. Progressive public matching, caps, or supplemental deposits may be needed to prevent the program from widening the very gap it claims to close.

### Citizenship and documentation warning

Eligibility based on citizenship, SSN, parental election, tax filing, or other documentation can exclude the children most exposed to poverty, migration instability, or informal household structures. Use the identity and conversion gates before counting the account as broad social inheritance.[S124][S137][S138]

### Timing warning

The account must mature when threshold moments occur. Locked retirement-only accounts may help lifetime security but cannot automatically substitute for first deposit, safe exit, training, or debt avoidance.

## Verdict use

Starter accounts rarely certify a whole wealth order. They are one lane inside open circulation. They can improve an otherwise poor order, but they cannot offset housing closure, group closure, fee leakage, weak floors, or top-tail rule on their own.
