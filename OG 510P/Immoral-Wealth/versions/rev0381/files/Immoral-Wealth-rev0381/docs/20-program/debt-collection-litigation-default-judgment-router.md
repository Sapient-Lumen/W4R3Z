# Debt-collection litigation and default-judgment router

Use this router when the fastest washout is a court judgment rather than the original debt.

## Router questions

- Was the person actually served?
- Does the complaint identify the original creditor, chain of title, itemization, and limitations period?
- Can the person answer without a lawyer?
- Are remote hearings, interpreters, plain-language forms, and legal aid available?
- Are default judgments automatically reviewed for service and proof defects?
- Can judgments be reopened when notice, identity, debt ownership, or amount is wrong?
- Does post-judgment collection preserve the recovery floor?

## Preferred reforms

Verified complaints, service audits, answer-by-phone/online routes, legal information at summons, default-judgment review, debt-buyer proof standards, interest caps, renewal limits, hardship installment plans, and one-stop judgment repair.[S307][S308][S304]
