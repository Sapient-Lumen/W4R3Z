# Public balance-sheet evidence pack

For any Gate 20 case, collect:

- comprehensive financial statements and long-term fiscal projections;
- debt held by public, gross debt, maturity, interest-rate sensitivity, and net interest;
- tax-expenditure and direct-spending comparisons;
- social-insurance actuarial reports and depletion dates;
- public-pension actuarial reports, funded ratios, discount rates, contribution discipline, and tiering;
- deposit-insurance fund balance, reserve ratio, assessments, systemic-risk exceptions, and loss recoveries;
- loan and guarantee exposure by program;
- crisis-facility terms, collateral, rates, counterparties, losses, and sunset;
- public asset/sovereign fund mandate, returns, benchmark, withdrawals, governance, and claimant link;
- distributional incidence of taxes, fees, benefit cuts, interest costs, and public asset payouts;
- evidence of who receives rescue priority in stress.[S345][S346][S351][S355][S357][S363][S369]

Missing public-balance-sheet evidence should block a comfort verdict when the case relies on public claims or public backstops.
