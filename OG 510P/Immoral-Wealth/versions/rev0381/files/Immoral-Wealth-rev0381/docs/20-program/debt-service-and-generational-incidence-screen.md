# Debt-service and generational-incidence screen

Use this screen when fiscal sustainability is invoked to justify cuts, taxes, public investment, or delay.

## Questions

1. Is debt financing building assets that lower future private costs or merely covering recurrent gaps?
2. Is interest cost rising faster than revenue and GDP?
3. Which programs are crowded out first?
4. Are tax expenditures protected while direct programs are cut?
5. Does adjustment fall on younger cohorts, renters, caregivers, disabled people, migrants, or low-wealth regions?
6. Are public investments with high distributional value protected from austerity?
7. Are creditors, asset holders, or firms receiving backstop protection while households face fees and service cuts?[S346][S347][S349]

Do not treat fiscal repair as neutral. The repair path itself can be a wealth-order mechanism.
