# Garnishment, levy, and judgment protection rails

Use this router when recovery is blocked by post-judgment collection.

## Rail requirements

- self-executing wage and bank-account minima;
- protection of exempt public benefits after deposit;
- plain-language and multilingual exemption notices;
- employer and bank compliance rules;
- realistic car, tools, household goods, and home-equity exemptions;
- limits on post-judgment interest and renewal;
- judgment-vacatur and hardship modification routes;
- proof-of-debt ownership and itemization before judgment;
- data on garnishment orders, bank freezes, default judgments, and recovery outcomes.[S304][S305][S306][S320]

If a debtor must know court doctrine, file forms quickly, attend a hearing, and prove exempt funds before rent is due, the rail is weak.
