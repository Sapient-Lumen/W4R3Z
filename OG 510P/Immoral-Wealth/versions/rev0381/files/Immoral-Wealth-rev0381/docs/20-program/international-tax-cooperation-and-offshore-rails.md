# International tax cooperation and offshore rails

Top-tail compression and public-counterweight finance cannot be treated as purely domestic when wealth can move through accounts, entities, trusts, digital assets, and multinational profit-shifting structures.

## Rail families

1. **Exchange of information on request** — ownership, banking, and accounting records obtainable by tax authorities.[S178]
2. **Automatic exchange of information** — annual exchange of predefined financial-account data under CRS/AEOI.[S180][S181]
3. **Beneficial ownership transparency** — legal persons, legal arrangements, verification, access rights, and enforcement.[S179]
4. **Global minimum tax / Pillar Two** — corporate profit-shifting floor, QDMTT primacy, safe harbours, and compliance simplification.[S166]
5. **UN tax cooperation** — broader forum for inclusive tax cooperation and developing-country voice.[S167]
6. **Asset recovery** — tracing, freezing, confiscating, managing, and returning stolen assets.[S182]

## Certification rules

- A country with high offshore exposure cannot claim a stable top-tail reading without exchange-of-information and beneficial-owner evidence.
- A domestic wealth tax or inheritance tax cannot receive an easy pass if exit/residence planning is untreated.
- A country dependent on corporate tax revenues cannot ignore Pillar Two, tax incentives, and profit-shifting.
- Developing-country cases should not be judged only by OECD coordination; UN processes and capacity-building matter.

## Proof-debt fields

- CRS/AEOI legal framework status;
- effectiveness rating or peer-review status;
- BO registry coverage and verification;
- trust/legal-arrangement coverage;
- crypto-asset reporting readiness;
- QDMTT and global minimum tax implementation;
- exchange partner network;
- asset-recovery tools and actual recoveries.
