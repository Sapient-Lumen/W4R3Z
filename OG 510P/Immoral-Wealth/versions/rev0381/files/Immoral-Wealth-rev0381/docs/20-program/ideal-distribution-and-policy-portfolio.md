# Ideal distribution and policy portfolio

## What this note is for

This note turns the archive's most direct answer into a **fuller stable direct-answer stack**.
It answers the archive's central practical question in one place:
**what wealth distribution should count as near-ideal, and how should a society move toward it without one-instrument fantasy, sloganistic equalism, or implementation theater?**

The archive's answer is **not** one sacred tuple, one flagship tax, or one heroic public fund.
It is a **bounded-top, thick-middle, real-floor, open-circulation order** paired with a **nearest-real-move program** that starts from the live breach, closes the fastest washout channel, and hardens the gains before the next cycle reverses them.[S02][S03][S13][S142][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33][S54]

It therefore also carries the archive's **smallest reusable breach-to-package map**, a compact **ideal-order pass bundle**, a compact **anti-flattery composition check**, a compact **excluded-group anti-averaging screen**, a compact **live proof panel**, a compact **proof-debt default under opacity**, and a compact **verdict strip** so front-door notes can point here instead of restating the same opening mixes in looser form.[S13][S15][S142][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S26][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S39][S139][S54]

## Direct answer in one screen

> **Epistemic status of the numbers.** The `20 / 50 / 20 / 10` center, its working bands, and the `top 0.1% <= 3%` threshold are archive-authored **normative proposals and sensitivity screens**. The cited WID/OECD sources inform observed distributions, concepts, and measurement choices; they do not by themselves derive or validate these exact moral thresholds. A future target-provenance review must compare rival ethical principles, public-wealth regimes, measurement perimeters, and feasible transition paths.

**Target family, not sacred point:** treat **20 / 50 / 20 / 10** as the archive's center for the bottom 50 / middle 40 / next 9 / top 1, read **15-25 / 45-55 / 15-25 / 8-15** as the rough working bands, aim for **3% or less** for the top **0.1%**, and require **clearly positive public/social wealth**. Read that four-part tuple as a map of **total private wealth shares**; public/social wealth is a separate required co-condition and counterweight rather than another slice inside the tuple. Use **household-based private net wealth shares** as the default comparable measurement lens, then force those through a **person-level title, control, and exit screen** so household totals do not masquerade as broad freedom.[S02][S03][S13][S142][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S27][S28][S29][S30]
Do **not** rank cases by raw distance from that center. When two cases sit in or near the acceptable family, prefer the one with the stronger real floor, earlier person-level footholds, weaker wealth-to-rule conversion, and stronger public/common counterweight even if its share table sits a bit farther from `20 / 50 / 20 / 10`; target distance is a **late tie-breaker after the constitutional screens**, not the archive's moral score.[S02][S03][S13][S142][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23]

**Near-ideal means six simultaneous passes, not one nice share table:** do **not** certify a case from shares alone. A case earns **near-ideal / acceptable** language only when all six of the archive's pass-bundle conditions substantially hold together: bounded distributional shape, a **real lower-half floor** that is actually **claimable, keepable, not conditioned on remaining nearly assetless or liquidating modest footholds before help arrives, not abruptly clawed back by sharp withdrawal cliffs on modest gains, ordinary-time usable rather than future-only or sale-contingent, ordinary-shock survivable, not quietly self-eroding under ordinary carrying costs or fee drag, and operable enough that ordinary holders do not need unusual legal, tax, or financial fluency or paid professional rescue just to preserve most of what is already supposed to be theirs, cheaply provable and updateable enough that ordinary holders can retrieve and update the records, certificates, beneficiary entries, co-title evidence, or similar proof they need without repeated brokerage, long-distance travel, specialist help, or prohibitive fees, and challenge-safe enough that ordinary use, complaint, appeal, organizing, separation, or exit does not itself predictably trigger reprisal before neutral protection can hold, and not mainly organized around unusually large private defensive buffers just to survive predictable illness, care, housing, earnings-interruption, or old-age risks because common insurance is too weak or late** in ordinary life, **open early circulation** that stays person-level, portable, **ordinary-exit compatible**, and non-forfeiting across ordinary job, provider, place, or household transitions rather than reset-prone or penalty-laden, not still family-gated through parental deposits, guarantees, inherited housing position, or comparable threshold backing, and territorially real rather than **place-closed** or bought mainly through punishing daily access burdens to the main compounding places, meaningfully **closed washout channels**, **bounded top-tail command**, and a real **public/common counterweight** with durability and broad rule-bound access/governance rather than discretionary patronage.[S02][S03][S13][S15][S142][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S24][S25][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S39][S139][S41][S45][S50][S51][S54]
A nominal or generous-looking statutory floor does **not** count as a real-floor pass when support is hard to discover, slow to claim, churn-heavy, humiliating to renew, or too expert-dependent to keep active in ordinary life.[S17][S21][S22][S24][S25][S39][S139][S45][S50]
**Support that still demands near-assetlessness does not clear the floor either:** count the case less generously when essential help still requires households to liquidate modest emergency reserves, child accounts, simple productive tools, safer transport, or other first footholds before support begins or continues. Treat that as **spend-down-trapped** rather than as a clean floor pass.[S17][S31][S39][S139]
**Sharp withdrawal cliffs do not clear the floor either:** count the case less generously when modest earnings, hours, or savings gains trigger abrupt loss, delay, or heavy dilution of essential support instead of smooth tapers, grace-period continuation, or partial disregards. Treat that as **cliff-punished** rather than as a clean floor pass.[S17][S31][S39][S139]
**Locked claims do not clear the floor or circulation pass either:** count the case less generously when the apparent foothold exists mainly behind future-only timing, hardship-gated access, narrow purpose-binding, or sale/refinance dependence on the core foothold itself. Paper wealth that cannot be used in ordinary time without heavy leakage, exceptional triggers, or destabilising the main asset should be treated as **locked-up** rather than as a clean floor or circulation pass.[S13][S17][S19][S41]
**Calm-weather footholds do not clear the floor by themselves:** count the case less generously when a short income interruption, a mild rate reset, a modest local price correction, or a predictable repair or care bill can quickly push households back into arrears, forced sale, destructive borrowing, or near-zero. Apparent lower-half gains that disappear under ordinary stress should be treated as **shock-fragile** rather than as a clean floor or circulation pass.[S07][S17][S21][S22][S24][S25][S45][S50][S51]
**Expert-maze footholds do not clear the floor either:** count the case less generously when preserving most of the claim's value requires decoding opaque option menus, timing tricky elections, avoiding hidden penalties, or paying for professional rescue that ordinary low- and middle-wealth holders cannot cheaply access or supervise. Apparent lower-half gains that still work mainly for people who can decode a maze should be treated as **maze-dependent** rather than as a clean floor or circulation pass.[S139][S45]
**Proof-fragile claims do not clear the floor either:** count the case less generously when ordinary holders cannot cheaply prove, retrieve, or update the claim once conflict, succession, separation, correction, or ordinary life change requires it. Apparent lower-half gains that turn into a records hunt requiring repeated brokerage, long-distance travel, specialist help, or prohibitive fees should be treated as **proof-brittle** rather than as a clean floor or circulation pass.[S28][S29][S139][S44]
**Formally appealable but functionally late remedies do not clear continuity either:** count the case less generously when ordinary disputes, clerical errors, status changes, or provider changes can interrupt practical control long enough to force dependence, retreat, or acceptance of bad terms before correction arrives. A formally available review or appeal that comes too late to preserve ordinary continuity should be treated as **late-remedy** rather than as a clean floor or circulation pass.[S28][S29][S30][S139][S44]
**Claims that work only while the holder stays quiet do not clear continuity either:** count the case less generously when ordinary use, complaint, appeal, organizing, separation, or exit predictably invites firing, eviction, household retaliation, benefit suspension, account lockout, blacklist, or similar punishment before neutral protection can hold. Apparent lower-half gains that survive only for people who stay compliant should be treated as **reprisal-exposed** rather than as a clean floor or circulation pass.[S08][S09][S11][S14][S28][S29][S30][S139][S41][S44]
**Portable-looking claims that reset or forfeit on ordinary movement do not clear circulation either:** count the case less generously when changing employer, provider, sector, place, or household status restarts vesting clocks, strands records, wipes prior matches, forces destructive liquidation, or imposes large transition penalties just to keep what is already supposed to belong to the holder. Apparent lower-half gains that work mainly for stayers should be treated as **reset-prone** rather than as a clean floor or circulation pass.[S28][S29][S30][S41]
**Cheap footholds in weak-opportunity places do not clear circulation either:** count the case less generously when apparent lower-half access is bought mainly by pushing households far from the main labor, education, care, or public-service nodes or by forcing punishing travel times, high transport costs, or brittle commuting chains in daily life. Cheap footholds in weak-opportunity places do **not** fully substitute for secure access to the main compounding places; treat that as **place-closed** rather than as a clean floor or circulation pass.[S07][S19][S48]

**Family-backed thresholds do not clear circulation either:** count the case less generously when ordinary entry still depends on parental deposits, guarantees, inherited housing position, or comparable family backing at the threshold moment. Apparent early circulation that still routes entrants through family balance sheets should be treated as **family-gated** rather than as a clean floor or circulation pass.[S05][S18][S19][S24][S52]

**Quietly self-eroding footholds do not clear the floor either:** count the case less generously when insurance, taxes, utilities, upkeep, platform or management fees, mandatory charges, or similar recurring carrying costs steadily eat most of the practical gain away even without a fresh shock. Apparent lower-half gains that require continuous new subsidy or repeated private sacrifice just to stand still should be treated as **self-eroding** rather than as a clean floor or circulation pass.[S07][S17][S21][S22][S24][S25][S42][S45]

**Forced defensive buffers do not clear the floor either:** count the case less generously when ordinary households must hold unusually large private buffers just to survive predictable illness, care, housing, earnings-interruption, or old-age risks because common insurance is too weak, too late, or too thin. Apparent lower-half wealth doing mainly defensive work that a decent common floor should already absorb should be treated as **buffer-coerced** rather than as a clean floor or circulation pass.[S07][S17][S21][S22]
**One thin household foothold does not clear the floor either:** count the case less generously when apparent lower-half adequacy depends mainly on overcrowding, delayed independent exit, or claimant dilution rather than enough room, privacy, margin, and direct claims per person. One modest shared foothold carrying several adults should not be treated as equivalent to several person-adequate footholds; treat that as **claimant-diluted** rather than as a clean floor or circulation pass.[S13][S19][S27][S43]
A real-floor pass should also be read as a **supermajority-plus-cohort-guardrails** claim, not as a bare-majority or average-balance claim: ordinary security should be broad across the bottom half, the next adult cohort should not sit far weaker than the all-age picture, and major excluded groups should not remain durably well below the national floor pass rate.[S142][S17][S18][S19][S25][S52]
**Nominally broad but manager-bottlenecked claims do not clear circulation or counterweight pass either:** count the case less generously when voting, stewardship, or practical control in pooled or intermediated ownership remains bottlenecked inside a narrow manager, trustee, or similar intermediary layer that ordinary holders cannot meaningfully inspect, direct, or replace. Apparent democratization that leaves the control chain essentially unchanged should be treated as **manager-bottlenecked** rather than as a clean circulation or counterweight pass.[S20][S23][S26]
**Low-gatekeeper continuity matters:** a nominal foothold counts less when one employer, provider, household intermediary, or sponsor can still freeze, stall, or materially condition its practical use, especially across ordinary exit, switching, separation, or place movement. Broadening that survives only while the holder stays with one gatekeeper is not yet a clean circulation pass.[S28][S29][S30][S139][S41]
**Public/common counterweight should not become substitute patronage:** count the case less generously when ordinary access, allocation, or continuity depends mainly on party, patron, employer, landlord, or local-broker discretion rather than on broad rule-bound entitlement, universal service, or democratically governed common provision. That is not yet a clean counterweight pass; it is hierarchy rerouted through another gate.[S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33][S39][S139]
If even one of those conditions remains badly broken, the case may still matter morally and may still be improving, but it has **not yet earned** the archive's near-ideal label.[S13][S15][S142][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33]
And even when the six-part bundle looks good in one reading, near-ideal language should wait for a **window-plus-ratchet pass**: the order should survive an ordinary political-budget cycle and an ordinary asset/credit cycle, and at least some important reversal channels should already be materially harder to use than before, rather than the case merely enjoying one friendly snapshot.[S20][S21][S22][S31][S32][S33]
**Aggregate pass does not override group closure:** a good national tuple, better household totals, or a stronger headline floor still do **not** clear the front door when historically subordinated groups remain predictably outside the real-floor, early-foothold, person-level-control, or ordinary-exit conditions on materially worse terms. Near-ideal language should stay blocked until that patterned exclusion stops governing the order.[S13][S15][S18][S19][S27][S28][S29][S30][S39][S139]
**Certification is bottlenecked, not averaged:** read the verdict from the **weakest still-live decisive surface** rather than from average progress across lanes. A case with a good tuple, stronger public/common wealth, and visible bottom-half gains still stays below near-ideal when one decisive surface remains broken — for example a thin real floor, late or household-masked footholds, live wealth-to-rule, or unresolved excluded-group closure. Stronger lanes elsewhere change routing and priority, not the certification rule.[S13][S15][S142][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S39][S139][S50][S54]
**Statuses below near-ideal should name their blocker:** do **not** leave **correction-required** or **provisional near-pass** floating as generic praise. Pair the verdict with the live blocking surface — paper-floor, floor-thin, spend-down-trapped, cliff-punished, locked-up, shock-fragile, self-eroding, buffer-coerced, maze-dependent, proof-brittle, late-remedy, reprisal-exposed, seizure-exposed, claimant-diluted, family-gated, gatekeeper-dependent, reset-prone, manager-bottlenecked, patronage-routed, late-entry, excluded-group closure, top-tail rule, opacity-blocked, or durability-not-yet-earned — so certification language still points to the next governing task.[S13][S15][S18][S19][S20][S23][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S39][S139][S41][S44]

**How to get there:** move through a **seven-lane stack** that lowers forced private exposure, rebuilds earnings power, moves footholds earlier, blocks housing and monopoly washout, compresses dynastic concentration, and hardens public/common anti-capture counterweight. The archive does **not** treat one tax, one public fund, one homeownership tool, or one wage reform as a sufficient answer on its own.[S04][S07][S08][S09][S10][S12][S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33][S34][S36]

**How to choose the first serious package:** start from the **dominant live breach** and the **fastest washout channel**. Floor-failure cases usually open with lanes **2, 3, and 5** while opening lane **4** early; late-entry cases usually open with **4 and 5**; rent-washout cases usually push **5** with support from **2, 3, and 7**; top-tail-rule cases usually push **6 and 7** much harder and earlier; thin-public-counterweight cases usually strengthen **7** together with **5** and whichever of **2-4** most directly reduces forced dependence.[S15][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33][S34][S35][S36][S39][S139][S45][S50][S52][S197][S54]

**How to choose prevention versus response posture:** keep the **constitutional target fixed** across modes. Use **prevention** only when there is **no major constitutional breach**, closure and capture are still weak enough that broadening can outrun hardening, and ordinary democratic correction is materially available on real rails; use **correction** when one major breach or several reinforcing moderate breaches are already live but ordinary democratic correction is still materially possible; use **anti-oligarchy response** when multiple major breaches, or one major breach plus strong **wealth-to-rule**, opacity, or veto power, mean anti-capture and deconcentration can no longer wait.[S05][S06][S13][S15][S18][S19][S20][S23][S31][S32][S33]
A harder posture must **not** crowd out floor repair, entrant protection, or ordinary-life continuity, and a softer posture must **not** postpone anti-capture, legibility, or anti-dynasty work once those have become structurally necessary.[S07][S15][S18][S19][S23][S24][S25][S31][S32][S33]

**Main false comforts:** count a case less generously when lower-half gains are mainly leveraged housing, debt-offset paper, or locked claims; when household totals hide weak person-level title or exit; when historically subordinated groups remain predictably later, thinner, less secure, or more reversible in the main asset channels; when the top tail still converts wealth into rule; or when controller, transfer, valuation, or influence visibility remains too weak to support calm certification.[S04][S07][S13][S15][S142][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S26][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S36][S39][S139]
Under that kind of proof debt, keep the case at **provisional near-pass** or below unless affirmative proof clears the decisive surfaces, while still letting floor repair, washout closure, registry-building, and other lower-regret correction move now.[S04][S07][S15][S21][S22][S23][S31][S39][S139]

**Bottom line:** the archive's answer is neither **pick nicer numbers** nor **pick one heroic instrument**. It is: **choose a near-ideal constitutional distribution, test it through the six-part pass bundle plus the archive's anti-averaging vetoes, choose the softest governing posture that still matches the live breach, and move toward the target through the lane mix that most directly closes the breach, blocks the fastest washout, and keeps the same gains from being hidden, delayed, or pulled back upward.**

## Smallest front-door use order

When the archive has to answer its two central questions quickly without rebuilding the whole stack, use the compact front-door blocks in this order:

1. **Name the target family first.** Default to `20 / 50 / 20 / 10` and move to an off-center acceptable envelope only when the case has really earned it rather than when the story is merely flattering or substitution-heavy.[S02][S03][S13][S142][S18][S19][S20][S23]
2. **Run the pass bundle together with the composition and group-closure screens.** Ask at the same time whether the case sits inside the acceptable family, what kind of wealth, title, control, or public counterweight is actually doing the work, and whether historically subordinated groups are reaching those footholds on anything like civic-equal terms.[S13][S142][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S26][S27][S28][S29][S30]
3. **Test claimed movement with the proof panel.** Force one line on the dominant breach, the fastest washout, the next-gain incidence surface, the top-tail or control surface, and the durability or reopen surface before calling the move real.[S07][S10][S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33]
4. **Apply the opacity asymmetry before comfort.** Missing controller, transfer, valuation, or influence visibility should harden certification more than it hardens lower-regret correction.[S04][S07][S15][S21][S22][S23][S31][S39][S139]
5. **Name the live verdict plainly.** Call the case **still failing**, **correction-required**, **provisional near-pass**, or **near-ideal / acceptable** rather than hiding behind vague improvement language.[S02][S03][S13][S15][S142][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33]
6. **Only then compose the opening package.** Route from the dominant breach and the fastest washout channel, then add the smallest durability lane that makes quick reversal harder.[S15][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33]

This is a sequencing rule for the archive's existing front-door kit, not a new doctrine branch. When the job turns into live diagnosis, package comparison, or contested certification, continue outward to the case-work path and the fuller anchors.

## Smallest reusable breach-to-package map

Before using the numbered map, keep this compact lane legend in view:

- **1 — target lane:** set the acceptable family, warning bands, and non-substitution rule.[S02][S03][S13][S142][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23]
- **2 — floor / claimability lane:** lower forced private exposure, protect modest buffers, and make the floor actually reachable and keepable.[S17][S21][S22][S39][S139][S41][S45][S50]
- **3 — earnings / saveable-room lane:** rebuild bargaining power, wage floors, hours security, and the share of ordinary income that can become durable saving.[S08][S09][S10][S11][S14]
- **4 — early-footholds lane:** move first durable claims earlier and keep them person-level, portable, and durable.[S12][S18][S19][S27][S28][S29][S30][S41][S47][S52]
- **5 — washout-closure lane:** stop housing, land, debt, fee, markup, and chokepoint reabsorption.[S07][S10][S13][S17][S20][S31][S34][S35][S36][S46][S197]
- **6 — top-tail compression lane:** weaken dynastic concentration, avoidance, and wealth-to-rule conversion.[S04][S05][S06][S18][S19][S23][S38][S54]
- **7 — public/common durability lane:** build public/social counterweight, legibility, and anti-capture firewalls strong enough to hold gains.[S02][S12][S15][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33][S54]

The map below uses those lane labels in compressed form so the note can stay self-contained at the front door; the fuller lane definitions remain in **The standing direct-answer stack** below.

Treat the archive's answer as **one target plus one compact routing map**:

- **floor failure** -> lead with **2**, force in **5**, add **3** early when weak earnings keep the floor thin, and use **7** where the gain would otherwise be easy to claw back.[S07][S17][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S36][S39][S139][S45][S50]
- **weak earnings / no saveable room** -> lead with **3**, force in **5** when rents, fees, or markups eat the gain, add **2** when current insecurity prevents households from holding wage gains, and use **7** where public underprovision would re-privatize the risk.[S08][S09][S10][S11][S14][S17][S20][S21][S22][S31][S36]
- **late entry / dynastic timing** -> lead with **4**, force in **5** so footholds do not just capitalise into closed places or incumbent prices, harden with **6** where inheritance is the real blocker, and add **2** when recipients could not realistically hold the foothold once received.[S07][S12][S18][S19][S20][S23][S27][S28][S29][S30][S34][S35][S36][S41][S47][S52][S197]
- **rent washout / closed opportunity** -> lead with **5**, support it with **2** and **3** so households can survive while closure is being forced, use **7** to keep reopened value from being quietly re-privatised, and move **6** earlier when rent extraction is protected by dynastic or political concentration.[S07][S10][S12][S17][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S34][S35][S36][S197][S54]
- **top-tail rule / oligarchic conversion** -> lead with **6** and **7** together, close whichever opacity, monopoly, procurement, or crisis-laundering channel is protecting the top tail, and keep **2** or **3** moving so ordinary households are not asked to bear the whole transition.[S04][S05][S06][S15][S18][S19][S20][S23][S31][S32][S33][S38][S54]
- **thin public/common counterweight** -> lead with **7**, force in **5** when public effort would otherwise capitalise into private land or monopoly gains, and pair it with whichever of **2-4** most directly lowers forced dependence now.[S02][S07][S12][S18][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S34][S36][S54]

For mixed cases, keep one rule: **lead with the breach doing the most mass constitutional damage, force in the fastest washout closure, and add the smallest durability lane that stops quick reversal.** For the four-slot operator form of that same rule, use [`opening-package-router.md`](opening-package-router.md).[S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33]

## Ideal-order pass bundle

Treat **ideal distribution** as a **simultaneous constitutional pass bundle**, not as a share tuple that can excuse live failure elsewhere. A case should count as genuinely near-ideal only when **all six** of the following are substantially true at once:

1. **Bounded distributional shape.** The society sits inside the archive's narrow acceptable family, does not trip major warning bands, and is not asking one stronger dimension to excuse a badly broken one.[S02][S03][S13][S142][S20]
2. **Real lower-half floor.** The bottom half clears a resilient person-usable floor in net wealth, liquidity, secure tenure, protected claims, and ordinary shock survivability, and that floor is actually claimable and keepable under ordinary administrative conditions, is not quietly self-eroding under recurring carrying costs or fee drag, does not mainly require unusually large private defensive buffers just to survive predictable illness, care, housing, earnings-interruption, or old-age risks because common insurance is too weak or late, is not being flattered by one thin household foothold carrying too many adults or dependents through overcrowding, delayed exit, or claimant dilution, is challenge-safe enough that ordinary use, complaint, appeal, organizing, separation, or exit does not itself predictably trigger reprisal before neutral protection can hold, and is protected enough that modest reserves, essential-use assets, and first long-run claims are not easy to sweep, garnish, set off, intercept, or force-sell away in the first ordinary debt conflict or left with too little protected restart wealth after debt repair, rather than existing mainly as nominal, churn-heavy protection on paper or as decorative or sweepable assets. Read that pass as a **supermajority-plus-cohort-guardrails** claim rather than as a bare majority or flattering average: the next adult cohort should not sit far weaker than the all-age bottom-half picture, and major excluded groups should not remain durably well below the headline pass rate.[S13][S142][S17][S18][S19][S21][S22][S24][S25][S27][S39][S139][S43][S45][S50][S51][S52]
3. **Open early circulation.** First durable footholds arrive early enough, stay person-level and portable, and no longer depend mainly on parental housing position, parental deposits, parental guarantees, late inheritance, or other dynastic timing advantages.[S12][S18][S19][S27][S28][S29][S30][S41][S47][S52]
4. **Closed washout channels.** Housing, land, debt, fees, markups, and chokepoint extraction no longer reabsorb most visible gains on the way down the distribution.[S07][S10][S17][S20][S31][S34][S35][S36][S46][S197]
5. **Bounded top-tail command.** Large fortunes cannot still convert wealth into rule through monopoly, opacity, procurement, media, rescue design, or dynastic closure even if their raw share has fallen somewhat.[S04][S05][S06][S15][S20][S23][S32][S33][S38][S54]
6. **Real public/common counterweight and durability.** Public/social wealth is clearly positive, legible, anti-captured, and broad and rule-bound enough that ordinary access, allocation, or continuity does not still depend mainly on patrons or brokers, and strong enough that lower-half security does not still depend on repeated private self-insurance or one fragile reform cycle.[S02][S12][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33][S39][S139][S54]

If even one of those conditions remains badly broken, the case may be improving and still matter morally, but it has **not yet earned** the archive's near-ideal label.

## Smallest anti-flattery composition check

When the archive has to say **what kind of wealth is actually doing the work** without dragging the whole composition branch into the front door, use this **four-part anti-flattery check**:

1. **Discount lower-half gains that are mostly thin or self-eroding.** Count the case less generously when bottom-half improvement is mainly leveraged housing equity, debt-offset balances, locked retirement claims, or other paper wealth that does little for present bargaining power, liquidity, or shock survival.[S13][S142][S17][S21][S22]
2. **Discount household totals that hide weak person-level control.** Household broadening counts for less when one adult lacks direct title, direct liquid access, direct retirement security, or credible exit rights even though the household balance sheet looks stronger.[S27][S28][S29][S30]
3. **Discount broadened claims when stewardship stays bottlenecked.** Broad portfolio ownership is only a partial pass when votes, engagement priorities, and governance power remain concentrated in a thin intermediary layer.[S20][S26]
4. **Discount decorative common wealth.** Public/social wealth should count less when it is illiquid, weakly usable, quietly captured, too thin to lower forced private buffering and rebalance command in ordinary life, or still routed mainly through discretionary patrons or brokers rather than broad rule-bound provision.[S02][S20][S21][S22][S23][S39][S139]

This keeps the front door from treating **better shares** as automatically **better asset reality**. For fuller composition, person-level title, and stewardship screens, continue to [`../10-framework/asset-composition-and-control.md`](../10-framework/asset-composition-and-control.md), [`../10-framework/individual-ownership-and-intra-household-power.md`](../10-framework/individual-ownership-and-intra-household-power.md), and [`../10-framework/intermediated-ownership-and-stewardship.md`](../10-framework/intermediated-ownership-and-stewardship.md).

## Excluded-group anti-averaging screen

When the archive has to ask **who is actually reaching and keeping the gains** without reopening the whole group-closure branch, use this compact **four-part anti-averaging screen**:

1. **Do not let aggregate improvement excuse patterned exclusion.** Count the case less generously when national shares or household totals improve while historically subordinated groups still reach main asset channels later, on thinner margins, or on more reversible terms.[S13][S18][S19][S27][S28][S29][S30]
2. **Do not treat household aggregation as civic equality.** Household-level gains count for less when person-level title, liquid access, exit, inheritance position, or retirement security remain predictably weaker within or across the groups being compared.[S27][S28][S29][S30]
3. **Do not count one better-situated subgroup as a group pass.** A case should not clear the front door because one subgroup or region has improved while the main excluded populations remain predictably blocked in the decisive channels.[S18][S19][S27][S28][S29][S30]
4. **Do not let missing disaggregation buy comfort.** When the decisive surfaces are not broken out cleanly enough to test group closure, keep the case below comfortable certification and treat the missingness as proof debt rather than as evidence of equality.[S15][S21][S22][S23][S27][S28][S29][S30][S39][S139]

This keeps the front door from laundering persistent exclusion through better national averages. For fuller group-closure, person-level, and life-course screens, continue to [`../10-framework/group-stratification-and-excluded-groups.md`](../10-framework/group-stratification-and-excluded-groups.md), [`../10-framework/individual-ownership-and-intra-household-power.md`](../10-framework/individual-ownership-and-intra-household-power.md), and [`../10-framework/life-course-timing-and-early-footholds.md`](../10-framework/life-course-timing-and-early-footholds.md).

## How to name the ideal family

When the archive has to **name** the near-ideal distribution in one line, use a **default-first, earned-off-center rule** rather than speaking as if every nearby envelope is equally primary.

1. **Name `20 / 50 / 20 / 10` first.** Treat the balanced broad-owner envelope as the archive's default public answer unless the case has a positive reason to move off-center.[S02][S03][S13][S142][S18][S19][S20]
2. **Move toward `15 / 55 / 20 / 10` only when the common side has truly earned it.** That means clearly positive and usable public/social wealth, a common floor that materially lowers required private buffers, real secure tenure, and no quiet dependence on family wealth to reach stable adulthood.[S02][S07][S18][S19][S21][S22]
3. **Move toward `25 / 45 / 20 / 10` only when the lower-half thickening is real.** That means earlier footholds, safer debt, liquid enough buffers, entrant-friendly access, and lower washout through housing, fees, leverage, or other quiet clawback.[S07][S13][S142][S17][S18][S19][S20]
4. **Snap back to the center when the justification is mixed, flattering, or substitution-heavy.** If the off-center story depends on decorative public wealth, leveraged housing wealth, household masking, late inheritance, or top-tail rule, do not present that envelope as the archive's ideal answer.[S05][S13][S142][S18][S19][S23]
5. **Never let envelope choice outrank the pass bundle.** The archive may sometimes name a different nearby envelope, but only after the six-part ideal-order pass bundle still looks substantially true.[S02][S03][S13][S142][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23]
6. **Treat target distance as a tie-breaker, not a ranking metric.** A case that sits slightly farther from the center but does better on floor reality, earlier person-level circulation, top-tail deconcentration, or public/common counterweight should usually be judged more favorably than a numerically nearer case that still flatters those decisive screens.[S02][S03][S13][S142][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23]

This keeps the archive's public answer short without pretending that every society has the same best mix or that every nearby mix is equally earned.

## Smallest live proof panel

When the archive has to say **how to tell whether movement is real** without dragging the whole scoreboard stack into the front door, use a **five-surface live proof panel** rather than one flattering headline.

1. **Dominant-breach surface.** Watch the main failure the package is supposed to reduce: floor passage, entrant footholds, dynastic timing, top-tail control, or whichever breach actually governs the case.[S142][S17][S18][S19][S23][S31]
2. **Fastest-washout surface.** Watch the channel most likely to reabsorb visible gains first: housing, land, debt, fees, markups, opacity, or rescue asymmetry.[S07][S10][S17][S20][S23][S31][S32][S33][S36]
3. **Next-gain incidence surface.** Force one line about where the next durable gains are actually landing: lower-half holders, younger entrants, excluded groups, person-level title, or public/common counterweight rather than incumbents alone.[S12][S18][S19][S20][S27][S28][S29][S30][S35]
4. **Top-tail / control surface.** Keep one direct line on controller concentration, beneficial-ownership visibility, rule-conversion channels, or another live path by which large fortunes could still govern the system after superficial broadening.[S04][S05][S06][S15][S20][S23][S26][S32][S33][S54]
5. **Durability or reopen surface.** Name the line that would show the gain has hardened—or that the case should reopen: retention through an ordinary cycle, claimant survivability, anti-capture hold, or another explicit ratchet trigger.[S05][S06][S17][S20][S21][S22][S31][S32][S33]

If a proposed success story cannot survive those five lines, the archive should treat it as **movement under watch**, not as a settled near-ideal approach. For the fuller five-axis measurement stack and for case-level watch-set selection, continue to [`measurement-and-scoreboard.md`](measurement-and-scoreboard.md) and [`next-scoreboard-and-evidence-debt-selection.md`](next-scoreboard-and-evidence-debt-selection.md).

## Smallest proof-debt default under opacity

When the archive has to say **what missing visibility does to certification** without dragging the whole confidence stack into the front door, use this **four-part asymmetry rule**:

1. **Opacity hardens certification first.** Missing visibility on controllers, beneficial owners, transfers, or top-tail valuation should usually block **near-ideal / acceptable** language and often cap the case at **provisional near-pass** or below.[S04][S15][S23][S31]
2. **Thin data do not buy calm.** Household aggregates, pooled vehicles, top-coding, or one favorable share table do not clear the control, circulation, or anti-capture screens by themselves.[S04][S15][S23][S31]
3. **Name the missing surface.** Say whether the live proof debt sits in controller visibility, transfer / inheritance visibility, valuation quality, influence channels, or enforcement capacity, and keep that exact surface on the watch set.[S04][S15][S23][S31]
4. **Let lower-regret correction move anyway.** Proof debt should usually slow comfort more than it slows floor repair, washout closure, registry-building, claimant-burden reduction, or anti-capture rail-building.[S07][S15][S21][S22][S23][S31][S39][S139]

This keeps the canonical direct answer aligned with the archive's broader burden-of-proof rule without pulling the full confidence note into every front-door use.

## Smallest verdict strip

When the archive has to say **where a case stands right now** without sending the reader through the full verdict anchor first, use this **four-state strip**.
Read it from the **weakest still-live decisive surface**, not from average progress across lanes, and when the case stays below near-ideal carry that blocking surface in the status line rather than speaking in free-floating verdict words:

1. **Still failing.** Use when the case remains clearly outside the acceptable family, is still badly broken on the floor, circulation, top-tail control, or public-counterweight screens, or still shows patterned excluded-group failure on the decisive floor, foothold, title, control, or exit surfaces even if one lane improved.[S02][S13][S15][S142][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S39][S139]
2. **Correction-required.** Use when the case is improving and no longer reads as pure abandonment, but one or more decisive pass-bundle conditions remain clearly broken or an anti-averaging veto is still plainly live, so the order is still outside the acceptable envelope.[S07][S13][S15][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S34][S36][S39][S139][S50]
3. **Provisional near-pass.** Use when the numbers look near the target family but the floor is still thin, **spend-down-trapped**, **cliff-punished**, **locked-up**, **shock-fragile**, **self-eroding**, **buffer-coerced**, **proof-brittle**, **late-remedy**, **reprisal-exposed**, **seizure-exposed**, **claimant-diluted**, **family-gated**, or **place-closed**, entrant access is still uneven, one gatekeeper can still materially interrupt or condition ordinary continuity, ordinary movement can still reset or forfeit the claim and leave it **reset-prone**, pooled or intermediated claims are still **manager-bottlenecked**, public/common access or continuity is still **patronage-routed**, excluded-group closure still blocks a clean pass, top-tail control is still unresolved, opacity is still blocking proof, or the durability window or ratchet against reversal has not yet been earned.[S13][S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S39][S139][S41][S44]
4. **Near-ideal / acceptable.** Use only when the case sits inside the target family, the six-part ideal-order pass bundle looks substantially true, no anti-averaging veto remains live against historically subordinated groups, and that reading has already survived an ordinary durability window **with some real ratchet against reversal** rather than one good snapshot, all with affirmative proof rather than inference alone.[S02][S03][S13][S15][S142][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S39][S139]

Treat this strip as a **compressed status readout**, not as a substitute for the pass bundle, the excluded-group anti-averaging screen, or the proof panel. When the strip lands below **Near-ideal / acceptable**, state the verdict as **status — blocking surface** or **status — proof debt** rather than leaving the blocker implicit; where the time-side blocker is the problem, say so plainly as **durability-not-yet-earned** or **ratchet-too-soft**. If the status call is contested, thinly evidenced, or likely to govern escalation or certification, go out to [`../10-framework/constitutional-verdicts-for-wealth-orders.md`](../10-framework/constitutional-verdicts-for-wealth-orders.md) for the fuller screens.[S13][S15][S18][S19][S20][S23][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S39][S139]

## Minimum operating rule

A serious answer to the archive's two central questions should usually answer **seven things at once**:

1. **Place the society inside a narrow acceptable family rather than chase one frozen tuple.** Start from **20 / 50 / 20 / 10** for the bottom 50 / middle 40 / next 9 / top 1, read the wider acceptable family through the archive's band and envelope rules, treat **top 0.1 at 3% or less** as the working constitutional target, and require **clearly positive public/social wealth** as part of the order rather than as a side note.[S02][S03]
2. **Require a real floor pass for the bottom half.** Broad ownership does not count if the lower half remains too indebted, illiquid, sweepable, shock-fragile, late-starting, or gatekeeper-dependent to live with ordinary independence.[S13][S142][S17][S24][S25][S39][S139][S45][S50][S51]
3. **Make footholds early, person-meaningful, and portable.** The archive discounts gains that arrive too late, sit only at the household level, or disappear across normal work, family, or place transitions.[S18][S19][S27][S28][S29][S30][S41][S47][S52]
4. **Rebuild earning power and saveable room from below.** A society should not ask households to solve weak wages, weak bargaining power, monopoly tolls, and forced self-insurance through private savings heroics alone.[S08][S09][S10][S11][S14][S17][S21][S22]
5. **Block the main washout channels.** Housing scarcity, land capture, buyer-subsidy capitalization, debt fragility, markup power, and hidden upward subsidy can quickly erase visible gains if they are left open.[S07][S10][S13][S20][S31][S34][S35][S36][S46][S197]
6. **Compress the top tail below wealth-to-rule and dynastic-closure levels.** The archive does not treat very large fortunes as ordinary holdings once they can purchase governing leverage, inheritance insulation, rescue privilege, or agenda power.[S04][S05][S06][S15][S18][S19][S23][S38][S54]
7. **Build public/common counterweight and anti-capture durability at the same time.** Better household shares count for less if the public side stays too weak, concentrated wealth stays too opaque to govern, or one good cycle can be quietly reversed through capture, rescue, hidden subsidy, or reconcentration.[S02][S12][S15][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33][S54]

Those seven questions are meant to be answered together.
The archive treats any package as weaker when it tries to let one lane substitute for the absence of the others.

## The seven main false passes

### 1. A nicer tuple is mistaken for a passing order

A society does not pass because one percentile table looks closer to the target.
The archive should discount the case when bottom-half wealth is still fragile, highly levered, late-arriving, household-masked, or too thin to survive ordinary shocks.[S13][S142][S17][S25][S27][S30][S45][S50]

### 2. Floor repair is mistaken for a full constitutional settlement

The archive should count less success when a somewhat better floor arrives without enough anti-washout, anti-dynasty, or anti-capture work to stop the same gains from flowing upward again.[S18][S19][S20][S23][S31][S32][S33]

### 3. Entry tools are mistaken for broadening when housing and land closure still govern access

Starter capital, matched saving, or credit help count for less when high-opportunity places remain closed, secure tenure remains weak, land uplift is still privatised, or buyer aid mainly capitalises into incumbent prices.[S07][S12][S18][S19][S34][S35][S36][S197]

### 4. Top-tax ambition is mistaken for a complete program

Aggressive rhetoric about wealth taxation counts for less when the rest of the system still protects monopoly tolls, weak wages, opacity, hidden subsidies, and weak public/social counterweight.
The archive rejects both **tax-only egalitarianism** and **tax-without-visibility**.[S04][S06][S10][S15][S20][S23][S31][S36][S38][S54]

### 5. Public or social wealth is mistaken for person-level circulation

A society can build stronger public wealth and still fail if ordinary people do not get earlier footholds, real claimability, person-level control, or lower day-to-day dependence.
Common wealth is part of the answer, not a permission slip to leave households near the bottom effectively assetless.[S02][S12][S18][S21][S22][S27][S28][S29][S30]

### 6. One good cycle is mistaken for a constitutional result

A society that improves during one labour upswing, one asset boom, one house-price phase, or one reform window but cannot hold the gain through normal budget, credit, or political cycles has not yet built the order it claims.[S13][S20][S31][S32][S33]

### 7. A long policy shopping list is mistaken for a nearest-real-move program

The archive should count less seriousness when governments can name many desirable reforms but cannot say which breach dominates now, which washout channel must close first, what should begin immediately, what should overlap, and what remains gated.[S15][S20][S23][S31][S32][S33]

## The standing direct-answer stack

The archive's answer is best read as a **seven-lane direct-answer stack**.
The first lane fixes the target.
The next six lanes explain how that target becomes real and stays real.

### 1. Set the target as a narrow acceptable family, not a sacred point

Core moves:

- start from **20 / 50 / 20 / 10** as the default attractor,
- read nearby admissible movement through the archive's acceptable-envelope logic rather than through free-form exception making,
- keep explicit warning attention on **bottom 50 below 10%**, **top 1 above 20%**, **top 0.1 above 5%**, and **persistently negative public wealth**,
- and refuse to let numerical closeness outrank floor failure, dynastic closure, top-tail rule, or rapid reconcentration.[S02][S03][S13][S142][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23]

This lane matters because the archive wants a clear destination without pretending that exact arithmetic alone decides justice.

### 2. Lower forced private exposure and make the floor claimable

Core moves:

- strengthen income stabilisation, health protection, pension adequacy, care support, and secure tenure,
- reduce forced private buffering against predictable risks,
- simplify claiming, renewal, and take-up,
- protect modest savings and first footholds from punitive spend-down rules,
- and preserve restart-capable minima when ordinary debt conflict arrives.[S17][S21][S22][S39][S139][S41][S45][S50]

This lane matters because the archive does not want households to need abnormal private wealth merely to survive ordinary life.
A stronger common floor changes what counts as an adequate private distribution, but it does not excuse leaving the bottom half without real footholds.

### 3. Rebuild earnings power and saveable room from below

Core moves:

- strengthen bargaining institutions and worker voice,
- maintain robust wage floors and regular uprating,
- reduce monopsony and lock-in,
- enforce labour standards across fissured work,
- and widen the share of income that can become durable saving rather than being absorbed by insecurity and extraction.[S08][S09][S10][S11][S14]

This lane matters because households with structurally weak wages are repeatedly told to solve distributional failure with private savings heroics.
The archive rejects that substitution.

### 4. Move footholds earlier and keep them person-level, portable, and durable

Core moves:

- move first durable claims earlier in life,
- use social inheritance, baby-bond-style tools, and safe low-balance saving channels,
- reduce first-step costs and debt pressure,
- improve person-level title and intra-household control,
- and make claims portable enough to survive ordinary transitions.[S12][S18][S19][S27][S28][S29][S30][S41][S47][S52]

This lane matters because the same quantum of wealth does different constitutional work depending on whether it arrives at 24, 44, or 74, and depending on whether the person who supposedly holds it can actually keep and use it.

### 5. Stop housing, land, and chokepoint washout

Core moves:

- reopen high-opportunity places,
- strengthen secure rental and non-speculative supply,
- recapture publicly created land uplift,
- clean up buyer subsidies and upside-down tax expenditures,
- discipline monopoly or chokepoint tolls,
- and treat anti-capitalization design as part of broadening rather than as an afterthought.[S07][S10][S13][S18][S19][S20][S31][S34][S35][S36][S46][S197]

This lane matters because broadening that is quickly capitalised into land values, rents, fees, or markups is not durable broadening.

### 6. Compress the top tail and weaken dynastic closure

Core moves:

- tax inheritances, gifts, and capital gains more seriously,
- use wealth taxes or wealth-equivalent tools where administratively justified,
- close avoidance and opacity channels,
- reduce large-fortune compounding advantages,
- and refuse the idea that very high concentrations are harmless so long as some floor policy exists.[S04][S05][S06][S18][S19][S23][S38][S54]

This lane matters because the archive does not treat very large fortunes as ordinary holdings once they can purchase political leverage, media influence, strategic land control, or dynastic insulation.

### 7. Build public/common counterweight and harden anti-capture durability

Core moves:

- build public, social, and common wealth that lowers household dependence,
- use distributional budgeting and visible incidence checks,
- strengthen beneficial-ownership, transfer, and controller legibility,
- harden political-finance, procurement, lobbying, and rescue-cost firewalls,
- and monitor reconcentration pressure actively rather than waiting for reversal to become obvious.[S02][S12][S15][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33][S54]

This lane matters because even a better distribution can remain constitutionally thin if the public side is weak and anti-capture rails are too soft to hold gains in place.

## How to choose the nearest real move from a live case

The archive does **not** ask every society to begin with the same first instrument.
It asks each society to move toward the **nearest real acceptable order** by starting from the currently dominant breach and the fastest washout channel.

### 1. Floor-failure cases

When the lower half is still too asset-poor, too indebted, too exposed to health, care, housing, or claimability shocks, the first package should usually lead with lanes **2, 3, and 5** together, while opening lane **4** early enough that today's younger and excluded entrants are not told to wait for later inheritance or later house-price luck.[S17][S18][S19][S21][S22][S39][S139][S45][S50][S52]

### 2. Late-entry and closed-opportunity cases

When the main failure is that footholds arrive too late, sit inside parental housing advantage, or remain too dependent on place and family background, the first package should usually lead with lanes **4 and 5**, supported by lane **2** where forced private exposure is still high.[S18][S19][S27][S28][S29][S30][S47][S48][S52][S197]

### 3. Rent-washout cases

When visible gains are being reabsorbed through land scarcity, rents, buyer-subsidy capitalization, or monopoly tolls, the first package should usually lead with lane **5**, but only as part of a broader mix that still protects households through lanes **2 and 3** while anti-capture work in lane **7** closes hidden refill channels.[S07][S10][S20][S31][S34][S35][S36][S46][S197]

### 4. Top-tail-rule cases

When concentrated wealth already buys governing leverage, obstructs visibility, or reproduces dynastic insulation, the first package should usually push lanes **6 and 7** much harder and earlier, while using lanes **2, 3, and 4** to keep the transition from being financed mainly by ordinary households.[S15][S18][S19][S23][S31][S32][S33][S38][S54]

### 5. Thin-public-counterweight cases

When household wealth is not the only problem but public/social wealth is too weak, too negative, or too unusable to offset private concentration, the first package should usually strengthen lane **7** together with lane **5** and whichever of lanes **2-4** most directly reduces forced dependence and entrant exclusion.[S02][S12][S20][S21][S22][S31][S34][S35]

### 6. Mixed cases

Most real societies are mixed.
In mixed cases the archive should ask two questions first:
**which breach is doing the most constitutional damage right now, and which washout channel will undo visible gains fastest if left open?**
That pairing should decide the opening package more than ideological preference for any one instrument.[S15][S20][S23][S31][S32][S33]

## How the same stack changes by mode

The standing stack does **not** disappear as conditions worsen or improve.
What changes is the **speed, legal posture, and relative emphasis** of the lanes.

### Prevention mode

Use prevention mode where closure risks are serious but ordinary corrective government is still functioning.
The emphasis is on moving early enough that hardening does not happen first:
lead with lanes **2, 3, 5, and 7**, and scale lane **6** as visibility and enforcement credibility strengthen.[S15][S18][S19][S31]

### Correction mode

Use correction mode when major constitutional breaches are already present but ordinary corrective government remains plausible.
The emphasis is on faster floor repair plus faster anti-washout and anti-dynasty work:
accelerate lanes **2, 4, 5, 6, and 7**, and use lane **3** to stop weak wages from undermining the repair.[S04][S05][S13][S19][S31]

### Anti-oligarchy response mode

Use anti-oligarchy response mode when concentrated wealth is already reproducing rule, blocking visibility, or vetoing reform.
The emphasis is on front-loaded anti-capture, legibility, and top-tail compression:
harden lanes **6 and 7** quickly, push lane **5** hard enough that land and chokepoint extraction cannot neutralise the rest of the package, and use lanes **2-4** to keep the transition from being borne mainly by ordinary households.[S15][S20][S23][S31][S32][S33][S54]

For the archive's stable mode doctrine, read [`prevention-and-response-doctrine.md`](prevention-and-response-doctrine.md) and [`mode-selection-and-escalation-heuristic.md`](mode-selection-and-escalation-heuristic.md).

## Fast operating rule

Use this note in five steps:

1. **Place the case inside the target family.**  
   Start from **20 / 50 / 20 / 10**, read the warning thresholds, and ask whether the case has actually earned any off-center envelope claim.[S02][S03]
2. **Identify the dominant live breach.**  
   Ask whether the case is failing mainly on floor integrity, late entry, rent washout, top-tail rule, or thin public/common counterweight.[S13][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23]
3. **Identify the fastest washout channel.**  
   In most cases that means housing, debt fragility, monopoly tolls, hidden subsidy, opacity, or public downside absorption rather than the absence of a clever new financial product.[S07][S10][S17][S20][S23][S31][S32][S33][S36]
4. **Choose the opening lane mix that closes that washout while protecting households now.**  
   Front-load the combination that most directly protects the lower half, reopens entry, and hardens visibility and anti-capture rails under the actual administrative and political constraints.[S15][S31][S32][S33]
5. **Judge from durability, not announcement.**  
   Read the package from what survives ordinary cycles, not from the press release, the gross fiscal headline, or the first-year snapshot.[S20][S31][S32][S33]

Default shortcut:

- **clear target + broad floor pass + earlier footholds + lower washout + lower wealth-to-rule + positive public/social counterweight + stronger durability rails** -> count as a serious answer to the archive's question;
- **better-looking shares without floor integrity, circulation, or anti-capture durability** -> assume the order is morally weaker than it looks;
- **mixed case** -> weight the judgment by bottom-half passage, entrant access, top-tail rule risk, and reconcentration pressure rather than by one impressive instrument.

## Non-substitution rule

Do **not** let any of the following excuse weakness elsewhere in the stack:

- a nicer tuple without durable floor passage,
- a stronger social floor without earlier footholds,
- early-capital tools without housing-entry and anti-capitalization repair,
- wage gains without lower rent and toll extraction,
- public/social wealth without person-level circulation,
- top-tax rhetoric without legibility and enforcement,
- or one successful reform cycle without proof against reconcentration.

Those gains may still matter.
They simply do **not** by themselves clear the archive's direct-answer note.

## Program implication

Once this note is read clearly, the archive's answer becomes easier to govern.
The question is no longer: **which single instrument do we believe in most?**
It becomes: **what target family are we aiming at, what live breach is most urgent, what washout channel will undo visible gains fastest, and what lane mix moves us toward the nearest real acceptable order?**

That changes sequencing.
It pushes governments to repair floor integrity, wages, entry timing, housing washout, visibility, and anti-capture rails earlier than they often prefer, because those are the places where later gains are most easily laundered away.

## Bottom line

For working sequence, use **Smallest front-door use order** and **Fast operating rule** above rather than another repeated step list here.

The archive's direct answer is therefore not: **pick nicer numbers** or **pick one heroic policy**.
It is: **place the case inside a near-ideal constitutional family, identify the dominant live breach and the fastest washout channel, and move through the lane mix that most directly closes that breach, blocks washout, and still survives ordinary cycles without restoring wealth-to-rule conversion.**
