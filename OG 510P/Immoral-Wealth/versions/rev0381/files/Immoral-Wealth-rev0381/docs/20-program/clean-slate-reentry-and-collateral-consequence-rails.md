# Clean slate, reentry, and collateral-consequence rails

Use this router when an old criminal legal record blocks wealth formation after sentence completion.

## Router questions

- Is clearance automatic or petition-based?
- Does implementation happen on time and with accurate data matching?
- Do private background-check vendors receive and obey deletion/suppression rules?
- Are occupational licensing exclusions narrow, time-limited, and individualized?
- Are housing, employment, education, voting, benefits, and family-law restrictions mapped?
- Are court debts and payment conditions separated from record clearance?

## Preferred reforms

Automatic record clearance, court-to-vendor deletion pipelines, audit logs, licensing individualized review, fair-chance housing/employment rules, court-debt relief, and collateral-consequence inventories tied to repeal or waiver pathways.[S315][S316][S317]
