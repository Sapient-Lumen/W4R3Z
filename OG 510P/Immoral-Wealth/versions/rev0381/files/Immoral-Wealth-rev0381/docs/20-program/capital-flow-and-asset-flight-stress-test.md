# Capital-flow and asset-flight stress test

## Purpose

This test asks whether a wealth reform can survive cross-border asset movement without panic design, elite veto, or macro instability.

## Minimum evidence pack

- stock of high-wealth taxpayers or owners by residence status;
- asset class split: listed securities, private business, real estate, trusts, crypto, bank deposits, pension, art/collectibles;
- share of wealth already offshore or entity-held;
- existing CRS/AEOI and beneficial-owner coverage;
- legal capacity for deemed disposal, exit tax, source tax, withholding, anti-deferral, and collection;
- FX reserves, current-account pressures, capital-account openness, and macroprudential risks when relevant;
- treaty and investment-law constraints;
- administrative ability to distinguish ordinary migration from tax-motivated exit.[S180][S181][S213][S214]

## Scenario ladder

1. **Narrative threat only:** record proof debt; do not weaken policy.
2. **Observable relocation of individuals:** test stock size, tax base, and asset location before rerouting.
3. **Observable asset flight:** strengthen reporting, withholding, exit, and BO rails.
4. **Macro-financial stress:** consider targeted, transparent, stability-oriented CFM/MPM under IMF-style principles.
5. **State-capacity failure:** lower ambition temporarily only if paired with rail-building and reopening triggers.

## Output

Every case should produce one sentence:

> Exit/mobility risk is `low / watch / material / blocking`, because [evidence], and the anti-blackmail rail is [named rail].
