# All-in price, junk-fee, and subscription guardrails

Use this router when a market extracts value through drip pricing, required add-ons, cancellation friction, recurring charges, or low-salience fees. The issue is not only consumer annoyance; it is whether low-buffer households lose control over the timing and total cost of necessities or quasi-necessities.[S269][S270]

## Guardrails

- total price shown before commitment;
- mandatory fees included in the headline price;
- recurring charges require affirmative, informed consent;
- cancellation is as easy as sign-up;
- refunds and stop-payment rights are usable;
- comparison shopping is not defeated by hidden fees;
- rules apply across channels and are not limited to one sector where the tollgate is broader.

## Legal-durability warning

Sectoral or vacated rules should not be scored as a general pass. If all-in price rules cover only lodging/tickets, or if subscription cancellation protections are uncertain, the case carries a household-market-extraction warning plus Gate 11 legal-durability evidence debt.[S269][S270]
