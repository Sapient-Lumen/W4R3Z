# Provisional passes and reopening triggers

## What this note is for

The archive can now say **what distribution is near-ideal**, **how to classify a live case**, **what evidence a case memo needs**, and **what opening package should follow from the dominant breach**.
What it still needs in one compact place is the next question:
**once a case looks improved, provisionally near-passing, or even close enough to de-escalate, what keeps that judgment from becoming lazy closure, and what should automatically reopen correction?**

This note gives the archive that **thin watch-and-reopen rule**.
It is not a new theory branch and it is not a permanent emergency posture.
Its purpose is narrower: to stop future case work from confusing **provisional near-pass** with **settled safety**, from confusing **quieter politics** with **weaker wealth-to-rule**, and from treating a nicer scoreboard moment as if it will maintain itself automatically.[S05][S06][S13][S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33]

## Minimum operating rule

Any case that is being read as **provisional near-pass**, **candidate for de-escalation**, or **near-ideal under live observation** should usually do **six things at once**:

1. say explicitly whether the case is **provisional** or **cleanly certified**, not let readers infer that from tone alone,
2. name the **three to five surfaces most likely to slip first** if the settlement weakens,
3. state which trigger or evidence change would **reopen correction immediately**,
4. keep **opacity, repair debt, and wealth-to-rule channels** under active watch rather than assuming they are gone because they are quieter,[S05][S06][S15][S23][S31][S32][S33]
5. refuse to treat one favorable cycle, one calmer coalition moment, or one improved share table as a self-maintaining constitutional order,[S05][S06][S13][S20][S31][S32][S33]
6. and let **serious slippage reopen the harder posture before comfort hardens into amnesia**.[S15][S20][S23][S31][S32][S33]

So the archive's standing rule is:
**there is no passive maintenance mode.**
There are only cases that have earned a softer reading for now and cases that have not.
A softer reading stays softer only while the settlement keeps passing the surfaces most likely to fail first.

## The six main false passes

### 1. Provisional near-pass is treated as full closure

A case fails watch discipline when a society moves inside the archive's preferred numbers or into a softer governing mode and is then spoken about as if the constitutional problem has been solved for good.[S02][S13][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23]
The archive should keep the distinction between **inside the numbers**, **provisionally near-passing**, and **clean enough to certify**.

### 2. De-escalation follows calmer politics rather than durable proof

A case counts less secure when the main reason for softer language is that elites are quieter, markets are calmer, or reform conflict is less visible, while the underlying channels of lobbying, media leverage, procurement favoritism, controller opacity, or mobility blackmail remain substantially live.[S05][S06][S15][S23][S31][S32][S33]

### 3. Near-center shares are treated as self-sustaining

A case fails when an attractive share vector is allowed to stand in for floor resilience, person-level control, earlier footholds, open circulation, and real public/common counterweight.
The archive does not certify a society merely because the table softened while the supporting constitutional surfaces remain thin.[S02][S13][S142][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23]

### 4. Repair debt is treated as historical rather than live

A case counts less safe when crisis privileges, emergency derogations, rescue asymmetries, or anti-capture repairs remain incomplete but are spoken of as if they belong only to the past.
Unrepaired emergency architecture is part of the current settlement until it is actually unwound or repaid.[S05][S06][S20][S31][S32][S33]

### 5. Group, cohort, or person-level slippage is ignored because the average is holding

A society should not keep a soft reading when younger cohorts, historically excluded groups, tenants, or subordinate adults inside households are slipping back out while the aggregate wealth table still looks respectable.[S18][S19][S27][S28][S29][S30][S47]
Averages can lag deterioration for the people whose passage matters most.

### 6. Opacity is tolerated because the trend line looks friendly

A case fails when positive trend language is allowed to outrun controller visibility, beneficial-ownership legibility, source confidence, or usable proof on the top tail.
The archive should harden certification more than concern whenever the new softness depends on things that still cannot be seen cleanly.[S15][S23][S31][S32][S33]

## The standing watch-and-reopen stack

### 1. Floor slippage and claimability erosion

The first watch question is whether the lower half is still keeping a **real floor** rather than a floor that only worked during one soft period.
Reopen correction when any of the following become materially worse:
- positive-net-wealth passage near the bottom,
- liquid-buffer resilience,
- housing security and non-punishing entry,
- claimability and low-friction retention of the floor,
- or protection of modest footholds against seizure, spend-down pressure, and fee drag.[S17][S21][S22][S39][S139][S41][S42][S45][S50]

A society can remain near the numbers and still need correction again if the floor starts thinning in ordinary life.

### 2. Entry reclosure and timing relapse

The second watch question is whether adult passage is staying open **early enough** and **widely enough**.
Reopen correction when younger cohorts become newly locked out, when starter positions depend more heavily again on parental housing wealth or late inheritance, when place closure hardens, or when earlier footholds are diluted into household masking rather than person-level command.[S18][S19][S27][S28][S29][S30][S34][S47][S52][S197]

This matters because a society can look stable in aggregate while quietly reinstalling the birth lottery for new entrants.

### 3. Washout relapse

The third watch question is whether the main gain channels are still being **reabsorbed upward**.
Reopen correction when housing costs, land values, debt service, fees, insurance, monopoly tolls, or other carrying-cost burdens again start eating the visible gains that justified the softer verdict.[S07][S10][S17][S20][S31][S34][S35][S36][S42][S197]

The archive should be especially alert here when the earlier package depended on wage gains, starter capital, or a stronger floor that can be capitalised away without changing the ownership table immediately.

### 4. Top-tail reconcentration or opacity hardening

The fourth watch question is whether the top tail is becoming **larger, harder to see, or harder to govern**.
Reopen correction, or block de-escalation outright, when the top 1% or top 0.1% begin drifting back toward warning territory, controller concentration tightens in strategic assets, beneficial-ownership visibility weakens, or inheritance and transfer channels become more shelterable again.[S02][S03][S15][S20][S23][S31][S32][S33]

A society does not stay near-ideal merely because the lower half is doing somewhat better while the ultra-top quietly reassembles command capacity.

### 5. Wealth-to-rule resurgence

The fifth watch question is whether concentrated wealth is regaining the ability to **buy governing leverage**.
Reopen correction or harden straight to anti-oligarchy response when lobbying dependence, party-finance dependence, media concentration, procurement favoritism, regulatory capture, downside socialization, or jurisdictional blackmail become materially stronger again.[S05][S06][S15][S20][S23][S31][S32][S33]

This is the archive's clearest no-complacency rule:
**a society does not keep a soft mode just because distribution improved somewhat if wealth is recovering veto power at the same time.**

### 6. Rail decay and repair default

The sixth watch question is whether the institutions that made the softer reading credible are still being maintained.
Reopen correction when delivery budgets are quietly cut, appeals and enforcement capacity thin out, registry quality slips, audit and review weaken, anti-capture firewalls are left ceremonial, or emergency repair duties remain booked but unpaid.[S04][S15][S23][S31][S32][S33][S36]

The archive should count less durability when the settlement depends on machinery that is no longer funded, defended, or repaired.

## How the watch stack changes the verdict rule

The archive should apply the stack asymmetrically.

- **One severe trigger** on wealth-to-rule resurgence, top-tail opacity hardening, or broad floor slippage can reopen **correction immediately**.[S15][S20][S23][S31][S32][S33]
- **Several reinforcing mild slippages** across floor, timing, washout, and rail decay should usually reopen correction even when no single metric looks catastrophic yet.[S05][S06][S13][S18][S19][S20][S31][S32][S33]
- **A stronger anti-oligarchy trigger** is justified when reopened slippage includes concentrated veto power, sabotage capacity, or controller invisibility rather than only weak broadening from below.[S15][S23][S31][S32][S33]
- **No clean certification** should be granted while the main remaining softness depends on unresolved opacity, strategic missingness, or a durability window that has not yet been passed.[S05][S06][S15][S23][S31][S32][S33]

That means the archive's softer verdicts are real, but they are never self-sealing.
They remain answerable to the same constitutional surfaces that justified them in the first place.

## Fast use rule

Use this note in five steps whenever a case memo is proposing **provisional near-pass**, **near-ideal**, or **de-escalation**:

1. **Name the watch status plainly.**  
   Say whether the case is provisional near-pass, near-ideal under live watch, or candidate for de-escalation rather than letting the tone imply permanence.
2. **Pick the three to five likeliest reopening surfaces.**  
   Use the standing stack: floor, timing, washout, top-tail / opacity, wealth-to-rule, and rails.[S05][S06][S15][S17][S18][S19][S20][S23][S31][S32][S33]
3. **State one downgrade trigger and one escalation trigger.**  
   The memo should say what would reopen correction and what would justify hardening beyond correction if the relapse is capture-heavy.
4. **Keep the proof debt visible.**  
   Any unresolved opacity, missing disaggregation, or untested durability should remain part of the memo's final burden statement.
5. **Refuse passive closure language.**  
   A near-pass is not a forever-pass; it is an order that is currently passing under visible conditions and could fail again if those conditions reverse.

Default shortcut:
- **inside the numbers but thin durability** -> provisional near-pass with explicit watch stack;
- **cleaner case with real durability but live proof debt** -> near-ideal under live watch rather than triumphal closure;
- **one severe trigger or several reinforcing mild slippages** -> reopen correction;
- **reopened slippage plus concentrated veto or opacity hardening** -> harden toward anti-oligarchy response.[S05][S06][S15][S20][S23][S31][S32][S33]

## Handoff to case work

A future case memo does **not** need a new heading just for this note.
It can usually carry the watch discipline inside the existing final block in [`case-application-protocol.md`](case-application-protocol.md):
- use **Next scoreboard** for the live watch surfaces,
- use **Evidence debt** for the still-open certification blockers,
- say explicitly which trigger would reopen correction or block de-escalation,
- and when a launched package is landing better than expected, pair this note with [`hardening-gains-into-ratchets.md`](hardening-gains-into-ratchets.md) so softer language comes only with a visible hardening plan.

That keeps the archive thin.
The point of this note is not to widen case memos.
It is to stop them from becoming falsely final.

## Bottom line

The archive now has a cleaner answer to a practical problem that appears whenever a case improves:
**what stops a softer verdict from quietly turning into complacency?**
The answer is simple.
A case stays softer only while the floor remains real, entry stays open, washout stays blocked, the top tail stays bounded and visible, wealth does not regain governing leverage, and the rails that made the settlement credible do not decay.
When those conditions slip, correction should reopen before hierarchy fully hardens again.[S05][S06][S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33]
