# Bankruptcy, exemption, and discharge router

Use this router when the dominant breach is post-shock debt overhang.

## Route

1. Identify the debts producing the lock: medical, student, tax, fines/fees, credit card, auto deficiency, mortgage arrears, rent, business guarantee, family debt, or collection judgment.
2. Separate discharge from exemption. A discharge that leaves the debtor without a car, tools, account balance, or home equity may still fail the recovery floor.
3. Score filing access: fees, counsel, forms, language, rural access, online access, documentation, repeat-filing limits, and Chapter 13 plan completion.
4. Score post-discharge data memory and reintegration: credit reports, specialty reports, rental screens, insurance, employment, bank accounts, and utilities.
5. Route debt categories that are excluded or hard to discharge to separate policy: hardship discharge, income-driven repayment, court-debt relief, tax compromise, or settlement supervision.

## Evidence pack

Filing volumes, chapter mix, discharge rates, Chapter 13 completion, attorney access, fee waivers, debt-type exclusions, exemption schedules, post-discharge report cleanup, and subgroup filing/outcome data.[S301][S302][S303][S319]
