# Legal-durability and design checklist

Use this checklist before recommending a package that changes taxes, ownership visibility, property rights, benefit entitlements, public/common assets, or anti-capture institutions.

## Checklist

1. **Authority:** identify the constitutional/statutory power.
2. **Perimeter:** define who is covered, what assets or claims count, and what is excluded.
3. **Valuation:** specify valuation dates, safe harbours, anti-discount rules, hardship rules, and dispute forums.
4. **Process:** satisfy notice, comment, parliamentary procedure, consultation, or local consent where required.
5. **Rights balance:** test property, privacy, equality, due process, data protection, and free-expression concerns.
6. **Standing:** name who may sue, appeal, complain, request information, or trigger enforcement.
7. **Remedy:** define interim relief, corrective payment, title correction, injunction, penalty, disgorgement, or asset recovery.
8. **Federalism:** allocate national, state/provincial, municipal, and tribal/Indigenous authority.
9. **International:** identify tax treaties, exchange-of-information rules, AML standards, and safe-harbour conflicts.[S166][S178][S180]
10. **Fallback:** design second-best instruments if the first-best instrument is enjoined, narrowed, or repealed.

## U.S. wealth-tax posture

After `Moore`, the cube should not write U.S. wealth-tax proposals as if realization doctrine and direct-tax constraints are settled.[S168] Use a ladder:

1. enforce existing recognized liabilities and nonfiling;
2. improve high-wealth audit capacity;
3. tighten estate/gift/dynasty-trust rules;
4. reform capital-gains realization, deferral, and step-up;
5. require entity, trust, and private-fund reporting;
6. use property/land/state instruments where lawful;
7. reserve direct wealth-tax pathways for designs with explicit constitutional analysis.

The moral target remains top-tail compression. The legal pathway must be durable enough to survive the institutions that advantaged wealth will use against it.
