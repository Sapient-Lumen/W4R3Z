# Remedy operability, standing, and collective-redress gate

A wealth-order repair does not pass because a right exists. It passes only when the ordinary claimant can use the remedy before the underlying wealth interest is destroyed.

## Operability test

For every case that relies on legal rights, public benefits, appeals, reporting systems, private enforcement, public enforcement, arbitration, ombuds, administrative review, or structural remedies, ask seven questions:

1. **Standing:** who can invoke the claim, and who is outside the claimant perimeter?
2. **Notice:** does the person know what happened, why, what proof is needed, and by when?
3. **Interim protection:** is there protection before eviction, shutoff, account freeze, garnishment, denial, credit damage, job loss, or health interruption becomes irreversible?
4. **Time to relief:** is the median and tail delay shorter than the household's survival window?
5. **Representation or collective action:** can small, repeated, or technically complex harms be aggregated or brought by a representative/public actor?
6. **Restoration:** does the remedy repair collateral harms, or only issue a late check or isolated award?
7. **Auditability:** are false positives, abandonment, vendor effects, forum effects, subgroup skew, and repeat-player patterns logged?

## Certification rule

A case cannot use `acceptable`, `near_ideal`, or public-counterweight language if the remedy is unavailable, too slow, too expensive, too individualized, too opaque, or too weak to restore collateral harms.

## Structured register

rev0321 scoreboards may include `remedy_operability_register`. Each item must identify:

- harm or claim;
- claimant perimeter;
- standing path;
- notice/explanation;
- interim protection;
- time to relief;
- review body;
- collective or representative action;
- restoration remedy;
- enforcement actor;
- evidence quality and source IDs.

This is the remedy analogue to Gate 20's public-balance-sheet register. It converts a normative concern into a queryable cube object.

## Interaction with existing gates

- Gate 02 fails when the remedy arrives after the floor collapses.
- Gate 08 fails when delay or forum friction converts a legal right into life interruption.
- Gate 11 is the primary home of this protocol.
- Gate 17 applies when identity, data, or score-mediated systems block access.
- Gate 18 applies when a late remedy does not repair post-shock afterlife.
- Gate 20 applies when public systems rescue intermediaries but leave ordinary claimants to chase slow individualized remedies.
