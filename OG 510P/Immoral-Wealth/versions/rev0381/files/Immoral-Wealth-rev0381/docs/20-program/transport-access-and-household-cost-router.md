# Transport access and household-cost router

Use this router when the apparent affordability of a place disappears after transportation cost, time, reliability, disability access, or job/care access is scored.[S254][S255][S259]

## Trigger questions

- Does combined housing plus transportation exceed an adequacy threshold for lower-income households?
- Are households forced into vehicle debt or high repair/insurance/fuel costs to reach work and care?
- Does transit exist only as a map line, or does it have frequency, reliability, safety, and off-hour access?
- Are disabled, elderly, rural, and caregiving households able to make necessary trips?
- Do fines, fees, tickets, impoundment, or license sanctions convert mobility need into debt?

## Package route

Lead with transportation when mobility is the fastest washout. Options include frequent transit, fare capping, paratransit, safe streets, repair vouchers, insurance stabilization, anti-impoundment/fine reforms, transit-oriented affordable housing, and job-access planning. Pair the route with housing policy when cheap housing is only cheap because public access is missing.[S254][S255][S259]
