# Workplace-power gate and pre-distribution scorecard

## Gate 14 — workplace power, pre-distribution, and productive ownership

A case cannot pass when ordinary people are expected to build wealth from work but the labor/productive system prevents them from earning, saving, switching, bargaining, converting claims, or sharing enterprise upside.

Gate 14 asks whether the order has a credible **saveable-room and productive-ownership path**.

## Pass conditions

A case can pass or watch-pass Gate 14 only when it can show:

- wages plus benefits leave saveable room after housing, care, transport, taxes, debt, and ordinary shocks;
- minimum-wage or wage-board rails prevent the lowest-paid work from being asset-erasing;
- collective-bargaining rights, coverage, or functional substitutes let workers contest wage-setting power;
- noncompetes, repayment traps, no-poach arrangements, platform lock-in, benefit lock-in, and visa/status lock-in do not make exit unreal;
- classification rules prevent dependent workers from being stripped of employee protections;
- responsibility follows control in franchise, subcontract, staffing, and platform chains;
- platform workers have employment-status, algorithmic-management, human-review, information, consultation, and retaliation-protection rails where relevant;
- broad-based employee ownership and profit sharing are scored for breadth, value, liquidity, diversification, and governance.[S220][S222][S223][S225][S227][S228][S229][S235][S236]

## Hard blocks

Gate 14 blocks comfort certification when:

- the lower half can only build wealth by working more hours in unstable, low-benefit jobs;
- job switching is blocked by contract, debt, status, platform, credential, or local monopsony constraints;
- lead firms capture surplus while legal responsibility sits with thin intermediaries;
- platform flexibility is counted without scoring algorithmic control, deactivation, appeal, and data portability;
- employee ownership is counted without vesting, valuation, liquidity, diversification, fiduciary, and downside-risk scoring;
- wage theft, retaliation, or first-contract failure makes collective rights non-converting.
