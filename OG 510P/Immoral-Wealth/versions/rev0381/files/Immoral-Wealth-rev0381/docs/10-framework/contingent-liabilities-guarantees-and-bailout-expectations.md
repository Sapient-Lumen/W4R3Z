# Contingent liabilities, guarantees, and bailout expectations

A contingent liability is a public promise whose cost appears only if a trigger occurs: bank failures, disasters, pension failures, student-loan losses, mortgage-insurance losses, GSE support, court judgments, SOE/PPP stress, or emergency lending.[S350][S357][S358][S361][S363]

The moral issue is not that governments guarantee things. Guarantees can make ordinary credit, housing, deposits, and crisis survival possible. The issue is whether guarantee value flows mostly to incumbent owners and creditors while households pay through fees, inflation, debt service, or public-service compression.

## Backstop design test

A backstop is safer when it has:

- ex ante fees or assessments;
- risk-sensitive pricing and stress tests;
- clear eligible claimant perimeter;
- loss-sharing, haircuts, or clawbacks for insiders;
- public warrants/equity/upside recovery when private entities are rescued;
- emergency transparency and sunset rules;
- subgroup and small-claimant impact tracking.

A backstop is dangerous when implicit rescue is stronger for large asset holders than for households facing eviction, utility shutoff, care cost, or disaster displacement.
