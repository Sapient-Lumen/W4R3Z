# Probate, intestacy, heirs property, and title finality

A home, farm, or parcel is not a full foothold unless it can be inherited, titled, insured, financed, repaired, defended, sold, partitioned fairly, and used as proof for public aid. Probate and title systems therefore belong inside the wealth-order test.

## Title-finality screen

Score the asset as **clouded** when:

- the estate did not clear probate;
- heirs hold fractional interests without marketable title;
- records still show a deceased owner;
- co-tenants are numerous, hard to locate, or vulnerable to partition pressure;
- families cannot access disaster aid, farm programs, loans, insurance, or sale/refinance because title is unclear.[S335][S336]

## Heirs-property warning

Heirs property is a classic false ownership pass. Family members may have use rights and emotional attachment, but they lack the title finality needed to convert the asset safely. The Uniform Partition of Heirs Property Act supplies one mitigation rail, but enactment and implementation remain uneven.[S336][S337]

## Routing

- If the problem is missing probate/title help, route to legal-aid and administrative-title repair.
- If the problem is forced sale/partition risk, route to UPHPA-type protections and buyout/fair-market safeguards.
- If the problem is disaster recovery, route to title-proof bridges so heirs-property families are not excluded from repair funds.
- If the problem is tax sale or delinquency, route to notice, payment-plan, and anti-speculation protections.

## Certification effect

A lower-wealth household cannot be credited with full owner control when probate cost, intestacy, or title cloud prevents the asset from functioning at repair, disaster, credit, transfer, or exit moments.
