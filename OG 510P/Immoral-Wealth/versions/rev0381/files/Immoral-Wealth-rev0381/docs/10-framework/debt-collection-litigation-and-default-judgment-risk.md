# Debt-collection litigation and default-judgment risk

Debt collection becomes a wealth-order problem when private claims are converted into judgments through mass litigation, weak service, low representation, opaque debt-buyer evidence, and default. The judgment then becomes a platform for wage garnishment, bank levy, liens, credit reporting, and settlement pressure.[S307][S308][S304]

## Test

Score:

- complaint and lawsuit volume relative to households with debt in collection;
- proof-of-ownership and itemization requirements for debt buyers;
- service-of-process quality and default-judgment rates;
- access to counsel, plain-language forms, remote participation, and interpreter access;
- post-judgment interest, renewal periods, and revival rules;
- complaint, enforcement, and restitution evidence;
- whether settlements preserve basic living and recovery capacity.

A collection system cannot pass by banning only the most abusive collector behavior while leaving a default-judgment machine intact.
