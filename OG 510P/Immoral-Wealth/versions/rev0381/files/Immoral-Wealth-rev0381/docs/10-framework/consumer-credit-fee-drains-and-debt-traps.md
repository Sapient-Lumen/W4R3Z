# Consumer credit fee drains and debt traps

Consumer credit is not automatically immoral. Credit can smooth shocks, preserve work, and bridge temporary mismatches. It becomes a wealth-order breach when credit is routinely used to buy necessities, when fees and rollover structures drain the first buffer, or when weak disclosure/remedy rails make the borrower absorb risks the provider can price, automate, and enforce.[S260][S268][S271][S279]

## The fee-drain test

A fee drain exists when low-wealth households pay recurring liquidity costs that wealthier households avoid through balances, credit scores, account buffers, or family liquidity. Overdraft/NSF fees, late fees, high-cost small-dollar loans, and repeated payment withdrawals can become a private tax on volatility.[S262][S268][S271]

## The fragmentation test

Modern credit can fragment across cards, BNPL, earned-wage access, apps, subscriptions, rent reporting, auto finance, and collections. A household may look solvent product-by-product while being overcommitted in aggregate. The cube should score total payment load, visibility to the household, visibility to supervisors, and the ease of dispute, cancellation, forbearance, and correction.[S264][S265][S272]

## The remedy test

Consumer-credit protection requires live remedies: safe banking alternatives, ability-to-repay or equivalent affordability tests, fee caps, payment-withdrawal limits, plain all-in cost, dispute rights, non-retaliatory complaint channels, and supervision that survives rule rollback. Where protection is state-by-state or agency-discretionary, the case should carry legal-durability and evidence-debt flags.[S263][S269][S271][S279]

## Routing

- **Small liquidity gap + low-cost public/credit-union rail:** route to claim conversion and low-fee defaults.
- **Repeated high-cost borrowing for necessities:** route to Gate 16 fail plus Gate 8 liability-stack watch.
- **Fee revenue concentrated on volatile households:** route to household-market-extraction repair.
- **Protection depends on a vacated, withdrawn, or non-enforced rule:** route to legal-durability and enforcement gates.
