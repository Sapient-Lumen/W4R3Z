# Claim seniority under public stress

rev0319 treats the public balance sheet as a **moral waterfall**. The question is not only whether the state owns assets, has tax capacity, issues safe debt, or operates social insurance. The question is who becomes senior when a stress event arrives.

A public balance sheet counts as a counterweight only when ordinary-claimant continuity is senior to asset-holder rescue, or when asset-holder rescue is paired with loss sharing, public-upside recovery, and enforceable protection for ordinary claimants.

## Stress-seniority terms

- **Automatic senior claim**: a claim paid or protected without discretionary politics, emergency improvisation, or opaque administrative rationing.
- **Appropriations-contingent claim**: a claim that depends on future legislative or budgetary action.
- **Liquidity-protected private claim**: a private asset or liability class that receives public support because failure would threaten financial, housing, payment, or macro stability.
- **Residual payer**: the household, worker, renter, taxpayer, future cohort, local government, or service claimant who absorbs the repair cost through taxes, fees, inflation, rate shock, reduced services, benefit haircuts, higher borrowing costs, or lower public investment.
- **Public-upside receiver**: the party that receives fees, warrants, equity, clawbacks, senior claims, recoveries, or other upside when the public absorbs downside.

## Doctrine

The public/common counterweight gate cannot pass merely because public assets or guarantees exist. A comfort verdict must show:

1. the legal authority for each exposure;
2. the claimant perimeter;
3. the direct and indirect beneficiaries;
4. maximum and expected exposure;
5. stress scenario and correlated-failure channels;
6. funding source and residual payer;
7. loss sharing and haircuts;
8. public-upside recovery;
9. ordinary-claimant continuity; and
10. review, sunset, audit, and enforceability.

## Cross-gate interaction

Gate 20 is a forcing layer for several older gates:

- Gate 7: public/common counterweight is not real if the counterweight silently supports asset holders first.
- Gate 11: remedy and standing matter because claimants need enforceable protection before stress.
- Gate 15: place public finance can be weakened by debt service, disaster obligations, special assessments, and intergovernmental burden shifts.
- Gate 16: household-market extraction can turn public repair into fees, premiums, tolls, junk charges, or rate shock.
- Gate 18: fresh-start capacity fails if public repair leaves households with non-dischargeable or unrecoverable burdens.
- Gate 19: intergenerational transfer changes when aging, care, pension, and debt stress shift repair costs across families and cohorts.

## Operator rule

When a case invokes public wealth or fiscal capacity as comfort evidence, require the seniority waterfall before certification. Without it, mark Gate 20 as `blocked` or `watch` and treat any public-counterweight claim as unproven.[S345][S346][S351][S357][S363][S373][S375][S376][S378][S381]
