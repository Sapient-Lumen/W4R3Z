# Household market extraction and cost-of-life tollgates

A wealth order can fail after income arrives. Gate 16 names the private-market tollgates that convert ordinary life into a stream of fees, opaque prices, payment traps, and time losses. These tollgates are not incidental consumer annoyances when they systematically absorb the lower-half surplus that was supposed to become savings, repair capacity, mobility, or a first foothold.[S260][S261][S262][S263]

## Core claim

A household does not have real wealth-forming capacity if routine survival requires buying short-term liquidity at high cost, rolling negative equity into necessary transport, paying hidden or hard-to-avoid fees, accepting recurring charges that are harder to exit than enter, or purchasing care privately at levels that interrupt labor-force attachment and family formation.[S264][S266][S267][S273][S274][S277]

## Tollgate types

1. **Liquidity tollgates:** overdraft, NSF fees, credit-card late fees, payday/title/pawn/high-cost installment loans, earned-wage advances, and BNPL loops.
2. **Mobility-asset tollgates:** auto loans with negative equity, repossession, deficiency balances, force-placed products, repair-cost debt, and transport-to-work dependence.
3. **Price-opacity tollgates:** drip pricing, junk fees, required add-ons, cancellation friction, subscription traps, and non-comparable all-in prices.
4. **Care tollgates:** childcare, elder care, disability care, long-term care, informal-care time loss, and asset spenddown.
5. **Account-rail tollgates:** unbanked/underbanked status, prepaid and nonbank fees, lack of safe low-cost payment rails, and complaint/remedy opacity.[S263][S269][S270][S272]

## Relation to existing gates

Gate 16 is not a replacement for the liability stack. The liability stack asks whether debts and obligations actively drag people below zero. Gate 16 asks whether ordinary markets repeatedly prevent households from accumulating the first positive buffer. It is the difference between **negative wealth as outcome** and **market tolls as mechanism**.

Gate 16 also does not replace place public finance. Place public finance asks whether public services are real locally. Gate 16 asks whether private cost-of-life markets are disciplined enough that households do not have to leak the public floor back to creditors, platforms, fee collectors, care markets, and mobility gatekeepers.

## Non-pass rule

Do not certify a case as acceptable when reported income, public benefits, or modest asset holdings are immediately eaten by mandatory or practically unavoidable tollgates. The comfort burden rises when the case depends on household “choice” in markets where people lack time, liquidity, transport alternatives, child/elder care alternatives, comparable all-in prices, or realistic exit rights.

## Evidence priority

Use administrative debt and delinquency data, fee-revenue data, household bank/nonbank use, contract terms, complaint data, repossession and deficiency records, childcare and long-term-care cost data, labor-force interruption evidence, subgroup incidence, and rule/enforcement status. Missing data is itself evidence debt when the product is opaque by design.[S260][S262][S263][S266][S273][S275]
