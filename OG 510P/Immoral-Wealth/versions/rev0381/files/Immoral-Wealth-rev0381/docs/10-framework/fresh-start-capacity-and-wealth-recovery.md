# Fresh-start capacity and wealth recovery

rev0316 adds the fresh-start layer. The cube already asks whether households can cross threshold moments, avoid washout, convert claims, and repair bad data shadows. The next question is whether a person can recover after a shock has already happened.

A wealth order fails this layer when a medical interruption, layoff, failed business, eviction filing, foreclosure, collection lawsuit, garnishment, bank levy, old conviction record, identity error, or disaster default becomes a long afterlife rather than a recoverable event.[S301][S304][S307][S309][S315]

## Core mechanism

Fresh-start failure has four steps:

1. an ordinary shock creates unpaid debt, missed rent, default, record, judgment, repossession, foreclosure, or conviction consequence;
2. the legal system converts that shock into a portable claim against wages, bank accounts, housing access, credit files, occupational licenses, or public benefits;
3. the household loses the minimum tools needed to rebuild: paycheck, car, work tools, account balance, home equity, rental access, license, or clean application path;
4. relief exists only through expert-heavy, petition-based, slow, or unaffordable procedures.

The moral problem is not that every debt or record must vanish. The problem is when the recovery system lets creditors, landlords, data vendors, employers, courts, or old legal status keep extracting from the very assets needed for rehabilitation.

## Fresh-start false passes

- **formal-discharge pass:** bankruptcy exists, but costs, exclusions, repeat-filing limits, nondischargeable debts, or thin exemptions prevent real recovery.
- **exemption-on-paper pass:** state exemptions exist, but are not self-executing, are too low for ordinary life, or require a fast court response from the debtor.
- **wage-floor pass:** federal garnishment limits exist, but remaining income is not enough to preserve rent, food, transport, care, and savings.
- **record-sealed pass:** sealing exists, but only by petition, after long delay, or without vendor deletion and screening enforcement.
- **loss-mitigation pass:** foreclosure-prevention tools exist, but servicer paperwork, fees, language access, and timing still push avoidable loss.
- **clean-slate pass:** records are sealed in court systems, but collateral consequences remain through licensing, housing, private databases, or exceptions.

## Certification rule

A case cannot comfort-certify ordinary shock survivability unless it proves a recoverable path from interruption to renewed control. The evidence must cover discharge, exemptions, wage and account protection, judgment enforcement, record cleanup, loss mitigation, and reentry collateral consequences.

The opening package should protect the tools of recovery first: wages, bank balances, cars, work tools, household goods, basic home equity, rental access, licenses, and transaction-preserving record correction.
