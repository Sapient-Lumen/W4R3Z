# Reentry, record clearance, and collateral consequences

Criminal legal records can continue to govern wealth formation after sentence completion through employment, occupational licensing, housing, education, voting, benefits, immigration, and family rules. The fresh-start question is whether the legal system allows reentry into ordinary wealth-building roles.[S315][S316][S317]

## Test

Score:

- automatic vs petition-based record clearance;
- implementation backlog and data-matching error risk;
- vendor deletion and background-screen compliance;
- occupational licensing limits and individualized review;
- housing and employment record-use restrictions;
- court debt, supervision fees, and payment-condition traps;
- restoration of voting, benefits, education, and family claims.

Clean slate is not enough if it seals a court record while collateral consequences and private databases keep the person excluded.
