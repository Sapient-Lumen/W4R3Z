# Bankruptcy discharge and exemption floor

Bankruptcy is the classic fresh-start institution, but it is not automatically a wealth-order pass. It must be judged by the usable discharge, the exemption floor, filing access, debt exclusions, repayment-plan feasibility, and post-discharge reintegration.[S301][S302][S303][S319]

## Test

A bankruptcy system provides a real fresh start only when:

- filing access is affordable and understandable;
- discharge covers the debts most likely to trap lower-wealth households, or has a realistic hardship route;
- exempt property leaves the debtor with housing, transport, work tools, household goods, and a protected account floor;
- Chapter 13 or repayment plans are feasible rather than serial failure machines;
- discharged debts and case records do not create a long score-mediated exclusion tail;
- repeat shocks can be handled without permanent civic and market exclusion.

A rising filing count is not itself failure or success. It may show distress, access, or both. The cube therefore treats filing data as a pressure signal and pairs it with discharge, exemption, and post-discharge evidence.
