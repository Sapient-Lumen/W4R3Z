# Asset exemption and protected recovery floor

A recovery floor is different from a poverty floor. It protects the assets needed to rebuild: a functioning bank account, car, work tools, household goods, basic home equity, communication devices, documents, and small emergency liquidity.[S304][S305][S306][S320]

The cube should score protected minima by replacement value and claimability, not by statutory labels. A car exemption that cannot replace a reliable work vehicle, a tools exemption below ordinary trade equipment costs, or a bank-account exemption that is not automatic does not clear the recovery floor.

## Rule

Exemptions are strong only when they are:

- high enough for current costs;
- self-executing where possible;
- portable across banks, employers, courts, and account types;
- protected after deposit and commingling;
- understandable without a lawyer;
- enforceable against debt buyers and judgment creditors;
- connected to record/report cleanup after the collection event ends.
