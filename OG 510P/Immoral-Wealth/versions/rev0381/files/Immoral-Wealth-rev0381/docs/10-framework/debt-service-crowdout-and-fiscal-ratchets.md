# Debt service crowdout and fiscal ratchets

Debt service is a wealth-order issue when it ratchets public capacity away from the lower half and toward creditors, austerity, tax-expenditure protection, or crisis-driven cuts. The question is not whether debt exists; it is whether debt and interest costs reduce the state’s ability to maintain floors, public investment, social insurance, enforcement, and crisis repair.[S346][S347][S349]

Gate 20 treats debt-service pressure as a hardening condition, not an automatic fail. Debt-financed public investment, crisis stabilization, and social insurance can strengthen a wealth order. But when interest costs rise faster than revenue capacity and policy response falls on services, benefits, infrastructure, or regressive fees, the public balance sheet becomes a washout channel.

Use this note with Gate 15, Gate 16, and Gate 18 whenever fiscal stress is likely to shift costs onto localities or households.
