# Productive ownership and workplace power

rev0312 adds the upstream productive-economy layer. A wealth order cannot be judged only after wages, jobs, contracts, equity, and workplace claims have already been allocated. The first question is whether ordinary people can form wealth **before** redistribution through decent pay, bargaining power, predictable hours, job mobility, enforceable classification, portable benefits, and a real chance to share in enterprise upside.[S220][S230][S231][S234]

The archive therefore treats workplace power as a wealth-order surface, not a separate labor-policy sidebar.

## Core rule

A case cannot comfort-certify if the bottom half and lower middle are expected to build wealth from earnings while the productive system denies them saveable room, mobility, remedies, or claim on enterprise surplus.

This matters because many apparent wealth-policy failures are actually pre-distribution failures:

- wages do not leave saveable room;
- hours, schedules, and benefits are too volatile to support borrowing, saving, or caregiving;
- workers cannot bargain collectively or individually because exit options are weak;
- noncompetes, repayment clauses, training-cost traps, immigration sponsorship, debt, or platform ratings restrict mobility;
- franchising, subcontracting, staffing, and platform intermediation detach workers from the entity that controls the economic terms;
- employees receive firm-specific risk without firm-specific upside;
- broad-based equity exists but is locked, concentrated, or not democratically governed.[S222][S223][S225][S227][S228][S229][S232][S235]

## False passes

| False pass | Meaning |
|---|---|
| wage-only pass | earnings look acceptable but hours, benefits, predictability, and mobility do not support wealth formation |
| flexibility pass | independent contracting or platform work is counted as autonomy without testing dependence, algorithmic control, and risk shifting |
| direct-employer pass | responsibility is scored only at the nominal employer even when the lead firm controls price, schedule, brand, data, or contract terms |
| ownership-rhetoric pass | employee ownership is counted without breadth, vesting, liquidity, diversification, governance, and downside-protection tests |
| union-density pass | collective bargaining is counted from membership alone without coverage, first-contract success, remedies, and sectoral reach |
| mobility pass | workers are formally free to quit but noncompetes, lock-in benefits, visas, monopsony, training debt, or local job scarcity make exit non-real |

## Certification consequence

Workplace power is Gate 14. It does not replace tax, floor, housing, public-wealth, democratic, or cross-border gates. It asks whether the order is trying to repair through transfers what it first destroys through wage-setting, contract form, and enterprise control.

The corrective package can include minimum-wage and wage-board rails, collective-bargaining coverage, noncompete and repayment-clause limits, misclassification enforcement, joint-employer responsibility, algorithmic-management transparency, portable benefits, wage-theft remedies, antitrust/monopsony enforcement, broad-based employee ownership, profit-sharing, and cooperative succession pathways.[S220][S225][S228][S229][S231][S232][S235][S236]
