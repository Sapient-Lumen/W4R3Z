# Health, disability, and care interruption

## Core claim

Health, disability, and care events are wealth-order tests. A system can show reasonable median wealth and still fail if illness, disability onset, long-term care, childbirth, caregiving, or family separation forces spend-down, debt, labor-market exit, or household-gatekeeper dependence.

The relevant unit is not only the household. The archive must ask whether the **person who experiences the interruption** keeps usable claim, income, housing, care, and exit security.

## Interruption channels

| Channel | Wealth-order failure |
|---|---|
| medical bill or uncovered service | converts illness into debt, collections, or credit damage |
| skipped care | preserves short-term cash by worsening long-term body and earnings capacity |
| disability onset | reduces earnings while adding recurring costs |
| care leave | interrupts income, savings, pension accrual, and promotions |
| asset tests / spend-down | punishes modest savings before public support arrives |
| household control | makes access to money, insurance, transport, or housing depend on spouse/family gatekeeper |
| administrative burden | turns formal rights into paper claims |

The Federal Reserve's current household-wellbeing material treats medical debt or care as a major/minor concern for a large share of adults and separately tracks skipped medical care because of cost.[S104] KFF's current health-cost work shows medical and dental bills routed through bank/lender debt, credit cards, and borrowing from family or friends.[S105]

## Disability as wealth closure

Disability is not merely an income variable. It is a wealth-order stressor because it can combine lower earnings, higher living costs, housing inaccessibility, transport dependence, medical equipment, care costs, administrative review, and household power imbalance.

A disability-onset threshold moment is `open` only when:

1. income replacement arrives before forced sale or debt;
2. assistive care and equipment are claimable without expert navigation;
3. housing remains accessible or relocation support is real;
4. savings are not destroyed by spend-down rules;
5. the person retains account, title, transport, and exit control;
6. disability and race/gender/migrant status do not compound into a blocked subgroup path.[S112][S113]

## Verdict rule

A case cannot receive a floor pass when health or disability protection depends on large private buffers. That is **forced self-insurance**, not ordinary wealth freedom.

A case cannot receive an open-circulation pass when the first serious health or care event predictably converts a lower-middle household into a debtor household.

## Program routing

- If medical debt or skipped care is dominant, route to liability-stack repair, universal/near-universal coverage, medical-debt shield, and charity-care/claims automation.
- If disability extra costs are dominant, route to asset-test repair, claimability redesign, accessible housing/transport, and person-level account control.
- If caregiving interruption is dominant, route to paid leave, care credits, pension accrual protection, direct-care infrastructure, and anti-eviction/anti-foreclosure pause rules.
- If household gatekeeping is dominant, route to individual title/control, safe-exit funds, legal aid, and non-retaliation rules.
