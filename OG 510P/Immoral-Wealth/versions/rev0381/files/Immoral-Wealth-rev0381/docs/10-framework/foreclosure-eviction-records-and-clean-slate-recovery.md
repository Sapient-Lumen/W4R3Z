# Foreclosure, eviction records, and clean-slate recovery

Housing loss has two wealth-order tails: the immediate loss of shelter/equity and the record shadow that blocks future housing, credit, insurance, and employment-adjacent screening. A fresh-start system must score both.[S309][S310][S311][S312][S313][S260]

## Test

A case must ask:

- whether loss mitigation is available before avoidable foreclosure;
- whether fees, dual tracking, language access, and document churn defeat the route;
- whether eviction filings are public before merits are resolved;
- whether dismissed, sealed, expunged, satisfied, or old records are removed from tenant-screening products;
- whether consumer reporting agencies and specialty vendors update records within a transaction-relevant window;
- whether foreclosure and eviction records can be contextualized, suppressed, or time-limited before they block housing entry.

The cube treats record cleanup as part of the fresh start, not as reputation management. A person cannot rebuild wealth if housing loss permanently excludes them from stable housing.
