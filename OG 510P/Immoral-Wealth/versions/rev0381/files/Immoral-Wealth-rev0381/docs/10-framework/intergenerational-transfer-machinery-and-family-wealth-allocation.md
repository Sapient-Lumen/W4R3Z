# Intergenerational transfer machinery and family wealth allocation

The cube already treats inheritance as an unequal-start channel. rev0317 adds the missing machinery layer: **who can move wealth across generations, when, through which legal form, with which tax treatment, under whose control, and with what title finality**.

The new claim is not that inheritance explains all wealth inequality. Some evidence treats inheritances as only a partial contributor to racial wealth gaps; the doctrine must not overclaim.[S323] The claim is narrower and more operational: transfer machinery often decides whether existing wealth becomes an early foothold, a late-life spenddown, a cloudy title, a family-law dispute, a trust-protected dynasty, or a recoverable public-care claim.[S321][S322][S324]

## Core mechanisms

1. **Timing.** Lifetime gifts, tuition support, down-payment assistance, co-signing, business stakes, and housing help matter earlier than bequests. A late inheritance may arrive after the deposit, degree, care, divorce, disability, or business-entry threshold moment has already passed.[S321][S324]
2. **Legal form.** Wills, trusts, beneficiary designations, joint title, payable-on-death accounts, retirement accounts, LLCs, and family partnerships can produce very different control and tax outcomes even for similar gross wealth.[S430][S326][S327]
3. **Administrative finality.** Probate cost, intestacy, missing heirs, heirs property, unclear title, and partition actions can turn nominal ownership into unusable or losable wealth.[S335][S336][S337]
4. **Family-law reallocation.** Divorce, QDROs, child support, unpaid care, and child-support pass-through decide whether household wealth remains person-level and child-supporting or becomes a collection/liability trap.[S328][S329][S330][S331]
5. **Late-life control.** Guardianship, conservatorship, fiduciary abuse, elder exploitation, and Medicaid estate recovery can transfer or destroy assets near the end of life.[S332][S338][S339][S340][S341]
6. **Dynastic insulation.** Trusts, GST planning, high exemption amounts, and perpetual/dynasty structures can shelter assets from ordinary taxation, creditors, spenddown, and democratic contest.[S430][S343]

## Moral screen

Intergenerational transfer becomes immoral when it does one of three things:

- **dependency:** ordinary people need family wealth or household gatekeepers to reach threshold moments;
- **closure:** transfer machinery preserves class, racial, caste, gender, or family-line advantage across generations while outsiders face late, cloudy, or costly entry;
- **rule:** dynastic vehicles and family-controlled capital convert inheritance into political, legal, philanthropic, or market power beyond ordinary public contest.

## Certification effect

A case cannot claim open circulation if early-entry opportunities depend on parental transfers, if probate/title/family-law systems systematically reduce the conversion of modest inheritances, or if high-end families can move wealth through trusts and exemptions while low-wealth families lose home equity through care spenddown, court friction, or fiduciary abuse.
