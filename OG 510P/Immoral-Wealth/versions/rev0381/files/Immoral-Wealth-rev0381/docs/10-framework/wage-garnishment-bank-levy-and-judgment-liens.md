# Wage garnishment, bank levy, and judgment liens

Post-judgment collection is a fresh-start stress test because it reaches the household's recovery tools after the underlying debt has already become legally enforceable. Wage garnishment, bank-account levy, judgment liens, and property execution can turn one unpaid debt into prolonged inability to rebuild.[S304][S305][S306][S320]

## Test

A case must ask:

- how much of wages are protected after taxes, rent, transport, food, care, and utilities;
- whether protected bank balances are automatic or require a debtor to appear and claim them;
- whether exempt funds keep protection after deposit and commingling;
- whether cars, work tools, household goods, and basic home equity are protected at real replacement values;
- whether judgment liens can force sale or block refinancing of modest homes;
- whether the debtor receives notice in time and in a language/channel they can use;
- whether protections differ sharply by state, county, employer, bank, or account type.

The cube reads non-self-executing exemptions as weak rails. A right that protects the debtor only after a fast, technical court response often functions as a paper floor.
