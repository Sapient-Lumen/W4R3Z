# Dynastic opacity, trusts, charitable vehicles, and control duration — rev0357

The cube should not score intergenerational wealth only by the moment of inheritance. Durable wealth is often routed through **control-duration machinery**: trusts, entities, advisers, donor-advised funds, private foundations, charitable trusts, family offices, and jurisdictional selection.

## Doctrine

A vehicle is not benign because it has a family, fiduciary, or charitable label. It must pass four tests:

1. **Control visibility:** the cube can see who controls, benefits from, advises, vetoes, migrates, or terminates the vehicle.
2. **Duration discipline:** the vehicle does not convert one transfer event into centuries of private command without circulation or public review.
3. **Public-subsidy accountability:** any deduction, exemption, tax expenditure, or public privilege is matched by measurable public benefit, payout, or recapture.
4. **Current-law freshness:** effective dates, reporting perimeters, and statutory thresholds are refreshed before certification.

## Interaction with gates

- Gate 10 sees the ownership/control chain.
- Gate 11 asks whether enforcement and beneficiary remedies can reach the vehicle.
- Gate 13 watches jurisdiction shopping and trust migration.
- Gate 19 asks whether family wealth circulates or locks in.
- Gate 20 asks whether public tax subsidy or foregone revenue creates a public balance-sheet exposure without public upside.
