# U.S. climate residual-insurance public backstop case — active Gate 20 case

## Verdict

**Emergency repair.** Residual and insurer-of-last-resort markets are no longer peripheral insurance plumbing. They are public/private balance-sheet instruments that can preserve mortgageable home equity while moving premium shock, deductibles, assessments, underinsurance, and post-disaster liquidity burdens to households and public systems. [S373] [S374] [S447]

## What must be scored

A policy cannot pass by proving only that coverage nominally exists. It must trace who pays when private insurers nonrenew, residual-market exposure grows, assessments are levied, rebuilding costs exceed coverage, and local tax bases weaken after repeated disasters. The cube should score owner, renter, small-landlord, municipal, state, lender, and insurer incidence separately. [S373] [S374] [S447]

## Active gates

- **Gate 04 — housing/land washout:** blocked where premiums, deductibles, or nonrenewal turn homeownership into an asset that consumes itself. [S373]
- **Gate 16 — household extraction:** blocked where insurance costs become an unavoidable cost-of-life channel. [S373] [S374]
- **Gate 20 — public balance sheet:** blocked until assessment authority, state exposure, contingent guarantees, and post-disaster public liquidity are visible. [S373] [S447]

## Seniority correction

A residual insurer preserves public wealth only if it avoids making ordinary households junior to insurable-value preservation for higher-value property. Required proof includes exposure data, rate adequacy, assessment formulas, cross-subsidy incidence, mitigation terms, affordability protection, renter pass-through treatment, and public stop-loss rules. [S373] [S374] [S447]

## Proof debt

Attach state-by-state FAIR/residual exposure, rate filings, assessment authority, reinsurance/recovery structure, affordability protections, and post-disaster rebuilding finance before passing any climate-insurance backstop claim.
