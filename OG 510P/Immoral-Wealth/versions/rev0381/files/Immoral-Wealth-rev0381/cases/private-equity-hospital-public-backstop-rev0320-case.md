# Private-equity hospital public backstop case — active Gate 20 case

## Verdict

**Emergency repair.** This case is now active because hospital continuity is an implicit public backstop while ownership, landlord, lender, and management entities may extract cash flow or assets before distress becomes visible. Public wealth fails when patients, workers, municipalities, Medicaid/Medicare systems, and state agencies inherit continuity-of-care risk after private extraction. [S394] [S395] [S396] [S397]

## What must be scored

A hospital deal cannot pass by citing private efficiency, capital access, or nominal service continuation. It must trace debt, dividends, management fees, sale-leasebacks, related-party leases, emergency-room access, maternity/behavioral-health closures, staffing, charity care, payer mix, and the public transition plan if the facility fails. [S394] [S396]

## Active gates

- **Gate 11 — enforceability/remedies:** blocked unless regulators and affected patients/workers/communities can see and challenge extraction-before-rescue structures. [S394] [S396]
- **Gate 16 — household extraction:** blocked where closures, surprise travel burdens, degraded staffing, or billing pressure shift costs to patients and workers. [S394] [S397]
- **Gate 20 — public balance sheet:** blocked until public continuity-of-care exposure, emergency transition costs, and clawback rights are explicit. [S394] [S396]

## Seniority correction

Essential-care continuity is senior to sponsor distributions, management fees, related-party rent, lender remedies, and landlord claims. A passable package includes ownership/control disclosure, asset-transfer review, dividend/fee clawbacks, service-line maintenance covenants, emergency transition funding, worker retention, and local access metrics. [S394] [S395] [S396]

## Proof debt

For each hospital system, attach the ownership/control chart, debt stack, sale-leaseback/real-estate documents, distributions and fees, service-line changes, staffing metrics, charity-care change, emergency transition exposure, and enforceable clawback authority.
