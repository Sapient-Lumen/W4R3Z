## New pattern: housing-market-power washout

rev0322 adds a pattern that cuts across housing affordability, antitrust, consumer protection, and ownership finality: **a resident can have formal shelter access or nominal ownership while still being residual to a private market-power stack.**

The three new stress cases divide the pattern into:

1. **Algorithmic coordination:** pricing tools and competitor data can weaken independent landlord pricing.[S414][S415][S416]
2. **Corporate-landlord operating systems:** fee, repair, renewal, and deposit practices can turn family-sized rental homes into recurring extraction surfaces.[S417][S418][S419][S420][S426]
3. **Manufactured-home land-lease lock-in:** homeownership without land ownership can strand home value when lot rent, community sale, financing, or moving infeasibility shifts bargaining power to landowners.[S421][S422][S423][S424][S425]
