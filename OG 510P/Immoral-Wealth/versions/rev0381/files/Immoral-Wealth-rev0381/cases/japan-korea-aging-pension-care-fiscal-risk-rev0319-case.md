# Japan/Korea aging, pension, care, and fiscal-risk case — rev0355

## Verdict

**correction required.** This is a Gate 20 stress case, not a full country certification. The unit of analysis is the public/private balance-sheet surface that can turn a nominal private market, tax preference, credit program, or public promise into a public-risk allocation.

## Dominant breach

rapid aging makes pension, health, long-term-care, housing, and inheritance systems one public-claim-security surface.

## Fastest washout

care costs and pension repairs shift burdens among workers, elders, families, women caregivers, public budgets, and future cohorts.

## Gate 20 subgate findings

- **20A_debt_interest_capacity — watch:** Age-related spending can pressure debt and public investment capacity.
- **20B_claim_security — blocked:** Public pension and care promises cannot count as wealth substitutes unless benefit security and care capacity are durable.
- **20H_generational_intergovernmental_incidence — blocked:** Aging repair is inherently intergenerational and gendered unless explicitly incidence-scored.

## Seniority waterfall under stress

1. **Current retirees and care recipients** — High reliance claimants. Public promises substitute for private savings and family capacity.
2. **Health/care providers and workforce** — Capacity gatekeepers. Care claim security fails without workers and affordable access.
3. **Working-age contributors and caregivers** — Repair and unpaid-care bearers. Contributions, taxes, and informal care loads can rise.
4. **Future cohorts** — Residual sustainability claimants. They inherit debt, contribution rates, and benefit rules.

## Main scoreboard fields

- `public_balance_sheet_gate`: warning — Aging turns pensions, health, care, and public debt into one claimant-security gate.
- `aging_pension_care_fiscal_sustainability`: warning — OECD pension and fiscal-aging sources make sustainability and adequacy a joint test.
- `long_term_care_public_claim_security`: warning — Long-term care capacity and spending pressures shape whether public claims substitute for private family wealth.
- `benefit_claim_security`: warning — Pension repairs affect promised claims and intergenerational burden.
- `generational_incidence`: warning — Repair can fall on younger workers, women caregivers, and future taxpayers if not incidence-scored.

## Opening package

The case should not receive a comfort verdict until it has a public balance-sheet register, a claimant seniority waterfall, stress assumptions, ordinary-claimant protection, and public-upside recovery where public downside is used.

## Evidence debt

- pension replacement and depletion/repair incidence by cohort/gender/income
- long-term-care eligibility, copayments, staffing, and family-care burden
- housing wealth spenddown and inheritance timing interaction
- worker contribution/tax burden and labor-market participation effects
- public debt and fiscal-rule stress under aging

## Research and speculation

The speculative risk is that this surface will look private or technocratic until a shock arrives. At that point, public authorities may protect market functioning, creditors, property values, or payment continuity first, while ordinary households absorb price increases, service cuts, claim delays, debt collection, or intergenerational repair. The cube should therefore score rescue priority before the rescue occurs, not only after an obvious bailout.
