# Eviction, foreclosure, and housing-record recovery case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Correction required.** Housing recovery remains fragile when foreclosure and eviction systems create both immediate loss and a durable record shadow.[S309][S310][S311]

## Dominant breach

The dominant breach is **record afterlife**. Even dismissed or old eviction filings can travel into tenant screening; foreclosure and mortgage distress can travel through credit files; loss mitigation may exist but fail if timing, fees, language, or document churn defeat use.[S312][S313][S260]

## Gate 18 finding

Gate 18 is **blocked for comfort certification** until housing-loss records are automatically, quickly, and vendor-effectively limited after merits, satisfaction, time, or relief.

## Opening package

Pre-filing diversion, filing-stage privacy, loss-mitigation review, automatic sealing, vendor deletion, pre-denial disclosure, time limits, record context, damages for stale/illegal use, and emergency housing bridges.

## Evidence debt

Filing-to-judgment ratios, sealing eligibility and completion, vendor deletion compliance, tenant-screen denial rates, foreclosure starts/new credit-report foreclosures, loss-mitigation success, language access, and subgroup outcomes.

Rev0331 makes this an active housing-record afterlife case. Relief must propagate from courts and servicers to tenant-screening, credit, specialty-report, insurance, and landlord/vendor systems before reentry is denied.
