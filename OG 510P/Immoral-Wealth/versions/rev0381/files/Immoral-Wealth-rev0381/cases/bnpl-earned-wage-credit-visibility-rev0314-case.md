# BNPL and earned-wage fragmented-credit visibility case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Proof debt / correction required.** BNPL and earned-wage products may reduce friction for some households, but the wealth-order risk is fragmentation. Small obligations can stack across providers, payroll rails, and apps while neither the household nor the regulator sees total payment load in time.[S264][S265][S272]

## Dominant breach

The dominant breach is **credit visibility failure**. Product-by-product affordability can hide aggregate overcommitment, late fees, charge-offs, fee/tip pressure, and payroll-priority claims.

## Core reading

The case should not assume harm from every BNPL or earned-wage transaction. The gate triggers when repeat use becomes a substitute for wages, savings, or public liquidity; when fees/tips/expedite charges are not clear; or when dispute/refund rights fail after the underlying purchase fails.[S264][S265]

## Gate 16 finding

Gate 16 is **unresolved but warning-heavy**. Gate 10 also applies because market visibility is incomplete. Certification requires provider-level and household-level total-obligation data, repeat-use incidence, late-fee/charge-off data, dispute outcomes, fee/tip pressure, and payroll-deduction effects.

## Opening package

Total-obligation dashboards, provider reporting, fee/tip disclosures, refund/dispute rights, repeat-use monitoring, hardship pauses, payroll deduction limits, public emergency-liquidity alternatives, and regulatory data access.

## Evidence debt

Cross-provider obligation stacking, repeat BNPL use for necessities, earned-wage fee/tip incidence, refund/dispute outcomes, credit-reporting effects, employer partnership terms, and subgroup incidence.

## Rev0334 hardening note

Rev0334 hardening: the case now separates product usefulness from total-obligation visibility. BNPL/EWA cannot pass while CFPB BNPL enforcement posture, EWA Regulation Z treatment, refund/dispute outcomes, and payroll-priority effects remain current-law/live-market proof burdens.[S264][S265][S272]
