# Critical-minerals industrial-policy public-upside case — active Gate 20 case

## Verdict

**Correction required.** Critical-minerals policy can be public-wealth-positive, but only if public support buys resilience, domestic/community benefit, remediation, and shared upside rather than private subsidy capture. Supply-chain urgency is not itself a pass. [S398] [S399] [S400] [S401] [S448]

## What must be scored

The cube must follow the whole public balance sheet: grants, loans, loan guarantees, tax credits, procurement premiums, permitting support, infrastructure, local environmental costs, water use, labor/community terms, offtake commitments, and upside-sharing. The public claim fails where private firms receive risk absorption but public systems do not receive enforceable benefit or recapture. [S398] [S399] [S400] [S448]

## Active gates

- **Gate 15 — place public finance:** blocked until local infrastructure, environmental, water, and service costs are assigned and remediated. [S400] [S401]
- **Gate 17 — tax expenditure visibility:** blocked where credits or deductions substitute for direct appropriations without comparable beneficiary, cost, and performance disclosure. [S399]
- **Gate 20 — public balance sheet:** blocked until public-upside recovery, milestone recapture, and supply-chain resilience are enforceable. [S398] [S399] [S448]

## Seniority correction

Strategic mineral supply is not automatically senior to ordinary public claims. Public support must buy measurable resilience and shared upside: warrants/equity, royalty or revenue participation, domestic processing milestones, community benefit agreements, labor standards, environmental remediation, clawbacks, and open reporting. [S398] [S400] [S448]

## Proof debt

Attach the award terms, tax-expenditure claim, performance milestones, offtake/processing commitments, local infrastructure burden, environmental remediation package, community benefit agreement, and upside/clawback provisions before certifying the policy as public wealth.
