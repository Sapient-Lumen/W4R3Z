# United States auto finance and repossession case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Correction required / emergency watch.** In car-dependent places, auto finance is a mobility rail and a household asset channel at the same time. Negative equity and repossession can convert a necessary car into a work-loss, credit-damage, and deficiency-debt cascade.[S254][S255][S266][S267]

## Dominant breach

The dominant breach is **mobility asset fragility**: the household needs a vehicle to reach work and care, but the financing structure can make that vehicle underwater, repossessable, and destructive at the first interruption.

## Core reading

This case turns the transport layer into a household-market-extraction layer. If transit and place access are weak, a financed car becomes private infrastructure. Counting it as wealth without scoring loan terms, insurance, repairs, negative equity, and repossession is a false pass.[S266][S267]

## Gate 16 finding

Gate 16 is **blocked** where vehicle access depends on high-cost financing, repeated negative-equity rollover, weak cure rights, deficiency balances, or repossession processes that interrupt work before remedy.

## Opening package

Auto-loan term transparency, negative-equity limits/disclosures, affordability checks, add-on and force-placed product controls, cure and reinstatement rights, deficiency-balance guardrails, repair assistance, and transport alternatives.

## Evidence debt

Repossession by subgroup and geography, deficiency balances after auction, negative-equity rollover incidence, force-placed insurance, dealer/lender concentration, repair-cost debt, transit alternatives, and job-access impacts.
