# Heirs property, probate, and title-finality case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Correction required.** Heirs property and probate/title failure turn ownership into a fragile, partly unusable claim. Families may have land or housing access but lack marketable title, leaving them vulnerable to partition, disaster-aid exclusion, credit denial, tax sale, and intergenerational loss.[S335][S336][S337]

## Dominant breach

The breach is title finality: nominal inherited property does not convert into full wealth control.

## Gate 19 finding

Gate 19 is **blocked** where title repair, small-estate administration, and partition protections are unavailable, unaffordable, or unevenly adopted.

## Opening package

Will/probate legal aid, small-estate simplification, heirs-property title clinics, UPHPA adoption and enforcement, fair partition buyout rules, disaster-aid title bridges, tax-sale protections, and subgroup reporting.

## Evidence debt

Heirs-property prevalence by county/race/region; partition outcomes; title-clearing cost and duration; small-estate access; disaster-aid denial due to title; and legal-aid capacity.
