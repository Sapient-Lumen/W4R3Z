# EU media pluralism and agenda-power case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Watch with market-plurality warning.** The EU has a live pluralism measurement and regulatory framework, but the case cannot treat media freedom as settled. Market plurality and political-independence risks remain relevant to wealth-order certification because media concentration and state-advertising dependence can shape whether reforms are publicly contestable.

## Dominant breach

The dominant breach is **agenda-power concentration risk** rather than direct wealth-share failure. It becomes decisive when media owners, state advertising, public-service governance, platform reach, or sponsored expertise shape the policy environment for tax, housing, labor, competition, public assets, and registries.[S190][S191]

## Why this case exists

This case gives the cube a non-U.S. agenda-power stress test and a methodology anchor for media pluralism.

## Core reading

The 2025 Media Pluralism Monitor places most EU Member States in medium risk for media freedom and pluralism, with significant risks persisting for all EU Member States on market plurality. It evaluates fundamental protection, market plurality, political independence, and social inclusiveness, and the European Media Freedom Act adds rules on ownership transparency, media concentration assessment, independent public-service media, and fair state advertising.[S190][S191]

## Gate 12 finding

Gate 12 is **watch**. Media/agenda conditions cannot provide democratic comfort until ownership transparency, concentration, state-advertising, political-independence, and sponsored-expertise risks are scored case by case.

## Opening package

Use the Media Pluralism Monitor dimensions as minimum fields, then cross-link media owners and sponsors to political donations, procurement, land, platform, and regulated-sector interests.

## Evidence debt

Evidence debt remains on country-specific owner-control linkage, state advertising allocation, public-service media governance, political advertising archives, think-tank sponsorship, and SLAPP/intimidation exposure.
