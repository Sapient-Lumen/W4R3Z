# United Kingdom case — rev0355

## Provisional verdict

`correction_required`.

The United Kingdom is a **pension/housing/age-hidden wealth case**. It has extensive pension wealth, owner-occupied housing wealth, and formal public institutions, but the pass is blocked by entry timing, renter/homeowner separation, regional housing pressure, age gradients, and top-tail/asset-income undermeasurement.

## Why this case was added

The UK tests whether an apparently broad household balance sheet is morally enough when wealth is locked in older cohorts, pension claims, and housing tenure. ONS reports that in April 2020 to March 2022 median household wealth in Great Britain was about £293,700, the wealthiest tenth had £1,200,500 or more, and the least wealthy tenth had £16,500 or less; net property wealth and private pension wealth together made up most recorded household wealth.[S87]

That mix creates a classic false pass: a society can look wealth-bearing because older owners and pension claimants have balance-sheet claims while young renters face deposit, rent, and location barriers.

## Dominant breach

`pension_housing_age_hidden_wealth_plus_young_renter_entry_failure`

## Gate reading

- **Share plausibility:** warning. Wealth is broad enough to avoid a simple collapse verdict, but not close to the cube's target family without deeper age, tenure, and top-tail correction.[S87][S88][S89]
- **Floor reality:** warning. Public services and benefits matter, but benefit design, housing costs, and insecure renting can leave households without a keepable asset floor.
- **Threshold moments:** fail for young renters and low-wealth households. The first deposit, parental guarantee, stable lease, and safe location moments are not broadly open.
- **Anti-averaging:** warning. Household and regional averages hide differences by age, tenure, ethnicity, disability, household form, and migration status.
- **Washout:** fail. Rent, deposits, mortgage entry, care, and regional house-price dynamics are the fastest drains.
- **Wealth-to-rule:** warning. Top-tail and land/property concentration remain politically relevant, but this seed case needs more controller-level evidence.
- **Public/common counterweight:** watch. Public institutions matter, but they do not eliminate the private deposit/ownership gate.

## Opening package

Use Pathway C plus Pathway B:

1. social/nonmarket housing expansion and repair;
2. renter security and deposit/guarantee alternatives;
3. first-foothold capital not tied to parental wealth;
4. inheritance/gift visibility for first-home support;
5. pension/liquidity distinction in official wealth reporting;
6. local land-value and planning capture screens.

## Evidence debt

Blocking certification requires:

- top 0.1% and top-tail corrected wealth estimate;
- ethnicity, region, age, tenure, disability, and migration wealth/control splits;
- direct parental deposit and guarantee evidence;
- renter wealth accumulation and eviction/insecurity evidence;
- separate liquidity scoring for pension wealth.

## Reopening trigger

Reopen if official or distributional-account data substantially revise top-tail wealth, renter/owner splits, pension valuation, or parental-transfer estimates.
