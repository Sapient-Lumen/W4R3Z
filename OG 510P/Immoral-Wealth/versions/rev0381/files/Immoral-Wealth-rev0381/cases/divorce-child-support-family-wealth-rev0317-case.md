# Divorce, child support, and family-wealth reallocation case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Correction required.** Household wealth cannot be treated as person-level wealth when divorce, retirement-account division, child-support distribution, unpaid care, and legal-aid gaps determine whether assets convert after family transition.[S328][S329][S330][S331]

## Dominant breach

The breach is person-level conversion: nominal rights to marital assets, retirement accounts, support, or caregiver recognition require orders, filings, plan compliance, and enforcement that low-wealth families may not be able to navigate.

## Gate 19 finding

Gate 19 is **blocked** when family-law systems leave caregivers, custodial parents, lower earners, or children dependent despite formal property/support rules.

## Opening package

QDRO assistance and model orders, retirement/beneficiary disclosure, affordable family-law counsel, full child-support pass-through where possible, arrears compromise for government-owed debt, ability-to-pay enforcement, caregiver credits, post-separation housing/account access, and data on gendered/racialized outcomes.

## Evidence debt

Divorce property outcomes by income/gender/race; QDRO completion; child-support distribution/pass-through; arrears owed to families vs government; sanctions by ability to pay; family-law legal-aid access; and caregiver wealth after separation.
