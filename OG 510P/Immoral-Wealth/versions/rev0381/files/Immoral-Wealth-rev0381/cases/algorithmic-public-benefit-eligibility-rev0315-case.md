# Algorithmic public-benefit eligibility and appeal-rights case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Proof debt / emergency repair in severe systems.** Digital public-benefit systems can widen access, but the cube cannot count a public floor as real when automated eligibility, fraud scoring, or data matching terminates or blocks support without notice, explanation, timely human review, and emergency continuation.[S295][S296][S297]

## Dominant breach

The breach is **automated entitlement fragility**. A claimant can be legally eligible but administratively defeated by data mismatch, model suspicion, inaccessible portals, identity proofing, vendor rules, or slow appeals.

## Core reading

The UN digital-welfare report warns that digital welfare systems can increase surveillance and rights risks for poor claimants.[S296] The EU AI Act is a comparative rail because it prohibits social scoring and addresses high-risk AI systems in consequential areas, including public-benefit-like decisions and other essential services.[S295] NIST provides a general lifecycle risk-management framework for AI systems.[S297]

## Gate 17 finding

Gate 17 is **unresolved but potentially blocking**. Certification requires model inventory, claimant notice, reasoned explanation, pre-deprivation review for severe harms, emergency continuation, offline access, appeal timing, and subgroup false-positive audits.

## Opening package

Public algorithm register; data-source notice; reason codes; no fully automated final denial for subsistence claims; human review before termination; emergency continuation; offline and assisted channels; vendor contract transparency; audit by protected group and vulnerability; and sunset/recertification.

## Evidence debt

False positives and false negatives by group, appeal reversal rate, time to restored benefits, portal/access failure, vendor model updates, human override rate, and subsistence harm during appeal.

## rev0330 activation note

The public-benefit algorithm case is now an active public-floor case. A benefit is not a real floor if an AI, data match, portal, identity vendor, or fraud model can stop support without usable notice, explanation, human review, offline access, and emergency continuation.

Operational certification remains blocked unless the scoreboard can prove transaction-preserving correction: specific notice, report/model visibility, human review, dispute or appeal timing before the threshold moment is lost, deletion/suppression after relief, and subgroup outcome evidence.
