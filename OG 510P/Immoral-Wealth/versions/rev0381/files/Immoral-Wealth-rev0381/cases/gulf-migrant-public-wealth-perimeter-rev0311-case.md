# Gulf migrant public-wealth perimeter case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Captured-perimeter warning / correction-required.** Resource wealth, sovereign assets, citizen benefits, and modernization programs cannot certify a wealth order when large migrant-worker populations remain outside the durable claimant perimeter for social protection, labor remedies, portability, family security, and voice.[S216][S217]

## Dominant breach

The dominant breach is **resident-worker exclusion**. Public/common wealth may be meaningful for citizens while the workers who build and sustain the economy have thin, employer-linked, temporary, or nonportable claims.

## Why this case exists

Earlier public-wealth cases asked whether a fund or public asset converted into claimant security. This case asks who is allowed to be a claimant. The ILO's GCC social-protection mapping is the core anchor for comparing legal and practical access.[S216]

## Gate 13 finding

Gate 13 is **blocked for comfort certification** unless a country-specific case shows migrant workers have enforceable labor, injury, wage, pension/end-of-service, health, housing, remedy, and portability claims.

## Opening package

Run the migrant claimant-perimeter scorecard: labor-law coverage, social protection, residence security, remedy access, family/care access, voice channel, and portability.

## Evidence debt

Country-specific migrant share, sector concentration, end-of-service coverage, injury and wage claims, employer-tie rules, domestic-worker coverage, complaint outcomes, deportation risk, and portability to origin countries.
