# United States bankruptcy fresh-start case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Correction required.** U.S. bankruptcy remains a real fresh-start institution, but current filing pressure and uneven exemption/discharge surfaces prevent comfort certification.[S301][S302][S303]

## Dominant breach

The breach is not the existence of bankruptcy. The breach is the gap between formal discharge and usable post-shock recovery: filing cost, chapter choice, repayment-plan feasibility, debt exclusions, asset exemptions, and post-discharge data shadows.[S319]

## Gate 18 finding

Gate 18 is **blocked for comfort certification** until the case can show that households exit bankruptcy with enough protected transport, account balance, tools, household goods, housing stability, and report repair to rebuild.

## Opening package

Affordable filing access, exemption modernization, discharge/outcome data by debt type and subgroup, Chapter 13 completion scrutiny, post-discharge report cleanup, and hardship routes for debts that otherwise become permanent.

## Evidence debt

Discharge rates by chapter and subgroup, plan completion, debt-type exclusions, attorney access, exemption adequacy by state, post-discharge rental/credit/account access, and repeat-shock outcomes.

[S301][S302][S303][S319]

## Rev0331 active scoreboard hardening

Rev0331 makes this an active post-shock recovery case. Bankruptcy is treated as a conversion rail, not a guarantee: filing access, discharge scope, exemption floors, self-execution, and post-discharge report cleanup must be proven before the fresh-start gate can pass.

The paired scoreboard remains blocked for comfort certification, but it is no longer a seed. It now carries case-specific threshold moments, source-backed fields, and evidence-debt rows that must be satisfied before any pass is permitted.
