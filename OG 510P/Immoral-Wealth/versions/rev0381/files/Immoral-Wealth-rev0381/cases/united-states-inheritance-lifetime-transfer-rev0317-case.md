# United States inheritance and lifetime-transfer case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Correction required.** U.S. wealth formation remains too dependent on family transfer timing: tuition help, down-payment support, guarantees, emergency family liquidity, and bequests. The case cannot be certified from bequest totals alone because lifetime transfers shape threshold moments before death-time inheritances arrive.[S321][S322][S323][S324]

## Dominant breach

The breach is early-entry closure: families with wealth can move assistance forward in time, while families without wealth face debt, delayed ownership, weaker risk tolerance, and missed compounding.

## Gate 19 finding

Gate 19 is **blocked for comfort certification** until public/social substitutes can offset private family transfer gaps at the deposit, tuition, guarantee, repair, and first-stake moments.

## Opening package

Progressive child/starter accounts, baby-bond-style public early assets, down-payment and guarantee substitutes not tied to parental wealth, tuition/debt reduction, inheritance/gift data by group, and public repair/emergency liquidity that arrives before crises become defaults.

## Evidence debt

Lifetime gifts by purpose and age; co-signing/guarantee prevalence; family business entry; expected bequests by group; local threshold-cost comparisons; and person-level control of transfers inside households.
