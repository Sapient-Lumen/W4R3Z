# Norway GPFG case — rev0355

## Provisional verdict

`acceptable_but_vulnerable`.

Norway is a **sovereign public-counterweight case**. The Government Pension Fund Global is a very large public/common balance-sheet institution; NBIM reports the fund value at 21,268 billion kroner at the end of 2025.[S93] This is a serious counterweight, not decorative public wealth.

The case still cannot be called `near_ideal` without household distribution, claimant-status, housing, migrant, and top-tail/control screens.

## Dominant breach

`public_counterweight_conversion_and_claimant_boundary`

## Gate reading

- **Share plausibility:** watch. Public wealth is unusually strong, but private household wealth distribution still needs separate scoring.[S92][S93]
- **Floor reality:** pass/watch. Public institutions likely reduce many defensive-buffer burdens, but person-level and subgroup evidence must confirm.
- **Threshold moments:** watch. Housing entry, debt, and region may still create early-entry barriers.
- **Anti-averaging:** warning. Migrant status, regional differences, Sami/Indigenous issues, gender/household control, and noncitizen claims need explicit scoring.
- **Washout:** watch. Housing and private debt may be more relevant than catastrophic floor failure.
- **Wealth-to-rule:** warning. Private top-tail, tax mobility, and ownership concentration still require evidence.
- **Public/common counterweight:** pass/watch. The GPFG is a major counterweight, but claimant conversion and governance boundaries matter.[S93]

## Opening package

Use Pathway F plus B/C watch:

1. preserve public-fund governance and transparency;
2. score who benefits from the fund by citizenship/residency and generation;
3. add household/top-tail and housing-entry evidence;
4. monitor whether public wealth reduces the private defensive-buffer burden;
5. maintain ownership and tax-legibility rails.

## Evidence debt

- direct bottom/middle/top wealth shares;
- immigrant and noncitizen wealth/security split;
- housing-entry data for young adults and renters;
- private top-tail controller data;
- tax mobility/exit effects using official/research sources, not anecdote.

## Reopening trigger

Reopen if GPFG governance rules, fiscal-use rules, housing affordability, household wealth inequality, or claimant boundaries materially change.
