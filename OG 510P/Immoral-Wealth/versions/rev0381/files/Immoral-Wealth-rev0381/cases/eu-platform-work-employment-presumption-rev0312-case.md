# European Union platform work employment-presumption case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Provisional watch.** Directive (EU) 2024/2831 gives the cube a strong rail template for platform work: legal presumption, supporting measures, algorithmic-management protections, information and consultation, human review, and protections against dismissal, termination, or account suspension for exercising rights.[S229]

## Dominant breach

The dominant breach is **platform-control conversion**: people performing platform work may have formal flexibility while algorithmic systems control access to work, pay, ratings, discipline, and termination.

## Core reading

The Directive is not a full pass until transposition and enforcement are visible. It is, however, a useful comparative benchmark for any case that claims platform workers are autonomous or protected.

## Gate 14 finding

Gate 14 is **watch**. The legal rail is strong on paper, but conversion depends on Member State transposition, agency capacity, social-partner implementation, worker awareness, and remedy access.

## Opening package

Score platform work by employment-status presumption, algorithmic transparency, human review, deactivation protection, data portability, intermediaries, consultation rights, and wage/benefit conversion.

## Evidence debt

Country-by-country transposition, enforcement staffing, platform compliance, appeal outcomes, false self-employment rates, worker awareness, and cross-border platform structures.
