# Tenant screening and eviction-record housing-entry case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Correction required.** Housing affordability, vouchers, renter protections, and social-housing systems cannot pass if applicants are screened out before occupancy by stale, overbroad, erroneous, or opaque tenant reports.[S286][S287]

## Dominant breach

The breach is **housing-entry score closure**. Tenant screens can repurpose credit scores, eviction filings, criminal records, income estimates, and proprietary rental-risk scores into denial recommendations.[S286]

## Core reading

The CFPB tenant-screening report identifies public records, credit reports/scores, and customized rental-risk scores/automated screening as central market challenges.[S286] HUD's guidance adds that the Fair Housing Act applies to third-party tenant screening and AI/algorithmic housing tools.[S287]

## Gate 17 finding

Gate 17 is **blocked for comfort certification** unless applicants receive transparent criteria, pre-denial record access, specific reasons, dispute opportunity before the unit is lost, and disparate-impact monitoring.

## Opening package

Eviction-record sealing and disposition hygiene; suppression of dismissed, sealed, old, irrelevant, and non-conviction records; applicant access before denial; unit-hold/dispute pauses; voucher and income-source protections; landlord override obligations; vendor disparate-impact audits; and public reporting of screening outcomes.

## Evidence debt

Denial shares by reason, eviction-record false positive rates, tenant-screen vendor coverage, unit-loss timing during disputes, protected-class disparate impact, and landlord override behavior.

## rev0330 activation note

The tenant-screening case is now an active housing-entry case. Affordability, vouchers, and tenant rights cannot pass if the applicant loses the unit before inspecting or correcting the report, eviction filing, criminal record, credit factor, or algorithmic recommendation.

Operational certification remains blocked unless the scoreboard can prove transaction-preserving correction: specific notice, report/model visibility, human review, dispute or appeal timing before the threshold moment is lost, deletion/suppression after relief, and subgroup outcome evidence.
