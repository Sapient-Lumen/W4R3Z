# EU investor citizenship and residence case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Correction-required / watch.** The EU has moved strongly against citizenship-by-investment, and the CJEU Malta decision gives the cube a hard legal calibration point. But residence-by-investment, property-linked access, intermediary incentives, tax-residence confusion, and due-diligence quality remain watch surfaces.[S203][S205][S206]

## Dominant breach

The dominant breach is **status-market privilege**: wealth can buy mobility, access, and sometimes tax or account-positioning advantages that ordinary migrants cannot obtain through comparable contribution or attachment.

## Why this case exists

The case separates legitimate migration/investment policy from a wealth-order breach. It prevents the cube from treating borders as morally neutral when money can buy membership while workers, refugees, and long-term residents face weak or slow routes.[S204][S215]

## Gate 13 finding

Gate 13 is **watch/blocked by scheme design**. It improves where citizenship-by-investment is prohibited or tightly limited; it remains under proof debt for residence schemes and property-led pathways unless genuine residence, source-of-funds, CRS, sanctions, housing, and oversight rails are shown.

## Opening package

Use the CBI/RBI screen: source-of-funds, genuine residence, beneficial-owner linkage, CRS risk, intermediary disclosure, housing effects, revocation, reporting, and ordinary-migrant comparison.

## Evidence debt

Country-by-country residence scheme data, approvals/refusals, property-market effects, tax-residence certificate use, source-of-funds failures, intermediaries, and post-approval revocation/enforcement.
