# United States credit reporting and medical-debt memory case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Correction required.** The United States has formal Fair Credit Reporting Act rails, free report rights, dispute duties, and adverse-action structures, but the score layer still converts past hardship into blocked access across multiple threshold moments.[S281][S282][S283]

## Dominant breach

The dominant breach is **credit-file memory of interruption**. Medical debt, collections, identity errors, thin files, and specialty reports can raise prices or block access even when the underlying event says more about illness, billing complexity, or precarity than future willingness to pay.[S284][S285]

## Core reading

Credit inclusion is not automatically good. A person with no score can be excluded, but a person with a damaged or specialty-report shadow can be included on punitive terms. The updated CFPB credit-invisibles work forces the cube to distinguish no-file exclusion from unscored or negatively scored inclusion.[S284]

The medical-debt surface remains legally unstable: the CFPB finalized a medical-debt credit-reporting rule in January 2025, but the current CFPB Regulation V status page records the July 2025 vacatur and says rule materials are now for reference only. The final-rule notice is retained as historical context, not as current operative law.[S285][S458]

## Gate 17 finding

Gate 17 is **blocked for comfort certification** until the case can show fast correction, suppression of weakly predictive hardship records, specialty-report visibility, and transaction-preserving disputes.

## Opening package

Suppress weakly predictive medical and small-dollar hardship records; expand clean-slate rules; require report-family disclosure; require specific adverse-action reasons; preserve pending transactions during plausible disputes; audit furnishers and specialty-report vendors; and publish correction timing/outcome data.

## Evidence debt

Subgroup dispute outcomes, correction time before transaction loss, medical-debt use by product and state, specialty-report incidence, adverse-action reason quality, and consumer relief after complaints.

## rev0330 activation note

The credit/medical-debt case is now an active legal-currentness and report-repair case. The medical-debt rule vacatur and data-broker proposal withdrawal mean the cube must treat medical-debt suppression, specialty-report coverage, and data-broker perimeter as unstable live law rather than solved consumer protection.

Operational certification remains blocked unless the scoreboard can prove transaction-preserving correction: specific notice, report/model visibility, human review, dispute or appeal timing before the threshold moment is lost, deletion/suppression after relief, and subgroup outcome evidence.
