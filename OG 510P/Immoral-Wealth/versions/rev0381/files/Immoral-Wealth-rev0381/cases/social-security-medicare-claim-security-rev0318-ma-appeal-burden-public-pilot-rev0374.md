# Medicare Advantage appeal-burden public pilot — rev0374

This is the first substantive public-data pilot after the rev0373 row contract. It remains **not certified current**.

## What the pilot now proves enough to require

- KFF's 2024 public analysis reports 52.8 million MA prior-authorization determinations, 4.1 million full or partial denials, an aggregate denial rate of 7.7%, only 11.5% of denials appealed, and 80.7% of appealed denials overturned [S582].
- HHS-OIG's June 2024 SNF review of 19 MAOs reports a 12% SNF denial rate, a 0.4% to 23% denial-rate range, 18% of denials appealed, and 95% of appealed denials overturned [S579].
- HHS-OIG's June 2024 LTCH/IRF review reports that the three largest MAOs denied LTCH/IRF requests at higher rates than most peers, appealed-denial overturn rates of 36% for LTCH and 43% for IRF, an IRF overturn-rate range from 14% to 86%, and contractor-driven denial concerns [S578].

The decision rule is now explicit: **a high overturn rate is not a self-correction pass when most denials are not appealed.** It is a reason to require unappealed-denial, delay, harm, and remedy rows.

## Reporting-scope audit

CMS Part C reporting and technical specifications require reporting completed organization determinations/reconsiderations and fully favorable, partially favorable, and adverse dispositions, and the specs distinguish request source, service/claim type, delegated-entity decisions, withdrawn/dismissed requests, reopenings, and exclusions such as IRE decisions and some concurrent reviews [S584][S585]. The Part C/D LDS route is therefore an acquisition path, not public proof [S591]. CMS-0057-F and the denial-notice route are remedy/data rails, not evidence that relief happened [S595][S596].
