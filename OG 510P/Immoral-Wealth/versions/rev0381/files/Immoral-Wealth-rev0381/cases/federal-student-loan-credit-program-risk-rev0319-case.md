# Federal student-loan and credit-program risk case — rev0355

## Verdict

**correction required.** This is a Gate 20 stress case, not a full country certification. The unit of analysis is the public/private balance-sheet surface that can turn a nominal private market, tax preference, credit program, or public promise into a public-risk allocation.

## Dominant breach

student-loan credit programs are both public asset and household liability; credit-cost accounting can hide claimant and taxpayer incidence.

## Fastest washout

education debt blocks fresh start, family formation, mobility, and public credit capacity while subsidy risk remains budget-technical.

## Gate 20 subgate findings

- **20B_claim_security — watch:** Repayment and forgiveness terms are public claims that borrowers plan around.
- **20C_contingent_liabilities_guarantees — blocked:** Federal credit exposure requires transparent subsidy and loss accounting.
- **20H_generational_intergovernmental_incidence — watch:** Borrowers, parents, and future taxpayers can absorb repair differently.

## Seniority waterfall under stress

1. **Federal government/Treasury** — Creditor and residual risk holder. Can collect, forgive, or absorb subsidy changes.
2. **Schools and credential providers** — Tuition upside holders. Often receive funds upfront without symmetric downside.
3. **Borrowers and families** — Primary liability bearers. Default and collection can impair housing, family, work, and retirement.
4. **Future taxpayers/borrowers** — Repair perimeter. Policy redesign can shift cost to future cohorts or reduce access.

## Main scoreboard fields

- `public_balance_sheet_gate`: warning — Student loans sit on the federal balance sheet as public credit assets and household liabilities.
- `student_loan_public_balance_sheet_exposure`: warning — Portfolio scale and subsidy accounting require public balance-sheet treatment.
- `federal_credit_program_subsidy_transparency`: warning — FCRA and fair-value estimates can diverge, affecting visibility of public cost and risk.
- `loan_guarantee_loss_visibility`: watch — Credit-program disclosures exist but borrower-level incidence is not a full Gate 20 pass.
- `debt_service_distributional_incidence`: missing — Borrower repayment, default, forgiveness, and collection incidence requires subgroup scoring.

## Opening package

The case should not receive a comfort verdict until it has a public balance-sheet register, a claimant seniority waterfall, stress assumptions, ordinary-claimant protection, and public-upside recovery where public downside is used.

## Evidence debt

- borrower default/collection incidence by income/race/gender/credential
- FCRA versus fair-value subsidy difference by loan program
- forgiveness/backlog and tax/appropriation incidence
- Treasury collection/offset hardship protection
- interaction with bankruptcy/fresh-start and family formation

## Research and speculation

The speculative risk is that this surface will look private or technocratic until a shock arrives. At that point, public authorities may protect market functioning, creditors, property values, or payment continuity first, while ordinary households absorb price increases, service cuts, claim delays, debt collection, or intergenerational repair. The cube should therefore score rescue priority before the rescue occurs, not only after an obvious bailout.
