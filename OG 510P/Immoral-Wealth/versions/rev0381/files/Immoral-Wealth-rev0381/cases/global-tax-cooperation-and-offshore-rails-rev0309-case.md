# Global tax cooperation and offshore rails case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Verdict:** `provisional` as a cross-border enforcement-rail case.  
**Mode:** international tax cooperation and anti-avoidance rail building.  
**Dominant breach:** domestic wealth-order packages leak when offshore accounts, entities, multinational profit shifting, crypto-assets, residence planning, and weak information exchange outrun national enforcement.  
**Fastest washout:** domestic top-tail reforms lose revenue and measurement accuracy unless exchange-of-information and minimum-tax rails remain effective.

## Why this case was added

The cube has many national cases. But top-tail wealth and corporate tax bases do not respect national borders. rev0309 adds a global rail case to prevent domestic package recommendations from pretending that offshore and multinational structures are local problems.

The OECD reports that the global minimum tax began applying from 2024 with the Income Inclusion Rule and that a January 2026 Side-by-Side package sets out simplifications, safe harbours, QDMTT primacy, and stocktake processes.[S166] The UN tax convention terms of reference create a parallel inclusive forum for international tax cooperation.[S167] The Global Forum describes exchange-of-information on request, AEOI, CRS, CARF, and capacity building, while its 2025 AEOI peer review shows broad legal-framework progress but incomplete practical effectiveness.[S178][S180]

1. Code CRS/AEOI legal-framework and practical-effectiveness status for each country case.
2. Add QDMTT/Pillar Two implementation status to corporate-tax-reliant cases.
3. Track crypto-asset reporting readiness.
4. Map beneficial-ownership peer-review gaps.
5. Treat developing-country cases as requiring capacity-building and UN-process evidence, not just OECD rules.

## Opening package

For high-offshore-risk cases, do not certify top-tail compression without exchange-of-information, beneficial-owner, and anti-avoidance rails. For lower-capacity countries, pair domestic reform with international cooperation and administrative capacity building.
