# Medicaid estate recovery and home-equity preservation case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Correction required.** Medicaid LTSS can protect care access while estate recovery and spenddown expose modest home equity to loss. Public care does not fully count as a social-wealth counterweight if it later recovers against the main asset available to low-income heirs.[S332][S333][S334]

## Dominant breach

The breach is late-life care spenddown plus posthumous recovery: the floor arrives, but it may consume the asset that would otherwise transmit a foothold.

## Gate 19 finding

Gate 19 is **blocked for comfort certification** unless recovery is narrow, hardship-protected, transparent, and measured against racialized and low-income home-equity effects.

## Opening package

Narrow recovery scope, automatic hardship waivers, caregiver-child and low-income-heir protections, clear pre-care notice, home-equity preservation thresholds, legal assistance, and public reporting of claims, collections, waivers, and estate outcomes.

## Evidence debt

Recovery claims and collections by state/service; expanded-estate rules; hardship waiver usage; home sales after recovery; heirs-property/title interactions; and demographic distribution of affected households.

## Rev0331 active scoreboard hardening

Rev0331 makes this an active care-versus-home-equity transfer case. Medicaid estate recovery must be scored by survivor exceptions, hardship waivers, state estate definition, liens, notice, and the family wealth lost relative to recovery revenue.

The paired scoreboard remains blocked for comfort certification, but it is no longer a seed. It now carries case-specific threshold moments, source-backed fields, and evidence-debt rows that must be satisfied before any pass is permitted.
