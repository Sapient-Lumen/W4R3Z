# Canada case — rev0355

## Provisional verdict

`correction_required`.

Canada is a **top-tail-undermeasurement plus housing-entry case**. The core lesson is that survey-based wealth data can materially understate top concentration while housing costs and intergenerational assistance shape who gets an early foothold.

## Why this case was added

The Parliamentary Budget Officer's 2025 update uses a high-net-worth adjustment and estimates the top 1% held 23.8% of family wealth, compared with 11.0% in the Survey of Financial Security; the same update estimates the top 20% at 69.2%, middle 40% at 27.6%, and bottom 40% at 3.3%.[S90] That is a direct warning against certifying from survey-only data.

Statistics Canada's distributional household accounts also make wealth-gap monitoring an official concern, reinforcing Canada as a good case for evidence-debt doctrine.[S91]

## Dominant breach

`top_tail_undermeasurement_plus_housing_entry_pressure`

## Gate reading

- **Share plausibility:** fail/warning. Corrected top-tail data move the case away from any soft pass.[S90][S91]
- **Floor reality:** warning. Public systems reduce some risks, but lower-wealth households remain exposed to housing and debt pressure.
- **Threshold moments:** fail. Deposit and parental support moments are likely central, especially in high-cost metros.
- **Anti-averaging:** warning. Indigenous, racialized, migrant, regional, and age/tenure gaps require a veto screen.
- **Washout:** fail. Housing cost and debt are the fast washout channels.
- **Wealth-to-rule:** warning. Top-tail correction, resource/real-estate wealth, and political finance require further evidence.
- **Public/common counterweight:** watch. Public programs matter but are insufficient to erase private asset entry gates.

## Opening package

Use Pathway B, C, and E:

1. parental-transfer and deposit/guarantee measurement;
2. renter wealth and first-home access reform;
3. housing supply plus nonmarket/land-bank instruments in high-cost markets;
4. top-tail correction and ownership-legibility rails;
5. inheritance/gift/capital-gains package review;
6. Indigenous land/title and wealth repair as a distinct veto track.

## Evidence debt

- high-net-worth data refresh;
- inter vivos transfers and family guarantees;
- Indigenous and racialized wealth/control data;
- city-level housing-entry thresholds;
- beneficial ownership and real-estate ownership structure.

## Reopening trigger

Reopen when a new PBO, Statistics Canada, or distributional wealth release changes top-tail correction, bottom/middle shares, or housing-entry evidence.
