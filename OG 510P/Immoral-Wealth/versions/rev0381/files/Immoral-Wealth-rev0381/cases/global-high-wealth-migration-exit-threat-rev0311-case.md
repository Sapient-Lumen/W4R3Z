# Global high-wealth migration and exit-threat case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Proof-debt watch.** High-wealth migration is real, but exit-threat politics often outruns evidence. The cube should treat HNWI mobility reports as an input, not a veto. Comfort or panic both require stronger stock, flow, taxpayer, asset, and revenue evidence.[S210][S211][S212]

## Dominant breach

The dominant breach is **exit blackmail risk**: the claim that top-tail actors can defeat redistribution, transparency, or enforcement by moving themselves or their assets.

## Core reading

Henley/New World Wealth projects record millionaire mobility in 2025, while Tax Justice Network warns that these figures can be misread and that reported millionaire migration can be tiny relative to national millionaire stocks. NBER evidence suggests wealthy migration responds to tax incentives, but aggregate effects depend on flows, stocks, and policy design.[S210][S211][S212]

## Gate 13 finding

Gate 13 is **watch**. Exit risk is neither zero nor automatically decisive. It should harden tax design through exit/deemed-disposal/source-tax/information-exchange rails rather than automatically lower ambition.[S56][S180][S181]

## Opening package

Build the anti-blackmail evidence pack: departures, arrivals, asset location, tax paid before/after, property/entity shifts, trust moves, FX/capital-flow pressure, and legal collection capacity.

## Evidence debt

Linked taxpayer migration data, destination data, capital-flow data, asset class data, revenue by cohort, and reliable counters to advisory-industry claims.
