# Stablecoin issuer reporting-data map — rev0364

Rev0364 is a data-cutover, not a comfort verdict.

The OCC PS-01 weekly form would identify the run-risk facts the cube needs: daily issuance/redemption, nonredeemable/restricted tokens, redemption delay, secondary-market price, reserve categories, wallet/exchange concentration, and counterparty exposure. [S550]

The OCC PS-02 quarterly form would identify the condition/capital facts the cube needs: income, balance sheet, off-balance-sheet exposures, derivative notional/fair values, capital elements, and operational-backstop assets. [S551]

The FDIC proposed rule supplies two important qualifiers: proposed two-business-day redemption and proposed no pass-through deposit insurance for reserve deposits backing payment stablecoins. [S552]

This still does **not** certify the case. The forms are proposed, some data is confidential supervisory reporting, actual filings are absent, and public-upside recovery remains missing. [S549] [S550] [S551] [S552]
