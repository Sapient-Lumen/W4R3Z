# United States tax-enforcement case — rev0355

## Verdict

**Verdict:** `correction_required` as an enforcement subsystem case.  
**Mode:** enforcement-capacity and legal-durability repair.  
**Dominant breach:** top-tail and high-complexity noncompliance cannot be morally ignored when the tax gap is large and enforcement capacity is politically fragile.  
**Fastest washout:** recognized liabilities and third-party-reported income fail to become public counterweight when staffing, case-selection, audit expertise, collection, or legal durability is insufficient.

## Why this case was added

The cube already treats taxes as one pathway for compressing top-tail rule and funding common counterweights. rev0309 adds the missing question: can the tax system actually enforce what it already claims?

IRS tax-gap projections for tax year 2022 show a gross tax gap of $696 billion, with underreporting as the largest component.[S162] Treasury reports that targeted high-income/high-wealth initiatives recovered $1.3 billion, including wealthy nonfiler and delinquent-debt work.[S163] GAO, however, finds that IRS high-income/high-wealth audit efforts still need better research data, model assessment, staffing, training, and centralized management.[S164]

This case therefore does not say “new tax first.” It says: no serious wealth-order verdict can ignore enforcement capacity.

## Legal durability note

`Moore` is a design warning for any U.S. package that jumps directly to a broad wealth tax without constitutional analysis.[S168] The legal path should keep multiple instruments alive: enforce existing liabilities, improve high-wealth audit capacity, tighten inheritance/gift/dynasty rules, reform capital-gains deferral, improve entity reporting, and use legally durable property/land/state instruments where appropriate.

## Proof debt

1. Update high-income/high-wealth audit workforce, case-cycle, and collection data.
2. Separate nonfiling, underreporting, and underpayment by income/wealth complexity.
3. Add partnership, trust, private-fund, and private-business enforcement indicators.
4. Track appropriations, staffing, and political defunding risk.
5. Map which top-tail instruments are constitutionally durable after `Moore`.

## Opening package

Lead with enforcement capacity and legal-durability repair before treating new top-tail taxes as executable.
