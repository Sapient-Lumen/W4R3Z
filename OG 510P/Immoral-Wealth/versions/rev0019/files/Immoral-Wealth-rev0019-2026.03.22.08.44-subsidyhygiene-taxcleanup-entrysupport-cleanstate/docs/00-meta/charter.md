# Charter

## Mission

Build a compact research archive that can answer, with increasing sharpness:

- what counts as **immoral wealth inequality**,
- what an **acceptable wealth distribution** would look like,
- what **institutional portfolio** can move a society toward it,
- how to detect when a proposed reform compresses inequality only cosmetically while leaving domination intact.

## Scope

This archive is primarily about **wealth**, not just income.
That means it cares about:

- assets and debt,
- inheritance,
- capital ownership,
- housing and land,
- public wealth,
- the conversion of economic wealth into political power.

## North-star discipline

The archive is oriented toward a society in which:

- almost everyone has real asset security,
- a large middle majority owns substantial wealth,
- oligarchic tails exist only in limited form,
- dynastic persistence is materially weaker,
- public institutions are not balance-sheet paupers,
- work matters again relative to inheritance and rent extraction.

## Non-goals

This archive is not trying to:

- defend strict sameness,
- produce a single-country tax memo only,
- collapse wealth justice into charity,
- treat GDP growth as an adequate moral score,
- hoard source artifacts for their own sake.
