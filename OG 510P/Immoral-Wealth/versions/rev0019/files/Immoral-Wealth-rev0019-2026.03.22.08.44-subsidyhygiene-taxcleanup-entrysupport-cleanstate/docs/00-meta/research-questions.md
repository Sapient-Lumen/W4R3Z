# Research questions

## Stable questions

1. What distribution best balances anti-domination, innovation, pluralism, and democratic stability?
2. How much inequality can remain before wealth predictably converts into political rule?
3. Which metrics are most decision-useful: top shares, wealth-to-income ratios, public wealth, inheritance shares, rent burdens, or some combined index?
4. How should public wealth be measured for program purposes: sovereign/social wealth funds, housing stock, infrastructure, pension reserves, or all of the above?
5. Under what conditions are recurrent net wealth taxes worth the administrative and political burden relative to stronger capital-gains and inheritance taxation?[S04][S05]
6. How large must a universal capital endowment be before it changes life chances in a durable way rather than merely providing symbolic inclusion?[S12]
7. How can housing reform broaden access without simply capitalizing subsidies into land prices?[S07][S13]
8. What institutional package most reduces oligarchic power conversion: tax, antitrust, campaign-finance reform, media pluralism, or some ordered bundle?[S15]
9. Which country archetypes require the biggest changes in sequencing: rich asset-price democracies, social democracies with aging homeowner majorities, middle-income oligarchies, or resource-rent states?
10. Which anti-capture measures must be installed early, before large redistribution fights trigger stronger elite resistance?[S15]
11. How should the archive calibrate the switch from prevention to correction to anti-oligarchy response: by red-line count alone, by capture indicators, or by a weighted composite?
12. How should the archive calibrate the **asset-floor pass rate** across age groups, family forms, and housing systems?[S07][S17]
13. Which forms of **asset composition** matter most for bottom-half independence: liquid assets, housing equity, pension rights, cooperative shares, or some mix?[S13][S16]
14. What are the best compact metrics for **control concentration** in firms, land, infrastructure, and media that can travel across countries without becoming too bespoke?[S15][S16]
15. What are the best compact metrics for **intergenerational circulation**: inheritance concentration, homeownership persistence, opportunity indices, young-adult positive-wealth rates, or some composite?[S18][S19]
16. How much parental advantage is tolerable before the archive should treat a society as sliding from inequality into **dynastic closure**?[S18][S19]
17. Which compact indicators best capture **reconcentration pressure**: markup trends, housing-entry ratios, common ownership, tax-compliance gaps at the top, or some composite?[S06][S10][S20]
18. What is the best compact indicator of the **stewardship split** between broad beneficial ownership and narrow exercised control: asset-manager voting concentration, pass-through voting coverage, mandate specificity, or some bundled index?[S20][S26]
19. Over what time horizon should the archive judge durability: one business cycle, one asset-price cycle, or a fixed decade window?
20. How should the archive calibrate the **social-floor condition**: which public protections most reduce the private wealth households need for genuine independence?[S21][S22]
21. How much weak healthcare or pension protection should count as a failure of wealth justice rather than merely a separate welfare-state problem?[S21][S22]
22. What is the minimum **legibility package** a state needs before strong top-tail taxation, anti-monopoly review, and anti-capture enforcement become realistically enforceable?[S04][S23]
23. How public should beneficial-ownership and transfer information be, and where should the archive draw the line between ordinary privacy and oligarchic secrecy?[S23]
24. Which compact indicators best capture whether broad ownership is **too debt-encumbered to count as durable independence**: debt-service burdens, debt-to-income, negative equity, refinancing dependence, or some composite?[S17][S24][S25]
25. What is the best compact indicator of **life-course timing failure**: age at first durable asset ownership, distribution of inheritance receipt ages, young-adult secure-tenure rates, or some composite?[S05][S13][S19]
26. Which compact indicators best capture whether apparently broad household wealth is hiding **person-level ownership failure**: title skew, individual account access, pension-entitlement gaps, or exit-security outcomes?[S13][S27][S28][S29][S30]
27. Which compact indicators best capture **group-stratification failure**: disaggregated wealth gaps, secure-tenure gaps, mortgage-access gaps, land-title gaps, business-asset gaps, or some composite that can travel across countries without becoming too bespoke?[S13][S19]
28. What is the smallest credible **distributional-budgeting package** needed to keep wealth reform on track: ex ante microsimulation, linked admin data, public distributional statements, independent modelling, or some ordered minimum bundle?[S31]
29. Which compact indicators best capture **non-extractive wealth formation**: entry-adjusted housing gains, rent / markup absorption of wage gains, revaluation-versus-new-saving splits, or a bundled source-quality index?[S07][S10][S13][S16][S20]
30. Which compact indicators best capture **downside-discipline failure**: rescue-cost recovery rates, breadth of untargeted asset support, shareholder / creditor write-down intensity, or some composite of public-loss transfer?[S32][S33]
31. Which compact indicators best capture **upside-down subsidy failure**: top-heavy tax-expenditure incidence, housing-subsidy capitalization, retirement-incentive skew, or some bundled subsidy-hygiene index?[S31][S36][S37]
