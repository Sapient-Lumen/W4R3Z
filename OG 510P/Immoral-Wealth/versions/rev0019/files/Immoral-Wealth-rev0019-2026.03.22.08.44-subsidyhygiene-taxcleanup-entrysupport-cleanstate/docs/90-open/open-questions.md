# Open questions

1. Should the archive eventually express the target distribution as a **set of acceptable envelopes** rather than one central attractor?
2. How much positive public wealth can substitute for private bottom-half wealth without leaving people too dependent on state administration alone?
3. Which country pathway should be treated as the archive's default reference case: Anglo asset-price democracies, Europe-style social democracies, or middle-income oligarchic states?
4. What are the best compact metrics for **wealth-to-rule conversion**: donation concentration, lobbying disclosures, beneficial-ownership opacity, procurement concentration, media concentration, or some composite?[S15]
5. Under what conditions do employee-ownership or profit-sharing schemes build durable broad wealth rather than merely supplement wages?
6. What anti-capture measures must be implemented **before** major top-tail taxation to keep reform from being quietly neutralized?[S15]
7. What is the best empirical trigger for moving from **correction** to **anti-oligarchy response**?
8. How strict should the archive be about the **asset-floor pass rate**: majority, supermajority, or cohort-specific thresholds?[S17]
9. What mix of liquid assets, housing security, and long-run asset claims should count as a floor pass in different country types?[S07][S17]
10. Which asset classes should count as **key assets** for topology purposes in different country types: housing, land, finance, media, utilities, digital infrastructure, or all of the above?
11. How should the archive measure **circulation failure**: parental-homeownership persistence, share of inequality explained by inherited circumstances, large-transfer concentration, or cohort wealth gaps?[S18][S19]
12. How much delayed inheritance and older-cohort asset hoarding can remain before the archive should treat the society as effectively **dynastic**, even if cross-sectional shares look better?
13. What is the best compact trigger for **reconcentration failure**: falling labor share, rising markups, worsening entry affordability, top-tail tax leakage, or a bundled index?[S06][S10][S20]
14. How many years of backsliding after reform should count as proof that the society improved only temporarily rather than constitutionally?
15. How should the archive compare countries where private wealth is broader but essential risks are more privatized with countries where private wealth is lower but the social floor is stronger?[S21][S22]
16. Which compact indicators best capture whether households are being forced into **excess precautionary self-insurance**?[S21][S22]
17. Which compact metrics best capture whether a country's wealth order is **visible enough to govern**: beneficial-ownership verification, registry interoperability, cross-border information exchange, audit coverage, or some composite?[S04][S23]
18. When should opacity itself be treated as a trigger for **anti-oligarchy response**, even before the measured distribution worsens further?[S15][S23]
19. What is the best compact trigger for **encumbrance failure**: debt-service stress, leverage at first entry, negative-equity incidence, or a bundled debt-quality index?[S13][S17][S24][S25]
20. What is the best compact trigger for **silent ownership failure**: extreme asset-manager voting concentration, low pass-through coverage, weak mandate specificity, or a bundled stewardship-concentration index?[S20][S26]
21. What is the best compact trigger for **timing failure**: late median inheritance age, weak under-40 positive-wealth rates, delayed secure tenure, or a bundled cohort-entry index?[S05][S13][S19]
22. What is the best compact trigger for **person-level ownership failure**: skewed title, weak individual account access, pension-entitlement gaps, poor divorce/widowhood protection, or a bundled intra-household power index?[S13][S27][S28][S29][S30]
23. What is the minimum distributional-budgeting stack a medium-capacity state needs before the archive's program becomes operational rather than rhetorical?[S31]
24. What is the best compact trigger for **non-extractive formation failure**: worsening entry-adjusted affordability, rising rent / markup absorption of wage gains, incumbent revaluation dominating wealth growth, or a bundled source-quality index?[S07][S10][S13][S16][S20]
25. What is the best compact trigger for **downside-discipline failure**: weak burden sharing, low recovery of rescue costs, repeated broad asset backstops, or a bundled public-loss-transfer index?[S32][S33]

26. What is the best compact trigger for **public-value-capture failure**: weak land-value recapture, unconditional public-land disposal, thin affordable-housing obligations relative to uplift, or a bundled common-uplift index?[S34][S35]

27. What is the best compact trigger for **upside-down subsidy failure**: top-heavy tax-expenditure incidence, high capitalization into prices, weak entrant benefit, or a bundled subsidy-hygiene index?[S31][S36][S37]
