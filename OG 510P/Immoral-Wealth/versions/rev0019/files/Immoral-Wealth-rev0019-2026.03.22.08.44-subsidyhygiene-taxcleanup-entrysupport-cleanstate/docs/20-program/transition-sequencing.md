## Suggested sequence in ordinary prevention mode

### Phase 1 — measurement, legitimacy, asset mapping, and early anti-capture

- publish top shares, bottom shares, public wealth, inheritance concentration, housing burdens,
- make clear that the target is **broad ownership**, not bureaucratic leveling,
- identify the main rent channels and main dynastic channels in the target country,
- map control over housing, land, infrastructure, finance, and major firms where concentration is likely to matter,
- establish basic beneficial-ownership, lobbying, and political-finance transparency where absent,
- connect land, company, trust, procurement, and tax records enough that the top tail is meaningfully visible,[S15][S23]
- publish a first inventory of major housing, retirement, and other asset-related tax expenditures and subsidies, with initial distributional incidence where feasible,[S31][S36][S37]
- review rescue, resolution, guarantee, and emergency-support architecture early enough that public backstops will not quietly preserve incumbent wealth on favourable terms in the next stress episode.[S32][S33]

### Phase 2 — lower extraction and build the floor

- universal basic services where feasible,
- housing relief and social housing build-out,
- debt relief where debt is blocking family formation or asset acquisition,
- early cohort-entry measures where younger and non-inheriting households are being asked to wait too long for secure tenure or positive wealth,
- early person-level title, account, and marital-property fixes where household wealth is broadening faster than person-level independence,
- early action on refinancing traps, arrears spirals, or dangerous leverage where nominal ownership is too debt-fragile to count as durable security,
- liquid-buffer or emergency-savings architecture where households remain one shock from reversal,
- early health, care, pension, and other social-floor measures where weak public protection is forcing excessive precautionary private saving,
- early cleanup or redesign of upside-down housing and retirement subsidies where hidden spending is reinforcing incumbent advantage or capitalising into prices.[S07][S17][S21][S22][S36][S37]

### Phase 3 — reopen circulation

- first-home access policy that does not simply bid up prices,
- early-life capital endowments / social inheritance,
- inheritance and gift reform aimed at large dynastic transfers,
- policies that shift asset access forward in time rather than relying on late bequests to do the distributive work,
- youth wealth-entry channels that do not depend on parental collateral.[S18][S19]

### Phase 4 — raise labor power

- bargaining coverage,
- minimum-wage architecture,
- anti-monopsony enforcement,
- worker voice and governance reforms.

### Phase 5 — spread capital ownership

- baby bonds / social inheritance,
- public wealth funds,
- employee ownership or profit-sharing channels where well designed.

### Phase 6 — compress the top tail

- capital gains reform,
- inheritance / gift tax reform,
- selective recurrent wealth taxation where administratively justified,
- anti-avoidance and transparency.

### Phase 7 — lock in anti-domination institutions

- campaign-finance reform,
- media pluralism and transparency,
- beneficial ownership transparency,
- stronger public-interest rulemaking capacity,
- procurement and competition safeguards that make capture harder over time.[S15]

## What changes in correction or anti-oligarchy mode

If the society has already crossed one or more major red lines, the sequence compresses.

- In **correction mode**, phases 2 through 6 should overlap much more heavily.
- In **anti-oligarchy response mode**, phases 1, 3, 6, and 7 cannot wait their turn. Anti-capture, asset transparency, youth-entry reopening, timing repair, top-tail compression, and rescue-governance hardening need to run alongside floor-building and ownership-spreading.

## Why this order

This order tries to avoid a common failure pattern: taxing concentrated wealth before the public has visible alternatives, administrative capacity, or broad-middle beneficiaries. A program that visibly improves ordinary life, lowers extraction, builds household resilience, strengthens the social floor, suppresses fast reconcentration channels, and reopens ownership to people without rich parents creates a constituency for the next steps.

But the archive now states more plainly that this is only true in settings where oligarchic resistance has not already become regime-defining. If the wealthy can still hide ownership, dominate lobbying channels, or fund politics opaquely, they will often blunt later redistributive steps before those steps mature.[S15]

## Budget-process capacity

rev0015 adds one more sequencing rule: build the **distributional-budgeting operating system** early, not after the main fight.
That means linked administrative data where lawful, a minimal modelling capability, and a budget routine that tests major measures before passage and revisits them after implementation.[S31]

Without that, even well-aimed reforms are too easy to mistarget, underfund, or reverse.
