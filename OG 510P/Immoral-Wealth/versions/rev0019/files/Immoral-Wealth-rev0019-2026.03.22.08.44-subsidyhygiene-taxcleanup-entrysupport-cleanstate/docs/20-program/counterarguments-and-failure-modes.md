# Counterarguments and failure modes

## "This kills incentives"

A broad-middle distribution still leaves room for high rewards. The target here is not flatness; it is preventing the point at which wealth becomes governing power.

## "Just grow the pie"

The IMF's 2024 G20 note argues that policy settings shape the growth-inequality relationship, and that redistribution, education spending, and worker-rights institutions can materially change inequality outcomes.[S11] Growth by itself is not enough.

## "A wealth tax solves everything"

This archive rejects single-instrument thinking. Recurrent net wealth taxes may help in some settings, but capital gains reform, inheritance taxation, labor power, housing reform, and public wealth often matter just as much or more.[S04][S05]

## "Inheritance taxes are politically doomed"

The archive's answer is that anti-dynasty policy should be judged by design, not by the worst version people imagine. The archive favors exemptions for modest transfers and harder treatment of large dynastic transfers, trusts, and engineered gifts.[S05][S19]

## Main failure modes

### 1. Capital flight and avoidance
Response: information exchange, anti-avoidance, exit rules, better asset records, and international cooperation.[S04][S05]

### 2. Subsidy capitalization into rents
Response: pair asset support with housing and land reform.[S07]

### 3. Middle-class backlash
Response: make the program visibly broadening rather than purely punitive; the target is a thicker middle, not merely a smaller elite.

### 4. Public-fund capture
Response: independent governance, transparency, clear mandates, and anti-patronage rules.

### 5. Symbolic inclusion without power shift
Response: track ownership shares, public wealth, inheritance concentration, and rent burdens rather than only cash transfers.

### 6. Snapshot improvement with dynastic persistence
Response: track parental-background effects, housing-entry inheritance, and young-adult footholds so a better-looking wealth distribution cannot hide a hardened birth lottery.[S18][S19]

### 7. One-cycle reform followed by rapid reconcentration
Response: track rents, markups, entry affordability, and top-tail tax/compliance gaps so temporary gains are not mistaken for a durable settlement.[S06][S10][S20]
