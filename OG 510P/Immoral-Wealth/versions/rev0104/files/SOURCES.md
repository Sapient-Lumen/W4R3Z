# SOURCES

A compact source ledger for the archive. IDs are stable once added.

## S01 — WID Methodology
- **Organization:** World Inequality Database
- **URL:** https://wid.world/methodology/
- **Why it matters here:** Measurement anchor for income and wealth distribution data.

## S02 — Regional Wealth Inequality / Global Economic Inequality
- **Organization:** World Inequality Report 2026 / WID
- **URL:** https://wir2026.wid.world/insight/regional-wealth-inequality/
- **Why it matters here:** Current benchmark for regional private/public wealth, top shares, bottom shares, and labor share trends.

## S03 — Income and wealth inequalities: Society at a Glance 2024
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/society-at-a-glance-2024_918d8db3-en/full-report/income-and-wealth-inequalities_7ac4178f.html
- **Why it matters here:** Compact OECD benchmark for wealth concentration and cross-country comparison.

## S04 — How to Tax Wealth
- **Organization:** IMF
- **URL:** https://www.imf.org/en/publications/imf-how-to-notes/issues/2024/03/08/how-to-tax-wealth-544948
- **Why it matters here:** Current guide to the tradeoffs between capital income taxes, wealth taxes, and inheritance taxes.

## S05 — Inheritance Taxation in OECD Countries
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2021/05/inheritance-taxation-in-oecd-countries_2d33ceae.html
- **Why it matters here:** Best compact source in this archive on inheritance-tax equity, efficiency, design, and the warning that wealth may stay concentrated among older cohorts for longer while inheritance age rises.

## S06 — Taxation and Inequality
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2024/07/taxation-and-inequality_b7cf450c.html
- **Why it matters here:** Recent overview of how tax systems can mitigate or worsen inequality.

## S07 — Affordable housing: Society at a Glance 2024
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/society-at-a-glance-2024_918d8db3-en/full-report/affordable-housing_1a2ec30f.html
- **Why it matters here:** Shows how housing cost burdens intensify inequality and why social housing matters.

## S08 — The Role of Firms in Wage Inequality
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/the-role-of-firms-in-wage-inequality_7d9b2208-en.html
- **Why it matters here:** Links employer concentration, bargaining power, and wage inequality.

## S09 — Collective bargaining and social dialogue
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/sub-issues/collective-bargaining-and-social-dialogue.html
- **Why it matters here:** Concise anchor for why bargaining institutions still matter.

## S10 — Competition and market dynamism
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/competition-and-market-dynamism.html
- **Why it matters here:** Shows concentration and markups as inequality-relevant market structure issues.

## S11 — G20 background note on the impact of growth on inequality
- **Organization:** IMF
- **URL:** https://www.imf.org/external/np/g20/pdf/2024/072224.pdf
- **Why it matters here:** Useful current synthesis on redistribution, education spending, labor institutions, and inequality dynamics.

## S12 — Modeling the Impact of a Federal Baby Bonds Program
- **Organization:** Urban Institute
- **URL:** https://www.urban.org/research/publication/modeling-impact-federal-baby-bonds-program
- **Why it matters here:** Operational evidence point for social inheritance / baby-bond design.

## S13 — Mapping trends and gaps in household wealth across OECD countries
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/mapping-trends-and-gaps-in-household-wealth-across-oecd-countries_4bb6ec53-en.html

## S14 — Membership of unions and employers' organisations, and bargaining coverage
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/membership-of-unions-and-employers-organisations-and-bargaining-coverage_fe47107c-en/full-report.html

## S15 — Anti-Corruption and Integrity Outlook 2024 — overview
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2024/03/anti-corruption-and-integrity-outlook-2024_6e7ad8ce/full-report/component-4.html
- **Why it matters here:** Anchor for lobbying, political-finance, and integrity-framework weaknesses relevant to wealth-to-rule conversion.

## S16 — Distribution of household wealth in line with national accounts
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/distribution-of-household-wealth-in-line-with-national-accounts_65d3d98c-en.html
- **Why it matters here:** Key new anchor for wealth composition: bottom quintile often negative wealth, inequality driven by financial assets, and housing as the largest household asset.

## S17 — Economic insecurity in Europe and potential policy responses
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/economic-insecurity-in-europe-and-potential-policy-responses_4323bb60-en.html
- **Why it matters here:** Compact anchor for liquid-asset resilience and household shock buffers; highlights widespread financial fragility and the policy need to build buffers against income shocks.

## S18 — Building pathways to opportunity
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/building-pathways-to-opportunity_239063a4-en/full-report.html
- **Why it matters here:** Compact 2025 opportunity anchor: over 25% of market-income inequality is tied to inherited circumstances across the OECD, parental background contributes over 60% of inequality of opportunity in most countries, and the policy mix includes child development accounts, inheritance taxation, and social housing.

## S19 — Informing policy: What can be done to ensure a more level playing field?
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/to-have-and-have-not-how-to-bridge-the-gap-in-opportunities_dec143ad-en/full-report/informing-policy-what-can-be-done-to-ensure-a-more-level-playing-field_870f6f4d.html
- **Why it matters here:** Compact 2025 anchor for the intergenerational persistence of homeownership, the larger wealth premium from earlier-life transfers, and the case for anti-dynasty housing and inheritance policy.

## S20 — Reigniting investment for more resilient growth
- **Organization:** OECD Economic Outlook 2025
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2025/06/oecd-economic-outlook-volume-2025-issue-1_1fd979a8/full-report/reigniting-investment-for-more-resilient-growth_99b36090.html
- **Why it matters here:** Narrow new anchor for rising concentration, higher markups, superstar-firm investment concentration, and common-ownership pressures that can rapidly reconcentrate wealth after reform.

## S21 — Financial hardship and out-of-pocket expenditure
- **Organization:** OECD Health at a Glance 2025
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/health-at-a-glance-2025_8f9e3f98-en/full-report/financial-hardship-and-out-of-pocket-expenditure_4160e6d2.html
- **Why it matters here:** Compact anchor for why healthcare financial protection changes how much precautionary private wealth households need; shows that weak protection can deepen poverty and inequality.

## S22 — Pensions at a Glance 2025
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pensions-at-a-glance-2025_e40274c1-en.html
- **Why it matters here:** Compact anchor for why pension design and first-tier benefits change how much private wealth households must hold to avoid old-age insecurity and inequality.

## S23 — Beneficial Ownership and Tax Transparency – Implementation and Remaining Challenges
- **Organization:** OECD / Global Forum on Transparency and Exchange of Information for Tax Purposes
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/beneficial-ownership-and-tax-transparency-implementation-and-remaining-challenges_f95790b1-en.html

## S24 — Household debt
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/data/indicators/household-debt.html

## S25 — Household financial fragility and asset poverty in OECD regions
- **Organization:** OECD

## S26 — Institutional Investor Engagement and Stewardship
- **Organization:** OECD

## S27 — To Have and Have Not – How to bridge the gap in opportunities

- **Why it matters here:** Compact 2025 anchor for why household-level inequality measures can understate person-level inequality; notes that gender effects become much stronger when measured using individual earnings rather than household market income.

## S30 — Gender pension gap: Pensions at a Glance 2025
- **Organization:** OECD

## S32 — Exceptional Public Solvency Support to the Banking Sector: Pitfalls and Good Practices
- **Organization:** IMF

## S33 — Addressing Market Dysfunction and Liquidity Stresses in Nonbank Financial Intermediaries
- **Organization:** IMF

## S34 — Global Compendium of Land Value Capture Policies

## S35 — Strengthening policies and institutions to increase housing affordability and investment in Czechia
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/housing-reforms-in-czechia-and-poland_4988c473-en/full-report/strengthening-policies-and-institutions-to-increase-housing-affordability-and-investment-in-czechia_9ce4b5be.html

## S36 — Housing Taxation in OECD Countries
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/housing-taxation-in-oecd-countries_03dfe007-en.html

## S37 — Financial Incentives and Retirement Savings
- **Organization:** OECD

## S39 — Comparative Assessment of Asset Requirements for Minimum Income Benefit Recipients

## S40 — Modernising Access to Social Protection
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/modernising-access-to-social-protection_af31746d-en.html

## S41 — Protection Gaps in Retirement Savings
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/protection-gaps-in-insurance-for-natural-hazards-and-retirement-savings-in-asia_294f044e-en/full-report/protection-gaps-in-retirement-savings_ab0a0abc.html
- **Why it matters here:** Current OECD anchor for why long vesting periods, weak portability across schemes, and employer- or sector-tied entitlements can strand mobile workers and make long-run asset claims less durable than headline enrolment suggests.

## S42 — HC1.2 Housing Costs over Income
- **Organization:** OECD Affordable Housing Database
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/data/datasets/affordable-housing-database/hc1-2-housing-costs-over-income.pdf
- **Why it matters here:** Narrow OECD anchor that total housing costs include structural insurance, mandatory services and charges, regular maintenance and repair, taxes, and utilities, and that these costs can materially raise burdens even for owners.

## S43 — HC2.1 Living Space
- **Organization:** OECD Affordable Housing Database
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/data/datasets/affordable-housing-database/hc2-1-living-space.pdf
- **Why it matters here:** Narrow OECD anchor that housing adequacy depends on household composition, that overcrowding adjusts for household members' age, gender, and relationship rather than room count alone, and that overcrowding is higher in low-income households in many countries.

## S44 — Land 2030 – About
- **Organization:** World Bank
- **URL:** https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/land-2030/overview
- **Why it matters here:** Current World Bank anchor that land and property-rights reform contributes to growth, urbanisation, domestic resource mobilisation, job creation, gender equality, conflict mitigation, and social cohesion, while many people still lack secure land rights and insecurity is worse for women and Indigenous Peoples.

## S45 — Consumer Finance Risk Monitor 2026
- **Organization:** OECD
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/consumer-finance-risk-monitor-2026_61f7dbe0-en/full-report.html
- **Why it matters here:** Current OECD anchor that low financial literacy remains a top consumer-side risk, that only a minority of adults reach the OECD/INFE minimum target score, and that ordinary people often do not seek independent advice when choosing financial products, which supports the archive's anti-maze / low-expertise-operability rule.
