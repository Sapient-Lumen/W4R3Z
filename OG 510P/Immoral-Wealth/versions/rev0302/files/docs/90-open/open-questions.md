# Open questions

These are the archive's unresolved design and measurement questions.
They should stay as a **question ledger**, not a shadow archive.
Use [`../00-meta/research-triage-and-closure-rules.md`](../00-meta/research-triage-and-closure-rules.md) to decide which ones deserve active work now and which should remain visible but parked.

1. What empirical thresholds should decide when a society can sit near **15 / 55 / 20 / 10**, when it should be held closer to **20 / 50 / 20 / 10**, and when a stronger **25 / 45 / 20 / 10** lower-half foothold is morally preferable?
2. What is the best empirical ceiling on **public/social substitution** before the lower half becomes too dependent on administration, repeated claiming, or low-take-up support rather than holding enough direct wealth in its own right?[S40]
3. Which country pathway should be treated as the archive's default reference case: Anglo asset-price democracies, Europe-style social democracies, or middle-income oligarchic states?
4. Given rev0031's pathway families, what is the best compact composite for **wealth-to-rule conversion** across political finance, lobbying, opacity, procurement / licensing, media agenda power, stewardship bottlenecks, and rescue favoritism?[S15][S23][S26][S32][S33]
5. Under what conditions do employee-ownership or profit-sharing schemes build durable broad wealth rather than merely supplement wages?
6. What anti-capture measures must be implemented **before** major top-tail taxation to keep reform from being quietly neutralized?[S15]
7. Given rev0031's provisional trigger rule, which empirical markers best predict when **one strong wealth-to-rule channel by itself** should already count as anti-oligarchy response, even before several other vetoes are simultaneously visible?
8. Given the archive's provisional **supermajority-plus-cohort** rule, what exact pass-rate threshold should be used across different wealth-data systems and country types?[S17]
9. What is the best cross-country operationalisation of the archive's **core floor pass**: which combinations of liquidity, tenure security, debt quality, and long-run claims travel best across data systems?[S07][S17]
10. Which asset classes should count as **key assets** for topology purposes in different country types: housing, land, finance, media, utilities, digital infrastructure, or all of the above?
11. How should the archive measure **circulation failure**: parental-homeownership persistence, share of inequality explained by inherited circumstances, large-transfer concentration, or cohort wealth gaps?[S18][S19]
12. How much delayed inheritance and older-cohort asset hoarding can remain before the archive should treat the society as effectively **dynastic**, even if cross-sectional shares look better?
13. What is the best compact trigger for **reconcentration failure**: falling labor share, rising markups, worsening entry affordability, top-tail tax leakage, or a bundled index?[S06][S10][S20]
14. How should the archive calibrate its provisional **durability window** across country types: one political-budget cycle and one asset / credit cycle by default, but how long should each be in practice?
15. How much **promissory credit** should the archive allow for legally specified but not yet delivered future claims, and which observable features best distinguish credible front-loaded transition protection from backloaded compensation stories?
16. How should the archive compare countries where private wealth is broader but essential risks are more privatized with countries where private wealth is lower but the social floor is stronger?[S21][S22]
17. Given rev0036's dedicated rule, what is the best compact trigger for **forced-self-insurance failure**: catastrophic out-of-pocket exposure, weak first-tier pensions, severe tenure insecurity, thin unemployment / disability protection, or a bundled balance-sheet-coercion index?[S17][S21][S22]
18. Which compact metrics best capture whether a country's wealth order is **visible enough to govern**: beneficial-ownership verification, registry interoperability, cross-border information exchange, audit coverage, or some composite?[S04][S23]
19. How strong should the archive's **missing-data / opacity penalty** be: when should severe opacity, contested valuation, or strategic missingness be enough by itself to block prevention-mode or near-acceptable status?[S04][S15][S23][S31]
20. What is the best compact trigger for **encumbrance failure**: debt-service stress, leverage at first entry, negative-equity incidence, or a bundled debt-quality index?[S13][S17][S24][S25]
21. What is the best compact trigger for **silent ownership failure**: extreme asset-manager voting concentration, low pass-through coverage, weak mandate specificity, or a bundled stewardship-concentration index?[S20][S26]
22. What is the best compact trigger for **timing failure**: late median inheritance age, weak under-40 positive-wealth rates, delayed secure tenure, or a bundled cohort-entry index?[S05][S13][S19]
23. What is the best compact trigger for **person-level ownership failure**: skewed title, weak individual account access, pension-entitlement gaps, poor divorce/widowhood protection, or a bundled intra-household power index?[S13][S27][S28][S29][S30]
24. What is the best compact trigger for **territorial-closure failure**: exclusion from high-opportunity places, anti-displacement failures, land-value windfalls without access widening, or a bundled place-openness index?[S07][S13][S19][S34][S35]
25. Which parts of the archive's provisional **minimum implementation stack** are truly indispensable in medium-capacity states, and which can be borrowed, proxied, or sequenced later without turning the program into rhetoric?[S04][S15][S23][S31][S32][S33]
26. What is the best compact trigger for **non-extractive formation failure**: worsening entry-adjusted affordability, rising rent / markup absorption of wage gains, incumbent revaluation dominating wealth growth, or a bundled source-quality index?[S07][S10][S13][S16][S20]
27. What is the best compact trigger for **downside-discipline failure**: weak burden sharing, low recovery of rescue costs, repeated broad asset backstops, or a bundled public-loss-transfer index?[S32][S33]

28. What is the best compact trigger for **public-value-capture failure**: weak land-value recapture, unconditional public-land disposal, thin affordable-housing obligations relative to uplift, or a bundled common-uplift index?[S34][S35]

29. How should the archive calibrate its provisional **upside-down subsidy / hidden-wealth-state rule**: top-heavy tax-expenditure incidence, high capitalization into prices, weak entrant benefit, or a bundled subsidy-hygiene index?[S31][S36][S37]

30. Which crisis or transition derogations should the archive treat as creating automatic **repair debt**, and how should it calibrate default sunsets, clawbacks, payout restrictions, recovery levies, and post-crisis re-entry tests?[S15][S23][S31][S32][S33][S36][S37]
31. Which compact indicators best distinguish **credible exit constraints** from **elite bargaining leverage**, and which immobile tax, rent-capture, subsidy-cleanup, and anti-capture measures should the archive treat as first movers even when full cross-border coordination is still incomplete?[S04][S15][S23][S31][S34][S36][S38]

32. What is the best compact trigger for **path-quality failure**: top-heavy marginal wealth incidence, high capitalization leakage, late-life concentration of new claims, thin-control gains, or some bundled direction-of-change index?[S05][S12][S13][S19][S31][S34][S36][S37]
33. What is the best compact trigger for **protected-foothold failure**: low asset thresholds, forced spend-down, abrupt benefit cliffs, asset-rule complexity, or some bundled anti-saving index?[S17][S31][S39]

35. What is the best compact trigger for **present-accessibility failure**: retirement-only lock-up, hardship-gated access, severe early-withdrawal penalties, sale-contingent housing access, narrow permitted uses, or some bundled usable-claims index?[S19][S41]
36. What is the best compact trigger for **anti-seizure failure**: weak exempt-asset floors, easy account sweeping or garnishment, thin housing / tool protection, low restart wealth after debt repair, or some bundled protected-minimum index?[S24][S25][S39]
37. What is the best compact trigger for **ordinary-shock-survivability failure**: post-shock asset poverty, mild negative-equity exposure, forced-sale risk, correlated labor-and-asset shocks, or some bundled calm-weather-illusion index?[S13][S17][S24][S25]
38. What is the best compact trigger for **self-eroding foothold failure**: total housing cost overburden including insurance, charges, repairs, taxes, and utilities, fee drag on small claims, deferred-maintenance dependence, or some bundled net-holdability index?[S07][S42]
39. What is the best compact trigger for **claimant-load / per-person-adequacy failure**: overcrowding by income and tenure, involuntary multi-adult co-residence, household wealth per equivalised adult, delayed independent household formation, or some bundled claimant-dilution index?[S13][S27][S43]

What is the best compact trigger for **gatekeeper-dependence failure**: sponsor-tied claim concentration, interruption risk on provider or household change, continuation without gatekeeper consent, appeal-without-loss protections, or some bundled chokepoint-dependence index?[S28][S29][S30][S40][S41]
