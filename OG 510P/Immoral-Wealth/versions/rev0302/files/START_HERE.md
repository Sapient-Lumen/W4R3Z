## Direct answer in one screen

This archive is trying to answer two questions at once:

1. **What wealth distribution would count as morally acceptable?**
2. **What program can move a society toward it without magical thinking?**

**Target family, not sacred point.**
The archive's near-ideal order is a **bounded-top, thick-middle, real-floor, open-circulation** distribution centered on **20 / 50 / 20 / 10** for the bottom 50 / middle 40 / next 9 / top 1, with rough working bands of **15-25 / 45-55 / 15-25 / 8-15**, a working target of **3% or less** for the top **0.1%**, and a requirement for **clearly positive public/social wealth**.[S02][S03][S16][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23]
Read that four-part tuple as a map of **total private wealth shares**; **public/social wealth** is a separate required co-condition and counterweight, not a fifth slice hidden inside the tuple.[S02][S03][S21][S22]
Use the tuple with the archive's default comparable measurement lens: **household-based private net wealth shares**, then run those through a **person-level title, control, and exit screen** so household totals do not masquerade as broad freedom.[S13][S27][S28][S29][S30]
Move off that center only when the case has really earned it: toward **15 / 55 / 20 / 10** when strong public/social provision materially lowers required private buffers, or toward **25 / 45 / 20 / 10** when lower-half thickening is early, durable, and entrant-friendly rather than leveraged or flattering.[S02][S07][S13][S16][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22]
Do **not** rank cases by raw distance from that center. When two cases sit in or near the acceptable family, prefer the one with the stronger real floor, earlier person-level footholds, weaker wealth-to-rule conversion, and stronger public/common counterweight even if its share table sits a bit farther from `20 / 50 / 20 / 10`; target distance is a **late tie-breaker after the constitutional screens**, not the archive's moral score.[S02][S03][S13][S16][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23]

**A distribution is near-ideal only if six things are true at once.**
Do **not** certify a case from shares alone. A case earns **near-ideal / acceptable** language only when all six of these substantially hold together: the society sits inside the acceptable share family; the lower half has a **real usable floor** that is actually **claimable, keepable, not conditioned on remaining nearly assetless or liquidating modest footholds before help arrives, not abruptly clawed back by sharp withdrawal cliffs on modest gains, ordinary-time usable rather than future-only or sale-contingent, ordinary-shock survivable, not quietly self-eroding under ordinary carrying costs or fee drag, protected enough that modest reserves, essential-use assets, and first long-run claims are not easy to sweep, garnish, set off, intercept, or force-sell away in the first ordinary debt conflict, operable enough that ordinary holders do not need unusual legal, tax, or financial fluency or paid professional rescue just to preserve most of what is already supposed to be theirs, cheaply provable and updateable enough that ordinary holders can retrieve and update the records, certificates, beneficiary entries, co-title evidence, or similar proof they need without repeated brokerage, long-distance travel, specialist help, or prohibitive fees, and challenge-safe enough that ordinary use, complaint, appeal, organizing, separation, or exit does not itself predictably trigger reprisal before neutral protection can hold, and not mainly organized around unusually large private defensive buffers just to survive predictable illness, care, housing, earnings-interruption, or old-age risks because common insurance is too weak or late** in ordinary life; first footholds arrive **early enough** and stay person-level, portable, and **ordinary-exit compatible** rather than sponsor- or gatekeeper-bound, and non-forfeiting across ordinary job, provider, place, or household transitions rather than reset-prone or penalty-laden, not still family-gated through parental deposits, guarantees, inherited housing position, or comparable threshold backing, and territorially real rather than **place-closed** or bought mainly through punishing daily access burdens to the main compounding places; the main **washout channels** are being closed; the top tail is pushed below **wealth-to-rule** and dynastic-closure levels; and public/common wealth is strong enough to act as a real **counterweight** rather than decoration, with broad rule-bound access and governance rather than mainly discretionary patronage.[S02][S03][S13][S15][S16][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S24][S25][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S39][S40][S41][S45][S50][S51][S54]
A nominal or generous-looking statutory floor does **not** count as a real-floor pass when support is hard to discover, slow to claim, churn-heavy, humiliating to renew, or too expert-dependent to keep active in ordinary life.[S17][S21][S22][S24][S25][S39][S40][S45][S50]
A floor also counts less when essential help still requires households to stay nearly assetless, liquidate modest emergency reserves, child accounts, simple productive tools, safer transport, or other first footholds before support begins or continues. Treat that as **spend-down-trapped** rather than as a clean floor pass.[S17][S31][S39][S40]
A floor also counts less when modest earnings, hours, or savings gains trigger abrupt loss, delay, or heavy dilution of essential support instead of smooth tapers, grace-period continuation, or partial disregards. Treat that as **cliff-punished** rather than as a clean floor pass.[S17][S31][S39][S40]
A floor or foothold also counts less when its value exists mainly behind **future-only timing, hardship-gated access, narrow purpose-binding, or sale/refinance dependence on the core foothold itself**. Paper wealth that cannot be used in ordinary time without heavy leakage, exceptional triggers, or destabilising the main asset should be treated as **locked-up** rather than as a clean pass.[S13][S17][S19][S41]
A floor or foothold also counts less when it survives only in **calm weather**: if a short income interruption, a mild rate reset, a modest local price correction, or a predictable repair or care expense can quickly push households back into arrears, forced sale, destructive borrowing, or near-zero again, treat the apparent gain as **shock-fragile** rather than as a clean pass.[S07][S17][S21][S22][S24][S25][S45][S50][S51]

A floor or foothold also counts less when it is **quietly self-eroding even without a discrete shock**: if insurance, taxes, utilities, upkeep, platform or management fees, mandatory charges, or similar recurring carrying costs absorb most of the practical gain, treat the apparent foothold as **self-eroding** rather than as a clean pass.[S07][S17][S21][S22][S24][S25][S42][S45]
A floor or foothold also counts less when households must hold unusually large private defensive buffers merely to survive predictable illness, care, housing, earnings-interruption, or old-age risks because common insurance is too weak, too late, or too thin. Apparent lower-half wealth doing mainly defensive work that a decent common floor should already absorb should be treated as **buffer-coerced** rather than as a clean pass.[S07][S17][S21][S22]
A floor or foothold also counts less when ordinary use still depends on **expert navigation**: if preserving most of the value requires decoding opaque menus, timing tricky elections, avoiding hidden penalties, or paying for professional rescue that people near the floor cannot cheaply access or supervise, treat the apparent gain as **maze-dependent** rather than as a clean pass.[S40][S45]
A floor or foothold also counts less when ordinary holders cannot cheaply **prove, retrieve, or update** it when conflict or ordinary life change requires it. If preserving the claim turns into a records hunt requiring repeated brokerage, long-distance travel, specialist help, or prohibitive fees, treat the apparent gain as **proof-brittle** rather than as a clean pass.[S28][S29][S40][S44]
A floor or foothold also counts less when ordinary disputes, clerical errors, status changes, or provider changes can interrupt practical control long enough to force dependence, retreat, or acceptance of bad terms before correction arrives. A formally available appeal that comes too late to preserve ordinary continuity should be treated as **late-remedy** rather than as a clean pass.[S28][S29][S30][S40][S44]
A floor or foothold also counts less when ordinary use, complaint, appeal, organizing, separation, or exit predictably invites firing, eviction, household retaliation, benefit suspension, account lockout, blacklist, or similar punishment before neutral protection can hold. A claim that works only while the holder stays quiet or compliant should be treated as **reprisal-exposed** rather than as a clean pass.[S08][S09][S11][S14][S28][S29][S30][S40][S41][S44]

A floor or foothold also counts less when modest reserves, essential-use assets, or first long-run claims are easy to sweep, garnish, offset, intercept, or force into destructive sale at the first ordinary debt conflict, or when debt repair still leaves too little protected restart wealth to make the supposed second chance real. Treat the apparent gain as **seizure-exposed** rather than as a clean pass.[S17][S24][S25][S39][S50]
A floor or foothold also counts less when one thin household foothold is doing the work of several missing footholds: if apparent adequacy depends mainly on overcrowding, delayed independent exit, or claimant dilution rather than enough room, privacy, margin, and direct claims per person, treat the case as **claimant-diluted** rather than as a clean pass.[S13][S19][S27][S43]

A foothold also counts less when ordinary early entry still depends on parental deposits, guarantees, inherited housing position, or comparable family backing at the threshold moment. Apparent early circulation that still routes entrants through family balance sheets should be treated as **family-gated** rather than as a clean pass.[S05][S18][S19][S24][S52]

A foothold also counts less when one employer, provider, household intermediary, or sponsor can still materially freeze, stall, or condition its practical use, especially across ordinary exit, switching, separation, or place movement. Near-ideal language should not ride on claims that work only so long as the holder stays obedient to one gatekeeper.[S28][S29][S30][S40][S41]
A foothold also counts less when ordinary movement through work, provider, place, or household change restarts vesting clocks, strands records, wipes prior matches, forces destructive liquidation, or imposes large transition penalties just to preserve what is already supposed to belong to the holder. Treat that as **reset-prone** rather than as a clean portability pass.[S28][S29][S30][S41]
A foothold also counts less when it is cheap only because it sits too far from the main labor, education, care, or public-service nodes, or because daily access depends on punishing travel times, high transport costs, or brittle commuting chains. Cheap footholds in weak-opportunity places do **not** fully substitute for secure access to the main compounding places; treat that as **place-closed** rather than as a clean circulation pass.[S07][S19][S48]
Public/common counterweight also counts less when ordinary access, allocation, or continuity depends mainly on party, patron, employer, landlord, or local-broker discretion rather than on broad rule-bound entitlement, universal service, or democratically governed common provision. A public substitute oligarchy is not a clean counterweight pass.[S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33][S39][S40]
A real-floor pass should also be read as a **supermajority-plus-cohort-guardrails** claim, not as a bare-majority or average-balance claim: ordinary security should be broad across the bottom half, the next adult cohort should not sit far weaker than the all-age picture, and major excluded groups should not remain durably well below the national floor pass rate.[S16][S17][S18][S19][S25][S52]
A floor, foothold, or public/common counterweight also counts less when apparently broad claims remain mostly **nominal while voting, stewardship, or practical control stays bottlenecked inside a narrow manager, trustee, or similar intermediary layer** that ordinary holders cannot meaningfully inspect, direct, or replace. Treat that as **manager-bottlenecked** rather than as a clean circulation or counterweight pass.[S20][S23][S26]
If even one of those conditions remains badly broken, the case may still be improving, but it has **not yet earned** the archive's near-ideal label.[S13][S15][S16][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33]
And even when the six-part bundle looks good in one reading, near-ideal language should wait for a **window-plus-ratchet pass**: the order should survive an ordinary political-budget cycle and an ordinary asset/credit cycle, and at least some important reversal channels should already be materially harder to use than before, rather than the case merely enjoying one friendly snapshot.[S20][S21][S22][S31][S32][S33]
**Aggregate pass cannot wash out patterned exclusion.** Even when the national tuple, household totals, or headline floor look acceptable, the case should still fail near-ideal certification when historically subordinated groups remain predictably outside the real-floor, early-foothold, person-level-control, or ordinary-exit conditions on materially worse terms. Better averages do **not** buy permission to average away group closure.[S13][S15][S18][S19][S27][S28][S29][S30][S39][S40]
**Certification follows the weakest decisive surface.** Do **not** average one still-broken constitutional surface together with stronger lanes and call the mix near-ideal. A good tuple, stronger public/common wealth, or visible lower-half gains do **not** compensate for a still-thin real floor, still-late or household-masked footholds, live wealth-to-rule, or unresolved excluded-group closure; the verdict should track the **worst still-live decisive breach** until that breach is actually repaired.[S13][S15][S16][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S39][S40][S50][S54]
**Below near-ideal, name the blocker with the verdict.** Do **not** stop at **correction-required** or **provisional near-pass** in the abstract. Pair the status with the live blocking surface — for example **paper-floor**, **floor-thin**, **spend-down-trapped**, **cliff-punished**, **locked-up**, **shock-fragile**, **self-eroding**, **maze-dependent**, **proof-brittle**, **late-remedy**, **reprisal-exposed**, **seizure-exposed**, **claimant-diluted**, **gatekeeper-dependent**, **reset-prone**, **place-closed**, **manager-bottlenecked**, **patronage-routed**, **late-entry**, **excluded-group closure**, **top-tail-rule**, **opacity-blocked**, **durability-not-yet-earned**, or **ratchet-too-soft** — so the verdict still tells operators what must move next instead of offering flattering half-certification.[S13][S15][S18][S19][S20][S23][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S39][S40][S41][S44]

**How to get there.**
The program answer is a **seven-lane stack**:

1. set the target as a **narrow acceptable family** rather than a frozen point,
2. lower forced private exposure and make the floor **claimable**,
3. rebuild earnings power and **saveable room** from below,
4. move footholds **earlier** and keep them person-level and portable,
5. stop **housing, land, debt, and monopoly washout**,
6. compress the top tail and weaken **dynastic closure**,
7. build **public/common counterweight** and anti-capture durability.[S04][S07][S08][S09][S10][S12][S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33][S34][S36][S39][S40][S50]

**How to choose the first serious package.**
Start from the **dominant live breach** and the **fastest washout channel**, not from the most rhetorical instrument.[S07][S15][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33]
Then route the opening mix by breach type: **floor-failure** cases usually open with lanes **2, 3, and 5** while opening **4** early; **late-entry** cases usually open with **4 and 5**; **rent-washout** cases usually push **5** with support from **2, 3, and 7**; **top-tail-rule** cases usually push **6 and 7** much harder and earlier; **thin-public-counterweight** cases usually strengthen **7** together with **5** and whichever of **2-4** most directly reduces forced dependence.[S15][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S31][S32][S33][S34][S35][S36][S39][S40][S45][S50][S52][S53][S54]

**How to choose prevention versus response posture.**
Keep the **constitutional target fixed** across modes. Use **prevention** only when there is **no major constitutional breach**, closure and capture are still weak enough that broadening can outrun hardening, and ordinary democratic correction is materially available on real rails; use **correction** when one major breach or several reinforcing moderate breaches are already live but ordinary democratic correction is still materially possible; use **anti-oligarchy response** when multiple major breaches, or one major breach plus strong **wealth-to-rule**, opacity, or veto power, mean anti-capture and deconcentration can no longer wait.[S05][S06][S13][S15][S18][S19][S20][S23][S31][S32][S33]
A harder posture must **not** crowd out floor repair, entrant protection, or ordinary-life continuity, and a softer posture must **not** postpone anti-capture, legibility, or anti-dynasty work once those have become structurally necessary.[S07][S15][S18][S19][S23][S24][S25][S31][S32][S33]

**Main false comforts.**
Do **not** count a case as near-ideal when lower-half gains are mainly leveraged housing, debt-offset paper, or locked claims; household totals hide weak person-level title or exit; ordinary movement still resets or forfeits footholds that were supposed to travel with the person; historically subordinated groups remain predictably later or thinner in the main asset channels; the top tail still converts wealth into rule; or opacity is still blocking controller, transfer, valuation, or influence visibility.[S07][S13][S16][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S26][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S36][S39][S40]
Under that kind of proof debt, certification should usually stop at **provisional near-pass** or below, even while lower-regret floor repair, registry-building, and washout closure move now.[S04][S07][S15][S21][S22][S23][S31][S39][S40]
