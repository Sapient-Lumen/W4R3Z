# Frontier salience snapshot — 2026-03-20 (103)

This pass did **not** add another warning family, another export gate, or another summary layer.
It deepened **P-0514 Crate Upgrade Pack Kit** by making one more receiver-facing truth explicit:

- **replay-bridge truth** — upgrade packs can now say whether imported evidence is reached by direct rerun, native-storage rerender, copied attachment inspection, manual reconstruction, or not replayable at all.

## Main judgment

After lineage, capture context, lane selection, coverage matrices, session honesty, export posture, public traceability, durable cues, freshness, and review provenance became explicit, one ordinary lie still remained:

1. a pack could still advertise replay posture without one reviewable replay route,
2. and a copied snapshot could still inherit the aura of native rerenderability merely because the underlying evidence was once native.

Those are not small provenance details.
They are ordinary ways a support artifact can overstate how reconstructible its witness family really is.

## Why this beat nearby work again

The archive already had enough to say:

- what was imported,
- which capture or session produced it,
- and how a public reader could trace exported claims.

What it still lacked was one compact way to say:

- "rerun this exact command,"
- "rerender this from native storage by id,"
- "open this copied attachment and compare manually,"
- or "do not call this replayable."

That is a real product refinement, not another naming flourish.
