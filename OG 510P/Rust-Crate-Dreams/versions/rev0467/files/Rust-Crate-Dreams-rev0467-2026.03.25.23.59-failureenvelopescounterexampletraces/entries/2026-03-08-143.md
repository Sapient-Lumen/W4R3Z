> **P-0469 Cargo Rebuild Explanation Kit**

## Main judgment

Cargo’s new `-Zbuild-analysis` and `cargo report` substrate changes the right move.

The missing value is now **not** another generic performance crate.
It is the downstream layer that can:

- import or capture one run,
- classify rebuilt vs reused units conservatively,
- preserve whether evidence came from Cargo reports, live capture, or optional fingerprint logs,
- and emit one small artifact another person can review.

## New planning stance

Read **P-0469** as a product with **three evidence lanes**:

1. imported Cargo report sessions,
2. live stable-first capture,
3. optional fingerprint overlays.

That is the right theory/practice split:
- let Cargo own the recorder,
- let this crate own the support artifact.

## Archive-level lesson

Once upstream Cargo exposes persisted sessions and report commands, the best downstream crate may no longer be a new recorder.
It may be the crate that freezes that moving upstream surface into one boring receipt and diff contract for CI, support, and review.

Future passes should resist turning this back into:
- a profiler,
- a dashboard,
- a historical warehouse,
- or a giant “Cargo doctor.”
