# 2026-03-05 (66) — SNMPv3 + RADIUS/EAP + CAN/UDS evidence kits

Added three new **bundle-first interop/conformance** proposals aimed at “real-world ops pain” surfaces where failures are common but hard to reproduce:

- **P-0286** SNMPv3 Interop & Evidence Kit (`*.snmpbundle.zip`)
  - Canonical/redactable SNMPv3 USM transcripts, capability matrices, replay and semantic diff.
  - Anchored to SNMPv3 USM (RFC 3414). citeturn0search0

- **P-0287** RADIUS + EAP Interop & Evidence Kit (`*.radiusbundle.zip`)
  - Registry-pinned attribute decoding, canonical exchange bundles, replay/diff for auth/challenge flows.
  - Anchored to RADIUS (RFC 2865) and IANA RADIUS Types registry. citeturn0search1turn0search17

- **P-0288** CAN + ISO-TP + UDS Interop & Evidence Kit (`*.canudsbundle.zip`)
  - Canonical CAN trace IR + ISO-TP/UDS transcripts with timing/state checks and redaction presets for lab/CI reproducibility.
  - Anchored to CAN (ISO 11898-1) and UDS (ISO 14229-1). citeturn0search7turn0search2
