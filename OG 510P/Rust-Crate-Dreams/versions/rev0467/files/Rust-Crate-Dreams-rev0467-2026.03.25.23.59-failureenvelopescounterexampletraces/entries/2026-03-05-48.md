# 2026-03-05 (48): OCPP interop/cert evidence kit + OpenXR CTS triage bundles + Nostr compliance harness

This pass focused on a recurring “missing middle” in Rust: **protocol ecosystems with lots of partial crates**, but no *default* way to produce **portable, redactable, reproducible evidence** for interop and conformance debugging.

## Added proposals

- `proposals/ocpp-interop-certification-evidence-kit.md` — **P-0230** — OCPP Interop & Certification Evidence Kit (`*.ocppbundle.zip`).
- `proposals/openxr-cts-triage-evidence-kit.md` — **P-0231** — OpenXR CTS Triage & Evidence Kit (`*.xrbundle.zip`).
- `proposals/nostr-interop-compliance-kit.md` — **P-0232** — Nostr Relay/Client Interop & Compliance Kit (`*.nostrbundle.zip`).

## Why these are “worthy”

- **OCPP**: certification-style testing exists, but Rust lacks a reusable capture/replay/diff layer for sharing field failures safely.
- **OpenXR CTS**: the CTS exists, but teams need bisectable diffs and environment-provenance bundles for day-to-day triage.
- **Nostr**: fast-moving spec surface (NIPs) creates a constant interop tax; a pinned-NIP harness plus evidence bundles makes upgrades safer.
