# Incident privacy and due process — rev0025

Incident records are the most dangerous layer because they join people, harm, law, time, place, identity, and narrative.

Rev0025 therefore makes incident privacy/due-process gates first-class. No event type, role, process link, or bridge rule creates a live incident record. The cube must first decide what source voice is speaking, whether identities are resolved or must be abstained, what civilian dignity rules apply, whether the officer was alleged/charged/found/cleared/exonerated, and what public display would actually serve accountability rather than extraction.
