# Wording rules: allegation, finding, settlement, adjudication

The cube’s language must preserve the epistemic status of each source.

Permanent rules:

- Alleged means alleged.
- Filed means filed.
- Charged means charged.
- Ordered means ordered.
- Sustained means sustained by the named body under the named standard.
- Unsustained means insufficient or non-sustained under that system; it is not exoneration.
- Exonerated means the source’s exoneration category, not universal truth.
- Settled means settled; no admission unless the text says so.
- Dismissed means dismissed; it may or may not say anything about merits.
- Missing means missing, unavailable, withheld, sealed, or not yet located; it is not absence.

Every public phrase should ask: would an officer, civilian, family, defender, journalist, or researcher know exactly who is speaking and what is being asserted?
