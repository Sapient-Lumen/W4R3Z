# Identity harm and public display — rev0024

Public identity display is not the same as public-record existence. Rev0024 keeps public identity display disabled.

The strongest default is minimization:

- Officers may eventually be displayed when identity proof, source voice, due-process posture, correction route, and public-interest thresholds are met.
- Civilians, witnesses, minors, survivors, family members, people in medical or mental-health crisis, undocumented people, sex workers, informants, and domestic-violence/sexual-assault contexts are protected by default.
- Agency identities may be less sensitive but still require parent/subunit, rename, jurisdiction, and denominator checks.
- Brady/Giglio and decertification identity surfaces are high-impact and cannot be casual lookup rows.
- Wandering detection is a future earned capability, not a name-matching feature.
