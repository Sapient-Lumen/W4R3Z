# Nonclaims and prohibited collapses

Machine-readable source: `NONCLAIMS.json`.

The corpus should be unusually explicit about what it is not saying.

## Core prohibited collapses

- Allegation is not finding.
- Finding is not criminal guilt.
- Settlement is not admission unless the settlement says so.
- Dismissal is not factual exoneration unless the decision says so.
- Exoneration by an agency is not identical to independent truth.
- Public record is not automatically ethical display.
- Same name is not same person.
- Official statement is not complete record.
- News coverage is not adjudication.
- Absence of record is not absence of event.
