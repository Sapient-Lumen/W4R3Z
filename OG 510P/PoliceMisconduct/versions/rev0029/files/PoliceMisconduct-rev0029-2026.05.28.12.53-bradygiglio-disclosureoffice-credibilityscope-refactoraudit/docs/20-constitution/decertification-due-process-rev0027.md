# Decertification, appeals, and due process — rev0027

Certification and decertification surfaces can be powerful accountability records, but they are also legally and temporally fragile. A decertification signal may be pending, stayed, appealed, reconsidered, vacated, limited to one jurisdiction, superseded, or followed by reinstatement.

Rules added in rev0027:

- active, inactive, suspended, revoked, surrendered, expired, denied, restricted, and probationary credential states are not interchangeable;
- voluntary surrender is not automatically an admission;
- expiration is not decertification;
- a pending appeal blocks final-status language;
- reinstatement does not erase history;
- absence from a registry is not a clearance, especially where participation/publication is incomplete;
- public display remains denied until finality, identity, privacy, right-of-reply, and wording gates close.
