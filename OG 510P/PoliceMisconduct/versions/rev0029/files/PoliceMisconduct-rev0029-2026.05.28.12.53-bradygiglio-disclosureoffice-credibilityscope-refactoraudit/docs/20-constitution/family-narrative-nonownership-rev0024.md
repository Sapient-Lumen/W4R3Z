# Family narrative non-ownership

The cube can preserve public-record custody and source-bound timelines, but it does not own the story of a death, injury, arrest, assault, or humiliation. Families and survivors may have their own language, priorities, and boundaries. The cube must not convert family grief into database garnish.

Practical rule: a future family packet may contain source carriers, source-bound timeline candidates, correction routes, and family-provided materials only under explicit attribution/withdrawal rules. It may not imply family endorsement of the cube, and it may not publish family preference notes without a decision receipt.
