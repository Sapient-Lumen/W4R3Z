# Plaintiff privacy and case voice — rev0026

Litigation records are public in many courts, but public record does not mean public display. Plaintiffs, family members, minors, witnesses, survivors, people in mental-health crisis, and people whose arrests were later contested may be named in filings for reasons that do not make broad republication necessary.

The cube should be able to account for lawsuits and public costs without gratuitously reproducing civilian identity. For most public accountability use cases, case-level source custody, defendant-agency identity, claim category, procedural posture, and aggregate finance fields matter more than plaintiff names.

Case voice must remain explicit: complaint, answer, motion, order, verdict, settlement, finance ledger, news report, and FOIA packet all speak differently.
