# No police-intelligence product — rev0023

The cube is for accountability, not for building a new intelligence layer against civilians, witnesses, families, organizers, immigrants, sex workers, or people in crisis.

Law-enforcement agencies can use publicly available materials like anyone else. Special access is not granted. Future bounded exceptions, such as POST certification or hiring-safety checks, require auditable purpose, identity proof, appeal-status handling, due-process labels, anti-targeting attestation, correction paths, and revocation.
