# Rev0029 — Brady/Giglio disclosure office

This office prepares the cube for future credibility-disclosure work without creating live person records or public adverse labels.

Governing rule: **a disclosure signal is not a credibility conclusion; a list signal is not a case-specific Brady violation.**

The office distinguishes source carriers, disclosure artifacts, disclosure signals, credibility issue categories, case/testimony links, prosecutor-office boundaries, protective orders, access boundaries, correction routes, and public-display gates.

Rev0029 intentionally creates no live officer records, no Brady-list records, no case-specific nondisclosure claims, no testimony links, no workbench accounts, no public displays, and no exports.
