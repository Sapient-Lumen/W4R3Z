# Nonclaim-to-claim crosswalk — rev0017

`data/lifecycle/rev0017_lifecycle_file_crosswalk.json` and `data/lifecycle/rev0017_record_kind_state_crosswalk.json` are routing tools. They are intentionally non-destructive and heuristic. They do not move files, revise source semantics, or authorize promotion.

Use the file crosswalk to find the lifecycle neighborhood of a ledger. Use the record-kind crosswalk to detect naming patterns that might imply a state. Then use the promotion templates and anti-collapse ledger before changing any record's epistemic status.

The most important habit: never let a noun do the work of a receipt. Words like `finding`, `settlement`, `closed`, `motion`, `order`, `certification`, `discipline`, `Brady`, or `sustained` only become claims after source, context, privacy, due-process, and rollback gates.
