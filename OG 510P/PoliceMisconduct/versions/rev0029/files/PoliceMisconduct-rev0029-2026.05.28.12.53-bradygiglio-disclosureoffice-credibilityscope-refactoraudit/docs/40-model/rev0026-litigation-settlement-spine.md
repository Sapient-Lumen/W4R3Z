# rev0026 litigation / settlement / finance spine

Governing rule: **A lawsuit carrier is not a merits finding; a settlement amount is not an admission; a case-party role is not identity.**

This office exists because the incident spine now needs a legal-process layer before the cube can ever earn live lawsuits or settlement ledgers. The seed asks for a lawsuit corpus and settlement ledger, but those are high-risk because complaints are pleadings, settlement agreements often contain no-admission language, parties have different privacy statuses, and repeated-defendant or cost metrics can become defamatory if identity and allocation are weak.

Rev0026 creates definitions only. It adds case artifact types, party roles, case lifecycle states, legal claim types, disposition/relief categories, settlement finance fields, no-admission guardrails, plaintiff privacy gates, qualified-immunity/procedural signal gates, incident bridge gates, public display gates, correction routes, source-family routes, and synthetic fixtures.

It creates no live case, lawsuit, settlement, party, amount, public display, or claim records.
