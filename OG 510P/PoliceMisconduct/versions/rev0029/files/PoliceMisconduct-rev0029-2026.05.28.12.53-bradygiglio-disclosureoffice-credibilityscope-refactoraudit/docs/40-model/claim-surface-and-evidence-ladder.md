# Claim surface and evidence ladder

`CLAIM-SURFACE.json` defines what the project is allowed to say in rev0001. `EVIDENCE-LADDER.json` defines future field-level claim states.

Rev0001 has only method claims. It has no person-level or incident-level claims.

Future revisions should keep the public claim surface small. The archive may contain leads, open questions, rejected candidates, and source notes that should not be
presented as public claims.
