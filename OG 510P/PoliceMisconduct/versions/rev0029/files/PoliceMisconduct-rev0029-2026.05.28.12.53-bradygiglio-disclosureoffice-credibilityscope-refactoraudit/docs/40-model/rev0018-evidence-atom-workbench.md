# Rev0018 evidence atom workbench

Rev0017 gave the cube a lifecycle. Rev0018 gives the lifecycle a working middle layer: **evidence atoms**.

An evidence atom is the smallest extraction unit the cube should allow future sessions to create from a preserved, privacy-cleared, source-bound record. It is not a misconduct claim. It is not a person record. It is not a public display. It is a bounded statement whose source voice, modality, locator, privacy class, and defeater exposure can be reviewed.

## Why this exists

The seed imagines a corpus that can eventually normalize departments, officers, incidents, lawsuits, settlements, discipline, decertification, officer mobility, Brady/Giglio, union contracts, and wandering-officer patterns. That is impossible unless extraction is smaller than accusation. A lawsuit filing, a settlement, a findings report, a court order, a press release, a policy, or a news article each supports different atom types and different claim strengths.

## Core rule

**An evidence atom is not a claim.**

The workbench defines 46 atom types across source foundation, court process, agency identity, status/process, future person identity, future incident, future lawsuit/settlement, Brady/Giglio, policy/contract, and correction/rollback families.

Every future atom must answer:

1. What exactly did the source say, show, file, order, report, allege, deny, or find?
2. Who is speaking: court, plaintiff, department, officer, prosecutor, monitor, POST body, news outlet, family, or correction requester?
3. What modality applies: observed, alleged, ordered, sustained, unsustained, settled, dismissed, unknown, superseded?
4. What is the tightest possible scope?
5. What privacy and due-process gates apply?
6. What would defeat or roll back this atom?

Rev0018 creates no live atoms. It only prepares the bench.
