# Wandering detection gates — rev0027

The cube's eventual highest-impact capability may be detecting officers who move between agencies after serious misconduct, discipline, termination, decertification, or credibility problems. Rev0027 explicitly keeps that future alive while refusing premature alerts.

A future alert requires, at minimum:

- high-confidence identity receipts;
- prior and receiving agency receipts;
- employment date receipts;
- separation reason receipts;
- adverse record modality and adjudication state;
- appeal, reinstatement, and correction review;
- no settlement/admission collapse;
- no motion/order collapse;
- no name-only merge;
- right-of-reply route;
- human review;
- rollback dependency.

In rev0027 every wandering gate is closed. The detector is modeled as something the cube must earn, not something it may imply.
