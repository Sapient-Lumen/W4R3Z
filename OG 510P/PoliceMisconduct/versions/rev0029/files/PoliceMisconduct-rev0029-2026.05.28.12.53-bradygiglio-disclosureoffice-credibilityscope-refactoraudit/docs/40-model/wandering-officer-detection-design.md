# Wandering officer detection design

The seed's highest-leverage long-run use case is making officer mobility after serious misconduct visible. This design remains blocked until identity and source gates mature.

## Required ingredients

- employment history records with department/date/rank/separation fields;
- misconduct/disposition/discipline/decertification records with exact outcome states;
- identity linkage confidence and unmerge route;
- source custody for each employment and outcome field;
- privacy and due-process review for display;
- alerting access model that cannot be abused.

## Nonclaim

A mobility pattern alone is not proof of misconduct. A prior allegation alone is not a bar to employment. The corpus can expose records; it must not silently become a punitive score.
