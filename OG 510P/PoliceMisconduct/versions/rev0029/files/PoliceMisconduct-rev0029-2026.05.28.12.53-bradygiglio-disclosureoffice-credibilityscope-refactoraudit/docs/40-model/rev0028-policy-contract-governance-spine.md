# rev0028 — Policy / contract governance spine

Governing rule: **A policy clause is not a policy-in-effect claim; a contract clause is not proof that a case was affected; comparison is not scoring.**

This revision opens the policy/manual/union-contract office as definitions only. It creates taxonomies for policy artifacts, provisions, union contract accountability clauses, version lifecycle states, effective-date gates, policy-to-incident bridges, policy-to-employment/discipline bridges, policy-to-litigation bridges, public display gates, correction routes, and synthetic fixtures.

The office is intentionally low-harm relative to live people: it can eventually support department-level policy archives, use-of-force policy history, union contract clause inventories, record-destruction context, and comparison research without admitting officer, civilian, incident, lawsuit, settlement, or discipline records.

Still blocked in rev0028: live policy source rows, payload capture, policy-in-effect claims, department grades, officer/person links, incident policy-violation claims, public comparisons, exports, and public claims.
