# Rev0025 incident/event spine office

Governing rule: **an incident candidate is not an incident record; an event role is not identity; event linkage is not adjudication.**

Rev0025 opens the incident/event spine as definitions only. It does not admit incidents. It prepares the cube to eventually connect source records, civilians, officers, lawsuits, settlements, discipline, decertification, Brady/Giglio, family packets, and public displays without flattening source voice or harming people.

The office is deliberately downstream of the identity office and the civilian/family dignity office. Any event involving people must pass identity-abstention, minimization, family-dignity, source-voice, and due-process gates before any live record can exist.

Rev0025 creates zero live incident records and zero public claims.
