# Atom-to-claim promotion receipts — rev0018

Rev0018 does not promote anything. It defines what promotion will require.

A future evidence atom may only contribute to a claim candidate when the atom has a locator/custody pointer, source voice, bounded scope, modality, and defeater scan. High-risk person, incident, lawsuit, settlement, Brady/Giglio, and civilian/family contexts also require privacy and due-process gates.

The transition remains:

`LC15_EVIDENCE_ATOM → LC16_CLAIM_CANDIDATE → LC17_CLAIM_REVIEWED → LC18_PRIVATE_CLAIM_ADMITTED → LC19_DISPLAY_CANDIDATE → LC20_PUBLIC_DISPLAY_APPROVED → LC21_PUBLIC_DISPLAY_PUBLISHED`

No future session should jump that chain because a source seems obvious.
