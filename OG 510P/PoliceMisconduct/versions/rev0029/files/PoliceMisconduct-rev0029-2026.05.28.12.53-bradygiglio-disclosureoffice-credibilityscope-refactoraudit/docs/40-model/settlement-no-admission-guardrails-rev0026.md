# Settlement no-admission guardrails — rev0026

A settlement fact can be valuable and still epistemically narrow. The cube may eventually say that a public entity paid a public amount on a public date from a public carrier. It may not say that the payment proves the alleged misconduct occurred unless an independent adjudication supports that wording.

Required future display principles:

- preserve no-admission language when it exists;
- distinguish settlement approval, signed agreement, dismissal, and payment execution;
- distinguish damages, fees, costs, indemnification, insurance, and aggregate fiscal-year totals;
- do not allocate a multi-defendant payment to a named officer without allocation evidence;
- treat unknown amount as missingness, not zero;
- treat confidential terms as a display block, not a license to infer.
