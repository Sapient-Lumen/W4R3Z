# Policy effective date and versioning — rev0028

The cube must not say a policy was in effect merely because a policy page currently displays it. A future policy-in-effect claim requires source owner, payload fixity, effective date, supersession chain, jurisdiction scope, agency/bargaining-unit scope, amendment/MOU review, local-law conflict review, and rollback path.

The first safe public surface is metadata-only: source owner, observed policy label, version uncertainty, and missingness banner.
