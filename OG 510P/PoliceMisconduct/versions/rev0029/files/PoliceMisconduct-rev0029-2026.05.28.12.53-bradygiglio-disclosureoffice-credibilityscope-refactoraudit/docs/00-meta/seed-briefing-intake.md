# Seed briefing intake

The seed brief is copied into `seed/02_police_misconduct.md` and represented by `SEED-INTAKE-RECEIPT.json`.

## Extracted project pressures

- The corpus must span many record families: court filings, settlements, internal affairs, discipline, POST/decertification, use-of-force records, civilian complaints, consent decrees, monitor reports, news, video/audio, medical examiner reports, union contracts, and policies.
- The normalization problem has at least four hard axes: officer identity, incident identity, department identity, and temporal/source versioning.
- The corpus must distinguish allegations, administrative findings, civil dispositions, criminal outcomes, decertification, settlements, and exonerations.
- The corpus must protect civilians and families while also preserving officer due process.
- The long-run goal is infrastructure, not a proprietary product or rhetorical dossier.

## Rev0001 choice

The seed is treated as a design prompt. Rev0001 does not independently verify external facts in the seed and does not populate real records.
