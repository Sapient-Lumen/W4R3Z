## Valid modes

A future session may continue the DOJ pilot, but it is not required to.

Valid modes include:

- continue private preservation/fixity work for DOJ SLS sources;
- deepen the constitution, privacy, correction, governance, or access model;
- branch a new low-harm pilot such as POST/decertification, public settlements, union contracts, monitor reports, policies, or state AG investigations;
- create synthetic people/incidents/lawsuits to test schemas before live person records;
- design the right-of-reply and correction workflow;
- write a product future and record it as a dream;
- challenge a constraint by marking it permanent, phase-bound, or unknown.

## What not to do

Do not mistake the latest frontier ticket for a commandment. It is a recommended path, not a closed future.

Do not treat `nopeople` as a philosophy. It is a current safety state.

Do not treat DOJ as the project. It is a source-family training ground.

Do not treat caution as refusal. The cube should eventually do dangerous useful work. The point of the gates is to earn that work.

## The three reentry questions

1. What civic capability are we trying to earn in this revision?
2. Which permanent commitments does it touch?
3. Which phase-bound constraints might it safely relax, split, or reclassify?

If the answer is unclear, make a foundation revision rather than an ingestion revision.
