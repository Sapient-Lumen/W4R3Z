# Sample agency identity card — New Orleans Police Department candidate

Status: identity candidate only.

Source basis: DOJ SLS law-enforcement source row and matter object.

Allowed display: source inventory stub with identity-under-review badge.

Blocked display: current status banner, officer roster, incident list, settlement totals, per-capita rates, map boundary, employment timeline, and wandering-officer indicator.

Promotion requirements: official agency source, denominator/identifier source, jurisdiction decision, status-proof bundle if status is displayed, privacy review, and unmerge path.
