## Revisions 11–25: pilot selection without live public person display

Candidate pilots:

1. Settlement-ledger pilot: city/county settlement records only, with officer names minimized until identity gates mature.
2. POST/decertification pilot: state-published decertification orders, with appeal/reinstatement status emphasis.
3. Consent-decree monitor pilot: department-level records with little person-level naming.
4. Policy/union contract pilot: department-level accountability infrastructure before misconduct entries.
5. Federal docket pilot: bounded §1983 docket extraction with no public officer aggregation until identity review.

## Revisions 26–50: first acquisition and extraction protocols

Build field extractors by hand first. Avoid automation until source and privacy failures are well understood.

## Revisions 51–75: entity resolution and correction machinery

Introduce tentative identity matching on synthetic and carefully reviewed public official records. Build unmerge receipts before aggregation.

## Revisions 76–100: first live data gate review

Only after source custody, privacy, right-of-reply, and rollback gates exist should the archive consider a small live record bundle.
