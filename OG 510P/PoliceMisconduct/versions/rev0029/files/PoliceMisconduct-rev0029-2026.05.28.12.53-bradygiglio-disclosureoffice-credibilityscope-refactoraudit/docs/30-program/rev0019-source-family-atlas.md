# Rev0019 source-family atlas

Rev0019 deliberately widens the cube after the evidence-atom workbench. The DOJ SLS pilot taught discipline, but it cannot be allowed to become the whole shape of the project.

Core rule:

> A source family is not a source row, a source row is not an evidence atom, and an evidence atom is not a claim.

This revision names 24 source families and gives each one a first safe record kind, hazards, gates, and corpus lanes. It admits no live source rows. It does not ingest lawsuits, settlements, decertification rows, complaint records, news stories, people, incidents, or payloads.

The most promising low-harm branch candidates are policy-version archives and union-contract source inventories. They are useful because they can deepen department-level accountability without beginning with adverse person records. The highest-value high-care branches remain lawsuits, settlements, POST/decertification, Brady/Giglio, death-in-custody, and wandering detection.
