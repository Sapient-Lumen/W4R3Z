# What we are trying to earn

The cube is trying to earn capabilities that are democratically valuable but unsafe if rushed.

## 1. The right to preserve

Before extracting, the cube must prove it can preserve source versions, links, redirects, fixity, source-family context, and mutation history.

## 2. The right to summarize

Before summarizing, the cube must know whether a source contains names, minors, witnesses, family details, sealed/sealable material, medical details, survivor identities, or misleading official phrasing.

## 3. The right to claim

Before claiming, the cube must distinguish source assertion, party assertion, administrative finding, judicial finding, settlement, dismissal, reversal, exoneration, acquittal, and correction.

## 4. The right to link

Before linking people, agencies, lawsuits, incidents, and policies, the cube must carry uncertainty, identifier strength, unmerge receipts, and display thresholds.

## 5. The right to display

Before public display, the cube must decide audience, harm, freshness, rollback, correction, role gate, and missingness language.

## 6. The right to alert

Before alerting on wandering officers or repeat patterns, the cube must pass the highest threshold: strong identity evidence, serious source-supported event state, employment timeline support, right-of-reply, and human review.

## 7. The right to become infrastructure

Before becoming a shared civic spine, the cube must support contribution, contestation, governance, source custody, long-term funding, and noncommercial alignment.

The word **right** here is not legal permission. It is earned moral and epistemic permission.
