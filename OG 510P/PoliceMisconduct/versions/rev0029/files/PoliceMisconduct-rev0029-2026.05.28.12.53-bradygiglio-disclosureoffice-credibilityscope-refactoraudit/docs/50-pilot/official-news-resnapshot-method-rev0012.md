# Official news resnapshot method — rev0012

Official DOJ news pages are valuable but volatile status carriers. They can describe dismissal, retraction, completion, termination, or motion activity in language that is meaningful but still not equivalent to a docket-level current-status claim.

Rev0012 resnapshots seven news carriers: the SLS news index plus Louisville/Minneapolis, Seattle, New Orleans, Newark, Orange County DA, and Cleveland release pages.

The method keeps three rules:

1. A press release is an official source signal, not an automatic current-status claim.
2. A motion to terminate is not a termination order.
3. Closure, termination, completion, or retraction language does not erase historical findings, agreements, complaints, or monitor/report carriers.
