# Rev0007 order proof atom report

Rev0007 moves the DOJ SLS pilot one gate deeper: from source-page labels and accession candidates into official DOJ-linked PDF URL accessions and bounded document-effect proof atoms.

The core distinction is:

> A document can say what happened in that document without the cube claiming current status.

This revision admits 13 official PDF URL accessions and 11 proof atoms. The atoms cover court orders, motions, responses, and DOJ closing letters. They remain noncurrent. They do not create officer records, civilian records, incident records, lawsuit-merits records, settlement records, or public current-status banners.

## Why this was necessary

Rev0006 opened a court/order accession queue. Without a document-effect atom layer, the cube had only two bad options: stay at source-label level forever, or promote terminal-looking documents directly into current-status claims. Rev0007 creates a middle layer.

## Main rule

A court order can support a document-stated procedural event. It still needs freshness, rollback, history-retention, and display review before a public current-status banner.

## Examples admitted as proof atoms

- New Orleans: order effect atom says sustainment plan approved and immediate termination denied.
- Minneapolis: order effect atom says federal case dismissed with prejudice.
- Albuquerque: order effect atom says termination granted and matter dismissed with prejudice.
- Newark: order effect atom says partial termination only, with remaining subject areas still in effect.
- Louisville and Seattle: motion/request atoms explicitly block status promotion because a motion is not an order.
- Ville Platte and Evangeline Parish: DOJ closing-letter atoms are official signals but not court orders.

## What remains blocked

No public current-status claims were admitted. The new public line candidates are not public copy. They are tests for wording that preserves carrier family: source page, court order, motion, response, closing letter, and freshness timestamp.
