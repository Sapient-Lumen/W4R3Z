# Subunit, parent, and adjacent-entity split examples — rev0010

Examples opened by the DOJ SLS pilot:

- Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department Antelope Valley stations: subunit/station-group candidate, not automatically the entire sheriff office.
- Springfield Police Department Narcotics Bureau: bureau/subunit candidate, not automatically the full department.
- New York Police Department Special Victims Division: division candidate with sensitive subject matter; parent merge and privacy review remain blocked.
- Orange County District Attorney's Office and Orange County Sheriff's Department: prosecutor-adjacent and sheriff-office candidates must remain split.
- Louisville Metro Police Department and Louisville/Jefferson County Metro Government: department and consolidated government party must remain split.
- City of Lexington and Lexington Police Department: city government party and police department candidate must remain split.
- Puerto Rico and Virgin Islands: territorial agency candidates require territory-aware schema fields.
