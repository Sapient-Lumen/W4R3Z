# Local and monitor source bridge method

DOJ sources are necessary but not sufficient. Local government pages, court notices, monitor pages, and civil-society sources can show termination events, missing local continuation obligations, stale local pages, or public-facing context.

Rev0006 admits local/monitor/court-adjacent rows only as source carriers. They do not assert current legal status.

## Source-family weights

- Court order/docket: highest for case status.
- Court public notice: locator and hearing signal, not an order.
- DOJ cases page: official source-page label, may lag.
- DOJ press release: official dated event signal, not a docket.
- Local government page: useful for public implementation and local continuation surfaces, may lag or simplify.
- Monitor/civil society source: useful context; not a governing order.

The bridge lets the cube ask: “What source family said what, when, and with what authority?”
