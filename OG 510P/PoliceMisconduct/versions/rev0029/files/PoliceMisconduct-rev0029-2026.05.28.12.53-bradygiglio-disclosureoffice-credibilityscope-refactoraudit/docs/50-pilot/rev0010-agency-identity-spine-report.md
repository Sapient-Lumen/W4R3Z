# Rev0010 agency identity spine report

Rev0010 moves the DOJ SLS pilot from matter/page-shell modeling into agency identity pre-canonicalization.

The new rule is: **an agency identity candidate is not a canonical department record.**

Why this matters: the long-term corpus cannot make wandering-officer, settlement-per-capita, lawsuit-rate, or department-comparison claims until it knows what agency a source label is actually naming. A DOJ matter row may name a police department, a sheriff office, a prosecutor office, a city/county government party, a bureau, a division, a station group, or a territorial agency. Those are not interchangeable.

Added in this revision:

- 30 agency identity candidates derived from the 27 DOJ SLS law-enforcement source rows.
- 30 alias packets, explicitly marked lookup-only and not merge proof.
- 30 jurisdiction scaffolds, with maps and denominator claims blocked.
- 8 denominator/external source bridge rows.
- 10 external identifier requirements.
- 14 agency merge blocker rules.
- 30 denominator bridge queue tickets.
- 30 public agency page gates.
- 7 official source observations shaping source roles and caveats.

No canonical agency records were created. No officer, civilian, incident, lawsuit-merits, settlement amount, denominator, geography, or public current-status claim was admitted.
