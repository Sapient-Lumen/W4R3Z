# Agency identity candidate method — rev0010

Agency identity candidates are reversible, nonclaim rows created from source-carrier labels. They are useful for queueing external identifier work, not for public department pages.

A candidate may preserve a label such as “Springfield Police Department Narcotics Bureau” while blocking a parent merge into Springfield Police Department until official org-chart or agency source evidence is reviewed.

Minimum promotion requirements:

1. Official agency or government source confirming current/former name and scope.
2. One external identifier or denominator-row candidate, such as CSLLEA, FBI/CDE/ORI, state POST directory, LEAIC, or equivalent.
3. Parent/subunit, government-party, prosecutor-adjacent, territorial, or sheriff/police split decision.
4. Reviewer-signed merge or non-merge receipt.
5. Unmerge path.

The candidate is not evidence of misconduct, current legal status, officer employment, officer identity, or agency staffing.
