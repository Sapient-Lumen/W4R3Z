# No triumphalism, no erasure copy rule

Police reform lifecycle pages invite two bad tones:

- triumphalism: “problem solved”; and
- erasure: “nothing happened because a later source changed posture.”

The cube should use restrained lifecycle language:

- source X reports termination/completion/closure;
- prior public source carriers remain in the historical chain;
- ongoing local obligations may exist outside the federal decree;
- the cube has not admitted a current-status claim until proof gates close.

The cube is a record, not a victory lap and not a denunciation page.
