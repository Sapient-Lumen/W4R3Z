# Public citation badge wireframe — rev0012

Rev0012 prepares internal badge candidates for future source-inventory pages.

Examples:

- `source-label-only`: listed source label; content not frozen.
- `url-resolved-not-preserved`: official URL resolved; payload not captured or hashed.
- `official-news-signal`: official press-release signal; not current-status proof.
- `motion-not-order`: motion/request observed; terminal order not established.
- `privacy-scan-required`: no summary until sensitive identifiers are reviewed.

No badge is public in rev0012. A badge cannot imply misconduct, current legal status, document contents, or person/incident facts.
