# No-person public summary template

Use this template for early public-facing department source-inventory pages.

## Safe summary pattern

> The cube has indexed public source records for this agency. Current release: source inventory only. No individual officer or civilian records are displayed. Source-page status labels are shown with observation times and may not reflect current court status.

## Unsafe copy patterns

Do not write:

- "This department committed misconduct" unless a source-bound department claim has passed claim gates.
- "This decree is active" unless current court status was verified.
- "These officers were involved" before person/incident extraction gates open.
- "Closed means resolved" because closed may mean many things.

## Dignity rule

Civilian names, family narratives, sexual-assault survivor identities, minors, witnesses, undocumented people, people in mental-health crisis, and confidential informants require a separate privacy and consent posture before display.
