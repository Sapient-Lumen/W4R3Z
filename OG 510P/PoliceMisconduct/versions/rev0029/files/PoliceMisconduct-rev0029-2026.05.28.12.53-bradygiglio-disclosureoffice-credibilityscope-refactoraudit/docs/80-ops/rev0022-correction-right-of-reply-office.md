# rev0022 Correction and right-of-reply office

Governing rule: **a correction request is not a correction, and a reply is not an adjudication.**

The cube cannot responsibly admit live officer records, incident records, lawsuit records, settlement records, Brady/Giglio surfaces, or public adverse claims until it has a repair office. Correction is not clerical. A correction may narrow a claim, widen a claim, split an agency, unmerge a person, protect a witness, reveal an appeal, expose a source mutation, or roll back dozens of dependent displays.

Rev0022 opens the correction office without opening live correction intake. The office defines request types, states, transitions, gates, counterevidence packet templates, right-of-reply routes, update actions, public correction-note gates, and rollback receipt templates.

The office is designed to hold several truths at once:

- public records can be wrong, stale, superseded, or misread;
- officers and agencies need due-process and reply pathways;
- civilians, witnesses, survivors, minors, and families need privacy/minimization pathways;
- powerful actors may misuse correction channels to suppress public records;
- public updates must correct without laundering allegations into findings or replies into adjudications;
- correction must propagate through dependent atoms, claims, public pages, aggregates, and exports.

Rev0022 creates no live correction requests and publishes no public correction notes.
