# Counterevidence packet method

Counterevidence packets are bounded evidence bundles for correction review. They must say what surface is contested, what source or evidence supports the correction, what scope is affected, who is speaking, which modality applies, what privacy risks are present, and which dependent surfaces may roll back.

A packet does not automatically change the cube. A packet can support acceptance, rejection, deferral, minimization, rollback, schema repair, or public-note review. Private assertions, unsupported threats, or unscoped documents are not enough to rewrite source-bound claims.
