# Right of reply and contestation routes

A right of reply is a display-safe contextual route, not a fact-finding override. The cube should eventually let agencies, officers, counsel, source owners, families, civilians, researchers, and community representatives contest or contextualize surfaces. But reply text cannot become an adjudication simply because it is attached to a page.

The route table separates agency replies, officer replies, family/civilian replies, source-owner corrections, court/adjudicatory updates, prosecutor/Brady updates, settlement-party updates, oversight/IA updates, researcher dataset corrections, policy/union/arbitration updates, rollback-only notices, and no-public-reply privacy suppression.

Every public reply candidate remains blocked until release review, privacy review when relevant, source-voice labeling, and public correction display gates are complete.
