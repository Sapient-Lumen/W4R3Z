# Rev0015 mile-high reentry checklist

Before continuing after rev0015:

1. Read `docs/00-meta/rev0015-mile-high-telos-charter.md`.
2. Choose a mode: continue, deepen, branch, synthesize, or dream.
3. Identify whether the move touches a permanent constraint, phase-bound constraint, or unknown constraint.
4. If it creates a dream, put it in `DREAM-REGISTER.json` or `OPEN-HORIZON-REGISTER.json`.
5. If it creates a capability, locate it on `EARNED-CAPABILITY-LADDER.json`.
6. If it creates a public posture, add display gates and rollback paths.
7. If it relaxes a no-person/no-incident/no-summary rule, write the evidence of what was earned first.
8. Run `make lint`.

A good next revision may be technical. A good next revision may also be philosophical. The only bad next revision is one that forgets why the project exists or forgets whom it can harm.
