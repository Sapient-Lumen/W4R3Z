
# Synthetic-first protocol

High-risk branches must be rehearsed with synthetic fixtures before live people, live incidents, lawsuits, settlements, decertification, Brady/Giglio, death-in-custody, wandering-officer, or family-packet records are opened.

A synthetic fixture must demonstrate at least one safe block, rejection, split, rollback, or scoped promotion receipt. The fixture is not a loophole for live data. It is a way to prove that the cube can refuse overclaiming before it handles human records.
