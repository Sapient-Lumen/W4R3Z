# Rev0020 review packet kernel

Rev0020 adds the operating shell future sessions should use before moving from
source-family routes into source rows, source capture, evidence atoms, claim
candidates, public displays, corrections, or rollbacks.

The governing rule is:

**A review packet is not an admission; a queue state is not a claim; a validator pass is not truth.**

The packet kernel exists because the cube is about to become capable of live
work. Rev0017 gave lifecycle states. Rev0018 gave atom and claim grammar.
Rev0019 gave source families and corpus lanes. Rev0020 now adds the packet
surface that ties them together.

A packet must name its source family, corpus lane, lifecycle target, source
custody status, privacy/due-process risk, source voice, modality, defeaters,
reviewers, decision receipt, and rollback plan. A packet can be rejected, split,
deferred, or admitted as nonclaim memory. It cannot promote itself.

Counts opened in this revision:

- 24 review-packet states;
- 20 packet types;
- 26 required packet sections;
- 38 transition gates;
- 14 reviewer roles;
- 10 risk-tier routes;
- 15 decision receipt templates;
- 24 source-family to packet crosswalk rows;
- 12 synthetic fixture blueprints.

Rev0020 admits no live packets, live source rows, payloads, evidence atoms,
claim candidates, people, incidents, lawsuits, settlements, public displays, or
public claims.
