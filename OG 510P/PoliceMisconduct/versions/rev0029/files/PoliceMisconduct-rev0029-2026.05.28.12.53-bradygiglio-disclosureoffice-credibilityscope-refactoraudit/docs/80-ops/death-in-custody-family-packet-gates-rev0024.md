# Death-in-custody family packet gates

Death-in-custody work combines every hard surface: source custody, medical/examiner language, department statements, lawsuits, settlements, video/audio, family grief, witness safety, and public interest. Rev0024 blocks live packets until synthetic gates are tested.

A future packet should begin as a source inventory, not a narrative. It must preserve source voice for cause/manner, department statements, lawsuit allegations, settlements, and court findings. It must avoid sensational page titles, unnecessary trauma-media previews, and public exposure of family contact or medical detail.
