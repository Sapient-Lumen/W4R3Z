# Role access and misuse boundaries — rev0023

The access office records eighteen audience roles. No role receives raw payloads, sensitive media, person-level adverse data, or bulk exports by default.

The most important boundary is that law-enforcement and prosecutor-adjacent uses do not get secret privileged access. A prosecutor Brady/Giglio route is limited to disclosure duties. A POST/certification route is limited to certification and appeal-status review. A law-enforcement employer hiring-safety route is only a future bounded exception, not a general search product.

The commercial data broker role is denied as a default category because resale into risk, policing, insurance, or employment products would invert the cube's telos.
