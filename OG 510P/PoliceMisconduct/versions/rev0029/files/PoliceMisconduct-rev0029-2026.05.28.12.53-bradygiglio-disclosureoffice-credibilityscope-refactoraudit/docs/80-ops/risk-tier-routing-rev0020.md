# Risk-tier routing — rev0020

Risk tiers are not moral scores. They are operating constraints.

The low tiers cover structural definitions and source inventory. The middle
tiers cover preservation, agency context, bounded summaries, evidence atoms, and
institutional claims. The high tiers cover person identity, adverse public
claims, Brady/Giglio, death-in-custody, family narratives, minors, sexual assault,
mental-health crisis, and other high-care civilian contexts.

A tier determines which reviewers must sign off before a packet can move. No
single reviewer role can promote a packet alone.
