# Decision receipts — rev0020

Decision receipts preserve curation memory. The cube should remember what it
admitted, deferred, rejected, split, merged, rolled back, or refused to display.

The most important rejection receipts are not failures. They protect the corpus:
name-only mismerges, settlement/admission overclaims, public-record/public-display
collapses, family-narrative extraction, and missingness/absence errors must all
be visible to future operators.
