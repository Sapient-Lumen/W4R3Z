# Rev0016 overconstraint audit

The purpose of this file is to keep future sessions brave without making them careless.

Permanent constraints are moral and epistemic commitments. Phase-bound constraints are scaffolding.

## Permanent constraints

- Every claim must remain source-bound and rollbackable.
- Public record does not imply public display.
- Do not collapse allegation, settlement, finding, adjudication, appeal, reversal, and exoneration.
- Civilian, witness, minor, family, survivor, immigration, mental-health, and sexual-misconduct privacy are not optional.
- Name-only identity merges are forbidden.

## Phase-bound constraints

- No officer/person records yet. — This is not philosophy; it is a current earned-capability gate.
- DOJ SLS first. — Useful pilot sequence, not the project boundary.
- Department/source pages before officer lookup. — Good safety order, but eventual officer lookup remains a dream to earn.
- No source payload summaries before preservation/privacy gates. — Keep unless a synthetic or internal-only exception is explicitly marked.
- No public current-status banners. — Keep until docket/order/status proof and display text are robust.
- No canonical agency records yet. — Temporary; next agency fixture may safely open this.
- No public denominator/geography claims yet. — Temporary; needs agency canonicalization and source freshness.
- No lawsuit/settlement records yet. — Temporary; a future corpus branch should earn this explicitly.

## The key correction

“No people yet” is not the soul of the project. The soul is to eventually earn the right to handle people, incidents, lawsuits, settlements, wandering, credibility, family packets, and department histories safely enough to matter.
