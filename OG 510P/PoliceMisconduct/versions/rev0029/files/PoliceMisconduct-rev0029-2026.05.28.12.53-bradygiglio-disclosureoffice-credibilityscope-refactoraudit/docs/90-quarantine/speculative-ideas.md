# Quarantine: speculative ideas

Ideas here are not canon claims.

## Q-0001 Community-controlled display tiers

Maybe affected communities should help decide which categories of records are displayable in their jurisdiction and at what granularity. This could prevent extractive publication,
but it could also produce uneven access and local pressure capture. Needs governance research.

## Q-0002 Misconduct-cost externality map

A map showing settlement costs by officer, supervisor, unit, neighborhood, and policy regime could be powerful. It also risks flattening settlement meaning and incentivizing
perverse interpretations. Needs outcome vocabulary and privacy review first.

## Q-0003 Brady/Giglio defender interface

A public defender interface could be high-impact. It also raises access, accuracy, timeliness, and correction stakes. Needs legal review and source custody far beyond rev0001.
