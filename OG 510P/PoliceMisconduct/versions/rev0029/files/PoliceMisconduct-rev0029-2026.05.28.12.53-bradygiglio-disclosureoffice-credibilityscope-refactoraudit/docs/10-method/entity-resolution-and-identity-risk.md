# Entity resolution and identity risk

Identity resolution is both a technical and ethical gate. Wrongly merging officers can defame. Wrongly splitting officers can hide wandering patterns.
Wrongly naming civilians can expose vulnerable people to harm.

## Officer identity

Name-only matches are insufficient. A merge should prefer authoritative IDs, department+badge+date context, employment history, court role, rank, and other
independent identifiers. Badge numbers do not travel across departments unless the department/date context travels with them.

## Civilian identity

Civilian identity should usually be minimized unless the civilian is a public plaintiff, has chosen public identification, or display is essential and ethically reviewed.
The corpus should be able to connect records internally while showing less externally.

## Unmerge is required

Every future canonical person record needs an unmerge route. Entity resolution is not a one-way act.
