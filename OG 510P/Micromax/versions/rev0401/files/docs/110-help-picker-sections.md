Help picker section browse budget
================================

This fixture exists to prove that docs-focused pickers keep multiple visible
sections represented in their initial browse window, even when one section has
many more rows than the others.

## Intro

The headings section should remain visible in `helpnavpick`.

## More headings

Another heading keeps the `Headings` section non-trivial.
