# Rev1000 audit — mission, product reality, and evidence honesty

Date: 2026-08-01

Micromax's heart is a calm, inhabitable editor for understandable
least-authority automation. The editor is the product and pressure test; the
small language and replayable VM are its configuration, macro, and extension
substrate. Headless truth, explicit effects, provenance, finite work, and
recoverable failure are the project's strongest differentiators.

The incoming rev0999 tree is exceptionally strong at trust engineering and weak
at product proof. It contains 995 docs, 278 test-tree files, 248 indexed
revisions, and a 34,506-line `editor.py` with 1,382 direct `Editor` methods. Since
the rev0873 audit warned about coordinator gravity and evidence becoming a
second product, `editor.py` grew about 36 percent, direct methods about 26
percent, and docs about 23 percent. The warning was preserved but did not change
the trajectory.

No single catastrophic correctness defect was found. The severe failure is
cumulative:

- proof/revision machinery receives more sustained attention than adoption,
  taste, or flow;
- first-contact surfaces still called a working editor a future prototype;
- the carried full-suite manifest was partial and stale while the ordinary audit
  emphasized that the runway existed; and
- repeated local survivor/optimization revisions remained easier to justify than
  messy sustained-user evidence.

Rev1000 makes three bounded corrections:

1. `mxaudit` now distinguishes release-manifest presence, current source/test
   inventory, internal consistency, completeness, pass state, counts, digest,
   and issues. Partial checkpoints remain useful but cannot imply current release
   evidence.
2. Package metadata, CLI help, the line-UI greeting, package/class docstrings,
   README, and tutorial describe the current editor product; first contact now
   includes checkout installation and the real console entry points.
3. The living mission/roadmap pivots the next sequence toward a five-session
   dogfood ledger, first-run/help clarity, executed release evidence, and only
   then coordinator extraction under a second-consumer plus net-deletion rule.

The largest remaining gaps are sustained-user/product proof, taste and flow
criteria, an executed and consumer-verified hosted release, a compact stable
extension “world,” grapheme/terminal-cell semantics, a declared filesystem and
power-loss matrix, Windows/macOS receipts, and a distinct public name. The
existing “Micromax” name collides with active technology brands; this is a
search and release-identity risk, not a legal conclusion.

The full diagnosis, external research, speculation, staged correction plan, and
source list are in
`docs/958-mission-product-reality-and-evidence-honesty-audit.md`.
