## Why it was worth doing

The other datacubes kept converging on the same lesson: support/publication objects need their own queue state, and handoff bundles should describe themselves instead of asking future readers to trust filenames and directory shape.

GlassTTY already had strong support-record and archive-verification instincts. The missing substrate was the object in between: a named support bundle that could be honestly held, reviewed, promoted, or published.
