# Handoff rev0130 — support publish gate, head guards, and transition receipts

## What changed

- added a **support publish gate** separate from both the support-bundle queue and the published/citable support surface
- hardened `support-bundle-transition` with a fail-closed queue-edge graph for `published-ready` / `published` promotion
- added expected-head **`publish_guard`** stamping so publication claims cannot silently ride newer support-record or truth-surface heads
- added durable **transition receipts/history** so promotion and demotion become auditable actions rather than only directory moves

## Why it was worth doing

The strongest remaining transferable pattern from the comparison cubes was not “more support docs,” but a narrower publication boundary: queue state, publication eligibility, and citable published truth are different objects and should fail closed when they diverge.

## Current honest posture

- GlassTTY still has **zero published support bundles**
- Claude still has a **held** bundle, and the publish gate blocks promotion because the archive still lacks live official-surface route/history/workflow evidence
- the new gate mainly exists to stop future overclaiming and to make the blocking reasons explicit

## Recommended next step

Capture one live logged-in Claude official-surface support bundle with route/history witness and before/after workflow evidence, then move that bundle through `published-ready` with a fresh `publish_guard` instead of citing the held bundle directly.
