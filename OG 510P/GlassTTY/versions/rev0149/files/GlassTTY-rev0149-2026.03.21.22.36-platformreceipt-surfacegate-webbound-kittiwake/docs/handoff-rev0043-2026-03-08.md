## Why rev0043 matters

rev0043 makes GlassTTY's planner think more like Playwright and less like a flat selector suggester. A saved fixture can now preserve where an action lives — page, named form, fieldset group, dialog, or iframe-rooted scope — and the generated playbook reflects that structure directly. This should reduce ambiguity for repeated controls and make future browser-sourced fixture comparison more informative.
