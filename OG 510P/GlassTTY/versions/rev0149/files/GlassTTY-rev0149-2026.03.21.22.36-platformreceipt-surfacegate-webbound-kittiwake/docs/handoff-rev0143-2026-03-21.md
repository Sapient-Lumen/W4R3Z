# Handoff rev0143 — support claim receipt and lane-bound support envelope

## What changed

- added `CHATGPT-SUPPORT-CLAIM-RECEIPT.json` with a machine-readable claim envelope for the route-first ChatGPT baseline
- integrated that new receipt into `refresh-truth-surfaces`, the ChatGPT first-proof kit, the ChatGPT candidate bundle, the ChatGPT support record, and selector notes
- updated status/changelog material so the repo now distinguishes repeatable proof from the strongest honest support wording that proof can justify

## Why this revision exists

rev0142 could now say whether repeated ChatGPT proof windows were stable enough to strengthen support language. That still left one honest failure mode: repeated Chromium proof could still be phrased too broadly, as though it covered other browsers, richer ChatGPT workspaces, or auth postures that were never actually proved.

rev0143 closes that gap by adding a separate claim-envelope receipt. The new receipt forces support wording to stay within the explicit browser lane, auth posture, route branch, and proved workflow slice attached to the evidence.

## New behavior

- repeated proof windows can now be graded as `provisional-lane-bound`, `experimental-lane-bound`, `hold-for-scope-expansion`, `investigated-only`, `planning-only`, or `stop`
- the strongest support story is now expressed as a claim envelope with explicit browser-lane scope, auth-posture scope, route scope, workflow scope, excluded branches, and excluded browser families
- support wording can no longer silently generalize one Chromium route-first baseline into Projects, Canvas, GPT builder, sidebar history search, or cross-browser parity

## Remaining limits

- there is still no live official-surface ChatGPT repeated proof bundle in this archive
- the new claim envelope is only as good as the repeated proof windows and scope fields it is given

## Recommended next move

Capture one real route/composer/submit/proof-bundle window on ChatGPT, then capture a second distinct window on the same baseline lane with the same benign exact-match probe. Require both windows to clear the proof-bundle receipt, require the promotion-stability receipt to say `ready-for-provisional` or `ready-with-caution`, and then require the support-claim receipt to phrase the final support story as explicitly lane-bound rather than broad ChatGPT parity.
