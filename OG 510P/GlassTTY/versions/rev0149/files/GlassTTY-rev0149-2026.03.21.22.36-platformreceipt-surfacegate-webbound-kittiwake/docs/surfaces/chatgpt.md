# ChatGPT surface profile

- **surface key:** `chatgpt`
- **priority:** recommended second adapter

## Why it matters
ChatGPT is a major browser-native AI surface and the best likely next proof that GlassTTY's generic core can support more than Claude.

## Current route posture
- start from the plain browser route at `chatgpt.com`
- treat Projects, GPT builder, Canvas, and other richer modes as follow-on branches after the generic chat lane is proven
- preserve auth-state and overlay posture in the first baseline bundle
- classify the landed shell with `CHATGPT-POSTURE-MATRIX.json` before assuming richer features are harmless
- evaluate the first route/auth/title/text witness with `CHATGPT-BRANCH-GUARD.json` before touching the composer
- require `CHATGPT-ROUTE-WITNESS-RECEIPT.json` to say the route is proof-ready before trusting composer evidence
- require `CHATGPT-COMPOSER-WITNESS-RECEIPT.json` to say the composer is writable before spending proof budget on submit

## First workflow targets
- surface-detect

## Risks to watch
- route and layout variation
- search-capable home-lane variants that still keep the generic chat shell intact
- tool/mode changes in the composer area
- streaming/latest-turn parsing differences
- transient overlays or modal interruptions

## Evidence to collect
- route/title/posture baseline bundle
- composer candidate bundle with one failed and one successful path
- `CHATGPT-FIRST-PROOF-KIT.json` for route-first selector contract, hazards, and failure taxonomy

- `CHATGPT-BRANCH-GUARD.json` for witness-driven continue-vs-stop decisions
- `CHATGPT-ROUTE-WITNESS-RECEIPT.json` for route-quality and proof-readiness grading
- `CHATGPT-COMPOSER-WITNESS-RECEIPT.json` for composer writability and readback grading
- candidate/held support bundle for compose/submit/read-latest
- comparison bundle after first drift event
