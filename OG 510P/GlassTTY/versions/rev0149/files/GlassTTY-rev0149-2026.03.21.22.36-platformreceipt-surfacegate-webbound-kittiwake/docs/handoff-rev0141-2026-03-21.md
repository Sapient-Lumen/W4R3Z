# Handoff rev0141 — submit witness receipt and proof-window bundle gate

## What changed

- added `CHATGPT-SUBMIT-WITNESS-RECEIPT.json` with a machine-readable quality rubric for submit/result witnesses so ChatGPT proof no longer overclaims a completed turn from typed text alone
- added `CHATGPT-PROOF-BUNDLE-RECEIPT.json` so one whole route/composer/submit proof window can be graded for held-bundle promotion instead of promoting isolated receipts
- integrated both receipts into `refresh-truth-surfaces`, the ChatGPT first-proof kit, the ChatGPT candidate bundle, focused tests, and living support docs

## Why this revision exists

rev0139 could prove a route and a writable composer, but it still left two honest failure modes:

1. a run could type into the composer and still over-trust submit success without preserving dispatch evidence, stable completion cues, and final latest-turn readback
2. a run could clear individual receipts and still overclaim bundle promotion without preserving one coherent live proof window and its core artifacts

rev0141 closes both gaps by separating **proved a writable composer**, **proved a completed turn**, and **proved one coherent proof window fit for held-bundle promotion**.

## New behavior

- submit/result witnesses can now be graded as `strong`, `usable`, `fragile`, or `insufficient`
- submit continuation can now land on `ready`, `ready-with-caution`, `hold-for-recapture`, `blocked`, `blocked-by-composer`, `blocked-by-route`, `insufficient`, or `stop`
- proof-window bundle continuation can now land on `ready-for-held`, `ready-with-caution`, `hold-for-recapture`, `blocked`, `blocked-by-composer`, `blocked-by-route`, `planning-only`, or `stop`
- a run can no longer promote on isolated receipt success alone; it now needs a coherent live proof window, core artifacts, and an explicit bundle-level gate

## Remaining limits

- there is still no live official-surface ChatGPT compose/submit/readback bundle in this archive
- the new proof-bundle receipt is only as good as the live-capture context, artifact refs, and proof-window token it is given

## Recommended next move

Capture one real ChatGPT route witness, require the route witness receipt to say `ready` or `ready-with-caution`, capture one concrete composer witness on that same pass, require the composer witness receipt to say `ready` or `ready-with-caution`, then capture one concrete submit/result witness on that same pass and require the submit witness receipt to say `ready` or `ready-with-caution`. Finally, preserve the route screenshot, receiver posture, composer-candidate record, submit evidence, and latest-turn readback under one capture-window token and require the proof-bundle receipt to say `ready-for-held` or `ready-with-caution` before spending promotion budget on a stronger held bundle.
