# Handoff — rev0145 — 2026-03-21

## What changed

rev0145 adds a machine-readable `CHATGPT-AUTH-WORKSPACE-RECEIPT.json` plus `scripts/chatgpt_auth_workspace_receipt.py` so repeated route-first ChatGPT proof can stay bound to one explicit session envelope instead of silently blurring guest, personal, Business, Enterprise, or Edu evidence together.

## Why it matters

The repo already had separate guards for route, composer, submit, proof-window coherence, repeated-window stability, support-claim scope, and tool/model profile. The next honest gap was session scope: a plain logged-out proof should not quietly become a signed-in history/export/share claim, and a personal workspace pass should not quietly become Business or Enterprise workspace support.

## Grounding imported this round

- The ChatGPT home-page help article still says logged-out use on `chatgpt.com` is limited to one conversation at a time and does not preserve chat history after logout.
- The Data Controls FAQ distinguishes signed-out versus signed-in settings, which makes auth posture a real support boundary.
- The Business workspace article says ChatGPT workspaces can be personal, Business, or Enterprise and that members of multiple workspaces can switch between them from the profile menu.
- Playwright still recommends user-facing locators, auto-waiting/actionability, and assertion-backed readback rather than brittle DOM coupling.
