## Why it matters

rev0057 made receiver choice deterministic and explainable in the daemon CLI, but GlassTTY's browser-side proof artifacts still only showed the selected winner plus raw receiver rows. That left live diagnostics and saved fixtures weaker than they needed to be. rev0058 closes that gap: the product now preserves the same receiver-choice reasoning inside `bridge.probe` and live fixture metadata, so future real browser proof bundles can explain *why* a receiver won without requiring a separate shell command.
