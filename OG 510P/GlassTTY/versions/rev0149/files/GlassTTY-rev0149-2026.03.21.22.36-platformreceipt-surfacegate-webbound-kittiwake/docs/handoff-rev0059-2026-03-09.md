## Why it matters

rev0058 explained **why one observed receiver won**, but it still left a common operator question mostly unanswered: *why is a receiver missing at all?* Chrome's frame model treats related `about:` / `data:` / `blob:` / `filesystem:` descendants as a special case, and GlassTTY was previously hiding that nuance behind a vague unsupported-URL bucket. rev0059 closes that observability gap without widening manifest reach. Future probe and fixture artifacts can now say whether a frame was already observed, already primed, currently primeable, truly unsupported, or one of Chrome's related-frame URL cases.
