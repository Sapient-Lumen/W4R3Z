## What changed

- audited and continued from `GlassTTY-rev0054-2026.03.09.08.29-receiverpriming-docinject-idempotentretry-wayfinder.zip`
- taught `extension/src/shared/receivers.ts` to prefer active or lifecycle-unknown receivers over known inactive `prerender` / `cached` / `pending_deletion` receivers during default receiver selection
- taught receiver merging to evict an older same-frame receiver entry when a new `documentId` is observed on that frame, which aligns GlassTTY with Chrome's rule that `documentId` changes across navigations while `frameId` can persist
- enriched `extension/src/background/main.ts` trace entries so `receivers.frame_navigation` now includes `documentLifecycle`
- expanded deterministic receiver validation, refreshed archive memory/status/research/worklog/session-end material, and saved focused validation in `validation/rev0055-focused/`

## Why it matters

rev0054 improved receiver discovery. rev0055 makes receiver choice and receiver memory more honest after navigation churn. Without lifecycle-aware choice, a prerendered outermost receiver could still outrank the actually useful active receiver. Without same-frame document replacement, GlassTTY could accumulate multiple document histories for one frame long enough to bias later routing or diagnostics.
