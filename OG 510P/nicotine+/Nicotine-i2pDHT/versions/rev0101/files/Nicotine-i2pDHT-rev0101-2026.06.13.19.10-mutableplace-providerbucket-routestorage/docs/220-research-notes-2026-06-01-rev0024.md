# Research notes — rev0024

Useful teachers for this revision:

- S/Kademlia: disjoint paths, sibling broadcast, and crypto-puzzle friction remain pressure tools for Kademlia-style routing.
- Dynamo anti-entropy: Merkle trees per key range are a useful model for finding differences cheaply before exact repair.
- libp2p Kad-DHT: validators and record selection are useful vocabulary for not treating signed bytes as dispatch-ready truth.
- I2P SAM: still the likely future non-Java integration surface, but transport assumptions stay shadowed.

None of those are copied as a full protocol. The cube keeps implementing local pressure tests first.
