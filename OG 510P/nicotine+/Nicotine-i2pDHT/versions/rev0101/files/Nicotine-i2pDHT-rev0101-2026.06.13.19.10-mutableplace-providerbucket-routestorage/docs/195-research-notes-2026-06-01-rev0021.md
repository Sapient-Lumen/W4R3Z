# Research notes — 2026-06-01 rev0021

This revision did not chase new external references.  It turned existing cube guesses into executable pressure surfaces.

The important research lesson carried forward is internal: single-round correctness is weaker than repeated-round correctness.  A signed mutable head, a signed repair offer, or a signed wire frame can each validate in isolation while still becoming stale replay, split view, colluding-family capture, or semantic-role confusion across time.

The cube should keep moving risk-first: make disagreement, replay, fork, and role mismatch boring before live transport starts making failures look like ordinary network weather.
