# Proof obligation — rev0048

Before public bridge publication can become a real network side effect, the cube should keep proving:

1. A publication guard cannot be rebound to another payload or request.
2. Shadow plans reject replay, drift, stale windows, and same-sequence forks.
3. Audit statements cannot turn one-family abundance into truth.
4. Redress cleanup cannot erase hard negatives by expiration, broad lift, or byte pressure.
5. Exact profile/service/subject/scope/request binding survives restart and migration.
6. Future SAM-shadow publication dry-runs do not leak raw subject identifiers by default.
