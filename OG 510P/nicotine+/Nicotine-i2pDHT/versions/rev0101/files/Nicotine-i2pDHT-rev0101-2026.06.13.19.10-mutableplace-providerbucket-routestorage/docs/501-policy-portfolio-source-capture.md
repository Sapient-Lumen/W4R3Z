# Policy portfolio source capture

Policy sources are local inputs: maintainer policy, local operator rules, garden sentinels, friend/community trust, emergency holds, and cached last-good defaults. They remain subjective local pressure, not DHT truth.

The risk is capture by apparent abundance. A portfolio must reject or hold when source-kind diversity, family diversity, path diversity, monotonic source memory, or exact scope/request binding fails. A source that denies or freezes blocks local publication unless later redress/appeal lanes explicitly bind at the same side-effect boundary.
