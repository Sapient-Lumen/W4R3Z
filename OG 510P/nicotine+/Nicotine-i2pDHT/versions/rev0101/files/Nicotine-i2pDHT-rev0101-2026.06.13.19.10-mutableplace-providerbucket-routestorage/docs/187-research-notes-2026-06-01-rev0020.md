# Research notes — 2026-06-01 rev0020

This revision did not chase live-network implementation.  It continued the cube's risk-first posture:

- Kademlia/S/Kademlia remain useful teachers for path separation and sibling pressure.
- BEP44 remains the compact teacher for signed mutable values, but rev0020 treats signed mutability as insufficient without local epoch history.
- I2P floodfill remains the garden-node caution: capacity tiers help, but capacity must not become authority.
- libp2p/IPFS provider records remain useful vocabulary for content-routing pointers, but provider records and repair offers are claims requiring local pressure.

The new guess is that future live transport should only arrive after canonical frame fixtures, epoch gates, repair-capacity selection, and audit surfaces are boring enough to stop changing every turn.
