# nicotine+

## Three related research interests, three distinct boundaries

These projects share a Nicotine-related setting, while asking different questions. Keeping them together makes their relationship visible without turning them into one release sequence.

1. **[Nicotine+DEV](Nicotine%2BDEV/README.md): model and source.** A test can encode intended behavior and miss what the implementation actually does. Read this for source-bound claims and the separation of current research packets from retained history.
2. **[Nicotineplusplusplus](Nicotineplusplusplus/README.md): a hardening layer and its new obligations.** A bounded mechanism creates admission and loss-policy questions of its own. Its findings must remain scoped to that experimental layer rather than being attributed wholesale to upstream.
3. **[Nicotine-i2pDHT](Nicotine-i2pDHT/README.md): accepted records and local state.** Admission at one boundary is not automatic authority for placement, provider or routing decisions at another.

This is a shelf of research and design prose accompanied by implementations, patches and evidence. It does not promise upstream acceptance, a qualified deployment or continued maintenance of every component. Original sources, histories and licenses remain under the respective projects.
