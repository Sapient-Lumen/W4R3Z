# EvidenceVault

## An archive can be useful without pretending to be whole

The supplied EvidenceVault artifact is a partial recovery overlay. That is its essential reading condition. It should not greet a reader as though all the source it refers to were present, or as though a successful local check could supply absent rights and missing material.

The work is interesting precisely at that edge: what can be recovered exactly, what remains unavailable, and how a gate can tie its conclusion to the material it actually examined.

## A second boundary: checking and doing

The latest supplied correction concerns the relation between verifying an object and executing that same object. At the design level, this is a powerful general question: what exactly does a successful check authorize us to believe about a later action? The overlay’s implementation and historical regressions remain in the source; this introduction does not reproduce or run them.

The recovery record reports limited exact coverage, absent selected StreamFold payloads and an unresolved canonical README difference. W4R3Z preserves the contributed overlay as an archival object, not as a new complete or publication-ready canonical EvidenceVault release.

Its value as prose includes the refusal to fill holes with plausible substitutes. A durable statement of what cannot yet be recovered can be more useful than a seamless-looking reconstruction that has silently changed the work.

Read beside [DelayBasin](../DelayBasin/README.md) for continuity without overstating its cause, and [Radical Governance](../Radical-Governance/README.md) for the warning that an artifact cannot appoint its own custodian.
