# Witness policy — rev0858 framed transcript receipt lane

The rev0858 lane is transparent. Its public inputs are payload absence receipts, hash commitments, a toy sumcheck transcript fixture, candidate-root negative controls, and the rev0857 parent checkpoint.

Private witness material is not included. Canonical streamfold payload bytes remain absent from this overlay. A future operator may provide a candidate root to the rev0857 payload candidate verifier, but rev0858 does not copy those bytes and does not grant rights.

Non-claims: not a SNARK, not zero knowledge, not succinct, not a production Fiat-Shamir transform, not a streamfold protocol-correctness proof, and not a publication rights grant.
