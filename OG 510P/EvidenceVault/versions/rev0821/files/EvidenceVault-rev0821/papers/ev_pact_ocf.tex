\begin{abstract}
Portable PACT / OCF evidence in this family is carried by one scoped decision package on one fixed decision scope.
That package carries the local decision output, any admission-only residual payload, and one bridge replay slice for the judged decision.
\end{abstract}

\section{Decision packages}


\begin{definition}[Scoped decision package]
A scoped decision package is the admitted replay-bearing carrier on that fixed decision scope and is the tuple
\[
\mathsf{Pkg}=(\psi,u,\theta,o,a,\Sigma),
\qquad
\psi=(k,\mathcal{B}),
\]
where $\psi$ is the carried replay-facing role package, with $k\in\{\mathsf{transition},\mathsf{operational},\mathsf{admission}\}$ selecting the local decision mode, $u$ is the canonical judged-scope digest of the decision object the package claims to cover, $\theta$ is the carried decision-input context (tools, versions, policies, relevant logs, and comparable scoped environment pins), $o$ is the local decision output (for example \textsf{ACCEPT}, \textsf{REJECT}, or \textsf{DEFER}), $a$ is the optional admission slot, empty when $k\in\{\mathsf{transition},\mathsf{operational}\}$ and otherwise the inline local tuple
\[
a=(d_{\mathrm{ABOM}},\Upsilon),
\]
with $d_{\mathrm{ABOM}}$ the signable manifest digest that content-addresses the evaluated artifact(s) and binds them to typed obligations, prerequisites, and evidence references, while $\Upsilon$ records any unsupported deployment-relevant change, compensating-control requirement, debt-limit blocker, authorized override material, rollback / repair marker, or restored discharge condition that policy says still matters for that package reading; and $\Sigma$ is the carried bridge replay slice.
The selector $k$ then determines the package-internal case: if $k=\mathsf{transition}$, then $o$ carries the local transition outcome while any before/after artifact pair, step witness, or toolchain justification needed to explain that transition remains carried evidence on $\Sigma$ or carried decision-input context inside $\theta$; if $k=\mathsf{operational}$, then $o$ carries the local operational verdict and $\Sigma$ carries the replayable supporting material behind it; and if $k=\mathsf{admission}$, then the inline slot is present.
\end{definition}
