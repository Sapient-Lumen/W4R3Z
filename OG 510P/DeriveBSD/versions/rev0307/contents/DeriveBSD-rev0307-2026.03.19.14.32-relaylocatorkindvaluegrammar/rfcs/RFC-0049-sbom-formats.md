# RFC-0049: SBOM formats (SPDX and CycloneDX)

- Status: draft
- Created: 2026-02-23

## Summary
Standardize how DeriveBSD emits SBOMs as DSSE/in-toto attestations using SPDX or CycloneDX payloads.

## Goals
- policy can require SBOM per channel/target
- stable schemas and deterministic content addressing
