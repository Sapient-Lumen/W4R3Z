# Burst borrow proof page — exception authority, live consumption, payback, and restoration basis

## Purpose

This page is the durable proof object for the strongest true sentence about temporary authorized out-of-envelope use.
It exists so the archive can say exactly whether extra room was borrowed lawfully, whether the exception stayed within its own narrower boundary, what harm it imposed, and whether reserve and claimant position were later restored.

## Sentences this page may support

- `claimant active with valid temporary burst borrow`
- `claimant active with valid protected-reserve exception`
- `claimant active but outside exception limit`
- `exception expired while extra room remained in use`
- `payback open after exception use`
- `payback complete and ordinary entitlement restored`
- `exception withdrawn and claimant throttled or reclaimed`

## Mandatory proof blocks

### A. Ordinary and extra-room basis

- ordinary award reference
- borrowed extra-room amount
- source of borrowed room
- authority basis and signer quality
- expiry and renewal rule

### B. Live exception evidence

- latest live consumption witness
- latest within-exception witness
- latest beyond-exception witness
- harmed-claimant witness
- reserve-impact witness
- measurement confidence basis

### C. Payback evidence

- payback class
- payback due time
- reserve-restoration witness
- claimant-restoration witness
- narrowed-follow-on-award witness if relevant
- unpaid-balance witness if still open

### D. Stronger blocked sentences

- strongest blocked sentence for the urgent claimant
- strongest blocked sentence for harmed neighbors or losers
- exact fact needed to upgrade either sentence

## Required badges

- `authorized-burst`
- `authorized-reserve-borrow`
- `beyond-exception-limit`
- `expired-in-use`
- `payback-open`
- `payback-complete`
- `exception-withdrawn`
- `restored-to-ordinary-envelope`

Badges must stack rather than overwrite lineage when later truth strengthens or weakens.

## Proof obligations

- prove temporary authority separately from ordinary entitlement
- prove payback separately from mere exception closure
- prove reserve restoration separately from reduced visible load
- preserve harmed-claimant consequence until relief is actually completed
- preserve normalization risk whenever the same claimant or authority keeps reopening burst borrow

## Stronger-sentence guard

This page may say `extra room was borrowed under typed temporary authority`.
It may not say `the claimant now owns that extra room` unless a later explicit allocation change supports it.
