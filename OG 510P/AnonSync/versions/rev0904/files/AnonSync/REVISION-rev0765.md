# AnonSync rev0765

Makes terminal retention an exact evidence-authorized transition. Selected ingress rows are reconstructed from canonical durable frames, strictly compared with parent projections, committed to as an ordered exact candidate set, deleted by exact identity in one transaction, and reported only after commit. Authority-denial retention receives equivalent exact-row commitments. The revision extracts retention from the lifecycle monolith, adds typed payload-schema capability, explicit decode limits, and adversarial rollback/collision/corruption tests.
