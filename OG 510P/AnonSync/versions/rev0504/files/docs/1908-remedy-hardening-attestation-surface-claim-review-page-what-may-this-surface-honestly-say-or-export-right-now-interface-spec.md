# Remedy-hardening-attestation surface-claim review page — what may this surface honestly say or export right now?

## Review question

Given the current governing receipt and current audience, what is the strongest sentence this exact surface may honestly render, copy, notify, cache, or export right now — and which older stronger sentence must be blocked, recalled, or visibly demoted instead?

## Review panels

### 1) Governing-receipt binding review

Force the operator to bind the surface to one explicit current governing receipt.
The review must preserve at least these distinctions:

- current strongest internal sentence versus current strongest audience-safe sentence
- named-cohort-safe sentence versus public-safe sentence
- current receipt versus historical receipt still visible on other surfaces
- live current rendering versus historical reference rendering

### 2) Surface fit review

For the selected surface, require the operator to score whether the surface can safely carry the required nuance.
The page must preserve at least these distinctions:

- rich detail page versus compressed badge or toast
- durable export versus transient notification
- local diagnostic page versus outward-facing artifact
- copyable label versus non-copyable status indicator

### 3) Audience budget review

For the selected audience, require the operator to score:

- whether the audience is entitled to the internal sentence or only a narrower one
- whether named-cohort language is still too strong for this audience
- whether off-world memory ceiling or other blockers require explicit qualification
- whether the audience may receive only a historical or superseded rendering

### 4) Stale-claim recall review

The page must compute and show:

- prior stronger artifacts still visible on any surface
- whether each artifact is recalled, bannered, watermarked, or still dangerously live
- whether exports must be frozen until stale artifacts are marked
- whether a downgrade requires outbound correction to named recipients

## Required outcomes

The page must support outcomes such as:

- `surface may render the named-cohort sentence with mandatory ceiling qualifier`
- `detail page allowed; notification too narrow for the same sentence`
- `export blocked until supersession banner is attached to prior receipt`
- `historical display allowed only with watermark and current-receipt pointer`
- `public surface limited to minimal sentence; stronger internal sentence remains blocked`

## Mandatory review discipline

The review must reject any path that tries to treat these as equivalent:

- current internal truth and audience-safe statement
- detail-page nuance and badge-level shorthand
- past export exists and past export remains authoritative
- named-recipient truth and global truth
- current receipt stronger and older surface automatically harmless

## Required warnings

The page must render plain warnings when:

- a compressed surface cannot carry required qualifiers
- a stale stronger artifact is still copyable or discoverable
- a UI-only label risks being mistaken for canonical case identity
- an outward relay path exists without a matching publication receipt
- a public surface is about to quote language that is only safe for named operators
