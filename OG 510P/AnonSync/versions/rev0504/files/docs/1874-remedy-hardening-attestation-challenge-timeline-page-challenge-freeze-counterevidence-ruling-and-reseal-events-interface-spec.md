# Remedy-hardening-attestation-challenge timeline page — challenge, freeze, counterevidence, ruling, and reseal events

## Purpose

This page is the chronological spine for what happened after the strongest attestation sentence was challenged.
It exists so a later reader can see whether the case was merely noisy, formally frozen, provisionally narrowed, adjudicated, reworked, resealed, appealed, or reopened.

## Event classes

The timeline must record at least these events:

- challenged sentence issued
- challenge opened
- stronger sentence frozen
- weaker sentence published
- decisive witness requested
- restart-gated capture started
- reconnect or re-add performed
- counterevidence attached
- contradiction cleared or worsened
- provisional ruling entered
- rework opened
- reseal completed
- stronger sentence restored
- challenge upheld and narrowed sentence published
- appeal opened
- later evidence reopened the ruling

## Timeline behavior

The page must keep these truths separate:

- objection arrival versus formal challenge opening
- evidence requested versus evidence returned
- workaround applied versus ruling earned
- ruling earned versus reseal completed
- restored sentence versus historical pre-challenge sentence
- closed challenge versus reopenable challenge

## Operator questions answered by this page

The timeline must let a later operator answer:

- when did the prior strongest sentence stop being safe to repeat
- what weaker sentence was allowed during the freeze window
- whether decisive evidence came from the same world or a successor world
- whether re-add or rebuild changed the lineage mid-review
- when the restored or narrowed sentence actually became speakable again
