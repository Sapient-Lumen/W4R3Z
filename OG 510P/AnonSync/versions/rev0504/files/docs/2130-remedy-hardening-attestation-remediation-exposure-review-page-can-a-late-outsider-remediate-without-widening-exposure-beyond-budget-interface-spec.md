# Remedy-hardening-attestation remediation-exposure review page — can a late outsider remediate without widening exposure beyond budget?

## Purpose

This page is the review surface for forcing the operator to confront whether outsider self-service remediation is also bounded and exposure-honest.
It exists to prevent the archive from settling for `they can fix themselves from here` when the harder question is `what does that path leak, widen, or leave behind?`

## Review question

The review must force a direct answer to:

**if a late outsider uses the corrected replacement to remediate stale reliance, does the path stay within the reviewed audience, authority, and retention budget, or does it create broader exposure?**

## Required review prompts

The page must ask at least:

- what carrier does the outsider use to remediate?
- who else could use that carrier if it is forwarded?
- does the carrier expire, click out, or remain indefinitely reusable?
- can the outsider reshare the delivered material or widen permissions further?
- what copies remain after disconnect, UI removal, expiration, or revocation?
- what stronger safe-remedy sentence would be false if rendered now?

## Required comparison panel

The review must keep side-by-side:

- self-service remedy versus bounded self-service remedy
- approval-gated path versus bearer path
- expiring carrier versus durable delivered residue
- read-only delivery versus onward-share or owner-widening potential
- strongest honest safe-remedy sentence versus blocked stronger sentence

## Interaction requirements

The reviewer must be able to:

- click any carrier and see its audience breadth, reuse ceiling, and residue implications separately
- open a resharing drawer that shows whether forwarding copies, keys, or permissions can widen exposure
- downgrade the strongest sentence in one gesture when any leak-budget component goes red
- compare Standard-folder, Advanced-folder, and single-file paths without losing sentence distinctions

## Hard rules

The page must never allow:

- `late outsider can remediate` to render as `late outsider can remediate safely`
- approval posture to hide that a bearer file link remains broadly reusable
- expiration or click limits to hide already delivered copy residue
- one narrow folder path to erase that the file-transfer fallback is uncontrolled
