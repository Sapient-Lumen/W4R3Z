# Remedy-hardening-attestation-finality timeline page — ruling, reliance, appeal lapse, reopen, and supersession events

## Purpose

This page is the chronological spine for what happened after a challenge received a ruling and the product had to decide whether that ruling stayed provisional, became relied upon, expired into history, reopened, or got superseded.
It exists so a later reader can see whether closure was stable, audience-limited, world-limited, or displaced by a later receipt.

## Event classes

The timeline must record at least these events:

- adjudicated ruling issued
- narrower sentence published
- stronger permanence sentence blocked
- reliance audience expanded
- reliance audience restricted
- appeal window opened
- appeal window lapsed
- later contradiction watch armed
- later contradiction attached
- evidence retention horizon crossed
- rotated or lost witness noted
- world-changing repair performed
- successor receipt issued
- receipt superseded
- ruling reopened
- finality class upgraded
- finality class downgraded
- receipt demoted to historical only

## Timeline behavior

The page must keep these truths separate:

- ruling issued versus finality earned
- finality earned versus non-reopenable closure
- reliance granted versus broad reliance granted
- predecessor receipt preserved versus predecessor receipt governing
- appeal expired versus contradiction risk eliminated
- reopened receipt versus superseded receipt

## Operator questions answered by this page

The timeline must let a later operator answer:

- when did the case first become speakable after adjudication
- when did the case become final enough for each reliance audience
- whether a world-changing repair happened before or after reliance was granted
- when a reopen trigger ceased to matter, if ever
- when a successor receipt took precedence over the older one
- when the older receipt stopped being current and became historical-only
