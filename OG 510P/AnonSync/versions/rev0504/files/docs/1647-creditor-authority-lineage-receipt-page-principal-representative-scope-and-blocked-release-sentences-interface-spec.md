# Creditor authority lineage receipt page — principal, representative, scope, and blocked release sentences

## Purpose

This page is the durable receipt for the strongest true authority sentence after review.
It exists so future operators can reopen the case without re-inventing who the principal was, which representative acted, what scope was valid, and which stronger sentences were still blocked.

## Required receipt blocks

### A. Source-case block

- source creditor claim contract sheet
- source creditor representation review
- source settlement-authority proof
- source restoration or debt case if present
- current case posture

### B. Authority-chain block

- principal creditor identity or cohort
- acting representative at the decisive event
- representative class
- authority basis used
- whether any countersigner or absorber was required
- whether a challenge remained open

### C. Scope-and-ceiling block

- exact actions the representative was allowed to take
- exact actions still blocked
- settlement cap or limit if any
- waiver scope if any
- reopen right that survives if any

### D. Freshness-and-revalidation block

- authority evidence relied on
- evidence freshness at decision time
- next rereview or expiry trigger
- whether revocation or role-change monitoring remains required

### E. Consequence block

- sentence honestly earned now
- stronger sentence still blocked now
- whether restoration remains partial because authority was limited
- whether future-burst posture remains tightened because closure authority was incomplete

## Sentences this receipt may support

- `principal confirmed; delegate accepted capped settlement; waiver still blocked`
- `role-based representative valid through review horizon; countersign still required for final release`
- `release signed by valid full-scope representative; reopen preserved only for fraud or new disqualifying evidence`
- `prior release withdrawn because authority lineage failed on revalidation`

## Failure modes the receipt must prevent

- forgetting whether the decisive actor was principal, delegate, role-holder, or absorber
- forgetting whether the actor had full, capped, or no waiver authority
- forgetting that some partial action may remain valid after a stronger sentence is reopened
- treating linked-device continuity as proof of human or policy authority
- allowing future readers to overstate what was actually signed and by whom

## Stronger-sentence guard

The receipt may say `delegate valid for capped settlement only; final waiver remained blocked`.
It may not say `all creditor rights extinguished` unless that stronger sentence was genuinely earned and recorded.
