# Remedy-hardening-attestation successor action-plan proof page — alternative-actuator elimination and proportionality ceiling

## Purpose

This page is the proof-facing object for showing whether the chosen successor-world execution actuator was proportionate to the purpose under the current knowledge set.
It exists so the product can preserve the evidence for `why this lane`, `what narrower lanes were considered`, and `what stronger least-harm sentence remains blocked` instead of collapsing everything into `the action worked`.

## Required proof bundles

Every proof page must preserve these bundles:

### 1. Outcome bundle

- source successor-action-authorization receipt
- named action identifier
- purpose basis
- beneficiary slice identifier
- required outcome summary

### 2. Candidate-actuator bundle

- evidence for each known candidate actuator
- evidence for each candidate's spread set and persistence horizon
- evidence for read-only, write, owner, local-only, one-shot, and rollout differences where applicable
- evidence for manual detours that were available but not chosen

### 3. Narrower-alternative-elimination bundle

- evidence that the narrowest plausible actuator fails or is insufficient
- evidence that the chosen actuator adds capabilities not strictly required
- evidence that emergency exception, if used, actually matched the allowed class
- evidence that convenience rather than necessity did not silently decide the plan

### 4. Side-effect bundle

- evidence for auto-expansion exposure
- evidence for onward-sharing or forwarding risk
- evidence for expiry or use-budget limitations
- evidence for rollback and revocation difficulty

### 5. Ceiling bundle

- highest justified least-harm sentence
- specific missing fact that blocks stronger proportionality standing
- contradictory evidence that would collapse the current plan standing

## Proof outputs

The page must output at least:

- outcome-fit assessment
- candidate-actuator completeness assessment
- narrower-alternative-elimination assessment
- spillover and forwardability assessment
- proportionality ceiling assessment
- blocked stronger least-harm sentence

## Strong proof classes

The page must distinguish at least these classes:

- no reliable action-plan evidence
- action authorized but actuator family unreviewed
- narrower alternatives known but not eliminated
- chosen actuator acceptable only under emergency ceiling
- chosen actuator proportionate for named slice only
- one acceptable narrow option among equals
- stronger least-harm standing not proved
- broader reusable planning rule not proved

## Contradiction triggers

The proof page must visibly downgrade if any of these appear:

- linked-device full spread was chosen even though a manual read-only or single-file lane would have satisfied the need
- folder-level share was chosen when the beneficiary needed only a one-time artifact
- config-mode or broader rollout was chosen for a local or single-peer need
- the chosen lane introduced forwardability, persistence, or auto-expansion that the purpose did not require
- a supposedly temporary actuator was actually non-expiring or effectively durable without review

## Hard rules

The proof page must never let:

- authorization impersonate proportionality
- execution success impersonate least-harm proof
- convenience defaults impersonate necessity
- the absence of complaint impersonate narrowness
- one proportionate plan impersonate a general rule that the same actuator is always acceptable
