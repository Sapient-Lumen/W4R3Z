# Remedy-convergence lineage receipt page — cohort convergence, returner safety, and blocked stronger cure sentences

## Purpose

This receipt is the durable compact proof of how an authoritative repair did or did not converge safely across present, returning, and newly admitted cohorts.
It exists so later readers can see whether the product was honest about the gap between `the cure is authoritative now` and `the cure is safely converged for the cohorts that still matter`.

## Receipt body

The receipt must record:

- case identifier
- source remedy-cutover receipt identifier
- current remedy-convergence posture rung
- intended authoritative object summary
- required present, returning, and possible future-joiner cohort summary
- connected full-sync adoption summary
- placeholder-only and disconnected cohort summary
- pending or auto-connect cohort summary
- linked-device auto-availability summary
- offline-writer return-risk summary
- read-only suspended-divergence summary
- quarantine summary
- strongest honest sentence
- strongest blocked stronger sentence
- exact blocker summary

## Receipt sentence families

The receipt must support concise summaries such as:

- `the cure is authoritative for active peers, but offline-return precedence still blocks returner-safe convergence`
- `required present cohorts converged, while pending and placeholder-only cohorts keep the stronger future-joiner-safe sentence blocked`
- `linked-device auto-availability widened the possible cohort, so quarantine remained active even after present-cohort convergence`
- `a late-arriving peer collapsed the stronger convergence sentence and forced re-review`
- `present visible calm held, but read-only suspended divergence kept the stronger convergence claim blocked`

## Invariants

- the receipt never compresses cutover, present-cohort convergence, returner-safe convergence, and future-joiner-safe convergence into one word
- the receipt never hides unconverged cohorts behind active-roster calm
- the receipt never lets approval memory or linked-device expansion disappear from the lineage
- the receipt always preserves the blocker that prevented the stronger convergence sentence
