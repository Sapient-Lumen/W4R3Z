# Burst authorization review page — sanctioned overdraw, reserve borrow, throttle-back, and denial routes

## Purpose

This page is the operator review for deciding what to do when an active claimant wants or needs room beyond the ordinary awarded envelope.
It must answer:

> do we deny the extra draw, authorize a narrow temporary burst, authorize reserve borrow with stronger guardrails, throttle others to create lawful room, or reject the request and force reclaim or reopened contention instead?

## Review inputs

- contention verdict
- activation and occupancy facts
- allocation envelope contract sheet
- burst borrow contract sheet
- urgency and mission reason
- harmed-claimant and reserve impact
- current promise-capacity and reservation consequences
- expiration and payback feasibility

## Required decision routes

### Route 1 — deny exception and hold ordinary envelope

Allowed when:

- no adequate urgent basis exists
- harmed-claimant or reserve impact would exceed policy
- ordinary corrective throttle is sufficient
- payback cannot be specified honestly

### Route 2 — authorize narrow temporary burst within shared headroom

Allowed only when:

- extra room is real and not protected reserve
- harmed claimants remain within policy
- expiry is short and explicit
- payback can be completed without later folklore

### Route 3 — authorize protected-reserve borrow

Allowed only with:

- typed emergency or recovery basis
- explicit borrowed amount
- explicit harmed-claimant sentence
- explicit payback route
- stricter review cadence than ordinary exceptions

### Route 4 — create lawful room by throttling or narrowing others first

Allowed when:

- the need is real
- the current overdraw can be avoided by an explicit re-shape rather than exception-normalizing a breach
- harmed claimants can be compensated or preserved through the reshaping verdict

### Route 5 — deny burst and reopen contention or reclaim

Allowed when:

- the need is recurrent rather than exceptional
- the requested extra room is too large for temporary treatment
- protected reserve would be hollowed out
- the claimant is already operating with unpaid burst debt

## Required outputs

- chosen exception verdict
- borrowed amount if any
- source of borrowed room
- expiry and renewal rule
- payback class and due time
- strongest surviving sentence for harmed claimants
- trigger for denial, withdrawal, reclaim, or reopened contention

## Required comparisons

The page must keep these comparisons explicit:

- `ordinary overdraw` vs `typed temporary exception`
- `shared headroom borrow` vs `protected-reserve borrow`
- `authorize burst` vs `reshape ordinary room first`
- `renew once` vs `normalize into new entitlement`
- `payback open` vs `payback complete`

## Failure modes the page must prevent

- treating any urgent story as enough to erase reserve doctrine
- authorizing extra room without naming what is owed afterward
- renewing again and again until exception becomes stealth entitlement
- hiding harmed-claimant consequence because the urgent claimant still looks productive

## Stronger-sentence guard

The review may say `temporary burst authorized under explicit exception`.
It may not say `ordinary entitlement expanded` unless a later typed re-award or baseline change supports that stronger sentence.
