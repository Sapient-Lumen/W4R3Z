# Remedy-convergence review page — did the authoritative repair actually converge across present, returning, and newly admitted cohorts?

## Decision question

This page answers one operational question:

**Did the authoritative repair actually converge for the required present cohort and stay safe against returning offline peers or late auto-joining participants — or are we still relying on placeholders, pending states, disconnected folders, approval memory, or offline-return precedence to keep the cure alive?**

## Review sections

### 1) Cutover versus convergence

The review must begin by comparing the already-proven cutover result with the stronger convergence claim actually available now.
It must show:

- what became authoritative
- which present cohorts truly adopted it
- which returning or late-joiner cohorts still matter
- what stronger convergence sentence is still blocked

### 2) Present-cohort adoption audit

The page must separate:

- connected full-sync peers
- selective-sync peers
- placeholder-only cohorts
- disconnected folders
- pending folders
- linked-device auto-available folders not yet reviewed as active participants

### 3) Returner-risk audit

The page must show what can still reintroduce old or competing state, including:

- offline writer return precedence
- pending peer auto-connect after remembered approval
- linked-device automatic folder spread
- read-only suspended local divergence
- previously disconnected cohorts reconnecting later
- delayed byte fetch for placeholder-only cohorts

### 4) Quarantine and admission scope

The page must model which cohorts are intentionally inside or outside the stronger convergence claim.
It must separate:

- named required present cohort
- named required returning cohort
- possible future joiners
- quarantined late joiners
- auto-approved but not yet reviewed participants
- Owner-linked identity expansion risk

### 5) Highest honest sentence

The page must always conclude with one highest honest sentence from this family:

- `cutover active pending convergence review`
- `connected full-sync convergence only`
- `named required present-cohort convergence`
- `convergence blocked by placeholder-only cohort`
- `convergence blocked by disconnected or pending cohort`
- `convergence blocked by offline-writer return risk`
- `convergence blocked by read-only suspended divergence`
- `returner-safe convergence`
- `future-joiner-safe convergence`
- `convergence collapsed by late arrival`
- `convergence verification collapsed`

It must also name the blocked stronger sentence and why it remains blocked.

## Review invariants

- the review never lets authoritative cutover impersonate convergence
- the review never lets folder visibility impersonate byte adoption
- the review never lets remembered approval impersonate fresh inclusion in this convergence sentence
- the review always names the exact blocker that prevents the stronger convergence sentence
