# Remedy-hardening-attestation successor-world inheritance review page — can this rebuilt or re-founded world legitimately carry the old world's standing forward?

## Purpose

This page is the operator-facing review that answers the practical inheritance question after catastrophe recovery is already understood: given that the successor world exists, which predecessor-world truths may honestly carry forward into it, and which must remain predecessor-bound or be explicitly reissued?

## Primary review prompts

The review must answer these prompts in order:

1. **Which catastrophe-recovery receipt is the starting basis for this inheritance review?**
2. **Is the successor world same-world recovery, partial-continuity successor, or explicit re-foundation?**
3. **Which predecessor artifacts are under review for carry-forward: receipts, obligations, policies, waivers, trust handles, approvals, or precedent?**
4. **What bridge authority or rulebook permits any inheritance here?**
5. **Which items are inherited, which are reissued, and which remain permanently predecessor-bound?**
6. **What is the strongest successor-inheritance sentence the product may honestly publish now?**

## Review sections

### 1. Predecessor-to-successor basis board

Show:

- source catastrophe-recovery receipt
- predecessor world identifier
- successor world identifier
- strongest honest continuity sentence before inheritance review

### 2. Carry-forward family board

Show:

- bytes and content carry-forward
- policy and precedent carry-forward
- obligation and remediation carry-forward
- waiver and exception carry-forward
- approval, trust-handle, and identity carry-forward
- receipt inheritance versus receipt reissue

### 3. Bridge-authority board

Show:

- governing rulebook
- adjudicator or ratifier identity
- whether inheritance is automatic, conditional, or prohibited
- whether carry-forward is slice-limited or family-limited

### 4. Blocker board

Show:

- regenerated identity blocker
- new synchronization-instance blocker
- storage-world fork blocker
- architectural-replacement blocker
- missing predecessor anchor blocker
- trust-handle or approval-memory blocker

### 5. Inheritance sentence chooser

The review must output one and only one primary sentence class such as:

- successor world exists, inheritance unreviewed
- bytes carry forward only, governance unknown
- policy and precedent inherited for named slice only
- remediation and notification obligations inherited for named slice only
- predecessor receipt cannot be inherited and must be reissued
- trust-handle inheritance blocked, successor approval required
- named carry-forward budget ratified for named slice only
- same-world continuity still blocked despite bounded inheritance

## Hard rules

The review must never let an operator hide:

- reissue behind `inherit`
- successor ratification behind `automatic carry-forward`
- regenerated identity behind `same trust`
- new-instance reconnection behind `old receipt still governs`
- policy similarity behind `all predecessor exceptions still apply`
