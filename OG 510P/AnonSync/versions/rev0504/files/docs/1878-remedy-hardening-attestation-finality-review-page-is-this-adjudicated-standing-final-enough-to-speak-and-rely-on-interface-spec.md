# Remedy-hardening-attestation-finality review page — is this adjudicated standing final enough to speak and rely on?

## Review question

Can the product honestly present this adjudicated standing as merely ruled, presently relied upon, final on the current record, superseded, reopened, or non-reopenable for the named claim floor?

## Review panels

### 1) Ruling carry-forward review

Show the exact sentence that survived challenge.
The review must preserve:

- the exact adjudicated surviving sentence
- the narrower sentence, if any, that was actually ruled
- the stronger sentence still blocked
- the receipt or proof that currently owns that ruling
- whether this ruling belongs to the same world or only to a successor world

### 2) Finality-basis review

Ask what actually makes the ruling durable enough to rely on.
The review must classify the basis at least as:

- only presently uncontradicted
- adjudicated under explicit burden and witness priority
- stabilized after reopen window expiry
- durable because decisive evidence is preserved and replayable
- lane-limited because only named consumers are safe to rely on it
- weakened because key evidence will naturally decay or has already decayed

### 3) Reopen-trigger review

Force the operator to review every live reason the ruling might not stay closed:

- active appeal window
- evidence rotation or short retention horizon
- world-changing repair performed after ruling
- clone or identity ambiguity discovered later
- higher-priority witness lane not yet checked
- stale-presentation risk
- future contradiction watcher still armed

False finalizations to reject include:

- treating calm UI as final when the decisive evidence is short-lived or presentation-sensitive
- treating a fresh instance rebuild as proof that the predecessor-world receipt was always final
- treating current lack of warnings as non-reopenable closure
- treating support capture as durable finality when the support artifact itself may expire or be incomplete

### 4) Supersession review

The review must make precedence explicit:

- does this receipt still own precedence
- has a later receipt narrowed or replaced it
- does the later receipt supersede only live reliance, or also historical interpretation
- are both receipts valid but for different worlds, lanes, or consumer classes

### 5) Reliance-audience review

Ask who may safely consume the sentence now:

- internal investigator
- named operational automation
- named downstream reviewer class
- broad external reader
- historical reader only

The review must reject any audience expansion that outruns the ruling's finality class.

### 6) Finality ruling review

The page must land on explicit ruling branches such as:

- adjudicated but reopenable
- final on current record
- final for named consumers only
- superseded by successor receipt
- historical only, not current
- reopened after later contradiction
- non-reopenable at the narrowed floor only

## Output states

The page must be able to land on at least these outputs:

- ruled but not final
- relied on with explicit audience limits
- final for current record, reopen triggers expired
- superseded without erasure
- reopened and downgraded
- permanently blocked from stronger permanence language
