# Attainment proof page — what actually landed where, and why stronger completion sentences stay blocked

## Purpose

This page is the durable proof object for the strongest honest attainment sentence earned by a given act version.
It exists so later operators can see not merely that execution occurred, but what outcome actually landed, for which cohort, under what verification basis, and why any stronger completion sentence stayed blocked.

## Sentences this page may support

- `the effect committed locally and propagated to all connected recipients, but required-cohort completion remains open while two named recipients stay offline`
- `the reversible effect is verified for the required cohort, but one ghost announcement remains preserved as non-blocking residue`
- `the irreversible effect executed correctly, yet stronger full-attainment language stayed blocked because watcher exhaustion and manual rescan dependence left the verification basis too weak`
- `the connected mesh looked calm, but later reconnect reopened the case and downgraded the earlier completion sentence`
- `the required cohort was eventually attained after rescan and source-peer return, and the earlier partial sentence was superseded rather than silently erased`

## Mandatory proof blocks

### A. Act-and-outcome block

- source act identifier
- source version identifier
- exact effect lane measured
- highest attainment sentence honestly earned
- strongest blocked stronger sentence
- exact reason the earned sentence stops where it does

### B. Coverage block

- required cohort definition
- covered cohort proven so far
- uncovered or contested cohort elements
- whether coverage evidence comes from local state, peer receipts, required-recipient confirmation, adjudicated override, or another named basis
- strongest blocked stronger sentence if coverage proof remains incomplete

### C. Residue block

- ghost / no-source residue identifiers
- partial-download or temp-file residue identifiers
- hidden-task or rescan dependence identifiers
- file-system or merge-failure identifiers
- whether residue is blocking, tolerated, or separately quarantined
- exact reason the product accepted or rejected stronger completion language in the presence of residue

### D. Verification block

- verification events bundle identifiers
- rescan / reconnect / touch / retry identifiers
- whether connected-peer calm was used and whether that is sufficient for this row
- whether durable verification has been earned
- whether later reopen remains possible and on what grounds

### E. Successor-event block

- next event that could strengthen the proof
- next event that could weaken the proof
- whether compensation or narrower fallback remains active
- whether the attainment verdict is safe for publication, reliance, or irreversible closure

## Required badges

- `local-commit-proven`
- `propagation-proven`
- `connected-cohort-only`
- `required-cohort-proven`
- `verification-basis-weak`
- `verification-basis-strong`
- `ghost-residue-blocking`
- `ghost-residue-nonblocking`
- `attainment-under-reopen-risk`
- `stronger-completion-sentence-blocked`

Badges must stack instead of collapsing meaning.
For example, `local-commit-proven`, `propagation-proven`, and `verification-basis-weak` may coexist with `stronger-completion-sentence-blocked` when a valid execution still has not earned durable required-cohort completion.

## Proof obligations

- prove execution separately from attainment
- prove connected-cohort calm separately from required-cohort completion
- prove residue absence separately from warning absence
- preserve whether hidden tasks or rescans were still relevant when the sentence was issued
- preserve why the product refused a stronger `fully completed` sentence
- preserve how later reconnect, rescan, or source loss could still reopen the verdict

## Stronger-sentence guard

This page may say `the irreversible effect executed under valid mandate, landed locally, propagated to all currently connected recipients, and remains short of verified required-cohort completion because one named required recipient is offline and one announced object is now no-source residue under manual review`.
It may not say `the effect is completely and durably attained for the whole required cohort` unless that stronger sentence was actually earned.
