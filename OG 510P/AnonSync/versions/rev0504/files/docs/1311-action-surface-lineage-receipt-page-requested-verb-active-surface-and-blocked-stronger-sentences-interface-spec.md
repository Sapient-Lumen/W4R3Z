## Action-surface lineage receipt

### Receipt purpose
Preserve one durable ordinary-language record for any serious action-locality decision.

### Required fields
- **Subject handle**
- **Requested verb family**
- **Current surface at action time**
- **Execution surface verdict**
- **Witness surface verdict**
- **Recovery surface verdict**
- **Runtime / platform basis**
- **Blocking basis**
- **Fallback class**
- **Semantic loss boundary**
- **Evidence basis**
- **Safer alternative offered**
- **Blocked stronger sentence**

### Example receipt sentence
`Archive restore for this seat was witnessable in the current WebUI-linked runtime but not executable there; the strongest honest recovery required file-browser action outside the product surface, so same-surface recovery remained unproved.`

### Product consequence
A later operator should never have to rediscover whether an apparently missing action was truly unsupported, merely on another surface, only recoverable out of band, or weakened by runtime/platform posture.
