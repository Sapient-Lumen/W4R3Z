# Remedy-hardening-attestation successor beneficiary-continuity proof page — enrollment lane, detachment, and continuity-sentence ceiling

## Purpose

This page is the durable proof artifact that preserves what copy the beneficiary held, what canonical lane it was supposed to follow, whether it stayed subscribed to future corrections, and what stronger continuity sentence therefore stayed blocked.

## Minimum proof bundle

The proof page must preserve at least:

- source beneficiary-custody receipt identifier
- named beneficiary identifier
- intended continuity class
- canonical successor source or update lane identifier
- current enrollment evidence
- detached-snapshot versus live-subscription evidence
- supersession and withdrawal reach evidence
- local divergence or stop-updating evidence
- reconnect, path-rebind, or topology-fragility evidence
- onward-forwarding or detached-fork evidence if any
- strongest safe continuity sentence at proof time
- strongest blocked stronger continuity sentence at proof time

## Proof sections

### 1. Claimed continuity summary

Show:

- what continuity class the beneficiary was supposed to end up with
- what canonical source or lane would have justified that claim
- what withdrawal and supersession reach was assumed
- what evidence would have justified upgrade

### 2. Observed continuity trace

For each observed continuity-shaping component show:

- event time
- source actor or process
- source world
- touched subject
- before state
- after state
- observer quality
- whether this widened or narrowed continuity confidence

### 3. Detachment and hazard ledger

For each hazard family show:

- why it matters
- whether it stayed hypothetical or became evidenced
- whether it is absolute, conditional, or operator-induced
- whether it alone blocks the stronger continuity sentence

### 4. Continuity ceiling statement

The page must end with a bounded statement such as:

- `beneficiary holds durable copy, but future-continuity sentence still blocked`
- `beneficiary has only a detached snapshot; canonical update-lane sentence blocked`
- `beneficiary can rejoin later through reconnect, but live continuity is not proved now`
- `beneficiary remains enrolled in the canonical future-update lane for named slice only; broader continuity sentence blocked`
- `later disconnect, divergence, or contradiction narrowed prior continuity confidence`

## Evidence grading

The proof page must support at least these grades:

- beneficiary custody proved, continuity still unproven
- detached snapshot only
- reconnectable but not live now
- live lane with fragility or divergence risk
- canonical future-update lane for named slice only
- later contradiction narrowed prior continuity confidence
