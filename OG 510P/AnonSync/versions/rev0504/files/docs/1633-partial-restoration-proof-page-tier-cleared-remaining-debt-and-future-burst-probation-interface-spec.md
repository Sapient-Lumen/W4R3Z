# Partial restoration proof page — tier cleared, remaining debt, and future-burst probation

## Purpose

This page is the durable proof object for the strongest true sentence available while a restoration waterfall is still in progress.
It exists so the archive can say exactly which creditor tiers are already repaired, which residue remains, and whether future bursts are blocked, manual-only, or narrowly allowed during probation.

## Sentences this page may support

- `reserve restored; named harmed claimants still partially open`
- `tier-2 pro-rata relief applied; cohort residue still open`
- `all creditors cleared, but future bursts remain manual-only during recurrence probation`
- `residue waived by authority; claimant not cleanly restored`
- `clean restoration released after tier clearance and probation lift`

## Mandatory proof blocks

### A. Waterfall-progress basis

- source restoration waterfall
- active tier just before this proof
- tier or creditors cleared by this step
- budget slice used
- residue remaining after the step
- evidence quality for the slice

### B. Cleared-vs-open creditor block

- cleared creditor tiers
- open creditor tiers
- named creditors still open if any
- reserve-floor witness
- harmed-neighbor-relief witness
- cohort-relief witness if applicable

### C. Probation block

- future-burst posture now
- probation duration served
- probation duration remaining
- recurrence-risk witness
- event that would tighten probation again
- event required to lift probation fully

### D. Strongest surviving sentence block

- strongest true sentence for claimant now
- strongest true sentence for harmed neighbors now
- strongest blocked cleaner sentence
- exact fact still needed to upgrade the sentence
- exact fact that would downgrade the sentence again

## Required badges

- `tier-1-cleared`
- `tier-2-open`
- `tier-3-open`
- `partial-relief-confirmed`
- `reserve-restored`
- `named-neighbor-relief-open`
- `cohort-relief-open`
- `probation-active`
- `future-burst-blocked`
- `future-burst-manual-only`
- `clean-restoration-released`

Badges must stack rather than erase lineage.
For example, `reserve-restored` may coexist with `named-neighbor-relief-open` and `probation-active`.

## Proof obligations

- prove tier clearance separately from lower live activity
- prove named-neighbor relief separately from reserve restoration
- preserve residue even when some tiers are already clean
- preserve probation even when all monetary or room debt is technically cleared but recurrence policy still binds
- preserve waiver consequences instead of letting them vanish after the open tier disappears

## Stronger-sentence guard

This page may say `higher-priority creditor tiers cleared; lower-tier residue still open under probation`.
It may not say `clean restoration released` until every required tier is cleared and any surviving probation lift condition is actually met.
