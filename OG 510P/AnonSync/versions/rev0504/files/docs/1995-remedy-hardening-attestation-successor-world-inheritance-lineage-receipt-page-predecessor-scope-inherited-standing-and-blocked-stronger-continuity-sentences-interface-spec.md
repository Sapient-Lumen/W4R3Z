# Remedy-hardening-attestation successor-world inheritance lineage receipt page — predecessor scope, inherited standing, and blocked stronger continuity sentences

## Purpose

This page is the durable receipt for the strongest honest successor-world inheritance sentence after reviewing what a rebuilt or re-founded world may legitimately carry forward from its predecessor.
It is the page a later operator opens when they need to know what crossed the boundary, what had to be reissued, which duties still bind, and which stronger continuity sentence remained blocked.

## Required receipt fields

The receipt must preserve:

- successor-inheritance identifier
- source catastrophe-recovery receipt identifier
- predecessor world identifier
- successor world identifier
- named governed slice
- current successor-inheritance class
- inherited-bytes status
- inherited-policy and precedent status
- inherited-obligation and remediation status
- inherited-waiver or exception status
- inherited-approval-memory status
- inherited-trust-handle status
- receipt inheritance versus receipt reissue status
- bridge-authority source
- rulebook version
- highest honest successor-inheritance sentence
- strongest blocked stronger sentence
- exact blocker summary
- next evidence needed for upgrade
- next evidence that would force downgrade
- issuance timestamp
- issuer identity

## Canonical sentence families

The receipt must support at least these sentence families:

- the successor world exists, but inheritance has not yet been reviewed
- bytes from the predecessor carry forward for the named slice, but governance continuity remains unproven
- policy or precedent carries forward for the named slice, while trust handles and approval memory remain blocked
- predecessor obligations to remediate, notify, or revoke carry forward for the named slice only
- the predecessor receipt cannot be inherited and has been replaced by a successor receipt
- bounded carry-forward is ratified for the named slice only
- same-world continuity remains blocked even though a successor inheritance bridge now exists
- broader full inheritance or universal continuity is blocked

## Hard rules

- The receipt must never collapse `successor works` into `predecessor standing inherited`.
- The receipt must never collapse `reissued` into `inherited`.
- The receipt must never collapse `same bytes` into `same trust`.
- The receipt must never collapse `same policy intent` into `same waiver or exception state`.
- The receipt must never collapse `bounded carry-forward` into `same-world continuity restored`.
