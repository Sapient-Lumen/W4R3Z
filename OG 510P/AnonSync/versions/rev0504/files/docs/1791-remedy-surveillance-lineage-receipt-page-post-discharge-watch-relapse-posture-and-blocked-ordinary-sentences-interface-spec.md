# Remedy-surveillance lineage receipt page — post-discharge watch, relapse posture, and blocked stronger ordinary-life sentences

## Purpose

This receipt is the durable compact proof of how a discharged case did or did not remain honestly guarded while back in ordinary life.
It exists so later readers can see whether the product was honest about the gap between `quarantine released` and `ordinary life still safely guarded against relapse`.

## Receipt body

The receipt must record:

- case identifier
- source remedy-discharge receipt identifier
- current remedy-surveillance posture rung
- intended authoritative object summary
- current ordinary-lane scope summary
- required surveillance cohort summary
- actually armed surveillance cohort summary
- platform blind-spot summary
- runtime blind-spot summary
- current signal-family coverage summary
- notification and history basis summary
- escalation and re-fence threshold summary
- strongest honest sentence
- strongest blocked stronger sentence
- exact blocker summary

## Receipt sentence families

The receipt must support concise summaries such as:

- `the case was discharged, but surveillance remained only partially armed, so stronger ordinary-life claims stayed blocked`
- `platform blind spots kept guarded continuation weaker than full relapse-detectable discharge`
- `a relapse signal was observed and crossed the re-fence threshold, triggering automatic reclose`
- `surveillance collapsed before broader harm resumed, so discharge could no longer remain the highest honest sentence`
- `ordinary life continued only while the named surveillance cohort, signal families, and threshold authority all remained armed`

## Invariants

- the receipt never compresses discharge, guarded continuation, relapse observation, threshold satisfaction, and re-fence into one word
- the receipt never hides coverage blind spots behind one quiet notification surface
- the receipt never lets synchronized notifications or short operational history impersonate case-scoped guarded discharge
- the receipt always preserves the blocker that prevented the stronger surveillance sentence
