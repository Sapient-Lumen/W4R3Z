# Remedy-hardening-attestation successor action-plan review page — is this the narrowest legitimate way to achieve the intended successor-world outcome?

## Purpose

This page is the human-facing decision review for whether the chosen successor-world execution plan is proportionate to the purpose and narrower alternatives have been honestly considered.
It exists so the product can separate `authorized`, `convenient`, `effective`, `least-broad enough`, and `still overreaching`.

## Mandatory review questions

Every review must answer these questions in order:

1. **What exact outcome is required?**
2. **Who actually needs to benefit or receive the effect?**
3. **Does the outcome require ongoing synchronization, or only one-time transfer or temporary visibility?**
4. **Which narrower actuators are known right now?**
5. **Why do those narrower actuators fail, if they do fail?**
6. **What broader side-effects does the chosen actuator introduce?**
7. **Is the broader choice necessary, emergency-tolerated, or merely convenient?**
8. **What stronger least-harm sentence must stay blocked even if execution succeeds?**

## Required review panels

### 1. Outcome-need panel

Show:

- the minimum thing the beneficiary needs
- whether the need is read-only, write, owner, local-only, or one-shot
- whether ongoing presence is actually required
- whether future arrivals or future devices must inherit access

### 2. Alternative comparison panel

Show side-by-side:

- chosen actuator
- narrowest plausible actuator
- blast-radius difference
- persistence difference
- forwarding and auto-expansion difference
- operator justification for preferring the broader lane

### 3. Proportionality verdict panel

Show:

- whether the chosen actuator is necessary, acceptable, excessive, or unresolved
- whether the emergency rationale changes the ceiling temporarily only
- whether the plan is proportionate for the named slice only
- whether a narrower follow-up cutover is mandatory

### 4. Sentence chooser

The review must output one and only one primary sentence class such as:

- authorization exists, proportionality unreviewed
- one-time transfer sufficient, persistent sync blocked
- read-only actuator sufficient, broader write lane blocked
- local-only route sufficient, remote spread blocked
- broader actuator justified by named necessity only
- emergency broad actuator accepted pending rapid narrowing
- chosen actuator still broader than purpose requires
- stronger least-harm sentence blocked

## Hard rules

The review must never let:

- successful authorization impersonate least-harm review
- `all linked devices` impersonate `the named beneficiary set`
- a read-write or Owner lane impersonate a read-only need
- persistent share impersonate a one-shot transfer need
- the absence of immediate contradiction impersonate proof that no safer alternative existed
