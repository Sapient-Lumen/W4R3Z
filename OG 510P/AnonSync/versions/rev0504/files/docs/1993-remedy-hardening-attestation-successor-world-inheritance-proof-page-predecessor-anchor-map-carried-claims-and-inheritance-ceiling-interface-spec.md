# Remedy-hardening-attestation successor-world inheritance proof page — predecessor anchor map, carried claims, and inheritance ceiling

## Purpose

This page is the evidence-heavy proof surface for determining exactly what a successor world may legitimately inherit from its predecessor after a break, catastrophe, or forced re-foundation.
It proves the ceiling on any claim that tries to say the successor is not merely usable, but entitled to carry forward named predecessor-world standing.

## Required evidence blocks

### 1. Predecessor-world ledger

Preserve:

- predecessor world identifier
- predecessor continuity class at moment of break
- predecessor receipts and standing under review
- predecessor obligations, waivers, approvals, and trust handles under review

### 2. Successor-world ledger

Preserve:

- successor world identifier
- successor creation path
- whether successor came from same-world recovery, partial carry-forward, or explicit re-foundation
- successor authority basis

### 3. Bridge-authority ledger

Preserve:

- rulebook that governs carry-forward
- ratifier or adjudicator identity
- family-specific inheritance permissions
- explicit prohibitions on automatic carry-forward

### 4. Carry-forward ledger

Preserve:

- bytes inherited
- policy or precedent inherited
- obligations inherited
- remediation state inherited
- waivers or exceptions inherited
- approvals, trust handles, and identity-derived permissions inherited or blocked
- receipt inherited, superseded, or reissued

### 5. Contradiction and downgrade ledger

Preserve:

- regenerated-identity exposure
- new-instance exposure
- storage-root fork exposure
- architectural replacement exposure
- missing-anchor exposure
- contradictory inheritance witnesses
- resulting downgrade class

### 6. Claim ceiling

Render:

- highest honest content-carry-forward sentence
- highest honest governance-carry-forward sentence
- highest honest trust-carry-forward sentence
- highest honest receipt-inheritance sentence
- blocked stronger sentence and exact blocker

## Evidence classes

The page must distinguish at least:

- successor exists only
- bytes carry-forward only
- policy inheritance only
- obligation inheritance only
- receipt must be reissued
- trust inheritance blocked
- bounded carry-forward ratified for named slice only
- same-world continuity still blocked
- broader full inheritance blocked

## Hard rules

The proof page must never treat:

- reconnect success as proof of inherited standing
- matching folder names as proof of shared lineage
- migrated settings as proof every receipt still governs
- same data bytes as proof of same approvals or trust handles
- one inherited family as proof that all predecessor standing carried forward
