# Remedy-surveillance review page — is this discharged case still being watched strongly enough to stay in ordinary life?

## Decision question

This page answers one operational question:

**After discharge, is this case still under strong enough watch to remain honestly in ordinary life, or do blind spots, weak delivery, missing signal coverage, or an unmet re-fence threshold mean the case is only weakly calm?**

## Review sections

### 1) Discharge versus guarded continuation

The review must begin by comparing the already-proven discharge result with the stronger guarded-continuation claim actually available now.
It must show:

- what discharge sentence was previously earned
- what surveillance family is now required
- what stronger guarded ordinary-life sentence is still blocked
- what would automatically reclose the case

### 2) Coverage audit

The page must separate:

- named surveillance cohort
- required surveillance cohort
- desktop coverage
- Linux coverage
- mobile coverage
- linked-device visibility versus named-case watch
- uncovered participant or platform classes

### 3) Signal-family audit

The page must show which relapse families are actually covered, including:

- permission widening or resharing
- pending or rejoining participant arrival
- warning-family activation
- runtime degradation
- no-source or stalled repair residue returning
- clock or database integrity drift
- re-fence threshold triggers and escalation authority

### 4) Delivery and evidence posture

The page must model which evidence lanes are actually live.
It must separate:

- bell or in-app signal visibility
- desktop notifications enabled or disabled
- Android notification and background-priority posture
- Linux outside-UI notification absence
- history window strength
- retained proof outside short operational history

### 5) Highest honest sentence

The page must always conclude with one highest honest sentence from this family:

- `discharge released pending surveillance arming`
- `surveillance armed for named cohort only`
- `surveillance armed with platform blind spots`
- `surveillance armed with runtime blind spots`
- `surveillance armed for required cohort`
- `ordinary-lane continuation guarded`
- `relapse signal observed pending threshold decision`
- `relapse threshold met pending re-fence`
- `automatic re-fence fired`
- `manual re-fence required`
- `surveillance collapsed`
- `discharge collapsed by relapse`

It must also name the blocked stronger sentence and why it remains blocked.

## Review invariants

- the review never lets discharge impersonate guarded continuation
- the review never lets one visible signal impersonate required-cohort watch
- the review never lets notification delivery impersonate covered relapse detection
- the review always names the exact blind spot or threshold reason that blocks the stronger sentence
