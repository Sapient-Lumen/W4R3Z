# Restoration waterfall lineage receipt page — creditor order, partial relief, and blocked clean-restoration sentences

## Purpose

This page is the durable receipt for the full lineage of staged restoration after burst debt.
A later operator should be able to open one page and answer:

> what creditor order governed this case, which tiers were partially relieved or waived, what probation survived, and what cleaner restoration sentence the product refused to make?

## Required receipt sections

### A. Origin section

- source burst-debt object
- claimant
- total original residue entering the waterfall
- waterfall mode
- authority that fixed the tier order

### B. Creditor-order section

- tier 1 class and outcome
- tier 2 class and outcome
- tier 3 class and outcome
- distribution rule used inside each tier
- any cross-tier waiver or exception

### C. Relief-history section

- total relief slices applied
- last successful slice
- missed slice count
- residue still open or absorbed
- conversion into narrowed future entitlement if used

### D. Final truth section

- strongest true sentence for claimant
- strongest true sentence for harmed neighbors
- probation posture after latest review
- strongest blocked cleaner sentence
- exact fact required for upgrade
- exact fact that would downgrade the sentence again

## Required receipt badges

- `waterfall-opened`
- `tier-order-fixed`
- `partial-relief-applied`
- `tier-cleared`
- `waiver-used`
- `probation-active`
- `future-burst-manual-only`
- `future-burst-blocked`
- `clean-restoration-released`

## Receipt obligations

- preserve creditor order even after later tiers clear
- preserve partial relief as a different truth from final release
- preserve waiver and carry-forward conversion as different truths from ordinary repayment
- preserve probation as a surviving consequence rather than a footnote
- preserve the harmed-neighbor sentence instead of centering only the formerly urgent claimant

## Stronger-sentence guard

The receipt may say `staged restoration completed under released probation` or `residue waived with surviving manual-only future bursts`, depending on truth.
It may not say `the old debt is now irrelevant` unless no residue, no waiver consequence, no narrowed future entitlement, and no probation consequence remain.
