# Resilio remedy-hardening attestation downstream audience stale-dependency retirement and audience-wide reliance truth fragmentation evaluation

## Why this seam matters now

The archive can already say:

- the beneficiary was legitimate
- the later correction was legitimate
- the beneficiary stayed on the canonical future-correction lane
- the beneficiary may have noticed, acknowledged, adopted, and durably adhered to the corrected working state

That is still weaker than a harder question:

**after the beneficiary stayed switched, did the governed downstream audience also stop relying on stale material, or did old exports, delegated peers, local derivatives, forwarded files, caches, and neighboring automations continue to act on stale state anyway?**

A beneficiary-local success claim is not an audience-wide reliance claim.
Audience-wide reliance truth needs an audience boundary, a dependency graph, stale-artifact classes, retirement carriers, and explicit coverage language.
Without those, `the correction took effect` quietly expands into folklore about everyone who mattered being updated.

## Why current Resilio still leaves this too diffuse to clone

Current official Resilio material is candid about several audience-fanout surfaces, but it still spreads them across separate pages:

- `Can Resilio team see and block/remove any Sync folders?` says Resilio neither hosts nor caches the content, cannot control link or folder distribution, and cannot remove data except on users' own devices
- `Sync Private Identity & Linking My Devices` says every folder added on one linked device automatically becomes available on all the other linked devices
- `User Management` says all linked devices under one identity act as Owners, and disconnecting a peer only suspends future updates while already-synchronized files remain in the folder
- `What's the difference between Standard and Advanced folders?` says Standard folders lack the Owner concept and any peer with the key can share the folder further without limitation
- `Sync Share Dialog (Desktop)` says Standard peers can share folders further and RO peers can share RO keys
- `Sharing single file` says one-time transfers can be shared further by recipients, and removing the file from Sync UI does not remove it from the device
- `Sharing a folder locally` says one parent share can be multiplied into several local shares, these do not sync with remote peers directly, and they do not auto-reconnect when the source share later reconnects

This is good operational candor.
It is not yet one first-class answer to **did this named governed audience actually retire stale dependencies, or did old material continue to circulate and drive behavior somewhere downstream?**

## The non-clone line

AnonSync should not clone a contract where all of these are allowed to blur together:

- the beneficiary switched
- linked devices got the folder automatically
- one downstream dependent refreshed
- future access was revoked for one peer
- the file transfer completed once
- the old copy still remains on devices that already received it
- recipients can re-share or forward further
- local derivatives multiplied near the point of use
- the governed audience therefore stopped relying on stale material

Those are separate truths.

## Product decision frozen in this tranche

This revision freezes a stronger line:

- **beneficiary-local durable adherence is weaker than governed-audience stale-dependency retirement**
- **one refreshed dependent is weaker than audience-wide reliance retirement**
- **future-access revocation is weaker than retirement of already-landed stale copies**
- **peer-list state, disconnect state, share permissions, and one green transfer checkmark may never impersonate `the governed audience stopped acting on stale state`**

## What AnonSync should model explicitly instead

AnonSync should add one first-class family for:

- source beneficiary-adherence receipt identifier
- governed audience identifier
- audience boundary rule
- dependency graph summary
- stale-artifact class set
- retirement carrier set
- downstream coverage class
- unresolved stale-dependency set
- strongest honest reliance sentence
- blocked stronger audience-wide sentence

## Interface consequence

That is why this tranche adds five more first-class pages:

- **downstream-audience reliance contract sheet**
- **downstream-audience reliance review**
- **downstream-audience reliance proof**
- **downstream-audience reliance timeline**
- **downstream-audience reliance lineage receipt**
