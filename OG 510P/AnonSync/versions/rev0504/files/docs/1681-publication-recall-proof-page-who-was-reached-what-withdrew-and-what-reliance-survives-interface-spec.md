# Publication-recall proof page — who was reached, what withdrew, and what reliance survives

## Purpose

This page is the durable proof object for the strongest honest recall sentence earned by a previously published act.
It exists so later operators can see not merely that a correction was attempted, but who was actually reached, what reliance was narrowed or withdrawn, what trace still survives, and what stronger clawback claim remained blocked.

## Sentences this page may support

- `all named participants were reached with a semantic correction; future reliance on the earlier sentence is withdrawn for that cohort`
- `public-summary withdrawal issued, but open-web reach remains incomplete and full clawback stays blocked`
- `audit trace preserved while ordinary participant reliance narrowed to the corrected sentence`
- `viewer lost access to current publication, but earlier semantic residue may still survive through prior delivery or export`
- `encrypted host custody survives, but no semantic correction was required because the host never held readable publication rights`

## Mandatory proof blocks

### A. Source-and-recall block

- source publication identifier
- recall class attempted
- exact reason for correction or withdrawal
- highest global recall sentence honestly earned
- strongest blocked stronger sentence

### B. Audience-reach proof block

- audience class or named audience
- whether originally reached
- whether correction / withdrawal later reached this audience
- delivery evidence
- acknowledgement evidence if required
- strongest audience-specific sentence honestly earned

### C. Reliance-aftermath block

- future reliance allowed after recall
- past reliance residue still acknowledged
- whether onward citation is now blocked
- whether old sentence remains audit-visible only
- what stronger extinguishment sentence is blocked

### D. Trace-and-residue block

- archive or history preservation status
- local copy / export / screenshot residue posture
- linked-device mirror residue posture
- encrypted-host residue posture
- exact reason trace must survive or may narrow further later

### E. Audit block

- source act identifiers
- source publication lineage identifiers
- recall approver or authority chain
- reach evidence bundle identifiers
- supersession / dispute / contest records
- next event that could widen or narrow the recall proof

## Required badges

- `correction-live`
- `withdrawal-live`
- `reach-partial`
- `reach-complete`
- `reliance-narrowed`
- `future-use-blocked`
- `audit-visible-only`
- `semantic-residue-survives`
- `export-risk-survives`
- `full-extinguishment-blocked`

Badges must stack instead of collapsing meaning.
For example, `withdrawal-live` may coexist with `reach-partial` and `semantic-residue-survives`, while `audit-visible-only` may coexist with `future-use-blocked`.

## Proof obligations

- prove audience reach separately from merely issuing a correction
- prove narrowed future reliance separately from surviving historic residue
- preserve whether trace stayed visible for audit only
- preserve which audiences still keep stronger clawback blocked
- preserve why the product refused a global erasure sentence

## Stronger-sentence guard

This page may say `the earlier participant notice is withdrawn for future use and the corrected notice reached all named participants, but exported copies remain possible so global extinguishment remains blocked`.
It may not say `the old publication no longer exists in any meaningful sense` unless that stronger sentence was actually earned.
