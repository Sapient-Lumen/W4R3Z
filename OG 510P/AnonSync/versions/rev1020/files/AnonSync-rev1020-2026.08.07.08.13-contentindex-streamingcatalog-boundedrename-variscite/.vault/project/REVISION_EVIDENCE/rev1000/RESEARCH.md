# Rev1000 research and speculation

The implementation deliberately stops short of a persistent global chunk index. The bounded current-visible page gives renamed media a useful local donor path without introducing a new durable database, an unbounded tree index, or a second admission authority.

The next decision should be measurement-driven. On sparse synthetic multi-terabyte trees, measure candidate full-hash latency, page-cache and process RSS, disk read/write amplification, restart behavior, 64 MiB network-window utilization, and direct/Tor/I2P latency. Those results should determine whether AnonSync needs a durable bounded chunk index, a multilevel fingerprint that rejects nonmatches cheaply, or only explicit rename/move identity.

A future persistent index must remain derivative acceleration evidence. Exact immutable payload reopening, chunk SHA-256 and size, final whole-payload SHA-256, and terminal causal admission should remain the authority path.
