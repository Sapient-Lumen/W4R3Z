# rev0984 research notes

SQLite backup completion commits the destination transaction when `sqlite3_backup_step()` returns `SQLITE_DONE`; throwable observation therefore remains forbidden after that terminal result. The immutable receipt is evidence for selecting an admitted restart state, not database-effect authority. The active database cutpoint remains the classifier.

The final pathname reproof is deliberately not a continuous reservation. A noncooperating same-UID or privileged process can replace a candidate, rollback, or receipt name after observation. Rev0984 detects drift at its final cutpoint and reports the nonclaim rather than presenting local pathname atomicity.
