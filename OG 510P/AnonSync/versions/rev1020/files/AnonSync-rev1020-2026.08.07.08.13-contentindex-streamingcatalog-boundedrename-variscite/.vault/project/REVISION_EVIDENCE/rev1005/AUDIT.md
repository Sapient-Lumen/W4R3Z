# Rev1005 audit

Rev1005 removes repeated payload-root enumeration from intermediate terminal SHA-256 continuation while preserving the complete namespace scan as the only final publication authority. The adjacent refactor makes the per-apply terminal-step budget exact when one response completes several ranged files: later completions durably retain exact bytes but defer all SHA continuation until the ordinary receiver-local pulse.

The lexical audits are not semantic proof. Load-bearing evidence is the runtime regression set, GCC and sanitizer product accounting, exact parent verification, binary-aware source reconstruction, active-projection recomputation, and final package/extraction verification.

The remaining product boundary is explicit: 1 GiB is an apply pulse, not a complete multi-terabyte qualification. A 4 TiB target may still require 4,096 payload-cold applies after transfer, first journal admission and final publication still observe the complete store, and source-side target-manifest construction can still read the complete source in one owner call.
