# Rev1010 authority incidents

A stale-validator guard survived from an earlier worktree and had been pointed at
the current GCC, Clang, and registry paths. It terminated legitimate validators.
The guard was disabled, every interrupted result was excluded, and all release
results cited by rev1010 were reproduced afterward from the frozen source.

The physical rev1009 archive was unavailable. Rev1010 uses the exact sealed
rev1008 package as its declared physical parent and records that discontinuity
rather than fabricating rev1009 byte lineage.
