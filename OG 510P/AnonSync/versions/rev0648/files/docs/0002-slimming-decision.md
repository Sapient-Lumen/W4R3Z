# Slimming decision

The cube should stay small enough to inspect, rebuild, and exercise every turn. Historical archives remain historical; current code should retain only evidence that protects a live boundary.

“Slim” must not mean deleting inconvenient evidence or banning directory names. It should mean measuring risk-adjusted value: source concentration, duplicated generated bytes, test coverage of trust transitions, executable provenance, and the amount of operator-visible configuration.

Rev0619 removes the no-op revision applier and stale generated audits, keeps the inherited rev0618 fixture corpus immutable, adds two focused regressions, and records why further fixture expansion is lower priority than establishing an independent runtime boundary.
