# Promotion authority envelopes

VHK now models a separate promotion question from shipping/startup/control/recovery/verification/performance/dispatch: **which Linux layer actually owns authority for this shipped surface?**

## Postures

- `session-userland`: a user-session service or resident app/config tree owns repeated behavior.
- `desktop-mediated`: a portal/backend/session contract mediates authority and may revoke it by policy or consent.
- `input-edge-privileged`: remapper ownership lives near evdev/uinput or compositor key-routing.
- `helper-daemon-privileged`: repeated authority depends on a helper daemon, its socket, and its `/dev/uinput` or related policy.
- `launch-userland`: the desktop shell/launcher owns discoverability and process start, but not deeper input authority.
- `mixed-review`: the planner cannot yet make a stronger authority claim and wants review.

## Why separate this from dispatch budgets?

A surface can be warm without being privileged. A surface can be privileged without being the cold/warm dispatch owner. A portal lane can be resumed and still be desktop-mediated rather than input-edge-owned. Linux-native review needs both questions visible.

## Intended follow-up

The next product step is to let setup/native-install/runtime generation consume these envelopes directly so the repo can default more honestly toward the service scope, permission model, and desktop integration lane each promoted surface actually needs.
