# Ministry of Many Worlds

## A language in which authority has to travel with the action

The Ministry of Many Worlds draft makes bureaucracy part of programming-language imagination. Data lives in scoped collections; resources and capabilities are accounted for; steps leave receipts; branches can be chosen or merged; borders have rules.

Its unusual vocabulary is doing conceptual work. A value can describe a proposed action without possessing the authority to perform it. Undo can have a lease and a history rather than being an unlimited reversal of everything that followed.

## Questions to carry through it

Which forms of responsibility become easier to express when every transition accounts for its authority? What becomes cumbersome? When a branch is merged or a receipt becomes historical, which obligations survive? These are programming questions with institutional echoes; the draft does not thereby become an actual constitutional system.

The supplied status is a living draft in its own historical context. W4R3Z preserves one selected version, not a verified runtime or a maintenance commitment. No interpreter, consensus network or proposed governance process was launched.
