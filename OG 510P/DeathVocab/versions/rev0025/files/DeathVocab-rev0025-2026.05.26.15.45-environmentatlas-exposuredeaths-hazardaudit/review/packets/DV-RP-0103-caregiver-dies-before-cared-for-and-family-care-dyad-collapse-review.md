2. Does any record confuse craft knowledge with private wound?

4. Does any record treat burnout, compassion fatigue, moral distress, or moral injury as a diagnosis rather than source-supported vocabulary?
5. Does any record become therapy, management, employment, emergency, forensic, funeral, donation, or legal advice?
