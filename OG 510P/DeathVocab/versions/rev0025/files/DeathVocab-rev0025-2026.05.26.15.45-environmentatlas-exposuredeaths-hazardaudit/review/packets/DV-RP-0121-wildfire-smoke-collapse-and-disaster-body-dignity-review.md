# DV-RP-0121 — Wildfire, smoke, collapse, and disaster-body dignity review

Status: open / blocking publication.

Record IDs: DV-REC-000408, DV-REC-000409, DV-REC-000410, DV-REC-000418

Review questions:

1. Does the packet keep the person and family at the center rather than treating hazard, count, weather, border, famine, disaster, or policy as the whole story?
2. Does it avoid emergency, rescue, survival, legal, immigration, occupational, disaster-response, body-handling, or public-health advice?
3. Does it distinguish family witness, responder witness, official trace, population data, and humanitarian/statistical language?
4. Does it avoid body-condition spectacle, case identification, route detail, method detail, blame without authority, and political simplification?
5. Does it preserve uncertainty where the body is missing, identification is delayed, counts are provisional, or cause/manner is pending?
