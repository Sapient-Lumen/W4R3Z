
# DV-RP-0009 — Goodbye, repair, estrangement, and permission language noncoercion

Scope: DV-REC-000038 through DV-REC-000040.

Question: does any phrase pressure love, thanks, apology, forgiveness, reconciliation, physical presence, or permission to die?

Required output:

- flag coercive script language;
- flag trauma-erasing forgiveness language;
- flag claims that family words cause or delay death;
- identify where abuse, estrangement, or no-contact boundaries require different wording.
