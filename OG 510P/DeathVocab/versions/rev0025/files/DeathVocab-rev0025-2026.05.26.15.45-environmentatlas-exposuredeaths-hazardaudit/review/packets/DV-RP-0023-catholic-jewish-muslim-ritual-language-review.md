## Review questions

1. Does the packet describe terms and scenes without teaching ritual steps?
2. Does it mark denomination, lineage, community, family, region, diaspora, and local-law variation instead of flattening?
3. Are any sacred, restricted, initiatory, child, private, or identifiable details present? If yes, quarantine or remove.
4. Are source-card weights honest, especially when a source is secondary, commercial, English-language, or research-facing?
5. Could a bereaved reader mistake this for instruction, permission, or judgment? If yes, rewrite or hold.
6. What local professional, family, tradition-holder, clergy, chaplain, celebrant, or legal route must be named before public use?

## Publication rule

No record in this packet can be public until the relevant tradition/community review, cultural-safety review, source-card review, local-law review where relevant, and plain-language review are complete.
