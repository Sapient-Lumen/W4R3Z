# DV-RP-0034 — Severe stroke and sudden neurologic trajectory review

Revision: rev0010

Records:
- DV-REC-000124
- DV-REC-000125

Required reviewers:
- stroke palliative reviewer
- ICU decision-language reviewer
- equity reviewer

Review questions:

- Does the packet keep trajectory separate from prognosis?
- Does any wording imply diagnosis, treatment advice, hospice eligibility, insurance/benefits logic, or countdown timing?
- Are condition-specific terms source-bound and understandable to a lay witness?
- Are disability, age, dementia, and communication changes handled without erasure or stigma?
- What must be removed before any public surface?
