## Required review stance

This packet must confirm that worker grief and deathwork burden are rendered without confession extraction, worker/patient/family/employer identifiability, therapy advice, staffing advice, legal advice, public accusation, or hero/martyr framing.

## Blocking questions

1. Does any record imply that a worker owes the archive a traumatic story?
2. Does any record confuse craft knowledge with private wound?
3. Does any record identify a patient, family, worker, employer, call, body, scene, institution, or rare circumstance?
4. Does any record treat burnout, compassion fatigue, moral distress, or moral injury as a diagnosis rather than source-supported vocabulary?
5. Does any record become therapy, management, employment, emergency, forensic, funeral, donation, or legal advice?
