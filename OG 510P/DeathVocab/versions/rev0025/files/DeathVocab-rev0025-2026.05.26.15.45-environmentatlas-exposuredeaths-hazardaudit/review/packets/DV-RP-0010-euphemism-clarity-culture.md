
# DV-RP-0010 — Euphemism, plain language, accessibility, and cultural wording

Scope: DV-REC-000031 and DV-REC-000043.

Question: how should the archive balance clear words such as died/death/dying with family, cultural, faith, and personal wording such as passed, transitioned, gone, or lost?

Required output:

- mark contexts requiring plain language for safety or accessibility;
- mark contexts where quoted or personal language should be preserved;
- identify child, learning-disability, dementia, translation, and acute-shock cautions;
- prevent tone policing of grief.
