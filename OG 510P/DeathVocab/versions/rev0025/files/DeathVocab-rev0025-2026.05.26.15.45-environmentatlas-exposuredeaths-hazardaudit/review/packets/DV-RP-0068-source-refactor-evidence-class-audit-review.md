## Blocking questions

1. Does any sentence upgrade reported, witnessed, inferred, remembered, clinically described, or unknown evidence into a stronger claim?
2. Does any interior-experience record imply afterlife proof, universal death map, clinical diagnosis, treatment instruction, prognosis, or timing prediction?
3. Does the record pathologize meaningful language too quickly or spiritualize clinical distress too quickly?
4. Does any survivor account become spectacle or stand in for people who did not survive to report?
5. Is silence preserved as silence, or did the archive fill it with narrative?

## Required reviewers before any public rendering

- Editorial reviewer.
- Ethics/dignity reviewer.
- Clinical reviewer for pain, dyspnea, delirium, sedation, lucidity, dementia, awareness, or resuscitation claims.
- Cultural/tradition/spiritual-care reviewer where dreams, visions, ancestors, spirits, prayer, nonbelief, or existential language appear.
- Evidence-method reviewer for survivor accounts, extraordinary claims, verification claims, or research-source summaries.

## Release rule

No record in this packet can become public because rev0017 created it. Public release requires independent review, source adequacy, evidence-channel labeling, and a separate release revision.
