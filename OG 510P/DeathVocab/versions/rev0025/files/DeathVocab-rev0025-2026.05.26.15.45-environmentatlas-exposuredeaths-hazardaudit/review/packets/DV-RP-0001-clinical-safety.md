# DV-RP-0001 — Clinical safety and symptom boundaries

Scope: all quarantined records in rev0005.

Reviewer should check: source support, symptom/treatment boundaries, medication and oxygen language, swallowing/hydration risk, movement/positioning risk, breathlessness urgency, delirium/agitation language, and timing/prediction restraint.

Required output: approve, revise, restrict, or remove for each record. No silent edits.
