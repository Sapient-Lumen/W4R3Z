
# DV-RP-0007 — Utterance and last-words myth review

Scope: DV-REC-000029 through DV-REC-000044.

Question: does any record overvalue final words, imply a universal last phrase, erase silence, or convert remembered speech into exact quotation?

Required output:

- mark every phrase that sounds like a universal pattern;
- verify every utterance is labeled as exact, paraphrased, public-source motif, or seed cue;
- ensure silence, ordinary talk, and no-response records are treated as first-class witnessed moments;
- block any public rendering that turns grief into a last-chance script.
