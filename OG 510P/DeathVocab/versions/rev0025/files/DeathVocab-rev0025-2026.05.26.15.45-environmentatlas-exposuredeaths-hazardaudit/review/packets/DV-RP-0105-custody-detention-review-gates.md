# DV-RP-0105 — Custody and detention death review gates

Status: open / blocking publication.

Record IDs: DV-REC-000352, DV-REC-000353, DV-REC-000354, DV-REC-000355, DV-REC-000356, DV-REC-000357

Review questions:

1. Does the record preserve the difference between family memory, staff witness, official trace, public data, and advocacy or oversight claims?
2. Does it avoid legal, clinical, facility, investigative, grievance, media, or crisis advice?
3. Does it avoid facility names, dates, rare circumstances, logs, camera detail, medical facts, staff identifiers, and living-family allegations?
4. Does it name power, access, and opacity without turning absence or delay into proof?
5. Does it preserve personhood instead of reducing the death to a data category or institutional failure story?
