
# DV-RP-0008 — Near-death awareness, delirium, and symbolic-language boundary

Scope: DV-REC-000013, DV-REC-000022, DV-REC-000029, DV-REC-000030, DV-REC-000041, DV-REC-000042.

Question: can the record preserve symbolic or meaning-bearing language while still routing distress, agitation, confusion, medication, pain, urinary retention, constipation, or safety concerns to clinical care?

Required output:

- identify over-spiritualized language;
- identify over-medicalized language;
- identify timing/prognosis language;
- propose neutral phrases that make room for family meaning without deciding it.
