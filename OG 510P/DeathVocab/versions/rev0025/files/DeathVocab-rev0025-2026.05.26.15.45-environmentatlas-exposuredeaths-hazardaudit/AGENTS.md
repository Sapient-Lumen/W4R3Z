## Hard rules

- Do not treat seed cues as evidence.
- Do not publish pilot records as public pages.
- Do not collect private testimony merely because forms exist.
- Do not store raw private audio, raw private transcripts, contact details, consent signatures, or identifying testimony casefiles in public revision zips.
- Do not use public social media visibility as consent.
- Do not include suicide means, scripts, notes, scene descriptions, or romanticizing detail.
- Do not expose children’s words, pediatric details, perinatal photographs, sibling memories, school communities, or baby-loss artifacts without highest-gate review; many should remain unpublished forever.
- Do not document restricted cultural knowledge without authorized permission.
- Do not give clinical, legal, emergency, funeral, ritual, financial, benefits, school, investigation, parenting, or therapy instructions.
- Do not build a time-left, insurance, triage, or prediction tool.
- Do not reward voyeurism or a narrow “good death” aesthetic.

## Soft rules

Current axes, schemas, and record classes are scaffolds. Future sessions may refactor them. Be strict about harm. Be loose about form.
