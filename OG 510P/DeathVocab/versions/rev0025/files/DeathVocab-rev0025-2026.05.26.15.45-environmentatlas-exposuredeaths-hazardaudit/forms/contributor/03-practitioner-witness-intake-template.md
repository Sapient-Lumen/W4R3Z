# Practitioner witness intake template — draft only

Do not collect identifiable patient, family, institution, or employer-confidential details.

Questions:

1. What bedside pattern do you wish new workers or families had language for?
2. What phrase do you use carefully, and why?
3. What do textbooks miss about how this looks, sounds, smells, feels, or unfolds?
4. What would be unsafe to turn into a public instruction?
5. What role boundaries should be shown?

Permission for practitioner craft does not equal permission to publish patient stories.
