# Family witness intake template — draft only

Use only after fieldwork gates open. Do not use for fresh bereavement, child material, suicide aftermath, custody death, restricted cultural material, or active investigation without high-gate review.

Preferred focus: one moment, phrase, room detail, or aftermath moment.

Questions:

1. What did you notice before you knew what to call it?
2. What words did people use in the room?
3. What did you later search for or wish someone had named?
4. What should not be public?
5. What would feel misleading if we paraphrased it too smoothly?

Stop whenever the contributor wants to stop.
