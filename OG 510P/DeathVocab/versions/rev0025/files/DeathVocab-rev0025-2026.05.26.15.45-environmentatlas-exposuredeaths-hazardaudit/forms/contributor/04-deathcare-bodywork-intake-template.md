# Deathcare/bodywork intake template — draft only

This template is for funeral directors, embalmers, crematory/cemetery workers, green burial workers, and related deathcare roles. It requires body-dignity review.

Questions:

1. What do families often not know about the second handoff?
2. What language protects dignity around clothing, rings, viewing, or disposition?
3. What should never be described for curiosity?
4. What legal or jurisdictional boundaries must be named without giving advice?
5. What part of your work is underdocumented but safe to orient readers toward?
