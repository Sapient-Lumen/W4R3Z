# High-gate deferral notice — draft only

Thank you for trusting us with the possibility of this material. DeathVocab is not currently collecting this kind of account.

This may be because the material involves children, suicide, custody or detention, domestic violence, disaster or mass death, active investigation, restricted cultural knowledge, or fresh grief. These areas require specialist review and may remain unpublished even with consent.

Deferral is not disbelief. It is an ethical boundary.
