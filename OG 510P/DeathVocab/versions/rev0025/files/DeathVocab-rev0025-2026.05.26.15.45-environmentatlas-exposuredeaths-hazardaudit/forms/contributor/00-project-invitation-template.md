# DeathVocab contributor invitation template — draft only

DeathVocab is building a governed archive of witnessed moments, language, care, body work, institution, ritual, and aftermath around dying and death.

You are not being asked to tell a story you do not want to tell. You can decline, pause, skip questions, stop, amend, or withdraw. An interview does not mean your words will become public. Public use would require additional review and permission.

This project is not therapy, crisis care, medical advice, legal advice, or ritual authority. Please do not send urgent medical questions or emergency needs here.
