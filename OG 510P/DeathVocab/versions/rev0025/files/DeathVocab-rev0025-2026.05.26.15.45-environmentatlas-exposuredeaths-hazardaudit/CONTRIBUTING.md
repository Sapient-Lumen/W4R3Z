# Contributing posture

This is not yet an open contribution project. Rev0015 adds draft fieldwork and consent machinery, but it does not open recruitment.

Future contributions should not be accepted until the consent dry run, review packets, protected data custody, compensation policy, and stopping/withdrawal paths are tested.

Every contributor-facing form should say plainly:

- You can choose what is public, private, anonymized, role-credited, named, quoted, paraphrased, or withheld.
- You can amend or withdraw according to the terms explained to you.
- An interview does not imply public use.
- Public visibility elsewhere is not consent for DeathVocab.
- Do not include identifying details of patients, family members, clinicians, institutions, children, or deceased persons unless permission exists and review gates allow it.
- Do not include suicide method details.
- Do not submit urgent medical, legal, mental-health, or emergency questions here.
- Cultural or religious practices may require permission from authorized holders and may be documented only partially or not at all.

Default state for new material: `no_public_use` and, if admitted later, `quarantined`.
