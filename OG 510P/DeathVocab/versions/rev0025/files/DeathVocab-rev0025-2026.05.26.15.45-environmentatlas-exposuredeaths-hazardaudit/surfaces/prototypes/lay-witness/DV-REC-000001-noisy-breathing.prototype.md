# Prototype lay witness rendering — noisy breathing

**Status:** prototype only. Not public. Not medical advice.

You may have heard breathing that sounded wet, rattly, or gurgly. Public end-of-life sources often call this `death rattle`, `terminal secretions`, or `noisy breathing`. They describe it as happening when saliva or mucus collects and the person is too weak to clear it.

What this page would need to say carefully:

- the sound can be frightening for people nearby;
- sources commonly distinguish it from the person necessarily feeling short of breath;
- it can occur near the end of life, but it is not an exact countdown;
- questions about position, fluids, suction, or medicine belong with the person’s hospice nurse, doctor, or care team.

This prototype tests tone only. A reviewed public page would need clinical signoff and citation display.
