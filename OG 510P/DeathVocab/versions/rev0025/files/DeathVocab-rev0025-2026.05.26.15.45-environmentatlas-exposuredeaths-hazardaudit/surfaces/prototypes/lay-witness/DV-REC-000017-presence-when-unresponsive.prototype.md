# Prototype lay witness rendering — DV-REC-000017

Status: internal prototype only.

When someone is dying and no longer answering, many people wonder whether speaking still matters. Public hospice and palliative sources often say hearing may remain possible even when a person seems asleep or unresponsive. The safest public wording is “may,” not “definitely.”

You can speak in an ordinary voice if that feels right. You can also sit quietly. Touch, music, prayer, or silence should follow the person’s wishes, the relationship, and the room.

This is not a script. There is no single correct sentence.
