# Prototype practitioner apprenticeship surface — Practice atlas index

Status: internal prototype only.

The practice layer groups small care moves:

- presence, voice, touch, and room tone;
- mouth, food, fluids, and guilt;
- breathing support language and positioning boundaries;
- confusion, restlessness, and meaning boundaries;
- skin, warmth, elimination, and bodily dignity;
- first calls, papers, body care, medicines, and equipment after death.

Each care move needs a phrase, a boundary, a route-back, and a review packet.
