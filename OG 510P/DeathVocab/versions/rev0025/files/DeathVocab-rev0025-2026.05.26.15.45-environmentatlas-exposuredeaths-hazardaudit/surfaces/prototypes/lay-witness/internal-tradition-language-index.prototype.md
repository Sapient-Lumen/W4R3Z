# Internal prototype — tradition-language index

Public status: disabled.

This prototype sketches how a future lay witness surface might route tradition-language searches without becoming a ritual manual.

- “What are last rites?” → route to Catholic bedside-language record and advise contacting Catholic pastoral care.
- “What is chevra kadisha?” → route to Jewish body-care vocabulary record and advise contacting local Jewish burial society or rabbinic support.
- “What is ghusl?” → route to Muslim body-care vocabulary record and advise contacting local Muslim burial/spiritual care support.
- “What is tukdam?” → route to high-gate Tibetan Buddhist record; do not render detailed public surface without Tibetan Buddhist authority.
- “Can we keep the body at home?” → route to home-funeral vocabulary record and local-law/funeral-professional boundary.

Every route must visibly say: this is vocabulary and orientation, not instruction.
