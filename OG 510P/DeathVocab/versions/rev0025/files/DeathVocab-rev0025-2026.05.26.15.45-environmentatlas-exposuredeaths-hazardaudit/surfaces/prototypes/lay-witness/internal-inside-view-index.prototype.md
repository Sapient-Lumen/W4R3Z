# Internal prototype — inside-view index

Not public. Quarantined.

A reader might arrive with questions like:

- “Did they know they were dying?”
- “Were they afraid?”
- “Were they in pain?”
- “Could they hear me?”
- “Were those visions real?”
- “What do people remember after cardiac arrest?”
- “Why did my mother with dementia suddenly seem clear?”
- “What if there were no last words and no sign?”

The index must answer with evidence verbs, not certainty theater:

- Some people **report** dreams or visions.
- Some survivors **remember** experiences after cardiac arrest.
- Families and clinicians **witness** utterances, gestures, and changes.
- Clinicians **assess** symptoms and delirium.
- Much remains **unknown**.

This index should not become a page of reassurance, proof, prophecy, or diagnosis.
