# Internal prototype — sensory room index

_Not public. This prototype tests reader routing only._

- I cannot forget the sound of the breathing → DV-REC-000188 / DV-REC-000189.
- The room went silent after the last breath → DV-REC-000190.
- Can they hear me if they do not respond? → DV-REC-000191.
- What music should we play? → DV-REC-000192.
- I remember the smell and feel ashamed → DV-REC-000193.
- Their hands were cold; should I cover them? → DV-REC-000194.
- Their mouth was dry; I only had swabs → DV-REC-000195.
- The bed, sheets, and pillows were the whole job → DV-REC-000196.
- I left the room for a minute and missed it → DV-REC-000208.
- The dog stayed by the bed → DV-REC-000205.
- My child remembers the tubes/smell/noise → DV-REC-000206, high gate.
- The house changed when the equipment left → DV-REC-000207.
- A song or smell brings the room back → DV-REC-000210.

Every route shows a safety interstitial and remains disabled for public use.
