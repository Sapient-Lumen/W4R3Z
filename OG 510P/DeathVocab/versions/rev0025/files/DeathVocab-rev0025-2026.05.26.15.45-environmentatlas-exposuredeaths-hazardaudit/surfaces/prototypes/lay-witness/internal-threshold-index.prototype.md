# Internal prototype: threshold index

This is not a public page. It sketches the kind of reader-facing routing that might become possible after review.

## I heard something frightening

Routes: DV-REC-000001, DV-REC-000002, DV-REC-000010, DV-REC-000011.

Boundary: not a countdown, not a pronouncement, not a care plan.

## I do not know whether to keep talking

Routes: DV-REC-000006, DV-REC-000013.

Boundary: possibility without certainty; no pressure to perform.

## I feel guilty because I missed it

Routes: DV-REC-000014, DV-REC-000015.

Boundary: the archive cannot know intention. Missing the moment is not failure.

## The body or room changed

Routes: DV-REC-000004, DV-REC-000008, DV-REC-000009, DV-REC-000016.

Boundary: dignity first; no voyeurism; no diagnosis.
