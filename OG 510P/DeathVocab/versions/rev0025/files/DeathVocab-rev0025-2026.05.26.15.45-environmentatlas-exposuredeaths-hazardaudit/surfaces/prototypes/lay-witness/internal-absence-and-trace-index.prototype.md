# Internal prototype: absence and official trace index

**Not public.** This prototype sketches how a reader might someday route from phrases like “no one saw him die,” “unclaimed body,” “pending death certificate,” “no body,” or “case number” to quarantined records.

Public rendering is blocked because these phrases can expose living families, encourage amateur investigation, launder official accounts, or promise closure where there is only uncertainty.

Candidate groups:

- No bedside witness and delayed discovery: DV-REC-000279..DV-REC-000281, DV-REC-000301..DV-REC-000302.
- No next of kin, unclaimed, unidentified: DV-REC-000282..DV-REC-000284, DV-REC-000292, DV-REC-000296, DV-REC-000298.
- Missing, unrecovered, ambiguous loss: DV-REC-000285..DV-REC-000286, DV-REC-000295, DV-REC-000300.
- Official custody, certificate, effects, release: DV-REC-000287..DV-REC-000291, DV-REC-000299.
- Disaster, conflict, mass fatality, and rapid-disposal myth: DV-REC-000293..DV-REC-000294, DV-REC-000300.

Safe line under test: “Sometimes the record begins after the death, with an official call, a case number, or a search for next of kin; that does not make the person less loved or the death less human.”
