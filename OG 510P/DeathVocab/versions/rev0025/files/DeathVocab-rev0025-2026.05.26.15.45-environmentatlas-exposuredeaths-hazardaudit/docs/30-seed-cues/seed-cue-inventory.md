# Seed cue inventory

`CUE-REGISTRY.json` turns selected motifs from the seed brief into research prompts. These are not records.

A seed cue can graduate only after it receives:

1. a source beyond the seed brief;
2. provenance and locator;
3. consent or public-source status;
4. axes;
5. interpretation separation;
6. safety review;
7. stability label;
8. publication state.

The most important early cue family may be phrase-to-record search: “reached for the air,” “wanted to go home,” “said Mama,” “rattling sound,” “seemed better,” “died when I stepped out.”
