# Source ladder for tradition records

The ordinary source ladder is not enough for tradition-axis material. Publicness is not permission. A source can be visible online and still be inadequate for ritual authority.

## Working ladder

- L0 seed cue: suggests an area but cannot generate a record alone.
- L1 public vocabulary source: can support a quarantined term or scene-context record.
- L2 public tradition/community source: can strengthen vocabulary and boundary language.
- L3 reviewed community/tradition-holder statement: can support a limited public orientation surface.
- L4 consented witnessed moment with family/community review: can support a textured public record if nonidentifiability and withdrawal paths are solved.
- L5 restricted or sovereignly withheld knowledge: does not enter the archive; its absence is recorded as a boundary, not a failure.
