# Official trace is not total truth

Official traces stabilize certain facts: custody, reportability, cause/manner language, certificate status, release status, property inventory, public list status, and database categories.

They do not exhaust the death. They do not carry the dying person's interior life, the family relationship, the ritual need, the chosen-family exclusion, the sensory room, the unpaid care, or the grief that continues after a certificate is issued.

Future sessions should preserve this split: official language is indispensable and dangerous. It should be indexed, source-bound, and reviewed, but never allowed to become the archive's whole truth.
