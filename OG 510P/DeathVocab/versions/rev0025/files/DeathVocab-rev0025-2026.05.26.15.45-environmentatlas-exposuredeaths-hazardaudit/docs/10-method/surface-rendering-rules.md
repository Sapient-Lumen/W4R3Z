# Surface rendering rules

The same governed record should not appear the same way everywhere.

## Lay witness surface

This surface meets frightened ordinary searches. It should say: this has been described before; here are the careful terms; here is what sources can and cannot say; this page is not a diagnosis or care plan; contact the local care team when worried.

## Practitioner apprenticeship surface

This surface preserves role-specific craft: how experienced workers describe, notice, explain, or avoid overexplaining a moment. It can use professional terms, but it still cannot replace local protocols or clinical judgment.

## Provenance and research surface

This surface shows source packets, review state, stability, limitations, nonclaims, contradictions, and rollback dependencies.

## Tradition-governed rooms

Some materials should be visible only within a permissioned or scoped room. The archive must be able to say: “This exists; details are withheld.”
