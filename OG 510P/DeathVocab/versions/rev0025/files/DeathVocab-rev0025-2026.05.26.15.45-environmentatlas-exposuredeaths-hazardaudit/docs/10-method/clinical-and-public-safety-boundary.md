# Clinical and public safety boundary

DeathVocab records may mention symptoms, signs, medications, breathing patterns, fluids, delirium, body changes, pronouncement, or after-death body care. Mentioning these is not advice.

Every future public page that touches clinical or legal matters should carry a boundary in plain language:

- This page describes records and language around dying.
- It does not diagnose, predict, treat, or advise.
- Urgent medical concerns should go to qualified local clinicians, hospice teams, emergency services, or other appropriate local support.
- Laws and clinical protocols vary by place and time.

The corpus should especially avoid exact time prediction. Timing language can comfort or harm; records should preserve uncertainty.
