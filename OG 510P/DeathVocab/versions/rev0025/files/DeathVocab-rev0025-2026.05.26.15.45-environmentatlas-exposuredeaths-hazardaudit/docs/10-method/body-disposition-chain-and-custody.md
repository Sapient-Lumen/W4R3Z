# Body disposition chain and custody method

A body-after-death record must distinguish five surfaces:

1. family witness and grief language;
2. provider workflow language;
3. official paperwork and jurisdictional authority;
4. cultural/tradition/family authority;
5. long-after continuing-bond or remains language.

A record may preserve terms such as release, authorizing agent, embalming, viewing, cremation, autopsy, anatomical gift, repatriation, and disinterment. It may not become a decision tool, procedure page, legal guide, provider recommendation, price guide, or eligibility checker.
