# De-identification and context preservation

DeathVocab cannot simply strip every detail. A record without context becomes useless; a record with too much context can become harmful. The task is to preserve meaning while removing identity.

## Details to remove or coarsen

- names, initials, exact dates, exact locations, facility names, room numbers
- rare combinations of diagnosis, age, place, profession, family structure, or newsworthy circumstance
- quotes that family or staff would recognize as uniquely identifying
- sequence details from suicide, violence, custody, or active legal cases
- cultural details that are not authorized for public sharing

## Details to preserve when safe

- broad phase of dying
- setting type
- trajectory category
- witness role
- sensory channel
- whether the account is family-facing, practitioner-facing, first-person, tradition-held, or institutional
- uncertainty and nonclaims

## The retained-detail rationale

Every private or contributor-derived record should say why each sensitive context field was retained. “It makes the story moving” is not enough. Valid reasons include preventing misinterpretation, locating the record on a trajectory axis, preserving cultural specificity with permission, or protecting against false universal claims.
