# Pediatric/perinatal high-gate method note

Rev0019 admits pediatric and perinatal death as architecture, not as story collection.

The method is deliberately conservative:

- name the domain;
- keep records source-bound;
- do not admit private child words, sibling testimony, photographs, drawings, school details, or perinatal images;
- require pediatric/perinatal specialist review before any public rendering;
- treat refusal and permanent restriction as successful outcomes.

The core distinction is: a public source may justify a gate, a vocabulary route, or a review question. It does not authorize the archive to display a child’s material.
