# Publication gate matrix

The archive now distinguishes “record exists” from “record is public.” This distinction is essential. Some records should be preserved privately, some should become training material only, some should be restricted by tradition or family permission, and some should never be published.

## Default posture

- Low-risk public sources may enter as quarantined pilots.
- Family, professional, dying-person, and tradition-holder contributions enter privately.
- Pediatric, suicide, custody, institutional, violent, or graphic records require a reason-to-publish memo and senior review.
- The most beautiful detail is often the detail most likely to identify someone; beauty does not override consent.

## Public page test

A public page must pass all of these questions:

1. Does it help the reader become more capable of presence, understanding, or care?
2. Is observation separated from interpretation?
3. Does it avoid acting as medical, legal, crisis, or spiritual instruction?
4. Is the source/permission state visible?
5. Could a family, contributor, institution, or community be harmed by the context retained?
6. Are restricted cultural details withheld unless permission exists?
7. Can the record be withdrawn or corrected without orphaning derived pages?
