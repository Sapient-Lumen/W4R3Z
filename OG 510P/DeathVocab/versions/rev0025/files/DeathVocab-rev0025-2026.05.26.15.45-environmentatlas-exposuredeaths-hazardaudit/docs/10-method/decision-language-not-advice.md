# Decision language is not advice

Rev0007 adds the first institutional-decision layer. These records are not legal pages, clinical guides, form-completion instructions, eligibility tools, or emergency decision trees.

The primary unit remains the witnessed moment: a family hears “DNR,” a nurse says “comfort measures,” an ICU team explains ventilator withdrawal, an OPO enters the conversation, or someone asks whether MAID or VSED is different from ordinary loss of appetite. The record preserves the term as it entered the room, what it can be mistaken for, what sources support the vocabulary, and which gates prevent public misuse.

Public rendering rule: define less, route more, and never let a term carry authority the archive does not have.
