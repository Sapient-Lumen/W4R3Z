# Trajectory records are not prognosis — rev0010

Trajectory helps the cube answer: “Why did this death unfold differently from the one I imagined?” It does not answer: “How long is left?”

A trajectory record may name course shapes: relatively steady decline, crisis-and-recovery organ failure, long frailty/dementia dwindling, sudden neurologic injury, or mixed decline. It may preserve family phrases like “better then worse,” “not bouncing back,” or “what are they dying of?”

It must not become a countdown, hospice eligibility page, treatment recommendation, insurance tool, or reason to delay or rush farewell.

Use `TRAJECTORY-AXIS-ATLAS.json`, `COURSE-FORM-MATRIX.json`, and `PROGNOSIS-GUARDRAIL-LEDGER.json` before editing any rev0010 record.
