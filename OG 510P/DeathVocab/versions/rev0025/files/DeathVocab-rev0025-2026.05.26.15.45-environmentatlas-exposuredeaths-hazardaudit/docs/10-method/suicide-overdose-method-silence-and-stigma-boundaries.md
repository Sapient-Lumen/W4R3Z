# Suicide / overdose method-silence and stigma boundaries

This cube must distinguish **postvention vocabulary** from **actionable detail**. A future reader may need to understand why family members say “there was no note,” why a certificate is pending, why a school avoids certain memorial practices, why overdose grief is stigmatized, or why public memorial language is difficult. The reader does not need method, scene, toxicology, note, photo, message, or response detail from this archive.

Method-silence is an invariant, not a tone preference.

For overdose, stigma-silence is not enough. The record must also avoid reducing a person to substance use, criminality, toxicology, or whether any one response might have changed the outcome.

For suicide, single-cause refusal is a core guardrail. The archive can record that survivors search for why; it must not provide a neat answer.
