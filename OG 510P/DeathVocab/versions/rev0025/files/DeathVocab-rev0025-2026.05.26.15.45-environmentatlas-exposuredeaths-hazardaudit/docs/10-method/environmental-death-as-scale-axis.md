# Environmental death as scale axis

The archive previously had setting, trajectory, official trace, absence, body disposition, and institutional opacity records that touched disasters and exposure. Rev0025 factors these into a specific environment/exposure axis.

The refactor is not a new claim that every heat, cold, water, smoke, famine, migration, or disaster death is the same kind of death. It is a finding aid and harm boundary: the office must separate one body, one family, one room, a public-health count, a missing-person database, a disaster list, and a political argument.

Hard boundary: records may name language and review gates; they may not become emergency, survival, rescue, legal, immigration, occupational, disaster-response, or body-handling advice.
