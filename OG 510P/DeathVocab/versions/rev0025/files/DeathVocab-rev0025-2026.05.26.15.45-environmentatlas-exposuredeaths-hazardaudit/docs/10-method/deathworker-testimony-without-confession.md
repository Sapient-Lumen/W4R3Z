# Deathworker testimony without confession

Future sessions should separate four things that are often collapsed:

1. craft knowledge: phrases, room-reading, hand skills, timing, objects, rituals, handoffs;
2. role boundaries: what the worker could see, touch, say, decide, or never know;
3. professional burden: grief, fatigue, moral distress, burnout, stigma, and exposure;
4. private wound: the details the worker does not owe the archive.

The field office should ask for craft before wound. A worker’s refusal to narrate the hardest death is not a failed interview. It is evidence that some material must remain protected.
