# Ethics and consent firebreak

The corpus depends on trust. The default state for sensitive material is not “publish after anonymizing.” The default state is “do not publish until the right kind of permission, review, and purpose exist.”

## Consent branches to design

- Living professional contributor, public name allowed.
- Living professional contributor, anonymized role only.
- Family contributor about a deceased person.
- First-person account by a dying person.
- Public literature source, no private consent needed but still safety reviewed.
- Public social-media post, ethically public but still potentially inappropriate to extract.
- Tradition-holder account with restricted details.
- Pediatric or sibling grief record, default unpublished.
- Suicide aftermath record, safe-messaging gate.

## Withdrawal rule

Withdrawal must propagate. Removing a record is not enough if its language remains in a glossary, topic page, phrase alias, search synonym, or summary.
