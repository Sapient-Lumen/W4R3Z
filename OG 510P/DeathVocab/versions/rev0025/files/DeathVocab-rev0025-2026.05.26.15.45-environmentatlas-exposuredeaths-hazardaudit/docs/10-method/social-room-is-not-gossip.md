# Social-room records are not gossip

A DeathVocab social-room record may preserve who was present, absent, recognized, excluded, burdened, interrupted, afraid, angry, or believed. It may not publish private conflict as spectacle.

Required separations:

- patient preference vs. family preference
- legal authority vs. emotional authority
- caregiver labor vs. decision authority
- witness account vs. editor interpretation
- safety concern vs. public accusation
- chosen family recognition vs. outing risk

Public rendering remains blocked until social-work, ethics, privacy, and where relevant clinical/legal/cultural review pass.
