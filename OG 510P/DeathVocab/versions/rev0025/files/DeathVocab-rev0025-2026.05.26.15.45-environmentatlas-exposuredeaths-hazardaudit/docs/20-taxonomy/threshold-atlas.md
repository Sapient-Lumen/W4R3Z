# Threshold atlas

The early corpus should not be browsed only by medical terms. People arrive by thresholds:

- the sound in the room changes
- feeding becomes mouth care
- the body begins to look unlike itself
- speech stops but address continues
- the person returns briefly or speaks to someone unseen
- the last breath becomes uncertain
- the living sit with the body

`THRESHOLD-ATLAS.json` groups the first sixteen quarantined records around these thresholds. This lets the project keep the scene-first rule while still supporting glossary and search surfaces.

The atlas is also a safety tool. Every cluster names its main danger: prediction, guilt, voyeurism, over-spiritualizing, over-medicalizing, legal overreach, or loss of dignity.
