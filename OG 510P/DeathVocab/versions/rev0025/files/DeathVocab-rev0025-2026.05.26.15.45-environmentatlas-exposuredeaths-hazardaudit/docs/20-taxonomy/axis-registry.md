# Axis registry notes

Axes are used so a record can be found from many directions. They should not become rigid boxes.

A future record about a spouse hearing Cheyne-Stokes breathing at home in the last day of cancer decline might be findable by:

- sound;
- days-to-hours;
- cancer trajectory;
- home setting;
- family perspective;
- clinical term and plain-language phrase;
- panic or fear interpretation;
- hospice explanation practice.

The archive should preserve multiple routes because readers arrive with different words.
