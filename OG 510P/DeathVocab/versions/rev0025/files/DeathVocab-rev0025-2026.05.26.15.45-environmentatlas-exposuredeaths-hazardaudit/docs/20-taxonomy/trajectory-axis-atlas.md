# Trajectory axis atlas — rev0010

Rev0010 adds records `DV-REC-000105` through `DV-REC-000128` to make trajectory a first-class axis: cancer, dementia/frailty, heart failure, chronic respiratory disease, kidney failure, liver failure, MND/ALS, Parkinson’s, stroke, frailty/very old age, and mixed decline.

The axis exists because the same sign can mean different things in different courses, and because families often experience dying through course-shape confusion: a decline that is clear, a crisis that partially reverses, a long dwindling that suddenly changes, or a sudden neurologic event with no slow bedside prelude.

All records remain quarantined.
