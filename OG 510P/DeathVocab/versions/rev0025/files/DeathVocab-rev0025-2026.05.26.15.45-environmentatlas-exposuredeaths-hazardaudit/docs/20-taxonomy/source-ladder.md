# Source ladder

The ladder defines entry posture, not truth. A public page can be wrong, and a single hospice aide may notice something no textbook names. The ladder asks: what kind of source is this, what permissions apply, what review lane is needed, and what can safely be shown?

## Ladder levels

- **L0 seed prompt**: project cue only. It cannot support a public record.
- **L1 public information**: public family-facing material. Useful for first pilots; needs clinical safety review when medical.
- **L2 professional or peer-reviewed public**: professional, clinical, scholarly, or peer-reviewed material. It may still lack bedside texture.
- **L3 consented single witness**: a living contributor’s bounded witnessed moment. Private by default until consent, de-identification, and review pass.
- **L4 consented cross-witness convergence**: multiple accounts converge, but are not collapsed into “this always happens.”
- **L5 tradition-governed or restricted**: the corpus may know that a room exists without having the right to enter it.

## Why this matters

The project is trying to hold a strange combination: public usefulness, lived detail, clinical caution, cultural humility, and withdrawal rights. A single global “source quality” score would erase those differences.
