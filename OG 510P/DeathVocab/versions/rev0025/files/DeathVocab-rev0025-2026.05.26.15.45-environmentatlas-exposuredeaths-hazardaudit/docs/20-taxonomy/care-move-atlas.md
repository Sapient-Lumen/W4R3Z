# Care move atlas

Revision: rev0005

A care move is a small bedside practice or boundary: speaking in an ordinary voice, offering mouth comfort, not pressuring food, asking whether music is welcome, checking noisy breathing with the care team, not arguing with a confused sentence, preserving dignity around urine or bowel changes, using the expected-death contact plan, leaving medicines and devices for trained staff.

The atlas groups these moves by room function, not by disease:

- presence and voice;
- mouth, food, fluids, and guilt;
- breathing and positioning;
- confusion and meaning;
- skin, warmth, elimination, and dignity;
- first calls, papers, body care, medicines, and equipment.

The atlas is not enabled for public rendering.
