# Not overly constrained

The early cube contains many gates. That is necessary because the subject is intimate, risky, and culturally charged. But gates can accidentally become a prison if future sessions forget which constraints are ethical and which are merely architectural.

## Ethical constraints are real

Consent, source integrity, cultural sovereignty, child protection, suicide-safe messaging, non-voyeurism, and non-advice are not negotiable constraints. They are the condition under which the archive can exist.

## Architectural constraints are invitations

The current axes, schemas, ledgers, and review packets are ways of thinking. They should be versioned, challenged, expanded, or replaced when better thinking appears.

## The archive needs wildness inside the fence

The subject demands imagination. Future sessions should look for what the seed warned builders might miss: smell, sound, pets, estranged children, money fights, the body’s weight, the first night alone, rings, clothes, the phone tree, the deaths outside hospice, and the deaths no one saw.

The fence is not there to make the archive tame. The fence is there so the archive can approach wild material without becoming predatory.
