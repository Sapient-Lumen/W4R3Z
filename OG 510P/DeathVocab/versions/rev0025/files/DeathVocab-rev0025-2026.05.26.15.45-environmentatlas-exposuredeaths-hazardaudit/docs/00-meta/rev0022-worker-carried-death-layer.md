# Rev0022 — Worker-carried death and deathwork burden layer

This revision adds the worker-carried-death subcube: the deaths held by hospice nurses, aides, palliative clinicians, social workers, chaplains, doulas, volunteers, funeral directors, embalmers, cemetery and crematory workers, medical examiners, EMS responders, 9-1-1 telecommunicators, organ donation coordinators, and family caregivers whose own death can collapse a care system.

The governing line is: **the archive may learn from deathworkers only by refusing to make their wounds public property.**

The layer exists because the seed premise depends on mostly oral knowledge held by people who age, leave the profession, or burn out. But a future field office must not confuse “these workers know” with “these workers owe.” The public-source records added here are scaffolds for careful fieldwork, not testimony.

No private worker account was collected. No public worker surface was enabled.
