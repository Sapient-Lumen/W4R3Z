# rev0023 — Institutional opacity, custody, congregate care, and accountability language

This revision adds the closed-room layer: deaths where an institution controls access, notification, records, review, body release, and much of the first language the family receives.

Governing line: **A closed room is evidence of authority, not proof of what happened.**

The cube can name death in custody, detention death review, nursing-home and group-home critical incident language, psychiatric-unit safety terms, sentinel event language, disclosure and candor vocabulary, restraint/seclusion death reporting, body-release delay, chosen-family exclusion, logs/cameras/data traces, and mortality dashboards. It cannot decide a case, accuse a facility, clear an institution, tell a family what to do, or publish identifying facts.

New records: `DV-REC-000351` through `DV-REC-000374`. Prior setting records `DV-REC-000133, DV-REC-000134, DV-REC-000135, DV-REC-000137, DV-REC-000138, DV-REC-000140, DV-REC-000141, DV-REC-000154` were backfilled into the institutional-opacity audit spine.
