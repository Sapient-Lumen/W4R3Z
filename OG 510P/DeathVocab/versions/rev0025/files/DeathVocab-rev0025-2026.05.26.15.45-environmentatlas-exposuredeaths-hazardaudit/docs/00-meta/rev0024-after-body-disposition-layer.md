# rev0024 — After-body / disposition-chain layer

Governing line: **The body after death is not logistics; it is custody, relationship, authority, paperwork, culture, labor, and continuing bond.**

This revision adds 24 quarantined records for what happens to the body after death: release, transport, holding, authorization, certificates, funeral arrangement, embalming, viewing, cremation, burial, green burial, alkaline hydrolysis, natural organic reduction, autopsy, body donation, organ/eye/tissue donation, repatriation, personal effects, disinterment, and boundary firebreaks.

No public surface is enabled. The layer exists so future offices do not treat body disposition as mere administration after the real death has ended.
