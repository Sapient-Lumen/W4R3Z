# Rev0016 — sensory room / material witness layer

Rev0016 returns to substance after the rev0015 fieldwork bridge. It follows a seed warning that the archive must record what dying looks, sounds, smells, feels, and practically means from the bedside, not merely the terms that professionals use.

No public corpus pages were created. The revision adds 24 quarantined records, all source-bound and blocked from publication.

## Governing move

**The room is not background. The room is part of the evidence.**

The same clinical sign can live differently in a room with a humming oxygen concentrator, a dog under the bed, harsh fluorescent light, a hallway alarm, a swab cup, a funeral-home doorbell, or a child remembering only a smell. These details are not decoration. They change what the death is for the witness.
