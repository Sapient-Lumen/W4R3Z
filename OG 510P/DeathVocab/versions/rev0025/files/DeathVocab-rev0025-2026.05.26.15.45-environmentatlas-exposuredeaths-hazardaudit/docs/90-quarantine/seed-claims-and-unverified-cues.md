# Seed claims and unverified cues quarantine

All motifs from the seed brief are quarantined as prompts until sourced.

This includes signs, time estimates, medication mentions, cultural practices, family-language patterns, hospice-worker practices, and claims about what is common or underdocumented.

A quarantined cue may be useful. It may be emotionally true to witnesses. It may still be unsafe to publish as fact.

Promotion requires source, provenance, review, and a publication state.
