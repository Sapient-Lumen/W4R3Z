
# Utterance records quarantine note

The rev0006 utterance records are public-source pilots only. They are not evidence of how often phrases occur, not exact bedside testimony, and not a public guide.

They exist to test whether the archive can safely hold language before private testimony enters. The next ethical move is not publication; it is review plus consented collection design.

Especially blocked:

- exact last-word quotations from private people;
- names, kinship words, or identifying family details;
- forgiveness or permission scripts framed as duties;
- home/travel/timing language framed as prophecy;
- symbolic speech framed as proof of a spiritual claim;
- distressing/confused speech framed as “just delirium” without clinical context.
