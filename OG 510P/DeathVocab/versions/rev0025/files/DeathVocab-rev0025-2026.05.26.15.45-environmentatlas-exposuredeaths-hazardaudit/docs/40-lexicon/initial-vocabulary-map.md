# Initial vocabulary map

This is a seed vocabulary map, not a definition set.

## Candidate clinical / bedside terms

Active dying; transitioning; comfort care; death rattle; terminal agitation; terminal lucidity; Cheyne-Stokes; air hunger; mottling; pallor mortis; algor mortis; rigor mortis; livor mortis; Lazarus signs; pronouncement; time of death; DNR; DNI; AND; POLST; MOLST; comfort kit; compassionate extubation; honor walk.

## Candidate care and room terms

Mouth care; repositioning; anticipatory meds; oxygen concentrator; vigil; holding space; witnessing; last office; laying out; release to funeral home; second goodbye.

## Candidate ritual / tradition terms

Chevra kadisha; ghusl; tahara; shiva; wake; viewing; visitation; funeral; memorial; celebration of life; repast; final disposition; green burial; home funeral.

## Candidate family and euphemism language

Passed; gone; lost; slipped away; left us; at peace; crossed over; transitioned; when she goes; if anything happens; when the time comes; he is not going to make it; we need to start planning.

Every future glossary entry should include: term, plain-language bridge, observed contexts, what it does not mean, source map, safety cautions, and cross-links.
