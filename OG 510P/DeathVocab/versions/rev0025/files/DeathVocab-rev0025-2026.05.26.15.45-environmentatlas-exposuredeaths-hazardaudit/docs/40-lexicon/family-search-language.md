# Family search language

The future reader will not usually arrive through professional language. They will type the sentence they can bear to type:

- my dad sounded like he was drowning
- can she hear me if unconscious
- stopped breathing then breathed again
- he died when I left the room
- smell of dying

`SEARCH-ALIAS-MAP.json` and `READER-QUERY-ROUTER.json` preserve those phrases as routes. They are never diagnoses, never predictions, and never instructions.

The core editorial rule is: meet the panic phrase, then lower the danger of the answer.
