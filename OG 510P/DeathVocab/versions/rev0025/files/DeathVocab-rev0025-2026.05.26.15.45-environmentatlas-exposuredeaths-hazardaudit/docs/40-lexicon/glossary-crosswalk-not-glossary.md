# Glossary crosswalk, not glossary-first corpus

`rev0003` adds the first glossary crosswalk, but the archive still refuses to become term-first.

A glossary term is useful when a reader searches `death rattle`, `Cheyne-Stokes`, `mottling`, or `terminal delirium`. But the lived need often begins elsewhere: “my dad sounded like he was drowning,” “her breathing stopped and started,” “why won’t he drink,” “her feet turned blue,” “he saw his mother.”

The crosswalk should therefore route:

ordinary phrase → witnessed moment → source packet → professional term → uncertainty → what-this-is-not → safe next orientation

The reverse route also matters for practitioners:

professional term → witnessed moments → family language → practice questions → evidence debts → review boundaries
