# Setting is not background

A death record without setting is unsafe. The room decides who is allowed to witness, who is believed, who can touch the body, what language is available, and what happens next. Rev0011 therefore treats setting as an axis of authority.

A home bedroom, ICU, ED bay, nursing-home room, prison cell, street corner, maternity ward, funeral home, morgue, crematory, and disaster site are not interchangeable scenes. They produce different witnesses, silences, documents, and injuries.

The archive must not rank places of death. It must preserve what each place makes possible and what each place hides.
