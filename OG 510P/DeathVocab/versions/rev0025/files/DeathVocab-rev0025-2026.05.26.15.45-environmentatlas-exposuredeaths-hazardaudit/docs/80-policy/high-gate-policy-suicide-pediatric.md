# High-gate policy: pediatric, suicide, and other high-risk domains

This revision adds no pediatric records and no suicide records.

The policy exists before the records because those domains carry special risks:

- children’s words can be identifying or exploitative even when anonymized
- bereaved parents and siblings require extraordinary care
- suicide aftermath must follow safe-messaging principles and must not include means details
- custody, institutional, and violent deaths can endanger living people or launder official narratives
- restricted cultural knowledge belongs to communities, not to the corpus by default

A public source is not enough to open a high-gate record. The gate requires domain review, consent reasoning, publication/no-publication reasoning, and rollback pathways.
