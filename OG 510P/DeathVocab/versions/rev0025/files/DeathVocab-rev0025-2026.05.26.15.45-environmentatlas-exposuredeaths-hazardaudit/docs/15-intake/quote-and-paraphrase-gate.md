# Quote and paraphrase gate

Exact words carry more identity and intimacy than summaries. They also create false authority: a single sentence can start sounding like the meaning of dying.

DeathVocab therefore treats quote permission as separate from interview permission.

## Blocking tests

A quote should be blocked if it reveals a third party, points to a rare death, includes suicide method detail, exposes a child, reveals restricted ritual material, creates employer or institutional risk, or becomes memorable mainly because it is sensational.

## Paraphrase is not a loophole

A paraphrase may be safer than a quote, but it must not launder words the contributor did not allow. It must preserve perspective and uncertainty.

## Synthetic scenes

Synthetic or composite scenes may be used only for office dry runs. They are never evidence and never corpus records.
