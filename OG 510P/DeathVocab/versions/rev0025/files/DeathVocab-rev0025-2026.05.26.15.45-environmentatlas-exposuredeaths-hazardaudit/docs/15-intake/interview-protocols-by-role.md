# Interview protocols by witness role

A generic interview guide would flatten the cube. The family witness, hospice aide, chaplain, nurse, funeral director, dispatcher, coroner, and tradition holder do not carry the same evidence.

Ask for scenes, language, uncertainty, and context. Do not ask anyone to perform wisdom.

## Repeating questions across roles

- What did you notice before there were words for it?
- What phrase did someone use in the room?
- What do people misunderstand about this moment?
- What would be unsafe or misleading to publish?
- What should remain private, restricted, or not mine to say?

## Practitioner craft

Ask what is in the hands, ears, nose, pacing, and language of the work. Do not ask for identifiable patient stories or advice.

## Family memory

Ask for one moment, not the whole death. Make stopping easy. Do not treat grief as raw material.

## Tradition-holder review

Ask what may be safely named, what must be restricted, who has authority, and what language should be preserved. Do not ask for interior practice.
