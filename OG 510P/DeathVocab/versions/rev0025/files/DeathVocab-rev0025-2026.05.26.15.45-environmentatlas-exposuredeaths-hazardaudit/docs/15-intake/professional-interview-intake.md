# Professional witness intake guide

This is a guide for interviewing hospice workers, nurses, doctors, doulas, chaplains, social workers, funeral directors, coroners, aides, volunteers, first responders, and others who repeatedly encounter dying or its aftermath.

## Before the story

- Explain the project as observational, not prescriptive.
- Explain that patient-identifying details are not wanted.
- Ask what role/title/geography can be shown, if any.
- Explain edit and withdrawal rights.
- Ask whether any institution, family, or living person could be exposed.

## Moment questions

- What was the bounded moment?
- Where in the dying timeline did it occur?
- What did you see, hear, smell, touch, or notice socially?
- What words did family or staff use?
- What term would your field use?
- What did you do, if anything?
- What did you avoid doing?
- What do new practitioners misunderstand about this?
- What would a family member search for after seeing this?
- What should this record not imply?

## Safety stops

Stop or redirect if the account begins to reveal patient identity, suicide method details, restricted ritual detail, active litigation, child content without high gate, or graphic description without a clear educational purpose.
