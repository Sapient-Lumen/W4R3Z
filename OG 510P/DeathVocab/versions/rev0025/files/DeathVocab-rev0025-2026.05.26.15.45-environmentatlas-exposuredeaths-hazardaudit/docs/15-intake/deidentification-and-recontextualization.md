# De-identification and recontextualization

Removing names is not enough. Dates, towns, institutions, rare diseases, family structures, newsworthy circumstances, rituals, and exact phrasing can identify people.

But stripping everything is also a harm: it produces generic death. The standard is to remove identifying detail while preserving the context that makes the record honest.

A safe record should still say whose perspective it carries, what setting and phase matter, what is known, what is unknown, and what the archive refuses to infer.
