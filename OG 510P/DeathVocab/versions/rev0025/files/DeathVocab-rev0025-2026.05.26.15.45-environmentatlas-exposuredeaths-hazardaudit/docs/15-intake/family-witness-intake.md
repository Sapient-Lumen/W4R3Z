# Family or bereaved witness intake guide

This guide is for accounts from relatives, friends, caregivers, and others who witnessed dying or the first aftermath.

## Before the story

- The contributor does not need to prove grief.
- They can pause, skip, or withdraw.
- The archive does not need names, exact dates, exact places, or rare identifying details.
- Publication is separate from telling the story.

## Moment questions

- What is the moment you most want understood?
- What did you notice with your senses?
- What did you call it at the time?
- What words did professionals use, if any?
- What did you search later, or wish you could have searched?
- Did anyone explain it in a way that helped or hurt?
- What should readers not assume from your account?
- Are there family members, cultural practices, conflicts, institutions, or legal concerns that make this unsafe to publish?

## High gate topics

Pediatric death, suicide, overdose, custody, violence, restricted rituals, and identifiable family conflict should usually remain private unless a senior review decides there is a careful reason to proceed.
