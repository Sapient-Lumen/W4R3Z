# Rev0007 decision-language review packets

Rev0007 adds five review packets:

- `DV-RP-0011`: advance directives, POLST, DNR/DNACPR/DNI, CPR, and code status.
- `DV-RP-0012`: comfort transitions, withholding/withdrawing, extubation, sedation, and comfort kits.
- `DV-RP-0013`: VSED and MAID.
- `DV-RP-0014`: brain death, DCD, OPO, honor walk, and pronouncement.
- `DV-RP-0015`: goals-of-care meetings, surrogate pressure, heroic-measures language, ethics consults, and no-surrogate cases.

All are blocking. No record in the rev0007 range may be rendered publicly until the relevant packet is closed with recorded deltas.
