
# Rev0006 review packets

Rev0006 adds four review packets:

- `DV-RP-0007` — utterance and last-words myth review.
- `DV-RP-0008` — near-death awareness, delirium, and symbolic-language boundary.
- `DV-RP-0009` — goodbye, forgiveness, estrangement, and permission language noncoercion.
- `DV-RP-0010` — euphemism, plain language, accessibility, and cultural wording.

These packets are blocking. No utterance record should become public until the relevant packet has produced durable review deltas.

The review should not merely polish tone. It should ask whether a reader might leave with a false duty, a false prediction, a false spiritual conclusion, a false clinical conclusion, or new guilt about words said or unsaid.
