# Clinical review checklist

`CLINICAL-REVIEW-CHECKLIST.json` exists because the corpus is now capable of sounding more authoritative than it is.

Before any public rendering, a reviewer with hospice or palliative expertise must check:

- source support for every clinical-sounding claim
- prediction boundaries
- medical-advice boundaries
- swallowing, fluids, breathlessness, medication, and body-handling risks
- tone around guilt, absence, hope, and distress
- skin-tone and body-description accuracy
- cultural humility around visions, rituals, and meaning

The builder or editor cannot self-certify this review.
