# Source intake map

Rev0001 does not yet define a full acquisition protocol. It names the lanes that protocol must cover.

## Source lanes

- Public books and essays.
- Peer-reviewed and professional literature.
- Hospice and palliative-care professional interviews.
- Death doula and chaplain interviews.
- Funeral director, coroner, medical examiner, crematory, cemetery, and first-responder accounts.
- Family and bereaved-person accounts.
- First-person accounts by dying people or survivors of resuscitation/near-death events.
- Tradition-holder accounts.
- Public oral/community sources.
- Underused institutional records, when ethically and legally usable.

## Intake questions

- What is the source type?
- Who has authority to tell this?
- What permission exists?
- What details must be withheld?
- What harm could publication cause?
- What terms would a reader search for?
- What stability label is honest?
- What would defeat or withdraw this record later?
