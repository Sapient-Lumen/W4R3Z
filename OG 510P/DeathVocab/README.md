# DeathVocab

## Language for what a witness carries away

DeathVocab asks how to give people words for dying, death and its aftermath without taking the remembered scene away from them. Someone may remember a sound, an unfinished sentence, a room emptied of visitors, or a familiar object that suddenly feels different. A dictionary can supply a term. This project wants the term to lead back into the circumstances: who noticed, what they could know, what they thought it meant, and what remains unsettled.

Its purpose is orientation rather than a reassuring explanation of everything. The [charter][charter] puts specificity beside care: a situated account can be more useful than a broad claim about what everyone experiences. The ambition extends beyond the bedside into work, kinship, institutions, ritual, paperwork and the objects left behind.

The supplied rev0025 is a substantial research foundation: 422 quarantined record files, linked vocabulary and query maps, source and review structures, prototype reader views, and executable consistency checks. These are public-source pilots and seed-bound scaffolds, not 422 collected personal testimonies. Its public reader surfaces remain disabled and it reports no private testimony. That distinction matters to reading the examples below as designs for a corpus, not already approved accounts or care guidance.

**Start with [the witnessed-moment method][moment], then compare [the phone-tree record][calls] and [the belongings record][objects].** They make the project's purpose concrete before the full machinery of its schema.

## The scene is the unit; a term is one way into it

The [record schema][schema] makes a witnessed moment more than an anecdote with keywords. A record joins a bounded scene to witness position, sensory channels, ordinary-language queries, professional terms, source basis and limitations. Separate content layers hold observation, source synthesis, interpretation, uncertainty and intended audience orientation. Publication and review states travel with that material. Some entries map a category of experience rather than narrating a particular witnessed event; the primary-unit label does not turn a synthesis into an observation.

This arrangement keeps two very different operations apart: finding something that resembles a memory, and establishing what happened in that particular case. A phrase can lead to several related records without selecting one explanation as the truth. Conversely, a professional term can lead back to the language people actually use, rather than treating their words as an error to be corrected away.

The [glossary crosswalk][crosswalk] and [query router][router] give that idea a concrete form. Crosswalk entries associate terms with record IDs and cautions. Router entries collect several reader phrases, their destination IDs and a short boundary statement. They are inspectable mapping data, not evidence that a live semantic search service has been built. In this snapshot the router explicitly disables public use.

An especially useful example is the term *phone tree*. The crosswalk connects it both to the first notifications after death and to a later sensory-room record about interruptions. One expression can locate an administrative responsibility and the sound or rhythm of a room. Search is richer when the archive does not insist that every phrase belongs in only one conceptual box.

## Two records that explain why texture matters

[Record63][calls] treats the first calls after a death as a social event, not simply a checklist. Its editorial question is how a network learns what has happened: who is told, who carries the news, who is overlooked, and which words are used. Its source synthesis keeps differing settings and local requirements visible rather than prescribing a universal sequence of calls. The useful knowledge sought is partly about the gap between the event and its becoming known to other people.

[Record77][objects] turns to belongings, clothes and the room. Here an object is neither decorative detail nor just property awaiting disposal. It can locate a memory, a pressure from other people, a sensory association or a conflict over timing. The draft asks how an archive could preserve those differences without converting grief into a task schedule.

This second record also exposes an important distinction in the project's evidence. It expressly says its precise belongings layer is under-sourced and admitted as a source-debt record. The presence of a detailed scene description is therefore not proof that a particular witness supplied it. Its current value is to formulate what future inquiry must attend to, with the missing evidence still attached. That is more useful than either hiding the gap or deleting a worthwhile question because the answer is not yet earned.

## A room distributes access, not only atmosphere

The [setting note][setting] treats place as a condition of knowledge and authority. A home, hospital, care facility or closed institution determines different opportunities to remain present, speak, touch, receive information or be excluded. Recording a setting is therefore part of explaining how an account became possible, not just painting its background.

The [chosen-family record][chosen] makes the problem especially legible. Knowledge of a person's life, recognition in paperwork and access to the room need not belong to the same people. Its design preserves the witness position and the limits of that position rather than declaring either personal intimacy or an official role sufficient authority over the whole event.

The [axis registry][axes] allows records to occupy multiple locations across perspective, setting, time, trajectory, sensory experience and other dimensions. These are finding aids open to revision, not a final classification of human death. A reader can compare what different witnesses encounter without flattening their accounts into interchangeable evidence.

The [inside-view method][inside] carries this distinction into the verbs used to describe experience. A person's report, another person's observation, an interpretation and a professional description are different kinds of support. A quiet body does not by itself establish a peaceful experience. The archive's proposed discipline is to preserve meaning without silently upgrading an external observation into access to someone's inner life.

## Three audiences, one accountable record

The [three-surface design][surfaces] proposes different views of the same corpus:

- A plain-language witness view begins with what someone remembers and the words they can find.
- A practitioner apprenticeship view asks what experienced people notice, how they explain it, and what novices miss.
- A provenance view exposes sources, evidence status, permission and review dependencies.

The three supplied prototypes for one breathing-related record show the difference in form. The [lay draft][lay] experiments with accessible explanation; the [apprenticeship draft][apprentice] is largely a set of questions for experienced workers; the [provenance draft][provenance] identifies sources and the outstanding review. They do not contain three independent confirmations of the underlying claims. They explore how a shared record could serve different needs without making those needs into separate truths.

For a researcher or editor, the current materials can already be read as a worked design: follow a phrase to a record, inspect its source basis, compare its audience views, and identify the testimony or review still missing. An eventual public service would require that further work. Its value cannot be established merely by writing a more polished prototype.

## Growing beyond a beautiful bedside

The [development history][history] broadens the inquiry from early observed cues into language, aftermath, cultural frames, settings, witness roles, material details, inside-view evidence and social relations. Later layers make absence, official traces, workers' burdens, institutional opacity, body disposition and environmental exposure explicit subjects. The project refuses to let peaceful, well-attended hospice rooms stand for every death.

The [rev0025 exposure layer][exposure] is a particularly important extension. Environment becomes part of the explanation when it changes who is exposed, who can witness, how a death enters records and how long families wait. This connects personal vocabulary to infrastructure and scale without turning the archive into a hazard-response manual.

The same history revises the apparatus rather than only adding topics. Rev0014 relaxes an obsolete fixed class enumeration while retaining a tag-format contract, and normalizes inconsistent field shapes. The [fast checker][check] compares record identities and counts, source references and card dependencies, alias destinations, review fields and specialized audit totals. These are implemented checks on the archive's structure. They do not perform the clinical, cultural or ethical judgment named by the fields, and a successful check would not make a synthetic scene into testimony.

## A foundation that can be continued without freezing its taxonomy

The short [note on overconstraint][freedom] distinguishes ethical commitments from replaceable architecture. Consent, source integrity and protection against exploitation are not reasons to make the current schema permanent. The categories, ledgers and reader views are proposals that a better account may challenge.

That is an important invitation to a recipient. Continuing this work might mean improving a crosswalk, resolving a source debt, discovering that two categories obscure the same experience, or simplifying a structure that no longer helps a reader. More records are not automatically more understanding. The serious test is whether a person or researcher can find language while retaining the particularity, uncertainty and dignity of what that language names.

Read beside [Parables](../Parables/README.md) for a different use of concrete objects and compressed scenes, [LivingChristFigures](../LivingChristFigures/README.md) for accounts of costly care, and [The Good](../The-Good/README.md) for explicit moral argument. These are neighboring inquiries, not substitutes for the source work or specialist review DeathVocab calls for.
