# DeathVocab

## Words for a room people may enter frightened

DeathVocab begins from a humane problem: ordinary people near dying and bereavement often need language before they possess professional vocabulary. What was heard, seen, remembered or misunderstood may be difficult to describe, and a search for words can itself expose someone to prediction, shame or spectacle.

The [charter](versions/rev0025/files/DeathVocab-rev0025-2026.05.26.15.45-environmentatlas-exposuredeaths-hazardaudit/docs/00-meta/charter.md) orders its duties deliberately: protection, specificity, retrieval and humility. The project is not valuable simply because it can accumulate more accounts. It asks whether a record can remain situated in a role, setting, time and source without pretending to speak for everybody.

## A scene-first reading route

1. Read the charter before the vocabulary. It explains why contributors, the dead, bereaved people and frightened readers are not merely sources of material.
2. Open the [threshold atlas](versions/rev0025/files/DeathVocab-rev0025-2026.05.26.15.45-environmentatlas-exposuredeaths-hazardaudit/docs/20-taxonomy/threshold-atlas.md). It organizes an entrance around changes someone might notice in a room rather than requiring a medical term first. The atlas also names dangers in interpretation: prediction, guilt, voyeurism and both over-spiritualizing and over-medicalizing.
3. Read the [ethics and consent boundary](versions/rev0025/files/DeathVocab-rev0025-2026.05.26.15.45-environmentatlas-exposuredeaths-hazardaudit/docs/10-method/ethics-and-consent-firebreak.md), then the [supplied archive orientation](versions/rev0025/files/DeathVocab-rev0025-2026.05.26.15.45-environmentatlas-exposuredeaths-hazardaudit/START_HERE.md) before browsing records.

## What this snapshot is—and is not

The supplied revision identifies quarantined public-source pilots and seed-bound scaffolds, with no private testimony admitted. “Quarantined” is an evidence status inside the work, not a promise that public publication has certified those records. The earlier count and revision headings retained in the README should be read with the later update.

This is a vocabulary and research archive. It does not predict an individual’s death, prescribe care, determine what a family should do, or establish one universal phenomenology of dying. A moving account can remain particular; the guide’s role is to keep that particularity visible.

Read beside [LivingChristFigures](../LivingChristFigures/README.md) for care at thresholds and [The Good](../The-Good/README.md) for the broader moral place of non-abandonment. Their connection is a question for reading, not a clinical recommendation.
