# DeathVocab

A vocabulary and research archive about dying, care, institutions, ritual, language and aftermath. The supplied START_HERE identifies 422 quarantined public-source pilots or seed-bound scaffolds and zero private testimony. This public archival copy preserves those internal quarantine labels; it does not admit the records as verified advice or authorize medical, rescue, legal or body-handling procedures.
