## What counts as progress from here

Progress means:

- tightening the canon,
- adding or removing notes only when the portfolio meaningfully improves,
- refining watchpoints,
- repairing internal inconsistencies,
- and preserving clear demotion routes when a note stops earning its place.

## What not to do

Do not let the archive become:

- a trend digest,
- a collection of quotes,
- a giant literature review,
- a single-domain obsession,
- or a pile of dramatic but untestable futures prose.
