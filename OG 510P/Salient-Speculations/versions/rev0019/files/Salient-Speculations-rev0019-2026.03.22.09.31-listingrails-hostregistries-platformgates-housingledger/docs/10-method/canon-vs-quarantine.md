# Canon vs quarantine

## Canon

Canon is for notes that are already useful for steering thought.
They do not need to be certain, but they must be:

- grounded,
- portable,
- broad,
- and disciplined enough to shape future additions.

## Quarantine

Quarantine is not a trash can.
It is the right place for:

- bold extensions of canon notes,
- under-evidenced but important hunches,
- architecture ideas that need more source pressure,
- and claims that are too attractive to trust immediately.

## Promotion rule

A quarantined note can be promoted only when:

1. its mechanism becomes clearer,
2. its watchpoints become sharper,
3. and it gains enough grounding to be more useful than dangerous.

## Demotion rule

Canon notes should be demoted when they become:

- redundant,
- stale,
- overfit to a moment,
- or structurally weaker than the rest of the portfolio.
