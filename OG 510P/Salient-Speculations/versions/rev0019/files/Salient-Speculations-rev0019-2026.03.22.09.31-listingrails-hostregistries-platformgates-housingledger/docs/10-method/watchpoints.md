# Cross-note watchpoints

These are portfolio-level watchpoints that may update multiple notes at once.

## Infrastructure

- special utility tariffs or queue reforms explicitly tailored to data centres or AI loads
- co-located generation deals for compute campuses
- rapid expansion of behind-the-meter power, storage, or microgrids for large AI sites

## Climate and territory

- expansion of state-backed residual insurance pools
- mortgage, bond, or disclosure rules tightening around climate-exposed property
- local fiscal stress correlated with insurance nonrenewal or premium spikes

## Demography and care

- bilateral care-worker migration agreements
- automation or robotics pilots in eldercare at national scale
- family or immigration policy framed explicitly around care-system stability

## Biology and security

- broader adoption of sequence screening requirements outside public procurement
- attestation and logging requirements for synthesis orders or benchtop tools
- cross-border convergence on screening norms

## Trust and media

- provenance requirements in procurement, journalism, or platform ranking
- default credentialing in capture and editing tools
- emergence of premium “credentialed media” lanes

## Security and airspace

- civil anti-drone hardening around substations, ports, depots, and public venues
- rapid procurement of directed-energy or low-cost counter-UAS systems
- new low-altitude regulatory or geofencing regimes

## Education

- growth in oral exams, practicals, supervised projects, or live performance assessment
- AI tutoring entering mainstream public funding frameworks
- increased emphasis on AI literacy and authentic assessment over content delivery alone

## Observability and geospatial audit

- remote sensing named as primary evidence rather than merely supporting evidence in subsidy, emissions, land-use, or disaster programs
- geolocation requirements entering trade, grants, insurance, or compliance workflows
- fights over false positives, due process, and appeals in machine-seen compliance regimes
- demand for shared public audit layers over land disturbance, emissions, or post-disaster damage

## Identity, access, and threshold proofs

- wallet or mDL acceptance expanding in banking, public services, education, or healthcare
- regulatory demand for highly effective age assurance or other narrow eligibility proofs
- verifier tooling and procurement language centred on selective disclosure, issuer trust, and credential revocation
- public fights over recovery, exclusion, pseudonymity, and coercive over-requesting of attributes

## Products, trade, and object passports

- sector-specific digital product passport rules moving from framework law into actual delegated acts or rollout deadlines
- customs, e-commerce, or procurement systems checking passport existence or querying passport data directly
- facility identifiers, conformity credentials, or traceability-event standards converging across sectors
- disputes over commercial secrecy, data quality, correction rights, and exclusion of smaller or foreign suppliers
