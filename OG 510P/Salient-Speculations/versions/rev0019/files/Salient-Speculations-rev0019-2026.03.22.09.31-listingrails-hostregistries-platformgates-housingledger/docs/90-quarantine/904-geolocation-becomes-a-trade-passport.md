
# 904 — Geolocation becomes a trade passport

**Status:** quarantine

## Thesis

Plot-level or facility-level geolocation may become a routine condition of market access in more sectors.
Not just “where did this come from?” but “can you prove it with coordinates that can be cross-checked against machine observation?”

## Why it is quarantined

The EU deforestation regime makes the direction legible, but the broader cross-sector generalization is not yet demonstrated strongly enough to canonize.
The archive should preserve the move without pretending it is already a settled template.

## Why preserve it anyway

If this pattern spreads, it changes who can sell into rich markets, how compliance vendors acquire power, how small producers are included or excluded, and how trade disputes get evidenced.

## Watchpoints

- more trade or procurement regimes requiring plot-level or facility-level coordinates
- compliance platforms built around geolocation validation rather than narrative attestation alone
- disputes over whether remote sensing can adequately represent mixed, informal, or smallholder production systems
- financial institutions or insurers treating coordinate quality as a risk variable
