# 902 — Actuarial retreat precedes municipal restructuring

**Status:** quarantine

## Thesis

In some regions, insurance retreat may begin a longer chain: weaker housing markets, weaker tax bases, strained schools and services, municipal debt stress, and eventually a quieter form of territorial unmaking.

## Why it is quarantined

Treasury, climate, and OECD catastrophic-risk evidence justify concern, but the full cascade is still more inferred than demonstrated here.
The archive should not treat this as inevitable without stronger direct evidence on municipal finance channels and timing.

## Why preserve it anyway

This is one of the most serious possible consequences of note 002.
If true, climate adaptation politics will not be only about physical exposure but about the solvency and governability of places.

## Watchpoints

- local bond spread changes linked to repeated insurance instability
- state takeovers or extraordinary support for climate-stressed municipalities
- school closures or service retrenchment in heavily repriced zones
- clustered housing illiquidity following nonrenewal waves
