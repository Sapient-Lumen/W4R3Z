# Charter

Salient Speculations exists to preserve **surprising ideas that are important early**.

## Primary aims

1. Find broad speculative claims that deserve attention before they become consensus.
2. Keep those claims small, inspectable, and connected to real mechanism sketches.
3. Preserve a distinction between what is canonized and what remains quarantined.
4. Prefer cross-domain leverage over single-domain cleverness.
5. Build a durable archive that future sessions can reopen without rereading huge transcripts.

## What counts as progress

Progress means converting a vague hunch into a compact object that has:

- a clear thesis,
- causal mechanism,
- source anchors,
- watchpoints,
- and explicit weakening conditions.

## Failure modes to resist

- mistaking mood for mechanism,
- mistaking novelty for salience,
- mistaking drama for breadth,
- mistaking source accumulation for understanding,
- mistaking one hot domain for the whole frontier,
- mistaking quarantine for embarrassment rather than good method.

## Working posture

The archive should be bold enough to notice real shifts early and disciplined enough to remain useful after the mood changes.
