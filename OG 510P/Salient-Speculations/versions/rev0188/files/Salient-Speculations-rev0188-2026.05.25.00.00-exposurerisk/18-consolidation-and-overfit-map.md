# Consolidation and Overfit Map

The archive is now large enough that uncontrolled growth is a risk. rev0183 introduces a consolidation map: which dossiers should remain standalone, which should become sub-states inside a lifecycle model, and which should be watched for overfit.

## Current overfit risk

The strongest overfit risk remains managed legibility: every domain can be redescribed as proof, state, notice, registry, appeal, and correction. That lens is productive but can become tautological.

A new dossier should therefore pass one extra test:

> Does this dossier teach the cube a new mechanism, or only rename an already-modeled state in a new sector?

## Keep standalone

Keep dossiers standalone when they introduce a genuinely transferable mechanism:

- `cryptographic-agility-registries-become-trust-transition-infrastructure`
- `notified-body-queue-position-becomes-market-access-infrastructure`
- `delegated-ai-agent-authority-logs-become-consumer-protection-infrastructure`
- `data-space-access-rules-become-industrial-border-controls`
- `provenance-nonparticipation-labels-become-media-trust-infrastructure`
- `graph-poisoning-becomes-a-standing-governance-attack-surface`
- `resolver-capture-becomes-a-hidden-passport-bottleneck`
- `recipient-graph-privacy-proofs-become-broker-trust-products`

## Consider consolidating into lifecycle models

Some dossiers are valuable but may now function better as states inside a model:

- appeal-stay labels;
- correction-materiality thresholds;
- non-reliance packet states;
- split/merge correction notices;
- amendment-recipient registries;
- identity-match appeals;
- source-object identity warranties;
- transformation-code escrow.

These should remain as dossiers for now, but the next major cleanup should add a short tag at the top of each:

> Lifecycle role: reliance-object lifecycle / dispute and correction phase.

## Consolidation candidates

| Candidate group | Why consolidate | Recommended treatment |
|---|---|---|
| freshness guarantees, expiry dates, revalidation windows, history-retention floors | all describe evidence shelf life | create one evidence-freshness model and keep only strongest sectoral examples standalone |
| successor maps, successor gaps, successor-map freshness | one handoff/fallback family | convert into a successor-governance mini-model |
| scoreboards, grade floors, public registries, scanner-ingestion surfaces | all become ranking surfaces | create a ranking-and-shortlisting model |
| exceptions, waivers, override constitutions, independent signers | one exception-governance family | keep emergency override and independent signer dossiers; fold minor states into model |
| rendering, style packs, readable/structured divergence | one authoritative-presentation family | create an evidentiary-rendering lifecycle |

## New negative rule

Do not promote a dossier merely because it can be written in the archive's voice.

Promote only when it adds one of these:

- a new artifact family;
- a new enforcement surface;
- a new adversarial pressure;
- a new distributional burden;
- a new lifecycle stage;
- a new transfer pattern;
- a new falsifier.

## rev0186 overfit check: delegation variants

The authority family is now at risk of noun proliferation. Future files should not be promoted merely because a new portal, wallet, API, AI framework, or professional role needs authorization.

Promote only if the candidate adds one of the following:

- a new scope grammar;
- a new representative-of-record object;
- a new authority chain involving legal persons, service accounts, or AI agents;
- a new revocation/propagation failure;
- a new offline or break-glass fallback model;
- a new abuse telemetry or safeguarding surface;
- a new dispute path for authority denial or misuse.

Otherwise, add it to `25-authority-and-delegation-lifecycle.md` or the seed bank rather than creating a standalone dossier.


## rev0187 consolidation warning: do not promote every provenance label

The lineage refactor demotes weak future candidates of the form “X gets provenance” unless they introduce a new lifecycle stage or failure mode. Prefer adding state terms to `28-provenance-and-lineage-lifecycle.md` over promoting a dossier when the novelty is merely a new market adopting signatures, manifests, passports, or content credentials.

Promote only if the candidate exposes one of: subject binding, capture retention, transformation replay, wrapper divergence, resolver successor state, redaction boundary, derived-use rights, provenance diffing, or custody-break certification.
