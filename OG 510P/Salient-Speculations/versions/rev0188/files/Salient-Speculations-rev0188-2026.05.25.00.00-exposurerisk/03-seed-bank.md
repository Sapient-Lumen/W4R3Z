# Seed Bank

## After stressgraph promotion

The queue should now privilege candidates that either stress-test an existing proof object, complete a product-biography state, or make anti-legibility operational. Avoid adding more status labels unless the candidate changes who may rely, who may refuse, who may appeal, or who pays for repair.

## Keep / promote soon

1. **Repair-event privacy profiles become resale infrastructure.**  
   Repair records will need to prove recall clearance, authorized service, parts replacement, or firmware remedy without exposing unrelated diagnostics, location histories, household behavior, or prior-owner identity.

2. **Refurbished-identity splits become passport disputes.**  
   Once components are replaced, firmware is changed, batteries are swapped, or products are remanufactured, parties will fight over whether the product is the same passport subject, a child object, a new product, or a non-comparable hybrid.

3. **Software-update histories become used-product diligence.**  
   Used cars, medical devices, appliances, industrial equipment, phones, batteries, and grid devices may need evidence that security, safety, and feature-critical updates were applied, skipped, rolled back, or blocked.

4. **Stay-label abuse filters become appeal-system infrastructure.**  
   Once a pending appeal can suspend eligibility, exclude scores, reserve holdbacks, or restrict processing, brokers need standardized frivolous, duplicate, unsupported, late-filed, wrong-forum, leverage-only, and out-of-scope labels that limit stay effect without erasing appeal records.

5. **Escrow-access arbitration becomes a confidentiality market.**  
   Once appeal reviewers need proprietary mapping code, security-sensitive lookup tables, raw source extracts, before/after subject graphs, recipient-graph details, materiality formulas, stay rationales, and manual-review notes, disputes need neutral access rules that allow replay without turning every buyer into a code auditor.

6. **Custody-failure fault classes become broker scorecard inputs.**  
   Once packet replay, amendment, recipient routing, stay labeling, materiality review, and appeal adjudication depend on retained code, schemas, source snapshots, runtime environments, validation artifacts, correction ledgers, recipient graphs, and materiality schedules, brokers may be scored separately for lost snapshots, unpinned schemas, stale replay environments, and incomplete appeal trails.

7. **Compliance-object incident reporting becomes a new notification class.**  
   Forged passports, spoofed stay labels, compromised issuer keys, fake due-diligence statement references, and suppressed non-reliance markers may need incident reporting that is neither ordinary cyber notification nor ordinary product-recall notice.

8. **Proof-request overreach becomes a procurement appeal category.**  
   If public proof profiles exist, a supplier should be able to challenge a buyer's request for unnecessary attributes, full histories, hidden recipient graphs, or trade-secret-bearing evidence as off-profile overreach.

9. **Resolver-continuity insurance becomes a product-passport add-on.**  
   If product-passport resolution gates customs, repair, recall, resale, and procurement, insurers may price endpoint uptime, mirror coverage, escrowed routing, and service-provider succession.

10. **Recall-clear proofs become marketplace listing conditions.**  
   Marketplaces may require a freshness-bounded proof that an item is not subject to an unresolved recall, has received remedy, or is outside scope despite similarity to affected batches.

## rev0184 seeds after freshness refactor

- **Freshness insurance riders.** Insurers may eventually price not only cyber posture or compliance status, but the freshness budget of the evidence used to represent that posture.
- **Archive-only abuse registries.** Markets may need lists of proof artifacts that are authentic and retained but prohibited for current reliance.
- **Proof-age UX regulation.** Consumer and public-service interfaces may be required to display when a status was last checked, not merely whether it passed.
- **Grace-state public goods.** Small suppliers and vulnerable citizens may need public grace registries to avoid exclusion when renewal queues or source outages are not their fault.

## rev0185 seeds after remedy-lifecycle refactor

- **Remedy escrow access markets.** Neutral reviewers may need access to raw evidence, source code, model traces, or recipient graphs under confidentiality constraints before an appeal can be meaningful.
- **Standing-denial metrics.** Appeal systems may need to publish how often they reject filings for lack of standing, insufficient authority, wrong forum, or incomplete identity proof.
- **Remedy-portability receipts.** A user or supplier may need to carry proof that an appeal is pending, sustained, denied, or externally escalated across platforms, brokers, insurers, and public agencies.
- **Language-access clocks.** Remedy clocks may not fairly start until the decision notice and appeal path are available in a language or format the affected party can use.
- **Reviewer-conflict ledgers.** Human-review markets may need evidence that reviewers were not the original decision maker, vendor, counterparty, or financially interested party.
