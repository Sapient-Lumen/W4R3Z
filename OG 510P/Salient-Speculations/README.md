# Salient Speculations

## Be early without being carried away

The project wants to preserve surprising ideas before they become ordinary, while keeping enough structure that the idea remains useful after the mood changes. Its charter names a demanding minimum: thesis, mechanism, source anchors, watchpoints and conditions that would weaken the claim.

That is a different ambition from a collection of predictions that only become legible after they appear correct. The reader should be able to see why a possibility seemed important and what would have counted against it.

## Where permission and loss move

The [listing-permission thesis](versions/rev0019/files/Salient-Speculations-rev0019-2026.03.22.09.31-listingrails-hostregistries-platformgates-housingledger/docs/20-canon/025-listing-permission-rails-and-the-end-of-post-hoc-lodging-enforcement.md) proposes a shift in where a housing rule has its practical effect. Instead of relying only on a complaint after a stay, an authority can require a registration identity, a platform can check it, and a booking may be stopped upstream. The research question then moves from the wording of a rule alone to who maintains the registry, which exceptions it represents, and who can correct or challenge a listing's status.

The note makes predictions rather than treating its examples as universal: more jurisdictions may adopt such checks, while people may also route around mainstream booking systems. Patchy implementation, successful resistance or a return to complaint-only enforcement would weaken the broader pattern. Those watchpoints make it possible to disagree about the forecast without pretending the cited arrangements never existed. They are historical research directions, not current housing-law advice.

The later [exposure lifecycle](versions/rev0188/files/Salient-Speculations-rev0188-2026.05.25.00.00-exposurerisk/31-exposure-liability-lifecycle.md) asks a related but distinct question: when a piece of evidence becomes consequential, who bears the loss and what actually responds? A certificate, a warranty, a notice deadline and an insurance payment are different objects. The proposed lifecycle follows a claim from allocation and attachment through notice, defense, payment, renewal and an exposure's possible afterlife. Its own proposed falsifier is that real counterparties continue treating proof quality as generic due diligence rather than a priced or actionable exposure, or that contract terms remain too particular to generalize. This is a model for inquiry, not proof of a legal or insurance outcome.

## What the apparatus should let you ask

A state label can sharpen a distinction without making the underlying uncertainty disappear. Ask whether a dossier names a mechanism, merely redescribes a trend, or identifies an observable event that could change the assessment. Ask also whether its cross-domain connection survives differences between those domains.

The later README preserves earlier release material under the current update. Its inventory counts are not substitutes for reading the claims. The source anchors and forecasts have not been revalidated merely by being archived.

The collection’s quarantine distinction is useful when it protects a live possibility without quietly presenting it as established. This edition offers an entrance, not a recommendation to make a financial, legal or policy decision from a dossier.

Read beside [Theory of Everything](../Theory-of-Everything/README.md) on the obligations of a proposed bridge, and [Immoral-Wealth](../Immoral-Wealth/README.md) on what a change in access can mean for power.
