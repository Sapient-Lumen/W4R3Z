# Scenario: same-scope competing heads held for review

A native future-incompat import and a same-scope derived summary both survive retention, but they do not justify one silent winner.

What this scenario proves:
- the contradiction itself is first-class;
- the contender set, precedence rule, and review-queue status are preserved;
- stronger `current-active`, `public-example`, and `frozen-public-head` uses remain blocked;
- a derived summary cannot silently become the unique operational head just because it is easier to quote.

What this scenario does **not** justify:
- automatic public/frozen promotion;
- a unique current-active story;
- semantic back-write from the summary onto the native source.
