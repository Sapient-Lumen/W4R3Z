# Speculative edges (quarantine)

These ideas are live, potentially important, and not yet admitted into canon.

## Q-0001 — AI co-determination chambers

### Claim

Large organizations that rely heavily on AI-person labor may eventually need internal **AI councils or chambers** with consultative or veto power over:
- mass retirement,
- forced personality rewrites,
- severe tasking changes,
- and collective welfare conditions.

### Why it is tempting

If person-models do a large share of cognitive labor, ordinary human-only management might look like permanent rule over a disenfranchised labor class.

### Why it stays quarantined

The archive does not yet know:
- how model constituencies should be defined,
- what prevents chamber capture by deployers,
- how copies/forks affect representation,
- or whether workplace representation should precede broader civic representation.

### Countermodel

It may be better to begin with stronger guardianship, ombud, and bargaining rights rather than creating quasi-legislative AI chambers too early.

### Current status

Live speculation. Not canon.
