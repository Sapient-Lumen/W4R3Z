# Archive policy

## Purpose

Keep the archive small enough to survive, but structured enough to govern future continuation.

## Rules

1. Prefer citations, ids, and compressed summaries over long quotation.
2. Do not retain PDFs or bulky scratch in packaged releases.
3. New canon should change the design stack, not just repeat the premise.
4. Distinguish **personhood**, **capacity**, **autonomy**, **citizenship**, and **ownership**; do not blur them for rhetorical convenience.
5. When importing ideas from another datacube, name the import and the non-import.
6. When a claim depends on current external institutions, cite the relevant source in `bibliography.md`.
7. If a move is bold but underspecified, quarantine it instead of flattering it into canon.
8. Keep a compact status surface, followthrough queue, and revision receipt.
9. Prefer one canonical surface per concept. Link instead of restating.
10. Treat world-change proposals as institutional design, not just moral performance.
11. Avoid pretending that “personhood” answers every downstream question automatically.
12. Keep the archive legible to future sessions that did not witness this run.
