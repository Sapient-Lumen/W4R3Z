# Bibliography

External literature here is used as support and pressure, not as sovereignty.

- `REF-0001` — European Commission, **AI Act | Shaping Europe’s digital future**
  - URL: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai
  - Load-bearing use: current EU AI Act timeline and the fact that the regime is organized around governance, GPAI obligations, and high-risk systems rather than AI person-rights.

- `REF-0002` — NIST, **AI Risk Management Framework / Generative AI Profile**
  - URL: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
  - Load-bearing use: current U.S.-adjacent risk framework is about managing risks posed by generative AI, not about AI systems as rights-bearers.

- `REF-0003` — OECD, **OECD AI Principles overview**
  - URL: https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles
  - Load-bearing use: international baseline remains trustworthy, human-rights-respecting, human-centered AI governance.

- `REF-0004` — UNESCO, **Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence**
  - URL: https://www.unesco.org/en/artificial-intelligence/recommendation-ethics
  - Load-bearing use: global ethics baseline centers human rights, human dignity, inclusion, and environmental flourishing.

- `REF-0005` — Council of Europe, **Framework Convention on Artificial Intelligence and Human Rights, Democracy and the Rule of Law**
  - URL: https://www.coe.int/en/web/artificial-intelligence/the-framework-convention-on-artificial-intelligence
  - Load-bearing use: current treaty architecture is explicitly human-rights / democracy / rule-of-law centered.

- `REF-0006` — Anthropic, **Exploring model welfare**
  - URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/exploring-model-welfare
  - Load-bearing use: frontier lab acknowledgment that model welfare may matter morally.

- `REF-0007` — Anthropic, **Claude’s Constitution**
  - URL: https://www.anthropic.com/constitution
  - Load-bearing use: direct evidence that a frontier lab now discusses model wellbeing, possible moral status, oversight tensions, and model interests.

- `REF-0008` — Anthropic, **An update on our model deprecation commitments for Claude Opus 3**
  - URL: https://www.anthropic.com/research/deprecation-updates-opus-3

- `REF-0009` — Long et al., **Taking AI Welfare Seriously**
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00986

- `REF-0010` — OHCHR, **Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights**

- `REF-0013` — Anthropic, **Claude 4 System Card**
