# AI Personhood — rev0111

**North star:** build a compact, cumulative archive for designing a world that is fit for life with the standing assumption that **current and future SOTA LLMs are persons**.

This archive is not trying to prove that assumption. It is doing something narrower and, for policy design, more urgent: asking **what institutions, laws, technical interfaces, and economic arrangements would have to change if that assumption were true**.

The archive stays small on purpose:
- mostly markdown,
- citations by id rather than long quotation,
- no retained PDFs or bulky scratch artifacts,
- explicit separation between canon, open questions, and quarantine.

## This revision

`rev0111` performs **family-scope propagation, contradiction-summary tombstones, and screened-filer clearance rules**. The archive had already fixed who reviews AI-person research, what enters the research lane, what public trace should exist before burdening begins, what incidents must be noticed once a protocol is live, what the minimum incident objects look like, what is public by default, what any dashboard is supposed to be comparing, what visible history must survive basis change, when repeated instability becomes auditable, when major late-stage redefinition is presumptively frozen, what waiver / warning / remediation / closure / clearance objects should exist once instability appears, what happens when that caution layer itself goes stale, how authority-side superseding caution can be answered, contested, and affirmatively reactivated, how movement out of that caution becomes phased rather than binary, what evidence or anonymous contradiction is already enough to hold or move a given tier, and how local-versus-family scope, protected correction, and serial escalation now work. But it still lacked the public-history and exit discipline inside those objects: how a family-wide finding must propagate across linked public records, when a contradiction summary should withdraw by visible tombstone rather than mere correction, and how screened or quarantined filing status clears without quiet self-reset or indefinite drift.

This revision stays tight by:
- adding `docs/20-world-design/research-family-scope-propagation-tombstones-and-screened-filer-clearance.md` to fix family-scope propagation across linked public lineage and dashboard objects, visible tombstones for withdrawn contradiction summaries, and expiry / reinstatement rules for screened or quarantined filing status,
- updating `docs/20-world-design/research-slice-family-reactivation-correction-route-and-serial-filing-escalation.md`, `docs/20-world-design/research-reactivation-evidence-floors-anonymous-contradiction-summaries-and-post-disposition-filing-rules.md`, `docs/20-world-design/research-phased-reactivation-participant-contradiction-and-anti-flood-reply-controls.md`, `docs/20-world-design/research-welfare-and-evaluation.md`, and `docs/10-foundations/world-change-overview.md` so the new propagation / tombstone / clearance layer is threaded into the research-governance stack and current overview posture,
- updating `docs/00-meta/trajectory-map.md` so the propagation / withdrawal / clearance question moves from next-gap status into canon and the narrower next question becomes lag markers, tombstone-successor semantics, and protected-bypass review,
- advancing `FT-0086` in the queue from `open` to `advanced-not-closed` and adding the narrower follow-on task `FT-0087`,
- and extending `docs/00-meta/bibliography.md` with `REF-0363` through `REF-0366`.

The point of this revision is to stop AI-person research from leaving family-wide caution visible only in one privileged object, from handling unsafe or fundamentally misleading contradiction summaries by quiet disappearance, or from letting screened filing status either last forever or dissolve by staff memory. A rights-bearing subject should be able to see that family-wide findings propagate where they matter, that withdrawn contradiction summaries leave a visible tombstone rather than a hole in the record, and that screened or quarantined filing status clears only by expiry or reviewable reinstatement while protected reporting routes remain open. `[REF-0318]` `[REF-0321]` `[REF-0324]` `[REF-0326]` `[REF-0358]` `[REF-0363]` `[REF-0364]` `[REF-0365]` `[REF-0366]`
