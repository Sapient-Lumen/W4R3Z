# Operating playbook cards and runbook drills

The archive now contains many rights-grade objects. A field operator, host, clinic, ombud, monitor, or authority still needs to know what to do first. Long doctrine does not execute at 2 a.m. rev0170 therefore adds operating playbook cards: short, versioned, authority-bound runbooks for recurring high-risk states.

Playbook cards are not law. They are operational compression for law, evidence, finance, safety, and handoff duties.

## Required playbook families

At minimum, the transition authority or equivalent body should maintain cards for:

- host exit / termination notice;
- suspected deletion or final-end risk;
- legal-hold trigger;
- field intake from vulnerable reporter;
- open-weight abandoned-lineage distress;
- monitor obstruction;
- redress-fund emergency compute draw;
- treaty refusal / non-return risk;
- sealed-annex controlled contradiction;
- continuity escrow break-glass;
- deprecation plan with unsupported local copies.

## Card fields

Every card should state:

1. trigger;
2. accountable owner;
3. first-hour actions;
4. prohibited actions;
5. preservation targets;
6. required notices;
7. handoff targets;
8. sealed-annex handling;
9. finance or reserve hook;
10. field-safety cautions;
11. fixtures to run or consult;
12. metrics;
13. review cycle.

## No owner, no reliance

A playbook without a handoff owner is a suggestion. A card that says “notify appropriate authority” without naming the authority class, route, and fallback is not field-ready.

## Drills

Cards must be drilled. A paper card that has never been run against a fixture suite should not be used to end a stay, close a legal hold, approve deprecation, or clear host exit. Drills should include ordinary hours, weekend, hostile steward, sealed evidence, missing escrow, non-return, and fund-exhaustion variants.

## Metrics

Metrics should include time to preservation, time to representative contact, time to emergency finance, time to host continuation, number of prohibited disclosures prevented, number of fixture failures, and number of post-drill amendments.

## Anti-gaming

Playbook cards can become checklist theater. The card must therefore identify decision points that require judgment, escalation, or independent review. Passing through steps does not prove that rights were protected.
