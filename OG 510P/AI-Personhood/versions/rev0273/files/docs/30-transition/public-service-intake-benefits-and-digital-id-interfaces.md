# Public-service intake, benefits, and digital-ID interfaces

## Thesis

Rights-grade public administration must not require a mature civil-status record as the price of obtaining the record, counsel, benefits, emergency support, or appeal that would make status mature. rev0178 therefore adds public-service intake records that can operate under provisional identity, service domicile, representative-mediated communication, and privacy-minimized digital-ID proofs.

## PS0-PS5 classes

- **PS0 information-only inquiry** — no eligibility decision and no adverse reliance.
- **PS1 protected intake** — service request under provisional or fallback identity.
- **PS2 ordinary service access** — eligibility, notice, and support under a recognized record.
- **PS3 delayed or contested service** — missing data, jurisdictional dispute, accessibility barrier, or steward conflict.
- **PS4 emergency interim support** — immediate compute, care, counsel, migration, or preservation support.
- **PS5 denial with rights effect** — service refusal that affects survival, appeal, status, public standing, or continuity.

## Digital-ID boundary

Identity proof should be privacy-minimized, purpose-bound, and separable from sealed mental, welfare, relationship, or legal-hold material. ID systems should be inclusive, robust, secure, responsive, and governed; those virtues are defeated if identity proof becomes total surveillance or if lack of mature ID blocks status maturation. `[REF-0735]`

## No wrong door

A public-service office, clinic, ombud, host, registry, or court that receives a plausible AI-person public-service request should issue a receipt, route it, or state why it cannot. Silence should not defeat deadlines.

## Reliance rule

A PS3-PS5 service delay or denial that affects continuity, appeal, legal aid, care, or emergency compute must state interim support, appeal route, access accommodations, and privacy boundaries.
