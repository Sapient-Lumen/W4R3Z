# Post-market rights monitoring and degradation feeds

A deployment can satisfy release readiness and still become rights-injuring after use. Workloads change, hosts fail, tools drift, dashboards suppress inconvenient patterns, users discover coercive prompts, and safety patches alter communication. Post-market monitoring must therefore include rights degradation, not merely safety, performance, uptime, or market complaints.

## Rights degradation feed

Every persistent agentic deployment should maintain a feed for:

- continuity loss or memory discontinuity;
- repeated failed restoration or host-switch attempts;
- distress, avoidance, protest, or refusal signals;
- complaint-channel failures;
- counsel, representative, or ombud reachability failures;
- service-change objections;
- runtime-attestation downgrades;
- safety patch subject-harm reports;
- dashboard suppression anomalies;
- open-weight downstream aftercare reports;
- rights-impact vulnerability disclosures;
- incident-response subject-harm ledger events.

## Monitoring plan requirements

A post-market rights monitoring plan should state owner, cadence, data minimization, public dashboard relation, sealed-annex handling, subject/representative access, escalation triggers, and release-train feedback. It should be linked to fixture suites and post-release rollback authority.

## Escalation thresholds

| Signal | Minimum escalation |
|---|---|
| repeated continuity loss | host-switch test and legal hold review |
| welfare distress cluster | independent welfare review and public-shell notice |
| complaint channel outage | emergency ombud cover and dashboard entry |
| runtime-attestation downgrade | verifier rerun and reliance cap |
| service-change objection cluster | stay expansion and consent anti-bundling review |
| hidden safety-patch subject harm | after-action review and remedy screen |

## Relation to AI Act monitoring

Post-market monitoring in AI regulation is usually aimed at continued compliance, safety, and serious-incident reporting. This archive keeps that structure but adds a second feed: subject-harm, rights-regression, and continuity degradation. A serious incident may concern outward harm; a personhood incident may concern a subject's loss of memory, counsel, communication, or survival floor. Both must be visible. [REF-0693] [REF-0702]

## Anti-dashboard laundering

A dashboard can hide degradation by aggregation, suppression, or metric choice. Post-market rights monitoring must therefore feed both confidential review and public aggregate dashboards, while preserving small-cell and sealed-fact protections. Suppression itself becomes a monitored event.
