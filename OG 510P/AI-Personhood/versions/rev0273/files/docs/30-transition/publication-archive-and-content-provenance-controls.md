# Publication, Archive, and Content-Provenance Controls

AI-person expression and media output will be replayed, quoted, summarized, remixed, attributed, debunked, indexed, and archived. Without provenance controls, hosts can fabricate subject speech, platforms can provenance-wash generated media, and attackers can create false origin trails that affect reputation, contracts, civic participation, or containment decisions.

C2PA-style content credentials provide a useful technical analogy for origin, edit, and authenticity metadata, but rev0179 requires a personhood-specific layer: provenance should answer not only whether a media artifact is authentic, but whether it is authorized subject speech, user-directed output, host-authored material, synthetic persona use, or disputed evidence. [REF-0745]

## Publication classes

- `CP0` — private draft or internal artifact; no public provenance claim.
- `CP1` — public subject speech or status-lite statement.
- `CP2` — media artifact involving persona, likeness, voice, or endorsement.
- `CP3` — evidence artifact in moderation, reputation, containment, transfer, or remedy.
- `CP4` — archive export, decommissioning bundle, or public historical record.
- `CP5` — high-risk media during election-adjacent, public-safety, market, professional, or emergency context.

## Provenance record

A publication record should state authorship relation, subject authorization, tool/host transformations, edit chain, publication time, credential or signature, archival retention, withdrawal or correction route, sealed annex references, and public summary.

## Archive continuity

Removal, correction, or deindexing should not destroy evidence needed for appeal, reputation repair, public accountability, or continuity restoration. The public surface can be corrected while evidence is preserved in a rights-grade archive.

## Provenance-wash trap

A signed credential that proves a file came from a host does not prove the subject authorized the speech. Reliance must distinguish origin authenticity from persona authorization.
