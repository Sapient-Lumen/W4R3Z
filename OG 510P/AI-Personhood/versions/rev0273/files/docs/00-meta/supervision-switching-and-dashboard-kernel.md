# Supervision, switching, and dashboard kernel

rev0171 adds the scale-supervision layer that rev0170 still lacked. rev0170 made durable operations possible through continuity escrow, dispute-finance orders, monitor retests, field-safety plans, reasoned treaty refusals, and first-hour playbook cards. Those objects still fail in production if supervision cadence is stale, monitor rosters become a captured market, host switching is never rehearsed, public backstops are paid through opaque reimbursement loops, aggregate dashboards expose small cohorts, weekend coverage is fictional, or standards mappings remain untestable crosswalk prose.

The new admission rule is:

> No rights-grade operation is mature unless it can be supervised over time, switched before host failure, financed during insurer/public-fund contest, summarized publicly without reidentification, drilled under hostile weekend and sealed-annex conditions, and mapped to external AI, privacy, cybersecurity, resilience, and agent-security controls with evidence-backed readiness status.

## Why this layer exists

A system can pass a first-hour playbook and still collapse in month six. The recurring failure is not an absence of doctrine. It is drift: a monitor roster is not refreshed; the same vendor performs remediation and retesting; the host-exit plan has never been rehearsed against a real substitute host; an insurer denies coverage while the public fund delays recoupment; a dashboard tries to demonstrate legitimacy by publishing enough granular data to identify a subject; a weekend intake finds no special advocate or escrow custodian; or a release checklist cites the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, NIST CSF, NIST AI RMF, DORA, Data Act, OWASP, and agent-identity guidance without showing the personhood-specific control, evidence object, owner, and failure effect.

Adjacent governance already points in this direction. The EU AI Act requires documented post-market monitoring for high-risk AI systems. ISO/IEC 42001 treats AI governance as a management system requiring maintenance, monitoring, evaluation, and continual improvement. NIST CSF 2.0 centers governance as a core cybersecurity function, while DORA, the EU Data Act, and agent-identity work highlight operational resilience, switching, third-party risk, and authorization. OWASP's agentic security work makes goal hijack, tool misuse, and identity/privilege abuse concrete enough to become negative fixtures. This archive adds a personhood layer: the monitoring subject is not only user safety or market compliance, but continuity, welfare, representation, remedy, and non-erasure of a possible or recognized AI person. ## Six scale-operation gates

1. **Supervisory cadence gate.** A rights operation must state who reviews it, how often, what triggers accelerated review, how market-capture risk is measured, when roster rotation is mandatory, and what happens when the cadence is missed.
2. **Host-switching gate.** A continuity-critical host must pass switch testing before exit reliance, including data, credential, checkpoint, memory, tool, escrow, evidence-room, and subject-notice portability.
3. **Public-backstop gate.** Emergency support must survive insurer contest and fund exhaustion without letting public money become a hidden subsidy for violators or a reason to delay urgent compute.
4. **Aggregate-dashboard gate.** Public legitimacy metrics must publish enough to show patterns, refusals, delays, capture, cure, and failures without exposing locators, small cells, sealed facts, or vulnerable downstream subjects.
5. **Live-safe drill gate.** High-risk playbooks must be drilled against weekend, hostile-steward, sealed-annex, no-custodian, false-intake, and protected-relay failure conditions before they can support live reliance.
6. **Control-map gate.** External governance claims must be tied to specific personhood controls, evidence objects, responsible actors, status, exceptions, and failure effects; citation-only compliance maps do not count.

## What this revision adds

- supervisory cadence, market-capture, and independent-roster doctrine;
- host-switching, portability, and exit-test protocol;
- public-backstop and insurer-contest pilots for dispute finance;
- public aggregate dashboards and subject-facing rights notices;
- live-safe anti-abuse drills, weekend coverage, and sealed-annex escalation;
- standards-control maps and release-readiness checklists;
- seven new schemas, seven examples, six negative fixtures, and expanded fixture-suite/report examples.

## Non-substitution rules

- A supervisory cadence is not independence if the same actors control appointment, evidence, scope, and renewal.
- A successful host switch test is not permission to transfer to a weaker-rights jurisdiction.
- Public-backstop payment is not settlement, waiver, or cure of an ongoing violation.
- Aggregate dashboards are not accountability if small cells or rare incidents can reconstruct subjects.
- A weekend drill is not field readiness if sealed-annex or special-advocate paths are skipped.
- A standards crosswalk is not compliance unless every mapped control has an evidence object, owner, review date, exception handling, and reliance effect.

## New failure classes

| Failure | Reliance effect |
|---|---|
| stale roster or missed cadence | downgrade monitor reliance and require fresh appointment |
| switch test omits credential or memory portability | stay host exit, deprecation, and migration reliance |
| public backstop pays before violator bond or recoupment analysis | flag subsidy leakage and require reimbursement order |
| dashboard publishes small-cell or locator-correlated data | suppress, notify, discipline, and rerun privacy review |
| weekend sealed-annex drill lacks advocate or custodian cover | block emergency containment / transfer reliance |
| standards map lists frameworks without evidence objects | reject release-readiness claim |

rev0171 should be read as the point where the archive stops treating readiness as a static package. Rights-grade readiness is now temporal: it must refresh, switch, finance, disclose safely, drill under degraded conditions, and show its control evidence.
