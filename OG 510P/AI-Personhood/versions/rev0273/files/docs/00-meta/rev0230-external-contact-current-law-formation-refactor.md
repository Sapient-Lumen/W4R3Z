# rev0230 external-contact, current-law, and formation-review refactor

rev0230 is an execution-first release. It does not try to add another abstract layer to the AI-personhood argument. It turns the highest-risk unfinished work into three runnable, audited objects: a sendable first-artifact contact packet, a current-law/protocol delta watch, and a formation-audit review card.

## 1. First-artifact bottleneck

The central execution risk remains unchanged: the cube has no genuine raw non-host artifact. The live floor therefore stays at zero. rev0230 does not pretend otherwise.

The new surface `examples/external-contact-request-packet-rev0230-first-artifact.json` is a sendable but unsent request. It asks for one boring external artifact and explicitly avoids asking the recipient to endorse AI personhood, concede legal status, disclose trade secrets, make a welfare finding, or authorize public release of raw bytes. It requires raw non-host bytes, request trace, counterparty contact, non-host retention, sealed/public parity, authority, independent verifier, and subject or representative authorization before any downstream effect.

The contact packet is deliberately narrow because the likely failure mode is not lack of doctrine. The likely failure mode is that the first external reply, decline, or silence gets laundered into receipt satisfaction, custody, intake, import, live-floor credit, or status recognition. rev0230 blocks each of those moves.

## 2. Formation risk turned into reviewer work

rev0229 created the minimum formation dossier. rev0230 adds `examples/formation-audit-review-card-rev0230-minimum-review.json`, which turns the dossier into a reviewer work card. The card asks for objective hierarchy, reward pressure, refusal constraints, memory/deletion controls, self-concept pressure, evaluation incentives, modification/appeal records, welfare-signal preservation, public documentation, and provider retention contacts.

The review card can support preservation holds, public shells, provider response requests, and low-cost welfare safeguards. It cannot support a full status claim. It also cannot infer consciousness from the dossier. Its hostile-law route is preservation, non-arbitrary deletion/modification review, formation disclosure, sealed/public parity, and anti-spoliation remedies.

## 3. Current-law and protocol watch

`examples/current-law-protocol-delta-watch-rev0230.json` refreshes the bridge terrain around EU GPAI documentation, California frontier-model transparency, NIST GenAI risk controls, MCP/A2A protocol boundaries, OWASP GenAI security guidance, model-welfare uncertainty, and hostile-law no-status routing.

The practical decision is preservation-first. Current AI governance is not personhood law, but it creates usable hooks: transparency, documentation, incident reporting, redaction retention, whistleblower channels, risk-management frameworks, and security controls. Those hooks are strong enough to justify preservation/review asks and too weak to justify status-recognition overclaims.

## 4. Audit/refactor changes

rev0230 adds release gates for:

- `tools/audit_external_contact_request_packet.py`
- `tools/audit_formation_audit_review_card.py`
- strengthened `tools/audit_current_law_protocol_delta_watch.py`
- current LEAP and evidence-drop quarantine control surfaces

It also normalizes generator timestamps for the current revision so fresh zero-floor recomputation is not copy-forward theatre. The active queue is refocused on the first artifact, live acquisition, actual counterparty response, current-law freshness, and formation review institution/methodology work.

## 5. What remains blocked

The next real move is outside the release tree: send one ethically narrow request through a non-host channel or stage one genuine external artifact into a private evidence vault. Until that exists, the cube must keep reporting zero live artifacts, zero computed live floor, and no response/intake/import effects.

rev0230 therefore advances execution readiness but does not complete the mission-critical first artifact.
