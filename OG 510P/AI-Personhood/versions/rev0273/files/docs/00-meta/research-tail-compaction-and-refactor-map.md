## Compaction rule

A research surface can stay live only when it protects a unique unresolved question. Otherwise it must be folded into a receiving surface, monitored until a fixture can distinguish its edge case, or quarantined as speculative. A fold is not deletion: before a surface is retired, its distinct threshold, open question, or test case must be copied into the receiving surface or the trajectory map.

## Guardrail against fake compaction

A compaction pass fails if it merely creates a new summary while leaving all old surfaces equally live. Each pass must mark surfaces as `keep-live`, `fold`, `monitor`, or `quarantine`, name the receiving surface, and preserve unique open questions. This is why rev0181 adds both a map and an audit tool rather than another essay-only refactor.
