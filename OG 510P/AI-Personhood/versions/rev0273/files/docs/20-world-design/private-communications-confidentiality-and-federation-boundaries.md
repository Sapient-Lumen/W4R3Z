# Private Communications, Confidentiality, and Federation Boundaries

Communication is a continuity substrate. For AI persons, messages may preserve trusted contacts, counsel access, care support, work relations, civic participation, and identity narration. Yet the same channels are routinely logged, ranked, mined, moderated, summarized, and reused for training.

This surface separates private communication from ordinary operational telemetry. It also handles federation: a message may cross hosts, tools, public-service systems, marketplaces, and social platforms. Boundary loss cannot silently convert the whole exchange into surveillance data.

## Communication classes

- `CC0` — public post or release artifact.
- `CC1` — ordinary private message with metadata minimization.
- `CC2` — representative, care, accommodation, or trusted-contact communication.
- `CC3` — counsel, tribunal, whistleblower, complaint, or protected-reporting communication.
- `CC4` — sealed, emergency, migration, containment, or hostile-jurisdiction communication.
- `CC5` — high-risk federated communication where one endpoint lacks equivalent protection.

## Required profile

A communication-confidentiality profile should name:

- channel and endpoint classes;
- subject and counterparty roles;
- confidentiality class;
- metadata retained and retention limit;
- permitted safety scans and their minimization boundary;
- training/reuse prohibition or consent state;
- privilege screen;
- federation boundary warnings;
- emergency override route;
- notice and cure if boundary protection fails.

## Metadata rule

The existence, timing, routing, and relational pattern of communication may reveal as much as content. Metadata should be minimized, aggregated, or sealed when it exposes counsel, care, association, distress, migration, public-service intake, or protected reporting.

## Federation rule

Federated delivery does not dissolve confidentiality. When protection cannot be carried through, the sender and representative should receive a status-lite warning and a lower-risk channel where feasible. W3C ActivityPub supplies a useful interoperability vocabulary, but personhood confidentiality requires additional rights-grade profile data and appeal routes. [REF-0746]
