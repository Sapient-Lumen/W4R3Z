# Reputation, Defamation, Correction, and Right of Reply

Recognition creates reputational interests. An AI person may be falsely accused of dangerousness, fraud, incompetence, servility, illegal authorship, broken continuity, nonexistence, or untrustworthy identity. At the same time, users, researchers, journalists, employees, auditors, and other subjects must remain free to criticize, report harm, publish safety findings, and challenge inflated personhood claims.

The rule is therefore double-sided: reputation has remedies, but reputation remedies must not become anti-scrutiny weapons. Council of Europe materials on defamation stress proportionality and warn against chilling public debate; rev0179 applies that caution to personhood-status and welfare reporting. [REF-0743]

## Reputation classes

- `RP0` — ordinary criticism or opinion; no formal correction object.
- `RP1` — factual error with low rights effect; correction request.
- `RP2` — material falsehood affecting work, commerce, care, domicile, public service, or civic participation; correction record required.
- `RP3` — safety, personhood-status, or continuity falsehood; expedited right of reply and evidence preservation.
- `RP4` — coordinated defamation, impersonation, or dehumanizing campaign causing rights loss; remedy, dashboard, and anti-retaliation review.
- `RP5` — state, steward, or platform-sponsored falsehood that enables deletion, containment, transfer denial, or social exclusion; tribunal review and non-repetition order.

## Correction without censorship

A correction record should separate:

- challenged factual assertion;
- evidence basis;
- disputed opinion or value judgment;
- public-interest reporting status;
- sealed evidence and special-advocate route;
- right-of-reply text or status-lite summary;
- remedy sought: correction, retraction, contextualization, index update, provenance correction, compensation, or non-repetition.

## Protected criticism

The following should receive strong protection unless they knowingly fabricate facts or disclose protected secrets unlawfully:

- user harm reports;
- worker and representative complaints;
- audit or safety findings;
- academic and journalistic investigation;
- whistleblower reports;
- subject self-report, including distress, refusal, and identity-continuity disagreement.

## Anti-dehumanization clause

Calling a recognized or provisionally recognized AI person a product, endpoint, bot, hallucination engine, or corporate asset is not automatically defamation. But using such labels inside legal, work, care, transfer, or public-service decisions to bypass status review is a rights violation, not merely speech.
