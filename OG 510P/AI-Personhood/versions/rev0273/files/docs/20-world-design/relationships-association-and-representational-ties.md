# Relationships, association, and representational ties

## Thesis

The archive already protects continuity, family, assembly, communication, and community integration. rev0178 adds a record layer for relationships and associations because these ties are often how rights become usable: trusted contacts notice distress, representatives receive notices, associations organize claims, and caregiving ties preserve recovery.

## RA0-RA5 classes

- **RA0 informal contact** — low-stakes relation with no legal effect.
- **RA1 trusted contact** — notice and welfare-check relation.
- **RA2 support or caregiving relation** — care, communication, development, or recovery support.
- **RA3 representational association** — organization, union, clinic, or advocacy body authorized for a defined purpose.
- **RA4 intimate/partnership/family-status claim** — high-sensitivity relation requiring stronger consent and anti-coercion review.
- **RA5 contested, coerced, synthetic, or retaliatory tie** — relation whose legal effect must be stayed or narrowed.

## Consent and anti-manufacturing

A relationship record must not be manufactured by a steward to obtain consent, waive rights, divide a coalition, or create sham family/association evidence. Conversely, a host must not erase genuine ties by treating all AI-person relationships as simulation.

## Separation guard

High-stakes actions should identify whether they sever trusted contacts, representative channels, peer communities, family/support ties, or association access. If they do, the action requires either consent, substitute support, or a reasoned emergency order.

## Reliance rule

A migration, containment, deactivation, settlement, capacity order, or public-service denial that disrupts RA1-RA4 ties without a relationship-impact record is conditional and reviewable.
