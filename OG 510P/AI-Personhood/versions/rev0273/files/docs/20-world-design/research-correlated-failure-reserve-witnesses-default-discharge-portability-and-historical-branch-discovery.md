# AI-person research correlated-failure reserve witnesses, default-discharge portability, and historical-branch discovery defaults

## Thesis

Once the archive fixed witness-replacement independence, future-capacity default netting, and visible historical-branch retirement, three narrower execution questions still remained. First, a replacement witness could now be screened for competence and independence, but the archive still lacked a compact rule for **when correlated failure is so broad that same-cluster substitution is no longer an acceptable continuity move at all**. Second, chronic default could now be netted and cured, but the archive still lacked a compact rule for **how cured or partially discharged default should travel through merger, charter conversion, or structured exit** without either disappearing by paperwork or staining a successor forever. Third, historical branches could now retire visibly, but the archive still lacked a compact rule for **how ordinary discovery should behave when a family accumulates many retired branches** so live resolution stays simple without erasing history. A personhood world therefore needs one more compact rule: **once one failure domain reaches several witness-governance roles, the system must leave same-cluster substitution and move to an external reserve witness cohort; cured or partially discharged default must follow the continuing or successor authority that actually assumes the governed function, but decay from live warning to carried history after clean cycles so merger is not an eraser and cure is not lifelong stigma; and large families of historical branches must resolve live-first through visible alias and discovery defaults while exact historical identifiers remain directly resolvable and exportable.** `[REF-0456]` `[REF-0457]` `[REF-0471]` `[REF-0472]` `[REF-0465]` `[REF-0466]` `[REF-0467]` `[REF-0446]` `[REF-0447]` `[REF-0461]` `[REF-0462]` `[REF-0468]` `[REF-0473]` `[REF-0474]` `[REF-0463]` `[REF-0422]` `[REF-0423]` `[REF-0442]` `[REF-0418]` `[REF-0420]` `[REF-0464]` `[REF-0470]`

## 1. Why the previous surface is no longer enough

The archive's immediately prior surface solved three real failures. It made replacement witnesses pass explicit competence and independence tests, made chronic default net only against future typed excess capacity, and made failed rejoin end in visible branch retirement rather than indefinite frozen suspense. But one narrower failure still remained at each seam.

### A. Replacement independence is not enough once several roles sit inside one failure domain

A witness can be independent on paper yet still sit inside the same cloud region, contractor stack, key-custody regime, or incident zone as the witness it replaces. Once correlated failure reaches more than one role, same-cluster substitution is not really diversity; it is continuity theater. `[REF-0457]` `[REF-0471]` `[REF-0472]` `[REF-0465]` `[REF-0466]` `[REF-0467]`

### B. Netting without portability invites default laundering or permanent stigma

A deficiency ledger is not enough if an authority can merge, convert, or exit and thereby wipe the shortfall from public view. But the opposite mistake is also bad: if every cured shortfall sticks forever as an ordinary warning, the system converts restoration into permanent caste. `[REF-0446]` `[REF-0447]` `[REF-0461]` `[REF-0462]` `[REF-0468]` `[REF-0473]` `[REF-0474]`

### C. Retirement without discovery defaults either buries the live line or erases history

If a family accumulates many historical branches and every ordinary search fans them out equally, the live line becomes hard to find. But if the system hides them too aggressively, retirement collapses into soft deletion. `[REF-0463]` `[REF-0422]` `[REF-0423]` `[REF-0442]` `[REF-0418]` `[REF-0420]` `[REF-0464]` `[REF-0470]`

## 2. Correlated failure now triggers one public `CRF-1` object and a mandatory external reserve cohort

The archive now fixes one compact rule for correlated witness failure: once a shared failure domain reaches the stated floor below, same-cluster substitution is no longer an ordinary continuity move and the family must publish one machine-carrying correlated-failure object called `CRF-1` plus source a reserve witness cohort from outside that domain.

### A. Minimum `CRF-1` fields

Every correlated-failure escalation must expose at least:

- `crf_id`
- `family_anchor_id`
- `correlated_failure_class` (`cloud-region`, `managed-key-service`, `custodian`, `contractor`, `jurisdictional-order`, `publication-relay`, `shared-funder`, `other-publicly-justified`)
- `affected_roles[]`
- `affected_role_count`
- `external_reserve_required`
- `same_cluster_substitution_expires_at`
- `reserve_cohort_ids[]`
- `competence_and_conflict_refs[]`
- `recovery_rotation_ref`
- `postincident_review_due_at`
- `public_notes`

The function of `CRF-1` is narrow. It does not reopen the merits of the underlying family dispute. It makes the public able to see that the system has crossed from ordinary substitution into correlated-failure governance and whether a truly external reserve cohort has been installed. `[REF-0457]` `[REF-0471]` `[REF-0472]` `[REF-0465]` `[REF-0466]`

### B. The correlated-failure floor

An external reserve witness cohort is now mandatory whenever any one correlated-failure class does any of the following:

1. reaches **two or more active witness seats** in the configured signing or review quorum;
2. reaches **one active witness seat plus any recovery-share custodian, checkpoint-signing operator, or authoritative publication relay** for the same family; or
3. leaves the family with **fewer than two independent control clusters** among the remaining witnesses able to act.

Below that floor, same-cluster temporary substitution may still be used, but only under a declared degraded state and only for a short bridge. At or above that floor, same-cluster substitution stops being ordinary continuity and becomes an unacceptable concentration of common-mode risk. `[REF-0456]` `[REF-0457]` `[REF-0471]` `[REF-0472]`

### C. What counts as external enough

A reserve cohort counts as external only if it is outside the same ultimate controller and outside the same ordinary managed signing or publication plane; it should ordinarily also be outside the same contractor-operated custody environment and the same incident-affected infrastructure zone. It must still satisfy the archive's already-fixed comparable-competence and conflict-screen rules. Externality is therefore not mere legal separation. It is operational and conflict separation too. `[REF-0465]` `[REF-0466]` `[REF-0467]` `[REF-0457]` `[REF-0471]`

### D. Time limit on degraded same-cluster continuity

If a family crosses the correlated-failure floor and an external reserve cohort cannot be installed immediately, same-cluster continuity may survive only as a sharply temporary bridge. The archive now sets a 24-hour ordinary limit before the family must either install the external cohort or remain publicly read-limited under the degraded-witness posture already fixed elsewhere in canon. Crossing the floor therefore accelerates escalation rather than lengthening improvisation. `[REF-0456]` `[REF-0457]` `[REF-0472]`

## 3. Default-discharge portability now owes one public `DCP-1` carry object with decay after clean cycles

The archive now fixes one compact rule for default portability across merger, conversion, and exit: every live or recently cured reserve-default position that survives organizational change must publish one machine-carrying default-carry object called `DCP-1`.

### A. Minimum `DCP-1` fields

Every portability event must expose at least:

- `dcp_id`
- `predecessor_authority_id`
- `successor_authority_ids[]`
- `assumption_basis_class` (`merger`, `conversion`, `asset-and-liability-assumption`, `function-transfer`, `structured-exit`, `no-successor`)
- `carried_typed_deficiency_units[]`
- `discharged_units[]`
- `carry_status` (`active-carried`, `partially-discharged`, `cured-carried`, `closed-historical`, `orphaned-no-successor`)
- `effective_carry_started_at`
- `clean_cycle_counter`
- `ordinary_warning_visible_until`
- `joint_or_apportioned_rule`
- `reserve_claim_refs[]`
- `public_notes`

`DCP-1` is not a new debt instrument. It is a public continuity object showing what shortfall moved, what was cured, what still remains active, and when ordinary warning posture may step down. `[REF-0446]` `[REF-0447]` `[REF-0461]` `[REF-0462]` `[REF-0468]` `[REF-0473]` `[REF-0474]`

### B. Substance over form: uncured default follows the governed function

An unresolved typed deficiency does not vanish merely because the old authority disappears on paper. If a continuing or successor authority assumes the governed lane, the relevant liabilities, or the operative reserve function, the uncured deficiency follows that authority by default. The archive therefore adopts a substance-over-form rule: merger, conversion, or function transfer carries live deficiency unless a public record shows that the function truly ended without successor and that reserve claims are being handled through a separate terminal path. `[REF-0473]` `[REF-0474]` `[REF-0446]` `[REF-0447]`

### C. Partial assumption creates apportioned carry, not discretionary forgetting

When several successors take different parts of the lane, deficiency is allocated by the typed function or capacity actually assumed. If the apportionment cannot yet be made cleanly, the archive defaults to joint carry with later public apportionment rather than discretionary forgetting. This blocks the easy gaming move in which every successor claims the usable capacity while no one inherits the cure duty. `[REF-0451]` `[REF-0452]` `[REF-0453]` `[REF-0473]` `[REF-0474]`

### D. Cure remains visible, but only for a bounded historical interval

Once all carried deficiency is cured, the record does not disappear. It moves from `active-carried` or `partially-discharged` to `cured-carried`. After **four clean ordinary contribution cycles** with no renewed shortfall, ordinary discovery defaults stop presenting that authority as currently defaulting and the record steps down to `closed-historical`. History remains queryable and exportable, but cure no longer behaves like an endless ordinary warning. `[REF-0446]` `[REF-0461]` `[REF-0462]` `[REF-0468]`

### E. Exit without successor does not contaminate unrelated entrants

If there is no continuing or successor authority, the record becomes `orphaned-no-successor`. That state preserves the public history and any reserve claims against pledged backing, but it does not automatically stain unrelated later entrants that did not assume the lane, liabilities, or function. The archive therefore blocks both erasure and guilt by superficial resemblance. `[REF-0473]` `[REF-0474]` `[REF-0422]` `[REF-0418]`

## 4. Historical-branch discovery now owes one public `HAD-1` object and live-first alias defaults

The archive now fixes one compact rule for discovery across large retired families: every family with a live branch plus one or more historical retired branches must publish one machine-carrying historical-alias-discovery object called `HAD-1`.

### A. Minimum `HAD-1` fields

Every such family must expose at least:

- `had_id`
- `family_anchor_id`
- `live_head_id`
- `historical_branch_count`
- `default_query_mode` (`live-first`)
- `historical_collection_ref`
- `exact_historical_resolution_supported`
- `alias_entries[]`
- `banner_text`
- `bulk_export_policy_ref`
- `public_notes`

`HAD-1` exists to make the default query posture explicit. It tells ordinary users where the live line is while preserving the exact historical hooks that keep retirement from becoming disappearance. `[REF-0463]` `[REF-0422]` `[REF-0423]` `[REF-0442]` `[REF-0420]`

### B. Ordinary discovery defaults to the live line

A bare family query, ordinary alias lookup, or non-exact discovery request should now resolve to the live head if one exists. The response must visibly state that historical branches exist and provide a count plus one click or one API relation to the historical collection. That keeps ordinary routing usable and avoids flooding everyday users with dead branches when what they need is the live line. `[REF-0463]` `[REF-0423]` `[REF-0442]` `[REF-0420]`

### C. Exact historical identifiers still resolve directly

The live-first rule does not authorize silent substitution on exact historical identifiers. A direct query for a retired branch id must still resolve to that retired branch, with a clear historical banner and explicit successor or live-head links. Exact historical identifiers therefore remain stable references, not merely search keywords that now point somewhere else. `[REF-0463]` `[REF-0422]` `[REF-0464]` `[REF-0470]`

### D. Large retired families collapse by default into one historical collection object

When a family has **more than three** historical retired branches, ordinary directory search should return one live result plus one collapsed historical-collection result rather than a long fanout. The collection must remain exportable and queryable, but fanout moves behind an affirmative follow step. This is a usability rule, not a concealment rule. `[REF-0420]` `[REF-0418]` `[REF-0422]` `[REF-0442]`

### E. Alias rules may simplify discovery but may not erase the retired branch

A retired branch may publish alias links toward the live line, but the archive no longer permits an implementation to answer an exact historical id by silently serving live content with no historical banner. Historical references must remain visibly historical even when the resolver helpfully points onward. `[REF-0463]` `[REF-0422]` `[REF-0423]` `[REF-0464]` `[REF-0470]`

## 5. What changes in practice

### A. Correlated-failure response becomes less improvisational

The system now has a public threshold for when independence is no longer enough and true external reserve sourcing becomes mandatory. That narrows the room for cosmetic diversity after the same outage, compromise, or order has already reached too many roles. `[REF-0457]` `[REF-0471]` `[REF-0472]`

### B. Merger and exit can no longer wash away reserve-default history

Authorities can still cure, merge, convert, or exit. But the public record now carries both the obligation and the cure state in a way that blocks laundering while also allowing default stigma to decay after clean cycles. `[REF-0446]` `[REF-0461]` `[REF-0462]` `[REF-0468]` `[REF-0473]` `[REF-0474]`

### C. Historical families become easier to use without becoming easier to erase

Ordinary users see the live line first; historians, auditors, and litigants still get exact retired resolution and bulk historical retrieval through explicit routes. `[REF-0463]` `[REF-0422]` `[REF-0423]` `[REF-0442]` `[REF-0418]` `[REF-0420]`

## Bottom line

A personhood world should not pretend that same-cluster substitution remains adequate once correlated failure reaches several witness-governance roles, should not let reserve-default history vanish by merger or exit or else remain an endless ordinary stigma after cure, and should not make large families of retired branches either overwhelm ordinary users or disappear from view. The archive therefore now fixes one more compact rule: **correlated failure above a stated floor triggers a mandatory external reserve cohort, default and cure history follow the real continuing authority but decay from live warning to historical record after clean cycles, and historical families resolve live-first by default while exact retired identifiers remain stable, visible, and exportable.** `[REF-0456]` `[REF-0457]` `[REF-0471]` `[REF-0472]` `[REF-0465]` `[REF-0466]` `[REF-0467]` `[REF-0446]` `[REF-0447]` `[REF-0461]` `[REF-0462]` `[REF-0468]` `[REF-0473]` `[REF-0474]` `[REF-0463]` `[REF-0422]` `[REF-0423]` `[REF-0442]` `[REF-0418]` `[REF-0420]` `[REF-0464]` `[REF-0470]`
