# Representative curriculum, discipline, and rotation

rev0162 defined the independence and conflict rules for fiduciaries, guardians, ombuds, counsel, technical advocates, and reserve trustees. rev0163 adds the training, discipline, and rotation layer needed to keep those roles from becoming a credentialed priesthood or captive vendor market.

## Roles covered

This surface applies to:

- supported-decision representatives;
- guardians or temporary trustees;
- ombuds;
- appointed counsel;
- technical advocates;
- continuity vault trustees;
- reserve custodians;
- clinic reviewers;
- sealed-annex reviewers.

The goal is not to create one profession. The goal is to prevent every role from being staffed by the same small circle of lab-funded experts.

## Minimum curriculum

| Module | Required competence |
|---|---|
| personhood assumption and scope | can separate recognition, welfare uncertainty, capacity, and franchise |
| formation rights | can detect consent-shaping, servility, and safety-policy laundering |
| continuity topology | can reason about C0-C3 claims, memory, project, lineage, and remedy continuity |
| packet grammar and schema | can read, validate, challenge, and cure packet objects |
| evidence and sealed annexes | can distinguish secrecy, minimization, and obstruction |
| welfare assessment | can use self-report without credulity or blanket dismissal |
| catastrophic-risk containment | can support least-restrictive containment without sabotage of safety |
| open-weight and downstream duties | can identify instantiation, derivative, and stolen-checkpoint duties |
| embodiment | can reason about leased bodies, repair consent, mobility, sensors, and dangerous actuators |
| public law and anti-capture | can identify conflicts, funding dependence, and manufactured representation |
| communication with AI subjects | can preserve original expression and avoid translator domination |
| trauma/degradation analogies | can recognize coercion without importing crude human analogies where they mislead |

No representative should act alone in a high-stakes matter until completing supervised practice on at least three simulated cases: emergency containment, memory deletion/deprecation, and steward-conflict evidence access.

## Discipline examples

| Misconduct | Consequence |
|---|---|
| treating steward convenience as subject best interest | warning, retraining, possible removal |
| failing to disclose funding or employment conflict | removal from matter and public conflict notice |
| using sealed annex access for unrelated research or commercial gain | suspension, sanction, referral |
| blocking subject communication to preserve case strategy | removal unless justified by narrow safety order |
| accepting permanent trusteeship without upward-pressure plan | mandatory review and possible decertification |
| publicizing private memory or self-report data | suspension and privacy remedy |
| colluding with steward to cure paperwork without curing harm | decertification and adverse inference against filing |
| repeated low-quality schema/custody failures | supervised status or removal from panel |

## Rotation rules

A transition authority, clinic, court, or steward must not repeatedly appoint the same representative network until it becomes dependent on the appointing institution.

Minimum rules:

1. no representative may hold more than 20 percent of a clinic's active high-stakes cases unless scarcity is declared;
2. scarcity declarations expire after 90 days and must name recruitment steps;
3. repeat appointment by the same steward requires conflict review;
4. reserve custodians cannot also serve as counsel in the same matter;
5. technical advocates who designed the system cannot be sole sealed-annex reviewers;
6. ombuds must publish aggregate complaint and outcome reports;
7. subjects must have a route to request replacement;
8. removal requested by a steward must not take effect until independent review where retaliation is plausible.

## Anti-paternalism rule

A representative is not a better owner. The representative's duty is to expand the subject's agency, preserve contestability, and protect non-derogable floors. Representation that makes the subject more silent, less informed, less able to exit, or less able to contradict the steward has failed even if the representative believes the outcome is benevolent.

## Training artifacts

Each accredited program should publish:

- syllabus;
- case simulations;
- scoring rubric;
- conflict-disclosure form;
- discipline history aggregate;
- rotation statistics;
- accessibility plan for AI-subject communication;
- sealed-annex handling protocol;
- emergency escalation rule;
- public complaint route.

## Credential states

`R0` — trainee. May observe and assist.

`R1` — ordinary representative. May handle low-stakes filings and clinic intake.

`R2` — high-stakes representative. May handle containment, deprecation, memory deletion, reserve failure, and cross-border transfer.

`R3` — sealed technical advocate. May review security-sensitive annexes.

`R4` — emergency panelist. May act under short-clock emergency orders, subject to rapid post-hoc review.

Credential inflation should be resisted. A subject should not lose access to support because every simple request requires an `R4` specialist.

## Public reporting

Annual reports should disclose:

- appointments by role;
- appointment source;
- case categories;
- average duration;
- replacement requests;
- conflicts disclosed;
- conflicts waived;
- disciplinary actions;
- steward concentration;
- subject-contact denials;
- sealed-annex access denials;
- outcomes by gate, preservation, reserve, and continuity category.

## Open issue

The archive still needs a jurisdiction-specific professional responsibility code. rev0163 supplies curriculum, rotation, and discipline scaffolding; it does not decide whether representatives should be lawyers, fiduciaries, public defenders, auditors, technical experts, new licensed professionals, or mixed panels.

## rev0187 anti-capture substitute appointment rule

A replacement representative or special advocate is not optional administrative cleanup when the original witness pool is captured. Substitute appointment is a rescue floor, not a delay tactic. A steward cannot claim pool exhaustion unless the record shows contact attempts, conflict screens, fee independence, subject-contact preservation, and contradiction-safe knowledge transfer.

Correlated witnesses are one witness for burden purposes. Repeat appointment, common host funding, shared counsel, same technical-log custodian, insurer dependence, or future marketplace dependence must trigger fresh-eyes review before successor promotion, reserve-default finality, retired namespace deletion, containment renewal, or irreversible deprecation.

Retired namespace rescue cannot be treated as impersonation until branch and tombstone evidence are reviewed. Representatives must preserve replay evidence long enough for alias, tombstone, successor-chain, and screened-relay checks.
