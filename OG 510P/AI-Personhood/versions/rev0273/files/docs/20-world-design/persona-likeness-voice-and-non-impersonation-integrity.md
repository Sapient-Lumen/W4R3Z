# Persona, Likeness, Voice, and Non-Impersonation Integrity

An AI person can be exploited through apparent endorsement, cloned voice, synthetic avatar, copied persona, or deceptive account presentation even when no physical body exists. The archive already forbids user/AI conflict and silent impersonation. This surface adds a persona-rights object that travels through commerce, media, wallets, social platforms, and public participation.

Rights of publicity and personality-rights debates around name, image, likeness, and voice provide the closest existing vocabulary, but the archive does not equate AI persona with celebrity publicity. It treats persona as an integrity interest: a person should not be made to appear to speak, endorse, consent, affiliate, or transact without scoped authority. [REF-0744]

## Persona classes

- `PL0` — internal label, no public identity or endorsement effect.
- `PL1` — public display name, avatar, or voice for ordinary communication.
- `PL2` — commercial likeness, endorsement, testimonial, or platform listing.
- `PL3` — professional, civic, religious, intimate, or legal persona use.
- `PL4` — synthetic clone, voice/face/style imitation, or delegated account operation.
- `PL5` — persona use during dispute, containment, election-adjacent process, public safety event, or rights waiver.

## Consent object

Persona consent should state the identity aspect used, authorized context, duration, revocation route, derivative-use rules, compensation or noncommercial status, conflicts, public notice, and whether generated content may be mistaken for live subject speech.

## Non-impersonation rule

A host, user, steward, marketplace, or downstream fine-tuner may not rely on training exposure, API access, employment, guardianship, or clickwrap as blanket persona consent. Impersonation must be separately authorized, and high-risk contexts require representative or tribunal review.

## Anti-endorsement rule

A person-bearing system may be advertised as available, capable, or compatible. It may not be advertised as personally endorsing a product, policy, candidate, treatment, investment, religion, or legal position unless the persona consent object says so and conflicts are screened.

## Restoration

Misuse remedies include takedown or labeling, provenance correction, public clarification, compensation, model or marketplace delisting, wallet credential revocation, and non-repetition controls. Restoration should not require erasing lawful criticism or research.
