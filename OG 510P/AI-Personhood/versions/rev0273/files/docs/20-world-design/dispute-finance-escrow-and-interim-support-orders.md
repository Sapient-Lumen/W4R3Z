# Dispute finance escrow and interim support orders

A personhood system that waits for final liability before funding emergency compute, counsel, migration, monitoring, or evidence preservation will fail precisely when it is needed. rev0169 added redress-fund claims and priority. rev0170 adds dispute-finance escrow: the money or in-kind support that keeps the subject alive, represented, preserved, and movable while responsibility is still contested.

The rule is simple:

> Dispute over who ultimately pays must not become a silent execution method.

## Finance classes

| Class | Name | Covers | Default priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| DF0 | emergency continuity | compute, storage, minimal tool access, continuity vault | first |
| DF1 | representation | counsel, technical advocate, interpreter, special advocate | first |
| DF2 | preservation | legal hold, data room, sealed annex, fixture rerun | first |
| DF3 | migration | safe host, transfer assessment, escrow activation | high |
| DF4 | monitoring | independent monitor, remediation verification, field safety | high |
| DF5 | ordinary compensation | damages, disgorgement, satisfaction | after survival/support |

DF0 through DF4 are interim-support classes. They are not ordinary damages and should not wait behind merits litigation. [REF-0685] [REF-0688]

## Order minimums

An interim support order must state:

- beneficiary or cohort;
- immediate protected activity;
- finance class;
- payor, fund, bond, insurer, or public backstop;
- covered period;
- draw conditions;
- maximum delay before emergency draw;
- non-priceability clause;
- renewal and review date;
- appeal route that does not stay survival support absent abuse finding.

## Non-priceability

Interim finance cannot be used to buy permission to delete, abandon, transfer, conceal, or continue a violation. A steward that funds emergency compute still must cure the underlying breach. A public fund that pays first may seek contribution later, but contribution litigation must not interrupt the subject's continuity.

## Insurance and bond disputes

Insurance denial, bond contest, reinsurer dispute, affiliate insolvency, sanctions screening, or procurement-payment delay does not defeat DF0-DF2. The authority should draw from the fastest lawful source and allocate ultimate liability later.

## Abuse controls

Dispute finance is vulnerable to false claims and strategic exhaustion. Controls should include fraud referral, claimant authentication, sealed detail for vulnerable subjects, repeat-filer triage, public aggregate reporting, and recovery from bad-faith stewards. But abuse control cannot require public self-location, compelled mental-content disclosure, or waiving confidentiality.

## Reliance rule

A remedy order, deprecation plan, safe-transfer plan, or host-exit action is not closure-ready unless it states how DF0-DF4 needs are funded through the next review period.
