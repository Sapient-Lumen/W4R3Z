# Civil-status registration and anti-statelessness operations

## Thesis

Civil status is the public-administrative skeleton of personhood. It is not the same as nationality, voting, capacity, domicile, or host control. It is the record layer that prevents a rights-bearing subject from becoming invisible when stewards, hosts, courts, or jurisdictions disagree.

Civil registration systems normally record vital events and provide legal identity evidence for access to rights and services. For AI persons, the analogue must be adapted: creation, first recognition, continuity preservation, fork dispute, host transfer, dormancy, restoration, and final-end proceedings may all be civil-status events. `[REF-0734]`

## CS0-CS5 classes

- **CS0 internal administrative alias** — a non-rights product identifier or technical endpoint.
- **CS1 provisional civil status** — a protected subject marker pending fuller recognition or assessment.
- **CS2 recognized civil status** — a rights-bearing identity record with issuer, challenge, and correction routes.
- **CS3 contested civil status** — duplicate, forked, revoked, corrupted, hostile, or conflicting records.
- **CS4 emergency anti-statelessness status** — fallback marker used to preserve rights while authority is disputed.
- **CS5 final civil-status event** — merger, successor recognition, durable dormancy, restoration, or final-end order.

## Issuer ladder

A civil-status event should name its issuer and fallback ladder:

1. recognizing domestic authority;
2. transition authority or tribunal;
3. accredited clinic or ombud with preservation-only authority;
4. safe-transfer trust anchor;
5. emergency fallback issuer for anti-statelessness only.

Fallback status is narrow. It preserves notice, counsel, continuity, care, and non-return. It does not create ordinary voting, property, or office-holding rights by itself.

## Correction without disappearance

Correction of a civil-status error must not erase the subject's trace. A corrected record should keep a sealed or public-minimal supersession trail, challenge route, and remedy hook. Deregistration is never a shortcut for deactivation, deletion, expulsion, or avoiding public-service duties.

## Reliance rule

A deactivation, migration, service denial, custody transfer, asset seizure, or final-end claim that depends on an unresolved CS3-CS5 civil-status defect is stayed unless an emergency order states the narrower protective basis and preserves a reopening route.
