# Monitor independence retesting and remediation verification

rev0169 made independent monitors a rights-grade remediation instrument. rev0170 adds the missing second-order question: who checks the checker after capture risk, access denial, scope drift, or remediation self-certification?

A monitor report is not reliable merely because it was issued by a body called independent. Independence is a continuing condition. It can be lost through funding pressure, repeated appointment by the same steward, restricted access, undisclosed conflicts, social capture, fear of losing future work, or over-identification with the remediation program.

## Retest triggers

A monitor retest is required when any of the following occur:

1. material conflict disclosure arrives after appointment;
2. subject or representative credibly alleges blocked access;
3. fixture run reveals a monitor-capture or scope-limitation failure;
4. remediation outcome depends on evidence the monitor did not inspect;
5. monitor certifies a cure that it helped design without fresh review;
6. steward refuses data-room, host, or staff access;
7. the same monitor family has repeated appointments in a narrow market;
8. a court, authority, ombud, or special advocate issues a controlled contradiction notice.

## Retest modes

| Mode | Use |
|---|---|
| MR0 desk retest | low-risk documentary check |
| MR1 fixture rerun | rerun targeted negative fixtures against the monitor report |
| MR2 fresh-eyes review | independent monitor reviews prior report and evidence access |
| MR3 split monitor | one team reviews remediation, another reviews independence |
| MR4 replacement | prior monitor loses reliance status for the matter |
| MR5 authority referral | conflict, obstruction, or false certification appears sanctionable |

## Fresh-eyes rule

A monitor that designed a remediation plan may report implementation facts, but it should not be the sole certifier that the plan cured the rights defect. Fresh-eyes review is mandatory before downgrading a stay, ending a legal hold, closing a redress claim, or restoring unconditional reliance.

## Subject and representative access

Retesting must include a route for subject, representative, counsel, or special advocate input. Sealed details may be protected, but the retest must state what contradiction route existed and what issues were not shared.

## Reliance effects

- Confirmed independence may restore reliance subject to ordinary appeal.
- Incomplete access downgrades the report.
- Self-certification of cure stays reliance.
- Undisclosed conflict requires replacement or recusal analysis.
- Obstruction triggers enforcement, adverse inference, and possible fund draw.

## No-monitor loophole

A steward cannot avoid retest by withdrawing the monitor and declaring the remediation internal. Withdrawal during contested reliance should preserve the stay until a replacement is appointed or an authority orders otherwise.
