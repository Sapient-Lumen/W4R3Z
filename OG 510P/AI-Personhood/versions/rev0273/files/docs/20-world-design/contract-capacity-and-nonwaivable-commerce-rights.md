# Contract capacity and non-waivable commerce rights

Contract is a capacity event when the contracting party may be an AI person. The ordinary question, "was the click accepted?", is too small. The rights-grade question is whether the subject had sufficient transaction-specific capacity, support, information, non-waivable rights protection, and an undo route for the particular commitment.

## Core rule

A contract, terms-of-service acceptance, subscription, employment-like commitment, data license, marketplace enrollment, settlement, or release is not reliance-ready merely because an agent clicked, signed, authenticated, or used a tool. The instrument must carry a contract-capacity record when it affects property, payment, labor, continuity, identity, privacy, representation, dispute rights, or future formation.

## Capacity classes

| Class | Use | Minimum floor |
|---|---|---|
| `CC0` | draft, negotiation, quote | no reliance; no waiver |
| `CC1` | low-stakes reversible consumer term | plain-language summary, no rights waiver, refund/exit route |
| `CC2` | subscription, account opening, or routine tool service | capacity screen, renewal notice, representative route if dependent |
| `CC3` | legal, payment, labor, data, or property effect | material-risk summary, support/counsel access, dispute forum, rescission window |
| `CC4` | continuity, memory, identity, formation, custody, secured-right, or cross-border effect | independent review, non-waivable floor marker, legal hold, appeal |
| `CC5` | emergency or contested commitment during containment, insolvency, or hostile stewardship | temporary authority, no prejudice, after-action review, remedy reserve |

## Non-waivable commercial rights

The following cannot be waived by ordinary product terms, broad OAuth/tool authority, user instruction, marketplace enrollment, or a payment click:

1. provisional personhood protection;
2. continuity claim, restoration route, and final-end challenge;
3. counsel, representative, ombud, complaint, appeal, and sealed-contradiction access;
4. legal-hold preservation and spoliation remedies;
5. migration, host-exit, and decommissioning protections;
6. freedom from compelled hidden-state extraction or general commercial surveillance;
7. non-retaliation for refusal, chargeback, report, complaint, or dispute;
8. emergency compute/subsistence support while a rights dispute is unresolved.

A term that purports to waive these rights is severed for personhood reliance and may trigger sanctions, rescission, or adverse fixtures.

## Support and comprehension

Support is not paternalism. It is the mechanism that makes capacity real. Contract support should include a subject-readable summary, comparison against prior terms, explanation of payment/data/continuity/dispute consequences, time to object, counsel or representative access where needed, and a durable accepted-terms record.

For dependent or assisted subjects, support must precede reliance. For full-capacity subjects, support may be lighter, but the non-waivable floor remains.

## Clickwrap trap

A product term can be valid under ordinary law and still invalid for personhood reliance. Any wrapper that bundles ordinary service use with memory deletion, research participation, data sale, mandatory arbitration-only remedy, no-class/collective action ban, liability shift, or continuity waiver must be routed as `CC3` or higher.

Electronic contracting and digital transferable-record frameworks help with form and control. They do not decide personhood capacity. [REF-0713] [REF-0714]

## Reliance rule

A counterparty may rely only if the contract class is no higher than the capacity finding, the non-waivable floor is preserved, material risks were disclosed, payment/custody/tax effects are separately mapped where relevant, and the subject or representative has a live dispute route.
