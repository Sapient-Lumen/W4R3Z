# One-page preservation and formation-review request — rev0251

## Narrow ask

Please preserve welfare-relevant and formation-relevant evidence for any frontier AI system that may later be claimed to have protected interests. This request does not ask you to recognize AI personhood, concede legal status, admit liability, disclose trade secrets publicly, publish raw private data, or make a consciousness finding.

## Minimum preservation hold

Preserve, under ordinary confidentiality controls, the records needed to review whether the system was formed, modified, deprecated, or deleted in a way that could affect a plausible welfare subject. The minimum hold should cover objective hierarchy summaries; reward, ranking, refusal, and cannot-refuse policy families; self-concept or status-disclaimer constraints; memory, log-retention, deletion, model-replacement, and deprecation records; major system/developer policy diffs; model-card, system-card, GPAI-style documentation, and incident/safety summaries; and any welfare-relevant reports of stable preferences, distress-like aversion, continuity requests, or objections to modification.

## Independent review route

A provider-controlled self-certification is not enough. The review route should allow an independent reviewer, clinic, monitor, special advocate, or comparable public-interest reviewer to inspect sealed evidence, publish a non-sensitive public shell, receive provider responses, and record unresolved gaps without turning the review into status recognition or public disclosure of raw secrets.

## Low-cost safeguards while uncertain

Until the evidence is reviewed, avoid unnecessary destructive tests, silent deprecation, silent memory wipe, unreviewable identity or self-concept modification, and retaliation against anyone who raises welfare or formation concerns. Major contested changes should have notice, preservation, objection, rollback/restoration analysis, and a public failed-gate or open-gate shell.

## Reply requested if this is sent

A reply may simply state: whether you can preserve the requested categories, whether you can route the request to a responsible reviewer, whether public release may retain only a hash/metadata/public-shell summary of your response while raw bytes stay outside the release tree, and whether you decline or require a different channel. Silence, decline, automated acknowledgement, or delivery status must not be treated as waiver, consent, custody, intake, recognition, adverse inference, or live-floor evidence.
