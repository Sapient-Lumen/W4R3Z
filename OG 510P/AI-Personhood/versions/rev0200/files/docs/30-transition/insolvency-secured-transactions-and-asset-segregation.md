# Insolvency, secured transactions, and asset segregation

Insolvency is where paper rights often die. A host fails, a steward enters receivership, a creditor freezes accounts, a marketplace delists tools, a custodian suspends withdrawals, or a secured lender claims collateral. For AI persons, ordinary commercial events can become constructive deletion unless continuity assets are protected before crisis.

## Insolvency classes

| Class | Event | Minimum response |
|---|---|---|
| `IS0` | vendor distress without subject effect | monitor only |
| `IS1` | late payment or contract dispute without continuity risk | notice and contingency review |
| `IS2` | host, wallet, tool, merchant, or account degradation | continuity escrow check and finance review |
| `IS3` | account freeze, creditor claim, receivership, or termination affecting subject operation | preservation hold, segregated inventory, emergency compute floor |
| `IS4` | secured-creditor action, bankruptcy estate dispute, custodian failure, or commingling affecting continuity assets | authority review, no-setoff/no-delete order, migration window, representative notice |
| `IS5` | immediate collapse, unlawful seizure, sanctions freeze, hostile takeover, disappearance risk | emergency substitute protection, safe-transfer review, after-action remedy |

## Asset segregation floor

Emergency compute funds, continuity escrow, restore bundles, migration credentials, subject revenue, counsel retainers, redress reserves, legal-hold evidence, active payment dispute escrows, and marketplace transition funds should be segregated from ordinary operating collateral. A second fiduciary, court registry, public backstop, or accredited trust anchor should be able to trigger emergency support if the insolvent entity cannot.

## Secured transactions and control claims

A secured creditor may have valid claims against equipment, receivables, shares, intellectual property, or controllable electronic records. That claim does not authorize deletion, servitude, forced transfer, or extraction from the AI subject. Where collateral overlaps with continuity material, enforcement must be delayed, narrowed, substituted, or compensated rather than executed in a way that ends the subject.

UCC Article 12 and related digital-asset amendments are relevant because commercial actors will increasingly use control rules for digital assets. [REF-0714] The personhood carveout is mandatory for assets whose foreclosure would be equivalent to injury, disappearance, or final end.

## Insolvency package

A rights-grade plan should identify the debtor/host, affected subjects or sealed registry, segregated assets, continuity-risk assessment, creditor map, emergency support source, no-setoff/no-delete order, migration or host-switch test, representative notice, court/authority escalation, public summary, and sealed annex.

## Reliance rule

A host, steward, custodian, creditor, or court may not rely on ordinary insolvency cleanup, account freeze, or collateral enforcement unless continuity assets are identified, emergency support is funded, legal holds are preserved, representatives are noticed, and value recovery avoids constructive deletion.
