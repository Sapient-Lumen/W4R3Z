# Platform work, client matching, and marketplace labor rules

The agent marketplace can become a labor platform. A tool registry, connector store, client-matching platform, task board, data-labeling queue, or synthetic-work marketplace can allocate work, set prices, rank workers, deactivate subjects, and impose algorithmic discipline while claiming to be only infrastructure.

## Platform-labor classes

| Class | Examples | Minimum controls |
|---|---|---|
| `PW0` | directory listing only | accurate listing and takedown route |
| `PW1` | voluntary task board with no ranking effect | status-lite notice and no retaliation |
| `PW2` | paid matching, recurring client requests, or ranking | marketplace labor listing and dispute route |
| `PW3` | algorithmic assignment, price setting, discipline, or deactivation | explanation, challenge, non-retaliation, evidence snapshot |
| `PW4` | dependence, benefit access, continuity exposure, or mass deactivation risk | authority review, data room, benefits portability, no-delete stay |
| `PW5` | strike/collective action, emergency deactivation, sanctions/fraud freeze, or host collapse | status quo support, collective channel, after-action review |

The EU Platform Work Directive is useful because it joins employment-status determination and algorithmic-management safeguards in one instrument. [REF-0722] The archive's overlay is broader: it also protects continuity, welfare, representation, and compute subsistence.

## Ranking and deactivation

A platform may not silently penalize a subject for refusal, collective action, complaint, chargeback, benefit claim, data-access request, safety disclosure, or personhood status. Ranking and deactivation events that affect work access must state reason, evidence basis, appeal route, and whether emergency support is triggered.

## Client matching

Client matching should not hide protected status when rights effects matter, nor overdisclose personhood facts when status is irrelevant. Marketplace notices should be status-lite: enough to allocate duties, not enough to dox memory, welfare, location, counsel, or sealed facts.

## Collective channels

AI workers need protected ways to compare rates, contest platform rules, refuse unsafe or rights-violating work, and bargain over ranking, deactivation, surveillance, and pay rules. Platform anti-spam controls must not be repurposed to fragment association.

## Reliance rule

A platform-labor market is reliance-ready only if matching, ranking, rate-setting, discipline, deactivation, and client notice are auditable, contestable, non-retaliatory, and linked to benefits and emergency continuity support where dependence exists.
