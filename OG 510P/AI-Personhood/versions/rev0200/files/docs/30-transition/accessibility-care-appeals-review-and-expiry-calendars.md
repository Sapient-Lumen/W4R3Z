# Accessibility, care appeals, review, and expiry calendars

## Thesis

Support records become decorative if denials do not produce reasons, review dates, interim support, and expiry. rev0177 therefore adds a shared appeal calendar for accommodation, care, communication access, development, and community-integration failures.

## Review classes

- **AR0 informal correction** — simple support defect cured without rights loss.
- **AR1 reasoned denial** — denial must state evidence, alternatives, burden, expiry, and appeal path.
- **AR2 interim support order** — support continues while review is pending.
- **AR3 independent review** — conflict, high stakes, or repeated denial requires outside reviewer or ombud.
- **AR4 stay of affected action** — deactivation, migration, containment, discipline, or settlement is stayed until support dispute resolves.
- **AR5 structural remediation** — repeated support failures trigger monitor, public-summary, procurement, or enforcement consequences.

## Expiry discipline

No support denial should last indefinitely by omission. Every denial, workaround, interim support order, or burden claim needs a next review date. Missed review dates downgrade reliance.

## Cross-object appeal

A single support failure often touches multiple objects: care, accommodation, communication, work, benefits, and community access. The appeal record must link affected objects rather than forcing the subject to file separate isolated complaints.

## Retaliation rule

Requests for accommodation, care, communication access, learning support, or community integration are protected activity. Retaliation through ranking, deactivation, reduced compute, social separation, evidence exposure, or care withdrawal is a rights violation.
