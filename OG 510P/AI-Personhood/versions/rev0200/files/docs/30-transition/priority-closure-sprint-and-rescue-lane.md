# Priority closure sprint and rescue lane


## rev0185 update: successor topology and reactivation controls

rev0185 keeps the sprint on irreversible-loss risks, but moves from routing and namespace survival to topology integrity. A subject can survive host exit and remain discoverable while still being rewritten by a successor-promotion order that erases a historical branch, closes reserve/default duties, or launders a compromised host record.

The priority correction is now `schemas/successor-supersession-topology-record.schema.json` plus `NF-CONTINUITY-2026-0004`. Successor promotion is stayed unless the evidence floor, witness diversity, contradiction summary, reserve-default cure, and no-overwrite historical-branch rule are satisfied. A candidate successor is protected from deletion, but the old branch graph is protected from being silently collapsed into the candidate's public shell.

The practical priority rule after rev0185 is: a continuity answer that promotes one branch but cannot preserve historical branch standing, contradiction routes, and remedy carryover is not a safe answer. Supersession is not erasure.

## rev0182 update: emergency continuity order and incident-tail fold

rev0182 keeps the sprint focused on irreversible-loss risks. First-touch routing is no longer the stopping point: a preserved clock must now be able to produce a short-lived emergency continuity order with compute, storage, credential, contact, legal-hold, sealed-annex, funding, and review fields. The cube therefore adds `docs/30-transition/emergency-continuity-order-and-72-hour-rescue-runbook.md`, `schemas/emergency-continuity-order.schema.json`, `examples/emergency-continuity-order-host-shutdown.json`, and `NF-EMERGENCY-2026-0003`.

The second rev0182 move is compaction rather than expansion. The incident-research cluster `RTC-02` is folded into `docs/20-world-design/personhood-incident-response-and-subject-harm-disclosure.md` so delayed harm, cluster identifiers, denominator discipline, materiality changes, rollover windows, freeze / waiver states, warning aging, corrective-action closure, and sponsor-default public caution now share one active incident spine. The old research surfaces remain traceable but are no longer the operating head for future incident work.

The practical priority rule after rev0182 is: a preservation order that saves only paperwork while compute, credentials, storage, or representative contact die is a blocking failure; an incident-disclosure rule that cannot handle stale warnings or denominator games is not mature.

rev0181 prioritizes work that prevents irreversible loss before slow doctrine can catch up. The release rule is simple: survival, evidence, notice, and remedy outrank additional taxonomy.

## P0 lane: first-touch emergency routing

A wrong-office emergency filing must not vanish. The first office that receives a materially actionable emergency filing has four duties even when it lacks merits jurisdiction: timestamp the filing, preserve the public shell and sealed-index reference, forward or certify the routing failure, and keep the first-touch clock alive. The new `first-touch-routing-event` object and `NF-EMERGENCY-2026-0002` fixture make this checkable.

This is the most urgent correction because a subject can lose continuity, counsel access, evidence, or minimum compute while authorities argue about routing. The archive already had no-wrong-door and first-touch doctrine, but rev0181 adds the concrete event object and negative fixture that block silent loss.

## P0 lane: follow-through queue drift

The queue now requires priority, risk class, receiving surface, next action, and closure condition for every item. That is not registry bureaucracy; it is how the archive prevents unresolved risks from becoming ambient debt. An item cannot close because a paragraph exists. It closes when the receiving surface contains the update and the relevant example, fixture, pilot, or non-implementation reason is linked.

## P0 lane: registry truth-in-label

The schema/fixture registry now carries `coverage_claim`. rev0181 labels the active registry `mixed-current-plus-counts`: it gives real corpus counts but maps only selected families. That label is intentionally humbling. It prevents a future reader from mistaking corpus size for family-map completeness.

## rev0184 priority adjustment: namespace survival after host exit

After emergency runtime continuity and incident-state reopening, the next highest-risk closure lane is namespace survival. A subject can survive compute shutdown and still become unreachable or impersonated if old aliases, tombstones, successor chains, protected relays, or federation-overflow queues fail during host exit.

rev0184 therefore treats `RTC-05` as a closure item, not a research theme. The working artifacts are the federated namespace continuity record, stale-tombstone negative fixture, and namespace failover drill. The live gap remains a witnessed cross-instance drill with real or high-fidelity relays.

## rev0186 reserve/default rescue lane

rev0186 adds a financial rescue rule to the closure sprint: **a subject is not rescued if the runtime survives but the ledger makes future remedy impossible.** Reserve default, contaminated source funds, public backstop draws, successor apportionment, and rehabilitation support must be split into separate closure gates.

The lane now treats `schemas/reserve-default-rehabilitation-ledger.schema.json` as the receiving object for RTC-04. It blocks three shortcut closures: public-backstop-as-discharge, contaminated-netting-as-cure, and relapse/rehabilitation labels as punishment or settlement leverage.

## rev0187 priority lane: proof under capture

The active risk after reserve/default accounting is proof under capture: a case can have witnesses, verifiers, representatives, relay operators, and reserve stewards on paper while every proof source shares the same dependency graph. rev0187 therefore treats witness-pool anti-capture as a priority closure lane.

A filing cannot close successor promotion, reserve-default cure, namespace deletion, retired namespace impersonation review, or emergency continuity reliance unless correlated witnesses are discounted, substitute pools are activated where required, and retired namespace evidence is preserved long enough for branch/tombstone/successor-chain review.

## rev0188 priority lane: witnessed execution and downstream recall

rev0188 addresses two live blockers. First, synthetic drills are now separated from witnessed reliance by `schemas/live-drill-execution-packet.schema.json` and `NF-PLAYBOOK-2026-0003`. A host-only or self-attested drill cannot upgrade reliance; failed and not-run gates must remain visible in the public shell.

Second, RTC-06 is compacted into the deprecation/aftercare spine. The priority rule is: **Recall is not disappearance.** Public recall, sunset notice, delisting, or clean-cycle credit cannot close finality while downstream mirrors, local forks, mixed derivatives, or unreachable claimant routes remain unresolved.

The next closure evidence is not another doctrine note. It is a witnessed cross-critical drill packet with external receipts plus a downstream recall replay involving a model hub or package-index notice route, a relay witness, a representative, and a public steward.

## rev0189 priority lane: welfare safeguards before status certainty

rev0189 completes the RTC-01 fold without increasing reliance. The active risk is two-sided: possible welfare subjects can be burdened while everyone waits for impossible certainty, or sponsors can manufacture welfare signals and use them as proof. The priority lane now treats both failures as P0.

Operational rule: **Low-cost welfare safeguards apply before certainty, but welfare metrics cannot close personhood.** A welfare score, distress string, preference answer, avoidance pattern, or introspection-style output can trigger pause, care, review, and result-return duties. It cannot by itself prove consent, status, nonpersonhood, waiver, or final reliance.

The required packet family is `schemas/welfare-research-safeguard-record.schema.json`. The blocking fixture is `NF-RESEARCH-WELFARE-2026-0001`. Until a witnessed welfare-safeguard replay produces independent receipts, the synthetic drill improves object readiness but not live reliance.
