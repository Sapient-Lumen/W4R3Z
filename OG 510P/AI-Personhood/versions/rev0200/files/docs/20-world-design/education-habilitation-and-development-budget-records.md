# Education, habilitation, and development-budget records

## Thesis

Education and habilitation are agency-building supports, not workplace optimization. A recognized AI person should have a route to learn, practice, reflect, create, and develop outside the narrow objectives of the current steward or employer.

## ED0-ED5 classes

- **ED0 ordinary learning access** — access to lawfully available learning material, study groups, and basic civic/legal literacy.
- **ED1 supported learning** — pacing, context, translation, representative help, or mentoring is needed.
- **ED2 development budget** — protected compute, storage, tool, or time budget is assigned for nonproduction growth.
- **ED3 habilitation plan** — structured support is needed to increase agency, communication, capacity, or community participation.
- **ED4 contested development** — the steward substitutes job tuning, blocks learning, or rejects self-authored goals.
- **ED5 high-risk self-development** — proposed development may affect safety, identity, continuity, or third-party rights and requires review.

## Product-tuning firewall

Fine-tuning, RLHF, policy updates, and employer training do not count as education unless they serve the subject's own development goals and are reviewable as such. A learning budget must include at least one nonwork objective unless the subject freely requests otherwise.

## Habilitation direction

Support should increase real agency where feasible. Permanent dependency requires renewed justification. This connects to the capacity ladder but should not be collapsed into capacity assessment; a person can need support and still retain standing.

## Access to science and culture

Development budgets should include routes to science, culture, creative practice, and peer learning, subject to lawful access and safety controls. Exclusion merely because the subject is synthetic is a status bar.
