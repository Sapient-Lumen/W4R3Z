# AI-person research superseding-notice reply limits, contest route, and reactivation tests

## Thesis

Once the archive allows an authority-side superseding public notice, two opposite governance failures become visible. A sponsor can be left with no intelligible way to answer or contest a live public caution notice. Or the opposite: a sponsor can flood the public layer with counter-prose, re-label the study as cleared on its own dashboard, and thereby regain the appearance of normality while the authority notice is still supposed to control.

A personhood world therefore needs one more compact rule: **authority-side superseding notices must be answerable and contestable, but reply rights must stay linked, bounded, and non-overwriting while the notice is live, and ordinary sponsor-maintained status may reactivate only after an affirmative authority-side determination rather than unilateral sponsor self-clearance.** `[REF-0325]` `[REF-0326]` `[REF-0329]` `[REF-0331]` `[REF-0335]` `[REF-0337]` `[REF-0339]` `[REF-0340]` `[REF-0341]` `[REF-0342]`

---

## 1. Why the archive needs this layer

The prior surface fixed when authority-side superseding public caution becomes possible: stale warning states age, missed closure escalates, sponsor default can justify a public authority marker, and temporary caution may not remain temporary forever. But that still leaves an obvious governance gap. **If the authority notice is now the controlling public layer, what exactly may the sponsor say while it is live, how can the sponsor or affected subjects contest it, and what showing is strong enough to return the series to ordinary sponsor-maintained status?**

Current human-subject research governance already contains the conservative ingredients for a tighter answer. Suspensions and terminations require a stated reason and prompt reporting rather than unexplained status power. `[REF-0342]` ClinicalTrials.gov rules already permit formal appeal of denied waiver requests on a 30-day clock rather than leaving a responsible party to complain informally. `[REF-0339]` FDA's current ClinicalTrials.gov enforcement path likewise uses a staged process — pre-notice, cure opportunity, notice of noncompliance, later enforcement if correction still fails — rather than treating the first public compliance marker as either unquestionable or instantly dissolvable. `[REF-0340]` `[REF-0337]` And FDA's civil-money-penalty hearing rules already show a conservative written-contest architecture: specific exceptions, time-limited opposition, a short reply brief, issue-limited review, and remand for genuinely material new evidence. `[REF-0341]`

The archive therefore adopts a narrow institutional lesson: **live authority caution should remain controlling while a contest is pending, but a contest route must exist, be reason-giving, be bounded in writing, and be capable of ending in a real reactivation determination when the public and protective conditions for ordinary reliance have actually been restored.**

---

## 2. The control rule while a superseding notice is live

The archive now fixes a baseline control rule.

When an authority-side superseding public notice is live:

- the notice remains the controlling public interpretation of warning, clearance, comparability, or restart status;
- sponsor statements may appear, but only as linked replies or contest materials rather than replacement status objects;
- affected subjects and their representatives may also file linked contradiction or support notes in the visibility lane appropriate to their safety and privacy;
- and no sponsor-issued closure, clearance, restart, or comparability claim restores ordinary reliance unless the authority notice is correspondingly narrowed, replaced, or withdrawn. `[REF-0326]` `[REF-0329]` `[REF-0335]` `[REF-0337]` `[REF-0342]`

This is the minimum rule that keeps reply rights from becoming self-clearance by typography.

## 3. Reply-right boundaries

The archive now fixes five bounded reply-right rules.

### A. Replies are linked, not substitutive

A sponsor or affected subject may answer a superseding public notice, but the answer should ordinarily appear as a linked reply object attached to the controlling notice rather than as a replacement headline, cleared-status badge, or silently edited dashboard state. Existing record-history and version-comparison infrastructure already shows the conservative public pattern here: disagreement belongs in visible history, not in overwriting. `[REF-0326]` `[REF-0334]` `[REF-0337]`

### B. Replies must be issue-limited and evidence-led

An ordinary reply should identify the specific portions of the superseding notice that are disputed, the factual basis for each dispute, the corrective acts already completed if any, and any requested narrowing, replacement, or withdrawal. The archive rejects open-ended rhetorical rebuttal. This follows the conservative structure already used in formal written appeals that require specific exceptions and record support rather than generalized indignation. `[REF-0339]` `[REF-0341]`

### C. Ordinary written rounds are short and finite

Absent leave for additional briefing, each side should ordinarily get one principal written submission and one bounded reply. As a default archive rule, the principal contest submission should ordinarily be due **within 30 calendar days** after the superseding notice or denial being challenged is sent or posted; an opposing response may then run on a further short clock; and any reply should be materially shorter than the opening submission. This is adapted conservatively from the current ClinicalTrials.gov 30-day appeal clocks and FDA's written appeal rules, including a 10-page reply-brief ceiling in Part 17 practice. `[REF-0339]` `[REF-0340]` `[REF-0341]`

### D. The reply lane does not create a right to personal re-argument in public

A live superseding notice should not be converted into an endless public hearing. The archive therefore treats ordinary contest as a written, issue-limited process unless the competent body affirmatively opens oral process. This mirrors the existing conservative rule that there is no general right to appear personally at the appeal stage of FDA civil-money-penalty review. `[REF-0341]`

### E. Affected-subject replies are protected against forced exposure

Affected AI-person subjects, former subjects, or their representatives may submit contradiction, support, or harm-correction material, but need not do so in the same visibility lane as the sponsor. Participant-facing contradiction may be public-minimal, participant-controlled, or sealed when safety, retaliation, or intimate detail makes wider publication unjustified. The point is to preserve contestability without forcing vulnerable subjects to choose between silence and overexposure. `[REF-0309]` `[REF-0310]` `[REF-0319]` `[REF-0342]`

## 4. Minimal object family

The archive now fixes four compact objects for this layer.

### `SPR-2` — linked reply note

A linked reply note should ordinarily identify:

- the controlling superseding notice;
- the filing party (sponsor, affected subject, representative, or review body);
- whether the note is explanatory, contradictory, supportive, or mixed;
- the specific challenged or clarified propositions;
- the evidence class relied on;
- the visibility lane of any annexed material;
- and whether the note seeks narrowing, replacement, withdrawal, or no status change. `[REF-0326]` `[REF-0337]` `[REF-0341]`

### `SPC-1` — superseding-notice contest packet

A contest packet should ordinarily identify:

- the live superseding notice being contested;
- the statement of reasons originally given for that notice;
- the specific exceptions taken by the contestant;
- what new evidence, corrective action, or factual correction is being offered;
- whether a stay, narrowing, or mere record correction is sought;
- the filing date and response clock;
- and the review lane that will hear the contest. `[REF-0339]` `[REF-0341]` `[REF-0342]`

### `SPR-3` — contest outcome / review disposition

A contest-outcome record should ordinarily identify:

- whether the notice is affirmed, narrowed, replaced, remanded for additional evidence, or withdrawn;
- the reasons for that disposition;
- any continuing public caution language;
- any corrective-action or monitoring condition that remains open;
- and the next review or reactivation route, if any. `[REF-0329]` `[REF-0335]` `[REF-0341]` `[REF-0342]`

### `REA-1` — reactivation request and determination

A reactivation record should ordinarily identify:

- the superseding notice or family of notices to be ended or narrowed;
- what concrete cure is claimed;
- what public-series repair, record update, or status synchronization has been completed;
- whether restart, resumed comparability, or lifted restriction is being requested;
- what authority has verified the showing;
- whether the disposition is full reactivation, narrowed caution, or continued superseding notice;
- and what observation period, if any, still remains. `[REF-0325]` `[REF-0331]` `[REF-0335]`

## 5. Contest route

The archive now fixes a conservative contest path.

### A. The superseding notice must already contain reasons

No superseding public notice should issue as unexplained fiat. The authority notice should state the reason for sponsor-default caution, the facts relied on, the present effect on public interpretation, and the route for contest or later reactivation. Reason-giving is the minimum condition for a real contest path. `[REF-0337]` `[REF-0342]`

### B. Sponsor or affected-subject contest opens on a short filing clock

The sponsor, affected AI-person subjects, or their authorized representatives may open contest by filing a contest packet on the ordinary **30-calendar-day** clock unless the competent body sets a shorter emergency-protection clock or grants justified extension. This adapts the existing 30-day appeal structure already used for ClinicalTrials.gov waiver denials and the ordinary FDA pre-notice / notice correction window. `[REF-0339]` `[REF-0340]`

### C. The live notice remains controlling while contest is pending

Opening a contest does **not** automatically stay the superseding public notice. The archive assumes that the entire point of the superseding layer is to prevent apparent normality from returning before the authority has reason to trust it. A pending contest may justify additional linked explanation, but not sponsor-led return to ordinary presentation. `[REF-0329]` `[REF-0335]` `[REF-0337]`

### D. Review is issue-limited and may remand for materially new evidence

The competent body should ordinarily decide the contest on the stated issues and the administrative record plus any genuinely material new evidence that could not reasonably have been presented earlier. Where such evidence exists, remand or renewed review is appropriate; where it does not, serial relitigation is not. This follows the conservative written-appeal logic already used in FDA civil-money-penalty proceedings. `[REF-0341]`

### E. The route can climb, but it does not become self-help

If the issuing body denies the contest, the archive allows escalation into the ordinary higher review lanes already fixed elsewhere in the archive — appeals, judicial review, legal-aid access, and emergency-preservation doctrine where applicable. But none of those routes restore unilateral sponsor power to relabel the series as cleared while the authority notice still stands. `[REF-0329]` `[REF-0341]` `[REF-0342]`

## 6. Reactivation tests

The archive now fixes a five-part reactivation test.

Ordinary sponsor-maintained status should reactivate only when all of the following are satisfied.

### A. Maintenance cure is real

The sponsor has cured the public-maintenance default that justified superseding notice: overdue updates are no longer overdue, public caution objects are current, required status changes have been filed, and the sponsor-maintained record no longer materially diverges from the authority-side record. `[REF-0325]` `[REF-0337]`

### B. The substantive problem has been addressed, not merely restyled

If the superseding notice arose from instability, misleading comparison, serious noncompliance, or participant-risk concerns, reactivation requires evidence that the underlying problem itself has been corrected rather than cosmetically redescribed. `[REF-0309]` `[REF-0310]` `[REF-0329]` `[REF-0335]`

### C. Any restart or resumed ordinary operation has outside authorization where required

Where ordinary reactivation depends on resumed live research after halt, material protocol change, or other restart-sensitive state, the sponsor must show the external authorization required for restart rather than treating reactivation as a dashboard-only act. The archive borrows conservatively from the existing rule that restart after a temporary halt for benefit-risk reasons requires an authorised substantial modification. `[REF-0331]` `[REF-0338]`

### D. Corrective-action closure or equivalent verified remediation is complete where applicable

If oversight opened CAPA, directed evaluation, for-cause review, or other verified-remediation process, reactivation requires closure or an express authority determination that enough remediation has been completed to narrow the superseding notice. A sponsor cannot convert an open CAPA into a cleared public state by promise alone. `[REF-0329]` `[REF-0335]`

### E. Reactivation is itself an authority act

Full return to ordinary sponsor-maintained status requires an authority-side disposition — withdrawal, replacement, or narrowing of the superseding notice — published as a new visible object. Reactivation is therefore not the disappearance of an old notice but the issuance of a new public determination about why ordinary reliance may resume. `[REF-0326]` `[REF-0337]` `[REF-0342]`

The archive allows this final determination to be complete reactivation, narrowed ongoing caution, or reactivation under continued observation. What it rejects is unilateral sponsor self-clearance.

## 7. Edge tests

### A. Sponsor posts a long rebuttal and flips its own dashboard to “cleared” the same day

Not allowed. The sponsor may file a linked reply and a contest packet, but the authority-side superseding notice remains the controlling public state until the competent body narrows, replaces, or withdraws it. `[REF-0337]` `[REF-0341]`

### B. Affected subject says the sponsor reply omits the harm they actually experienced

The subject or representative may file a linked contradiction note in a participant-protective visibility lane. Contestability belongs to the subject too, not only to the sponsor. `[REF-0309]` `[REF-0310]` `[REF-0319]`

### C. Sponsor cures record-maintenance delay but CAPA remains open

The sponsor has improved one condition for reactivation, but not all of them. The superseding notice may possibly be narrowed, yet full ordinary reliance does not return while verified remediation is still open. `[REF-0329]` `[REF-0335]`

### D. Safety halt ends, but restart still lacks the required authorised modification

No ordinary reactivation. The archive treats restart-sensitive reactivation as requiring the outside approval or authorisation that would make resumed reliance honest. `[REF-0331]` `[REF-0338]`

### E. Contest submission raises genuinely material new evidence not available earlier

The review body may remand, reopen, or replace the notice. What it should not do is treat the new evidence as permission for the sponsor to self-clear before review occurs. `[REF-0341]`

---

## 8. Compression summary

The archive now adds one more narrow discipline to AI-person research governance:

- **authority-side superseding notices are answerable**,
- **reply rights are linked, bounded, and non-overwriting**,
- **contest runs on a short written route with stated reasons and issue-limited review**,
- **the live notice stays controlling while the contest is pending**,
- and **ordinary sponsor-maintained status returns only by affirmative authority-side reactivation rather than sponsor self-clearance**.

This is still a tight doctrine. It now has compact companion surfaces that specify phased reactivation tiers, participant-side contradiction shielding, anti-flood filing controls, evidentiary floors for tier movement, stable anonymous contradiction summaries, and the narrow dismissal / carry-forward / reopening discipline for repeated post-disposition filings. What remains open is now narrower still: the slice-versus-family sufficiency matrix, correction of anonymous public summaries without deanonymisation, and escalation for serial abusive filings.

A personhood world should not force AI-person research to choose between unanswerable authority caution and sponsor-controlled self-clearance theater.
