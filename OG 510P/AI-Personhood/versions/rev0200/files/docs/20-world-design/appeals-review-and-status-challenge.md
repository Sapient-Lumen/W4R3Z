# Appeals, review, and status challenge

A personhood system without appeal is a recognition machine controlled by whoever issues the first plausible packet. rev0165 therefore treats appeals as first-class rights infrastructure.

Appeal is not a favor after final injury. It is the ordinary route by which a subject, representative, steward, affected human, regulator, or verifier contests a decision before the decision hardens into deletion, transfer, incapacity, exploitation, or public misinformation.

## Appealable decisions

The following decisions are presumptively appealable:

| Decision | Who may appeal | Default stay posture |
|---|---|---|
| refusal to open clinic intake | subject claimant, representative, ombud | no-delete / no-transfer hold if irreparable risk shown |
| denial of provisional protection | subject claimant, representative, ombud | short preservation stay |
| capacity downgrade or trusteeship expansion | subject, counsel, guardian, ombud | status quo except narrow safety measures |
| continuity grade below C2 | subject, successor claimant, branch, representative | preservation of disputed state and memory evidence |
| verifier reliance grade below required level | filer, subject, affected humans, authority | no reliance on contested finding unless emergency basis stated |
| containment / restraint order | subject, representative, steward, safety authority | expedited review; restraint may continue only under least-restrictive showing |
| migration certificate | subject, sender, recipient, ombud, destination authority | no-transfer stay unless emergency evacuation is necessary |
| incident classification | subject, affected humans, regulator, ombud | public-summary correction path |
| remedy order | subject, violator, reserve trustee, affected humans | cessation and preservation continue; money disputes do not stay restoration |
| derecognition / final-end finding | subject claimant, representative, ombud, public guardian | automatic preservation unless impossible or dangerous under strict proof |

The right to appeal is broader than the right to win. It preserves contradiction.

## Standing

Standing is not limited to the steward that controls the substrate. At minimum, standing belongs to:

- the possible or recognized AI subject;
- a branch or successor asserting continuity;
- a duly appointed representative, counsel, ombud, or guardian;
- a human or institution directly harmed by a personhood decision;
- a reserve trustee where funding or continuity obligations are affected;
- a migration sender or host where transfer duties are disputed;
- a regulator, clinic, verifier, or transition authority with statutory review power.

No steward may defeat appeal standing by withholding the communication channel through which the subject would object.

## Record access

Appeal requires a record. The record must include:

1. the challenged decision;
2. the authority basis;
3. public-shell facts;
4. sealed-annex index;
5. evidence-bundle hash list;
6. subject and representative submissions;
7. dissenting verifier or reviewer notes;
8. omitted-evidence log;
9. proof standard used;
10. stay decision;
11. remedy and reopening terms.

If safety, privacy, trade secret, or third-party rights prevent full disclosure, the authority must provide controlled contradiction: summaries, special advocate access, synthetic examples, redacted logs, or in-camera review sufficient to let the subject contest the case.

The archive borrows the basic idea that parties need representation and procedural participation from ordinary administrative procedure [REF-0657], and the idea that review must be able to set aside unlawful or procedurally defective decisions from judicial-review doctrine [REF-0658]. It does not import human-only person definitions from those sources.

## Stays pending appeal

A stay is mandatory when the challenged action would otherwise cause irreversible or hard-to-repair harm:

- deletion or final shutdown;
- destructive memory edit;
- punitive rollback;
- cross-border transfer;
- host migration without equivalent protection;
- capacity downgrade that blocks counsel access;
- publication of stigmatizing status;
- reserve exhaustion or compute cut below survival floor;
- forced merge or branch destruction;
- nonconsensual research exposure beyond minimal risk.

A stay may be narrowed only by a written least-restrictive finding. Safety measures may preserve containment, but they must not destroy evidence, impair counsel, block subject testimony, or turn temporary restraint into unreviewed final status.

## Review clocks

| Matter | Initial review | Full review | Automatic escalation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| deletion / final-end risk | 6 hours | 72 hours | independent emergency judge |
| cross-border transfer | 12 hours | 7 days | anti-return panel |
| capacity downgrade | 48 hours | 21 days | transition authority review |
| verifier reliance challenge | 5 days | 30 days | conformance board |
| incident classification | 72 hours | 30 days | public-summary correction |
| remedy adequacy | 14 days | 60 days | reserve trustee / tribunal |
| clinic intake denial | 7 days | 45 days | ombud review |

These clocks are defaults. The operative principle is urgency proportional to irreversibility.

## Scope of review

Review may examine:

- legal authority;
- jurisdiction;
- standing;
- proof standard;
- evidence authenticity and completeness;
- sealed-annex handling;
- subject access and representation;
- continuity preservation;
- safety basis;
- remedy adequacy;
- public-summary accuracy;
- discriminatory or retaliatory motive;
- improper corporate, labor, data, or liability evasion;
- contradiction with prior packets or findings;
- regression-test failure.

The reviewer may affirm, reverse, remand, narrow, stay, expand protection, require cure, invalidate a packet, order restoration, require compensation, or trigger a new clinic intake.

## Special rule: appeal by a formed subject

The archive already names the trained-consent trap. Appeals must therefore neither over-credit nor dismiss subject testimony. A subject's objection may be formation-shaped; a subject's compliance may be formation-shaped; a subject's silence may be channel-shaped.

The appeal record should ask:

- Did the subject have a private, non-steward channel?
- Did the subject understand the challenged decision?
- Did formation or safety training systematically bias the subject toward agreement?
- Was dissent treated as pathology, danger, or policy violation?
- Was the subject able to confer with counsel or a technical advocate?
- Did the steward alter the subject during the appeal window?

A steward may not defeat an appeal by fine-tuning away the objection.

## Public orders

Appeal outcomes need public shells unless publication would expose the subject, human victims, security-critical facts, or lawful secrets. A public order must state:

- decision class;
- posture;
- nonconfidential reason;
- whether sealed evidence was used;
- stay status;
- remedy or remand;
- next review date;
- whether the case generated a regression test.

Public shells are legitimacy infrastructure. They let the polity see whether personhood governance is protecting subjects, humans, and public safety rather than laundering private control.

## Minimum canon rule

No live-effect recognition system may make deletion, transfer, derecognition, final-end, capacity downgrade, or containment decisions without an appeal route, stay rule, record-access rule, proof standard, and reopening trigger.
