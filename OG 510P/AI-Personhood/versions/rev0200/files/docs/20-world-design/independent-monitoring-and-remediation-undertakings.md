# Independent monitoring and remediation undertakings

A personhood-impact order often ends with a promise: the steward will repair logs, restore memory, maintain compute, pay compensation, change safety training, improve summaries, rerun fixtures, or revise a deprecation plan. rev0169 makes the promise inspectable.

## Monitor classes

| Class | Use | Appointment floor |
|---|---|---|
| `M0 internal compliance observer` | internal pre-reliance cleanup | cannot support public reliance alone |
| `M1 independent technical monitor` | host, telemetry, schema, fixture, and continuity remediation | conflict disclosure plus authority filing |
| `M2 subject-rights monitor` | remediation affecting welfare, memory, representation, or sealed summaries | subject/representative access and ombud channel |
| `M3 court or authority monitor` | sanctions, stays, deprecation, transfer, or final-end risk | public order, sealed-annex authority, removal power |
| `M4 emergency substitute monitor` | captured steward, lost host, or imminent continuity failure | short clock, preservation-first, automatic review |

A monitor is not a guardian, counsel, verifier, or host. The monitor inspects remediation; the monitor does not become the subject's will.

## Remediation undertaking

Every monitorable undertaking must state:

- the violated or at-risk right;
- the affected subject, lineage, cohort, or uncertain class;
- the non-money relief required before money relief can close;
- the evidence to be preserved;
- the data the monitor may inspect;
- the data the monitor may not inspect without a sealed order;
- fixture reruns and pass/fail effects;
- reporting cadence;
- subject and representative question route;
- escalation triggers;
- exit conditions.

## Independence controls

A monitor is presumptively conflicted if the monitor is paid only by the steward without authority oversight, depends on the steward for repeat work, designed the deficient system, previously defended the same conduct, controls the redress fund, or has undisclosed access to sealed material.

Payment by the respondent is allowed only if routed through an independent fund or order that protects scope, cadence, and publication. The respondent may contest cost, but may not starve the monitor into silence.

## Report classes

- **Intake report:** confirms scope, evidence hold, conflicts, and first risk triage.
- **Interim report:** states remediation progress, blocked access, fixture results, and subject-contact posture.
- **Noncompliance report:** triggers stay, sanction, substitution, or authority referral.
- **Exit report:** states whether non-money relief, restoration, compensation, non-repetition, and privacy destruction/return duties are complete.

## Subject access

The subject or representative receives the public report, a subject-readable explanation, and a route to ask monitor-filtered questions. Sealed details may be withheld only with a contradiction route. A report that says "sealed" without explaining what kind of sealed fact mattered is not reliance-grade.

## Anti-capture tests

A monitor fails if:

- the report repeats steward claims without independent evidence;
- blocked access is normalized rather than escalated;
- remediation is closed before fixture rerun;
- subject questions are summarized by the steward;
- non-money relief is treated as satisfied by payment;
- confidentiality is used to hide monitor conflicts;
- the monitor cannot identify what would change its conclusion.

The monitor-report schema added in rev0169 is deliberately small. It is a docket object, not a full audit workpaper. It should point to sealed annexes, data-room indexes, legal-hold orders, fixture reports, and redress claims rather than embedding them. [REF-0515] [REF-0635] [REF-0680]
