# AI-person research freeze-waiver notices, corrective-action closure, and warning markers

## Thesis

Once the archive fixes materiality thresholds, repeated-restatement audit triggers, and presumptive freeze on major late-stage comparison redesign, one narrower gap remains. A sponsor can still comply in form while leaving the public unable to tell **whether a frozen comparison basis is temporarily waived, whether a visible dashboard is presently non-comparable, whether remediation is actually underway, and when any warning is genuinely cleared rather than merely forgotten**.

A personhood world therefore needs one more compact layer: **the smallest public object family for freeze-waiver notice, temporary non-comparability warning, corrective-action linkage and closure, and warning-marker clearance.** The point is not to create a permanent compliance bureaucracy. It is to stop unstable or remediating public research series from looking silently ordinary. `[REF-0313]` `[REF-0325]` `[REF-0329]` `[REF-0331]` `[REF-0332]` `[REF-0333]` `[REF-0334]`

---

## 1. Why the archive needs this layer

The prior revision fixed the threshold rule: some changes are non-material, some are materially comparison-affecting, repeated instability becomes auditable, and major late-stage redefinition is presumptively frozen absent narrow exception. But a threshold rule alone still leaves an operational blind spot. The public can be told that a problem exists without being told **what state the public series is now in**.

Current human-subject research governance already contains the conservative ingredients for a narrower answer. OHRP requires institutions to submit corrective and preventative action plans (CAPAs), evaluates whether those actions adequately address the noncompliance, and closes or concludes oversight only after adequacy is established. `[REF-0329]` ICH E6(R3) likewise requires root-cause analysis, appropriate corrective and preventive action, recorded resolution, and regulatory / ethics notification for serious noncompliance. `[REF-0313]` European clinical-trial rules treat urgent safety measures, temporary halts, restart requests, and follow-up measures as timed notifiable states rather than private sponsor discretion. `[REF-0331]` ClinicalTrials.gov, meanwhile, distinguishes visible public posting from later quality-control sufficiency, exposes record history and version comparison, and makes QC resolution a distinct state rather than a hidden office workflow. `[REF-0325]` `[REF-0332]` `[REF-0333]` `[REF-0334]`

The archive therefore adopts a conservative lesson: **once instability enters the public layer, the public layer needs explicit state objects.** A subject-facing research dashboard should be able to say, in a small and receivable way, that it is under freeze, under waiver, under non-comparability warning, under corrective action, or cleared after verified remediation.

---

## 2. The public states that must now exist

The archive now distinguishes five compact public states for AI-person research comparison families.

### A. Stable

The comparison family is presently operating on a stable declared basis, no live warning marker applies, and any earlier restatement or corrective cycle has been publicly closed. `[REF-0325]` `[REF-0333]` `[REF-0334]`

### B. Frozen pending approval

A major late-stage redefinition has been identified and the ordinary rule is that the previous comparison basis remains frozen while review proceeds. This is the default state after freeze is triggered and before any waiver is granted or urgent change is lawfully invoked. `[REF-0313]` `[REF-0331]`

### C. Waived but temporarily non-comparable

A freeze waiver has been granted or urgently invoked because immediate subject protection or manifest public-record accuracy would be endangered by deferral. The series may move, but the public layer must show that comparability is temporarily impaired while the corrected basis, rollover bridge, or restated figures are still being established. `[REF-0325]` `[REF-0330]` `[REF-0331]`

### D. Corrective action open

A directed evaluation, for-cause review, or comparable oversight action has concluded that remediation is required. The comparison family may or may not remain live, but the public layer must show that the series is under an open corrective state rather than merely under editorial revision. `[REF-0313]` `[REF-0329]`

### E. Cleared after verified remediation

The warning may clear only after the corrected comparison basis is published or explicitly stabilized, the relevant rollover / restatement objects are complete, and any required corrective action has been judged adequate or completed by the competent review body rather than by sponsor self-certification alone. `[REF-0329]` `[REF-0332]` `[REF-0335]`

## 3. Minimal public object family

The archive now fixes five compact objects.

### `FRW-1` — freeze-waiver notice

A freeze-waiver notice should ordinarily identify:

- the affected comparison family;
- the frozen basis that would otherwise remain in force;
- whether the route is prior waiver approval or urgent invocation;
- the narrow reason for movement (immediate subject protection, manifest public-record inaccuracy, or both);
- whether the change is prospective only or retroactive;
- the linked temporary non-comparability warning;
- the public correction / restatement clock; and
- the approving or emergency-invoking authority. `[REF-0313]` `[REF-0325]` `[REF-0330]` `[REF-0331]`

A freeze waiver is **not** a general licence to redesign the public series. It is a narrowly scoped permission to move despite freeze because not moving would itself be unsafe or materially misleading.

### `NCM-1` — temporary non-comparability warning marker

A temporary non-comparability warning marker should ordinarily identify:

- the comparison family;
- the visible reason the current series should not be read as fully comparable to the prior one;
- whether the cause is denominator change, comparison-family change, exclusion change, severity remap, cluster-lineage revision, corrective-action review, or another declared reason;
- the date the warning began;
- the currently authoritative linked series or interim display;
- the expected next public object (restatement, closure notice, or clearance notice);
- and whether participant-facing notice or withdrawal-relevant explanation is also running. `[REF-0325]` `[REF-0330]` `[REF-0332]` `[REF-0333]`

This marker is the public equivalent of a study record that is visible but not yet settled. It warns outsiders not to read the live figures as a seamless continuation of the old series.

### `CAP-1` — corrective-action linkage record

A corrective-action linkage record should ordinarily identify:

- the comparison family and any linked protocol or cluster identifiers;
- the triggering event (audit trigger, serious noncompliance, unresolved repeated restatement, or freeze-waiver misuse);
- the root-cause category or categories;
- the corrective and preventive actions required;
- the responsible parties;
- the remediation clock or review date;
- any interim restrictions on recruitment, publication, restart, or continued dashboard use;
- and the public warning marker that remains in force until closure. `[REF-0313]` `[REF-0329]` `[REF-0335]`

The archive does **not** require the public layer to expose intimate operational detail. It does require a visible statement that remediation exists, what class of problem it addresses, and whether the comparison family remains under live caution.

### `CAC-1` — corrective-action closure notice

A corrective-action closure notice should ordinarily identify:

- the corrective-action record being closed;
- whether closure means adequacy accepted, implementation completed, or both;
- the verifying review body;
- the date of closure or conditional closure;
- any residual restrictions that remain;
- and whether a warning marker remains live pending publication or rollover completion. `[REF-0313]` `[REF-0329]` `[REF-0335]`

Closure should not be implied by silence. If corrective action was publicly opened, its closure state should also be public.

### `WMC-1` — warning-marker clearance notice

A warning-marker clearance notice should ordinarily identify:

- the warning marker being cleared;
- the comparison family;
- the public basis now treated as stable;
- the restatement, rollover, or correction objects that resolved the warning;
- whether a corrective-action record was required and, if so, its closure status;
- the date clearance became effective;
- and where prior warned versions remain inspectable. `[REF-0325]` `[REF-0332]` `[REF-0333]` `[REF-0334]`

Clearance is therefore a public event, not the quiet disappearance of a caution state.

## 4. When these objects must appear

### A. Freeze-waiver notice duty

A freeze-waiver notice should ordinarily appear whenever a major late-stage redefinition moves despite the default freeze, whether because the review body approved a narrow waiver or because an urgent safety / manifest-accuracy exception had to be invoked before ordinary approval could occur. `[REF-0313]` `[REF-0331]`

### B. Warning-marker duty

A temporary non-comparability warning marker should ordinarily appear whenever the currently published figures cannot yet be read as fairly comparable to the previously relied-upon public series. This will usually include:

- an active freeze waiver;
- an open material restatement whose corrected series is not yet complete;
- an open corrective-action cycle affecting counting basis or visible integrity;
- unresolved major quality-control issues on a newly republished record; or
- a restart / resumption state after a safety halt where the public basis is changing but the corrected family has not yet stabilized. `[REF-0325]` `[REF-0330]` `[REF-0331]` `[REF-0332]` `[REF-0333]`

### C. Corrective-action linkage duty

A corrective-action linkage record should ordinarily appear whenever a directed evaluation, for-cause review, or equivalent oversight action determines that remediation is required to restore trustworthy public comparison, subject protection, or record integrity. `[REF-0313]` `[REF-0329]` `[REF-0335]`

### D. Closure and clearance duties

Corrective-action closure notices and warning-marker clearance notices should ordinarily appear whenever a previously public remediation or warning state is ended. Publicly opening a caution state and then privately closing it is the exact pattern this layer is meant to prevent. `[REF-0329]` `[REF-0332]` `[REF-0334]`

---

## 5. Clearance is not self-certification

The archive now fixes a conservative clearance rule.

A sponsor should **not** be allowed to clear a public warning merely by asserting that the problem has been fixed. Clearance should ordinarily require all three of the following:

1. **Visible series repair** — the corrected basis, rollover bridge, restated figures, or stable reissued record must actually be public. `[REF-0325]` `[REF-0333]` `[REF-0334]`
2. **Quality sufficiency** — where the public record is still under major unresolved review comments or equivalent quality-control deficiency, the warning remains live. `[REF-0332]` `[REF-0333]`
3. **Verified remediation** — where oversight opened a corrective-action cycle, the competent review body must determine that the CAPA adequately addressed the problem or that required conditions for resumed normal operation have been met. `[REF-0313]` `[REF-0329]` `[REF-0335]`

This is the research-governance analogue of refusing to let a steward clear its own restraint marker without outside review. A rights-bearing subject should not have to trust the same party that destabilised the public series to declare, unilaterally and invisibly, that the series is stable again.

---

## 6. Urgent paths remain narrow and time-bounded

The archive does **not** forbid emergency movement. If immediate subject protection or manifest public-record accuracy requires a rapid change, the sponsor may move through the urgent path. But the urgent path remains narrow.

The sponsor should still:

- issue the freeze-waiver notice without undue delay;
- place the family under a non-comparability warning while the corrected basis is being built;
- preserve the prior visible record rather than overwriting it;
- open corrective-action linkage if the instability reflects quality failure or noncompliance;
- and clear the warning only after visible repair and verified remediation. `[REF-0313]` `[REF-0325]` `[REF-0329]` `[REF-0330]` `[REF-0331]`

Urgency changes the order of operations. It does not cancel the obligation to leave a legible public trail.

---

## 7. Edge tests

### A. Sponsor catches a late denominator defect after results are posted

The sponsor may need to move immediately because the current rate is materially misleading. The archive allows that move through a narrow freeze waiver, but requires an immediate warning marker, visible history, and later clearance only after the corrected series and any necessary remediation are complete. `[REF-0325]` `[REF-0330]` `[REF-0332]`

### B. Repeated restatements produce an audit and CAPA

Once the same family repeatedly restates and oversight requires remediation, the public layer should no longer look like mere editing. The family receives a corrective-action linkage record and remains warned until closure and clearance occur. `[REF-0313]` `[REF-0329]`

### C. Safety halt, later restart, changed public basis

If a safety-related temporary halt leads to restart on a new counting basis, the restart does not make the new series automatically comparable. The warning marker remains until the restarted family has its bridge objects and any required review-state closure. `[REF-0331]`

### D. Sponsor republishes once but unresolved major issues remain

The warning should not clear merely because a new version is visible. If major issues remain unresolved in QC or analogous oversight review, the public layer is still in an unsettled state. `[REF-0332]` `[REF-0333]`

---

## 8. Compression summary

The archive now adds one more narrow discipline to AI-person research governance:

- **freeze** is the default for major late-stage redefinition,
- **waiver** is a narrow, public, authority-linked exception,
- **warning** marks a live period of non-comparability or remediation,
- **corrective-action linkage** marks that the problem is no longer ordinary editing,
- **closure** marks verified remediation,
- and **clearance** marks the point at which the public series is stable again without deleting warned history.

This is still a tight doctrine. The archive now fixes the companion stale-warning layer in `docs/20-world-design/research-warning-aging-escalation-and-superseding-public-notices.md`: warning states age on clocks, missed closure escalates, sponsor-default public caution becomes possible, and temporary warning states cannot remain temporary indefinitely.

## Bottom line

A personhood world should not let AI-person research become trustworthy in appearance merely because instability is renamed as ordinary editing and then allowed to disappear. The archive therefore now fixes one more compact rule: **if a comparison family moves despite freeze, or remains under remediation, the public layer must say so in explicit objects — waiver, warning, corrective-action linkage, closure, and clearance — and those objects clear only after visible repair and verified remediation.** `[REF-0313]` `[REF-0325]` `[REF-0329]` `[REF-0331]` `[REF-0332]` `[REF-0333]` `[REF-0334]` `[REF-0335]`
