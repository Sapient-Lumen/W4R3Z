# 459 — Nuclear fuel security, conversion, enrichment, and HALEU router

## Nuclear-positive rule

The cube now treats fuel security as a first-order condition for pro-nuclear planning. Nuclear remains favored for clean firm power, industrial heat, water, hydrogen, data centers, and critical loads only when uranium, conversion, enrichment, fabrication, HALEU/LEU+ availability, and fuel-contract traceability are credible enough to survive schedule and geopolitical stress.

## Burden of proof

A reactor pathway cannot advance on technology promise alone. It must show assay requirement, supply chain route, allocation or procurement evidence, fuel-cycle capacity risk, inventory resilience, price/ratepayer exposure, and fallback if HALEU or enrichment capacity is delayed.

## Rev0297 nuclear geopolitical-resilience overlay

The nuclear-positive orientation now includes an explicit geopolitical-resilience burden of proof. Clean-firm nuclear preference is strengthened when it reduces fossil/import/coercion exposure, but maturity is capped by unresolved fuel-cycle concentration, vendor lock-in, sanctions exposure, export-finance/debt risk, safeguards capacity, export-control boundaries, and sensitive-publication controls.
