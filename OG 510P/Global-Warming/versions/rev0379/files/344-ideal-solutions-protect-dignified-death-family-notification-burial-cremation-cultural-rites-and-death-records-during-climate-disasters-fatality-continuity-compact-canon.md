# 344 — Ideal Solutions: Protect dignified death, family notification, burial, cremation, cultural rites, and death records during climate disasters

## Claim

Mass casualty is not finished when response ends. **Dignified management of the dead, family notification, death certification, burial / cremation access, cultural rites, and survivor support are climate-critical service floors.**

WHO / PAHO / ICRC guidance says management of the dead is one of the most difficult disaster-response functions, has profound consequences for survivors and communities, and is often overlooked in planning [S614]. ASPR TRACIE's fatality-management resources reinforce that mass-fatality preparedness requires legal authority, capacity, and coordination [S615].

## Compact rule

**Every mass-casualty climate packet needs a fatality-management and family-assistance annex.**

The annex should cover retrieval, identification, chain of custody, temporary storage, death certification, family notification, missing-persons reconciliation, cultural and religious handling, burial / cremation access, funeral-cost help, records portability, and trauma support. It should protect dignity without hiding accountability for preventable deaths.

## Minimum packet

A fatality-continuity packet includes:

- medical examiner / coroner / public-health authority map;
- surge morgue and cold-storage plan;
- disaster victim identification and chain-of-custody protocols;
- family assistance center and multilingual notification process;
- cultural, religious, Indigenous, and community liaison roster;
- death-record continuity and benefit / inheritance routing;
- privacy and public-accountability rules;
- after-action mortality and service-failure review.

## Failure modes

The bad version treats bodies as logistics. Families wait without information. Death certificates lag benefits, inheritance, insurance, and custody decisions. Unidentified dead and missing-person lists diverge. Cultural rites are ignored. Officials undercount deaths because deaths are indirect, delayed, or administratively inconvenient.

The archive's rule: **dignity, records, and accountability must survive the shock.**

## Cube routing

Route this file whenever a packet mentions mass casualty, death, mortality, missing persons, family reunification, death certificates, funeral support, morgue capacity, cultural rites, burial, cremation, or disaster victim identification. Pair with `304`, `315`, `335`, `336`, `337`, and `339`.
