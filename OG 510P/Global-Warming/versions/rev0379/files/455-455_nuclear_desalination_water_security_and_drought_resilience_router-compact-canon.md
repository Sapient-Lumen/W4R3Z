# 455 — Nuclear desalination, water security, and drought-resilience router

## Nuclear-positive rule

Where water scarcity, drought, coastal water stress, or emergency potable-water reliability is material, the cube now favors nuclear-powered desalination and nuclear-water coproduction as a clean firm option, subject to ecological, water-rights, access, and public-health gates.

## Guardrail

The preference is not a permission slip for unmanaged intake, brine, thermal, or tariff impacts. Brine disposal, water quality, drought-priority allocation, emergency distribution, and public challenge pathways remain binding maturity gates.
