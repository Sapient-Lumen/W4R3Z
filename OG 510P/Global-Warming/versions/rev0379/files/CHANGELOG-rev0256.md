## Core change

Rev0255 asked what happens when people have to stay, move, arrive, and carry rights / services / records / benefits / remedies across climate mobility.
Rev0256 asks what happens before, during, and after that movement if food access and nutrition fail.

The revision adds two rules:

1. **Food security and nutrition are climate-critical services.** Adaptation, recovery, mobility, social-protection, agrifood, water, health, city-receiving, loss-and-damage, and climate-finance packets are not complete unless they protect food access and nutrition through the shock.
2. **Food systems need shock absorbers before hunger cascades.** Market transparency, price triggers, reserves, trade discipline, fertilizer and input access, logistics corridors, cash / voucher / in-kind switching rules, local procurement, and anticipatory finance must be formed before forecasts and price signals mature into hunger, malnutrition, forced movement, or famine.

## Compression

**Do not treat hunger as humanitarian cleanup after the climate plan. Protect food and nutrition as critical services, and govern the food-system conditions that turn heat, drought, flood, disaster loss, input shocks, trade panic, and price spikes into household depletion.**
