## Doctrine shift

Rev0258 extends the water / food / mobility / critical-service front door into health operations. It asks climate packets to prove that surveillance, early warning, care floors, medicines, vaccines, health workers, vector control, waterborne-outbreak prevention, One Health interfaces, and after-action correction are ready before climate exposure becomes illness or preventable death.

## Compression

Climate protection now includes public-health operating proof: climate-informed signals must trigger action, care floors must survive shocks, disease ecology must be governed before outbreaks cascade, and prevention must become part of adaptation rather than a separate health-sector afterthought.
