## Why this revision

The archive already had compact rules for defaults, anti-capture, managed fossil decline, fallback behaviour, and politically durable delivery. What it still lacked was the shortest explicit rule for climate exceptions and carve-outs.

This revision adds that missing rule in compact form:

- some hardship, safety, reliability, or transition exceptions may be necessary
- but they should stay narrow, explicit, visible, reviewable, and sunset-bound
- they should include a route back to the main clean path
- and repeated exception demand should usually trigger repair of the baseline design rather than an endless accumulation of loopholes

## Result

The archive can now answer not only what the main climate path should be and what happens on bad days, but also how exceptions should be governed so they do not quietly become the real policy.
