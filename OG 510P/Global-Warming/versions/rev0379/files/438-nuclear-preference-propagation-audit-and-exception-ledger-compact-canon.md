# Nuclear preference propagation audit and exception ledger

A declared nuclear-positive orientation is not enough. It must propagate into routes, service floors, scorecards, exception ledgers and query views. Otherwise the preference stays rhetorical while old assumptions continue to route energy reliability to fossil fallback or generic flexibility.

The cube now treats nuclear exclusion as an auditable event. A service floor may reject, defer, or bracket nuclear, but the reason should be visible. Valid exceptions include legal prohibition, no credible site, water infeasibility, safety or emergency-planning failure, unclosed waste/fuel gap, inferior economics, public legitimacy failure, schedule mismatch, lower-cost non-nuclear clean-firm solution, or non-energy relevance.

## Rule

For energy, grid, industrial heat, high-load, water-security and critical-load service floors, the cube must now contain one of three records:

- **favored_with_gates** — nuclear is part of the preferred solution set, subject to gates;
- **route_to_nuclear_assessment** — nuclear must be considered but is not yet selected;
- **nuclear_exception_recorded** — nuclear is excluded or deferred with an auditable reason.

This rule is grounded in the same clean-firm and delivery-risk logic as the nuclear-positive files [S771][S773][S774][S776][S790][S791]. System-cost, SMR-readiness and counterargument sources prevent the preference from becoming hidden scoring [S775][S780][S784][S792].

## Refactor instruction

Every future energy or high-load canon addition should update `nuclear-preference-propagation-audit.csv`. Missing propagation rows are data-quality defects.
