# 478 — Nuclear human-capital scorecard and institutional-capacity audit

## Audit function

This file closes the rev0298 refactor by making human capital queryable. It adds scorecards, maturity caps, workforce gap backlogs, training/credential crosswalks, and publication controls so the cube can favor nuclear without pretending that people, regulators, inspectors, operators, craft labor, and quality culture appear automatically.

## Nuclear-positive rule

Nuclear remains the preferred clean-firm backbone where it can beat fossil counterfactuals and satisfy safety, waste, water, fuel, security, finance, legitimacy, and system-benefit gates. Rev0298 adds a human-capital condition: nuclear is preferred only when the human system can build, license, inspect, operate, maintain, learn, and govern it.

## Audit/refactor output

The revision introduces `cube/nuclear-human-capital-scorecard.csv`, `cube/nuclear-human-capital-propagation-audit.csv`, `cube/nuclear-workforce-source-authority-audit.csv`, and `cube/nuclear-human-capital-maturity-cap-execution.csv`.

## Publication boundary

Public reporting should expose whether staffing, training, and regulator capacity gates are met. It should not publish sensitive staffing vulnerabilities, exact security-role rosters, personal qualification records, or details that would weaken site security or emergency response.
