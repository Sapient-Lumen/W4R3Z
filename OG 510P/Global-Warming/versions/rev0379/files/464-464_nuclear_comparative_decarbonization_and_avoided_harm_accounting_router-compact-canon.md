# 464 — Nuclear comparative decarbonization and avoided-harm accounting router

## Nuclear-positive rule

The cube now favors nuclear not merely because it is low-carbon in isolation, but because it can measurably avoid fossil generation, air pollution, reliability shortfall, land/material pressure, or fuel-price exposure when placed in the right system context.

## Burden of proof

Every benefit claim requires a counterfactual. The cube records what nuclear displaces, which emissions and public-health harms are avoided, how the claim is attributed, and whether the same benefit is being double counted by another resource or policy.

## Rev0297 nuclear geopolitical-resilience overlay

The nuclear-positive orientation now includes an explicit geopolitical-resilience burden of proof. Clean-firm nuclear preference is strengthened when it reduces fossil/import/coercion exposure, but maturity is capped by unresolved fuel-cycle concentration, vendor lock-in, sanctions exposure, export-finance/debt risk, safeguards capacity, export-control boundaries, and sensitive-publication controls.
