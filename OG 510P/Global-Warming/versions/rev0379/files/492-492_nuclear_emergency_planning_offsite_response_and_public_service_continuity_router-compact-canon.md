# 492 — Nuclear emergency planning, offsite response, and public-service continuity router

## Function

This service-continuity router connects nuclear resilience with civil protection: evacuation, shelter, hospitals and critical loads, public alerts, language access, disability access, flooded routes, heat/smoke conflicts, communications outages, and offsite response infrastructure.

## Nuclear-positive rule

Nuclear is favored as a public-service stabilizer only if emergency planning and offsite response can function during the climate hazards most likely to stress the plant and surrounding communities.

## Refactor output

The revision adds emergency-planning climate-access tables, critical-load priority protocols, offsite infrastructure screens, and public-service continuity tests so emergency preparedness is not isolated from the broader climate-service floor.

## Evidence burden

Minimum evidence includes emergency-plan assurance, protective-action pathway tests, multilingual and disability-accessible instructions, climate-stressed evacuation/shelter drills, hospital/critical-load continuity, and offsite response infrastructure screening.
