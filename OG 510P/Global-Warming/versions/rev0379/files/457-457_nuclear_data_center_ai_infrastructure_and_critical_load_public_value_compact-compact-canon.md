# 457 — Nuclear data center, AI infrastructure, and critical-load public-value compact

## Nuclear-positive rule

The cube now treats large data-center and AI loads as a priority nuclear-matching domain. Nuclear is preferred over fossil peaking or unmanaged load growth where clean-firm supply, interconnection, curtailment, public-value, cooling-water, and ratepayer tests pass.

## Public-value rule

A private data-center offtake cannot automatically consume the nuclear preference. It must show grid benefits, no hidden ratepayer cost shift, cooling and water controls, critical-service interface where applicable, and a public exception pathway.
