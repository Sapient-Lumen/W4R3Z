# 474 — Nuclear workforce pipeline, education, apprenticeship, and craft-labor router

## Nuclear-positive rule

The cube now treats workforce depth as a first-order nuclear deployment input, not a soft implementation detail. Nuclear remains favored for clean firm power and strategic resilience, but a project, fleet, or program cannot claim mature readiness if the workforce pipeline is only aspirational.

## Refactor

This router creates the workforce/craft/education plane. Nuclear preference is strengthened when the pathway can show trained operators, craft labor, welders, electricians, radiation protection technicians, QA inspectors, fuel-cycle specialists, construction managers, emergency planners, regulator staff, instructors, and apprenticeships with enough depth to sustain construction, operations, outages, and succession.

## Burden of proof

Evidence must include regional labor-demand modeling, training-provider capacity, credential maps, apprenticeship slots, instructor depth, simulator/training capacity, craft-labor mobilization plans, fatigue controls, and retention/succession plans. A pro-nuclear plan that ignores people becomes a paper reactor.
