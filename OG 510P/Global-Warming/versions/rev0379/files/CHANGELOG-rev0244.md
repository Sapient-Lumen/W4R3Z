# CHANGELOG — rev0244

## Revision focus

Rev0244 is a **stress-test and critical-service continuity front-door** revision.

Rev0243 made packets buildable, operable, maintainable, and countable only at stable clean service.
Rev0244 adds the bad-day test: a packet is not ready merely because it works under normal conditions.
It should be stress-tested against compound shocks, dependency failures, degraded-service states, vulnerable-user needs, backup limits, drills, recovery rules, and correction triggers.
It also adds a critical-service continuity doctrine so hospitals, schools, water systems, shelters, telecoms, public housing, lifeline mobility, food / medicine cold chains, and grid-control functions are protected in low-carbon form rather than through ad hoc private backup or open-ended diesel dependence.

## New doctrine in one sentence

**Do not call a climate packet ready just because it works on a normal day; stress-test it against compound shocks and protect critical services with efficient, passive, clean, flexible, maintained, drilled, accessible continuity packages while keeping fossil backup narrow, measured, temporary, and on an exit path.**
