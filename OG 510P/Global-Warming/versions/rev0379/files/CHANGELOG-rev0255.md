## Core change

Rev0254 asked what happens to dirty places after fossil production and contaminated-site use. Rev0255 asks what happens to people when climate stress, repeated loss, insurance exit, recovery standards, or relocation decisions make movement necessary or likely.

The revision adds two rules:

1. **Climate mobility needs receiving capacity.** Retreat, relocation, evacuation, migration, and recovery movement are not mature policies unless destination housing, water, power, cooling, health, schools, work, host-community benefits, anti-displacement safeguards, service-expansion finance, and durable-solution triggers are formed.
2. **Climate movement needs portability.** Rights, services, records, benefits, claims, school and health continuity, adaptive social protection, and remedies should travel with people without becoming surveillance, proof mazes, or new exclusion systems.

## Compression

**Do not call retreat dignified, relocation planned, migration adaptive, or recovery complete unless people can arrive somewhere prepared to receive them and carry essential rights, services, records, benefits, claims, and remedies across the move.**
