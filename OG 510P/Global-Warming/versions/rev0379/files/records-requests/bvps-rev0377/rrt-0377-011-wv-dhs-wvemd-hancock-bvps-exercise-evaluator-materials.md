# RRT-0377-011 — West Virginia DHS / WV Emergency Management Division: BVPS exercise evaluator materials and corrective-action records

Status: ready-not-sent  
Revision: rev0377  
Route class: West Virginia FOIA / component-agency custodian request  
Claim boundary: request template only; not dispatch proof and not readiness evidence

## Submission route

Submit as a written West Virginia FOIA request to the West Virginia Emergency Management Division / relevant Department of Homeland Security component-agency head or FOIA contact. The WV DHS FOIA page says component-agency records requests should be submitted to that agency and addressed to the agency head; it also lists the Office of the Secretary FOIA fallback email/route. WV Code §29B-1-3 requires requests to be made directly to the custodian, with reasonable specificity, and requires a response as soon as practicable but within five business days excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and legal holidays.

## Request text

Subject: Public records request — Beaver Valley Power Station 2026 REP exercise, West Virginia / Hancock County evidence packet

Please provide public records prepared, owned, received, or retained by the West Virginia Emergency Management Division, the West Virginia Department of Homeland Security, or any participating West Virginia REP / radiological emergency preparedness program office concerning the 2026 Beaver Valley Power Station biennial Radiological Emergency Preparedness exercise and related public meeting / preliminary-findings process.

Please include records from January 1, 2026 through the date of search concerning:

1. Exercise objectives, scenario summaries, controller/evaluator materials, master scenario events lists, extent-of-play agreements, evaluation guides, and any FEMA or NRC communications relating to West Virginia participation.
2. West Virginia state EOC, radiological health, public information, alert and warning, protective-action, transportation/access-control, reception/decontamination, medical/public-health, ingestion-pathway, and recovery/reentry exercise logs or summaries.
3. Hancock County coordination records, including notification logs, resource requests, inject responses, hotwash notes, strengths, areas for improvement, corrective actions, and retest or closure plans.
4. Any FEMA preliminary findings, evaluation letters, deficiency/ARC/Level 1/Level 2 finding notices, improvement-plan items, or correspondence to or from FEMA Region 3 concerning West Virginia or Hancock County.
5. Any public information, accessibility, functional-needs, language-access, alerting, siren, EAS/IPAWS/WEA/Rave/Swift911 or equivalent public-warning records used or evaluated for West Virginia / Hancock County exercise participation.
6. Any records withheld or redacted, with a record-by-record index identifying the record title/date/source, the exemption asserted, and whether a segregable public portion can be released.

Please provide responsive records electronically if feasible. Please do not include private medical data, private contact lists, credential secrets, passwords, security-sensitive facility details, or personal information not needed to understand exercise performance. If any responsive record contains such information, please redact the protected fields and release the segregable public portions.
