# RRT-0377-012 — Hancock County WV OEM: BVPS exercise local logs, alerting, EOC, AFN, and corrective action

Status: ready-not-sent  
Revision: rev0377  
Route class: West Virginia FOIA / county custodian request  
Claim boundary: request template only; not dispatch proof and not readiness evidence

## Submission route

Submit as a written West Virginia FOIA/public-records request to Hancock County Office of Emergency Management / 911, with copy to the County Commission or county records custodian if local practice requires. Hancock County’s site lists the Office of Emergency Management and a staff directory naming the OEM/911 Director. WV Code §29B-1-3 states that public-records requests are made directly to the custodian and must be reasonably specific.

## Request text

Subject: Public records request — Hancock County Beaver Valley Power Station 2026 exercise records

Please provide public records prepared, owned, received, or retained by Hancock County, West Virginia, the Hancock County Office of Emergency Management / 911, or participating county response organizations concerning the 2026 Beaver Valley Power Station biennial Radiological Emergency Preparedness exercise and any preliminary-results, hotwash, critique, or corrective-action process.

Please include records from January 1, 2026 through the date of search concerning:

1. Local exercise schedules, scenario summaries, controller/evaluator injects, extent-of-play agreements, participating organization rosters by agency/role, and evaluation guides.
2. EOC activation and staffing logs, 911/dispatch notification records, radio/phone/backup communications logs, common operating picture records, decision logs, and resource requests.
3. Public alert and warning records, including siren, EAS, IPAWS/WEA, Rave/Swift911 or other notification-system records, test messages, message viewer confirmations, delivery reports, exception logs, and backup route-alerting records.
4. Protective-action decision records, evacuation/shelter/reception-center/decontamination/medical/public-health/functional-needs/language-access/accessibility records, and any public-information or rumor-control logs.
5. Hotwash notes, evaluator comments, preliminary findings, strengths, areas requiring corrective action, improvement plans, retest records, closure records, or correspondence with WV EMD, FEMA Region 3, NRC, Pennsylvania, Ohio, Beaver County, Columbiana County, or Vistra/BVPS.
6. A withheld-record index for any records withheld or redacted, stating the record type/date, exemption asserted, and whether a segregable public portion can be released.

Please provide responsive records electronically if feasible. Please redact private medical data, personal contact lists, credentials, passwords, facility-security-sensitive details, and other protected information while releasing segregable public portions.
