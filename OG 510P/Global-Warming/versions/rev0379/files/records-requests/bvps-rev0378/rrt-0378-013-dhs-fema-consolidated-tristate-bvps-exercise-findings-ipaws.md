## Request text

Subject: FOIA request — Beaver Valley Power Station 2026 tri-state REP exercise findings, evaluator materials, public meetings, and IPAWS/alerting records

Please provide records prepared, owned, received, transmitted, or retained by the Federal Emergency Management Agency, including FEMA Region 3, FEMA Region 5, the Radiological Emergency Preparedness Program, and any FEMA public-affairs or evaluation office concerning the 2026 Beaver Valley Power Station biennial Radiological Emergency Preparedness exercise and related public-meeting / preliminary-findings process.

Please search records from January 1, 2026 through the date of search concerning:

1. The Beaver Valley Power Station 2026 REP exercise, including exercise objectives, extent-of-play agreements, controller/evaluator materials, master scenario events lists, evaluation guides, injects, evaluator notes, hotwash materials, preliminary findings, strengths, deficiencies, areas requiring corrective action, improvement-plan items, corrective-action program status, retest requirements, and final-report preparation materials.
2. FEMA Region 3 materials concerning Pennsylvania and West Virginia participation, including Pennsylvania Emergency Management Agency, Beaver County, West Virginia Emergency Management Division, Hancock County, and any other state/local/tribal/private-sector entities evaluated or observed.
3. FEMA Region 5 materials concerning Ohio/Columbiana County participation, including any 11:00 a.m. June 12, 2026 local results announcement, evaluator briefing, or results summary associated with Columbiana County EMA or Ohio EMA.
4. Public-meeting records for the June 12, 2026 5:30 p.m. meeting concerning preliminary findings and the ANS/IPAWS demonstration, including agenda, slides, speaker notes, attendance/sign-in sheets if public, written questions, official answers, recordings, transcripts, chat logs, exhibits, and after-meeting summaries.
5. Alert and warning records evaluated or referenced by FEMA, including ANS, ENS, WENS, IPAWS, EAS, WEA, sirens, public information, language access, access and functional needs, backup alerting, alert delivery confirmation, missed/failed/exception logs, and message approval records.
6. Communications with NRC, Vistra / Beaver Valley, PEMA, Beaver County, West Virginia, Hancock County, Ohio EMA, Columbiana County, or other evaluated jurisdictions concerning exercise findings, preliminary findings, deficiencies, corrective actions, retests, and final-report preparation.
7. Any records withheld or redacted, with a record-by-record index identifying the record title/date/source, the exemption asserted, and whether segregable public portions can be released.

Please provide electronic records where feasible. Please do not include private medical data, private contact lists, credential secrets, passwords, detailed facility-security-sensitive information, or personal information not needed to understand exercise performance. If responsive records contain such information, please redact protected fields and release segregable public portions.
