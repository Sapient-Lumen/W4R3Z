## Request text

Subject: NRC records request — Beaver Valley EN58200 EOF power loss repair/retest/CAP and 2026 REP exercise transmittals

Please provide publicly available NRC records, or process this as a FOIA/PDR request if records are not already public, concerning Beaver Valley Power Station Event Notification 58200 and any NRC records concerning the 2026 Beaver Valley Radiological Emergency Preparedness exercise.

Please search records from March 14, 2026 through the date of search for:

1. Records concerning Event Notification 58200, including licensee follow-up notifications, LERs or equivalent reports if generated, NRC inspection or resident-inspector notes, correspondence, ADAMS accessions, cause analysis, repair records provided to NRC, retest records, compensatory-measure termination, and corrective-action program closure or tracking records concerning loss of normal and alternate electrical power to the Emergency Operations Facility.
2. Records showing whether normal EOF power and the alternate generator or alternate backup power capability were restored, tested, accepted, or remained under compensatory measures after the March 14, 2026 notification.
3. NRC communications with FEMA, PEMA, WVEMD, Ohio EMA, Beaver County, Columbiana County, Hancock County, or Vistra concerning the 2026 Beaver Valley REP exercise, preliminary findings, exercise evaluation, EOF functionality, emergency assessment capability, alert/notification, public information, or corrective actions.
4. Any NRC receipt, forwarding, review, or docketing of FEMA preliminary findings, final reports, deficiency notices, Areas Requiring Corrective Action, or corrective-action closure packages concerning the 2026 Beaver Valley exercise.
5. Any records withheld or redacted, with a record-by-record index identifying the record title/date/source, the exemption asserted, and whether segregable public portions can be released.

Please provide ADAMS accession numbers where available and electronic copies where feasible. Please redact private, security-sensitive, safeguards, cyber, physical-security, or credential information as appropriate while releasing segregable public portions.

## Intake requirements

Do not use a search result, request receipt, or event-notification text as closure evidence. EOF closure requires records that specifically show repair, retest, acceptance, CAP closure, or a regulator/licensee statement that compensatory measures were terminated.
