## Dispatch logging requirement

After actual dispatch, create or update a dispatch receipt row with: sent_at, route used, submission confirmation number, custodian/portal, exact submitted text, requester identity used, fee status, and any immediate acknowledgement. Do not change any proofcut to closed until a response packet is imported, hashed, DLP/redaction-screened, and adjudicated.

## Minimum handling rule

Ask for records, not explanations. Request releasable records and accept legally required redactions for security-sensitive, law-enforcement-sensitive, personal, medical, or special-needs registry details. The purpose is proofcut adjudication, not disclosure of protected tactics or personal data.
