## Request purpose

This packet is designed to acquire the releasable record packet needed to adjudicate the June 2026 Beaver Valley Power Station REP exercise. It must not be treated as evidence merely because the route exists or because a request template has been drafted.

## Draft request language

Please provide releasable records concerning the June 2026 Beaver Valley Power Station Radiological Emergency Preparedness exercise for Pennsylvania and West Virginia, including any Region III preliminary findings presented or prepared for the June 12, 2026 public meeting.

Requested records include:

1. Preliminary findings, slides, handouts, speaking notes, meeting agenda, transcript, recording, attendee Q&A, or public-meeting release materials.
2. Records supporting the Alert and Notification System demonstration finding, including issue logs, evaluator observations, fail/degraded observations, corrective actions, retest status, and accessible-warning / AFN / language-access artifacts where releasable.
3. Exercise evaluation materials, extent-of-play materials, controller/evaluator notes, after-action/improvement-plan metadata, corrective-action program records, and closeout or retest evidence.
4. Records linking, excluding, or otherwise addressing the March 2026 Beaver Valley Emergency Operations Facility power-loss event / NRC EN58200 in the exercise scope, readiness discussion, or corrective-action record.
5. Any FEMA transmittal package to NRC related to the exercise, or records identifying the expected release path, accession number, tracking number, or estimated availability.

Search terms: Beaver Valley; BVPS; Beaver Valley Power Station; REP exercise; Radiological Emergency Preparedness; Region III; Pennsylvania; West Virginia; Alert and Notification System; ANS; public meeting; June 12 2026; emergency operations facility; EOF; EN58200; after-action; improvement plan.

## Format and release preferences

Electronic production is preferred. Rolling release is acceptable. Redactions for personal privacy, security-sensitive details, or protected critical infrastructure information are acceptable; please release segregable portions and provide the exemption basis for withheld material.

## Cube use rule

A dispatch receipt is only a clock-start artifact. A response packet must be imported, hashed, DLP/redacted as needed, and adjudicated against proofcuts before any BVPS readiness claim changes.
