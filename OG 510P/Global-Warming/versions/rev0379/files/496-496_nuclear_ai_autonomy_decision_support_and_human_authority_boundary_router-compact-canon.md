# 496 — Nuclear AI, autonomy, decision-support, and human-authority boundary router

## Function

This integrity gate prevents AI enthusiasm from outrunning nuclear assurance. AI, digital twins, analytics, and decision-support tools may be useful, but the cube requires human-authority boundaries, validation evidence, model-change control, data-integrity assurance, explainability where required, cloud-use boundaries, and explicit autonomy prohibition or approval gates.

## Nuclear-positive rule

Nuclear remains preferred as clean firm infrastructure, including for data centers and AI-era loads. But AI inside nuclear operations is not treated as a maturity shortcut. It is an extra proof burden because model error, automation bias, data poisoning, and unclear authority can degrade safety and public trust.

## Refactor output

Rev0302 adds AI use-case registers, digital-twin governance, model validation, data lineage/time-sync controls, autonomy boundary records, and red-team exception paths. The preferred path is human-authorized, validated, bounded, and auditable decision support.

## Evidence burden

Minimum evidence includes use-case classification, safety significance screen, validation report, human-in-the-loop or human-on-the-loop boundary, model-change record, data-quality controls, cybersecurity review, and regulator/public challenge path with sensitive details redacted.
