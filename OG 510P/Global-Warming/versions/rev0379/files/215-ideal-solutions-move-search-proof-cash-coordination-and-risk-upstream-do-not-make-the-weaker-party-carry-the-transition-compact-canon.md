# 215 — Ideal Solutions: Move Search, Proof, Cash, Coordination, and Risk Upstream — Do Not Make the Weaker Party Carry the Transition

## Claim

The archive already says that serious climate programmes should protect clean necessity, make the clean path the easy default, and arrive as coherent offers rather than scavenger hunts [S69][S79][S80][S81][S118][S119][S137][S140][S141][S143].

One more front-door rule now belongs beside those notes:

**when a climate programme is aimed at households, tenants, drivers, small firms, schools, clinics, farms, or other weaker actors, it should move search, proof, cash, coordination, and performance risk upstream as far as honesty allows.**

In plain terms, the intended beneficiary should not have to become the project developer, paperwork engine, bridge financier, systems integrator, and loss absorber all at once.
If the pathway works only for actors who can search across programmes, front the money, coordinate contractors, master the forms, and survive failure, then the pathway is still badly designed.

So the archive’s shortest burden-shape rule is:

**push complexity upward into programme design, public authority, regulated service, prime contractors, finance platforms, and accountable leads. Pull burden downward off the weaker party.** [S69][S70][S72][S74][S79][S80][S81][S118][S119][S143]

## Why this belongs in the front door

### 1. Weak burden shape can cancel strong policy intent

A climate programme can look generous on paper and still fail because it silently asks the weaker actor to do too much.
Search burden, proof burden, coordination burden, cash burden, and performance-risk burden often compound rather than substitute for one another [S69][S70][S72][S74][S79][S118][S119][S143].

That means a programme can fail even when each individual fragment looks reasonable in isolation.
The front door therefore needs the shortest anti-fragmentation rule in operational terms:

**do not stack the transition’s hardest administrative and financial work onto the least powerful actor in the chain.**

### 2. This is the practical counterpart to the public bargain

`212` says a serious climate programme should protect clean necessity and carry people through turnover rather than forcing ordinary need to absorb the first shock.
This note gives that bargain an operational shape:

**protection is not only about money levels or formal eligibility. It is also about who has to do the work, who has to wait, and who has to absorb the miss when the system does not line up.** [S69][S70][S72][S74][S79][S118][S119][S143]

### 3. This is how coherent offers stay coherent in real life

`214` says the transition should arrive as a coherent offer rather than a scavenger hunt.
This note says what that means when stress actually enters the system:

**a coherent offer should still look coherent when someone lacks spare cash, spare time, spare expertise, or spare tolerance for failure.** [S80][S81][S118][S119][S137][S140][S141][S143]

If the offer becomes fragmented the moment the user cannot self-finance, self-coordinate, or self-advocate, then the offer was only superficially coherent.

## What this rule promotes

Promote:
- prefilled or presumptive eligibility where possible instead of proof mazes [S72][S118][S119][S143]
- low-upfront or pay-over-time pathways instead of reimbursement-first designs [S70][S118][S143]
- one accountable lead and warm handoffs instead of user-managed coordination [S80][S81][S118][S119][S140][S143]
- standard offers with joined finance, delivery, proof, and remedy [S71][S80][S81][S118][S141][S143]
- performance risk sitting mainly with actors who can diversify, insure, manage, or correct it [S68][S74][S81][S137][S143]

## What this rule demotes

Demote:
- programmes that assume the beneficiary can discover and assemble the pathway alone [S79][S80][S118][S119][S143]
- reimbursement structures that require weak actors to bridge large upfront costs [S70][S118][S143]
- fragmented proof chains where the user has to reconcile separate data, forms, offices, and exceptions [S72][S79][S118][S119][S140][S143]
- delivery models that quietly export contractor management, fault isolation, or remedy-chasing to the end user [S74][S80][S81][S137][S143]
- climate pathways that socialize rhetorical benefit while privatizing coordination pain and downside risk [S68][S69][S79][S118][S143]

## One-paragraph compression

**Ideal climate solutions should move search, proof, cash, coordination, and performance risk upstream as far as honesty allows. The weaker party should not have to act as programme navigator, bridge financier, systems integrator, evidence assembler, and loss absorber in order to receive the clean state. A serious climate programme protects ordinary necessity not only by what it pays for, but by how much burden it removes from the actor least able to carry failure.** [S68][S69][S70][S72][S74][S79][S80][S81][S118][S119][S137][S140][S141][S143]
