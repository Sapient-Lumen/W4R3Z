# 130 — Ideal Solutions, Physical Portfolio: Compact Canon

## Claim

The archive already has a compact **reference portfolio** (`57`), a ranked intervention stack (`01`), a three-lane time rule (`62`), and context notes for different place types (`60`).
That is strong, but it still leaves one front-door gap:

**there is no single shortest joined note for the physical portfolio itself — what actually gets built, retired, protected, and reserved if the archive’s answer is taken seriously.**

This note compresses that physical layer into one place.
It does not replace `57-ideal-solutions-reference-portfolio-2026-2035.md`.
It joins the package spine, the ranked stack, and the scarcity rules so a reader can open one file and see the real-world portfolio shape before reopening deeper governance, readiness, or detour questions [S1][S4][S8][S9][S11][S12][S14][S15][S16][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S23][S24][S25][S26][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S34][S35][S36][S37][S38][S39][S40][S41][S42][S43][S44][S45][S46][S47][S48][S50][S51][S52][S53][S54][S55][S56][S57][S60][S62][S97][S98][S99][S102][S103][S104][S105][S106].

## The physical portfolio

### 1. Build the clean electric backbone first
The portfolio starts with abundant clean electricity, grids, storage, flexibility, and selected firm clean capacity where residual reliability needs remain.
This is the backbone because electricity is becoming the organising carrier for buildings, transport, and part of industry, while grid expansion and interconnection now decide how much clean supply can actually land [S4][S14][S47][S48][S55][S57][S102][S103].

House rule: **build the system that lets many other sectors clean themselves.**

### 2. Cut waste, then electrify the service that remains
The portfolio is not only about cleaner supply.
It is also about using less energy and material to deliver the same or better service.
That means envelopes, heat pumps, passive cooling, efficient appliances, motors, EVs, transit, compact urban form, flexible demand, and industrial efficiency before reaching for scarce fuels or oversized supply expansion [S16][S17][S26][S28][S51][S52][S53][S54][S56][S58][S97][S98][S99][S106].

House rule: **stop waste before scaling scarce clean supply to meet it.**

### 3. Run a methane emergency lane in parallel
The portfolio treats methane as a fast, operational lane rather than a footnote inside long-run carbon planning.
That means measured leak detection and repair, venting and flaring control, landfill and wastewater action, livestock and rice measures, and enforcement strong enough to force correction of visible large releases [S8][S11][S19][S21][S22][S66][S67][S133].

House rule: **fast warming pollutants get fast physical response.**

### 4. Force deliberate fossil decline rather than waiting for drift
The portfolio is not complete if clean additions merely coexist with durable dirty lock-in.
It therefore includes coal retirement, no new long-lived unabated lock-in except narrow security cases, reform of fossil support, and explicit management of worker, regional, and cleanup liabilities [S9][S23][S24][S25][S102].

House rule: **build clean and retire dirty on purpose.**

### 5. Protect land, food systems, water, and human safety as core climate work
The portfolio treats forests, peatlands, wetlands, mangroves, agrifood methane, nitrogen losses, water security, sustainable cooling, heat-health systems, and resilient local protection as central climate lanes rather than side quests [S12][S15][S16][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S36][S37][S38][S39][S40][S41][S72][S73][S74][S75][S76][S104][S105].

House rule: **cut emissions and cut exposure together.**

### 6. Use material discipline and reserve scarce tools for the hard remainder
The portfolio expands circularity, repair, reuse, better materials productivity, low-emissions steel and cement, and targeted industrial decarbonisation.
It does not spread scarce hydrogen, e-fuels, biomass, carbon capture, or removals across easy uses that already have better substitutes [S26][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S34][S35][S42][S43][S44][S45][S46][S47][S48][S50][S112][S165][S166][S167].

House rule: **easy uses get direct clean substitutes; scarce tools stay with the hard remainder.**

### 7. Keep the global portfolio buildable under real scarcity
The physical portfolio has to work not only in unusually rich settings but under real constraints of grid capacity, capital cost, debt space, mineral concentration, workforce limits, administrative capacity, and public legitimacy.
That means choosing options that are copyable, affordable enough to spread, and compatible with faster buildout across many jurisdictions rather than a few showcase cases [S47][S48][S59][S60][S61][S62][S63][S64][S68][S69][S70][S71][S91][S92][S93][S94][S95][S96][S103][S106].

House rule: **the portfolio is only ideal if it scales beyond exceptional places.**

## Compression

The shortest joined physical-portfolio answer in the archive is now this:

**build abundant clean power and grid capacity; stop waste and electrify what can be electrified; slash methane fast; retire fossil dependence deliberately; protect ecosystems, food systems, water, cooling, and people directly; reserve scarce clean fuels, biomass, capture, and removals for the hard remainder; and choose a portfolio that can really scale across capital-, grid-, and capacity-constrained settings.**
