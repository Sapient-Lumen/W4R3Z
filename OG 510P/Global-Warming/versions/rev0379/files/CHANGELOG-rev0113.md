## Why
The archive already had compact notes for readiness before a bad day, rehearsal so readiness does not stay ceremonial, readiness assurance so claims require current proof, and compensating duties for when readiness is known to be partial. What it still lacked was the shortest rule for what a serious climate state should do when readiness is simply **unknown or stale** because evidence has decayed or verification has not caught up. Rev0113 closes that boundary by forcing a conservative default: downgrade status to not proven, verify the highest-consequence path first, narrow claims and complexity while uncertainty remains, tighten protection and tripwire sensitivity, and keep dirty fallback on strict proof gates until the uncertainty is closed.
