# 476 — Nuclear regulator workforce capacity, review clock, and independence assurance

## Nuclear-positive rule

A pro-nuclear cube must be pro-regulator. Faster licensing is not mature unless the regulator has enough independent technical staff, inspection capacity, public-docket support, security/safeguards competence, and institutional memory to review efficiently without hollowing safety.

## Refactor

This file turns regulator capacity into a maturity cap. Nuclear preference is strengthened by clear review pathways and competent licensing capacity, but capped by unsupported review clocks, thin inspection staffing, captured expertise, or under-resourced public participation.

## Evidence required

Evidence includes workload forecasts, reviewer skill matrices, inspection coverage, public meeting/docket capacity, conflict-of-interest controls, knowledge retention, review-clock assumptions, and surge staffing plans.

## Cube products

The normalized tables are `cube/nuclear-regulator-workforce-capacity.csv`, `cube/nuclear-institutional-capacity-risk-register.csv`, `cube/nuclear-workforce-source-authority-audit.csv`, and `cube/nuclear-human-capital-maturity-cap-execution.csv`.
