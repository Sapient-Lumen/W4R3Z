## Core change

Rev0242 adds this operating rule:

**Do not wait for money, grid capacity, disaster recovery, or political attention to appear before deciding what should be built, repaired, financed, procured, protected, retired, or copied. Maintain a ready project shelf and procurement platforms so high-value work is absorbable, bankable, buyable, maintainable, measurable, and repeatable.**

## Why this matters

The archive now distinguishes:

1. the **work queue** — what must be done;
2. the **delivery packet** — who owns each item and how it is measured;
3. the **allocation rule** — what gets scarce capacity first;
4. the **project shelf** — what is prepared enough to move when a window opens;
5. the **procurement platform** — how repeated clean needs become routine demand and service rather than one-off bespoke deals.

This closes a practical gap between climate-finance headlines and physical deployment.
