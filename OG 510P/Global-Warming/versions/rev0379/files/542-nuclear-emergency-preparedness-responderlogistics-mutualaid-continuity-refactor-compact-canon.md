# Nuclear emergency preparedness: responder logistics, mutual aid, and continuity firebreak

Revision: **rev0335**  
Scope: **REAL_BVPS_PUBLIC_ONLY remains public-context-only. No real readiness or unreadiness claim is made.**

## Why this revision exists

Previous revisions built proof chains for alerting, field receipt, action uptake, CRC/decon, first receivers, dose/PAR, ingestion, and late-phase recovery. The next failure mode is less visible but more systemic: **the same limited people, radios, vehicles, fuel, field teams, dosimeters, generators, mission requests, purchase orders, and relief shifts are silently assumed by every branch.**

Rev0335 adds a support-system proof spine. It says that a plan, public guidance page, mutual-aid compact, resource list, course catalog, public exercise schedule, or continuity template can create a demand, clock, cap, contradiction, or reopen signal. It cannot prove that the incident can be staffed, supplied, communicated, credentialed, sustained, relieved, reimbursed, or protected.

## Hard rule

**A public NIMS/FEMA/SAFECOM/ERHMS/OSHA/EMAC/ESF source, public mutual-aid compact, public logistics annex, communications guidance, continuity template, resource inventory, or source ID can route evidence. It cannot close local responder-sustainment or logistics-readiness rows.**

## Operational chain now required

`incident objectives → ICS/EOC/EOF staffing roster → typed resource request → mutual-aid mission package → credential and badging check → logistics/fuel/comms cache → shift-relief and fatigue control → responder health monitoring → family/lodging/feeding support → cost/procurement/reimbursement capture → CAP/retest/verifier → public-claim gate`

## What this prevents

- treating an EMAC/NIMS mutual-aid reference as evidence that a radiation field team, traffic-control team, CRC staffing wave, hospital support cell, or public-information team will arrive;
- treating an EOC/COOP template as proof that the EOC/EOF can operate through power, comms, weather, cyber, or staffing degradation;
- treating a radio interoperability plan as proof that cross-border PA/OH/WV field teams can talk during the exercise;
- treating a staffing roster as proof that people are credentialed, rested, dosimetered, fed, lodged, transported, and monitored;
- treating a vendor quote, fuel cache, generator, vehicle list, or reimbursement code as proof that the response can be sustained;
- allowing duplicate source IDs or public guidance to inflate evidence independence.

## New files

The revision adds concrete packet surfaces for resource typing, mutual aid, logistics/fuel/comms, EOC/EOF continuity, responder health/fatigue, spontaneous volunteers, cost/reimbursement, dependency DAGs, critical cutsets, validator fixtures, SQLite views, and source-canonicalization audits.

## Still not local evidence

No real/anonymized staffing roster, EMAC mission package, WebEOC/resource-board export, fuel ticket, radio test log, shift-relief record, ERHMS deployment roster, responder injury/exposure log, procurement record, reimbursement package, or CAP/retest/verifier packet is imported in this revision.
