## Core change

Rev0257 treats safe drinking water, sanitation, hygiene, water quality, groundwater, drought allocation, basin accounts, reuse, demand management, and priority-use rules as climate operating infrastructure. Climate packets are no longer considered complete merely because they name water security in general. They should say how WASH service continues for exposed people and how water systems absorb scarcity, flood, contamination, groundwater, and allocation shocks before they cascade.
