## Why this revision exists

the archive already had real substance on EMDE finance, burden allocation, and world-scale bargaining.
What it still did not say front-door plainly enough was that once the needed technologies are mostly mature, a large part of climate speed outside the richest countries is now set by finance architecture itself: cost of capital, private-capital mobilisation, local-currency finance, guarantees, and payment / off-taker reliability.

This revision adds the smallest useful bridge: a dedicated compact canon saying that at world scale, finance speed is climate speed too.

## Design note

This is a small front-door recentering toward world-scale deployability rather than a new subtree.
It reuses and compresses stronger existing doctrine from `25`, `135`, `156`, `194`, and the EMDE-finance sources already in the archive, while adding one sharper IEA source on private-finance scale and one sharper OECD source on local-capital-market depth.
