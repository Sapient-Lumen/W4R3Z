# 485 — Nuclear tailings, radon, water, reclamation, and postclosure stewardship router

## Router rule

Pro-nuclear policy requires credible upstream environmental closure. Tailings, radon, groundwater, reclamation, and postclosure stewardship are not side issues; they are maturity gates for responsible nuclear supply.

## Control output

The cube now requires tailings design, radon monitoring, groundwater baselines, reclamation bonding, postclosure surveillance, emergency plans, and completion criteria to be visible as upstream assurance artifacts.

## Maturity cap

If tailings, radon, water, reclamation, or postclosure evidence is missing, the nuclear pathway remains preferred in principle but cannot be scored as mature public value.
