## Doctrine change

Rev0251 adds two new tests to the work queue:

1. **Cross-border dependency test:** high-volume or high-criticality packets should name key traded inputs, source concentration, routes, lead times, export-control or tariff exposure, critical spares, circular-supply route, responsible-sourcing risks, and fallback rules.
2. **Market-access interoperability test:** climate-relevant products, materials, services, and proof claims should name customs classification, applicable standards, conformity-assessment path, accepted evidence, mutual-recognition or equivalence route, market-surveillance owner, lifecycle duties, and update clock.

## Compression

A climate programme that can finance, permit, digitize, secure, automate, and enforce packets can still stall if the hardware cannot arrive, if proof cannot travel, or if standards fragment markets. Rev0251 therefore treats clean supply chains, customs codes, standards, testing, certification, quality infrastructure, and mutual recognition as climate deployment infrastructure.
