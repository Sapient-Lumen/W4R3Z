## Core doctrine added

- Audit without consequence is only documentation.
- A climate duty is not formed until the programme can say who owes what, to whom, by when, with what evidence, under what authority, and what remedy follows if the duty is missed.
- Correct first where good-faith capacity is the barrier; escalate against deception, evasion, capture, repeated non-response, and harm.
- Route failures to the forum that can actually fix them: operating office, regulator, funder, auditor, procurement challenge route, ombuds office, court, Paris transparency / compliance channel, public ledger, or other accountable forum.
- Use courts and international accountability as backstops and correction levers, not substitutes for public delivery capacity.
