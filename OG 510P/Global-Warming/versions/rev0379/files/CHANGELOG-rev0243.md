## Core change

Rev0243 adds this operating rule:

**Build the crews before the queue needs them, and count completion at stable clean service rather than first installation. Every serious climate packet should include workforce and public-capacity planning, commissioning, O&M, warranty, spare parts, user support, aftercare, and performance feedback.**

## Why this matters

The archive now distinguishes:

1. the **work queue** — what must be done;
2. the **delivery packet** — who owns each item and how it is measured;
3. the **allocation rule** — what gets scarce capacity first;
4. the **project shelf** — what is prepared enough to move when a window opens;
5. the **procurement platform** — how repeated needs become routine demand and service;
6. the **crew-and-capability plan** — who will actually approve, build, inspect, operate, maintain, and support the packet;
7. the **stable-service gate** — when the packet is truly complete because the cleaner or safer service persists.

This closes a practical gap between bankable projects and lived clean service.
