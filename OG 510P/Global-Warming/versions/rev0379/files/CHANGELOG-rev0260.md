## Doctrine shift

Rev0260 extends the child / school / health / water / food / mobility / recovery / critical-service front door into care continuity and accessibility. It asks climate packets to prove that older adults, disabled people, people with chronic illness, medically dependent people, home-care users, long-term-care residents, caregivers, assistive devices, medications, accessible communication, accessible transport, accessible shelters, backup power, supported decision-making, adult safeguarding, and recovery of independence are ready before climate shocks become isolation, institutionalization, preventable death, or loss of autonomy.

## Compression

Climate protection now includes access-and-care operating proof: warnings must be received and actionable, stay-or-go decisions must be supported, transport and shelters must be usable, devices and medicines must travel, home care and long-term care must surge, adult safeguarding must function, OPDs and older-persons organizations must shape decisions, and recovery is not complete until independence-supporting assets and rights are restored.
