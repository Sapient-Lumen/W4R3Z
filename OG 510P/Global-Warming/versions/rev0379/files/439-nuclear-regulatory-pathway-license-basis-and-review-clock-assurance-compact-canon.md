# Nuclear regulatory pathway, license-basis, and review-clock assurance

A nuclear-positive cube should accelerate good nuclear projects, not pretend the license basis is optional. Rev0291 therefore treats licensing as a service floor. A project may be favored, but it remains capped until the regulatory pathway, license basis, docket, review assumptions, safety analysis and regulatory capacity are visible.

The NRC's Part 53 work is the relevant technology-inclusive, risk-informed regulatory pathway for advanced reactors [S793]. DOE's advanced nuclear deployment material is useful for delivery strategy, but it cannot substitute for a regulator-visible license basis [S776]. NRC public-involvement rules and practices mean the public docket is part of the maturity evidence, not a communications appendix [S794].

## Rule

Every nuclear pathway must name its licensing route, pre-application status, safety-case artifact, review clock assumption, unresolved exemptions or departures, and the office or actor responsible for answering public and regulator questions.

## Cap

If review-clock claims are not tied to a named regulatory path, docket, scope, staffing assumption and license-basis crosswalk, the project remains nuclear-favored but capped as **regulatory-template-only**.
