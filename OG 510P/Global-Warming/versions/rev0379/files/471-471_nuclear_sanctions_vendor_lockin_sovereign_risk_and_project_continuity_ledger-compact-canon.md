# 471 — Nuclear sanctions, vendor lock-in, sovereign risk, and project-continuity ledger

## Nuclear-positive rule

The cube should not confuse pro-nuclear policy with vendor-blind policy. Nuclear remains favored, but sanctions exposure, foreign ownership/control, single-vendor dependence, spares bottlenecks and technology black boxes now cap maturity.

## Continuity test

Every favored pathway needs a continuity answer: how does the plant, project or fleet keep operating if a vendor, fuel supplier, component source, finance provider or geopolitical partner becomes unavailable?
