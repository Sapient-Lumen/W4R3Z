# BVPS rev0333 ingestion lab / embargo-release field kit

Use this runbook to preserve local or anonymized evidence for ingestion-pathway decisions without publishing sensitive producer, water-utility, laboratory, or claims data.

Minimum capture order:

1. record trigger, plume/field basis, and affected commodity/water sectors;
2. preserve sampling plan, sample IDs, custody transfers, lab receipt, QA/QC, and result amendment lineage;
3. preserve embargo/hold/release order authority, geographic scope, commodity scope, and public advisory versions;
4. preserve alternate water, milk processor, producer/processor/retailer, and private-well evidence;
5. preserve claims/compensation intake and redacted public surrogate maps;
6. route any lab amendment, producer counterevidence, or public-message conflict to reopen adjudication.

Do not use public guidance, a public brochure, a lab name, or a public advisory screenshot as readiness closure.
