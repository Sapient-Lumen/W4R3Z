# BVPS rev0331 first-receiver / hospital-surge field capture runbook

This runbook is for a real or anonymized evidence packet. Do not include PHI in public artifacts.

Capture at minimum:

1. EMS dispatch/call-taking contamination flag and patient severity surrogate.
2. Ambulance PPE, patient packaging, hospital prenotification, vehicle survey/decon, out-of-service/return-to-service times.
3. ED self-presenter and ambulance entrance split-flow observations.
4. First-receiver PPE/training/don-doff/dosimetry issue-return-readout records.
5. Radiation survey instrument QA/calibration/battery/background records.
6. Triage decision trail showing lifesaving care was not delayed by contamination-control procedures.
7. HCC/ESF-8 bed polling and transfer acceptance/refusal logs.
8. Radiation medicine consult or exercise-inject response log.
9. Patient registry/follow-up/privacy handoff surrogate.
10. CAP/retest/verifier packet for every observed defect.

Every artifact needs a timestamp, owner, scope, hash, redaction class, custody event, verifier, counterevidence path, and public claim gate.
