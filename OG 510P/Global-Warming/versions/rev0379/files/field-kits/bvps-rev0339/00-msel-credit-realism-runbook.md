# BVPS rev0339 MSEL / exercise-realism / no-credit field runbook

Use this before, during, and immediately after the June 2026 exercise window.

1. Freeze the final extent-of-play agreement and record every excluded objective, branch, jurisdiction, population, facility, channel, and load case.
2. Export the MSEL with event IDs, inject times, expected actions, contingency injects, target players, controller IDs, evaluator IDs, and simulation flags.
3. Preserve controller logs, simcell logs, evaluator notes, player communications, safety interventions, pause/restart records, and controller-rescue events.
4. Tag simulated resources, simulated alert delivery, scripted readings, scripted lab results, time compression, public-caller simulation, and public-action simulation as no-credit for physical performance.
5. Require objective-to-EEG/critical-task observation artifacts before any branch receives exercise-derived credit.
6. Route preliminary public meeting statements, hotwash notes, and public AAR paragraphs to evidence demands only. They do not close readiness.
7. Apply no-credit rules before scoring, then route candidate packets to adjudication, CAP/retest, verifier, and public-claim gate.
