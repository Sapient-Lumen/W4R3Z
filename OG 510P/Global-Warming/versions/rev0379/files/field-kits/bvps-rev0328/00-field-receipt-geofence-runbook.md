# BVPS rev0328 Field Receipt and Geofence Edge Runbook

## Non-negotiable rule

A CAP message, IPAWS ACK, OpenFEMA archive row, EAS monitor log, WEA screenshot, or public-meeting sentence is not field-receipt closure. Receipt evidence must show who/what stratum, where in relation to the target area, what channel, what message, when received, whether it was intelligible/actionable, whether accessibility/language needs were served, and how privacy was protected.

## Minimum capture set

1. Inside-target receipts across PA/OH/WV strata.
2. Edge-of-target receipts close to the polygon boundary.
3. Just-outside-target nonreceipt or overshoot probes.
4. Low-signal/rural, dense/urban, school, LTC, hospital, AFN, and producer/farmer probes.
5. English, Spanish, multilingual-template, accessibility, and plain-language checks.
6. Cross-channel comparison: PAD/PAR, CAP, WEA, EAS, SNB/siren, county alert, JIC/press, and public links.
7. Nonreceipt triage that treats silence as a question, not as proof.
8. Redacted surrogate records that preserve auditability without exposing PII, phone identifiers, exact home locations, or medical/AFN status.

## Closure rule

A complete field packet is a candidate for adjudication only. Closure still requires CAP/retest/verifier linkage and public-claim gating.
