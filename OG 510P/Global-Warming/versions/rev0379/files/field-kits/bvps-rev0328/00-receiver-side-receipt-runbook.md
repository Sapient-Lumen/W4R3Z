# BVPS rev0328 Receiver-Side Receipt Field Kit

Use this kit for actual or anonymized June 2026 alert-receipt evidence. Do not include names, phone numbers, exact home addresses, or unredacted screenshots in the public surrogate.

Minimum capture for each observation:

1. observer slot ID from `nuclear-emergency-bvps-field-observer-receipt-grid-rev0328.csv`;
2. approximate observation cell, state/county, inside/boundary/outside relation;
3. carrier/provider and device class;
4. message receipt state: received, not received, delayed, duplicate, correction/update/cancel issue;
5. timestamp with timezone and clock-source note;
6. raw screenshot/video hash and redacted surrogate hash;
7. language/accessibility mode;
8. public-link behavior;
9. counterevidence or rumor/call-center issue linkage;
10. custody event, owner, verifier, and public-claim gate.

A receiver-side packet is never automatic closure. It enters adjudication only.
