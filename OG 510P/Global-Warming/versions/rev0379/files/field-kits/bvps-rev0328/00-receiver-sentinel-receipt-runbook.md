# BVPS rev0328 Receiver/Sentinel Receipt Field Kit

Use this kit during the June 2026 exercise capture window to preserve receiver-side proof without exposing sensitive locations or personal data.

1. Assign sentinel classes, not exact public coordinates, for WEA, EAS, NWR/SAME, siren/SNB, school, LTC/hospital, AFN/no-car, route-control, reception, CRC, and farmer/producer message streams.
2. Capture timestamped evidence: screenshot/audio/video/log export, device/station hash, message id, receipt state, failure state, language/accessibility observation, and custody event.
3. Preserve ACK/error and public archive context separately. They corroborate or contradict; they do not prove community receipt.
4. Route no-hit, late, partial, wrong-language, broken-link, overshoot, undershoot, or facility-missing observations to CAP/retest and the public-claim gate.
5. Split sensitive annexes from public-safe surrogates before external sharing.

No packet created by this kit can auto-close a readiness row. Complete packets enter adjudication only.
