# BVPS rev0334 recovery evidence capture runbook

Use this kit to collect **anonymized or redacted** late-phase recovery evidence.
Do not place PII, exact sensitive routes, security-sensitive staging locations, or
medical records in public-surrogate artifacts.

Minimum capture order:
1. recovery branch authority and zone/reentry map surrogate;
2. access-pass, escort, dosimetry, and rollback records;
3. relocation housing/cash/claims/appeal packet;
4. cleanup characterization grid and cleanup-goal decision record;
5. waste segregation/staging/transport/receiver acceptance packet;
6. property/business/school release and retest records;
7. registry authority, privacy, dedupe, follow-up, and claims linkage;
8. decedent/mortuary survey and chain-of-custody evidence;
9. public advisory/message correction clock and public claim board minutes.

Every artifact needs owner, timestamp, scope, SHA-256 hash, verifier, sensitive-annex
routing, counterevidence path, and CAP/retest status. Complete packets are candidates
for adjudication only; they do not auto-close readiness.
