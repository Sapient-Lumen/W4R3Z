# BVPS rev0329 action-proof and reception-handoff runbook

Use this card during or immediately after the evaluated-exercise window to avoid losing action evidence.  Capture only privacy-safe or authorized local/anonymized records.

1. Pair every PAD/PAR and public message with an action probe: receipt, comprehension, action start, action completion, or no-action reason.
2. For evacuation, preserve route-control checkpoint logs, redirect logs, congestion observations, and evaluator notes.
3. For reception/CRC, preserve activation time, intake counts, station logs, instrument checks, decon split-flow, medical redirects, and queue bottlenecks.
4. For shelter-in-place, preserve shelter-start time, self-decon instruction, release basis, release message, and post-release destination.
5. For AFN, school, LTC, hospital, and other special facilities, preserve pickup/dropoff, medication/equipment continuity, reunification, and verifier evidence.
6. Treat silence as unknown until active probes or adjudicated evidence exist.
7. Public summaries, archive hits, and future AAR paragraphs route evidence demands; they do not close readiness.
