# BVPS rev0335 responder logistics / mutual aid / continuity runbook

Capture these before, during, and immediately after the exercise:

1. Resource-board export with objective, request, kind/type, priority, owner, source, acceptance/denial, ETA, arrival, assignment, demob, cost code, and verifier.
2. Staffing roster with roles, backups, shift relief, fatigue checks, and sensitive-annex split.
3. Mission request package for every mutual-aid or cross-border resource.
4. Fuel, generator, radio, vehicle, PPE, dosimetry, CRC, field-monitoring, and hospital-support cache manifest.
5. Communications interoperability test records and failures.
6. EOC/EOF continuity proof, including alternate site/remote access and record continuity.
7. ERHMS/responder health/fatigue records with privacy-safe surrogate.
8. Emergency procurement/cost-capture package.

Do not mark any packet closed. Accept it only as rejected, hold/no-upgrade, context/no-upgrade, reopen signal, or candidate for adjudication.
