## Summary

Rev0261 extends the care-continuity sequence into the labour layer. Climate packets now need worker-continuity and labour-shock-absorber fields when they touch public services, food, water, health, schools, care, recovery, utilities, transport, agriculture, construction, clean buildout, informal work, migrant work, or social protection.

The practical shift: worker protection is no longer treated as an HR appendix or a just-transition slogan. It is part of climate adaptation and service continuity. Heat, smoke, UV, flood, disease exposure, unsafe cleanup, wage loss, piece-rate pressure, informal-worker exclusion, migrant-worker vulnerability, and unpaid safety now route to explicit thresholds, work modification, paid breaks / shutdowns, wage replacement, procurement duties, worker voice, enforcement, and after-action correction.
