# Nuclear emergency preparedness — exercise realism, MSEL fidelity, no-credit boundaries and evaluation integrity refactor

Revision: **rev0339**  
Date: **2026-06-05 05:15 America/New_York**  
Status: **compact canon / operational proof spine**

## Why this revision exists

The previous revision hardened common-operating-picture clocks, handoffs and decision traces. The next false-green failure is an exercise that appears successful because the exercise itself smoothed away the hard parts: the MSEL paced responders into the expected answer, an extent-of-play agreement excluded the hardest branch, controllers coached players, evaluators could not observe the critical action, simulated resources were credited as if delivered, or a public preliminary statement was treated as final proof.

This revision adds an exercise-realism firebreak. It does not weaken the value of evaluated exercises. It makes them more useful by requiring every exercise-derived claim to carry its realism boundary, credit rule, evaluator basis, artificiality list, no-credit condition, and CAP/retest route.

## New hard rule

Public HSEEP, REP, NRC/FEMA schedule, MSEL, extent-of-play, controller/evaluator, exercise-builder, public meeting, public AAR, EEG, template, course, or source-ID material can create a demand, clock, cap, contradiction, or reopen signal. It cannot close local emergency-readiness evidence.

A complete exercise packet can be accepted only as **candidate_for_adjudication**. It must still pass no-credit checks, objective/critical-task coverage, evaluator independence, artificiality disclosure, CAP/retest linkage, counterevidence review, and public-claim gating.

## What is now blocked

1. **MSEL theater** — injects that guide players to the required answer without measuring independent performance.
2. **Extent-of-play erasure** — objectives, jurisdictions, populations, or branches excluded from play while downstream claims stay broad.
3. **Simulation credit inflation** — simulated staff, simulated routes, simulated resource arrival, simulated alert delivery, simulated field readings, simulated lab results, or simulated public action counted as real performance.
4. **Controller coaching** — controllers or simulators correcting players in ways that should instead be observations or findings.
5. **Evaluator blind spots** — unobserved critical tasks being credited from hearsay, public summaries, or self-attestation.
6. **Preknowledge and exercise contamination** — players receiving scenario specifics, target outcomes, or answer keys in advance.
7. **No-credit bypass** — hotwash notes, preliminary findings, revised templates, public meeting statements, or public AAR paragraphs used as closure without CAP/retest/verifier proof.

## Canonical route

`exercise objective → extent-of-play boundary → MSEL event/inject → expected action → critical task/EEG → controller/evaluator assignment → observation/capture evidence → artificiality/no-credit check → finding/AAR/IP → CAP/retest/verifier → public claim gate`
