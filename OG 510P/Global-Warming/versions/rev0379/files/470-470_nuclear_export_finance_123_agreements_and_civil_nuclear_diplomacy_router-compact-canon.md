# 470 — Nuclear export finance, 123 agreements, and civil-nuclear diplomacy router

## Nuclear-positive rule

The cube favors allied civil-nuclear exports and financing where they displace fossil dependence, strengthen reliable power, and meet nonproliferation and public-value conditions.

## Assurance implication

Export finance, SMR diplomacy, and 123-agreement readiness become service floors. A nuclear export package must show legal cooperation authority, safeguards commitments, regulator capacity, debt sustainability, public-value compact and challengeable procurement.
