## Why

The archive already had the physical-jobs note (`208`), the grow / shrink / stop note (`209`), the abundance / rationing / retirement note (`210`), the public-meaning note (`211`), the public-bargain note (`212`), and the deeper low-friction doctrine (`185`).
What it still lacked was the smallest front-door bridge from those ideas to the decisive moment when something is actually being replaced, financed, approved, connected, or installed.

That gap matters because a climate program can be physically right and politically attractive in the abstract yet still underperform if the clean option remains harder to find, qualify for, pay for, or coordinate than the dirty fallback.

So this revision adds the archive's shortest uptake rule: **make the clean path the easy default at the point of decision, and strip the dirty repeat choice of silent convenience, silent subsidy, and procedural privilege.**
