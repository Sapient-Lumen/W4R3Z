# Nuclear fuel-cycle, HALEU, and waste-custody assurance

A nuclear-positive cube must be fuel-cycle honest. A reactor pathway is not mature merely because the reactor design looks promising. It needs uranium, conversion, enrichment, fuel fabrication, transport, safeguards, security, refueling logistics, spent-fuel custody and waste stewardship.

For advanced reactors, the HALEU question is especially binding. DOE describes its HALEU Availability Program as a bridge to spur private fuel-supply investment [S785]. The World Nuclear Association defines HALEU as uranium enriched above 5% and below 20% U-235 and notes that many advanced and SMR designs require it [S786]. NEA's SMR readiness work also treats fuel as a deployment-readiness dimension [S784]. Fleet facts and operating status should remain tied to PRIS rather than project claims [S777].

## Rule

A nuclear project or pathway cannot be scored above template maturity unless the cube can point to a fuel-cycle assurance row that names:

- fuel form and enrichment level;
- conversion, enrichment and fabrication pathway;
- fuel-contract or allocation status;
- safeguards, material-accounting and security controls;
- transport constraints;
- spent-fuel and waste-custody owner;
- funding and stewardship horizon;
- regulatory status.

## Advanced-reactor cap

If the technology requires HALEU and no credible HALEU supply, fabrication, transport and regulatory path is visible, the project remains favored in principle but capped as **fuel-constrained template maturity**.
