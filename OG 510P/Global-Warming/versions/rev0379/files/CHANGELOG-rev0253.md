## Core change

Rev0252 made the reverse side of clean buildout visible: repair, reuse, decommissioning, recycling, recovered-material proof, and waste-shipment compliance.

Rev0253 adds the place and host-legitimacy layer: a packet can be circular, compliant, financeable, and technically sound, yet still fail if land, water, seabed, corridors, biodiversity, cultural places, cumulative impacts, host communities, Indigenous consent duties, benefit-sharing, and grievance repair are handled as afterthoughts.

The new rule is that footprint-bearing packets need a **place-capacity ledger** and a **host-community / rights ledger** before being called ready.

## Why it matters

Clean deployment has to accelerate, but acceleration cannot mean pretending place is empty or rights are friction.
The durable speed path is upstream planning, low-conflict siting, rights-respecting consent where applicable, enforceable benefits, accessible grievance, and correction triggers before projects become all-or-nothing conflicts.
