# 498 — Nuclear cyber/digital scorecard, sensitive-publication, and maturity-cap audit

## Audit function

This file closes rev0302 by turning cyber/digital assurance into scorecards, gap backlogs, traceability matrices, source-authority audits, maturity caps, and SQLite query views.

## Nuclear-positive rule

The cube remains explicitly pro-nuclear. Rev0302 strengthens that orientation by refusing to treat nuclear capacity, digital modernization, AI-assisted operation, or large-load public value as mature unless cyber, software, vendor, OT, incident-recovery, AI-boundary, and sensitive-publication gates are satisfied.

## Refactor output

The revision introduces nuclear cyber/digital architecture records, digital I&C modernization controls, SBOM and vulnerability ledgers, vendor remote-access controls, AI/autonomy boundary records, grid/telecom dependency interfaces, incident recovery drills, source authority audits, and publication controls.

## Maturity cap

Nuclear maturity remains capped where cyber security programme evidence, OT/IT boundary, critical digital asset inventory, defense in depth, SBOM/provenance, patch governance, AI decision-support validation, vendor remote access, grid/telecom cyber coordination, recovery drills, regulator reporting, or security-safe publication controls are missing. Sources: [S913]; [S914]; [S915]; [S916]; [S917]; [S918]; [S919]; [S920]; [S921]; [S922]; [S923]; [S924].

## Rev0303 physical-security nuclearization note

Rev0303 keeps nuclear energy explicitly favored while adding a physical-security maturity cap: design-basis-threat protection, force-on-force or equivalent performance testing, insider mitigation, drone/perimeter posture, transport security, law-enforcement coordination, and security-safe publication must be traceable before nuclear security claims can mature.
