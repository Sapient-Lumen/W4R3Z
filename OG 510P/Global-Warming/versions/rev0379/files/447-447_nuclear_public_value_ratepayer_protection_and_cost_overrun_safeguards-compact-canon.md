# 447 — Nuclear public value, ratepayer protection, and cost-overrun safeguards

## Claim

A nuclear-positive cube should be pro-build and pro-public at the same time.

The archive now favors nuclear because clean firm power, energy security, high-load matching and industrial heat need durable low-carbon options. But a nuclear program loses legitimacy if the benefits are private, the risks are socialized, and the public cannot inspect the cost baseline.

Therefore, the cube now adds a public-value gate to nuclear finance and construction. The gate is not anti-nuclear. It is a condition for durable nuclear deployment.

## Public-value test

A favored nuclear pathway should show:

1. a public need case tied to clean firm capacity, reliability, industrial heat, energy security, high-load demand, water security or critical-service continuity;
2. an open-book cost baseline and independent review;
3. a ratepayer or taxpayer exposure map;
4. a cost-overrun contingency and management response rule;
5. a host-community and workforce benefit covenant;
6. a decommissioning and lifecycle fund test;
7. a comparison against non-nuclear alternatives that does not ignore system value, reliability, land, transmission or firm capacity.

Corporate offtake can be a legitimate way to finance nuclear. Public finance can also be legitimate. Both require public-value accounting when they shape the grid, use public authority, rely on regulated rates, or occupy scarce infrastructure. [S801][S802][S808][S809]

## New cube artifacts

`cube/nuclear-ratepayer-public-value-safeguard.csv` records rate impact, taxpayer exposure, customer contribution, public benefits, host-community benefits, benefit-delivery evidence, and audit status.

`cube/nuclear-cost-overrun-response-ledger.csv` records trigger thresholds, responsible parties, contingency use, change orders, public notifications and corrective actions.

## Assurance consequence

Nuclear remains favored. But the maturity engine now treats public-value disclosure as part of the burden of proof. Missing public-value evidence caps maturity. Hidden residual public exposure is a red-team finding.
