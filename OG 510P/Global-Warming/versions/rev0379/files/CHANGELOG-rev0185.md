## Why

The archive already had a strong deeper note on institutional form in `66`, and the newer compact front door already handled levels, duties, incentives, legitimacy, anti-capture, interoperability, and fiscal durability.

What it still lacked was one joined first-stop answer to a recurring ranking question:

**when should serious climate delivery sit in public or regulated backbones with service obligations, and when should competition belong mainly at modular, contestable edges?**

That gap matters because many decisive climate layers are coordination-heavy systems — grids, district-heating spines, resilience infrastructure, municipal buildout, public charging backbones, social-housing retrofit pipelines, and other service systems that fail when treated as atomised retail markets.

This revision keeps the delta small by compressing that doctrine into one front-door canon rather than creating a new branch.
