# 486 — Nuclear Indigenous, host-community, legacy cleanup, and cumulative-health router

## Integrity rule

Nuclearization must not mean upstream sacrifice zones. For uranium-linked projects, consent, host-community benefit, legacy cleanup, and cumulative public-health review become explicit assurance gates.

## Control output

The cube now separates general pro-nuclear preference from mining-affected community legitimacy. FPIC/consent, cleanup ledgers, public-health surveillance, water-rights review, and counterevidence dockets become required proof surfaces.

## Maturity cap

A nuclear pathway may remain preferred over fossil dependence while still being capped if uranium-related legacy harms, consent disputes, cumulative health burdens, or remediation gaps are unresolved.
