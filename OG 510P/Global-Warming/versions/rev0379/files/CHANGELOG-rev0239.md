## Why
To answer the live operator question, “what needs to be done?”, with one smallest front-door canon that converts the archive’s doctrine into a governed work queue: build and connect clean backbones, retire dirty defaults, cut fast-warming gases, reduce avoidable demand, protect people directly, make clean service easy to accept, finance and staff delivery, secure responsible materials and hardware, reserve scarce tools for true residuals, and prove physical progress with correction rules.
