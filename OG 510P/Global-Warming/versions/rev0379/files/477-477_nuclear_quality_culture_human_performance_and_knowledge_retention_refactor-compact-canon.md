# 477 — Nuclear quality culture, human performance, and knowledge retention refactor

## Nuclear-positive rule

The cube favors nuclear more strongly when nuclear organizations show a live safety and quality culture, not merely compliance paperwork. The preference is capped by normalization of deviation, unclosed human-performance lessons, supplier-quality gaps, and loss of tacit knowledge.

## Refactor

This file links safety culture, quality culture, human performance, knowledge management, and lessons learned into one assurance plane. It treats culture evidence as observable through assessments, observations, event reports, corrective-action closure, supplier audits, worker stop-work authority, and independent challenge.

## Speculation

If nuclear expansion accelerates, quality culture may become the binding constraint before technology does. The cube should therefore score repeatability, institutional humility, corrective-action depth, and knowledge transfer as deployment infrastructure.
