# 456 — Nuclear hydrogen, synthetic fuels, ammonia, and clean-molecules router

## Nuclear-positive rule

The cube now prefers nuclear-backed hydrogen and clean molecules where they replace fossil hydrogen, fossil industrial fuel, or reliability-damaging peaking, and where lifecycle accounting, safety, water, storage, and offtake evidence are public enough to audit.

## Burden of proof

A nuclear hydrogen or clean-fuel pathway must show clean-energy matching, safety boundaries, water effects, end-use priority, and lifecycle emissions. It cannot claim maturity from nameplate electrolyzer capacity alone.
