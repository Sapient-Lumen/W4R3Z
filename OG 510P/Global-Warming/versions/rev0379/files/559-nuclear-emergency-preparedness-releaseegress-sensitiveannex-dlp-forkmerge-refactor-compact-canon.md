Package role: compact canon for release-egress, sensitive-annex DLP, public-safe surrogate control, and fork-merge of the rev0351 release-egress side branch into the linked offline-blackout base.

## Why this exists

The linked rev0351 base made the evidence operation survivable during console, network, printer, scanner, timestamp, identity-provider, and shared-drive failures. The cloudtainer also contained a same-revision side artifact focused on release egress and sensitive-annex controls. Rev0352 merges that useful branch without copying its conflicting numbered file.

The operational risk is now the reverse of capture loss: after evidence is captured or rehydrated, a public release, public-meeting handout, press note, shared folder, dashboard export, or redacted surrogate can leak sensitive material or imply readiness before adjudication.

## Hard rule

A folder, README, placeholder, label, paper receipt, offline form, delayed hash, rehydrated packet, redacted surrogate, public notice, public meeting statement, preliminary finding, AAR paragraph, dashboard, PI page, MSEL event, extent-of-play statement, source ID, duplicate URL, sensitive-annex label, DLP pass, release manifest row, public-safe surrogate, or complete-looking packet can demand, cap, route, contradict, or reopen a claim. It cannot automatically close local emergency-readiness evidence.

## Release-egress route

```text
captured or rehydrated packet
  -> sensitive-annex classifier
  -> denylist and DLP scan
  -> surrogate pair check
  -> two-person release board
  -> public-claim embargo lint
  -> release manifest
  -> adjudication candidate only
  -> CAP / retest / verifier if applicable
  -> integrated claim kernel
```

## What is protected

The release-egress gate blocks or holds material that may expose:

* PII, PHI, minor/student records, AFN rosters, patient registry fields, call-center caller identity, or decedent/family records;
* alerting credentials, IPAWS/COG/account details, certificate details, security-sensitive route vulnerabilities, exact target polygons, bridge/fuel/generator vulnerabilities, cyber logs, or vendor secrets;
* raw field measurements, source-term/model details, unreleased evaluator notes, controller/simcell logs, disputes, preliminary findings, CAP owner disputes, or sensitive annexes;
* any phrase that would imply ready, green, passed, safe, sufficient, certified, complete, demonstrated, or closed before adjudication.

## Fork-merge decision

The canonical base for rev0352 is the linked offline-blackout rev0351 artifact. The sibling release-egress rev0351 artifact is recorded as a side branch and its useful theme is merged as new rev0352 content. Its conflicting `558-...` numbered canon is not copied.

## Claim boundary

No real/anonymized June 2026 Beaver Valley evidence is imported by this revision. The release-egress machinery makes public release safer; it does not make any readiness finding.
