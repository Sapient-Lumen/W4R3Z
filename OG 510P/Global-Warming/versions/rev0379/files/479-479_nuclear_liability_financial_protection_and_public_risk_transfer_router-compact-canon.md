# 479 — Nuclear liability, financial protection, and public-risk-transfer router

## Audit function

This router makes nuclear-positive policy explicit without hiding who carries downside risk. Nuclear can remain a preferred clean-firm backbone only where liability, primary financial protection, retrospective or pooled compensation capacity, indemnity boundaries, and public backstop exposure are visible and tested.

## Nuclear-positive rule

The cube favors nuclear because reliable clean firm power can avoid fossil harm and improve public-service continuity. Rev0299 adds a hard public-risk-transfer condition: no project, restart, uprate, SMR, fuel facility, or integrated energy pathway should claim high maturity unless the liability regime and compensation pathway are legible before the project is treated as publicly beneficial.
