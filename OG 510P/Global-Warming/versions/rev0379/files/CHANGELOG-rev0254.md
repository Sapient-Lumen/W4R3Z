## Core change

Rev0253 made the archive ask where clean infrastructure goes and who hosts it.
Rev0254 asks what happens to the dirty places already in the system.

The revision adds two rules:

1. **Fossil retirement needs closure proof.** Dirty assets are not retired by paperwork, sale, bankruptcy, mothballing, or accounting treatment. They need asset-by-asset closure ledgers covering methane, hazards, financial assurance, worker and community terms, anti-orphaning controls, post-closure monitoring, and proof that dirty service is actually displaced.
2. **Legacy sites need remediation-and-reuse passports.** Former fossil and contaminated places can release siting bottlenecks and become clean infrastructure, restoration, public-service, or community-value assets only when cleanup, residual risk, monitoring, benefit governance, and long-term liability travel with the site.

## Compression

**Do not let the transition create ghost liabilities. Retire dirty assets physically, fund cleanup before revenues vanish, stop orphaning through transfer and bankruptcy, and reuse legacy places only when the new use repairs the place rather than hiding old harm under clean branding.**
