## Why
The archive already had compact notes for readiness before a bad day, live discipline during the detour, rehearsal so readiness does not stay paper-only, and readiness assurance so readiness claims are not ceremonial. What it still lacked was the shortest rule for what a serious climate state should do when it has checked honestly and found that readiness is still partial. Rev0112 closes that gap by turning visible downgrade into an operating posture: de-rate the claim, simplify to the core mission, tighten protection and escalation sensitivity, force the highest-consequence gaps onto a live repair clock, and borrow support until readiness is restored.
