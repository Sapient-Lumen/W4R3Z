# 488 — Nuclear upstream scorecard, responsible supply, and extraction-externality audit

## Audit function

This file closes rev0300 by converting upstream uranium impacts into scorecards, gap backlogs, source-authority checks, exception ledgers, and maturity caps.

## Nuclear-positive rule

The cube remains explicitly pro-nuclear. Rev0300 strengthens that preference by requiring responsible upstream supply rather than pretending uranium extraction has no public-health, environmental, rights, or legacy-cleanup burden.

## Refactor output

The revision introduces upstream mining, tailings, water, reclamation, FPIC/host-community, legacy cleanup, provenance, transport, and responsible-supply tables. These artifacts make uranium sourcing auditable without publishing security-sensitive details.

## Maturity cap

Nuclear maturity remains capped where upstream sourcing, tailings, radon, water, reclamation, FPIC/host-community consent, legacy cleanup, material provenance, transport, or public-health evidence is missing.
