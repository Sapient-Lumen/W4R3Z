## Why
The archive already had compact notes for readiness duties, rehearsal, readiness assurance, compensating duties for partial readiness, conservative defaults for unknown readiness, and a four-state readiness ladder. What it still lacked was the shortest rule for **when an institution may actually move upward on that ladder**. Rev0115 closes that gap by adding five promotion tests so upgrades are earned by current proof rather than by elapsed time, paperwork, or returning confidence.
