## Doctrine change

Rev0247 says:

1. A climate claim should be allowed to move trust, money, permissions, or priority only when it is specific, bounded, comparable, auditable, physically linked, and correctable.
2. A climate value channel should move fast through standard, transparent, contestable, auditable routes, with ownership and conflict visibility where value is high, milestone proof before value release, and clawback or redesign when integrity fails.
3. Claims integrity and anti-capture design are not reputational accessories; they are delivery infrastructure.
