# CHANGELOG — rev0245

## Revision focus

Rev0245 is a **recovery and protection-gap front-door** revision.

Rev0244 asked whether delivery packets survive compound shocks and whether critical services keep running under stress.
Rev0245 asks what happens after the bad day: whether recovery money reduces risk or repeats loss, whether uninsured losses are named or silently shifted, whether public backstops lower exposure or hide it, and whether retreat is prepared as a rights-respecting public option before uninsurability becomes private abandonment.
