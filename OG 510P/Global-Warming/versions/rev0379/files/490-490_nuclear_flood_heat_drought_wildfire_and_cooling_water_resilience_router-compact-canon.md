# 490 — Nuclear flood, heat, drought, wildfire, and cooling-water resilience router

## Function

This router converts climate-hardening claims into tables for flood and storm-surge protection, heat and thermal-margin controls, drought and water-availability resilience, wildfire-smoke interfaces, cooling-water intake resilience, and ultimate heat sink adequacy.

## Nuclear-positive rule

Nuclear remains preferred where it can maintain firm output without hiding derate, cooling-water, intake, flood-barrier, or heat-wave constraints. A reactor that becomes unavailable during the very climate loadcase it is supposed to stabilize cannot be counted as mature clean-firm resilience.

## Refactor output

The revision adds resilience screens, inspection backlogs, cooling-water stress records, derate/public-power continuity records, and exception ledgers so that climate-hardening investments are visible before project or fleet maturity is upgraded.

## Evidence burden

Minimum evidence includes flood protection inspection, cooling-water and ultimate-heat-sink stress tests, heat derate modeling, drought priority rules, wildfire-smoke/air-intake controls, and public reporting of residual vulnerabilities.
