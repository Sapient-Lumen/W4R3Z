# 201 — Ideal Solutions: Basic Unit of Scale — A Replicable Archetype with One Proof Ledger Compact Canon

## Claim

The archive now has a shortest answer (`195`), a transition-mechanism note (`198`), a replacement-proof note (`199`), a basic-unit note (`200`), a replication note (`168`), a place-sensitivity note (`176`), a routinization note (`171`), and a propagation note (`147`).
What it still lacked was one very small front-door note answering the next practical question after the atomic unit is named:

**What is the smallest thing a serious climate programme should try to spread if it wants one verified win to become many without reopening bespoke design from zero each time?**

That gap matters because climate programmes often jump from one good project straight to the language of scale.
But scale does not really mean a pile of unrelated local victories.
It means a working replacement package has been turned into a **replicable archetype**: one stable causal spine, one usable delivery form, one proof ledger, and one bounded set of place adjustments that similar control points can adopt without doctrinal drift.
This note is therefore a compression note, not a new doctrinal branch.
Its job is to gather the archive’s replication rule, place-sensitivity rule, routinization rule, interoperability rule, and atomic-unit rule into one first-stop answer for the archive’s unit of scale [S117][S118][S119][S123][S124][S125][S150][S151][S152][S153][S172][S173][S175][S180][S181][S182][S183].

## Fast rule

**Do not try to scale isolated projects. Scale a replicable archetype: a verified replacement package at a recurring control point, expressed as a standard offer, reference design, delivery playbook, and proof ledger that similar places can adapt without starting from a blank page.**

## The five parts of the unit of scale

### 1. Keep the causal core stable
A serious scaling form should preserve the core replacement logic that made the first package work: what cleaner service carries load, what switching moment is captured, what dirty path is constrained, what protection duty is included, and what physical outcomes prove success.
If the causal core keeps changing from place to place, the archive should treat the programme as repeated redesign rather than real scale [S123][S124][S150][S152][S181].

### 2. Standardise the offer enough that the next place does not begin from zero
A serious scaling form should travel as a usable archetype: model rules, standard contracts, procurement templates, standard technical packages, finance pathways, queue treatment, training routines, and operating checklists.
The point is not rigidity for its own sake.
The point is to make the next adoption start from a prepared form instead of bespoke negotiation and rediscovery [S118][S119][S150][S151][S153][S180].

### 3. Adapt the edges, not the whole machine
A serious scaling form should allow bounded local adjustment for climate, settlement form, grid condition, labour mix, building stock, fiscal room, exposure profile, and legal context.
But those place adjustments should happen at the edges rather than dissolving the archetype into one-off local stories.
Copy the core, fit the perimeter [S117][S152][S172][S173][S175].

### 4. Carry one proof ledger across the family
A serious scaling form should move with common metrics, comparable monitoring, visible retirement or avoidance evidence, and recurring review so similar packages can be compared honestly across sites.
If each place proves success in a different way, the programme loses learning, comparability, and correction discipline just when it claims to be scaling [S123][S124][S125][S181][S182][S183].

### 5. Repeat through recurring control points and ordinary cadence
A serious scaling form should attach itself to recurring control points and calendars: auctions, procurement rounds, product standards, retrofit waves, rebuild standards, fleet replacement, permit windows, and financing cycles.
Scale is not many inspirational exceptions.
It is a routine that can be pulled again and again across similar sites and years [S119][S152][S180][S181][S182][S183].

## Preferred use

Open this note first when the live question is any of the following:
- what the archive thinks the **unit of scale** actually is
- what a team should replicate after it has one verified replacement package
- how one good climate intervention becomes many without doctrinal drift
- what should stay fixed and what may vary when a working package travels across places
- why standard offers, proof ledgers, and recurring cadence matter more than heroic one-off wins

Then reopen `147`, `168`, `171`, `176`, or `200` depending on whether the surviving question is about propagation after gains are banked, fast replication, recurring cadence, place-sensitive adaptation, or the atomic unit of progress itself.

## Compression rule

**The archive should scale not isolated projects but replicable archetypes: keep the replacement logic stable, package it as a standard offer and playbook, adapt only the bounded local edges, carry one proof ledger across the family, and repeat it through recurring control points and ordinary institutional cadence.**
