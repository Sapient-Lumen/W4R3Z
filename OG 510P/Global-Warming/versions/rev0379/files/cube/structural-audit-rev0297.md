## Audit focus

Rev0297 audits the geopolitical-resilience side of the nuclear-positive cube. Prior revisions made nuclear favored for clean-firm power, finance, buildability, operations, integrated energy, fuel cycle/backend and system benefit. The remaining risk was that nuclear energy-security claims could substitute one fragile dependence for another: fuel-cycle concentration, vendor lock-in, export-credit leverage, sanctions exposure, weak partner capacity or unsafe publication boundaries.

## Refactor result

The package now includes first-class tables for geopolitical resilience, allied supply-chain scoring, export-finance/civil-nuclear diplomacy, sanctions/vendor lock-in, strategic fuel reserve posture, nonproliferation/export-control crosswalk, sensitive-publication control, red-team challenges and source-authority classification. It also rebuilds the stale source-use ledger and refreshes route metrics.
