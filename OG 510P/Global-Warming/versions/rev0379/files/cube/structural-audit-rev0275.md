## Structural rule added

Any file that claims recovery, repair, relocation, rebuilding, reopening, or restoration should answer eight gates:

1. property / occupancy / tenancy / loss proof;
2. risk-exit or no-rebuild decision;
3. procurement and contracting path;
4. reimbursement, match, liquidity, and audit path;
5. code, permit, inspection, and upgrade-finance state;
6. essential-worker sustainment;
7. shared-asset / private-governance control structure;
8. unmet-needs closure or residual-need disclosure.

## Next likely gaps

The cube is approaching saturation in service-floor and recovery-rail doctrine. The next high-value pass should probably move from admitting new domains to building scoring and queries: readiness grades, route queries, owner tables, stale-source checks, duplicate-source collapse, and automated tests for whether each service floor has its gates filled. Candidate content gaps remain: cross-border mutual aid, sovereign fiscal stress, transboundary water / river-basin conflict, insurance retreat and mortgage-market repricing, prisons / hospitals evacuation under cyber outage, and heat survivability in informal labour camps. But the next structural benefit may come from **scoring, not more canon**.
