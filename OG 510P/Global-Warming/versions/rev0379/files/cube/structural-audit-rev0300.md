# Structural audit rev0300 — nuclear upstream uranium, tailings, consent, provenance refactor

Created: 2026-05-30T16:05:00-04:00
Base revision: rev0299

## Audit/refactor target

Rev0300 continues the explicit nuclear-positive orientation but adds an upstream accountability plane. The cube now favors nuclear only when uranium sourcing does not hide mining, milling, tailings, radon, groundwater, reclamation, Indigenous/host-community, legacy-cleanup, material-provenance, ore-transport, or cumulative public-health burdens.

## Major new controls

- Responsible uranium sourcing and material provenance.
- Mine/mill EIA, baseline monitoring, worker protection, and public dockets.
- Tailings design, radon control, groundwater protection, reclamation bonding, and postclosure surveillance.
- Indigenous/host-community consent, benefit, grievance, and counterevidence paths.
- Legacy mine cleanup and cumulative health/environmental monitoring.
- Security-safe publication boundaries for material movement and sensitive controls.
