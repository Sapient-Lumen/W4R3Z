# Structural audit rev0336

Rev0336 adds a cyber/IT data-continuity layer beneath emergency preparedness operations. The main structural correction is that downstream branches now depend on system inventory, IAM/credential proof, backup/restore proof, data exchange integrity, vendor/SaaS fallback, and cyber incident clocks.

The legacy universal nuclear crossproduct tables remain compatibility surfaces only. Emergency-readiness queries should route through sparse/local evidence and the relevant branch-specific proof spines.

No real-site cyber readiness or unreadiness claim is made. REAL_BVPS_PUBLIC_ONLY remains public-context-only.
