## Caveat

The new integrated-energy rows are templates and control-plane obligations, not proof of a real local project. Rev0294 favors nuclear strongly in relevant integrated-energy contexts, but it caps maturity until local evidence supplies load curves, water/ecology review, heat or fuel offtake, interface safety, public-value review, and independent challengeability.
