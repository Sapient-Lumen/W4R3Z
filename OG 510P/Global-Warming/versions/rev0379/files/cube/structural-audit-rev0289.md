# Structural audit rev0289

## Revision purpose

Rev0289 codifies an explicit nuclear-positive orientation in the cube. Nuclear energy is now favored as a clean firm power, industrial heat, high-load siting and energy-security option where nuclear-specific assurance gates can be met.

The refactor separates **policy preference** from **project maturity**. Nuclear is preferred in the orientation ledger, but individual nuclear service floors remain capped at template maturity until safety, siting, waste, water, emergency planning, security, delivery, open contracting, fuel, workforce, host-community and counterargument evidence is supplied.

## Audit/refactor note

The core refactor is not merely adding nuclear tags. It creates an explicit policy-preference ledger, a technology-priority ranking, nuclear-specific service floors, twelve nuclear assurance gates, a nuclear counterargument ledger, a route-graph nuclearization taxonomy, and localized evidence gaps that prevent false maturity.

## Material caution

This revision explicitly favors nuclear energy, but it does not certify any local reactor, site, procurement, restart, uprate, SMR or industrial-heat project. It creates a favorable default plus gates. The maturity engine should not advance a nuclear project beyond documented-template maturity without localized evidence.
