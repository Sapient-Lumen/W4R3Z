# Structural audit rev0335

Rev0335 adds a support-system proof spine rather than another broad doctrine layer. The riskiest unmodeled assumption was that every response/recovery branch can draw on the same limited staff, radios, vehicles, fuel, generators, dosimeters, credentials, vendors, mutual-aid channels, and finance staff without conflict.

## Correction

The package now routes downstream readiness through an operational dependency DAG and P0 critical cutsets. Public NIMS, EMAC, ESF #7, SAFECOM, COOP, OSHA, and ERHMS materials remain context-only. They cannot close local staffing, logistics, mutual-aid, communications, continuity, finance, or responder-health proof rows.

## Residual risk

No real/anonymized resource board, mission request, roster, fuel receipt, radio test log, shift-relief board, ERHMS roster, or cost package has been imported. The next concrete import should be a sanitized exercise resource-board export with mission request, arrival, assignment, demob, and cost fields.
