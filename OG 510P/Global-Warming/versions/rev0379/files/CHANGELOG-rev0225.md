## Why this revision exists

the archive already said that delivery capacity matters.
What it still did not say front-door plainly enough was that once technology is mature and finance is at least partly mobilised, a large part of climate speed is now set by the throughput of planning, permitting, procurement, inspections, queue handling, staffing, and workforce formation itself.

This revision adds the smallest useful bridge: a dedicated compact canon saying that at mass scale, institutional throughput is climate speed too.

## Design note
