# 244 — Ideal Solutions: Convert the Work Queue into Owner-Led Delivery Packets — Minimum Implementation Ledger Compact Canon

## Claim

The archive now has a governed work queue (`242`) and an overshoot-era discipline (`243`).
The next missing piece is the smallest unit of execution:

**when is a climate-work item formed enough that it can be governed, funded, inspected, corrected, copied, or stopped?**

This note answers with a minimum implementation ledger.
It is not a new sector doctrine.
It is a formation rule for the work queue.

A work item is not formed merely because a target, law, fund, pilot, pledge, strategy, procurement line, or technology name exists.
It is formed when it names the physical outcome, the control point, the owner, the authority, the money, the delivery bottleneck, the affected users or communities, the proof metric, the correction trigger, and the dirty-default retirement or protection link.

Rev0242 adds a shelf-status rule: a packet should also say whether it is only formed, shelf-ready, window-ready, in delivery, operating, copyable, closed, or quarantined. That status prevents a correct idea from being mistaken for an absorbable project, and prevents a good project from missing the finance, grid-capacity, procurement, or recovery window it is supposed to use [S247][S248].

Rev0243 adds a crew-and-completion rule: a packet should also say what people and public offices must exist to deliver it, and what commissioning, O&M, warranty, user-support, and aftercare tests must be passed before it counts as complete. This prevents an installable project from being mistaken for a stable clean service [S176][S178][S259][S260].

Rev0246 adds a balance-sheet and affordability rule: a packet should also say who carries the risk and cost before and after implementation, how costs are recovered, which hidden liabilities it reduces, and which affordability firebreak prevents bills, premiums, rents, rates, fees, deductibles, debt, or time burdens from breaking consent [S269][S270][S271][S272][S273][S274].

Rev0247 adds a claims-integrity and integrity-throughput rule: a packet should also say which climate claims it makes, how those claims are bounded and corrected, and how its value channels avoid capture, fraud, collusion, inflated prices, poor quality, or rent extraction [S275][S276][S280][S281][S282][S283][S284].

Rev0248 adds an enforcement-remedy and accountability-routing rule: a packet should also say what duty it creates, what evidence proves breach, what cure route applies, what support is available, what escalation trigger fires, what remedy follows, how essential users are protected, and which forum can correct the failed condition [S285][S286][S287][S288][S290][S291][S292].

Rev0249 adds a data-spine and proof-passport rule: a packet should also say what persistent record identifies it, which systems must link to it, what proof travels with any relevant product, building, project, site, claim, or service relationship, who stewards the record, which fields are public or protected, and what correction route fires when the record is wrong, stale, missing, or misleading [S293][S294][S295][S296][S297][S298][S299][S300].

Rev0250 adds a cyber-resilience / AI-accountability rule: a digitally dependent packet should also say what connected systems, vendors, control surfaces, personal or security-sensitive data, models, digital twins, scoring tools, and automated rules it depends on; what fallback and fail-safe states protect people and critical service; and what human review, contest, monitoring, incident response, and retirement triggers apply [S301][S302][S303][S304][S305][S306][S307][S308][S309][S310].

Rev0251 adds a cross-border supply-chain / market-access rule: a high-volume or high-criticality packet should also say what traded materials, components, spares, routes, supplier obligations, customs classifications, standards, conformity-assessment paths, certificates, product-passport fields, market-surveillance duties, circular-supply routes, and mutual-recognition or equivalence paths it depends on [S244][S311][S312][S313][S314][S315][S316][S317][S318][S319].

Rev0252 adds a circularity / end-of-life rule: a high-volume, high-criticality, public-funded, or hazardous clean-asset packet should also say how the asset will be maintained, repaired, supported, reused, taken back, decommissioned, recycled, and safely retired, and what proof closes the asset at end of life [S320][S321][S322][S324][S325][S326][S327][S328].

Rev0257 adds a water-continuity and water-system shock-absorber rule: a packet that touches adaptation, recovery, mobility, food, health, schools, shelters, utilities, cities, large loads, agriculture, or affordability should also say how safe water, sanitation, hygiene, water quality, groundwater, drought allocation, and priority-use rules remain safe and governed through climate stress [S371][S372][S373][S374][S375][S379][S380][S381].

Rev0258 adds a climate-health continuity and disease-ecology shock-absorber rule: a packet that touches adaptation, recovery, mobility, food, water, WASH, health, schools, shelters, housing, utilities, cities, labour, land use, agriculture, or climate finance should also say how surveillance, early warning, care floors, health-worker protection, medicines, vaccines, vector control, waterborne-outbreak prevention, One Health interfaces, and after-action correction remain governed through climate stress [S382][S383][S384][S386][S387][S388][S389][S390][S391][S392][S393].

## Fast rule

**Every serious climate-work item should be written as an owner-led delivery packet: change _what physical state_, at _which control point_, by _which owner with what authority and budget_, on _which clock_, through _which delivery path_, measured by _which physical metric_, with _which equity guardrail_, _which affordability firebreak_, _which balance-sheet view_, _which claims-integrity packet_, _which anti-capture value-channel controls_, _which data-spine / proof-passport record_, _which cyber-resilience and privacy controls_, _which AI / model-accountability controls if automation is used_, _which cross-border dependency and market-access interoperability records_, _which circularity and end-of-life record_, _which food-security / nutrition and food-system shock-absorber record when relevant_, _which water-continuity / WASH and water-system shock-absorber record when relevant_, _which fallback_, and _which dirty-default retirement, avoided-exposure, or residual-repair rule_** [S65][S132][S189][S197][S198][S200][S201][S202][S203][S207][S228][S242][S243][S269][S270][S271][S272][S273][S274].

## Why this note is not redundant

`242` says the climate answer should become a governed work queue.
This note says how to tell whether an item in that queue is formed enough to be acted on.

The distinction matters because climate programmes often contain many entries that look like work but cannot yet be delivered:
- targets with no control point
- funds with no bankable project-preparation path
- standards with no inspection capacity
- clean-build promises with no paired retirement rule
- adaptation plans with no protected population, asset, or recovery metric
- methane pledges with no leak-response clock
- pilots with no replication test
- procurement rules with no product definition or market-formation path
- removals claims with no durability, storage, or liability rule

House rule: **if no one can say who must do what differently by when, with what proof and what correction if it fails, the item is not yet implementation.**

## The minimum implementation ledger

| Field | What must be named | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Physical outcome | The state that changes in the real world | prevents paper targets from masquerading as delivery |
| Control point | The decision, asset, replacement moment, permit, procurement, tariff, inspection, or recovery file where change occurs | meets the system where it can actually move |
| Owner | The institution or role accountable for delivery | prevents everyone-responsible nobody-responsible failure |
| Authority | The legal, regulatory, contractual, budgetary, or operating power used | prevents wishlist packets |
| Money | Capital, operating budget, subsidy, guarantee, tariff treatment, concessional layer, or payment-risk design | prevents unfunded mandates and stranded plans |
| Balance-sheet view | Who carries risk and cost before, during, and after the packet: household, landlord, insurer, lender, utility, municipality, treasury, firm, or public authority | prevents hidden liabilities, disorderly repricing, and silent loss transfer |
| Claims-integrity view | What climate claims the packet makes, with boundary, baseline, metric, verification, uncertainty, decision use, and correction consequence | prevents greenwashing and claims outrunning proof |
| Integrity-throughput view | How money, queue priority, permits, guarantees, procurement preference, rate recovery, or public backstops move through transparent, contestable, auditable channels | prevents capture, fraud, inflated costs, weak delivery, and rent extraction |
| Data-spine view | Persistent identifier, linked systems, data steward, update cadence, access rule, privacy / security protection, and correction trigger | prevents finance, permits, procurement, claims, risk ledgers, asset records, and remedies from fragmenting into incompatible truths |
| Cross-border dependency view | Materials, components, spares, suppliers, source regions, routes, lead times, concentration risk, export-control or tariff exposure, circular-supply route, critical-spares rule, and delay fallback | prevents a packet from assuming traded inputs will appear on time under stress |
| Market-access interoperability view | HS / customs classification, applicable standards, conformity-assessment path, testing / certification capacity, accepted evidence format, mutual-recognition or equivalence route, market-surveillance owner, and update clock | prevents clean products and proof from stalling at borders, labs, procurement desks, or installation sites |
| Food-security / nutrition view | exposed groups, food access, nutrition floor, production and market shocks, public-service dependencies, protection instrument, anticipatory trigger, owner, finance, and recovery proof | prevents climate packets from becoming hunger, malnutrition, asset depletion, or forced movement |
| Food-system shock-absorber view | prices, reserves, stocks, import / export exposure, fertilizer and input exposure, routes, logistics, cash / voucher / in-kind switching rule, local procurement, anti-hoarding guardrails, and after-action correction | prevents food-price, trade, input, and logistics shocks from cascading before governance can act |
| Water-continuity / WASH view | exposed users, minimum WASH floor, source / system risk, quality / sanitation risk, service dependencies, continuity instrument, priority-use rule, trigger, owner, finance, and recovery proof | prevents climate packets from becoming unsafe water, sanitation collapse, disease, indignity, or forced movement |
| Water-system shock-absorber view | basin / aquifer account, groundwater, surface / storage, quality, priority uses, drought / flood / salinity triggers, demand / loss controls, allocation switching, equity, and after-action rule changes | prevents drought, groundwater, allocation, and water-quality shocks from cascading before governance can act |

| Climate-health continuity view | exposed groups, health hazards, surveillance signals, care floors, facility dependencies, public-health instruments, triggers, owners, equity, low-carbon continuity, and recovery proof | prevents climate packets from becoming unmanaged heat illness, care interruption, medicine failure, or preventable death |
| Disease-ecology shock-absorber view | pathogen / vector / reservoir pathways, climate and environmental drivers, WASH, surveillance stack, trigger ladder, prevention, supplies, One Health owners, equity, and after-action correction | prevents dengue, malaria, cholera, arbovirus, zoonotic, and waterborne risks from becoming avoidable outbreak cascades |
| Proof-passport view | Product, building, project, site, meter, claim, or service record that carries life-cycle proof through procurement, commissioning, O&M, handoff, reuse, recycling, warranty, and remedy | prevents climate attributes from becoming one-time labels that disappear after award or installation |
| Affordability firebreak | Bill, premium, rent, rate, tax, fare, fee, deductible, debt, or time-burden guardrail for essential users | prevents clean or protective work from losing consent through burden pile-ups |
| Capacity | Workforce, procurement, permitting, grid, supply-chain, maintenance, casework, or data capacity needed | makes bottlenecks visible before they become excuses |
| Crew plan | Occupations, certifications, training seats, inspectors, operators, maintainers, and public-office capacity | prevents imaginary labour and understaffed offices from becoming hidden vetoes |
| Completion gate | Commissioning, O&M, warranty, spare parts, user support, and aftercare standard | prevents first installation from masquerading as stable service |
| Users and communities | Who must accept, use, host, pay for, or be protected by the change | prevents technically right but socially brittle delivery |
| Clock | Emergency, stock-turnover, infrastructure, learning, or retreat timing | keeps slow-clock work from delaying fast-clock work |
| Metric | The physical scoreline that proves progress | makes the packet auditable |
| Correction trigger | The weak signal that forces redesign, escalation, demotion, or stop | turns truth infrastructure into control, not ceremony |
| Dirty-default / exposure / residual link | What gets retired, prevented, protected, or reserved for hard residuals | ensures the packet changes the climate ledger rather than adding activity |

House rule: **a packet can be small, but it cannot be vague.**

## The compact canon

### 1. Start with the physical state change
A packet should begin with a verb that changes the world:
- retire a coal unit
- connect a feeder
- upgrade a substation
- install and verify clean heat
- reduce peak load
- repair a methane leak
- enforce a cooling standard
- protect a care facility from heat and outages
- prevent rebuilding in a repeat-loss location
- lower the cost of capital for a specific clean-development class
- define and procure low-emissions material
- durably store a measured removal tonne

House rule: **do not let “support,” “promote,” “encourage,” or “explore” be the main verb unless the packet also names the physical state that changes.**

### 2. Put the control point in the packet
Climate work becomes real at control points: asset replacement, interconnection, permitting, tariff setting, procurement, building renovation, vehicle purchase, disaster recovery, lease change, land-use approval, fossil reinvestment, field inspection, project finance, and public-benefit enrollment [S200][S201][S213][S222][S223][S224][S225].

A packet with no control point usually becomes awareness, strategy, or exhortation.
A packet with a control point can become default design.

House rule: **meet the file while it is open.**

### 3. Pair clean build with dirty-default loss
A clean packet should say what dirty default loses dispatch, renewal, subsidy, permit, market share, replacement opportunity, or political protection when the cleaner service is ready [S198][S199][S207][S238][S242][S247].

Examples:
- a grid-upgrade packet paired with fossil-peaker retirement or non-reinvestment rules
- a clean-heat packet paired with end-of-life fossil-equipment replacement restrictions
- a clean-bus procurement packet paired with diesel-bus retirement schedules
- a low-carbon-material procurement packet paired with high-carbon-material disclosure and phase-down rules

House rule: **addition without displacement is not yet decarbonization proof.**

### 4. Put protection packets on the same ledger as mitigation packets
Adaptation work also needs owner-led packets.
A heat plan is not formed until it names cooling locations, outreach channels, transport, staffing, backup power, care-facility protocols, tenant protections, and mortality / morbidity / outage metrics.
A flood or fire plan is not formed until it names land-use rules, hardening, evacuation, recovery finance, repeat-loss rules, and where retreat becomes more honest than defence [S12][S15][S40][S41][S230][S237][S243].

House rule: **protection is not a paragraph in the mitigation plan; it is a delivery queue with accountable packets.**

### 5. Make bottlenecks first-class fields
The packet should name the constraint most likely to stop it:
- interconnection queue
- transformer, conductor, cable, or switchgear supply
- permitting staff
- trained installers
- crew availability and certification
- contractor quality
- public procurement skill
- commissioning and inspection capacity
- operations, maintenance, warranty, and aftercare capacity
- social license
- project-preparation capacity
- local-currency finance
- tariff solvency
- affordability firebreak design
- balance-sheet and contingent-liability visibility
- data access
- inspection and enforcement capacity
- large-load competition for scarce grid headroom
- water, heat, land, or local resilience stress

A packet that ignores its bottleneck is usually a target wearing implementation clothing [S132][S176][S197][S198][S226][S227][S228][S231][S241].

House rule: **write the bottleneck into the packet before the bottleneck writes the schedule.** If the bottleneck is shared with other packets, write the allocation claim too: why should this packet receive scarce capacity before the next one? Use `245` and `246` for the allocation and large-load tests [S252][S253].

### 6. Assign authority and fallback together
The owner must have authority or a route to authority.
Fallback should also be named before failure:
- if a grid queue slips, which triage rule fires?
- if methane repairs are late, which enforcement step fires?
- if retrofit quality fails, who pays for remedy?
- if a community rejects a siting proposal, what redesign or alternative route exists?
- if finance does not reach close, which risk layer changes?
- if retreat support is inadequate, which housing or fiscal backstop activates?
- if bills, premiums, rents, rates, or deductibles exceed the no-worse floor, which affordability firebreak activates?
- if risk shifts to households, tenants, municipalities, utilities, insurers, lenders, or public budgets, which balance-sheet correction fires?

House rule: **a packet without fallback is a wish that has not met reality.**

### 7. Separate proof from publicity
Packet proof should sit in the physical ledger:
- emissions reduced, not just credits purchased
- leaks repaired, not just surveys completed
- capacity connected, not just permitted
- peak stress reduced, not just devices enrolled
- homes warmer or cooler at lower burden, not just rebates issued
- households protected or relocated, not just risk mapped
- dirty assets retired, not just clean assets announced
- removals durably stored, not just contracted
- hidden liabilities reduced, not just disclosed
- essential-user burden controlled, not just averaged across all users [S65][S113][S133][S138][S189][S207][S242][S269][S270][S271][S272][S273][S274]

House rule: **publicity may explain a packet; it must not substitute for its proof.**

### 8. Keep the user path inside the packet
Ordinary people should not have to assemble climate delivery from fragmented paperwork.
Where the packet touches households, tenants, small firms, farmers, local governments, or exposed communities, it should state the offer path: standing channel, eligibility, one informed yes, low upfront burden, contractor or caseworker support, performance remedy, and continuity across handoffs [S213][S214][S215][S216][S217][S218][S219][S220][S221][S222][S223][S224][S225].

House rule: **do not call a packet deliverable if the weaker party still has to carry search, proof, cash, coordination, and performance risk alone.**

### 9. Give learning packets a replication test
Pilots can be useful, but only if they state what would make them travel.
A learning packet should specify:
- the archetype being tested
- the control point it uses
- the measured outcome
- the cost and staffing model
- the failure conditions
- the replication template
- the rule for promotion, redesign, or retirement [S200][S201][S202][S203]

House rule: **a pilot with no replication test is often delay with a ribbon.**

### 10. Link every packet to the overshoot discipline
Because overshoot risk is now live, every packet should state which overshoot duty it serves:
- lower peak warming
- shorten overshoot duration
- reduce cumulative emissions
- reduce short-latency forcing
- prevent new exposure
- protect people during heat and shocks
- reserve removals and scarce tools for true residuals
- preserve return-to-safer-conditions capacity [S243][S249][S250][S251]

House rule: **a packet that cannot say how it lowers danger, exposure, waste, or future cleanup burden probably belongs lower in the queue.**

## Examples of formed packets

### Grid packet
Upgrade a named feeder and substation cluster by a named date, using an identified budget and procurement route, to connect a specified volume of distributed solar, storage, heat pumps, chargers, or industrial load; measure connection time, curtailment, peak stress, reliability, and avoided fossil fallback; fire a triage rule if procurement or queue targets slip [S226][S231][S235].

### Methane packet
Detect, verify, and repair super-emitting oil, gas, waste, or coal sources within a specified response clock; name the regulator, operator, data source, penalty ladder, and public ledger; measure confirmed repair, recurrence, and emissions reduction rather than inspections alone [S232][S248].

### Retrofit and cooling packet
Deliver a one-stop clean-heat, insulation, passive-cooling, efficient-cooling, and bill-protection offer through utilities, housing agencies, landlords, or benefit systems; measure comfort, peak load, bills, equipment performance, and callback remedy rather than rebate uptake alone [S214][S217][S219][S224][S234][S239].

### Coal or fossil-reinvestment packet
Publish asset-by-asset retirement, non-reinvestment, remediation, worker, community, reliability, and replacement-service terms; measure gross-emissions decline, reliability, local fiscal support, and whether clean service actually displaces fossil operation [S24][S25][S238][S247].

### Protection and retreat packet
For a repeat-loss neighbourhood, watershed, or fire-prone interface, specify the protection standard, land-use rule, insurance or recovery rule, social-protection pathway, voluntary buyout or relocation terms, replacement housing, and community decision process; measure reduced exposure and dignified continuity, not only money spent [S230][S237][S243].

### EMDE finance packet
For a repeatable project class such as solar-plus-storage, grids, clean cooking, buses, cooling, or industrial efficiency, specify project-preparation support, guarantee layer, local-currency channel, tariff or payment-risk treatment, affordability guardrail, and standard contract; measure financial close, weighted average cost of capital, service delivered, and replication rate [S91][S92][S93][S95][S96][S227].

### Large-load entry packet
Approve, delay, redesign, or condition a data centre, AI campus, electrolyser, industrial plant, mine, port, depot, or cooling district using a public load-entry test: purpose, efficiency, location, additional clean supply, flexibility, fair grid-cost contribution, water and heat disclosure, fossil-backup limits, transparency, and correction triggers; see `245` and `246` [S252][S253].

### Hard-residual packet
For cement, steel, chemicals, shipping, aviation, or durable removals, specify why simpler reduction is insufficient, what scarce tool is allocated, the lifecycle standard, storage or fuel integrity, procurement route, and no-leakage rule; measure true residual treatment rather than premium-tool volume alone [S14][S42][S43][S44][S45][S46][S165][S166][S167].

### Critical-service continuity packet
For a hospital, clinic, school, shelter, water system, public-housing cluster, telecom node, lifeline transit route, food / medicine cold chain, or grid-control function, specify the minimum service floor during heat, outage, flood, fire, smoke, drought, cyber, fuel, staff, or supply-chain stress; name the passive, clean, flexible, storage, islanding, grid-hardening, O&M, staffing, drill, access, and fallback measures; measure service continuity and emissions together; see `251` and `252` [S40][S261][S262][S263].

### Recovery-discipline packet
For a post-disaster recovery programme, specify the trigger, authority, immediate service floor, pre-arranged finance, temporary shelter and income support, exposure diagnosis, reconstruction path, clean-default rule, residual-loss ledger, equity guardrail, and correction trigger; measure stable safer service, not only money obligated, debris cleared, or like-for-like repair; see `253` [S264][S266][S267].

### Protection-gap and retreat-governance packet
For a jurisdiction, neighbourhood, watershed, coast, fire-prone interface, housing market, or public-insurance pool under rising losses, specify the insured / underinsured / uninsured ledger, affordability and availability stress, public contingent liability, risk-reduction path, backstop conditions, repeat-loss rule, and voluntary buyout / relocation / receiving-area package; measure whether losses are reduced and people are protected before private exit becomes de facto retreat; see `254` [S264][S265][S268].

### Climate balance-sheet packet
For a housing market, utility plan, public-insurance pool, municipal budget, recovery programme, grid investment, credit portfolio, or critical-service network, specify the hazard or transition pressure, current risk holder, post-policy risk holder, avoided-loss pathway, public contingent liability, disclosure / stress-test route, risk-reduction condition, and correction trigger; measure whether risk is reduced or fairly transferred rather than merely hidden until a household, lender, insurer, utility, or treasury absorbs it; see `255` [S269][S270][S271][S272][S274].

### Affordability-firebreak packet
For a tariff, retrofit offer, electrification programme, grid investment, resilience assessment, insurance reform, public backstop, recovery plan, or clean-service contract, specify the cost path across bills, premiums, rents, rates, taxes, fares, fees, deductibles, debt, and time; name affected users, targeted support, automatic or low-friction enrolment, no-worse service floor, hardship trigger, and correction owner; measure burden, service quality, and avoided cost together; see `256` [S69][S199][S200][S273].

### Claims-integrity packet
For a net-zero, transition-plan, carbon-credit, green-product, clean-power, green-finance, low-carbon-material, procurement, or resilience claim, specify the exact claim sentence, claim type, owner, boundary, baseline, time period, metric, data source, verification route, credit / certificate / removal role if any, decision use, public ledger, correction trigger, and consequence; measure whether the claim matches the physical outcome it is used to sell, finance, procure, permit, or reassure; see `257` [S275][S276][S280][S281].

### Integrity-throughput packet
For a procurement platform, grant programme, guarantee, grid-capacity allocation, public backstop, recovery fund, rate-recovery pathway, or permit/land decision, specify the value at stake, eligibility rule, procurement or allocation route, beneficial-ownership and conflict checks, price benchmark, competition test, milestone proof, audit plan, grievance route, clawback, and correction owner; measure whether public value moved fast without capture, inflated cost, or poor service; see `258` [S282][S283][S284].

### Enforcement-remedy packet

### Rev0252 bridge: completion now includes the exit route
A delivery packet should not be considered formed if it can install a clean asset but cannot say how the asset is maintained, repaired, handed off, reused, recycled, or safely retired. ## Packet states

A serious work queue should classify packets by state:

| State | Meaning | Required next action |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | physical outcome named, but owner / authority / money / metric incomplete | complete the packet or demote it |
| Admitted | minimum fields complete and priority justified | schedule, fund, and assign |
| Live | delivery has started and metrics are reporting | monitor bottlenecks and weak signals |
| Blocked | packet has hit a named constraint | fire fallback, redesign, or escalate |
| Corrected | packet was changed because evidence showed underperformance | verify whether correction worked |
| Replicable | packet achieved proof and has a transferable archetype | standardize and copy |
| Retired | packet is obsolete, inferior, captured, or completed | remove it so the queue stays honest |

House rule: **the queue should age packets; stale packets should not be allowed to impersonate active work forever.**

## Rev0253 addendum — place, rights, benefits, and grievance repair

Rev0253 adds a missing control to the delivery stack: **place is climate infrastructure**.
A packet can be financed, permitted, procured, staffed, commissioned, cyber-secured, standards-compliant, supply-chain mapped, circular, and recyclable, yet still fail if its land, water, seabed, corridor, biodiversity, cultural, cumulative-impact, host-community, Indigenous-rights, benefit-sharing, and grievance conditions are unformed.

The new minimum is two ledgers for footprint-bearing or place-intensive packets:

1. a **place-capacity ledger** covering alternatives, low-conflict siting, no-go screens, biodiversity and water filters, cumulative impacts, grid / access dependencies, climate exposure, restoration, decommissioning, and correction triggers; and
2. a **host-community and rights ledger** covering affected people, Indigenous Peoples and FPIC where applicable, independent support, enforceable benefits, grievance routes, remedy triggers, and promise durability across ownership changes.

The practical rule is not “slow everything down for process.”
It is “move siting, rights, benefits, and grievance design upstream so fast clean deployment is legitimate enough to survive.”
See `269` and `270` [S60][S64][S329][S330][S331][S332][S333][S334][S335][S337][S338][S339][S340].

## Rev0254 addendum — add closure and reuse ledgers to delivery packets

A delivery packet that retires, buys out, replaces, repurposes, subsidizes, refinances, or transfers a fossil asset now needs two additional fields when relevant:

1. **Fossil-liability ledger** — what physical closure, methane control, cleanup finance, owner responsibility, anti-orphaning rule, worker/community term, monitoring, and remedy route travel with the dirty asset.
2. **Remediation-and-reuse passport** — what contamination, residual risk, future-use standard, cleanup duty, monitoring obligation, benefit-sharing rule, and long-term liability travel with a legacy site before it becomes a clean project, restoration site, industrial asset, public service, or housing asset.

This prevents a false packet from looking complete merely because the replacement asset is buildable. A replacement packet is not complete if the dirty asset it displaces becomes a hidden public liability [S341][S342][S344][S347][S348][S349].

## Rev0255 addendum — mobility packets need destination and continuity fields
