# 224 — Ideal Solutions: The Clean Transition Should Usually Arrive as a Drafted Offer, Not a Blank Page of Self-Assembly

## Claim

The archive already says the clean path should be easy (`213`), arrive as a coherent offer rather than a scavenger hunt (`214`), show up through standing channels people already use (`222`), meet the real decision while it is already open (`223`), ask most users to choose a clean result rather than manage a stack (`221`), and keep search, proof, cash, coordination, and risk off the weaker party where honesty allows (`215`).
One more compact front-door rule belongs in the canon:

**when the clean path reaches an ordinary actor, it should usually arrive already drafted around the known situation — with reused records, likely-fit routing, standard terms, and bounded choices — not as a blank page that asks the user to assemble the climate pathway from scratch.**

In plain terms:
- if the programme already knows the building type, meter class, tariff, ownership status, likely eligibility band, replacement trigger, or service relationship, it should use that knowledge to draft a serious path rather than pretending nothing is known [S69][S71][S72][S77][S80][S118][S119][S137][S143]
- the ordinary user should usually be choosing among a small number of legible clean packages or confirming a drafted route, not constructing the route line by line across multiple forms, quotes, proof demands, and agencies [S53][S69][S71][S77][S80][S118][S119][S137][S141][S143]
- and where the system cannot draft with confidence, it should move to assisted drafting rather than fall all the way back to blank-page self-start [S69][S77][S80][S118][S119][S137][S143]

So the shortest rule is:

**send a drafted clean path, not a blank task.**

## The compact canon

### 1. Blank pages are hidden burden-shifting
A blank application, blank marketplace search, or blank project brief looks neutral, but it is usually just burden-shifting in disguise.
It asks the weaker actor to supply initiative, information, coordination, and sequence design before the programme has delivered anything tangible [S69][S72][S77][S79][S80][S118][S119][S137][S143].

House rule: **treat blank-page intake as a cost, not as administrative innocence.**

### 2. The system should draft from what it already knows
If a utility, landlord, municipality, recovery office, payroll system, public buyer, or programme administrator already knows enough to narrow the field materially, it should usually do so.
That means reusing verified records, pre-filling stable facts, narrowing to likely-fit routes, and presenting a serious next step instead of reopening the whole question from zero [S69][S71][S72][S77][S118][S119][S137][S143].

House rule: **reuse known facts to draft the path before asking the user for more work.**

### 3. Leave bounded choices, not total self-assembly
A drafted offer is not the same as a single compulsory script.
Users may still need to choose between package sizes, finance forms, appointment windows, performance tiers, landlord or tenant variants, or resilience add-ons.
But the choice set should usually be bounded and legible, not an invitation to become the project architect, procurement lead, and compliance officer all at once [S53][S69][S71][S80][S81][S118][S119][S141][S143].

House rule: **keep real choices where they matter, but draft the sequence around them.**

### 4. Draft the joined packet, not just one programme fragment
What should arrive drafted is usually not a lone rebate or a single technical measure.
It is the joined minimum packet that can actually move the clean state: likely eligibility, indicative payment path, standard offer form, delivery route, accountable lead, and the next one or two concrete actions [S69][S70][S71][S72][S77][S80][S81][S118][S119][S143][S144].

House rule: **draft a path that can move, not a teaser that sends the user back into assembly.**

### 5. Drafted does not mean unchecked or unappealable
A stronger drafted path still needs correction channels.
The user must be able to fix wrong assumptions, disclose material differences, reject an unsuitable package, or escalate when the standard route does not fit the case.
The point is not to eliminate agency; it is to eliminate avoidable blank-page labor [S71][S72][S77][S80][S81][S118][S119][S143].

House rule: **default to drafted routing, but keep appeal, correction, and bounded override real.**

### 6. When confidence is weak, move to assisted drafting rather than full reset
Some cases will be too uncertain, novel, or fragmented for a strong draft from existing records alone.
Then the archive should still prefer assisted drafting — navigator help, pre-screened options, guided intake, short follow-up forms, or local intermediaries — over dumping the actor into self-directed discovery from zero [S69][S77][S80][S118][S119][S137][S143].

House rule: **degrade from drafted to assisted, not from drafted to blank.**

**Ideal climate solutions should usually reach ordinary actors as a drafted clean offer — using existing records, likely-fit routing, standard terms, and bounded choices — not as a blank page of self-assembly. The stronger pathway is the one that does most of the architecture upstream before the weaker party ever has to decide.**
