# Sources Register

This archive cites a deliberately small source base. It prefers high-authority synthesis and trend documents over large literature dumps.

## Core sources

**[S1] IPCC, _Climate Change 2023: AR6 Synthesis Report_ (2023).**  
Scope: best-available synthesis of physical science, impacts, mitigation, and adaptation.  
Use in archive: baseline scientific constraints; required pace of reductions; role of CDR.  
Link: https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/syr/  
Key lines used: 1.5°C-consistent pathways require very rapid near-term cuts; deep mitigation across all sectors is still technically possible.
