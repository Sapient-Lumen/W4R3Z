# CHANGELOG — rev0070

## Summary

This revision adds one compact note on **verification form**.

The archive already had recent compact rules for delivery form, risk-bearing, upside allocation, payment form, contract form, and eligibility form.
What it still lacked was the smallest rule for how ordinary climate claims should usually be proved once a programme is running.

The new note says ideal climate solutions should use the **lightest proof form that is still credible**:
- direct data where available,
- standard methods for routine measures,
- sampled audit where sensible,
- and tighter bespoke MRV reserved for large, novel, high-value, or highly gameable claims.

That keeps the archive aligned with its broader doctrine:
- physical reality over paper claims,
- low friction over preventable administrative drag,
- integrity where it matters most,
- and scale through repeatable ordinary delivery rather than consultant-heavy exception handling.
