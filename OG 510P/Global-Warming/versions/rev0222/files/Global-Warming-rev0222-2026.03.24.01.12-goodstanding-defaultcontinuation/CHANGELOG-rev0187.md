## Why this change

The archive already had the deeper doctrine in `32`, the reference and physical-package notes in `57` and `130`, the joined delivery note in `132`, the sequencing map in `140`, and the dependency-light note in `172`.
What it still lacked was one compact front-door canon for a recurring first-stop question:

**why must ideal climate solutions come as smallest sufficient bundles of complements rather than isolated “best moves”?**

That gap mattered because the archive could already say what to build, how to score it, how to keep claims honest, and how to shape institutional form — but not yet in one shortest joined note how to tell whether a proposal is a real package or just a respectable orphan.

## Result

The archive now has a compact front-door canon that compresses the package rule into one first-stop answer:
bundle the enabling conditions that make the cleaner path real, release the bottlenecks that would otherwise trap it on paper, and sequence enabling conditions before obligations that depend on them.
