## The compact canon

### 1. No paper-substitution boundary
A serious climate programme should not count narrated effort, accounting cleverness, or institutional motion as a substitute for physical outcomes.
Claims should cash out in the atmosphere, at the smokestack, at the meter, in the building, on the grid, in the refinery, on the farm, in the river basin, or in the exposure profile of real places.
If paper progress is outrunning physical progress, the programme is not ahead; it is drifting [S58][S59][S65][S66][S67][S68][S133][S138].

House rule: **if the gain is not physically legible, treat it as unproven rather than banked.**

### 2. No overshoot-as-plan boundary
A serious climate programme should not normalize delay by pretending later removals, later protection, or later institutional perfection will cheaply repair avoidable overshoot.
The default should remain: cut early, reduce the denominator, release bottlenecks, and avoid lock-in that turns a temporary under-response into a durable repair burden [S28][S59][S97][S100][S101][S103][S104][S140].

House rule: **overshoot is damage and future repair burden, not a harmless waiting room.**

### 3. No scarce-clean-capacity-for-dirty-preservation boundary
A serious climate programme should not spend scarce clean electricity, clean fuels, fiscal room, administrative attention, or political capital mainly to preserve dirty defaults in easy uses.
Direct electrification, efficiency, demand shaping, and simpler physical substitutions should usually outrank complex bridges whose main political value is incumbent comfort [S28][S30][S32][S34][S59][S97][S99][S130][S132][S139].

House rule: **do not waste scarce clean capacity to make the dirty incumbent feel modern.**

### 4. No protection-or-legitimacy-sacrifice boundary
A serious climate programme should not buy speed by silently offloading cost, risk, proof burden, heat exposure, insurance collapse, or displacement onto households, workers, tenants, poorer places, or weaker states.
If the social contract breaks, delivery eventually breaks too. Protection, affordability, and visible benefit-sharing are not side issues; they are continuity infrastructure for the whole programme [S40][S41][S69][S70][S71][S81][S90][S131][S134][S137].

House rule: **a programme that works only by stacking downside on the weak is not durable enough to count as ideal.**

### 5. No off-normal-path-without-proof-gates boundary
A serious climate programme should not let dirty fallback, weak exception, or delay-by-convenience inherit the status of realism.
Any off-normal path should have to prove necessity, lack of a cleaner near-term alternative, strict containment, explicit exit, and visible owner-plus-liability; otherwise it should usually be denied, narrowed, or redesigned [S84][S85][S86][S87][S103][S104][S108][S109][S110][S123].

House rule: **detours are not self-justifying merely because they are easier to ask for under stress.**

### 6. No archive-growth-without-sharper-judgment boundary
A serious archive should not grow merely because another true thing can be said.
New notes should normally earn admission by adding a net-new screen, boundary, state rule, routing gain, or compression gain; otherwise the better move is usually merge, cross-link, tighten, or refuse [S88][S89][S90][S91][S92][S94][S95][S127].

House rule: **the archive is improving when judgment per page rises, not merely when file count rises.**

## Preferred use

Open this note first when the live question is any of the following:
- what the mature programme must refuse rather than optimize
- what does **not** count as success even if it looks active or politically convenient
- what should be quarantined, proof-gated, or demoted before it distorts the whole system
- how the archive’s negative canon, proof gates, and truth discipline fit together in one stop
- where the whole-system no-go zones are before choosing leverage points or sequencing

Then reopen the deeper file that matches the surviving boundary question.

## Compression rule

**The mature archive should now be able to say not only what exists, how it works, what to watch, where to push, and in what order to push, but also what must be refused or proof-gated. If paper progress outruns physical outcomes, delay is sold as realism, scarce clean capacity is spent to preserve dirty defaults, social protection is traded away for apparent speed, detours escape proof gates, or archive growth adds volume without sharper judgment, the system is crossing a boundary rather than advancing.**
