# 216 — Ideal Solutions: The Clean Transition Should Work as One Accountable Path, Not a Fragmented Sequence

## Claim

The archive already says that a serious climate programme should make the clean path easier at the point of decision, arrive as a coherent offer rather than a scavenger hunt, and move search, proof, cash, coordination, and risk upstream instead of stacking them on the weaker party [S53][S69][S70][S72][S74][S79][S80][S81][S108][S118][S119][S137][S141][S143].

One more front-door bridge belongs beside those notes:

**the clean transition should usually work as one accountable path from first contact to persistent clean state, not as a chain of individually reasonable fragments that fail when stitched together.**

In plain terms, the user or place should not face a good interface, then a bad proof step; or a good subsidy, then a bad contract; or a good installation, then no tuning, no remedy, and no durable settlement into the cleaner state.
If the sequence breaks at any routine handoff, the programme is still weaker than it looks.

So the archive’s shortest joined-path rule is:

**one front door, light proof, tolerable payment, standard offer, prequalified delivery, one accountable lead, fast recourse, and active aftercare until the cleaner state holds.** [S53][S69][S70][S72][S74][S76][S77][S78][S79][S80][S81][S82][S108][S118][S119][S120][S121][S137][S141][S143][S144]

## Why this belongs in the front door

### 1. A coherent offer still needs a coherent sequence

`214` says the transition should arrive as a coherent offer.
That is necessary, but not quite enough.
A pathway can still look joined in advertising while breaking apart in actual use at enrolment, proof, payment, delivery, remedy, or retention.
So the front door also needs the shortest rule for the whole chain:

**judge the pathway as one sequence, not as a pile of locally defensible steps.** [S53][S69][S70][S72][S74][S79][S80][S81][S118][S119][S120][S121][S137][S141][S143][S144]

### 2. Burden shape is really about the whole journey

`215` says serious programmes should move burden and risk upstream.
This note makes that visible across time rather than at one moment only.
The weaker actor is still carrying too much if they must repeatedly re-prove eligibility, chase contractors, float interim costs, debug failures alone, or preserve gains without aftercare.

So the archive needs the shortest joined-path rule for burden over time:

**do not remove burden at the front door only to reintroduce it at the next handoff.** [S69][S70][S72][S74][S79][S80][S81][S118][S119][S120][S121][S141][S143][S144]

### 3. The clean state is not real until it becomes ordinary routine

A programme is not done when the asset is installed or the contract is signed.
It is done when the cleaner state actually holds in routine life: the bill is understandable, the service performs, faults get corrected, and the user does not quietly fall back to the dirtier default.
That is why active commissioning, early follow-up, and fast remedy belong inside the programme shape rather than outside it [S16][S17][S53][S58][S74][S80][S81][S82][S119][S120][S121][S143][S144].

## What this rule promotes

Promote:
- one front door and service-first language rather than programme-maze entry [S53][S72][S76][S108][S118][S119][S143]
- light proof and existing-data reuse where honesty allows rather than repeated evidence burdens [S72][S118][S119][S143]
- low-upfront or pay-over-time payment shapes rather than reimbursement-first uptake [S69][S70][S118][S143]
- standard offers, prequalified delivery, and one accountable lead rather than user-side systems integration [S71][S80][S81][S118][S119][S140][S141][S143][S144]
- fast correction, active aftercare, and first-season tuning so the cleaner state becomes durable routine rather than fragile installation [S74][S80][S81][S82][S119][S120][S121][S143][S144]

## What this rule demotes

Demote:
- programmes that optimise publicity, subsidy level, or point-of-sale counts while leaving hidden vetoes in the middle of the pathway [S53][S69][S70][S72][S74][S79][S118][S119][S143]
- designs that remove friction at signup but dump coordination and performance burden back on the user later [S74][S79][S80][S81][S120][S121][S143][S144]
- climate offers that treat installation or purchase as the finish line rather than the start of performance retention [S58][S74][S82][S120][S121][S123][S143][S144]
- fragmented public offers that require the weaker actor to keep reassembling the pathway after every handoff [S79][S80][S81][S118][S119][S140][S141][S143]

## One-paragraph compression

**Ideal climate solutions should work as one accountable path from first contact to persistent clean state. The pathway should be easy to enter, light on proof, tolerable to pay for, clear in contract form, prequalified in delivery, owned by one accountable lead, quick to correct when something goes wrong, and actively supported until the cleaner state holds in routine life. Do not mistake a pile of good fragments for a good pathway; the pathway is only as strong as its weakest routine handoff.** [S53][S69][S70][S72][S74][S76][S77][S78][S79][S80][S81][S82][S108][S118][S119][S120][S121][S137][S141][S143][S144]
