# 06 — Quarantined Note on Solar Radiation Modification (SRM)

SRM is **not** part of the archive’s ideal core solution to global warming.

## Why it is quarantined

1. It does not solve the root problem of greenhouse-gas accumulation.
2. It leaves major governance, justice, termination, and geopolitical-risk questions unresolved.
3. It can become a political excuse for mitigation delay.
4. The archive already has a deployable core stack that is ethically cleaner and institutionally healthier.

## Provisional doctrine

- keep SRM research governance separate from mitigation policy
- do not treat SRM as a substitute for emissions cuts, methane cuts, adaptation, or land protection
- default against deployment
- require high transparency, international oversight, and strong conflict-of-interest safeguards for any research program

## Archive stance

The archive is allowed to monitor SRM as a risk-governance issue.  
It is **not** allowed to treat SRM as the answer to global warming.
