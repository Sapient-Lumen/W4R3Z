# 195 — Ideal Solutions: Seven Sentences / Shortest Honest Answer Compact Canon

## Claim

The archive already has a one-page answer (`58`), a joined programme-shape note (`191`), a hard-conclusions note (`192`), a first-moves note (`193`), and a world-bargain note (`194`).
What it still lacked was the smallest honest front door of all: a note short enough to answer the whole question before the reader is routed through the larger tree.

This is not a new doctrinal branch.
It is a compression note.
Its job is to say, in seven sentences, what the archive presently thinks the ideal solution to global warming actually is [S101][S102][S103][S104][S106][S112][S143][S145].

## Fast rule

**The ideal solution is not a single miracle technology or a single price signal. It is a governed clean-development package that cuts gross emissions fast, minimizes overshoot, protects people directly, and makes the cleaner path cheaper, easier, and more reliable to build and live with.**

## The answer in seven sentences

1. **Cut gross emissions fast, because the world is still off target and delay now raises overshoot, damage, and later dependence on removals.** [S101][S105]
2. **Build the clean backbone first: grids, clean electricity, flexibility, storage, efficiency, and cooling, because these unlock many later gains and are now more bottlenecked by delivery than by basic technical possibility.** [S102][S103][S104][S106]
3. **Electrify the ordinary things that can already run well on electricity — cars, much building heat, much low-temperature industrial heat, and a growing share of freight — instead of saving clean power and scarce premium fuels for uses that do not need them.** [S30][S31][S32][S33][S34][S100]
4. **Run methane, forests, wetlands, peatlands, and agrifood as core lanes rather than side quests, because fast warming cannot be slowed credibly by power-sector change alone.** [S11][S18][S19][S36][S37][S38]
5. **Treat adaptation as part of the solution itself: cooling access, heat-health systems, flood and drought protection, resilient water and care systems, and retreat where repeated defence is no longer honest.** [S12][S15][S16][S17][S40][S41]
6. **Make the burden allocation real: richer countries move earlier and finance diffusion, poorer countries get a buildable clean-development path, fossil producers face managed decline, and scarce premium tools are rationed to true residuals.** [S13][S96][S101][S143][S145]
7. **Judge success by physical outcomes and everyday fit — lower emissions, lower exposure, lower bills, higher reliability, faster delivery, and less lock-in — not by paper claims, spending totals, or elegant future stories.** [S101][S103][S106][S112][S143]

## Compression rule

**Solve global warming by cutting gross emissions fast, building clean electric backbones, electrifying ordinary uses, running methane and ecosystems as core lanes, protecting people directly, sharing burdens honestly across countries and incumbents, and judging progress by physical outcomes rather than paper claims.**
