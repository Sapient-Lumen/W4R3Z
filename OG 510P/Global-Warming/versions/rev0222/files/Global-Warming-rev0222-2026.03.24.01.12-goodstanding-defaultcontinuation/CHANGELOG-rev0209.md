## Why

The archive already had the physical-jobs note (`208`), the grow / shrink / stop note (`209`), the abundance / rationing / retirement note (`210`), and the public-meaning note (`211`).
What it still lacked was the smallest front-door bridge from those ideas to ordinary domestic politics: **what a serious climate program should protect, what it should support, and what it should tighten against first.**

That gap matters because climate talk often oscillates between two weak stories:
- a technology menu with no social bargain
- a sacrifice story with no sorting between necessity, turnover risk, luxury waste, and organized delay

So this revision adds the archive's shortest domestic public-bargain rule: **make clean necessity easier, carry people and places through turnover, and constrain luxury waste and dirty delay earlier and harder.**
