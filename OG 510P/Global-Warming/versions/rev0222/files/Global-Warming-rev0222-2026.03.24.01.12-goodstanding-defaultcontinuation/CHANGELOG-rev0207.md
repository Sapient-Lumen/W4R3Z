## Why

The archive already had the physical-jobs note (`208`), the grow/shrink/stop note (`209`), the working-scorelines note (`207`), and deeper notes on scarcity, hard residuals, and managed decline.
What it still lacked was the smallest front-door resource strategy: **make common clean service abundant, ration scarce premium tools to genuine hard cases, and retire dirty defaults on purpose**.

That is a useful compression because many weak climate plans fail by mixing up three different questions:
- what should become common and easy
- what should remain scarce and carefully allocated
- what should be actively phased out rather than left to fade by hope
