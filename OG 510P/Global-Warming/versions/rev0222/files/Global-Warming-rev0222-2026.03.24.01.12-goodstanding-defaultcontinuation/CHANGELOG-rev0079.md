## Why this revision

The archive already covered delivery, orchestration, verification, remedy, and continuity.
What it still lacked was the shortest rule for the vulnerable stage **between handover and stable use**.
That stage matters because many climate failures happen not before delivery and not only after a formal complaint, but in the first weeks or months when controls are unfamiliar, settings are wrong, refill or service chains are shaky, and small annoyances turn into fallback, mistrust, or quiet underperformance.

rev0079 fills that gap without adding much archive surface area.
