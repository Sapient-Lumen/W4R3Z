## Why this change

The archive already had a shortest answer, an objective function, a physical-first note, a package-logic note, and a programme-shape note.
What it still lacked was one even more direct front-door compression for a recurring question:

**what are the hard conclusions or non-optional commitments of any serious attempt to solve global warming?**

That gap mattered because readers could already recover the answer by combining several files, but the archive still lacked one compact note that states, in plain practical language, what should now be treated as basically non-optional: fast gross cuts, clean-electrified backbones, methane action, managed fossil decline, ecosystem protection, adaptation and civil protection, fair burden allocation, low-friction delivery, international diffusion, live proof, and scarcity discipline for removals and other premium tools.

## Result

The archive now has a sharper front door for direct use in design, ranking, refusal, and restatement.
It can answer the question “what would ideal solutions actually require?” more directly without adding a new doctrinal branch or swelling the source base.
