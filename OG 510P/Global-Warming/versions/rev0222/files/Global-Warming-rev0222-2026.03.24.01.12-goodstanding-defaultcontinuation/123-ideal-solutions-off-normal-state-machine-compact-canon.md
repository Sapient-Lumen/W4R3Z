## The compact canon

### 1. Keep bad-day readiness live before stress
Off-normal governance starts before the incident.
A serious climate state should already have the trigger map, protection priorities, surge tools, truth infrastructure, and drilled handoff chain that make escalation a switch rather than a brainstorm [S34][S55][S83][S103][S111][S113][S114][S145][S147].

Best next file:
- `111-ideal-solutions-five-readiness-duties-before-escalation-fallback-or-exception.md`

### 2. Escalate on visible tripwires, not on vibes
The move from routine governance to stronger correction should be forced by a small number of visible failure signals: clogged substitute pipelines, real performance misses, growing dirty relapse, broken affordability or legitimacy, or exposure outrunning protection [S55][S65][S84][S103][S104][S145][S147][S181][S182].

Best next file:
- `103-ideal-solutions-five-escalation-tripwires-for-a-serious-climate-state.md`

### 3. Choose the response family that actually matches the failure
Once a tripwire fires, a serious climate state should not improvise from scratch.
It should enter the response mode that matches the problem: unblock the clean path, repair real performance, shield exposed people, constrain dirty relapse, or surge protection where harms are outrunning cover [S34][S55][S83][S84][S103][S104][S145][S147][S181][S183].

Best next file:
- `104-ideal-solutions-five-escalation-responses-after-a-tripwire-fires.md`

### 4. Admit dirty delay, fallback, or exception only through proof gates
An off-normal path is not self-justifying.
The actor seeking delay, fallback, or exception should carry the burden of proving real necessity, lack of a cleaner near-term alternative, narrow containment, explicit exit logic, and visible liability [S83][S84][S85][S86][S87][S107][S108][S145][S147].

Best next file:
- `108-ideal-solutions-five-burdens-of-proof-for-dirty-delay-fallback-or-exception-claims.md`

### 5. Govern the live detour as something that must shrink
Once the detour is open, keep one live truth picture, protect exposed people first, keep repair of the cleaner mainline ahead of stabilization of the workaround, re-test necessity on a short clock, and force the detour to narrow rather than normalize [S34][S55][S83][S84][S85][S87][S103][S104][S108][S109][S110][S112][S145][S147].

Best next file:
- `112-ideal-solutions-five-live-duties-during-escalation-fallback-or-exception.md`

### 6. Return to routine only on explicit tests
Do not close the detour because politics is tired of it.
Close it only when the trigger is controlled, protection is restored, the cleaner mainline is back or overtaking the workaround, the exception boundary is shrinking, and recurrence risk is lower than at entry [S83][S84][S85][S86][S103][S109][S110][S145][S147].

Best next file:
- `109-ideal-solutions-five-return-to-routine-tests-after-escalation-fallback-or-exception.md`

### 7. Convert closure into hardening
A serious climate state should not merely survive the incident.
It should publish what happened, repair the root cause, harden the mainline, tighten the proof gates, and update drills and readiness doctrine so the next incident becomes less likely and less damaging [S34][S55][S83][S103][S109][S110][S111][S113][S145][S147].

Best next file:
- `110-ideal-solutions-five-after-action-duties-after-escalation-fallback-or-exception.md`
