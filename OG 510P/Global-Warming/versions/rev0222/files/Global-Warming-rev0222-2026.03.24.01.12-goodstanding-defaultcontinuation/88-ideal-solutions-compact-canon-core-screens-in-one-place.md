# 88 — Ideal Solutions, Compact Canon: Core Screens in One Place

## Claim

The archive now contains many short, useful rules about what ideal climate solutions should be and how they should be delivered.
That is a strength, but it also raises navigation cost.
So one more compression now belongs in the canon:

**ideal solutions to global warming should be readable through a small number of core screens. If a proposal fails one of those screens, it does not matter that it sounds clever somewhere else.** [S100][S101][S102][S103][S104][S106][S118][S119][S123][S143][S146][S147]

This note does not replace the underlying files.
It gathers them into one compact evaluation order so the archive can stay dense without becoming cumbersome.

## The core screens

### 1. Physical portfolio screen
Does the proposal mainly do one of the real jobs?
- build clean electricity, grids, storage, flexibility, and firming where needed
- cut waste and then electrify remaining demand
- slash methane quickly
- retire fossil systems deliberately
- protect ecosystems, water, food systems, and people directly
- reserve scarce clean molecules and removals for hard residual uses rather than easy uses that already have better substitutes [S4][S8][S9][S11][S14][S15][S16][S17][S18][S19][S20][S21][S22][S26][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S34][S35][S36][S37][S38][S39][S55][S57][S66][S67][S72][S73][S74][S75][S76][S81][S82][S83][S84][S85][S86][S87][S88][S89][S90][S102][S103][S104][S105][S106][S112][S133][S165][S166][S167]

If not, it is probably peripheral, supporting, or noise.
See `57-ideal-solutions-reference-portfolio-2026-2035.md` and `58-ideal-solutions-one-page-answer.md`.

### 2. Justice screen
Does it raise essential service floors while lowering affluent waste ceilings?
Ideal solutions widen access to electricity, cooling safety, clean cooking, resilient shelter, health, water, and affordable mobility, while shrinking luxury throughput, waste, and lock-in at the top [S3][S12][S15][S16][S17][S20][S24][S41][S58][S69][S72][S73][S74][S75][S76][S81][S82][S83][S84][S85][S86][S87][S88][S89][S90][S97][S98][S104][S105][S106].

See `61-ideal-solutions-service-floors-waste-ceilings-progressive-restraint.md`.

### 3. Time screen
Is it in the right lane?
- emergency lane for fast forcing cuts, waste cuts, and direct protection
- replacement lane for durable clean buildout and system turnover
- residual lane for the hard remainder only

Ideal solutions do not invert those lanes by using residual tools as excuses for slow replacement [S8][S9][S11][S15][S16][S17][S18][S26][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S34][S35][S39][S55][S57][S66][S67][S72][S73][S74][S75][S76][S84][S102][S103][S104][S105][S106][S112][S133][S165][S166][S167].

### 4. Leverage-design screen
Is the burden placed where the leverage is?
Ideal solutions usually push obligations upward toward concentrated control points, change defaults before relying on household heroics, and use public or tightly regulated backbones where the task is a network, a universal-service obligation, or a coordination-heavy system [S13][S40][S55][S56][S57][S59][S60][S61][S62][S63][S64][S65][S66][S67][S68][S69][S80][S91][S92][S93][S94][S95][S96][S102][S103].

### 5. User-pathway screen
Does the clean path arrive as one joined sequence rather than a pile of fragments?
Ideal solutions should be easy to find, easy to start, easy to qualify for, low in upfront burden, simple to sign, actively delivered, supported after handover, easy to verify, easy to correct when wrong, and hard to reset back to dirty defaults [S16][S17][S40][S55][S56][S58][S59][S65][S66][S67][S68][S69][S76][S80][S81][S82][S83][S84][S85][S86][S87][S88][S89][S90][S97][S98][S104][S105][S106][S118][S119][S121][S123][S126][S127][S128][S129][S130][S131][S132][S135][S136][S137][S143][S144][S146][S147].

### 6. Trust-and-accountability screen
Can the proposal be trusted without fantasy?

### 7. Place-and-scarcity screen
Does it still make sense when translated across place types and scarce resources?
Ideal solutions keep one common climate spine but translate by country context, exposure profile, energy-access conditions, fiscal space, and resource scarcity.
They also reserve scarce clean energy, scarce capital, scarce administrative bandwidth, and scarce public tolerance for the hardest uses rather than the easiest [S13][S25][S26][S27][S28][S29][S30][S31][S32][S33][S34][S35][S42][S43][S44][S45][S46][S57][S59][S60][S61][S62][S63][S64][S68][S72][S73][S74][S75][S76][S84][S91][S92][S93][S94][S95][S96][S103][S104][S105][S112][S165][S166][S167].

### 8. Edge-conditions screen
What happens when the normal path is stressed, interrupted, or temporarily suspended?
Ideal solutions have a good bad-day mode, narrow and visible carve-outs, and real exit logic for anything claimed as temporary.
They do not let fallback, waiver drift, or emergency dirty bridges quietly become the real operating system [S9][S23][S24][S40][S55][S56][S58][S64][S68][S76][S80][S84][S85][S121][S123][S135][S136][S137][S143][S146][S147].

### 9. Reality screen
Will it survive contact with permitting, workforce, supply chains, finance, politics, and copyability?
The archive should keep preferring solutions that can be financed, permitted, staffed, measured, repeated, and defended across many jurisdictions over solutions that depend on rare coordination, bespoke expertise, or permanently heroic politics [S13][S40][S45][S46][S47][S48][S49][S50][S51][S52][S53][S54][S55][S56][S57][S59][S60][S61][S62][S63][S64][S65][S66][S67][S68][S80][S91][S92][S93][S94][S95][S96][S103].

## Ordered use

A future proposal should usually be screened in this order:
1. Does it belong in the real physical portfolio?
2. Does it raise floors and cut waste rather than reverse that priority?
3. Is it in the right time lane?
4. Are leverage, mechanism, and institutional form sensible?
5. Can a normal user or institution actually get through the path?
6. Are proof, correction, risk, upside, and progress measurement honest?
7. Does it still make sense across contexts and under scarcity?
8. Does it behave well at the edges?
9. Will it survive real delivery conditions?

If a proposal fails early, later elegance should usually not rescue it.

## One-paragraph compression

**The archive’s compact canon is now readable through nine core screens: physical portfolio, justice, time, leverage design, user pathway, trust and accountability, place and scarcity, edge conditions, and delivery reality. Ideal climate solutions pass most or all of these together. The point is not to multiply doctrine forever, but to keep one harsh, reusable screen for distinguishing serious climate packages from attractive fragments, loophole systems, and nice-sounding distractions.** [S100][S101][S102][S103][S104][S106][S118][S119][S123][S143][S146][S147]
