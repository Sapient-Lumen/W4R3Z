# CHANGELOG — rev0071

## Summary

This revision adds one compact note on **remedy form**.

The archive already had recent compact rules for delivery form, risk-bearing, upside allocation, payment form, contract form, eligibility form, and verification form.
What it still lacked was the smallest rule for what should happen when a climate promise fails in ordinary use.

The new note says ideal climate solutions should make remedy **simple, fast, and biased toward correction**:
- put clear recourse close to the service,
- use existing records to auto-correct obvious errors where possible,
- keep the burden of explanation and remedy with the actor that controls the system or holds the data,
- and reserve long appeals for unusual contested cases rather than making them the default operating model.

That keeps the archive aligned with its broader doctrine:
- low friction over preventable administrative drag,
- ordinary delivery over heroic navigation,
- accountability where control sits,
- and political durability through visible fairness in real use.
