## Rationale

The archive already had compact rules for delivery form, risk bearing, upside allocation, and payment form.
What it still lacked was the shortest rule for the **agreement layer** itself: standard offers where possible, prequalified delivery where useful, transparent post-contract obligations, and performance-backed terms when providers claim savings or service.

That addition is small but useful because climate programmes often fail in the space between policy design and a signable trustworthy offer.
