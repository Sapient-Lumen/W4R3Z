## Why this revision

The archive already had compact rules for buffering, aftercare, continuity, and assurance. What it still lacked was the shortest explicit rule for the off-normal path: what should happen when outages, delays, stress, or underperformance still breach the clean pathway.

This revision adds that rule in compact form:

- fail soft rather than collapse into the dirtiest familiar baseline
- use the least-damaging available backup
- keep fallback visible and clearly temporary
- design a fast return to the clean path instead of accepting sticky dirty relapse

## Result

The archive can now answer not only what ideal climate solutions look like in the steady state, but also what their bad-day behaviour should be. That makes the canon more realistic without making it much larger.
