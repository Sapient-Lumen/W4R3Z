# 59 — Ideal Solutions: Fast Screen for Promote / Demote / Quarantine

## Purpose

The archive is now large enough that the main danger is no longer missing obvious climate lanes.
The main danger is letting new proposals into the canon **without a fast, disciplined screen**.

This note is therefore a compression device.
It does not replace `28-ideal-solutions-selection-rules-and-exclusions.md` or the 30–56 doctrine series.
It gives the shortest reusable answer to a practical archive question:

**when a proposed “solution” appears, should the archive promote it, demote it, or quarantine it?**

For the archive’s shortest actor-allocation companion to this screen, see `63-ideal-solutions-by-actor-concentrated-control-points-and-no-heroic-consumers.md`.
For the archive’s shortest scoreboard companion to this screen, see `65-ideal-solutions-by-scoreboard-physical-outcomes-service-gains-and-bottleneck-release.md`.

## Fast rule

Promote proposals that do most of the following **before 2035**:
- cut greenhouse gases in the physical world
- reduce future overshoot and repair burden
- lower exposure, bills, or system fragility in ordinary life
- work under real finance, grid, permitting, workforce, and governance constraints
- remain valid in poorer and infrastructure-thin settings
- can be measured well enough to govern honestly
- work through concentrated control points instead of offloading the hardest work onto households and small actors [S27][S53][S56][S68][S69][S80][S91][S96][S100][S101][S102][S103][S104][S106][S117][S118][S119][S137]

Demote or quarantine proposals that mainly do the opposite: move effort into later decades, depend on paper claims, preserve dirty systems, consume scarce clean resources for easy uses, or require ideal institutions that do not exist at scale [S28][S42][S43][S44][S45][S46][S91][S96][S100][S101].

## The twelve tests

A candidate solution should usually pass most of these.

### 1. Early-cut test
Does it deliver large physical gains in the overshoot-sensitive period through 2035 rather than mainly later? [S100][S101][S105]

### 2. Physical-first test
Is success proved mainly by measured emissions cuts, leak reduction, fossil displacement, protection of ecosystems, or exposure reduction rather than by certificates, netting stories, or other paper substitutions? [S11][S65][S66][S67][S68][S113]

### 3. Denominator test
Does it reduce wasted electricity, fuels, materials, land pressure, or peak load instead of only trying to clean an ever-growing baseline? [S16][S17][S58][S97][S98][S99][S103][S106]

### 4. Scarcity-discipline test
Does it reserve scarce clean molecules, scarce biomass, scarce storage capacity, scarce skilled labour, and scarce fiscal room for the hard cases rather than spending them on easy uses? [S26][S30][S32][S33][S34][S35][S42][S112][S165][S166][S167]

### 5. Lock-in test
Does it avoid creating long-lived fossil dependence, high future retrofit cost, maladaptive settlement, or other path dependencies that are expensive to undo later? [S23][S24][S31][S40][S41][S81][S83][S100]

### 6. Ordinary-life test
Does it make daily life tangibly better by lowering bills, improving comfort, cleaning the air, reducing heat stress, or making basic services more reliable? [S16][S17][S69][S85][S86][S87][S88][S90][S104][S106]

### 7. Delivery test
Can it actually be financed, permitted, procured, interconnected, staffed, maintained, and repeated by real institutions rather than idealised ones? [S13][S14][S59][S60][S61][S62][S63][S64][S91][S95][S96][S103]

### 8. EMDE-validity test
Does it still make sense where capital is expensive, grid quality is uneven, administrative capacity is thin, and development needs are rising? [S70][S71][S91][S92][S93][S94][S95][S96]

### 9. Measurability test
Can the claim be audited with interoperable metrics, public reporting, and enforceable standards, or is it mostly a narration problem? And is the proposal scored mainly by physical, service, and bottleneck outcomes rather than by announcements or spend alone? [S27][S59][S65][S66][S67][S68][S113][S123][S136]

### 10. Fiscal-durability test
Does public support build durable assets, lower future bills or losses, and create a glide path away from permanent subsidy dependence? [S69][S91][S94][S107][S138][S143][S144][S145][S146]

### 11. Political-durability test
Will visible benefits, fairness, and institutional continuity make the measure easier to keep over time rather than easy to reverse after one backlash cycle? [S58][S69][S94][S138][S140][S141][S142]

### 12. Copyability test
Can other cities, states, firms, and countries adopt it without bespoke heroics, or does it only work in one unusually capable place? [S56][S59][S60][S61][S63][S64][S96][S103]

## Triage rule

Use the screen roughly like this.

### Promote to core canon
Promote when a proposal passes most tests and strongly passes the big ones: **early-cut, physical-first, denominator, delivery, EMDE-validity, and measurability**.

Typical promotes:
- clean electricity plus grids, storage, flexibility, and selected firm clean capacity [S4][S14][S55][S56][S57][S103]
- efficiency, passive design, better buildings, efficient cooling, and demand flexibility [S16][S17][S51][S52][S53][S54][S106]
- methane leak detection and repair, landfill and wastewater methane control, and agrifood methane / nitrogen action [S8][S11][S19][S20][S21][S22]
- managed fossil decline, subsidy reform, and worker / regional transition policy [S9][S23][S24][S25]
- ecosystem protection, water security, and adaptation as civil protection [S12][S15][S36][S37][S38][S39][S72][S73][S74][S75][S76][S104]

### Demote to secondary or conditional
Demote when a proposal has a role but only inside tight boundaries, hard-case niches, or enabling contexts.

Typical demotions:
- hydrogen, e-fuels, and biomass outside genuine hard-to-abate uses [S33][S34][S35][S42][S112][S165][S166][S167]
- CCS where it mainly preserves avoidable fossil dependence rather than addressing residual process emissions or durable storage needs [S43][S44][S45][S46]
- adaptation measures that protect people usefully but do not substitute for mitigation [S12][S40][S41][S81][S82][S85]
- finance instruments or disclosure systems that help coordination but are too indirect to count as the main climate effect [S65][S68][S91][S94]

### Quarantine or exclude
Quarantine when a proposal repeatedly fails the screen or survives only by rhetoric.

Typical quarantines:
- removal-first or overshoot-as-plan strategies [S42][S43][S44][S45][S46][S100][S101]
- offset-heavy or paper-claims-heavy strategies with weak physical additionality [S113][S123][S135][S136]
- rich-country-only packages that ignore EMDE finance and access constraints [S70][S71][S91][S92][S93][S94][S95][S96]
- fossil-lock-in strategies disguised as transition realism [S9][S23][S24][S102]

## What the archive should now do with new proposals

When a future topic appears, the archive should ask four questions in order.

1. **Is this a core physical lane, or merely adjacent to one?**
2. **Does it beat the current core portfolio on any of the twelve tests, or only re-describe it?**
3. **Does it clarify a boundary that is still confused in practice?**
4. **If added, would it make the archive sharper, or only larger?**

If the answer to the last question is “only larger,” the default should be **no new main-canon note**.

## Compression

The ideal solution to global warming is not every plausible intervention.
It is the subset that passes a harsh screen: **fast physical cuts, lower demand and exposure, honest measurability, real-world deliverability, EMDE validity, and durable public legitimacy**.

Everything else is secondary, conditional, or noise.
