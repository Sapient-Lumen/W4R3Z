## Why this change

The archive already had a shortest answer, a joined scoring rule, a physical-first note, a package-logic note, and separate compact canons for physical portfolio, political economy, delivery, truth, protection, and the international layer.
What it still lacked was one compact first-stop note for a recurring front-door question:

**what is the ideal solution, in overall programme shape, once those layers are held together at once?**

That gap mattered because the archive could already say what to build, how to score it, what counts as real progress, how to bundle complements, how to keep claims honest, and how to govern bottlenecks — but not yet in one shortest joined note what kind of thing the ideal solution actually is.

## Result

The archive now has a compact front-door canon that compresses the answer into one governed programme shape:
fast gross-emissions decline, clean service backbones, direct protection against climate harms, fair and financeable burden allocation, proof and correction discipline, and international diffusion through cheaper capital, standards, and coordination.
