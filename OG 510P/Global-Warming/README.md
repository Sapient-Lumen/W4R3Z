# Global-Warming

## From an energy transition to the people and institutions that must carry it

The early archive opens with clean power, electrification, efficiency and a practical delivery problem: a desirable transition still needs grids, workers, supply chains, reliable service and legitimate local decisions. Its later supplied material narrows dramatically into nuclear emergency-preparedness evidence and records-request design.

A reader should encounter both scales. Beginning only at the latest release would make the work look like a specialized intake system and hide the broader question that gave it direction.

## Read from the wide question into the narrow case

1. Use [the early reading map](versions/rev0015/files/START_HERE.md) to find the mission, the proposed energy stack and its delivery constraints. Workforce and adoption are part of that account, not afterthoughts to technical capacity.
2. Compare the intermediate rev0222 guide below for the project’s concern with continued standing and service arrangements.
3. Read [the later preparedness entry](versions/rev0379/files/README.md). Its workorders and intake surfaces ask how a claim would acquire records, responses and an inspectable basis. They are not proof that those records have arrived.

## The narrowing is worth examining

The progression invites a substantive question: when does concentrating on one institution make a broad project accountable to reality, and when can the apparatus of a case displace the original purpose? The preserved versions allow the reader to ask that without having to choose between ignoring details and mistaking details for an answer.

Rev0379 expressly says it claims no local readiness, has sent no records request, imported no response and closed no proofcut. Its named-place context must not be converted into an allegation of an actual safety failure. This edition sends no requests and makes no emergency-preparedness determination.

The latest snapshot arrived in three transport segments. Those originals, the verified contained ZIP and the separately extracted project remain available with the transport note; the multipart packaging does not change the work’s evidence status.

Read beside [Radical Governance](../Radical-Governance/README.md) on institutions that must remain answerable, and [The Good](../The-Good/README.md) on the conditions of ordinary security and participation. Source and policy claims remain historical research rather than current planning advice.
