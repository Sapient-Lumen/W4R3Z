# Righteousness

A compact, revisable datacube for researching salient theories of **righteousness**.

This archive starts from a simple judgment: *righteousness* is not well-captured by any single neighboring term such as goodness, justice, compliance, piety, or integrity. The archive therefore tries to build a narrow waist: small enough to reopen, rich enough to guide serious comparative work.

- Working aim: identify the most portable structure shared by serious accounts of righteousness without flattening their differences
- Current sharpened stance: righteousness is **relation-first, person-carried, and institution-shaped**, with public office as the clearest stress test
- Current portability rule: righteousness has a **portable core** and **tradition-thick forms**
- Current repair rule: righteousness becomes **breach-responsive and reparative** once wrong, wrongful benefit, or damaged standing is in view
- New clarification: portability claims must specify **recognition status** and **public-translation status**
- New threshold rule: righteousness is above bare nonwrongdoing and below universal heroism
- New defeater rule: some standing defects presumptively defeat a righteousness claim even when some good is achieved
- New requalification rule: where standing can be re-earned, it runs through avowal, interruption, repair, and time rather than instant self-purification
- New contestability rule: offices and institutions cannot rely on self-certifying virtue alone; public righteousness requires review-capable reasons, record, and stronger burdens where secrecy blocks ordinary visibility
- New conflict rule: where valid claims collide, righteousness requires disciplined priority plus honest carriage of the unmet remainder rather than clean-win rhetoric
- New temporal rule: righteousness verdicts should distinguish episode, standing, and trajectory rather than reading one act as total proof or one failure as permanent essence
- New uncertainty rule: under material uncertainty, righteousness requires proportionate inquiry, fair uptake of testimony, explicit uncertainty, and revisable or repairable action rather than overconfidence or paralysis
- New delegation rule: delegated action may distribute labor or expertise, but claim-setting, guardrails, contestability, and repair ownership cannot simply disappear into proxies
- New priority-cue rule: righteous conflict-judgment is guided by need, vulnerability, induced reliance, creator-burden, and reversibility rather than a fake neutral balance among all claims
- New equity rule: righteous judgment may correct rule underfit through publicly articulable, like-cases-disciplined particularization rather than blind rule-literalism or favoritist exception
- New interruption rule: where continued participation would launder abuse, righteousness may require objection, recusal, protected disclosure, resignation, or burdened public witness rather than compliant role-fidelity
- New mercy rule: righteous sanction names the wrong and protects against recurrence without cruelty theater, while righteous mercy is authority-side restraint that cannot be purchased by victim pressure, favoritism, or unrepaired exposure
- New forecast rule: righteousness is burdened when projected future offending or dangerousness becomes title to present suspicion, patrol, stop, or restraint without individualized, conduct-linked, and reviewable grounds
- New face-library rule: righteousness is burdened when ordinary public or online appearance is turned into a searchable facial reservoir through untargeted scraping, so that later identification or tracking rides on database manufacture rather than a bounded, lawfully acquired, necessity-disciplined relation
- New friction rule: righteousness is burdened when interface asymmetry makes signup, assent, disclosure, or payment easier than refusal, privacy protection, comparison, or cancellation, so friction rather than fair reason does the real work of keeping a person subscribed, paying, disclosed, or locked in
- New public-trust rule: office powers are held rather than owned, so office, office-information, secrecy, discretion, and opportunity may not be treated as private or factional property
- New authority-bounds rule: no act may claim righteous office merely by aiming at a good end; public power must also be exercised by the right bearer, with adequate competence, and under real authorization rather than office usurpation
- New civic-label rule: righteousness is burdened when stigmatizing labels tied to foreign funding or affiliation do the real work of isolating, delegitimizing, or disabling association rather than serving a narrow, neutral, and reviewable transparency function
- New emergency rule: crisis may justify temporary extraordinary measures only under strict necessity, narrow scope, sunset, preserved review, and visible return-to-normalcy burdens rather than self-purifying exception talk
- New subsistence-floor rule: righteousness is burdened when persons or peoples are left without reasonable access to food, water, basic shelter or housing, and basic care, or when scarcity is handled through favoritism, hidden ranking, or normalized abandonment rather than public reasons and dignified floor protection
- New anti-displacement rule: righteousness is burdened when persons, families, or populations are uprooted, expelled, transferred, or blocked from return mainly to simplify control, alter demography, retaliate, privatize access, or externalize risk; even justified evacuation or relocation requires necessity, explored alternatives, dignified accommodation, family-preserving conditions, effective remedy, and a real path toward return, restitution, or non-degrading resettlement
- New access-to-justice rule: righteousness is burdened when rights are recognized only in forms that cannot be timely claimed, heard, or repaired because complaint, review, or remedy routes are too delayed, inaccessible, retaliatory, powerless, or exhausting to use before decisive harm hardens
- New land rule: righteousness is burdened when land, territory, tenure, or return are governed as if only administratively preferred title, registry, or ownership form could create standing, so that customary, informal, collective, ancestral, or gender-equal claims can be erased through maps, acquisition law, inheritance scripts, or concession design
- New bodily-autonomy rule: righteousness is burdened when pregnancy, contraception, abortion, sterilization, fertility, or intimate medical choice are governed mainly through criminal threat, third-party authorization, coerced intervention, or substitute judgment rather than through the standing of the person whose body bears the consequence
- New culture-language rule: righteousness is burdened when schooling, services, work, or civic participation are made fully usable mainly for those willing to privatize, subordinate, or practically abandon their own language or cultural continuity, so that public belonging becomes conditional on assimilation rather than equal-standing plural intelligibility
- New corruption rule: righteousness is burdened when public power, service, or protection becomes informally saleable through unofficial payment, patronage, conflict-laden self-dealing, or bought discretion, so that permits, contracts, hiring, adjudication, care, welfare, schooling, or ordinary safety go more readily to the connected than to those with the strongest public claim
- New outsourcing rule: righteousness is burdened when states, offices, or lead enterprises treat privatization, concessions, procurement, platformization, or supply-chain distance as if these dissolved duties to protect, oversee, and repair rights-bearing services, so that the burdened are left inside gaps between contractor, vendor, parent, platform, and public authority
- New housing rule: righteousness is burdened when people are made homeless, eviction-vulnerable, or clearance-exposed mainly to simplify control, urban image, creditor leverage, or administrative tidiness; even justified housing burdens require serious alternative-testing, due process, preserved dignity and belongings, and a credible route to secure housing rather than repeated emergency shelter or public-space punishment
- New social-security rule: righteousness is burdened when subsistence support is governed through punitive conditionality, inaccessible administration, opaque suspicion, or sanction that can push people below a minimally dignified floor; bounded verification and bounded conditions may exist, but not governance through destitution risk
- New education rule: righteousness is burdened when ordinary learning, school presence, or educational credentials are governed through exclusion, segregation, inaccessible design, documentary precarity, degrading removal, or coerced conformity; bounded standards, discipline, and curriculum authority may exist, but not schooling through exclusion
- New work rule: righteousness is burdened when access to livelihood is governed through dangerous, degrading, discriminatorily closed, union-broken, inaccessible, or practically unleaveable work; bounded standards, supervision, and productivity demands may exist, but not livelihood by domination
- New algorithmic-governance rule: righteousness is burdened when liberty, welfare, work, schooling, housing, movement, safety, or public voice are substantially governed through opaque, unchallengeable, proxy-discriminatory, surveillance-hungry, or vendor-laundered automated systems; bounded technical assistance may exist, but not rightlessness by model
- New legal-capacity rule: righteousness is burdened when support for decision-making, guardianship, incapacity law, or protective administration makes a person live in law mainly through somebody else's will; support may be necessary and often righteous, but it must preserve the person's own authority rather than convert assistance into civil death by guardianship
- New water-and-sanitation rule: righteousness is burdened when safe water, sanitation, hygiene, or wastewater protection are governed through shutoff, unaffordability, contamination, inaccessible design, or degrading dependence; bounded regulation and scarcity triage may exist, but not thirst or unsafe sanitation by administration
- New anti-collective-punishment rule: righteousness is burdened when an actor imposes hunger, blocked relief, service cutoff, confinement, or other serious area-wide hardship mainly to punish, intimidate, or pressure persons within a group; even where siege, closure, or emergency restriction is not per se forbidden, civilian deprivation may not be the method
- New family-separation rule: righteousness is burdened when power splits families, blocks contact, conceals whereabouts, or drags out reunion mainly to punish, deter, or simplify control; even justified separation must stay individualized, promptly notified, contact-preserving where safe, and under a live burden to reunite rather than normalize fracture
- New care-access rule: righteousness is burdened when essential care is denied, delayed, obstructed, or politically sorted, or when healers are punished or compelled to serve coercive aims; triage, quarantine, and emergency scarcity may justify narrower burdens only if they remain medically grounded, non-discriminatory, minimally punitive, and reviewable
- New documentation rule: righteousness is burdened when legal existence, ordinary services, or family continuity depend on papers, proofs, or digital credentials that people cannot safely get, keep, or correct; identity assurance may be necessary, but it must stay universalizing, challengeable, and privacy-disciplined enough that no one becomes effectively rightless by documentary failure
- New disappearance rule: righteousness is burdened when power takes custody while concealing fate or whereabouts, blocking locating channels, or falsifying records; limited confidentiality may sometimes be justified, but never secret detention, unrecorded transfer, fabricated release, or any arrangement that makes a person legally unfindable
- New anti-torture rule: righteousness is burdened when persons are governed through severe pain, terror, sensory assault, or other cruel treatment in order to extract information, punish, intimidate, discriminate, or break the will; emergency, official order, and mere noncompliance do not purify that defect, and righteous order also carries burdens of inspection, complaint, impartial investigation, and exclusion of torture-tainted proof
- New anti-arbitrary-detention rule: righteousness is burdened when power deprives people of liberty without a clear, necessary, and reviewable basis, or treats detention as the administratively easy answer; criminal custody requires prompt reasons, prompt judicial control, and release as the ordinary baseline, while administrative relabeling does not erase liberty burdens
- New anti-arbitrary-killing rule: righteousness is burdened when power takes life without strict necessity, or structures force so that killing becomes easier than capture, care, delay, or de-escalation; life-taking in policing, custody, borders, and other enforcement settings remains exceptional, and every potentially unlawful death carries investigation and truth duties
- New anti-statelessness rule: righteousness is burdened when nationality is denied, stripped, or conditioned in ways that arbitrarily or discriminatorily create statelessness, blocked return, civic unreality, or other forms of exile by paper; membership administration may sometimes be bounded, but it must remain non-discriminatory, reviewable, and protective against making people disappear from belonging
- New fair-trial rule: righteousness is burdened when guilt, liability, expulsion, or other serious penalty is imposed through a process the burdened person cannot meaningfully understand, answer, test, or appeal; serious adjudication needs competent and impartial forum, intelligible notice, practical defense, secrecy-disciplining safeguards, and review stronger than symbolic form
- New assembly rule: righteousness is burdened when power suppresses peaceful assembly mainly to avoid criticism, disperse visibility, or simplify control; non-violent protest remains protected even when unauthorized or disruptive, authorities owe facilitation and differentiated protection duties, and force or dispersal must remain exceptional, minimized, and answerable
- New affected-standing rule: where public power materially burdens identifiable people or communities, righteousness normally requires a domain-fit way of making them present through hearing, meaningful participation, accountable representation, or a justified emergency substitute rather than over-the-head rule
- New absent-claimant rule: inability to appear directly does not erase claim; righteousness requires support for direct agency where possible, accountable proxy voice where necessary, and stewardship rather than ventriloquism for future or diffuse claimants
- New special-ties rule: righteousness is neither flat impartiality nor unrestricted loyalty; special ties may thicken what is due, but private ties inside impartial public office often trigger restraint rather than privileged service
- New anti-domination rule: righteousness is not reducible to non-domination, but benevolent mastery is not enough; where one side lives at another's mercy under uncontrolled power, the righteousness claim is burdened
- New trafficking-protection rule: righteousness is burdened when anti-trafficking, migration, labour, or cybercrime systems answer trafficking mainly through detention, prosecution, deportation, closed shelters, or compelled cooperation rather than through non-punishment, rights-preserving protection, and real exit from exploiters
- New candor rule: righteousness is burdened not only by lies but also by misleading half-truth, manipulative framing, and false reassurance; where full disclosure cannot rightly be given, burdened candor or protected refusal is usually better than deceptive confidence
- New keeping-faith rule: promises, oaths, assurances, and office undertakings can thicken what is due, but wrongful or unauthorized undertakings do not become righteous merely by being kept
- New gift rule: gratitude may be fitting for real beneficence, but gifts, favors, hospitality, rescue, and sponsorship do not buy silence, deference, or preferential treatment; in office they are standing burdens when they threaten capture or private conversion of public trust
- New exploitation rule: formal consent, waiver, or mutually advantageous exchange do not by themselves make an arrangement righteous when the stronger party is extracting gain from desperation, dependence, ignorance, or severely constrained refusal conditions
- New captive-service rule: righteousness is burdened when work, service, or continued role-performance is held in place by menace, debt, document seizure, wage withholding, status dependence, or other conditions that make exit unreal; some civic or emergency duties may be justified, but captivity is not laundered by a past signature or nominal right to quit
- New complicity rule: righteousness is burdened not only by direct wrongdoing but also by knowingly enabling, legitimizing, benefiting from, or staying entangled with others' wrongdoing where leverage, inquiry, mitigation, or withdrawal became due
- New nontransfer rule: guilt, punishment, and civic disability do not travel by bloodline, shared label, or mere association, though real group authorship, office responsibility, and knowing participation still generate burdens that righteousness must trace rather than smear or erase
- New nonabasement rule: even where sanction, custody, or public censure is due, righteousness is burdened by humiliation, degrading treatment, and spectacle that turns persons into warnings, trophies, or objects of contempt
- New equal-burden rule: righteousness is burdened when norms are enforced mainly downward, outward, or against rivals while insiders or one's own side receive indulgence without a publicly relevant role-, vulnerability-, or jurisdiction-based reason
- New anti-entrapment rule: righteousness is burdened when the accuser or office materially engineers the very breach later cited as proof of guilt, danger, or unfitness; mere opportunity is not the same as provoked failure
- New pretext rule: righteousness is burdened when a clean label, facially valid rule, or procedurally available channel is used as cover for a reason that could not be openly owned; proper-purpose process and genuine justification matter especially in office
- New anti-reprisal rule: righteousness is burdened when complaint, testimony, disclosure, refusal of illegality, or cooperation with review is punished, chilled, or strategically burdened; offices and institutions may not protect themselves from answerability by making protected voice costly
- New fair-notice rule: righteousness is burdened when serious accusation, discipline, exclusion, or penalty runs through hidden, retroactive, or unduly vague standards; people should not have to discover the real rule only by already failing it
- New anti-attrition rule: righteousness is burdened when valid claims are quietly defeated through unreasonable delay, repeated hoops, or exhausting provisional limbo; time costs and procedural friction are part of the burden, not neutral background
- New anti-hostage rule: righteousness is burdened when one person is used as a bargaining chip, shield, or collateral pressure point to move another person's will; authority over the immediate person does not by itself license leverage over a third party
- New anti-cover-up rule: righteousness is burdened when records, evidence, witness channels, or decision traces are destroyed, suppressed, kept off the books, or corrupted so that later challenge, review, or repair cannot proceed on fair terms
- New no-coerced-confession rule: avowal can matter for truth and requalification, but confession, apology, or ideological recantation extracted by custody, threat, dependency, or humiliative scripting does not by itself establish truth or restore righteous standing
- New no-compelled-creed rule: offices and institutions may require truthful duty-fit commitments, but righteousness is burdened when office, schooling, work, safety, or belonging is conditioned on ideological profession, factional loyalty, religious test, or ritual assent beyond what lawful role performance actually requires
- New accommodation rule: righteousness is burdened when people are judged, excluded, silenced, or penalized for failing to clear communicative, cognitive, sensory, mobility, temporal, or administrative barriers that the stronger party could reasonably remove, modify, or route around; identical treatment is not enough where access itself is broken
- New anti-self-judging rule: righteousness is burdened when the accuser, beneficiary, or implicated office also controls the verdict or appeal without real recusal, structural separation, or independent review; sincerity alone cannot cleanse a captured forum
- New privacy / proportional-legibility rule: righteousness is burdened when a person, office, or institution demands, collects, retains, searches, or exposes more personal visibility than the task, risk, or due can justify; accountable institutions need enough proof to answer for their acts, not unlimited legibility of the persons under their power
- New no-identity-by-extraction rule: righteousness is burdened when face, fingerprint, iris, voice, gait, DNA, or other biometric or genetic capture becomes the ordinary condition of access, suspicion management, or public-space legibility rather than a narrowly bounded, contestable, retention-disciplined, and non-discriminatory form of verification

- New expression / press rule: righteousness is burdened when power suppresses criticism, reporting, or access to public-interest information mainly to avoid scrutiny, monopolize narrative, or make answerability practically unusable; some narrow restrictions may remain justified, but opinion, public information access, media freedom, and journalist protection carry a steep burden against silencing by power
- New association / union rule: righteousness is burdened when power makes collective organization contingent on prior indulgence, selective permission, employer domination, or retaliatory risk; people may be regulated narrowly for real protective reasons, but they may not be left to organize only on sufferance, only through captured bodies, or only at punishing personal cost
- New political-participation / elections rule: righteousness is burdened when power excludes citizens from public affairs through unreasonable voting barriers, sham electoral design, intimidation, discriminatory candidacy rules, or unequal access to public service; participation must stay genuinely usable, independently supervised, and broad enough that rule is not preserved by managed inclusion
- New freedom-of-thought / conscience / religion rule: righteousness is burdened when power coerces belief, punishes conversion or nonbelief, suppresses worship or observance beyond narrow necessity, or distributes safety, belonging, education, or public standing through favored orthodoxy; the inward forum is not public property, and plural order must be built through coexistence rather than orthodoxy management
- New freedom-of-movement / residence / return rule: righteousness is burdened when power traps people in place, withholds practical exit, blocks ordinary movement or residence choice beyond narrow necessity, or arbitrarily denies re-entry to one's own country; movement limits may sometimes be justified, but closure, encampment, passport denial, document control, and no-return administration carry a steep burden against governing by immobilization
- New equality / non-discrimination rule: righteousness is burdened when law, office, or institutions distribute standing, safety, opportunity, burden, remedy, movement, work, education, care, voice, or civic belonging by protected status or by facially neutral design whose purpose or effect is exclusion or subordination; formal sameness is not enough where ranked standing persists, so accommodation, corrective measures, and usable remedy may be part of equality rather than deviations from it
- New child-standing / evolving-capacities rule: righteousness is burdened when parents, guardians, schools, welfare authorities, or custodians treat a child as property, silent object, deterrent instrument, honor-bearer, or ideological extension rather than as a rights-bearing person; guidance and protection may justify asymmetry, but only under child-specific reasons, best-interests discipline, due weight to the child's views, family/contact safeguards where safe, and a live duty to cultivate rather than stunt developing agency
- New connectivity rule: righteousness is burdened when speech, schooling, work, welfare access, family contact, emergency information, complaint, or political participation are made to depend on connection that can be arbitrarily shut down, throttled, priced beyond reach, or made the only usable route without realistic alternatives; meaningful connectivity, offline fallback, narrow tailoring, and rapid restoration matter because digital disconnection now functions as a direct standing defect
