# Neurotechnology, neurodata, and no-thought-by-extraction

## Core claim

> **Righteousness is burdened, and often defeated, when public or private actors record, infer from, retain, trade in, or alter brain activity or neurodata so that access to or manipulation of a person's thought, attention, emotion, preference, or decision tendency does the real work of governance, persuasion, productivity, screening, or compliance rather than serving a tightly bounded, consent-governed, privacy-hardened, medically necessary, and independently reviewable purpose.**[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S93][S439][S440][S441][S442][S443][S444][S445]

## 1. The missing moral question is when the mind stops being protected interiority and becomes an institutional interface

The archive already rejected no-identity-by-extraction, no-rightlessness-by-model, no-orthodoxy-by-power, and no-standing-by-score. But it still lacked a direct rule for a newer temptation: **treating thought-adjacent access as just another optimization layer**.[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49]

That gap matters because neurotechnology is not only a medical story. It is also a governance story. Once an institution can record brain activity, infer stress or attention, classify emotional or cognitive state, or alter neural activity, it has a route into the person that is more intimate than ordinary documentation, surveillance, or behavioral data. The danger is not simply that the institution learns more. It is that it starts treating mental access itself as a permissible source of permission, ranking, persuasion, or control.[S439][S441][S442][S444][S445]

## 2. Therapeutic and assistive use are not the same as governance through brain access

The archive does **not** need to say that every brain-computer interface, neurostimulation tool, neuroimaging workflow, or assistive neurotechnology is automatically unrighteous. UNESCO and OECD materials are useful precisely because they keep the field's benefits in view while insisting on anticipatory governance, safety, and rights discipline.[S439][S440][S445]

That distinction is load-bearing for righteousness. A bounded therapeutic or accessibility use directed to the person's own health, communication, or agency is one thing. A workplace focus monitor, classroom attention tracker, neuro-marketing instrument, custody or interrogation aid, insurance screen, or public-service gate that makes neural inference do the real work of compliance, persuasion, or suspicion is another. The second is not merely more advanced evidence. It is a different governing posture.[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S441][S442][S444][S445]

## 3. Neurodata is not ordinary data

The OHCHR privacy materials sharpen the moral point by treating neurodata as highly sensitive personal data directly related to cognitive state and by linking it to mental privacy, mental integrity, and thought processes.[S442] UNESCO's current neurotechnology materials say brain data can reveal identity, emotions, and thoughts and must be protected from illegitimate interference or misuse.[S439][S440]

That matters because neurodata is unusually hard to confine once collected. It can be retained, combined with other data, repurposed for inference, sold into secondary markets, or used to build scores and interventions that the person never meaningfully agreed to. A one-time access event can therefore become a durable reformatting of what others are allowed to know, predict, or try to influence about the person from the inside out.[S439][S440][S442]

## 4. Extraction and modulation are twin burdens

Righteousness should not only ask whether someone's brain data were collected. It should also ask whether brain activity was being shaped, nudged, or altered in ways that thinned agency, responsibility, or interior freedom. UNESCO's materials are morally revealing here because they name not only access but manipulation and external interference with freedom of thought, cognitive liberty, free will, and decision-making.[S439][S440]

So the archive should reject another laundering move: because a device is framed as assistive, personalized, non-invasive, or high-tech, institutions assume that deeper access is morally cleaner access. But some uses are standing defects precisely because they make the person newly governable at the level of attention, emotion, cognition, or neural susceptibility.[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S439][S440][S442][S444]

## 5. Current governance materials treat neurotechnology as a live human-rights frontier

This is no longer only a speculative philosophy problem. UNESCO's Recommendation on the Ethics of Neurotechnology was adopted in November 2025, and UNESCO's public materials describe the field as requiring a comprehensive ethical framework to safeguard human dignity and rights.[S439][S440] The Human Rights Council's 2024 study and its 2025 resolution 58/6 show that the Council is now building guidelines for applying the existing human-rights framework to neurotechnology.[S441][S443] Council of Europe materials explicitly frame neurotechnology as raising issues of privacy, personhood, discrimination, cognitive liberty, mental privacy, and mental integrity, while OECD materials treat responsible innovation and governance as a standing requirement rather than an afterthought.[S444][S445]

The archive therefore now adds a neurotechnology / neurodata / no-thought-by-extraction rule: where access to or alteration of brain activity does the real work of governance, persuasion, productivity, screening, or compliance rather than serving a tightly bounded, consent-governed, privacy-hardened, medically necessary, and independently reviewable purpose, righteousness is heavily burdened and often defeated.[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S93][S439][S440][S441][S442][S443][S444][S445]

## 6. Working rule

When an actor claims righteousness while relying on neurotechnology or neurodata, ask seven questions:
1. **What exactly was being accessed or altered?** Brain signals, inferred attention, emotional state, fatigue, preference, memory, intention, stimulation pattern, or some weaker neural proxy?[S439][S440][S442]
2. **What relation was the system supposedly serving?** Treatment, communication, accessibility, safety, learning support, authentication, hiring, workplace management, marketing, policing, border control, or something vaguer such as optimization or behavioral insight?[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S441][S445]
3. **What made consent real?** Understandable disclosure, voluntariness, refusal without penalty, revocation, data deletion, device removal, and accessible alternatives — or only a formal click, waiver, or institutional dependency?[S442][S443][S445]
4. **What secondary uses became possible once the data existed?** Scoring, profiling, sharing, sale, retention, manager-side visibility, law-enforcement access, insurer use, or cross-context inference?[S439][S440][S442]
5. **What narrower route was available?** Ordinary clinical evidence, symptom report, non-neural assistive tools, ergonomic redesign, non-invasive non-neural monitoring, or case-specific human review rather than mind-proximate capture?[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S445]
6. **What review and repair path existed?** Independent oversight, ethics review, audit, complaint, override, exit, deletion, compensation, or bounded expiration?[S441][S443][S444][S445]
7. **Was brain access doing the real work?** Did the institution quietly replace ordinary reasons, bounded evidence, and relation-specific judgment with access to or influence over the person's mental life?[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S439][S440][S442]

## Compression sentence

> **Do not let access to or alteration of brain activity become a routine source of permission, persuasion, scrutiny, productivity, or compliance: where neurotechnology or neurodata makes thought, attention, emotion, or neural vulnerability newly governable, righteousness fails unless the use remains tightly bounded, genuinely consent-governed, privacy-hardened, medically or accessibility justified, and independently reviewable.**[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S93][S439][S440][S441][S442][S443][S444][S445]
