# Work, Livelihood, and No-Livelihood-by-Domination

## Why this surface matters

The archive already rejects forced labour, exploitation, anti-union suppression, inaccessible compliance, status-ranked standing, and destitution by sanction. What it still lacked was a compact rule for **ordinary work itself as a site where domination can hide without literal captivity** — where survival is made to depend on dangerous conditions, unlivable pay, discriminatory exclusion, blocked exit, or the practical impossibility of refusing managerial power without falling through the floor below.[S18][S19][S32][S34][S35][S41][S47][S49][S137][S138][S141][S142][S164][S222][S223][S246][S247][S249][S250]

That gap matters because unrighteousness in work is often laundered through the language of free contract, flexibility, productivity, or market realism. A job may be technically voluntary while still being structured so that workers must accept injury, degradation, exclusion, surveillance, unpaid waiting, retaliation, or union-breaking because the alternative is hunger, blacklisting, deportability, or family collapse. The archive needed a cleaner rule against that slide.[S18][S19][S32][S34][S35][S41][S47][S49][S66][S67][S93][S137][S138][S141][S142][S145][S146][S147][S164][S222][S223][S232][S233][S246][S247][S248][S249][S250]

## Working-canon thesis

> **Righteousness is burdened, and often defeated, when a person, office, or institution makes access to livelihood depend on work that is not meaningfully free to choose or leave, or on dangerous, degrading, discriminatorily closed, under-remunerated, union-broken, inaccessible, or rest-denying conditions mainly in order to cheapen labour, externalize risk, sort status, quiet complaint, or preserve managerial domination. Work is not exhausted by possession of a job. It is access to the opportunity to gain a living by work freely chosen or accepted, under just and favourable conditions, with fair remuneration, safe and healthy conditions, equal standing, and real room for worker voice and organization. Hiring standards, scheduling, licensing, discipline, and productivity demands may sometimes be justified, but only under non-discrimination, safe conditions, fair pay, bounded hours, meaningful rest, accommodation, associational freedom, and a realistic exit that does not collapse into survival blackmail.**[S137][S138][S141][S142][S164][S222][S223][S246][S247][S248][S249][S250]

## 1. The right to work is not a right to any job whatever

Work materials matter because they do not define the right merely as possession of employment. OHCHR's current work pages frame it as the opportunity to gain a living by work freely chosen or accepted, explicitly link article 6 to just and favourable conditions of work and trade-union rights, and treat the right as bound up with dignity and social justice rather than with raw labour-market placement alone.[S246][S247] That already blocks a shallow mistake. An arrangement does not become righteous merely because it puts people into paid activity.

The ILO's decent-work framing sharpens the point. Decent work is not just getting a job; it involves fair income, workplace security, social protection, and the freedom to express concerns and organize, while work that traps people in bondage, poverty, hazards, discrimination, or insecurity does not let them advance or fulfill their potential.[S249] So the archive should now reject a familiar laundering move: because someone is "employed," any complaint about wages, danger, exclusion, retaliation, or voice can be redescribed as ingratitude toward opportunity. Once livelihood is offered only on terms that treat workers as cheaply replaceable or practically unfree, the mere fact of employment no longer does the moral work that righteousness requires.[S18][S19][S32][S34][S35][S41][S47][S49][S137][S138][S141][S142][S164][S222][S223][S246][S247][S249]

## 2. Availability, accessibility, and quality constrain labour-market design

OHCHR's current framing is morally useful because it identifies availability, accessibility, and acceptability-and-quality as essential aspects of the right to work.[S247] Accessibility is not only a formal permission to apply. It includes non-discrimination, physical accessibility, and access to information about employment and its routes.[S247] That means the administrative path into work matters. Hiring screens, credential recognition, language policy, workplace design, transport assumptions, scheduling demands, probation rules, digital platforms, recruitment intermediaries, and documentation checks all become morally load-bearing once they decide who can actually reach and remain in work.[S66][S67][S93][S145][S146][S147][S232][S233][S247]

So the archive should now reject another laundering move: because the labour market is "open" in principle, those excluded by inaccessible design, discriminatory sorting, or document-heavy gatekeeping are treated as unfortunate edge cases rather than as persons structurally denied a central route into standing. A labour system that is nominally open but practically unusable for disabled workers, migrants with lawful claims, caregivers, poorer applicants, religious minorities, pregnant workers, rural workers, or those living under data and paperwork instability is not simply imperfect. It is governing access to livelihood through exclusionary design.[S18][S19][S32][S34][S35][S41][S66][S67][S93][S145][S146][S147][S232][S233][S247]

## 3. Just and favourable conditions of work are not optional extras

Article 7 of the Covenant matters because it does not treat wages, safety, rest, and equality as negotiable luxuries. It recognizes the right to just and favourable conditions of work including fair wages, equal remuneration for work of equal value, a decent living for workers and their families, safe and healthy working conditions, equal opportunity, and rest, leisure, and reasonable limitation of working hours.[S164] General Comment No. 23 matters for the same reason: it is the treaty-body anchor for the right to just and favourable conditions of work, rather than a soft moral preference.[S248] The ILO's current materials add that a safe and healthy working environment now sits within the framework of fundamental principles and rights at work.[S250]

That means the archive should reject the thought that danger, exhaustion, or below-floor pay become righteous so long as they are contractually agreed or widely normalized. Some work is hard, some scarcity is real, and some risk cannot be eliminated. But once the system predictably makes workers buy access to livelihood with injury, chronic overwork, humiliating dependency, or pay too thin for minimally decent living, the arrangement is no longer merely allocating market burdens. It is governing by making survival answer to domination through work.[S18][S19][S32][S34][S35][S41][S47][S49][S137][S138][S141][S142][S164][S248][S249][S250]

## 4. Exit, association, and complaint matter even when the job is not literally forced labour

This surface does **not** collapse all bad work into slavery or forced labour. The archive already has a narrower anti-captivity rule for cases of coercion, document retention, debt bondage, and blocked exit.[S137][S138][S140][S141][S142] But the work surface now adds a broader point: livelihood can still be governed by domination when formal exit exists but practical exit does not, or when workers may technically remain free individuals only on condition that they do not organize, complain, strike, or demand safer and fairer conditions.[S137][S138][S141][S142][S222][S223][S246][S247]

Association materials matter here because Convention No. 87 protects workers' organizations without prior authorization and from public-authority interference, while Convention No. 98 protects against anti-union discrimination and against interference or domination by employers.[S222][S223] So the archive should now reject another laundering move: because the worker can resign, managerial retaliation, blacklist risk, union-breaking, subcontracted fragmentation, wage theft, retaliatory dismissal, and complaint punishment are treated as external to the righteousness of work itself. Once a livelihood system makes worker voice, collective bargaining, or safety complaint practically career-ending or survival-threatening, it is not simply preserving flexibility. It is governing work through domination and silence.[S18][S19][S32][S34][S35][S41][S104][S137][S138][S141][S142][S222][S223][S249]

## 5. Working rule

When righteousness is claimed in a work or labour case, ask seven questions:
1. **What work claim was actually at stake?** Entry to work, equal access to hiring, safe conditions, fair remuneration, rest, accommodation, voice, collective organization, or the practical ability to leave?[S137][S141][S164][S222][S223][S246][S247][S248][S249][S250]
2. **What made the work genuinely free to choose or leave?** Could the worker realistically refuse, quit, transfer, organize, or complain without losing papers, pay already earned, housing, food, lawful status, or basic family stability?[S137][S138][S140][S141][S142][S164][S249]
3. **Which condition did the real domination work?** Wages, hours, safety hazards, surveillance, retaliation, blacklisting, staffing design, subcontracting, impossible targets, anti-union tactics, or inaccessible workplace and hiring design?[S66][S67][S93][S104][S137][S138][S141][S142][S222][S223][S232][S233][S247][S248][S249][S250]
4. **Did the arrangement preserve just and favourable conditions?** Fair remuneration, equal pay for work of equal value, safe and healthy conditions, rest, promotion without irrelevant discrimination, and accessibility are not marginal features.[S164][S232][S233][S247][S248][S250]
5. **Whose convenience was really being served?** Cheap labour, reputational display, anti-union quiet, manager-side flexibility, status sorting, migration leverage, or externalized injury should all raise standing alarm.[S18][S19][S32][S34][S35][S41][S137][S138][S141][S142][S222][S223][S249]
6. **What worker voice and remedy remained?** Could the worker organize, bargain, report danger, challenge discrimination, recover unpaid wages, and obtain correction before decisive harm hardened?[S93][S104][S137][S141][S222][S223][S246][S247][S248][S250]
7. **What redesign would remove domination without pretending work can be frictionless?** Safer staffing, pay floors, accessible design, predictable schedules, anti-retaliation enforcement, complaint channels, and real associational freedom all matter to remedy.[S104][S164][S222][S223][S247][S248][S249][S250]

## 6. Compression

> **Do not govern livelihood by making work depend on domination, danger, discriminatory exclusion, or conditions too degrading or insecure to refuse.** Righteousness may justify bounded standards, bounded supervision, and bounded productivity demands, but it is burdened when employment becomes the device by which people are made cheaply governable through fear of hunger, exclusion, retaliation, or unanswerable managerial power.[S137][S141][S142][S164][S222][S223][S246][S247][S248][S249][S250]
