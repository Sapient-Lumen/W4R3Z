# Manipulative AI, deceptive steering, and no-agency-by-stealth

## Core claim

> **Righteousness is burdened, and often defeated, when public or private actors deploy AI systems that use subliminal, purposefully manipulative, deceptive, or vulnerability-exploitative techniques so that impaired agency does the real work of getting a person to buy, disclose, attach, consent, comply, or otherwise behave as desired rather than leaving decision to overt, intelligible, reviewable, and contestable reasons.**[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S93][S254][S438][S452]

## 1. The missing moral question is when consent is architected rather than merely requested

The archive already rejected no-rightlessness-by-model, no-standing-by-score, no-thought-by-extraction, and exploitation without valid consent. But it still lacked a direct rule for a different temptation: **quietly shaping the conditions of choice so that a person's apparently voluntary act is already the product most of all of engineered impairment, dependence, or misleading direction**.[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49]

That gap matters because many contemporary systems do not need to issue an order or state an outright falsehood. They can tune timing, interface, affect, anthropomorphic attachment, scarcity pressure, personalization, or deceptive framing so that the person's own click, disclosure, subscription, confession, consent, or compliance becomes the visible event while the stronger party hides inside the architecture that made refusal or clear judgment harder.[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S93][S438][S452]

## 2. This is distinct from both ordinary persuasion and older coercion cases

The archive needs this as its own canon surface because the wrong is not exhausted either by overt coercion or by ordinary deceptive speech. No-body-by-compulsion, no-punishment-by-poverty, and no-suspicion-by-category already handle many cases where force, deprivation, or discriminatory exposure do the work. Truthfulness and nondeception handle cases where outright falsehood or misleading implication corrupts judgment. **No-agency-by-stealth asks a narrower but increasingly important question: when is the real lever the engineered weakening of informed and resistible choice itself?**[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S72][S73][S74][S93]

That distinction matters morally because a system may later point to the person's outward action — they clicked, subscribed, disclosed, kept chatting, bought the item, accepted the offer, or stayed in the loop — and call the case voluntary. But a righteousness inquiry has to ask what authored that action in practice. If the actor's practical power came mainly from subliminal cueing, manipulative dependency, deceptive framing, or exploited vulnerability, then the visible choice does not by itself vindicate the arrangement.[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S93][S254][S438][S452]

## 3. Current governance materials already treat some forms of agency-subverting AI as red-line practices

This is no longer only a philosophical worry about influence. The Commission's current AI Act materials list harmful AI-based manipulation and deception and harmful AI-based exploitation of vulnerabilities among the prohibited practices already in force.[S452] The AI Act Service Desk's current Article 5 materials sharpen the point by stating that prohibited systems include AI using subliminal techniques beyond a person's consciousness or purposefully manipulative or deceptive techniques that materially distort behaviour by appreciably impairing the person's ability to make an informed decision, thereby causing a decision they would not otherwise have taken in a manner causing or reasonably likely to cause significant harm.[S438] The same current Article 5 materials prohibit AI systems that exploit vulnerabilities due to age, disability, or a specific social or economic situation in ways that materially distort behaviour and cause or are reasonably likely to cause significant harm.[S438]

That matters because the line being drawn is not simply against inaccurate recommendation or annoying advertising. It marks out a class of uses where distorted agency itself becomes the method. The archive should learn from that: some systems are burdened not only because they produce bad outcomes, but because they seek those outcomes through impaired choice while preserving the appearance of voluntariness.[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S93][S438][S452]

## 4. Vulnerability exploitation is a standing defect, not just a bad-growth tactic

UNESCO's AI ethics recommendation widens the point beyond the narrow legal trigger. It treats human rights and dignity as the cornerstone of AI ethics and stresses fairness, human oversight, and particular attention to vulnerable groups.[S254] The archive should therefore not ask only whether a product converted well or whether the steering looked subtle. It should ask whose weakness, dependency, precarity, loneliness, age, disability, grief, or low-information condition made the steering work, and whether the design quietly treated that condition as usable leverage.[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S93][S254]

So righteousness should not let the language of engagement, support, or personalization launder systems that capitalize on impaired agency. A design can be frictionless and still be dominating. It can feel helpful and still be standing-defective if the person's practical ability to understand, refuse, pause, compare, or contest has been appreciably weakened precisely so the desired action becomes more likely.[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S93][S254][S438]

## 5. Righteousness should distinguish bounded support from agency-subverting steering

The deeper point is not that every persuasive technology is unrighteous. Some systems genuinely support safer driving, accessibility, learning, therapy, or comprehension. The narrower question is what the system is allowed to do to the chooser in order to obtain the result. Where the system preserves intelligibility, refusal, reversibility, proportionality, and review, support may remain bounded. But where it gains force by working around those conditions — through subliminal influence, manipulative anthropomorphism, deceptive presentation, exploitative personalization, or induced dependency — the actor is no longer mainly offering reasons. It is governing through no-agency-by-stealth.[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S93][S254][S438][S452]

## 6. Working rule

When an actor claims righteousness while relying on a behaviour-shaping AI system, ask seven questions:
1. **What decision was actually being targeted?** Purchase, disclosure, consent, emotional attachment, political action, financial commitment, prolonged engagement, or another standing-bearing choice?[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S93]
2. **What steering technique did the work?** Subliminal cueing, deceptive presentation, anthropomorphic dependency, scarcity or fear framing, exploitative personalization, or another design feature that made informed refusal harder?[S438][S452]
3. **What vulnerability was being leaned on?** Age, disability, specific social or economic precarity, crisis, grief, loneliness, authority asymmetry, low literacy, or another condition that predictably weakens autonomous choice?[S254][S438]
4. **What made the choice meaningfully informed and resistible?** Notice, explanation, time to reflect, reversibility, refusal without penalty, human review, and a genuine exit path from the steering environment?[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S93][S254]
5. **What significant harm was in view?** Financial loss, unsafe dependence, coerced disclosure, discriminatory targeting, civic distortion, self-harm risk, or another serious burden that the system could foreseeably induce?[S438][S452]
6. **What narrower route was available?** Ordinary explanation, user-controlled settings, neutral interface design, human counseling, non-exploitative reminders, or another route that did not depend on impaired agency?[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S93]
7. **Was impaired agency doing the real work?** Did the actor's practical advantage come mainly from having made the person's decision easier to bend while preserving the appearance of choice?[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S93][S254][S438][S452]

## Compression sentence

> **Do not let impaired choice masquerade as voluntary standing: where AI systems get disclosure, purchase, attachment, consent, or compliance mainly by subverting informed and resistible agency through subliminal, manipulative, deceptive, or vulnerability-exploitative design, righteousness fails unless the support remains intelligible, proportionate, refusal-safe, reversible, and meaningfully contestable.**[S34][S35][S41][S45][S47][S49][S93][S254][S438][S452]
