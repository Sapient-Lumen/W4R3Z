# Live Speculations

These claims are useful enough to keep, but not yet admitted into canon.

## QW-0001 — Righteousness may be the agentive face of justice and the temporal face of the good

This is an attractive compression:
- justice names the structure of due,
- goodness names the wider evaluative horizon,
- righteousness names the lived, temporally extended enactment of the due under real conditions.

Why quarantined:
- it may over-unify concepts that should remain partially independent;
- it may privilege Abrahamic and virtue-ethical resonances over other moral vocabularies.

## QW-0002 — Righteous repair may extend to inherited advantage and structural damage even without direct personal culpability

The canon now admits breach-responsive repair, but the strongest unresolved version is wider: present agents or institutions may owe reparative action because they stand inside wrongful benefit, damaged civic standing, or continuing structure, even when they did not personally commit the originating wrong.

Why quarantined:
- this can easily collapse responsibility, guilt, benefit, and standing into one bundle;
- some traditions may sharply distinguish repentance for one's own wrongs from duties arising from inherited institutions.

## QW-0003 — Righteousness may be a better bridge concept for AI-era moral governance than “alignment”

Possible reason:
- it joins duty, relation, power, restraint, and repair in a way “alignment” often does not.

Why quarantined:
- this archive has not yet done the necessary AI / governance comparison work.

## QW-0004 — Some public domains may justify a stronger fiduciary theory than the archive now admits

The canon now admits a qualified public-trust / nonownership cue for office. The stronger remaining speculation is that some public roles — perhaps judging, custodial administration, or management of segregated public assets — may warrant something closer to full fiduciary treatment rather than the archive's current qualified cue.

Why quarantined:
- the Yale critique and related work warn that fiduciary theorizing does not fit every public-law domain equally well;
- a stronger identity thesis could still over-import private-law structures into democratic and constitutional roles that answer to many principals at once.[S57][S58]


## QW-0005 — Some traditions may force outright rejection rather than analogue or incomplete identity

The canon now admits typed recognition statuses. The stronger remaining speculation is that some traditions will not merely treat the portable core as analogue, but will reject the translation as conceptually deformed.

Why quarantined:
- the archive has not yet done the tradition-by-tradition internal work needed to justify a strong rejection verdict;
- premature rejection could shrink the comparative program more than the present evidence warrants.


## QW-0006 — There may be a portable lexical order among claim-types

One stronger possibility behind the new conflict surface is that righteousness may eventually need a cross-tradition lexical order, perhaps giving durable priority to non-domination, protection from grave harm, repair of one's own wrongs, and role-borne fiduciary duties over thinner gains.

Why quarantined:
- current evidence supports portable priority cues, but not yet a single stable ranking;
- lexical ordering may be too rigid for traditions that treat context-sensitive judgment as irreducible.
