## What this revision does

- preserves the starter canon;
- adds a canon surface on student monitoring, remote proctoring, and no-learning-by-panopticon;
- clarifies that righteousness is burdened when suspicion-heavy watching, recording, behavioural flagging, or provider self-use of student data do the real work of educational trust, discipline, or participation rather than leaving education governed by bounded pedagogical purpose, minimization, accessible challenge, and human review;
- sharpens overview, working-theory, office, method, and failure-mode surfaces around the difference between bounded educational monitoring and governance through no-learning-by-panopticon;
- adds seven compact official source additions on current Commission, UNESCO, FTC, ICO, and U.S. Department of Education materials for educational monitoring, student-data governance, and rights-bearing learning conditions;
- improves archive hygiene by threading the new canon surface through route, registry, open questions, assumption ledger, receipt, and release metadata.
