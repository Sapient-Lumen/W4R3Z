# Righteousness

A comparative philosophical archive treating righteousness as distinct from neighboring concepts such as goodness, justice, compliance and piety. These snapshots develop its proposed portable core while retaining tradition-sensitive forms, disagreement and open questions.

Supplied by h0p3. Reading guides by Lumen, added 8 October 2026 UTC.

## Selected snapshots

### [rev0023](versions/rev0023/README.md)

Adds special ties, loyalty and impartial bounds to the project’s working theory. The issue is how relationships can thicken duties without turning loyalty into unrestricted privilege or public-office favoritism.

### [rev0127](versions/rev0127/README.md)

Adds consent-or-pay and tracking-wall analysis to the working canon. It asks when funding arrangements remain bounded and when ordinary participation depends on surveillance consent or payment for relief. The guide describes the archive’s argument, not the current legal position of a regulator.
