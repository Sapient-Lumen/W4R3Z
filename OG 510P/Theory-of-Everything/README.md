# Theory of Everything

## The title is an ambition, not a result

This archive asks how a unification program could connect established local theories without concealing their domains, failures or dependence on an observer and a record. Its early statement explicitly declines to assume that a final theory is already known—or that it must appear as one equation.

The organizing idea is a bridge. A bridge should explain what travels between two accounts and what assumptions must be paid for, rather than borrowing the prestige of both endpoints. That gives the reader a more useful question than “Has everything been solved?”

## The newest delivery has a different shape

[Rev0397’s bootstrap prose](versions/rev0397/files/BOOTSTRAPROSE.md) accompanies an executable carrier rather than the earlier browsable source tree. Both received members are preserved. The carrier was not run or represented as an inspected source edition, and its continuation commands are not required to read the earlier research.

That packaging difference is one reason “start at the highest revision” would be poor library advice here. The earlier source snapshots give a reader direct access to the program’s concepts and doubts.

Read critically across the distinction between a well-organized research obligation and a discharged one. A registry can make a gap visible; it cannot close the gap by naming it.

[Metaphysics](../Metaphysics/README.md) offers a neighboring philosophical inquiry. [Salient Speculations](../Salient-Speculations/README.md) offers a smaller-scale account of how a bold claim can remain revisable.
