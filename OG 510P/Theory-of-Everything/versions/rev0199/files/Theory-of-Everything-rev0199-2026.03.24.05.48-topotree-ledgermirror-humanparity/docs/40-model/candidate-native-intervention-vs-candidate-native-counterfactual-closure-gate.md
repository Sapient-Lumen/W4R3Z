# Candidate-native-intervention versus candidate-native-counterfactual-closure gate

Purpose: split the archive's **witness-package progress** one step further.
A candidate should not earn the same witness credit merely because it can **say, in its own bridge variables, what it means to prepare the relevant evidential state, choose and trigger the interaction, condition on an outcome, and deliberately write, perturb, reroute, or erase the record** and because it can **also say, in those same variables, what other interventions are available, when two interventions count as the same or different across branches / frames / labs, and what contrastive record classes should follow from experiments of our choosing**.
Those are related, but they are not the same achievement.

## Compression verdict

Inside the archive's witness package, once a lane has already moved beyond fresh-host reinstantiation, independent implementation, independent evidence generation, candidate-native witness language, and candidate-native intervention language, the current score should now distinguish eight nested layers:

1. **public evidence-custody closure** — the decisive evidence bundle or reference object is stably extractable, versioned, fixity-checkable, preservable, citable, and reopenable beyond one privileged repository or operator chain;
2. **fresh-host reinstantiation closure** — outsiders can take that public bundle, identify the exact workflow / software / environment objects it depends on, materialize an executable environment on a fresh host or stack, and rerun or rehydrate the evidential process under declared equivalence rules without privileged manual setup;
3. **independent implementation closure** — outsiders can implement the relevant workflow semantics, protocol, model, or measurement logic through a genuinely separate code lineage, test it against a public conformance or agreement criterion, and still recover the same evidential class without carrying the originating executable bundle itself;
4. **independent evidence-generation closure** — outsiders can obtain the same evidential class through separately acquired, separately prepared, or blind / holdout record-generation routes, interlaboratory comparison pathways, or independent measurement chains rather than only through multiple implementations over one inherited evidence source;
5. **candidate-native witness closure** — the candidate's own bridge variables name the record carrier, stabilization story, reread path, and objectivity / publicity conditions closely enough that the empirical loop is no longer mostly redescribed in borrowed detector-side or boundary-side language;
6. **candidate-native intervention closure** — the candidate's own bridge variables also say what counts as preparing, perturbing, selectively updating, branching, writing, steering, or erasing those evidence-bearing states rather than leaving experiment-choice semantics in ordinary external apparatus language;
7. **candidate-native counterfactual closure** — the candidate's own bridge variables also define a stable menu of admissible alternative interventions, say when two interventions are the same or different across branches / frames / labs, and commit to contrastive record classes for experiments of our choosing rather than only one showcased intervention path;
8. **local public witness closure** — a lane can say where the physical records live, what stabilizes them, how they are reread, how they are prepared and deliberately manipulated, which alternative interventions are really available and how they differ, what those alternatives would change in the record, and why that empirical loop is not mainly outsourced.

A lane may earn real credit on layer 6 while still being weak on layer 7.
That is progress.
It is just not yet candidate-native counterfactual closure, much less full local public witness closure.

## Why this gate exists

Shared witness-gate scaffolding now lives in `docs/40-model/witness-closure-gate-family-frame.md`. This document only names the pair-specific inflation move below.

**native preparation / update / write language being retold as if the candidate therefore already closes the space of experiments of our choosing and their contrastive outcomes.**

That inflation can happen in at least seven ways:
- a lane can describe one native detector or probe coupling while the space of alternative probes, settings, or preparation branches still lives in ordinary lab-side or EFT-side language;
- a lane can say how one realized intervention updates the state while saying little about how different allowed interventions would have been represented in the same native variables;
- a lane can import the sameness or difference of interventions across branches / frames / labs from external coordinates, apparatus labels, or analyst convention rather than from the candidate itself;
- a lane can exhibit one successful observer-relative or subregion-relative measurement while the candidate-wide intervention menu remains underdescribed;
- a lane can redescribe one performed experiment in native variables while all counterfactual experimental design remains parasitic on an external theory of the lab;
- a lane can define local operators or observer algebras while the contrastive outcome map for alternative measurements stays implicit or imported;
- or a lane can rely on one especially favorable construction where intervention works, without paying for a general account of which alternatives are possible, equivalent, forbidden, or empirically different.

## The five-step candidate-native-counterfactual test

### 1. Native menu semantics, not just one realized intervention

Before awarding candidate-native-counterfactual credit, the archive should ask whether the candidate says what counts as an **admissible family of alternative interventions** in its own variables rather than merely redescribing one realized intervention.
At minimum, a lane should disclose:
- which native knobs, sectors, subalgebras, sources, probe couplings, or state-preparation branches are being varied,
- which alternatives the candidate says are genuinely distinct rather than notationally different,
- which parts of that experimental menu still live in ordinary apparatus or coordinate language,
- and what would make an attempted alternative intervention ill-posed or physically unavailable.

A lane that wins only here still earns **candidate-native intervention credit**, not yet candidate-native counterfactual closure.

### 2. Native sameness / difference criterion for interventions

A lane earns more than intervention credit only when the candidate explains what makes two interventions count as the **same** or **different** experiment in native terms across branches, frames, or labs.
At minimum, the archive should ask:
- what native comparison rule identifies an intervention across different branches or reference choices,
- whether that identification is exact, approximate, frame-relative, or regime-limited,
- how the candidate avoids smuggling sameness back in through external labels or human intent,
- and what breakdown regime makes intervention identity cease to be well-defined.

Without that route, apparent native actuation may still inherit its decisive comparison standard from outside the candidate.

### 3. Native contrastive outcome map

A lane earns stronger candidate-native-counterfactual credit only when alternative interventions lead to an explicit **contrastive record map** in the candidate's own variables.
At minimum, the archive should ask:
- what different coarse-grained record classes should result from different admissible interventions,
- whether those differences are exact, approximate, effective, or branch-relative,
- how the candidate expresses the contrast without collapsing back into ordinary external detector lore,
- and which parts of the counterfactual outcome map are still borrowed from EFT-side or lab-side reasoning.

Without those answers, the lane may have native actuation but not a native theory of what alternative experimental choices would actually change.

### 4. Native infeasibility and equivalence boundary

A lane earns still stronger candidate-native-counterfactual credit only when it says which apparent alternatives are **forbidden, equivalent, or under-resolved** in native terms.
At minimum, the archive should ask:
- which interventions are impossible because of gauge, constraint, backreaction, or observer-capacity limits,
- which alternative-looking interventions are actually the same up to native symmetry or frame change,
- which branches or contexts lose the ability to compare interventions cleanly,
- and what empirical differences would expose an error in that infeasibility / equivalence story.

Without that boundary, a candidate can look richer than it is by quietly borrowing an unrestricted experiment menu from ordinary lab imagination.

### 5. Native-to-lab experimental design bridge must be explicit

A lane earns **candidate-native counterfactual closure** credit only when later users can connect actual laboratory design choices back to one native counterfactual story.
At minimum, it should name:
- the native menu of admissible interventions,
- the native sameness / difference criterion for comparing interventions,
- the native contrastive outcome map,
- the native infeasibility / equivalence boundary,
- the mapping from those native structures to actual external experimental choices,
- and the residual debt to full local public witness closure.

This is still not the same thing as full local public witness closure.
A lane may support candidate-native counterfactual closure quite well while still depending on ordinary laboratory infrastructure for the final public record ecology.
But that is a much smaller debt than merely having one native intervention story while borrowing the whole counterfactual design space from outside.

## Common failure modes

1. **one-off showcase inflation** — one native intervention path is retold as if it already closes the whole experiment menu.
2. **external-menu smuggling** — the decisive alternative settings or probe families still live in ordinary lab language outside the candidate.
3. **branch-identity laundering** — what counts as the same intervention across branches or frames is inherited from external coordinates or analyst convention.
4. **contrastive-silence** — the candidate can describe one update but not what different admissible choices would change in the record.
5. **unbounded-design rhetoric** — the argument talks as if every imagined intervention were available, without naming native infeasibility or equivalence limits.
6. **special-construction overcrediting** — one observer or subregion model yields native action semantics, but the candidate-wide counterfactual menu remains unpaid.
7. **theory-external experiment fallback** — when pressure rises, the account reverts to ordinary experimental design language, revealing that the candidate's own contrastive menu is not yet closed.

## Compact current readout

- **Witten's observer-algebra work** makes the split visible by separating what an observer can see from what the observer can both see and influence, which means a native observable algebra is weaker than a native account of alternative interventions.
- **Harlow, Usatyuk, and Zhao's closed-universe observer framework** sharpens the burden by asking what predicts the likely outcome of an experiment of our choosing inside a closed universe, making clear that native update language is weaker than a native counterfactual experiment menu.
- **Vilasini, Chen, Ye, and Renner's lab-relative event framework** shows that localization claims can depend on the choice of Lab reference and that distinct assumptions on the Labs alter conclusions about agents' interventions, so intervention success is weaker than closure of intervention identity and alternatives.
- **Kabel, de la Hamette, Apadula, Cepollaro, Gomes, Butterfield, and Brukner's quantum-coordinate analysis** shows that a choice of QRF supplies the comparison map by which events count as the same or different across branches, which matters directly for whether alternative interventions have a native identity criterion.
- **Geng's quantum rods and clock construction** shows real progress toward local operators for a local experimentalist, but a native operator construction is still weaker than a native menu of alternative experimental choices and contrastive record commitments.

That does not refute current bridge programs or completion bids.
It just means they have not yet paid this stronger candidate-native-counterfactual debt.

## Net rule

Within the archive:
- candidate-native intervention closure earns real witness-package credit;
- but it does **not** yet count as candidate-native counterfactual closure;
- and candidate-native counterfactual closure does **not** yet count as full local public witness closure.

Any future completion bid or bridge-family claim that wants stronger witness credit should now say not only how the candidate natively prepares, triggers, updates, and manipulates one evidential path, but also how the candidate's own bridge variables define the admissible alternative intervention menu, the sameness / difference criterion for those interventions, and the contrastive record classes for experiments of our choosing without collapsing back into borrowed laboratory design language.
