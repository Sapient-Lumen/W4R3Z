## Why this document exists

The archive already blocks two cheap moves:
- family B may not widen by non-equilibrium rescue vocabulary unless it cashes out as coarse-grained traces with real witness channels,
- family C may not widen by entanglement rhetoric unless quantity, split, regime, observable content, and record carriers are explicit.

What was still missing was a **shared score surface** for the remaining debt.
The key debt is not just “records are hard.”
The sharper question is:

> how much of a candidate's empirical contact is carried in its own bridge language, and how much is still imported from an already-familiar laboratory or boundary description?

That is what the witness borrowing ladder measures.

## Minimal definition

A candidate is **borrowing witness structure** whenever its claimed observables or records remain intelligible only because ordinary matter-side detectors, boundary variables, environments, or memory chains are doing the actual stabilizing and rereading work.

Borrowing is not automatically bad.
It becomes decisive when a candidate is advertised as deeper than its rivals while still outsourcing the empirical loop to them.

## Ladder levels

### `WB-0` — External witness import

The candidate names a formal quantity or geometric relation, but stable records live entirely in ordinary external detector or matter language.
The bridge itself does not yet say how those records are carried, stabilized, or reread.

### `WB-1` — Trace-tagged borrowing

The candidate now points to explicit coarse-grained traces or witness channels.
This is better than pure slogan, but the stabilizing carriers are still imported from an external matter/environment description rather than supplied by the candidate's own bridge variables.

### `WB-2` — Structured interface borrowing

The candidate names a disciplined interface between its native variables and the place where records live.
Observable content is no longer free-floating, and the interface is specific enough to constrain what counts as evidence.
But persistence, rereadability, and objectivity are still largely inherited from the borrowed side.

### `WB-3` — Partial native witness structure

Some part of record stabilization, redundancy, or rereadability is now expressed in the candidate's own language rather than merely imported.
The candidate still does not fully close the loop, but it has begun to reduce the borrowing debt in a real way.

### `WB-4` — Candidate-native witness closure

Record carriers, stabilization, rereadability, and objectivity are all specified in the candidate's own bridge language closely enough that the empirical loop is no longer mainly outsourced.
No active archive family has earned this level.

## What would count as a real climb?

A family climbs the ladder only when all of the following become more explicit than before:

1. **record carrier** — where the evidence-bearing distinction physically lives,
2. **stabilization story** — what keeps it persistent long enough to function as evidence,
3. **reread path** — how later interactions recover the same coarse-grained fact,
4. **objectivity condition** — whether the same fact is redundantly accessible beyond one privileged memory chain, whether the comparison still depends on a silently borrowed public reference or adequacy standard, whether the same evidence remains reidentifiable across time rather than only in one episode, and whether the evidential claim can be reinstantiated across independent sites or stacks rather than only inside one inherited chain,
5. **control condition** — whether the same evidential state can be deliberately written, steered, conditionally rerouted, stabilized, or erased across independent sites under public actuation and feedback rules rather than merely re-read reproducibly,
6. **auditability condition** — whether the reasons, thresholds, state context, and action lineage behind those interventions are public enough for outside audit, replay, and contest rather than preserved only as private operator memory or opaque logs,
7. **assertibility condition** — whether categorical public claims are issued only under publicly inspectable and contestable entitlement artifacts, declared scope, and mandatory abstention or undetermined rules rather than fluent summary or hidden assert / deny thresholds,
8. **adjudication condition** — whether outside challengers can trigger a bounded review horizon, recover the frozen replay or comparison bundle, force reruns or comparison checks under declared rules, and obtain public uphold / replace / withdraw / undetermined / inconclusive outcomes rather than only post-hoc commentary,
9. **public-custody condition** — whether the evidence-bearing bundle, comparison artifact, or reference object can be extracted, fixity-checked, versioned, preserved, cited, and later reopened beyond one privileged host rather than only challenged through that host,
10. **fresh-host-reinstantiation condition** — whether the public bundle also carries enough executable workflow identity, software identity, environment materialization, and rerun-equivalence structure for outsiders to reinstantiate the evidential process on fresh infrastructure rather than merely possess its frozen artifacts,
11. **independent-implementation condition** — whether outsiders can derive a genuinely separate implementation from public semantics, test it against a public conformance clause, and recover the same evidential class without carrying the originating executable lineage itself,
12. **independent-evidence-generation condition** — whether those implementations or comparison routes are then challenged on separately acquired, separately prepared, or blind / holdout record streams with declared comparison rules rather than merely on one inherited evidence source,
13. **candidate-native witness condition** — whether those fresh evidence routes can be connected back to record carriers, stabilization, rereadability, and publicity conditions named in the candidate's own bridge language rather than only in external detector-side or boundary-side prose,
14. **candidate-native intervention condition** — whether those native carriers can also be prepared, triggered, selectively updated, routed, stabilized, or erased in the candidate's own bridge language rather than only by imported apparatus-side semantics,
15. **candidate-native counterfactual condition** — whether those native interventions come with an internally specified menu of alternative experimental choices, a native sameness / difference criterion across branches or frames, and contrastive predicted record classes rather than only one showcased read / write path,
16. **candidate-native threshold condition** — whether those native alternatives also come with candidate-native resolution scales, tolerance / coarse-graining rules, uncertainty budgets, and under-resolution boundaries that say when apparent differences count as evidence rather than approximation chatter,
17. **candidate-native identifiability condition** — whether those native thresholds are paired with a candidate-native account of which underlying states, couplings, geometries, frame-choices, or histories are actually learnable, which remain gauge-equivalent or empirically collapsed, and when inversion from record classes is incomplete or unstable,
18. **candidate-native content** — which of the above are expressed in the candidate's own bridge language rather than merely in imported lab-side prose.

Extra equations, extra entropy terms, or a wider dictionary do **not** count by themselves.

## Immediate archive use

### For family B

A stronger non-equilibrium family-B claim should count as progress only if it can move from `WB-1` toward `WB-2` by specifying more than borrowed dissipative traces.
It must say what in the candidate's own bridge language narrows the gap to witness stabilization.
Otherwise it remains better bookkeeping, not better closure.

### For family C

A stronger reconstruction claim should count as progress only if it can move from `WB-2` toward `WB-3` by reducing boundary borrowing on records or objectivity.
A broader geometric dictionary alone does not qualify.
`docs/40-model/family-c-witness-closure-gate.md` now spells out the minimum five-part family-C test before any future family-C result may claim that climb.

### For promotion

No bridge family should be promoted while stuck at a level whose empirical loop is mainly outsourced.
A beautiful bridge with `WB-0` or `WB-1` status may still be important, but it is not yet close to archive-level closure.

## Failure modes to keep blocked

Do not let this ladder be used to imply:
- that borrowing is shameful rather than diagnostic,
- that family D has already solved the full outcome problem,
- that boundary observables are unreal because they are borrowed,
- or that a candidate earns a climb merely by renaming the imported witness side.

## Net result

The archive now has a compact answer to the question "what would it mean to reduce witness borrowing?"
That answer is no longer just rhetorical.
It is an eighteen-part ladder that can be applied to any future bridge claim before the archive grants promotion credit.
