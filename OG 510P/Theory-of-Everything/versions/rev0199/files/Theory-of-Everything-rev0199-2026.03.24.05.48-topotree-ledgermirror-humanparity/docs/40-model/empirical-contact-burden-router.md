## Shared rule

Empirical contact should be scored in order:
- first by fixing the minimum physical conditions for record, rereadability, and objectivity,
- then by asking how much witness burden is still being borrowed rather than carried natively,
- and only then by asking which witness-package closure and identifiability burdens have actually been reduced rather than merely renamed.

A record criterion is not yet a lower witness-borrowing score.
A lower witness-borrowing score is not yet witness-package closure.
A stronger witness-package route is not yet candidate-native identifiability closure.

Stable, restart, program, and current-head surfaces should therefore name this router when they only need the archive's broad empirical-contact architecture.
For integrated local-law / cosmological-package / witness-package burden routing, route onward through `docs/40-model/broad-toe-credit-router.md` or `docs/40-model/current-head-control-router.md` instead.
Do not replay `observer-record-minimum.md`, `witness-borrowing-ladder.md`, and `witness-package-burden-router.md` as separate broad-surface obligations unless the revision is actually changing one of those subordinate surfaces. Pairwise witness-closure gates should point broad empirical-contact prerequisites here plus `docs/40-model/witness-closure-gate-family-frame.md` rather than restating the record-minimum and borrowing surfaces gate by gate.
