# Candidate bridges

Read this document together with `docs/40-model/invariant-matrix.md` and `docs/40-model/discriminator-wedges.md`. For broad stable / restart / program routing across that cross-family trio, use `docs/40-model/cross-family-pressure-router.md` rather than replaying the three surfaces separately.
The bridge families below are not scorecard winners; they are compact live hypotheses or pressure lanes.
For the broader atlas of UV-completion bids and diagnostic / reconstruction tools, use `docs/40-model/uv-completion-atlas.md`; not every powerful formalism belongs in this bridge-family list.

## Bridge family A — EFT-first gravity baseline

- Core thought: treat gravity as a quantum EFT at accessible energies, and do not mistake lack of UV completion for lack of low-energy rigor.
- Strength: preserves known physics cleanly.
- Pressure: does not by itself solve the deep microstructure question.
- Key refs: `REF-0005`, `REF-0006`, `REF-0014`
- Current posture: accepted baseline, not full answer.

## Bridge family B — Thermodynamic / entropy gravity

- Core thought: some geometric dynamics may be reconstructible from entropy and thermodynamic consistency.
- Strength: links geometry to horizon and entropy data in a sharp way.
- Pressure: interpretation may outrun the derivation if used carelessly.
- Key refs: `REF-0007`, `REF-0008`, `REF-0009`, `REF-0018`
- Current posture: live bridge family with one canonical broad control route through `docs/40-model/family-b-burden-router.md`, which now keeps the equilibrium assumption audit, bounded non-equilibrium trace / witness rule, explicit `WB-1` no-climb audit, and minimum beyond-equilibrium gain gate in one ordered lane; see that router plus `docs/40-model/witness-borrowing-ladder.md` for the current reading.

## Bridge family C — Holography / entanglement geometry

- Core thought: gravitational and nongravitational descriptions may be dual charts, with entanglement supplying part of the bridge.
- Strength: unusually concrete geometry–information connections.
- Pressure: translation beyond highly symmetric settings is incomplete.
- Key refs: `REF-0011`, `REF-0012`, `REF-0013`, `REF-0019`, `REF-0020`
- Current posture: live bridge family with admission checklist, first concrete RT-style audit, first bounded surplus-over-thermodynamics result, first covariant-carry audit beyond static RT, first stronger-reconstruction no-climb audit, one broad family-C control route through `docs/40-model/family-c-burden-router.md`, and a current `WB-2` witness-borrowing placement installed; see `docs/40-model/entanglement-admission-checklist.md`, `docs/40-model/rt-claim-audit.md`, `docs/40-model/entanglement-thermodynamic-surplus-audit.md`, `docs/40-model/covariant-regime-stress-audit.md`, `docs/40-model/family-c-stronger-reconstruction-audit.md`, `docs/40-model/family-c-burden-router.md`, and `docs/40-model/witness-borrowing-ladder.md`.

## Archive-wide boundary/law discipline

Across all families, use `docs/40-model/local-law-vs-cosmological-boundary-split.md` to keep local-law gains separate from global boundary/state package gains.
No family should get generic local-dynamics credit for a result that only lands inside a special asymptotic, vacuum, or measure package, and no family should get cosmological-package credit merely for recovering local EFT or semiclassical dynamics once a global package is fixed.

## Bridge family D — Measurement / nonlocality pressure

- Core thought: any deep theory must face Bell-type quantum correlation structure and explain how records and classical evidence remain possible.
- Strength: keeps empirical contact central.
- Pressure: often under-integrated into unification talk.
- Key refs: `REF-0010`, `REF-0015`, `REF-0016`
- Current posture: underbuilt but necessary; strongest current archive lane on witness stabilization and rereadability, but not yet a stand-alone gravity bridge.
