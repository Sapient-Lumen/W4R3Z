## Shared rule

A completion bid should earn broad archive credit only in order:
- first by being scored as a **completion bid** rather than a bridge family or tool lane,
- then by paying the five-axis cash-out sieve,
- then by surviving the ordered post-cash-out comparison ladder,
- and only then by asking what local-law, cosmological-package, or witness-package debt has actually been reduced.

Stable, restart, and program surfaces should therefore name this router when they only need the established completion-bid control architecture.
Do not replay the atlas, sieve, stack, and shared gate-family frame separately unless the revision is actually changing one of those surfaces.
If the issue is integrated broad ToE credit rather than completion-bid pressure specifically, route through `docs/40-model/broad-toe-credit-router.md` instead.
