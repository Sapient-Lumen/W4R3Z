# UV-completion atlas

Purpose: keep `WS-0006` honest by separating **completion bids**, **bridge families**, and **diagnostic / reconstruction tools**.
Read this together with `docs/40-model/completion-bid-credit-stack.md`: the atlas keeps role sorting upstream of cash-out, ordered comparison, shared gate scaffolding, and broad three-book ToE credit. Use the credit stack when a surface only needs the established completion-bid control route rather than a hand-replayed ladder.

The archive already carries bridge families `A`-`D` in `docs/40-model/candidate-bridges.md`.
This surface does **not** widen that family list.
It does something narrower: it prevents framework names from being compared as though they were all the same type of explanatory object.

## Sorting rule

- **Completion bid** — proposes a candidate ultraviolet completion or deep substrate for gravity plus quantum theory.
- **Bridge family** — explains, translates, or reconstructs one hard interface between known theories, but does not yet claim full completion.
- **Diagnostic / reconstruction tool** — sharply constrains theory space or observables, but is not yet a standalone ontology.

A lane may occupy more than one role in the long run, but the archive should score only its **earned current role**.

## Archive consequences

1. **Do not count a tool as an ontology.**
   Bootstrap, amplitude, or positive-geometry success is important, but current success there should raise pressure on other families before it is scored as a full ToE.

2. **Do not count a completion bid as a bridge win or archive-wide solution it has not yet cashed out.**
   A framework may be ambitious enough to be a ToE candidate while still being weak on witness closure, cosmological burden, low-energy uniqueness, or even basic observable cash-out. Use `docs/40-model/completion-bid-cashout-sieve.md` before scoring broader progress.

3. **Keep current family scores unchanged unless a lane changes a shared pressure surface.**
   This surface is a sorting discipline, not a hidden scoreboard rewrite.

4. **Route concrete bridge claims back to the active families.**
   Entanglement / geometry claims still belong with family `C`; entropy / spacetime claims still belong with family `B`; record closure still routes through family `D` and the witness ladder.

## Current archive posture

- Family `A` remains the non-negotiable low-energy baseline.
- Families `B` and `C` remain the live bridge lanes.
- Family `D` remains the record / witness pressure lane.
- `WS-0006` now has a compact sorter for the broader atlas so completion bids, bridge families, and tool lanes are no longer compared under one blurred heading.

## Use rule

Use this document when a future revision is tempted to say that a program is “closest to a ToE.”
Before any such claim is allowed, ask three separate questions:

1. Is this lane currently earning credit as a **completion bid**, a **bridge**, or a **tool**?
2. Which invariant or wedge does it actually improve?
3.
