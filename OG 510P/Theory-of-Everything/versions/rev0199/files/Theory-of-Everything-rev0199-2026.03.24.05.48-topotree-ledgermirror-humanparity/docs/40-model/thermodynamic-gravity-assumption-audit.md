## Compression verdict

The strongest current reading is narrow but important:
- thermodynamic gravity is a **serious derivational clue** about Einstein-like dynamics under local equilibrium assumptions,
- it is **not yet** a clean replacement for the EFT gravity baseline,
- and attempts to widen the derivation typically pay extra assumptions or move into non-equilibrium territory.

This keeps bridge family B alive, but blocks premature promotion.

## What the classic results actually buy

### 1. Bekenstein supplies pressure, not a full derivation

`REF-0007` makes horizon entropy non-optional for deep theory-building.
It does not by itself derive gravitational field equations, but it makes the entropy lane scientifically load-bearing.

### 2. Wald gives a precise entropy map inside diffeomorphism-invariant theories

`REF-0008` shows that black-hole entropy can be expressed as a Noether charge in a broad class of gravitational theories.
This is powerful because it ties entropy, symmetry, and geometry together without reducing entropy talk to metaphor.
But by itself it does **not** show that thermodynamics uniquely determines the dynamics.

### 3. Jacobson derives Einstein dynamics only under a specific equilibrium package

`REF-0009` derives the Einstein equation as an equation of state by imposing a Clausius relation on all local Rindler horizons through each spacetime point.
The operative package includes: local causal horizons, Unruh temperature, entropy proportional to horizon area, and a local-equilibrium setup around the chosen point.
That is strong evidence that geometry and thermodynamics are tightly linked.
It is weaker evidence that gravity is *nothing but* thermodynamics.
For the archive's bounded second pass on what happens once this equilibrium package is widened, see `docs/40-model/non-equilibrium-observable-record-map.md`.

### 4. Generalization beyond the equilibrium package is costly

`REF-0018` shows that curvature-corrected entropy of the relevant sort requires a **non-equilibrium** treatment with entropy production terms.
That matters because it means the simplest equilibrium story does not freely scale into broader gravitational dynamics without new structure.

## Assumption table

| Assumption | Why it matters | Failure mode if overstated |
|---|---|---|
| Local Rindler / causal horizon construction | creates the thermodynamic arena point-by-point | can be retold as if global spacetime already had a built-in fluid interpretation |
| Unruh temperature for accelerated observers | supplies the temperature entering the Clausius relation | can be treated as if thermality were fundamental rather than observer/regime dependent |
| Entropy proportional to area or controlled local entropy density | turns horizon change into a state variable | can be inflated into a claim that all geometry is already explained by entropy alone |
| Local equilibrium at the chosen point | allows the clean Einstein-equation derivation | often omitted in summaries that present the result as more universal than it is |
| Heat flux identified with matter/energy crossing the horizon | couples stress-energy to thermodynamic bookkeeping | can smuggle in the desired dynamical content while pretending it emerged from entropy alone |
| Integrability / conservation requirements in wider theories | keeps the generalized equations coherent | forces extra entropy-production or constitutive terms once the simple setup is left |

## What the audit rules out

This audit rejects three common overclaims:

1. **Overclaim:** Wald + Jacobson already show that gravity is fully emergent from thermodynamics.
   - Correction: they show a deep thermodynamic/variational relation, not a complete microphysical replacement theory.

2. **Overclaim:** the thermodynamic lane outruns the EFT baseline.
   - Correction: the EFT baseline still carries low-energy recovery more cleanly and with fewer interpretive leaps.

3. **Overclaim:** all entropy-first generalizations preserve the same clean equilibrium logic.
   - Correction: the moment curvature corrections widen the story, non-equilibrium structure appears explicitly.

## Archive-state consequence

For `W-0001`, the current archive should hold:
- family A remains the accepted baseline for `INV-0001`, `INV-0002`, and `INV-0004`,
- family B remains a live bridge family with real explanatory promise on `INV-0005`,
- but family B is **not promoted** beyond “live bridge” until it shows what is gained beyond equilibrium reconstruction clues while staying honest about records, observables, and low-energy recovery.

## Promotion / demotion triggers

### Promote B only if
- a thermodynamic derivation cleanly states its equilibrium domain,
- shows what extra dynamics or structure it earns beyond the EFT baseline,
- and does not outsource records/observables to an external classical cut.

### Demote B if
- future use keeps citing Jacobson-style derivations as if they were already a complete microphysical account,
- or if every apparent gain over family A turns out to be interpretive rhetoric rather than new recovery power.

## Net result

Thermodynamic gravity remains a **valuable clue family**.
Its current strongest status is not “winner,” but **disciplined pressure source with partial derivational success**.
