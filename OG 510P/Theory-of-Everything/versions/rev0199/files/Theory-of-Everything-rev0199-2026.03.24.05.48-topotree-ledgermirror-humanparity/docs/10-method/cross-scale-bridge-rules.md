# Cross-scale bridge rules

A real bridge claim must connect successful descriptions across scale, regime, or ontology without handwaving over the hard parts.

## Minimum requirements

A bridge claim should say:
1. what entities or structures are fundamental in that bridge,
2. what counts as observable versus gauge / redundancy,
3. how known low-energy structure is recovered,
4. where entropy or information enters if it is claimed to be load-bearing,
5. how classicality / records / measurement emerge or are represented,
6. and what would count against the bridge.

## Failure modes

Reject or quarantine bridge claims that:
- replace derivation with metaphor,
- treat “emergence” as a synonym for “unexplained,”
- hide observational content behind formal redundancy,
- or skip the recovery of effective field theory and semiclassical gravity.
