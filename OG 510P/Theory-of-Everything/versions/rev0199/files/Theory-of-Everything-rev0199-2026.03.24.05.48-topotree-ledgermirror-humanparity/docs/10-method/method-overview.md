# Method overview

This archive treats theory-of-everything research as a problem of **disciplined compression under cross-scale pressure**.

The job is not to collect every idea. The job is to preserve the smallest set of distinctions that keeps the live scientific problem visible.

## Method commitments

1. **Baseline before novelty.** Any candidate must answer to established low-energy success.
2. **Invariants before metaphors.** Track what must be recovered before debating what the world “really is.”
3. **Bridge claims before worldview claims.** A bridge claim says how one successful regime connects to another.
4. **Quarantine before inflation.** Keep bold ideas live without laundering them into canon.
5. **Compression with replayability.** Reduce text while preserving the ability to reconstruct the next good move.

## Canonical decomposition

- baseline constraints,
- invariants,
- candidate bridge families,
- open discriminators,
- next experiments,
- bibliography of load-bearing sources only.
