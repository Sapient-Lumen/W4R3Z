# Bibliography

External literature here is **signal, not sovereignty**. We keep only load-bearing references.

- `REF-0001` — Noether, **Invariant Variation Problems** (1918 / translation on arXiv)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0503066
  - Load-bearing use: symmetry-to-conservation correspondence; a baseline reason to treat invariants as first-class.

- `REF-0002` — Yang & Mills, **Conservation of Isotopic Spin and Isotopic Gauge Invariance** (1954)
  - URL: https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRev.96.191
  - Load-bearing use: gauge structure as generative physics, not decorative formalism.

- `REF-0003` — Wilson, **The renormalization group and critical phenomena** (1983)
  - URL: https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/RevModPhys.55.583

- `REF-0004` — Weinberg, **Phenomenological Lagrangians** (1979)
  - URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0378437179902231

- `REF-0005` — Donoghue, **General relativity as an effective field theory: The leading quantum corrections** (1994)

- `REF-0006` — Burgess, **Quantum Gravity in Everyday Life: General Relativity as an Effective Field Theory** (2003)
