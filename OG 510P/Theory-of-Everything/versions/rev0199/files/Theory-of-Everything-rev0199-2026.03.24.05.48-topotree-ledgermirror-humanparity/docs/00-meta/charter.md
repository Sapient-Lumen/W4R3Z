# Charter

## North star

Build a compact archive for a **salient theory of everything**.

The target is not a slogan like “everything is information” or “everything is geometry.” The target is a disciplined program that can eventually identify a **minimal generative spine** able to recover:
- effective quantum field theory where it works,
- classical and semiclassical gravity where it works,
- thermodynamic and entropic structure where it becomes unavoidable,
- observer / measurement / record structure where empirical contact happens,
- and the failure surfaces that show where current descriptions stop being enough.

## Scope

In scope:
- foundations of unification,
- bridges among QFT, gravity, entropy, information, and cosmology,
- mathematical compression of known successful structure,
- explicit comparison among candidate bridge families,
- archive machinery that keeps this work cumulative.

Out of scope for now:
- exhaustive literature mapping,
- detailed model-building for every framework,
- raw experimental data warehousing,
- retaining large binaries or paper dumps,
- pretending that one research taste has already become canon.

## Working definition of success

A future theory deserves serious promotion only if it does all of the following:
1. compresses multiple existing tensions with fewer ad hoc patches,
2. recovers the best-tested low-energy descriptions in their domains,
3. makes clear what is gauge, what is observable, and what is emergent,
4. clarifies the role of entropy, horizons, or information where those are structurally unavoidable,
5. and yields sharper discriminating questions than its rivals.
