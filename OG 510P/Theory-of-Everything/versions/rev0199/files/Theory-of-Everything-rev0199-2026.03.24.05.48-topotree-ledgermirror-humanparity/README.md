# Theory of Everything: salient archive for a bridge-first unification program

**North star:** keep a compact, falsifiable, cumulative archive for a *salient* theory-of-everything program.

Here, *salient* means:
- small enough to reopen in one sitting,
- explicit about accepted, inferred, speculative, and adversarial status,
- organized around invariants, bridge claims, and decisive pressure points,
- citation-rich and quotation-light,
- ruthless about canonical homes, deduping, and refusal to keep bulky artifacts.

This archive does **not** assume that a final theory is already known, or that unification will arrive as one finished equation. The working posture is narrower: find the **smallest generative spine** that can recover established local theories, name their domains and failure modes, and keep observer / record structure inside the explanation rather than outside it.

## What the archive is for

Use this archive to:
- reopen the current state quickly,
- preserve only load-bearing distinctions,
- compare bridge families on shared pressure surfaces, keeping cross-family routing in canonical homes rather than scattered trios,
- keep speculation quarantined until promotion is earned,
- and package each revision as a small, inspectable continuation object.

## What the archive keeps

The canon is organized around:
- **core registries:** claims, invariants, open questions, and move classes,
- **program:** workstreams, frontiers, and bridge experiments,
- **model:** the theory spine, bridge-family audits, witness / record tests, and cosmological burden splits plus compact proposal-class routers,
- **meta control:** archive policy, canonical homes, trajectory, handoff, and release surfaces.

## What the archive refuses

- no retained PDFs or other bulky binaries in release bundles,
- no literature landfill,
- no giant quotations where a citation and one-line summary would do,
- no duplicate conceptual homes,
- no framework label treated as if it were already an explanation,
- no collapse of speculative branches into canon without explicit basis.

## Re-entry surfaces

Use:
- `START_HERE.md` for the human-readable restart plus the compact tool-rendered exact **restart-mirror family**: canonical entry surfaces, current release identity, current revision delta, restart tiers, current posture, public head status, priority restart open questions, operator warnings, followthrough / standby state, assumption state, and rebuild commands,
- `context-pack.json` for the canonical machine handoff,
- `RELEASE-MANIFEST.json` for the current bundle identity,
- `SURFACE-STATUS.json` for the machine-readable public head signal and warning line,
- `REVISION-RECEIPT.json` for the minimal durable rationale: what changed, why it counts, and which upstream bundle it answered.
