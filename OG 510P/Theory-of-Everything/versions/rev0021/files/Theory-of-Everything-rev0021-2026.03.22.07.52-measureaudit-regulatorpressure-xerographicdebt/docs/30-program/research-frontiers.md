# Research frontiers

## RF-01 — QFT on fixed background vs dynamical geometry
Known local quantum theory works astonishingly well, but gravity makes spacetime itself dynamical. Any bridge must say how local field structure survives or emerges when background structure becomes part of the dynamics.

## RF-02 — Black-hole entropy and information
Horizons, entropy, and unitarity produce some of the sharpest cross-domain pressure in modern physics. The archive now holds a bounded fourth-pass answer on the thermodynamic lane: widening beyond equilibrium can be meaningful only when extra constitutive structure, local-law role, observable discriminator, witness route, and regime portability are all named explicitly. Current family B earns stricter discipline and better trace bookkeeping, but still no clean beyond-equilibrium explanatory surplus and no climb beyond `WB-1`.

## RF-03 — Entanglement, geometry, and causal order
Multiple programs now treat entanglement as a bridge quantity. The live question is which part is clue, which part is chart artifact, which part is regime-specific, and which part survives once record-bearing observables are demanded. The current archive answer is now five-step and bounded: an RT-style claim survives provisional admission, its real surplus over thermodynamic language is isolated as partition-sensitive subregion geometry linkage, a first covariant stress test says that some of that surplus survives beyond the static RT lane, a first stronger-reconstruction audit says that a sharper boundary-to-bulk dictionary still does not reduce witness borrowing below `WB-2`, and a new witness-closure gate now states exactly what any future family-C lane would have to supply before it could claim a climb. The durable record story is still borrowed from the boundary/laboratory side, and stronger regime robustness remains unresolved.

## RF-04 — Measurement, records, and classicality
A ToE that says little about how stable records arise risks becoming mathematically elegant but empirically underdescribed. The archive now carries both a witness-borrowing ladder and a first family-B no-climb audit, so future bridge claims can be judged not only by whether they mention records, but by whether they actually internalize more of the empirical loop.

## RF-05 — Cosmological boundary conditions and constants
The archive now carries a local-law / cosmological-boundary split, a concrete cosmological-constant debt audit, plus explicit unimodular, sequestering, self-tuning / relaxation, anthropic / landscape-selection, and measure / population / typicality proposal-class passes. The current bounded answer is now five-step: unimodular moves mostly clarify bookkeeping, sequestering-style moves earn bounded partial local credit by directly targeting vacuum-energy sourcing, self-tuning / relaxation moves add genuine dynamical ambition plus sometimes a sharper empirical wedge, anthropic / landscape-selection moves earn explicit cosmological-package credit when they supply scanning plus selection while still not reducing local threshold sensitivity, and explicit measure / population / xerographic rules refine that package side further without yet making it unique or regulator-robust. The next live pressure is therefore a concrete causal-diamond / entropic-weighting worked example rather than more broad measure prose.
