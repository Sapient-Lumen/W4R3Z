# Bibliography

External literature here is **signal, not sovereignty**. We keep only load-bearing references.

- `REF-0001` — Noether, **Invariant Variation Problems** (1918 / translation on arXiv)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0503066
  - Load-bearing use: symmetry-to-conservation correspondence; a baseline reason to treat invariants as first-class.

- `REF-0002` — Yang & Mills, **Conservation of Isotopic Spin and Isotopic Gauge Invariance** (1954)
  - URL: https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRev.96.191
  - Load-bearing use: gauge structure as generative physics, not decorative formalism.

- `REF-0003` — Wilson, **The renormalization group and critical phenomena** (1983)
  - URL: https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/RevModPhys.55.583
  - Load-bearing use: scale dependence and coarse-graining as non-optional structure.

- `REF-0004` — Weinberg, **Phenomenological Lagrangians** (1979)
  - URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0378437179902231
  - Load-bearing use: effective field theory posture; low-energy truth can be real without being fundamental.

- `REF-0005` — Donoghue, **General relativity as an effective field theory: The leading quantum corrections** (1994)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9405057
  - Load-bearing use: gravity works as a quantum EFT at ordinary energies; low-energy consistency is not the same as UV completion.

- `REF-0006` — Burgess, **Quantum Gravity in Everyday Life: General Relativity as an Effective Field Theory** (2003)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0311082
  - Load-bearing use: crisp baseline for the EFT-first stance on gravity.

- `REF-0007` — Bekenstein, **Black Holes and Entropy** (1973)
  - URL: https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevD.7.2333
  - Load-bearing use: horizon entropy as a structural pressure surface for any deep theory.

- `REF-0008` — Wald, **Black Hole Entropy is Noether Charge** (1993)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9307038
  - Load-bearing use: bridges symmetry, geometry, and entropy in a precise formal way.

- `REF-0009` — Jacobson, **Thermodynamics of Spacetime: The Einstein Equation of State** (1995)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9504004
  - Load-bearing use: serious evidence that gravity/geometry may admit thermodynamic reconstruction.

- `REF-0010` — Bell, **On the Einstein Podolsky Rosen Paradox** (1964)
  - URL: https://cds.cern.ch/record/111654/files/
  - Load-bearing use: locality, realism, and quantum correlations cannot all survive untouched.

- `REF-0011` — Maldacena, **The Large N Limit of Superconformal Field Theories and Supergravity** (1997)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9711200
  - Load-bearing use: duality / holography as a serious bridge between gravity and nongravitational descriptions.

- `REF-0012` — Calabrese & Cardy, **Entanglement Entropy and Quantum Field Theory** (2004)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0405152
  - Load-bearing use: entanglement entropy as a controlled bridge quantity, not loose metaphor.

- `REF-0013` — Ryu & Takayanagi, **Holographic Derivation of Entanglement Entropy from AdS/CFT** (2006)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0603001
  - Load-bearing use: concrete geometry–entanglement bridge.

- `REF-0014` — Donoghue, **Quantum General Relativity and Effective Field Theory** (2022)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09902
  - Load-bearing use: modern review of the EFT stance; useful baseline before making stronger claims.

- `REF-0015` — Zurek, **Decoherence, einselection, and the quantum origins of the classical** (2003)
  - URL: https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/RevModPhys.75.715
  - Load-bearing use: minimal physics of persistent records, pointer states, and classical evidence traces.

- `REF-0016` — Ollivier, Poulin & Zurek, **Objective Properties from Subjective Quantum States: Environment as a Witness** (2004)
  - URL: https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.93.220401
  - Load-bearing use: redundant accessibility as a compact operational criterion for objectivity.

- `REF-0017` — Rovelli, **Partial observables** (2002)
  - URL: https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevD.65.124013
  - Load-bearing use: keeps observable language tied to relationally retrievable content in generally covariant settings.

- `REF-0018` — Eling, Guedens & Jacobson, **Non-equilibrium Thermodynamics of Spacetime** (2006)
  - URL: https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.96.121301
  - Load-bearing use: shows that widening Jacobson-style entropy derivations beyond the simple equilibrium package introduces explicit entropy-production structure.

- `REF-0019` — Hubeny, Rangamani & Takayanagi, **A Covariant Holographic Entanglement Entropy Proposal** (2007)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.0016
  - Load-bearing use: shows that the RT-style entanglement / geometry bridge extends in a controlled way to time-dependent covariant holographic settings without thereby becoming regime-free.

- `REF-0020` — Dong, Harlow & Wall, **Reconstruction of Bulk Operators within the Entanglement Wedge in Gauge-Gravity Duality** (2016)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.05416
  - Load-bearing use: sharpens family C from surface-area linkage toward a controlled subregion reconstruction claim without thereby solving witness closure or regime dependence.

- `REF-0021` — Weinberg, **The cosmological constant problem** (1989)
  - URL: https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/RevModPhys.61.1
  - Load-bearing use: canonical pressure surface for separating local vacuum-energy sensitivity from broader proposal classes that try to explain the observed small value of `\Lambda`.

- `REF-0022` — Jiroušek, **Unimodular approaches to the cosmological constant problem** (2023)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.01662
  - Load-bearing use: compact review of unimodular / integration-constant approaches, especially the way apparent vacuum-energy relief relies on a global degree of freedom, initial-data choice, or four-volume constraint rather than automatically reducing local EFT threshold sensitivity.

- `REF-0023` — Kaloper, Padilla, Stefanyszyn & Zahariade, **A Manifestly Local Theory of Vacuum Energy Sequestering** (2016)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.01492
  - Load-bearing use: strongest compact source for the claim that local sequestering aims to cancel matter-loop vacuum energy contributions while leaving a finite residual curvature that is radiatively stable.

- `REF-0024` — D'Amico, Kaloper, Padilla, Stefanyszyn, Westphal & Zahariade, **An Etude on Global Vacuum Energy Sequester** (2017)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.08950
  - Load-bearing use: compact source for how sequestering compares global and local formulations, and for why residual cosmological-package burden remains even when radiative bookkeeping improves.


- `REF-0025` — Sobral Blanco & Lombriser, **Local self-tuning mechanism for the cosmological constant** (2020)
  - URL: https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevD.102.043506
  - Load-bearing use: compact representative self-tuning source for bounded claims about dynamical screening / degravitation of vacuum energy and for keeping branch, extra-sector, and radiative-stability costs explicit.

- `REF-0026` — Graham, Kaplan & Rajendran, **Relaxation of the Cosmological Constant** (2019)
  - URL: https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevD.100.015048
  - Load-bearing use: compact representative relaxation source for bounded claims that dynamical adjustment can create a real trajectory and empirical wedge without thereby generically closing threshold sensitivity or cosmological-package burden.

- `REF-0027` — Weinberg, **Anthropic Bound on the Cosmological Constant** (1987)
  - URL: https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.59.2607
  - Load-bearing use: compact anchor for the strongest bounded anthropic claim in this archive — that large positive `Lambda` can be selection-disfavored because it suppresses structure formation, which gives anthropic reasoning real cosmological-package content without thereby solving local radiative stability.

- `REF-0028` — Bousso & Polchinski, **Quantization of Four-form Fluxes and Dynamical Neutralization of the Cosmological Constant** (2000)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0004134
  - Load-bearing use: compact anchor for the landscape side of the class — a dense discretuum / flux-scan support space that makes anthropic or selection reasoning more than empty ensemble rhetoric while still not generically reducing local EFT threshold sensitivity.

- `REF-0029` — Bousso, **Holographic Probabilities in Eternal Inflation** (2006)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0605263
  - Load-bearing use: compact anchor for the strongest local / causal measure lane in this archive — a causal-diamond style attempt to regulate eternal-inflation probabilities by restricting to one causally connected region and an explicit weighting rule rather than arbitrary global slicing.

- `REF-0030` — Srednicki & Hartle, **The Xerographic Distribution: Scientific Reasoning in a Large Universe** (2010)
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.3816
  - Load-bearing use: compact anchor for the claim that first-person predictions in a very large universe require an explicit self-location rule; typicality or atypicality is part of the framework, not automatically fixed by the third-person theory alone.
