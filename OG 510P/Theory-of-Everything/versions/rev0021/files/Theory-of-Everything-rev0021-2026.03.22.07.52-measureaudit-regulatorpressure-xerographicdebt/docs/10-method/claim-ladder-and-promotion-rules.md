# Claim ladder and promotion rules

## Status labels

- `accepted` — archive-level working discipline or baseline constraint.
- `inferred` — strongly suggested by accepted theory or compact source set, but still interpretive.
- `speculative` — live hypothesis not yet earned.
- `adversarial-countermodel` — credible rival or pressure case that tests an accepted story.

## Promotion rules

A claim should be promoted only when it has:
1. a clear canonical statement,
2. a named basis in `REF-####` entries or accepted constraints,
3. a reason it beats nearby alternatives,
4. and an explicit failure or demotion trigger.

## Demotion rules

Demote when a claim:
- loses contact with observables,
- fails low-energy recovery,
- duplicates a stronger claim elsewhere,
- or depends on sources or assumptions no longer trusted.
