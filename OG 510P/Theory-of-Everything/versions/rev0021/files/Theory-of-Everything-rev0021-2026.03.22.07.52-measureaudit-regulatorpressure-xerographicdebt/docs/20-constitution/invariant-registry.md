# Invariant registry

These are non-negotiable recovery targets or structural pressures.

- `INV-0001` — **Low-energy QFT recovery**
  - Meaning: the candidate must recover effective quantum field theory where EFT is known to work.
  - Basis: `REF-0003`, `REF-0004`, `REF-0005`, `REF-0014`

- `INV-0002` — **Classical / semiclassical gravity recovery**
  - Meaning: the candidate must recover general-relativistic behavior in the appropriate domain.
  - Basis: `REF-0005`, `REF-0006`

- `INV-0003` — **Gauge / redundancy honesty**
  - Meaning: the candidate must distinguish real observables from coordinate or gauge artifacts.
  - Basis: `REF-0001`, `REF-0002`, `REF-0008`

- `INV-0004` — **Scale / renormalization discipline**
  - Meaning: coarse-graining and scale dependence cannot be ignored or wished away.
  - Basis: `REF-0003`, `REF-0004`

- `INV-0005` — **Horizon entropy pressure**
  - Meaning: black-hole thermodynamics must fit naturally or be decisively reinterpreted.
  - Basis: `REF-0007`, `REF-0008`, `REF-0009`

- `INV-0006` — **Quantum correlation pressure**
  - Meaning: Bell-style constraints must be taken seriously in any account of locality, separability, or realism.
  - Basis: `REF-0010`

- `INV-0007` — **Entanglement bridge discipline**
  - Meaning: if entanglement is used as a bridge variable, its role must be technically explicit.
  - Basis: `REF-0012`, `REF-0013`

- `INV-0008` — **Empirical-record recoverability**
  - Meaning: the theory must leave room for persistent, causally generated, re-readable records and stable classical evidence.
  - Basis: `REF-0010`, `REF-0012`, `REF-0015`, `REF-0016`
