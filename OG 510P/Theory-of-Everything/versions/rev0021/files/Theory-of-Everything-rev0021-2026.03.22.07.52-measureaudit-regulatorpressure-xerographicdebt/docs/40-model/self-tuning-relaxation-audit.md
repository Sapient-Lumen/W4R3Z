# Self-tuning / relaxation audit

This document is the **third concrete mixed-proposal-class pass** under the archive's cosmological-constant debt split.
Its job is not to settle self-tuning or relaxation programs in general.
Its job is to stop the archive from calling a dynamical trajectory, screening branch, or late-time adjustment mechanism a solved two-burden vacuum-energy story before it has earned that status.

## Compression verdict

The current archive should treat **self-tuning / relaxation proposal classes as heterogeneous mixed vacuum-energy moves that can earn real dynamical credit, but not generic two-burden closure**.

Bounded result:

1. **What they genuinely change:** unlike pure bookkeeping relocation, these proposals try to make a small effective curvature arise dynamically through screening, attractor behavior, rolling, branch selection, or vacuum-step descent.
2. **What they still owe:** in generic form they continue to pay with special branches, background assumptions, instability or reheating costs, residual constant-setting burden, or model-specific trigger sectors.
3. **What they therefore currently earn:** mixed-class credit for trying to reduce the burden dynamically, and sometimes a sharper empirical wedge than unimodular or sequestering stories, but not generic closure of both local threshold sensitivity and cosmological-package burden.

That is already a useful archive state.
It means the archive can now distinguish four non-identical vacuum-energy proposal modes:
- **bookkeeping relocation**,
- **direct radiative targeting with residual global package**,
- **dynamical self-adjustment with branch or trajectory cost**,
- and any future class that might actually reduce both burdens without hiding new debt elsewhere.

## Why this document exists

The unimodular pass said: debt can move without shrinking.
The sequestering pass said: direct targeting can buy bounded local credit without closing the global package.
Self-tuning and relaxation proposals are the next necessary stress test because they promise something different again.
They do not merely rename `Lambda`.
They try to **drive the effective vacuum response toward a small value through dynamics**.

That makes them the right place to ask the archive's next harder question:

**Is the small value reached because the proposal really shrank local threshold sensitivity and global-package burden together, or because the burden reappeared as branch choice, slow-roll timing, reheating cost, instability control, or hidden background package?**

## What the self-tuning / relaxation move actually buys

In the archive's current bounded reading, this proposal class can genuinely buy four things.

### 1. A real dynamical story rather than pure reclassification

This is the most important difference from the unimodular pass.
The class does not stop at saying the constant should be read differently.
It says the effective curvature should **move** toward a small value because some field, branch, or screening structure changes the vacuum response over time.

That means the archive should now score this class as more than bookkeeping.

### 2. A possible route to partial local burden reduction

Some self-tuning or relaxation proposals are built precisely to weaken how vacuum energy gravitates or to drive the response toward a small residual value.
That is stronger than merely shifting the bookkeeping into a global datum.

But the archive should still phrase this as **possible and model-dependent**, not generic.
The dynamical route only earns local credit when it really reduces threshold sensitivity instead of just postponing, screening, or rebranching it.

### 3. A clearer place to score branch and trigger costs

This class makes a different kind of debt visible.
Even when the effective curvature is dynamically pushed downward, the proposal may still rely on:
- special background branches,
- particular cosmological histories,
- phase transitions or bounce sectors,
- residual scanning assumptions,
- or stability conditions that are nontrivial to preserve.

That is real explanatory cost.
The archive should now score it explicitly instead of letting “dynamic relaxation” sound like a free pass.

### 4. Sometimes a sharper empirical wedge than other mixed classes

A dynamical screening or rolling sector can in principle leave observable consequences:
- time variation,
- equation-of-state drift,
- extra light fields,
- reheating or bounce signatures,
- or other deviations from plain GR plus a tiny constant.

That does not make the class correct.
But it does mean the archive should sometimes score it as **empirically thinner than needed, yet still more testable than pure bookkeeping or pure global-package selection**.

## What the self-tuning / relaxation move still does **not** buy automatically

### 1. Generic local-law closure

A dynamical trajectory toward small curvature is not yet the same as showing that ordinary EFT threshold sensitivity has disappeared.
The archive should still ask:
- which sectors are screened or relaxed,
- whether heavy-threshold contributions remain under control,
- whether radiative stability survives once the adjustment sector is included,
- and whether the mechanism works without re-tuning after every new threshold or transition.

Current archive verdict: **the class can earn bounded dynamical local credit in model-specific cases, but generic local-law closure is still not earned.**

### 2. Cosmological-package closure

A small late-time value reached dynamically is not automatically a full explanation of why the realized universe lands in the required branch, survives the required history, or ends in the required residual state.
The archive should still ask:
- why this branch,
- why this trigger timing,
- why this transition chain,
- why this reheating or bounce package,
- and why the final residual value is the observed one rather than merely small-ish or branch-dependent.

So the archive should not let “the system can roll there” be retold as “the realized universe is now fully explained.”

### 3. Free stability or naturalness

Many such models buy adjustment by adding a new light field, screening structure, or branch-sensitive sector.
That can introduce its own:
- radiative vulnerability,
- gradient or ghost concerns,
- empty-universe or reheating problems,
- or special initial-condition dependence.

Those may or may not be fatal in a given model.
But they are explanatory debt and should stay on the scorecard.

### 4. A thick empirical wedge by default

Even when the class is more testable in principle, its practical wedge may remain thin if the allowed parameter region hugs ordinary `\Lambda`CDM or if the required additional sector is very weakly coupled.
The archive should count a merely possible wedge separately from a thick discriminator.

## Scoring self-tuning / relaxation proposals under the debt split

### Local radiative burden

**Current archive score:** possibly reduced in bounded model-specific ways, not generically closed.

This class earns more local-facing credit than plain integration-constant bookkeeping because it tries to alter the actual vacuum response dynamically.
But the archive does not yet promote the class as a generic threshold solution because the mechanism often depends on extra sectors, branch conditions, or residual tuning that can reintroduce sensitivity elsewhere.

### Cosmological-package burden

**Current archive score:** still open.

The class often replaces “why this small constant?” with “why this branch, history, trigger, or residual state?”
That can be a more structured question, but it is still a cosmological-package burden unless the realized path and endpoint are themselves explained.

### Mixed debt-shift burden

**Current archive score:** strongly active here.

This remains a mixed proposal class.
Its virtue is that it tries to shrink the burden dynamically rather than only relabel it.
Its limitation is that the debt often reappears as special trajectory cost, stability maintenance, or residual package dependence.

So the current archive posture should now be:
- **stronger than pure bookkeeping relocation on dynamical ambition**,
- **sometimes more observationally exposed than sequestering**,
- **not yet generic two-burden closure**.

## Five checks the archive should now apply

### 1. What exactly is relaxing or self-tuning?

Name the object explicitly:
- vacuum response,
- curvature itself,
- a scalar potential offset,
- a branch parameter,
- a four-form step,
- or only a low-energy residual.

A proposal should not get full credit for moving one effective parameter if the deeper vacuum sensitivity simply resurfaces elsewhere.

### 2. Which branch, trigger, or history assumptions are indispensable?

Say whether the mechanism needs:
- a special attractor branch,
- slow roll over a huge field range,
- a bounce or reheating sector,
- a special phase transition,
- or a carefully staged cosmological history.

If those are essential, they count as package burden rather than free dynamical closure.

### 3. What happens to threshold sensitivity after new sectors are included?

The archive should ask whether the adjustment sector itself is radiatively stable and whether heavy thresholds simply re-enter through the back door.
If the cure imports a new tuning problem, the net gain must be scored more modestly.

### 4. What is the residual value, and who fixes it?

Even after relaxation or tuning, there is often a leftover endpoint, step size, or branch-dependent residual constant.
Name who fixes it:
- branch selection,
- initial conditions,
- trigger sector,
- anthropic selection,
- measurement,
- or some global package.

If that fixing remains external to the local dynamical story, the cosmological-package burden is still present.

### 5. What is the empirical discriminator?

Does the proposal imply any real signature in:
- late-time dynamics,
- dark-energy equation of state,
- light-field couplings,
- reheating or bounce traces,
- Lorentz-violating or screening effects,
- or deviations from plain GR plus a tiny constant?

If not, the main gain remains classificatory or structural rather than strongly empirical.

## Family-by-family relevance

### Family A — EFT-first baseline

Family A remains decisive here.
It asks whether the proposed dynamical mechanism really shrinks threshold sensitivity or only displaces it into extra sectors and special histories.

### Family B — Thermodynamic / entropy gravity

Family B remains mostly peripheral.
Its current use is only disciplinary: do not let gravitational metaphor or coarse-grained entropy language impersonate a vacuum-energy adjustment mechanism.

### Family C — Holography / entanglement geometry

Family C again matters mainly as a comparison class.
A rich global package or reconstruction story can be structurally impressive without explaining why a specific small late-time curvature is selected dynamically.
That same anti-smuggling rule applies here.

### Family D — Measurement / nonlocality pressure

Family D remains indirect but helpful.
If a proposal ultimately leans on branch selection, history filtering, or observer-conditioned endpoint language, the archive should score that as part of the package burden instead of hiding it inside local dynamics.

## What closes with this document

`FQ-0013` can now close in bounded form.

The archive now has a **third concrete mixed-class score** under the cosmological-constant debt split:
- self-tuning / relaxation proposals earn more than bookkeeping credit because they attempt genuine dynamical adjustment,
- they sometimes offer a sharper empirical wedge than earlier mixed classes,
- but they still do not generically close local threshold sensitivity plus cosmological-package burden together,
- and they often reintroduce debt as branch cost, trigger cost, stability maintenance, or residual endpoint selection.

## Net result

This is again a **compression move** more than a literature move.
It lets the archive say, more sharply than before:

- a dynamical trajectory is not automatically a threshold solution,
- a screened or relaxed endpoint is not automatically a fully explained realized universe,
- branch cost and trigger cost are real explanatory debt,
- and the next useful audit is now a concrete measure / population / typicality pass on anthropic / landscape-selection proposals rather than reopening self-tuning promises in broader prose.
