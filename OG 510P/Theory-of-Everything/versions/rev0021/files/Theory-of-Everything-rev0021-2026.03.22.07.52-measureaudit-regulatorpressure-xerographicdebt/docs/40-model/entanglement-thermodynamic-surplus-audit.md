# Entanglement surplus over thermodynamic language

This document is a **compact application of `W-0002`**.
It asks what the archive should count as the smallest real gain of family C over family B after the first RT-style worked example is on the table.

Read this together with:
- `docs/40-model/discriminator-wedges.md`
- `docs/40-model/rt-claim-audit.md`
- `docs/40-model/thermodynamic-gravity-assumption-audit.md`
- `docs/40-model/observer-record-minimum.md`

## Compression verdict

The current archive answer is narrow but decisive:
- family C does retain a **real surplus** over family B,
- that surplus is **subregion- and partition-sensitive geometry linkage** rather than generic entropy talk,
- and the surplus is still **bounded to a special holographic regime** and does **not** solve the archive's record / objectivity problem.

So `W-0002` should now be read as **bounded yes** rather than open confusion.
That keeps family C distinct from family B without promoting it.

## What family B already buys

The thermodynamic lane already earns several serious things.

- `REF-0007`, `REF-0008`, and `REF-0009` make entropy and horizon bookkeeping structurally non-optional.
- `docs/40-model/thermodynamic-gravity-assumption-audit.md` already shows that family B can reconstruct Einstein-like dynamics under a local-equilibrium package.
- Family B therefore deserves real explanatory credit on the link among entropy, area, and geometry.

But the family-B gain is mostly **coarse and equilibrium-organized**.
It does not by itself provide a sharp, partition-indexed map from a chosen subsystem's entanglement structure to a corresponding geometric object.

## What family C adds that B does not already supply

The RT-style case isolates a smaller but sharper gain.

### 1. The partition is load-bearing

Family C does not merely say that some entropy tracks some area.
It starts from an explicit boundary split and asks for the entanglement entropy of that chosen subregion.
That means the geometric claim is indexed by a concrete partition choice rather than only by horizon thermodynamic bookkeeping.

### 2. The geometric object is subregion-specific

In the bounded RT regime, the relevant bulk surface is tied to the chosen subregion.
This is stronger than generic entropy/area rhetoric because different partitions imply different candidate surfaces and different geometric constraints.

### 3. The gain is dictionary-like, not merely analogical

The archive should count family C's success only where the entanglement quantity and the geometric object are linked in a controlled dual description.
That is a tighter bridge than saying that gravity and thermodynamics seem suggestively related.

### 4. The observable-bearing route stays anchored to the boundary side

This is still a gain over free-floating geometric metaphor.
The content remains tethered to boundary observables, state structure, and calculable changes in a bounded dual description.
That keeps the claim closer to `CL-0014` discipline than a generic “spacetime is information” slogan would.

## Why the surplus is real but not promotional

The archive should now make a three-part distinction.

1. **Real surplus over B:**
   family C contributes partition-sensitive, subregion-specific structure that coarse thermodynamic language does not automatically supply.

2. **No general victory over A or B:**
   this surplus does not replace the EFT baseline and does not show that thermodynamic language was a mistake.
   It only shows that the best current family-C bridge is not reducible to generic equilibrium bookkeeping.

3. **No closure on records:**
   the surplus does not answer where stable records live.
   The witness structure still rides on boundary degrees of freedom, apparatus couplings, environments, and memory chains rather than on a candidate-native bulk closure story.

## Failure modes to keep blocked

Do not let this document be used to smuggle in any of the following:

- “entanglement wins” as a regime-free conclusion,
- “thermodynamics was only a warm-up notation for entanglement” without proof,
- “geometry from information” without an explicit partition and bounded dual setting,
- or any suggestion that the record problem is solved because a geometric dictionary exists.

## Archive-state consequence

The archive should now hold:
- `W-0002` has a **first bounded answer**: family C is not merely family B rewritten.
- The distinct gain is **partition-sensitive geometric structure** in a bounded holographic regime.
- `W-0003` remains open because the record/objectivity story is still borrowed.
- Stronger family-C claims should only be admitted if they either:
  - reduce boundary borrowing on records,
  - or extend the surplus beyond the present narrow regime without dissolving into slogan.

## Net result

The archive no longer needs to ask whether family C has **any** gain over family B.
It now needs to ask whether that gain can become less regime-fragile and less dependent on imported witness structure.
