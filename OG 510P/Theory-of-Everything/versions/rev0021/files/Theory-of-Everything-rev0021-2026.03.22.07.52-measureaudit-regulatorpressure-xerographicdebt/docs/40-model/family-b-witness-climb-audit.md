## Climb test

A non-equilibrium family-B extension counts as a real ladder climb only if it improves at least one of the following in its **own** bridge language.

1. **Carrier specification**
   - What variable inside the candidate actually carries the record rather than merely correlating with an external detector history?

2. **Stabilization rule**
   - What keeps the record-like structure persistent instead of washing out as a transient bookkeeping correction?

3. **Rereadability rule**
   - How is the same coarse-grained fact recoverable more than once without immediately translating back into ordinary matter-side witness language?

4. **Objectivity route**
   - What makes the record redundantly accessible or intersubjectively stable in the candidate's own terms rather than by appeal to already-familiar environmental decoherence language?

5. **Distinct empirical closure**
   - What new observational constraint becomes available that is not just a relabeling of borrowed dissipative traces?

If none of these improve, the family has clarified its interface but has not climbed the ladder.

## Current answers for family B

### 1. Carrier specification: **no native carrier yet**

The present non-equilibrium lane can name entropy-production terms, response coefficients, or shear/flux corrections.
Those are useful effective descriptors.
They are not yet candidate-native record carriers.
The record still lives where the downstream trace is stored: matter configurations, detector states, environment fragments, boundary readouts, or memory chains.

### 2. Stabilization rule: **still borrowed**

The current family-B story can say when irreversible coarse-grained response occurs.
It does not yet say, in its own bridge variables, why a trace becomes a stable, persistent witness rather than a transient thermodynamic mismatch.
Stability is still inherited from the substrate that stores the trace.

### 3. Rereadability rule: **still borrowed**

Repeated access to the same fact is still described through ordinary measurement chains, apparatus histories, or boundary-side bookkeeping.
The non-equilibrium correction sharpens what should be looked for, but does not itself provide a candidate-native rereading mechanism.

### 4. Objectivity route: **still borrowed**

Nothing in the current family-B widening replaces the role of redundant environmental accessibility or ordinary laboratory consensus conditions.
At best, the thermodynamic lane points to where such witness channels may carry evidence.
It does not yet internalize objectivity in its own language.

### 5. Distinct empirical closure: **partial but not ladder-changing**

There is one real improvement: the archive can now reject purely formal entropy-production patches and demand named coarse-grained traces.
That is a meaningful discipline gain.
But it is not yet a new closure mechanism for records.
It improves admissibility, not witness ownership.

## False climbs the archive should reject

The archive should explicitly reject four ways of pretending family B has climbed the ladder.

1. **More symbols is not a climb**
   - Additional constitutive coefficients or production terms do not change ladder level by themselves.

2. **Better tagging is not native storage**
   - Naming a dissipative trace more clearly is useful, but it does not make the trace carrier internal to the candidate.

3. **Protocol dependence is not objectivity**
   - Hysteresis or history sensitivity can create a recordable difference without explaining why that difference becomes stably and redundantly accessible.

4. **Boundary or apparatus readability is not family-B closure**
   - If the evidence still has to be read off from ordinary matter-side or boundary-side witnesses, borrowing remains decisive.

## What would count as a real climb

### `WB-2` for family B would require
- a disciplined interface in which specific bridge-native variables constrain where records can live and how they are read,
- plus a nontrivial restriction on admissible witness channels that is expressed in thermodynamic-gravity language rather than only imported from ordinary measurement talk.

### `WB-3` for family B would require
- some part of persistence, redundancy, or rereadability to be stated in the candidate's own bridge variables,
- so that the theory does more than point toward borrowed traces and actually begins to encode why they stay witnessable.

Nothing in the current archive earns either step.
