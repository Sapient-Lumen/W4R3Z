## Gate: what future family-C progress must supply

A future family-C claim earns **witness-closure credit** only if all five checks are answered in the candidate's own bridge language.

### 1. Native record carrier

The claim must say where an evidence-bearing distinction physically lives **inside the family-C bridge description**.
A reconstructable operator, an extremal surface, or a code-subspace relation is not enough by itself.

Minimum acceptable form:
- what the carrier is,
- what degrees of freedom host it,
- and why it is not merely a renamed borrowed boundary detector state.

### 2. Native stabilization route

The claim must say what keeps that carrier persistent long enough to function as evidence.
“Protected by duality” or “encoded somewhere in the boundary” is too weak unless the stabilizing mechanism is stated as physical witness structure rather than as representational availability.

Minimum acceptable form:
- what prevents immediate washout,
- what coarse-grained persistence condition is claimed,
- and why the answer is not just imported environment-side decoherence prose with family-C labels added.

### 3. Native reread path

The claim must say how later interactions can recover the same coarse-grained fact from the proposed carrier.
Reconstruction in principle is not enough.
The question is whether a later physical interaction can reread the same fact without quietly cashing out in an already-borrowed laboratory or boundary memory chain.

Minimum acceptable form:
- a retrieval path,
- a persistence interval or regime,
- and a statement of what counts as the same reread fact.

### 4. Native objectivity condition

The claim must say what makes the fact more than a single privileged encoding.
For archive purposes, “multiple ways to reconstruct the same operator” is not yet the same as redundant witness accessibility.

Minimum acceptable form:
- what the redundancy or agreement condition is,
- what independent access means in the candidate's own language,
- and why this is more than code-subspace representability.

### 5. Irreducible empirical gain

The claim must say what becomes empirically better explained once the above four items are supplied.
If the answer is only “the dictionary is sharper,” the claim has not climbed.
The gain must be a real reduction in borrowed witness structure.

Minimum acceptable form:
- which part of the empirical loop is now candidate-native,
- which prior borrowing step is no longer needed,
- and what observable-bearing difference the new closure makes.

## What does not count

The archive should keep blocking the following false promotions:

- stronger subregion reconstruction without a native record carrier,
- broader covariant generality without a native stabilization story,
- code-subspace redundancy presented as if it were witness redundancy,
- boundary readability presented as if it were candidate-native rereadability,
- or objectivity language that still cashes out entirely in ordinary borrowed boundary or environment-side witness talk.
