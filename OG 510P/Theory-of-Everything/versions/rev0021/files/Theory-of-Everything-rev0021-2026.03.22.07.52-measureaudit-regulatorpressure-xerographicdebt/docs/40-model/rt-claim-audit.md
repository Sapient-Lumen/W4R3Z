## Candidate claim under audit

In a bounded holographic setting, the entanglement entropy of a boundary subregion is related to the area of a corresponding bulk surface.
For archive purposes, the point is not to restate the full formalism, but to ask what this claim actually earns.

## Seven-question pass

### 1. Which quantity?

- Boundary entanglement entropy of a chosen subregion.
- In archive terms: this is **not** generic “information” language; it is a named entropy object tied to a specific reduced description.

### 2. Across which split?

- A boundary region `A` versus its complement or corresponding algebraic split.
- This is already a meaningful gain over loose entanglement rhetoric because the partition is explicit and anchored.
- It also means the claim inherits all the seriousness and fragility of that partition choice.

### 3. In which regime?

- Holographic / AdS-CFT setting.
- Large-`N`, semiclassical bulk regime.
- Static or suitably bounded settings close to the classic RT use-case.

This is a real, admissible regime statement.
It is also a warning label: the claim is not a regime-free theory-of-everything statement.

### 4. What is the claimed gain?

The distinctive gain is **not** “entropy exists.”
The gain is that a partition-specific entanglement quantity is mapped to a controlled geometric object, which gives family C something sharper than generic thermodynamic redescription.

That matters for `W-0002`.
A coarse equilibrium entropy story does not, by itself, supply this same subregion-anchored geometry map.
So the RT-style lane is enough to keep family C from collapsing entirely into family B.

### 5. What is the observable-bearing consequence?

The cleanest consequence is indirect but real:
- boundary-side entanglement structure constrains which bulk geometries are compatible with the claim,
- subregion changes track geometric changes in a way that can be computed inside the bounded dual description,
- and the relevant content remains tied to boundary observables and state structure rather than to a free-floating metaphor.

The archive still treats this as **observable-bearing but not detector-click primitive**.
The empirical contact comes through the boundary theory and its operational retrieval routes, not through bare geometric rhetoric.

### 6. Where do records live?

This is where the claim remains incomplete.
The durable records do **not** obviously live in the extremal surface itself.
They live in boundary degrees of freedom, apparatus couplings, environment channels, memory chains, and other witness structures already familiar from ordinary quantum theory and `docs/40-model/observer-record-minimum.md`.

So the current audit says:
- family C can supply a disciplined geometry–entanglement bridge,
- but the persistent record/objectivity story is still **borrowed from the boundary/laboratory side** rather than natively closed by the bridge claim itself.

This keeps `W-0003` open.

### 7. What would demote the claim?

Demote or narrow the claim if any of the following happen:
- the regime restrictions are ignored and the claim is retold as universal geometry-from-information,
- the entanglement object and partition stop being explicit,
- the apparent gain over thermodynamic language vanishes under careful comparison,
- or record carriers remain a permanent external import with no candidate-native closure story.

## Decision state

**Provisional admit.**

Why not full admit?
- quantity, split, regime, and distinctive gain are all explicit,
- but the record-carrier and objectivity story still rides on imported boundary witness structure.

Why not reject?
- because this is a real, regime-bounded, technically disciplined bridge claim with sharper content than slogan-level entanglement talk.

## Archive consequence

This first concrete audit yields a compact two-part result.

1. **For `W-0002`:** family C survives as genuinely distinct from family B in at least one bounded regime, because the subregion-specific geometry map is tighter than generic thermodynamic bookkeeping.
2. **For `W-0003`:** family C still does not close the empirical loop on its own; records remain boundary-borrowed.

That is enough to keep family C alive and disciplined without promoting it. The distinct gain over family B is now compressed separately in `docs/40-model/entanglement-thermodynamic-surplus-audit.md`.

## Compression verdict

The RT-style claim earns:
- **bounded explanatory credit** on geometry/entanglement linkage,
- **no license for universal-solvent rhetoric**,
- and **no exemption** from naming where stable evidence actually lives.

This is the archive posture to preserve going forward.

## Witness-borrowing placement

The current archive places this bounded RT-style claim at `WB-2` in `docs/40-model/witness-borrowing-ladder.md`.
That is stronger than mere trace-tagged borrowing because the boundary interface is specific and observable-bearing.
It still falls short of native witness closure because persistence, rereadability, and objectivity remain borrowed from the boundary / laboratory side.
