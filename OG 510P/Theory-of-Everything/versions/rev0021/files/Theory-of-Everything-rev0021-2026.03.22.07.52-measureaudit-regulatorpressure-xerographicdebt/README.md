# Theory of Everything: salient archive for a bridge-first unification program

**North star:** build a compact, falsifiable, cumulative research archive for a *salient* theory of everything.

Here, *salient* means:
- compact enough to reopen without hauling an encyclopedia,
- explicit about what is accepted, inferred, speculative, or adversarial,
- organized around **invariants, bridge claims, and decisive pressure points** rather than literature accumulation,
- citation-rich and quotation-light,
- ruthless about deduping, slimming, and refusal to keep non-crucial binary artifacts.

This archive does **not** assume that a theory of everything is already known, or that it will appear as one final equation. The current working posture is more modest: a viable theory of everything should be the **smallest generative spine** that can recover the best established local theories, their domains of validity, their failure modes, and the observer/record structures by which they become empirical science.

## Current posture

- **Baseline accepted:** effective field theory, renormalization, gauge structure, black-hole thermodynamics, and quantum nonlocality are not optional background trivia; they are pressure surfaces any unification program must answer.
- **Live bridge families:** thermodynamic gravity, holography / entanglement geometry, and atlas-style formalism translation remain active but unresolved.
- **New comparison surface:** the invariant matrix turns the archive from a list of families into a pressure map.
- **Observer discipline:** record formation now has a minimum specification, which blocks casual appeals to “observation” or “classicality.”
- **Sharper decision surface:** pairwise wedges now show what could actually merge, demote, or promote bridge families.
- **Thermodynamic discipline:** the Jacobson/Wald lane is now compressed into an explicit assumption audit rather than broad slogan.
- **Non-equilibrium discipline:** family-B widening now only counts when entropy-production terms are tied to coarse-grained traces and real witness channels.
- **Family-B no-climb verdict:** the strongest current non-equilibrium lane still does not climb beyond `WB-1`; it sharpens borrowed trace discipline without yet owning persistence, rereadability, or objectivity.
- **Family-B minimum-gain gate installed:** beyond-equilibrium thermodynamic claims now also have to name extra constitutive content, local-law status, an observable discriminator, a witness route, and regime portability before they can claim explanatory surplus at all.
- **Witness-borrowing discipline:** bridge families are now explicitly scored by how much record structure they still import from laboratory or boundary language.
- **Entanglement discipline:** entanglement claims now need a named quantity, split, regime, observable consequence, and record carrier.
- **Worked example:** one RT-style claim now survives as a bounded provisional admit while still borrowing its record story from the boundary/laboratory side.
- **Family-C surplus isolated:** the archive now states exactly what family C adds beyond family B: partition-sensitive subregion geometry linkage in a narrow holographic regime.
- **Covariant carry installed:** the archive now states that some family-C surplus survives one disciplined move beyond static RT, but still without reducing witness borrowing or escaping special holographic control.
- **Stronger reconstruction no-climb installed:** family C now has a first explicit audit showing that entanglement-wedge-style reconstruction sharpens the interface to bulk operator content without yet turning representational redundancy into native witness structure.
- **Witness-closure gate installed:** future family-C promotion now requires native carrier, stabilization, rereadability, objectivity, and empirical-gain answers rather than repeated promises that a sharper borrowed interface will eventually close the record loop.
- **Boundary/law split installed:** future explanations now have to say whether they are earning local-law credit, cosmological-boundary credit, or both, rather than blending special global packages into generic local-dynamics claims.
- **Vacuum-energy debt split installed:** cosmological-constant claims are now scored by separate local radiative burden, cosmological-package burden, and mixed debt-shift burden rather than one undifferentiated “solution” label.
- **Unimodular mixed-class audit installed:** integration-constant moves now earn bookkeeping and package-separation credit without being mistaken for generic local threshold relief.
- **Sequestering mixed-class audit installed:** direct vacuum-energy targeting now gets bounded partial local credit without being mistaken for full local-law or cosmological-package closure.
- **Self-tuning mixed-class audit installed:** dynamic adjustment now gets scored separately from bookkeeping relocation and sequestering, with explicit branch-cost, trigger-cost, and residual-package discipline.
- **Anthropic / landscape mixed-class audit installed:** selection-conditioned explanations now earn explicit cosmological-package credit when they supply scanning plus selection, while still not being mistaken for local threshold relief and while measure / population debt remains open.
- **Measure / population / typicality audit installed:** explicit counting, weighting, and self-location rules now earn bounded package credit and sometimes weak extra pressure, while regulator sensitivity and xerographic dependence remain open rather than hidden.
- **Salience budget:** prefer one sharp bridge document over five overlapping summaries.
