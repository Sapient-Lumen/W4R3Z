# Locality, microcausality, and cluster-decomposition discipline

This surface owns the rev0302 rule that locality-like support is denominator-bound. A route may use locality, local commutativity, no-signalling, local algebra, detector locality, cluster decomposition, isolability, or local-QFT recovery language only through the executable rows in `MICROCAUSALITY-LOCALITY-LEDGER.json`, `CLUSTER-DECOMPOSITION-LEDGER.json`, and `LOCAL-QFT-RECOVERY-LEDGER.json`.

The key separation is noncompensatory: microcausality is not cluster decomposition; cluster decomposition is not local-QFT recovery; local-QFT recovery is not observed-sector recovery; and none of them is candidate-native closure.

The route-local rows must declare regions or algebras, commutator/no-signalling target, detector or witness coupling, cluster/factorization target, falloff or limit notion, vacuum/sector assumptions, state/representation domain, and public-record carrier before stronger wording can be used.
