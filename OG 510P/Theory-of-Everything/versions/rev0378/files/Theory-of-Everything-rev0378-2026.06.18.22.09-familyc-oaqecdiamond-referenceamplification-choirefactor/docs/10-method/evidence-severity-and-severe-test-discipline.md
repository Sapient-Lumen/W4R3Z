# Evidence severity and severe-test discipline

Purpose: prevent weak positive evidence from being promoted merely because it is public, impressive, or route-relevant.

A route result is severe only when the archive can say what would probably have failed if the target claim were false or if a listed negative control were the real explanation. This does not require one philosophical account of confirmation; it requires a route-local answer to four operational questions:

1. What hypothesis or route field is under test?
2. Which alternatives, confounds, quotients, nuisance models, or surrogate targets are live?
3. What public artifact carries the test?
4. What is the maximum credit if the test passes, and what rollback fires if it fails?

`EVIDENCE-SEVERITY-LEDGER.json` is intentionally capped. Passing a severity row can preserve or sharpen a route field, but it cannot bypass carrier/protocol custody, promotion gates, observed-sector recovery, candidate quotient, or residual caps.

## Weak-positive patterns blocked

- a benchmark score without target-quotient and holdout/shift pressure;
- a lab entanglement signal without mediator/subsystem/nuisance separation;
- a survey likelihood shift without parameterization and systematic controls;
- a gravitational-wave consistency result retold as ToE identification;
- a proof replay without theorem-scope and target-regime audit;
- a future mission forecast retold as acquired evidence.

## Stronger-but-still-capped pattern

The strongest acceptable form is:

```text
public artifact + protocol replay + negative-control split + declared alternatives + rollback-if-failed + maximum route credit
```

Even that form is not closure. It is route-local severe-test credit.
