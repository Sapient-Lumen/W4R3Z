# Prospective prediction, preregistration, and blinding discipline

This surface separates prediction timing, registered protocol, and bias-control claims. It prevents retrospective fits, benchmark wins, simulator transfers, or post-hoc analyses from being retold as severe prospective tests.

## Noncompensation rule

Prospective timestamp, preregistered analysis, and blind-analysis discipline are separate route debts. A route can satisfy one and fail the others. None of them promotes a candidate beyond the candidate route state machine.

## Required denominator

A route that uses prediction/preregistration/blinding language must declare: the public timestamp and information set; the registered protocol and outcomes; the analysis-lock and unblinding policy; material deviations; and the maximum authority effect.
