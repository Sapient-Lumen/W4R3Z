# State-preparation, detector-response, and decoherence discipline

This surface owns the rev0293 quantum-record denominator. A route may not use state-preparation, detector-response, decoherence, pointer-record, objectivity, direct-observation, collapse-like, or public quantum-record wording unless the three executable ledgers are current:

- `STATE-PREPARATION-LEDGER.json`
- `DETECTOR-RESPONSE-LEDGER.json`
- `DECOHERENCE-POINTER-LEDGER.json`

## Noncompensation

Prepared-state support does not imply a detector response. Detector response does not imply decoherence or objectivity. Decoherence does not imply collapse, public-record closure, or candidate-native identifiability.

## Required split

Each route must declare the prepared state or source state, the detector/witness/POVM or response map, and the pointer/record/objectivity criterion separately. The metadata/provenance wrapper can support custody only; it is not a state, detector, or pointer record.
