# Lorentz, CPT, and spin-statistics discipline

This surface makes Lorentz covariance, spin-statistics, and CPT/discrete-symmetry support separate route debts.

A route may have Lorentz-covariant-looking equations, a null Lorentz-violation test corridor, a spin/helicity assignment, a statistics convention, or a CPT-compatible sector without recovering the observed matter sector or identifying a quantum-gravity candidate. The executable denominators are `LORENTZ-COVARIANCE-LEDGER.json`, `SPIN-STATISTICS-LEDGER.json`, and `CPT-DISCRETE-SYMMETRY-LEDGER.json`.

The ledgers may cap or roll back Lorentz, Poincare, local-frame, SME, spin, helicity, exchange, CPT, CP/T/parity, antimatter, and discrete-symmetry wording. They do not promote any route.
