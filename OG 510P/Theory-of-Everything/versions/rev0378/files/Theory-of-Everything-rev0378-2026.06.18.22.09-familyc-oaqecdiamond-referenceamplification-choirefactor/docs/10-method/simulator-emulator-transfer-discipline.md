# Simulator fidelity, surrogate emulation, and sim-to-real transfer discipline

Simulator, emulator, and digital-twin language is now route-denominator-bound. A route must distinguish the simulator or generator, the surrogate/emulator approximation, and the transfer or calibration step into a public record.

The live rule is noncompensation: simulator fidelity does not validate a surrogate, surrogate accuracy does not prove real-world transfer, and a successful transfer does not identify a candidate ontology.

Executable controls live in `SIMULATOR-FIDELITY-LEDGER.json`, `SURROGATE-EMULATOR-LEDGER.json`, and `SIM-TO-REAL-TRANSFER-LEDGER.json`.
