# Information-flow, entropy-accounting, and no-go discipline

## Purpose

This surface controls a failure mode that remained after physical viability checks: a route may be unitary, causal, stable, compositional, and public-record-bearing while still failing to say how information is carried, what entropy is counted, and which quantum-information no-go constraints are respected.

The executable owners are:

- `INFORMATION-FLOW-LEDGER.json`
- `ENTROPY-ACCOUNTING-LEDGER.json`
- `NO-GO-COMPLIANCE-LEDGER.json`
- `docs/30-program/information-entropy-summary.generated.md`

## Discipline

A route may not spend information-preserving, information-recovering, entropy-accounted, Page-curve, generalized-entropy, holographic-entropy, no-cloning, no-signalling, data-processing, or recovery-map language unless it names:

1. the source and target information objects;
2. the channel, map, decoder, detector pipeline, frame transport, likelihood update, or coarse-graining operation;
3. the entropy quantity or monotone being accounted;
4. the no-go theorem, inequality, or monotonicity constraint at stake;
5. the residual loss, leakage, duplication, signalling, overcounting, or recovery gap;
6. the route ceiling and rollback rule.

## Non-promotion rule

Information-flow, entropy-accounting, and no-go-compliance rows are necessary denominators for strong information language. They are not promotion gates. They can cap, freeze, or rollback route wording; they cannot create `S4`, `S5`, observed-sector closure, public-record closure, or candidate-native identifiability closure.

## Metadata demotion

Metadata, manifests, generated summaries, package provenance, and dependency graphs may preserve custody. They are not information channels, entropy quantities, no-cloning tests, no-signalling tests, data-processing tests, or recovery maps.
