# Quantum-record and decoherence stop rule

`OQ-0091` blocks a common overclaim: a prepared state, detector response, or decohered pointer record is treated as direct public access to candidate ontology.

A route must instead answer:

1. What is prepared?
2. What detects or witnesses it?
3. What response map or instrument is used?
4. What decoheres, and relative to which system/environment split?
5. What pointer or redundant record is public?
6. What recoherence, backaction, SPAM, frame, or basis residual remains?

A positive answer can at most support the route-local ceiling already declared in the route-state ledger.
