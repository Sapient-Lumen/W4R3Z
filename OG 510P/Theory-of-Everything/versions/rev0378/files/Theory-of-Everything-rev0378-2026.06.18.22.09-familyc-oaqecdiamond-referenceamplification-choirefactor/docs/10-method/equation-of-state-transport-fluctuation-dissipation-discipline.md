# Equation-of-state, transport-coefficient, and fluctuation-dissipation discipline

This surface owns the rev0306 hydrodynamic / transport / response denominator.

A route may not treat an effective fluid, equation of state, transport coefficient, Kubo formula, membrane-fluid analogy, Schwinger-Keldysh hydrodynamic EFT, KMS relation, stochastic/noise kernel, or fluctuation-dissipation relation as candidate-native closure unless the route has current rows in:

- `EQUATION-OF-STATE-LEDGER.json`,
- `TRANSPORT-COEFFICIENT-LEDGER.json`,
- `FLUCTUATION-DISSIPATION-LEDGER.json`.

The three rows are noncompensatory.  An equation of state does not determine transport.  A transport coefficient does not determine the microscopic state or candidate ontology.  A fluctuation-dissipation relation does not prove public-record closure, quantum completion, thermalization closure, black-hole information recovery, cosmology recovery, matter recovery, or observed-sector recovery.

## Required denominator questions

Each route must answer:

1. What are the state variables?
2. What equation or closure relation connects them?
3. Which hydrodynamic frame, ensemble, boundary condition, regulator, or state is fixed?
4. Which transport coefficient is claimed, and by what Kubo / response / constitutive relation?
5. Which noise, fluctuation, KMS, detailed-balance, or Schwinger-Keldysh structure is claimed?
6. What public record, detector, catalog, benchmark, horizon, or mathematical artifact carries the claim?
7. What rollback fires if any of the hydrodynamic rows fail?

## Non-promotion rule

Hydrodynamic success is an effective-description success unless the route separately pays public-record, measurement, stress-energy, matter, cosmology, black-hole, local-QFT, quantization, and observed-sector debts.
