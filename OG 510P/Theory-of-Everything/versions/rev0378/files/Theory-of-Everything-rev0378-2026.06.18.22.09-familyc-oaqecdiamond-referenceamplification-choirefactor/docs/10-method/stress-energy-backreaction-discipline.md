# Stress-energy source and backreaction discipline

## Rule

A stress tensor, effective source, matter current, detector source, or cosmological fluid is not a candidate-native source until its state, renormalization/subtraction rule, conservation law, coupling target, gauge/constraint status, and public-record carrier are declared.

## Noncompensation

- source consistency does not imply backreaction consistency;
- backreaction consistency does not imply an energy condition;
- an energy condition does not imply singularity resolution, censorship, wormhole exclusion, black-hole closure, cosmology recovery, or candidate identity.

## Current use

`STRESS-ENERGY-SOURCE-LEDGER.json`, `SEMICLASSICAL-BACKREACTION-LEDGER.json`, and `ENERGY-CONDITION-LEDGER.json` are the executable route denominators. They cap source/backreaction language under `OQ-0089`.
