# Local-QFT recovery stop rule

`OQ-0100` blocks local-QFT recovery language unless the route has current microcausality/locality, cluster-decomposition, and local-QFT-recovery rows.

The stop rule is designed to prevent these upgrades: local commutator to public record, no-signalling corridor to local-QFT recovery, cluster factorization to independent evidence, local algebra to observed-sector recovery, or effective field locality to candidate identity.

A local-QFT package may require locality, covariance, spectrum/positivity, time-slice/primitive causality, representation/state control, cluster behavior, detector/public-record discipline, and observed-sector recovery. Satisfying a subset can constrain a route but cannot promote it.
