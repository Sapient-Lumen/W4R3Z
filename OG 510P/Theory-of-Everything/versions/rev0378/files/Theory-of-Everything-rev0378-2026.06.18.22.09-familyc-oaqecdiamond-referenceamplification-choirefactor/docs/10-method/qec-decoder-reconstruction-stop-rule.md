# QEC decoder reconstruction stop rule

`OQ-0102` blocks code-subspace, logical-operator, recovery-map, decoder, correctable-region, erasure-correction, and holographic-QEC language unless `QEC-CODE-SUBSPACE-LEDGER.json`, `LOGICAL-OPERATOR-RECONSTRUCTION-LEDGER.json`, and `DECODER-CERTIFICATION-LEDGER.json` are declared and current.

Decoder success is evidence only for the declared code, noise model, approximation norm, and public replay target. It is not public-record closure and not ToE identification.
