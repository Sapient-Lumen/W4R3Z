# Uncertainty, significance, and coverage discipline

## Purpose

This surface makes interval, significance, and calibration language route-local. A route may report an error bar, credible interval, confidence belt, p-value, sigma-equivalent, discovery threshold, or coverage diagnostic only after the corresponding executable rows declare the construction and its limits.

## Noncompensation rule

An interval does not imply discovery. A discovery threshold does not imply coverage. Coverage in a declared ensemble does not imply candidate truth. None of these controls pays likelihood/prior, measurement, selection, benchmark, stochastic, public-record, or observed-sector debts.

## Required objects

Each route must name: the target parameter or statistic, the interval or region construction, the test statistic and null/alternative contrast, the look-elsewhere or multiplicity denominator, the calibration ensemble, and the rollback rule.

## Metadata wrapper

Archive row counts, lint status, generated-summary counts, and revision ranges are metadata. They can support custody and replay only; they are not statistical intervals or significance tests for physics.
