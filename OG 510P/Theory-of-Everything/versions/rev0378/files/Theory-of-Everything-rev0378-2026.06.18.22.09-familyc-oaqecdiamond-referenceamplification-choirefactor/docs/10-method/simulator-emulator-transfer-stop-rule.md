# Simulator, emulator, and sim-to-real transfer stop rule

`OQ-0108` blocks simulator-fidelity, synthetic-data, emulator, digital-twin, model-calibration, and sim-to-real claims unless the three executable ledgers are current.

A simulator result can constrain a route only after model-discrepancy, calibration, validation target, emulator error, and domain-gap controls are declared. No route is promoted by these rows.
