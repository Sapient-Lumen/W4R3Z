# Selection-function and multiplicity discipline

Revision: `rev0270`.

This surface makes selected-positive support auditable. A route may not call a record surprising, predictive, anomalous, discovery-grade, robust-after-search, or globally significant merely because the final visible artifact looks strong. It must declare the search frame, inclusion and exclusion rules, missingness, analyst degrees of freedom, and multiplicity control that turn a local positive into a support packet.

## Rule

```text
visible positive
+ public carrier
+ calibrated likelihood
+ contrast class
+ transportable domain
+ causal denominator
≠ discovery or surprise
```

unless the archive also declares:

```text
selection function
+ effective trial/search family
+ multiplicity or look-elsewhere control
+ missing/null/failed-attempt visibility
+ rollback handle
+ maximum authority effect
```

## Selection function

A selection function is the archive's description of how a record became visible to the route. It includes the searched candidate presentations, model classes, priors, gauges, benchmarks, event cuts, survey products, simulation targets, and discarded alternatives. Retrospective selection is allowed as a description, but it cannot be narrated as preregistered discovery.

## Multiplicity control

Multiplicity control asks how many effective chances the archive, field, route, or analysis had to find a favorable-looking result. The control can be a trial factor, false-discovery-rate discipline, blind/holdout protocol, hierarchical model comparison, or an explicit admission that the claim is uncorrected and therefore capped.

## Stop rule

`OQ-0067` blocks discovery, surprise, anomaly, prediction, robustness-after-search, benchmark-win, and selected-positive language unless `SELECTION-FUNCTION-LEDGER.json`, `MULTIPLICITY-CONTROL-LEDGER.json`, and `REPORTING-BIAS-LEDGER.json` permit the wording.

## Non-promotion rule

Selection and multiplicity rows can only cap, split, freeze, or roll back support. They do not promote any route beyond its state-machine, public-record, measurement, validity, causal, observed-sector, or residual-cap limits.
