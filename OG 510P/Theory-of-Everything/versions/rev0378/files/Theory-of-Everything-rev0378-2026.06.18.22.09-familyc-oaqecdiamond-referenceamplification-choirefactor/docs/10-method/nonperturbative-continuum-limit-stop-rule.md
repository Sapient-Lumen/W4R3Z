# Nonperturbative continuum-limit stop rule

No route may use nonperturbative definition, lattice completion, continuum limit, infinite-volume limit, finite-size scaling, Regge/simplicial recovery, CDT recovery, or simulation-to-physics wording unless `DISCRETIZATION-REGIME-LEDGER.json`, `FINITE-VOLUME-SCALING-LEDGER.json`, and `CONTINUUM-EXTRAPOLATION-LEDGER.json` are current.

A valid row can cap, narrow, or roll back wording. It cannot promote a route beyond its state-machine ceiling.
