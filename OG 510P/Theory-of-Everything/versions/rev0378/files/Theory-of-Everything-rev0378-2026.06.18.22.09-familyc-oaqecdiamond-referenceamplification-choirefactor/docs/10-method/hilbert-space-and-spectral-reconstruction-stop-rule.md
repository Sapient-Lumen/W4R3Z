# Hilbert-space and spectral-reconstruction stop rule

No route may use Hilbert-space closure, state-space recovered, GNS representation, Fock-sector identity, representation equivalence, spectral completeness, mass spectrum recovered, spectral density, spectral geometry, or spectrum-identifies-candidate language unless the Hilbert-space, representation-map, and spectral-reconstruction rows are declared and current.

The permitted output is usually a bounded route-local denominator. Stronger wording still requires public-record custody, observed-sector recovery, quotient control, gauge/constraint control, detector/measurement control, and state-machine promotion gates.
