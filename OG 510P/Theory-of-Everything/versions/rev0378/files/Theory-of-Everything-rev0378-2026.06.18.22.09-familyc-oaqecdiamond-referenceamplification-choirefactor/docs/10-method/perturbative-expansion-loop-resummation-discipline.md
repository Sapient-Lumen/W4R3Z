# Perturbative expansion / loop-order / resummation discipline

Perturbative success is useful but denominator-bound. A route that invokes a coupling expansion, loop calculation, EFT truncation, post-Newtonian series, genus expansion, 1/N expansion, weak-field approximation, or likelihood perturbation must declare the expansion parameter, expansion point, domain of smallness, truncation order, error estimate, regularization/matching scheme, and public record.

The core noncompensation rule is:

```text
low-order agreement
+ finite counterterm statement
+ useful resummation
≠ all-order control
≠ nonperturbative completion
≠ UV completion
≠ candidate-native identifiability
```

Perturbative quantum gravity as an effective field theory can be predictive at low energies while still carrying high-energy and nonperturbative debts. Loop-order and counterterm rows therefore cap wording rather than promote routes.
