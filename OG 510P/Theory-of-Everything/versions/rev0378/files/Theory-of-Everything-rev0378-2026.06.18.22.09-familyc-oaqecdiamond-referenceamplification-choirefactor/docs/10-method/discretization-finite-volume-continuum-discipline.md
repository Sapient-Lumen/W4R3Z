# Discretization, finite-volume, and continuum-extrapolation discipline

`OQ-0093` keeps finite regulator support from becoming nonperturbative completion. A route must separate its discretization object, finite-volume or finite-size scaling object, and continuum-extrapolation object before it may use lattice, triangulation, simulator, thermodynamic-limit, continuum-limit, or nonperturbative-definition language.

The discipline is noncompensatory: a good discretization does not pay finite-volume debt, finite-volume scaling does not pay continuum-extrapolation debt, and a continuum extrapolation does not pay public-record, observed-sector, gauge, topology, measure, or candidate-native debts.
