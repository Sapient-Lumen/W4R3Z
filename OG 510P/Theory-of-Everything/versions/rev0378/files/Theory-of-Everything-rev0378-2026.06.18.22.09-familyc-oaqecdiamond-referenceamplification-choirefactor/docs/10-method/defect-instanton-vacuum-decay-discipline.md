# Defect / instanton / vacuum-decay discipline

This method surface separates three nonperturbative-sector claims that are often merged in prose: topological defects, instanton/saddle data, and vacuum-decay/tunneling channels.

A route may use defect, soliton, wall, cosmic-string, brane, flux, instanton, Euclidean-saddle, bounce, transseries, metastability, false-vacuum, or tunneling-rate language only through the route-local rows in `TOPOLOGICAL-DEFECT-LEDGER.json`, `INSTANTON-SADDLE-LEDGER.json`, and `VACUUM-DECAY-TUNNELING-LEDGER.json`.

The rows are cap/rollback controls. They do not identify a candidate, select a vacuum, recover cosmology, prove Standard Model stability, or establish nonperturbative completion.
