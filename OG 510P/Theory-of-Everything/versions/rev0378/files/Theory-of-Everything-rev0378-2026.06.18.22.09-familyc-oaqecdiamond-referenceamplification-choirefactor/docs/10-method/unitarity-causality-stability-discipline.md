# Unitarity, causality, and stability discipline

This surface owns the rev0281 viability-denominator rule.

A route may have formal consistency, public records, matched scales, and composable modules while still failing physical viability. The archive therefore separates three questions:

1. what norm, probability, inner product, state space, S-matrix, code subspace, transition kernel, or likelihood object is claimed to be unitary or normalized;
2. what causal object is claimed to preserve microcausality, analyticity, retarded support, signal order, hyperbolicity, causal-set order, or cone structure;
3. what stability/positivity object is claimed to exclude or bound ghosts, tachyons, gradient instabilities, negative spectral weights, unstable vacua, runaway modes, or unstable benchmarks.

The rows in `UNITARITY-CHECK-LEDGER.json`, `CAUSALITY-CONE-LEDGER.json`, and `STABILITY-POSITIVITY-LEDGER.json` are not promotion devices. They are denominators and rollback handles. A route that lacks them may still have clue, constraint, or bounded partial-identification value, but it may not spend physical-viability, healthy-degree-of-freedom, microcausal, analytic, positive-energy, ghost-free, tachyon-free, or stable-vacuum language.

## Non-compensation rule

A strong global-composition row cannot compensate for a failed unitarity row. A clean unitarity check cannot compensate for an acausal propagation row. A causal analytic amplitude cannot compensate for an unstable vacuum or ghost. Stability of a numerical pipeline cannot compensate for physical stability of a candidate ontology. Each row spends only its own kind of credit.

## Metadata demotion

The metadata/provenance rows only certify package custody and generated-surface replay. They do not certify a Hilbert space, a causal cone, an S-matrix, a spectral density, a Hamiltonian bound, a vacuum lifetime, or a mode spectrum.
