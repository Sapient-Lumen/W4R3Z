# Boundary, initial-data, and sector-selection discipline

## Rule

A result is local to the boundary, initial data, ensemble, background, gauge, vacuum, regulator, apparatus state, survey window, map pipeline, or solution sector under which it was obtained until the route declares how that choice is fixed, varied, transported, or defeated.

The archive therefore treats boundary conditions, initial data, and sector selection as support denominators, not as background prose.

## Stop rule

No route may use background-independent, unique solution, selected branch, boundary-condition independent, vacuum-selected, ensemble-robust, gauge-independent, sector-global, or candidate-native language unless the executable rows in `BOUNDARY-CONDITION-LEDGER.json`, `INITIAL-DATA-LEDGER.json`, and `SECTOR-SELECTION-LEDGER.json` are current.

## Non-promotion rule

These rows may cap, freeze, demote, or roll back support. They do not promote a route beyond its state-machine ceiling or public-record/observed-sector/residual-cap burdens.
