# Compressed-record and summary-statistic stop rule

OQ-0107 blocks compressed-record overclaim. No route may use data-reduced, compressed, embedded, feature-extracted, score-compressed, Fisher-preserving, information-bottleneck, sufficient-statistic, ABC, SBI, or likelihood-free compression language unless data-reduction, feature-extraction, and summary-statistic sufficiency rows are declared and current.

A summary is not a public record. A feature is not an ontology. A compressed statistic is not an exact likelihood unless the route states the sufficiency target, residual loss, replay protocol, and rollback rule.
