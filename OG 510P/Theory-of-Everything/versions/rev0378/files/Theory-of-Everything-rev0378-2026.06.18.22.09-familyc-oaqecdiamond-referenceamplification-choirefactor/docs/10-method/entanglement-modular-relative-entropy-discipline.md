# Entanglement, modular flow, and relative-entropy discipline

Introduced in `rev0303`.

This surface prevents entanglement, modular-flow, and recovery-map language from becoming candidate identity by repetition. The route must declare the subsystem or algebra, the state pair, the modular object, and the recovery target before it can spend entanglement-wedge, geometry-from-entanglement, modular-Hamiltonian, relative-entropy, JLMS, QES, or Petz/recovery wording.

The governing executable ledgers are:

- `ENTANGLEMENT-STRUCTURE-LEDGER.json`
- `MODULAR-FLOW-LEDGER.json`
- `RELATIVE-ENTROPY-RECOVERY-LEDGER.json`

Noncompensation rule:

```text
entanglement structure
+ modular flow
+ relative entropy or recovery map
≠ public-record closure
≠ geometry recovery
≠ black-hole information solution
≠ candidate-native identifiability
```

The rows are cap, rollback, and denominator rows. They do not promote routes.
