# Residual-deficiency scorecard

## Purpose

The archive often says that a route is bounded, partial, or underdetermined. This surface gives those words a shared scorecard so route rows can carry comparable caps without pretending that all routes are measured by the same metric.

## Deficiency grades

| Grade | Meaning | Typical ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| `D0` | no declared inverse or public experiment | `S0`/`S1` |
| `D1` | target and record named, but inverse is many-to-one or surrogate-bound | `S2` |
| `D2` | bounded inverse over selected regime, with major borrowed bridge fields | `S2`/`S3` |
| `D3` | restricted inverse survives declared quotient, stability, and negative controls | bounded `S3` |
| `D4` | native public experiment has low unresolved loss under adversarial controls | candidate for `S4` |
| `D5` | cross-family countermodels and observed-sector recovery collapse at the claimed ToE target | candidate for `S5` |

No current route row earns `D4` or `D5`.

## Deficiency components

A route's residual deficiency is the unresolved loss across at least seven channels:

1. **Target loss** — the route identifies a surrogate, presentation, compactification, or equation rather than the claimed target.
2. **Record loss** — the record is borrowed from a lab, boundary theory, simulator, or public software ecology.
3. **Acquisition loss** — the public record cannot be acquired at the resolution required by the target quotient.
4. **Inverse loss** — multiple candidate structures collapse to the same record.
5. **Stability loss** — the result changes under gauge, frame, regulator, code-space, prior, or nuisance variation.
6. **Abstention loss** — the route lacks a no-verdict state and therefore forces misleading best answers.
7. **Public-bridge loss** — custody, replay, challenge, or equivalence is not candidate-native.

The scorecard is qualitative for now. It can become quantitative only where a route supplies a real experiment-comparison or partial-identification region.

## Use in route rows

Every route row should carry an `earliest_blocker` and a `residual_cap`. The cap must survive exports, release notes, generated mirrors, and external summaries. If the cap disappears, the route re-enters through quarantine rather than promotion.
