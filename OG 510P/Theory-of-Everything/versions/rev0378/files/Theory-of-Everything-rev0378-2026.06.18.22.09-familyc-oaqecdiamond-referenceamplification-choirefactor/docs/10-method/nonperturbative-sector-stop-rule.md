# Nonperturbative-sector stop rule

`OQ-0097` blocks nonperturbative-sector promotion unless the route declares: the defect or topological sector; the instanton, saddle, contour, negative modes, determinant, and continuation rule; and the vacuum pair, decay channel, tunneling rate, prefactor, and residual observed-sector debts.

No compensation rule: a defect row does not pay saddle debt; a saddle row does not pay vacuum-decay debt; a decay row does not pay candidate identity, observed-sector, matter, cosmology, or public-record debt.
