# Physical viability and stability stop rule

`OQ-0078` blocks viability vocabulary until the route has declared the relevant viability denominator.

Forbidden escalation pattern:

```text
global consistency
+ matched EFT / RG flow
+ formal theorem
+ public likelihood
+ code replay
+ plausible physical story
= physically viable route
```

Allowed wording must be narrower:

- a proof can be a proof under assumptions;
- a likelihood can be an update under a contrast class;
- a matched EFT can be a scale-local constraint;
- a composed module can be a local-to-global candidate object;
- a unitarity/causality/stability row can cap viability language.

Only after all relevant rows are current may the archive say that a route has route-local viability support, and even then the route remains capped by its state-machine ceiling and observed-sector/public-record debts.

## Rollback triggers

Rollback fires when a route loses norm preservation, fails reflection/spectral positivity, violates a declared causal cone, fails analyticity/crossing assumptions used by an update, develops ghosts or gradient instabilities, has an unbounded Hamiltonian in the declared sector, or relies on a metadata/computational artifact to stand in for physical viability.
