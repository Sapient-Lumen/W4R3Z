# Asymptotic scattering and infrared stop rule

No route may use `S-matrix`, `asymptotic completeness`, `IR finite`, `soft theorem`, `memory effect`, `BMS charge`, `Faddeev-Kulish`, `inclusive cross section`, `finite-time scattering`, or `scattering observable` language unless the route names current rows in:

- `ASYMPTOTIC-STATE-LEDGER.json`,
- `INFRARED-DRESSING-LEDGER.json`,
- `SCATTERING-OBSERVABLE-LEDGER.json`.

The rows are noncompensatory. Asymptotic-state control does not pay IR debt; IR dressing does not pay detector/public-record debt; inclusive observables do not identify a ToE candidate.
