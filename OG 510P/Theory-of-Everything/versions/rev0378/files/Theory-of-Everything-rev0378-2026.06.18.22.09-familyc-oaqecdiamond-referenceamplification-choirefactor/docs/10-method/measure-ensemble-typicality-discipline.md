# Measure / ensemble / typicality discipline

This surface owns the rev0286 rule that probability and prediction language must be denominator-bound. A route may not spend measure, ensemble, naturalness, anthropic, observer-weighted, or typicality language until `MEASURE-DEFINITION-LEDGER.json`, `ENSEMBLE-SAMPLING-LEDGER.json`, and `TYPICALITY-WEIGHTING-LEDGER.json` say what is being measured, sampled, conditioned, and weighted.

The key noncompensation rule is:

```text
measure exists
+ ensemble exists
+ typical member or observer weight exists
≠ prediction
≠ candidate identity
≠ observed-sector recovery
≠ ToE closure
```

The row must name the measured space, the quotient/cutoff/normalization rule, the ensemble generation rule, the member-vs-average distinction, the observer/reference class, the rare-event risk, and the rollback trigger. Metadata/provenance rows remain custody-only and do not define a physical measure.
