# Prediction / preregistration / blinding stop rule

`OQ-0110` blocks prediction, preregistration, registered report, blind-analysis, unblinding, confirmatory, severe-test, and protocol-deviation language unless three executable rows are current:

- `PROSPECTIVE-PREDICTION-LEDGER.json`
- `PREREGISTRATION-PROTOCOL-LEDGER.json`
- `BLINDING-DEVIATION-LEDGER.json`

## Forbidden inference

Do not infer candidate-native support from a forecast timestamp, preregistration, registered report, blinding/unblinding practice, or deviation disclosure. These rows make bias and timing auditable; they do not create physical evidence.
