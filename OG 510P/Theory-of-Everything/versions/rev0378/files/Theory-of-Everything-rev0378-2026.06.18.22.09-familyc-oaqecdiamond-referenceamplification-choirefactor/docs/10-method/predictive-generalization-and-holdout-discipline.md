# Predictive generalization and holdout discipline

This surface separates retrospective agreement from predictive or out-of-sample support.

A result can be called predictive or generalizing only if the route names the construction record, the test or holdout record, the public carrier, the acquisition protocol, the capacity row, the complexity penalty, and the rollback handle.

Generalization can be formal, benchmark-based, catalog-based, cross-domain, apparatus-transfer, or forecast-realization. Each is narrower than candidate-native identifiability.

Forbidden inference:

```text
retrospective fit -> prediction
benchmark score -> general theory adequacy
same catalog replay -> independent validation
metadata replay -> physical generalization
```
