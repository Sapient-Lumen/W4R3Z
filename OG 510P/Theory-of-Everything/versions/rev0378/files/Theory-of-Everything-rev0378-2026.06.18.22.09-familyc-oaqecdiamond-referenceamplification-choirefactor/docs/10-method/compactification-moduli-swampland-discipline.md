# Compactification, moduli, and swampland discipline

This surface makes compactification geometry, moduli stabilization, and swampland/landscape compatibility separate route debts.

A route may work in an internal geometry, flux sector, compactified target, benchmark geometry, apparatus domain, or cosmological parameter domain without selecting the observed world. A route may stabilize a modulus or effective parameter without fixing all low-energy scales, towers, runaways, tadpoles, Standard Model structure, cosmology, or public-record bridges. A route may satisfy or pressure a swampland-style condition without becoming a UV-complete candidate.

The executable denominator is carried by `COMPACTIFICATION-GEOMETRY-LEDGER.json`, `MODULI-STABILIZATION-LEDGER.json`, and `SWAMPLAND-COMPATIBILITY-LEDGER.json`. The ledgers may cap or roll back compactification, landscape, de Sitter, tower, distance, WGC, tadpole, and vacuum-selection language. They do not promote any route.
