# Black-hole horizon / thermodynamics / evaporation discipline

Black-hole-sector language is now denominator-controlled.

A route may recover a horizon-like surface, a thermodynamic relation, an entropy formula, a microstate count, or a radiation channel without thereby recovering black-hole physics as a candidate-native sector. The archive therefore separates three objects:

1. `HORIZON-STRUCTURE-LEDGER.json` — what surface, boundary, observer, area, charge, or horizon proxy is being used.
2. `BLACK-HOLE-THERMODYNAMICS-LEDGER.json` — which first-law, second-law, generalized-entropy, Wald/Noether, temperature, heat-capacity, or microstate object is claimed.
3. `EVAPORATION-RADIATION-LEDGER.json` — which Hawking-radiation, greybody, backreaction, endpoint, Page-curve, or public-radiation object is claimed.

## Noncompensation rule

Horizon mechanics does not pay entropy/microstate debt. Entropy accounting does not pay radiation/endpoint debt. Radiation or Page-curve language does not pay public-record, subsystem, no-go, information-flow, or observed-sector debt.

## Promotion rule

These rows can only cap, freeze, demote, or roll back route language. They do not promote a route to `S4` or `S5`.
