# Unit conventions, constants, and scale-setting discipline

rev0313 makes unit conventions, fundamental constants, and physical scale-setting executable route denominators.

A route must separate:

- arbitrary unit choice;
- dimensional-analysis or natural-unit convention;
- defining constants used by a unit system;
- measured or adjusted constants;
- dimensionless physical ratios;
- candidate-native predictions of constants;
- scale-setting and hierarchy claims.

## Noncompensation rule

Dimensional consistency, natural units, Planck units, CODATA values, or scale normalization can make a formula easier to read. They cannot recover the observed sector, solve a hierarchy problem, predict constants, or identify a candidate unless the route pays its measurement, renormalization, matter, cosmology, public-record, and residual debts.
