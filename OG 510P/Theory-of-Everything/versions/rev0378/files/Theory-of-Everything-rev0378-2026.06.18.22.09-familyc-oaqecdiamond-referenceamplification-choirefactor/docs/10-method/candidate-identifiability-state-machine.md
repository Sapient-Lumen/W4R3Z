# Candidate identifiability state machine

## Purpose

This surface compresses the `OQ-0057` identifiability custody stack into one durable state machine. The many route, challenge, export, reimport, lineage, release, head, authority, transition, and rollback dockets remain valid worksheets, but their archive role is now defined by this method surface: they are transition evidence, not separate sources of scientific promotion.

The aim is to keep the physics spine visible while preserving the anti-overclaiming discipline that the prior dockets earned.

## Canonical states

| State | Meaning | Entry condition | Exit condition | Forbidden move |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `S0` | registered candidate or route with no live identifiability evidence | candidate, family, or route is named | a relevant clue, record, or formal map appears | promotion by reputation, elegance, or framework label |
| `S1` | clue / trace | evidence is relevant to the target family | owned record object and route fields are named | closure language, even if the clue is impressive |
| `S2` | structured partial route | target, record, acquisition, equivalence, inverse, stability, abstention, and public-bridge fields are declared | the route works under bounded native conditions | surrogate-target laundering or same-package repetition |
| `S3` | bounded candidate-native partial identifiability | native route succeeds only under declared target grain, record denominator, quotient, regime, and residual cap | public bridge, residual deficiency, and adversarial controls close within scope | global ToE, witness-closure, or ontology-closure wording |
| `S4` | candidate-native operational closure | native public record route is replayable, adversarially tested, and equivalent under declared frame / publicness rules | defeat, stronger replacement, or scope failure | borrowed public bridge, hidden witness owner, cap erasure |
| `S5` | full candidate-native identifiability closure | all `S4` conditions plus surviving cross-family countermodels and observed-sector recovery at the claimed target | only by defeat or replacement | any use in the current archive without an explicit extraordinary proof burden |
| `AT` | authority transition pending | current authority is proposed to change, narrow, split, vacate, or be replaced | authority-transition row resolves or freezes | silent replacement by later bundle, cleaner mirror, or stronger summary |
| `AR` | authority rollback pending | a transition fails, is withdrawn, or becomes unreplayable | rollback row resolves fallback, split, vacancy, or quarantine | slogan-level undo, predecessor nostalgia, or successor deletion without owner-row audit |
| `Q` | quarantine | reimport, branch, challenge, carrier, public-bridge, or route evidence cannot yet be replayed | explicit requalification through the state machine | re-entry by summary, paraphrase, or citation polish |
| `T` | terminal / retired | route is closed, abandoned, displaced, or kept as historical pointer | exceptional reopening with trigger and owner replay | cosmetic revival or residual-cap deletion |

`S5` is retained only to prevent later terminology drift. No current live lane is allowed to spend `S5` language.

## Worksheet map

The prior `candidate-native-identifiability-*` files are now read as worksheets for state transitions:

- field worksheets: cash-out template, route ledger, equivalence-collapse, acquisition-realizability, inverse-stability, abstention, and public-bridge challenge;
- evidence worksheets: evidence intake, supersession / decay, dependency propagation, conflict adjudication, decision trace, replay / rollback, challenge response, and challenge closure;
- stress worksheets: adversarial-control battery, calibration-anchor docket, and residual-cap ledger;
- carrier worksheets: export-claim docket, reimport firewall, lineage-merge docket, release seal, and seal verification;
- authority worksheets: head adoption, succession, branch arbitration, retirement / vacancy, reinstatement / thaw, current-authority ledger, authority transition, and authority rollback.

The state machine owns the semantics. The worksheets own the receipt details.

## Compression rule

A new identifiability-control surface is not earned merely because the archive names one more failure mode. It is earned only if it changes at least one state transition, one denominator field, one linter-enforceable invariant, or one worked example.

Existing dockets should not be deleted blindly because old releases and route rows point to them. Future revisions should demote or merge them only after a replacement map preserves links, claims, and rollback handles.

## No-promotion clause

Installing this state machine does not promote any candidate. It lowers accidental promotion risk by making the promotion ceiling explicit:

- Family C remains the strongest bounded `S3` partial-identification case.
- Formal completion bids remain mostly `S1`/`S2` with candidate-specific pockets.
- Low-energy laboratory routes can earn discriminator-class `S3` pockets without identifying a full ToE candidate.
- Frame / observer routes remain access-discipline progress unless they provide same-fact transport and public-reference closure.

## Speculative next formalization

The likely mathematical form of the state machine is an experiment-comparison diagram:

```text
candidate-native structure -> candidate-native record experiment -> public record experiment -> comparison / decision rule
```

`S2` means the diagram exists. `S3` means part of it is invertible up to a declared quotient and residual cap. `S4` would mean the public bridge and adversarial controls survive without borrowed witness ownership. `S5` would mean that the surviving quotient identifies the candidate at the claimed ToE target grain.
