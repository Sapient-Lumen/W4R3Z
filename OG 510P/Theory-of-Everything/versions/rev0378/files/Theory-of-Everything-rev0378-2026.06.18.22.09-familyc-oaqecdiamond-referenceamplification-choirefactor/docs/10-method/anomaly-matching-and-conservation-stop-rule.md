# Anomaly matching and conservation stop rule

## Stop rule

If a route uses any of the following phrases, it must route through `SYMMETRY-REALIZATION-LEDGER.json`, `ANOMALY-MATCHING-LEDGER.json`, and `CONSERVATION-LAW-LEDGER.json`:

- exact symmetry,
- generalized symmetry,
- non-invertible symmetry,
- no global symmetry,
- anomaly free,
- anomaly matched,
- anomaly inflow,
- Ward identity,
- conserved current,
- charge conservation,
- soft charge,
- symmetry protected,
- duality symmetry,
- symmetry completion.

The rows must say what is being acted on, which anomaly datum or obstruction is checked, which conserved object is tracked, and what failure triggers rollback.

## Conservation is not closure

A conservation law can survive inside a selected sector while the route still fails observed-sector recovery, public-record custody, gauge quotienting, or candidate-native identifiability. Conservation wording therefore spends at most the authority ceiling declared by the route row.
