# Landscape, swampland, and vacuum-selection stop rule

`OQ-0098` blocks the inference from a compactification, stabilized modulus, de Sitter/uplift package, landscape membership, or swampland-compatible constraint to candidate identity.

The stop rule asks five questions before stronger wording is allowed:

1. What internal geometry or domain has actually been fixed?
2. Which moduli or effective parameters are stabilized, and by what rule?
3. Which tower, distance, tadpole, WGC, de Sitter, no-go, or EFT compatibility condition is load-bearing?
4. Which matter-sector, cosmology, public-record, viability, and observed-sector debts remain unpaid?
5. What rollback fires if compactification, stabilization, or compatibility fails?

No route may use compactification, moduli-stabilized, landscape, swampland-safe, de Sitter-controlled, tower-controlled, WGC-safe, tadpole-cancelled, or vacuum-selected wording unless the route-local rows are declared and current.
