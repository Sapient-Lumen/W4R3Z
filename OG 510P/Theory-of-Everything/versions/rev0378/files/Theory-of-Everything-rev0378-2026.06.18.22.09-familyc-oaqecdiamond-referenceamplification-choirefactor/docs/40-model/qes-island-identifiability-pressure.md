# QES / island identifiability pressure

## Purpose

Quantum extremal surfaces and island-style reconstruction sharpen Family C, but they also sharpen the warning against overclaiming. Reconstruction can move information-access boundaries without automatically defining candidate-native public records or full target identifiability.

## Load-bearing anchors

`REF-0191` gives a compact entanglement-wedge reconstruction / Page-curve route in which interior reconstruction can depend on code-space and state-information conditions. `REF-0192` anchors the island / quantum-extremal-surface pressure in evaporating black-hole settings.

## Pressure questions

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| What is the record object? | radiation entropy, boundary data, decoded operator, island region, or detector history are not identical records |
| What is the target quotient? | reconstructing an interior operator is not the same as identifying a candidate ontology |
| What is the code space? | code-subspace dependence can preserve bounded reconstruction while blocking global identifiability |
| What errors are allowed? | tiny nonperturbative errors may matter differently for Page curves, decoding, and public bridge claims |
| Who owns publicness? | boundary theory, radiation collector, decoder, or candidate-native witness substrate may carry different debts |
| What negative control mimics the gain? | a dual-description or decoder-prior decoy may reproduce the public pattern without fixing the ontology |

## Family-C score effect

QES / island results strengthen the bounded Family-C reconstruction corridor. They do not erase the archive's ceiling:

- reconstruction is stronger than metaphor;
- Page-curve recovery is stronger than a thermodynamic slogan;
- code-subspace success is stronger than no inverse map;
- but none of those by itself closes public witness ownership, same-fact transport, or full candidate-native identifiability.

The default promotion ceiling is bounded `S3` unless the public record denominator and negative controls are filled.

## Speculative pressure

Islands may imply that the archive's naive public/private partition is too simple. The record accessible to one exterior system may include interior degrees by reconstruction, while another public route may not. Future route rows should therefore distinguish physical possession of a record, reconstructive access to a record, and candidate-native definition of the record.
