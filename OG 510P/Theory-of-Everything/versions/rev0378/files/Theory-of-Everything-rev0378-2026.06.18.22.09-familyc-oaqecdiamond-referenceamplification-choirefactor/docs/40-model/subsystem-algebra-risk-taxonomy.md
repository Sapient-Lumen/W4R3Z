# Subsystem algebra risk taxonomy

Risks tracked by `OQ-0083`:

- tensor-factor notation used where only an operator algebra is declared;
- gauge constraints or gravitational dressing creating boundary/edge degrees of freedom;
- type-III local algebras mistaken for density-matrix subsystems;
- type-II or crossed-product algebras mistaken for candidate closure;
- center choice changing entropy while the route calls the entropy invariant;
- detector thresholds, survey bins, map masks, or benchmark namespaces mistaken for physical centers;
- metadata partitions counted as physical independence.
