# Evidence aggregation risk taxonomy

This surface names model-side ways that evidence can look diverse while sharing the same support chain.

## Repackaged custody

A result may appear as a paper, a dataset, a repository, a metadata record, a generated table, and a benchmark artifact. That improves auditability, but it may still be one evidence unit.

## Shared quotient

Two descriptions may agree because they are presentations of the same duality, gauge, frame, compactification, regulator, or operational quotient. Agreement at that level is not independent candidate selection.

## Shared nuisance stack

Survey likelihoods, waveform catalogs, foreground-cleaned maps, and detector pipelines can share calibration, covariance, priors, selection functions, templates, or nuisance parameterizations. Agreement across analyses is not automatically independent if the same nuisance stack carries the contrast.

## Shared benchmark target

A learned inverse map, decoder, simulator, or reconstruction pipeline can overfit the same target object or scorer. Multiple implementations may still share one benchmark evidence unit.

## Shared theoretical package

Formal derivations can share the same locality, code-subspace, asymptotic, regulator, or thermodynamic assumptions. Parallel derivations do not become independent until the shared assumptions are stress-tested.

## Genuine independent challenge

The strongest aggregation case is a public record produced through a different acquisition route that attacks a live negative control or quotient. Even then the result spends only the route-local credit declared in `CREDIT-ALLOCATION-LEDGER.json`.
