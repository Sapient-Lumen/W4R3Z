# Quantization / correspondence risk taxonomy

Route risks now tracked by rev0282:

- inequivalent quantizations;
- operator-ordering, measure, contour, polarization, or representation ambiguity;
- noncommuting reduction and quantization;
- missing or nonunique Hilbert/state space;
- problem-of-time or clock-selection mismatch;
- WKB / Born-Oppenheimer backreaction failure;
- decoherence mistaken for ontology;
- classical recovery mistaken for quantum completion;
- metadata or proof artifact mistaken for a quantum-classical bridge.
