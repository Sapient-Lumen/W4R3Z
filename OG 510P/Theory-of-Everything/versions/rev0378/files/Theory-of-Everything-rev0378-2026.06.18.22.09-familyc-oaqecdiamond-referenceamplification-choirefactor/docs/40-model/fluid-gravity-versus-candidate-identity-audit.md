# Fluid/gravity correspondence versus candidate identity audit

Fluid/gravity results can show that a gravitational solution class encodes hydrodynamic evolution or transport data under specific boundary, horizon, derivative-expansion, and state assumptions.  They do not by themselves show that the hydrodynamic effective description is the microscopic theory, that the candidate recovers the observed sector, or that a public detector record has candidate-native status.

Route rows must therefore name the equation-of-state, transport-coefficient, and fluctuation-dissipation handles before spending fluid/gravity language.
