# Entanglement / modular-flow risk taxonomy

Introduced in `rev0303`.

Risk classes:

- subsystem or algebra not declared;
- edge mode, center, gauge, or gravitational dressing hidden;
- RT/HRT/QES surface treated as global geometry rather than route-local bridge;
- modular flow treated as physical time without frame, algebra, and state discipline;
- code-subspace reconstruction exported as global reconstruction;
- public-record carrier missing or only benchmark-local.

The taxonomy is enforced through `ENTANGLEMENT-STRUCTURE-LEDGER.json`, `MODULAR-FLOW-LEDGER.json`, and `RELATIVE-ENTROPY-RECOVERY-LEDGER.json`.
