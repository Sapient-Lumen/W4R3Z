# Hydrodynamic and transport risk taxonomy

Hydrodynamic language creates several distinct risks:

- equation-of-state resemblance can be confused with microscopic identity;
- viscosity, conductivity, or diffusion coefficients can be confused with full constitutive closure;
- fluid/gravity maps can be confused with bidirectional candidate identity;
- KMS or fluctuation-dissipation relations can be confused with public quantum records;
- effective dark-sector fluids can be confused with dark-sector ontology;
- detector, catalog, or benchmark response coefficients can be confused with candidate-native observables.

The rev0306 ledgers force these claims to remain route-local.
