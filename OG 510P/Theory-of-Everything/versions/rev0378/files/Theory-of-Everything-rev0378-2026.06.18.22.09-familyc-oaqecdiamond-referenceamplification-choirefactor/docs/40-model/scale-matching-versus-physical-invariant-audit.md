# Scale matching versus physical invariant audit

## Audit question

When a route says a scale-dependent calculation supports a candidate, the archive asks whether the support belongs to:

```text
scheme coordinate
running parameter
flow trajectory
matched invariant
public-record observable
candidate-native observable
```

Only the last three can carry physical route credit, and only with declared ceilings.

## Family C

Holographic/radial scale and code-subspace RG language must be separated from physical bulk observables and public records. A reconstruction theorem can remain bounded `S3`, but radial cutoff, boundary counterterm, or dictionary-scale control does not become global candidate identity.

## Asymptotic safety

A fixed point, critical surface, relevant direction, or effective-average-action flow is route-local until regulator, truncation, field-redefinition, essential-coupling, and observable-matching questions are controlled.

## EFT / amplitudes / bootstrap

Wilson coefficients, positivity bounds, running parameters, and low-energy amplitudes must be matched to physical observables under a declared cutoff, power counting, and field-basis quotient. Basis-dependent coefficients are not candidate ontology.

## Empirical corridors

Lab, GW, DESI/BAO, and CMB corridors use apparatus windows, waveform scales, survey parameterizations, map pipelines, and nuisance priors. These are matching/regularization objects, not direct route identity.

## Metadata

Package version, generated summary, lint pass, and provenance chain are custody objects. They do not define physical scale, RG flow, or matching conditions.
