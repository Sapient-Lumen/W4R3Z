# Transport coefficient versus observed-sector recovery audit

A transport coefficient is a response object.  It may be extracted from a Kubo formula, a hydrodynamic EFT, a membrane paradigm, a black-brane calculation, a detector response, a cosmological perturbation model, or a benchmark.  None of those extractions is observed-sector recovery unless the route also pays the relevant measurement, public-record, matter, cosmology, black-hole, local-QFT, and validity-domain debts.
