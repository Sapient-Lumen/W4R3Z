# Intervention versus observation audit

Revision: `rev0269`

The archive's empirical routes span direct laboratory control, indirect perturbation, formal variation, simulation ablation, natural experiments, and unmanipulable observational catalogs. These are not the same kind of causal evidence.

## Audit rule

- BMV/GIE-style laboratory rows can earn direct or indirect intervention language only for apparatus-controlled variables and mediator-class discriminators, not for full candidate ontology.
- Gravitational-wave, BAO, and CMB rows are observational or natural-experiment rows. They can constrain models and mechanisms but cannot be described as randomized interventions.
- Family C, asymptotic safety, amplitudes, string/M, thermodynamic, and causal-set rows mostly use formal variations or simulation ablations. They may probe stability but not direct physical manipulability.
- Quantum-reference-frame rows use frame transformations and relational counterfactuals; they require equivalence/transport rules before public-record language can be causal.

The result is conservative: causal rows refine wording and rollback, not route state.
