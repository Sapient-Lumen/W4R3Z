# Physical viability versus formal consistency audit

Formal consistency is not physical viability.

A route may satisfy its composition, interface, and global-consistency rows while still failing the physical viability audit. The audit asks:

1. Does the route have a physical state space or only a formal/statistical object?
2. Is probability conserved in the relevant dynamics, S-matrix, transition kernel, or reconstruction map?
3. Does the route control analytic continuation, commutator support, hyperbolicity, or causal order?
4. Are ghost, tachyonic, gradient, spectral, vacuum, and mode instabilities absent, bounded, or explicitly residualized?
5. Which public record, proof artifact, or benchmark can actually replay the viability test?
6. What rollback fires if viability fails?

Until those questions are answered, the archive may retain local support but not healthy-theory, physical-viability, or ToE-closure wording.
