# Gauge and constraint risk taxonomy

Common route risks:

1. **Representative risk:** a coordinate, gauge-fixed field, potential, polarization representative, simulator label, or perturbation variable is treated as the physical record.
2. **Constraint-nonclosure risk:** a first-class algebra, Ward identity, BRST/BV structure, transition rule, or regulator-modified algebra fails to close.
3. **Anomaly risk:** a symmetry used to define the route is broken by quantization, regulator choice, boundary term, gauge fixing, or truncation.
4. **Reduction-order risk:** reduce-then-quantize and quantize-then-reduce are silently treated as interchangeable.
5. **Global gauge risk:** local gauge fixing hides Gribov-copy-like or global orbit problems.
6. **Metadata risk:** stable labels or generated summaries are mistaken for gauge-invariant physical observables.

The risk taxonomy is diagnostic only; row authority is owned by the executable ledgers.
