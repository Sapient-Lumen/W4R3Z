## Compression verdict

The residual-cap ledger prevents bounded labels from losing their cap when copied across internal archive surfaces.
That still leaves a separate export path:

- a release slug can say `identifiability` without saying it installed a brake rather than closure;
- a README line can shorten `bounded package-grain S3` into `strongest identifiability route`;
- a user-facing capsule can say `public bridge` without `borrowed-public` or `challenge-conditioned`;
- a talk abstract can say `adversarially controlled` without naming which controls were missing or weaker;
- an outside comparison can rank family C, lab routes, and completion bids on one ladder without carrying target grain, record denominator, or owner boundary;
- a handoff note can say `route stack complete` and be read as the scientific route being complete, rather than the bookkeeping stack being complete;
- a future model-generated summary can preserve the full cap in one sentence and erase it in the next.

The correct posture is:

**any outward-facing or top-level compressed claim about candidate-native identifiability must be export-safe: it must name whether the export is a brake, protocol, bounded row, comparison, or promotion; carry the minimum cap needed for the audience; and preserve the no-closure sentence whenever the source row has not reached `S4` or `S5`.**

This docket is downstream of the residual-cap ledger.
The residual-cap ledger asks whether a consuming archive surface carried the cap.
The export-claim docket asks whether a compact claim can survive being read without the owning table nearby.

Use it when:
- a release name, slug, changelog entry, receipt summary, README line, START_HERE warning, context-pack posture, generated index heading, or archive answer summarizes `OQ-0057` state;
- a comparison sentence says a lane is strongest, closest, public, record-bearing, controlled, calibrated, capped, ready, or near promotion;
- an abstract, external note, handoff capsule, presentation title, email, or manuscript paragraph exports archive posture;
- an internal table row is shortened into one phrase for a non-specialist or future restart reader;
- a future revision wants readability by dropping `bounded`, `package-grain`, `mixed-record`, `borrowed-public`, `weaker-control`, `witness-separated`, `non-comparable`, or `no live lane reaches S4/S5` language.

Do not use it for purely local row edits that are not exported, compared, used in release identity, or summarized outside the owning surface.

## Export-claim states

These codes are not route scores, field states, promotion states, evidence states, decay states, propagation states, adjudication states, trace states, replay states, challenge states, closure states, adversarial-control states, calibration states, or residual-cap states.
They say whether a compressed claim is safe to export.

| Code | Export state | Meaning | Required archive action | Blocked overclaim |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `X0` | no export claim | The edit does not summarize, compare, release-label, handoff, or externally state candidate-native identifiability posture. | Leave this docket unused. | Turning every local row repair into an export audit. |
| `X1` | source row missing | The export says something about `OQ-0057` but does not point to a route row, lifecycle decision, calibration row, residual-cap row, or accepted claim. | Add the source row or downgrade to generic unresolved posture. | Free-floating statements such as `identifiability improved`. |
| `X2` | cap dropped in export | The source row is bounded, conditional, weaker-control, borrowed-public, witness-separated, or non-comparable, but the export omits the cap. | Restore the cap or replace the phrase with no-closure wording. | Turning `bounded package-grain S3` into `S3` or `near S4`. |
| `X3` | owner or target ambiguous | The export does not say whether credit belongs to candidate-native identifiability, witness-package, lab-record, method-quality, local-law, cosmological-package, or completion-bid credit. | Retag the owner and target grain before export. | Spending witness or method credit as identifiability credit. |
| `X4` | comparison-denominator overreach | The export ranks or compares rows without a shared target grain, record denominator, control difficulty, publicness condition, and owner boundary. | Mark rows non-comparable or restate the comparison denominator. | Saying one lane is simply `closer` without saying closer to what. |
| `X5` | public / witness overread | The export treats publication, code, public dashboard, lab publicness, independent implementation, or challenge response as public-reference-standard or witness closure. | Restore the public / witness condition or route through witness-package surfaces. | Letting public availability sound like public witness closure. |
| `X6` | stale or challenge-unsafe export | The export reuses credit touched by supersession, replay failure, unanswered challenge, unclosed challenge, control damage, calibration failure, or cap conflict. | Freeze, narrow, or rerun the lifecycle before export. | Exporting a pre-damage summary as current posture. |
| `X7` | export-safe bounded claim | The claim names the kind of update, source owner, bounded label, first remaining blocker, and no-closure status with enough precision for the audience. | Export is allowed with that wording and rollback handle. | Making precision impossible by requiring a full table everywhere. |
| `X8` | export failure / rollback | The export cannot be sourced, capped, owner-tagged, compared, replayed, or challenge-closed without changing meaning. | Roll back, split, retag, or replace the export with unresolved posture. | Letting polished summary prose outrun the route ledger. |

## Export rules

### 1. Release slugs name the docket, not a scientific closure

A release slug may name the control surface added in that release.
It must not imply that identifiability itself has been solved.
Allowed shape: `identifiability-export-claim-docket`.
Blocked shape: `identifiability-closure`, `candidate-native-identifiability-solved`, `family-c-near-s4`, or any slug that makes a brake sound like a route success.

### 2. One-sentence summaries must carry one blocker

A one-sentence export does not need the full route ledger.
It must carry at least one of these brakes:
- `no live lane reaches S4/S5`;
- `family C remains bounded package-grain S3`;
- `completion bids remain target-rich but acquired-record-poor`;
- `lab / simulation rows remain record-richer but candidate-target-limited`;
- `public / witness support remains conditionally borrowed or witness-separated`.

If none of those brakes can be included, the sentence should say only that a protocol or docket was added.

### 3. Strongest-row language is denominator-bound

`Strongest` is legal only when the denominator travels with it.
Family C may be called the strongest **bounded package-grain partial-identification row**.
It may not be exported as the strongest route to identifiability without saying that completion bids, labs, and frame routes are strong on different denominators and remain non-closure rows.

### 4. Publicness is not public witness closure

An export may say that a route has public artifacts, public challenges, independent replay, or custody fragments only if it also says whether those artifacts are candidate-native, witness-borrowed, challenge-closed, independently replayable, and owner-separated.
A public bridge fragment is not public reference-standard closure.

### 5. Adversarial-control success is not generic robustness

A passed or conditional control battery should be exported with the control difficulty that was actually run.
Friendly-package decoys, simulator-local hard negatives, or one-public-carrier fragility checks are not generic adversarial robustness.

### 6. Lifecycle damage beats polished wording

If supersession, challenge, replay, closure, adversarial-control, calibration, or residual-cap review narrows a row, the export must narrow too.
A release summary cannot preserve earlier wider wording merely because it is clearer or more exciting.

### 7. External abstracts need no-closure ballast

Any abstract, talk title, manuscript paragraph, or external note that uses `candidate-native identifiability`, `closure`, `public bridge`, `witness`, `adversarial`, `calibrated`, or `S`-state language must also carry the minimum no-closure ballast.
For the current archive that ballast is: no live lane reaches `S4` or `S5`, and no live lane closes public witness closure, public reference-standard closure, or candidate-native identifiability.

### 8. The export handle must be replayable

A future reader should be able to reconstruct why the exported sentence was legal.
If the export points only to a broad release note and not to the source row, cap, and blocker, the export is `X1` or `X6`, not `X7`.

## Export-safe answer template

When asked what changed in a release that touched `OQ-0057`, use this shape unless a concrete row earned stronger wording:

> This revision adds a control surface for candidate-native identifiability bookkeeping. It does not promote any lane. Family C remains the strongest bounded package-grain `S3` partial-identification row; completion bids remain target-rich but acquired-record-poor; lab / simulation rows remain record-richer but candidate-target-limited; no live lane reaches `S4` or `S5` or closes public witness / public reference-standard debt.

Shorter answers may mention only the new docket, but they must not say or imply closure, near-closure, solved identifiability, or broad public witness closure.

## Interaction with residual-cap preservation

The residual-cap ledger remains the owner for cap transfer inside archive surfaces.
This docket begins when that capped result becomes outward-facing or top-level compressed prose.
A valid export normally needs both:

1. `RC7` or an explicit residual-cap repair path from `docs/40-model/candidate-native-identifiability-residual-cap-ledger.md`;
2. `X7` wording from this docket.

If the residual cap is missing, do not try to fix the export locally.
Repair the cap first, then export.

## Interaction with reimport firewall

Export approval is outbound only. A release note, restart card, abstract, generated index line, user-facing answer, or external paraphrase that passed this docket may still not return later as route evidence, public witness credit, calibration support, or promotion pressure merely because it is well worded. Any later use of exported wording as support should pass through `docs/40-model/candidate-native-identifiability-reimport-firewall.md`: the derivative surface must reanchor to canonical owner rows, preserve caps and freshness, and split echoed archive wording from genuinely new content.

## Net result

This docket prevents the archive's own summaries from becoming the next inflation channel.
It keeps release names, restart cards, broad mirrors, user-facing capsules, and external abstracts downstream of the route ledger rather than letting them create new posture by rhetoric.

No current lane is promoted to `S4` or `S5`.
No current lane is demoted.
The followthrough queue remains empty until a concrete exported claim, challenge, or external handoff earns a real `X` row.
