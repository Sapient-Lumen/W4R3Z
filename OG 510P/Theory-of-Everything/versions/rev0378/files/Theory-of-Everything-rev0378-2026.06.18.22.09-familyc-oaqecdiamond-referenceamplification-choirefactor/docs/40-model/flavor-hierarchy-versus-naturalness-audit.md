# Flavor hierarchy versus naturalness audit

Flavor and hierarchy claims are especially easy to overstate. A route may fit a hierarchy, impose a texture, or select a compactification/vacuum that reproduces some masses and mixings, while leaving parameter freedom, neutrino ordering, CP phases, sterile-sector alternatives, or dark-sector choices unresolved.

This audit keeps flavor, Higgs, neutrino, and naturalness language under `MASS-HIERARCHY-LEDGER.json` and `INTERACTION-COUPLING-LEDGER.json`. It should be read together with selection, multiplicity, model-capacity, measure/typicality, and regularization/matching controls.
