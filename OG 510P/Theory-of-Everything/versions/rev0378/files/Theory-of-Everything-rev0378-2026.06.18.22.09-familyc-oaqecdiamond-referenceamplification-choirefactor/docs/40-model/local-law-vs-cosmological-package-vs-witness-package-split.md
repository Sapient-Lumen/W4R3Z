Purpose: stop broad ToE credit from being over-awarded once a lane starts to look impressive on more than one surface. ## The compact rule

A candidate can look deep for three very different reasons:

1. it reduces **local-law debt**,
2. it reduces **cosmological-package debt**,
3. it reduces **witness-package debt**.

Those are not the same achievement.
A lane that moves one book should not quietly collect credit from the other two.

## The three books

### 1. Local-law book

This book asks what the candidate explains about dynamical law, local degrees of freedom, effective recovery, and ordinary low-energy structure.
Typical payments here include cleaner recovery of semiclassical gravity, sharper observed-sector recovery, or a real reduction in unexplained local threshold sensitivity.

### 2. Cosmological-package book

This book asks what the candidate is still borrowing from global state, boundary conditions, vacuum choice, compactification, asymptotics, initial state, measure, or selection package.
A candidate may look powerful because the package is favorable.
That is real information, but it is not the same as local-law payment.

### 3. Witness-package book

This book asks what the candidate is still borrowing in order to turn formal quantities into public, persistent, rereadable empirical contact.
A lane may have elegant observables on paper yet still rely on imported rods, clocks, boundary laboratories, semiclassical matter sectors, or generic external detector language to close the evidential loop.
That debt should be tracked explicitly rather than hidden inside words like “observable,” “relational,” or “information.” Route witness-stage order and pairwise closure tests through `docs/40-model/witness-package-burden-router.md` rather than replaying the full gate ladder inline here. That router already names the borrowing ladder, ordered subgate stack, shared gate-family frame, and strongest current family route, so this three-book stop rule can stay focused on the credit split itself rather than reciting every witness stage by hand.

## Why this split is needed now

The archive already kept local-law credit distinct from cosmological-package credit.
It also already tracked witness borrowing in its own lane.
What was missing was one compact surface stating that **broad ToE credit must be scored across all three books at once**.
Without that rule, the archive can still over-credit a lane that is:

- strong on local-law structure but weak on cosmology and witness closure,
- strong on cosmological-package bookkeeping but weak on local-law reduction,
- or strong on observable rhetoric while still borrowing its durable witness carriers from elsewhere.

## Current archive consequence

No live lane currently pays all three books cleanly.

- Family-B thermodynamic gravity can earn bounded clue or reconstruction credit on local-law-adjacent structure, but still carries strong witness borrowing and does not close cosmological debt.
- Family-C holographic / entanglement bridges can earn bounded geometric surplus and a structured boundary-side interface, but still carry witness-package debt and do not thereby settle cosmological package.
- Broad completion bids can look impressive on UV or recovery grounds, but still have to disclose cosmological package and witness package separately before they count as integrated ToE progress.

## Use rule

When a future revision is tempted to say a lane is “closest to a theory of everything,” ask:

1. Which book actually moved: local law, cosmological package, witness package, or more than one?
2. Which book is still mostly borrowed?
3. Is the claimed gain native, or does it depend on a package that should be scored elsewhere?

If those questions are not answered explicitly, do not award broad ToE credit.
