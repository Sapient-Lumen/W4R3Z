# Semantic drift and equivocation risk taxonomy

Revision: `rev0272`

## Risks now blocked

- **Synonym promotion**: a weaker route-local term is replaced by a stronger public term.
- **Duality-to-identity conversion**: equivalent descriptions are called the same ontology.
- **Record-to-observable conversion by wording**: a public carrier is called a candidate-native observable without the route paying the bridge debt.
- **Structural-to-ontic escalation**: retained mathematical structure is retold as full furniture of the world.
- **Metadata-to-confirmation drift**: release/custody language is retold as physical support.
- **Closure by name**: a surface, slug, theorem, benchmark, or familiar framework name is allowed to imply more authority than the route state allows.

## Route pressure

Family C is the highest-risk semantic lane because reconstruction language sounds ontological. String/M is high-risk because duality and vacuum language can become identity language. Cosmology and CMB rows are high-risk because parameter language can sound like physical cause or vacuum ontology. Metadata rows are high-risk because clean packaging can mimic publicness.
