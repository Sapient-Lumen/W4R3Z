# Intersubjectivity coarse-graining audit

This document keeps one witness-side brake load-bearing without widening the archive.
It asks what the archive should count when “several observers agree” stops meaning one lucky measurement description and instead earns a stronger coarse-graining-aware operational criterion.

## Compression verdict

The archive should now hold one tighter sentence:

**public-observable rhetoric is stronger than one-description or one-resolution agreement, because recent intersubjectivity work makes coarse-graining-stable agreement load-bearing, while related thermodynamic work shows that ideal intersubjectivity is finite-resource impossible and can only be approximated under explicit cooling or coarse-graining subsidy; but that gain is still weaker than cross-frame witness portability, public reference-standard closure, and local witness closure.** `REF-0064`, `REF-0065`

That means the honest archive move is neither:
- “observer agreement at one resolution already buys public evidence,”
- nor “intersubjectivity is too idealized to matter at all.”

The right score is in between.

## What has genuinely improved

### 1. The archive now has a sharper criterion for when an observable is more than one chosen measurement description

Umekawa, Ono, and Arai show that a POVM is a PVM **iff every coarse-graining of it is intersubjective**.
That matters because the archive should not treat one successful observer-agreement regime as if it had already earned public-observable status. `REF-0064`

A description that agrees only at one chosen grain is weaker than one that remains intersubjective under every coarsening.
So the archive can now score “public observable” claims more sharply than by simply asking whether two observers happened to agree once.

### 2. The archive now has a sharper target for classicality talk

The same work shows that a system is classical **iff intersubjectivity is preserved under any coarse-graining**.
So classicality rhetoric now owes more than one stable-looking description or one favored measurement basis. `REF-0064`

That is useful because witness-package inflation often hides inside phrases like “effectively classical,” “objective,” or “publicly accessible” while remaining fragile under description change.
The archive can now price that fragility more honestly.

### 3. The archive now has a sharper price tag for agreement

Candeloro, Debarba, and Binder show that ideal intersubjectivity is impossible under finite thermodynamic resources, derive bounds on agreement and bias that depend on the environment's initial state, and show that cooling or coarse-graining can only approximate the ideal case.
That means apparent publicity can be thermodynamically subsidized rather than free. `REF-0065`

So any lane that advertises observer agreement without naming its resource budget, cooling assumption, or coarse-graining subsidy is still borrowing part of the evidential loop.

## What this does **not** buy

### 1. Not yet cross-frame witness portability

A coarse-graining-stable intersubjectivity condition is not yet an explicit transport rule for the *same coarse-grained fact* across nearby physical frame or observer changes.
The archive still needs:
- a declared fact-identity condition,
- an explicit transport or switching map,
- and a statement of what survives that transport and what remains frame-indexed. `REF-0062`, `REF-0063`, `REF-0120`

So this gain sharpens the target for public observability, but it does not by itself solve cross-frame portability.

### 2. Not yet public reference-standard closure

Even a stronger intersubjectivity criterion does not by itself tell the archive how axes, clocks, orientations, fragment adequacy, or comparison conventions become publicly available inside the candidate’s own language.
It tells us what *stronger agreement* would look like, not how the shared standard is generated and stabilized. `REF-0065`, `REF-0066`

So this gain also remains weaker than public reference-standard closure.

### 3. Not yet local witness closure

Nothing here names record carriers, stabilization mechanisms, reread paths, or durable evidence custody.
The results sharpen what should count as observable agreement and how costly that agreement is, but they do not yet tell us where public records live or why they persist. `REF-0015`, `REF-0016`, `REF-0017`, `REF-0064`, `REF-0065`

## The archive score change

The witness-side lane should now remember one smaller distinction explicitly:

1. **one-description / one-resolution agreement** — one chosen observer or measurement description appears to agree;
2. **coarse-graining-aware intersubjectivity target** — the lane says what stronger public-observable status would require across coarsenings and what thermodynamic price constrains it;
3. **cross-frame witness portability** — multiple physical observers or frame choices can recover and agree on the same coarse-grained fact under a declared transport map;
4. **public reference-standard closure** — the shared comparison standard itself becomes public enough for multi-observer use;
5. **local witness closure** — records, stabilization, rereadability, and publicity are candidate-native.

The archive does **not** need to insert a new formal stage into the global witness ladder for this.
But it should keep this internal brake live inside `OQ-0040` and `OQ-0041`.

## False promotions to reject

1. **one measurement setting is not public-observable closure**
   - Agreement for one chosen POVM or one chosen grain is weaker than coarse-graining-stable intersubjectivity.

2. **coarse-graining rescue is not free objectivity**
   - If agreement appears only after extra coarsening, the archive should name that loss of resolution rather than silently score objectivity.

3. **thermodynamically subsidized agreement is not costless publicity**
   - Cooling, environmental preparation, and resource expenditure remain part of the burden.

4. **intersubjectivity is not record closure**
   - Stronger agreement criteria still do not answer where records live, how they persist, or how they are reread.

## What would move the archive further

A later revision should score the witness lane as materially stronger only if one compact route adds most of the following together:
1. an explicit fact-identity rule across frame changes,
2. a transport map between those frames,
3. a coarse-graining-stable agreement condition,
4. a declared thermodynamic / calibration budget,
5. and a record-carrier / rereadability route that turns agreement into public evidence.

Without that package, the archive should preserve this gain as a bounded intersubjectivity brake and stop there.

## Net result

The archive now has a better brake sentence for `OQ-0040` and `OQ-0041`:

> one observer-agreement regime is weaker than coarse-graining-stable intersubjectivity, and even that stronger target still sits below cross-frame portability, public reference-standard closure, and local witness closure.

That keeps the gain load-bearing without letting it inflate. `REF-0064`, `REF-0065`
