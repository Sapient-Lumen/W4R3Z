# Completion-bid cash-out sieve

Purpose: apply `docs/40-model/uv-completion-atlas.md` only to lanes that currently count as **completion bids**.
The atlas sorts what kind of thing a lane is.
This sieve asks a narrower question: **when does a completion bid earn archive-wide explanatory credit rather than only ambition credit?**

Read this document after `docs/40-model/uv-completion-atlas.md` and together with `docs/40-model/completion-bid-credit-stack.md`, `docs/40-model/local-law-vs-cosmological-boundary-split.md`, `docs/40-model/local-law-vs-cosmological-package-vs-witness-package-split.md`, `docs/40-model/asymptotic-access-vs-local-witness-closure-gate.md`, and `docs/40-model/observer-record-minimum.md`. The credit stack is now the canonical broad router for atlas → sieve → comparison handling; this file keeps only the five-axis payment check.

## Stage 0 — role check

If a lane is currently a **bridge family** or a **diagnostic / reconstruction tool**, stop here.
Do not force it through a completion score it has not yet claimed or earned.
Bootstrap / positive-geometry work stays outside this sieve unless it grows into a genuine completion bid rather than a sharper consistency chart.

## The five-axis sieve

A completion bid should now be scored on five distinct axes.
A gain on one axis does not automatically carry the others.

1. **UV story** — what is claimed to remain well-defined, predictive, or non-pathological beyond the ordinary EFT regime?
2. **Recovery story** — how do local QFT and semiclassical gravity reappear, and what if anything is being claimed about the observed matter sector? Use `docs/40-model/matter-sector-recovery-gate.md` to split generic regime recovery from stronger observed-sector recovery, `docs/40-model/parameter-fixation-gate.md` to keep observed matter architecture, pattern restriction, and actual observed-parameter fixation distinct, and `docs/40-model/benchmark-portability-gate.md` to keep one worked benchmark distinct from portable lane-level fixation.
3. **Package disclosure** — which vacuum, compactification, asymptotic, initial-state, or cosmological package is carrying the result?
4. **Observable story** — what are the physical observables or relationally retrievable quantities, how is gauge / diffeomorphism honesty preserved, and how much of the advertised gain lives only in an off-shell effective-action package? Use `docs/40-model/asymptotic-access-vs-local-witness-closure-gate.md` when the claim moves from boundary access to local-observer access.
5. **Witness / wedge story** — what stable record carriers could bear on those quantities, and what bounded empirical or inter-framework wedge would differ from nearby rivals? Do not let asymptotic or observer-relative observable access count as local witness closure by default.

A completion bid that is strong on axis 1 but weak on axes 2-5 earns **atlas seriousness**, not integrated archive promotion. And even a lane that looks strong across those five axes should still be checked against `docs/40-model/local-law-vs-cosmological-package-vs-witness-package-split.md` before it is awarded broad ToE credit.

## Common failure modes

- **Scope inflation** — a very broad framework label is mistaken for solved recovery.
- **Recovery by package smuggling** — a special vacuum, boundary condition, compactification, or asymptotic control window is treated as though it were generic low-energy closure.
- **Observable deferral** — gauge-invariant or relational observables are postponed indefinitely while ontological ambition continues to rise.
- **Wedge evaporation** — the lane remains impressive in consistency space but cannot yet separate itself from nearby rivals on a bounded pressure surface.

## Archive consequences

1. **Compare completion bids by unpaid debt, not by ambition label.**
   “Broad” does not mean “integrated.”

   Inside the recovery axis itself, do not let regime recovery automatically count as observed-sector recovery.

2. **Do not grant archive-wide completion credit on UV evidence alone.**
   Recovery, package disclosure, observables, and wedge thickness remain independent burdens.

3. **Route observable debt back to the canonical witness surfaces.**
   If a completion bid cannot say what is observable or how records could witness it, route that debt to `docs/40-model/observer-record-minimum.md`, `docs/40-model/witness-borrowing-ladder.md`, and `docs/40-model/asymptotic-access-vs-local-witness-closure-gate.md` rather than letting it disappear inside formal scope.

4. **Route cosmological package debt back to the split surfaces.**
   If a completion bid relies on special asymptotics, compactification, state selection, or measure assumptions, score that through `docs/40-model/local-law-vs-cosmological-boundary-split.md` and the higher-order three-book router `docs/40-model/local-law-vs-cosmological-package-vs-witness-package-split.md` rather than as generic local-law recovery.

## Use rule

Use this document when a future revision is tempted to say that some completion bid is “closest to a ToE.”
Before allowing that sentence, ask:

1. Which axis actually moved: UV control, regime recovery, observed-sector recovery, package disclosure, observable closure, exposed surplus, or genuinely discriminating wedge?
2. Did the move reduce debt on more than one axis, or only on one?
3. Which unpaid debt still blocks promotion?

For broad completion-bid control routing after those questions are answered, use `docs/40-model/completion-bid-credit-stack.md`; for the ordered post-cash-out stage ladder itself, use `docs/40-model/completion-bid-comparison-stack.md` rather than replaying the whole recovery-to-closure ladder in each summary surface.
