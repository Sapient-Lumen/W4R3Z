# Social-authority risk taxonomy

Revision: `rev0273`

## Risk pattern

The archive now treats social authority as a possible source of inspection, not as an evidential shortcut. The major risks are prestige cascades, disciplinary deference, citation-network herding, opaque peer review, hidden failed replications, consensus without visible denominator, and institutional hosting confused with physical evidence.

## Route-level risks

- Family C: dictionary/code-subspace success can be inflated by field familiarity or holographic prestige.
- String/M: long-standing community attention can be mistaken for vacuum/candidate selection.
- Lab/GW/cosmology/CMB: collaboration, instrument, or survey authority can be mistaken for ToE identification.
- Learned inverse / benchmark routes: benchmark leaderboards can be mistaken for independent replication.
- Metadata wrapper: clean archive packaging can be mistaken for social validation.

## Required response

Every social claim must be routed through `SOCIAL-AUTHORITY-LEDGER.json`, `REVIEW-REPLICATION-LEDGER.json`, and `CONSENSUS-ELICITATION-LEDGER.json` before wording can propagate into route authority.
