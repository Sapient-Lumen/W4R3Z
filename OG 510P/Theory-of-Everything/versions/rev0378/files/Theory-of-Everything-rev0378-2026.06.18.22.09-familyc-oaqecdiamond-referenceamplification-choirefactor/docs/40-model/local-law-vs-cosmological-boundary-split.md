# Local-law vs cosmological-boundary split

This document installs a **small explanatory split** that the archive has needed for several revisions.
Its job is simple:
**do not let a candidate claim local-law credit for a result that really depends on a global state, asymptotic boundary package, measure choice, or vacuum-selection assumption; and do not let a local recovery result pretend it has solved the cosmological package.**

## Compression verdict

The archive should now carry a **two-column discipline**:

- **local-law content** gets credit for explaining local propagation, constraints, couplings, effective recoveries, and gauge/diffeomorphism-honest observable structure,
- **cosmological-boundary content** gets credit for explaining initial-state package, asymptotics, vacuum selection, global topology sector, measure, horizon/global ensemble assumptions, and large-scale constant-setting,
- and a candidate earns full explanatory credit only by saying clearly **which side it is actually discharging**.

That means the archive should now reject two common fake wins:

1. **smuggled locality** — a claim sounds like local dynamics but only works once a very special global package is quietly fixed,
2. **smuggled cosmology** — a claim recovers local EFT or semiclassical gravity and is then narrated as if it had explained the cosmological constant, initial conditions, or the global state.

## Why this document exists

The archive already had the sentence:
> cosmological boundary conditions should be kept distinct from local dynamical laws.

That sentence was true but too weak.
Without a sharper split, bridge families can gain unearned explanatory status by blending:
- local equations with special asymptotic setups,
- reconstruction with boundary package,
- horizon thermodynamics with universal state assumptions,
- or low-energy recovery with cosmological closure.

This document turns that general warning into an operational audit surface.

## Split axes

### 1. Local-law content

A claim is primarily **local-law** if its core gain is about one or more of the following:
- what local degrees of freedom or effective variables exist,
- how they propagate or constrain each other,
- what symmetry or redundancy structure organizes them,
- what low-energy or semiclassical recovery follows in a wide class of states,
- what observable-bearing local or relational quantities are available,
- or what local coarse-grained record channels can carry those quantities.

Local-law success should be robust across more than one admissible global package.
If the gain disappears as soon as the asymptotic or cosmological package changes, the claim has probably been over-credited.

### 2. Cosmological-boundary content

A claim is primarily **cosmological-boundary** if its core gain is about one or more of the following:
- what global state is selected,
- what asymptotic boundary conditions are fixed,
- what vacuum is chosen or stabilized,
- what measure over histories or sectors is being assumed,
- what global topology, horizon, or ensemble package is installed,
- or how large-scale constants or initial conditions are constrained.

This can still be scientifically deep.
It just should not be mislabeled as a derivation of local microdynamics.

## The five anti-smuggling checks

A future explanation should answer all five checks explicitly.

### 1. What is being held fixed?

Name the fixed package:
- asymptotic boundary condition,
- initial state,
- vacuum choice,
- measure,
- topology sector,
- horizon/ensemble assumption,
- or ordinary matter/environment witness substrate.

If the claim needs one of these, the archive should say so plainly.

### 2. What is actually being derived?

Separate:
- local equations or constraints,
- recovery of an EFT regime,
- a relation among observables,
- global state restriction,
- constant selection,
- or a special boundary dictionary.

One result may touch more than one layer, but the archive should not fuse them into one undifferentiated “explanation.”

### 3. How portable is the gain?

Ask whether the claimed gain survives under changes of global package.

- If yes, it is stronger local-law evidence.
- If no, it is probably a boundary/state-specific result and should be credited that way.

### 4. Where do the observables and records live?

A local-law claim must still answer where observable content and record carriers live.
A cosmological-boundary claim must still say how its global package reaches empirical contact.
Neither side is allowed to borrow the other's success silently.

### 5. Which debt is actually reduced?

The archive should ask which unresolved debt shrank:
- local recovery debt,
- witness / record debt,
- constant / vacuum debt,
- initial-condition debt,
- or regime / asymptotic-specialness debt.

If no debt is clearly reduced, the claim may be mostly vocabulary motion.

## Family-by-family current read

### Family A — EFT-first gravity baseline

- **What it really earns:** strong local-law credit.
- **What it does not earn by itself:** cosmological-boundary closure.
- **Archive discipline:** do not let local EFT honesty be narrated as if it solved vacuum selection, initial conditions, or the cosmological constant.

### Family B — Thermodynamic / entropy gravity

- **What it may earn:** some local-law reconstruction pressure under explicit equilibrium or coarse-grained assumption packages.
- **What it often borrows:** state assumptions, horizon setup, and witness structure.
- **Archive discipline:** do not let horizon thermodynamic derivations quietly inherit universal-state or cosmological-package credit.

### Family C — Holography / entanglement geometry

- **What it may earn:** a real, bounded local-law bridge surplus on subregion-sensitive geometry and reconstruction structure in special holographic regimes.
- **What it often also assumes:** a strong asymptotic boundary package and special large-`N` / symmetry control.
- **Archive discipline:** do not let a special boundary dictionary be narrated as if it were already a local-law derivation for generic cosmology, and do not let boundary readability masquerade as cosmological closure.

### Family D — Measurement / nonlocality pressure

- **What it really earns:** pressure on record, witness, and empirical-contact structure.
- **What it does not yet earn:** a stand-alone answer to local gravity microdynamics or the cosmological package.
- **Archive discipline:** do not let record success be over-promoted into a cosmological-law answer.

## Immediate archive rules

The archive should now score future claims with these simple rules:

1. **No local-law promotion from a boundary package alone.**
   A special asymptotic setting or boundary dictionary may be powerful, but it does not automatically explain generic local law.

2. **No cosmological-credit laundering from local recovery alone.**
   Recovering low-energy field dynamics or semiclassical gravity does not by itself explain vacuum energy, initial conditions, or global state selection.

3. **Two-bucket explanations must be written in two buckets.**
   If a candidate really addresses both local law and cosmological boundary package, the archive should require two explicit answer blocks.

4. **Special-regime results keep their regime label.**
   AdS asymptotics, large-`N`, near-equilibrium horizons, or special observer cuts do not become generic explanation just because the underlying mathematics is elegant.

5. **Observable/record contact must be tracked on both sides.**
   Local-law and cosmological-boundary content each still owe a route to observable-bearing records; one side cannot borrow empirical contact from the other without being marked as borrowed.

## What closes with this document

`FQ-0009` can now close in bounded form.

The archive now has an explicit split surface that says:
- what counts as local-law gain,
- what counts as cosmological-boundary gain,
- how future claims can be blocked from smuggling one into the other,
- and why both debts remain scientifically real even when only one is being reduced.

## Net result

This is a **compression move** more than a literature move.
It reduces future archive drift by forcing explanations to say:

- which burden they discharge,
- which burden remains,
- and whether the gain survives outside the global package that made it visible.

That keeps the archive smaller and more honest.
