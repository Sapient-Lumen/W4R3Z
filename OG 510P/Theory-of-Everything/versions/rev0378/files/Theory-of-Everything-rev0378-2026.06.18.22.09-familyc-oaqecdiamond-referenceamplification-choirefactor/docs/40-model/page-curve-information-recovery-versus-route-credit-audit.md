# Page-curve / information-recovery versus route credit audit

A Page-curve, island, entropy-cone, no-cloning, or recovery-map result can be route-relevant without being candidate-identifying. This audit keeps those claims bounded.

## Questions for every route

1. What is the information object?
2. Who or what has access to it?
3. Is the same information accessible twice, or merely described twice?
4. Is the channel unitary, lossy, coarse-grained, code-subspace-limited, or public-record-limited?
5. Which entropy or relative entropy is counted?
6. Which no-go theorem is live?
7. What remains unrecovered, unobservable, gauge-dependent, sector-dependent, or public-record-unbound?

A positive answer on information accounting can make a route cleaner. It cannot by itself promote the route to `S4` or `S5`.
