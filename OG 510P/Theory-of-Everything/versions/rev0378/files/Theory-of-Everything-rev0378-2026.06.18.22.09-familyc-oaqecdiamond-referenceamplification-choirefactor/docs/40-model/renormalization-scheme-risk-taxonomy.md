# Renormalization-scheme risk taxonomy

## Risks

- **Regulator artifact:** a result changes under cutoff shape, regulator kernel, lattice/discrete resolution, background split, or subtraction convention.
- **Running-coordinate artifact:** a coupling or beta function is treated as ontology even though it is coordinate-, scheme-, or field-redefinition-dependent.
- **Truncation artifact:** a fixed point or relevant direction exists only in a finite projection of theory space.
- **Threshold artifact:** a heavy mode, boundary term, counterterm, or nuisance sector is not matched into the lower-scale description.
- **Naturalness artifact:** a hierarchy or tuning claim is stated in one parameterization and exported as physical without scheme-stress testing.
- **Pipeline artifact:** survey, waveform, CMB, detector, or benchmark parameterizations behave like regularization/matching schemes but are narrated as raw evidence.
- **Metadata artifact:** package identity or generated summaries are mistaken for scheme control.

## Archive response

The response is not to forbid RG, EFT, matching, or fixed-point reasoning. The response is to make the denominator explicit. Each route gets a regulator row, flow row, and matching row, with rollback through `RB-0014-REGULATOR-PORTABILITY-ROLLBACK` when portability fails.
