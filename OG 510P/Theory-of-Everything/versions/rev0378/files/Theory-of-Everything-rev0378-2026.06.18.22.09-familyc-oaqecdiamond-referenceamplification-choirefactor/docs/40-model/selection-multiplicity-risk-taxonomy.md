# Selection / multiplicity risk taxonomy

Revision: `rev0270`.

Risk classes now tracked by executable rows:

- dictionary or gauge chart selected after success;
- learned-inverse benchmark or architecture selected after leaderboard search;
- elegant derivation selected from failed derivation families;
- compactification, vacuum, or measure choice selected post hoc;
- regulator, truncation, or cutoff selected after a fixed-point-like result;
- simulation target selected after continuum-like behavior;
- amplitude/EFT constraint selected after scanning ansätze and bounds;
- lab protocol, cut, noise model, or apparatus state selected after a favorable signal;
- gravitational-wave event, waveform, or catalog anomaly selected after many tests;
- cosmology model, prior, likelihood, survey combination, or nuisance stack selected after tension;
- CMB map, delensing, foreground, or reheating corridor selected after many pipelines;
- metadata wrapper selected for salience and mistaken for evidence.

Each class must route through `SELECTION-FUNCTION-LEDGER.json`, `MULTIPLICITY-CONTROL-LEDGER.json`, and `REPORTING-BIAS-LEDGER.json` before support language can be strengthened.
