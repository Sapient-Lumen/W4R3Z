## Shared rule

Empirical contact should be scored in order:
- first by fixing the minimum physical conditions for record, rereadability, and objectivity,
- then by asking how much witness burden is still being borrowed rather than carried natively,
- and only then by asking which witness-package closure and identifiability burdens have actually been reduced rather than merely renamed.

A record criterion is not yet a lower witness-borrowing score.
A lower witness-borrowing score is not yet witness-package closure.
A stronger witness-package route is not yet candidate-native identifiability closure unless its native target / record / equivalence / acquisition / inverse / abstention / public-bridge route can be cashed out explicitly and then passes the route-level promotion gate without compensatory aggregation. Use `docs/40-model/candidate-native-acquisition-realizability-protocol.md` when the claimed witness gain is a formal observable, simulation output, detector concept, boundary dictionary, or special access route being upgraded into acquired evidence. Use `docs/40-model/candidate-native-inverse-completeness-stability-protocol.md` when the claimed witness gain is a reconstruction, fit, regularized output, benchmark result, or local margin being upgraded into stable identification. Use `docs/40-model/candidate-native-abstention-no-verdict-protocol.md` when the claimed witness gain is a null result, no-record condition, no-inversion condition, calibrated bundle, or deferral policy being upgraded into native refusal discipline. Use `docs/40-model/candidate-native-public-bridge-challenge-protocol.md` when public records, code, hosted replay, laboratory publicness, or boundary access are being upgraded into field-8 public bridge credit. Use `docs/40-model/candidate-native-identifiability-promotion-gate.md` when any field-level gain is being retold as a route-level promotion.
Use `docs/40-model/candidate-native-identifiability-promotion-readiness-matrix.md` when record-rich laboratory, simulation, or public-replay lanes need to be compared with target-rich formal rows without granting broad candidate-native identifiability by publicness alone.

Stable, restart, program, and current-head surfaces should therefore name this router when they only need the archive's broad empirical-contact architecture.
For integrated local-law / cosmological-package / witness-package burden routing, route onward through `docs/40-model/broad-toe-credit-router.md` or `docs/40-model/current-head-control-router.md` instead.
Do not replay `observer-record-minimum.md`, `witness-borrowing-ladder.md`, and `witness-package-burden-router.md` as separate broad-surface obligations unless the revision is actually changing one of those subordinate surfaces. Pairwise witness-closure gates should point broad empirical-contact prerequisites here plus `docs/40-model/witness-closure-gate-family-frame.md` rather than restating the record-minimum and borrowing surfaces gate by gate.
