# Relative entropy / recovery versus candidate identity audit

Introduced in `rev0303`.

Relative-entropy equality, data-processing saturation, Petz-like recovery, JLMS-like relations, and entanglement-wedge reconstruction can constrain route structure. They do not by themselves identify a candidate ontology. This surface keeps recovery-map success bounded by code-subspace, approximation, state-pair, dictionary, public-record, and observed-sector limits.
