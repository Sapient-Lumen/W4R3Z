# Acquisition-versus-attribution gate

Purpose: split the **acquisition-grade** layer inside `docs/40-model/discrimination-vs-acquisition-gate.md` one step further.
A completion bid should not earn the same empirical credit merely because a separating signal has a **credible acquisition path** and because that signal can be **stably attributed back to the lane** rather than to nearby forward-model choices, nuisance packages, reconstruction assumptions, or theory-discrepancy patches.
Shared family scaffolding and ordered routing live in `docs/40-model/completion-bid-comparison-gate-family-frame.md` and `docs/40-model/completion-bid-comparison-stack.md`; this file keeps only the pair-specific split between acquisition-grade traction and attribution-grade traction.

## Compression verdict

The archive should now score claimed empirical traction in **three post-acquisition layers** after a wedge already has a credible acquisition path:

1. **acquisition-grade discrimination** — a real campaign could plausibly obtain the separating signal through a named channel, regime, exposure, and systematic program;
2. **attribution-path disclosure** — the lane states the forward model, inversion path, nuisance package, theory-discrepancy handling, and auxiliary assumptions required to turn that raw signal into lane credit;
3. **attribution-grade discriminatory traction** — nearby admissible analysis pipelines or discrepancy models do not erase, reverse, or radically re-route the claimed ranking change.

A lane may earn real credit on layer 1 while still being weak on layers 2 and 3.
That is still progress.
It is just not yet stable theory-attribution credit.

## Why this gate exists

`docs/40-model/discrimination-vs-acquisition-gate.md` already blocked one inflation move:
**a rival-separating wedge in principle pretending to be an actually acquirable empirical wedge.**

A further inflation move remained too easy:
**an acquirable signal pretending to be stably attributable support for one lane.**

That confusion appears whenever a completion bid is praised because it:
- names a channel that can really be measured, but the theory-to-observable map runs through heavy astrophysical, cosmological, or effective-model reconstruction;
- presents one preferred inference pipeline while leaving nearby nuisance packages or theory-error models unscored;
- treats an extracted parameter or anomaly as lane credit even though nearby forward models could absorb it with comparable plausibility;
- or assumes that a recovered signal automatically points to microscopic structure rather than to a broader equivalence class;
- or leans on one explicit off-shell effective-action package as though that already fixed the on-shell / relational observable story;
- or treats one low-energy coefficient sign or positivity slogan as lane support without naming the running corridor, graviton-loop package, or species / coupling assumptions that make the sign interpretable.

A wedge can be real, discriminating, and even acquirable while still being attribution-thin.
This gate keeps those credits separate.

## The three-step attribution test

### 1. Forward-model and inversion disclosure

Before counting an acquired wedge as lane support, the archive should ask:
- which forward model maps lane-level structure to the observable,
- which inverse or reconstruction pipeline maps data back to the claimed quantity,
- which nuisance parameters, environmental histories, or effective descriptions are being marginalized over,
- and which parts of the inference are theory-conditioned rather than directly read off the data.

A lane that cannot name this map may still have an interesting observable target.
It does not yet have attribution-grade traction.

### 2. Discrepancy and nuisance honesty

A wedge earns more than acquisition credit only if the archive is told how theory uncertainty enters.
At minimum, the lane should disclose:
- plausible model discrepancy or emulator / truncation error,
- nearby nuisance packages that can mimic or absorb the signal,
- whether the separating relation survives those choices,
- whether the claimed support depends on one especially favorable inference path,
- and whether the lane's advertised observable credit actually lives on an on-shell / relational quantity rather than one preferred off-shell gauge package,
- and whether any sign-based support survives nearby admissible scale choices, graviton-loop treatments, species bounds, or non-minimal-coupling packages.

The archive should not demand a universal discrepancy model.
It should demand enough honesty to tell the difference between **signal present** and **lane identified**.

### 3. Pipeline-stable ranking change

Even after acquisition and disclosure, the archive should still ask whether nearby admissible analysis choices preserve the same theory movement.
A wedge earns **attribution-grade discriminatory** credit only when:
- success or failure still materially reorders named rivals across more than one admissible inference path,
- the result is not swallowed by forward-model flexibility or nuisance refits,
- and the lane can say what part of the signal is truly lane-specific rather than merely package-generic.

A wedge may be worth pursuing and still not yet be a stable attribution surface.
That is a planning fact, not a refutation.
Even stable attribution is not yet the same as ranking-grade archive movement; the downstream split lives in `docs/40-model/attribution-vs-ranking-gate.md`.

## Common failure modes

1. **Inverse-problem laundering** — a raw observable is treated as microscopic support without naming the reconstruction map.
2. **Nuisance shadowing** — nearby astrophysical, cosmological, or effective-model freedom can mimic the same signal but is left unscored.
3. **Discrepancy silence** — emulator, truncation, or theory-error debt is omitted even though it materially affects extraction.
4. **Off-shell laundering** — one preferred effective-action or background-gauge package is narrated as physical support without saying which on-shell or relational quantity is actually carrying the lane credit.
5. **Sign-envelope laundering** — one coefficient sign or positivity slogan is narrated as lane support without naming scale, graviton-loop treatment, or species / coupling assumptions.
6. **One-pipeline triumphalism** — one favorable analysis path is narrated as stable lane support without nearby-pipeline checks.

## Immediate archive rules

1. **Do not count an acquirable signal as lane support unless the forward-model and inversion path are named.**
2. **Require at least one nearby nuisance / discrepancy / pipeline / gauge-honesty split whenever a lane claims attribution-grade discrimination.**
3. **Give extra credit only when the claimed ranking change survives nearby admissible analysis choices rather than one favorable extraction path.**
4. **Do not punish honest admission that a wedge is acquisition-grade but attribution-thin.**
   Punish only the inflation move that treats such a wedge as already stable theory support.
5. **Keep this gate subordinate to the discrimination-versus-acquisition gate and the canonical discriminator surfaces.**
   This surface sharpens comparison inside acquired empirical traction; it does not replace separate debts on recovery, package disclosure, observables, or witness closure.

## Net result

The archive now has a smaller and more honest sentence available:

> a completion bid may offer an actually acquirable separating signal and still be weak on attribution-grade theory support.

That sentence blocks another prestige inflation move without widening the archive.
