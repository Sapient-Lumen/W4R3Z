## Compression verdict

The archive should now score witness-facing progress in **three nested layers**:

1. **asymptotic / boundary / special-access control** — a lane can define, reconstruct, or control physical quantities at infinity, on a boundary, on a horizon edge sector, or in another special access window;
2. **observer-relative / local relational access** — a lane can define quantities relative to rods, clocks, observers, or other local reference structure in nontrivial bulk or closed settings;
3. **cross-frame witness portability** — a lane can say how the same coarse-grained fact survives nearby physical frame or observer changes without relying on one silent privileged perspective;
4. **local witness closure** — a lane can say where persistent, rereadable, objectivity-capable records live, what stabilizes them, and how empirical agreement is carried without mainly outsourcing that burden to borrowed detector or boundary language.

A lane may earn real credit on layer 1 or 2 while still being weak on layer 3.
That is progress.
It is just not yet witness closure.

## Why this gate exists

**boundary or observer-relative observable success being retold as if the witness package were already solved.**

That inflation can happen in at least three ways:
- boundary correlators, asymptotic algebras, or S-matrix control are narrated as if they were already generic local empirical closure,
- observer-relative Hilbert spaces or local relational operators are narrated as if they were already public, persistent witness structure,
- or a special observer, boundary laboratory, or quantum reference system is treated as if it had already paid the portability and objectivity debt for ordinary evidence.

## The three-step witness-access test

### 1. Access-domain disclosure

Before awarding witness-package credit, the archive should ask what access window is actually being used.
At minimum, a lane should disclose:
- whether the relevant quantities live only at infinity, on a boundary, in a finite-horizon or codimension-2 edge sector, in a special asymptotic package, or relative to one privileged observer construction,
- whether the claimed observables depend on a special reference structure that has not been made generic,
- and which parts of the evidential loop still sit outside the candidate's own language.

A lane that wins here earns **access credit**, not yet local witness closure.

### 2. Local relational upgrade

A lane earns more than asymptotic access only when it can define or control observables in a genuinely local or observer-relative way away from pure boundary control.
At minimum, the archive should ask:
- whether rods, clocks, observers, or relational markers are physically specified rather than merely invoked,
- whether the construction works only in one privileged closed-universe, black-hole-interior, or reference-system setup or transports across a broader class,
- and whether the resulting quantities are still thinly tied to one observer history rather than to public rereadable evidence.

This is real progress.
But local relational access is still not the same thing as witness closure.

### 3. Witness-closure test

A lane earns **local witness-closure** credit only when it can answer the archive's ordinary record questions in its own language closely enough that the empirical loop is no longer mainly borrowed.
At minimum, it should name:
- the physical record carrier,
- the stabilization story,
- the reread path,
- the objectivity / redundancy condition,
- and the extent to which all of that is candidate-native rather than imported from boundary, laboratory, or ordinary semiclassical detector language.

Without those answers, the lane still owes witness-package debt even if its observable story has become more sophisticated.

## Common failure modes

1. **Boundary-complete inflation** — boundary or asymptotic control is retold as generic local witness closure.
2. **Observer-singling inflation** — one privileged observer construction is retold as public objectivity.
3. **Local-region inflation** — one explicit local gauge-invariant operator is retold as if reliable extended-local-region or island-grade operator control had already been earned.
4. **Clock inflation** — one informational clock or observer algebra is retold as if it had already earned genuinely gravitational observer credit in settings where weakly backreacting sectors can remain spectators.
5. **Detector-import silence** — borrowed rods, clocks, detectors, or memory chains still do the real work, but the debt disappears behind the word “observable.”
6. **Reference-system laundering** — a special quantum reference structure is treated as though it had already become generic empirical contact.

## Compact current readout

- **Holographic / boundary windows** remain the archive's strongest asymptotic or boundary-access lane, and they can also support disciplined structured interfaces.
  A first bounded flat / celestial asymptotic-dictionary audit now makes that more concrete: the lane has one generalized universal soft-sector action plus one free-spinor Milne dictionary, but that still leaves family-C style record closure at `WB-2` rather than witness-native closure.
- **De Sitter / special-horizon windows** are now slightly sharper too.
  A first bounded de Sitter horizon-edge audit now says codimension-2 edge spectra and horizon-fluctuation interpretations are real finite-region structure gains, but still weaker than generic local observables or local witness closure.
- **De Sitter observer language** is now slightly sharper from the witness side.
  A new bounded gravitational-observer audit now says worldlines and informational clocks are real observer-language structure, but weakly backreacting or topological sectors can still factorize as spectators and leave the universal de Sitter phase untouched; one clock or information-bearing subalgebra is therefore still weaker than a genuinely gravitational observer and far weaker than local witness closure.
- **Observer-relative gravitational path-integral constructions** are real progress because they show that nontrivial local physics need not disappear in closed-universe or interior settings.
  But they still do not by themselves give public, persistent, multi-channel witness closure.
- **Quantum rods / clocks and related local-relational constructions** are real progress because they move beyond pure asymptotic dressing toward local operators.
  A first bounded isometry-breaking local-operator audit now makes that more concrete: explicit Stückelberg / clock-and-rod operators are real observer-relative access gains, but they still remain weaker than reliable local-region closure because spacetime-point fluctuation can accumulate unless the background strongly breaks the relevant isometry.
  They therefore still owe stabilization, rereadability, objectivity, and portability debt before they count as ordinary witness closure.

## Immediate archive rules

1. **Do not count asymptotic or boundary access as local witness closure.**
2. **Do not count observer-relative or rod/clock-defined local access as reliable local-region closure unless accumulated point-fluctuation drift and validity timescale are named explicitly.**
3. **Do not count observer-relative or rod/clock-defined local access as public witness closure unless record carrier, stabilization, rereadability, and objectivity are named explicitly.**
4. **Keep special boundary, observer, or reference-system packages visible rather than laundering them into generic empirical closure.**
5. **Keep this gate subordinate to the witness-borrowing ladder and the three-book split.**
   This surface sharpens the witness-package book; it does not replace the archive's broader local-law or cosmological-package bookkeeping.

## Net result

The archive now has a smaller and more honest sentence available:

That sentence blocks another common inflation move without widening the archive.
