# Candidate-route defeater taxonomy

The archive now treats defeaters as route-specific pressure objects.

## Undercutting defeaters

These do not necessarily support the opposite candidate. They remove the route from the record to the claim: replay failure, custody failure, source-integrity loss, proof-scope gaps, benchmark surrogate targets, model misspecification, and frame-transport failure.

## Rebutting defeaters

These support a contrary route-level conclusion: an observed-sector contradiction, a public record inconsistent with the claimed target, or a negative-control result that shows the positive pattern is not discriminating.

## Quotient defeaters

These do not say the evidence is false. They say the archive was asking the wrong identity question: two descriptions may be the same under duality, gauge/frame transformation, operational equivalence, or regime split.

## Gravity-specific defeaters

Gravity adds local-subsystem and witness-class defeat: a record can be real while the exact local tensor factor, compact region, or public local observable that the route wanted is unavailable.

## Repair discipline

A repair must answer the defeater type. More data does not repair a quotient collapse. Better metadata does not repair a negative-control survival. Better detector precision does not repair a local-subsystem obstruction unless the witness class itself is redefined.
