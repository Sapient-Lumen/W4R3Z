# Single-graviton and graviton-statistics discriminator roadmap

## Purpose

This surface gives the single-graviton / graviton-counting lane its own route home. The lane is empirically exciting because it presses closer to direct quantum-gravity interaction records than ordinary strong-field GR tests. It is also easy to overstate, so it enters the route ledger as `R-OQ0057-LAB-GRAVITON-COUNTING` with an `S2` state and `S3` ceiling.

## Current route interpretation

The route covers two related record types:

1. **single-graviton stimulated absorption / detector transition records** — a quantum resonator or detector records transitions correlated with independently observed gravitational waves;
2. **graviton-counting statistics / gravitational-wave quantum-state characterization** — counting distributions and correlations distinguish radiation-state classes such as coherent, squeezed, or thermal descriptions.

These records could be profound. But the route target is still an interaction channel, detector response, or source-state class. It is not a unique quantum-gravity ontology.

## Why the cap remains low

The earliest blocker is target attribution. A low-energy graviton-access record can be compatible with many candidate completions that share the same effective interaction. A state-tomography record can identify a radiation-state hypothesis without identifying the microscopic theory of gravity.

The route therefore owes:

- source-state controls;
- detector-response controls;
- background and trigger-correlation controls;
- public custody and independent replay;
- candidate-native target quotient;
- observed-sector integration.

## Negative controls

- `NC-LAB-GRAVITON-SINGLE-CLICK-NOT-ONTOLOGY`
- `NC-LAB-GRAVITON-SOURCE-STATE-AMBIGUITY`

## Current verdict

The route is a serious empirical-discriminator corridor. It is not candidate-native identifiability closure.
