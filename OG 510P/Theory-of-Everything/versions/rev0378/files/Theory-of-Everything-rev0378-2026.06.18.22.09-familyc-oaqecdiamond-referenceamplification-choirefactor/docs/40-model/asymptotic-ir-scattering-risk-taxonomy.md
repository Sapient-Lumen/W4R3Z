# Asymptotic / IR / scattering risk taxonomy

Risk classes:

- Fock-space laundering: treating a convenient Fock in/out state as a physical state space in the presence of massless fields.
- Dressing laundering: treating a soft dressing or memory-sector rule as a complete observable.
- Inclusive-rate laundering: treating an inclusive cross section or catalog likelihood as candidate identity.
- Finite-time laundering: treating a finite-time detector record as an asymptotic completeness proof.
- Memory laundering: treating gravitational/electromagnetic memory as black-hole information recovery or ToE closure.
