# Summary statistic versus candidate identity audit

A summary statistic can be sufficient for a narrow statistical model while remaining useless for candidate identity. The audit separates Fisher preservation, likelihood-ratio preservation, posterior calibration, model-comparison sufficiency, public-record replay, and candidate-native ontology.

No route may treat an ABC/SBI summary, score-compressed statistic, learned embedding, or MOPED statistic as a full public record unless the route declares the sufficiency target and residual loss.
