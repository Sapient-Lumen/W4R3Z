# Cosmology prior-sensitivity audit

Cosmology routes are public-record rich but contrast-sensitive. BAO, supernova, CMB, B-mode, gravitational-wave, and likelihood-chain artifacts can be excellent constraints without selecting a unique ToE candidate.

## Audit rule

A cosmology update must name:

- parameterization family,
- prior bounds and prior density,
- nuisance covariance,
- survey/systematics treatment,
- foreground or delensing model if applicable,
- model-comparison denominator,
- observed-sector obligation touched,
- maximum route effect.

## Current cap

`R-OQ0057-COSMO-DARK-ENERGY-BAO` and `R-OQ0057-PRIMORDIAL-TENSOR-BMODES` remain `S2` constraint corridors. A change in dark-energy or tensor likelihood language is not candidate-native identifiability unless the contrast class, prior sensitivity, observed-sector recovery, public-record custody, and candidate-native inverse all close.
