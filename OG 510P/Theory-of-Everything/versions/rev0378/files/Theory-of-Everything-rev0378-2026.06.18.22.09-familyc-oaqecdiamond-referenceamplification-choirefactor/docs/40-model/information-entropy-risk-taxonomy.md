# Information and entropy risk taxonomy

## Main risks

- **Recovery without channel:** claiming information recovery without a declared source, target, channel, quotient, or recovery map.
- **Entropy double counting:** counting the same state, dual presentation, public record, or metadata wrapper twice.
- **No-cloning by perspective:** allowing two perspectives or reconstructions to function as two accessible copies of one quantum state.
- **No-signalling by mediator:** using entanglement, frame transport, or a gravitational mediator as if it directly transmits controllable classical information.
- **Data-processing reversal:** treating a lossy coarse-graining, likelihood update, benchmark score, or public-record map as invertible without a declared recovery map.
- **Entropy-cone overreach:** treating one entropy inequality or holographic entropy-cone result as global candidate identity.
- **Metadata inflation:** treating package provenance, generated summaries, or dependency graphs as physical information flow.
