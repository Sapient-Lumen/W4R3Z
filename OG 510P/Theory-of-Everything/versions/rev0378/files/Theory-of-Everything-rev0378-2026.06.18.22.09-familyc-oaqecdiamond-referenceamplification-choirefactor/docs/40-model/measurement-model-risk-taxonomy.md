# Measurement-model risk taxonomy

The new rev0267 risk classes are wrong measurand, raw-to-observable leakage, correction-model opacity, uncertainty-budget collapse, traceability break, and metadata laundering. These risks affect all live routes, but they are most acute in lab GIE/BMV, gravitational-wave catalogs, DESI/BAO likelihood products, CMB B-mode searches, learned inverse benchmarks, and regulator/truncation routes.
