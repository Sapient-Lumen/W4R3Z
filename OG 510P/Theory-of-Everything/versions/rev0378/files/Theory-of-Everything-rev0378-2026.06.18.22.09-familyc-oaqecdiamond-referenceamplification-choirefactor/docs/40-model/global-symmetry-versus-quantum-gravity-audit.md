# Global symmetry versus quantum-gravity audit

Quantum-gravity candidates must not use exact global-symmetry language casually. The archive treats no-global-symmetry, completeness, compact gauge group, generalized symmetry, and anomaly constraints as route-local obligations rather than slogans.

Audit questions:

1. Is the symmetry global, gauged, approximate, emergent, broken, higher-form, non-invertible, or duality-like?
2. What object carries the charge or defect?
3. Is the symmetry compatible with dynamical gravity, black-hole reasoning, holographic constraints, and route-local public records?
4. Does an anomaly, inflow, or matching condition survive boundary, sector, gauge, regulator, and quotient controls?
5. What rollback fires if the symmetry is only a selected-sector artifact?
