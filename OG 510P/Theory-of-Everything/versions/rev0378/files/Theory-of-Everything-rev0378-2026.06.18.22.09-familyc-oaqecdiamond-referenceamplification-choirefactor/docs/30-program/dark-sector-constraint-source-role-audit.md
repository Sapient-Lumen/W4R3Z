## Non-promotion boundary

Dark-sector search limits are S0 denominator pressure. They can narrow or block dark-matter, axion-like, sterile-sector, hidden-sector, abundance, and growth wording, but they cannot identify dark matter, solve vacuum energy, supply candidate-native support, or promote any route.
