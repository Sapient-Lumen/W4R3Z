# rev0344 equivalence-principle / fifth-force / weak-field denominator audit

This revision closes a weak-field gravity denominator seam without adding a new policy file. The existing classical-GR observed-sector policy now also checks equivalence-principle, fifth-force, quantum-free-fall, ACES/PHARAO clock/redshift, and torsion-balance source roles.

## Risk repaired

Routes could sound metric, free-falling, classical-GR-compatible, or fifth-force-aware while spending broad weak-field language but skipping WEP/EEP/SEP, composition dependence, local-position/clock universality, atom-interferometer quantum-free-fall wording, short-range inverse-square/Yukawa deviations, and torsion-balance constraints. That is a real overcredit path: null or compatibility records can silently become route-native support unless the cube marks them as denominator pressure only.

## Source-role boundary

`REF-0437` and `REF-0725` through `REF-0728` are route-local S0 denominator refs only. They may force a route to write a public replay map or roll back equivalence-principle/fifth-force/weak-field wording, but they cannot enter acquired evidence-unit `source_refs`, metadata-wrapper freshness, dark-sector detection, metric-theory proof, quantum-gravity evidence, or route promotion.

## Non-promotion rule

Passing these denominator checks only says that a route has not contradicted current equivalence-principle, weak-field, clock/redshift, or fifth-force public constraints in the named sector. It does not identify the route, solve dark matter, prove metricity, detect quantum gravity, or advance any route authority state.
