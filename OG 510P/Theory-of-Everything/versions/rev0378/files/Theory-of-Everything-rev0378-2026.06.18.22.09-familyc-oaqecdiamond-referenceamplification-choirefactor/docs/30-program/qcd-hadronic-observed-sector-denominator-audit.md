## Why this was the riskiest remaining seam

The cube already made ordinary observed matter, QM/QFT, classical GR, dark-sector, CMB, DESI, GW, lab-GIE, FamilyC, String/M, asymptotic-safety, causal-set, amplitudes, and QRF source roles executable. One important ordinary-denominator seam was still too implicit: QCD and hadronic recovery. Broad `OSR-STANDARD-MODEL-MATTER` pressure could mention particle content, Higgs, muon precision, and neutrino mass without forcing a route to state what it does about color, confinement, quark masses, hadron spectra, strong-coupling scheme/running, lattice scale setting, finite-volume/continuum limits, and jet/PDF correlations.

That is a real scientific risk. A route can look Standard-Model-compatible while importing the hard QCD part as background vocabulary. `rev0341` makes those QCD/hadronic burdens explicit but keeps them at `S0`: passing them is mandatory denominator satisfaction, not candidate-native evidence.

## Source-role split

New QCD/hadronic refs:

- `REF-0712` — FLAG lattice-QCD averages and low-energy Standard Model limit review.
- `REF-0713` — precision lattice calculation of the quark–gluon coupling / `alpha_s`.
- `REF-0714` — CMS inclusive-jet alpha_s/PDF/running analysis.

Allowed role:

- route-local observed-sector denominator pressure;
- public replay burden for QCD/hadronic wording;
- finite-volume, continuum, lattice-to-perturbative, scheme, scale, uncertainty, and correlation constraints.

Forbidden role:

- acquired evidence-unit source credit;
- metadata-wrapper freshness;
- Standard Model derivation;
- dark-sector identity;
- quantum-gravity evidence;
- route promotion.

## Refactor choice

I deliberately did not add `tools/qcd_hadronic_observed_sector_policy.py`. The existing observed-sector matter policy is the right home: QCD/hadronic recovery is not a separate doctrine surface, it is a missing hard sub-denominator inside observed-sector matter recovery.

## Non-promotion rule

`REF-0712` through `REF-0714` can only make QCD/hadronic claims harder to spend. They cannot promote a route, cannot supply acquired evidence-unit credit, and cannot make a route count as having derived the observed Standard Model.
