# Evidence-unit ledger

Executable source: `EVIDENCE-UNIT-LEDGER.json`.

Evidence units are the route-local support packets used by the archive for credit accounting. They bind route rows to public-record carriers, acquisition protocols, empirical deltas, severity rows, negative controls, independence assumptions, shared-support clusters, and rollback conditions.

Read this ledger before treating multiple artifacts as multiple independent confirmations. A unit may have many citations or carriers while still spending only one bounded route credit.
