# Extrapolation-fence ledger

`EXTRAPOLATION-FENCE-LEDGER.json` is the executable forbidden-inference ledger.

Each row states what a local result is **not** allowed to imply. Fence rows are especially important for code-subspace reconstruction, EFT/truncation corridors, cosmology likelihoods, laboratory quantum-gravity discriminators, and metadata/provenance wrappers.

The generated summary is `docs/30-program/validity-transport-summary.generated.md`.
