# Causal-set correlator / horizon-entropy source-role audit

## Risk found

The causal-set route already carried QSG, continuum-emergence, and horizon-diagnostic pressure, but its matter sector and black-hole thermodynamics hooks were still too easy to overread. In particular, a matter-correlator or scattering construction can be useful without proving an observed-sector matter theory, and a horizon-molecule entropy calculation can be useful without closing black-hole thermodynamics, microstate accounting, evaporation, or continuum recovery.

## Change made in rev0329

`DF-0018-CAUSAL-SET-CORRELATOR-HORIZON-ENTROPY-REPLAY` and `ED-0024-CAUSAL-SET-MATTER-CORRELATOR-HORIZON-ENTROPY-PRESSURE` make that pressure explicit and route-local for `R-OQ0057-CAUSAL-SET`. The decision row `DX-0010-CAUSAL-SET-MATTER-CONTINUUM-RECOVERY` now requires public replay of interacting-QFT in-in / in-out correlators or scattering conventions together with horizon-molecule / fuzzy-ladder diagnostics, continuum comparison, microstate quotient, evaporation or temperature convention, and observed-sector bridge limits.

`EU-0006-CAUSAL-SET-DYNAMICS` reciprocally names the new empirical delta so the handoff is visible, but it does not absorb the fresh causal-set matter/horizon references into `source_refs`. Those references remain pressure sources, not acquired evidence-unit support.

## Source-role boundary

Fresh causal-set refs are allowed on route-local forecasts, empirical deltas, decision rows, and relevant control rows. They are forbidden as acquired source credit on `EU-0006` until there is a public, replayable causal-set record that combines dynamics, continuum recovery, matter correlators or scattering, horizon or entropy diagnostics, and observed-sector bridge conventions.

## Non-promotion rule

`R-OQ0057-CAUSAL-SET` remains current `S1` with an `S2` ceiling. rev0329 adds denominators and replay pressure only. It does not promote the route, establish continuum emergence, solve the matter sector, or close black-hole thermodynamics.
