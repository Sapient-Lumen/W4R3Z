# Systematic-uncertainty ledger

Executable ledger: `SYSTEMATIC-UNCERTAINTY-LEDGER.json`
Schema: `schemas/systematic-uncertainty-ledger.schema.json`

This ledger records route-local systematic risks: detector calibration, environmental noise, survey covariance, foreground residual, benchmark leakage, regulator/truncation artifacts, dictionary choices, prior/model-class sensitivity, and formal proof gaps where applicable. A systematic row can cap, split, or roll back support. It cannot promote a route.
