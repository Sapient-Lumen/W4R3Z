# FamilyC subregion-state portability and source-role audit

`rev0322` targets the highest-authority live route: `R-OQ0057-FAMILYC-EW-CODE` remains the only current S3 lane, so the risk is not that it lacks scaffolding. The risk is that theorem/decoder/code-subspace success becomes a narrative shield instead of being forced to answer current subregion-state and gravitating-region portability questions.

## What changed

The revision adds a route-local discriminator forecast, `DF-0015-FAMILYC-SUBREGION-STATE-DICTIONARY-PORTABILITY`, and a route-local empirical/source-pressure row, `ED-0020-FAMILYC-SUBREGION-STATE-PORTABILITY-PRESSURE`. These rows require any strengthened FamilyC claim to name the finite-boundary or subregion-state definition, edge-mode/algebra dictionary, observer or frozen-region choice, quotient, reconstruction map, and failure modes.

The fresh 2026 references are deliberately not added to `EU-0001-FAMILYC-EW-RECONSTRUCTION`. They are pressure on the route's portability burden, not acquired evidence-unit support. `tools/familyc_subregion_state_policy.py` now makes this distinction executable.

## Why this is risky

Recent gravitating-region and subregion-state work appears to widen the conceptual target from asymptotic/boundary reconstructions toward finite-boundary and subregion-state language. That is promising for FamilyC, but it also increases dictionary debt: the route must now say which subregion state, which edge-mode algebra, which boundary data, and which observer/frozen-region convention are actually being made public and replayable.
