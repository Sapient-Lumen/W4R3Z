# Discriminator forecast ledger

## Purpose

`DISCRIMINATOR-FORECAST-LEDGER.json` records what future empirical or computational artifacts would actually change a route row. It is not a prediction market and not a promise that the artifact will occur. It is a guardrail against vague phrases like “future experiments will decide.”

Each forecast row must say:

- which route it touches;
- what public artifact would count;
- what a positive result would buy;
- what a negative result would buy;
- which negative controls remain active;
- the maximum credit under the current architecture;
- what overclaim remains forbidden.

## Current emphasis

The ledger now separates three empirical corridors that are often blurred:

1. gravity-induced-entanglement / mediator-dynamics records;
2. single-graviton stimulated absorption and graviton-counting statistics;
3. strong-field gravitational-wave consistency or deviation records.

All three are important. None by itself identifies a full ToE candidate.
