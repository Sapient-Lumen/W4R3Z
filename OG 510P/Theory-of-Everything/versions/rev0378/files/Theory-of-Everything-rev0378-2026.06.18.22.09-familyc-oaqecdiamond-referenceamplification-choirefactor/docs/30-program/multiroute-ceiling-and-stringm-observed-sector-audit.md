# Multi-route ceiling and String/M observed-sector audit

Revision: `rev0326`  
Route focus: `R-OQ0057-STRINGM-ATLAS`

## Risk addressed

Two risks were coupled. First, route-local rows were already capped by route promotion ceilings, but multi-route S-level rows could still carry a family-level authority envelope without an explicit route-by-route spendable cap. That made it possible for a shared claim-binding, metadata, or control row to imply more authority for an S1/S2 route than the route-state ledger permits.

Second, the String/M atlas lane was vulnerable to source-role overcredit. Current flux-vacuum enumeration, Landau-Ginzburg / Minkowski stabilization, and de Sitter / DESI-swampland pressure are scientifically useful, but they do not by themselves provide an observed-sector inverse map, a unique vacuum selection rule, or a ToE-level identity claim.

## Substantive change

`rev0326` makes every multi-route row with an S-level authority field carry explicit `route_authority_ceilings`. The spendable cap for each route is bounded by both the row's S-level envelope and that route's own promotion ceiling. The route-condition ceiling audit now treats these rows as executable coverage rather than an excluded residual class.

For `R-OQ0057-STRINGM-ATLAS`, the revision adds `DF-0017-STRINGM-FLUX-ATLAS-OBSERVED-SECTOR-QUOTIENT` and `ED-0022-STRINGM-ATLAS-MODULI-MEASURE-COSMOLOGY-PRESSURE`. These rows require a machine-readable flux-vacuum / compactification record, moduli-stabilization equations, tadpole and charge constraints, duality quotient, measure prior, spectrum/EFT tables, vacuum-energy or dynamical-dark-energy convention, failed-vacuum accounting, and replay script before observed-sector inverse language can be spent.

## Source-role boundary

Fresh refs `REF-0673` through `REF-0676` are route-local pressure refs. They are attached to forecasts, empirical deltas, decision experiments, and route-control rows, but not to `EU-0004-STRINGM-ATLAS-DUALITY-VACUUM.source_refs` as acquired evidence-unit credit. The evidence unit may expose the `ED-0022` handoff, but the current literature remains denominator and pressure evidence rather than support for promotion.

## Non-promotion rule

Flux-vacuum enumeration, isolated or stabilized Minkowski examples, and DESI/de Sitter swampland pressure can strengthen the String/M constraint corridor, but they do not promote the route without an observed-sector inverse, measure/typicality account, duality quotient, low-energy spectrum match, cosmological background convention, and independent replayable public artifacts. `R-OQ0057-STRINGM-ATLAS` remains current `S2` with no promotion in this revision.
