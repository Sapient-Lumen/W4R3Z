# Amplitudes gravity/IR positivity and empirical-delta namespace audit

## Risk repaired

The amplitudes/bootstrap lane is scientifically useful, but it is easy to overread it as a route to ontology: a clean-looking positivity or bootstrap bound can look like candidate identity unless gravity-specific denominators are route-facing. rev0323 therefore treats current gravitational S-matrix/bootstrap work as pressure on replay, IR control, loop stability, light-spectrum assumptions, and kinematic-domain tags, not as acquired evidence or promotion.

A second concrete integrity bug was found during the same pass: two different empirical-delta rows reused the `ED-0018` ordinal stem. Full slugged IDs were unique, so normal row-ID uniqueness did not catch it, but humans and generated references can still collapse the rows when referring to “ED-0018”. rev0323 repairs that collision by preserving `ED-0018-GRAVITON-REALIZATION-AND-QUANTIZATION-SPLIT-PRESSURE`, renumbering the FamilyC subregion-state row to `ED-0020-FAMILYC-SUBREGION-STATE-PORTABILITY-PRESSURE`, and assigning the amplitudes gravity/IR pressure row to `ED-0021-AMPLITUDES-GRAVITY-IR-POSITIVITY-PRESSURE`.

## Scientific pressure added

The route now requires any strengthened amplitudes/bootstrap claim to publish the massless-graviton pole treatment, subtraction count, high-spin or spectral truncation, numerical-stability window, loop correction convention, IR/light-spectrum assumptions, and kinematic-domain tags. This is intentionally stricter than a generic “positivity bound exists” statement.

The source split is asymmetric. Crossing-symmetric dispersion and finite-resolution graviton-pole methods are allowed to strengthen replayable S2 constraint pressure. Graviton-loop negativity, near-forward singularities, light-particle IR sensitivity, and complex/Klein kinematic statements are explicitly allowed to cap overclaim. None of these rows converts `EU-0007-AMPLITUDES-BOOTSTRAP-CONSISTENCY` into acquired evidence for candidate identity.

## Non-promotion rule

`R-OQ0057-AMPLITUDES-BOOTSTRAP` remains S2. A future promotion would require an independent public candidate-native bridge, observed-sector recovery, real-Lorentzian observable quotient, and replayable IR/loop/light-spectrum/kinematic-domain stability controls. rev0323 supplies pressure and rollback visibility, not promotion.
