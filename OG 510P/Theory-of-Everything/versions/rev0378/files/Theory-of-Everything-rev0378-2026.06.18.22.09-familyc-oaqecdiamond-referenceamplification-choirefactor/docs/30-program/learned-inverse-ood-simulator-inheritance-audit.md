# Learned-inverse OOD / simulator-inheritance pressure audit

Revision: `rev0325`

## Why this was the risky seam

The learned-inverse route is useful precisely because it can make hidden reconstruction assumptions executable. It is risky for the same reason: a clean neural reconstruction plot can look like bulk geometry, ontology, or public witness identity even when it is only a benchmark result inside a finite frequency window, radial cutoff, simulator, architecture/loss, seed, or training-support ecology.

This revision keeps `R-OQ0057-FAMILYC-LEARNED-INVERSE` at current `S2`. It does not promote the route. The change is to make the route pay the current denominator burden: finite-frequency/cutoff sweeps, baseline inverse solvers, PINN/Neural-ODE failure diagnostics, seed and hyperparameter regime maps, OOD abstention, simulator-inheritance controls, and uncertainty/coverage calibration.

## Source-role boundary

Fresh refs `REF-0131`/`REF-0143` plus `REF-0670` through `REF-0672` are pressure refs only. They are attached to the forecast, empirical delta, decision experiment, and route-local method-control rows. They are deliberately absent from `EU-0002-FAMILYC-LEARNED-INVERSE-BENCHMARK` source credit.

That separation matters: a current paper can sharpen what the route must do, but it cannot retroactively turn an older benchmark evidence unit into acquired candidate-native evidence.

## Practical gate

A learned-inverse claim may spend only S2 method/constraint language unless the public artifact names: target quotient, finite-frequency/cutoff regime, generator and split manifest, baseline inverse solvers, architecture/loss, seed/hyperparameter regime map, residual-vs-solution diagnostics, uncertainty/coverage calibration, OOD abstention thresholds, negative controls for simulator inheritance, and failure cases.

## Non-promotion rule

A successful learned inverse benchmark is reconstruction pressure, not ToE identity. The route remains surrogate-target, finite-frequency/cutoff, training-regime, and source-domain conditioned until public witness and candidate-native quotient controls exist.
