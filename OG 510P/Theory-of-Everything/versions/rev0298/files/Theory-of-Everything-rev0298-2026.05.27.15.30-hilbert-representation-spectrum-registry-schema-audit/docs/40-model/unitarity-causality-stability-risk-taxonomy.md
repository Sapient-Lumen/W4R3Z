# Unitarity / causality / stability risk taxonomy

The main risks are:

- **unitarity laundering:** treating a normalized likelihood, replayable code, or formal map as physical probability conservation;
- **reflection-positivity gap:** treating Euclidean data as Lorentzian unitary QFT data without a reconstruction condition;
- **amplitude causality gap:** treating positivity or crossing constraints as full causal propagation;
- **gravity cone mismatch:** treating a background cone, gauge-fixed perturbation, or EFT metric as the candidate-native causal object;
- **ghost/tachyon/gradient instability:** treating a formal solution or fit as viable while the perturbation spectrum is unhealthy;
- **vacuum/sector instability:** treating a selected branch as viable without a lifetime or boundedness analysis;
- **metadata substitution:** treating package integrity as physical viability.

These risks are route-local: the same word can mean S-matrix unitarity in one lane, likelihood normalization in another, code-subspace norm preservation in another, and no physical unitarity claim at all in metadata rows.
