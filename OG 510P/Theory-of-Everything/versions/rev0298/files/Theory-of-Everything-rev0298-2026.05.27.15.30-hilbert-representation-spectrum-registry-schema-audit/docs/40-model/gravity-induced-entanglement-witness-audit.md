# Gravity-induced entanglement witness audit

This document keeps one laboratory gravity-witness dispute load-bearing without letting it sprawl into a foundations omnibus.
It asks what the archive should count when BMV-style gravity-induced-entanglement experiments are advertised as a theory-independent proof that gravity must already be quantum.

## Compression verdict

The archive should now hold one tighter sentence:

**pure detection of gravity-induced entanglement should currently be scored as a concrete theory discriminator, not as theory-independent mediator-quantumness closure, because recent work makes explicit that the usual no-go story depends on stronger statespace / mediation assumptions than generic spacetime locality and because at least one explicit hybrid classical-mediator theory generates entanglement.** `REF-0164`, `REF-0165`

That means the right archive move is neither:
- "BMV-style experiments prove gravity is quantum by logic alone,"
- nor "gravity-induced-entanglement experiments are useless because the witness claim is contested."

The honest score is in between.

## What has genuinely improved

### 1. The hidden assumptions in the usual no-go reading are now sharper

Di Biagio argues that the common gloss — *if gravity generates entanglement, gravity must be nonclassical or nonlocal* — compresses several stronger assumptions into the word "locality."
The load-bearing split is tighter: the usual argument depends on assumptions about how the gravitational degrees of freedom sit in a statespace and how the interaction mediates between subsystems, not simply on generic relativistic locality in the everyday spacetime sense. `REF-0164`

That matters for the archive because family D should reward explicit assumption bookkeeping, not slogans.
A claimed witness theorem is stronger once its assumptions are named cleanly, but it can also become narrower.

### 2. The archive now has an explicit classical-mediator entanglement example, not only a conceptual warning

Ulbricht, Bermúdez Manjarres, and Reginatto present a hybrid van Hove example in which two quantum systems become entangled while coupled through a classical harmonic-oscillator mediator.
That is not yet a gravity-specific closure result, but it is enough to block the archive from treating entanglement generation by itself as a universal proof that the mediator must already be quantum. `REF-0165`

This is important because the archive prefers one concrete counterpressure example over a large amount of rhetorical controversy.

### 3. Family D now has a first concrete low-energy gravity-witness interpretation audit

Before this revision, family D was strong on general observer / record discipline but still underbuilt on how to score a prominent laboratory-style quantum-gravity proposal.
The GIE dispute now gives family D one compact experimental-interpretation brake: **the evidential jump from entanglement detection to mediator-quantumness must be scored separately from the experimental fact of entanglement itself.** `REF-0164`, `REF-0165`

That is a real archive gain.
It turns a vague controversy into a small reusable audit rule.

## What this does **not** buy

### 1. Not yet a theory-independent proof that gravity is quantum

The archive should now reject the stronger slogan that *observed GIE by itself closes the question*.
Current work is enough to show that the interpretation depends on which mediation and statespace assumptions are actually in force. `REF-0164`, `REF-0165`

So the strongest safe reading is weaker than theory-independent mediator-quantumness closure.

### 2. Not yet a reason to demote the experiments themselves

The corrective here is interpretive, not dismissive.
Di Biagio's own conclusion is not that GIE experiments stop mattering, but that they should discriminate among concrete theories rather than be oversold as one-step logical proof. `REF-0164`

The archive should therefore treat this lane as experimentally important but interpretation-sensitive.

### 3. Not yet witness-package closure

Even a successful entanglement experiment still leaves the archive's larger witness burdens in place:
- what exact intervention menu is native to the candidate,
- what threshold / tolerance budgets make the difference count,
- what empirical-equivalence classes remain,
- and how record carriers, public comparison standards, and reproducible evidence are stabilized. `REF-0039`, `REF-0058`, `REF-0059`, `REF-0060`, `REF-0061`

So this revision sharpens a **witness-interpretation brake**, not a closure stage.

## The archive score change

The archive should now keep one smaller distinction explicit:

1. **entanglement observed in a gravity-sensitive setup** — an experimental effect has been seen;
2. **concrete theory discrimination** — the result constrains or separates explicit quantum, semiclassical, collapse, or hybrid mediator stories;
3. **mediator-specific witness claim under declared assumptions** — the result supports a quantum-mediator inference only relative to named mediation / statespace assumptions;
4. **theory-independent quantum-gravity witness closure** — the result rules out the relevant classical / hybrid space without hidden scaffolding.

Current GIE discussion is strong enough to keep stage 2 alive and to sharpen what stage 3 would require.
It is **not** yet stage 4.

The archive does **not** need a new global witness subgate for this.
But family D should now remember that one prominent low-energy gravity experiment sits at the level of **discriminator-grade evidence with assumption-sensitive witness interpretation**, not theory-independent closure.

## False promotions to reject

1. **"GIE proves gravity is quantum by logic alone"**
   - The inference depends on stronger mediation / statespace assumptions than that slogan usually admits.

2. **"Any classical-mediator example makes the experiments worthless"**
   - A contested witness theorem does not erase the value of a concrete discriminator among explicit theories.

3. **"Entanglement yes/no is already the whole experimental question"**
   - The archive should want theory-separated quantitative predictions and assumption-visible interpretation, not only a binary prestige headline.

4. **"One laboratory entanglement result would settle broad ToE credit"**
   - Even a major witness gain would still have to be scored against the archive's local-law, cosmological-package, and wider witness-package burdens.

## What would move the archive further

A later revision should score this lane as materially stronger only if one compact route adds most of the following together:
1. apparatus-level certification of the mediation / statespace assumptions actually used by the inference,
2. quantitative theory-separated predictions across viable quantum, classical, collapse, or hybrid gravity stories,
3. a clean explanation of which parts of the evidential chain are gravity-specific rather than generic entanglement machinery,
4. threshold / tolerance budgets showing what difference would count as discriminatory,
5. and a clearer bridge from experimental discrimination to broader witness-package reduction.

Without that package, the archive should preserve the gain as a bounded **GIE witness-interpretation audit** and stop there.

## Net result

The archive now has a better brake sentence for empirical-contact work:

> gravity-induced entanglement remains experimentally important, but entanglement detection alone is currently weaker than theory-independent proof that the mediator is quantum.

That keeps the experiment load-bearing without letting it overclose the archive. `REF-0164`, `REF-0165`
