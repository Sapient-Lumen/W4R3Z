# Symmetry / anomaly risk taxonomy

## Main risks

- **Symmetry resemblance risk:** two routes use the same group, defect, duality, or transformation word but act on different objects.
- **Selected-sector symmetry risk:** a symmetry holds only after choosing a vacuum, boundary condition, regulator, gauge, apparatus state, survey window, or benchmark target.
- **Exact-global-symmetry risk:** a quantum-gravity route invokes an exact global symmetry without gauging, breaking, completeness, or no-global-symmetry residual control.
- **Anomaly-matching risk:** anomaly cancellation or matching is asserted for a presentation rather than a route-native object.
- **Conservation leakage risk:** a current, charge, or Ward identity closes only after hidden boundary flux, coarse-graining loss, detector selection, or metadata convention is ignored.

## Use

Use this taxonomy when a candidate lane tries to convert symmetry elegance, anomaly language, or conservation-law vocabulary into candidate-native support.
