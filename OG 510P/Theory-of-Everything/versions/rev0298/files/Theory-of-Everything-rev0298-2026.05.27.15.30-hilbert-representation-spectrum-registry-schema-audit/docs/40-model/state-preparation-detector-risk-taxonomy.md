# State-preparation and detector-response risk taxonomy

Main risks:

- source state differs from assumed prepared state;
- SPAM errors correlate preparation and measurement;
- detector response is a surrogate for the desired observable;
- backaction changes the target state;
- response thresholds, efficiency, noise, dead time, or calibration move the public record;
- detector or witness is frame-, sector-, gauge-, or apparatus-local.

These risks cap detector/witness language and do not promote candidate routes.
