# Composition and gluing risk taxonomy

Risks now tracked by rev0280:

- local sector success without a lawful composition operation;
- interface mismatch across boundary, scale, frame, gauge, semantic, or public-record seams;
- double counting shared support when modules are glued;
- nonassociative or nonfunctorial composition under route quotients;
- obstruction classes, anomaly mismatches, or noncommuting diagrams;
- cluster/factorization failure across distant or nominally independent components;
- package-level joins mistaken for physical gluing;
- unification vocabulary applied before local-to-global integrability is demonstrated.

The default authority effect is to cap global language, not to defeat the local result.
