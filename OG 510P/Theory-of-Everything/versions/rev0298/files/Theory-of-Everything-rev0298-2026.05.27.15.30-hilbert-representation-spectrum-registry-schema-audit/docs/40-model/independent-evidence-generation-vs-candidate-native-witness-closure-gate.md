# Independent-evidence-generation versus candidate-native-witness-closure gate

Purpose: split the archive's **witness-package progress** one step further.
A candidate should not earn the same witness credit merely because it can **let outsiders generate the same evidential class through separately acquired, separately prepared, or blind / holdout routes under declared comparison rules** and because it can **say, in its own bridge variables, where the record carriers live, what stabilizes them, how they are reread, and why the empirical loop is not still mainly outsourced to ordinary laboratory witness language**.
Those are related, but they are not the same achievement.

## Compression verdict

Inside the archive's witness package, once a lane has already moved beyond fresh-host reinstantiation, independent implementation, and independent evidence generation, the current score should now distinguish six nested layers:

1. **public evidence-custody closure** — the decisive evidence bundle or reference object is stably extractable, versioned, fixity-checkable, preservable, citable, and reopenable beyond one privileged repository or operator chain;
2. **fresh-host reinstantiation closure** — outsiders can take that public bundle, identify the exact workflow / software / environment objects it depends on, materialize an executable environment on a fresh host or stack, and rerun or rehydrate the evidential process under declared equivalence rules without privileged manual setup;
3. **independent implementation closure** — outsiders can implement the relevant workflow semantics, protocol, model, or measurement logic through a genuinely separate code lineage, test it against a public conformance or agreement criterion, and still recover the same evidential class without carrying the originating executable bundle itself;
4. **independent evidence-generation closure** — outsiders can obtain the same evidential class through separately acquired, separately prepared, or blind / holdout record-generation routes, interlaboratory comparison pathways, or independent measurement chains rather than only through multiple implementations over one inherited evidence source;
5. **candidate-native witness closure** — the candidate's own bridge variables name the record carrier, stabilization story, reread path, and objectivity / publicity conditions closely enough that the empirical loop is no longer mostly redescribed in borrowed detector-side or boundary-side language;
6. **local public witness closure** — a lane can say where the physical records live, what stabilizes them, how they are reread, why they remain public evidence, and why that empirical loop is not mainly outsourced.

A lane may earn real credit on layer 4 while still being weak on layer 5.
That is progress.
It is just not yet candidate-native witness closure, much less full local public witness closure.

## Why this gate exists

Shared witness-gate scaffolding now lives in `docs/40-model/witness-closure-gate-family-frame.md`. This document only names the pair-specific inflation move below.

**fresh independently generated evidence being retold as if the candidate itself therefore already explains, in its own native variables, what the evidence-bearing records are and how they remain stable, rereadable, and publicly comparable.**

That inflation can happen in at least seven ways:
- a lane can pass blind / holdout or interlaboratory tests while every actual record token still lives in ordinary detector, archive, or environment language that the candidate merely assumes;
- a lane can construct relational or dressed observables while leaving the long-lived record carrier, coarse-graining rule, or reread path to ordinary laboratory practice;
- a lane can introduce clocks, rods, subregions, or observers internally while the conditions for public comparison and durable objectivity still arrive from imported calibration or pointer-basis lore;
- a lane can show that predictions survive partial observability or observer conditioning while the candidate-native account of what physically stores the observational fact remains underdescribed;
- a lane can obtain several successful native-looking observable constructions that disagree on what counts as the relevant frame, subsystem, conditioning surface, or record algebra, revealing that the public witness semantics are not yet closed;
- a lane can redescribe ordinary external witness channels in geometric, informational, or relational vocabulary without deriving the borrowed witness work in that vocabulary;
- or a lane can cite external empirical success for the candidate while the candidate's own bridge language still lacks a native account of stabilization, redundancy, or rereadability.

## The five-step candidate-native-witness test

### 1. Native carrier identification, not just external evidence success

Before awarding candidate-native-witness credit, the archive should ask whether the candidate names the evidence-bearing distinction in its own variables rather than merely pointing to successful external records.
At minimum, a lane should disclose:
- what in the candidate's own language plays the role of the record-bearing distinction,
- how that carrier is related to the external evidence route that actually generated support,
- which parts of the record story remain in ordinary detector, boundary, or laboratory vocabulary,
- and what would count as the same record token inside the candidate rather than only in the lab notebook.

A lane that wins only here earns **independent evidence-generation credit**, not yet candidate-native witness closure.

### 2. Native stabilization story

A lane earns more than evidence-generation credit only when the candidate explains what keeps those native record distinctions stable long enough to function as evidence.
At minimum, the archive should ask:
- what dynamical or algebraic structure protects the relevant coarse-grained distinction,
- whether that stability is episodic, asymptotic, pointer-basis-like, redundancy-based, or something else,
- what host system or environmental assumptions are still imported rather than derived,
- and what breakdown regime makes the record cease to count as evidence.

Without that route, apparent native witness structure may still be relabeled laboratory persistence.

### 3. Native reread and publicity path

A lane earns stronger candidate-native-witness credit only when later access to the same coarse-grained fact is described natively as well.
At minimum, the archive should ask:
- how the same fact is reread without reconstructing the full microscopic state,
- what makes that reread path public or at least non-solipsistic rather than tied to one privileged observer or frame,
- how cross-frame, cross-time, or cross-subregion agreement is recovered in the candidate's own variables,
- and which public comparison standard is still silently borrowed from outside the candidate.

Without those answers, apparent native witness structure may still be one-off access dressed up as objectivity.

### 4. Native-to-lab bridge must be explicit

A lane earns still stronger candidate-native-witness credit only when the relation between native witness structure and actual laboratory evidence is explicit rather than tacit.
At minimum, the archive should ask:
- how native carriers map onto externally acquired records, archives, or detectors,
- whether this map is many-to-one, approximate, state-dependent, or regime-limited,
- what empirical differences would appear if the native account were wrong,
- and whether the candidate can say more than that ordinary lab evidence is merely “represented” somewhere in its formalism.

Without that bridge, apparent native witness closure may still be a semantic halo around external evidence success.

### 5. Independent evidence must survive as candidate-native witness rather than collapse back to borrowed prose

A lane earns **candidate-native witness closure** credit only when later users can connect independent evidence routes back to one native record story.
At minimum, it should name:
- the native record carrier,
- the native stabilization and reread path,
- the public or cross-context comparison condition in native variables,
- the mapping from those native structures to fresh independently generated evidence,
- and the residual debt to full local public witness closure.

This is still not the same thing as full local public witness closure.
A lane may support candidate-native witness closure quite well while still depending on ordinary laboratory infrastructure for the final public record ecology.
But that is a much smaller debt than merely having independent evidence with no native witness account.

## Common failure modes

1. **empirical-halo inflation** — fresh independent evidence is retold as if it automatically closed the candidate's own record semantics.
2. **borrowed-carrier laundering** — a candidate redescribes detector outputs, boundary data, or archive tokens in native language without deriving why those objects count as records.
3. **observer black-boxing** — a lane invokes observers, clocks, rods, or frames internally while leaving their persistence and rereadability to unmodeled ordinary practice.
4. **native-wording without native loop** — the bridge has a vocabulary for observables but not a closed record-formation, stabilization, and reread path.
5. **comparison-standard smuggling** — public adequacy, calibration, or objectivity conditions are silently imported from the lab side.
6. **subregion / frame plurality without witness closure** — several elegant native constructions exist, but no single account yet carries the full public witness burden.
7. **theory-external record fallback** — when pressure rises, the argument retreats to ordinary external evidence language, showing that the native witness account is still incomplete.

## Compact current readout

- **Giddings' 2025 review** makes the overarching burden explicit by arguing that observations and observables are central to quantum gravity and by pressing the unresolved question of how information is localized once gravitational dressing replaces ordinary local operator structure.
- **Geng's rods-and-clocks construction** shows real candidate-native progress by giving an affirmative quantum-gravity route to local operators using quantum rods and clocks, while also emphasizing that familiar boundary dressings are only quasi-local and do not by themselves settle the deeper witness burden.
- **Marchetti and Wilson-Ewing's GFT relational-observable construction** shows that one can define observables in a perspective-neutral full-QG setting, but it also records limitations and competing constructions, making clear that native observable success is still not the same as closure of one public witness account.
- **Harlow, Usatyuk, and Zhao's closed-universe observer framework** shows that observers can be systematically incorporated into a closed-universe quantum-gravity description, but this still turns on explicit observer conditioning and residual environment structure rather than a universally closed native public-record story.
- **Blommaert and Chen's 2026 subregion work** shows that gauge-invariant observables can be dressed to features of spacetime itself without an external observer, while explicitly leaving further open questions about how the resulting constructions generalize and how their entropic structure connects to broader quantum-gravity settings.

That does not refute current bridge programs or completion bids.
It just means they have not yet paid this stronger candidate-native-witness debt.

## Net rule

Within the archive:
- independent evidence-generation closure earns real witness-package credit;
- but it does **not** yet count as candidate-native witness closure;
- and candidate-native witness closure does **not** yet count as full local public witness closure.

Any future completion bid or bridge-family claim that wants stronger witness credit should now say not only how outsiders can generate fresh independent evidence, but how the candidate's own bridge variables identify the record carrier, stabilize it, support reread and publicity, and map that native account back to the actual external evidence route without collapsing into borrowed laboratory prose.
