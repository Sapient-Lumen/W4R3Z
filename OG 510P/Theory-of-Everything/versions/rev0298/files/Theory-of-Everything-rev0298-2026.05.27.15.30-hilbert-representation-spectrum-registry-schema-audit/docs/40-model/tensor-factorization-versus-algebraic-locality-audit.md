# Tensor factorization versus algebraic locality audit

A route may have a useful local algebra without a tensor product decomposition of the full Hilbert space. This audit asks whether the route has only algebraic locality, an approximate split inclusion, an extended-Hilbert-space construction, a code-subspace factor, an asymptotic split, an operational detector split, or no physical factorization claim.

The audit is especially strict for gravity and gauge theories, where constraints, dressing, edge modes, centers, and boundary charges can make naive region factorization misleading.
