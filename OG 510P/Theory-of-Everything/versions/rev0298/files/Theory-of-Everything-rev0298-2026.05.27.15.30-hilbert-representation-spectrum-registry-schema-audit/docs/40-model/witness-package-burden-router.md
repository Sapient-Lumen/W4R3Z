## Shared rule

Witness-package credit should be scored in order:
- first by naming what witness debt is still borrowed rather than candidate-native,
- then by locating any claimed progress on the ordered subgate stack,
- then by keeping pairwise gate-family scaffolding shared rather than cloned across every gate,
- and only then by asking whether any family actually reduced candidate-native identifiability debt.

Earlier-stage payment is real progress, not later-stage closure.
A lowered borrowing score is not by itself a later closure stage.
Pairwise gate wins do not erase remaining witness debt elsewhere on the stack.

Witness-focused stable, restart, and program surfaces should therefore name this router when they only need the established witness-control architecture.
Do not replay the borrowing ladder, stack, and shared gate-family frame separately unless the revision is actually changing one of those surfaces.
If the issue is integrated broad ToE credit rather than witness pressure specifically, route through `docs/40-model/broad-toe-credit-router.md` instead.
