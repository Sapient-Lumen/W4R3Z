# Attribution-versus-ranking gate

Purpose: split the **attribution-grade** layer inside `docs/40-model/acquisition-vs-attribution-gate.md` one step further.
A completion bid should not earn the same archive-moving credit merely because some acquired signal can be **stably attributed back to the lane** and because that signal would **materially and robustly update the archive's comparative ranking of the lane once reasonable prior-volume, class-breadth, and complexity choices are faced honestly**.
Shared family scaffolding and ordered routing live in `docs/40-model/completion-bid-comparison-gate-family-frame.md` and `docs/40-model/completion-bid-comparison-stack.md`; this file keeps only the pair-specific split between attribution-grade traction and ranking-grade movement.

## Compression verdict

The archive should now score claimed empirical traction in **three post-attribution layers** after a wedge already has a credible acquisition path and a stable attribution story:

1. **attribution-grade discriminatory traction** — nearby admissible forward-model, nuisance, discrepancy, and inversion choices do not erase or reverse the lane credit;
2. **ranking-basis disclosure** — the lane states the compared rival set, prior-volume / package-breadth choices, complexity cost, and evidence-aggregation scope under which the claimed ranking movement is being asserted;
3. **ranking-grade comparative update** — the same broad theory movement survives nearby reasonable prior, class-width, or complexity choices strongly enough to matter for archive ordering, not merely for one favorable scoring convention.

A lane may earn real credit on layer 1 while still being weak on layers 2 and 3.
That is still progress.
It is just not yet ranking-stable theory movement.

## Why this gate exists

`docs/40-model/acquisition-vs-attribution-gate.md` already blocked one inflation move:
**an acquirable signal pretending to be stably attributable support for one lane.**

A further inflation move remained too easy:
**attribution-grade support pretending to be an archive-moving comparative verdict.**

That confusion appears whenever a completion bid is praised because it:
- gains some attributable support under one favored prior width or one narrow package weighting,
- posts a positive pairwise odds ratio while the wider rival set or lane breadth is left unspecified,
- treats one attractive attributed wedge as though it settles overall standing despite earlier recovery, observable, or package debt,
- or ignores the fact that broad flexible lanes and narrow rigid lanes pay different complexity or prior-volume costs before evidence is turned into ranking movement.

A wedge can be real, acquirable, and even attribution-stable while still being ranking-thin.
This gate keeps those credits separate.

## The three-step ranking test

### 1. Comparative basis disclosure

Before counting attribution-grade support as archive movement, the archive should ask:
- which rivals or rival families are being ranked,
- whether the claim is pairwise, local-to-a-subfamily, or archive-wide,
- which prior-volume, package-breadth, or class-width choices define the comparison,
- and whether missing debts elsewhere in the lane are being held fixed or silently ignored.

A lane that cannot name this basis may still have real empirical support.
It does not yet have ranking-grade support.

### 2. Prior-volume and complexity honesty

A wedge earns more than attribution credit only if the archive is told how broad the compared lane really is.
At minimum, the lane should disclose:
- whether the credited support attaches to a narrow package or to a broad lane family,
- how much unused parameter or package volume is still being carried,
- whether nearby reasonable prior choices or complexity penalties materially change the claimed evidence,
- and whether the comparison is robust to more than one non-pathological scoring convention.

The archive should not demand one uniquely correct prior.
It should demand enough honesty to tell the difference between **support conditional on one scoring basis** and **support that actually survives reasonable comparative bases**.

### 3. Ranking-stable update

Even after attribution and disclosure, the archive should still ask whether the same evidence would really move theory ordering.
A wedge earns **ranking-grade comparative** credit only when:
- the claimed support still materially reorders the named rival set across nearby reasonable prior / complexity choices,
- the result is not an artifact of comparing one narrow package to one broad family under a selectively favorable weighting,
- and the lane can say what archive-level movement follows: local subfamily encouragement, broader lane promotion, or no meaningful ranking shift yet.

A wedge may justify more research and still not yet justify a stronger archive verdict.
That is a scoring fact, not a refutation.

## Common failure modes

1. **Conditional-verdict laundering** — support obtained under one favored prior or weighting is narrated as unconditional ranking movement.
2. **Pairwise overreach** — a win against one rival or null baseline is retold as archive-wide superiority.
3. **Complexity silence** — broad model-class or package-volume debt is omitted when evidence is converted into verdict language.
4. **Debt amnesia** — one successful attributable wedge is allowed to erase unresolved recovery, observable, or package burdens elsewhere in the lane.

## Immediate archive rules

1. **Do not count attribution-grade support as archive-moving ranking credit unless the comparative basis is named.**
2. **Require at least one nearby reasonable prior-volume / class-breadth / complexity variation whenever a lane claims verdict movement.**
3. **Do not let one favorable pairwise Bayes factor or odds ratio stand in for archive-wide rank movement.**
4. **Do not punish honest admission that a wedge supports further work but leaves ranking movement conditional.**
   Punish only the inflation move that treats such support as already decisive comparative closure.
5. **Keep this gate subordinate to the acquisition-versus-attribution gate and the canonical atlas surfaces.**
   This surface sharpens comparison inside attribution-grade support; it does not replace separate debts on recovery, observables, package disclosure, or witness closure.

## Net result

The archive now has a smaller and more honest sentence available:

> a completion bid may earn attribution-grade empirical support and still be weak on ranking-stable comparative movement once prior-volume and complexity debt are faced honestly.

That sentence blocks another prestige inflation move without widening the archive.
