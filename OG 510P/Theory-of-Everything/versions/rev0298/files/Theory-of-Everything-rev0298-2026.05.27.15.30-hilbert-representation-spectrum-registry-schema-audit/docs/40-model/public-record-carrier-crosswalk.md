# Public-record carrier crosswalk

## Purpose

This crosswalk maps physical route families to the public-record carrier classes they currently use. It is deliberately conservative: a carrier can strengthen record custody without resolving candidate identity.

## Gravity-specific caution

Gravity makes public carriers unusually tricky because local-subsystem, asymptotic, relational, dressed, code-subspace, detector, and frame-relative records are not automatically equivalent. The carrier ledger therefore names the carrier class and replay path instead of letting all public evidence collapse into one bucket.
