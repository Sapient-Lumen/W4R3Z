# Raw-record to likelihood audit

A route-local likelihood/update row should be rejected or capped unless the audit can name the raw or formal record, measurand or recoverand, transformation and correction model, calibration or traceability chain, systematic uncertainty budget, nuisance/prior/model-class sensitivity, contrast class, evidence unit and credit allocation, and rollback rule if any component fails.

This audit prevents the phrase "public likelihood" from hiding detector response, survey covariance, foreground cleaning, benchmark target leakage, regulator truncation, or proof-environment dependence.
