# Causal mechanism risk taxonomy

Revision: `rev0269`

This model-side surface names the physics-specific risks that the rev0269 ledgers control.

## Risk classes

1. **Formal-dependence inflation** — a reconstruction, fixed point, amplitude constraint, or compactification chart is retold as a causal mechanism without intervention or manipulability semantics.
2. **Simulator-ablation inflation** — a benchmark or simulation ablation is retold as intervention on the target physical ontology.
3. **Mediator-ontology inflation** — a lab discriminator for mediator class is retold as identification of the full gravity ontology.
4. **Natural-experiment inflation** — astrophysical or cosmological variation is retold as if it were randomized manipulation.
5. **Counterfactual-world looseness** — the route appeals to unspecified alternative worlds rather than named model interventions or structural changes.
6. **Metadata mechanism laundering** — provenance, replay, or package identity is counted as causal or mechanistic evidence.

The remedy is not to ban causal language. The remedy is to put it through `CAUSAL-MECHANISM-LEDGER.json`, `INTERVENTION-PROTOCOL-LEDGER.json`, and `COUNTERFACTUAL-ROBUSTNESS-LEDGER.json` before it is allowed to carry authority.
