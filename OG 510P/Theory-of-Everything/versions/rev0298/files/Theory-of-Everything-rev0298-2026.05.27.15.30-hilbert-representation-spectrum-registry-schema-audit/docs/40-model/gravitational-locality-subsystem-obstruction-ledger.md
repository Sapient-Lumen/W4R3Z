# Gravitational locality / subsystem obstruction ledger

## Purpose

The archive should not import local QFT's subsystem language into gravity without paying for the obstruction. Diffeomorphism invariance and gravitational dressing make local observables, local subalgebras, and public witnesses harder than their nongravitational analogues.

## Load-bearing claim

`REF-0189` anchors the key pressure: once coupled to gravity, compactly supported operators carrying nonzero Poincare charges require gravitational dressing with arbitrarily long-distance metric dependence. That makes exact local commuting subalgebras and clean subsystem factorization unavailable as default assumptions.

## Ledger

| Witness object | What works | Obstruction | Archive ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed-background local QFT operator | sharply local algebra in nongravitational QFT | not gauge invariant after coupling to gravity when charges are present | cannot define gravity-side public witness by copy-paste |
| Relational observable | can be diffeomorphism invariant by referencing dynamical rods, clocks, or geometry | state dependence, dressing, and reference-field fluctuations can enter | access credit, not public closure |
| Asymptotic observable | natural in scattering or boundary settings | may miss finite-region / local-subsystem data | asymptotic-access credit |
| Gravitationally dressed quasi-local operator | gives a controlled perturbative handle | long-range dressing contaminates subsystem independence | bounded local-operator corridor |
| Code-subspace observable | reconstructable within a controlled holographic sector | depends on code space, boundary dictionary, and state / cutoff choices | bounded Family-C `S3` partial at most |
| Laboratory record | public and persistent in ordinary practice | may identify mediator class rather than ToE ontology | discriminator credit, not full identifiability |
| Frame-relative record | meaningful inside one relational frame | same-fact transport can fail or be underdefined | access-discipline credit |

## Consequence for `OQ-0057`

A candidate-native route that relies on local public witnesses must say whether its record is local-QFT-like, relational, asymptotic, dressed, code-subspace, laboratory, or frame-relative. If it cannot classify the witness object, the route remains below `S3` regardless of formal elegance.

## Speculative pressure

The archive's public-bridge target may be too local. In gravity, the correct public witness may need to be relational, asymptotic, dressed, finite-region approximate, code-subspace, or detector-history based. That does not weaken the public-bridge requirement; it changes what an honest bridge can look like.
