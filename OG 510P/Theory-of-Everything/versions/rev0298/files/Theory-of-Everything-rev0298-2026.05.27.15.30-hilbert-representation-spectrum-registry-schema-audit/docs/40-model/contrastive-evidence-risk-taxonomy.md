# Contrastive evidence risk taxonomy

A route can fail contrastively even when its evidence is public, severe, replayable, and not double-counted.

## Risk classes

| Risk | Pattern | Archive response |
|---|---|---|
| Rival-set omission | evidence is called support without alternatives | freeze update wording |
| Wrong contrast grain | family-level discriminator retold as candidate identity | cap at family/parameter credit |
| Quotient collapse | two alternatives differ only by duality, gauge, frame, or presentation | merge candidates until quotient is resolved |
| Prior-bound sensitivity | model preference changes under plausible prior bounds | route remains prior-sensitive |
| Nuisance-stack dependence | conclusion depends on covariance, foreground, waveform, detector, or simulator assumptions | require sensitivity / rollback row |
| Transfer-of-confirmation leak | success of a package is assigned to one part | require transfer rule or block part-level claim |
| Metadata nonupdate | custody wrapper is counted as evidence | custody-only credit |

## Current use

This taxonomy is especially active for late-time cosmology, primordial tensors, strong-field gravitational waves, low-energy quantum-gravity lab routes, Family-C reconstruction benchmarks, asymptotic-safety truncation rows, and string/M landscape-vacuum sorting. It is not limited to those lanes.
