# Physical observable versus gauge representative audit

A route that produces a useful quantity must still answer whether the quantity is physical. The audit asks:

- What redundancy acts on the object?
- Which constraint algebra or Ward/BRST/BV structure is being used?
- Does the algebra close under the route's regulator, boundary, sector, and approximation choices?
- What is the quotient object or cohomology class?
- What changes under gauge fixing, coordinate transformation, field redefinition, frame transport, or representative swap?
- Which public record is invariant enough to be replayed or challenged?

Until those questions are answered, the archive treats the result as representative-local support, not physical-observable or candidate-native support.
