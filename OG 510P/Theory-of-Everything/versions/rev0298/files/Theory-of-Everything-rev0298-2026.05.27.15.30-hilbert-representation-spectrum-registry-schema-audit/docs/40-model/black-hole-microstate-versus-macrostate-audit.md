# Black-hole microstate versus macrostate audit

Macrostate recovery and microstate recovery are separate debts.

A route may reproduce mass, angular momentum, charge, area, surface gravity, or temperature while leaving the microscopic state count, degeneracy quotient, ensemble, and observable public record unresolved.

The string/M route gets explicit microstate-counting pressure, but extremal or supersymmetric microstate counts cannot be exported to all black holes, all sectors, all signatures, or all observed black-hole records without the route paying sector, topology, signature, measure, and public-record debts.
