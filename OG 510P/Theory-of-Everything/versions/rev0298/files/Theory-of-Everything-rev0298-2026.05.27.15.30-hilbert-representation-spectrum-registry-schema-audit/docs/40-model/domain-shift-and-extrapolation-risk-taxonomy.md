# Domain-shift and extrapolation risk taxonomy

This model-side surface translates the rev0268 validity/transport/fence ledgers into physics-facing risks.

## Main risk classes

- **Scale shift:** EFT or low-energy evidence exported beyond its cutoff or power-counting range.
- **State-support shift:** code-subspace, island/QES, simulator, or training-support evidence exported to arbitrary states.
- **Frame/gauge shift:** a frame-relative record exported without an explicit transport/equivalence law.
- **Regulator/truncation shift:** RG or asymptotic-safety evidence exported before regulator and truncation portability are shown.
- **Catalog/survey shift:** waveform, BAO, or CMB likelihood results exported beyond detector, foreground, covariance, prior, and model-class assumptions.
- **Laboratory-regime shift:** GIE/BMV or graviton-counting records exported from one mass/separation/noise/source-state regime to quantum-gravity ontology.
- **Atlas/chart shift:** string/M or duality-chart structure exported to unique vacuum or candidate identity.
- **Metadata shift:** provenance, generated summaries, or release packaging exported to physical support.

## Archive consequence

Every class is a source of cap, rollback, or quarantine. None is a promotion source by itself.
