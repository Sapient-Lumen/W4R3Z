# Cross-site-reproducibility versus intervention-grade-control-closure gate

Purpose: split the archive's **witness-package progress** one step further.
A candidate should not earn the same witness credit merely because it can **reproduce the same coarse-grained evidential claim across independently maintained sites or stacks** and because it has **closed intervention-grade control strongly enough that the same coarse-grained evidential state can be deliberately prepared, steered, conditionally rerouted, or erased under public, transportable control rules**.
Those are related, but they are not the same achievement.

## Compression verdict

Inside the archive's witness package, once a lane has already moved beyond one inherited evidential chain and has named a public route to cross-site reproducibility, the current score should now distinguish four nested layers:

1. **cross-site reproducibility closure** — independently maintained sites or stacks can reinstate the same coarse-grained evidential claim under transportable public protocols;
2. **intervention-grade control closure** — independently maintained sites or stacks can not only recover that claim, but deliberately write, steer, branch on, stabilize, or erase the same coarse-grained evidential state under public actuation and feedback rules;
3. **drift-resilient autonomy closure** — that same control loop stays live through drift, recalibration, relocation, or maintenance events across long runtimes without a silently inherited babysitting ecology;
4. **local public witness closure** — a lane can say where the physical records live, what stabilizes them, how they are reread, why they remain public evidence, and why that empirical loop is not mainly outsourced.

A lane may earn real credit on layer 1 while still being weak on layers 2 and 3.
That is progress.
It is just not yet intervention-grade control closure, much less drift-resilient autonomy closure or full witness closure.

## Why this gate exists

Shared witness-gate scaffolding now lives in `docs/40-model/witness-closure-gate-family-frame.md`. This document only names the pair-specific inflation move below.

**reproducible readout across sites being retold as if portable intervention, feedback, and actuation control were already solved.**

That inflation can happen in at least four ways:
- the same benchmark can be rerun across several sites while the write / steer / erase semantics still live inside one hidden control stack;
- a theory can explain how the same evidential claim is reobserved elsewhere while saying little about how the same state is actively prepared, branched on, or reversed under transportable timing and feedback constraints;
- apparent control can depend on local latency budgets, FPGA layouts, pulse timing, or hand-tuned actuator semantics that never become part of the public witness story;
- or a lane can advertise dynamic or feedback-heavy success while the active branch logic still rides on one provider's electronics, scheduler, or qubit-classical routing architecture.

## The four-step intervention-grade control test

### 1. Intervention target disclosure

Before awarding intervention-grade control credit, the archive should ask what is being actively controlled.
At minimum, a lane should disclose:
- which coarse-grained state, logical branch, syndrome-conditioned action, or record-bearing distinction is being written, steered, stabilized, or erased,
- what preparation freedom and control objective are public rather than local folklore,
- and whether the claim concerns one passive rerun or an actual family of counterfactually different interventions.

A lane that wins only here earns **cross-site reproducibility credit**, not yet intervention-grade control closure.

### 2. Actuation and feedback route

A lane earns more than reproducibility credit only when it can say how actuation closes the loop.
At minimum, the archive should ask:
- what actuators, control primitives, or branch operations are used,
- what latency, timing, bandwidth, or synchronization budget the loop requires,
- how measurement outcomes, decoder output, or control-state updates are routed back into the next physical action,
- and which parts of that loop remain provider-specific, vendor-locked, or tacitly hand-tuned.

Without that route, apparent control may still be readout plus local scripting.

### 3. Cross-site transport and nearby robustness

A lane earns stronger intervention credit only when the same actuation semantics survive across independently maintained stacks.
At minimum, the archive should ask:
- what public control description or typed protocol travels between heterogeneous sites,
- whether the same write / branch / erase claim survives nearby changes in hardware family, electronics stack, scheduler, or control software,
- whether reversal, conditional branching, or active stabilization still work without silent local reauthoring,
- and what residual manual retuning or hidden platform assumptions remain.

Without those answers, apparent control portability may be a same-stack artifact with a public benchmark veneer.

### 4. Closure boundary

A lane earns **intervention-grade control closure** credit only when it can say how a cross-site reproducible evidential claim becomes a cross-site portable actuation claim without a silently inherited control stack.
At minimum, it should name:
- the controlled coarse-grained state or evidential distinction,
- the public intervention / feedback semantics,
- the timing, latency, and routing conditions that must be met,
- the heterogeneity range across which the control claim survives,
- and the remaining debt to full local record-carrier, stabilization, rereadability, and objectivity closure.

This is still not the same thing as drift-resilient autonomy closure or full local witness closure.
A lane may control a state skilfully while still borrowing the maintenance ecology, public record carrier, and objectivity side from ordinary laboratory witness structure.

## Common failure modes

1. **Read-only inflation** — cross-site reruns are retold as if write / steer / erase control were already solved.
2. **Script-portability laundering** — one transportable benchmark script is mistaken for portable actuation semantics.
3. **Hidden latency borrowing** — the active loop works only because one local timing, routing, or feedback stack is silently inherited.
4. **One-direction control** — preparation works, but conditional branch, reversal, stabilization, or erasure do not travel with the claim.
5. **Control-to-autonomy inflation** — intervention-grade control is retold as if drift-resilient autonomy were therefore solved too.
6. **Control-to-closure inflation** — intervention-grade control is retold as if full local witness closure were therefore solved too.

## Compact current readout

- **Recent portable-control stack work** sharpens the target by separating control or tuning intent from instrument realization and exposing typed, transportable protocol libraries rather than rewriting routines against each lab's local stack.
- **Recent control-architecture work** makes the stronger burden visible by emphasizing that active quantum control demands nanosecond-scale timing, microsecond-latency feedback, and low-friction hardware / software co-design that ordinary passive reproducibility does not automatically buy.
- **Recent mid-circuit feedforward experiments** show the empirical difference directly: reproducible measurement alone is weaker than branch-conditioned intervention that must close the qubit-classical loop before decoherence or move the control logic into the quantum layer itself.

That does not refute current observer-relative or bridge-theory programs.
It just means they have not yet paid this stronger actuation debt.

## Net rule

Within the archive:
- cross-site reproducibility closure earns real witness-package credit;
- but it does **not** yet count as intervention-grade control closure;
- intervention-grade control closure does **not** yet count as drift-resilient autonomy closure;
- and none of those automatically counts as local public witness closure.

Any future completion bid or bridge-family claim that wants stronger witness credit should now say not only how the same evidence is independently reproduced, but how the same coarse-grained evidential state is actively written, steered, branched on, stabilized, or erased across heterogeneous sites under public, transportable feedback rules.
