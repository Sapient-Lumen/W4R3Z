## Compression verdict

The archive should now hold a **bounded partial yes**.

- Family C's surplus does survive one disciplined step beyond the narrow static RT lane.
- The surviving gain is still the same kind of gain: **partition-sensitive subregion geometry linkage** in a controlled holographic dual description.
- The widening does **not** remove the archive's main caution labels: the regime remains special, the dual dictionary remains heavily structured, and the record / objectivity story remains boundary-borrowed.

So `FQ-0006` should no longer be treated as open confusion.
It now has a first bounded answer:
**family C retains real surplus in a covariant HRT-style extension, but not yet in a way that justifies promotion or a higher witness-ladder placement.**

## What is being widened

The current archive's first family-C worked example was deliberately narrow:
- explicit boundary subregion,
- static or near-static RT-style setting,
- large-`N` / semiclassical holographic control.

The smallest honest regime widening is the covariant HRT-style move in which:
- the boundary partition remains explicit,
- the geometric object becomes a codimension-two extremal surface rather than a purely time-slice minimal surface,
- and the claim is allowed to track time-dependent states inside the same broad AdS/CFT-style dual setting.

This is a real widening of family C.
It is not yet a regime-free theory-of-everything advance.

## What survives the widening

### 1. The partition remains load-bearing

The family-C gain does not evaporate once the setup becomes covariant.
A chosen boundary subregion still matters nontrivially, and the geometric claim remains indexed by that split rather than by generic entropy language alone.

### 2. The geometry link is not merely equilibrium bookkeeping

The widened HRT-style lane still ties a boundary entanglement object to a specific geometric surface rule in a dynamical setting.
That means the family-C surplus is not confined to static thermodynamic analogy.
It survives at least one step into time-dependent holographic reconstruction. `REF-0013`, `REF-0019`

### 3. The dictionary stays sharper than generic thermodynamic language

Even after widening, family C still earns more than family B's coarse entropy / area bookkeeping.
The active content is still a subregion-indexed dictionary between boundary entanglement structure and bulk geometry, not just a claim that entropy constrains dynamics somehow. `REF-0011`, `REF-0013`, `REF-0019`

## What does **not** survive as a strong claim

### 1. No regime-free victory

The widened result still depends on a strongly organized holographic setting.
It should not be retold as evidence that generic quantum gravity or generic spacetime emergence has been solved.
The archive still owes an explanation of what survives once AdS/CFT control, large-`N`, or semiclassical structure are relaxed. `REF-0011`, `REF-0019`

### 2. No witness-ladder climb

The covariant extension does not by itself reduce witness borrowing.
Stable records, rereadability, and objectivity still live on the boundary / laboratory side, in the same broad sense already diagnosed by the RT-style audit and witness ladder.
So the correct archive move is to preserve family C at `WB-2`, not to promote it to `WB-3`. `REF-0015`, `REF-0016`, `REF-0017`

### 3. No closure on stronger reconstruction claims

A covariant surface rule is not yet the same thing as candidate-native witness structure, and it is not yet the same thing as robust reconstruction across broader settings.
The archive should therefore separate:
- **covariant carry:** some family-C surplus survives beyond static RT,
- from **full regime robustness:** unresolved,
- and from **reduced borrowing:** unresolved.

## Clean failure modes to keep blocked

Do not let this audit be used to smuggle in any of the following:

- “HRT proves geometry from entanglement in general,”
- “time dependence dissolves the regime problem,”
- “a covariant extremal-surface rule already explains records,”
- or “covariant extension alone is enough to climb the witness-borrowing ladder.”
