# Calibration and traceability crosswalk

Physical calibration and formal/computational traceability are not identical, but they play the same archive-control role: they expose the chain by which a public record becomes a support-bearing update object.

| Route family | Traceability analogue | Non-promotion warning |
|---|---|---|
| Laboratory GIE/BMV | instrument, mass, timing, environmental, and readout calibration | entanglement-like statistics remain mediator/candidate underdetermined |
| Gravitational waves | detector response, strain calibration, event selection, waveform code | strong-field GR consistency is not ToE identification |
| DESI/BAO cosmology | survey release, covariance, reconstruction, likelihood, prior bounds | dark-energy pressure is not candidate-native support |
| CMB B-modes | beam/polarization calibration, mapmaking, foreground cleaning, delensing | tensor constraints are many-mechanism pressure |
| Holographic/learned inverse | dictionary/proof/benchmark/simulator lineage | reconstruction is code-subspace and quotient bounded |
| RG / causal-set / amplitudes | regulator, generator, coefficient, proof, or benchmark lineage | formal consistency is not observed-sector recovery |
| Metadata wrappers | provenance and version lineage | custody only; no physical update |
