# Cosmology dark-energy discriminator roadmap

## Purpose

This surface gives the new `R-OQ0057-COSMO-DARK-ENERGY-BAO` row a model home. The row exists because late-time expansion, BAO, supernova, CMB-combination, and growth records can create real pressure on candidate packages. It is capped because those records are many-to-one with respect to final ontology.

## What counts

Cosmology records count as route pressure only when they name:

- the public record object: catalog, likelihood, covariance, maps, selection function, or combined posterior;
- the parameterization or model family used to express the deviation;
- the candidate-native derivation, if any, of that parameterization;
- the nuisance / systematic controls;
- the equivalence quotient under which two packages are observationally same;
- the residual cap.

## Current cap

This is an `S2` constraint corridor. A DESI-style dynamical-dark-energy preference, a later restoration of `Lambda`CDM, or a growth/geometry tension can constrain classes. None of those outcomes by itself identifies a theory of everything, settles quantum gravity, or closes candidate-native public witness debt.

## Failure modes

The two explicit negative controls are:

- `NC-COSMO-PARAMETERIZATION-DECOY` — a phenomenological parameter fit is mistaken for candidate-native support;
- `NC-COSMO-SURVEY-SYSTEMATIC-DECOY` — survey or data-combination dependence is mistaken for new physics.

## Bridge to decision experiments

The relevant decision row is `DX-0004-DESI-LATE-TIME-DARK-ENERGY-DYNAMICS`. Its job is not to predict which cosmological model wins; its job is to say what a robust outcome would be allowed to change.
