# Cross-family identifiability scorecards

## Purpose

This surface summarizes route comparison without replaying every candidate-native docket. The machine-readable source is `CANDIDATE-ROUTE-STATE-LEDGER.json`.

## Scorecard principle

Compare routes by paid denominator fields, not by prestige or ambition. A mathematically rich completion bid may be target-rich and record-poor. A laboratory route may be record-rich and target-poor. A reconstruction route may be inverse-rich inside one package and public-bridge-poor globally.
