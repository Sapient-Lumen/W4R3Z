## Shared rule

Broad ToE credit should be scored in order:
- first by making sure the lane has been classed and cash-tested as the right kind of object,
- then by forcing broad credit to stop at the local-law / cosmological-package / witness-package split,
- then by asking which cosmological-package and witness-package burdens were actually reduced rather than merely rearranged,
- and only then by asking what the current live-family readout really earns.

A good completion-bid comparison is not yet integrated ToE credit.
A local-law payment is not by itself a cosmological-package payment.
A lowered witness-borrowing score is not candidate-native identifiability closure.
A stronger current family readout is not a license to skip the broader burden books.

Stable, restart, and program surfaces should therefore name this router when they only need the archive's established broad-control architecture.
For broader default current-head routing that also needs the smallest current spine, cross-family pressure map, and witness-borrowing score surface, route through `docs/40-model/current-head-control-router.md` instead.
Do not replay the completion-bid credit stack, the three-book split, the vacuum-energy burden router, the witness-package burden router, and the current-family readout router as separate obligations unless the revision is actually changing one of those subordinate surfaces.
