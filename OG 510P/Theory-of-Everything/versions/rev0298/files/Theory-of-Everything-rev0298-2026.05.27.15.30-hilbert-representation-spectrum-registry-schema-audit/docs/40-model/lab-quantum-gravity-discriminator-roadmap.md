# Lab quantum-gravity discriminator roadmap

## Purpose

Low-energy laboratory probes are among the most public-record-rich routes in the archive, but they are not automatically candidate-native ToE identifiers. This surface scores them as discriminator corridors.

## Current discriminator ladder

| Rung | Record object | Positive credit | Standing cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| gravitationally induced entanglement / BMV | entanglement witness, interferometric phase, spin/path correlations | can challenge classical/separable mediator stories under assumptions | owes subsystem independence, nuisance controls, and alternative mediator models |
| mediator dynamics beyond entanglement | fidelity susceptibility, regime-dependent dynamics, simulator/platform signatures | can distinguish mediator regimes more finely than entanglement-only logic | may still be quantum-inspired simulation, not gravity ontology |
| single-graviton access | stimulated absorption or event-like single-quantum signatures | can move from “impossible” rhetoric to low-energy quantum-gravity access | single-click access is weaker than state characterization |
| graviton counting / state statistics | counting probabilities, correlation functions, phase-sensitive tomography | can discriminate radiation-state classes | still not full quantization or ToE closure |
| strong-field gravitational waves | waveform consistency/deviation tests | constrains many candidate deviations in dynamical gravity | many candidates reduce to GR in tested windows |

## Control requirements

A lab route row must carry at least two negative-control classes:

- a **classical/hybrid/decohered mediator** control;
- a **subsystem-independence / locality-assumption** control;
- where relevant, a **nuisance / environmental / simulator-inherited** control;
- where relevant, a **many-to-one observed-sector** control.

## No-overclaim rule

A positive lab result can be a major scientific event and still remain a discriminator-class `S3` pocket. To move toward `S4`, the route would have to define the candidate-native target quotient, public bridge, same-record equivalence, negative controls, and residual cap without borrowing the decisive witness owner from ordinary lab practice.
