# Measure / ensemble / typicality risk taxonomy

Risk classes introduced in rev0286:

- undeclared sample space;
- nonnormalizable or cutoff-order-dependent measure;
- gauge-copy, dual-copy, vacuum-copy, observer-copy, or catalog-copy double counting;
- ensemble average retold as single-theory prediction;
- selected ensemble member retold as typical;
- anthropic/reference-class ambiguity;
- Boltzmann-brain or rare-observer dominance;
- benchmark or survey sampler retold as physical typicality;
- metadata/provenance count retold as physical probability.
