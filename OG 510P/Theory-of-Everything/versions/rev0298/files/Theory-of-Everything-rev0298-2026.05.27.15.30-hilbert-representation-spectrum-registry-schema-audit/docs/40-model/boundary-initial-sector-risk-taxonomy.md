# Boundary / initial-data / sector risk taxonomy

## Main risks

- Fixed-background success retold as background independence.
- AdS or asymptotic-boundary success retold as global quantum-gravity identity.
- Vacuum, compactification, regulator, or truncation choice retold as unique candidate selection.
- Apparatus, catalog, survey, or map-pipeline window retold as global physical support.
- Metadata/package boundary retold as physical boundary support.

## Conservative rule

The support stays inside its declared boundary, initial-data, and sector rows unless a transport, robustness, and rollback-tested export rule says otherwise.
