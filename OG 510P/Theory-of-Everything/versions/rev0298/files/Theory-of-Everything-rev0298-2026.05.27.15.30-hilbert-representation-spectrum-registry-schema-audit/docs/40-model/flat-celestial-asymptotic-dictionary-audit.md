# Flat / celestial asymptotic-dictionary audit

This document keeps one real flat / celestial holography gain from inflating into broader completion or witness-closure rhetoric.
It asks what the archive should count when recent flat-holography work is praised not only for building a better asymptotic dictionary, but for supposedly already supplying a full flat-space holographic theory or a meaningful reduction of witness-package debt.

## Compression verdict

The archive should now hold one tighter sentence:

**inside the asymptotic-access lane, recent flat / celestial holography work earns bounded dictionary credit rather than closure credit. A generalized celestial soft action now reproduces several universal infrared features of four-dimensional gauge and gravitational scattering, and separate Milne-slicing work gives an explicit flat-space holographic dictionary for a free massive spinor. Those are real asymptotic-dictionary gains, but they are still weaker than an independent interacting CCFT, generic local observables, or local witness closure.** `REF-0171`, `REF-0172`

That is not a demotion of the lane.
It is a sharper rule for what current success actually buys.

## What has genuinely improved

### 1. The archive now has one stronger explicit soft-sector flat-holography gain

`REF-0171` constructs a two-dimensional action on the celestial sphere for the universal infrared sector of four-dimensional Abelian gauge theory and gravity.
In archive terms, that matters because the result is not merely another slogan about asymptotic symmetry; it reproduces antipodal matching, leading soft theorems, infrared factorization, and the infrared-finite dressed-state story in one compact asymptotic package. `REF-0171`

That is a real asymptotic-dictionary gain.
The archive should score it above vague celestial rhetoric.

### 2. The lane now has one additional explicit field-content extension beyond scalar-first dictionary talk

`REF-0172` develops a flat-space holographic dictionary for a free massive spinor in four-dimensional Minkowski space using Milne slicing by `H^3`, extracts the renormalized on-shell action as a functional of boundary data, and derives the corresponding celestial two-point structure.
That matters because it widens the lane from scalar- or soft-sector examples toward spin-`1/2` matter, which is a more serious test of whether the dictionary is becoming structurally reusable rather than anecdotal. `REF-0172`

This is still a bounded gain.
But it is broader than one isolated scalar or symmetry example.

### 3. The incompleteness debt is now named more explicitly from inside the lane itself

Both sources sharpen the archive in a second way: they make it easier to say what is still missing.
`REF-0171` states directly that celestial holography still lacks an independent CCFT definition rather than a transform-based definition-by-scattering-use, and `REF-0172` frames its construction as a step toward a systematic flat-space holographic dictionary while leaving interactions, positivity, and fuller celestial completion to future work. `REF-0171`, `REF-0172`

That is useful because the archive prefers explicit incompleteness admission to prestige ambiguity.

## What this does **not** buy

### 1. Not an independent interacting flat-space holographic theory

The stronger current gain is a better asymptotic dictionary for special sectors, not a completed interacting CCFT or a nonperturbative flat-space dual.
The lane still depends on asymptotic control, special sector decomposition, free-field or universal-soft structure, and unresolved completion of the full interacting dictionary. `REF-0171`, `REF-0172`

### 2. Not generic local observable or witness closure

An asymptotic or celestial dictionary can still leave the archive's witness-package burden almost untouched.
The archive still needs local record carriers, stabilization, rereadability, public comparison standards, and objectivity-capable evidence transport in candidate-native language.
Neither source closes that gap. `REF-0171`, `REF-0172`

### 3. Not a completion-bid promotion by itself

This revision does not move flat / celestial holography from a powerful boundary / asymptotic tool lane into a standalone completion bid.
The result is better access-domain control, not a broader settlement of local-law, cosmological-package, or witness-package debt. `REF-0171`, `REF-0172`

## The archive score change

The archive should now keep one smaller distinction explicit inside the asymptotic-access lane:

1. **formal asymptotic rhetoric** — symmetries, transforms, and boundary language are advertised without a reusable concrete model;
2. **bounded asymptotic dictionary** — one special sector or free-field class is encoded by a concrete boundary / celestial construction;
3. **interacting asymptotic theory** — the lane can define and compute a broader interacting boundary theory without simply re-importing bulk amplitudes by definition;
4. **local witness closure** — the lane can explain how persistent local evidence is carried, stabilized, and reread without mainly outsourcing that work to ordinary detector or laboratory language.

Current flat / celestial work now sits more solidly at stage 2.
It does **not** yet reach stages 3 or 4.

## False promotions to reject

1. **"A better celestial action means flat holography is basically solved."**
   - The archive should score the result as a better universal-sector dictionary, not as an independent interacting dual.

2. **"Once the boundary dictionary handles soft physics or free spinors, witness-package debt is mostly gone."**
   - Asymptotic access remains weaker than local record closure.

3. **"A structured asymptotic dictionary is already the same kind of gain as a completion bid."**
   - The lane still lacks broad local-law, cosmological-package, and witness-package closure.

## What would move the archive further

A later revision should score this lane as materially stronger only if one compact package adds most of the following together:
1. an independent interacting CCFT or comparably explicit flat-space boundary theory,
2. clearer bulk-to-boundary control beyond universal soft sectors and free fields,
3. explicit treatment of positivity / inner-product / state-operator structure where needed,
4. a route from asymptotic dictionary success to genuinely local or observer-portable observables,
5. and some nontrivial transport into witness-package reduction rather than pure asymptotic elegance.

Without that package, the archive should preserve the gain as a bounded **flat / celestial asymptotic-dictionary audit** and stop there.

## Net result

The archive now has a better asymptotic-access sentence available:

> flat / celestial holography now has stronger bounded dictionary credit, but asymptotic soft-sector or free-field success is still weaker than an independent interacting dual or local witness closure.

That keeps the lane genuinely strengthened without letting asymptotic elegance overclose the archive. `REF-0171`, `REF-0172`
