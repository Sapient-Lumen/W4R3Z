# Public-record carrier ledger

## Purpose

`PUBLIC-RECORD-CARRIER-LEDGER.json` is the executable list of record carriers used by current `OQ-0057` route rows. It classifies publicness, custody, provenance, replay, challenge routes, versioning, failure modes, and maximum authority credit.

## Current carrier families

The current carrier families are:

- cross-route FAIR / PROV / DataCite-style metadata;
- published theory/proof artifacts;
- holographic reconstruction benchmarks;
- computational code/data benchmarks;
- GIE/BMV laboratory custody packages;
- single-graviton / graviton-counting laboratory or detector catalogs;
- GWOSC-style strain and event catalogs;
- DESI-style BAO likelihood and chain products;
- CMB map/likelihood archives;
- HEPData-style high-energy physics tables;
- frame-transport equivalence assays.

## Non-promotion rule

A public carrier can make evidence more inspectable, citable, replayable, or challengeable. It does not make the target candidate identifiable unless the route denominator, acquisition protocol, negative controls, empirical deltas, promotion gates, observed-sector obligations, and claim binding all allow the stronger wording.

See `docs/30-program/record-carrier-and-acquisition-summary.generated.md` for the generated current readout.
