# Claim-route binding ledger

## Purpose

`CLAIM-ROUTE-BINDING-LEDGER.json` is the executable control against claim-language drift. It ties high-risk claims and open questions to the exact route rows, public-record carriers, acquisition protocols, and controlling ledgers they may cite.

## Current role

The ledger is not a new source of scientific support. It is an export-control layer: it decides which wording may leave the route ledgers and which wording must be frozen or rolled back when a carrier, protocol, or route field is missing.

## Generated readout

The claim-binding coverage is summarized in `docs/30-program/record-carrier-and-acquisition-summary.generated.md` after `make index`.
