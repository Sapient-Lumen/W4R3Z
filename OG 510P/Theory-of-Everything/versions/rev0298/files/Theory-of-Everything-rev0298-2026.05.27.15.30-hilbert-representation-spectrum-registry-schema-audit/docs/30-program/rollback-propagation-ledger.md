# Rollback propagation ledger

Executable source: `ROLLBACK-PROPAGATION-LEDGER.json`.

Rollback rows answer five questions:

1. Which defeater fired?
2. Which routes, claims/open questions, carriers, and protocols are affected?
3. What floor or quarantine state applies immediately?
4. What public wording must change?
5. What repair conditions are required before re-entry?

Rollback is not chronological undo. It is dependency-directed recomputation from surviving support.
