# Epistemic defeater ledger

Executable source: `EPISTEMIC-DEFEATER-LEDGER.json`.

Use this ledger when a new result, correction, replay failure, quotient map, public-data revision, benchmark audit, or observed-sector conflict threatens already-spent support. The ledger distinguishes **what kind of support is defeated** rather than treating all negative news as a generic caveat.

Each row states the target scope, affected routes, carriers, protocols, claims/open questions, negative controls, trigger condition, minimum evidence to activate, artifacts needed for adjudication, propagation action, rollback rule ids, and source anchors.

A defeater can downgrade or quarantine authority. It does not prove a rival candidate.
