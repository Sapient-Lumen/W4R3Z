# Research frontiers

## RF-01 — QFT on fixed background vs dynamical geometry
Known local quantum theory works astonishingly well, but gravity makes spacetime itself dynamical. Any bridge must say how local field structure survives or emerges when background structure becomes part of the dynamics.
