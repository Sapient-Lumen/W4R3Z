# Acquisition-protocol ledger

## Purpose

`ACQUISITION-PROTOCOL-LEDGER.json` tells the archive what must happen for a record carrier to count as acquired, replayable, challengeable, or only forecast-public.

## Current protocol classes

The current protocols cover proof replay, holographic decoder replay, learned inverse benchmarks, GIE/BMV custody, single-graviton trigger correlation, gravitational-wave catalog reanalysis, DESI likelihood replay, CMB polarization likelihood replay, HEPData/EFT coefficient tracing, frame-transport assays, RG/truncation replay, causal-set simulation replay, and observed-sector phenomenology crosswalks.

## Stop rule

No acquisition protocol may raise a route above its own `maximum_route_effect`. A stronger-than-expected result must be re-entered as an empirical delta and then pass the route row, controls, gates, observed-sector obligations, and claim binding.
