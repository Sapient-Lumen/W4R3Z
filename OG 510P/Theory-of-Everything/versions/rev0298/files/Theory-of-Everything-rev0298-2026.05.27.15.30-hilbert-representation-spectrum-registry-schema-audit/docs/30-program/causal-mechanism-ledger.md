# Causal mechanism ledger

Human mirror for `CAUSAL-MECHANISM-LEDGER.json`.

The machine ledger owns the live rows. This surface explains the columns: mechanism id, route id, evidence units, contrast classes, measurement and validity handles, mechanism class, causal question, proposed structure, manipulability gap, common-cause/confounding controls, invariance test, maximum authority effect, defeaters, rollback rules, and sources.

Mechanism rows do not promote routes. They only determine whether mechanism language is admissible at the route's existing ceiling.
