# Empirical delta roadmap

## Purpose

The archive needs to separate empirical pressure from candidate-native identification. `EMPIRICAL-DELTA-LEDGER.json` records public or potentially public evidence deltas and their route effects.

## Current empirical-delta classes

| Delta class | What it can improve | What it cannot do alone |
|---|---|---|
| GIE / BMV entanglement-style tests | mediator-class discrimination, subsystem-assumption pressure, lab record acquisition | identify a full ToE candidate or prove a unique mediator ontology |
| Mediator-dynamics probes beyond entanglement | distinguish heavy/light, classical/hybrid/decohered, or quantum-inspired mediator regimes | erase target quotient, subsystem, or public-bridge debt |
| Subregion / edge-mode / dressed-observable work | classify witness objects and local-publicness obstructions | close public witness ownership by itself |
| Strong-field GW catalog tests | constrain deviations in dynamical GR regimes | identify quantum gravity or final ontology when many candidates reduce to GR |
| Duality / underdetermination pressure | prevent presentation-level uniqueness rhetoric | settle candidate inequivalence without public experiment and quotient |
| Late-time cosmology / dark-energy records | constrain expansion / growth candidate packages | identify final ontology or bypass parameterization/systematics debt |
| Primordial tensor / B-mode forecasts and records | constrain early-universe package classes | identify quantum-gravity ontology through one tensor likelihood |

## Roadmap rule

Empirical contact is necessary but not sufficient. A record-rich result can still be target-poor; a target-rich formal result can still be record-poor. Promotion occurs only where both sides meet through a denominator and the remaining residual deficiency is capped.

## Near-term discriminator menu

The most useful empirical moves are not generic “more data” moves. They are moves that separate live decoys:

1. GIE plus subsystem-independence disclosure and classical/hybrid mediator controls.
2. Mediator dynamics beyond entanglement-only witnesses.
3. Graviton counting/statistical probes that distinguish radiation-state classes.
4. Strong-field gravitational-wave tests whose predicted deviation is candidate-specific rather than generic EFT drift.
5. Publicly replayable null results with declared no-verdict thresholds.
6. Forecast-to-record outcome rows for LISA, CMB-S4, DESI-style cosmology, and Family-C reconstruction benchmarks.

None of those moves is currently enough for `S4`. Together they can shrink residual caps and upgrade specific discriminator rows.
