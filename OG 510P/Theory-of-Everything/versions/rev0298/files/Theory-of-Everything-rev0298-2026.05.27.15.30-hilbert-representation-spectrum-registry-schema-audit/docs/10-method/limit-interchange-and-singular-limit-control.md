# Limit-interchange and singular-limit control

`LIMIT-INTERCHANGE-LEDGER.json` blocks exact-limit support from being exported into a finite regime without a declared bridge.

Every route that uses limiting language must name the limit sequence: continuum, thermodynamic, infinite-volume, large-N, semiclassical, weak/strong coupling, regulator removal, low-energy, long-time, zero-noise, delensing/foreground removal, or public-record replay.

The row then asks three questions:

1. Do the relevant limits commute?
2. Is the limit singular?
3. What finite-regime recovery or error bound carries the support back to the target?

When those questions are unanswered, the result may remain mathematically or heuristically useful, but it cannot spend finite-target, observed-sector, or candidate-native closure language.
