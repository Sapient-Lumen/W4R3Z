# Classical GR recovery discipline

Classical GR recovery is split into three noncompensatory denominators:

1. equivalence-principle / metric-coupling recovery;
2. weak-field, Newtonian-limit, and PPN recovery;
3. gravitational-radiation, radiation-reaction, waveform, and propagation recovery.

A route can constrain one corridor without recovering the others. Equivalence-principle agreement does not prove PPN closure; PPN agreement does not prove radiative dynamics; radiative consistency does not prove observed-sector or candidate-native identity.

The executable surfaces are:

- `EQUIVALENCE-PRINCIPLE-LEDGER.json`
- `WEAK-FIELD-PPN-LEDGER.json`
- `GRAVITATIONAL-RADIATION-LEDGER.json`
- `docs/30-program/classical-gr-recovery-summary.generated.md`

## Non-promotion rule

Classical-GR rows can cap, freeze, demote, or roll back route wording. They cannot promote a route beyond its state-machine ceiling, observed-sector obligations, public-record custody, matter-sector rows, stress-energy/backreaction rows, black-hole/cosmology/singularity rows, or residual caps.
