# Observed-sector recovery burden

## Purpose

`OBSERVED-SECTOR-RECOVERY-LEDGER.json` states the recovery obligations that any `S4` / `S5` candidate-native-identifiability claim would have to face. This is not a demand that every route solve every sector before earning local credit. It is a rule against retelling a local or bounded success as a theory-of-everything closure.

## Core distinction

Observed-sector recovery is **mandatory but not identifying**.

A candidate can fail by not recovering classical gravity, quantum/EFT behavior, matter content, cosmology, black-hole thermodynamics, low-energy quantum-gravity records, or public witness custody. But satisfying one of those sectors does not identify the candidate by itself.

## Current obligation rows

The ledger currently tracks:

- `OSR-CLASSICAL-GR`
- `OSR-QM-QFT`
- `OSR-STANDARD-MODEL-MATTER`
- `OSR-COSMOLOGY-VACUUM`
- `OSR-BLACK-HOLE-THERMO`
- `OSR-LOW-ENERGY-QG-DISCRIMINATORS`
- `OSR-PUBLIC-RECORD-CUSTODY`

No row is closed for `S5` use. Several rows have strong constraint or partial-recovery material, but the archive treats those as burdens and corridors, not as candidate identity.

## Practical rule

When a route claims `S4`, the relevant observed-sector obligations must either be satisfied at the claimed regime or explicitly declared out of scope with a residual cap. When a route claims `S5`, the relevant obligations cannot be out of scope.
