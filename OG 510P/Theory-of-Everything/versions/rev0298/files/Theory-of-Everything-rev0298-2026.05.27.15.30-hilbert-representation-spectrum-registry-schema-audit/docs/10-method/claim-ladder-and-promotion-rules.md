# Claim ladder and promotion rules

## Status labels

- `accepted` — archive-level working discipline or baseline constraint.
- `inferred` — strongly suggested by accepted theory or compact source set, but still interpretive.
- `speculative` — live hypothesis not yet earned.
- `adversarial-countermodel` — credible rival or pressure case that tests an accepted story.

## Promotion rules

A claim should be promoted only when it has:
1. a clear canonical statement,
2. a named basis in `REF-####` entries or accepted constraints,
3. a reason it beats nearby alternatives,
4. and an explicit failure or demotion trigger.

## Demotion rules

Demote when a claim:
- loses contact with observables,
- fails low-energy recovery,
- duplicates a stronger claim elsewhere,
- or depends on sources or assumptions no longer trusted.


## Broad-credit stop rules

When a claim proposes a new meta-level split beyond the current theory-facing control stack, promote it only if it:
1. names a distinct inflation step not already covered by the current physics-facing surfaces,
2. changes how at least one live lane would be scored or routed now, and
3. reduces rather than increases mirror-surface bloat once routed canonically.

If those conditions are not met, keep the issue inside an existing surface, a registry note, or an open question instead of adding a new canonical surface.


## Restart-surface parity rules

When a claim changes archive-control policy about whether canon may grow, route, or reopen, promote it only if the human and machine restart surfaces are updated in the same revision.
Otherwise the archive records the rule in canon while leaving the operative handoff stale.

## Restart-mirror family rules

When a claim changes a compact exact `START_HERE.md` restart-mirror family member, promote it only if the corresponding generated family block is refreshed in the same revision and lint coverage remains sufficient to verify it against the canonical source(s) through the shared tooling mirror-spec table rather than a bespoke parity branch or hand-maintained mirror edit.
The current family covers restart tiers, current posture, priority restart open questions, operator warnings, followthrough / standby state, assumption state, rebuild commands, entry surfaces, public head status, current revision delta, and current release identity.
Add a new family member only if the source is compact, continuation-critical, and removes real restart rediscovery rather than replaying another stable surface.
Otherwise the archive bloats control prose faster than continuation quality improves.
