# Cosmological background / vacuum / thermal-history discipline

Purpose: prevent cosmology-looking support from being retold as ToE recovery.

A route must declare three separate denominators before it can use cosmology-recovery language:

1. `COSMOLOGICAL-BACKGROUND-LEDGER.json` — background geometry, expansion history, curvature, redshift domain, survey/detector/source background, and cosmological parameterization.
2. `VACUUM-ENERGY-LEDGER.json` — cosmological constant, dark-energy equation of state, vacuum selection, dark-sector population, naturalness, and renormalization residuals.
3. `THERMAL-HISTORY-LEDGER.json` — inflation, reheating, BBN, recombination, reionization, structure formation, growth history, and source-population timing.

Noncompensation rule: a good background fit does not solve vacuum energy; a dark-energy likelihood does not identify dark matter; a BBN or CMB consistency check does not recover all thermal history; and none of these promote candidate-native identifiability.
