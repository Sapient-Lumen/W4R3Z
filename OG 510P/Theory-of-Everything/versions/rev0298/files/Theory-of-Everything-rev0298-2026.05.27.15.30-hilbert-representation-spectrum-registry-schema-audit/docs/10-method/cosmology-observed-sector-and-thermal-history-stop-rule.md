# Cosmology observed-sector and thermal-history stop rule

`OQ-0086` blocks cosmology language unless the route pays background, vacuum/dark-sector, and thermal-history debts.

Forbidden without current rows:

- Lambda-CDM recovered,
- cosmological constant solved,
- dark energy identified,
- dark matter identified,
- inflation or reheating recovered,
- BBN / thermal history recovered,
- structure formation solved,
- complete cosmology,
- candidate-native cosmology.

Allowed wording is route-local: constraint corridor, background proxy, parameterization pressure, dark-sector residual, thermal-history partial, or public-record update with declared ceiling.
