# Standard Model recovery and flavor stop rule

`OQ-0085` blocks a common overclaim: a candidate recovers some particle-like structure, some coupling-like relation, or some mass-like scale, and that local success is retold as Standard Model recovery.

Before any route may spend Standard Model, chiral matter, Higgs, electroweak symmetry breaking, Yukawa, CKM/PMNS, neutrino, flavor hierarchy, dark-sector, coupling-unification, or matter-coupled-gravity language, it must declare:

1. the spectrum object and representation/chirality/generation rule;
2. the interaction and coupling texture, including basis, scheme, matching, and running assumptions;
3. the mass-generation or hierarchy object, including vacuum/order-parameter and neutrino/flavor residuals;
4. the public record or phenomenology crosswalk that carries the claim;
5. the rollback rule if any of those denominators fail.

The route may still remain useful as a constraint corridor, benchmark, or discriminator. It does not become a full observed-sector recovery row unless `OBSERVED-SECTOR-RECOVERY-LEDGER.json` and promotion gates agree.
