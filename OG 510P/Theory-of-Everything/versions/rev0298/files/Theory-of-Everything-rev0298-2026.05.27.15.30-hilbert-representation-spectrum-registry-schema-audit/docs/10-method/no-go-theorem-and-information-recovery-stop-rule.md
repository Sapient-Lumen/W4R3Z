# No-go theorem and information-recovery stop rule

## Stop rule

No route may use information recovery, Page curve, no-cloning-safe, no-signalling-safe, data-processing-respecting, entropy-accounted, generalized-entropy-complete, holographic-entropy-valid, or information-complete language unless the information-flow, entropy-accounting, and no-go-compliance rows are declared and current.

## What must be explicit

- **No cloning / no broadcasting:** what state or information object would otherwise be duplicated, and why the route does not duplicate it.
- **No signalling:** what channel, mediator, frame map, detector pipeline, or boundary dictionary would otherwise transmit forbidden information.
- **Data processing:** what coarse-graining, public-record map, decoder, likelihood update, or frame transport is being treated as lossy or recoverable.
- **Entropy inequalities:** which entropy, mutual information, relative entropy, generalized entropy, or holographic entropy inequality is actually invoked.
- **Recovery:** what map recovers what information, under what state, quotient, code subspace, sector, or public-record denominator.

## Forbidden compression

Do not compress this layer into unitarity, causality, stability, gauge invariance, or global consistency. Those are adjacent constraints, not substitutes for information accounting.
