# Solution-sector and background-selection control

A candidate route often succeeds in a selected sector: a code subspace, vacuum, compactification, regulator truncation, causal-set phase, amplitude channel, apparatus operating regime, event catalog, frame/gauge convention, survey parameterization, or foreground-cleaning pipeline.

This surface records the archive rule: sector-local success is not global theory support unless rival sectors are named, the selection rule is declared, the boundary and initial-data choices are stable under stress, and rollback is wired.

## Required denominator

Each sector row should name the selected branch, the rule by which it is selected, rival branches that remain live, forbidden inference, maximum authority effect, and rollback handle.
