# Subsystem algebra and factorization discipline

This surface owns `OQ-0083` on the algebraic side. A region label, Hilbert-space symbol, entanglement diagram, detector/source split, catalog partition, survey window, or benchmark split is not a physical subsystem unless the route declares the algebraic object and the factorization rule.

## Rule

```text
local algebra
+ region label
+ Hilbert-space notation
+ public artifact
≠ tensor factor
≠ independent subsystem
≠ local entropy
≠ candidate-native observable
```

A route must declare whether it uses a net of algebras, a type-I approximation, a type-III QFT algebra, a type-II or crossed-product gravitational algebra, an extended-Hilbert-space construction, an approximate split inclusion, an operational detector split, a benchmark split, or no subsystem claim at all.

## Noncompensation

Algebraic locality cannot compensate for missing edge/center control. A factorization rule cannot compensate for missing gauge, constraint, boundary, or topology control. A detector or benchmark partition cannot become independent evidence without the evidence-credit and selection/multiplicity ledgers.

## Metadata demotion

Archive paths, package roots, registry families, and generated-summary columns are custody partitions only. They do not define physical algebras or independent subsystems.
