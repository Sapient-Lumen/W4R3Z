# Formalization coverage and kernel-trust discipline

This surface separates mechanized proof coverage from route authority.

## Coverage cases

- informal derivation only;
- partial formalization;
- kernel-checked fragment;
- proof certificate with independent checker;
- benchmark or statistical specification checked as a formal contract;
- metadata wrapper with no formal-proof effect.

## Trust-base rule

A proof assistant or checker reduces one class of error only inside its trusted base: kernel, axioms, imported libraries, elaborator/tactic reconstruction policy, checker version, proof-certificate format, and translation path. The archive may cite the checked fragment but may not inflate it into full candidate closure.

## Translation rule

A theorem formalized in one system, one notation, or one candidate dictionary does not automatically transport to another proof assistant, physical regime, public-record carrier, duality presentation, gauge/frame choice, or observed-sector target. Transport remains governed by the validity, semantic, and formalization rows.
