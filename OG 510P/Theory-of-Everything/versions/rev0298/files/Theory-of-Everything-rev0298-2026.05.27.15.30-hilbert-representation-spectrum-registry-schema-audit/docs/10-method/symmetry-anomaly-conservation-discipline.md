# Symmetry, anomaly, and conservation discipline

## Purpose

This surface owns the `OQ-0081` denominator. A route may not spend symmetry, generalized-symmetry, exact-global-symmetry, duality-symmetry, anomaly-free, Ward-identity, conserved-current, no-global-symmetry, symmetry-protected, or charge-conservation language merely because a calculation, proof, likelihood, map, or package uses symmetry vocabulary.

## Rule

A route must declare three separate objects:

1. the symmetry realization: what acts, on what objects, and whether the action is exact, approximate, emergent, gauged, explicitly broken, spontaneously broken, higher-form, non-invertible, duality-like, asymptotic, frame-relative, or only conventional;
2. the anomaly / matching condition: what anomaly datum must vanish, match, inflow, be cancelled, or remain residual;
3. the conservation or Ward budget: what current, charge, flux, continuity equation, soft charge, Ward identity, or record-level invariant is actually tracked.

These rows are necessary for the corresponding language but never sufficient for route promotion. They must still pass public-record, observed-sector, gauge/quotient, viability, quantization, information-flow, and residual-cap controls.

## Non-promotion rule

Symmetry can explain structure. Anomaly matching can constrain flows. Conservation laws can protect quantities. None of those facts alone identifies a quantum-gravity candidate, solves observed-sector recovery, or supplies a public-record inverse map.
