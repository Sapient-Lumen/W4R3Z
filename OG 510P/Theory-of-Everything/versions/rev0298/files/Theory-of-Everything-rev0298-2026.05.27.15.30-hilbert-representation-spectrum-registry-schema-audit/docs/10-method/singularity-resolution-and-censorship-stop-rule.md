# Singularity resolution and censorship stop rule

`OQ-0088` blocks singularity resolved, geodesically complete, bounce complete, weak/strong cosmic censorship satisfied, Cauchy development closed, globally hyperbolic, deterministic evolution, and generic predictability wording unless the singularity/censorship ledgers are current.

The stop rule is intentionally nonpromotional. A route can use the rows to cap or demote support. It cannot use them to jump from a local high-curvature calculation to candidate-native identifiability.

## Rollback triggers

Rollback fires if a claimed high-curvature regime leaves its declared cutoff, if a singularity-resolution mechanism only regularizes coordinates or selected observables, if geodesic/invariant evidence is missing, if a Cauchy horizon or naked-singularity counterexample remains live, or if the claim depends on untracked topology, signature, boundary, gauge, matter, or quantization assumptions.
