# Extrapolation-fence and domain-shift control

An extrapolation fence is a negative rule: it names the inference that the archive must not make from a local result.

The common laundering pattern is:

```text
local reconstruction / local experiment / local catalog / local likelihood / local proof
+ plausible theory story
+ public artifact
= global candidate support
```

rev0268 makes that pattern non-spendable. Each fence row declares:

- the route and evidence units affected;
- the domain and transport rows it depends on;
- the forbidden inference;
- the allowed inference;
- the escalation condition required to move beyond the fence;
- the rollback floor if the fence is violated.

## Typical fences

- code-subspace evidence cannot become full bulk or ToE ontology;
- benchmark performance cannot become out-of-distribution candidate identity;
- thermodynamic equation recovery cannot become microscopic ontology;
- string/M atlas structure cannot become unique vacuum selection;
- RG/truncation behavior cannot become regulator-independent prediction;
- causal-set simulation behavior cannot become continuum recovery;
- amplitude/EFT consistency cannot become off-shell ontology;
- GIE/BMV or graviton-trigger results cannot become global quantum-gravity identity;
- gravitational-wave consistency or deviation cannot become ToE identification;
- BAO/CMB likelihoods cannot become dark-energy or primordial ontology without model-class and prior closure;
- metadata cannot become physical evidence.

## Repair rule

Fence repair is not promotion. Repair restores only the bounded target-domain wording actually earned by a fresh carrier, acquisition protocol, measurement model, systematic budget, calibration chain, contrast class, prior stress test, negative controls, and rollback-safe route row.
