# Counterfactual robustness and mechanism transfer

Revision: `rev0269`

Counterfactual robustness is stronger than a positive fit and weaker than candidate-native closure. It asks whether the route relation would survive named interventions, perturbations, model substitutions, ablations, or source-target shifts while preserving the route denominator.

## Counterfactual denominator

A counterfactual row must name:

```text
counterfactual query
mechanism row
intervention or perturbation row
evidence unit
contrast class
transportability row
alternative worlds / model interventions
invariance requirement
failure modes
maximum authority effect
rollback rule
```

The archive should refuse unrestricted possible-worlds rhetoric. Counterfactuals are admitted only when the altered world, structural equation, ablation, model-class substitution, reference-frame transformation, or observational variation is declared.

## Transfer rule

A mechanism that is robust inside one domain does not automatically travel to another domain. Mechanism transfer must still pass:

- validity-domain rows,
- transportability rows,
- extrapolation fences,
- measurement/systematics rows,
- contrast/update/prior rows,
- evidence-credit rows,
- and route-state promotion gates.

## Failure patterns

Common failure patterns:

- observational catalog fit retold as causal mechanism;
- formal consistency retold as intervention evidence;
- simulator ablation retold as physical manipulation;
- natural experiment retold as randomized intervention;
- mediator-class discriminator retold as candidate ontology;
- metadata/provenance retold as mechanism support;
- counterfactual robustness inside a source domain exported across a forbidden extrapolation fence.
