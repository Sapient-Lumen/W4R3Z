# Reporting-bias and survivorship control

Revision: `rev0270`.

A visible literature, benchmark table, survey anomaly, elegant proof, positive lab result, or archive package may overrepresent successes and underrepresent nulls, abandoned candidates, failed replications, rejected priors, unshared benchmarks, discarded cuts, or unsuccessful route variants.

`REPORTING-BIAS-LEDGER.json` records the file-drawer, survivorship, positive-result, leaderboard, and anomaly-selection risks that cap support language.

## Authority rule

```text
published success
+ public citation
+ replayable artifact
≠ unbiased evidence population
```

unless nulls, failed attempts, missing records, and abandoned search paths are represented or explicitly capped.

## Metadata warning

Metadata and provenance can make a package easier to audit. They cannot make the underlying evidence population complete, unbiased, surprising, or discovery-grade.
