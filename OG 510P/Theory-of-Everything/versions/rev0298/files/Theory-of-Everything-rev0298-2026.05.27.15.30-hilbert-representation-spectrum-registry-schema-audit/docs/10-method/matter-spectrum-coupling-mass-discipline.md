# Matter-spectrum, coupling, and mass/hierarchy discipline

Matter-sector language is a high-risk bridge. A route can look impressive because it names particles, reproduces an effective spectrum, fits couplings, invokes a Higgs or symmetry-breaking mechanism, or gestures toward flavor and neutrino masses. None of those moves by itself recovers the observed Standard Model sector.

The executable layer has three denominators:

- `PARTICLE-SPECTRUM-LEDGER.json` — what states, representations, generations, source populations, hidden sectors, or effective excitations are being claimed;
- `INTERACTION-COUPLING-LEDGER.json` — what gauge, Yukawa, Wilson, waveform, source/detector, cosmological, or benchmark coupling is being claimed;
- `MASS-HIERARCHY-LEDGER.json` — what Higgs/EWSB, vacuum, mass-generation, neutrino, threshold, hierarchy, or scale object is being claimed.

The noncompensation rule is strict: spectrum does not imply couplings, couplings do not imply mass generation, mass generation does not solve flavor, and none of them supplies candidate-native ToE identification. This file is governed by `OQ-0085` and remains cap/rollback only.
