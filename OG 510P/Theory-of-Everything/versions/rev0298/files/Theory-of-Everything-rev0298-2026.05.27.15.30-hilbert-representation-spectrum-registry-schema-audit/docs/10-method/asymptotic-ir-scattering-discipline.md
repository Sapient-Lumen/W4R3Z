# Asymptotic / IR / scattering discipline

`OQ-0092` separates three debts that are often collapsed in theory-candidate prose:

1. the state-space debt: what in/out, boundary, finite-time, detector, catalog, simulator, or algebraic state is being used;
2. the infrared debt: what soft sector, memory, dressing, inclusive sum, or charge representation controls divergences and unobserved radiation;
3. the observable debt: what public inclusive rate, memory readout, finite-time event, amplitude, waveform/catalog likelihood, or detector response is actually recorded.

A route may gain local constraint credit from one of these objects, but none of them may promote a route without the other route-support ledgers. In particular, an amplitude is not automatically an observable; an inclusive rate is not automatically candidate identity; and a memory/dressing construction is not automatically public-record closure.
