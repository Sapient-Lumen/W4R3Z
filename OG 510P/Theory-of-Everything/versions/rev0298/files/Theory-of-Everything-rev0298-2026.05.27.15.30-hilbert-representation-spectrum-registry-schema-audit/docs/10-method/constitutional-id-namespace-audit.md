# Constitutional ID namespace audit

The claim and open-question registries are now large enough that namespace drift is a governance risk. `docs/30-program/constitutional-id-namespace-audit.generated.md` is generated from `docs/20-constitution/claim-registry.md` and `docs/20-constitution/open-question-registry.md`.

The audit is not a scientific authority signal. It catches duplicated, missing, or out-of-order `CL-####` and `OQ-####` entries so claim and question rows cannot hide in prose or collide during later route-layer additions.
