# Hilbert-space / representation / spectral-reconstruction discipline

Hilbert spaces, state spaces, algebra representations, Fock sectors, spectra, and spectral densities are route-local denominators. They can make a claim more precise, but they do not by themselves identify a candidate ontology.

The discipline is noncompensatory:

- a Hilbert or state space does not prove representation uniqueness;
- a representation map does not prove spectral completeness;
- a spectrum does not prove geometry, matter, public-record closure, or candidate identity;
- spectral agreement does not defeat isospectral, representation-inequivalent, or quotient-equivalent countermodels.

`OQ-0096` owns this layer. The executable owners are `HILBERT-SPACE-LEDGER.json`, `REPRESENTATION-MAP-LEDGER.json`, and `SPECTRAL-RECONSTRUCTION-LEDGER.json`.
