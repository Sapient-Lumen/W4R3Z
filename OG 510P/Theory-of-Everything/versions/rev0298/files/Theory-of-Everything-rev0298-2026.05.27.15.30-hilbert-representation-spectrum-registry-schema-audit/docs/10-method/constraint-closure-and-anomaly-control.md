# Constraint-closure and anomaly control

This surface owns the executable distinction between a formal constraint statement and a constraint system that actually closes for the route target.

For constrained systems, first-class constraints generate gauge transformations and physical claims require a quotient or invariant object. In quantum or regulated settings the same burden reappears as BRST/BV consistency, Ward identities, anomaly cancellation, regulator compatibility, or proof that reduction and quantization have been related rather than silently exchanged.

`CONSTRAINT-CLOSURE-LEDGER.json` therefore records, for each route, the relevant constraint set or algebra, the local closure status, anomaly or second-class risk, the reduction/quantization order, and rollback handle. A route that has a clean theorem, simulation, likelihood, or benchmark but an unclosed or undeclared constraint algebra remains capped.

Metadata can pass package consistency checks. It cannot close a physical constraint algebra.
