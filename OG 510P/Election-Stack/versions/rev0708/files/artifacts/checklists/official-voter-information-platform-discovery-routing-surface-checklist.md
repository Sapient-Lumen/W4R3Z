# Official voter-information platform discovery-routing-surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if official voter-information media can be encountered first through platform search results, homepage recommendations, browse feeds, or similar discovery-routing surfaces before the media page itself is opened**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which discovery surfaces were reviewed: search-result pages, homepage rows, browse/explore pages, recommendation shelves, followed-channel feeds, or organization video homepages.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from office-curated collection pages, follow/reminder state, autoplay handoffs, and the media page itself.
- [ ] Keep “the platform surfaced this media” separate from “the office designated this as the complete current official answer.”

## Search, ranking, and personalization review

- [ ] Review whether ranking, sorting, filters, watch/search history, or other platform signals can materially change which official media appears first.
- [ ] Review whether discovery can mix official media with nonofficial or neighboring content in ways that blur authority.
- [ ] Do not talk as though “search for our video/channel” is one stable reviewed path unless the real discovery variants were actually reviewed.

## Card / row / feed presentation review

- [ ] Review whether titles, thumbnails, view counts, upload age, live-event labels, or replay cues overstate currentness at card level.
- [ ] Review whether older, upcoming, replay, or historical artifacts can surface prominently beside the current official item.
- [ ] Keep discovery prominence from standing in for explicit freshness, review, or official-routing status.

## Downstream-surface routing review

- [ ] Review which downstream surfaces discovery can send voters into: published recordings, clips, collection pages, upcoming-event shells, or replay pages.
- [ ] Keep the upstream discovery surface distinct from the downstream media surface it opens.
- [ ] Preserve enough review notes that later observers can reconstruct how the voter first got there, not only what page eventually opened.

## Recovery and current-state review

- [ ] Keep the current written page, notice, FAQ/help entry, or named office contact recoverable once a voter lands through platform discovery.
- [ ] Re-review discovery surfaces when election-cycle turnover, replay availability, or new scheduled events can change what looks “current.”
- [ ] Keep mixed-source recommendations, feeds, or browse pages from becoming the practical current-answer lane.

## Authority-boundary review

- [ ] Treat search results, homepage rows, and browse feeds as discovery/routing context rather than proof that the surfaced media is the controlling operational answer.
- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed discovery surfaces, ranking/personalization notes, recovery anchors, and last review time rather than private watch histories, search logs, or recommendation telemetry.
- [ ] Keep office-curated routing (`507`) distinct from platform-ranked routing (`510`) even when both expose the same official media.
