# Official voter-information browser-generated image-description surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if a browser or platform generates descriptions for unlabeled images on the already-open official page**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which public routes were reviewed for browser-generated image-description behavior.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from authored screen-reader semantics, OCR/text extraction, reader mode, and page-audio narration.
- [ ] Keep “browser generated a descriptive caption” separate from “the office published reviewed alt text or explanatory copy.”

## High-risk image review

- [ ] Identify images whose meaning can change what a voter does next: maps, photos, diagrams, ID examples, envelope examples, icons, legends, or directional graphics.
- [ ] Check whether those images have durable authored text or trustworthy text alternatives nearby.
- [ ] Do not let decisive meaning live only in a browser-generated guess about an unlabeled image.

## Availability and recovery review

- [ ] Review what happens when generated image-description features are disabled, unsupported, or unavailable.
- [ ] Treat browser/platform-generated descriptions as optional accessibility aids rather than required delivery channels.
- [ ] Keep a visible recovery path to the current official help lane when an image's meaning is ambiguous or high risk.

## Authority-boundary review

- [ ] Make sure office identity, scope, and current-help routing stay legible in authored page text, not just in or around the image.
- [ ] Keep generated descriptions visibly subordinate to the current official page/help lane.
- [ ] Avoid treating inferred captions as reviewed official explanatory text or trustworthy portable records.

## Privacy and evidence review

- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed routes, image classes, recovery/help anchors, and last review time.
- [ ] Do not preserve individualized generated captions, user assistive-technology fingerprints, or raw screenshot collections merely to prove the posture was reviewed.
- [ ] Keep any future idea of standardized generated-caption provenance or cross-browser reconstruction in quarantine until trustworthy hooks actually exist.
