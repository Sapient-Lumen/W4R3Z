# Official voter-information reader-mode surface checklist

Use this quickcheck when an election office expects the public to read official voter information through browser Reader View, simplified print preview, or other extracted-main-content presentations.

## Inventory and scope

- Identify routes where voters may rely on browser/app simplified reading views, not just the ordinary live page.
- Distinguish this from print/PDF fidelity, language translation, and return-state freshness.
- Re-check routes where a simplified view could drop the office identity, deadline qualifier, help lane, or action boundary.

## Extracted-content integrity

- Review whether the controlling answer survives inside the extractable main content.
- Check whether office identity, jurisdiction/scope, last-updated or as-of cues, and the critical condition/exception remain visible.
- Do not place the only controlling facts in removable banners, sidebars, headers, or footer chrome.

## Action boundary

- Decide whether the simplified surface is reader-safe, summary-only, or full-view-required.
- If the simplified surface is not action-complete, say so clearly in the main content and point to the full official page or help lane.
- Do not let a calm simplified view masquerade as the complete operative route when key controls or qualifiers did not survive extraction.

## Reader classes

- Review browser Reader View, simplified print preview, embedded/in-app reader presentation, and extraction-unavailable or partial fallback separately.
- Preserve which classes were checked and what posture controlled each.
- Do not assume one successful reader-mode path proves every simplified presentation is safe.

## Evidence posture

- Preserve reviewed route labels, checked reader/simplified classes, must-survive facts, reader-safe vs summary-only vs full-view-required posture, return/help cue, and last review time.
- Do not preserve per-user reader-mode telemetry, browser-extension inventories, named-user reading histories, or individualized simplified-view traces when bounded policy reconstruction is sufficient.
