# Official voter-information platform registration / invite-gate surface checklist

Use this checklist when official voter-information media is fronted by a registration form, invite-only join link, approval workflow, membership gate, or similar audience-selection shell.

## Scope

- [ ] Reviewed the exact pre-access gate the public encounters, not just the media object behind it.
- [ ] Distinguished reminder registration from actual watch/join gating.
- [ ] Recorded which route, audience segment, and device/app contexts were materially relied on.

## Gate review

- [ ] Verified whether the route was public, registration-required, invited-only, approval-gated, members-only, private/unlisted, or mixed.
- [ ] Verified whether discovery without entry was possible on platform search, feeds, subscriptions, or shared links.
- [ ] Verified whether the current written/help lane remained recoverable for viewers who could not satisfy the gate.

## Delivery and device review

- [ ] Checked whether invite email, approval, sign-in, or membership state was required to enter.
- [ ] Checked whether browser/app differences changed whether the gate was visible or usable.
- [ ] Logged any route-specific limitations that could affect public understanding or accessibility.

## Failure and fallback posture

- [ ] Verified the office did not let a failed gate sound like event cancellation or the whole official answer.
- [ ] Verified there was a legible fallback or contact path for non-admitted viewers.
- [ ] Logged whether gated-entry behavior differed across native, embedded, shared-link, or inbox contexts.

## Public proof posture

- [ ] Logged the gate type, affected route, and when the gate review occurred.
- [ ] Avoided publishing attendee rosters, payment state, individualized registration responses, or private platform-admin traces.
