# Official voter-information community-partner distribution checklist

- Inventory the partner channels the office expects voters to rely on for relayed official information.
- Make the authority boundary explicit: the partner is relaying or hosting information; the election office and linked official destination control the answer.
- Identify which partner classes may distribute unchanged official assets, co-branded assets, or approved relay copy.
- Keep the official source, scope, and help route visible enough that a shared copy still leaves the voter a safe next step.
- Require a canonical official page, notice, FAQ/help article, or office-help route for action-changing partner-relayed materials.
- Do not let partner channels publish partner-only rule changes for deadlines, locations, registration windows, ballot paths, or incident procedures.
- Define the co-branding boundary so partner branding does not displace the authoritative election office.
- Prefer unchanged official assets for action-changing guidance unless bounded tailoring is truly needed for the audience.
- If tailoring is allowed, preserve the same current official answer across language, accessibility, and audience-fit variants.
- Keep crucial action-changing content out of inaccessible image-only assets when the partner channel is expected to move voters toward action.
- Publish an explicit refresh, withdrawal, or superseding path when a partner-carried artifact no longer controls.
- Tell partners when stale assets should be replaced, removed, or marked stale, especially near deadlines, location changes, or incidents.
- Keep the partner lane aligned with the office's current page, FAQ/help article, hotline packet, print/download artifact, and alert surfaces covering the same answer.
- Preserve a bounded record of approved asset families and material withdrawals without defaulting to exhaustive partner rosters or private contact lists.
- Publish `last_verified_at` for the partner-distribution surface and re-check it after deadline, location, incident, or partner-program changes.
