# Official voter-information page-context AI surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if a browser-integrated page summary, selected-text summarize/explain action, or page-context AI sidebar compresses the currently open official page**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which public routes were reviewed for browser-integrated page-summary or selected-text AI behavior.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from off-platform AI answers, reader mode, text-fragment highlights, and browser find-in-page.
- [ ] Keep same-page summary failures explicit instead of burying them inside generic “AI,” content, or browser residue.

## Current-page context review

- [ ] Check whether office identity, jurisdiction scope, election/date scope, and current-state cues remain near the decisive answer on the page itself.
- [ ] Review whether the current official help lane stays visible enough that a summary remains subordinate to the page.
- [ ] Avoid designs where the browser-generated sidebar summary looks like the real answer and the page looks like decorative backing.

## Selected-text qualifier-loss review

- [ ] Review likely excerpts a voter could highlight or summarize out of context.
- [ ] Check whether important exceptions, routing notes, or cure/help qualifiers live close enough to the operative sentence to survive compression.
- [ ] If the answer is not safely compressible, make the page say so plainly and route the voter to the current office/help lane.

## Feature-availability honesty review

- [ ] Do not assume page-summary / page-context AI features are universal across browsers, platforms, languages, regions, account states, or enterprise settings.
- [ ] Keep the authoritative answer usable even when the summary feature is absent, blocked, or disabled.
- [ ] Do not preserve individualized prompts, transcripts, or page-summary telemetry merely to prove the posture was reviewed.

## High-risk routing and evidence review

- [ ] Review volatile or local-fact-dependent topics for safe routing back to `305` ordinary help or `307` rights/safety escalation.
- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed routes, summary contexts, and last review time.
- [ ] Keep any future idea of browser summary provenance or transcript auditing in quarantine until standardized, trustworthy hooks actually exist.
