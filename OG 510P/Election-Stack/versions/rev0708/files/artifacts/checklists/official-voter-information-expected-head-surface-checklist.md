# Official voter-information expected-head surface checklist

Use this quickcheck when an election office needs a bounded way to keep preserved or resumed progress from implying that the voter is still acting under the same governing public basis.

## Inventory and review scope

- Identify public routes that may span enough time or state that the governing public basis can change between start, resume, and acceptance.
- Distinguish this from generic queue/local-state honesty, generic timeout recovery, or generic citation-head review alone.
- Re-check routes where resumed drafts, restored steps, queued sends, or long-lived form packets can survive beyond a material official update.

## Expected-head honesty

- Keep the previously reviewed basis and the current controlling basis visibly distinct when a material change can alter the next action.
- Do not treat same URL, same local draft, same restored page instance, or same page shell as proof that the same governing reviewed basis still controls.
- Distinguish cosmetic edits from material basis changes that affect deadline semantics, eligibility, destination office, required instructions, or acceptance conditions.
- Require visible re-review, bounded restart, or authoritative help when the earlier reviewed basis no longer safely governs completion.

## Resume, queue, and confirmation behavior

- Ensure resumed or restored state tells the voter whether the earlier reviewed basis still controls.
- If a request may queue before later send, keep queue truthfulness and expected-head truthfulness separate so preserved state does not imply unchanged authority.
- Preserve user-entered data when practical without implying that preserved data means preserved governing basis.
- Ensure durable confirmations or receipts name the actual controlling head or edition when that distinction matters later.

## Accessibility and reviewability

- Keep expected-head / re-review cues available in durable accessible text rather than only in color, icons, or transient notices.
- Ensure restart, re-review, and authoritative-help paths remain understandable on mobile, at zoom, with keyboard navigation, and with screen readers.
- Test return/resume behavior in project context instead of assuming continuity alone is safe.
- Verify that basis-drift handling does not silently bury the reason a restart or re-review was required.

## Evidence posture

- Preserve only route labels, expected-head classes, material-change policy posture, re-review/restart posture, resumed-basis honesty posture, confirmation-basis naming posture, and last review time.
- Do not preserve per-user resume timelines, raw draft diff logs, queue internal event histories, or individualized replay traces when bounded policy reconstruction is sufficient.
