# Official voter-information off-platform AI answer surface checklist

- Inventory the public AI-answer entrypoints most likely to summarize official voter information before click-through.
- Make the authority boundary explicit: the AI answer layer may assist discovery, but the current official page, notice, or office/help route remains controlling.
- Identify the official pages most likely to be cited for action-changing topics and keep those pages clearly official, clearly scoped, and easy to recognize quickly.
- Ensure cited pages make jurisdiction or office identity and page purpose legible without requiring deep scrolling.
- Add visible current-state cues for volatile topics when omission would let a plausible summary look current after the fact changes.
- Keep office/help recovery obvious for address-specific, district-specific, record-specific, or rights-sensitive questions.
- Prefer direct current-cycle or current-election landing pages over generic timeless prose for deadlines, hours, and location-sensitive topics.
- Recover stale or ambiguous AI summaries through clear current notices, redirects, or help-rich landing pages rather than assuming the summary will always be accurate.
- Do not rely on opaque vendor-specific prompt hacks as the main safety control; keep the underlying official pages citation-ready instead.
- Re-check AI-answer handoff after major template, content-structure, URL, localization, emergency-notice, or election-rollover changes.
- Preserve a bounded handoff trace: page-class refs, policy version, current-state cue expectations, fallback route, and verification timestamp.
- Minimize telemetry: do not collect individualized third-party AI usage data when bounded reconstruction of the official handoff policy is enough.
