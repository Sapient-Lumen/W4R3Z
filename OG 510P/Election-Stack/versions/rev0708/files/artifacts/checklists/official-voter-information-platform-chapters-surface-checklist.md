# Official voter-information platform chapter-surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if a player or platform exposes official media through chapter markers, key moments, or a shareable/copyable chapter list**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which public recordings, embeds, clips, or audio-first routes were reviewed for chapter-marker or key-moment behavior.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from the recording lane itself, from transcript panes/search, and from ordinary page-level copy/share controls.
- [ ] Keep “the platform exposed titled sections” separate from “the office published and reviewed this exact stand-alone wording as current official guidance.”

## Chapter provenance review

- [ ] Review whether chapter labels were office-authored/reviewed, platform-generated, or mixed.
- [ ] Keep any office-reviewed summary, FAQ, or transcript edition easy to identify and recover when one exists.
- [ ] Do not let platform-generated chapter labels or key moments quietly stand in for reviewed official policy headings.

## Navigation, discovery, and sharing review

- [ ] Review seek-bar chapter markers, chapter panels/tables of contents, and jump-to-section behavior for qualifier loss on action-changing recordings.
- [ ] Review whether chapter labels or key moments surface in search/discovery routes and keep those routes subordinate to the current written destination/help lane.
- [ ] Review whether a single chapter can be shared or a chapter list copied, and keep that distinct from an office-issued portable publication.

## Recovery and current-state review

- [ ] Keep recording-date, scope, and still-current cues near the recording or adjacent page context.
- [ ] Maintain a durable written recovery path to the current official page, notice, FAQ/help entry, or office lane when a chapter title is too coarse, stale, or overconfident.
- [ ] Treat volatile or corrected topics as requiring written recovery rather than chapter-title confidence alone.

## Authority-boundary review

- [ ] Keep chapter markers, key moments, and chapter lists visibly subordinate to the official recording and the named help lane.
- [ ] Avoid treating shared chapter links or copied chapter lists as current official portable records by default.
- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed routes, chapter contexts, recovery anchors, and last review time rather than user-specific watch history or seek logs.
