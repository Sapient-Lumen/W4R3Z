# Official voter-information platform social-layer surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if already-open official media also exposes comments, live chat, Q&A, polls, or reactions on the player or event page**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which public recordings, watch pages, embeds, or live-event pages were reviewed for comments/chat/Q&A/polls/reactions.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from the recording lane itself, from follow/reminder state, from clips, and from player-native AI answer modules.
- [ ] Keep “the platform exposed an interaction layer” separate from “the office reviewed and adopted that interaction as current official guidance.”

## Elevated-interaction review

- [ ] Review whether the platform can pin, feature, approve, filter, summarize, or otherwise elevate some comments, questions, or chat fragments above others.
- [ ] Review whether Top Chat, pinned comments, approved Q&A cards, featured comments, or visible reaction counts can make one fragment look like the settled answer.
- [ ] Do not let organizer moderation, platform filtering, or social proof imply reviewed authority, unchanged currentness, or complete jurisdictional fit by default.

## Replay, copy, and portability review

- [ ] Review whether comments can be linked directly, copied, highlighted, downloaded, or exported.
- [ ] Review whether live chat, Q&A, or poll behavior survives into replay and whether replay preserves enough surrounding context to remain subordinate to the current written lane.
- [ ] Treat copied comments, replay snippets, and exported chat/comment artifacts as context-limited conversation fragments rather than automatic official records.

## High-risk-topic recovery review

- [ ] Re-review routes covering deadlines, locations, ID rules, accommodations, absentee steps, or other topics that can change faster than the media ages.
- [ ] Keep the current written page, reviewed FAQ/help entry, or named office contact recoverable when interaction becomes ambiguous, stale, contradictory, or overconfident.
- [ ] Prefer explicit recheck-the-current-route recovery over product copy that makes comments/chat/Q&A feel like the help desk itself.

## Evidence-minimization and moderation review

- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed routes, elevated-interaction states, replay/copy posture, recovery anchors, and last review time rather than per-user comment histories, moderation logs, or reaction telemetry.
- [ ] Review whether anonymous questions, subscriber-only modes, or blocked/archived states change what viewers can infer from what remains visible.
- [ ] Re-review routes whenever platform comment, chat, Q&A, poll, or reaction posture changes materially.
