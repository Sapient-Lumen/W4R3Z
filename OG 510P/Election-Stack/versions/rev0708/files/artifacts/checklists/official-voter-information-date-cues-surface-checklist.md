# Official voter-information date-cues and byline checklist

Use this quickcheck when official voter-information pages need trustworthy publication/update dates, search-result byline cues, or clear separation between page freshness and election-event dates.

## Before changing date cues

- Confirm the page is still the right current official destination for the underlying voter question.
- Confirm the office/help fallback remains visible and actionable if the page is superseded or historical.
- Confirm the page class is understood: current operational notice, evergreen help page, event/deadline page, or historical/archive page.
- Confirm the date cue you are changing is meant to describe the page freshness, not the election event itself.

## Visible freshness-cue discipline

- Use a labeled visible freshness cue such as Published or Last updated on volatile public-answer pages.
- Do not rely on unlabeled footer timestamps or generic CMS chrome as the only freshness signal.
- Re-check pages that show multiple dates near the headline, especially deadline pages and archive pages.
- Keep superseded or historical pages explicitly labeled so they do not look newly operative.

## Machine-readable date discipline

- Keep visible and machine-readable freshness cues consistent.
- Provide correct time and timezone in machine-readable fields when same-day precision materially matters.
- Do not use future dates or the date of the election event as the page freshness date.
- Keep page freshness dates distinct from event start/end dates on event-like pages.

## Meaningful-update discipline

- Change a visible Last updated cue only when the public answer changed materially.
- Do not let footer, theme, analytics, or migration churn create a misleading fresh-looking operational page.
- Re-check archive pages after CMS migrations, template changes, and embedded-media refreshes.

## Verification and evidence posture

- Preserve only the bounded trace needed to reconstruct page classes, chosen date-signal states, page-vs-event date separation, archive posture, and verification time.
- Do not retain private webmaster exports, individualized search telemetry, or operator screenshots when bounded public-policy reconstruction is sufficient.
