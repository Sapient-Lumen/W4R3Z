# Official voter-information platform alternate-audio surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if already-open official media can also be heard through alternate audio tracks such as original audio, dubbed audio, descriptive audio, or commentary**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which public recordings, watch pages, embeds, or official channel routes were reviewed for alternate-audio behavior.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from site language selectors, caption/subtitle layers, saved-media replay, and AI-over-video answer modules.
- [ ] Keep “the platform exposed another spoken track” separate from “the office reviewed and adopted that exact spoken wording as the current controlling official answer.”

## Track provenance review

- [ ] Review whether the available tracks are original audio, creator-uploaded dubs, automatic dubs, descriptive audio, commentary, or a mix.
- [ ] Review whether the player labels tracks clearly enough that the voter can tell what kind of spoken layer they selected.
- [ ] Do not let auto-generated or commentary-like spoken tracks quietly imply reviewed authority, unchanged currentness, or complete jurisdictional fit by default.

## Language, switching, and persistence review

- [ ] Review whether viewers can switch tracks in player settings and whether account/device language preferences can change what they hear on revisits.
- [ ] Review whether alternate spoken tracks exist in languages for which the office has or has not published a reviewed translated/help lane.
- [ ] Keep the source-language recording date, scope, and current written recovery route easy to recover after an audio-track switch.

## High-risk-topic recovery review

- [ ] Re-review routes covering deadlines, locations, ID rules, accommodations, absentee steps, or other topics that can change faster than media tracks age.
- [ ] Keep the current written page, reviewed FAQ/help entry, or named office contact recoverable when alternate spoken tracks are partial, stale, or doubtful.
- [ ] Prefer explicit recheck-the-current-route recovery over product copy that makes the selected audio track sound like the settled answer by itself.

## Evidence-minimization and portability review

- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed routes, track classes, recovery anchors, and last review time rather than per-user language preferences, watch history, or dubbing telemetry.
- [ ] Treat audio-track switching as playback state rather than proof that a durable current translated edition exists.
- [ ] Re-review routes whenever platform dubbing posture, audio-track availability, or translated/help-lane review state changes materially.
