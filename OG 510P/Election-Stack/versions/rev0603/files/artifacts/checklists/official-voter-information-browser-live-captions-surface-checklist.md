# Official voter-information browser-integrated live-captions surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if a browser or platform generates captions or translated subtitles for already-open official media**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which public recordings, embeds, clips, or audio-first routes were reviewed for generated-caption behavior.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from office-published captions/transcripts, page-text translation, page-text narration, and generated image descriptions.
- [ ] Keep “the browser/platform generated caption text” separate from “the office published and reviewed this exact transcript or subtitle text.”

## Generated-caption and translation review

- [ ] Review same-language generated captions for qualifier loss, speaker-loss, or timing ambiguity on action-changing recordings.
- [ ] Review translated subtitle behavior separately from same-language captions.
- [ ] Do not let translated generated subtitles stand in for a reviewed official multilingual publication when the office has not actually published one.

## Recovery and current-state review

- [ ] Keep visible recording-date, scope, and still-current cues near the media or its adjacent page context.
- [ ] Maintain a durable written recovery path to the current official page, notice, FAQ/help entry, or office lane when generated captions are wrong, absent, delayed, or ambiguous.
- [ ] Treat volatile or corrected topics as requiring written recovery rather than caption confidence alone.

## Privacy and portable-record review

- [ ] Review whether the platform allows copied, retained, or exported caption text, and keep that distinct from an office-issued transcript edition.
- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed routes, caption contexts, recovery anchors, and last review time.
- [ ] Do not preserve individualized generated caption transcripts, microphone captures, call-caption logs, or device/accessibility fingerprints merely to prove the posture was reviewed.

## Authority-boundary review

- [ ] Keep generated captions and translated subtitles visibly subordinate to the official recording, any office-reviewed transcript/caption, and the named help lane.
- [ ] Avoid treating copied/generated caption text as a current official portable record by default.
- [ ] State honestly that availability, privacy posture, and language support vary by browser, platform, device, and settings.
