# Official voter-information platform playback-quality surface checklist

Use this checklist when official voter-information media may remain playable while the practical visual detail changes because of auto/adaptive quality, data-saver modes, embed defaults, or manual resolution selection.

## Scope

- [ ] Reviewed the actual quality states in scope (auto/adaptive, manual lower-quality selection, data-saver, fixed embed default, capability-limited player).
- [ ] Distinguished playback-quality variation from the underlying current written/help lane.
- [ ] Recorded which source pages, embeds, or partner wrappers were materially relied on.

## Quality-state review

- [ ] Verified the same official media was checked in the fidelity states most likely to matter publicly.
- [ ] Verified quality-selection or auto-quality behavior does not overstate how much decisive visual detail a viewer actually receives.
- [ ] Verified older-device or wrapper-specific limits on quality controls were reviewed where materially relevant.

## Detail-dependent content

- [ ] Checked whether small on-screen text, map detail, QR codes, fine-print exceptions, or other dense visual cues are load-bearing.
- [ ] Verified action-changing details remain recoverable from the current written/help lane when lower-fidelity playback is insufficient.
- [ ] Verified successful playback is not being mistaken for sufficient visual detail.

## Embed and recovery posture

- [ ] Checked whether embedded official media uses a pinned/default quality different from the source media page.
- [ ] Checked whether viewers can still recover the canonical office page or named office contact when the video is technically playable but visually insufficient.
- [ ] Verified quality-state differences stay subordinate to the current written/help lane for action-changing questions.

## Public proof posture

- [ ] Logged which quality states were reviewed and when.
- [ ] Avoided publishing individualized bandwidth telemetry, device fingerprints, or watch-history detail.
