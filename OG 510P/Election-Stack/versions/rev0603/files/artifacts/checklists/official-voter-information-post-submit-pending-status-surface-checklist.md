# Official voter-information post-submit pending-status surface checklist

Use this quickcheck when an election office needs a bounded way to keep official voter-information routes workable after a request has been accepted but before the final substantive outcome is known.

## Inventory and review scope

- Identify public routes that move from immediate confirmation into a longer-lived pending, under-review, check-back-later, or follow-up state.
- Distinguish this from in-session waiting, immediate confirmation, or the underlying substantive status vocabulary for a specific voter-question family.
- Re-check routes where deadlines, cure windows, or mail delivery make vague pending states especially risky.

## Pending-state clarity and check-back posture

- State in ordinary language what the unresolved post-submit state means.
- Distinguish office-side review from voter-action-needed, completion, closure, and status unavailability when those differences matter.
- Tell the voter what ordinary waiting looks like, when to check again, and what follow-up channel to expect.
- Do not leave “pending” as a content-free state with no time window or next-step meaning.

## Status path, reference reuse, and escalation

- Make clear whether a reference or case number is only a receipt or can be used later on an official status/help path.
- Identify one authoritative later-status page, phone path, or office-help lane when status lookup matters.
- Say when silence stops being ordinary and should trigger a status lookup, office contact, or another escalation path.
- Ensure later-status posture matches the underlying substantive surface and any immediate confirmation language.

## Accessibility and reviewability

- Keep pending-state labels, follow-up instructions, and later status/help transitions available in accessible text on mobile, at zoom, with keyboard navigation, and with screen readers.
- Do not make the only meaning-bearing state a color badge, icon, or transient banner.
- Preserve enough visible context that a voter can tell whether the office is waiting on them or they are waiting on the office.
- Test the post-submit unresolved state in project context, not just the first confirmation page.

## Evidence posture

- Preserve only route labels, reviewed pending-state paths, vocabulary/check-back/escalation review state, and last review time.
- Do not preserve per-voter case histories, internal review queues or notes, personalized follow-up delivery logs, or sensitive identifiers when bounded policy reconstruction is sufficient.
