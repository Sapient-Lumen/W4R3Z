# Official voter-information portable record surface checklist

Use this quickcheck when an election office expects a voter, helper, or later reviewer to keep an official answer outside the live browser context.

## Inventory and scope

- Identify routes where the office expects the public to print, save to PDF, carry on a phone, or otherwise keep the answer for later use.
- Distinguish this from generic document-download posture, confirmation existence, and shared-device exit/privacy posture.
- Re-check routes where the kept record may govern a deadline, polling-place trip, cure step, appointment, submission reference number, or office follow-up.

## Must-survive facts

- Decide which few facts must remain visible once the live page is gone.
- Preserve the office/service identity, route purpose, key answer, timing context, and next-step/help meaning when those facts matter.
- Do not let the kept record depend on hover-only UI, collapsed panels, sticky headers, tabs, or interactive maps with no textual fallback.

## Print and save-to-PDF review

- Review ordinary browser print output and save-to-PDF output for the route.
- Check for clipping, missing labels, missing values, broken page breaks, or dropped timing/reference information.
- Confirm that the kept record still makes sense when shared forward to a helper or reopened later without the live browser state.
- Re-check mobile record-keeping posture instead of assuming a desktop printer.

## HTML/PDF authority posture

- Keep clear whether the HTML route, a PDF companion, or both carry the authoritative/current-state burden.
- Prefer HTML as the current authoritative answer surface unless there is a real operational reason to rely on a PDF artifact.
- If a PDF companion exists, make sure the relationship is reviewable enough that users do not mistake a convenience file for the current official state.

## Evidence posture

- Preserve route labels, must-survive fields, reviewed output modes, HTML-vs-PDF authority notes, portable-help routing, and last review time.
- Do not preserve real voters’ saved PDFs, screenshots of personalized answer lanes, raw browser print logs, or per-user save/share telemetry when bounded policy reconstruction is sufficient.
