# Official voter-information place-card checklist

- Inventory every platform office card or listing the office expects voters to rely on, especially Google/Apple-style cards that expose hours, phone, directions, and website actions before the voter reaches the official site.
- Make the routing role explicit: the place card helps people find the office, but it does not become the legal rule source by itself.
- Claim, verify, or otherwise control major listings where feasible so the office can correct name, address, hours, and website state during election-critical changes.
- Keep office name, category, address, phone, and website actions recognizable and aligned with the current official election office/help lane.
- Review regular hours and special/temporary hours together; do not let platform cards silently show stale holiday, emergency-closure, or deadline-week office state.
- Keep website actions pointed at recognizable current official domains/help pages, not retired microsites, contractor pages, or other detached shells.
- Review phone numbers and directions state whenever offices move, hotlines change, or seasonal/satellite offices open or close.
- Resolve duplicates, relocated listings, and legacy office cards with visible successor recovery instead of silent drift.
- Preserve a bounded trace of listing refs, phone/website/address/hours snapshots, special-hours or closure refs, duplicate/relocation transitions, and timestamps.
- Minimize telemetry: bounded public-surface reconstruction is the goal, not individualized map-click or call-detail surveillance.
