# Official voter-information form surface checklist

- Inventory the downloadable and printable voter-facing forms, applications, affidavits, and declaration packets the public is expected to rely on.
- Make the delivery role explicit: a public election form is an action artifact, not a standalone replacement for the current controlling page, notice, FAQ/help article, or office-help route.
- Give each relied-on packet a visible issue date, edition marker, or similarly bounded current-state identifier when form drift could matter.
- Make the issuing jurisdiction, receiving office, and submission channel clear enough that a copied or forwarded packet still leaves a safe next step.
- Keep state-specific instructions and any packet-specific acceptance conditions easy to find.
- Do not leave signature, witness, attestation, notary, assistant, or submission requirements hidden only in staff scripts or unlinked webpages.
- State predictable wrong-form boundaries explicitly when they matter to real users.
- When a different packet controls for a known group or path, point to that alternate packet or the official help route directly.
- Treat the packet as a public accessibility object: use plain language, accessible electronic text where offered, and language variants where the jurisdiction maintains them.
- Do not rely only on image-only scans when a voter-facing digital form is meant to be read, routed, or completed electronically.
- Keep the packet aligned with the current official page, FAQ/help article, hotline packet, office directory, and signed notice state that govern the same answer.
- When a materially different packet replaces the old one, publish an explicit superseding or withdrawal notice instead of only swapping the file silently.
- Preserve a bounded note about which packet edition was replaced and what now controls.
- Do not default to publishing completed voter submissions, signatures, supporting documents, or case-management records.
- Publish `last_verified_at` for the form lane and re-check it after acceptance-rule, deadline, routing, or jurisdiction-scope changes.
