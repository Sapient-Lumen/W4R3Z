# Official voter-information platform fullscreen-surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if already-open official media can expand into fullscreen, theater mode, forced-fullscreen mobile playback, or another immersive player state on the same device**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which public recordings, livestream replays, clips, embeds, or watch pages were reviewed for fullscreen, theater, or immersive-player behavior.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from detached playback, second-screen transfer, constrained-container escape problems, and autoplay / play-next adjacency.
- [ ] Keep “the player expanded on the same device” separate from “the office intentionally published a stand-alone fullscreen-safe official edition.”

## Immersive-mode review

- [ ] Review whether the official recording can enter fullscreen, theater mode, a large-player view, forced-fullscreen mobile playback, or another immersive layout.
- [ ] Review what the voter sees first when immersive playback starts and whether the source page, written FAQ/help lane, and correction posture remain practically recoverable.
- [ ] Review whether the platform or player keeps comments, next-video state, captions, or alternate tracks available around the immersive layout and avoid blurring those adjacent layers into the official answer.

## Context-loss review

- [ ] Review which date, scope, correction, and help-route cues disappear, move off-screen, or become non-default once the player expands.
- [ ] Do not let the mere fact that the recording fills more of the screen imply unchanged authority, freshness, or jurisdictional scope.
- [ ] Treat fullscreen/theater convenience as a presentation mode, not as proof that the enlarged player is the whole current official answer lane.

## Availability and variation review

- [ ] Review whether fullscreen availability varies by player settings, embed code, iframe nesting, permissions, or constrained-container behavior.
- [ ] Do not promise universal fullscreen support if some embeds, browsers, or app shells remove or block the affordance.
- [ ] Keep “fullscreen available” separate from “fullscreen reviewed as a sufficient stand-alone route.”

## Recovery and current-state review

- [ ] Keep the current written page, notice, FAQ/help entry, or named office contact practically recoverable after immersive playback begins.
- [ ] Treat volatile or corrected topics as requiring explicit recheck-the-current-route recovery rather than “the player is still open in fullscreen.”
- [ ] Re-review routes where immersive playback can later detach into PiP/background play, move to a second screen, or autoplay onward into adjacent media.

## Authority-boundary review

- [ ] Keep fullscreen, theater mode, and other immersive player layouts visibly subordinate to the full recording plus the current written/help lane.
- [ ] Avoid treating a large-player or fullscreen presentation as proof that the player is the controlling official page.
- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed immersive modes, recovery anchors, and last review time rather than individualized watch telemetry or orientation histories.
