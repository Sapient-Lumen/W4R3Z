# Official voter-information platform saved-media surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if already-open official media can be saved into Watch Later, playlists, offline downloads, or other platform-managed saved-media shelves**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which public recordings, replays, clips, or embeds were reviewed for Watch Later, playlist, or offline-library behavior.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from the recording lane itself, from browser-kept saved-page copies, from portable clips/highlights, and from autoplay / play-next handoffs.
- [ ] Keep “the voter saved this media item in the platform” separate from “the office intentionally published a durable current portable edition.”

## Saved-media provenance review

- [ ] Review whether saved-media contexts were viewer-managed, office-authored, platform-shaped, or mixed.
- [ ] Review whether the official recording can be saved to Watch Later, a playlist, or an offline library and whether those surfaces preserve clear recovery to the current written help lane.
- [ ] Do not let the mere presence of a saved or offline item imply unchanged authority, freshness, or jurisdictional scope.

## Playlist and offline-library review

- [ ] Review whether mixed playlists, reordered playlists, or auto-filled download libraries can place unrelated or older videos beside the official recording.
- [ ] Review whether offline reopening can happen without revisiting the current official page, notice, FAQ/help entry, or named office contact.
- [ ] Keep any saved-media shelf that mixes official and nonofficial items distinct from the current official answer lane.

## Recovery and current-state review

- [ ] Keep the current written page, notice, FAQ/help entry, or named office contact recoverable from the saved-media route.
- [ ] Treat volatile or corrected topics as requiring explicit recheck-the-current-route recovery rather than “the saved offline video still opens.”
- [ ] Re-review routes where platform save behavior, download retention, or auto-filled download libraries can change without the office rewriting the surrounding page.

## Authority-boundary review

- [ ] Keep Watch Later, playlists, and offline libraries visibly subordinate to the current recording and the named help lane.
- [ ] Avoid treating saved-media convenience as proof that the item is still current.
- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed routes, saved-media contexts, recovery anchors, and last review time rather than private playlist exports, watch histories, or recommendation dumps.
