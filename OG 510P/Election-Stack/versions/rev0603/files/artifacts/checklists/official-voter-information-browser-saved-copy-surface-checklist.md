# Official voter-information browser-saved-copy / offline-page surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if a browser reopens the already-open official page later from a Reading List item, offline saved-page file, or archived page copy**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which public routes were reviewed for browser-managed saved-copy behavior.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from bookmark/share-safe URL posture, live cache/service-worker freshness, installable web apps, and intentionally published portable print/download artifacts.
- [ ] Keep “browser kept a copy of the page” separate from “the office published a reviewed portable edition.”

## Freshness and currentness review

- [ ] Check whether freshness, election/date scope, and recheck-the-live-route cues survive inside the captured page content itself.
- [ ] Review whether the saved copy could still look like the current live page after a deadline, location, or help route changes.
- [ ] Avoid relying only on browser chrome, reload assumptions, or live banners that may not survive reopening the copy later.

## Degraded-copy and recovery review

- [ ] Review reading-list copies, locally saved page files, and any browser-archive formats as distinct states.
- [ ] Check whether scripts, linked assets, maps, forms, or disclosure widgets can degrade when reopened from a saved copy.
- [ ] If the saved copy is incomplete or volatile, keep a visible recovery path back to the current official live page/help lane.

## Authority and portable-edition review

- [ ] Review whether volatile topics (locations, hours, deadlines, status, cure, emergency changes) clearly bias the reader back toward the current live route.
- [ ] Do not let a user-created browser capture quietly masquerade as the office's reviewed portable publication object.
- [ ] Keep official office/help routing legible enough that later readers can recover the current answer without guesswork.

## Privacy and evidence review

- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed routes, saved-copy contexts, recovery/help anchors, and last review time.
- [ ] Do not preserve individualized Reading List contents, local file paths, browser histories, or uploaded saved-page artifacts merely to prove the posture was reviewed.
- [ ] Keep any future idea of browser-native saved-copy provenance or universal stale-copy invalidation in quarantine until trustworthy standardized hooks actually exist.
