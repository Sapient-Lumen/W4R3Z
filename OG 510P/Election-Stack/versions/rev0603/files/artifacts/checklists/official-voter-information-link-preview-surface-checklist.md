# Official voter-information shared-link preview checklist

Use this quickcheck when official voter-information links may circulate through chat apps, social sharing, collaboration tools, or other unfurl/preview-card surfaces before people open the actual page.

## Before publishing or revising a likely-to-circulate page

- Confirm the page is still the right **current official destination** for the task, not a stale election-cycle URL.
- Confirm the page title and short description make the destination class clear (office page, FAQ/help answer, notice, lookup tool, directory, etc.).
- Confirm preview-facing metadata does not imply a broader or more current answer than the landing page can actually support.
- Confirm any preview image is non-essential for the legal meaning of the instruction and will not become misleading if cached.
- Confirm the click-through page exposes current-state or recovery cues quickly enough for volatile topics.

## For high-risk / action-changing topics

- Prefer stable current landing pages over brittle one-cycle URLs when possible.
- Make the responsible office or jurisdiction visible quickly after click-through.
- Make current notice/help routing obvious when the preview card alone is not enough.
- Treat hours, closures, locations, and deadline-sensitive topics as stale-preview risks.
- Re-check shared-link preview behavior after major notice, redirect, office-hours, or election-rollover changes.

## Recovery and drift control

- Check at least one common shared-link preview path for the page class under review.
- Assume some platforms may cache or truncate preview state; verify the landing page still recovers safely.
- If an older preview card is still circulating, make sure the landing page or current notice can correct it without guesswork.
- Avoid depending on one platform-specific unfurl feature for operational correctness.
- Keep the office/help phone or current official contact path easy to reach when the previewed link leads to a partial answer.

## Privacy / minimization

- Do not retain individualized share logs or message telemetry just to prove the preview policy existed.
- Preserve only the bounded preview-policy trace needed to reconstruct intended destination class, canonical routing, and stale-preview recovery posture.
