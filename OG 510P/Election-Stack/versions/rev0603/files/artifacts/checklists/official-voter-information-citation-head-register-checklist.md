# Official voter-information citation-head-register checklist

Use this checklist when an election office maintains **multiple mutable official pages, snapshots, or superseding public-answer states that may all be mistaken for “the thing to cite.”**

## Register scope and lineage review

- [ ] Record which mutable official route families or bounded lineages belong in the citation-head register.
- [ ] Review whether each included lineage has a clearly named operational head and, if available, a clearly named citation-safe head.
- [ ] Re-check lineages where the public may encounter more than one plausible current page, snapshot, or superseding answer state.

## Unique-head and warning review

- [ ] Review whether the register says explicitly when a lineage has exactly one unique citation head.
- [ ] Review whether the register publishes a bounded warning when no unique citation head exists yet.
- [ ] Review whether branching, pending-freeze, preview/queue boundary, or superseding conditions appear as explicit warnings rather than forcing readers to infer ambiguity from silence.
- [ ] Review whether the register keeps operationally current heads distinct from exact citation-safe heads.

## Public handoff review

- [ ] Review whether the register is small and legible enough for partner explainers, hotline packets, journalists, observer review, and AI-answer recovery.
- [ ] Review whether the register gives a bounded recovery note for cases where the live page is current but not yet uniquely citation-safe.
- [ ] Review whether older citation heads that still circulate are marked as superseded, corrected, or otherwise relationship-labeled.

## Boundary and evidence posture

- [ ] Review whether preview URLs, staged copies, queue-state artifacts, and other non-public materials stay out of the public head register.
- [ ] Preserve a compact public digest of reviewed lineages, operational heads, citation heads, warning posture, and last review time.
- [ ] Do not publish full internal CMS histories, private preview links, queue dashboards, or giant lineage graphs merely to prove head-register status.
