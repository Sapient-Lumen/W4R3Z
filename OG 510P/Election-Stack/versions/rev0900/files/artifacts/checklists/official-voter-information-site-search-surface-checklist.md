# Official voter-information site search surface checklist

- Inventory each public site-search box, autocomplete layer, synonym map, and search-result page the public is expected to rely on for voter information.
- Make the delivery role explicit: search helps users reach the current controlling official destination; it does not become the rule source by itself.
- Keep action-changing topics routed toward current official pages, signed notices, FAQ entries, form packets, and office/help destinations rather than stale fragments.
- Make sure autocomplete and suggested-query behavior uses current plain-language voter terms and does not fabricate jurisdiction-specific legal conclusions.
- Demote, mark, or remove superseded pages, withdrawn notices, and stale PDFs from ordinary result pages when they no longer control.
- Do not let snippets present stale deadlines, office hours, or old form/version text as if still current.
- Use a small result-state taxonomy so the search surface can distinguish current direct result, current routed result, conflict/uncertain result, and unsupported query.
- Preserve a bounded search trace for action-changing result sets, including policy version, normalized-query or query-class identifier, result-anchor identifiers, result-state class, and timestamp.
- Route low-certainty or conflicting cases to the current office/help path instead of pretending ranking settled the rule.
- Keep the rights/safety escalation path visible when the question has moved beyond ordinary voter-information routing.
- Minimize query retention and do not keep raw typed queries, click histories, or address-level search trails longer than the published policy requires.
- Do not bind public search behavior to identifiable voter records absent a clearly disclosed separate authority.
- Re-check the search surface after source-anchor, FAQ/article, form-version, office-routing, deadline, or superseding-notice changes.
- When material ranking, suggestion, or source-anchor behavior changes, publish an explicit update/superseding note instead of relying only on silent relevance tuning.
