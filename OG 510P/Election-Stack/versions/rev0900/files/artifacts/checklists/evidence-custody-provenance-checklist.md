# Evidence custody/provenance checklist

**Synthetic support checklist only. This is not legal advice, not chain-of-custody certification, and not live election evidence.**

Use this before live-pilot promotion or publication of local evidence-derived artifacts. Record reviewer names, dates, artifact digests, and exception reasons.

## Minimum custody checks

1. Capture scope and approving role are recorded before collection.
2. Collector identity or role token, affiliation/COI note, and equipment ownership are recorded.
3. Tool version, archive version, time source, vantage point, and limitations are preserved.
4. Payload, manifest, generated-report, and public-derivative digests are linked.
5. Every handoff has from/to custodian, timestamp, pre-transfer digest, post-transfer digest, and storage medium.
6. Access logs record role, purpose, time window, private-field visibility, and dissent/correction pointers.
7. Private, privileged, safety-sensitive, and security-sensitive materials are separated from public derivatives.
8. Public derivative lineage links the public artifact to source evidence, redaction rule, review status, and correction pointer.
9. Incident evidence is frozen before interpretation and carries non-claim language.
10. Offline drill transcripts preserve ZIP digest, verifier role, machine description, transcript digest, and manifest result.
11. Source review and pinning decisions identify reviewer role, review date, pin/demotion reason, and affected artifacts.
12. Chain gaps and exceptions are visible, owned, bounded, and time-limited.
13. Retention, disposition, destruction, return, or sealing actions carry digest, date, rule pointer, owner, and hold status.
14. Live promotion remains no-go until every applicable custody row is closed or locally excepted.

## Stop conditions

Stop promotion if a capture lacks authorization, a transfer lacks digest continuity, a reviewer cannot identify private/public fields, a chain gap is undocumented, a public derivative cannot be traced to source evidence, or a disposition action lacks an owner and rule pointer.
