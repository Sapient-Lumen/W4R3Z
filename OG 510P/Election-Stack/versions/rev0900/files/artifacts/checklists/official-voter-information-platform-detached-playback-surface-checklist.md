# Official voter-information platform detached-playback surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if already-open official media can continue in picture-in-picture, popout, mini-player, or background-play states**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which public recordings, replays, clips, or embeds were reviewed for detached-playback behavior.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from the recording lane itself, from saved-media shelves, from autoplay / play-next adjacency, and from alternate-audio track switches.
- [ ] Keep “the browser or platform let the current media keep playing” separate from “the office intentionally published a standalone portable official edition.”

## Detached-playback mode review

- [ ] Review whether the official recording can enter picture-in-picture, floating mini-player, popout-tab/window, or background-play states.
- [ ] Review whether captions, transcript cues, or alternate-audio choices continue inside detached playback and could make the stripped-down player feel self-sufficient.
- [ ] Review whether constrained app shells or embedded browsers change how easily a voter can get back to the source page after detaching playback.

## Context-loss review

- [ ] Review which date, scope, correction, and help-route cues disappear or become hard to recover once playback detaches from the full page.
- [ ] Do not let the mere fact that audio/video keeps playing imply unchanged authority, freshness, or jurisdictional scope.
- [ ] Treat lock-screen / now-playing controls and screen-off audio as playback conveniences, not as a sufficient current official answer lane.

## Recovery and current-state review

- [ ] Keep the current written page, notice, FAQ/help entry, or named office contact recoverable from the detached-playback route.
- [ ] Treat volatile or corrected topics as requiring explicit recheck-the-current-route recovery rather than “the floating player is still open.”
- [ ] Re-review routes where a popout player, PiP window, or background-play state can continue after the controlling written answer changed.

## Authority-boundary review

- [ ] Keep detached playback visibly subordinate to the full recording and the named help lane.
- [ ] Avoid treating detached-playback convenience as proof that the stripped-down playback state is the controlling official page.
- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed detached-playback modes, recovery anchors, and last review time rather than individualized watch histories, device media-session logs, or background-play analytics.
