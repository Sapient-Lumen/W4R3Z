# Official voter-information platform clip-surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if a player or platform lets offices or viewers turn already-open official media into public clips, highlights, or shareable segment links**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which public recordings, embeds, livestream replays, or audio-first routes were reviewed for clip/highlight/share-a-segment behavior.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from the recording lane itself, from transcript panes/search, from chapter/key-moment jump maps, and from ordinary page-level copy/share controls.
- [ ] Keep “the platform made this excerpt portable” separate from “the office published and reviewed this exact shorter edition as current official guidance.”

## Excerpt provenance review

- [ ] Review whether portable excerpts were office-created highlights, viewer-created clips, deep links into the original recording, or a mix.
- [ ] Review whether viewer-created clips are enabled, disabled, or limited on the platform/channel.
- [ ] Do not let user-created clip titles, clip shelves, or forwarded excerpt links quietly stand in for reviewed official policy headings.

## Sharing, publicity, and discovery review

- [ ] Review embed/share/copy routes for clips or segment links that can travel farther than the original page or recording context.
- [ ] Review whether clips or highlights can surface publicly in discovery, channel shelves, or external shares and keep those routes subordinate to the current written destination/help lane.
- [ ] Keep office-published highlights distinct from viewer-created clips when describing what the office actually stands behind.

## Recovery and current-state review

- [ ] Keep the full recording, recording date/scope, and still-current cues recoverable from the excerpt surface.
- [ ] Maintain a durable written recovery path to the current official page, notice, FAQ/help entry, or office lane when the excerpt is too narrow, stale, or decontextualized.
- [ ] Treat volatile or corrected topics as requiring explicit recheck-the-current-route recovery rather than clip confidence alone.

## Authority-boundary review

- [ ] Keep clips, highlights, and shareable segment links visibly subordinate to the full recording and the named help lane.
- [ ] Avoid treating a portable excerpt as a reviewed official portable publication by default.
- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed routes, excerpt contexts, recovery anchors, and last review time rather than watch history, audience analytics, or exhaustive share logs.
