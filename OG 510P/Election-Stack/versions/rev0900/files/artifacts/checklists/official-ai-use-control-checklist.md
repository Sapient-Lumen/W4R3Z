# Official AI-use control checklist

Use this before any AI-assisted official public artifact is drafted, translated, summarized, or routed.

- [ ] Human owner named for the artifact.
- [ ] No voter PII, nonpublic incident details, credentials, or protected internal material placed into an AI prompt.
- [ ] AI was not used to make eligibility, legal, ballot-status, cure, or deadline determinations without deterministic official authority and human review.
- [ ] Generated text was checked against current official sources for dates, hours, locations, deadlines, and required forms.
- [ ] Translation or accessibility output was reviewed by a qualified human or approved local process.
- [ ] A disclosure/review note was recorded when AI materially assisted the artifact.
- [ ] Final publication was approved by a named human authority.
