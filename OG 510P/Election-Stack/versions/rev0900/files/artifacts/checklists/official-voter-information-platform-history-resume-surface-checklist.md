# Official voter-information platform history-resume-surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if already-open official media later resurfaces through watch history, continue-watching rows, recent-videos shelves, or remembered resume state**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which public recordings, replays, player pages, or media hubs were reviewed for history, continue-watching, recents, and resume behavior.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from autoplay / play-next continuation, explicit Watch Later or playlist saves, follow/reminder state, and in-player scan controls.
- [ ] Keep “the platform remembered prior viewing” separate from “the office intentionally published this as the current return path.”

## History and re-entry review

- [ ] Review whether the official recording can reappear in watch history, a continue-watching row, a recent-videos list, or another resurfacing shelf.
- [ ] Review whether re-entry works differently across desktop, mobile, TV, embedded, signed-in, or work-suite contexts.
- [ ] Do not let the mere fact that a video resurfaced because it was watched before imply unchanged authority, freshness, or jurisdictional scope.

## Resume-state and partial-view review

- [ ] Review whether remembered progress reopens the recording at a point that skips date, scope, correction, or escalation cues.
- [ ] Review whether “minutes remaining,” in-progress state, or cross-device resume creates a misleading sense that the voter is returning to a still-current official answer lane.
- [ ] Keep “resume where you left off” separate from “you are back in the current controlling route.”

## Recommendation and continuity review

- [ ] Review whether history-driven recommendations or continue-watching continuity can place older or adjacent videos beside the official recording.
- [ ] Review whether series, playlist, or up-next behavior inside continue-watching surfaces makes resurfaced media feel like one coherent official sequence.
- [ ] Keep history-shaped resurfacing distinct from office-reviewed continuity or intentional publication sequence.

## Recovery and current-state review

- [ ] Keep the current written page, notice, FAQ/help entry, or named office contact recoverable after reopening through history or recents.
- [ ] Treat volatile or corrected topics as requiring explicit recheck-the-current-route recovery rather than “continue watching the old recording.”
- [ ] Re-review routes where device sync, account state, or work-suite recents can revive older recordings without revisiting the current official page first.

## Authority-boundary review

- [ ] Keep watch history, continue-watching, recent-videos, and resume-state visibly subordinate to the current recording and the named help lane.
- [ ] Avoid treating prior-view memory as proof that a resurfaced recording is still current.
- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed re-entry contexts, recovery anchors, and last review time rather than individualized watch histories or behavioral logs.
