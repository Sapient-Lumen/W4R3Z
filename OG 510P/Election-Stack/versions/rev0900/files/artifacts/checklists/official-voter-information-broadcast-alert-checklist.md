# Official voter-information broadcast alert checklist

- Identify the official short-form channels the office uses for voter-information alerts (social accounts, SMS lists, app notifications, or equivalent).
- Make the delivery role explicit: short-form alerts are advisories/routing aids, not the standalone authority for jurisdiction-specific rules.
- Require a canonical official link or help-route for action-changing alerts about sites, hours, deadlines, closures, reroutes, ballot-return steps, or other next-step-changing topics.
- Include scope and timing clearly enough that the reader can tell what election/jurisdiction/window the alert applies to.
- Do not compress complex edge-case legal rules into slogan-length platform posts; route those questions to the controlling page or office instead.
- Publish explicit correction/superseding alerts when a material public answer changes; do not rely only on silent deletion or quiet replacement.
- Retire, unpin, or visibly stale-mark short-lived alerts when practical so old posts do not keep masquerading as current instructions.
- Keep the short-form alert aligned with the linked official page/notice, FAQ/help article, and hotline script packet that govern the same answer.
- Provide alt text or equivalent accessible text for image-based posts and do not hide action-changing facts only inside graphics.
- Keep language variants aligned where the jurisdiction offers or requires them.
- Monitor comments/replies during active periods and respond with accurate information or direct users to trusted official sources.
- Do not perform record-specific casework in public comments or ordinary social-media DMs; route sensitive matters to the official secure/help channel.
- Preserve a bounded digestable record of the channel registry, alert policy, and material corrections without defaulting to raw platform exports or engagement analytics.
- Publish `last_verified_at` for the alert surface and re-check the policy after major deadline, site, office-hour, or incident-routing changes.
