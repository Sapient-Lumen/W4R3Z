# Local pilot intake checklist

**Pre-live gate. This checklist is not authorization, not live election evidence, not current voter instruction, and not legal advice.**

A pilot may stay synthetic with incomplete rows. A live or public-support pilot must close every blocking item or record an explicit local exception signed by the accountable owner.

## Required before live promotion

- Local scope and sponsor recorded.
- Official channels and fallback channels listed.
- Source review refreshed, pinned, or locally confirmed for current-authority claims.
- Data minimization and redaction rules recorded.
- Publication contracts and deadline-bearing artifacts listed.
- Witness-set roles, replacement rules, and disagreement handling recorded.
- Offline verification transcript preserved for the target ZIP and pilot packet set.
- Human-review escalation roles assigned.
- Retention and disposition route recorded.
- External review or challenge session scheduled and logged.
- Accessibility and language-access review completed for public summaries.
- Counsel-reviewed boundaries recorded before court, admissibility, or public-records language.
- AI-assisted output, if any, has human approval and PII discipline.
- Incident public-communications owner and safety escalation path recorded.

## Public-language rule

Use bounded evidence statements: what was expected, what was observed, what could not be verified, what was preserved, and who is reviewing it. Do not infer motive, fraud, eligibility, rights, or outcome effects from the intake checklist.
