# Official voter-information browser-OCR / image-text-extraction surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if a browser or platform extracts text from a scanned PDF, image-based notice, infographic, or photographed official artifact that is already open**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which public routes, images, or scanned documents were reviewed for browser-integrated OCR or image-text-extraction behavior.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from detached print artifacts, file-delivery/viewer posture, reader mode, browser-integrated summaries, and browser-integrated translation.
- [ ] Keep “browser-extracted text from the source artifact” separate from “reviewed authored text equivalent the office actually stands behind.”

## Authoring and equivalence review

- [ ] Check whether decisive instructions, qualifiers, office identity, and election/date scope are available in ordinary text when they reasonably can be.
- [ ] Review whether image-only or scan-only wording is forcing voters to depend on OCR for the primary meaning.
- [ ] If the office must publish an image-heavy or scanned artifact, keep the current official text/help lane visible enough that extracted text remains subordinate rather than looking like the canonical text edition.

## Layout and excerpt portability review

- [ ] Review whether table headers, footnotes, captions, warnings, or grouped conditions are necessary for the extracted text to make sense.
- [ ] Check likely copied excerpts or searchable snippets a voter could pull from the artifact out of context.
- [ ] If the answer is not safely portable as extracted plain text, make the page say so plainly and route the voter to the current official text/help lane.

## Downstream-transform review

- [ ] Review whether OCR-derived text is likely to be translated, summarized, searched, or action-triggered by browser/platform features.
- [ ] Do not let OCR-derived text quietly become the office’s de facto official translation, summary, or transcript.
- [ ] Keep original-artifact recovery and official help routing legible even after copy/select/search/share actions.

## Privacy and evidence review

- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed routes/artifacts, extraction contexts, recovery/help anchors, and last review time.
- [ ] Do not preserve copied OCR transcripts, clipboard histories, or per-user extraction telemetry merely to prove the posture was reviewed.
- [ ] Keep any future idea of browser-native OCR confidence feeds or perfect extraction provenance in quarantine until trustworthy standardized hooks actually exist.
