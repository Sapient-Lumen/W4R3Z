# Official voter-information platform AI video-answer surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if already-open official media also exposes a player-native AI summary, ask-this-video module, or transcript-grounded question-and-answer surface**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which public recordings, embeds, player pages, or official channel routes were reviewed for AI summary/question-answer behavior.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from off-platform AI answers, browser page summaries, transcript panes, chapter maps, and clipped excerpts.
- [ ] Keep “the platform generated an answer over the recording” separate from “the office reviewed and published that exact wording as official guidance.”

## Answer provenance review

- [ ] Review whether answers are grounded in the transcript, other platform/web context, or a mixture of both.
- [ ] Review whether suggested questions, short answer chips, or moment links can make one answer look more settled than the full recording or written route.
- [ ] Do not let generated answers imply reviewed authority, unchanged currentness, or complete jurisdictional fit by default.

## Transcript, language, and jump-link review

- [ ] Review whether transcript presence, transcript length, or transcript-language mismatch can change what the AI module can answer.
- [ ] Review whether answers can be requested in other languages and whether those answers stay subordinate to any reviewed official translated/help lane.
- [ ] Review whether jump-to-moment links preserve enough surrounding context that one located segment does not masquerade as the whole current answer.

## High-risk-topic recovery review

- [ ] Re-review routes covering deadlines, locations, ID rules, accommodations, absentee steps, or other topics that can change faster than the recording ages.
- [ ] Keep the current written page, reviewed FAQ/help entry, or named office contact recoverable when the AI answer is partial, stale, or overconfident.
- [ ] Prefer explicit recheck-the-current-route recovery over product copy that sounds like the AI module itself settles operational questions.

## Evidence-minimization and portability review

- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed routes, answer-module posture, grounding notes, recovery anchors, and last review time rather than prompt logs, per-user answer histories, or recommendation telemetry.
- [ ] Keep copied answer text, suggested questions, and moment links visibly subordinate to the full recording and current written lane.
- [ ] Re-review routes whenever transcript generation, language support, player AI settings, or platform answer posture changes materially.
