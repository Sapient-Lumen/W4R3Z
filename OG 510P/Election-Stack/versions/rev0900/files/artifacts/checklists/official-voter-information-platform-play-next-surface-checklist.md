# Official voter-information platform play-next surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if a player or platform can autoplay, recommend, or link viewers onward from already-open official media through end screens, cards, or other play-next surfaces**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which public recordings, embeds, livestream replays, or audio-first routes were reviewed for autoplay, end-screen, card, or more-videos behavior.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from the recording lane itself, from chapter/key-moment jump maps, and from portable clips/highlights.
- [ ] Keep “the player offered a next step” separate from “the office published and reviewed that next destination as the same current official answer lane.”

## Next-step provenance review

- [ ] Review whether next-step surfaces were office-curated, platform-shaped, or mixed.
- [ ] Review whether autoplay can continue to another video and whether that continuation stays inside a reviewed official scope.
- [ ] Do not let end screens, cards, or more-videos shelves quietly imply unchanged authority, freshness, or jurisdictional scope.

## Embed and handoff review

- [ ] Review embedded-player behavior, including autoplay parameters, after-video surfaces, and in-player links that can move the voter without an obvious origin change.
- [ ] Review whether a card, CTA, or end screen can hand the voter to an external site, partner route, or broader platform surface.
- [ ] Keep any external or cross-surface handoff distinct from the current official page/help lane and make recovery easy.

## Recovery and current-state review

- [ ] Keep the current written page, notice, FAQ/help entry, or named office contact recoverable before and after any player-driven handoff.
- [ ] Treat volatile or corrected topics as requiring explicit recheck-the-current-route recovery rather than “the next video probably explains it.”
- [ ] Re-review routes where autoplay, playlist continuation, or player presets can change without the office rewriting the surrounding page.

## Authority-boundary review

- [ ] Keep autoplay, cards, end screens, and more-videos surfaces visibly subordinate to the current recording and the named help lane.
- [ ] Avoid treating frictionless continuation as proof that the next video or destination carries the same review state.
- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed routes, next-step contexts, recovery anchors, and last review time rather than watch histories, recommendation dumps, or clickstream analytics.
