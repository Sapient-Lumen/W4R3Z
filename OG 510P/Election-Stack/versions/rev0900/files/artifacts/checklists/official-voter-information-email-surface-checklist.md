# Official voter-information email surface checklist

- Inventory the official outbound voter-information email lanes the office expects the public to rely on.
- Make the delivery role explicit: email is an inbox delivery lane for voter information, not a standalone replacement for the current controlling page/notice/help path.
- Identify the official sending office, sender address/domain, and reply/help route clearly enough that a forwarded message still looks official.
- Prefer official `.gov` sender accounts for action-changing election email when the jurisdiction controls the account.
- Require a canonical official link or help-route for action-changing emails about sites, hours, deadlines, registration/update windows, ballot-return steps, or incident changes.
- Include scope and issue timing clearly enough that a recipient can tell what election/jurisdiction/window the email applies to.
- Do not hide action-changing facts only inside inaccessible images or attachments.
- When attachments are meant to be relied on, give them an issue date or edition marker and keep them linked back to the current official destination when practical.
- Make the safe next step visible inside the email so a forwarded copy, screenshot, or printout does not strand the recipient.
- Publish an explicit correction or superseding update when a material emailed answer changes.
- Keep the email lane aligned with the linked official page/notice, FAQ/help article, hotline packet, and any attached portable artifact that govern the same answer.
- A monitored public reply address can be real, but do not let a newsletter mailbox become an undocumented shadow case-management system.
- Route rights-sensitive, record-specific, or time-critical matters to the correct official help or escalation lane.
- Treat emails as electronic documents for accessibility purposes: use plain language and provide an equivalent accessible text version when an attachment cannot carry the whole public answer accessibly.
- Preserve a bounded record of sender identities, message policy, and material corrections without defaulting to recipient lists, click analytics, or private reply archives.
- Publish `last_verified_at` for the email surface and re-check it after deadline, location, incident, or sender-identity changes.
