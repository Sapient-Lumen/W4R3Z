# Official voter-information text-fragment surface checklist

Use this checklist for official voter-information routes that **may be reached through browser-generated, search-generated, bookmarked, or user-shared highlighted-text deep links such as `#:~:text=...`**.

## Route inventory and review scope

- [ ] Record which important routes are likely to be reached through quoted-text deep links, highlighted search-result landings, or browser-generated “link to highlight” style flows.
- [ ] Identify the route families where a highlighted sentence could change a voter’s understanding of deadline, eligibility, ID requirement, site location, return step, or cure path.
- [ ] Re-check long or frequently edited pages where quoted text is likely to drift even when the overall page remains current.

## Context and subordination review

- [ ] Review whether a highlighted excerpt remains visibly subordinate to the current official page context, heading structure, freshness cues, and help lane.
- [ ] Review whether a highlighted sentence can be misread as a standalone official ruling when nearby qualifiers, jurisdiction scope, or exceptions are needed.
- [ ] Review compact/mobile layouts so the highlight does not crowd out the page title, heading, office identity, or recovery path.

## Durability and fail-open review

- [ ] Review whether maintained section anchors, share-safe canonical URLs, or citation-safe heads are preferred for durable official references when text fragments are too brittle.
- [ ] Review what happens when the highlighted text no longer matches, the browser ignores the text fragment, or automatic scrolling/highlighting is suppressed.
- [ ] Review whether those missing/unsupported cases fail open with bounded refinding help instead of implying exact-quote recovery.

## Visibility-boundary and evidence review

- [ ] Review whether page logic or public messaging overclaims that the office can directly see and certify the exact incoming text fragment.
- [ ] Review whether browser-generated bookmarks or shares carrying the highlight are treated as user-agent convenience handoffs rather than as office-issued portable records.
- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed route classes, context posture, durability posture, and fail-open behavior.
- [ ] Do not retain individualized fragment strings, bookmark histories, or per-user highlight telemetry merely to prove that highlighted-text links exist.
