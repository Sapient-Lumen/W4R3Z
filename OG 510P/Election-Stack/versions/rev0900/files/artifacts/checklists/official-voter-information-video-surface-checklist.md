# Official voter-information video-surface checklist

- Inventory the official voter-information audiovisual lane: explainer videos, livestreams, recorded briefings, webinars, short clips, audio-first explainers, and similar recordings the office expects the public to use.
- Make the delivery role explicit: public videos and livestreams are audiovisual delivery lanes for voter information, not standalone replacements for the current controlling page/notice/help path.
- For action-changing recordings, include the question covered, jurisdiction/election scope, a recording or publication date, whether the recording is still current, the current official destination, and the help route.
- Do not publish floating clips or replays with no visible date, no scope, no help path, and no way to tell whether the viewer is watching historical footage or current guidance.
- Keep a human-readable official URL or help route visible in the video description, transcript page, adjacent webpage, or pinned context.
- Do not hide crucial instructions only in spoken audio; provide captions and transcript or equivalent text support for action-changing content.
- Review captions or transcript text for action-changing content when the office is relying on them as part of the public answer.
- Keep public videos aligned with the linked webpage, FAQ/help article, hotline packet, press statement, and signed notice that govern the same answer.
- Distinguish live briefings from archived replays and evergreen explainers; an available replay is not automatically a still-current answer.
- Treat comment threads and user replies as non-authoritative unless the office deliberately republishes a correction through an official controlled surface.
- Publish explicit corrections or superseding notes when a material public answer changes; do not rely only on silent description edits or the assumption that viewers will find a newer upload on their own.
- Where clips or excerpts are republished, keep a route back to fuller context or the current written destination.
- Use plain language, accessible playback patterns where controlled, and translated/interpreted variants where the jurisdiction offers them.
- Preserve a bounded digestable record of the video-surface policy and superseding events without defaulting to viewer analytics, comment archives, or full media preservation.
- Keep sensitive casework and individualized records out of general-purpose public videos; route those matters to the official help or secure channel instead.
- Publish `last_verified_at` for the lane and re-check the policy after major date, location, office-contact, ballot-path, or incident-routing changes.
