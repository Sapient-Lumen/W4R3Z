# Official voter-information platform share/export surface checklist

Use this checklist when official voter-information media is expected to travel through a platform-native share panel, copied link, current-time link, QR/share handoff, or embed export.

## Scope

- [ ] Reviewed the actual share/export surfaces in scope (copy link, start-at/current-time link, email/share-sheet, embed export, QR/share wrapper).
- [ ] Distinguished whole-media portability from clip/highlight creation.
- [ ] Recorded which downstream wrappers (chat, email, partner page, document, embed destination) were materially relied on.

## Currentness and recovery

- [ ] Verified the shared or embedded media stays subordinate to the current written/help lane for action-changing questions.
- [ ] Verified copied links and embeds still leave a practical path back to the canonical office page or named office contact.
- [ ] Verified timestamp/current-time links do not overclaim one moment as the whole current answer.
- [ ] Verified older shared links remain clearly subordinate after corrections or superseding notices.

## Wrapper context and embedding

- [ ] Tested embedded playback in the wrappers the office materially relies on.
- [ ] Checked whether wrapper text, surrounding layout, or publication context could make old media look newly published or self-sufficient.
- [ ] Checked whether domain restrictions, redirects, or click-through behavior materially change the public experience.

## Public proof posture

- [ ] Logged which portability states were reviewed and when.
- [ ] Avoided publishing private recipient lists, analytics, or per-user share telemetry.
