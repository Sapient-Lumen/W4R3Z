# Independent review and conflict gate checklist

**Synthetic support only. This checklist is not third-party validation, not certification, not live-pilot authorization, not legal advice, and not authorization to test live systems.**

Use this before describing any review as independent or external.

1. Name the review scope and exclusions.
2. Record reviewer identity, role, affiliation, and conflict disclosures.
3. Record qualifications and method limits.
4. Pre-register artifact/sample selection or explain why the review is comprehensive.
5. Preserve a reproducibility transcript with release ZIP hash and commands.
6. Preserve reviewer access/custody records for private or sealed material.
7. Provide a dissent/minority-report route.
8. Bound any adversarial challenge with explicit authorization and stop conditions.
9. Route legal, public-records, accessibility, privacy, and custody questions to local owners.
10. Require reviewer approval of public summary language and non-claims.
11. Record remediation, retest, residual risk, and unresolved issues before closure.
12. Keep live-pilot, certification, current-authority, and third-party-validation claims no-go until the matrix is closed or locally excepted.
