# Polling-place intimidation public-answer template

You have the right to ask a poll worker or election official for help if someone is blocking, threatening, following, photographing, or pressuring you at a voting location.

If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services. For non-emergency voting issues, contact the official election office or tell a poll worker at the site.

For an evidence record, write down the time, place, what happened, who was involved if known, and any official incident number. Do not post private voter details publicly.

This answer does not decide whether a law was violated. It routes the voter to the official help path and preserves a bounded record.
