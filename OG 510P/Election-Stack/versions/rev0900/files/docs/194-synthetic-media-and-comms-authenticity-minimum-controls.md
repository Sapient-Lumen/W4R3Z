## Minimum rule

An official communication, media item, clip, transcript, derivative, AI summary, repost, or platform wrapper must not be treated as authoritative merely because it looks official, carries a platform cue, or repeats earlier official language. Authority comes from the accountable official channel, current governing source, provenance record, and reviewable publication trail.

## Required controls

- identify the accountable official source and current publication head;
- preserve digest-bound captures for disputed artifacts;
- separate content authenticity, account/channel authenticity, platform cues, and viewer/wrapper states;
- record capture time, freshness, supersession, and conflict status;
- publish correction routes when a voter-facing artifact could mislead;
- never let synthetic media, AI summaries, or detached derivatives substitute for jurisdiction-specific voter instructions.
