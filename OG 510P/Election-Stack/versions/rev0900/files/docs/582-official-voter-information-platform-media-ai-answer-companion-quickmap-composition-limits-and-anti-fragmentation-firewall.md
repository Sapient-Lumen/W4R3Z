## Rule

AI answer companions are wrappers. They may point to official source content, expose a mismatch, or require a correction packet, but they do not become the official voting instruction unless an accountable official channel publishes and signs that content as the instruction.

## Minimum packet shape

- official source being summarized;
- AI answer text or summary digest;
- capture time and product surface;
- whether the answer matches, omits, contradicts, or invents content;
- public correction route when voters could be misled.

## Anti-fragmentation rule

Do not spawn a new numbered surface for every AI companion placement. Route by function: source summary, question-answer pane, suggested follow-up, citation card, generated transcript excerpt, or contradiction/correction packet.
