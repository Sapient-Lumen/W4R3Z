## Rule

Cite the current governing official source first. Historical platform legs, archived captures, reposts, mirrors, clips, transcript panes, and derivative packets may explain provenance or disagreement, but they do not outrank the current official head unless the jurisdiction explicitly designates them.

## Required packet fields

- current official head reference;
- historical leg reference, when used;
- capture time and freshness status;
- whether the leg is superseded, corroborating, conflicting, or context-only;
- reviewer note when a historical leg is the only available evidence.
