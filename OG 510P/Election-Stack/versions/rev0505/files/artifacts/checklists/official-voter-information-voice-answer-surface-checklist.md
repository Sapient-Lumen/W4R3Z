# Official voter-information voice-answer surface checklist

- Inventory the voice and screenless entrypoints most likely to speak official voter information before click-through.
- Make the authority boundary explicit: the spoken layer may assist discovery, but the current official page, notice, office record, or office/help route remains controlling.
- Identify the official pages and office-card records most likely to feed spoken answers for action-changing topics.
- Ensure office identity, page purpose, and next-step recovery are easy to recognize immediately after spoken handoff.
- Keep office-card name, hours, phone, website, and directions state synchronized with the current official website or notice posture.
- Prefer direct current-cycle or current-office destinations over generic timeless prose for hours, locations, closures, and deadlines.
- Treat address-specific, district-specific, record-specific, or rights-sensitive spoken answers as routing cues, not final legal statements.
- Keep office/help recovery obvious when spoken output is partial, ambiguous, stale, device-limited, or language-limited.
- Re-check spoken-answer handoff after major directory, place-card, search-result, localization, emergency-notice, or election-rollover changes.
- Preserve a bounded handoff trace: destination-class refs, policy version, current-state cue expectations, fallback route, and verification timestamp.
- Minimize telemetry: do not collect individualized third-party audio or voice-usage logs when bounded reconstruction of the official handoff policy is enough.
