# Official voter-information section-target surface checklist

Use this quickcheck when an election office publishes long or sectioned official voter-information pages that people may navigate with jump links, “On this page” menus, or copied fragment-bearing links.

## Inventory and scope

- Identify long official pages where the controlling answer lives in a specific section rather than at the top of the page.
- Distinguish this from whole-URL portability, stale-link recovery between pages, reader-mode extraction, and browser-tab identity.
- Re-check routes where offices expect people to copy, bookmark, or relay a section-level reference such as “ID requirements,” “ballot cure,” or “drop box rules.”

## Section-target clarity

- Make critical answer-bearing sections directly targetable or clearly summarized near the top with an unambiguous path to the full section.
- Use clear, descriptive heading text so users can predict where a same-page link will take them.
- Do not rely on vague “learn more” or “details below” links when the next section changes what the voter believes or does next.

## In-page navigation and heading alignment

- Verify that any “On this page” list or jump-link set matches the real heading order and section structure on the live page.
- Check that current-location cues, keyboard movement, and screen-reader announcements remain intelligible when navigating between sections.
- Do not let in-page navigation drift into a stale mirror of headings that have been reordered, renamed, hidden, or removed.

## Fragment continuity and revision hygiene

- Review whether important section targets keep working after ordinary content edits and page reorganizations.
- Preserve or consciously review stable section identifiers where practical for widely used or operationally important section links.
- Do not silently strand prior fragment-bearing references at the wrong section or a generic top-of-page shell without structural clues that help users recover.

## Hidden content and evidence posture

- Check whether critical answer sections are hidden in collapsed, delayed, or script-injected containers without a clear direct path.
- Preserve reviewed routes, critical section targets, navigation-alignment notes, fragment-continuity posture, and last review time.
- Do not preserve named-user clickstreams, scroll telemetry, or individualized session-replay exhaust when bounded policy reconstruction is sufficient.
