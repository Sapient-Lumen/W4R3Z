# Official voter-information multi-step progress surface checklist

Use this quickcheck when an election office needs a bounded way to keep official voter-information routes workable when the public must move through multiple ordered steps before the current answer/help lane can continue or an important submission is finalized.

## Inventory and review scope

- Identify the critical public-answer routes that span multiple pages, dynamic stages, or review/confirm steps before completion.
- Distinguish this from router/selector logic, generic field-entry review, sign-in/session review, and upload review.
- Re-check routes that branch based on prior answers, stage sensitive questions, or disclose important submission consequences only near the end.

## Process shape and progress cues

- Make the current step visible and, when practical, indicate how many steps the process includes.
- Ensure the current stage is reflected in titles/headings, not only in a visual progress bar.
- Mark optional or skippable stages clearly before the voter enters them.
- Keep next, back, and help controls easy to find without guesswork.

## State preservation, invalidation, and review

- Warn before an earlier-answer change invalidates later steps or clears later entries.
- Preserve safely preservable state across ordinary back/forward movement and validation correction.
- If save/resume exists, disclose it early; if it does not, do not imply that progress will survive interruption.
- Provide a bounded review/correct posture before finalizing important submissions.

## Guidance, accessibility, and help

- Keep critical explanatory content in-context when practical instead of forcing off-page detours that lose place.
- Test the staged route in project context on mobile, at zoom, with keyboard navigation, and with screen readers.
- Ensure headings, progress cues, warnings, review summaries, and help lanes stay consistent across the whole process.
- Keep a plainly visible first-party help or alternate recovery lane available when the staged route itself becomes the weak link.

## Evidence posture

- Preserve only route labels, reviewed process paths, progress-orientation review state, invalidation-warning review state, review/confirm posture state, save/resume posture state, and last review time.
- Do not preserve real voter answers, per-user clickstreams, long-lived session identifiers, screen recordings of real users, or exhaustive step replay when bounded policy reconstruction is sufficient.
