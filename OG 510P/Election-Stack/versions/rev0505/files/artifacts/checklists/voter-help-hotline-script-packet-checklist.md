# Voter-help hotline script-packet checklist

- Publish which public voter-help number(s) or phone-answer lane(s) are official for the election scope.
- Keep a bounded current script packet / FAQ card set / talking-points bundle for action-changing topics.
- Version the packet and publish or retain a stable digest for the current approved version.
- Name the current official source anchors the packet depends on (page, notice, directory, office-routing surface).
- Require exact packet-backed answers or live office confirmation for dates, hours, locations, ID rules, registration/update paths, absentee request/return rules, and office closures/reroutes.
- Stop on material conflict instead of improvising from memory; transfer or escalate to the responsible office/path.
- Publish after-hours behavior and degraded-service fallback rather than implying live help that does not exist.
- Keep TTY / relay / accessible-path information visible when the hotline is an official public-help lane.
- Publish language-availability or alternate language-help routing for the hotline surface.
- Update the packet when signed notices, office hours, service state, or action-changing public answers change.
- Treat hotline outage, overload, or callback-delay conditions as a public-surface state that may need a bounded notice or snapshot.
- Check parity between hotline guidance and the current website / FAQ / signed notice state.
- Keep a compact note of who last verified the packet and when.
- Do not rely on raw recordings or indefinite transcript retention when bounded version/digest evidence is enough.
- Preserve a direct path to `305` ordinary office routing and `307` rights/safety escalation when the hotline cannot safely resolve the issue.
