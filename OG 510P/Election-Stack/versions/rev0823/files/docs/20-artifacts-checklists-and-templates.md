## v582 additions (platform audience-metrics wrapper boundary)

- `artifacts/checklists/official-voter-information-platform-audience-metrics-surface-checklist.md`
  - Use when public election media routes expose view counts, concurrent-viewer labels, likes, registrations, attendance totals, or similar platform metrics that could be mistaken for proof of currentness, legitimacy, or controlling status.

- `artifacts/templates/official-voter-information-platform-audience-metrics-surface-payload.json`
  - Minimal review payload for recording the metric-bearing route, the metric names reviewed, whether counts were public or exported, and how the current written/help lane stayed controlling.

## v583 additions (platform source-identity-wrapper boundary)

- `artifacts/checklists/official-voter-information-platform-source-identity-wrapper-surface-checklist.md`
  - Use when official election media is presented with bylines, handles, verification badges, profile photos, creator URLs, uploader names, or profile cards that could be mistaken for the whole proof that the route is official and current.

- `artifacts/templates/official-voter-information-platform-source-identity-wrapper-surface-payload.json`
  - Minimal review payload for recording which source-identity cues were shown around the media route, whether they matched the office’s directory-backed channel map, and how the current written/help lane stayed controlling.
