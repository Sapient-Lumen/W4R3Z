# Official voter-information platform media provenance-signal checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if official media also carries Content Credentials, “how this content was made” disclosures, provenance pins/badges, or similar origin/history signals**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which official media routes were reviewed for provenance/authenticity signals.
- [ ] Distinguish provenance signals from source-identity wrappers, policy/context wrappers, titles/descriptions, and digest-first notice authenticity.
- [ ] Keep “this route carried provenance” separate from “this is still the current controlling office answer.”

## Presence, absence, and breakage review

- [ ] Review whether provenance was visibly present, absent, or only partially exposed on the route inspected.
- [ ] Do not treat missing provenance as proof that the media is false or altered.
- [ ] Do not treat present provenance as proof that the media is still current, unsuperseded, or legally sufficient by itself.

## Derivative-route review

- [ ] Review whether embeds, clips, downloads, screenshots, saved copies, or reuploads preserved, stripped, or materially changed provenance cues.
- [ ] Keep the original office-published object distinct from derivative routes that only restate or partially preserve provenance.
- [ ] When derivative handling breaks the provenance chain, preserve that as a bounded note rather than a blanket authenticity verdict.

## Verification-handoff review

- [ ] Record whether viewers are offered a provenance-inspection handoff or third-party verification flow.
- [ ] Keep the current official page/help lane recoverable without forcing viewers into a third-party provenance tool.
- [ ] Do not preserve large verification transcripts, upload logs, or user-specific provenance telemetry.

## Currentness and recovery review

- [ ] Check that the route still exposes a current written/help recovery path for action-changing questions.
- [ ] Review whether provenance signals could be mistaken for the office’s whole authority or supersession proof.
- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed routes, visible provenance state, derivative differences, and last review time.
