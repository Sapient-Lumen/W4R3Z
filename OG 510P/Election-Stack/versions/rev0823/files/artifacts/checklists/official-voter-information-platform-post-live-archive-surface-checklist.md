# Official voter-information platform post-live archive-surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if official voter-information media stays publicly reachable through an archived replay, ended-event page, recurring-event replay route, or other post-live shell after the live moment has passed**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which archived replay pages, ended-event shells, recurring-event routes, or post-live event players were reviewed.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from the general recording lane, social interaction replay, collection pages, pre-start event shells, and history-driven resurfacing.
- [ ] Keep “the office preserved a replay” separate from “the replay shell itself is the complete current official answer.”

## Archive / replay identity review

- [ ] Review whether general event pages and specific archive URLs are meaningfully distinguishable after the event ends.
- [ ] Review whether a stable event page, “latest video” state, or recurring-event route can quietly swap which archive a voter sees.
- [ ] Do not let archive continuity silently turn the post-live shell into the primary current-answer lane.

## Post-event routing review

- [ ] Review whether redirect, autoplay-next, playlist, or loop behavior can appear after the event ends.
- [ ] Review whether those platform-managed continuations are clearly subordinate to the current written/help route.
- [ ] Keep archival sequence or platform continuation from standing in for explicit current-answer recovery.

## Archived interaction review

- [ ] Review whether chat replay, Q&A traces, polls, comments, or adjacent interaction remain visible after the event ends.
- [ ] Review whether social residue on the replay page makes the shell feel like the office's durable help desk.
- [ ] Keep archived interaction subordinate to the current written/help lane rather than the only visible current answer path.

## Recovery and current-state review

- [ ] Keep the current written page, notice, FAQ/help entry, or named office contact recoverable from the post-live replay shell.
- [ ] Re-review replay shells that may remain public across delays, new events, cycle turnover, or superseding written guidance.
- [ ] Keep recurring-event pages and stable event links from silently carrying last event's authority into the next question.

## Authority-boundary review

- [ ] Treat archived replays, ended-event shells, and recurring-event replay routes as archival/distribution surfaces rather than proof that the controlling operational answer still lives there.
- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed replay-shell states, routing posture, recovery anchors, and last review time rather than private watch telemetry or viewer traces.
- [ ] Keep the specific replay archive distinct from the general event shell or collection surface that may host or resurface it.
