# Official voter-information platform transcript surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if a player or platform exposes official media through a transcript pane, searchable interactive transcript, or downloadable/copyable transcript sidecar**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which public recordings, embeds, clips, or audio-first routes were reviewed for transcript-pane behavior.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from the office-published video lane itself, from caption overlays during playback, and from a separately published reviewed transcript edition.
- [ ] Keep “the player/platform shows transcript text” separate from “the office published and reviewed this exact transcript edition.”

## Transcript generation and edition review

- [ ] Review whether the transcript pane is derived from reviewed caption files, autogenerated caption/transcription output, or another platform-managed transcript source.
- [ ] Keep any reviewed official transcript edition easy to identify and recover when one exists.
- [ ] Do not let autogenerated transcript text quietly stand in for a reviewed official transcript edition.

## Search and clip-jump review

- [ ] Review transcript keyword search and transcript-line click-to-jump behavior for qualifier loss on action-changing recordings.
- [ ] Keep recording-date, scope, and still-current cues near the recording or adjacent page context.
- [ ] Maintain a durable written recovery path to the current official page, notice, FAQ/help entry, or office lane when transcript search or clip-jump overstates one line.

## Portable-record and searchability review

- [ ] Review whether the platform allows copying, downloading, or exporting transcript text, and keep that distinct from an office-issued portable publication.
- [ ] Review whether transcript text affects platform/enterprise search or discovery, and keep discovery subordinate to the current written destination/help lane.
- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed routes, transcript contexts, recovery anchors, and last review time rather than user-specific transcript search or click logs.

## Authority-boundary review

- [ ] Keep transcript panes and transcript sidecars visibly subordinate to the official recording, any office-reviewed transcript edition, and the named help lane.
- [ ] Avoid treating copied/downloaded transcript text as a current official portable record by default.
- [ ] State honestly that transcript availability, search, storage, permissions, and download behavior vary by platform, player, and permission state.
