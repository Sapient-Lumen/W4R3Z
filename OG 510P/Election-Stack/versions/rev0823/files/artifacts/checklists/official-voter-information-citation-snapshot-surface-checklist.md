# Official voter-information citation-snapshot surface checklist

Use this checklist for official voter-information routes that **remain live and mutable over time yet are likely to be cited, quoted, summarized, or handed off by search, AI, chatbots, hotline scripts, media, or later reviewers**.

## Route inventory and head classification

- [ ] Record which mutable official pages are likely to function as citation anchors for action-changing voter information.
- [ ] Distinguish the current live operational head from the latest point-in-time citation-safe head for each reviewed route.
- [ ] Re-check pages where a silent live edit could materially change how a citation, AI summary, hotline answer, or media quote is interpreted later.

## Citation-head honesty review

- [ ] Review whether the office avoids implying exact point-in-time citation certainty when only a mutable live page exists.
- [ ] Review whether a bounded snapshot/capture artifact exists when the route needs a public citation-safe head.
- [ ] Review whether current-state cues on the live page remain visible enough that readers can distinguish live-now guidance from earlier captured state.
- [ ] Review whether the relationship between older and newer citation heads is expressed as superseding, correcting, or updating rather than left implicit.

## Preview/staging boundary review

- [ ] Review whether preview URLs, staged copies, drafts, or queue-state artifacts are clearly kept out of the public citation-head lane.
- [ ] Review whether staff-only reviewed copies are not mistaken for what the public actually saw.
- [ ] Review whether compact public artifacts are enough to reconstruct citation head status without exposing private CMS or editorial logs.

## Evidence posture

- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of operational-head / citation-head status, snapshot availability, preview/staging boundary policy, and last review time.
- [ ] Do not publish full internal CMS histories, private editorial diffs, or queue dashboards merely to prove that a mutable page once had a citation-safe head.
