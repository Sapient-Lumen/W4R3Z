# Official voter-information submission confirmation surface checklist

Use this quickcheck when an election office needs a bounded way to keep official voter-information routes workable after a voter submits an important request or action.

## Inventory and review scope

- Identify public routes that culminate in submission, acceptance, pending review, or another post-submit state the voter must interpret.
- Distinguish this from generic field-entry review, multi-step progress review, upload review, or packet/version governance.
- Re-check routes where deadlines are close enough that duplicate-submission uncertainty or missing confirmation can change what the voter does next.

## Outcome clarity and retained record

- State explicitly whether the action was submitted, is still pending review, needs more action, or did not submit.
- Keep any reference, case, or receipt number visible in accessible text when one exists.
- Provide a bounded record the voter can print, save, or otherwise keep, including what was submitted and when.
- Include next steps, timing cues, or expected follow-up signals instead of ending with a content-free thank-you.

## Safe retry and recovery

- Make clear whether retrying could create a duplicate, is unnecessary because the action already landed, or is appropriate only after a visible failure state.
- Do not rely only on a transient toast, dismissible overlay, or auto-closing modal as the durable confirmation state.
- Keep visible help or office-routing guidance available for cases where the confirmation state remains uncertain.
- Ensure retry/help guidance matches the underlying substantive surface and any deadline posture.

## Accessibility and reviewability

- Keep confirmation, pending, and failure messages available long enough to review on mobile, at zoom, with keyboard navigation, and with screen readers.
- Expose important result messages as meaningful status updates rather than forcing users to hunt for outcome text.
- Ensure any printable or savable record preserves essential meaning in accessible text.
- Test the destination confirmation state in project context, not just the pre-submit form.

## Evidence posture

- Preserve only route labels, reviewed confirmation paths, outcome/retry review state, retained-record posture state, and last review time.
- Do not preserve completed voter submissions, uploaded personal documents, broad per-user confirmation-number logs, or exhaustive clickstreams when bounded policy reconstruction is sufficient.
