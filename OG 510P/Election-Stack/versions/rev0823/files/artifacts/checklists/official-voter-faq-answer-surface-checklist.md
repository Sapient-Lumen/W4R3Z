# Official voter FAQ answer-surface checklist

- Publish which FAQ/help page, article cluster, or knowledge-base lane is official for the election scope.
- Keep action-changing FAQ/help entries versioned or editioned enough that the office can reconstruct which answer controlled at time `T`.
- Name the current official source anchors the FAQ/help entry depends on (page, notice, directory, office-routing surface).
- Keep the article inside the correct underlying voter-question family instead of letting a generic FAQ absorb adjacent rule surfaces.
- Require a visible office-routing handoff for unresolved or record-specific questions (`305`).
- Preserve a direct path to `307` when the issue has become intimidation, discrimination, wrongful denial, emergency safety, or another escalation case.
- Stop on material conflict instead of silently smoothing over stale or inconsistent official sources.
- Treat material answer changes as superseding article states or linked notices rather than silent overwrites.
- Check parity between the web FAQ/help entry, printable/downloadable version where offered, translated variants where applicable, hotline packet, and signed notices.
- Use plain language, clear structure, and accessible presentation for online voter-information articles.
- Keep `last_reviewed_at` current enough that near-deadline or same-day answers are not presented as timeless prose.
- Publish or retain a stable pointer to the current answer edition/digest for action-changing entries.
- Do not rely on full CMS revision histories, analytics exports, or raw internal editing commentary when bounded public-surface evidence is enough.
