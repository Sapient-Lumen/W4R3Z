# Official voter-information media release checklist

- Inventory the official newsroom / press-release page, press feed, media-advisory lane, and any written briefing-summary or transcript page the office uses.
- Make the delivery role explicit: press releases, public statements, media advisories, and spokesperson remarks are delivery lanes for voter information, not standalone replacements for the current controlling page/notice/help path.
- Distinguish artifact types clearly: press release, public statement, media advisory, and briefing summary should not silently collapse into one another.
- Do not place a material voter instruction only inside a media advisory, quoted remark, press conference answer, or livestream clip.
- For action-changing updates, include scope, timing, affected voters/locations, the current official destination for full detail, and the public help path.
- During active voting periods, make clear whether affected voters should continue as normal or follow a changed instruction; do not assume the press will infer the safe next step.
- Identify which spokesperson roles are authorized to speak for the office and keep talking points synchronized with the latest official page/notice/help state.
- Do not let unsynchronized spokesperson quotes become de facto policy updates.
- If a briefing or interview contains action-changing voter guidance, publish a bounded written follow-up (release, statement, FAQ/help update, signed notice, or briefing summary/transcript) that ordinary users can reach directly.
- Publish explicit corrections or superseding releases when a material public answer changes; do not rely only on quiet newsroom edits.
- Keep the newsroom / release page aligned with the linked official page, FAQ/help article, hotline packet, and office-routing surface that govern the same answer.
- Use plain language and accessible publication formats; provide captions or transcripts when live or recorded briefings carry action-changing instructions.
- Do not perform record-specific casework or solicit sensitive personal details through press interactions.
- Preserve a bounded digestable record of the media-release surface policy and material corrections without defaulting to clipping services, private inquiry archives, or full raw media-monitoring exports.
- Publish `last_verified_at` for the surface and re-check the policy after major incident-routing, deadline, site, or office-contact changes.
