# Official voter-information platform channel-collection-surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if official recordings are grouped into a platform-native channel home, featured-video slot, playlist, showcase, or other collection page**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which channel homes, featured-video slots, playlists, showcases, or collection pages were reviewed.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from player-driven play-next handoffs, viewer-managed saved-media shelves, follow/reminder state, and history/resume resurfacing.
- [ ] Keep “the office grouped these recordings together” separate from “this collection is itself the complete current answer map.”

## Collection-layout and ordering review

- [ ] Review which item appears first, is featured most prominently, or is otherwise highlighted as the likely first stop.
- [ ] Review whether sort order, custom ordering, or featured shelves can cause stale or historical recordings to outrank the current explainer.
- [ ] Do not let visual prominence or collection order stand in for explicit freshness or currentness review.

## Scope and aging review

- [ ] Review whether evergreen, archival, and election-cycle-specific recordings are mixed inside the same collection.
- [ ] Review whether collection titles, descriptions, thumbnails, or card-detail settings overclaim the scope or freshness of included items.
- [ ] Keep historical transparency recordings distinct from action-changing current instruction paths.

## Portability and sharing review

- [ ] Review whether the collection can be shared or embedded outside the office website.
- [ ] Review whether shared or embedded collection variants still preserve practical recovery to the current written/help lane.
- [ ] Keep exported collection layout and ordering from silently becoming the only visible official router.

## Recovery and current-state review

- [ ] Keep the current written page, notice, FAQ/help entry, or named office contact recoverable from the collection surface.
- [ ] Treat time-sensitive topics as requiring explicit recheck-the-current-route recovery rather than reliance on collection order alone.
- [ ] Re-review routes where favorites, alerts, or homepage quick-access shelves can make the collection feel more current than the office intended.

## Authority-boundary review

- [ ] Keep channel homes, playlists, showcases, and collection pages visibly subordinate to the current written/help lane.
- [ ] Avoid treating collection curation as proof that every included recording is still current and mutually consistent.
- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed collection surfaces, expected order/featured state, recovery anchors, and last review time rather than private analytics or viewer traces.
