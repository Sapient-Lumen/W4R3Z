# Official voter-information platform follow/reminder surface checklist

Use this checklist when an election office needs the **current answer/help lane to remain reconstructible even if already-open official media or official platform channels can be followed, subscribed to, or registered for a platform-native reminder**.

## Scope and boundary review

- [ ] Record which public channels, recordings, premieres, scheduled livestreams, or event pages were reviewed for follow/subscribe/reminder behavior.
- [ ] Distinguish this surface from the recording lane itself, from browser-origin web push, from calendar exports, and from saved-media shelves.
- [ ] Keep “the voter followed or subscribed in the platform” separate from “the office operates a durable complete notice lane it directly controls.”

## Relationship and delivery review

- [ ] Review whether relationship state is follow, subscribe, notification-bell selection, or single-event reminder registration.
- [ ] Review whether delivery is personalized, all-available, delayed, unsupported, or otherwise platform-limited.
- [ ] Do not let a platform-managed follow or reminder state imply guaranteed completeness, unchanged authority, or unchanged currentness.

## Feed, inbox, and adjacency review

- [ ] Review whether subscriptions feeds, notification inboxes, or followed-channel views can mix recommendations, unrelated channels, or older items beside the official item.
- [ ] Review whether the same relationship can appear differently across browser, mobile app, branded app, or account settings.
- [ ] Keep recommendation or discovery adjacency distinct from the current official answer lane.

## Reminder and export review

- [ ] Review whether scheduled-event reminders can fire after the current written route changed and whether the reminder still points clearly back to that route.
- [ ] Review whether reminders can also be exported to a device calendar or other reminder surface and whether that export stays subordinate to the current written lane.
- [ ] Re-review routes where reminder timing, relationship defaults, or notification support can change without the office rewriting the surrounding page.

## Recovery and authority-boundary review

- [ ] Keep the current written page, notice, FAQ/help entry, or named office contact recoverable from the follow/reminder route.
- [ ] Treat volatile or corrected topics as requiring explicit recheck-the-current-route recovery rather than “followers will be notified.”
- [ ] Preserve a small public digest of reviewed routes, relationship contexts, delivery limits, recovery anchors, and last review time rather than subscriber rosters, notification histories, or watch histories.
