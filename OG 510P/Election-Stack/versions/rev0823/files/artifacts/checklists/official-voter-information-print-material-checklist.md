# Official voter-information print-material checklist

- Inventory the official printable/downloadable voter-information lane: flyers, postcards, brochures, pocket guides, booklets, infographic cards, downloadable PDFs, and similar handouts the office expects the public or partner organizations to use.
- Make the delivery role explicit: printable artifacts are portable delivery lanes for voter information, not standalone replacements for the current controlling page/notice/help path.
- For action-changing materials, include the question covered, jurisdiction/election scope, an edition label or issue date, the current official destination, and the help route.
- Do not publish detached print assets with no visible time scope, no help path, and no way to tell whether the reader is holding a stale copy.
- Keep a human-readable official URL or help route visible even when the artifact also uses a QR code or shortlink.
- Do not hide crucial instructions only behind the QR destination; the printed piece should remain understandable if the code cannot be scanned.
- Keep printable artifacts aligned with the linked webpage, FAQ/help article, hotline packet, and signed notice that govern the same answer.
- Use plain language and readable layout; provide accessible downloadable variants and translated/community-language variants where the jurisdiction offers them.
- Do not let translated flyers, accessible PDFs, postcard mailers, and web copies drift into different effective answers.
- Publish explicit corrections or superseding print editions when a material public answer changes; do not rely only on silent website updates.
- Where physical distribution matters, give front desks and partner organizations a bounded stale-stock retirement instruction for old handouts.
- Preserve a bounded digestable record of the print-material policy and material superseding events without defaulting to inventory systems, vendor records, or recipient lists.
- Keep record-specific casework and sensitive personal-data collection out of general-purpose printed materials; route those matters to the official help or secure channel instead.
- Publish `last_verified_at` for the lane and re-check the policy after major date, location, office-contact, ballot-path, or incident-routing changes.
