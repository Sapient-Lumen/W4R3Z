# VERIFIED-TEST

This note asks whether `verified` really belongs inside the minimal `evidence_posture` axis.

## Question

After the thin traceability model survived P4,
is `verified` still part of the thinnest reusable hook,
or is it better treated as an overlay or stronger profile-local process?

## Current result

**Move `verified` out of the minimal hook.**

The minimal `evidence_posture` axis is now:
- `claimed`
- `traceable`
- `unknown`

`verified` may still exist,
but not as part of the thinnest common hook.

## Why this reduction is justified

### 1. `traceable` is already a substantive state
The source base treats traceability as depending on a recognized reference plus known and documented uncertainty.
That is already much stronger than a casual claim.
It is enough to do real hook work.

### 2. Finance uses verification as an extra layer
The finance material does not merely say “traceable.”
It also describes additional arrangements to verify that the delivered time remains accurate near the customer.
That looks like a stronger overlay or service,
not the minimum common boundary state.

### 3. P5 and P4 do not force the extra state
P5 presses traceability to UTC with time quality and leap-status.
P4 presses traceability state plus adjacent holdover / control behavior.
Neither currently forces a general `verified` state in the hook.

## Archive judgment

The minimal traceability hook should not absorb every stronger assurance practice.
So:
- keep `verified` available as a profile-local overlay or adjacent process
- remove it from the smallest reusable axis

## New minimal evidence axis

### `claimed`
A reference tie is asserted,
but the archive is not treating it as traceable in the metrological sense.

### `traceable`
The tie is strong enough to be treated as traceable to the stated anchor.

### `unknown`
No useful evidence posture is currently available.

## What changed conceptually

The hook is now cleaner:
- `reference_anchor` answers what it is tied to
- `evidence_posture` answers how seriously to treat that tie at minimum
- stronger verification belongs adjacent to the hook

## Next useful move

The next reduction question is no longer on the evidence axis.
It is on `reference_anchor`:
can the archive keep anchor names literal,
or should it reduce them into categories without losing too much?
