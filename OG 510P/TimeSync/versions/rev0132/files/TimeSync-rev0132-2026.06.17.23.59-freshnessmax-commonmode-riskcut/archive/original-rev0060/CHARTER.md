# CHARTER

## Purpose

TimeSync exists to determine what a trustworthy time synchronization system should be at global scale while remaining honest about local failure, operational dependence, and institutional reality.

## Primary aims

1. Keep the problem frame ahead of premature architecture.
2. Preserve both practical and greenfield design space.
3. Use comparison surfaces to discover what is invariant.
4. Turn invariant claims into minimal, testable core objects.
5. Keep the archive compact, cumulative, and legible.
