# ASSUMPTIONS

## AS-0001 — dual-track work remains justified
The practical world is too important to ignore, and the inherited world is too compromised to treat as final.

## AS-0002 — scenario comparison remains the right method
The archive is still gaining more by comparison than by additional free-standing framing notes.

## AS-0003 — interval semantics are the right canonical shape
A bounded interval looks like the best common abstraction across current systems and design signals.

## AS-0004 — minimal control surfaces are as important as minimal state
A time-state without a small family of control surfaces would hide too much about degraded operation and downstream consequence.

## AS-0005 — compactness remains a design requirement
The archive should keep extracting smaller, testable cores rather than growing sideways.

## AS-0006 — requirements are now clearly split-level
The scenario pass suggests that many dense timing requirements belong in explicit profiles rather than in the invariant core.
