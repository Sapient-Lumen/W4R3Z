# CHARTER

## Purpose

TimeSync exists to figure out what a trustworthy, globally relevant time synchronization system should actually be.

## Primary aims

1. Avoid premature commitment to the wrong system shape.
2. Treat uncertainty, provenance, and failure regime as first-class.
3. Keep technical and governance questions coupled where they actually are coupled.
4. Preserve a durable design record so the project can continue across long horizons.
5. Prefer bounded, inspectable progress over impressive but fragile totalizations.

## What counts as progress

Progress means turning ambiguity into named, inspectable surfaces such as:

- a crisp problem statement,
- a vocabulary that does not launder uncertainty,
- a map of candidate identities for TimeSync,
- explicit contradictions,
- explicit assumptions,
- live frontier questions,
- and small, revisable design objects.

## Failure modes to resist

- mistaking "secure protocol" for "trustworthy timing system"
- mistaking agreement for correctness
- mistaking correctness for governability
- mistaking one sector's requirements for universal requirements
- mistaking clean architecture drawings for operational truth
- mistaking early enthusiasm for justified commitment

## Working posture

TimeSync begins as a careful inquiry with a bias toward explicitness, not as a finished design.
