# LivingChristFigures

## Help that does not take possession

LivingChristFigures studies the work of making care, recognition and passage possible for people whom ordinary institutions leave outside. Its question is not simply who deserves admiration. It asks **what a practice makes possible, who carries its costs, and whether receiving help gives the helper power over the recipient's life or story**. Food, transport, a reachable record, a place to grieve and time to decide are substantial goods. They need not become purchases of gratitude, allegiance or narrative ownership.

The project's central figure is a *threshold office*: a recurring responsibility at a point where money, paperwork, mobility, kinship, respectability or a particular identity controls access. An office need not be a building, job title or extraordinary individual. It can be a household, collective, service, administrative handoff or repeated act of staying. The difficulty is double: make a real way through, and limit the authority acquired by providing it. Perfectly restrained assistance that never reaches anyone misses the first demand; effective assistance that claims the person in return misses the second.

Start with [Threshold Offices: Costly Mercy Without Ownership][edition], the rev0105 reading edition. Its six connected essays develop the earlier research into a comparative argument. The historical cube supplies office cards, examples, claims, sources and disagreements; the reading edition turns that material into sustained prose a reader can inspect without first mastering the ledgers. It is an authored interpretation, not a new survey or certification of the organizations in the archive.

## Six ways to read a threshold

The essays are connected by a problem, not by an assertion that all forms of care do the same thing.

1. **Recognition after a loss.** Identification, burial, accessible memory and family authority answer different needs. A correct name does not settle a life's meaning; a burial does not settle responsibility for a death. The [earlier name-after-death essay][name-essay] develops these separations. Rev0105 adds an important pressure against automatic obscurity: public witness can be chosen and necessary. Protecting people from appropriation must not become an editor's unilateral authority to make them disappear again.
2. **Moving the door.** Street medicine makes an institutional burden visible by reversing it: care travels toward someone who cannot readily reach the clinic. The [office card][street] separates clinical attention, consent, knowledge of where a person lives and housing justice. Access can improve while surveillance power also grows. “Outreach is not housing” preserves the unfinished obligation; it does not make the immediate care worthless.
3. **An exit that leaves a choice.** Safe-exit and religious-exit practices raise the difference between a genuinely useful passage and every condition attached to it. A person may reasonably prefer an imperfect offer to danger without thereby justifying a demand for conversion, publicity or permanent allegiance. The essay asks which conditions enable the passage, which protect other participants, and which serve the provider's additional ends. An exit should leave choices after the crossing, not merely transfer control.
4. **Staying while the answer is delayed.** A meal, warmth or a charged phone matters while a larger institution has not resolved someone's claim. Repeated maintenance is morally significant work, not a lesser version of a dramatic rescue. The [border-waiting card][waiting] also resists making people scenery for the helper's devotion. Immediate provision and responsibility for the continuing constraint require different accounts: feeding someone today neither repairs nor excuses the institution that keeps them waiting.
5. **Making a right usable.** The [law-to-door card][law] follows the distance between a formal right and a reachable protection: explanation, transport, confidentiality, enforcement and handoffs can determine whether a person can use it. Administrative completion or reconciliation cannot simply substitute for the claimant's safety and agency. A broken implementation does not make the right meaningless, but the existence of the right is not evidence that the passage works.
6. **Knowing when a question has changed.** The [service-gap card][gap] refuses to invent an exemplary provider where the evidence has not established a usable door. The reading edition distinguishes three statements: a service has not been found; no service exists; this project will not collect further information. None entails the others. Narrowing an optional comparison can be better than pursuing intimate details, but a stopping decision must remain open to criticism rather than shelter a favored interpretation from correction.

These are ways into the essays, not present-day service recommendations. They also make the work useful beyond its original examples: a reader can examine where a process transfers burdens, acquires power or claims premature completion without awarding a score to the people involved.

## How the comparison works

The reading edition's **What travels between offices** offers five questions:

- What must someone supply before help begins?
- Which part of that burden could the institution carry instead?
- What power to locate, classify, narrate, direct or retain does the helper acquire?
- What duty remains after this particular act succeeds?
- Who can refuse an additional use, correct the account, challenge a condition or leave?

Consider an explicitly hypothetical service that removes fees and provides transport but makes assistance conditional on a promotional interview. It carries an access burden while creating narrative dependence. Conversely, an organization might protect every right of refusal yet provide so little material help that the offered options remain nominal. These are different failures. Sincere concern alone does not distinguish them; nor does a count of people reached.

The questions are a comparative method, not a rating instrument. Recognition after death cannot be tested by asking whether the deceased can now exit. A meal is not an enforceable right. The point is to preserve the distinct good accomplished while making the resulting powers and unfinished duties discussable. Nonpossession also does not mean absence of all authority: finite services can require decisions, expertise and protections for others. The reasons for those conditions remain part of the inquiry.

## From an example to an argument

The rev0104 [mission charter][charter] separates five units that an admiring story can otherwise collapse:

- **Office:** the practice being compared.
- **Candidate:** a person, collective, program, law or institution serving as a possible example, not a rank or moral certificate.
- **Claim:** a particular factual statement, interpretation or caution.
- **Source:** evidence for that bounded statement, not blanket authority for everything said about the example.
- **Evidence debt or boundary:** what remains unknown, requires a narrower claim, or should not be pursued for this project's purpose.

The earlier [burial boundary essay][burial] gives the distinction substance. “Unclaimed,” “unidentified,” “indigent” and “family unwilling” do not describe the same situation. An administrative category cannot supply the missing account of family knowledge, resources or consent. Likewise, searchable records can restore access without licensing every republication of their contents. An office's documented shape, the interpretation of its significance and evidence of its outcomes must remain separable.

The [source trail][trail] connects each rev0105 essay to exact rev0104 files. It gives the interpretation something to answer to. It does not make the earlier evidence current or establish agreement from participants. The substantial achievement is a readable argument with a route back to its materials, rather than a claim that the materials have settled every disagreement.

## Development without a triumphal story

The early rev0002 [framing][early] already distinguishes persons from offices; its [counterweights][counterweights] reject confusing awards with virtue and insist that the person helped remain central. The later emphasis on practices therefore sharpens an existing concern rather than discovering restraint after an entirely uncritical beginning.

The history also contains a criticism of the project's own construction. The carried [rev0103 diagnostic][diagnostic] argues that reports, mirrors and release machinery had become more legible than the human insight they were meant to support. Rev0104's [thin-core mode][thin] starts consolidating the evidence-task views and adds a substantive office essay. Its [revision summary][revision] records the conversion of two unresolved consent/category debts into boundaries on inference, not newly established facts. The proposed restricted/reviewer/public architecture was still a proposal, not access control supplied by a label.

Rev0105 responds with six essays and a comparison, while leaving the historical snapshots intact. It does not implement the proposed vault, community-review process or takedown system. Nor does it treat every earlier course correction as vindicated: its objections defend positive assistance, warn that caution can become self-protective, and deny that a smaller selection is automatically truer. This is a substantive literary-philosophical descendant, not a failed service directory or merely an archive of precautions.

## A situated theological language

The title retains a question about costly, faithful care. Its religious imagination makes ordinary maintenance and refusal of abandonment available to moral attention; neutral classification is not the only legitimate form of thought. But the metaphor belongs to the interpretation. It does not assign Christian identity, redemptive status or religious commitment to the people being discussed. A reader can contest that frame and still examine the practices and arguments it brings into view.

Read beside [Parables](../Parables/README.md) for literary compression of an act of care, and [DeathVocab](../DeathVocab/README.md) for the relation between a description, a vulnerable moment and the authority to speak about it. Their methods differ: these office essays compare burdens and powers; DeathVocab organizes language around scenes and evidence roles. Neither should make a person interchangeable with the category used to understand them.

### Preservation and use

Every original filename and ZIP byte is retained. Each version's files directory retains the complete original ZIP member path, including its enclosing export directory. Generated guides and machine inventories sit outside those historical paths. No source was silently corrected, renamed or redacted. See [technical review](STATIC_REVIEW.md), [carried rights](CARRIED-RIGHTS.md), and [machine provenance](machine/provenance-and-versions.json).

Archived public contact, service or event details can be stale. This presentation supplies no new person/contact/referral index and is not a service directory, medical or legal recommendation, or verification of an organization's operation. Historical work orders and scripts are preserved as evidence and were not run.
