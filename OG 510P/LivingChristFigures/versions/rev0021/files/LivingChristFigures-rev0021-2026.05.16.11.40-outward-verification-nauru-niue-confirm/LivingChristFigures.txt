LIVING CHRIST FIGURES — rev0020

This is a law-to-door, anti-coerced-reconciliation, and Pacific legal-implementation revision.

The ledger now has 65 candidates and 41 office cards. Eight candidates were added:

- Nauru counselling and social-work qualification pool / service-capacity precursor.

- Regional Working Group on the Implementation of Family Protection / Domestic Violence Legislation.

Three office cards were added:
- Law to door — family protection implementation.
- GBV health first responder and evidence chain.
- Service gap map and negative space.

Rule change:
- CUBE-RULES rule 17 now says that a law, order, MoU, working group, or legal-capacity review is not a survivor-accessible office unless connected to safe, confidential, non-coercive support; compulsory or reconciliation-oriented counselling is flagged as an ownership risk.

Meaning note:
rev0020 keeps the central sentence under pressure: the office is the threshold held open without owning the one who crosses it. Family-protection law can hold open a threshold, but it can also send a survivor back into the family, church, court, police, or community structure that owns the next step. The revision admits legal implementation work only with this caution: a law is not mercy until it can be crossed safely.
