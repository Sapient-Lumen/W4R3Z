CUBE RULES — as of rev0020

This file consolidates the rules that have accumulated across revisions, so that the SearchTrails' "standing rules" list does not stay the only home for load-bearing constraints. Rev0016 corrected the duplicate rule numbering that appeared in rev0015 and added the file-naming convention requested by Johnny. Rev0018 added an operational-safety rule for shelter, helpline, refuge, and survivor-care entries. Rev0019 added a capacity/status distinction rule for helplines, referral networks, frameworks, training pools, and first crisis centres. Rev0020 adds a law-to-door / anti-coerced-reconciliation rule for family-protection and domestic-violence legal frameworks.

1. DEFINITIONAL RULE (rev0010, retained)
"Christ figure" is a severe metaphor for costly mercy, not a theological claim and not an identification of divinity.

2. CENTRAL SENTENCE (rev0010, retained)
The office is the threshold held open without owning the one who crosses it.

3. DOOR ETHICS (rev0010, retained)
See DOOR-ETHICS.txt. Nine questions, all still live. Question 3 is specifically about images, not names; do not confuse the two.

4. STATUS CATEGORIES (rev0010 onward)
KEEP; PROVISIONAL KEEP; SCOUT / POSSIBLE KEEP; OFFICE-CANON; PERSON-BENCHMARK / HOLD; NETWORK-CANDIDATE / INSTITUTION-CANDIDATE (rev0011); COMMUNAL-DOCTRINAL (rev0012). The cube allows entries to carry multiple statuses.

5. SOURCING RULES (rev0010 onward, retained)
Facebook-only leads held until non-Facebook corroboration. State-media-only leads flagged and refreshed. Where a religious or communal authority has done threshold work, the cube records both the welcome and its stated limits in the same entry (rev0012).

6. CANDIDACY RULES (rev0010 onward, retained but loosened over time)
Unknown / non-powerful work is preferred. Famous figures and well-funded networks may be admitted as PERSON-BENCHMARK or OFFICE-CANON when the office is unintelligible without them. Recognition does not by itself disqualify; the cube has bent this many times and rev0011 made the bend explicit.

7. PRECONDITION RULE (rev0013, retained)
Conversion-required offices are flagged with an explicit precondition flag. The cube does not let "the alternative is death" silence the question of what the office still asks of the person it helps — conversion to a frame, sobriety, gratitude, public identity, photogenic suffering.

8. PEER / HELPER DISTINCTION (rev0013, retained)
Peer-led offices are a distinct shape from helper-led offices and carry distinct DOOR-ETHICS readings — particularly question 1 ("Can the person leave the helper?"), which reads differently when the helper is also being helped.

9. ERROR-LOGGING RULE (rev0013, retained, now general)
The cube logs its own errors honestly rather than retroactively rewriting earlier files. SearchTrails, REASONING-NOTEs, and REFRESH notes are the right places for corrections; earlier files retain their original wording with a refresh or correction noted alongside.

10. NAMING RULE (rev0014, retained)
The cube does not censor what its sources publish openly, but names in cube prose must be load-bearing rather than decorative. Source-side anonymisation is respected. DOOR-ETHICS question 3 remains about images; question 9 now asks whether names are load-bearing.

11. ANTI-BROCHURE RULE (rev0010 onward, retained)
The cube does not iterate into a brochure for any single organisation, home, or network. Description that reads like fundraising copy is the warning sign.

12. OFFICE-CARDS-AS-LENSES, NOT BINS (rev0013, retained)
A single person or network can hold several offices open simultaneously. The cards are descriptions of shapes of mercy, not exclusive categories. Cube entries may cite multiple office cards.

13. LINEAGE / SUCCESSOR RULE (rev0015)
Where a candidate file was originally framed around a named founder and later searching shows the founder has left, split from, or been succeeded by the organisation, the cube records lineage, successor leadership, and current office separately.

14. REVISION FILE-NAMING RULE (rev0016)
Beginning with rev0016, exported cube zip filenames use this pattern:
LivingChristFigures-rev####-YYYY.MM.DD.HH.MM-summary-highlight-codename.zip
The timestamp is in the user's America/New_York timezone unless the user asks otherwise. The internal root folder should match the zip filename without the .zip extension. Older archived zips keep their historical filenames.


15. OPERATIONAL-SAFETY RULE FOR SHELTERS, HELPLINES, AND SURVIVOR OFFICES (rev0018)
When an entry involves a shelter, safe house, crisis/refuge service, helpline, or survivor-care route, the cube records only public, load-bearing institutional shape. Do not add precise shelter locations, routes, staff rosters, private phone trees, survivor identifiers, child images, or operational details that could increase risk. If a public source publishes sensitive material, the cube still asks whether repeating it is necessary.


16. CAPACITY / CURRENT-STATUS DISTINCTION RULE (rev0019)
When sources show a helpline, referral network, training pool, legal framework, counselling framework, crisis-centre launch, or donor-supported project, the cube distinguishes public institutional shape from current accessible capacity. Training is not the same as service; a public number is not a guaranteed live referral; a framework is not implementation; a first centre is often evidence of scarcity. Entries must flag current-status uncertainty, donor dependence, outer-island access, survivor-choice limits, and implementation gaps rather than smoothing them into success language.


17. LAW-TO-DOOR / ANTI-COERCED-RECONCILIATION RULE (rev0020)
A family-protection law, domestic-violence law, safety order, protection order, court page, implementation adviser, inter-ministerial memorandum, working group, or legal-capacity review is not counted as a survivor-accessible office unless the cube can describe a real path toward safe, confidential, non-coercive support. Legal frameworks must be distinguished from enforcement, accompaniment, shelter/safe accommodation, health care, legal aid, transport, language access, disability access, privacy protection, and current service capacity. Counselling, mediation, church/community intervention, or family-preservation language must be flagged when it is compulsory, joint, reconciliation-oriented, or likely to pressure the survivor to keep the family together at the expense of safety and consent.
