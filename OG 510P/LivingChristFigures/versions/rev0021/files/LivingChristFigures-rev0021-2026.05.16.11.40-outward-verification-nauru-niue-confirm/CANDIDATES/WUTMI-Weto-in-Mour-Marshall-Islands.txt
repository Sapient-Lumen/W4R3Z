NAME: Women United Together Marshall Islands (WUTMI) / Weto In Mour
LOCATION: Republic of the Marshall Islands; Majuro and Ebeye public line
OFFICE: Weto In Mour “Place of Life” counselling and support service for women and girl survivors
STATUS: KEEP; NORTH-PACIFIC FIRST-COUNSELLING-SERVICE LINE; OUTER-ISLAND-ACCESS AND DONOR-FUNDING FLAGS

SUMMARY:
Women United Together Marshall Islands operates Weto In Mour, translated as “A Place of Life,” a Violence Against Women and Girls support service. Pacific Data Hub describes WIM as RMI’s first counselling and support service for women and girl survivors of gender-based violence. A 2021 WUTMI media release says the Ebeye service was launched to expand support from the original Majuro WIM service to island/remote locations; the Ebeye centre provides support to survivors of violence plus education and awareness to help prevent gender-based violence. Pacific Women Lead describes WIM counselling centres in Majuro and Ebeye as one of only two North Pacific crisis-centre lines providing counselling services for survivors of violence against women.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office is not only a counselling room; it is the attempt to make a place-of-life reachable in an atoll state where “come to the centre” may already be an impossible instruction. The threshold is counselling, referral, awareness, and outer-island expansion.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- First RMI counselling/support service for women and girl GBV survivors.
- Majuro and Ebeye service line.
- Survivor support plus education/awareness.
- Referral-network improvement.
- North Pacific telephone-counselling and supervision lineage.

EVIDENCE:
1. Pacific Data Hub WUTMI record: WIM is RMI’s first counselling and support service for women and girl survivors of GBV.
2. WUTMI 2021 Ebeye media release: new Ebeye service expands original Majuro WIM to island/remote locations and provides survivor support plus education/awareness.
3. Pacific Women Lead North Pacific article: WIM and TICC are the two North Pacific crisis centres providing counselling to survivors of violence against women.

DOOR-ETHICS FLAGS:
- Do not expose survivor stories, shelter/safe-haven specifics, or staff-operational details.
- Funding line includes Pacific Women and Spotlight Initiative; future pass should check current service capacity and locally controlled evaluation.
- Maintain distinction between prevention/awareness and crisis response.
