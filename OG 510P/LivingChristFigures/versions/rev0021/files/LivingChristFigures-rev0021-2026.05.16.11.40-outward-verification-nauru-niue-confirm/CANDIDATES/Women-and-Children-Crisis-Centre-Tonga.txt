NAME: Women and Children Crisis Centre (WCCC) Tonga
LOCATION: Tonga
OFFICE: Island feminist crisis centre / helpline, refuge, counselling, mobile one-stop survivor threshold
STATUS: KEEP; PACIFIC SURVIVOR-CARE FIND; NGO-CANDIDATE WITH WEBSITE-QUALITY AND SAFETY FLAGS

SUMMARY:
WCCC Tonga presents itself as an NGO working for a Tonga free from violence, disempowerment, and discrimination against women and children in all their diversity. Its public materials describe counselling, refuge, advocacy, safety planning, 24/7 crisis intervention, safe housing, counselling and advocacy, mobile counselling, psychosocial support during crises and emergencies, and a 24-hour toll-free helpline (0800444) for immediate assistance and basic counselling to survivors of violence. A June 2025 mobile-counselling note describes trained counsellors, legal advisors, nurses, and police services being brought directly to remote island communities through a one-stop model.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office is the door brought closer to the woman or child who cannot safely reach the door: helpline, refuge, mobile counselling, legal/medical/police accompaniment, and the possibility of privacy close to home.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- 24-hour toll-free helpline.
- Counselling, refuge, advocacy, safety planning.
- Safe-housing / temporary safety threshold.
- Mobile one-stop services for outer-island communities.
- Youth safety and relationship education.

EVIDENCE:
1. WCCC home/about pages: Tonga free from violence and discrimination; counselling, refuge, advocacy; nationwide 24-hour toll-free helpline; English/Tongan support.
2. WCCC site navigation and about page: response services include safety planning, 24/7 crisis intervention, safe housing, counselling/advocacy, mobile counselling, and psychosocial support during crises and emergencies.
3. June 2025 mobile-counselling article: WCCC brought trained counsellors, legal advisors, nurses, and police services directly to Fotua and Fangale’ounga through a one-stop service model.
