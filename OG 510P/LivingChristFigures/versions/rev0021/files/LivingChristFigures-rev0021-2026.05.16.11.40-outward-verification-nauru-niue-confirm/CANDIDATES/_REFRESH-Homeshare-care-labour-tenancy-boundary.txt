SUBJECT: Homeshare as protected-housing comparator, not substitute care

Homeshare UK / HomeShare International is a useful comparator for the elder-house line because it shows a threshold of ordinary night-presence rather than a new building or institution. But it also carries a sharp exploitation risk.

Future checks needed:
- Does the matching provider define and enforce the boundary between companionship/light help and personal care?
- What fees are paid, to whom, and what tenancy or license protections exist?
- What safeguarding, background checks, complaints, breakdown/exit procedures, and emergency escalation routes are in place?
- Who is excluded because their care needs are too high?
- Are participant voices available beyond feel-good stories?

Do not let the cube turn homeshare into a cheap substitute for public social care or a housing-market workaround that hides domestic labour.
