SUMMARY:
FFLAG is a registered charity in England and Wales supporting families with LGBT+ loved ones. Its public materials describe compassionate guidance and support for families, information/resources to help families stand against homophobia and transphobia, parent stories, a confidential helpline, email support, Zoom peer support, parent groups, UK affiliate groups, and free downloadable guides for family/friends of gay, lesbian, bisexual, transgender, and non-binary people. Its Parent Groups page lists UK parent groups, affiliate groups, and parent-family organisations in other countries.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office is the parent taking their panic, shame, fear, or confusion to another adult rather than laying it on the child. The child or young person is not the doorway for the parent’s entire conversion of imagination.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- Confidential helpline.
- Parent peer groups and affiliate groups.
- Free guides and resource packs.
- International family-support map.
- Public stance against homophobia and transphobia.

DOOR-ETHICS FLAGS:

- UK gender-politics environment makes trans inclusion especially contested; keep youth autonomy and safeguarding visible.
