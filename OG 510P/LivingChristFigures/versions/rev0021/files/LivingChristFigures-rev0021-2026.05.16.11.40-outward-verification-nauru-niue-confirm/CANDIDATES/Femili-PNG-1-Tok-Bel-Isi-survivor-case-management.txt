NAME: Femili PNG / 1-Tok Kaunselin Helpim Lain / Bel Isi PNG
LOCATION: Papua New Guinea
OFFICE: Family and sexual violence survivor case-management / safety planning / safe-house doorway
STATUS: KEEP; SURVIVOR-SAFETY AND CONFIDENTIALITY FLAGS; LOCAL-LED WITH DONOR/PROGRAMME COMPLEXITY

SUMMARY:
Femili PNG provides free case management for survivors of family and sexual violence, with support centres in Lae, Port Moresby, and Goroka. Its caseworkers help clients move through immediate safety, longer-term planning, legal/medical/police/court referrals, and practical support. Bel Isi PNG adds a public-private-community-government service pathway including safe-house and case-management support. The 1-Tok Kaunselin Helpim Lain is a national counselling helpline established in 2015 and expanded during COVID-19, with counselling, safety planning, suicide intervention, and referral.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office is the survivor not having to cross police, welfare, hospital, court, transport, and housing thresholds alone. The mercy is not a dramatic rescue scene; it is accompanied navigation in a dangerous institutional landscape.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- Survivor case management.
- Safety planning and crisis counselling.
- Safe-house / emergency accommodation threshold.
- Legal/medical/police/court referral and accompaniment.

EVIDENCE:
1. Femili PNG official site: helps survivors of family and sexual violence access support and care; free case-management centres in Lae, Port Moresby, and Goroka.
2. Devpolicy tenth-anniversary report: PNG NGO, specialist FSV case management, approximately 70 staff and roughly 100 new clients monthly, working with police, welfare, hospitals, courts, churches, NGOs.
3. Femili PNG Australia: local PNG staff and support centres; Bel Isi safe-house pathway; thousands of clients served since 2014.
4. Australian High Commission: Bel Isi partnership and integrated case management to counselling, safe house, emergency accommodation, police, medical, referrals, advocacy, protection orders, and court.
5. UNICEF / ChildFund: 1-Tok national helpline, confidential phone counselling since 2015, 23,000+ calls in 2019, crisis counselling, safety planning, suicide intervention, and referral.
6. ANU evaluation summary: specialist well-funded FSV service made a significant difference in Lae while operating in a difficult public-sector and high-violence environment.

DOOR-ETHICS FLAGS:
- Survivor privacy and safety outrank narrative detail.
- Donor/international pages must be balanced with PNG staff, survivor, and community voices.
- Do not turn a case-management office into a saviour story; it is infrastructure, not possession.
