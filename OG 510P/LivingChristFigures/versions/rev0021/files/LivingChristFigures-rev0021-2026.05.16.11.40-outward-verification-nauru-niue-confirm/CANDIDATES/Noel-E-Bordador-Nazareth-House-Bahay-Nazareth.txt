Noel E. Bordador / Nazareth House (Bahay Nazareth)

Status: KEEP WITH RELIGIOUS-MOVEMENT FLAG
Location: Manila / Meycauayan, Philippines
Office: HIV house / queer and family-rejected hospitality / long-term belonging

Work:
Founder/operator of a Catholic Worker house of hospitality for people living with HIV/AIDS who lack family support and financial means, also connected to food distribution and solidarity with families harmed by extrajudicial violence.

Why it belongs / might belong:
The office is not just shelter. It is the counter-family built where stigma has made diagnosis, sexuality, poverty, and orphaning overlap.

Flags:
Faith-movement context is explicit; public reports are movement/community sources more than mainstream independent reporting.
