SUMMARY:
Samoa Victim Support Group describes its mission as empowering victims, survivors, and those in need through service delivery, advocacy, and training. Its services page describes a comprehensive casework system, legal support through the justice process, assistance with interim protection orders, a 24-hour free helpline (800-7874), emergency welfare assistance including temporary accommodation, food, and clothing, and specialised advocacy/counselling programs. A 2026 Spotlight Initiative report describes Campus of Hope, founded in 2009 by SVSG, as Samoa’s only domestic violence shelter, providing comprehensive care for women and children leaving violent households, including legal support, psychosocial counselling, education for children, economic-empowerment training, and continuous helpline operation.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office is a shelter that tries to become a temporary village: law, night phone, classroom, nursery, counselling, protection orders, food, and the non-spectacular work of beginning again.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- Casework intake and intervention decisions.
- 24-hour free helpline.
- Temporary accommodation and emergency welfare.
- Legal support, protection-order support, psychosocial counselling.
- Child education and family shelter at Campus of Hope.

EVIDENCE:
