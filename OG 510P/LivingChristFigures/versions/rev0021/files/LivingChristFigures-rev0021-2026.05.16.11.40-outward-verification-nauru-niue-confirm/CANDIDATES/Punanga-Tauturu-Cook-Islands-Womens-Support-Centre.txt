NAME: Punanga Tauturu / Punanga Turuturu Itivaine / Cook Islands Women’s Support Centre
LOCATION: Cook Islands
OFFICE: Women’s counselling centre / legal-aid and survivor-centred training threshold
STATUS: SCOUT / PROVISIONAL KEEP; DIRECT-SITE GAP; NAME-VARIANT AND CURRENT-CAPACITY FLAGS

SUMMARY:
Punanga Tauturu appears in regional sources as a Cook Islands women’s counselling centre and one of the early Pacific crisis-centre lineages. A Pacific Data Hub item records a 2018 MOU between Punanga Tauturu Inc and the Cook Islands Gender and Development Office to make legal aid available for domestic-violence survivors. FWCC’s 2025 Cook Islands training release describes a two-week training program with Punanga Turuturu Itivaine / Cook Islands Women’s Support Centre focused on gender, violence against women and girls, human rights, and counselling skills, and says FWCC’s relationship with Punanga Tauturu dates to the first Pacific Women’s Network Against Violence Against Women meeting in 1992.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office is a small-country counselling and legal-aid threshold that appears to have helped carry the Pacific crisis-centre lineage before it had many formal doors. It remains provisional because direct current organisational sources need to be located.

OFFICE SHAPE:

- Legal aid for domestic-violence survivors.
- Survivor-centred counselling/human-rights training.
- PWNAVAW early-lineage centre.
