NAME: Gender Creative Kids / pflag Canada parent and family support
LOCATION: Canada / Quebec
OFFICE: Parent and family reorientation after a child comes out / trans-youth affirmation within family, school, and community
STATUS: PROVISIONAL KEEP; ORGANISATION/NETWORK CANDIDATE; YOUTH-AUTONOMY FLAG

SUMMARY:
Gender Creative Kids is described as a support organisation for trans, non-binary, and gender-creative children and families, supporting affirmation in families, schools, and communities since 2013. pflag Canada is a national charity created by parents of 2SLGBTQIA+ people looking for help supporting their loved ones; it provides peer support, education, and advocacy, including a Parents & Support Circle.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office is a parent or family system being turned from panic, shame, ideology, or isolation toward ordinary faithful kinship with the child in front of them. This extends the rev0016 parent-reorientation card beyond US and Australia sources.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- Parent peer support.
- Evidence-informed family/school/community affirmation.
- Local chapter and support-circle structure.
- Advocacy beside interpersonal reorientation.

EVIDENCE:
1. Fierté Montréal / Gender Creative Kids profile: supports trans, non-binary, and gender-creative children/families; helps affirmation within families, schools, and communities since 2013.
2. pflag Canada homepage: national charitable organisation created by parents seeking help to support 2SLGBTQIA+ loved ones; peer support, education, advocacy.
3. pflag Canada Parents & Support Circle: resources for parents/loved ones, true stories, chapter/community connection.

DOOR-ETHICS FLAGS:
- Parent comfort cannot displace youth autonomy, consent, privacy, or safety.
- Needs more sources on Indigenous, immigrant, Francophone, rural, and region-specific Canadian contexts.
- Avoid framing the child as a crisis to be managed; the family system is the one being reoriented.
