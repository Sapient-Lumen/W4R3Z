NAME: FSM Pohnpei / Kosrae Family Protection Adviser
LOCATION: Federated States of Micronesia; Pohnpei State and Kosrae State implementation line
OFFICE: Family-protection-law implementation adviser / service collaboration and gap repair
STATUS: PROVISIONAL KEEP; LAW-TO-DOOR IMPLEMENTATION ENTRY; ADVISER-NOT-SERVICE WARNING

SUMMARY:
Pacific Data Hub records a Family Protection Adviser working with the FSM Department of Health and Social Affairs to support implementation of the Pohnpei Domestic Violence Act 2017 and the Kosrae Family Protection Act 2014. The described focus is strengthening services and collaboration between service providers, advocacy, and policy reform.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
This is a threshold-maintenance office: not the law, not the courtroom, not the shelter, but the connective work that asks whether Pohnpei and Kosrae’s family-protection laws actually produce coordinated survivor support.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- Adviser support for Pohnpei and Kosrae family-protection law implementation.
- Service-provider collaboration.
- Advocacy and policy-reform line.
- Bridge between legal framework and essential services.

EVIDENCE:
1. Pacific Data Hub entry: Family Protection Adviser works with FSM Department of Health and Social Affairs on Pohnpei DV Act 2017 and Kosrae Family Protection Act 2014 implementation.
2. Same entry: focus on strengthening services, collaboration between service providers, advocacy, and policy reform.
3. FSM EVAW mapping entry identifies progress but also quality essential-service gaps, especially for women and children with disabilities, rural/remote communities, poverty, and other vulnerabilities.

DOOR-ETHICS FLAGS:
- Adviser support is not the same as accessible counselling, shelter, legal aid, or safe transport.
- Requires current-source refresh beyond 2019.
- “Collaboration” must not mean survivor information circulates without consent.
