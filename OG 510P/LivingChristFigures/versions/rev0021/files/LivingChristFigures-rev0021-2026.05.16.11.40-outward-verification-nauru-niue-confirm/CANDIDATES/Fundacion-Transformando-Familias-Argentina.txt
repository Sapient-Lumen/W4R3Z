NAME: Fundación Transformando Familias
LOCATION: Argentina
OFFICE: Family reorientation and accompaniment around trans children, adolescents, and self-perceived identity
STATUS: PROVISIONAL KEEP; LATIN-AMERICA PARENT/FAMILY-REORIENTATION FIND; SELF-DESCRIPTION AND MINOR-PRIVACY FLAGS

SUMMARY:
Fundación Transformando Familias describes itself as created to address the historic social debt of non-recognition of transgender people’s rights. Its site says it offers support/containment to relatives, friends, partners, and others of transgender people seeking accompaniment; training around transness and the normalisation of diverse gender identities during childhood; accompaniment in acceptance of self-perceived identity, transformation/transition processes, and socio-family inclusion; and research support. 2025/2026 site posts describe training around trans childhood and adolescence and promote visibility, accompaniment, respect, and inclusion for trans children.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office is a family system learning to accompany rather than correct, delay, isolate, or make the child/adolescent responsible for everyone else’s fear.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- Family/friend/partner support.
- Training around trans childhood and gender-diverse identities.
- Accompaniment through acceptance, transition, and socio-family inclusion.
- Spanish-language/Argentina context for the parent-reorientation line.

EVIDENCE:
1. Transformando Familias homepage: support to relatives/friends/partners, training around transness and gender-diverse identities in childhood, accompaniment in acceptance and socio-family inclusion.
2. 2026/2025 posts: promotion of trans-child visibility/respect and training for accompaniment of trans childhood and adolescence.
