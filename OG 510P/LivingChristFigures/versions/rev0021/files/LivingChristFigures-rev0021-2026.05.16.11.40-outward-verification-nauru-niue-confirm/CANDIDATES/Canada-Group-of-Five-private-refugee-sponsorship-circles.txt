Canada Group of Five / Private Sponsorship of Refugees circles

Status: OFFICE-CANON; NETWORK-CANDIDATE; KEEP WITH STATE-GATE, CAPACITY, CLASS, AND CURRENT-PAUSE FLAGS
Location: Canada, with sponsored refugees overseas and settlement communities in Canada
Office: Private sponsorship settlement circle / five-or-more-person household-and-community threshold for refugee arrival

Work:
Canada’s Private Sponsorship of Refugees program allows citizens and permanent residents, through Sponsorship Agreement Holders, Community Sponsors, and Groups of Five, to sponsor refugees from abroad. The Refugee Sponsorship Training Program says the PSR program began in 1979 and that sponsor commitments typically last 12 months from the sponsored person’s arrival. IRCC’s Group of Five page says a group of five or more Canadians can sponsor refugees abroad to live permanently in Canada and will support them emotionally and financially, usually for one year. Current status matters: IRCC/RSTP materials say new Group of Five and Community Sponsor applications under PSR were paused from 29 November 2024 and the pause has been extended to 31 December 2026, while existing applications continue and Groups of Five can apply through BVOR at this time.

Why it belongs / might belong:
Rev0015 left refugee third-country private-sponsorship circles as an explicit gap. This is a threshold office where ordinary people bind themselves to rent, groceries, school forms, winter clothes, interpretation, appointments, and the long after-arrival work that begins where airport welcome photographs end. It is not a single Christ figure; it is a covenantal circle with signatures, budgets, and months of ordinary availability.

Flags:
This office is powerful and risky precisely because the sponsor is close. A private sponsor can become a door, but also a landlord, patron, moral scriptwriter, or immigration broker. The current pause means this is not an open unlimited pathway in 2026. The cube must record state eligibility rules, financial thresholds, and backlog/pause conditions rather than narrating sponsorship as pure hospitality. The sponsored person’s dependency on the group makes DOOR-ETHICS question 1 especially sharp: Can the person leave the helper?

REV0017 REFRESH — POWER / BURDEN CRITIQUE:
Keep the candidate, but do not narrate private sponsorship as pure hospitality. IRCC's 2026 Group of Five page notes a current application pause except via BVOR pathways and requires financial/emotional support, settlement planning, and evidence capacity. Sponsorship literature describes the first-year role as airport reception, housing, funds, and linkage to health, education, language, and employment. This can become care, but also surveillance, administrative overreach, gratitude pressure, and unequal intimacy. The refugee must remain more than the sponsor circle's project.
