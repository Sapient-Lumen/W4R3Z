Mama Dragons / Mama Bears / parent-reorientation offices for LGBTQ+ and transgender children

Status: SCOUT / POSSIBLE KEEP; NETWORK-CANDIDATE; KEEP WITH US-CENTRIC, RELIGION-REORIENTATION, MINOR-PRIVACY, AND POLITICAL-POLARISATION FLAGS
Location: United States origin, with online / translocal participation; Mama Dragons rooted in Utah/LDS mother networks, Mama Bears rooted in Texas/evangelical mother networks
Office: Parent-reorientation-after-child-comes-out / peer-to-peer family acceptance / the parent stops making the child carry the parent’s crisis

Work:
Mama Dragons and Mama Bears are parent-led or parent-centered networks for mothers and families of LGBTQ+ children. Mama Dragons describes itself as a 501(c)(3) that supports, educates, and empowers mothers of LGBTQ+ children, with over 25,000 parents using education resources, parenting classes, and community, including peer-led private groups, regional groups, a T-Mamas subgroup for mothers of transgender and non-binary children, Parachute eLearning, QPR suicide-prevention training, and non-affirming religion affinity groups. Mama Bears describes itself as supporting, educating, and empowering families with LGBTQ+ members; it offers private online communities, resources, more than 60 chapters, a Moms of Trans Kids group, care packages for people who have lost family support, and members who stand in at weddings and other events when requested. Both networks have roots in religious family systems where the parent’s conversion from fear or non-affirming doctrine into protection becomes the threshold.

Why it belongs / might belong:
The rev0013 gap list asked for parent-led trans-youth equivalents where the subject is the parent’s reorientation. This is the clearest office shape found so far. The threshold is not the child becoming a lesson object. It is the adult moving from panic, shame, doctrine, or politics into a competent posture of protection, so the child does not have to parent the parent. The office is peer support for the adult so the child can remain the child.

Flags:
This remains a scout. The source base is largely organisational self-description and friendly reporting; a future pass should look for independent evaluation, safeguarding structure, moderation practice, and how the groups handle disagreement, detransition discourse, medical advice boundaries, and privacy for minors. The cube should not name children as evidence. It should also not flatten all LGBTQ+ child support into trans-specific support: Mama Dragons has a T-Mamas subgroup and Mama Bears has a Moms of Trans Kids group, but both organisations are wider than trans-youth support. Because these are US-centered and politically visible, they are not “unknown canon,” but the office may be important enough to keep.
