REFRESH NOTE — rev0019
SUBJECT: Tuvalu — training capacity is not full service capacity

Tuvalu/FatuLei is admitted only as a provisional capacity-building entry. The strongest current source is the October 2024 FWCC GBV counsellor training completion: 34 community advocates from police, judiciary, community, church, Social Welfare, youth, and FatuLei completed training.

This does not prove:
- a 24/7 helpline;
- a permanent staffed crisis centre;
- a safe house with stable capacity;
- outer-island transport access;
- independent complaint routes;
- survivor trust in police/church/community pathways.

The next pass should locate direct FatuLei materials, Tuvalu Gender Affairs service documentation, CEDAW shadow materials, and any survivor-safe independent evaluations.
