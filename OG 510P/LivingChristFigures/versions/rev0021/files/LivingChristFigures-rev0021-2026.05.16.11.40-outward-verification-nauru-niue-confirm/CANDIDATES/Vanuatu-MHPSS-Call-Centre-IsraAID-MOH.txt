NAME: Vanuatu MHPSS Call Centre / IsraAID + Ministry of Health
LOCATION: Vanuatu
OFFICE: Post-disaster island mental-health call centre / multilingual psychosocial first-response threshold
STATUS: SCOUT / PROVISIONAL KEEP; TIME-LIMITED RESPONSE FLAG; VERIFY CONTINUATION AFTER PILOT

SUMMARY:
IsraAID and the Vanuatu Ministry of Health launched a mental-health and psychosocial support call centre described as free, confidential, and available in Bislama, English, and French. The published schedule was seven days per week, 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., with a twelve-week programme running into mid-April. A Vanuatu Ministry of Health release separately reports suicide-prevention awareness work in schools and communities in Sanma Province and names increased self-harm/suicide deaths as a public-health concern.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office is a voice-door after disaster and social strain, where language and stigma determine whether help is reachable. Its provisionality is part of the record: not every needed threshold becomes a stable institution.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- Multilingual call centre.
- Post-disaster psychosocial support.
- School/community suicide-prevention awareness.
- Government-humanitarian partnership.

EVIDENCE:
1. Sista/Daily Post report: MHPSS call centre, seven-day schedule, free/confidential support in Bislama, English, and French; IsraAID/Vanuatu Ministry of Health partnership.
2. Vanuatu Ministry of Health: suicide-prevention awareness in Sanma schools and communities; stigma-reduction and referral orientation.

DOOR-ETHICS FLAGS:
- Time-limited pilot / response programme; must not be described as permanent without later proof.
- NGO-state frame needs Vanuatu-led follow-up.
- Hotline hours and numbers may change; do not turn contact details into a permanent claim.
