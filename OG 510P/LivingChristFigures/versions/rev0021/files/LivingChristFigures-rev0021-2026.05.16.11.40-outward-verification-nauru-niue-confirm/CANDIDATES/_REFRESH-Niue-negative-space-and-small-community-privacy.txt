SUBJECT: Niue as negative-space entry, not service entry

Niue is intentionally added as a negative-space/gap marker. The Pacific counselling-services review reports no counselling services for survivors of violence in Niue at the time of review; the family-tree mapping article describes lack of research/attention and develops a privacy-conscious family-space approach.

Do not turn this into a service listing. The research approach may be useful for prevention/accountability in a small community, but it does not replace counselling, shelter, legal advice, health response, police accountability, or survivor-controlled safety planning.

Search carry-forward:
- current Niue government/social-services documentation;
- any updated family-violence legislation or service pathway;
- Council of Women / health / police / church/community roles;
- privacy and retaliation safeguards in a very small population context.
