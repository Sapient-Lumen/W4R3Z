NAME: Homeshare UK / HomeShare International
LOCATION: United Kingdom; international programme network
OFFICE: Homeshare night-presence / anti-isolation and anti-institutional ordinary dwelling
STATUS: SCOUT / PROVISIONAL KEEP; PROTECTED-HOUSING COMPARATOR; CARE-LABOUR-TENANCY BOUNDARY FLAGS

SUMMARY:
Homeshare UK describes homeshare as a mutual-benefit arrangement in which an older person with a spare room is carefully matched with a younger person who provides an agreed amount of support in exchange for good-quality affordable accommodation. It says homeshare helps people stay living independently at home for longer, provides affordable accommodation, and gives families peace of mind. HomeShare International describes homesharing as help with daily living, companionship, and the security of having someone in the house, especially at night, with benefits for older/disabled people, single parents, students, young people, and key workers.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office is not a shelter bed or care home. It is the threshold of ordinary night-presence: someone else in the house, a shared cup, help with errands, an alarm heard, a loneliness interrupted before institutionalisation becomes the only available grammar.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- Spare-room / affordable-accommodation exchange.
- Companionship and light practical help.
- Security of someone in the house, especially at night.
- Independence-at-home support without formal care-home placement.

EVIDENCE:
1. Homeshare UK: older householder and younger homesharer matched for support in exchange for affordable accommodation; benefits include independent living, affordable accommodation, companionship, and family peace of mind.
2. HomeShare International: daily living help, companionship, security at night, loneliness alleviation, and cross-generational/cross-cultural benefits.

DOOR-ETHICS FLAGS:
- Homeshare is not a substitute for professional care; unmet care needs must not be hidden inside cheap rent.
- Boundaries, tenancy rights, safeguarding, domestic labour, and power imbalance need strong review.
- Future pass should include specific providers, safeguarding standards, complaint routes, and participant voice.
