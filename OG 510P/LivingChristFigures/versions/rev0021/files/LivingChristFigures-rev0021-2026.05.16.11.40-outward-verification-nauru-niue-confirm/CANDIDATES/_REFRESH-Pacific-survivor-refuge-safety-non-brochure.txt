REFRESH NOTE — rev0018
SUBJECT: Pacific survivor-refuge entries and safety/non-brochure handling

The rev0018 Pacific survivor-care additions are strong, but they are also safety-sensitive. They should never be used as donor brochure copy or as a map to shelters. The cube should describe public institutional shape — counselling, helpline, refuge, legal support, referrals, mobile outreach, regional training — while omitting operational detail.

Specific cautions:
- WCCC Tonga’s site contains some template/placeholder remnants; rely on its direct service descriptions but mark website-quality limitations.
- Vanuatu Women’s Centre explicitly says safe-house stays may be time-limited due to resources; do not imply unlimited access.
- SVSG/Campus of Hope has a vivid donor story with child imagery; do not reproduce that image logic in the cube.
- Solomon Islands must keep FSC, CCC, and SAFENET distinct; they do different work and carry different faith/secular/autonomy questions.
- Punanga Tauturu remains provisional until a direct current official source is found.

Rule implication:
CUBE-RULES rev0018 adds the operational-safety rule: no precise shelter routes, staff rosters, survivor identifiers, or operational details unless already public and strictly necessary.
