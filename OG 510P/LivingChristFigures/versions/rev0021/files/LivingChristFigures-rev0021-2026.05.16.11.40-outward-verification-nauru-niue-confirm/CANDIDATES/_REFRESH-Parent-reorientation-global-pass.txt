Ethical caution:
Parent groups can help a child by giving the adult somewhere else to take fear. They can also become a soft gatekeeping system if the parent’s need for comfort displaces the young person’s privacy, consent, autonomy, or safety. Keep the child/youth autonomy flag primary.
