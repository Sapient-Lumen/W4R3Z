REFRESH NOTE — rev0020
SUBJECT: Compulsory counselling / family preservation as ownership risk

The Nauru Judiciary page says counselling is compulsory when a victim seeks a protection order and frames it as an effort to repair the relationship and keep the family together. That statement requires a cube-level caution.

The cube should not treat counselling as mercy when:
- it is imposed on the survivor as a condition or consequence of seeking protection;
- it pressures the survivor to reconcile;
- it values family unity over safety;
- it creates a forum for coercion, intimidation, or retaliation;
- it uses church/community authority to own the survivor’s next step.

Future search should look for the exact legal basis, court practice, survivor-rights safeguards, alternatives to joint counselling, and whether counselling can be trauma-informed, separate, voluntary, and safety-led.
