SUMMARY:
The Foundation for Human Rights programme on the Unfinished Business of the TRC supports survivors and families awaiting justice and reparations, including advocacy, litigation, public-awareness work, coordination with Khulumani, and support for Missing Persons Task Team investigative activity. In January 2025, 25 families/survivors and FHR brought a constitutional-damages case alleging political suppression and delay of apartheid-era TRC prosecutions; a Khampepe commission of inquiry was established in May 2025. Later 2025 and 2026 updates show the case and inquiry process still contested and live.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office refuses cheap closure. Returning remains, holding memorials, or naming injustice are necessary but not enough when delay has outlived witnesses and perpetrators. This candidate sits beside Khulumani and the Missing Persons Task Team as the litigation / accountability threshold.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- Family-led refusal of delay.
- Litigation after TRC non-prosecution.
- Psychosocial support for unresolved justice.
- Public education and memorial demands tied to constitutional remedy.

EVIDENCE:
1. FHR Unfinished Business of the TRC: supports survivors/families awaiting justice and reparations; advocacy/litigation; psychosocial support; coordination with Khulumani and MPTT-related activities.
2. UnfinishedTRC constitutional-damages case page: 20 January 2025 filing by 25 survivors/families and FHR; claims political suppression/delay; Khampepe commission established 29 May 2025.
3. Oct 2025 FHR/UnfinishedTRC press release: High Court dismissed government stay application; families opposed delay.
4. Feb 2026 family statement: families describe deliberate suppression and continued delay tactics.
5. Reuters Jan 2025: families/survivors suing government, seeking damages and commission inquiry.

DOOR-ETHICS FLAGS:
- Active litigation/inquiry: monitor outcomes and do not claim vindication before judgment.
- Do not use this as an argument that exhumation/reburial work is irrelevant; it shows that burial and justice remain distinct thresholds.
- Family grief is not evidence theatre.
