NAME: Chuuk Women’s Council / Tongen Inepwineu Counselling Centre (TICC) / APIMAR SafeNet
LOCATION: Chuuk State, Federated States of Micronesia
OFFICE: First crisis support counselling centre in FSM / safe counsellors and referral mesh
STATUS: KEEP; NORTH-PACIFIC FIRST-CRISIS-CENTRE LINE; NETWORK-CANDIDATE WITH DONOR-AND-CAPACITY FLAGS

SUMMARY:
The Chuuk Women’s Council launched the Tongen Inepwineu Counselling Centre, translated as “Love of the Family,” in March 2020. Pacific Data Hub describes it as the first crisis support centre for women survivors of violence in the Federated States of Micronesia, providing free, confidential, non-judgmental counselling and trained “safe counsellors” for survivors of domestic violence, rape, sexual harassment, and child sexual abuse. Pacific Women Lead later described TICC as one of only two crisis centres in the North Pacific providing counselling services for survivors of violence against women, with APIMAR SafeNet serving as the Chuuk referral system linking EVAW stakeholders such as government ministries, police, the Attorney General’s office, and CWC.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office is a small first door in a scattered-island setting: a named counselling centre, trained safe counsellors, and a referral mesh where the police or Attorney General’s office can call the counsellors rather than leaving the survivor alone with the system.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- First FSM crisis support centre for women survivors of violence.
- Free, confidential, non-judgmental counselling.
- Trained “safe counsellors.”
- Referral through APIMAR SafeNet.
- Telephone counselling skills and counselling supervision through North Pacific technical-support line.

EVIDENCE:
1. Pacific Data Hub March 2020 release: launch of TICC as first FSM crisis support centre; free/confidential/non-judgmental counselling; trained safe counsellors for survivors of domestic violence, rape, sexual harassment, and child sexual abuse.
2. Pacific Women Lead North Pacific article: TICC and WUTMI/WIM are the only two North Pacific crisis centres providing counselling services for survivors of violence against women.
3. Same source: APIMAR SafeNet links EVAW stakeholders, and TICC has received referrals from police and the Attorney General’s office.

DOOR-ETHICS FLAGS:
- “First” should not become triumphalist; first doors are fragile and capacity-limited.
- Donor/technical-support language is present; future pass should seek local staff voice and survivor-safe evaluation without exposing clients.
- Keep APIMAR SafeNet as a referral mesh, not a guarantee that every pathway is safe or well-resourced.
