NAME: FatuLei / Tuvalu Women for Change Association and Tuvalu GBV counsellor-training pool
LOCATION: Tuvalu; Funafuti public line
OFFICE: Counsellor-pool where service infrastructure is still thin / training-to-door threshold
STATUS: PROVISIONAL KEEP; CAPACITY-BUILDING NOT EQUIVALENT TO SERVICE-CAPACITY; TUVALU GAP ENTRY

SUMMARY:
Tuvalu remains a gap-heavy entry rather than a completed service map. FWCC reported that a two-week Gender-Based Violence Counsellor Training took place in Funafuti in October 2024 with support from the Government of Tuvalu, World Bank, Australian Aid, and FatuLei / Tuvalu Women for Change Association. Thirty-four participants completed the training, including police, judiciary, community representatives, church groups, Social Welfare Department, youth representatives, and FatuLei. Related FWCC reporting notes collaboration with FatuLei and the Tuvalu Gender Affairs Department through the Pacific Women’s Network Against Violence Against Women.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
This is the office before the office: a country where a survivor-centred door is being built through trained people spread across police, judiciary, church, welfare, community, youth, and women’s-organisation settings. The cube keeps it provisional because trained capacity is not the same as guaranteed accessible service.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- GBV counsellor-training pool.
- Cross-sector training: police, judiciary, welfare, community, church, youth, and FatuLei.
- PWNAVAW/FWCC regional training support.
- Possible local women’s-organisation doorway, pending direct current source confirmation.

EVIDENCE:
1. FWCC October 2024: 34 community advocates completed GBV counsellor training in Funafuti, including police, judiciary, community representatives, church groups, Social Welfare, youth representatives, and FatuLei.
2. FWCC October 2024 start note: training supported by Government of Tuvalu, World Bank, Australian Aid, and FatuLei; participants included the same cross-sector group.
3. Tuvalu national/UN material and CEDAW reporting indicate survivor-service development remains capacity-limited and includes temporary/safe-space efforts, but this needs direct local follow-up.

DOOR-ETHICS FLAGS:
- Training is not the same as 24/7 crisis response, legal aid, shelter access, or safe transport.
- Faith/church representatives in the pool require autonomy/precondition checks.
- Do not imply a full crisis centre exists unless a current direct source confirms it.
- Keep this as “capacity being built under scarcity,” not a brochure of success.
