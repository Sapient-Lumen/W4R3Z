SUMMARY:
Fa'ataua Le Ola (FLO) / Samoa Lifeline appears as a direct island-country crisis-line and suicide-prevention office. TherapyRoute describes FLO as offering 24-hour free mental-health support for people in Samoa and Samoans abroad, with crisis counselling, advocacy, referrals, and community programmes. Samoa Observer coverage names Papalii Caroline Ah Chong as Executive Director and identifies public bus outreach and the 800-5433 helpline.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office is not abstract awareness. It is a culturally near phone, bus/mobile presence, and referral door for the person whose danger is not only internal crisis but silence, shame, distance, church/family/community pressure, and lack of ordinary access to mental-health care.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- Lifeline / crisis phone.
- Suicide-prevention advocacy.
- Community education and outreach.
- Referral/protection-order pathway in the broader Samoa support map.
