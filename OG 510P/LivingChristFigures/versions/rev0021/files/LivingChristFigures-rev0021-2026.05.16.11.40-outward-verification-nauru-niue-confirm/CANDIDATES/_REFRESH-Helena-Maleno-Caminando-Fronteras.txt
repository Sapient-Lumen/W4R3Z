REFRESH NOTE — Helena Maleno / Caminando Fronteras (rev0010 entry — refreshed in rev0015)

Status: OFFICE-CANON retained. The 2026 refresh strengthens the entry rather than changing its category.

What this refresh found:
Caminando Fronteras’ 2025 monitoring page records 3,090 people dead or disappeared at Spanish borders between 1 January and 15 December 2025, including 192 women and 437 children and adolescents, across 303 tragedies and 70 boats that disappeared without trace. It identifies omission of rescue duties, late or non-activation of search and rescue, lack of coordination among states, border externalisation, longer routes, and precarious boats as factors increasing risk. The first-five-months 2025 report explains the method as data gathered from primary sources, migrant communities, relatives, victims, and grassroots organisations.

What this permits the cube to say more clearly:
The office is not only “the phone that stays on.” It is also a counter-forensic ledger built with families and migrant communities when state procedures are too slow, too narrow, or too politically interested in deterrence. The 2025 data also make clear that the child/minor issue is not decorative; children and adolescents are part of the death count and the cube must keep image and detail risks high.

Why no candidate-file rewrite this turn:
The existing Helena Maleno file is already short and essentially correct. A future revision should rewrite it with the 2025 data and the family-search / missing-body dimension from recent reporting, but rev0015 logs the refresh without expanding the file.
