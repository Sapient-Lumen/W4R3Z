What this allows the cube to record more precisely:
- Location: Bukit Dumbar, Penang, Malaysia (with additional sites including Jalan Thomas, George Town).
- Founded ~2009; Tan Swee Ban was 56 in 2020 (so ~62 in 2026); the home has cared for over 300 sick or neglected elderly to date.
- Notable individual case: helping Ngan Dou Tai (105 in 2020), believed to be Malaysia's last surviving ma-jie, navigate the Malaysian National Registration Department to recover identity documents.
- Funding: ad-hoc, no fixed government support; relies on donations and Tan's own resources. Tan has reportedly given up career, savings, home, and elements of his own health to keep the home operating; sources include his mother's perspective via the 2025 RHB Bank short film.
