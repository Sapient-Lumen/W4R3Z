REASONING NOTE — rev0021

Reason for the revision:
rev0020's own REASONING-NOTE recorded an evidence debt against itself: "Most
sources in this revision are official, regional, court, development, or
research sources. They are adequate to map public institutional shape and
gaps, but they do not prove current accessible services. The next pass should
seek direct current local service information, legal-aid routes, enforcement
evidence, and survivor-safe critique."

rev0021 does not add candidates, office cards, or rules. It is an
outward-verification pass only. It tests the two rev0020 entries the cube
itself marked as weakest-verified (Nauru, as the strongest caution entry; Niue,
as the negative-space entry) against primary and direct sources, and records
what was found — including a confirmation that is unwelcome.

This is consistent with rule 9 (the cube logs its own errors and findings
honestly rather than rewriting earlier files) and rule 16 (framework is not
implementation). Earlier files retain their original wording. Verification
outcomes are recorded here and in the two REFRESH notes added this revision,
not by editing rev0020 prose.

Finding 1 — Nauru compulsory-counselling caution is CONFIRMED and SHARPENED.
rev0020 admitted Nauru as "the strongest caution entry because public court
language around compulsory counselling and family repair could pressure
reconciliation," and flagged this as a risk to investigate. Verification
against a primary judicial-training source (the Nauru Bench Book chapter on the
Domestic Violence and Family Protection Act 2017) confirms the risk is not
hypothetical: the Act provides for counselling with the counsellor required to
report to the court on a fortnightly basis or as the court directs (s 27), and
for the Director of Women to register and keep a register of counsellors
(s 28). A counselling process whose reports flow to the court, attached to
protection-order proceedings, is structurally the coercion/retaliation-forum
risk rule 17 was written to flag. The cube's instinct was correct; the status
of the caution moves from "flagged risk" to "documented structural risk,"
while the underlying safety question (does this in practice pressure survivors
to reconcile or stay silent) remains open and survivor-side evidence is still
absent. A separate older OHCHR-filed government submission describes Nauru's
safe house as not secure or equipped for longer stays, averaging roughly one
woman per month, with stored-food limits — corroborating the rev0020 capacity
caution. This older source is not treated as current status; it is recorded as
consistent-direction historical evidence only.

Finding 2 — Niue negative-space entry HOLDS.
A direct present-tense search for current Niue survivor counselling/family
violence services returned services for New Zealand, Singapore, Australia,
Texas, Canada — i.e. everywhere except a current Niuean survivor door. A null
result returned by a direct service search is itself the corroborating
evidence for a negative-space entry. rev0020's decision to record Niue as
absence-recorded-as-absence, not as a service listing, is unchanged and
verified. The carry-forward search items in the Niue REFRESH remain open.

Finding 3 — No over-correction.
Verification did not find grounds to remove or upgrade any rev0020 entry. The
RWG remains a law-to-practice backbone, not a survivor door. UNDP/MLSC Justice
Pathways remains a legal-capacity map, not a service or legal advice. Nothing
in this pass justifies softening any capacity caution into success language;
the pass moved one caution from suspected to documented and left the rest
standing.

Limits of this pass:
This verification used open web search only. It did not obtain survivor-side
testimony, current local service confirmation, enforcement data, legal-aid
route confirmation, or independent evaluation for any entry. The Nauru finding
sharpens a structural risk from public legal/judicial-training text; it is not
survivor-reported evidence and must not be read as such. The evidence debt
rev0020 named is reduced, not discharged.

Door-ethics note:
This pass deliberately did not search for, and does not record, any shelter
location, route, intake detail, staff identity, helpline tree, or survivor
identifier for any entry, in keeping with rule 15. Verifying that a door is
unsafe or absent must never be done in a way that makes a door less safe.
