OFFICE CARD: HOMESHARE NIGHT-PRESENCE
REVISION ADDED: rev0018

SHAPE:
An older or disabled person with a spare room and a person needing affordable housing are carefully matched so that ordinary living becomes mutual support: presence in the house, conversation, light help, security at night, and the possibility of staying home longer.

NOT THE SAME AS:
- A care home.
- A landlord workaround.
- Cheap domestic labour hidden under companionship language.

CANDIDATES / LINEAGE:
- Homeshare UK / HomeShare International (rev0018, scout/provisional).
- Fondazione Carpinetum / Centri Don Vecchi protected housing (rev0017) as adjacent protected-dwelling line.
- Sant’Egidio elder co-housing (rev0017 adjacent lineage).

WHY IT IS A CHRIST-FIGURE OFFICE:
The threshold is ordinary night-presence: someone hears the fall, notices the silence, shares the table, and makes home less solitary without turning the elder into a case or the younger person into an unpaid carer.

DOOR-ETHICS QUESTIONS:
1. Is the arrangement safe for both householder and homesharer?
2. Is there a clear boundary between companionship/light help and professional care?
3. Are tenancy, fees, domestic labour, privacy, and complaint routes explicit?
4. Can either person leave without coercion or retaliation?
5. Does the model protect people with greater care needs from being hidden inside an unsuitable arrangement?

IMAGE / LANGUAGE RULE:
No sentimental “lonely elder rescued by young person” frame. Show mutuality, safeguards, limits, and ordinary dwelling.
