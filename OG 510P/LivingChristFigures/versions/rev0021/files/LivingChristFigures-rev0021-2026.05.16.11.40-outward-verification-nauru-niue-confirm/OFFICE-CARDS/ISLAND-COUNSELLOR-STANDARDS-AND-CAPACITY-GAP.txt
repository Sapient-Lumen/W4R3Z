OFFICE CARD: ISLAND COUNSELLOR STANDARDS AND CAPACITY GAP
REVISION ADDED: rev0019

SHAPE:
A country or island system may have laws and training before it has a fully resourced crisis centre. This office names the fragile middle: counsellor pools, regional standards, supervision, telephone skills, and minimum ethical practice under scarcity.

CANDIDATES / LINEAGE:
- Tuvalu / FatuLei / GBV counsellor-training pool (rev0019, provisional).
- Regional GBV Counselling Framework (rev0019).
- Fiji Women’s Crisis Centre / PWNAVAW regional training backbone (rev0018).
- North Pacific TICC/WIM telephone-counselling training lineage (rev0019).

WHY IT IS A CHRIST-FIGURE OFFICE:
The threshold is being built before the walls are finished. It is costly mercy at the level of training, confidentiality, standards, supervision, and the refusal to leave a survivor with only a law and no person safe to tell.

DOOR-ETHICS QUESTIONS:
1. Is the cube confusing training with accessible service?
2. Are minimum standards implemented, supervised, and funded?
3. Are church/community/police actors trained in survivor choice, confidentiality, and non-blame?
4. Are there complaint and accountability routes when counsellors harm?
5. Does the system include safe referral for children, disabled survivors, queer/trans survivors, and outer-island survivors?

IMAGE / LANGUAGE RULE:
Avoid graduation-photo triumphalism. Training is a beginning, not proof that the survivor has a door.


REV0020 APPEND:
Nauru’s counselling/social-work qualification pool and Niue’s no-counselling-service marker extend this card. The distinction sharpened in rev0020 is negative as well as positive: training, a diploma pathway, or family-space research can be important, but none of them is a specialist survivor-service door.
