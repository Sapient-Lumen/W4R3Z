OFFICE CARD: LAW TO DOOR — FAMILY PROTECTION IMPLEMENTATION
REVISION ADDED: rev0020

SHAPE:
A statute, policy, court form, inter-ministerial memorandum, working group, or implementation adviser becomes an office only when it is connected to a reachable survivor door: safe reporting, urgent protection, legal explanation, health care, confidential referral, enforcement, transport, shelter/safe accommodation where needed, and the freedom not to reconcile.

CANDIDATES / LINEAGE:
- Palau Family Protection Act Working Group / inter-ministerial GBV first-response line (rev0020).
- Nauru Domestic Violence and Family Protection Act / Family Protection Coordination Committee / safety-order threshold (rev0020).
- FSM Pohnpei / Kosrae Family Protection Adviser (rev0020).
- Regional Working Group on the Implementation of Family Protection / Domestic Violence Legislation (rev0020).
- Regional GBV Counselling Framework (rev0019).

WHY IT IS A CHRIST-FIGURE OFFICE:
The mercy is not that a law exists. The mercy is that someone can reach a living threshold through the law without being sent back into danger, shame, family control, or agency limbo.

DOOR-ETHICS QUESTIONS:
1. Can a survivor use the law without exposing themselves to retaliation?
2. Is there accompaniment through police/court/health handoffs?
3. Are protection orders enforceable in practice?
4. Does the system pressure reconciliation, family preservation, silence, or gratitude?
5. Are disabled, queer/trans, child, rural, remote, migrant, and language-minority survivors included?

IMAGE / LANGUAGE RULE:
No court-case details, survivor stories, or operational shelter routes. Record law-to-door infrastructure without implying that law alone is safety.
