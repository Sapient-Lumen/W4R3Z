OFFICE CARD: GBV HEALTH FIRST RESPONDER AND EVIDENCE CHAIN
REVISION ADDED: rev0020

SHAPE:
The first door after violence may be a clinic, hospital, emergency room, nurse, case manager, police intake, or evidence file. This office names the chain where care, documentation, confidentiality, and legal possibility meet.

CANDIDATES / LINEAGE:
- Palau Family Protection Act Working Group / health-sector GBV training and evidence-file support (rev0020).
- Nauru Child Protection / Women’s Affairs / Safe House / Police Domestic Violence Unit / Education / Health orientation line (rev0020).
- LifeLine Port Moresby and survivor case-management lines (rev0019 / rev0017).

WHY IT IS A CHRIST-FIGURE OFFICE:
The survivor arrives with injury, fear, and often no proof the system will believe them. A careful first responder can protect the body, the story, the evidence, and the future choices of the person seeking help.

DOOR-ETHICS QUESTIONS:
1. Is treatment available without forcing a police report?
2. Is evidence collected with informed consent and privacy?
3. Can the survivor choose what happens next?
4. Are case files protected from family, church, workplace, media, or state exposure?
5. Are health workers trained not to blame, minimise, or reconcile?

IMAGE / LANGUAGE RULE:
Do not include medical details, evidence-chain specifics, staff rosters, or survivor-identifying facts.
