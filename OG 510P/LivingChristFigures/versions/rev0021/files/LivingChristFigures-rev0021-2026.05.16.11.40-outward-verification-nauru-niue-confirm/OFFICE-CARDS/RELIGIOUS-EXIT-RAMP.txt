The religious-exit-ramp office is the rare departure from a violent organisation that the organisation itself will permit. In the Salvadoran and Honduran case, gangs do not let members retire; the exits are emigration and conversion. The office is a church door that stays open under accusation of being "a pastor for gangsters," and a pastor who keeps opening it though the state of emergency is arresting his congregation. The danger is that conversion becomes a precondition for dignity, and that the surveilled visible-piety the gang demands replaces one set of bars with another; the cube must hold the office and the precondition together without letting one cancel the other.
