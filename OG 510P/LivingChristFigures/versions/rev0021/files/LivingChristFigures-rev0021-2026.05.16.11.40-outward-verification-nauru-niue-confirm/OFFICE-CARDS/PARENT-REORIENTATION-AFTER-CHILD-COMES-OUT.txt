The parent-reorientation office keeps the child from having to carry the parent through the parent’s fear. The adult finds peers, language, and courage elsewhere so the child can remain the child.

rev0016 Transcend Australia note: this office is not limited to US Christian/Mormon family-repair frames. It also includes non-US, community-led, evidence-informed family support where parents and father-figures learn how not to make the child carry the whole burden of their reorientation.
