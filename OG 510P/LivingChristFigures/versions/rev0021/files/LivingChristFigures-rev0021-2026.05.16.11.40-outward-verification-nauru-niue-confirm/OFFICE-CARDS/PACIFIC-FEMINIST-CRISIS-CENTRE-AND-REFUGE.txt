OFFICE CARD: PACIFIC FEMINIST CRISIS CENTRE AND REFUGE
REVISION ADDED: rev0018

SHAPE:
A woman or child facing family, sexual, child, or gender-based violence needs more than advice. This office holds together helpline, counselling, legal support, court/police/medical accompaniment, safe-house or refuge, mobile outreach, and referral networks across island geography.

NOT THE SAME AS:
- Generic women’s empowerment branding.
- Donor project language.
- A shelter-as-image story that exposes survivors or staff.

CANDIDATES / LINEAGE:
- Fiji Women’s Crisis Centre / PWNAVAW regional backbone (rev0018).
- Women and Children Crisis Centre Tonga (rev0018).
- Vanuatu Women’s Centre (rev0018).
- Samoa Victim Support Group / Campus of Hope (rev0018).
- Solomon Islands Family Support Centre / Christian Care Centre / SAFENET (rev0018).
- Punanga Tauturu / Cook Islands Women’s Support Centre (rev0018, provisional).
- Femili PNG / 1-Tok / Bel Isi PNG (rev0017).

WHY IT IS A CHRIST-FIGURE OFFICE:
The threshold is held open in danger and bureaucracy: phone, plan, car, form, clinic, police desk, courtroom, classroom, and a bed whose location must remain protected. The worker or organisation does not own the survivor; the survivor’s path is the centre.

DOOR-ETHICS QUESTIONS:
1. Are survivor safety and confidentiality stronger than the story’s usefulness?
2. Does the office offer options without making gratitude, prosecution, publicity, or reconciliation a condition of care?
3. Are rural/outer-island women and children actually able to reach the service?
4. Are local feminist workers centered over donor-branding language?
5. Is the shelter/refuge described without revealing operational detail?

IMAGE / LANGUAGE RULE:
No survivor images, no children in care, no exact shelter routes, and no decorative violence details. Use public institutional descriptions only.


REV0019 APPEND:
The Pacific survivor-care line now extends into the North Pacific and micro-state capacity gap: KWCSC and Kiribati SafeNet/OLSH; CWC/TICC/APIMAR SafeNet in Chuuk/FSM; WUTMI/Weto In Mour in RMI; Tuvalu/FatuLei counsellor-pool; and LifeLine Port Moresby.
