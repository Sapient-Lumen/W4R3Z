CUBE RULES — as of rev0039

This file consolidates the rules that have accumulated across revisions, so that the SearchTrails' "standing rules" list does not stay the only home for load-bearing constraints. Rev0016 corrected the duplicate rule numbering that appeared in rev0015 and added the file-naming convention requested by Johnny. Rev0018 added an operational-safety rule for shelter, helpline, refuge, and survivor-care entries. Rev0019 added a capacity/status distinction rule for helplines, referral networks, frameworks, training pools, and first crisis centres. Rev0020 adds a law-to-door / anti-coerced-reconciliation rule for family-protection and domestic-violence legal frameworks.

1. DEFINITIONAL RULE (rev0010, retained)
"Christ figure" is a severe metaphor for costly mercy, not a theological claim and not an identification of divinity.

2. CENTRAL SENTENCE (rev0010, retained)
The office is the threshold held open without owning the one who crosses it.

3. DOOR ETHICS (rev0010, retained)
See DOOR-ETHICS.txt. Nine questions, all still live. Question 3 is specifically about images, not names; do not confuse the two.

4. STATUS CATEGORIES (rev0010 onward)
KEEP; PROVISIONAL KEEP; SCOUT / POSSIBLE KEEP; OFFICE-CANON; PERSON-BENCHMARK / HOLD; NETWORK-CANDIDATE / INSTITUTION-CANDIDATE (rev0011); COMMUNAL-DOCTRINAL (rev0012). The cube allows entries to carry multiple statuses.

5. SOURCING RULES (rev0010 onward, retained)
Facebook-only leads held until non-Facebook corroboration. State-media-only leads flagged and refreshed. Where a religious or communal authority has done threshold work, the cube records both the welcome and its stated limits in the same entry (rev0012).

6. CANDIDACY RULES (rev0010 onward, retained but loosened over time)
Unknown / non-powerful work is preferred. Famous figures and well-funded networks may be admitted as PERSON-BENCHMARK or OFFICE-CANON when the office is unintelligible without them. Recognition does not by itself disqualify; the cube has bent this many times and rev0011 made the bend explicit.

7. PRECONDITION RULE (rev0013, retained)
Conversion-required offices are flagged with an explicit precondition flag. The cube does not let "the alternative is death" silence the question of what the office still asks of the person it helps — conversion to a frame, sobriety, gratitude, public identity, photogenic suffering.

8. PEER / HELPER DISTINCTION (rev0013, retained)
Peer-led offices are a distinct shape from helper-led offices and carry distinct DOOR-ETHICS readings — particularly question 1 ("Can the person leave the helper?"), which reads differently when the helper is also being helped.

9. ERROR-LOGGING RULE (rev0013, retained, now general)
The cube logs its own errors honestly rather than retroactively rewriting earlier files. SearchTrails, REASONING-NOTEs, and REFRESH notes are the right places for corrections; earlier files retain their original wording with a refresh or correction noted alongside.

10. NAMING RULE (rev0014, retained)
The cube does not censor what its sources publish openly, but names in cube prose must be load-bearing rather than decorative. Source-side anonymisation is respected. DOOR-ETHICS question 3 remains about images; question 9 now asks whether names are load-bearing.

11. ANTI-BROCHURE RULE (rev0010 onward, retained)
The cube does not iterate into a brochure for any single organisation, home, or network. Description that reads like fundraising copy is the warning sign.

12. OFFICE-CARDS-AS-LENSES, NOT BINS (rev0013, retained)
A single person or network can hold several offices open simultaneously. The cards are descriptions of shapes of mercy, not exclusive categories. Cube entries may cite multiple office cards.

13. LINEAGE / SUCCESSOR RULE (rev0015)
Where a candidate file was originally framed around a named founder and later searching shows the founder has left, split from, or been succeeded by the organisation, the cube records lineage, successor leadership, and current office separately.

14. REVISION FILE-NAMING RULE (rev0016)
Beginning with rev0016, exported cube zip filenames use this pattern:
LivingChristFigures-rev####-YYYY.MM.DD.HH.MM-summary-highlight-codename.zip
The timestamp is in the user's America/New_York timezone unless the user asks otherwise. The internal root folder should match the zip filename without the .zip extension. Older archived zips keep their historical filenames.


15. OPERATIONAL-SAFETY RULE FOR SHELTERS, HELPLINES, AND SURVIVOR OFFICES (rev0018)
When an entry involves a shelter, safe house, crisis/refuge service, helpline, or survivor-care route, the cube records only public, load-bearing institutional shape. Do not add precise shelter locations, routes, staff rosters, private phone trees, survivor identifiers, child images, or operational details that could increase risk. If a public source publishes sensitive material, the cube still asks whether repeating it is necessary.


16. CAPACITY / CURRENT-STATUS DISTINCTION RULE (rev0019)
When sources show a helpline, referral network, training pool, legal framework, counselling framework, crisis-centre launch, or donor-supported project, the cube distinguishes public institutional shape from current accessible capacity. Training is not the same as service; a public number is not a guaranteed live referral; a framework is not implementation; a first centre is often evidence of scarcity. Entries must flag current-status uncertainty, donor dependence, outer-island access, survivor-choice limits, and implementation gaps rather than smoothing them into success language.


17. LAW-TO-DOOR / ANTI-COERCED-RECONCILIATION RULE (rev0020)
A family-protection law, domestic-violence law, safety order, protection order, court page, implementation adviser, inter-ministerial memorandum, working group, or legal-capacity review is not counted as a survivor-accessible office unless the cube can describe a real path toward safe, confidential, non-coercive support. Legal frameworks must be distinguished from enforcement, accompaniment, shelter/safe accommodation, health care, legal aid, transport, language access, disability access, privacy protection, and current service capacity. Counselling, mediation, church/community intervention, or family-preservation language must be flagged when it is compulsory, joint, reconciliation-oriented, or likely to pressure the survivor to keep the family together at the expense of safety and consent.


18. CLAIM / SOURCE / CAPACITY / NARRATIVE-SEPARATION RULE (rev0024)
A candidate is not a single fact. It is a bundle of public institutional shape, interpretive office reading, source evidence, evidence limits, current-status uncertainty, capacity boundaries, safety constraints, and future narrative responsibility. The cube must keep those layers distinct. Machine-readable metadata may strengthen auditability, but it must not demote long-form narrative, moral argument, or careful prose into decorative afterthoughts. Future narrative files may be first-class artifacts with their own growth, revision, and seriousness.


19. IDENTITY-COHERENCE / SEMANTIC-DRIFT RULE (rev0025)
A candidate_id, front-matter name, file path, candidate ledger row, claim ledger rows, evidence-debt rows, public index row, and CUBE-MAP display label must refer to the same candidate unless an explicit lineage/successor/refresh relationship is being recorded. The cube must not let one candidate wear another candidate's metadata, claims, or evidence text. QA must check at least duplicate names, ledger/front-matter agreement, cross-ledger candidate_name agreement, public-index agreement, and CUBE-MAP label agreement. Semantic identity drift is a data-integrity failure even when all files, hashes, and row counts are structurally valid.



20. POSTHUMOUS / CHILD / UNCLAIMED-REMAINS CONSENT-BOUNDARY RULE (rev0026)
When an entry involves dead children, stillbirth, abandoned or unidentified children, unclaimed remains, indigent burial, medical-examiner custody, or posthumous naming, the cube distinguishes dignity from consent. Burial, naming, ritual, public attendance, photography, and press coverage may be mercy-shapes, but they are not evidence that the dead person or absent family consented to the narrative. The cube must keep abandoned, unidentified, unclaimed, stillborn, indigent, family-unable, and family-unwilling categories separate; avoid repeating child names, images, measurements, and case details unless load-bearing; and create evidence debt where family knowledge, agency custody, religious framing, or category assignment is unclear.


21. PUBLIC CEMETERY / SEARCHABLE MEMORIAL DATABASE BOUNDARY RULE (rev0027)
When an entry involves public cemeteries, potter's fields, mass graves, searchable burial databases, grave-location maps, AR/interpretive cemetery tools, family/chosen-family visitation systems, public tours, fetal-remains records, or story platforms for the dead, the cube distinguishes access from ownership. A searchable record is not return; a story submission is not universal consent; a public tour is not the same as family access; a green/natural burial frame is not proof that a poverty-shaped public burial was chosen. Do not repeat individual names, plot numbers, Medical Examiner numbers, precise grave-location details, fetal-remains data, family stories, images, or mementos unless directly necessary to an audited claim and redaction-reviewed. Keep city authority, public record, family/chosen-family grief, artist/platform mediation, and the dead person's unknowable consent separate.


22. STREET MEDICINE / OUTREACH / ENCAMPMENT PRIVACY RULE (rev0028)
When an entry involves street medicine, street outreach, unsheltered health care, encampments, mobile clinical care, outreach mapping, hospital-to-street follow-up, or care delivered in places people sleep outside, the cube distinguishes care from housing and outreach from operational intelligence. Do not record or reproduce patient names, clinical details, case stories, photos, camp locations, outreach routes, staff patterns, schedules, geocoded data, private referral instructions, or anything that could help identify or displace a person. Mapping, warm handoffs, and interagency coordination can be mercy-shapes, but they can also become surveillance or sweep infrastructure. Outreach is not housing; medical care outside must not become the city’s absolution for leaving people outside.

23. OPERATOR-DISSENT / BEHAVIORAL-UPDATE RULE (rev0029)
When a critique challenges the cube's method, operator centrality, absorption of dissent, public-export posture, or update failures, the cube must not treat quotation, praise, gratitude, or aesthetic preservation as correction. The dissent must be logged with a behavior-change field. A dissent is not closed unless it changes at least one rule, QA check, ledger field, redaction policy, candidate/office/longform text, source standard, or future-pass priority. A beautiful dissent registry is not yet a veto mechanism.

24. MANIFEST / INDEX TRUTHFULNESS RULE (rev0029)
The package manifest, indexes, maps, and public export metadata must agree with the actual package state. Row counts, revision number, export name, QA pointer, rules summary, longform layer description, and live-referral posture are audit objects, not decorative metadata. QA must fail when manifest counts or revision fields disagree with actual files and ledgers. The rev0028 stale-manifest bug is the canonical reason for this rule.

25. PUBLIC-EXPORT GATE RULE (rev0029)
A public reading edition is not a raw copy of the working cube. Before public export, the cube must pass live-referral safety, claim/source/capacity separation, redaction, names/images, source-role, evidence-debt, boundary-first reading order, and operator-dissent review gates. Public prose should begin with boundaries and false-door cautions before beautiful candidate narration. The public layer is a mercy instrument only if it refuses to become a brochure, doxxing surface, referral guide, or hagiographic trophy room.

26. REDACTION-RISK SCANNER / NON-REFERRAL CONTACT-DIGIT RULE (rev0030)
The cube is not a live referral directory. Exact phone, helpline, hotline, toll-free, consultation, intake, route, coordinate, address, plot, grave, Medical Examiner, camp-location, patient, survivor, child, or family-story details must not be reproduced merely because a public source publishes them. Public availability is not permission to republish operational contact data. Exact live contact digits are absent by default unless a file is explicitly marked as a referral artifact, reviewed for current capacity and safety, and excluded from public export unless separately justified. `tools/redaction_scan.py` and QA enforce configured high-risk patterns, while manual public-export review remains required for narrative privacy risks the scanner cannot understand.



27. SCHEMA CONTRACT / HANDOFF SAFETY RULE (rev0031)
A cube revision is not handoff-safe merely because its prose is careful and its
hashes pass. Front matter, ledger headers, JSON mirrors, source-type labels,
public export manifests, and package-level release contracts must also agree
with the current package state. Schema contracts are not a replacement for
judgment; they are a guard against quiet machine-readable drift. QA must fail
when the schema validator finds required-field, header, JSON mirror, unmapped
source-type, or public-manifest revision errors. Source-role labels may not
silently launder public profiles, self-descriptions, critical press, academic
context, and public-authority records into the same evidentiary weight.


28. EVIDENCE-LIFECYCLE / REFRESH-PRIORITY RULE (rev0032)
Supported enough to remain in the working cube is not the same as fresh enough for public use. Claims involving current capacity, live service access, helplines, shelters, referrals, visitation, outreach, or implementation must carry a shorter refresh cadence than historical, interpretive, or office-shape claims. The cube now derives claim-level evidence-strength and candidate-level refresh-priority reports; these are routing instruments, not final truth scores.

29. NEGATIVE-CASE / DEFERRED-CANDIDATE MEMORY RULE (rev0033)
The cube must remember refusals, demotions, gap markers, deferred leads, capacity precursors, and admitted-with-boundary cases, not only successful admissions. A candidate-like object may be morally important precisely because it is not yet a door: a law without implementation, training without service capacity, a contact-rich source unsafe for public export, a gap map that exposes absence, or a beautiful office that requires counterevidence beside it. `META/Negative-Case-Ledger-current.*` records these cases so future public prose cannot convert caution into silence or success language.

30. ONLINE RESEARCH INTAKE / SOURCE-PROMOTION GATE RULE (rev0033)
Same-pass online research does not automatically become claim evidence. Newly found sources first enter `META/Online-Research-Intake-current.*` with a source-role label, what-it-supports field, what-it-does-not-support field, next action, and safety caution. Contact-rich service pages, recognition profiles, official anniversary pages, critical scholarship, PDFs, and news items must not be treated as interchangeable. A source is promoted into `Source-Registry-current.*`, claim rows, candidate prose, or public wording only in a deliberate candidate-specific pass with redaction and source-role review.

31. SOURCE-PROMOTION VETO / PAIRED-EVIDENCE RULE (rev0034)
Every online intake source requires an explicit promote/hold/block decision before it may enter Source-Registry, claim rows, candidate prose, or public wording. Contact-rich live-service pages default to hold/block. Official self-description requires independent, beneficiary, or counterpressure pairing where appropriate. Counterpressure PDFs can support boundaries without becoming operational maps. A source can be true and still vetoed for promotion.

32. PUBLIC-CURRENT-CAPACITY QUARANTINE RULE (rev0034)
A claim classified as `now_or_before_any_public_claim` is quarantined from public present-tense service, capacity, availability, implementation, or referral wording until candidate-specific refresh, source-role pairing, redaction review, and claim-row update are complete. Supported enough to remain in the cube is not fresh enough to make a public current-capacity claim.


33. SOURCE-PROMOTION TRANSACTION / PUBLIC-CLAIM RELEASE RULE (rev0035)
A source-promotion pass must be atomic. If an intake source becomes claim evidence, the cube must update Source-Registry, claim rows, source-promotion decisions, evidence debt, lifecycle classification, quarantine/release ledgers, operator-update receipts, and QA together. If a public-current quarantine row is removed, a release ledger must state exactly what wording is allowed and what remains blocked. Promotion may be partial: one source in a cluster can be promoted while another remains held.


34. COUNTEREVIDENCE PROMOTION / NO-RELEASE RULE (rev0036)
A counterevidence source may be promoted because it constrains a beloved or canonical entry, not because it supplies flattering corroboration. Promotion of critique is not a public-claim release. When a critical, beneficiary-side, scholarly, or counterpressure source is promoted, the cube must record whether it changes claim text, debt status, source-role decisions, and public wording permissions separately. A source that strengthens the honesty of a claim may still leave every present-tense capacity, referral, route, selection-access, or outcome claim blocked. Counterevidence belongs beside praise, not in an appendix where beautiful narration can outrun it.

35. CONFLICT-ZONE MUTUAL-AID RECOGNITION / NO-MAP RULE (rev0037)
When an entry involves active war, siege, famine, mutual-aid networks, emergency rooms, community kitchens, evacuations, local committees, donor pipelines, cash-transfer workarounds, protected volunteers, or armed-actor suspicion, public recognition is itself a risk surface. Awards, donor pages, localization reports, and official praise may support aggregate shape or structural-pressure claims only after conflict-safety review. Do not publish room names, kitchen sites, routes, schedules, local committee lists, transfer channels, communication patterns, volunteer identities, recipient details, current access points, or donor-routing instructions. Recognition is not capacity evidence; funding routes are not public instructions; the cube may honor the office only by refusing to map it.

36. BORDER COUNTERPRESSURE / NO-ROUTE OBSERVATION RULE (rev0038)
When an entry involves border zones, informal living sites, police evictions, anti-fixation policy, counterpressure observation, camp imagery, accommodation buses, aid ecosystems, or migration-route pressure, documentation is not a public map. HRO-style reports, observation indices, NGO reports, and media investigations may support aggregate state-pressure, criminalisation, seizure, eviction, accommodation-failure, and boundary claims only after no-route/no-location/no-image review. Do not republish living-site names, camp locations, routes, schedules, police timing patterns, bus destinations as instructions, images, individual testimonies, contact details, volunteer patterns, aid-distribution details, or anything that helps locate or reach displaced people. Counterpressure is not service-capacity evidence; observation is not referral; a report that shows harm must not become a guide to where harmed people are.

37. SURVIVOR-SERVICE STANDARDS/FUNDING / NO-REFERRAL RULE (rev0039)
When an entry involves GBV crisis centres, survivor services, helplines, safe houses, refuges, counselling, case management, PAVE-style regional funding, counselling standards, live directories, or contact-rich service pages, regional funding and standards sources may support only high-level strengthening, standards, gap, and accountability claims. They do not prove live access, current capacity, safe referral, staffing, waiting times, shelter/refuge availability, confidentiality in practice, or outcome quality for any person now. Contact fields, access paths, exact phone/helpline/email/address data, intake hours, staff-as-access routes, safe-house/refuge logistics, transport instructions, survivor case details, and live referral language are blocked from the public cube unless a separate referral-safety artifact is intentionally created outside this package. Funding is not capacity; standards are not implementation; a crisis-centre page is not a directory entry.

38. FAMILY-LED MISSING-PERSON SEARCH / NO-FIELD-MAP RULE (rev0040)
When an entry involves missing-person search collectives, family-led search brigades, clandestine graves, forensic discovery, anonymous tips, state registries, protection mechanisms, or mothers/families searching for disappeared persons, public recognition can expose people, places, and the dead. Aggregate NGO, IACHR/OAS, Amnesty, and news evidence may support structural context only. Do not publish search fields, routes, sites, tip channels, grave or cremation details, forensic identifiers, registry IDs, clothing/remains descriptions, images, family contacts, current brigade locations, current protection arrangements, or individual case-story details. A registry reclassification is not return; a discovery is not consent to narrate the dead; search is not a map.


39. PROTECTION-ORDER / LEGAL-FRAMEWORK IS NOT A SURVIVOR-SERVICE DOOR RULE (rev0041)
When an entry involves domestic/family violence legislation, protection or safety orders, courts, police, MoUs, UPR/state reports, legal-capacity reviews, law-to-door advisers, justice-pathways maps, No Drop policies, or compulsory counselling, legal-framework evidence may support only framework, procedure-shape, implementation-gap, and accountability-boundary claims. It must not become legal advice, court or police filing instructions, safe-access proof, enforcement guarantee, service capacity, survivor-choice proof, or referral language. Compulsory counselling, No Drop, evidence/file integrity, and multi-sector coordination are risk surfaces unless paired with survivor-autonomy, confidentiality, enforcement, accompaniment, and safeguarding evidence. A law is not a door; a protection order is not safety; a justice-pathways report is not counsel.

40. POSTHUMOUS CHILD-BURIAL MEDIA / NO-CASE-EXTRACTION RULE (rev0042)
When an entry involves abandoned, unidentified, unclaimed, stillborn, or otherwise posthumously named children, public source material may support only high-level office shape and boundary debt unless it passes a separate no-case-extraction review. Do not republish assigned child names, poems, images, video stills, service notices, public attendance prompts, cemetery/location cues, contact fields, discovery details, family-status claims, dates, measurements, identifiers, toys, blankets, caskets, urns, grave/service details, or donor/religious narration merely because a source publishes them. Naming can be dignity without becoming consent to public narration; a ceremony can be mercy without becoming a public script; a child-specific story can be evidence of why the cube must stay quiet.

41. PUBLIC CEMETERY SEARCHABLE-MEMORY / NO-RECORD-EXTRACTION RULE (rev0043)
When an entry involves public cemeteries, potter's fields, burial-record databases, city open-data burial datasets, grave-location maps, cemetery-management systems, public tours, family/chosen-family visitation, story platforms, anonymity clocks, posthumous name recovery, or capital plans for burial grounds, public access is not public consent. Official records and public databases may support high-level system shape, record-integrity gaps, privacy redaction, capacity-study, and planning-boundary claims only after no-record-extraction review. Do not republish individual names, plot/section/grave identifiers, permit or Medical Examiner metadata, dates or places of death tied to an individual, institutional death-location lists, fetal-remains details, family-submitted stories, images, map coordinates, search instructions, visit/scheduling instructions, contact fields, or public-tour logistics. Family access is not a tourism script; a dataset is not a memorial license; searchable memory is not return.

42. STREET-MEDICINE MEDIA/DATA / NO-ENCAMPMENT-EXTRACTION RULE (rev0044)
When an entry involves street medicine, unsheltered outreach, encampment health care, field rounds, peer/lived-experience guides, outreach media, program maps, geocoding, clinical triage technology, AI-assisted intake/diagnosis, law-enforcement partnership, coordinated encampment response, 311 or complaint data, or public descriptions of where people live outside, sources may support only high-level office shape, ethical boundary, field-building, housing-substitution, privacy, and surveillance-risk claims unless a separate patient-consent and data-governance review is passed. Do not republish patient names, faces, stories, clinical details, photographs, consent scenes, sleeping locations, routes, outreach schedules, staff patterns, camp names, GPS/geocoded data, by-name lists, AI prompts/outputs, law-enforcement handoff details, complaint-derived locations, contact fields, or referral instructions. A house call to the street is not permission to map the street; clinical presence is not consent to publicity; data that finds care can also find policing.

43. HOMELESS MEMORIAL / MORTALITY DATA / NO-NAME RECORD-EXTRACTION RULE (rev0045)
When an entry involves people who died while homeless, Homeless Persons Memorial Day, Dying Homeless-style projects, homeless-death counts, local vigils, mortality-review reports, public-health death data, Medical Examiner/coroner-derived homeless mortality work, memorial pages, by-name rolls, tribute submissions, photographs, symbolic coffins, shoes, candles, or community grief rituals, public remembrance is not public consent for the cube to repeat a death record. Sources may support aggregate memorial-office shape, mortality-review method, prevention/accountability pressure, undercount caution, and housing-not-memorial boundary claims only. Do not republish individual names, ages, exact death dates or locations, causes or manners of death, case identifiers, images, family tributes, biographies, quotations, memorial-page text, event locations, livestream/registration or submit-a-name paths, contact fields, FOIA/contact mechanics, or reader-directed death-record collection. A name read in love is not a data field; a count is not prevention; a vigil is not housing.


44. OVERDOSE MEMORIAL / TRIBUTE-WALL / NO-EVENT-MAP RULE (rev0046)
When an entry involves overdose memorials, International Overdose Awareness Day, fentanyl memorial exhibits, tribute walls, name readings, ribbons, candles, overdose-vigil events, event maps, landmark light-ups, prevention campaigns, naloxone or overdose-response training events, family-submitted photos/stories, drug/cause-of-death detail, or stigma-reduction language, public remembrance is not public consent for extraction. Sources may support high-level campaign shape, aggregate mortality/prevention pressure, and person-first language boundaries only. Do not republish names, relationships, photographs, tribute text, exact death dates, cause or drug details, event names, event locations or times, map markers, organizer contacts, registration paths, landmark details, memorial-exhibit submissions, broad photo/story reuse licenses, training/supply access paths, current service capacity, medical advice, referral wording, or punishment/war-on-drugs framing as memorial proof. Overdose is not a moral verdict; a tribute is not a data field; an event map is not a cube map.


45. MIGRANT DEATH MONITORING / DISTRESS-CALL / NO-ROUTE NO-SIGNAL EXTRACTION RULE (rev0047)
When an entry involves migrant deaths or disappearances, distress-call witness, missing boats, route-death counts, family searches for people missing on migration routes, shipwreck reporting, counter-forensic border ledgers, Missing Migrants-style datasets, maritime SAR gaps, or public material about where/how people crossed, accountability evidence is not public route intelligence. Sources may support aggregate death/disappearance monitoring, undercount/method caution, right-to-life accountability, and family-memory pressure only. Do not republish route details as instructions, coordinates, departure/landing points beyond non-operational aggregate labels, boat traces, distress-call logs, alert timing, search-and-rescue operational patterns, family-search contact paths, missing-person leads, names, ages/nationalities tied to incidents, images, case rows, database coordinates, smugglers/guide details, rescue-capacity claims, legal advice, or referral wording. A distress call is not a route log; a death count is not a map; a family-search form is not a public lead.


46. MMIWG2S+ FAMILY-LED SEARCH / NO-CASE-LIST NO-VIGIL-MAP RULE (rev0048)
When an entry involves MMIWG2S+ family-led search, Families of Sisters in Spirit, Sisters in Spirit, Red Dress Day, red-dress installations, family vigils, missing and murdered Indigenous women/girls/two-spirit people, inquiry testimony, Calls for Justice reporting, Indigenous data projects, trafficking/highway/cross-border/resource-extraction vulnerability patterns, or survivor/family support surfaces, public witness is not public extraction permission. Sources may support aggregate family-led witness, inquiry context, implementation-gap pressure, and accountability claims only after no-case-list/no-vigil-map review. Do not republish person-by-person case lists, victim/survivor/family names except after family-chosen/load-bearing review, photographs, family tributes, testimony excerpts, police-file details, death or disappearance circumstances, red-dress imagery, vigil/event logistics, march planning paths, submit-a-name or report-a-case routes, social-media group routes, support/contact fields, trafficking/highway/cross-border pathway details, or scorecard language implying completion, safety, family satisfaction, service capacity, or closure. A family-led search is not a case list; a vigil is not a map; an inquiry scorecard is not safety.
