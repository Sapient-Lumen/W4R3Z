# Public Redaction Policy — current

The working cube may contain public-source URLs and detailed research reasoning. A public edition must be smaller and safer.

Automatically exclude or generalise:
1. Precise refuge, shelter, safe-house, resident, staff, route, phone-tree, or intake details.
2. Any non-load-bearing names, especially children, survivors, residents, clients, or grieving relatives.
3. Child images or identifying descriptions.
4. Case facts that turn suffering into illustration rather than evidence.
5. Language implying a service is available now unless current capacity and referral safety have been separately verified.
6. Brochure-style fundraising copy.

Keep:
- candidate IDs, office IDs, high-level institutional shape, status boundaries, evidence-debt categories, and anti-overclaim notes.


rev0026 addition:
- For unclaimed, stillborn, abandoned, or unidentified children, public editions should generalise names, measurements, case facts, and ceremony details unless directly necessary to an audited claim. A public burial service is not automatic permission to make the child an illustrative object.


rev0027 addition:
- For public cemeteries, potter's fields, burial databases, grave-location maps, and memorial story platforms, public editions should not replicate individual names, plot numbers, Medical Examiner numbers, exact grave-location data, fetal-remains details, family stories, images, or visit/scheduling details unless directly necessary to an audited claim. Searchable memory is not return, and public record access is not permission to re-own the dead.


rev0028 addition:
- For street-medicine, outreach, encampment, and unsheltered-health entries, public editions should suppress patient names, clinical details, images, case stories, camp locations, outreach routes, staff patterns, schedules, geocoded data, and live-referral instructions. Treat encampment knowledge as operationally sensitive even when a public source names a general setting. Outreach is not housing, and medical care outside should not be used as proof that the person has been socially housed.

rev0030 addition:
- Publicly posted contact digits, phone/helpline/hotline/toll-free numbers, intake numbers, consultation lines, exact coordinates, street addresses, plot numbers, Medical Examiner numbers, camp locations, route/schedule details, and case identifiers are not repeated by default. This cube is not a live referral directory. Public-source availability is not enough; contact or locator details must be load-bearing, safety-reviewed, current-capacity-reviewed, and explicitly exempted before inclusion.

rev0031 addition:
- A public export is contract-gated. Public manifest revision, source-role
  vocabulary, ledger/JSON parity, and schema validation must pass before any
  public reading edition is treated as handoff-safe.

## rev0034 source-promotion / quarantine policy

Online research intake is not public evidence by default. Contact-rich service pages, help-directory listings, counterpressure PDFs, recognition profiles, and official self-descriptions require explicit promotion decisions before they can support public wording. Public present-tense capacity language is blocked for all quarantined claim rows.


rev0036 addition:
- Counterevidence promotion is not a public-current claim release. Critical or beneficiary/staff-grounded research may be used to constrain praise, but it does not permit present-tense capacity, route, selection-access, or referral wording.

## rev0037 conflict-zone mutual-aid additions

For active-war mutual-aid networks, redact or omit operational surfaces: room names, kitchen sites, aid routes, schedules, volunteer identities, local committee lists, communication patterns, transfer channels, donor-routing steps, recipient details, and current access points. Aggregate recognition and localization context may remain only after conflict-safety review.


## rev0038 border counterpressure additions

For border-observation and counterpressure sources, redact or generalise living-site names, camp locations, routes, schedules, police timing patterns, bus/accommodation instructions, images, individual testimonies, contact details, volunteer patterns, and aid-distribution details. Aggregate eviction/seizure/pressure language may remain only after no-route/no-location/no-image review.


## rev0039 survivor-service additions

For survivor-service, GBV crisis-centre, helpline, safe-house, refuge, counselling, and case-management sources, redact or generalise exact contact fields, office/refuge/shelter locations, intake hours, access routes, staff names used as access points, transport instructions, survivor stories, child/family details, images, and capacity indicators. Regional standards/funding may be named only as context, never as referral or current-capacity proof.

## rev0040 family-search no-field-map addition
For missing-person search collectives and family-led search entries, public prose must not reproduce search fields, routes, sites, tips, grave or clandestine-site details, forensic identifiers, registry IDs, images, family contacts, current brigade locations, protection arrangements, or individual case stories. Registry reclassification is not return or closure.


## rev0041 law-to-door no-service-capacity addition

Legal/protection-order/court/police/adviser/report sources are not public service doors. Public export must remove or block legal advice, court or police filing instructions, protection-order-as-safety language, No Drop-as-survivor-choice language, compulsory-counselling or family-repair benefit language, live referral phrasing, service-capacity claims, enforcement guarantees, shelter/refuge access, case-file/evidence-chain detail, and staff/contact routes. A law is not a door; a protection order is not safety; a justice-pathways report is not counsel.

## rev0042 / rule 40 — posthumous child-burial no-case-extraction

For posthumous child-burial entries, the public layer must not reproduce assigned child names, poems, images, video stills, ceremony/service details, public attendance prompts, cemetery/location cues, contact fields, discovery details, family-status claims, dates, measurements, identifiers, grave/service specifics, toys, blankets, caskets, urns, or donor/religious narration as if public source visibility were consent. High-level office shape may be considered only after boundary-first manual review.

## rev0043 / rule 41 — public cemetery no-record-extraction

For public cemetery, potter's-field, burial-database, grave-map, cemetery-management, story-platform, and public-visitation entries, the public layer must not reproduce individual names, plot/section/grave identifiers, permit or Medical Examiner metadata, dates or places of death tied to people, fetal-remains details, family-submitted stories, images, map coordinates, search instructions, visit/scheduling instructions, contact fields, public-tour logistics, or dataset rows. Public data may support only system-shape and boundary claims after manual review.

## rev0046 / rule 44 — overdose memorial no-tribute/no-event-map extraction

For overdose-memorial, IOAD, fentanyl memorial, tribute-wall, local-vigil, landmark-lighting, and prevention-campaign entries, the public layer must not reproduce names, relationships, photographs, tribute text, exact death dates, cause/drug details, event names, event locations or times, map markers, organizer contacts, registration paths, landmark details, memorial-exhibit submissions, broad photo/story reuse licenses, training/naloxone/supply access paths, current service capacity, medical advice, referral wording, or punishment/war-on-drugs framing as memorial proof. High-level campaign shape, aggregate mortality/prevention pressure, and stigma-safe language may be considered only after boundary-first review.


### Migrant death monitoring / distress-call evidence

Do not publish routes as instructions, coordinates, departure/landing points, boat traces, distress-call logs, alert timing, SAR operational patterns, family-search contacts, missing-person leads, incident rows, names, images, or age/nationality clusters tied to incidents. Death counts and undercount evidence may appear only as aggregate accountability context after no-route/no-record review.


## rev0048 / rule 46 — MMIWG2S+ family-led search no-case-list/no-vigil-map extraction

For MMIWG2S+ family-led search, Families of Sisters in Spirit, Sisters in Spirit, Red Dress Day, inquiry, Indigenous data, and family-vigil entries, the public layer must not reproduce case lists, victim/survivor/family name rolls, photographs, family tributes, testimony excerpts, police-file details, death or disappearance circumstances, red-dress imagery, vigil/event logistics, submit-a-name or report-a-case routes, social-media group routes, support/contact fields, trafficking/highway/cross-border/resource-extraction pathway details, or progress-scorecard language as proof of completion, safety, family satisfaction, service capacity, or closure. Aggregate family-led witness and implementation-gap context may be considered only after boundary-first review.
