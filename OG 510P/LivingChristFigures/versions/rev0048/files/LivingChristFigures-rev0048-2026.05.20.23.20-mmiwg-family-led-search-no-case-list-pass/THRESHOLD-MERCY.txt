THRESHOLD MERCY

A threshold is not a vibe.
A threshold is a change in status, place, legality, health, memory, or name.
It is where systems ask for paperwork, money, family, sobriety, respectability, address, diagnosis, citizenship, kinship, capacity, or a corpse-claimant.

The cube is learning that abandonment often happens at thresholds:
- hospital discharge;
- hospice admission;
- prison entry;
- refugee crossing;
- cemetery claim;
- dementia onset;
- addiction crisis;
- sexual exploitation exit;
- family rejection after diagnosis;
- name loss after street death.

The threshold office is not “I found you.”
It is “I will not let this crossing erase you.”

But threshold mercy has dangers:
- The helper may control the door.
- The helper may make exit conditional on belief, obedience, sobriety, gratitude, donor pictures, or conversion into a success story.
- The helper may confuse rescue with ownership.

Therefore the rev0010 law:
The office is the threshold held open without owning the one who crosses it.
