REASONING NOTE rev0029 — why the next pass was meta, not candidate expansion

The cube has enough candidate momentum. The higher risk now is that the cube becomes increasingly persuasive while its self-audit remains weaker than its prose.

The rev0028 package itself showed a small but important example: the payload and QA were coherent, but `manifest.json` still carried stale rev0027 counts and summary language. That is not catastrophic, but it is the kind of quiet metadata drift that future readers and tools will trust before they read the body.

The more serious method risk is operator absorption of dissent. The cube has excellent norms: evidence before desire, scale before devotion, exit before intimacy, procedure before myth. But norms are not instruments. Rev0029 therefore adds instruments:

- A dissent ledger with behavior-change fields.
- A manifest audit and QA check.
- A claim taxonomy to keep evidence standards visible.
- A source provenance audit to prevent source-role flattening.
- An evidence debt dashboard so debt remains a work queue.
- A public export checklist so the cube does not accidentally become a referral guide, hagiography, or doxxing surface.

No claim here resolves the operator-centrality problem. It makes the problem auditable enough to continue.

Boundary sentence for this pass:
A beautiful dissent registry is not yet a veto mechanism.
