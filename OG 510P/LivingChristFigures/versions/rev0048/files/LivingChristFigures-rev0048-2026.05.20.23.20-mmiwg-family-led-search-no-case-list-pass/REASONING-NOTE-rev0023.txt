Verification target 1: Pohnpei/Kosrae law-to-door line

Before rev0023:
CANDIDATES/FSM-Pohnpei-Kosrae-Family-Protection-Adviser.txt was a PROVISIONAL
KEEP / law-to-door implementation entry. Its key caution was that adviser
support is not itself accessible counselling, shelter, legal aid, or safe
transport, and that current evidence beyond 2019 was needed.

What rev0023 adds:
- Pacific Data Hub confirms the Family Protection Adviser worked with FSM DHSA
  to support implementation of the Pohnpei Domestic Violence Act 2017 and the
  Kosrae Family Protection Act 2014, with a focus on strengthening services,
  collaboration between providers, advocacy, and policy reform.
- Pohnpei primary law confirms protection-order routes, ex parte orders, private
  hearings, no filing fees for actions seeking only chapter relief, law
  enforcement procedures/recording, and an offender-oriented counselling /
  education / treatment program for persons ordered because of diversion,
  deferred plea, conviction, or adjudication.
- Pohnpei assessment material warns against overclaim: gaps in safe short-term
  accommodation, material/financial aid, food, transport, crisis information,
  counselling, safe accommodation, referral-network operationalization, and
  legal weaknesses remained visible in the 2018 mapping.
- Kosrae primary law confirms protection and occupation orders, ex parte interim
  orders, police duties to respond to every request for assistance, treat calls
  as priority, assess primary aggressor, not coerce/dissuade arrest, arrange
  transport to medical care or a safe place/shelter where needed, accompany a
  victim to retrieve belongings, file reports, and give written notice of
  rights/remedies/services. Orders by consent can include counselling only with
  consent of the parties, so this is not a Nauru-style compulsory survivor-
  binding counselling configuration.
- UNDP/MLSC North Pacific work confirms the larger capacity caution: FSM's
  decentralized structure results in disjointed services/protections, and
  integrated APIMAR-style referral in Chuuk has not been widely replicated across
  FSM states.

rev0023 reading:
The Pohnpei/Kosrae entry HOLDS, but only as law-to-door / coordination / gap-
repair evidence. It is not upgraded to a crisis-centre or steady-state survivor-
service claim. Current capacity remains debt, not fact.
