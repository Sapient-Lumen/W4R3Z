NAME: Nauru counselling and social-work qualification pool / service-capacity precursor
LOCATION: Republic of Nauru
OFFICE: Counselling and social-work capacity where formal survivor services are thin
STATUS: PROVISIONAL KEEP; CAPACITY-BUILDING ENTRY; NOT SERVICE-CAPACITY

SUMMARY:
Pacific Data Hub’s Nauru EVAW collection includes an “Enhancing Social Work and Counselling Qualifications of Nauruans” entry: five women and one man undertaking a Diploma in Community Health and Wellbeing through a Pacific Community Health and Wellbeing Education project. The same Nauru collection points to child-protection, South-South exchange, COVID response, counselling-service review, and evidence/data materials. This entry therefore records a service-capacity precursor, not a crisis centre.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The law is too thin without people trained enough to receive disclosure, manage risk, refer safely, and avoid retraumatising the person seeking help. This is another “office before the office” entry: the fragile formation of local social-work/counselling capacity.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- Local Diploma-level counselling/social-work capacity-building.
- EVAW service-strengthening context.
- Adjacent child-protection and domestic-violence implementation line.
- Gap marker between legislation and accessible counselling/safe support.

EVIDENCE:
1. Pacific Data Hub Nauru dataset: five women and one man undertaking Diploma in Community Health and Wellbeing to enhance local counselling/social-work capacity.
2. Nauru dataset list: related entries on child protection, South-South exchanges, COVID response, review of counselling services, and EVAW evidence/data.
3. Nauru Judiciary and DFAT sources show legal architecture exists, but the cube keeps trained capacity distinct from service availability.

DOOR-ETHICS FLAGS:
- Training or diploma participation is not a live service.
- Current employment, supervision, caseload, privacy, language access, and survivor trust remain unknown.
- Do not use individual trainees as hero figures.
