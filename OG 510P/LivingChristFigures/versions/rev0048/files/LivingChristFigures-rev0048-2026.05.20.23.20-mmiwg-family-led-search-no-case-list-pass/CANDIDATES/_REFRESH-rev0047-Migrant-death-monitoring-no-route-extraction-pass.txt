# rev0047 — Migrant death monitoring / no-route no-distress-signal pass

CENTRAL SENTENCE: A distress call is not a route log; a death count is not a map; a family-search form is not a public lead.

## Why this pass exists

The cube already held Helena Maleno / Caminando Fronteras as a distress-call witness and counter-forensic border-death office. Rev0047 deepens that entry because the same source ecology that proves the office also contains a dangerous public-extraction surface: route categories, missing-boat reports, family-search contact paths, death/disappearance counts, and incident datasets.

The positive office is real: people at sea and families after disappearance should not be erased by border policy, delayed rescue, or statistical silence. The new boundary is equally real: the cube must not turn that witness into a public route log, a distress-call prompt, a family-search intake surface, or an incident database.

## Source decisions

- Caminando Fronteras Monitoring the Right to Life 2025: existing source row updated for aggregate Western Euro-African border death/disappearance monitoring and right-to-life accountability only. Route labels and family-search proximity remain non-extractive.
- IOM 2026 news on 2025 migrant deaths: promoted for aggregate global context and undercount pressure only.
- IOM Missing Migrants methodology: promoted for methodology/data-boundary context only. It supports the fact that migrant-death data are incident/route-adjacent and therefore require no-coordinate/no-record handling.
- Reuters April 2026 reporting: promoted as independent undercount, funding, and tracking-access caution only.
- arXiv hidden-fatalities preprint: held as method-caution intake. It may become useful if peer reviewed or replicated, but rev0047 does not promote it or use it to change a public death toll.
- Caminando first-five-months 2025 contact surface: held as redaction-risk intake. A family-search link or distress-contact path is not a cube lead.
