REFRESH NOTE — rev0023
SUBJECT: Pohnpei / Kosrae law-to-door line — verification outcome: HOLDS, but only as law/coordination/gap-repair evidence; current accessible capacity unverified

Earlier status:
CANDIDATES/FSM-Pohnpei-Kosrae-Family-Protection-Adviser.txt was admitted as a
PROVISIONAL KEEP / LAW-TO-DOOR IMPLEMENTATION ENTRY, with an explicit
ADVISER-NOT-SERVICE WARNING.

rev0023 verification outcome:
The entry still holds, but its status should be read narrowly. Public sources
support a real legal/coordination line. They do not support a claim that a
survivor in Pohnpei or Kosrae can reliably access confidential counselling,
shelter, legal aid, safe transport, or survivor-led support at the door today.

What holds:
- Pacific Data Hub records a Family Protection Adviser working with the FSM
  Department of Health and Social Affairs to support implementation of the
  Pohnpei Domestic Violence Act 2017 and the Kosrae Family Protection Act 2014,
  with focus on strengthening services, collaboration between service providers,
  advocacy, and policy reform.
- UNDP/MLSC North Pacific work confirms that Kosrae and Pohnpei have domestic
  violence laws, while FSM decentralization produces disjointed services and
  protections across states.

Pohnpei — primary/legal and assessment findings:
- Pohnpei's Domestic Violence Act provides victim-filed protection-order routes,
  private hearings, ex parte temporary orders without respondent notice where
  probable cause/immediate danger is found, no filing fees for actions seeking
  only chapter relief, and law-enforcement procedures/record systems for
  protection orders.
- The counselling/education/treatment provision located in the primary law is
  directed at a person ordered to participate because of diversion, deferred
  plea, conviction, or adjudication. This is offender/perpetrator-oriented and
  should not be read as guaranteed survivor counselling.
- The 2018 Pohnpei/Chuuk EVAW assessment warns that the Act was a good start but
  still weak on sentencing, lacked police-issued protection orders, contained
  contradictory provisions, and needed practical referral-network implementation.
- The same assessment names concrete service gaps: at least overnight safe
  accommodation, material/financial aid, food, transport, crisis information,
  counselling, and safe accommodation access.

Kosrae — primary/legal findings:
- Kosrae's Family Protection Act includes protection/occupation orders and ex
  parte interim orders.
- Police duties include responding to every request for assistance, giving
  priority to domestic-violence calls, developing departmental policy/protocol,
  assessing primary aggressor, avoiding coercion or dissuasion against arrest,
  using lawful means to prevent further violence, transporting victims to
  medical treatment or a safe place/shelter where needed, filing reports,
  accompanying a victim to retrieve belongings, and giving immediate written
  notice of rights/remedies/services.
- The orders-by-consent section allows counselling only where the parties
  consent. This is structurally different from Nauru's mandatory, court-reported,
  joint-or-several counselling. Kosrae is therefore not upgraded, but it is also
  not assigned the same coerced-reconciliation flag on this text.

Status after rev0023:
- KEEP the Pohnpei/Kosrae adviser line.
- Read it as law-to-door / implementation / collaboration / gap-repair evidence.
- Do NOT read it as a verified current crisis-service or survivor-accessible
  steady-state support claim.
- Capacity/current status remains unresolved under rule 16.

Carry-forward:
- current counsellor/shelter/legal-aid/safe-transport capacity;
- whether referral networks are operational and survivor-consent-based;
- disability, language, rural/outer-island, poverty, and child-safety access;
- privacy and data-sharing safeguards;
- enforcement reliability and protection-order implementation;
- survivor-side or independent evaluation.

Door-ethics note:
This note records public legal and institutional shape only. It does not record
shelter locations, route details, live intake paths, staff rosters, or survivor
identifiers.
