REFRESH NOTE — rev0019
SUBJECT: North Pacific crisis-centre line; donor, capacity, and “first door” risk

rev0019 adds KWCSC, Kiribati SafeNet/OLSH, CWC/TICC/APIMAR SafeNet, and WUTMI/Weto In Mour. The shared pattern is small-island or atoll survivor-care infrastructure built under scarcity. Many sources are from Pacific Women, SPC, UN Women, or donor/partner channels; they are credible for institutional shape but not sufficient for final judgment.

Cautions:
- “First crisis centre” is evidence of need and scarcity, not a trophy.
- Referral networks are not always actual safe transport, shelter beds, legal remedies, or community protection.
- Police involvement can be protective or harmful depending on training, survivor consent, and accountability.
- Faith-based shelter or accommodation lines need autonomy/precondition checks.
- Outer-island survivors may still face distance, privacy, language, family, transport, and retaliation barriers.
