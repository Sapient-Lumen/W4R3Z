# rev0042 — Garden of Innocence child-burial no-case-extraction pass

CENTRAL SENTENCE: A child's name, image, ceremony, or public service notice is evidence of redaction risk before it is evidence for public prose.

## Why this pass exists

The rev0040 roadmap named a non-extractive image/name/case-story audit as the next useful pass before any public death, disappearance, forensic, or search-collective prose. Rev0042 applies that audit to the most vulnerable posthumous child-burial entry: Garden of Innocence.

The office remains real enough for the cube: public sources support a child-burial, naming, and communal-witness shape. The danger is that the same public sources also contain the exact kinds of material the public cube must not amplify: names assigned to children after death, poems, ceremony images, current service prompts, cemetery/location cues, contact fields, and case-story fragments.

## Sources reviewed and promotion decision

- Garden of Innocence official home page: already registered as mission/shape evidence. Re-reviewed only as a contact-rich, image-rich, donor/volunteer-facing primary source. It is not a live referral or public-capacity source.
- Garden of Innocence founder/story page: held as intake, not promoted. It demonstrates why official narrative can become too detailed for public extraction: the page describes naming, ritual, case-story fragments, and public participation while also publishing contact fields.
- The Southern Cross 2024 anniversary report: already registered. It supports the San Diego origin line and rough service-shape context, but its ceremony details, images, assigned child names, and religious/ritual texture are treated as redaction-risk material.
- Orange County Cemetery District Garden of Innocence services page: already registered. It supports public-cemetery partnership shape only. Its service notices, public attendance invitation, cemetery/location information, and contact fields are not copied into the cube as instructions.
- Sacramento News & Review critical reporting: already registered. It remains the category-boundary pressure source: abandoned, unidentified, unclaimed, stillborn, indigent, family-unable, and family-unwilling categories cannot be collapsed.

## Candidate effects

- The Garden of Innocence candidate is refreshed to rev0042 and remains admitted only as high-level posthumous care / unclaimed-child burial shape.
- `claim_0250`–`claim_0254` keep their quarantine status. This pass does not lower the evidence lifecycle risk because the risk is not just source count; it is narrative extraction.
- `debt_0150`, `debt_0151`, and `debt_0152` are sharpened around category truth, family knowledge/consent, and public ceremony/image risk.
- `negcase_0016` records that publicly visible child-burial material is not permission to repeat a child's assigned name, image, story, service date, burial location, or contact path.

## Public claim release

None. Rev0042 releases no public claim, no current service description, no ceremony schedule, no location, no contact field, no child name, no poem, no image, and no case-story detail. The only allowed future public use is boundary-first, high-level shape language after manual review.

## Blocked by rule 40

- assigned child names and poems;
- images or video stills of services, graves, children, caskets, urns, toys, blankets, or ceremony participants;
- current or future service notices, public attendance prompts, cemetery/location details, and contact fields;
- individual case-story fragments, discovery details, family/parental-status claims, measurements, dates, or identifiers;
- religious or donor narration presented as proof of the child's consent, family consent, or category truth.

## Next review

A future public-writing pass may use this entry only if it begins from the boundary: burial can be dignity without becoming consent to narration. The public version should describe the office shape without importing the child-specific details that made the source emotionally powerful.
