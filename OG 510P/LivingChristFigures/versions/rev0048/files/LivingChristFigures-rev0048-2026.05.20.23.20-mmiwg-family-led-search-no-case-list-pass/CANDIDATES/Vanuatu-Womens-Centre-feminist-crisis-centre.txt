NAME: Vanuatu Women’s Centre (VWC)
LOCATION: Vanuatu
OFFICE: Feminist crisis centre / counselling, legal advocacy, safe house, outer-island committees
STATUS: KEEP; PERMANENT VANUATU SURVIVOR-CARE FIND; LOCAL-INSTITUTION CANDIDATE WITH LIMITED-RESOURCE FLAG

SUMMARY:
The Vanuatu Women’s Centre provides free and confidential counselling for women and children across Vanuatu who have experienced domestic violence, sexual assault, child abuse, and sexual harassment. Its services include centre-based counselling, mobile outreach to rural villages and settlements, legal information and advocacy, referrals/accompaniment to courts, police stations, hospitals, and CAVAW committees, five provincial counselling centres, community awareness, 39 active Committees Against Violence Against Women (CAVAWs), and a safe house for women and children needing temporary shelter.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
This office sits between the survivor and several dangerous thresholds at once: family, island geography, court, police, medical care, protection orders, and temporary shelter. The mercy is practical navigation, not rescue ownership.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- Free/confidential counselling.
- Mobile outreach to rural/outer-island communities.
- Legal advice, accompaniment, referral.
- CAVAW volunteer/community network.
- Safe-house threshold with resource/time limits.

EVIDENCE:
1. VWC Services page: centre-based and mobile counselling, free/confidential services, community workshops/radio/education, 39 active CAVAWs, legal advocacy, and safe-house provision.
2. VWC Counselling page: free/confidential counselling for women and children; legal advice and referrals to courts, police, hospitals, and CAVAWs; five provincial counselling centres.
3. VWC Legal/Get Support pages: legal information/advice, court/police/medical/community-leader accompaniment, protection-order advice, and law reform/human-rights education.

DOOR-ETHICS FLAGS:
- Safe-house stays are publicly described as time-limited due to resources; do not romanticise capacity.
- CAVAW/community volunteers need support and safeguarding; do not assume all local referral paths are equally safe.
- Future pass should seek current annual data, survivor-safe voice, and independent evaluation.
