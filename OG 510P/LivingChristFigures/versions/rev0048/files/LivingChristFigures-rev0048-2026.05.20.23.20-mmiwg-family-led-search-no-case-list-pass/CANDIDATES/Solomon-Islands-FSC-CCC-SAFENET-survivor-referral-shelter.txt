NAME: Solomon Islands Family Support Centre / Christian Care Centre / SAFENET
LOCATION: Solomon Islands
OFFICE: Survivor counselling-referral network plus short-term shelter threshold
STATUS: KEEP AS NETWORK-LINEAGE; MIXED SECULAR/FAITH-BASED SURVIVOR-CARE OFFICE; SHELTER-SAFETY FLAGS

SUMMARY:
The Family Support Centre (FSC) was established in 1995 to address domestic violence, sexual abuse, child abuse, and rape in the Solomon Islands. Its site lists a toll-free number and describes confidential therapeutic and domestic-violence counselling, legal advice on women’s rights, free confidential counselling and case-management support for women and girls who are survivors of violence. IWDA describes FSC as the first and only secular organisation providing counselling and legal support services to GBV survivors, facilitating access to health, legal, and justice services through SAFENET, and serving more than 1,000 women experiencing violence in 2025 through its head office, provincial branch, and committees of volunteers. The Christian Care Centre (CCC), operated by Anglican sisters, is described by the Anglican Church of Melanesia and the Australian High Commission as the sole/key provider of short-term shelter for women and children affected by gender-based violence, providing emergency accommodation, counselling, mediation, referrals, and practical support.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office is split but connected: secular counselling and legal support; a national referral network; and a church-run shelter where the door is literal. The survivor should not have to make the police/clinic/court/shelter map alone.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- Confidential counselling and case management.
- Legal advice and women’s-rights support.
- SAFENET referral to health, justice, and legal services.
- Short-term shelter for women and children.
- Rural/provincial volunteer-referral pathways.

EVIDENCE:
1. FSC official site: toll-free public helpline [number redacted — non-referral cube]; established in 1995; confidential therapeutic/DV counselling; legal advice; free confidential counselling and case-management for survivors.
2. IWDA FSC page: secular counselling/legal-support role, SAFENET referrals, 2025 service reach, rural volunteer networks.
3. Anglican Church of Melanesia CCC page and Australian High Commission award page: CCC as short-term shelter/key provider for women and children affected by GBV, operated by Anglican sisters, with emergency accommodation and other services.

DOOR-ETHICS FLAGS:
- Keep FSC, CCC, and SAFENET distinct; do not merge counselling, legal support, referral, and shelter into one brand.
- Faith-based shelter context requires later precondition and autonomy checks.
- Do not name survivors or describe shelter routes/operations.
- Sources are partner/organisation heavy; needs survivor-safe and independent evaluation.
