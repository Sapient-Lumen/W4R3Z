# rev0040 refresh — Mexico buscadoras / family-search collectives no-field-map pass

## Pass type
Candidate-specific source-promotion and boundary hardening. The purpose is not to make the entry more vivid, but to make it less extractive. The cube can honor family-led search only by refusing to turn search into a field map.

## Sources promoted in this pass
- `src_amnesty_org_c3d47c42` — Amnesty International, July 2025. Use as structural evidence for scale, gendered risk, violence/harm experienced by women searchers, and the right/protection frame. Do not extract anonymous testimony, named case details, or access/contact surfaces.
- `src_oas_org_d12a4efb` — IACHR/OAS, 2026 report. Use as multilateral context for insufficient state response, family/collective search, forensic and protection gaps, and persistent risk. Do not extract locations, operational examples, case details, protection arrangements, or forensic descriptions.
- `src_theguardian_com_fb8f4d98` — Guardian coverage of the IACHR report, May 2026. Use as current public-reporting context for the crisis and for the danger faced by people searching for family members. Do not use as case-story material or image permission.
- `src_reuters_com_b7abea3d` — Reuters registry cross-checking/reclassification report, March 2026. Use as a registry-boundary source: possible database activity is not return, safe closure, family consent, or proof that search can stop.

## Claim effects
- `claim_0118` now carries current structural/protection evidence for the office shape, but still cannot become a map of brigades, sites, routes, tips, or discoveries.
- `claim_0119` remains interpretive; promoted sources constrain the interpretation by insisting that search is a right and a risk-bearing practice, not an aesthetic symbol.
- `claim_0120` is strengthened as the main no-field-map caution. It now blocks field sites, routes, tips, grave or clandestine-site details, forensic identifiers, registry identifiers, clothing/remains descriptions, images, family contacts, current brigade locations, protection arrangements, and individual case-story extraction.

## Promotion decision
Promoted, but only as aggregate structural/protection/context evidence. No public-current claim is released. No live-referral, live-protection, current-safety, operational-access, forensic-location, or family-case wording is authorized.

## Negative case recorded
A family-search entry can become harmful when it transforms courageous public advocacy into a usable map of where people search, how they find sites, which families are exposed, or which remains/cases are narratively available. rev0040 records the negative case: visibility is not a field map; registry reclassification is not return; discovery is not consent to narrate the dead.

## Remaining blocks
Public prose must not reproduce:
- search fields, routes, site names, tip channels, current brigade locations, or protection arrangements;
- grave, clandestine-site, cremation, exhumation, or forensic detail;
- registry identifiers, file numbers, clothing/remains descriptions, images, or individual case stories;
- family contacts, staff/access channels, security patterns, or current “safe to search” claims.

## Next useful pass
A non-extractive image/name/case-story audit before any public prose on death, disappearance, cemetery, forensic, or family-search entries. The audit should ask not “is this public?” but “does repeating it create a new exposure surface?”
