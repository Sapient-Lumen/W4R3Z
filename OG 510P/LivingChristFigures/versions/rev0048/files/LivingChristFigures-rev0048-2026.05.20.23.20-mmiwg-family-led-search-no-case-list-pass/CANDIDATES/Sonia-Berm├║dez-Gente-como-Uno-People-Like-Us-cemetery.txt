Sonia Bermúdez / Gente como Uno / People Like Us cemetery

Status: KEEP WITH AGE/RECOGNITION FLAG
Location: Riohacha, La Guajira, Colombia
Office: Migrant grave / no-name burial / kin to the stranger dead

Work:
Retired forensic/thanatology worker who maintains burial space and funerary care for poor, unclaimed, homeless, and migrant dead, including Venezuelan refugees and migrants whose families cannot afford burial.

Why it belongs / might belong:
The office is a cemetery gate held open for the person whose last border is poverty. She is not rescuing a life; she is refusing the last administrative insult after life.

Flags:
Publicly recognized by UNHCR, the New Yorker, and academic publication; older sources must be refreshed in later passes for current operating status. The aestheticised "migrant grave-keeper" framing is itself a risk — the New Yorker documentary, like the 2025 RHB Bank short film on Tan Swee Ban, can convert costly mercy into image-content. The cube takes the work as documented and does not iterate the imagery.
