Sudan Emergency Response Rooms (ERRs) / غرف الطوارئ — and the nafir tradition that underlies them

Status: OFFICE-CANON; NETWORK-CANDIDATE, NOT SINGLE-FOUNDER CANON; KEEP WITH WAR-CONTEXT, DONOR-LEVERAGE, AND ANONYMITY FLAGS
Location: Sudan — decentralized conflict-zone mutual-aid networks; public cube must not map base rooms, kitchens, routes, transfer paths, local contacts, or volunteer identities
Office: Collapsed-state-neighborhood-kitchen / civilian-led survival threshold during armed conflict and famine

Work:
ERRs are decentralised, volunteer, civilian-led mutual-aid networks that emerged from Sudanese neighbourhood and resistance-committee traditions, first mobilised around COVID-era emergency rooms, and expanded after April 2023 into a conflict-survival infrastructure where state institutions and international access fell short. Published recognition sources now describe a nationwide movement of nearly 26,000 unpaid volunteers providing food, water, medical assistance, evacuations, infrastructure repair, education, and gender-responsive support; SSHAP's 2024 interview-based brief gives the safer internal frame that the 'room' began as online planning rooms and local emergency groups rooted in nafeer / nafir, a call to collective action. Rev0037 deliberately removes more granular room-level counts from the main work sentence: aggregate shape is evidence; operational map is harm.

Why it belongs / might belong:
The cube needed an active-war office where mercy is not a vertical service delivered to passive victims but horizontal survival governance among neighbours who are themselves exposed. The threshold is not just 'neighbourhood with infrastructure' to 'neighbourhood under bombardment'; it is also the moment recognition, donor interest, and international localization language can either protect local responders or convert them into a visible target. This office remains canon only if it is read as a no-map office.

Flags:
ERR volunteers face detention, attack, killing, funding chokepoints, registration barriers, transfer constraints, shrinking civic space, and accusations of collaboration or espionage from armed actors. Rev0037 promotes localization and structural-risk sources only in aggregate. The cube may say recognition and funding debates exist; it may not list rooms, kitchens, routes, transfer channels, local committees, volunteer names or roles, communication patterns, or current access points. Awards, donor invitations, and funding mechanisms are not current-capacity evidence and not a public aid-routing surface.
