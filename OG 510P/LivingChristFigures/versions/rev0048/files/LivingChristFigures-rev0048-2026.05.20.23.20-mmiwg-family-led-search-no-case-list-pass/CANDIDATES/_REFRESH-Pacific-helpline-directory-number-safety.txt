REFRESH NOTE — rev0019
SUBJECT: Helpline and contact-number safety in the cube

The cube increasingly contains helplines, crisis lines, and counselling services. Public numbers can be load-bearing evidence of institutional shape, but the cube should not become a live emergency directory.

Working rule applied in rev0019:
- Record that a public source lists a helpline only as evidence of the office.
- Do not promise that a number is live, safe, confidential, free, or available unless the source itself says so and the entry is current.
- Prefer direct official/service pages over third-party directories.
- Where sources conflict, flag current-status uncertainty rather than smoothing it away.
- Do not copy private phone trees, staff personal contacts, or shelter routes.

This note especially affects KWCSC, WCCC Tonga, SVSG Samoa, FSC Solomon Islands, LifeLine Port Moresby, and any future suicide/postvention entries.
