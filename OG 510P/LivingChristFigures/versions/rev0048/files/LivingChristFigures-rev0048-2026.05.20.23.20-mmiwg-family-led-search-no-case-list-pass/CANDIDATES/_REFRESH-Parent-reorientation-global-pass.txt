REFRESH NOTE — rev0018
SUBJECT: Parent/family reorientation beyond US/Australia/Canada

rev0018 adds FFLAG UK and Fundación Transformando Familias in Argentina to widen the parent-reorientation line. This is still incomplete. Leads to continue:
- Familias por la Diversidad Sexual (Mexico) and other Latin American family networks.
- CONTACT France, BEFAH Germany, Tehila Israel, LGBT Families & Friends Japan, LISTAG Turkey, Tęczowe Rodziny Poland.
- Indigenous, migrant, Black, rural, and faith-specific family support outside anglophone settings.

Ethical caution:
Parent groups can help a child by giving the adult somewhere else to take fear. They can also become a soft gatekeeping system if the parent’s need for comfort displaces the young person’s privacy, consent, autonomy, or safety. Keep the child/youth autonomy flag primary.
