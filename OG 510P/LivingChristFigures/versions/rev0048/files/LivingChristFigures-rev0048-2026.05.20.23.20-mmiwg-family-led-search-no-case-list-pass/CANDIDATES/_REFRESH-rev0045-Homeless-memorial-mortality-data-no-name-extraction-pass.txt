# rev0045 — Homeless memorial / mortality-data no-name extraction pass

CENTRAL SENTENCE: A name read at a vigil is not permission for the cube to republish a death record.

## Why this pass exists

The cube had a homeless memorial office, but its earlier boundary was too soft: "do not publish private names casually." Current memorial and mortality sources show that the risk is sharper. Public vigils, digital memorials, local reporting, county mortality reviews, and death-count projects may be both acts of love and sources of extractable person-level death detail.

Rev0045 therefore treats homeless-memorial and mortality-counting sources as aggregate prevention/accountability evidence first. The cube can honor the office without becoming an obituary scraper, event guide, mortality database, or grief anthology.

## Source decisions

- Museum of Homelessness Dying Homeless Project current page: promoted for UK-wide project shape, annual aggregate findings, memorial practice, and verification-source categories only. Its memorial-page and submission surfaces remain non-extractive.
- Museum of Homelessness 2025 update on 2024 findings: promoted for current aggregate mortality context and the boundary that better counting alone is not enough.
- National Coalition for the Homeless Homeless Persons Memorial Day page: promoted for annual memorial-office shape and prevention framing only; event and name-submission pathways remain blocked.
- Homeless Deaths Count homepage and Help Us Count page: promoted for aggregate US counting, undercount, and no-individual-death submission boundary; contact and FOIA mechanics remain blocked.
- Los Angeles County 2026 homeless-mortality release: promoted for aggregate public-health trend, leading-cause category, prevention, and method-source context only; contact fields and service/referral language remain blocked.
- Multnomah County 2025 Domicile Unknown release: promoted for aggregate local mortality-review method and risk context only; event logistics, names/family stories, and method-overread are blocked.
- Street Sense D.C. vigil reporting: held as redaction-risk intake because it is useful but contains image/event/person-proximity risk; it was not promoted.

## Public claim release

None. Rev0045 releases no name, age, exact death date, death location, cause or manner of death, image, family tribute, Medical Examiner/coroner field, event location, livestream/registration/submission path, contact field, referral instruction, service-capacity claim, medical advice, or housing-success claim.

## Blocked by rule 43

- individual names, ages, exact dates, death locations, causes or manners of death, and case identifiers;
- photographs, video stills, family tributes, personal quotations, memorial-page text, biographies, shoes/candles/coffin scenes, and image descriptions used for pathos;
- Medical Examiner/coroner fields, hospital/facility/death-place detail, registry rows, county report case detail, and family-submitted material;
- event locations, livestream/registration links, local attendance instructions, submit-a-name pathways, organization contact fields, FOIA/contact mechanics, and reader-directed death-record collection;
- claims that memorial, counting, outreach, or public health reporting proves housing access, service capacity, prevention adequacy, or current referral safety.

## Next review

Use this pass before any public prose about Dying Homeless, Homeless Persons Memorial Day, local homeless-death vigils, mortality-review reports, or homeless-death data projects. The public version may say that the deaths were counted and mourned; it must not repeat the death records.
