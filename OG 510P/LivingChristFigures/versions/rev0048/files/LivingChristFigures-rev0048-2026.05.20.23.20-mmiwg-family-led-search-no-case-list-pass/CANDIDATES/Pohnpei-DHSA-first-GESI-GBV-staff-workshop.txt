NAME: Pohnpei Division of FSM Department of Health and Social Affairs — first GESI/GBV staff workshop
LOCATION: Pohnpei State, Federated States of Micronesia
OFFICE: Government-staff orientation as pre-referral door for GBV survivors
STATUS: SCOUT / PROVISIONAL KEEP; CAPACITY-BUILDING ENTRY; NOT CRISIS-CENTRE CAPACITY

SUMMARY:
Pacific Data Hub records that the Pohnpei Division of the FSM Department of Health and Social Affairs held its first Gender Equality and Gender-Based Violence staff workshop for new staff in October 2021. The training was intended to help new staff understand gender equality and social inclusion, gender-based violence, intersectionality, and how these relate to their everyday life and work in government.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
A survivor’s first government contact may not be a specialist counsellor. It may be a health or social-affairs staff member. This entry tracks the thin but important pre-referral threshold: whether ordinary government staff know enough not to blame, expose, dismiss, or misroute someone.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- First GESI/GBV workshop for new Pohnpei DHSA staff.
- Staff understanding of GBV, gender equality, social inclusion, and intersectionality.
- Government pre-referral capacity, not specialist crisis counselling.

EVIDENCE:
1. Pacific Data Hub Pohnpei dataset: first GESI/GBV staff workshop for new Pohnpei DHSA staff.
2. The training aims to connect GESI, GBV, intersectionality, and everyday government work.
3. Pacific Women Lead North Pacific counselling page later identifies specialist crisis counselling scarcity in the region, helping keep this as pre-referral capacity rather than a crisis-centre claim.

DOOR-ETHICS FLAGS:
- Training does not establish survivor access to a specialist service.
- Government staff may also be points of exposure, judgement, or unsafe referral unless confidentiality and consent are enforced.
- Needs current follow-up on whether training became practice.
