NEW CAUTION:
The Foundation for Human Rights and the UnfinishedTRC constitutional-damages case show that reburial, memorial, and partial identification can be necessary but insufficient when prosecutions and reparations are delayed for decades. The FHR programme explicitly supports survivors and families awaiting justice and reparations and includes psychosocial support; the 2025 case by 25 families/survivors and FHR alleges political suppression and delay of TRC prosecutions.

CUBE EFFECT:
The return-of-the-dead office now carries a second-line question: after the body is returned, who keeps the demand for prosecution, reparations, inquiry, memorial, and public education alive?
