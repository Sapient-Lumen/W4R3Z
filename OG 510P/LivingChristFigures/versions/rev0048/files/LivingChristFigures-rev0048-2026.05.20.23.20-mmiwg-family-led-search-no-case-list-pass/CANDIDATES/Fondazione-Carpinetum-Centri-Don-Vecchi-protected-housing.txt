NAME: Fondazione Carpinetum / Centri Don Vecchi protected housing
LOCATION: Mestre / Venice, Italy
OFFICE: Protected housing for self-sufficient vulnerable elders and fragile households
STATUS: SCOUT / PROVISIONAL KEEP; SOURCE-HEAVY; RESIDENT-VOICE AND ELIGIBILITY-EXCLUSION FLAGS

SUMMARY:
Fondazione Carpinetum describes its main activity as managing the Centri Don Vecchi, providing protected housing for weaker population groups. Centri Don Vecchi 1 and 2 include 194 small apartments reserved for self-sufficient elderly people. The request page states eligibility for many centres includes ages 65-85, modest economic condition, normal self-sufficiency, and a guarantor; Centri Don Vecchi 6 and 7 instead serve separated parents or families with small children. The Centro 6/7 page describes 127 units for separated mothers/fathers with dependent children, young couples, self-sufficient disabled people, and others in temporary difficulty.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office is not only a bed. It is an ordinary address kept open before abandonment, forced institutionalisation, or family breakdown becomes the only doorway. The best reading is not charity spectacle but protected ordinariness.

ADJACENT LINEAGE:
Sant'Egidio's co-housing page describes small flats for self-sufficient vulnerable elderly people who are evicted, homeless, or relationally isolated, as well as family-home settings for elders who are not entirely self-sufficient. This is a related protected-housing office, not merged into Don Vecchi.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- Small apartment / protected dwelling.
- Anti-institutionalisation threshold.
- Fragile-family housing threshold.
- Relational watchfulness without full residential institution.

EVIDENCE:
1. Fondazione Carpinetum homepage: foundation manages Centri Don Vecchi and guarantees protected housing for weaker population groups.
2. Centro Don Vecchi 1 and 2 page: 194 apartments, 20-56 sqm, reserved for self-sufficient elderly people.
3. Request-a-home page: eligibility includes 65-85 age band, modest economic condition, normal self-sufficiency, guarantor; Centres 6 and 7 for separated parents or families with small children.
4. Centro Don Vecchi 6 and 7: 127 units for separated parents with dependent children, young couples, self-sufficient disabled people, and people in temporary difficulty.
5. Sant'Egidio co-housing: shelter housing with small flats for self-sufficient vulnerable elderly people and family-home settings for less self-sufficient elders.

DOOR-ETHICS FLAGS:
- Eligibility rules may exclude the most fragile people; guarantor requirement is especially important.
- Needs resident voice, affordability detail, waiting-list/selection process, and independent assessment.
- Catholic foundation framing must not be copied into brochure voice.
