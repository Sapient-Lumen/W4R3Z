NAME: Kiribati Women and Children Support Centre (KWCSC)
LOCATION: Kiribati; Tarawa and Kiritimati public-office line
OFFICE: First women-and-children SGBV support centre / helpline, counselling, legal advice, case management, and crisis shelter
STATUS: KEEP; PACIFIC SURVIVOR-CARE FIND; SMALL-ISLAND SUPPORT-CENTRE ENTRY WITH OPERATIONAL-SAFETY FLAG

SUMMARY:
KWCSC formally opened on 31 January 2018 and describes itself as a feminist non-profit government organisation with a specific role of supporting women and children who are survivors of sexual and gender-based violence. Its public site says it was the first support centre in Kiribati specifically focused on women and children who have experienced sexual and gender-based violence. Services listed publicly include SGBV counselling, legal advice and support, case management, a 24/7 telephone helpline, advocacy and awareness, a survivor support fund, and the Orange Door crisis-shelter initiative.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office is the first named door in a country where the official site itself says women and children experiencing violence lacked proper support, counselling, and legal advice. The threshold is practical: phone, counsellor, legal explanation, case file, safe referral, and a room whose details must not be turned into a map.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- Women-and-children SGBV support centre.
- 24/7 helpline and public office contact line.
- Counselling, legal advice/support, and case management.
- Survivor support fund and crisis-shelter initiative.
- Advocacy and awareness.

EVIDENCE:
1. KWCSC official site: opened 31 January 2018; supports women and children survivors of SGBV; first support centre of its type in Kiribati; services include counselling, legal advice/support, case management, 24/7 telephone helpline, survivor support fund, and crisis shelter.
2. KIT/KWCSC training source: KWCSC signed an MOU to provide counselling support and referrals for female students experiencing SGBV, and trained staff on survivor-centred response and confidential referral.
3. FindAHelpline page: corroborates a public helpline and describes free/confidential 24/7 support with counselling, legal information, case support, and referrals.
