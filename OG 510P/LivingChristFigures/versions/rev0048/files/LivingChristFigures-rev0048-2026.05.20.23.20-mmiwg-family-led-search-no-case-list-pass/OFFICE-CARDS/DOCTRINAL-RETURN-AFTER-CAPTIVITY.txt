The doctrinal-return-after-captivity office is not individual but communal. A religious authority decides that the survivor of forced conversion and sexual enslavement remains a member of the community, and writes that decision into doctrine. The threshold is the temple gate; the office is the rebaptism, the public welcome, the refusal to let the perpetrator's verdict become the community's. The danger is that the welcome arrives partial, with limits the community has not yet been willing to face — and the cube must hold both the welcome and its limits in the same hand.
