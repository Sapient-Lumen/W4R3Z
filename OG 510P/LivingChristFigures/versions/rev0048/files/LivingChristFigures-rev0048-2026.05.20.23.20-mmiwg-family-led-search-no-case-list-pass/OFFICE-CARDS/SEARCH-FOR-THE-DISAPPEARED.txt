The search-for-the-disappeared office takes up the work the state has refused. It does not wait for an investigator; it brings a shovel, a phone, a vigil. The threshold is the moment "missing" stops becoming "actively searched for" — and the office holds that threshold open with the labour of relatives who are themselves at risk of being made to disappear.
