OFFICE CARD: FAMILY-LED JUSTICE AFTER TRC DELAY
REVISION ADDED: rev0017

SHAPE:
A family, survivor group, or legal-rights office refuses the administrative afterlife of atrocity: the case closed without prosecution, the body returned without accountability, the memorial offered as a substitute for remedy, the witness dying while the file waits.

CANDIDATES / LINEAGE:
- Khulumani Support Group (rev0015).
- South Africa Missing Persons Task Team / TRC Unit / Gallows Exhumation work (rev0016, state-linked return-of-remains threshold).
- Foundation for Human Rights / Unfinished Business of the TRC / constitutional-damages families (rev0017).

WHY IT IS A CHRIST-FIGURE OFFICE:
The threshold is kept open after everyone official wants to call the matter historical. Costly mercy is refusing to let the dead and their families be archived before justice is even attempted.

DOOR-ETHICS QUESTIONS:
1. Does burial become closure theatre, or does it leave justice demands intact?
2. Are families agents of the demand, not evidence props?
3. Does litigation seek public repair, not just symbolic recognition?
4. Are state-linked bodies read beside survivor/family critique?
5. What remains owed when witnesses and perpetrators have died during delay?

IMAGE / LANGUAGE RULE:
Do not use exhumation, bones, coffins, or grief images as atmosphere. Names and images must be load-bearing and source-respected.
