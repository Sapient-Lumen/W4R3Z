The suicide-postvention office serves not the dying but the bereaved. Exposure to a suicide raises the risk of further suicides; the office is therefore prevention by another name. The threshold is the survivor's first day after the death, and the office sits with the family for years rather than weeks. Where the office is indigenous-led, it may also be recovering a pre-colonial framework in which suicide was met with compassion rather than shame — the office reframes the stigma the survivor did not invent.
