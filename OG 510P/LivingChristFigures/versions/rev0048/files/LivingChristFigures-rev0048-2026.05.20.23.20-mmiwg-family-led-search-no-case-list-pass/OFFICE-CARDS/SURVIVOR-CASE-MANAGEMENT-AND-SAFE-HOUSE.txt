OFFICE CARD: SURVIVOR CASE-MANAGEMENT AND SAFE-HOUSE DOORWAY
REVISION ADDED: rev0017

SHAPE:
A survivor of family, sexual, sorcery-accusation-related, or gender-based violence is not left to cross police, hospital, welfare, transport, court, shelter, and protection-order thresholds alone. The office is accompaniment through institutions that can save, fail, shame, or endanger.

CANDIDATES / LINEAGE:
- Femili PNG / 1-Tok Kaunselin Helpim Lain / Bel Isi PNG (rev0017).
- Future scouts: Solomon Islands Family Support Centre / SafeNet; other Pacific survivor-referral networks.

WHY IT IS A CHRIST-FIGURE OFFICE:
Costly mercy here is administrative accompaniment under danger, not dramatic rescue ownership. The worker/case manager is not the saviour; the held-open door is the pattern.

DOOR-ETHICS QUESTIONS:
1. Is survivor confidentiality stronger than the story's usefulness to outsiders?
2. Can the survivor refuse an option without losing care?
3. Are police/court/medical referrals genuinely navigable, or just named?
4. Are local staff and survivor voices centered over donor-branding language?
5. Does the office protect from retaliation and institutional harm?

IMAGE / LANGUAGE RULE:
No survivor images or identifying details unless the source and survivor clearly make them public and the detail is load-bearing. Default to structural description.
