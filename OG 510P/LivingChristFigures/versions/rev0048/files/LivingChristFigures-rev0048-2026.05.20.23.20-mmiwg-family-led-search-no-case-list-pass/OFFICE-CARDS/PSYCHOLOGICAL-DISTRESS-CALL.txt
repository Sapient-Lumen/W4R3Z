The psychological-distress-call office is the cube's domestic sibling of the migrant distress-call witness. Both are phones that stay on. But where the migrant distress call is short and concerns coordinates, this call is long and concerns whether the person on the line will be alive tomorrow. The office must be both available and bearable — the volunteer or counsellor at the other end is part of the threshold. In compounding-crisis contexts (collapsed currency, explosion, war, displacement, pandemic) the office may be the last functioning service the state has not absorbed; this is also its danger.
