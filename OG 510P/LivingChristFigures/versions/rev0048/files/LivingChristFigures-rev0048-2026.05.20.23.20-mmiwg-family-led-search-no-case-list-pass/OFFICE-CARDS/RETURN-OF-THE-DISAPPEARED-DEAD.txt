The return-of-the-disappeared-dead office gives a hidden body back to kin, name, ceremony, and place. It can restore dignity without settling justice; reburial must not be made to stand in for prosecution, reparations, or truth.

REV0017 APPEND:
South Africa's FHR / Unfinished Business of the TRC constitutional-damages case adds the justice-after-return layer. Exhumation, identification, handover, and reburial are thresholds, not closure. This card should now point readers to FAMILY-LED-JUSTICE-AFTER-TRC-DELAY whenever a state return-of-remains office risks becoming closure theatre.
