OFFICE CARD: REGIONAL TRAINING AND STANDARDS BACKBONE
REVISION ADDED: rev0018

SHAPE:
Some threshold offices do not mainly appear as one room. They teach other rooms how to exist: training counsellors, sharing survivor-centred frameworks, building regional networks, convening practitioners, and transmitting hard-won standards across places where a new door is still fragile.

CANDIDATES / LINEAGE:
- Fiji Women’s Crisis Centre / Pacific Women’s Network Against Violence Against Women (rev0018).
- Punanga Tauturu / Cook Islands Women’s Support Centre as early-lineage and current training partner (rev0018).
- WCCC Tonga, VWC, FSC/CCC/SAFENET, Femili PNG as receiving/parallel local offices, not subsidiaries.

WHY IT IS A CHRIST-FIGURE OFFICE:
Costly mercy sometimes looks like capacity-building that nobody outside the field notices. The threshold is not owned by the trainer; it is multiplied so another survivor can find a door later.

DOOR-ETHICS QUESTIONS:
1. Does regional leadership strengthen local offices without colonising them?
2. Are standards survivor-centred, locally adapted, and regularly reviewed?
3. Are trainees and local workers protected from burnout and political backlash?
4. Are donor metrics crowding out survivor choice and confidentiality?
5. Does the cube name the backbone without making one organisation the owner of all Pacific feminist work?

IMAGE / LANGUAGE RULE:
Prefer diagrams of lineage, training, and referral shape over heroic portraits. Avoid “founder owns network” language.


REV0019 APPEND:
The 2026 Regional GBV Counselling Framework is now logged as a standards-backbone candidate: Pacific-led/Pacific-owned guidance for minimum safe, ethical, survivor-centred counselling practice. Treat it as infrastructure underlay, not as proof that services are accessible in every country.

REV0020 APPEND:
The RWG and Justice Pathways entries add a legal/institutional backbone alongside counselling standards. These are map-and-implementation offices, not direct service capacity. Their value is in tracking law-to-practice gaps without claiming the gaps are closed.
