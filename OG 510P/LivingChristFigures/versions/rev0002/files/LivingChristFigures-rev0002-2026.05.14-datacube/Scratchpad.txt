SELECTION PRESSURE USED THIS TURN

Positive gates:
- direct bodily work,
- long duration,
- personal cost,
- public documentation,
- beneficiary remains central,
- not primarily policy/advocacy/thought leadership.

Negative gates:
- too famous,
- too institutionally powerful,
- donor-page only,
- single-source only,
- unresolved conflicts or source contradictions,
- story too beautiful to be trusted without counterweight.
