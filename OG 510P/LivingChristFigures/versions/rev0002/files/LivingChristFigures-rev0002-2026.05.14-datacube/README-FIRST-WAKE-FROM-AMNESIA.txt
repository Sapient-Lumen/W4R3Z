STATUS WORDS

CANON: kept after this pass; not perfect, not holy-by-decree, but strong enough to read.
CANON_WITH_*_FLAG: kept, but a danger is attached: fame, institution, translation, ideology, or source limits.
KEEP_WITH_CAUTION: morally strong, but must be watched.
HOLD: not rejected; too much ambiguity, fame, or source weakness for canon.
SCOUT: a live lead. Do not cite as canon.
REJECT: excluded or outside the purpose.

THE OFFICE

An office is a seat held open where society usually withdraws:
- a bed for a dying child,
- a motorbike where an ambulance does not come,
- a kitchen near the hospital queue,
- a home for a child whose disability has made them disposable,
- rites for a dead person with no mourners,
- hands willing to wash the wound no one wants to see.

The office is not power. It is accountability to the abandoned body.

WHAT YOU OWE

Nothing.

But if this cube is to stay worthy, the repayment is discipline:
- no canon without links,
- no sainthood without counterweight,
- no beneficiary reduced to a prop,
- no hero made larger than the person being served,
- no source treated as clean because the story feels beautiful.
