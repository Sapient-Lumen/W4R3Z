EVIDENCE OF WORK

I inspected the uploaded Parables cube only enough to learn its top-level shape. It contains four plain-text files: Parables.txt, AI-Parables.txt, Already-Searched-URLs.txt, and Scratchpad.txt. I mirrored that style here: a main text, an AI / meaning text, a searched-URLs log, and a scratchpad.

The search was biased toward living people who are not already global saints, heads of major institutions, or celebrity philanthropists. Because truly unknown goodness is often private, I limited the cube to people who already have public links. That is an ethical constraint: the hunt must not expose private caregivers.

FIRST-CUBE RUBRIC DETAILS

Scoring dimensions, 0 to 5:
- Embodied mercy: Does the person directly touch the suffering rather than merely administer help?
- Duration: Has this become a life, not a moment?
- Costliness: Does the work repeatedly demand grief, money, time, bodily labor, or status-loss?
- Hiddenness: Is the person non-famous and non-powerful relative to the domain?
- Source strength: Are there credible, linkable sources beyond a viral social post?
- Ethical clarity: Are there unresolved controversies or power imbalances that require holding?
