LivingChristFigures-rev0001
Created: 2026-05-14
Purpose: a public, linkable hunt for living, non-powerful, not-too-famous people whose lives read as costly mercy.
Caution: "Christ figure" is used here as literary/ethical shorthand, not as a theological identification.

THE SELECTION MECHANISM

This cube is not a sainthood court. It is a severe reading list.

A candidate must pass five gates:

1. Living gate: there must be credible current or recent evidence the person is alive / still active.
2. Public-consent gate: the person must already be publicly documented. No private doxxing of hidden goodness.
3. Power/fame gate: heads of state, billionaires, celebrity philanthropists, and media-built moral brands are excluded or penalized.
4. Embodied-mercy gate: the work must be direct, repeated, intimate service to vulnerable people, not abstract advocacy alone.
5. Cost gate: the life must show sustained personal sacrifice after the moment stopped being glamorous.

A candidate can be rejected for seeming too famous, too institutionally powerful, too self-branded, too thinly sourced, or too ethically ambiguous. The standard is intentionally unfair because the target is rare.

REV0001 CANON: FIRST FIVE KEPT

001 — Mohamed Bzeek
Place: Los Angeles / Azusa, California, USA; born in Libya.
Axis: terminally ill and medically fragile foster children.
Decision: KEEP / strongest first-cube candidate.
Why kept: He takes children most people, systems, and sometimes families cannot bear to hold. The moral center is not saving lives; it is refusing to let a child die without family. The work is intimate, exhausting, repetitive, and grief-heavy. He has media attention, but not conventional power.

REJECTED / EXCLUDED FOR REV0001

- Deceased exemplars: Abdul Sattar Edhi, Mother Teresa, Sindhutai Sapkal, Jean Vanier, Irene Gleeson, etc. Not living.
- The famous-and-powerful: heads of major global NGOs, celebrity philanthropists, billionaires, politicians, public moral influencers whose work is mediated mainly by brand or institution.
- Single-act virality: a beautiful act may be real, but this cube seeks lives, not clips.

NEXT TURN SEARCH DIRECTIONS

1. Hospice / end-of-life caregivers who take the abandoned into family.
2. Rural psychiatric-rescue workers and street-medicine people with small local sources.
3. Foster parents of medically fragile children outside the US.
4. People who bury the unclaimed dead or accompany the dying without pay or publicity.
5. Indigenous or local women running small homes for children after abuse, trafficking, or family rejection.
