REV0002 MOVEMENT

The cube now shifts from a list of people into a map of offices. The office is the form of mercy held in the world:

- final family,
- open door for abandoned disability,
- road-to-hospital where the state fails,
- street rescue and washing of wounds,
- kinship to the unclaimed dead,
- patient reunification,
- hospital-queue feeding,
- counterweight against false sainthood.

008 — Cori and Mark Salchert
Place: Sheboygan, Wisconsin, United States.
Office: Final Family.
Decision: CANON.
Why kept: They adopt/foster medically fragile children knowing the child may die. This is the opposite of sentimental charity: it is volunteered bereavement, daily care, and family membership.
Cautions: US-media stories can sentimentalize disability; retain the child as center, not the heroic parent.
Sources:
