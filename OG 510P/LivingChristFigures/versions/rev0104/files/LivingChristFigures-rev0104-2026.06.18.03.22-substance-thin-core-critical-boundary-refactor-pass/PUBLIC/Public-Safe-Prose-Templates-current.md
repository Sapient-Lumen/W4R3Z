# Public Safe Prose Templates — current rev0049

These templates are gates, not publication approval. They provide safer first
shapes for review.

## boundary_only_generic

This entry is retained only as a high-level boundary and evidence-context shape.
It does not release contact details, routes, maps, case details, images,
service-capacity claims, legal or medical advice, or referral wording.

## mmiwg_family_led_boundary

This entry is retained only as an accountability-boundary example concerning
family-led search and witness. It does not release a case list, vigil or event
map, family-contact or support path, family-story reuse, testimony fragment,
image reuse, red-dress imagery, pathway map, police-file detail, or
implementation-completion claim.

## policy_context_no_safety

This source may support policy or implementation context only. It does not prove
that families are safe, services are available, justice is complete, cases are
resolved, or current support/referral paths are reliable.

## link_boundary

A public source link is not automatically safe to expose. Omit links that route
readers toward contact-rich, case-extractable, testimony-rich, image-sensitive,
event/vigil, map, route, or support surfaces unless a public-link policy review
explicitly allows the link and its boundary note.
