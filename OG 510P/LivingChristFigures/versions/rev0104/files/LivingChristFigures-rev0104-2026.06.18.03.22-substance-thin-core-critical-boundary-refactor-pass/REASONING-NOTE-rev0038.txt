Reasoning note — rev0038

After rev0037, the cube had a no-map model for active-war mutual aid. The next deep test was not another service page. It was a border-observation source whose images, site references, and operational texture could easily become a map while pretending to be solidarity.

Calais/HRO is therefore used as a border counterpressure canary. Two sources are promoted, but only in the narrowest useful way: the March 2026 Calais-area report as aggregate counterpressure, and the Monthly Observations index as evidence of publication infrastructure. The pass deliberately does not release public current-capacity, aid-access, accommodation, route, site, schedule, image, or referral wording.

The change is behavioral because the candidate and claims are less map-like after promotion than before it. Counterpressure should make the cube harder to misuse, not more vivid at the expense of people in exile.
