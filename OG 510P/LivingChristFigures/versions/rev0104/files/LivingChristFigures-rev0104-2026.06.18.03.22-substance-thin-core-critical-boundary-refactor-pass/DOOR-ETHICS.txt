DOOR ETHICS

Every door in this cube must be asked:
1. Can the person leave the helper?
2. Can the person refuse the script?
3. Are images of children, patients, migrants, survivors, or grieving families necessary and consentful?
4. Is the helper accountable to anyone besides donors and admirers?
5. Does the work reduce stigma or produce a new public label?
6. Are numbers being used honestly?
7. Is the helper still close enough to touch the work, or has the office become brand management?
8. Is the story too clean?
9. Where the cube names people, are the names load-bearing rather than decorative?

A beautiful doorway can still become a cage.

The cube should never let gratitude cancel these questions.
