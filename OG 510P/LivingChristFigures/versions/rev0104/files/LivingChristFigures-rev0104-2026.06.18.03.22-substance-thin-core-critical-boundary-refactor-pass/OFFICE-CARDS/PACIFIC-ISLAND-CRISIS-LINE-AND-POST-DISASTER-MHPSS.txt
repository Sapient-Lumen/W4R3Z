OFFICE CARD: PACIFIC ISLAND CRISIS-LINE AND POST-DISASTER MHPSS
REVISION ADDED: rev0017

SHAPE:
A phone, outreach bus, community session, or post-disaster call centre becomes a threshold in an island context where stigma, church/family pressure, language, geography, and disaster aftershock shape whether help is reachable.

NOT THE SAME AS:
- Generic mental-health awareness.
- Imported NGO branding.
- Suicide statistics used as spectacle.

CANDIDATES / LINEAGE:
- Lifeline Fiji (rev0016).
- Fa'ataua Le Ola / Samoa Lifeline (rev0017).
- Le Va Pasifika Suicide Prevention Community Fund (rev0015; Aotearoa/Pasifika funding/fono layer).
- Vanuatu MHPSS Call Centre / IsraAID + Ministry of Health (rev0017, provisional/time-limited).

DOOR-ETHICS QUESTIONS:
1. Is the line culturally and linguistically reachable, or only administratively available?
2. Does the office protect confidentiality in close-knit communities?
3. Does it produce local capacity, or only a short externally funded response?
4. Does it speak safely about suicide, avoiding method, sensationalism, and moral panic?
5. Can the person leave the helper and still retain dignity and care?

IMAGE / LANGUAGE RULE:
No stock despair images. No suicide methods. No isolated island-as-pathology frame.
