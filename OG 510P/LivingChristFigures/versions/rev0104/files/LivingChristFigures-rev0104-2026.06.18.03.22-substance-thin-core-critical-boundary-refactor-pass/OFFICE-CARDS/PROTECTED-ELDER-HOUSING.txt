OFFICE CARD: PROTECTED ELDER HOUSING / ANTI-INSTITUTIONAL ORDINARY DWELLING
REVISION ADDED: rev0017

SHAPE:
A small flat, co-housing structure, or protected dwelling keeps an older or fragile person housed in ordinary life before abandonment, homelessness, or forced institutionalisation becomes the only door.

CANDIDATES / LINEAGE:
- Tan Swee Ban / Peace Harmony Home (rev0010 lineage; abandoned elder-house frame).
- Fondazione Carpinetum / Centri Don Vecchi protected housing (rev0017, provisional scout).
- Sant'Egidio elder co-housing / family-home model (rev0017 adjacent lineage; not a separate candidate yet).

WHY IT IS A CHRIST-FIGURE OFFICE:
The office is not a spectacular rescue bed but durable ordinariness: an address, neighbours, shared services, watchfulness, and a way not to disappear from civic and relational life.

DOOR-ETHICS QUESTIONS:
1. Who is excluded by self-sufficiency, guarantor, age, income, or behavioural rules?
2. Are residents tenants, guests, dependents, or subjects of charity?
3. Is there resident voice and governance, or only foundation description?
4. Does the dwelling delay institutionalisation without hiding unmet care needs?
5. Can a resident complain, leave, or refuse gratitude without losing the home?

IMAGE / LANGUAGE RULE:
No sentimental elder-abandonment imagery. Show the threshold — housing, tenancy, relation, support — rather than helplessness.


REV0018 APPEND:
Homeshare UK / HomeShare International adds a lighter, more relational comparator: not protected flats and not institutional care, but spare-room matching, presence, and agreed support. The ethical danger is substituting cheap housing or unpaid domestic labour for real care. See HOMESHARE-NIGHT-PRESENCE.
