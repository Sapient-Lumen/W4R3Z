The civil-identity-threshold office is the inverse of name-after-death: it is name-before-life. Without registration, a child cannot enter the first school, the first clinic, the first job, the first bank, the first border. The threshold is paperwork the state has chosen not to issue; the office is the slow, repetitive, decades-long labour of producing a self the state will recognise.
