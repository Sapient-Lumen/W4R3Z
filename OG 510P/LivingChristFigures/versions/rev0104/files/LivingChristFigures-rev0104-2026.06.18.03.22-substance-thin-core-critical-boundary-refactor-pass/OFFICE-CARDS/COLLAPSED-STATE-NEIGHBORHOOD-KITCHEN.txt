The collapsed-state-neighborhood-kitchen office is mutual. The helper is also being helped. The threshold is the moment public utilities, hospitals, ambulances, and international aid all withdraw or are denied access — and the neighbourhood decides to keep cooking. This is not vertical mercy; it is horizontal survival, and the cube must not flatten it into "heroism" because the volunteers are themselves at risk of being killed for serving food.
