The pain-relief-at-dying office faces a state that has decided morphine is more dangerous than untreated death. The threshold is a prescription pad and a doctor who is willing to risk professional consequence to use it. The office must accept that politics will sometimes ask it to choose between proximity to power and purity of position — and that the dying patient cannot postpone their pain while the office answers that question.
