The disconnected-dead-no-kin office answers a problem the migrant-grave office does not: a person who lived in a wealthy society long enough to age, run out of relatives, and outlive the fee structure that would have kept their grave. The threshold is the absence of a successor. The office is interment together — bones combined into a statue, ashes interred under shared trees — so that "alone" stops being the last word on the life.
