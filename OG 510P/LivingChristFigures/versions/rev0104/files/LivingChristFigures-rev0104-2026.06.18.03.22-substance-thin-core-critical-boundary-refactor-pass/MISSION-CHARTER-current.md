# Mission Charter — rev0104

Current package revision: rev0104  
Charter status: proposed governing charter for the next architecture and writing passes  
Public posture: closed; no candidate/source expansion

## Mission in one sentence

**Document and compare the practices that hold a human threshold open without owning the person crossing it, while keeping memory accountable, evidence disciplined, and vulnerable people unavailable for extraction.**

## The moral object

The cube is strongest when it studies a *threshold office*: a repeatable human or institutional practice at a point where systems commonly demand paperwork, money, kinship, sobriety, respectability, citizenship, diagnosis, capacity, a claimant, or a clean story before granting passage, recognition, care, burial, identity, safety, or return.

“Mercy” here is not sentiment or publicity. It is the costly work of refusing erasure while accepting accountability. The cube’s own best formulation remains: **the office is the threshold held open without owning the one who crosses it.**

## Unit of analysis

1. **Office / threshold pattern — primary.** The transferable practice or institutional design.
2. **Candidate — evidentiary example.** A person, collective, program, law, or institution that may instantiate part of an office. It is not a saint, rank, endorsement, or impact certificate.
3. **Claim — atomic statement.** Kept separate as supported fact, interpretation, caution, or boundary.
4. **Source — bounded evidence.** Public availability never equals permission for reuse.
5. **Evidence debt — visible uncertainty.** A reason to narrow a claim or stop, not an invitation to harvest more data by default.

## Intended users and decisions

The working cube is for an accountable editor, domain reviewer, archivist, and invited community or subject-side reviewer. It supports decisions about whether an office pattern is real, what evidence supports it, where mercy becomes coercion or branding, what must remain restricted, and what institutional lesson can be written without turning people into material.

It is not designed for people seeking live services, referrals, contacts, routes, case records, crisis guidance, legal or medical advice, donor stories, rankings, or a searchable roster of exemplary people.

## Theory of change

Carefully separated evidence and interpretation make cross-case comparison possible. Cross-case comparison can reveal design principles and failure modes: consent versus capture, recognition versus exposure, outreach versus substitution, memory versus extraction, law versus delivery, and presence versus ownership. Those principles can then improve public writing and institutional practice—provided the cube returns authority to affected people and does not confuse documentation with demonstrated outcomes.

## Success tests

Success is not file count, gate count, candidate count, or apparent completeness. Success means:

- the office taxonomy becomes clearer and more useful than the candidate roster;
- factual, interpretive, outcome, capacity, and consent claims remain visibly distinct;
- affected communities can contest, remove, narrow, or govern reuse;
- restricted data is technically separated, not merely surrounded by policy prose;
- recurring release failures stop recurring;
- the cube produces humane synthesis—office essays, comparisons, and design lessons—without expanding harm-near detail;
- the package becomes smaller and easier for a human reviewer to understand.

## Failure tests

The mission is failing when governance machinery grows faster than insight; a temporary test is presented as proof of the delivered artifact; a public boundary is mistaken for access control; a Christian metaphor is imposed on people who did not choose it; candidate accumulation substitutes for community authority; or the cube protects itself so thoroughly from writing that it never returns intelligible value.

## Stop rules

- No candidate expansion until the two critical evidence debts have an accountable disposition and an external/community-authority review plan exists.
- No new persistent report family unless it retires an existing family or demonstrably blocks a recurring high-severity failure.
- No public candidate/source expansion until a technically separate public edition and takedown/appeal mechanism exist.
- No claim of outcome, service capacity, consent, compliance, or community authorization without evidence specific to that claim.

## Naming posture

The project filename remains LivingChristFigures for continuity. For public-facing work, the safer leading name is **Threshold Offices: Costly Mercy Without Ownership**. “Living Christ Figures” can remain an explicitly situated theological lens of the editor, rather than a category assigned to Indigenous, Muslim, Yazidi, secular, trans, survivor-led, or other subjects and collectives.


## rev0104 operating clarification

The riskiest work is not additional discovery. It is deciding when an evidence gap should stop being pursued because pursuing it would require extracting the very people the cube claims to protect. In this pass, the Garden of Innocence child-category and family-consent debts are converted from critical open debts into permanent boundary controls. The cube may describe the office shape; it may not infer abandonment, family knowledge, family refusal, family incapacity, child consent, or posthumous consent.

The mission test after rev0104: every added report, queue, or protocol must either help a human complete a real decision or be deleted/moved to history. Human synthesis has priority over mirror production.
