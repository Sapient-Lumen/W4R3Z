# Governance Decision and Review Protocol — current rev0050

This protocol records why a boundary decision exists and what would be needed to change it.

## Decision classes

- `policy_gate_added`: a new rule, protocol, schema, or tool gate has been added.
- `public_release_blocked`: a proposed public release surface remains blocked.
- `boundary_shape_allowed`: only a narrow, non-referral, non-extractive public shape is allowed.
- `source_link_restricted`: public URL exposure is blocked or boundary-noted.
- `manual_review_required`: a human governance review must occur before expansion.

## Review standard

A decision is not changed by confidence, urgency, aesthetics, or public-source availability. A decision can change only when the required review artifact records what changed, what remains blocked, and how takedown/reclassification would work if risk changes again.

## Current decision ledger

`GOVERNANCE/Governance-Decision-Ledger-current.*` records the rev0050 package-level decisions. It is not a substitute for community or family authorization.
