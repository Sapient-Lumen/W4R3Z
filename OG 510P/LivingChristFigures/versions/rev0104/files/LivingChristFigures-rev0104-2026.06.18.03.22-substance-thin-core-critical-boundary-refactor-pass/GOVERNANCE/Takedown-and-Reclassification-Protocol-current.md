# Takedown and Reclassification Protocol — current rev0049

## Principle

Public availability is not permanent permission. A source can remain public
elsewhere while this cube removes, quarantines, downgrades, or refuses to link it.

## Status labels

- `active_private_working`
- `public_shape_allowed`
- `public_shape_quarantined`
- `public_link_quarantined`
- `name_use_quarantined`
- `image_use_quarantined`
- `testimony_use_quarantined`
- `removed_after_request`
- `removed_after_context_change`
- `requires_family_or_community_review`
- `internal_only_contact_rich`
- `internal_only_case_extractable`

## Triggers

Reclassify or remove material when any of these occur:

- family/community objection or takedown request;
- new evidence that a link exposes contact, case, image, testimony, map, route,
  event, support, or social-media paths;
- public source changes from aggregate/policy to case-detail rich;
- a public link becomes a live referral or support surface;
- a dashboard/status source is being used as safety, closure, or completion;
- a memorial, vigil, or family story is being reused as narrative ornament;
- an Indigenous data-governance concern is raised and no authority is recorded.

## Minimum action

1. Mark the source, claim, or candidate as quarantined in the relevant ledger.
2. Remove public links until reviewed.
3. Replace prose with an allowed boundary shape.
4. Record what changed and why in the next refresh note or governance ledger.
5. Do not require the requester to prove harm before applying temporary
   quarantine.

## Public prose replacement

When downgrading an unsafe entry, use a statement like:

> This material is retained internally only as a boundary/governance reminder.
> Public reuse is paused because source visibility is not consent and the cube
> has no recorded family/community authorization for expansion.
