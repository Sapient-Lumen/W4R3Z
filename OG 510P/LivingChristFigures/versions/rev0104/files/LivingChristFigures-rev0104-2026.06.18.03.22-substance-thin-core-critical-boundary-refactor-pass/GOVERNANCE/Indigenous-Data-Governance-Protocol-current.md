# Indigenous Data-Governance Protocol — current rev0049

## Scope

This protocol applies to MMIWG2S+, Indigenous, family-led search, testimony,
memorial, vigil, data-project, support-surface, and case-adjacent material in the
cube.

## Core posture

The cube is Indigenous-data-sovereignty-aware and CARE/OCAP-aware, but it does
not claim OCAP® compliance, Indigenous governance endorsement, community
authorization, family consent, or permission to reuse family stories, images,
testimony, names, case details, support paths, or public links unless a specific
ledger row records that basis.

## Required distinctions

- Public source availability is not consent.
- A family-chosen public telling is not blanket permission to reuse other family
  stories.
- An official inquiry page is not permission to quote testimony fragments.
- A public dashboard is not proof of safety, closure, or completed justice.
- A public link can itself expose a contact path, case-list route, image route,
  event/vigil route, or support route.
- Indigenous data projects are not raw material for this cube unless the cube has
  an explicit governance basis for reuse.

## Minimum fields before public expansion

Before any public prose expands beyond safe aggregate shape, record the following
fields in the consent/case-name ledger or a successor governance object:

- `family_chosen_public_name_status`
- `community_review_status`
- `review_authority`
- `review_scope`
- `consent_basis`
- `consent_limitations`
- `harm_proximity`
- `public_reuse_allowed`
- `public_link_allowed`
- `image_use_allowed`
- `takedown_contact_internal_only`
- `do_not_extract_case_details`

## Allowed without further governance review

Only high-level boundary statements may be considered, such as:

> This entry is retained as an accountability-boundary example of family-led
> search and witness. It does not release a case list, vigil/event map,
> contact/support path, family-story reuse, image reuse, testimony fragment,
> pathway map, or implementation-completion claim.

## Not allowed without separate review

- person-by-person case lists;
- victim/survivor/family name rolls;
- family tributes or case-story fragments;
- testimony excerpts;
- photographs, red-dress images, posters, memorial images, or alt text;
- vigil, march, event, or installation logistics;
- submit-a-name, report-a-case, family-search, social-media, or support/contact
  routes;
- police-file, legal-file, forensic, or incident details;
- trafficking/highway/cross-border/resource-extraction pathway detail;
- public-link exposure to contact-rich, case-extractable, testimony-rich, or
  image-sensitive pages;
- implementation scorecard language that implies family safety, closure,
  satisfaction, service capacity, or completion.

## Review rule

If a future editor is unsure whether a source is safe to summarize, link, quote,
or template into public prose, the answer is `do not release yet`; route the item
to takedown/reclassification or governance review.
