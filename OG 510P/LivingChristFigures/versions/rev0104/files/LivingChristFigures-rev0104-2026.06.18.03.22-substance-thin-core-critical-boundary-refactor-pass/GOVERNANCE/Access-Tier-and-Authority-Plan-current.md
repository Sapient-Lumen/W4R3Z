# Access Tier and Authority Plan — rev0103

Status: proposed architecture; no claim of legal, CARE, OCAP, UNDRIP, archival, or privacy compliance

## Tier 1 — restricted evidence vault

Holds source URLs and any contact-, route-, shelter-, support-, case-, testimony-, image-, family-, DNA-, forensic-, legal-, or current-capacity-near notes that remain necessary for a stated research purpose. It must be encrypted at rest, access-controlled, logged, and distributed separately from reviewer/public artifacts. Every field family receives a purpose, access role, retention review date, and deletion disposition.

## Tier 2 — accountable reviewer cube

Holds canonical office/candidate/claim/debt tables with redacted evidence identifiers and enough provenance for invited review. It excludes live contact paths, route details, case extracts, image assets, testimony text, and service-capacity detail. It includes the challenge/takedown workflow and records whose authority is required to loosen a boundary.

## Tier 3 — public reading edition

Contains office essays, aggregate comparisons, method, selection limits, failure modes, and a transparent statement of what is not claimed. It has no internal source registry, no candidate/source URL graph, no referral or service directory, no case list, and no route or contact reconstruction surface.

## Authority model

- The operator may always narrow, quarantine, or remove material.
- The operator cannot alone convert public visibility into consent or loosen community, family, survivor, Indigenous, service-user, or subject-side boundaries.
- Authority must be recorded for the specific community and use; a generic framework or training course is not authorization.
- Reviewers need a reachable appeal/takedown path, a response target, and an immutable decision record that can point to deletion without retaining the deleted content.
- Community/subject reviewers should be offered clear scope, compensation where feasible, the ability to decline, and the ability to see how their review changed the work.

## Minimum-necessary and retention questions

For each restricted field: What decision does it support? Could a safer aggregate or evidence ID support that decision? Who needs access? When will the purpose expire? What harm could arise through combination with other data? What event triggers deletion or re-review?

## Current gap

The present full ZIP contains internal and public surfaces together. The closed public directory is a content policy, not a technical separation. Until the tier split exists, distribution of the full cube should be treated as distribution of the restricted evidence vault.
