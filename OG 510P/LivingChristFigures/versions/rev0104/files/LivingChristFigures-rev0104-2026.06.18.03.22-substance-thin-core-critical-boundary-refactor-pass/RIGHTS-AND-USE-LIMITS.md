# Rights and Use Limits — rev0065

This package is a boundary-governed research datacube. It is not a public referral directory, not a case list, not a vigil or event map, not a safety dashboard, not a public contact index, and not an endorsement system.

Public availability of a source does not create permission to republish contact-rich, case-adjacent, family-led, Indigenous, survivor-side, shelter/refuge, route, grave/record, image, testimony, or current-capacity detail.

The public layer remains closed to index/policy/template/manifest files only. The working cube may support internal review, but internal evidence is not public-release permission.

Boundary loosening requires recorded governance basis. For family-led, Indigenous, survivor-side, case-adjacent, and harm-near material, the operator may tighten or remove material but may not unilaterally expand the public surface.

No warranty of safety, capacity, availability, suitability, or referral accuracy is made. Do not use this package to contact people, locate services, infer live capacity, identify vulnerable people, map routes, compile cases, or rank holiness.
