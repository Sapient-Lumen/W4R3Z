Reasoning note rev0040 — family search is not a map

The cube had already learned no-map behavior for conflict-zone mutual aid and no-route behavior for border observation. The next deep move was to apply that lesson to missing-person search, where beautiful public recognition can expose searchers, families, sites, and the dead.

I selected the Mexico buscadoras entry because fresh sources strengthen the entry while also increasing extraction risk: Amnesty gives aggregate risk evidence; IACHR/OAS gives structural right-to-search/protection context; Guardian makes the issue highly visible; Reuters shows how registry reclassification can be misread as closure. The pass therefore promotes evidence only by narrowing public use.

No public claim is released. The revision adds a rule, a refresh note, a negative case, a transaction receipt, and claim/debt/source updates. The next best continuation is a non-extractive image/name/case-story audit across death, disappearance, cemetery, forensic, survivor, and family-search entries.
