# External Method Anchors — rev0103

These sources informed the diagnostic review. They are method anchors, not compliance claims or authority delegated to this project.

| Source / institution | Relevant principle | Application to this cube | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Global Indigenous Data Alliance — CARE Principles | People and purpose, collective benefit, authority to control, responsibility, ethics; complements FAIR | Replace openness-as-default with governed benefit and authority | Reading CARE is not community authorization or certification |
| First Nations Information Governance Centre — OCAP® | First Nations ownership, control, access, possession; Nation-specific expression | Do not infer control or consent from public availability; record real authority | OCAP® is specifically First Nations, not a generic pan-Indigenous label |
| International Committee of the Red Cross — mosaic effect guidance | Purpose limitation, proportionality, minimization, deletion, need-to-know sharing | Split the full vault from reviewer/public editions and reduce retained harm-near fields | Humanitarian guidance requires context-specific risk analysis |
| Gebru et al. — Datasheets for Datasets | Motivation, composition, collection, intended/unsuitable use, distribution, maintenance, legal/ethical questions | Make mission, selection, users, maintenance and limitations concise and explicit | A datasheet cannot create consent or governance authority |
| Wilkinson et al. — FAIR Guiding Principles | Findable, accessible, interoperable, reusable for people and machines | Keep a small canonical core and explicit provenance | FAIR does not by itself answer power, legitimacy, or appropriate openness |
| Society of American Archivists — Protocols for Native American Archival Materials | Culturally responsive care; each tribe, band, and community is unique | Replace universal operator assumptions with relationship-specific governance | Protocols require interpretation with communities, not mechanical adoption |
| NIST privacy guidance | Collect only necessary personal information; retention raises unauthorized-use risk | Add purpose, minimum-necessary fields, retention review, deletion and selective disclosure | Identity-system guidance is an analogy, not a complete legal rule for this archive |
