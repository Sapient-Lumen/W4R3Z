REFRESH NOTE — rev0020
SUBJECT: Law-to-door is not law-alone

rev0020 follows the Pacific family-protection/domestic-violence legislation line. It adds Palau, Nauru, FSM/Pohnpei-Kosrae, the RWG, and UNDP/MLSC Justice Pathways as law-to-door infrastructure. The key correction is that a statute or order is not itself the office.

Questions for every legal-framework entry:
- Can a survivor reach the law without retaliation, cost, transport failure, language exclusion, or disability exclusion?
- Is there safe accompaniment through police, court, health, shelter, legal-aid, and social-welfare handoffs?
- Are orders enforced?
- Is confidentiality protected?
- Is there real survivor choice, including the choice not to reconcile?
- Does the law reach outer-island, rural, remote, child, queer/trans, migrant, disabled, and poor survivors?

This note is now attached to the cube through rule 17.
