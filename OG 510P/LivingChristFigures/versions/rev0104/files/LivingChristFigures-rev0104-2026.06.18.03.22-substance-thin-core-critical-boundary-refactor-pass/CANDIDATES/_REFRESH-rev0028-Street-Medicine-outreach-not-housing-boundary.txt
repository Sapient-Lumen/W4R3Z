REFRESH NOTE — rev0028: Street medicine / outreach-is-not-housing boundary pass

This refresh admits Jim Withers / Operation Safety Net / Street Medicine Institute as a KEEP and office-canon candidate for street medicine: clinical care, relationship, and navigation brought to unsheltered people where they are, without treating that location as an adequate home.

The pass is deliberately boundary-heavy. The public sources support the origin story, current Pittsburgh service shape, hospital-interface training ecology, and translocal field-building work. The boundary sources require sharper handling of privacy, confidentiality, law-enforcement proximity, technology/geocoding, scope of practice, and the housing substitution risk.

Standing caution: do not record or infer camp locations, patient names, routines, staff patterns, operational maps, outreach schedules, case stories, or live referral instructions. The cube is not a medical advice tool, outreach guide, encampment map, referral guide, or service directory.
