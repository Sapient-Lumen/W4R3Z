Movement of Mothers of Srebrenica and Žepa Enclaves — family-led truth, remembrance, and accountability office

Status: PROVISIONAL KEEP; FAMILY-LED GENOCIDE MEMORY / TRUTH-AND-JUSTICE OFFICE; NO-CASE-LIST, NO-TESTIMONY-EXTRACTION, NO-MEMORIAL-MAP, NO-CONTACT BOUNDARY
Location: Bosnia and Herzegovina; Srebrenica and Žepa survivor/family networks; Sarajevo, Srebrenica, Potočari, diaspora, and international remembrance/accountability contexts
Office: Family-led search and witness after genocide; return of names/remains; memory against denial; accountability after institutional failure

Work:
The Movement of Mothers of Srebrenica and Žepa Enclaves is a family-led survivor organization created after the 1995 genocide. Its high-level work-shape is the refusal to let disappearance, mass killing, denial, and institutional failure become the last public word over the dead. The office gathers survivors and family members, presses for truth and justice, participates in missing-persons and identification contexts, and keeps remembrance tied to accountability rather than spectacle.

Current public shape:
Current public reporting shows the office still active in truth, justice, anti-denial, and remembrance contexts, including 2025 UN Bosnia and Herzegovina and UN Geneva public reports manually rechecked in rev0086. The cube treats that as current witness context only. It is not a claim of service capacity, public contact safety, litigation guidance, event participation, memorial access, or public reuse permission.

Why it belongs / might belong:
The cube already contains search-for-the-disappeared, forensic return-of-names, public-cemetery access, migrant grave, identity-restitution, and family-led justice offices. This candidate adds a distinct family-led genocide-memory hinge: mothers and relatives become the long public threshold where missing persons, named dead, buried remains, court accountability, and anti-denial witness meet. The figure is not maternal sainthood or survivor extraction. The office is the collective refusal to let the disappeared remain administratively, politically, or spiritually erased.
