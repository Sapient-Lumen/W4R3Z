TARGET: SantEgidio-humanitarian-corridors-safe-passage-office.txt
THEME: Selection ethics, civil-society professionalism, and independent evaluation

WHY ADDED:
rev0016 admitted humanitarian corridors as a safe-passage office. rev0017 adds independent selection-ethics and paternalism cautions so that the entry does not become programme brochure copy.

NEW CAUTION:
The 2025 Sage article on humanitarian corridors treats beneficiary selection as a key issue and notes limits including politicized selection, images of beneficiaries as good/suitable subjects, volunteer unpreparedness, professionalism gaps, paternalism, and the power relation between host group and arriving refugees. The Humanitarian Corridors official page remains useful for basic MoU/visa/reception mechanics, but not sufficient by itself.

CUBE EFFECT:
Status remains OFFICE-CANON / KEEP, but the card should ask who selects, who can refuse, what happens to those not selected, and whether hospitality becomes possession.
