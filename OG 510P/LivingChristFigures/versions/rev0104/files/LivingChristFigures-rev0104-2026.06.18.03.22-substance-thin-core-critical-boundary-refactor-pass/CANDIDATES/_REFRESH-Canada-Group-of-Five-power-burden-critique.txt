REFRESH NOTE — rev0017
TARGET: Canada-Group-of-Five-private-refugee-sponsorship-circles.txt
THEME: Power, burden, and administrative critique

WHY ADDED:
rev0016 admitted Group of Five private-sponsorship circles as a settlement-threshold office but carried forward the need for lived-experience/power critique. rev0017 adds a caution note rather than making the sponsor circle sound clean.

NEW CAUTION:
IRCC's Group of Five page indicates that, as of March 2026, new Group of Five applications are paused except via Blended Visa Office-Referred sponsorship, and it emphasizes financial/emotional support responsibilities and settlement-plan requirements. Hyndman et al. describe sponsors as responsible during the first year for airport reception, housing, funds, and linking newcomers to health, education, language, and employment. These duties can be a threshold of care, but also create administrative burden, intimacy, surveillance, gratitude pressure, and power imbalance.

CUBE EFFECT:
Status remains KEEP / OFFICE-CANON, but the private-sponsorship card must include a power/burden flag. The office is valid only if the refugee remains more than a project of the sponsor group.
