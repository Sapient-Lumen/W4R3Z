# rev0091 suicide/crisis hotline operational redaction

This pass audits suicide, crisis-line, lifeline, and postvention entries as an operational-detail risk cluster. The concrete repair is not a new doctrine layer: rev0091 removes a retained hotline shortcode, availability/hour fragments, call-volume/caller-category fragments, and misleading ordinary `supported_public_shape` statuses from the handoff-facing shape.

Rev0091 changes the selected suicide/postvention/lifeline institutional-shape claim statuses to explicit quarantine from hotline shortcode/contact-digit, service-hours, call-volume, method, triage, current-capacity, referral/contact, and case/caller-detail extraction.
