NAME: LifeLine Port Moresby / Lifeline PNG counselling and refuge line
LOCATION: Port Moresby, Papua New Guinea
OFFICE: Counselling plus crisis accommodation for women experiencing violence / emotional-support threshold
STATUS: PROVISIONAL KEEP; PARTIALLY REVERIFIED rev0023; NOT A LIVE REFERRAL; CURRENT CAPACITY UNVERIFIED; PACIFIC-GBV-SERVICE-CAPACITY-QUARANTINE rev0090

SUMMARY:
LifeLine International’s current member page describes LifeLine Port Moresby as one of the oldest not-for-profit counselling organisations in PNG, providing emotional support and addressing social issues confronting PNG communities. It says LifeLine offers telephone and face-to-face counselling and operates a refuge next to a large settlement area in Port Moresby. The same page describes it as a primary community-based service for women experiencing violence and lists drop-in face-to-face counselling, crisis accommodation, case management, and repatriation services. An Australian High Commission page on a refuge renovation describes Lifeline-run accommodation for survivors of family and sexual violence and says Lifeline provides counselling and referral services.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office is a threshold where counselling, safe accommodation, case management, and repatriation meet — a place for someone whose danger is immediate and whose options may be fragmented across Port Moresby’s settlement, policing, family, and transport realities.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- Telephone and face-to-face counselling.
- Drop-in counselling.
- Crisis accommodation/refuge.
- Case management and repatriation services.
- Counselling and referral for survivors of family and sexual violence.

EVIDENCE:
1. LifeLine International member page: LifeLine Port Moresby provides emotional support, telephone/face-to-face counselling, and a refuge; lists working hours and drop-in/case-management/crisis-accommodation services.
2. Australian High Commission page: Australia supported Lifeline refuge refurbishment; refuge housed survivors of family and sexual violence; Lifeline Centre provides counselling and referral services.
