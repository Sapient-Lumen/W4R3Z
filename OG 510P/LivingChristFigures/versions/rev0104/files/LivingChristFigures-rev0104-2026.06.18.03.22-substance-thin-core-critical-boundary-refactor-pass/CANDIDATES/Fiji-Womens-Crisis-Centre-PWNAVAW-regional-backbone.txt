NAME: Fiji Women’s Crisis Centre / Pacific Women’s Network Against Violence Against Women
LOCATION: Fiji; Pacific regional network
OFFICE: Feminist crisis counselling / regional training and standards backbone for survivor offices
STATUS: OFFICE-CANON; HIGH-VISIBILITY REGIONAL BACKBONE; KEEP WITH DONOR/POWER AND SOURCE-BALANCE FLAGS; PACIFIC-GBV-SERVICE-CAPACITY-QUARANTINE rev0090

SUMMARY:
Fiji Women’s Crisis Centre (FWCC) provides free, confidential, non-judgmental crisis counselling for survivors of domestic violence, sexual assault, child abuse, and sexual harassment; legal advice; accompaniment or referrals to courts, police, hospitals, and other agencies; 24-hour telephone counselling; emotional support; choice-based options; and mobile counselling. FWCC also serves as Secretariat of the Pacific Women’s Network Against Violence Against Women (PWNAVAW), whose regional training has run since 1995 and has trained more than 900 women and men from the region. A 2025 Cook Islands training note describes PWNAVAW as operating in more than ten Pacific countries and as a regional catalyst, standards-setter, and leadership incubator for GBV response and prevention.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
The office is not only a counselling room or phone number. It is also the slow construction of other doors: training, curriculum, regional standards, sister-centre support, and feminist analysis that lets a survivor-centred threshold exist in smaller island settings.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- Free/confidential crisis counselling.
- Legal advice and referral/accompaniment.
- 24-hour telephone counselling and mobile counselling.
- Regional training and standards-sharing through PWNAVAW.
- Incubation/support of other Pacific crisis centres.

EVIDENCE:
1. FWCC Counselling and Support page: free/confidential crisis counselling, legal advice, accompaniment/referrals, 24-hour telephone counselling, emotional support, choice-based options, and mobile counselling.
2. FWCC Regional Training Program: ongoing since 1995, more than 900 participants, network of more than 20 organisations addressing violence against women and children.
3. FWCC Cook Islands 2025 release: PWNAVAW in more than ten Pacific countries, Secretariat at FWCC, roots in the 1992 Suva regional meeting, and emergence of counselling centres across Kiribati, PNG, Samoa, Solomon Islands, Tonga, Tuvalu, and Vanuatu.

DOOR-ETHICS FLAGS:
- High-visibility and regional leadership can flatten smaller local offices if the cube is careless.
- Donor/evaluation language should be balanced with local staff and survivor-safe voices.
- Do not treat “regional standards” as ownership of local women’s work.
- Avoid turning named coordinators into solitary heroes; the office is collective and institutional.
