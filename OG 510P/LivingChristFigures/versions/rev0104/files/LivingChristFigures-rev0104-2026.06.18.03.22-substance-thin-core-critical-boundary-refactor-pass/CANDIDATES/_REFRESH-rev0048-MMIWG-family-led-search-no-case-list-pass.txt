# rev0048 — MMIWG2S+ family-led search and memorial / no-case-list pass

CENTRAL SENTENCE: A family-led search is not a case list; a vigil is not a map; an inquiry scorecard is not safety.

## Why this pass exists

The cube already held Bridget Tolley / Families of Sisters in Spirit as an office-canon candidate for Indigenous-led search, vigil, and anti-erasure work around missing and murdered Indigenous women, girls, and two-spirit people. Rev0048 deepens that entry because the sources that make the office legible also carry a difficult extraction surface: family stories, names, case histories, police-accountability disputes, annual-vigil logistics, red-dress imagery, government progress dashboards, support surfaces, and trafficking/cross-border language.

The office is real. Families and survivors have had to force attention where state systems failed to search, listen, or account. The boundary is also real: the cube must not convert those family-led acts of witness into a public case list, event guide, family-contact surface, victim roll, image archive, implementation trophy, or support-service directory.

## Source decisions

- CIRNAC Calls for Justice Reporting Tool: promoted as aggregate official implementation-status context only. The 138 actioned / 82 pending / two completed framing is useful because it separates activity from completion and safety.
- AFN 2025 MMIWG Calls for Justice progress press release: promoted as Indigenous counterpressure and implementation-gap evidence only, especially the underfunded/inconsistent response and family/survivor exclusion cautions.
- National Inquiry Final Report page: promoted as national-inquiry, truths, and Calls for Justice context only. Testimony volume does not authorize testimony extraction.
- Amnesty Canada interview with Bridget Tolley: promoted as family-led witness/profile context only. One family-chosen public telling cannot become a license to copy other family stories.
- Justice Canada FSIS compendium page: held as contact-rich and service-description intake. It contains historical program details, contact/service/accommodation language, and social-media routes; it is not safe public/referral evidence.
- National Action Plan site: held as broad framework/context intake because the public page includes health-support and partner-surface navigation and does not prove current access or safety.

None. Rev0048 releases no public case-list, victim/survivor/family name roll, photograph, red-dress image, vigil/event map, family-contact path, support-line/contact detail, police-file detail, highway/cross-border/trafficking pathway, testimony fragment, or implementation-completion claim.

## Blocked by rule 46

- names, case lists, photos, family tributes, testimony excerpts, police-file details, and death/disappearance circumstances unless family-chosen, load-bearing, and manually reviewed;
- vigil/event locations or logistics, red-dress installation reuse, march planning paths, submit-a-name/report-a-case routes, social-media group routes, and support/contact pathways;
- scorecard or dashboard wording that implies the crisis is solved, families are satisfied, or service/support capacity is safe now;
- trafficking, highway, resource-extraction, cross-border, or jurisdictional material used as a public map or lead.
