rev0023 verification outcome:
LifeLine Port Moresby's public institutional/service-shape line still holds,
but not as a live referral or capacity guarantee.

What holds:
- LifeLine International's current member page lists LifeLine Port Moresby as a
  not-for-profit counselling organisation in Papua New Guinea, providing
  telephone and face-to-face counselling and operating a refuge.
- The same member page lists drop-in face-to-face counselling, crisis
  accommodation, case management, and repatriation services, with public working
  hours.
- An older Australian High Commission page corroborates a Lifeline-run refuge,
  counselling/referral services, and refurbishment support for emergency
  accommodation for survivors of family and sexual violence.

What remains unverified:
- current staffing;
- bed/refuge capacity and whether any space is available at a given time;
- whether public working hours match actual intake capacity;
- case-management/repatriation availability in practice;
- accessibility for disability, language, settlement/rural-origin, and child
  safety needs;
- current funding/governance stability;
- safeguarding, privacy, and survivor-side evaluation.

Important distinction:
LifeLine Port Moresby must not be merged with 1-Tok Kaunselin Helpim Lain,
Femili PNG, Bel Isi PNG, or other PNG survivor-service pathways. 1-Tok is a
separate national 24-hour telephone counselling/support service; LifeLine Port
Moresby is a separate Port-Moresby-based counselling/refuge line with its own
public listing and unresolved capacity questions.
