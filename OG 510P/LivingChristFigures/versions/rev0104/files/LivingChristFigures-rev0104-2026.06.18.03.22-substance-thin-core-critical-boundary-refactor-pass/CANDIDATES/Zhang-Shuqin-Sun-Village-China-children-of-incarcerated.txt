Zhang Shuqin / Sun Village (太阳村) — China — and a refresh of the rev0010 Lin Minming / Red Apple Public Welfare entry

Status: KEEP WITH PROXIMITY-TO-PRISON-SYSTEM AND DONOR-TOURISM FLAGS; OFFICE-CANON FOR THE BETTER-DOCUMENTED CHINESE CASE OF THE CHILD-OF-THE-IMPRISONED OFFICE
Location: Beijing (Shunyi District), Shaanxi Province (the original 1996 home), and additional sites — about nine to ten Sun Village locations across China
Office: Child-of-the-imprisoned / long-term residential care, schooling, prison-visit continuity, and anti-stigma family contact

Work:
Zhang Shuqin worked as a newspaper editor in China's Bureau of Prison Administration for twelve years before opening the first Sun Village in Shaanxi Province in 1995-1996 to take in children whose parents had been imprisoned and who had no other reliable caregiver. The network has since grown to roughly nine to ten group homes hosting 400-500 children at a time, with the children attending neighbourhood public schools, visiting their incarcerated parents typically once or twice a year, and phoning them roughly monthly. Sun Village funds part of its operations through orchard sales (jujube and peach trees planted by children and staff, with public "sponsor a tree" campaigns) and through volunteer-tourism arrangements.

Why it belongs / might belong:
The rev0010 entry on Lin Minming / Red Apple Public Welfare was kept provisionally with a state-media-proximity flag. The rev0011 SearchTrail asked the cube to seek post-2019 non-state-media corroboration. The rev0012 refresh found none: Lin Minming's coverage remains state-media-only (China Daily 2018-2019). Sun Village, by contrast, has substantial independent and academic corroboration over a 30-year operating history — Dui Hua Foundation analysis (an independent Hong Kong-based human-rights NGO), Harvard Fairbank Center student account, Humanium (2024), and earlier Washington Post and Beijing-Kids reporting. Sun Village is therefore added as the better-documented Chinese case for the CHILD-OF-THE-IMPRISONED office, while Lin Minming / Red Apple is retained at unchanged status in the ledger with the refresh note recorded here.

Flags:
The state-proximity flag from rev0010 applies here too. Zhang Shuqin's prior career was inside the prison administration; Sun Village must coordinate with provincial prison systems to arrange parental contact and visits, and operates with implicit state tolerance. Independent reporting notes that the homes' regimes — whistles to organise activities, segregated dormitories, scheduled chores — read to some Western observers as institutional in a way Chinese observers describe as ordinary boarding-school form. The volunteer-tourism arrangements (paid half-day visits sold on platforms like Tripadvisor) raise donor-theatre concerns the cube's DOOR-ETHICS questions flag. The published reporting includes children's first names and partial life stories; the cube treats those as the source's own choices and does not amplify them into the cube's own prose beyond what is load-bearing for understanding the office.
