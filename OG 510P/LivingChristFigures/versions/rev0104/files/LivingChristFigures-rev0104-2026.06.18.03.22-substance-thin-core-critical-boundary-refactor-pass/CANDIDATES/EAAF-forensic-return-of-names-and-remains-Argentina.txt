Equipo Argentino de Antropología Forense (EAAF) — forensic return of names and remains

Status: PROVISIONAL KEEP; FORENSIC RETURN-OF-NAMES-AND-REMAINS OFFICE; NO-DNA-ROUTE, NO-CASE-LIST, NO-EXCAVATION-SITE, NO-CONTACT BOUNDARY
Location: Argentina; international human-rights forensic investigations; family, court, and community search contexts
Office: Forensic return of the disappeared dead; search for the disappeared; civil-identity threshold after enforced disappearance

Work:
The Equipo Argentino de Antropología Forense (EAAF) is a scientific, non-governmental, non-profit human-rights forensic institution associated with search, recovery, identification, and return of disappeared persons. Its work-shape joins archaeology, anthropology, genetics, documentation, family accompaniment, and legal evidence: the hidden body is not treated as anonymous disposal but as a person whose name, kinship, and evidentiary truth can be restored.

Current public shape:
Current 2026 public reporting describes EAAF-linked identifications from the former La Perla clandestine detention context in Córdoba and emphasizes that family notification and consent govern public disclosure of identities. The cube treats that as current context for the office, not as a public case list, excavation map, DNA-sampling route, family-contact path, or judicial evidence guide.

Why it belongs / might belong:
The cube already contains search-for-the-disappeared, public cemetery, migrant grave, identity-restitution, and unclaimed-burial offices. EAAF adds a distinct forensic hinge: the return of name and remains through scientific work accountable to families, courts, and collective memory. The figure is not science-as-savior. The office is the disciplined refusal to let a disappeared person remain disappeared because paperwork, violence, and burial systems colluded in erasure.
