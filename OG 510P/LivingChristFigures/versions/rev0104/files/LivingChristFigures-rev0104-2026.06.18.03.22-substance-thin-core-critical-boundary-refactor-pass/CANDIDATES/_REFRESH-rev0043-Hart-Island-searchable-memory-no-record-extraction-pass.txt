# rev0043 — Hart Island searchable-memory no-record-extraction pass

CENTRAL SENTENCE: A public burial record can end anonymity without giving the cube permission to republish the record.

## Why this pass exists

Rev0042 proved that child-burial sources can be emotionally vivid and still be held back from public prose. Hart Island is the next necessary test because the risk is not only a moving story or image. The risk is structured data: searchable names, plot and grave fields, permits, public maps, dates, institutional death-location fields, fetal-remains privacy notes, family story interfaces, and visit/tour infrastructure.

The office remains strongly admitted: public records and story infrastructure can reduce civic disappearance. But rev0043 refuses to let the working cube become a second index of the dead.

## Sources reviewed and promotion decision

- Hart Island Project burial-search surface: held as redaction-risk intake. It proves the interface exposes individual records, plot/grave metadata, place-of-death fields, and story filters; it is not promoted as a source to copy records from.
- NYC Hart Island current page: already registered and re-reviewed. It supports high-level CMTS/visit/fetal-remains-redaction/record-gap shape, but its contact, search, map, and visit surfaces remain blocked from public export.
- NYC Council Data Team Hart Island page: promoted as aggregate public-agency/legislative context for public-cemetery administration, capacity-study pressure, record-integrity gaps, and the distinction between public burial, family access, and public tours.
- Data.gov DOC Hart Island Burial Records metadata: promoted as public dataset/governance evidence only. Its value is to show that public data includes personal death/burial fields while also acknowledging fetal-remains privacy redaction; it is not a record source for cube prose.
- NYC Council Legistar hearing / Int. 1408 record: promoted as legislative-process context for burial-capacity study and family-input requirements; not as proof that reform, infrastructure, accessibility, or capacity has happened.
- Citymeetings transcript/summary: held as meeting-context intake only. It can help route future questions but is not promoted in this pass because it is a third-party transcript and contains operational/tour/logistics surfaces.

## Public claim release

None. Rev0043 releases no public current claim, no visit guidance, no search instruction, no map, no contact field, no story excerpt, no individual name, no plot/grave/case/permit metadata, no death-location detail, and no public-tour logistics.

## Blocked by rule 41

- individual burial records and names;
- plot, section, grave, permit, Medical Examiner, or cemetery-management identifiers;
- dates or places of death tied to individuals;
- fetal-remains details and family privacy surfaces;
- institutional death-location lists when used as a person-finding surface;
- family-submitted stories, images, anonymity clocks, and story filters;
- maps, search instructions, visit/scheduling instructions, contact fields, and public-tour logistics.
