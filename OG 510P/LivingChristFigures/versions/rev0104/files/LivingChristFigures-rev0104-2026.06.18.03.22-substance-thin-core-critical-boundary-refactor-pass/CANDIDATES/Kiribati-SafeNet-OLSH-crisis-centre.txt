NAME: Kiribati SafeNet / Our Lady of the Sacred Heart crisis centre line
LOCATION: Kiribati
OFFICE: National GBV referral mesh plus long-term accommodation threshold
STATUS: NETWORK-CANDIDATE / INSTITUTION-CANDIDATE; KEEP WITH FAITH-BASED-SHELTER AND NETWORK-CAPACITY FLAGS; PACIFIC-GBV-SERVICE-CAPACITY-QUARANTINE rev0090

SUMMARY:
A 2025 UN Women / United Nations in Micronesia story describes Kiribati SafeNet as a national network of government and non-government frontline service providers led by the Ministry of Women, Youth, Sport and Social Affairs, with the aim of providing the best possible services for women and girls who have experienced violence. The same story says police response reforms include safety orders, documentation/investigation, and referrals to SafeNet members. It also identifies Our Lady of the Sacred Heart (OLSH) crisis centre as the only centre in Kiribati providing long-term accommodation for women and girls who have survived violence and are referred by police or other service providers.

WHY THIS FITS THE CUBE:
This is the mesh behind the first door: the survivor should not have to know which office, police unit, shelter, fund, clinic, or counsellor to approach. The mercy is routing without abandonment — a network able to move a person from disclosure into safety, medical/legal/psychosocial services, and longer accommodation where needed.

OFFICE SHAPE:
- National frontline-provider referral network.
- Police standing orders and GBV training connected to referral.
- Temporary shelter / safe-place response through DCSU line.
- Long-term accommodation through OLSH crisis centre.
- Survivor Support Fund access connected to justice, medical, and psychosocial services.

EVIDENCE:
1. UN Micronesia / UN Women 2025 story: SafeNet is a national network of government and non-government providers for GBV survivors.
2. Same story: police reforms include safety orders, documentation/investigation, and referral to SafeNet members.
3. Same story: DCSU temporary shelter and Survivor Support Fund access in South Tarawa; OLSH crisis centre as the only centre providing long-term accommodation for women and girls who have survived violence.
