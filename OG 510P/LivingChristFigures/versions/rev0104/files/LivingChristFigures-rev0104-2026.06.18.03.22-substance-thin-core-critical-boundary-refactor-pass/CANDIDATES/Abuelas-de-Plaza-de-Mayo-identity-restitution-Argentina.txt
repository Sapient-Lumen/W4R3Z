Abuelas de Plaza de Mayo — right-to-identity search for stolen grandchildren

Status: PROVISIONAL KEEP; IDENTITY-RESTITUTION / FAMILY-LED SEARCH OFFICE; NO-CASE-LIST, NO-DNA-INTAKE, AND NO-PUBLIC-URL-EXPANSION BOUNDARY
Location: Argentina; transnational right-to-identity network through families, grandchildren, CONADI, BNDG, and public memory institutions
Office: Search-for-the-disappeared plus civil-identity threshold after state theft / family-led truth work that uses science without reducing persons to samples

Work:
Abuelas de Plaza de Mayo was created in 1977 to locate and restore identity to children stolen during Argentina's last military dictatorship, including children born in captivity or disappeared with their parents. The work is not simply memorial witness: it keeps a living threshold open for adults whose names, kinship, and origins were falsified by state terror. The office joins relatives' long search, legal action, archival labor, public memory, CONADI, and the Banco Nacional de Datos Genéticos. The scientific side matters because the parents of many stolen children were disappeared, so identity proof had to be built through grandparents and other relatives rather than through the missing parents themselves.

Current public shape:
Current public reporting and Abuelas' own public surfaces state that 140 grandchildren have had their identities restored, with more still sought. This cube treats that number only as high-level historical/current context. It does not release names, case records, DNA-intake pathways, forms, family contacts, images, or public search instructions.

Why it belongs / might belong:
The cube already contains offices of the disappeared dead, public cemeteries, missing-person searches, and civil-identity thresholds. Abuelas joins those lines but changes the tense: the disappeared child is now often an adult, and the return is not a body but a true name, kinship, and history. The office is severe because it refuses the state's false adoption/naming story while still honoring the adult person's agency and timing around identity. It is a Christ-figure candidate only in the cube's limited metaphorical sense: costly, long-duration mercy at the boundary where a person is made findable to family and history.

Flags:
This is family-led and case-adjacent material. The official site contains individual case pages, photographs, press material, and contact/intake surfaces; news coverage often names people and recounts highly personal histories. Those materials can support internal work-shape and boundary claims, but they do not authorize a public case list, a database extract, a DNA/intake explainer, a contact guide, image reuse, or heroic simplification. The cube also records current political/funding pressure around identity infrastructure as context, not as proof of present operational capacity or safety.
