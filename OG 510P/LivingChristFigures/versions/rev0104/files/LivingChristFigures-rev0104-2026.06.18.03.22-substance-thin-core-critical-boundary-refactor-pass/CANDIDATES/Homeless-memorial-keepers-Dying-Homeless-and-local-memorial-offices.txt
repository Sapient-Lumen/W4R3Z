Homeless memorial keepers / Dying Homeless and local memorial offices

Status: OFFICE-CANON; DO-NOT-NAME-BY-DEFAULT; reverified boundary rev0045
Location: UK / US examples; translocal office
Office: Name-after-death / public memory for the unhoused dead

Work:
Local and national memorial efforts, mortality-review projects, and community vigils collect aggregate death data, keep public grief from disappearing into official undercount, and press for housing and prevention. Some source projects and events read or preserve names; the cube treats that fact as evidence of a memorial office, not as permission to republish names, tribute text, death dates, locations, ages, causes, images, or family stories.

Why it belongs / might belong:
This office says: even when no rescue came, the person will not be permitted to become merely "body found" or "statistics." Rev0045 adds the necessary negative: a person being named by a memorial project, county report, family, journalist, or annual vigil is not a license for the cube to extract the death record.

Flags:
Memorial is not enough. The office is downstream of housing failure, not a substitute for housing work; the cube should not let a vigil substitute for policy. Mortality reports and memorial rolls are prevention/accountability evidence at aggregate level only. Do not reproduce individual names, ages, exact death dates, death locations, causes or manners of death, photographs, memorial tributes, family quotations, Medical Examiner/coroner detail, event locations, submit-a-name pathways, contact fields, or local attendance instructions. Use public-source names only when a future public edition passes a separate consent/load-bearing review; default to no-name extraction.

Evidence links / source classes:
- Museum of Homelessness Dying Homeless Project current page and 2025 update: aggregate UK mortality-counting and memorial-office shape; no tribute/name extraction.
- National Coalition for the Homeless Homeless Persons' Memorial Day page: annual memorial-office shape and prevention framing; no event-guide or name-submission extraction.
- Homeless Deaths Count homepage and help-count page: US aggregate counting and coverage-limit boundary; no individual-death submissions or contact mechanics.
- Los Angeles County and Multnomah County public-health releases: aggregate mortality-review and method-caution context; no person-level death, event-location, contact, service-referral, or medical-advice use.
