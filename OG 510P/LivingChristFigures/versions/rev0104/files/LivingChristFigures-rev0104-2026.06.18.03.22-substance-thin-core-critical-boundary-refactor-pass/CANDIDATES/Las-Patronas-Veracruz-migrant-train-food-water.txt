Las Patronas / Norma Romero — migrant train food-and-water threshold office

Status: PROVISIONAL KEEP; BORDER-WAITING KITCHEN / MIGRANT TRAIN FOOD-AND-WATER OFFICE; NO-ROUTE, NO-REFERRAL, NO-CONTACT, NO-SHELTER-DIRECTORY BOUNDARY
Location: La Patrona / Guadalupe, Amatlan de los Reyes, Veracruz, Mexico; migrants in transit along the freight-train and overland route through Veracruz
Office: Food-and-water at the moving-train threshold / border-waiting kitchen before any lawful or safe door appears

Work:
Las Patronas is a women-led volunteer group from the La Patrona community in Veracruz, associated in public reporting with Norma Romero and the Romero Vazquez family line. The work-shape is simple and severe: cook, pack, carry, and pass food and water to migrants riding or moving near the freight-train route known as La Bestia, while adapting over time toward broader hospitality, shelter-adjacent support, medical/funeral help, and migrant-defense accompaniment.

Current public shape:
Current and retrospective public reporting describe more than thirty years of work, the 2025 death of founder Leonila Vazquez Alvizar, and continuing leadership by Norma Romero. One 2025 report attributes more than 6,000 migrant assists in 2024 to the organization. The cube treats that as contextual scale only, not as a live-capacity, contact, shelter, or referral claim.

Why it belongs / might belong:
The cube already has border-waiting, migrant-grave, distress-call, and humanitarian-corridor offices. Las Patronas adds a distinct threshold: mercy at speed, where the person may never step inside a formal institution and the gift has to be ready before the train has passed. The figure is not a rescuer-hero profile. The office is the kitchen and the rail edge: bread, water, beans, risk, and repetition before law or reception has made a door.
