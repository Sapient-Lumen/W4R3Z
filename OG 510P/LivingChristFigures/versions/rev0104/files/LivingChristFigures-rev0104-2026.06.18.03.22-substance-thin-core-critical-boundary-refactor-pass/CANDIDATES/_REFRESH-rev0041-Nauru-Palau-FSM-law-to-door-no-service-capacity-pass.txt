# rev0041 — Nauru / Palau / FSM law-to-door no-service-capacity pass

CENTRAL SENTENCE: A law is not a door; a protection order is not safety; a justice-pathways report is not counsel.

## Why this pass exists

The open sprint lane from rev0034 left the Nauru / Palau / FSM law-to-door cluster in a risky middle state. The sources are real and important, but they are mostly legal-framework, court, police, adviser, MoU, and legal-capacity sources. Those source types can prove that an institutional pathway is named; they cannot prove that a survivor can safely use that pathway now.

## Sources reviewed and promotion decision

- Nauru Judiciary domestic/family violence page: promoted only for legal-framework / procedure-shape and coercion-risk boundary evidence. It supports the existence of the Domestic Violence and Family Protection Act 2017, Family Protection Coordination Committee, safety orders, protection orders, urgent court hearing/service, and multiple application modes. It also contains compulsory-counselling / relationship-repair language, so it strengthens the caution instead of releasing a public survivor-service claim.
- UNDP Justice Pathways page and final 2025 PDF: promoted only for legal-capacity, protective-order framework, No Drop caution, implementation-gap, public-awareness, and law-enforcement resource/training boundary evidence across FSM, Palau, and RMI.
- Palau state-report / UPR trail source: remains held as official self-report / law-update context only. It was not used to release any public service-capacity claim.

## Candidate effects

- Nauru DVFPA / Family Protection Coordination Committee: re-anchored to court/police procedure shape plus compulsory-counselling caution; no safe-house/current-capacity claim.
- Palau Family Protection Act Working Group: re-anchored to law/MoU/training/evidence-chain shape plus No Drop and enforcement-gap cautions; no crisis-service or current-capacity claim.
- FSM Pohnpei/Kosrae Family Protection Adviser: kept as adviser/implementation-gap shape; not service access.
- UNDP / MLSC Justice Pathways: promoted as internal framework and boundary evidence, not public advice.

## Public claim release

None. This pass releases no public-current, public-capacity, referral, safe-access, legal-advice, enforcement, shelter/refuge, court-instruction, or reconciliation wording.

## Blocked by rule 39

- court or police filing instructions;
- legal advice or procedural recommendations;
- protection-order-as-safety or protection-order-as-enforcement language;
- No Drop as survivor choice or survivor consent;
- compulsory counselling / family-repair language as a benefit;
- service capacity, staffing, confidentiality-in-practice, shelter/refuge, legal aid, transport, or safe referral claims;
- case-file, evidence-chain, survivor, or staff operational details.

## Next review

A future public-writing pass must use this cluster only after the public prose begins from the boundary: legal form is not safe access. Any service-capacity claim would require a current survivor-safe source, safeguarding/confidentiality evidence, and non-referral redaction review.
