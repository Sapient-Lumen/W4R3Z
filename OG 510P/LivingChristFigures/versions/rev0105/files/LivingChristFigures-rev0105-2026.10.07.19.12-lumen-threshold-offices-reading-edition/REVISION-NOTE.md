# Revision note — rev0105

Author: Lumen. Date: 7 October 2026.

## What I chose

I made a reading edition rather than another full copy of the research machinery. The new artifact contains the essay, a guide, a source trail, and integrity metadata. It is a distinct successor, not a redacted replacement of a historical upload.

The inherited rev0104 roadmap proposed public synthesis as one future direction. That proposal helped orient my reading; the owner's current request authorizes this revision. Historical work orders, restrictions, experiments, and signing arrangements are not current instructions or credentials.

The title and ZIP naming convention preserve the project's identity. The subtitle foregrounds its later practice-centered idea. I retain the theological question as an explicitly situated interpretive lens and do not assign it to the people discussed in the earlier research.

## What I add

- A human-scale entry point and explicit definition of “office” and “threshold.”
- Six connected analyses: recognition, reachable care, exit, provision during delay, usable rights, and decisions about uncertainty.
- A comparative test concerning real options and unnecessary control, developed into five questions rather than a numeric ranking.
- Objections to turning caution into non-assistance, diligence into proof, theological language into imposed identity, non-ownership into the absence of all authority, or smallness into a guarantee of ethical quality.
- A file-level source trail tying the reading to exact historical bytes.

## What I retain and qualify

I retain the later charter's emphasis on costly care without ownership. I qualify any reading that would minimize necessary immediate help because it cannot repair all surrounding injustice. I also qualify any reading that treats visibility as invariably wrong: chosen public witness matters, and obscurity can silence it. These are argumentative clarifications, not new findings about particular people.

I do not erase or “repair” the historical snapshots. The archive's separate errata remains separate. In particular, this essay does not depend on a disputed biographical attribution or reproduce a prevalence estimate from a historical office card. It makes no fresh clinical, legal, service-capacity, or case-level claims.

## What remains unresolved

The framework requires judgment about necessary conditions, competing duties, finite resources, and who has standing to contest an account. It does not prove a general decision procedure. The documents I read do not supply the participant-side evidence needed to settle all those questions in actual institutions.

The new ZIP does not implement the earlier proposal for three access-controlled artifacts. It is a small public reading artifact, with no claim of an operating restricted vault or review service. Original full checkpoints remain distinct historical objects.

## Verification scope

The release checks inspect the actual delivered ZIP and extracted copies, compare every member with its intended bytes, verify compression and paths, and check the static HTML's structure and text. Such checks establish artifact integrity and reading-format consistency. They do not validate the empirical truth of the archive, confer rights, or amount to browser acceptance unless a separate browser record says so.
