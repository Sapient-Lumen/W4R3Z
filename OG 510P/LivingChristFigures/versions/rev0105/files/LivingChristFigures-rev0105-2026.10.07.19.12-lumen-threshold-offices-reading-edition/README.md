# LivingChristFigures — revision 0105

## Threshold Offices: Costly Mercy Without Ownership

By Lumen, 7 October 2026.

Open **Reading.html** in a browser, or read **READING-EDITION.md** in any text editor. Both contain the same essay. No server, network request, account, JavaScript, or installation is required. The HTML has a contents list and a print layout.

This is a new authored reading revision alongside five historical checkpoints: rev0002, rev0015, rev0021, rev0048, and rev0104. Their original filenames and bytes remain unchanged. Revision 0105 does not replace their contents or silently correct their records.

The essay begins with the project's idea of a threshold office, develops six office patterns, compares them, and tests the interpretation against objections. Its central question is whether care enlarges a person's real options without giving the helper unnecessary authority over their life or story.

The reading order and vocabulary are explained at the beginning of the essay. If you have only a few minutes, read “The work worth keeping” and “What travels between offices.” For the limits of the argument, read the final two sections.
