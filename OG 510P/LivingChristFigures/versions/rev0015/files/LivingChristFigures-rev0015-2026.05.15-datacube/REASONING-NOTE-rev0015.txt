REASONING NOTE — rev0015

This revision returns to content-addition after rev0014's rule correction.

The most important search finding was not a new candidate but a correction: the Nicole Bell / Safe Exit Initiative entry had gone stale. The old file made a founder-current-organisation frame do work it no longer could do honestly. Fresh searching showed that Bell left the LIFT CEO role in 2023, that LIFT rebranded as Safe Exit Initiative under successor leadership, and that Bell now appears as Nikki Bell Peña, co-founder and co-executive director of Her Way Home. The cube therefore rewrote the file as a lineage entry.

That correction produced a new rule: a threshold office can survive a founder, and a founder can carry the office into a new house, but the cube must not let an old heroic caption pretend continuity is simple. This is now CUBE-RULES rule 11.

The four new additions each fill a carried-forward gap:

Calais fills asylum-seeker waiting-period accompaniment, but in a form sharper than the original phrasing. The border is not only a waiting room; it is an anti-fixation machine that repeatedly destroys the conditions of waiting. The threshold office is therefore not a caseworker's desk but a dispersed ecosystem: kitchen, warehouse, wood, charging, multilingual information, emergency phone, and human-rights observation.

Khulumani fills the South African TRC-successor gap. Its theological/metaphorical fit is not forgiveness but unfinished recognition. The office keeps open a door the state closed rhetorically while bodies, reparations, victim statements, and prosecutions remained unresolved.

Le Va / the Pasifika Suicide Prevention Community Fund only partially satisfies the Pacific gap. It is Aotearoa Pasifika and fund-mediated; it should not be mistaken for island-country-specific postvention. It enters as PROVISIONAL KEEP, mostly because the office shape is clear: suicide grief and risk held through family, fono, church, youth, men, Rainbow communities, language, and trusted leadership.

Mama Dragons / Mama Bears enter as SCOUT because the source base is not yet strong enough for canon, but the office is conceptually important. The child should not have to carry the parent's crisis. Parent-peer groups can be threshold offices because they relocate the adult's fear, shame, religious conflict, and political confusion away from the child and into adult peer-learning spaces.

The rev0014 naming correction was also carried into DOOR-ETHICS as a ninth question: where the cube names people, are the names load-bearing rather than decorative? This matters especially for Mama Dragons / Mama Bears, where minors should not become proof-texts, and for Calais, where images can easily make people in exile into volunteer scenery.

What remains open:
The cube still needs more direct Pacific island-country entries, a deeper private-sponsorship pass, and a richer Helena Maleno rewrite. It also needs to keep watching the Safe Exit / Her Way Home lineage because service capacity, funding, and governance are moving targets.
