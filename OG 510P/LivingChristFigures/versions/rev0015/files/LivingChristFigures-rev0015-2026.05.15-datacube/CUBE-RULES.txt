CUBE RULES — as of rev0015

This file consolidates the rules that have accumulated across revisions, so that the SearchTrails' "standing rules" list does not stay the only home for load-bearing constraints.

1. DEFINITIONAL RULE (rev0010, retained)
"Christ figure" is a severe metaphor for costly mercy, not a theological claim and not an identification of divinity.

2. CENTRAL SENTENCE (rev0010, retained)
The office is the threshold held open without owning the one who crosses it.

3. DOOR ETHICS (rev0010, retained)
See DOOR-ETHICS.txt. Eight questions, all still live. Question 3 is specifically about images, not names; do not confuse the two.

4. STATUS CATEGORIES (rev0010 onward)
KEEP; PROVISIONAL KEEP; SCOUT / POSSIBLE KEEP; OFFICE-CANON; PERSON-BENCHMARK / HOLD; NETWORK-CANDIDATE / INSTITUTION-CANDIDATE (rev0011); COMMUNAL-DOCTRINAL (rev0012). The cube allows entries to carry multiple statuses.

5. SOURCING RULES (rev0010 onward, retained)
Facebook-only leads held until non-Facebook corroboration.
State-media-only leads flagged and refreshed.
Where a religious or communal authority has done threshold work, the cube records both the welcome and its stated limits in the same entry (rev0012).

6. CANDIDACY RULES (rev0010 onward, retained but loosened over time)
Unknown / non-powerful work is preferred. Famous figures and well-funded networks may be admitted as PERSON-BENCHMARK or OFFICE-CANON when the office is unintelligible without them. Recognition does not by itself disqualify; the cube has bent this many times and rev0011 made the bend explicit.

7. PRECONDITION RULE (rev0013, retained)
Conversion-required offices are flagged with an explicit precondition flag. The cube does not let "the alternative is death" silence the question of what the office still asks of the person it helps — conversion to a frame, sobriety, gratitude, public identity, photogenic suffering.

8. PEER / HELPER DISTINCTION (rev0013, retained)
Peer-led offices (Hearing Voices Movement, Mães de Maio, Sudan ERRs, Madres Buscadoras) are a distinct shape from helper-led offices and carry distinct DOOR-ETHICS readings — particularly question 1 ("Can the person leave the helper?"), which reads differently when the helper is also being helped.

9. ERROR-LOGGING RULE (rev0013, retained, now general)
The cube logs its own errors honestly rather than retroactively rewriting earlier files. SearchTrails, REASONING-NOTEs, and REFRESH notes are the right places for corrections; earlier files retain their original wording with a refresh or correction noted alongside.

10. NAMING RULE (rev0014, REPLACES the rev0010-through-rev0013 redaction rule)
The rule the cube used to carry — "no naming of vulnerable individuals as proof of virtue / no naming of individual X as proof of office" — has been retired. It was performative. The candidate files were performing a redaction the evidence-links section was making meaningless thirty lines below, and the rule's main practical effect was to keep cube prose vaguer than the sources it cited.

The rule that replaces it has three parts:

10a. Links do the talking. Every candidate file has an evidence section. The cube does not censor what its sources publish openly. Names that appear in linked reporting may appear in cube prose where they are load-bearing.

10b. Load-bearing, not decorative. Names appear in cube prose because the office is unintelligible without them, because the office is named after the person, because the person has taken on a public role specifically as representative of the harm, or because precision about who did what requires it. Names do not appear for emotional impact, human-interest texture, or volume.

10c. Source discretion respected. Where a published source has deliberately withheld a name (pseudonyms like Newsweek's "Pastor Alwin Flores," the unnamed SPASS source in America Magazine, Yazidi survivors whose faces were not shown), the cube does not retroactively un-anonymise. This is a writing rule, not a redaction rule: it tracks the source's own choice rather than imposing one of the cube's own.

DOOR-ETHICS question 3 remains intact. It is about images, and images do different work from names. A name in a news article is in the public record. An image of a grieving mother deployed for emotional fundraising effect is doing something else, and the question about whether the image is necessary and consentful still applies.

Where the prior rule was doing genuine work it has been replaced with more specific rules in the candidate files themselves:
- For suicide reporting: the cube follows safe-messaging practice on method and detail (IASP/WHO guidance), as a writing practice. Names are not the issue; details that could enable copycatting are.
- For currently-active people at risk: the cube uses the names that public sources use; where a source has anonymised for safety reasons, the cube respects that choice and notes the anonymisation.
- For aestheticisation: the New Yorker Sonia Bermúdez documentary, the 2025 RHB Bank Tan Swee Ban short film, and similar productions can convert costly mercy into image-content; the cube takes the underlying work as documented and does not iterate the imagery.

11. ANTI-BROCHURE RULE (rev0010 onward, retained)
The cube does not iterate into a brochure for any single organisation, home, or network. Description that reads like fundraising copy is the warning sign.

12. OFFICE-CARDS-AS-LENSES, NOT BINS (rev0013, retained)
A single person or network can hold several offices open simultaneously. The cards are descriptions of shapes of mercy, not exclusive categories. Cube entries may cite multiple office cards.


11. LINEAGE / SUCCESSOR RULE (rev0015)
Where a candidate file was originally framed around a named founder and later searching shows the founder has left, split from, or been succeeded by the organisation, the cube does not pretend continuity is simple. It records lineage, successor leadership, and current office separately. A threshold office can survive a founder; a founder can carry the same office into a new house; either way, the cube must say what is current rather than letting an old heroic caption keep doing the work.
