The overdose-memorial office is parallel to but distinct from the name-after-death office for the unhoused. The double stigma of illicit substance and "their own fault" prevents many families from grieving openly. The threshold is the funeral or vigil at which the family discovers whether the death will be permitted into the public record without footnotes. The office is silver ribbon, candle, named reading — and the refusal to let "overdose" be a moral verdict on the life.
