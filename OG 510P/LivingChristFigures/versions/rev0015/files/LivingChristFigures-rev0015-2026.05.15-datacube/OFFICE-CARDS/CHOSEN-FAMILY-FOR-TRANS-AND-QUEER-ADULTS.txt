The chosen-family-for-trans-and-queer-adults office is the cube's second chosen-family office and the first focused on adults already pushed out of biological family. The threshold is the moment the trans woman, gay man, or queer migrant arrives without housing — released from prison, evicted with a hotel closure, fleeing a homicidal country — and discovers that someone has built her a house. The danger of the office is that "chosen family" can be aestheticised into spectacle; the office must remain a place where the person can leave, refuse the script, and not be photographed for it.
