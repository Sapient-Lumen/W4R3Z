Mia Atoui / Embrace Lebanon — Embrace LifeLine 1564

Status: PROVISIONAL KEEP / OFFICE-CANON FOR THE PSYCHOLOGICAL DISTRESS LINE IN COMPOUNDING-CRISIS CONTEXT; KEEP WITH NGO-PLATFORM AND DONOR FLAGS
Location: Lebanon — Beirut-based, with the helpline serving the whole country and parts of the wider Arabic-speaking Middle East
Office: Suicide-prevention hotline / psychological-distress voice / phone-that-stays-on during compounding societal collapse

Work:
Embrace was co-founded in 2013 by Mia Atoui (also rendered Mia Atwi or Mia Malda Atoui), a clinical psychologist at the American University of Beirut, and colleagues. After a 2015 crowdfunding campaign, in 2017 Embrace launched Lebanon's first national emotional-support and suicide-prevention helpline, Embrace LifeLine (1564). The helpline received about 2,500 calls in 2019 and over 6,100 in 2020, with a significant spike after the 4 August 2020 Beirut port explosion that killed at least 200, injured 6,000+, and displaced more than 300,000 people; in the two months after the blast the line took 2,239 calls of which roughly 67% reported emotional distress and 28% suicidal ideation. The line operates 12:00 PM to 5:30 AM daily as it works toward 24/7 coverage. Embrace also runs an in-person clinic and trains first responders, health workers, and community organisations in suicide-prevention, active listening, and psychological first aid.

Embrace's office is the line that does not close as the country's other systems do.

Flags:
Embrace is a relatively well-resourced NGO with international corporate partners (the Facebook suicide-prevention safety-centre integration is one example); the cube ordinarily prefers smaller, less platform-connected work. It is also closely coordinated with Lebanon's Ministry of Public Health — a state-proximity flag in a state that has been substantively non-functional through much of the period in question. Mia Atoui is a public figure with international appearances (Global Thinkers Forum, IOF), more in the PERSON-BENCHMARK range. As with all suicide-related work, the cube does not reproduce caller statistics or details about specific callers; the 1564 number is published widely and is the only operational detail this entry includes.
