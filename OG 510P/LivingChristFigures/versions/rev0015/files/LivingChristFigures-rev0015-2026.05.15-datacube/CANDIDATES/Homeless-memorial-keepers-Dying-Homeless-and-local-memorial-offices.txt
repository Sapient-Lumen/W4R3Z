Homeless memorial keepers / Dying Homeless and local memorial offices

Status: OFFICE-CANON
Location: UK / US examples; translocal office
Office: Name-after-death / public memory for the unhoused dead

Work:
Local and national memorial efforts collect and read the names of people who died while homeless, create records where official systems flatten lives, and turn grief into pressure for housing and prevention.

Why it belongs / might belong:
This office says: even when no rescue came, the person will not be permitted to become merely "body found" or "statistics."

Flags:
Memorial is not enough. The office is downstream of housing failure, not a substitute for housing work; the cube should not let a vigil substitute for policy. The Museum of Homelessness "Dying Homeless" project, OSV News, the Denver Homeless Persons Memorial Vigil and similar networks publish the names the bereaved have asked them to publish; the cube uses the names that are already in those rolls.
