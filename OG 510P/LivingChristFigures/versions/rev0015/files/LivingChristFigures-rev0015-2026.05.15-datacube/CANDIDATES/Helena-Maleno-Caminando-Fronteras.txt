Helena Maleno / Caminando Fronteras

Status: OFFICE-CANON; PERSON-BENCHMARK, NOT UNKNOWN CANON
Location: Morocco/Spain / Western Euro-African border
Office: Distress-call witness / family search for the sea-dead / border anti-erasure

Work:
Founder of Caminando Fronteras, which documents border deaths, receives distress/family calls, and advocates for rescue, identification, and accountability at the Western Euro-African border.

Why it belongs / might belong:
The office is a phone and a ledger against water. It does not make the migrant locatable by ownership; it makes the state’s non-response locatable by evidence.

Flags:
