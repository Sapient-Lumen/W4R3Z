Le Va / Pasifika Suicide Prevention Community Fund — with Link4Life, Gateway Community Trust, Vaka Pasifika, and other Pasifika-led initiatives

Status: PROVISIONAL KEEP; NETWORK-CANDIDATE; KEEP WITH FUNDER-MEDIATED, AOTEAROA-DIASPORA, AND CLINICAL-SAFETY FLAGS
Location: Aotearoa New Zealand — Pasifika communities nationally, with Samoan, Tongan, Fijian, Cook Islands, Niuean, Tokelauan, Tuvaluan and other Pacific peoples represented across funded initiatives
Office: Pasifika suicide-prevention and postvention / fono, family, church, culture, and community-champion threshold after suicidal distress or suicide death

Work:
Le Va administers the Pasifika Suicide Prevention Community Fund, a Ministry-linked fund that enables Pasifika families, community groups, churches, youth spaces, digital projects, and local organisations to design and deliver culturally grounded suicide-prevention and postvention work. The 2025 outcomes report says the fund awarded $3.55 million from 2020 to 2023 to 41 groups and organisations, representing 66 successful applications, with more than 590,000 direct engagements and more than 2.4 million people reached on social media. The fund’s stated objectives include supporting Pasifika families and communities experiencing suicide risk or bereaved by suicide, strengthening resilience and connectedness, prioritising higher-risk populations such as men, young people, Rainbow communities, and rural communities, and building confidence to speak about mental distress. One example, Gateway Community Trust’s Link4Life, worked with families affected by suicide through workshops, family champions, a virtual village, podcasts, support groups, and an app for group connection.

Why it belongs / might belong:
The rev0013 gap list asked for Pacific Island offices beyond Aotearoa. This is not yet the clean island-country find; it is a diaspora-and-Aotearoa Pasifika find. It still belongs provisionally because the office shape is clear: suicide bereavement and risk are not treated as private pathology alone, but as a family, church, language, culture, youth, men’s, Rainbow, and community-leadership threshold. The office is the fono or support group that lets a family become resourced without becoming a spectacle.

Flags:
This entry is fund-mediated. Le Va is an intermediary and evaluator, not identical with each local office. The cube should not convert a grant portfolio into a single heroic organisation. It should also avoid treating “Pasifika” as one culture. The strongest future pass should locate specific Tongan, Samoan, Fijian, Cook Islands, Niuean, Tokelauan, Tuvaluan, and island-country-led postvention offices on their own terms, including work outside Aotearoa. Because suicide reporting has contagion and stigma risks, the cube keeps method details out and follows safe-messaging practice.
