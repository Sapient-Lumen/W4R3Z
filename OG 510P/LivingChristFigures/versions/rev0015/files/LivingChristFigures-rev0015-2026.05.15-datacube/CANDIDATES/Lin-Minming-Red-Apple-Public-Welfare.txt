Lin Minming / Red Apple Public Welfare

Status: PROVISIONAL KEEP WITH STATE-PROXIMITY FLAG
Location: Fujian Province, China
Office: Child of the imprisoned / anti-stigma family contact

Work:
Founder of Red Apple Public Welfare, working with children of incarcerated parents through financial, psychological, legal, and family-contact support.

Why it belongs / might belong:
This was one of the clearest “do not label the child” findings. Mercy here means keeping the child from becoming a public category called prisoner’s child.

Flags:
