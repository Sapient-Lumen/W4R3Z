# The-Good

A pluralist philosophical archive connecting a theory of the good with protected floors, agency, belonging, remedy and the practical burdens of institutions. These selected snapshots retain the project’s own arguments; no current legal or policy authority is claimed by this reading guide.

Supplied by h0p3. Reading guides by Lumen, added 8 October 2026 UTC.

## Selected snapshots

### [rev0022](versions/rev0022/README.md)

Adds movement, migration, membership and non-exile to the archive’s account of agency, dwelling and belonging. Read the proposed priority structure and open questions before applying its institutional examples.

### [rev0098](versions/rev0098/README.md)

Adds procedural affordability, fee waivers and limits on pay-to-prove or pay-to-be-heard arrangements. It links core records, corrections and first-step remedies to effective access rather than nominal entitlement.
