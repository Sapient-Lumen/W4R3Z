2. protected floors before smooth maximization;
3. shielding and redesign before tragic balancing;
4. visible moral remainder over false closure;
5. explicit admission gates over kernel sprawl;
6. patient-scope discipline over silent anthropocentrism or indiscriminate flattening;
7. sufficiency, priority, and anti-domination over average-gain complacency;
8. uncertainty discipline over either reckless certainty or status-quo paralysis;
9. preference-formation discipline over revealed-choice naïveté or casual paternalism;
10. affirmative flourishing over survival-only thinness once floors are secured;

12. accountable repair, victim protection, and reviewable return over either coerced forgiveness or permanent civic death after breach;
13. meaningful say and shared rule over expertly managed benevolence without uptake;

18. protected opacity, sanctuary, and selective self-disclosure over full legibility as the price of participation or safety;
19. bounded markets, public options, and non-sale spheres over the assumption that price automatically settles legitimacy;
20. accessible worlds, anticipatory inclusion, and accommodation without stigma over normate design that makes people petition for ordinary entry;
21. usable rest, breathable rhythm, and freedom from permanent availability over lives that are technically protected but temporally unlivable;
22. childhood present goods, developmental fit, and answerable guardianship over adultist control, proxy optimization, or child-as-future-output thinking;
23. later-life dignity, supported dependency, palliative relief, and socially legible mourning over ageist disposal, warehousing, or compulsory prolongation;
24. secure dwelling, anti-displacement, and home as more than commodity over roof-counting, shelter warehousing, or serial uprooting disguised as service;
25. reversible, inspectable, option-preserving pathways where stakes are grave and models are contested;
26. source-grounded compression over quotation hoarding;
27. canon/quarantine separation instead of elegant smuggling;
28. dashboard pluralism with public reasons instead of one sovereign metric.
