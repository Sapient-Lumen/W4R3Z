# 010 — Priority kernels: a pluralist working synthesis

## One-line thesis

The strongest current candidate for canon is a **priority-kernel pluralism**: the good consists in protecting a small set of high-weight value kernels first and then enlarging plural flourishing above those floors; however, future additions to the kernel set should be governed by the admission protocol in `archive/014-kernel-admission-common-good-scaffolding-and-value-gates.md`, not by value accumulation.

## Why this matters

The archive needs more than a map of theory families. It needs a working synthesis that can guide judgment while remaining corrigible. The most promising move so far is not full monism and not shapeless pluralism, but a structured pluralism with priority order.

## Proposed priority kernels

### 1. Survival and bodily integrity

No humane theory of the good can treat avoidable severe suffering, starvation, torture, assault, or bodily devastation as morally secondary. This kernel also bars treating bodies as open infrastructure for families, markets, states, or institutions: intimate intervention needs valid consent or an unusually strong, reviewable justification, and reproductive agency belongs inside the kernel rather than at its margins. `archive/030-bodily-integrity-consent-and-reproductive-agency.md` sharpens this embodied-authority requirement.

### 2. Care floor and anti-cruelty

Dependent beings need reliable care. Cruelty is not merely one disvalue among others; it is a deep antagonist of the good. Where life is shaped by serious decline, chronic illness, or approaching death, this kernel also requires humane long-term care, palliative relief, and non-abandoning accompaniment rather than ageist disposal or warehouse-style management. `archive/028-aging-frailty-mortality-and-bereavement.md` sharpens this later-life requirement.

### 3. Agency, dignity, meaningful say, accessible world-fit, usable time, non-servile livelihood, and somewhere to dwell

Persons should not be governed merely as containers of welfare or as well-served clients of institutional expertise. The good includes the standing and practical space to choose, refuse, revise, count, retain some meaningful say over the public and organizational rules that structure shared life, inhabit environments and communications that do not silently assume one narrow normate bodymind, preserve some protected opacity against compulsory exposure, possess some genuinely usable time that is not wholly captured by schedules set elsewhere, secure livelihood without having to submit to degrading or dangerous work on ruinous terms, live from some reasonably secure place rather than under constant threat of uprooting or warehousing, and move, stay, leave, return, and regularize status without arbitrary exile, camp limbo, or humiliating precarity. Where lives begin in dependency rather than competence, this kernel requires answerable guidance, developmental fit, and growing voice rather than ownership or adultist overcontrol. Where lives move into frailty, serious illness, or approaching death, it also requires supported decision-making, privacy-preserving assistance, and truthful options rather than presuming that declining capacity erases voice. `archive/022-voice-participation-and-co-authorship.md` sharpens the authorship dimension; `archive/023-privacy-sanctuary-and-protected-opacity.md` sharpens the sanctuary dimension; `archive/025-rest-rhythm-and-time-sovereignty.md` sharpens the temporal one; `archive/026-accessibility-accommodation-and-world-fit.md` sharpens the access-fit dimension; `archive/027-childhood-development-and-just-guardianship.md` sharpens the developmental one; `archive/028-aging-frailty-mortality-and-bereavement.md` sharpens the later-life one; `archive/029-home-dwelling-place-and-non-displacement.md` sharpens the dwelling dimension; `archive/032-livelihood-meaningful-work-and-non-servility.md` sharpens the work-and-livelihood dimension; `archive/033-movement-migration-membership-and-non-exile.md` sharpens the movement-and-membership dimension.

### 4. Truthful world-contact

The good includes living in contact with reality rather than within systematic deception, epistemic exclusion, or manufactured ignorance.

### 5. Non-domination, fair standing, and non-humiliation

Social and political arrangements should reduce arbitrary power, civic subordination, degrading treatment, and caste-like ranking. `archive/020-dignity-recognition-humiliation-and-belonging.md` sharpens this equal-standing requirement.

### 6. Relationship goods, recognition, social belonging, and place continuity

Love, friendship, decent companionship, chosen kin, participation in shared life, some workable continuity of home and neighborhood, and some non-humiliating path to territorial presence and political membership are constitutive goods for many beings and often enabling conditions for agency itself. These goods are not secured merely by any available household form: the archive should distinguish freely sustained attachment from coercive kinship, chosen belonging from degrading dependence, and meaningful continuity from captivity. Belonging on humiliating or second-class terms, or under chronic threat of displacement, should not count as a satisfactory substitute. `archive/031-intimacy-friendship-chosen-kin-and-exit.md` sharpens this intimate-association dimension.

### 7. Generative plurality and affirmative flourishing

Above the protected floor, a good order should make room for multiple life paths, forms of excellence, cultures, projects, aesthetic experiences, practices of play, and shared celebration, rather than forcing convergence onto one model of success or reducing public value to managed survival. `archive/019-affirmative-flourishing-play-beauty-learning-and-celebration.md` clarifies this positive layer. `archive/024-non-fungibility-bounded-markets-and-protected-spheres.md` adds that many such goods are degraded when treated as only items of commercial inventory.

### 8. Intergenerational continuity

A good present does not undermine the material, ecological, and institutional basis of future good.

## How the synthesis works

### 0. Classify the value claim before invoking it

Before adding a new alleged kernel or building policy around a new value word, use `archive/014-kernel-admission-common-good-scaffolding-and-value-gates.md` to determine whether the claim is about a final good, common-good scaffolding, a dashboard indicator, or a local derivative aim.

### 1. Protect the kernels

First ask whether a proposal violates a kernel floor. Read these kernels as extending to all beings whose welfare or standing morally matters, with the scope distinctions in `archive/015-patient-scope-sentience-and-living-systems.md` clarifying how anti-cruelty floors, agency claims, and system protections differ.

### 2. Repair the floor before optimizing above it

Where floors are breached, emergency moral attention should usually go there before polishing higher-order gains.

### 3. Distribute improvements with sufficiency, priority, and anti-domination in view

When deciding who receives the next protection, transfer, service, or institutional reform, use the rule in `archive/016-distribution-sufficiency-priority-and-anti-oligarchy.md`: repair floor breaches first, give extra weight to equivalent gains for the worse-off, and resist concentrations that turn advantage into arbitrary power.

### 4. Audit preference formation before calling a choice good

Where judgments depend on consent, demand, satisfaction, or uptake, also use `archive/018-preference-formation-adaptive-desire-and-manipulation.md`. Treat first-person reports as serious evidence, but do not let deprivation, humiliation, addictive design, or manipulative steering masquerade as clean endorsement. Where those affected are children, also use `archive/027-childhood-development-and-just-guardianship.md` so protection, school discipline, family discretion, and developmental guidance are not mistaken either for pure domination or for cleanly autonomous choice.

### 5. Enlarge plural flourishing

Once the floor is secured, distributional shape is being governed, and preference has been checked for captivity or manipulation, the aim is not uniformity but widening the set of good lives people can actually live, including lives rich in beauty, play, learning, craft, festivity, non-instrumental activity, decent contribution, and enough breathable time to inhabit those goods rather than merely admire them from an exhausted distance.

### 6. Preserve contestability, participatory say, and bounded market rule

Because weights and boundaries remain disputable, the synthesis must remain publicly criticizable. It should also avoid treating contestation as enough when those affected are granted only complaint rights after design. Where stakes are high and rule is durable, build meaningful channels of participation and uptake as part of the good itself, while preserving confidentiality, selective disclosure, and non-performative space for those asked to appear before institutions. Also resist the slide from market existence to sale justification: `archive/024-non-fungibility-bounded-markets-and-protected-spheres.md` clarifies when markets are acceptable tools, when they require strong bounds, and when they misdescribe the good entirely.

### 7. Build repair-and-return paths after breach

When wrong has been done, debt has been incurred, or trust has been fractured, the aim should not default to permanent exile. Use `archive/021-repair-accountability-forgiveness-and-second-chances.md` to distinguish accountability from humiliation, forgiveness from reconciliation, and protective consequence from endless civic death.

### 8. Handle collisions with a remainder protocol

When protected kernels collide, the synthesis should not collapse into a hidden aggregate score. It should instead use the conflict protocol in `archive/013-kernel-collisions-triage-tragic-remainder-and-repair.md`: attempt shielding first, resist irreversible floor breaches, and carry forward repair duties when tragic loss cannot be avoided.

### 9. Act under uncertainty with reversibility and precaution in view

When stakes are serious but evidence, scope, or value ranking remains unsettled, the synthesis should also use `archive/017-uncertainty-precaution-reversibility-and-burden-shifting.md`: do not let uncertainty excuse grave avoidable harm, prefer reversible and option-preserving pathways where possible, and scrutinize who is being asked to bear uncertain downside.

## Advantages

- It explains why some harms feel non-compensable or only tragically compensable.
- It leaves room for objective goods without requiring a fully ranked master list.
- It honors agency without ignoring welfare.
- It connects intimate, social, institutional, and ecological dimensions.
- It gives governance a usable structure without surrendering to one index.
- It prevents the archive from naming protected goods while staying agnostic about who keeps getting them.
- It handles deep uncertainty without pretending that only full certainty can justify protection.
- It treats fallibility and wrongdoing as conditions a good order must metabolize through repair and reviewable return rather than only through exclusion.
- It resists both preference fetishism and casual paternal override.
- It keeps a good order from collapsing into expertly managed benevolence by making room for participatory co-authorship, not only contestability after the fact.
- It keeps the archive from degenerating into a survival-and-compliance ethic that forgets what protected life is for.
- It makes temporal integrity visible so a life with nominal liberties but permanent exhaustion, volatility, or availability does not get misclassified as good enough.
- It makes dwelling integrity visible so a life with nominal shelter but chronic eviction threat, serial displacement, or shelter warehousing does not get misclassified as protected enough.

## Risks

- The kernels might be too coarse.
- The priority order might be culturally biased.
- Borderline cases may still demand controversial comparison.
- Collisions among kernels can tempt actors back into covert scalar tradeoffs.
- “Plurality” can become evasive if not linked to protected floors.
- Floor protection can be hollowed out if distributional burdens are repeatedly dumped on the already burdened.
- Preference-formation audit can slide into condescension if outsiders assume too quickly that unfamiliar choices are damaged choices.
- Repair-and-return language can be abused as a cheap rhetorical shortcut if institutions skip victim protection, truthful acknowledgment, or real change.

## Guardrails

- Do not let the kernel list expand until it becomes merely a restatement of everything good; use the admission protocol in `archive/014-kernel-admission-common-good-scaffolding-and-value-gates.md`.
- Do not let kernel talk conceal hard disputes about order and exceptions.
- Do not let protected floors be invoked selectively for the already powerful.
- Do not let reported demand or engagement override the question of whether the choice architecture itself was fit for freedom.
- Do not let participation be reduced to ceremonial consultation or service satisfaction while real authorship stays elsewhere.
- Do not let safety, convenience, or data hunger dissolve ordinary zones of sanctuary, confidentiality, and selective self-disclosure.
- Do not let ability to pay masquerade as a sufficient public reason for selling access to standing, care, bodily integrity, culture, or core civic goods.
- Do not let formally granted rights conceal that people lack the time, predictability, or recovery needed to use them.
- Do not let formal indoor status conceal that people lack the tenure security, privacy, neighborhood continuity, or anti-displacement protection needed to actually dwell.
- Do not let accountability harden into permanent civic banishment when protection, truth, and reparative aims no longer require it.

## Compression rule for the archive

Protect the conditions without which lives are maimed or ruled, then widen the space in which many different good lives can genuinely be lived.
