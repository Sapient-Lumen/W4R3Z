# 011 — Quarantine: world-contact, non-domination, and regenerative flourishing

## One-line thesis

**Quarantined hypothesis:** a large share of moral theory may compress into one deep criterion—goodness is the expansion of zones where beings can flourish in reality, without domination, and without depleting the regenerative conditions of future life.

## Why quarantine instead of canon

This hypothesis is attractive because it seems to gather several powerful strands at once:

- capability language about real freedom;
- republican language about non-domination;
- care language about dependency and support;
- epistemic language about truth and reality-contact;
- sustainability language about regeneration across time.

It might therefore be a strong field equation for the good.

But it remains under-tested. It may still compress too fast, privilege institutional forms of value over sacred or aesthetic ones, and risk translating all goodness into a civilizational systems vocabulary.

## Why the hypothesis is promising

### 1. It explains why deception feels morally deep

Systematic deception harms not only outcomes but a being’s relation to the world.

### 2. It explains why domination is not redeemed by benevolent outputs

To flourish under arbitrary mastery is already a compromised condition.

### 3. It explains why ecological liquidation is a value failure even before collapse arrives

A regime that destroys its own regenerative basis is misdescribing short-run extraction as good.

### 4. It connects personal and political ethics

The same three antagonists—reality-severance, domination, and regenerative depletion—appear at multiple scales.

## Why the hypothesis may still be wrong or incomplete

### 1. It may understate transcendence, sanctity, beauty, or excellence

Some traditions may insist that the good includes forms of value not well captured by the flourishing-in-reality frame.

### 2. It may over-systematize tragedy

Some goods conflict in ways no field equation can absorb without residue.

### 3. It may smuggle in a modern political picture

The language of domination, public knowledge, and regenerative systems could reflect a historically specific institutional imagination.

## Suggested tests before canonization

1. Can the hypothesis explain intimate goods such as love and friendship without reducing them to support functions?
2. Can it explain artistic or contemplative excellence?
3. Can it handle cases where truth harms comfort but is still good?
4. Can it distinguish justified authority from domination without collapsing authority entirely?
5. Can it explain why some self-sacrifice for future persons is admirable but not always required?

## Compression rule for the archive

This is the archive’s boldest current compression, but it has not yet earned canon. Keep it live, test it hard, and do not let its elegance outrun its proof.
