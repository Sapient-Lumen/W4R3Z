# 010 — Priority kernels: a pluralist working synthesis

## One-line thesis

The strongest current candidate for canon is a **priority-kernel pluralism**: the good consists in protecting a small set of high-weight value kernels first and then enlarging plural flourishing above those floors; however, future additions to the kernel set should be governed by the admission protocol in `archive/014-kernel-admission-common-good-scaffolding-and-value-gates.md`, not by value accumulation.

## Why this matters

The archive needs more than a map of theory families. It needs a working synthesis that can guide judgment while remaining corrigible. The most promising move so far is not full monism and not shapeless pluralism, but a structured pluralism with priority order.

## Proposed priority kernels

### 1. Survival and bodily integrity

No humane theory of the good can treat avoidable severe suffering, starvation, torture, assault, coercive threat, femicide risk, preventable injury, bodily devastation, or the organized destruction of civilian life through bombardment, siege, occupation, or forced recruitment as morally secondary. This kernel also bars treating bodies as open infrastructure for families, markets, states, or institutions: intimate intervention needs valid consent or an unusually strong, reviewable justification, and ordinary life should not be organized so that assault risk, dangerous routes, explosive violence, aid denial, or avoidable bodily hazard become routine prices of participation. `archive/030-bodily-integrity-consent-and-reproductive-agency.md` sharpens the embodied-authority requirement, `archive/040-ordinary-security-nonviolence-and-preventable-injury.md` sharpens the lived-safety requirement, and `archive/052-civilian-peace-demilitarization-and-non-siege-life.md` sharpens the civilian-peace and non-siege requirement.

### 2. Care floor, health continuity, and anti-cruelty

Dependent beings need reliable care, and people need some reasonably continuous access to physical and mental health support without financial, spatial, or procedural ruin. Cruelty is not merely one disvalue among others; it is a deep antagonist of the good, and that anti-cruelty floor extends beyond humans to sentient animals whose lives are shaped by human systems, markets, and habitat pressures. This kernel also requires that ordinary environments remain breathable, non-toxic, and thermally survivable enough that people are not routinely made ill, developmentally harmed, or driven from public life by smoke, poison, or dangerous heat. It also requires care and support systems that meet dependency without abandonment, gendered exhaustion, or custodial warehousing, while protecting both those who require care and those who provide it. Where life is shaped by serious decline, chronic illness, disability, psychological distress, or approaching death, this kernel also requires prevention where possible, timely treatment, humane long-term care, respite, palliative relief, and non-abandoning accompaniment rather than ageist disposal, therapeutic drift, or warehouse-style management. `archive/028-aging-frailty-mortality-and-bereavement.md` sharpens the later-life requirement, `archive/038-health-mental-well-being-and-care-continuity.md` sharpens the broader health-continuity one, `archive/050-care-supported-dependency-and-non-abandonment.md` sharpens the supported-dependency and non-abandonment one, `archive/041-clean-air-non-toxic-environments-and-thermal-safety.md` sharpens the environmental-habitability one, and `archive/053-animal-welfare-anti-cruelty-and-multispecies-coexistence.md` sharpens the animal-welfare and multispecies-coexistence one.

### 4. Truthful world-contact

The good includes living in contact with reality rather than within systematic deception, epistemic exclusion, manufactured ignorance, or disorienting information environments that strand people without reliable ways to test what matters. It also has temporal depth: a good order should preserve enough archives, memorial practices, public records, and truth processes that the past cannot be cheaply denied, sealed away, or administratively dissolved. `archive/034-truthful-world-contact-education-and-epistemic-habitat.md` sharpens this epistemic-habitat requirement, and `archive/057-public-memory-historical-truth-and-non-erasure.md` sharpens its public-memory and historical-truth dimension.

### 5. Non-domination, fair standing, and non-humiliation

Social and political arrangements should reduce arbitrary power, civic subordination, degrading treatment, caste-like ranking, and the quiet conversion of public office into private access, patronage, or saleable influence. `archive/020-dignity-recognition-humiliation-and-belonging.md` sharpens this equal-standing requirement, and `archive/047-public-integrity-anti-corruption-and-non-kleptocratic-rule.md` sharpens the anti-kleptocratic one.

### 6. Relationship goods, recognition, social belonging, and place continuity

Love, friendship, decent companionship, chosen kin, participation in shared life, some workable continuity of home and neighborhood, and some non-humiliating path to territorial presence and political membership are constitutive goods for many beings and often enabling conditions for agency itself. These goods are not secured merely by any available household form: the archive should distinguish freely sustained attachment from coercive kinship, chosen belonging from degrading dependence, and meaningful continuity from captivity. Belonging on humiliating or second-class terms, or under chronic threat of displacement, should not count as a satisfactory substitute. `archive/031-intimacy-friendship-chosen-kin-and-exit.md` sharpens this intimate-association dimension.

### 7. Generative plurality and affirmative flourishing

Above the protected floor, a good order should make room for multiple life paths, forms of excellence, cultures, projects, aesthetic experiences, practices of play, and shared celebration, rather than forcing convergence onto one model of success or reducing public value to managed survival. `archive/019-affirmative-flourishing-play-beauty-learning-and-celebration.md` clarifies this positive layer. `archive/060-aspiration-excellence-and-non-humiliating-standards.md` adds that demanding standards, mastery, and aspiration can belong inside the good without becoming a ranking of human worth or a luxury pathway for the already advantaged. `archive/056-public-realm-common-world-and-low-threshold-shared-life.md` adds that such flourishing also depends on maintained shared spaces and institutions where people can appear, linger, and participate without price, invitation, or prestige filtering. `archive/070-everyday-nature-green-access-and-contact-with-the-living-world.md` adds that these shared worlds also go thin when daily life is ecologically barren and ordinary contact with trees, shade, weather, water, and nearby nonhuman life survives mainly as a purchasable or destination good. `archive/071-ordinary-beauty-aesthetic-dignity-and-non-degrading-environments.md` adds that shared worlds are also degraded when ordinary schools, clinics, housing, shelters, transit, and public institutions are left bleak or contemptuous by design while beauty and design care are privatized. `archive/024-non-fungibility-bounded-markets-and-protected-spheres.md` adds that many such goods are degraded when treated as only items of commercial inventory.

### 8. Intergenerational continuity

A good present does not undermine the material, ecological, and institutional basis of future good. `archive/055-ecological-integrity-biodiversity-and-more-than-human-inheritance.md` sharpens this by treating biodiversity, habitat continuity, and the more-than-human inheritance as high-weight common goods rather than as disposable service stock.
 `archive/058-maintenance-repair-and-ordinary-stewardship.md` adds that continuity is also lost through neglect: a society can quietly liquidate shared goods by deferring upkeep, exhausting custodial labor, and normalizing breakdown long before formal collapse or privatization is announced.

## How the synthesis works

### 0. Classify the value claim before invoking it

Before adding a new alleged kernel or building policy around a new value word, use `archive/014-kernel-admission-common-good-scaffolding-and-value-gates.md` to determine whether the claim is about a final good, common-good scaffolding, a dashboard indicator, or a local derivative aim.

### 1. Protect the kernels

First ask whether a proposal violates a kernel floor. Read these kernels as extending to all beings whose welfare or standing morally matters, with the scope distinctions in `archive/015-patient-scope-sentience-and-living-systems.md` clarifying how anti-cruelty floors, agency claims, and system protections differ.

### 2. Repair the floor before optimizing above it

Where floors are breached, emergency moral attention should usually go there before polishing higher-order gains.

### 3. Distribute improvements with sufficiency, priority, and anti-domination in view

When deciding who receives the next protection, transfer, service, or institutional reform, use the rule in `archive/016-distribution-sufficiency-priority-and-anti-oligarchy.md`: repair floor breaches first, give extra weight to equivalent gains for the worse-off, and resist concentrations that turn advantage into arbitrary power.

### 4. Audit preference formation before calling a choice good

Where judgments depend on consent, demand, satisfaction, or uptake, also use `archive/018-preference-formation-adaptive-desire-and-manipulation.md`. Treat first-person reports as serious evidence, but do not let deprivation, humiliation, addictive design, manipulative steering, or attention-capturing default environments masquerade as clean endorsement. Where those affected are children, also use `archive/027-childhood-development-and-just-guardianship.md`, and where the environment itself is tuned to interruption or compulsive continuation also use `archive/035-attention-cognitive-liberty-and-anti-capture.md`, so protection, school discipline, family discretion, developmental guidance, and engagement-maximizing design are not mistaken either for pure domination or for cleanly autonomous choice.

### 5. Enlarge plural flourishing

Once the floor is secured, distributional shape is being governed, and preference has been checked for captivity or manipulation, the aim is not uniformity but widening the set of good lives people can actually live, including lives rich in care, maintenance, companionship, witness, devotion, beauty, play, learning, craft, festivity, non-instrumental activity, disciplined aspiration where desired, decent contribution, and enough breathable time to inhabit those goods rather than merely admire them from an exhausted distance. `archive/079-meaning-ordinary-worth-and-non-heroic-flourishing.md` sharpens the rule that meaningful life should not be socially monopolized by prestige, heroism, sacrificial vocation, or public visibility. `archive/078-public-reason-plural-flourishing-and-non-orthodox-common-life.md` sharpens the public-reason test on this layer: public institutions may widen access to these goods without claiming authority to impose one final doctrine of the best life. `archive/086-enoughness-anti-maximization-and-room-for-unoptimized-life.md` adds that this widening should not mutate into total-life performance pressure, metric-backed self-upgrade, or the demand that every good become answerable to maximization.

### 6. Preserve contestability, participatory say, and bounded market rule

Because weights and boundaries remain disputable, the synthesis must remain publicly criticizable. It should also avoid treating contestation as enough when those affected are granted only complaint rights after design. Where stakes are high and rule is durable, build meaningful channels of participation and uptake as part of the good itself, while preserving confidentiality, selective disclosure, non-performative space for those asked to appear before institutions, and freedom from tone-gated standing in which help or remedy depends on displaying the right emotional style to power. It should also protect public office against bribery, patronage, nepotism, unmanaged conflicts of interest, opaque influence, procurement extraction, and other forms of capture that make public rule answer privately rather than publicly. It should also resist approval creep: do not let coordination, safety, or administrative convenience turn harmless ordinary action into a standing request for discretionary leave where general rules, facilitative notice, and ex post accountability would suffice. It should also resist unusable notice: do not let rights, remedies, coverage, hearings, or ordinary compliance depend on cryptic warnings, generic denials, buried deadlines, or action paths intelligible only to insiders. It should also resist black-hole intake: do not let appeals, renewals, evidence, accommodations, or other high-stakes submissions disappear inside reference-less forms, payload-blind uploads, or disputed receipt states. It should also resist cliff-edge procedural loss: do not let curable defects, missing items, modest mismatches, or repairable incompleteness automatically convert timely engagement into restart-from-zero, forfeiture, or serious loss where targeted correction could still preserve the rule’s purpose. It should also resist review-after-collapse: do not let serious homes, services, placements, benefits, or anti-retaliation protections fail first and get reviewed only later when a stay, continuation rule, or narrower bridge could have preserved the point of the first challenge. It should also resist paper victory: do not let favorable hearings, disputes, corrections, or reviews stop at symbolic vindication while records remain wrong elsewhere, pay or benefits remain unrestored, effective dates are lost, or access still depends on the person hand-carrying their own win through a second maze. It should also resist pay-to-prove or pay-to-be-heard: do not let core records, correction routes, first-step hearings, or other floor-adjacent procedural paths become usable only after people purchase the copies, replacements, filings, or fallback channels that make standing actionable. `archive/080-special-obligations-legitimate-partiality-and-anti-nepotism-boundaries.md` sharpens the needed line here: relationship-grounded partiality is real in personal life and care, but entrusted decisions over common goods should not flow through kinship, friendship, or sponsor access. Also resist the slide from market existence to sale justification: `archive/024-non-fungibility-bounded-markets-and-protected-spheres.md` clarifies when markets are acceptable tools, when they require strong bounds, and when they misdescribe the good entirely.

### 7. Build repair-and-return paths after breach

When wrong has been done, debt has been incurred, or trust has been fractured, the aim should not default to permanent exile. Use `archive/021-repair-accountability-forgiveness-and-second-chances.md` to distinguish accountability from humiliation, forgiveness from reconciliation, and protective consequence from endless civic death.

### 8. Handle collisions with a remainder protocol

When protected kernels collide, the synthesis should not collapse into a hidden aggregate score. It should instead use the conflict protocol in `archive/013-kernel-collisions-triage-tragic-remainder-and-repair.md`: attempt shielding first, resist irreversible floor breaches, and carry forward repair duties when tragic loss cannot be avoided.

### 9. Act under uncertainty with reversibility and precaution in view

When stakes are serious but evidence, scope, or value ranking remains unsettled, the synthesis should also use `archive/017-uncertainty-precaution-reversibility-and-burden-shifting.md`: do not let uncertainty excuse grave avoidable harm, prefer reversible and option-preserving pathways where possible, and scrutinize who is being asked to bear uncertain downside.

## Advantages

- It explains why some harms feel non-compensable or only tragically compensable.
- It leaves room for objective goods without requiring a fully ranked master list.
- It honors agency without ignoring welfare.
- It connects intimate, social, institutional, and ecological dimensions.
- It gives governance a usable structure without surrendering to one index.
- It prevents the archive from naming protected goods while staying agnostic about who keeps getting them.
- It handles deep uncertainty without pretending that only full certainty can justify protection.
- It treats fallibility and wrongdoing as conditions a good order must metabolize through repair and reviewable return rather than only through exclusion.
- It resists both preference fetishism and casual paternal override.
- It keeps a good order from collapsing into expertly managed benevolence by making room for participatory co-authorship, not only contestability after the fact.
- It keeps the archive from degenerating into a survival-and-compliance ethic that forgets what protected life is for.
- It keeps nonhuman suffering visible without collapsing persons, animals, and ecosystems into one undifferentiated score.
- It makes transition integrity visible so discharge, referral, release, and transfer are not misclassified as successful while the next step is still dead, ownerless, or survivable only through private stitch-work.
- It makes temporal integrity visible so a life with nominal liberties but permanent exhaustion, volatility, or availability does not get misclassified as good enough.
- It makes dwelling integrity visible so a life with nominal shelter but chronic eviction threat, serial displacement, or shelter warehousing does not get misclassified as protected enough.
- It makes lived safety visible so a life with formal liberty but chronic assault risk, coercive threat, dangerous routes, or fear-shaped withdrawal does not get misclassified as secure enough.
- It makes maintenance visible so a life with nominal services, rights, or infrastructure but chronic outage, decay, brittle single-point failure, or forced workaround does not get misclassified as adequately protected.
- It makes response design visible so a life with nominal rights or services but one-mistake sanctions, fee spirals, technical-violation punishment, or false-positive lockout does not get misclassified as adequately protected.
- It makes scale-fit visible so a life with formal participation or service provision but extracted local knowledge, jurisdictional blur, map-blind uniformity, or unfunded devolution does not get misclassified as genuinely shared rule.
- It makes sensory refuge visible so a life with nominal housing, care, schooling, or public access but chronic glare, noise, and overstimulation does not get misclassified as adequately inhabitable.
- It makes portable autonomy visible so a life with formal rights, services, or admission but no secure custody of documents, medicines, devices, or assistive tools does not get misclassified as practically agency-bearing.
- It makes status security visible so a life with nominal shelter, work, care, or legal recognition but endless renewal, pendingness, or sponsor-conditioned durability does not get misclassified as stably inhabitable.
- It makes presumptive permission visible so a life with formal liberties but permit choke points, sponsor signoffs, broker dependence, or discretionary preclearance for ordinary harmless action does not get misclassified as genuinely free.

## Risks

- The kernels might be too coarse.
- The priority order might be culturally biased.
- Borderline cases may still demand controversial comparison.
- Collisions among kernels can tempt actors back into covert scalar tradeoffs.
- “Plurality” can become evasive if not linked to protected floors.
- Floor protection can be hollowed out if distributional burdens are repeatedly dumped on the already burdened.
- Preference-formation audit can slide into condescension if outsiders assume too quickly that unfamiliar choices are damaged choices.
- Repair-and-return language can be abused as a cheap rhetorical shortcut if institutions skip victim protection, truthful acknowledgment, or real change.

## Guardrails

- Do not let the kernel list expand until it becomes merely a restatement of everything good; use the admission protocol in `archive/014-kernel-admission-common-good-scaffolding-and-value-gates.md`.
- Do not let kernel talk conceal hard disputes about order and exceptions.
- Do not let protected floors be invoked selectively for the already powerful.
- Do not let reported demand or engagement override the question of whether the choice architecture itself was fit for freedom.
- Do not let participation be reduced to ceremonial consultation or service satisfaction while real authorship stays elsewhere.
- Do not let safety, convenience, or data hunger dissolve ordinary zones of sanctuary, confidentiality, and selective self-disclosure.
- Do not let ability to pay masquerade as a sufficient public reason for selling access to standing, care, bodily integrity, culture, or core civic goods.
- Do not let formally granted rights conceal that people lack the time, predictability, or recovery needed to use them.
- Do not let formal indoor status conceal that people lack the tenure security, privacy, neighborhood continuity, anti-displacement protection, or basic food, water, sanitation, and household-energy floor needed to actually dwell.
- Do not let accountability harden into permanent civic banishment when protection, truth, and reparative aims no longer require it.

## Compression rule for the archive

Protect the conditions without which lives are maimed or ruled, then widen the space in which many different good lives can genuinely be lived.
