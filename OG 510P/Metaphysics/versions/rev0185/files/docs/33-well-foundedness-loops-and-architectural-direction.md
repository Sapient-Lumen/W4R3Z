# Well-Foundedness, Loops, and Architectural Direction

## Why this file exists

The archive now has files on dependence, levels, supervenience, causation, teleology, relations, and process. But it still lacked a direct rule for **architectural direction**.

That omission matters because the archive already says all of the following:

- dependence is plural,
- some order of priority is real,
- higher-level realities can be derivative but objective,
- living and social systems often involve feedback,
- and some structures may be holistic without being illusory.

Without a dedicated rule, the archive risks two opposite mistakes:

1. treating **all loops as vicious**, so every feedback-rich system is forced into a crude one-way ladder,
2. or treating **all hierarchy as optional**, so grounding, fundamentality, and architectural direction lose their bite.

This file exists to prevent both mistakes.

## Minimal distinction

For any difficult case, distinguish at least five architectural possibilities.

### 1. Strict hierarchy

A target is fully dependent on what is below it, and the relevant order is asymmetric and terminating.

Use this when the case really does require a one-way order of metaphysical priority.

### 2. Bounded infinite descent

A target belongs to an infinite descending chain, yet the chain is still anchored by a lower-bounding source, basis, or foundation.

Use this when infinite articulation does not eliminate architectural direction.

### 3. Unbounded descent

A target belongs to a chain that never bottoms out and has no lower-bounding source.

Treat this as a live rival, but not as the archive default.

### 4. Local reciprocal organization inside a wider order

Some domain contains reciprocal constraint, co-adjustment, or feedback among components or levels, while the wider metaphysical picture still preserves typed direction somewhere.

This is often the right profile for organisms, mechanisms, institutions, and control systems.

### 5. Symmetric or coherent dependence

A domain is modeled as mutually dependent, looped, or holistic in a way that weakens or rejects a purely hierarchical picture.

Treat this as a serious option only when a case cannot be modeled adequately by typed asymmetries plus reciprocal causal or organizational feedback.

## Archive default

The archive should now adopt the following architectural default:

> **Prefer directional metaphysical architecture, but do not assume that every loop is vicious.**

More explicitly:

- treat **grounding** as directional by default,
- allow that not every acceptable architecture must be **set-theoretically well-founded**,
- keep open the possibility of **bounded or otherwise anchored infinite descent**,
- distinguish **metaphysical dependence loops** from **causal, organizational, or regulatory feedback loops**,
- and allow **local reciprocal organization** without thereby abandoning all priority structure.

The archive should therefore resist both:

- **flat coherentist inflation**, where all being becomes mutual support with no architectural contrast left,
- and **ladder simplification**, where every real reciprocal system is falsely redescribed as a simple one-direction chain.

## What counts as a vicious loop here?

A loop is architecturally vicious when it destroys rather than clarifies ontological direction.

Use the following test.

A purported circle is suspect when:

- the same exact explanatory job is being passed around without gain,
- no level, node, or structure supplies a genuine basis, constraint, or limiting condition,
- the circle makes priority talk empty,
- or the proposal cannot distinguish metaphysical dependence from causal or functional feedback.

A circle is less suspect when:

- reciprocal organization is local and typed,
- the circle is embedded within a wider asymmetric or otherwise anchored order,
- the relata are doing different jobs,
- or the case is better modeled as mutual constraint, stabilization, regulation, or co-constitution rather than as brute symmetric grounding.

## Working policy for the archive

### 1. Do not infer anti-foundationalism from feedback

Many real systems contain feedback, regulation, and reciprocal adjustment. That alone does not show that all metaphysical priority is illusory.

### 2. Do not infer vicious regress from infinite articulation alone

An infinite descending chain can still be acceptable if the case remains anchored, bounded, or otherwise fully accounted for.

### 3. Separate metaphysical architecture from explanatory circuitry

A mechanism can be causally cyclic, functionally self-maintaining, or operationally reciprocal without thereby exhibiting symmetric grounding.

### 4. Prefer local architectural diagnoses

Ask whether the loop is:

- causal,
- constitutive,
- realizational,
- grounding-like,
- explanatory,
- regulatory,
- semantic,
- institutional,
- or mixed.

Do not treat “circular” as one flat verdict.

### 5. Keep fundamentality and architecture distinct but linked

A case can reject strong set-theoretic well-foundedness without rejecting all fundamentality. Likewise, a case can preserve some foundation while allowing local reciprocal organization.

## Architectural profile questions

Future revisions should now ask, for any hard case:

1. Is the relevant order **strictly hierarchical**, **bounded but infinite**, **unbounded**, **locally reciprocal**, or **fully coherentist**?
2. What is the relevant relation: **grounding, constitution, realization, causation, regulation, supervenience, or mixed**?
3. Does the case preserve any **lower bound, anchor, source, or complete basis**?
4. Is the loop **metaphysical**, or only **causal/organizational/functional**?
5. If a circular model is proposed, what explanatory gain does it achieve that a typed asymmetric model cannot?
6. What breaks if the archive insists on a simple one-way ladder here?
7. What breaks if the archive abandons all architectural direction here?

## Compression payoff

This file should reduce slippage across at least six existing archive zones:

- **dependence relations**,
- **basal options**,
- **laws, powers, and structure**,
- **teleology and organization**,
- **levels and cross-level explanation**,
- and **supervenience / realization / grounding**.

In short: the archive should now ask not only **what depends on what**, but also **what kind of architecture the dependence belongs to**.
