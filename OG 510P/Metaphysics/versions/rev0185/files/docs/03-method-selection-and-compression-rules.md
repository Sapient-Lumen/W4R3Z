# Method, Selection, and Compression Rules

This archive must stay small enough to remain usable.

## 1. Prefer operators over surveys

New material should primarily add one of the following:

- an ontological distinction,
- an explanatory operator,
- a discriminating test,
- a compression of existing doctrine,
- or a decisive objection.

Avoid broad historical exposition unless it sharpens one of those.

## 2. Treat metaphysical vocabularies as tools with costs

Every major term should earn its place by doing work:

- **substance** should explain endurance,
- **form** should explain organization,
- **grounding** should explain constitutive dependence,
- **power** should explain modality and production,
- **process** should explain becoming,
- **structure** should explain relational constitution,
- **emergence** should explain higher-order novelty without magic,
- **substance** should explain persisting bearerhood without forcing every real thing into one object-model.

If a term does no differentiating work, compress or delete it.

## 3. Keep explanation plural

Do not collapse:

- grounding into causation,
- constitution into grounding by default,
- realization into identity by default,
- supervenience into grounding or reduction by default,
- form into shape,
- powers into mere summaries of regularity,
- or emergence into ignorance.

## 3A. Use the narrowest adequate dependence relation

Before writing “grounded”, ask whether the case is better handled first as:

- composition,
- constitution,
- realization,
- grounding,
- or causation.

The archive should treat generic grounding-talk as a last step of unification, not a first step of analysis.

## 3B. Identify the entity-kind before the persistence claim

Before asking whether something survives change, say what kind of thing it is supposed to be: organism, artifact, institution, event, process, role, person, office, or something else.

Do not force one persistence template onto unlike categories.

## 3C. Specify the modality before using it

Before writing that something **must**, **could**, or **cannot** be the case, ask whether the modality is:

- logical,
- epistemic,
- nomic,
- metaphysical,
- practical,
- or normative.

Then ask whether the modal status is grounded in:

- essence,
- powers,
- structure,
- laws,
- institutions,
- or something else.

Do not let conceivability, world-semantics, and metaphysical possibility collapse into one another.


## 3D. Fix the ontological category before importing its logic

Before arguing about identity, persistence, truthmakers, reduction, or fundamentality, ask whether the target is chiefly:

- an object / individual,
- an event / process,
- a property / power,
- a relation / structure,
- a fact / state of affairs,
- or a kind / form / role.

Do not ask object-questions of facts, fact-questions of powers, or event-questions of institutions unless you are clear that a cross-category translation is intended and justified.

## 3E. Fix the part-whole and boundary profile before inflating or deflating ontology

Before treating a candidate as one serious thing, state:

- what the relevant parts are,
- whether the case is a mere sum, an integrated whole, a constituted individual, or a bounded system,
- what kind of boundary matters,
- and whether part turnover defeats persistence in that domain.

This blocks two recurring archive errors: turning arbitrary fusions into full objects, and dissolving organized wholes merely because their boundaries are vague or permeable.

## 3F. Fix the causal profile before sliding between production and dependence

Before calling something a cause, specify whether the case is chiefly about:

- token production,
- sustaining or enabling,
- prevention or inhibition,
- variable-level influence under intervention,
- omission or blocked support,
- or some merely apparent causal vocabulary masking grounding, constitution, or realization.

Otherwise the archive will treat all explanation as causal, or all causation as counterfactual bookkeeping.

## 3G. Fix the temporal profile before turning time-talk into ontology

Before claiming that a case shows something important about time, specify whether the work is being done by:

- temporal order or succession,
- metric or duration,
- persistence across change,
- tense or presentness,
- passage or becoming,
- asymmetry or irreversibility,
- or merely temporal perspective, practice, or indexical location.

Otherwise the archive will keep confusing endurance/perdurance disputes with presentism/eternalism disputes, and both with the reality of change itself.

## 3H. Fix the spatial profile before turning occupancy into ontology

Before claiming that a case shows something important about space or place, specify whether the work is being done by:

- exact location,
- weak or entire location,
- region or site,
- locale or position in a structure,
- overlap or co-location,
- boundary or operational reach,
- or merely practical or representational placement.

Then ask whether the target is best understood as:

- an object occupying a region,
- a process distributed through a region,
- a field-like profile,
- a network or institution with mixed physical and normative siting,
- or some layered combination.

Otherwise the archive will keep confusing where something is with what it is, and confusing co-location with identity, constitution, or explanatory priority.

## 3I. Fix the teleological profile before turning usefulness into function

Before calling something functional, purposive, goal-directed, or teleological, specify whether the case is chiefly about:

- selected-effect function,
- organizational self-maintenance or control,
- design purpose,
- agential end,
- institutional role-end,
- or a merely useful consequence.

Then state what counts as success, failure, dysfunction, misuse, or corruption in the case.

Otherwise the archive will keep confusing efficient causation with final explanation, and useful outcomes with genuine function.

## 3J. Fix the abstracta profile before turning formal discourse into ontology

Before inferring a metaphysical verdict from mathematics, logic, semantics, or idealized modeling, specify whether the case is chiefly about:

- mathematical objects,
- propositions or content,
- formal structures,
- idealized model-entities,
- or some mixed abstractum.

Then state whether the best profile is:

- basic and independent,
- derivative but robust,
- immanent structural,
- practice-dependent but objective,
- or merely representational.

Otherwise the archive will keep confusing objectivity with independence, and formal usefulness with ontological seriousness.

## 3K. Fix the potentiality profile before turning “can” into ontology

Before inferring a metaphysical verdict from powers, development, readiness, manifestation, or fulfillment, specify whether the case is chiefly about:

- mere modal space,
- a standing disposition or power,
- developmental potency,
- organized readiness or first actuality,
- ongoing actualization,
- achieved manifestation or fulfillment,
- or blocked, frustrated, or corrupted actualization.

Then state:

- what the bearer is,
- what enables or disables the capacity,
- whether the case is passive, active, developmental, agential, institutional, or mixed,
- and whether the actuality in question is a current state of readiness, an occurrent exercise, or a completed end-state.

Otherwise the archive will keep confusing bare possibility with real power, and occurrent activity with fulfillment.

## 3L. Fix the relation profile before turning structure into ontology

Before inferring a metaphysical verdict from order, connection, constitution, adjacency, network position, or holistic dependence, specify whether the case is chiefly about:

- an internal relation,
- an external relation,
- a thin comparative or ordering relation,
- a thick constitutive or productive relation,
- a symmetric, asymmetric, ordered, or directed relation,
- a locative, causal, semantic, normative, or institutional relation,
- or a mixed relational profile.

Then state:

- what the relata are,
- whether the relation helps constitute the relata or only links already-settled relata,
- whether the case earns only local structural dependence or a stronger holistic thesis,
- and whether the relation is being confused with grounding, causation, composition, or mere classification.

Otherwise the archive will keep using “structure” and “holism” as aura-words rather than as disciplined metaphysical claims.


## 3M. Fix the levels profile before turning “higher-level” into ontology

Before inferring a metaphysical verdict from talk of higher and lower levels, specify whether the case is chiefly about:

- metaphysical fundamentality,
- explanatory level,
- a local mechanistic level,
- scale or resolution,
- intertheoretic reduction,
- emergence,
- cross-level constraint or top-down organization,
- or a mixed profile.

Then state:

- what the target phenomenon is,
- whether the levels are local to that phenomenon or supposed to be global,
- whether the relation is constitutive, realizational, causal, mathematical/representational, or mixed,
- and whether the case earns autonomy, derivativeness, or both.

Otherwise the archive will keep confusing ontological depth with explanatory grain and local stratification with a universal layer-cake.

## 3N. Fix the indeterminacy profile before turning borderline cases into ontology

Before inferring a metaphysical verdict from language like **vague**, **borderline**, **indeterminate**, **unclear whether**, **open whether**, or **many equally good candidates**, specify whether the case is chiefly about:

- semantic vagueness,
- epistemic limitation,
- representational or model-relative coarseness,
- metaphysical indeterminacy,
- or a mixed profile.

Then state:

- what exactly is indeterminate,
- what the target domain is,
- what larger metaphysical issue is being pressured,
- whether the case supports only local indeterminacy or a stronger global thesis,
- and what explanatory loss follows if the case is forced to be perfectly sharp or if the target is eliminated.

Otherwise the archive will keep confusing loose language, coarse modeling, ignorance, and genuine worldly unsettledness.

## 3O. Fix the substance profile before turning objecthood into ontology

Before inferring a metaphysical verdict from talk of substances, bearers, subjects, thin particulars, bundles, or “what underlies the properties”, specify whether the case is chiefly about:

- a persisting bearer or subject of change,
- a hylomorphic or form-structured compound,
- a bundle-like unity of tropes, properties, or roles,
- a bare particular or substratum,
- a process-stabilized individual,
- or only an analogical extension of substance language.

Then state:

- what category the target belongs to,
- what makes it one,
- what changes it can survive,
- whether it is basic, derivative, or only domain-relative as a substance,
- and what explanatory loss follows if the target is treated as only a bundle, only a substratum, or only a process with no bearer.

Otherwise the archive will keep confusing real individuals with all objects whatsoever, and classical substance-talk with every form of organized persistence.

## 3P. Fix the naturalness profile before turning successful vocabulary into ontology

Before inferring a metaphysical verdict from language like **natural**, **joint-carving**, **elite**, **projectible**, **structurally privileged**, **better variable**, or **more objective kind**, specify whether the case is chiefly about:

- fundamentality,
- comparative naturalness,
- explanatory usefulness,
- structural privilege,
- projectibility,
- or ontological commitment.

Then state:

- what category the target belongs to,
- what is doing the selectivity work,
- what the rival classifications are,
- whether the case supports one privileged carve or several disciplined local carves,
- and what explanatory loss follows if the target is treated as merely convenient.

Otherwise the archive will keep confusing usefulness with elite structure, and joint-carving with one final vocabulary.

## 3Q. Fix the life profile before turning organized chemistry into ontology

Before inferring a metaphysical verdict from language like **life**, **living**, **organism**, **biological individual**, **alive**, **dead**, **self-maintaining**, **metabolic**, **developmental**, **autonomous system**, or **vital organization**, specify whether the case is chiefly about:

- a cell or multicellular organism,
- a colony or symbiotic consortium,
- a lineage-stage or dormant stage,
- a metabolic or developmental unit,
- an adaptive or agentially loaded living system,
- or only an analogical or heuristic extension of organism-language.

Then state:

- what kind of unity condition is doing the work,
- what boundary profile the case has,
- what counts as self-maintenance, repair, or developmental continuation,
- whether the teleology is organizational, etiological, developmental, or mixed,
- and what explanatory loss follows if the case is treated as merely mechanical aggregate, merely ecosystemic, or already a full agent or person.

Otherwise the archive will keep sliding from organized chemistry to organism, from organism to agency, and from life to personhood as if one term settled them all.

## 4. Demand reality-tests

A metaphysical posit is stronger when it improves our handling of at least one of these:

- composition,
- unity and counting,
- boundary discipline,
- persistence,
- laws,
- modality,
- life,
- consciousness,
- social institutions,
- mathematics,
- normativity,
- or temporal passage,
- or causal production and intervention-sensitive difference-making,
- or temporal order, asymmetry, and passage,
- or spatial location, co-location, region, and distributed presence,
- or existence, ontological commitment, and ontological seriousness across disputed domains,
- or teleology, function, malfunction, and directed organization across organisms, artifacts, agents, and institutions,
- or abstracta, propositions, mathematics, and formal structures,
- or potentiality, readiness, actualization, frustration, and fulfillment,
- or relation, order, direction, and typed holism,
- or levels, scales, emergence, realization, and cross-level explanation,
- or vagueness, indeterminacy, borderline existence, and the problem of the many,
- or naturalness, projectibility, elite structure, and non-arbitrary classification across levels and domains,
- or socially instituted reality, normative standing, organizational persistence, and dependent-yet-robust institutions,
- or life, organismality, dormancy, regeneration, symbiosis, and the unity conditions of living beings,
- or consciousness, subjecthood, access, report, phenomenality, and mental causation pressure,
- or powers, dispositions, abilities, manifestation, and domain-typed modal capacities.

## 6. Separate three statuses

Every major claim should be classed as one of:

- **default** — currently favored,
- **live option** — serious but not favored,
- **open question** — unresolved.

## 6A. Separate existence, fundamentality, and seriousness

Do not treat the following as interchangeable:

- what exists,
- what is fundamental,
- what a theory is committed to,
- what is merely a useful representation,
- and what is thinly but still genuinely real.

For disputed cases, prefer a six-step profile:

- category,
- dependence,
- truthmaker,
- modal role,
- explanatory loss under elimination,
- and final status verdict.

This avoids both inflation (“the discourse is useful, so a heavyweight entity exists”) and deflation (“the discourse can be paraphrased, so nothing real is there”).

## 7. Prefer constrained synthesis to doctrinal purity

The archive is allowed to combine traditions, but only when the joints are explicit. Synthetic metaphysics without named joints becomes decorative.

## 8. Preserve adversarial pressure

The archive should keep nearby rivals alive enough to stop self-flattery. Reductive physicalism, austere grounding theory, and object-skeptical process views should remain active opponents.

## 9. Apply a subtraction test

Whenever a new concept is proposed, ask: **what explanatory loss follows if we remove it?**

- If no clear loss appears, cut it.
- If the loss can be absorbed by an existing operator, merge it.
- If the loss is distinctive and recurrent, retain it.

## 10. Prefer matrices over manifestos

When possible, record a theory by stating:

- what it explains,
- what it presupposes,
- what breaks without it,
- and what rival it improves on.

This compresses better than discursive proclamation.

## 11. Add only one ambiguity-resolver at a time

When possible, prefer files that remove a recurrent ambiguity for the whole archive. A small map of dependence relations or a compact scorecard for basal options is worth more than a long generic essay.

## 12. Compression target

Whenever possible, future revisions should aim to make the archive **smaller and sharper**, not merely larger. A good addition should either sharpen a core operator, reduce slippage among dependence relations, improve modal or category discipline, clarify part-whole structure, clarify a domain's causal profile, clarify a domain's temporal profile, clarify a domain's spatial profile, clarify a domain's ontological-commitment profile, clarify a domain's ontological-status profile, clarify a domain's teleological profile, clarify a domain's abstracta profile, clarify a domain's potentiality profile, clarify a domain's relation profile, clarify a domain's levels profile, clarify a domain's indeterminacy profile, clarify a domain's naturalness profile, clarify a domain's grain profile, clarify a domain's supervenience profile, clarify a domain's architectural profile, clarify a domain's social-ontology profile, clarify a domain's consciousness profile, clarify a domain's power profile, clarify a domain's fact profile, clarify a domain's truth profile, clarify a domain's status-robustness profile, or clarify a domain's objectivity profile, or clarify a domain's convergence/triangulation profile.


## New rule: identity-profile discipline

When a proposal uses language like **same individual**, **this very entity**, **haecceity**, **individuation**, **indiscernibility**, or **transworld identity**, it must state whether the issue is numerical identity, counting, persistence, reidentification, or modal de re reference, and what is doing the individuating work.

Good revisions should say whether the case turns on kind, form, matter, place, relation, organization, causal history, structural position, or primitive thisness.

If a proposal treats all identity-talk as one flat issue, it is under-compressed.

## New rule: naturalness-profile discipline

When a proposal uses language like **natural property**, **joint-carving kind**, **elite variable**, **structurally privileged distinction**, or **better classification**, it must state whether the issue is fundamentality, comparative naturalness, explanatory usefulness, projectibility, structural privilege, or ontological commitment, and what is doing the selectivity work.

Good revisions should say what the rival classifications are, whether the case supports one privileged carve or several disciplined local carves, and what explanatory loss follows if the target is treated as merely convenient.

If a proposal treats all successful vocabulary as equally reality-revealing, it is under-compressed.


## 3QA. Fix the grounding/fundamentality profile before using depth-language as a verdict

Before inferring a metaphysical verdict from language like **fundamental**, **basic**, **ground-level**, **ultimate**, **in virtue of**, **metaphysically explains**, **depends on**, **more real**, or **nothing over and above**, specify whether the case is chiefly about:

If a proposal treats all depth-language as one flat relation, it is under-compressed.

## 3Q. Fix the grain profile before turning greater specificity into ontology

Before inferring a metaphysical verdict from language like **more specific**, **fine-grained**, **coarse-grained**, **broad capacity**, **threshold**, **determinable**, **determinate**, or **proportionate explanation**, specify whether the case is chiefly about:

- determinable/determinate order,

- threshold structure,

- or ontological depth.

Then state:

- what the rival grains are,
- whether the broader determinable is doing real modal or explanatory work,
- whether the narrower determinate is essential, accidental, or overfitted,
- whether the dispute is really about level, scale, or merely resolution,
- and what breaks if the case is described at the wrong grain.

Otherwise the archive will keep mistaking finer description for deeper reality, and structured determinables for mere underdescription.

## 3R. Fix the supervenience profile before turning no-difference into full explanation

- what stronger relation, if any, explains why the supervenience pattern holds,
- whether the target remains explanatory, causally relevant, lawlike, or teleologically structured,
- and what is lost if the target is treated as merely a shadow of its base.

Otherwise the archive will keep confusing modal covariance with realization, grounding, identity, reduction, or ontological insignificance.

A manifest, YAML file, schema, script, checklist, or dashboard may improve package hygiene and findability; it does not by itself settle metaphysical truth, source currency, public reliance, independent review, domain authority, or operational monitoring. If the validation scope cannot be stated, downgrade the claim to manual local check, prose-only record, or open validation debt.

## Release Workflow / Runbook Compression Rules

Use `162-release-workflow-runbooks-execution-traces-and-handoff-governance.md` when the question is not merely whether an artifact validates, but whether the release process that produced it is repeatable, evidenced, and successor-readable.

### Compress to workflow governance when

- a package claims to be release-ready, handoff-ready, workflow-controlled, runbook-executed, repeatably produced, or locally process-validated;
- a validation script passed, but it is unclear whether it ran after final edits or after fresh extraction;
- a maintainer can describe the final package but not the stage order that produced it;
- a fork, derivative, or recovered package imitates final files but lacks execution trace, handoff limits, or workflow debt;
- a release note claims clean process, but skipped stages, waivers, or exceptions are not visible.

### Do not compress to workflow governance when

- the issue is conceptual truth or source accuracy; use review/source protocols instead;
- the issue is file continuity or manifest integrity only; use `161` and the validation script;
- the issue is public use or reliance permission; use `158`;
- the issue is who owes duties after public or derivative use; use `159`;
- the issue is where duties are remembered over time; use `160`.

### Neighboring-rival test

Workflow is not validation, provenance, review, release gating, or stewardship. Validation asks what checks passed. Provenance asks where the artifact came from. Review asks what authority inspected it. Release gating asks whether it should become a package. Stewardship asks who carries duties. Workflow asks whether the ordered process tying these together was executed, evidenced, and handed off.

### Minimal method result

A workflow judgment should state: runbook, workflow status, stage order, entry and exit criteria, evidence artifacts, validation point, manifest/package point, fresh-extraction result, skipped or waived stages, exceptions, handoff limits, allowed wording, forbidden wording, open workflow debt, and next trigger.

## Rev0160 method rule: incident response after deployment-boundary review

When an archive-derived artifact has been used, nearly used, or plausibly relied on for action, do not treat the next event as merely reception feedback or ordinary errata. First ask whether the event is an incident under `166`: harmful action, near miss, unauthorized escalation, warning failure, evidence loss, rollback failure, derivative misuse, public reliance failure, automation/tool boundary failure, semantic-fidelity failure with action consequence, or domain-sensitive reliance event.

Compression rule: do not compress incident review to "fixed" or "not fixed." Preserve at least five fields in any short report: **incident status, harm/severity class, recovery class, evidence preserved, and residual risk**. For public, derivative, automated, or domain-sensitive cases, also preserve affected scope, notice path, rollback/withdrawal path, external review requirement, and successor-memory trigger.

Promotion rule: an event should promote a doctrinal or governance revision only after the archive distinguishes user misunderstanding, warning failure, source-boundary failure, status bleaching, unauthorized deployment, defective public reliance language, automation permission breach, semantic-fidelity loss, and genuine counterexample pressure. Incidents can reveal theoretical defects, but incident pressure is not automatically metaphysical defeat.

## Rev0161 method rule: learning closure after incident recovery

When an incident, near miss, warning failure, public-reliance failure, deployment-boundary failure, derivative misuse, validation misclaim, or recovery dispute has been handled under `166`, do not compress the result to "fixed." First ask whether the event has generated a learning obligation.

Compression rule: do not compress post-incident learning to a generic "lesson learned." Preserve at least six fields in any short report: **learning status, root-cause profile, recurrence-risk class, corrective action, preventive action, and verification evidence**. If those fields are missing, say "recovered but not yet learned" rather than "closed."

Promotion rule: a post-incident learning packet should promote a method revision only after distinguishing isolated execution error, wording/warning design failure, compression failure, status/terminology failure, propagation/register failure, deployment/public-reliance failure, automation/workflow failure, source/domain failure, and systemic governance design failure. The archive should not turn a one-off typo into doctrine, but it should also not hide a systemic pattern behind a local patch.

Verification rule: a preventive action is not verified merely because the archive states it. Verification must identify an inspection check, regression case, semantic-fidelity check, workflow gate, deployment boundary, public/derivative correction, or external domain review as appropriate.


## Rev0162 method rule: durable prevention requires monitoring and sunset discipline

Do not compress a learned control into ordinary archive background merely because it passed one verification step.

Use `168-longitudinal-monitoring-effectiveness-review-sunset-and-residual-risk-governance.md` when a control, warning, schema field, validator check, deployment boundary, public-reliance limit, source-dependent claim, derivative restriction, release gate, or post-incident preventive action needs to remain available across releases.

### Promote to effectiveness-monitoring governance when

- a post-incident learning packet creates an ongoing preventive action;
- a warning must survive future summaries, teaching outputs, generated answers, or derivative artifacts;
- a validator or runbook check was added because of a known drift pattern;
- a deployment boundary or public-use restriction may be relaxed, inherited, or forgotten;
- a source-dependent rule requires refresh before continued use;
- a fork, migration, derivative, or public citation could change residual risk;
- a control might justifiably sunset but only after evidence;
- maintainers need to distinguish "exists", "ran", "worked once", "works repeatedly", and "independently/domain-confirmed".

### Compress away from effectiveness-monitoring governance when

- the item is purely historical and no longer guides current work;
- the risk is trivial, local, and already handled by a stronger validator or release gate;
- the claim makes no durability, monitoring, effectiveness, retirement, or future-use assertion;
- the matter is a one-off wording repair with no recurrence pattern;
- the proposed monitoring item would pretend to watch public, derivative, source, legal, clinical, safety, financial, employment, or institutional domains the archive does not actually monitor.

### Required fields

Any nontrivial monitoring packet should state:

1. target item,
2. origin layer,
3. monitoring status,
4. evidence source,
5. effectiveness class,
6. residual-risk trend,
7. sunset or renewal class,
8. review trigger,
9. owner or declined responsibility,
10. escalation route,
11. allowed wording,
12. forbidden wording,
13. open monitoring debt.

### Compression warning

A single validation pass is not evidence of durable effectiveness. A local register is not active public monitoring. A trigger in prose is not a maintained watch queue. A sunset claim is not safe unless the archive can state what evidence justifies retirement and where the old control remains historically visible.

## Method rule: cross-case risk portfolios

After `168`, method selection should add one more check: do not evaluate residual risks only one at a time when several records, warnings, dependencies, derivative uses, source-refresh needs, deployment boundaries, incident patterns, monitoring duties, validation checks, or stewardship obligations are active together.

Use `169-cross-case-risk-portfolio-systemic-exposure-and-prioritization-governance.md` when a revision, public-use packet, derivative family, source-dependent cluster, deployment-boundary group, incident pattern, validation harness, or monitoring set could create cumulative exposure. The minimum compression rule is:

> **State PF/EXP/COR/PRI classes before claiming portfolio manageability.**

A compressed output may say "local portfolio review only" or "portfolio not assessed." It should not say "low risk," "fully managed," "safe to defer," "publicly monitored," "independently audited," or "domain certified" unless the supporting packet actually warrants those claims.

## Method rule: capacity after portfolio priority

After `169`, method selection should add one more check: do not treat priority as capacity. A risk can be correctly ranked, portfolio-mapped, and release-relevant while still being unowned, unscheduled, under-resourced, blocked, unsafe to defer, or outside archive authority.

Use `170-capacity-planning-resource-allocation-backlog-and-work-in-progress-governance.md` when a source-refresh debt, validation gap, monitoring trigger, public-use dispute, derivative request, deployment-boundary review, incident lesson, CAPA verification, release debt, stewardship duty, or risk-portfolio priority requires actual work.

The minimum compression rule is:

> **State CAP/BL/WIP/DEF classes before claiming that priority work is assigned, scheduled, manageable, monitored, safe to defer, or handled.**

A compressed output may say "priority identified but capacity not assigned," "local backlog only," "blocked for lack of domain authority," or "release with declared capacity debt." It should not say "assigned," "scheduled," "actively maintained," "monitored," "covered," or "handled" unless the supporting packet actually states resource basis, owner or declined responsibility, next action, WIP limit, stop condition, and allowed/forbidden claim language.
