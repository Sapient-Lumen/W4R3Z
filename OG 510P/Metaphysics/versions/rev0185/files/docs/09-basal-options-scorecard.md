# Basal Options Scorecard

This file gives the archive a compact way to compare candidate pictures of the most basic level of reality.

## Why this file exists

The archive has already said that reality is layered and that higher levels can be real without being fundamental. But it remained too open about **what the base is like**. This file keeps that openness, while making it usable.

## Evaluation criteria

A good basal picture should do reasonably well on all or most of the following.

1. **Dynamism** — can it accommodate change, maintenance, and temporal becoming?
2. **Individuation** — can it explain what makes a thing one thing?
3. **Modality** — can it explain powers, tendencies, or laws without smuggling them in?
4. **Structure** — can relations and organization be genuinely constitutive?
5. **Local objecthood** — can ordinary and medium-scale entities remain real?
6. **Higher-level reality** — can life, mind, and social domains be real without inflation?
7. **Mind-integration** — can consciousness fit without panic?
8. **Parsimony with adequacy** — is the picture lean without becoming empty?

## Candidate options

### 1. Substance-first pluralism

**Picture:** the world is fundamentally made of enduring individual things bearing properties.

**Pros:**
- strong on individuation,
- strong on local objecthood,
- intuitive bearer structure.

**Cons:**
- weaker on dynamism,
- often treats structure and process as secondary,
- must work harder to explain emergence and material turnover.

**Archive status:** live option, but not favored.

### 2. Process-first pluralism

**Picture:** reality is fundamentally made of events, activities, or ongoing processes; objects are stabilized processual organizations.

**Pros:**
- excellent on dynamism,
- good on life and maintenance,
- strong fit with layered becoming.

**Cons:**
- needs a stronger account of individuation,
- risks making objecthood too derivative,
- can become rhetorically fluid if form is underplayed.

**Archive status:** major ingredient and leading basal candidate.

### 3. Power-first ontology

**Picture:** what is basic is not inert stuff but power-bearing being, where capacities and tendencies are metaphysically central.

**Pros:**
- strong on modality,
- helps with laws,
- resists Humean flattening.

**Cons:**
- needs pairing with form and structure,
- can become obscure about bearers,
- may explain too much by “further powers”.

**Archive status:** major ingredient and leading basal candidate.

### 4. Structural baseline

**Picture:** relations and organization are basic enough that objects are derivative from structure.

**Pros:**
- strong on constitutive relations,
- strong on systemic explanation,
- good contact with physics-friendly structural views.

**Cons:**
- risks underdescribing concrete individuals,
- can become overly abstract,
- needs a story about powers and bearerhood.

**Archive status:** indispensable corrective, but not sufficient on its own.

### 5. Priority monism

**Picture:** the whole cosmos is the one basic concrete entity; parts are derivative.

**Pros:**
- excellent on global structure,
- elegant account of part-whole priority,
- can preserve local reality as derivative.

**Cons:**
- can weaken medium-scale objecthood,
- may overprivilege the global scale,
- needs a clearer path from cosmic priority to local powers and forms.

**Archive status:** serious live base-option.

### 6. Neutral monism / dual-aspect baseline

**Picture:** what is fundamental is neither straightforwardly physical nor mental, but some neutral or aspect-bearing basis from which both derive.

**Pros:**
- strong on mind-integration,
- resists crude reduction and dualism,
- keeps the base from being parochially physical.

**Cons:**
- often too schematic,
- weak on concrete individuation,
- needs more structure about laws, forms, and levels.

**Archive status:** serious live base-option, especially for consciousness pressure.

## Current ranking by archive fit

### Tier A: best current fit as ingredients of the default view

- process-first pluralism
- power-first ontology
- hylomorphic/form-based organization
- structure as constitutive where needed

### Tier B: serious candidate ways of cashing out the basal layer

- priority monism
- neutral monism / dual-aspect baseline
- substance-first pluralism

## Current basal hunch in one sentence

The archive's present lean is:

> **the basic layer is more plausibly structured powers-in-process than a stock of inert self-sufficient particles.**

This is still not a final doctrine. It is a disciplined provisional result from the scorecard.

## Why this hunch currently wins

It best preserves all of the following at once:

- becoming without illusion,
- local entities without naïve atomism,
- modality without Humean thinness,
- organization without spooky forms,
- and higher-level reality without severing dependence.

## What would overturn it

The archive should revise this hunch if one of the following happens:

1. a substance-first picture handles turnover, organization, and modality equally well with fewer primitives,
2. a monist view explains local entities and local powers more cleanly than layered pluralism,
3. a neutral baseline integrates consciousness while retaining strong accounts of laws, form, and dependence,
4. or the distinction between powers, process, and structure collapses into a cleaner primitive.
