- when the archive says that some property, kind, variable, or level is “better”, “more objective”, “elite”, or “joint-carving”, is the real issue fundamentality, comparative naturalness, structural privilege, projectibility, or merely pragmatic usefulness?
- when the archive keeps more than one domain-typed classification alive, are those multiple non-arbitrary carves of reality or just convenience classes with inflated metaphysical rhetoric?
- when higher-level kinds or variables earn explanatory priority, what exactly is doing the selectivity work: law, power, form, structure, mechanism, teleology, institution, or mathematical role?
