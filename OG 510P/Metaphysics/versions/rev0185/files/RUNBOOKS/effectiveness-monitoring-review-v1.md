# Effectiveness Monitoring Review Runbook v1

## Purpose

Use this runbook when a local archive control, warning, deployment boundary, semantic-fidelity rule, validation check, automation boundary, public-use limit, incident response, post-incident learning packet, source-dependent claim, derivative restriction, release gate, or stewardship obligation needs longitudinal review.

This runbook supports local package hygiene and successor memory. It does not create public monitoring, live surveillance, automated source watching, affected-party notice, legal compliance, clinical governance, engineering safety assurance, financial suitability review, public incident management, or independent effectiveness certification.

## Entry criteria

Run this review when any of the following is true:

1. A `167` post-incident learning packet creates a corrective/preventive action.
2. A deployment-boundary or public-reliance record permits any nontrivial reuse.
3. A semantic-fidelity warning must survive summaries, diagrams, teaching uses, generated outputs, or derivative artifacts.
4. A validation check, runbook stage, or schema field was added because of a known drift or near miss.
5. A source-dependent rule may become stale.
6. A release, fork, migration, derivative, public citation, or operational request could change residual risk.
7. A prior monitoring packet reaches its trigger, renewal point, escalation condition, or sunset condition.

## Review steps

1. **Identify the target.** State the exact file, field, warning, rule, record, runbook, validator check, deployment boundary, public-use limit, source-dependent claim, or derivative restriction being reviewed.
2. **Name the origin layer.** State whether the obligation came from reception feedback, release gating, lineage, provenance, review warrant, public reliance, stewardship, continuity memory, validation, workflow, automation, semantic fidelity, deployment boundary, incident response, post-incident learning, or another layer.
3. **Assign monitoring status.** Use MON0–MON9 from `docs/168`.
4. **State the evidence basis.** Distinguish existence evidence, execution evidence, regression evidence, transmission evidence, deployment evidence, incident evidence, source-refresh evidence, and independent/domain evidence.
5. **Assign effectiveness class.** Use EFF0–EFF9. Do not upgrade inspection evidence into repeated-cycle, independent, or domain confirmation.
6. **Assess residual-risk trend.** Use RRT0–RRT8. State whether risk is decreasing, stable, transformed, migrated, increasing, unbounded, or misclassified.
7. **Assign sunset or renewal class.** Use SUN0–SUN9.
8. **Define the next trigger.** Prefer concrete triggers: next release, derivative export, source refresh, deployment request, public dispute, incident/near miss, fork/migration, schema change, or named successor review.
9. **Preserve claim limits.** Record allowed and forbidden claim language.
10. **Route escalation.** If the control failed or risk increased, route back to `166`, `167`, `147`, `154`, `161`, `164`, `165`, or the relevant upstream layer.
11. **Close or continue.** Close only with evidence. Otherwise record open monitoring debt and successor-memory destination.

## Exit criteria

A monitoring review is complete only when the record states:

- monitoring ID;
- target item;
- origin layer;
- MON class;
- EFF class;
- RRT class;
- SUN class;
- evidence source;
- review trigger;
- owner or declined responsibility;
- allowed wording;
- forbidden wording;
- escalation route;
- closure/renewal evidence or open debt.

## Forbidden upgrades

Do not infer any of the following from this runbook alone:

- active public monitoring;
- automated source-watch operation;
- derivative ecosystem monitoring;
- affected-party notice;
- public incident handling;
- safety-case operation;
- legal, clinical, engineering, financial, employment, educational, or public-administration compliance;
- independent effectiveness audit;
- future prevention guarantee.

## Minimal local record

When a full register entry is too heavy, record:

- target;
- MON class;
- EFF class;
- RRT class;
- SUN class;
- next trigger;
- allowed claim;
- forbidden claim;
- open debt.
