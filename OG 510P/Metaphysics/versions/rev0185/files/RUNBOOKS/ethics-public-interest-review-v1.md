# Ethics/Public-Interest Review Runbook v1

1. Identify any new claim that invokes ethics, public interest, affected parties, fairness, bias, misuse, dual-use sensitivity, benefit, harm, public value, or safety.
2. Record the claim in the appropriate rev0179 surface: ethical impact, public-interest balancing, affected-party analysis, fairness/bias review, misuse-sensitive release, or benefit/harm.
3. Check that the row has local evidence artifacts and does not claim legal, institutional, democratic, safety, fairness-audit, or public-consultation authority.
4. Run `python3 tools/check_ethics_public_interest.py .`.
5. Regenerate query, fixture, gate, reference, status, schema, custody, and manifest reports before packaging.

This runbook is local archive discipline only. It is not research-ethics review, IRB approval, public consultation, legal compliance, social license, or safety certification.
