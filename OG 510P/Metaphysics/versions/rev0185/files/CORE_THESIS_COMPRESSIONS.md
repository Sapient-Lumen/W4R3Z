# Core thesis compressions — rev0181

These summaries are orientation aids, not replacements for the archive. They do not prove reader comprehension, philosophical completeness, or public suitability.

## 100-word orientation

Layered Process Realism treats reality as dynamic, structured, power-bearing, and layered. Being is not reduced to static objects, but it is also not dissolved into flux. Processes, powers, relations, forms, dependence, grounding, emergence, identity, and access all matter. Higher-level realities can be genuine without being fundamental. The archive’s governance stack exists to stop that thesis from being overstated as certification, compliance, deployment readiness, or public authority. The cube now records not only files and schemas, but debts, sources, claims, access barriers, concept relations, and discovery questions.

## 500-word working compression

Layered Process Realism is the archive’s default metaphysical synthesis. It says that being is processual without being formless, structured without being frozen, power-laden without turning modality into mere description, and layered without reducing higher-level realities to convenient talk. Objects, facts, events, processes, relations, kinds, powers, boundaries, social forms, and normative structures are not treated as one flat category. They are routed through operator families that ask what persists, what changes, what grounds what, what depends on what, what counts as the same, what is merely represented, what has causal or modal profile, and what is only a warning or analogy.

The archive is also a governance artifact. It contains claim ledgers, schemas, control steps, release gates, provenance surfaces, validation tools, query regression tests, fixture corpora, public-release warnings, accountability surfaces, privacy/security/legal/ethical/accessibility boundaries, and now expanded cube datasets. These controls do not certify the theory. They discipline the language around it. A local validation pass means that declared files, rows, fields, status tokens, and forbidden-claim boundaries are present and internally checked. It does not mean external audit, public deployment, legal compliance, accessibility conformance, reader comprehension, source currency, RDF publication, SHACL validation, or philosophical completeness.

The most important practical rule is rows before rhetoric. If the archive wants to claim a control, warrant, debt, source, concept, accessibility observation, or public-use boundary, that claim should be visible as a row, schema, source artifact, query surface, and forbidden-upgrade rule. Rev0181 turns that rule into a stronger data-cube discipline: debts, sources, claims, controls, access tests, concepts, and discovery questions are now separate observation datasets rather than prose hidden in a monolithic index.
