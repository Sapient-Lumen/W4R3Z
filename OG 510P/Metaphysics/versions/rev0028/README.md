# Metaphysics — rev0028

## How can a thought belong to the body and still explain an action?

Imagine someone deliberately putting down a painfully hot cup. We can ask about bodily processes, the felt pain, and the person's response. Are these rival accounts of what happened, different descriptions of one occurrence, or different things connected by a law? This edition makes that question explicit. It tries to preserve the reality and causal significance of mental life without picturing a separate pilot steering a body from outside.

The new [psychophysical-unity essay](files/Metaphysics-rev0028-2026.03.22.11.58-mindbodyhinge-embodimentbridge-mentalcausation-psychophysicalunity/archive/036-psychophysical-unity-embodiment-and-mental-causation.md) is the best entrance. Its proposal is that minded life is embodied, organized and physically realized, while mental explanation need not disappear into a description at another level. The point is not simply that minds and bodies are connected. It is to ask what kind of connection could make those commitments hold together.

[The wider Metaphysics guide](../../README.md) places this early synthesis beside the later process-oriented work. Here, follow one argument through the distinctions it needs rather than beginning with the whole taxonomy.

## A correlation does not choose the relation

The [dependence map](files/Metaphysics-rev0028-2026.03.22.11.58-mindbodyhinge-embodimentbridge-mentalcausation-psychophysicalunity/archive/013-dependence-maps-building-relations-and-explanatory-discipline.md) distinguishes several questions often hidden inside “depends on.” Earlier kiln events can cause a statue's fragility, while its present structure is what that fragility obtains in virtue of. Production over time and present constitutive explanation are different jobs.

Applied to the mind-body problem, the distinctions matter:

- **Correlation** records that mental and bodily events vary together. It does not by itself decide what either event is.
- **Identity** would say that the apparent two are one occurrence, not merely reliably paired occurrences.
- **Realization** asks how a capacity or state is implemented by an underlying organization, potentially in different ways.
- **Supervenience** constrains differences: no difference in the higher property without some difference in its base. It does not yet supply the explanation of that constraint.

Grounding, constitution and emergence remain further possibilities to distinguish, not interchangeable labels for whatever connection has been observed. The work's useful demand is to name the relation being defended and the evidence or argument that could support it.

## One occurrence need not mean one kind of explanation

The psychophysical essay separates **token unity** from **type reduction**. Even if a particular mental episode is numerically the same concrete occurrence as a bodily episode, that would not alone show that every mental kind maps neatly onto a physical kind. Conversely, resisting a reduction of kinds does not by itself require two detached substances.

That opens room for a layered explanation of action. An intention or pain may matter to what a being does without supplying a second independent push alongside its bodily realization. Embodiment then does more than provide a vehicle: perception, movement, vulnerability, memory and orientation help constitute the kind of minded life being explained.

This is the proposal's central ambition, and also its hardest test. Saying that two descriptions are true does not yet show that the mental description is causally explanatory rather than a redundant gloss. If the bodily account already seems sufficient, what work does the mental explanation do, and why does recognizing that work not double-count the cause? The essay makes this obligation explicit; its layered vocabulary is a way to pursue the problem, not a completed solution merely because both descriptions have been retained.

## A subject, an agent and a person are not one question

Read the preceding [persons and selves essay](files/Metaphysics-rev0028-2026.03.22.11.58-mindbodyhinge-embodimentbridge-mentalcausation-psychophysicalunity/archive/035-persons-selves-and-practical-historical-unity.md) next. It distinguishes biological unity, a center of experience, reasons-guided action, historically extended personhood, and the first-personal or integrative dimension called selfhood.

The distinction lets several questions remain open at once. Is this the same being over time? What can it presently do? Can it answer for an action? What standing does it have? A change in current competence does not automatically settle the others. The essay treats infancy, dementia, coma and coercion as pressure cases requiring finer distinctions, not as invitations to erase persons.

It also separates minimal selfhood from autobiography. In its account, a first-personal character of experience need not wait for a fluent life story; conversely, a story or legal recognition is not sufficient on its own to constitute the richer unity the essay proposes. This is a philosophical account to examine, not an operational test for awarding or withdrawing anyone's rights, nor a finding about the experience of a particular artificial system.

## Read the preferred theory against its rivals

The [theory portfolio](files/Metaphysics-rev0028-2026.03.22.11.58-mindbodyhinge-embodimentbridge-mentalcausation-psychophysicalunity/archive/008-theory-portfolio-and-adjudication-matrix.md) calls its current leader **articulated layered realism**: dependent and organized beings can be real without all being fundamental in the same sense. It retains neo-Aristotelian, monist, process and strongly naturalistic alternatives as pressures on that synthesis.

The comparison should cut both ways. A reductionist need not deny that pain exists merely by seeking a physical explanation, and an account of mental autonomy owes more than an insistence that something important would otherwise be lost. The psychophysical essay itself keeps identity theories, non-reductive physicalisms, emergentisms, anomalous monisms, hylomorphic views and selective dualisms in play. Ask which candidate best explains embodiment and causal efficacy, and what would actually count against the preferred account.
