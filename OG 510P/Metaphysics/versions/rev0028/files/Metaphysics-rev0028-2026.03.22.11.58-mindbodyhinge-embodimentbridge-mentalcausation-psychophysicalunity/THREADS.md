# Threads

Threaded view for rev0028.

## abstracta

- `016-abstract-objects-mathematical-structure-and-nonspatiotemporal-being.md` — A salient non-reductive metaphysics should treat **abstracta and non-spatiotemporal being** with disciplined seriousness: neither eliminative panic nor cost-free platonist inflation is adequate; the better stance is selective realism in which propositions, mathematical structures, and some other abstract candidates earn their place by explanatory role, structural discipline, and ontological fit.

## agency

- `022-agency-action-freedom-and-reasons-responsive-control.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should treat agency as a real mode of organized power: actions are not mere bodily happenings, free action is not bare randomness, and agential control is best understood through authorship, reasons-responsiveness, and vulnerable self-governance rather than through either passive mechanism or ungrounded will-magic.

## bearerhood

- `033-concrete-individuals-bearerhood-and-bundle-pressure.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should treat many concrete individuals as **real organized bearers or thick particulars** rather than as either mere bundles of properties or featureless bare substrata: property-instantiation needs subjects or unities, but those unities should be understood through structured bearerhood, form, history, and organization rather than through a mysterious propertyless hook.

## becoming

- `005-time-persistence-and-historical-being.md` — Metaphysics should treat temporality as load-bearing: reality is not fully intelligible unless it can explain change, persistence, succession, and historical constitution, and no single persistence model should be presumed to fit every kind of being.
- `024-potentiality-actuality-and-developmental-being.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should treat potentiality and actuality as real modes of being: capacities, readinesses, and developmental trajectories are not mere absences of actuality nor just possible-world bookkeeping, but structured ways beings can be poised toward activity, fulfillment, or change.

## being

- `014-existence-quantification-and-ontological-commitment.md` — A salient non-reductive metaphysics should distinguish **what exists**, **what a theory is committed to**, and **what is fundamental**: existence is broader than fundamentality, ontological commitment is a useful but fallible diagnostic, and quantificational form should not be treated as the sole sovereign guide to reality.

## categories

- `003-categories-of-being-substance-process-relation-structure.md` — A salient non-reductive metaphysics should be category-plural: reality is not adequately described as only substances, only processes, only relations, or only structures, but as an articulated world in which several ontological categories are jointly indispensable.

## classification

- `019-kinds-naturalness-and-world-jointed-classification.md` — A salient non-reductive metaphysics should treat **kinds as real but plural, level-sensitive joints in reality**: some classifications answer more directly to the world’s structure than others, yet genuine kindhood need not be monopolized by one fundamental inventory or restricted to timeless “natural” kinds alone.

## constitution

- `010-composition-constitution-and-formed-wholes.md` — A salient non-reductive metaphysics should sharply distinguish parthood, composition, constitution, and identity, while treating many ordinary and organized wholes as real formed beings rather than as either mere verbal conveniences or uncontrolled explosions of coincident entities.

## contingency

- `030-contingency-chance-and-open-futural-being.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should treat contingency, objective chance, and futural openness as **real modes of how reality can be settled or unsettled**: not every not-yet is mere ignorance, not every contingent truth is brute unintelligibility, and not every indeterminacy is an enemy of explanation.

## dependence

- `013-dependence-maps-building-relations-and-explanatory-discipline.md` — A salient non-reductive metaphysics should not speak of “dependence” as if it were one flat relation: grounding, ontological dependence, supervenience, realization, constitution, composition, and causation do different explanatory jobs, and the archive needs an explicit map of those jobs.

## depth

- `002-essence-grounding-and-typed-fundamentality.md` — The archive should treat essence, grounding, and fundamentality as the core explanatory triad of metaphysics, while refusing the assumption that there must be one single untyped deepest inventory from which every other aspect of reality is read off without residue.

## explanation

- `004-powers-laws-and-causation.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should treat causation, laws, and powers as a linked explanatory cluster: laws should not be mere brute regularities if stronger grounding is available, and causes should be understood in a way answerable both to worldly production and to the structured capacities of things.

## facts

- `015-facts-states-of-affairs-and-structured-truthmaking.md` — A salient non-reductive metaphysics should treat reality as **structured**, not as a mere list of objects: propositions are best treated as truth-bearers, facts or obtaining states of affairs as worldly truthmakers, and the archive should distinguish these roles without inflating every truth into a special entity by default.

## form

- `027-form-matter-and-organized-unity.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should treat **form and matter** as a real explanatory pair: matter is not self-explanatory mere stuff, form is not a detachable ghost, and concrete beings are often intelligible only as **organized unities** whose material basis and formal organization jointly matter.

## identity

- `012-identity-discernibility-sortals-and-thisness.md` — A salient non-reductive metaphysics should treat numerical identity as strict but not self-interpreting, pairing it with sortal and historical criteria of individuation, discernibility discipline, and only cautious appeal to primitive thisness.

## intentionality

- `026-intentionality-representation-and-aboutness.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should treat intentionality as a **real mode of directedness**: some states, acts, symbols, and institutions are about things, possibilities, absences, values, or situations, and this aboutness cannot be reduced either to bare causal covariance, formal syntax, or free social stipulation alone.

## intrinsicness

- `031-intrinsicness-duplication-and-local-being.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should treat **intrinsicness** as a real and useful distinction about what a thing is like in itself, while refusing two common overreaches: that the intrinsic is just the non-relational, and that all reality must be recoverable from a perfectly local intrinsic mosaic.

## levels

- `006-emergence-levels-and-non-eliminative-reality.md` — A salient non-reductive metaphysics should treat emergence as the joint presence of dependence and autonomy: higher-level realities are often grounded in lower-level conditions without being thereby explained away, and metaphysical seriousness requires criteria for when that autonomy is real rather than rhetorical.

## life

- `023-life-organisms-and-biological-individuality.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should treat life and organismic individuality as real modes of organized being: living beings are not mere heaps, machines, or socially projected conveniences, but historically lineaged, boundary-maintaining, self-organizing unities whose reality is compatible with dependence, symbiosis, and multi-level biological complexity.

## modality

- `009-modality-world-structure-and-truthmakers.md` — A salient non-skeptical metaphysics should treat modal structure as real but not automatically as a separate realm of objects: possibility and necessity are features of reality’s articulation, possible-worlds talk is often a modeling device rather than an ontological verdict, and truthmaker discipline helps keep modal claims answerable to being.

## negativity

- `025-absence-privation-and-negative-being.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should treat absences, privations, and some negative conditions as **real but derivative features of being**: holes, lacks, omissions, vacancies, and failures can be metaphysically and explanatorily serious without forcing a shadow ontology of one negative entity per sentence.

## nonexistence

- `028-nonexistent-objects-fictional-entities-and-merely-intentional-being.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should treat **aboutness as able to outrun existence** without therefore admitting one full-strength object for every target of thought, story, planning, or impossibility: fictional entities may be real in a derivative and anchored way, but merely intentional, merely possible, and impossible "objects" should not be imported into ontology by default.

## occurrents

- `017-events-processes-and-occurrent-ontology.md` — A salient non-reductive metaphysics should adopt **disciplined occurrent realism**: events and processes are real concrete occurrents, not mere shadows of object-talk; but they must be distinguished from facts and propositions, and individuated with enough discipline to avoid redescription explosion or process imperialism.

## personhood

- `035-persons-selves-and-practical-historical-unity.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should distinguish **organisms, subjects, agents, persons, and selves** while treating persons as embodied, historically extended, socially addressable centers of first-personal and agential life rather than as mere biological units, momentary experiencers, or detached ghost-egos.

## plurality

- `034-pluralities-collections-sets-and-aggregate-being.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should distinguish **pluralities, aggregates, sets, and organized unities**: many things together are not automatically one further thing, abstract sethood is not the same as concrete togetherness, and some collective beings earn reality through organization, practice, structure, or action rather than through sheer numerousness alone.

## portfolio

- `008-theory-portfolio-and-adjudication-matrix.md` — The archive should organize metaphysics as a disciplined portfolio rather than a single prematurely crowned doctrine, while treating a layered, category-plural, essence-and-grounding-centered realism as the current leading synthesis under active pressure from monist, process, and strongly naturalistic rivals.

## psychophysical

- `036-psychophysical-unity-embodiment-and-mental-causation.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should treat **mental life as embodied, psychophysically realized, and genuinely causally efficacious** without either reducing mindedness to a residue-free physical description or splitting mind and body into two externally glued substances.

## qualities

- `011-properties-universals-tropes-and-individuation.md` — A salient non-reductive metaphysics should treat properties and relations as real features of being, and should prefer a disciplined middle posture on which repeatable qualitative natures and particularized instances both do explanatory work in accounting for similarity, causation, and individuation.

## social

- `007-social-and-normative-reality-without-fictionalism.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should treat social and normative realities as genuinely real but non-fundamental: institutions, statuses, obligations, permissions, and collective structures are constituted through practices, recognitional structures, and histories, yet they are not thereby mere fictions.

## space

- `018-space-place-location-and-boundaries.md` — A salient non-reductive metaphysics should treat **spatiality as real but not automatically fundamental**: place, location, extension, region, and boundary are load-bearing for concrete being, yet space should be handled through a disciplined middle posture between empty-container substantivalism, flat relationism, and easy spatial eliminativism.

## stance

- `001-metaphysics-after-skepticism-and-reduction.md` — A serious metaphysics should begin from the claim that reality is knowable enough to be articulated, richer than any one reductive inventory, and structured enough that dependence, modality, time, and social reality can be studied as real features of the world rather than as mere shadows of language or physics.

## subjectivity

- `021-consciousness-subjectivity-and-first-person-being.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should treat consciousness and subjectivity as real modes of being: experience is not eliminable into third-person description, but neither should irreducibility force us into cost-free panpsychist inflation or a return to ghostly substance dualism.

## teleology

- `020-teleology-functions-and-real-directedness.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should treat teleology as a disciplined dimension of reality: some beings, structures, and activities are intelligible in terms of what they are for, but teleological explanation must be tied to form, organization, history, or agency rather than floated as a free-standing cosmic slogan.

## vagueness

- `032-vagueness-borderlines-and-indeterminate-being.md` — A non-reductive metaphysics should treat **vagueness and borderline status as real pressure points on ontology without assuming that every borderline case reveals a vague object**: some indeterminacy is semantic or representational, some may be worldly, and the archive should distinguish these instead of forcing either false precision or blur absolutism.

## worldhood

- `029-worldhood-totality-and-cosmic-unity.md` — A salient non-reductive metaphysics should treat **the world as a real maximal concrete whole and explanatory horizon** without either collapsing all plurality into one full-strength thing or dismissing world-level structure as mere façon de parler: cosmic unity is a live pressure, but derivative plurality remains real unless stronger argument overturns it.
