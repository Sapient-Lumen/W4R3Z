# Metaphysics — rev0028

Metaphysics is a compact research archive for a **non-skeptical, non-reductive** theory portfolio of reality.

## Current posture

The archive currently treats **articulated layered realism** as its leading synthesis:

- reality is real and metaphysical inquiry is legitimate;
- derivative being is still real being;
- essence, grounding, and fundamentality do explanatory work;
- categories of being are plural rather than flat;
- modality is real but not forced into one inflated worlds ontology;
- emergence, time, history, and social ontology are load-bearing;
- composition and constitution require their own discipline rather than being collapsed into identity;
- property theory and individuation need explicit treatment rather than being left implicit inside talk of essence, laws, and structure;
- identity is strict, but real identity judgments are often sortal-, form-, and history-sensitive rather than brute;
- dependence language is plural and disciplined rather than a blur of grounding, supervenience, realization, constitution, and causation;
- existence is broader than fundamentality, and ontological commitment is a diagnostic rather than a sovereign criterion;
- structured worldly unities matter: propositions, facts, states of affairs, and truthmakers should be distinguished rather than blurred into either object-lists or sentence-shadows;
- events and processes are real occurrents, not mere sentence-shadows or dispensable shadows of object-talk; but they require disciplined individuation and must be distinguished from facts and propositions;
- abstract and non-spatiotemporal candidates should be handled by selective realism and structural discipline rather than by concrete-only prejudice or cost-free platonist expansion;
- spatiality is real and explanatory for concrete beings, but place, location, extension, and boundary need not all be fundamental in the same way.
- kinds are real where classifications are world-anchored and explanatorily stable, but naturalness is plural and level-sensitive rather than monopolized by one basic inventory.
- teleology is real where directed organization is real: functions, ends, and purposive structures can be explanatory without displacing mechanism or inflating local finality into a cosmic master-purpose.
- consciousness and subjectivity are real modes of being: first-person presence should not be erased by third-person reduction, yet subjects should be treated as organized centers of experiential life rather than as automatic proof of ghostly substances or universal sentience.
- agency is a real mode of organized power: actions are not mere happenings, and agential control should be understood through authorship, reasons-responsiveness, and vulnerable self-governance rather than through either passive mechanism or will-magic.
- life and organismic individuality are real modes of organized being: living beings are materially open yet organizationally bounded, historically lineaged, teleologically structured, and not reducible either to mere mechanism or to vitalist extra force.
- potentiality and actuality are real modes of being: capacities, readinesses, developmental trajectories, and blocked powers should be treated as structured features of beings rather than as mere absences, snapshot deficits, or free-floating world-bookkeeping.
- absences and privations can be real in a derivative, anchored way: holes, omissions, vacancies, and lacks are often structured features of reality rather than mere verbal shadows, but they do not automatically force one negative entity per true negation.
- intentionality and representation are real modes of directedness: some subjects, signs, and institutions are genuinely about things, possibilities, absences, and values, but aboutness should not be reduced either to bare covariance, syntax, or free stipulation.
- form and matter are real explanatory aspects of many concrete beings: organized unities are not exhausted by part-lists alone, but form should be treated as realized organization rather than as a detachable ghost-component.
- concrete individuals are real organized bearers: many beings are best treated as thick, structured particulars rather than as either mere bundles of qualities or featureless bare substrata.
- pluralities are real without automatic object inflation: mere many-ness, aggregates, sets, and organized collectives must be distinguished rather than collapsed into one another.
- the world as a whole is metaphysically serious: the maximal concrete whole may matter explanatorily and may even be a candidate for priority, but cosmic unity should not erase derivative plurality by default.
- contingency, chance, and open futurity are real modes of how reality can be settled or unsettled: some truths could have been otherwise, some processes may be objectively chancy, and future openness need not collapse either into ignorance or into metaphysical chaos.
- intrinsicness is real but not sovereign: some features belong to beings in virtue of what they are in themselves, yet intrinsicness, locality, duplication, and separability must be kept distinct, and local intrinsic structure need not exhaust all reality.
- vagueness is metaphysically serious but not self-interpreting: borderline cases, higher-order vagueness, and indeterminacy pressure real theories of kinds, boundaries, composition, and ordinary objects, yet not every vague case licenses a vague object or an ontically blurry world.
- persons and selves are real historically extended modes of organized being: a person is not just an organism, subject, or agent taken singly, but an embodied, socially addressable center of first-personal and practical-historical unity.
- psychophysical unity is real and explanatory: minded life is embodied, causally efficacious, and answerable to physical realization without being flattened into residue-free reduction or split into externally glued substances.
