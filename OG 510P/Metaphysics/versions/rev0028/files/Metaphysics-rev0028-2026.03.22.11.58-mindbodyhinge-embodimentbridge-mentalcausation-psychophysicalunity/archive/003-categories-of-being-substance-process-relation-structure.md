# 003 — Categories of being: substance, process, relation, and structure

## One-line thesis

A salient non-reductive metaphysics should be category-plural: reality is not adequately described as only substances, only processes, only relations, or only structures, but as an articulated world in which several ontological categories are jointly indispensable.

## Why this matters

Many grand metaphysical systems become elegant by overcommitting to one category. Substance metaphysics captures persistence and identity but can underplay becoming. Process metaphysics captures change but can underplay stable bearers. Structural views capture relational order but can underplay concrete individuals. Monisms capture unity but can blur plurality.

The archive should resist the temptation to let one virtue become a monopoly.

## Pattern pack

### 1. Preserve substances where persistence and bearerhood matter

There are good reasons to treat some beings as substances or substance-like individuals: organisms, persons, artifacts, perhaps some larger natural kinds. Substance language remains useful wherever identity through change, bearerhood of properties, and bounded agency are central.

### 2. Preserve processes where becoming is primary

Many realities are better understood as happenings, developments, or ongoing activities than as static bearers with optional motion attached later. Weather systems, conversations, biological development, institutions in operation, and histories are process-involving through and through.

### 3. Preserve relations and structures where dependence is irreducibly networked

Some features of reality are best captured relationally. Spatial order, causal networks, legal statuses, kinship systems, markets, and scientific structures are not well modeled as simple bundles inside isolated individuals. Relations and structures are not mere decorative overlays.

### 4. Refuse one-category imperialism

The archive should not try to reduce all processes to disguised substances, all substances to temporary process-stabilizations, or all individuals to nodes in pure structure unless a theory can show that nothing explanatory is lost. Usually something is lost.

### 5. Use category pluralism to guide, not to bloat

Plural categories do not mean infinite ontology. The point is not to multiply entities recklessly, but to preserve the minimum categories needed to describe the world honestly. Parsimony matters, but explanatory adequacy matters more than rhetorical thinness.

### 6. Keep monism and process metaphysics as pressure tests

Priority monism and process philosophy should remain live rivals and correctives. The archive should learn from them:

- from monism: the world may have deeper unity than ordinary pluralism notices;
- from process philosophy: becoming and change may be more basic than substance-first metaphysics admits.

But neither should be granted total victory by default.

## Guardrails

- Do not treat category pluralism as permission for uncontrolled proliferation.
- Do not assume stable objects are metaphysically primitive in every case.
- Do not assume becoming is the only honest ontological language.
- Do not reduce relations to accidental add-ons.
- Do not let “structure” become a vague prestige word detached from clear explanatory roles.

## Failure modes

- **substance imperialism**: everything is treated as fundamentally thing-like.
- **process imperialism**: persistence and identity are treated as mere illusions or secondary conveniences.
- **relation erasure**: networks and structures are forced back into isolated individuals.
- **structural vapor**: concrete bearers disappear into empty pattern talk.
- **monist overreach**: unity is purchased at the cost of denying real plurality.

## Practical tests

1. Can the theory explain both stable individuals and real change?
2. Can it explain why some entities are best understood as processes rather than objects?
3. Can it treat relational and structural facts as real without eliminating concrete individuals?
4. Can it preserve ontological unity without denying derivative plurality?
5. Can it stay parsimonious without becoming explanatorily blind?

## Closing synthesis

The archive should adopt **category pluralism with discipline**: substances, processes, relations, and structures are all metaphysically serious, and no single category should be presumed capable of capturing the whole shape of being by itself.
