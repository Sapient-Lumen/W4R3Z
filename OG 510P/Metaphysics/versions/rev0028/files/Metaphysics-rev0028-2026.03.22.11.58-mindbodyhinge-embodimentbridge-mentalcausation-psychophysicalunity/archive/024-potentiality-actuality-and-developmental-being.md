# 024 — Potentiality, actuality, and developmental being

## One-line thesis

A non-reductive metaphysics should treat potentiality and actuality as real modes of being: capacities, readinesses, and developmental trajectories are not mere absences of actuality nor just possible-world bookkeeping, but structured ways beings can be poised toward activity, fulfillment, or change.

## Why this matters

The archive already relies on powers, teleology, time, life, and agency. But those notes keep presupposing something they do not yet state directly: many beings are intelligible not only by what they are actually doing now, but by what they can do, are ready to do, are developing toward, or are prevented from doing. Without an explicit note on **potentiality and actuality**, the archive risks treating becoming as either a disguised sequence of fully actual states or a merely semantic modal convenience.

The danger again runs both ways. One mistake treats only what is fully actual now as metaphysically serious, so capacities, readinesses, developmental tendencies, and blocked powers become shadows or shorthand. The opposite mistake reifies potentiality into a ghostly stock of half-beings floating free of concrete natures, forms, and organizations. The better path is a disciplined middle: **anchored potency realism**.

## Pattern pack

### 1. Distinguish immanent potentiality from bare possibility

Not every possibility is a genuine potentiality of a thing. A lump of bronze may possibly become many things, but only some possibilities are anchored in its nature, organization, or circumstances. A salient metaphysics should distinguish sheer logical or nomic possibility from a being’s own powers, susceptibilities, readinesses, and developmental openings.

### 2. Distinguish active, passive, and ready-to-exercise potentials

Potentiality is not one flat category. Some potentials are active powers to bring about change; some are passive capacities to undergo change; and some are standing readinesses whose exercise depends on opportunity rather than on acquiring a brand-new capacity. A good theory should not collapse having a capacity, being prepared to exercise it, and actually exercising it.

### 3. Treat actuality as more than instantaneous occupancy

Actuality is not just “whatever is presently the case” in a thin snapshot sense. Actualization can be a process, an activity, or the fulfillment of a form. Development, maturation, performance, and sustained exercise can all matter. The archive should therefore resist models in which actualization is always a punctual binary flip from not-yet to now.

### 4. Connect potentiality to form, powers, and teleology

Potentialities are most metaphysically serious where they are anchored in what a thing is, how it is organized, and what sorts of completion or activity belong to it. Seeds grow, organs function, agents act, and institutions authorize not because “anything might happen,” but because forms, powers, and directed organizations open determinate pathways of development and exercise.

### 5. Leave room for blocked, masked, and frustrated potentials

A potential can be real without being manifested. Fragility can be masked, a skill can go unexercised, a developmental trajectory can be thwarted, and an institution can have powers it is prevented from using. The archive should therefore not infer from non-manifestation to unreality.

### 6. Distinguish developmental being from mere future outcome

To say that an acorn is potentially an oak or that a novice musician can become a pianist is not merely to predict a later state. It is to say something about the present being itself: how it is structured, what it is poised for, and how change would count as its development, exercise, or corruption. Potentiality belongs to present being even when its fulfillment is future.

### 7. Relate potentiality to agency without reducing it to modal semantics

Abilities and agential powers are a crucial case of potentiality. But the openness of action should not be reduced too quickly either to abstract world-quantification or to brute inner willing. A non-reductive metaphysics should treat many agential potentials as organized, reasons-sensitive powers of subjects whose exercise depends on circumstances, self-governance, and obstacles.

## Guardrails

- Do not collapse potentiality into bare logical possibility.
- Do not treat every unactualized description as a real potency of the thing.
- Do not treat actuality as the only metaphysically serious mode of being.
- Do not reify potentials into free-floating ghost entities detached from concrete forms and powers.
- Do not infer from blocked or unexercised capacity to the nonexistence of the capacity.

## Failure modes

- **actuality chauvinism**: only fully manifested actuality is treated as real, so powers, abilities, and developmental tendencies become verbal conveniences.
- **possibility blur**: a thing’s own potentialities are collapsed into whatever is broadly possible in some world or model.
- **ghost potency inflation**: potentialities are treated as mysterious quasi-entities rather than as structured modes of concrete being.
- **binary-flip ontology**: actualization is modeled only as an instantaneous switch, erasing development, exercise, maturation, and activity.
- **frustration blindness**: masked, blocked, or thwarted capacities are dismissed because they fail to manifest.

## Practical tests

1. Is the alleged potentiality anchored in the thing’s nature, form, organization, or powers, or is it only an external possibility description?
2. Is this an active power, a passive capacity, a standing readiness, or a developmental tendency?
3. What would count as actualization here: a punctual event, an extended activity, a maturation process, or a fulfilled stable state?
4. Can the account explain masked, blocked, or frustrated potentials without either erasing them or multiplying occult entities?
5. How does this case connect to teleology, agency, life, or causal power without collapsing into one of them?

## Closing synthesis

The archive should treat potentiality as **structured readiness in being** and actuality as **fulfilled or exercising being**: real modes of reality that help explain growth, action, manifestation, learning, development, and blocked power without either flattening everything into present actuality or inflating possibility into a second shadow world.
