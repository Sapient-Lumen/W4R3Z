# 008 — Theory portfolio and adjudication matrix

## One-line thesis

The archive should organize metaphysics as a disciplined portfolio rather than a single prematurely crowned doctrine, while treating a layered, category-plural, essence-and-grounding-centered realism as the current leading synthesis under active pressure from monist, process, and strongly naturalistic rivals.

## Why this matters

Metaphysics becomes fragile when it confuses elegance with victory. The archive needs an explicit portfolio so that alternative systems remain visible, comparable, and pressure-tested instead of being erased by local enthusiasm.

## Pattern pack

### 1. Current leading synthesis: articulated layered realism

The archive’s present best synthesis can be named **articulated layered realism**.

Its core commitments are:

- realism about the world and metaphysical inquiry;
- category pluralism across substances, processes, relations, and structures;
- serious use of essence, grounding, and fundamentality;
- openness to typed rather than flat fundamentality;
- real emergence without eliminativism;
- temporal seriousness and historical constitution;
- modal structure anchored by essence, law, and disciplined world-modeling;
- social and normative reality as grounded but real;
- composition and constitution treated as distinct dependence problems;
- a layered property realism in which repeatable natures, particularized instances, and real relations all do explanatory work;
- strict identity together with domain-sensitive criteria of individuation, and a high price on primitive thisness;
- a plural dependence map in which grounding, supervenience, realization, constitution, composition, and causation are kept distinct enough to do different explanatory work;
- a layered meta-ontology in which existence, ontological commitment, ideology, and fundamentality are kept connected but non-identical;
- a structured-truthmaking posture in which propositions, facts, states of affairs, and truthmakers are kept distinct enough to explain how reality makes truths true without collapsing into either sentence-first metaphysics or one-fact-per-sentence inflation;
- a selective and structurally disciplined realism about abstracta, in which propositions, mathematical structures, and related non-spatiotemporal candidates are neither dismissed by concrete-only prejudice nor admitted by default;
- a disciplined occurrent realism in which events and processes are real concrete occurrents, are not simply identical with facts, and are individuated tightly enough to avoid redescription explosion;
- a disciplined realism about spatiality in which place, location, extension, and boundaries are metaphysically serious for concrete beings without forcing a crude container ontology or assuming spatiality is fundamental all the way down.
- a plural, world-anchored realism about kinds in which classificatory joints can be natural, historical, social, functional, or mixed, without collapsing all real kindhood into one fundamental inventory or treating every useful grouping as ontologically serious.
- a disciplined teleological realism in which ends, functions, and purposive structures can be real and explanatory where anchored in form, powers, history, agency, or organization, without opposing teleology to mechanism or inflating local directedness into a cosmic master-purpose.
- a disciplined realism about consciousness and subjectivity in which first-person being is real, conscious subjects are treated as integrated centers of experiential life, and personhood is not collapsed either into bare sentience or into pure social narrative.
- a disciplined realism about agency in which actions are genuine doings of organized subjects, reasons-explanations are metaphysically serious, and self-governing freedom is understood through sourcehood, authorship, and vulnerable control rather than by either passive mechanism or will-magic.
- a disciplined realism about life and organisms in which living beings are treated as self-maintaining, historically lineaged, materially open yet organizationally bounded unities, rather than as either mere machine-like aggregates or bearers of a mysterious vital surplus.
- a disciplined realism about potentiality and actuality in which capacities, readinesses, developmental tendencies, and blocked powers are treated as real modes of being anchored in form, organization, and causal role, rather than as either mere possible-world bookkeeping or ghostly half-entities.
- a disciplined realism about absence and privation in which holes, lacks, omissions, vacancies, and kind-relative deprivations are treated as structured derivative features of reality, rather than as either unreal wordplay or a second shadow inventory of negative entities.
- a disciplined realism about intentionality in which aboutness is a real and articulated feature of subjects, signs, and institutions, often embodied and socially scaffolded, without being reduced either to bare covariance, syntax, or stipulation.
- a disciplined hylomorphic realism in which form and matter are treated as explanatory aspects of many concrete beings, so organized unity, kindhood, and realization are not collapsed either into bare part-lists or into detachable ghost-components.;
- a disciplined asymmetry about nonexistents in which intentionality and discourse can outrun actuality, fictional entities may be derivatively real where socially and textually anchored, and merely possible or impossible "objects" are not admitted one-for-one by default.
- a disciplined realism about worldhood in which the maximal concrete whole is metaphysically serious and a live candidate for explanatory priority, while derivative plurality remains real unless stronger argument establishes an exclusive cosmic monism.
- a disciplined realism about intrinsicness in which some features belong to beings in virtue of what they are in themselves, while intrinsicness, locality, duplication, and separability are kept distinct and local intrinsic structure is not assumed to exhaust all reality by default.
- a structured-bearer realism in which many concrete individuals are treated as thick, organized bearers whose unity is not exhausted either by bare compresence among qualities or by a featureless substratum hiding underneath predication.
- a plurality-sensitive realism in which mere many-ness, aggregates, sets, and organized collectives are kept distinct, so plurality is not erased, singularity is not inflated by default, and abstract collection is not confused with concrete togetherness.
- a disciplined realism about persons and selves in which personhood is not collapsed into bare organismic life, momentary consciousness, or isolated agency, but treated as an embodied, historically extended, socially addressable mode of unity shaped by first-person presence, reflexive awareness, practical integration, and memory.
- a disciplined psychophysical realism in which minded life is embodied, causally efficacious, and physically realized, while identity, realization, emergence, and explanatory autonomy are kept distinct enough to avoid both residue-free reduction and external-pilot dualism.

This is not final doctrine. It is the current best organizer of the archive.

### 2. First rival: neo-Aristotelian metaphysics

Neo-Aristotelian metaphysics is not really an external enemy; it is the archive’s closest ally and one of its principal sources of discipline. Its strengths are essence, substance, grounding, hylomorphic and category-sensitive explanation, and resistance to flattening modality into syntax or convention.

Pressure point: it can still become too substance-first or too eager for a single explanatory hierarchy.

### 3. Second rival: priority monism

Priority monism is valuable because it tests whether the world as a whole is ontologically prior to its parts. It keeps questions of cosmic unity alive.

Pressure point: it risks obscuring the local integrity and explanatory standing of derivative plurality.

### 4. Third rival: process philosophy

Process metaphysics is valuable because it insists that becoming, change, and dynamic organization are not secondary embarrassments for a static ontology.

Pressure point: it can underdescribe persistence, bearerhood, and stable identity conditions.

### 5. Fourth rival: strong naturalistic reductionism

Strong reductionism has the virtue of parsimony and respect for empirical science.

Pressure point: it too often treats derivative, emergent, social, and normative entities as unreal merely because they are not fundamental in one favored sense.

### 6. Adjudication criteria

The archive should judge candidate theories by whether they can:

- distinguish existence, grounding, ontological dependence, and causation;
- preserve both stable identity and real change;
- explain modality without reducing it to syntax or inflating semantics into ontology by default;
- say when a case calls for grounding, when only supervenience has been shown, when realization is at issue, and when constitution or composition must do the work;
- handle emergence without mystification or elimination;
- account for composition and constitution without either eliminativism or coincidence explosion;
- explain qualitative recurrence, local instantiation, and individuation without collapsing properties into language or multiplying entities recklessly;
- distinguish strict identity from looser sameness relations, while explaining when sortal, historical, or formal criteria govern identity judgments and when brute thisness is actually warranted;
- account for social and normative reality without fictionalism;
- distinguish what exists from what is fundamental, and keep ontological commitment, ideological commitment, and truthmaker pressure from collapsing into one another;
- distinguish propositions, facts, and states of affairs well enough to explain structured truthmaking without either object-list metaphysics or reckless fact multiplication;
- handle abstract objects, mathematical structure, and other non-spatiotemporal candidates without either eliminative panic or cost-free platonist inflation;
- treat events, processes, actions, and other occurrents as real where needed, while distinguishing them from facts and avoiding both event erasure and indiscriminate event multiplication;
- handle space, place, location, and boundaries without either empty-container reification, spatial erasure, or reckless shadow-entity inflation;
- distinguish categories, kinds, sortals, and predicates, while explaining when classifications are genuinely world-anchored and when they are merely convenient, and without reducing all real kindhood to one favored level of description;
- distinguish agential, artifact, biological, and organizational teleology, while explaining when functions and ends are metaphysically serious and how teleological explanation relates to mechanistic explanation;
- distinguish consciousness, self-consciousness, subjecthood, and personhood, while explaining first-person givenness, experiential unity, and embodiment without either third-person erasure or inflationary overreaction;
- distinguish mere happenings, organized behavior, intentional action, free agency, and responsibility-adjacent answerability, while explaining sourcehood, reasons-explanation, self-governance, and the significance of manipulation, compulsion, and coercion without either agency erasure or mysterious willing;
- distinguish living beings from aggregates, artifacts, colonies, lineages, and merely complex machines, while explaining organismic individuality, self-maintenance, boundary-discipline, symbiosis, and developmental history without either organism erasure or vitalist inflation;
- distinguish immanent potentiality from bare possibility, while explaining capacities, readinesses, actualization, development, masking, and thwarted powers without either actuality chauvinism or ghost-potency inflation;
- distinguish sheer negation, structured absence, and genuine privation, while explaining holes, omissions, vacancies, negative truths, and blocked protections without either positivity chauvinism or shadow-entity inflation;
- distinguish causal tracking, indication, representation, reference, and full intentional directedness, while explaining error, empty or absent-directed content, practical intention, and collective intentionality without either magical inner pictures or flat syntax-only deflation;
- distinguish form, matter, structure, organization, and unity, while explaining when wholes are genuine beings rather than heaps and how material realization constrains formal explanation without either stuff fetishism or ghost-form inflation;
- distinguish fictional entities, merely possible objects, impossible objects, absences, and merely intentional targets, while explaining empty reference, story-truth, planning, and contradictory content without either unrestricted object inflation or hard discourse erasure;
- distinguish a mere plurality of things from a genuine maximal concrete whole, while handling totality conditions, existence monism, priority monism, and world-level explanation without either cosmic inflation or world-level erasure;
- distinguish contingency, objective chance, open futurity, and agential openness, while explaining probabilistic law, branching possibilities, future-directed truth, and reasons-responsive action without either deterministic closure or randomness rescue;
- distinguish intrinsicness, extrinsicality, locality, duplication, and separability, while explaining internal and external relations, positional features, and holistic or nonseparable structure without either mosaic absolutism or anti-intrinsic collapse;
- distinguish semantic vagueness, representational imprecision, and metaphysical indeterminacy, while explaining borderline cases, higher-order vagueness, composition pressure, and ordinary-object ontology without either forced precision or blur absolutism;
- distinguish concrete individuals, property-bearers, bundles, thick particulars, substrata, and processual nodes, while explaining unity, accidental change, compresence, and individuation without either hook-mystery or object-erasure;
- distinguish pluralities, aggregates, sets, and organized collective beings, while explaining when many things amount to one further entity, when plural reference should stay plural, and when member-identity diverges from collective identity without either many-to-one inflation or plurality erasure;
- distinguish organisms, subjects, agents, persons, and selves, while explaining embodiment, personal persistence, reflexive awareness, practical integration, social addressability, and compromised or developing personhood without either ghost-self inflation or reductive flattening;
- distinguish psychophysical identity, realization, emergence, and lawful co-variation, while explaining embodiment, mental causation, first-person experience, and scientific tractability without either epiphenomenal drift or external-pilot dualism;
- remain parsimonious without becoming explanatorily blind.

## Guardrails

- Do not crown a single system because it sounds grand.
- Do not keep rivals only as decorative names.
- Do not let portfolio thinking become indecision forever.
- Do not treat theoretical simplicity as the only virtue.
- Do not ignore whole domains merely because a favorite theory handles one domain elegantly.

## Failure modes

- **doctrinal capture**: one framework silently monopolizes the archive.
- **museum portfolio**: rivals are listed but never really compared.
- **criterion drift**: theories are judged by convenience rather than by stable questions.
- **parsimony theater**: explanatory losses are ignored because a view looks ontologically lean.
- **pluralist vagueness**: the leading synthesis becomes a mere compromise blob.

## Practical tests

1. Is the current leading synthesis stated clearly enough to be criticized?
2. Are rival theories kept in view for real reasons?
3. Are there stable criteria for comparing theories across domains?
4. Does the archive know where its current synthesis is strongest and weakest?
5. Can future revisions move notes between canon and pressure-testing surfaces without erasing the reasons?

## Closing synthesis

The archive should proceed as a **disciplined portfolio with a current leader**: for now that leader is articulated layered realism, but it remains answerable to neo-Aristotelian, monist, process, and reductionist pressure at every major metaphysical fault line.
