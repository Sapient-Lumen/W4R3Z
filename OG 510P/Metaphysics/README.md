# Metaphysics

## Being dependent does not mean being unreal

A storm depends on moving air; an organism persists through material turnover; a legal office depends on rules, records and practices. Metaphysics asks how such things can be genuinely real without all being fundamental in the same way. Its central refusal is to let explaining what something depends on become an excuse for explaining it away.

The work is also suspicious of the opposite shortcut. A useful description does not automatically identify a new kind of being. Calling something a process, an emergent whole or a social construction leaves the difficult questions open: what holds it together, what it can do, how it persists, and what would make the proposed explanation fail.

The two preserved snapshots develop that inquiry differently. The early **articulated layered realism** presents a broad philosophical portfolio. The later **Layered Process Realism** gives maintained organization a more central role, develops a five-part explanatory kernel and many concrete diagnostic distinctions, then builds machinery for tracking its claims and unresolved obligations. The [later basal scorecard](versions/rev0185/files/docs/09-basal-options-scorecard.md) treats process-first and power-first accounts as leading ingredients, while substance-first, monist and neutral or dual-aspect alternatives remain live. This is a change of emphasis and architecture, not evidence that the alternatives have been defeated. The philosophical work is much larger than the latest release's administrative front page suggests.

## A small vocabulary doing several different jobs

The later [kernel](versions/rev0185/files/docs/06-kernel-operators.md) uses five operators. These are organizing questions, not software functions or five independently proven substances:

- **Form:** what makes these constituents one thing of this kind rather than a heap?
- **Process:** what activity, development or maintenance lets it persist while changing?
- **Power:** what can it do or undergo, including capacities not currently exercised?
- **Structure:** when are relations constitutive of what the thing is?
- **Grounding:** in virtue of what does the relevant fact obtain?

Their proposed connection is intelligible. Organization can determine capacities; ongoing activity can sustain that organization; relations can constitute a role; a higher-level fact can depend on a basis without disappearing into a bare list of components. The theory calls many entities stabilized processual organizations: neither inert things with change added afterward nor a flux in which no individual survives.

But naming five jobs does not establish that five irreducible operators are needed. The [objections and replies](versions/rev0185/files/docs/11-objections-and-replies.md) explicitly consider whether the synthesis is an eclectic bundle, whether powers and structure double-count, whether process dissolves objects, and whether grounding merely labels what remains unexplained. A leaner rival that performs the same explanatory work would be a reason to demote the preferred theory. That possibility is part of the work, not an objection its reader must invent from outside.

## “Depends on” is not one relation

The early [dependence map](versions/rev0028/files/Metaphysics-rev0028-2026.03.22.11.58-mindbodyhinge-embodimentbridge-mentalcausation-psychophysicalunity/archive/013-dependence-maps-building-relations-and-explanatory-discipline.md) supplies a concrete distinction: earlier kiln events can cause a statue's fragility, while its present structure grounds that fragility. Production over time and the structure in virtue of which a property obtains are not the same explanation.

The map also distinguishes a capacity's implementation from strict identity, parts composing a whole from one thing constituting another, and supervenience from a full explanation. Saying that no difference of one kind occurs without a difference in its base constrains the possibilities; it does not yet explain why the higher fact obtains. The archive's point is not to multiply synonyms for dependence, but to stop one successful relation from being silently promoted into an answer to every question.

That distinction matters wherever a description changes levels. A role may be realized by different components. An institution may require material infrastructure without being identical to a particular building. A model may track a phenomenon without being its constituent. The reader is asked to specify which relation does the explanatory work before claiming a metaphysical result.

## Emergence must earn its meaning

The early [emergence essay](versions/rev0028/files/Metaphysics-rev0028-2026.03.22.11.58-mindbodyhinge-embodimentbridge-mentalcausation-psychophysicalunity/archive/006-emergence-levels-and-non-eliminative-reality.md) holds dependence and autonomy together. The later [admission test](versions/rev0185/files/docs/07-real-emergence-admission-test.md) makes the demand more explicit: identify the lower-level basis, the organization that matters, and a stable explanatory or modal profile that a mere parts-list fails to capture.

Its contrasting examples are revealing. A whirlpool is a real stabilized process but is usually assigned weak/systemic emergence. A living cell is proposed as at least real non-fundamental emergence because regulation and organization matter to its powers. A corporation or legal office is offered as a social-normative case grounded in recognition, rules and practices. Conscious experience remains open, potentially requiring a stronger emergence claim or a revised basal picture.

These are the archive's proposed classifications, not measurements established by the classification scheme. In particular, assigning a lower grade does not mean a whirlpool is unreal, and the word emergence is not a substitute for explaining consciousness. The test's value lies in exposing what must be defended. Its vulnerability lies in whether explanatory indispensability and a stable modal profile really support the ontological promotion claimed, rather than reflecting our preferred level of description. A lower-level account that includes relations and dynamics is a stronger comparator than a bare parts-list; reduction need not mean elimination. That is an important test of the archive's favored inference, not a reason to dismiss organization.

## Pause, replacement and copying are different tests of persistence

The later essays become especially useful when they force familiar words to carry less unexamined weight.

[Dormancy and standby](versions/rev0185/files/docs/79-dormancy-latency-standby-and-suspended-activity.md) test a process-oriented view against inactivity. A seed, sleeping organism, unexercised skill or standing office need not cease to exist when its characteristic performance stops. The question is what organization, powers, records or reactivation conditions remain. Yet keeping a record of a defunct institution is not the same as keeping that institution dormant. Inactivity does not prove nonexistence; a possible restart does not prove continued identity.

[Substitution](versions/rev0185/files/docs/124-substitution-replacement-stand-ins-and-functional-equivalence.md) then asks what is preserved when something else does the job. A component swap may sustain one machine; a substitute teacher occupies a role without becoming the absent teacher. Functional equivalence, continuity of an office and identity of its occupant answer different questions. Neither every replaced part nor every preserved service settles the whole's persistence.

[Duplication and branching](versions/rev0185/files/docs/87-duplication-copying-fission-and-branching-continuity.md) make the challenge sharper. A backup can preserve state; a software fork can preserve code and history; biological descent can preserve lineage. If several continuers coexist, those relations cannot simply be called one numerically identical bearer without further argument. The archive preserves the possibility of real continuity without forcing every case into either exact sameness or total discontinuity.

These distinctions provide a way to read recovery, migration and custody claims carefully. They do not establish that any particular restored program is a conscious subject or that every continuity relation has the same moral significance.

## Mind, purpose and institutions belong in the inquiry

The early [psychophysical essay](versions/rev0028/files/Metaphysics-rev0028-2026.03.22.11.58-mindbodyhinge-embodimentbridge-mentalcausation-psychophysicalunity/archive/036-psychophysical-unity-embodiment-and-mental-causation.md) tries to preserve embodied mental life without either a detached pilot inside a body or an explanatory reduction that leaves mental kinds doing no work. It distinguishes token-level unity from type-level reduction: one concrete episode might be describable both mentally and physically without every mental kind thereby reducing to a physical kind. That is a position to defend against causal-exclusion and rival accounts, not a solved mind-body problem.

The [persons and selves essay](versions/rev0028/files/Metaphysics-rev0028-2026.03.22.11.58-mindbodyhinge-embodimentbridge-mentalcausation-psychophysicalunity/archive/035-persons-selves-and-practical-historical-unity.md) likewise separates organism, subject, agent, person and self. Persistence, current competence and standing do not have to receive the same verdict. Its historically extended, embodied account gives memory, practical integration and social addressability real roles without making a biography or fluent self-description sufficient to constitute a person.

[Teleology](versions/rev0028/files/Metaphysics-rev0028-2026.03.22.11.58-mindbodyhinge-embodimentbridge-mentalcausation-psychophysicalunity/archive/020-teleology-functions-and-real-directedness.md) asks when explaining what something is for earns its place alongside explaining how it works. Designed artifacts, biological functions, represented aims and organizational roles have different bases. The text explicitly resists inferring a cosmic purpose from local directedness or equating successful functioning with moral goodness.

The later [normativity chapter](versions/rev0185/files/docs/44-normativity-reasons-and-deontic-structure.md) develops the corresponding distinction between a reason favoring an act, a final requirement, permission and authority. What causes someone to act is not automatically what justifies the action. A norm's reality is not the same question as its fundamentality, and institutional, epistemic, functional and moral evaluations should not be flattened into one use of “ought.”

## A method for challenging the preferred description

The [family map](versions/rev0185/files/docs/144-operator-family-map-and-triage-grid.md) and [adversarial protocol](versions/rev0185/files/docs/145-adversarial-stress-tests-and-revision-protocol.md) respond to a danger created by the archive's own abundance: with enough named distinctions, almost any case can be forced into a plausible-sounding category.

Their practical countermeasure is to vary what supposedly matters. Keep the same word while changing the underlying basis; keep the basis while changing the word. Preserve an office but change its occupant. Preserve a record while interrupting the relevant organization. Duplicate a basis and ask whether the identity verdict has become incoherent. Change a detector while holding its target fixed. A diagnosis should follow the relevant structure rather than the label or proxy.

This method is useful precisely because the preferred theory can fail it. Its insistence on a target, explanatory scale, nearest rivals and possible defeaters is more informative than simply declaring the theory pluralist. Whether the growing taxonomy consistently earns its complexity remains a serious question.

## What the later data machinery actually does

The [rev0185 DebtCube layer](versions/rev0185/files/docs/191-debt-cube-lifecycle-prioritization-remediation-audit-governance.md) records unresolved obligations as queryable observations: source artifact, category, priority, recurrence, lifecycle and the stronger claim that missing evidence blocks. That can help a later reader distinguish an open philosophical question from a missing source check, unassigned task or unsupported external-review claim.

The [generator's actual implementation](versions/rev0185/files/tools/generate_debt_observations.py) extracts designated open/debt fields from YAML, normalizes text, assigns categories through ordered keyword matches and records repeated wording. Its priority and lifecycle labels are therefore local rules, not independent assessments of philosophical importance or actual remediation. The [structural checker](versions/rev0185/files/tools/check_debt_cube_refactor.py) checks counts, required fields, identifiers and relation coverage, and rejects generated rows that claim closure. It does not decide whether the metaphysics is true.

The distinction cuts both ways. Administrative structure can preserve intellectual debts without paying them; philosophical incompleteness does not make a carefully preserved counterexample worthless. The work deserves to be read both for its proposed ontology and for the unresolved cost of maintaining such a large apparatus around it. Later organization is not automatically later wisdom.
