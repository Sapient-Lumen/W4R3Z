# Play now

You do not need to learn Lacuna to play with it.

Say:

```text
Will you DM?
```

Then speak and act normally. The DM should begin promptly, ask at most one setup question when a preference is genuinely missing, and keep files, schemas, checkpoints, and model routing backstage.

Your words describe dialogue, choices, requests, or attempts. They do not automatically prove that an attempted action succeeded; accepted narration establishes what happens.

## In an ordinary ChatGPT conversation

Play can begin immediately. When no connected Lacuna host exists, ChatGPT should say once:

> We can play immediately; this chat is not yet committed to a Lacuna cube.

That is only a persistence notice. It should not interrupt play or be repeated every turn.
