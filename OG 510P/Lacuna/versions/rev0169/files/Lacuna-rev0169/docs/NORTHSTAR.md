# North star

Lacuna exists so an adaptive world can remain flexible without becoming dishonest.

## Product sentence

**Lacuna is an epistemic ledger for worlds that have not finished becoming true.**

It should let a human or model distinguish what happened, what was observed, what was reported, what somebody believes, what a planner currently hypothesizes, how planning attention changed, what is constrained, what explicitly depends on what, what has become expensive to change, and what remains unknown.

## Doctrine

### 1. Preserve plural worlds

Do not collapse ambiguity merely because one explanation is convenient. Candidate worlds may disagree. Selection and weight are planning policy, not canon.

### 2. Weight without pretending to know

A normalized weight can guide scarce planning effort. It is not automatically a calibrated probability, truth score, narrative-quality score, or measure of player agency. Concentration diagnostics describe the vector; they do not certify the worlds.

### 3. Count one evidence assertion once—and maintain its reasons

Evidence updates must have explicit custody and a complete denominator. Applying the same assertion twice is accidental exponentiation. If an applied assertion is later superseded, preserve the update, expose repair debt, and repair current weights only through a separately reviewed replay from an immutable baseline.

### 4. Keep reweighting, resampling, selection, and canonization separate

Changing attention is not eliminating a world. Eliminating a world is not selecting one for a scene. Selecting one is not anchoring its claims. Each act needs its own authority, review, event, and nonclaims.

### 5. Commit only when consequences require it

Unobserved interpretation can remain flexible. Experienced physical facts, explicit disclosures, promises, and depended-upon premises should become progressively more expensive to change.

### 6. Precommit selectively and describe the proof honestly

An adaptive world should not freeze every unused secret. When a genre or host promises that one hidden value was fixed earlier, bind that exact opening and publish a retainable receipt. A seal proves byte continuity only; it must not masquerade as truth, authorship, uniqueness, trusted time, clue sufficiency, or artistic fairness.

### 7. Revision must leave custody

Never overwrite a hidden-world fact in place. Review the current impact, preserve the predecessor, create a successor, record the reason, and keep unresolved downstream debt visible.

### 8. Repair forward; never launder the past

An obsolete dependency or evidence factor may require repair, but its predecessor, successor or disposition, reason, review, and repair act must remain separate custody. Deterministic factor replay is allowed only when its baseline, complete factor set, arithmetic, and authority are all recorded. Do not call automatic inheritance or invisible recomputation a repair.

### 9. Proximity is not causality

Assertion history, questions, and constraints may be relevant to review. Only an explicit authored consequence link claims dependence. Do not turn every detail into foreshadowing by algorithmic enthusiasm.

### 10. Separate commitment from truth and confidence

A premise can be uncertain but hard because it was precommitted for fairness. A premise can be likely but tentative because nothing depends on it. Labels must not do each other’s jobs.

### 11. Separate constraint from inference

A rule may reject an incompatible state. It must not silently manufacture an unstored truth. Any future closure should remain a disposable projection with premises and rules.

### 12. Separate fact, belief, hypothesis, and meaning

Physical and causal custody should be stricter than interpretation. Character beliefs are perspective-scoped. Candidate worlds are planner hypotheses. Thematic meaning may remain plastic long after experienced consequences become fixed.

### 13. Make access boundaries noninterfering

Audience context is not planner context with a few fields deleted. A perspective must not infer hidden ontology through identifiers, links, diagnostics, weights, ancestry, omission patterns, or raw event payloads.

### 14. Make custody queryable

A user should be able to ask where a record came from, what directly depends on it, why a weight changed, which factor was used, why revision is expensive, and what a perspective may see. Provenance must never masquerade as proof.

### 15. Refuse beautifully

A stale review, reused factor, incomplete population, contradictory anchor, hard commitment, binding consequence, unsafe visibility request, malformed identity, or cyclic dependency should produce a specific machine-readable refusal without partial mutation.

### 16. Make the entrance explicit and capability-honest

Give the player a one-page entrance that requires no protocol vocabulary. Do not assume a fresh model will infer whether the user wants play, repository work, or inspection; whether the current surface has local tools; whether the cube is ready; what a bridge must do; or when delegation is warranted. Emit a read-only entrance that states capabilities and non-capabilities, the exact governed loop, least-context handoffs, and the receipt that proves acceptance. Chat-only play may be useful, but it must not impersonate committed cube state.

### 17. Make context boundaries executable

Do not merely tell a coordinator to remember which worker may see what. Manufacture strict role-specific task cards from one audited packet, retain the chain in a request-scoped run, bind every later artifact to packet and upstream digests, emit exactly one next owner/input/schema/command, and fail closed when a pointer, template, review, role, stage, or handoff is incomplete. For multi-role checkpoints, freeze the complete provider-route map before delegation, retain ordered accepted and failed invocation custody, and reconstruct the exact chain before every transition. When compression is the treatment, bind the accepted post-checkpoint audience/private bottleneck and exact next player input to one fresh source-bound ordinary turn, then start a new narrator context; four fresh workers followed by an old contaminated narrator is not forgetting. If prose-only public continuity is supplied, authenticate its exact text against durable sources in a parent artifact, distinguish an explicitly partial run list from a checkpoint-bound complete ledger census, and compile a least-context prose-only view instead of handing source-custody IDs and hashes to the narrator or smuggling the whole parent transcript. Routes, model names, context IDs, capsule digests, invocation IDs, and timing remain host declarations—not provider attestations. A turn run, checkpoint run, narrator capsule, continuation dispatch, public history, plan, task card, or verifier opinion remains advisory sidecar custody—not ledger truth, provider isolation, forgetting proof, or commit authority.

### 18. Make isolation claims falsifiable, not ceremonial

Fresh-context declarations, empty tool lists, and least-context cards are useful controls but not attestations. Pre-register exact operator-only and filesystem-only canaries, scan the frozen retained artifact boundary before blind evaluation, preserve every detected match, and state the clean-scan nonclaim explicitly. Do not discard or rerun a contaminated cell merely because it weakens the treatment.

### 19. Be a kernel, not a kingdom

Lacuna should enable model hosts, game engines, narrative planners, and human tools without absorbing their policy. It is not the storyteller, optimizer, transcript service, deployment system, particle proposer, resampling policy, model invoker, or self-improving agent.

## Success condition

A downstream system should be able to ask:

- What was directly observed?
- Who merely reported it?
- What does this character believe?
- Which candidate worlds survive?
- Where do they disagree or duplicate the same explicit valuation?
- Why did planning attention move?
- Which evidence factors were applied, which are active, excluded, retired, or awaiting reconciliation?
- From which immutable baseline and structural epoch was the current weight vector derived?
- Is a weight vector merely normalized, or free of known reweighting debt?
- What is anchored or hard?
- What explicitly depends on this premise?
- What merely happens to be nearby?
- What would block revision?
- What repair debt would remain?
- What was replaced, by whom, under which reviewed state?
- What is explicitly unknown?
- Which hidden promises were precommitted, and can their openings be verified?
- What may this perspective see?
- Which execution profile is this model actually using, and what capabilities are absent?
- Is the cube verified and role-ready, and can this profile begin governed play without a bridge?
- Which orchestration topology was selected, from which explicit packet signals, and was it overridden?
- Which run and exact task card/packet/upstream digests bind each delegated worker?
- Which provider route was fixed for each checkpoint role, and which accepted or failed host-declared invocation receipts exist?
- Which exact capsule and continuation dispatch carry accepted post-checkpoint context, next player input, and ordinary turn authority, and what was excluded?
- Which exact public-history turns were supplied, in what immutable order, and is the artifact an explicit partial list or a checkpoint-bound complete durable-turn census?
- What single next action does the audited run authorize?
- Can an edited, relabelled, wrong-stage, or cross-turn artifact be mixed into this handoff without refusal?
- Which sidecar artifacts are advisory, and who alone may commit?
- Which accepted receipt field is safe to present to the player?

—and receive typed, replayable answers rather than a persuasive paragraph pretending to be a database.
