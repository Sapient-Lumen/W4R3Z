### Important correction (lexicon)

Earlier archives treated `NZ₂` as if it were a canonical root for “goodwill/benevolence.” The official New Ithkuil lexicon instead gives dedicated roots such as `RTR` (kindness/benevolence/helpfulness), `VÇ₃` (altruism/self‑sacrifice), and the “motive/principle” roots `CB` / `RCB` used for the essay’s *volitional* aspect of love.

Accordingly, in the **glossary** we now treat `NZ₂` as a **project macro label** for the composite *ethical love = morally‑upright motive/principle + benevolence/altruism*, rather than as a single canonical lexicon root.

We are *not* embedding full reference PDFs here; instead we record which kinds of resources each decision came from:
- Root gloss lists for the v0.2-style inventory (as previously consulted).
- Case/illocution/validation tables (for directives, inferentials, etc.) when needed.

1) preserve the author’s intent via paraphrase, and
2) mark the phrase as a carrier: `S«…»`.

- **Illocution CNJ (Conjectural)** — used for English “if …” clauses framed as conjecture/hypothesis.
- **Affix EXT*** (Exactness/Comparability of Identity) — used as our working way to express “in the right way / well enough” via degree 6.
- **Affix CPC*** (Capacitative modalities) — we use degree 2 (“have opportunity to”) as our current proxy for “at the right time / when circumstances are opportune”.
