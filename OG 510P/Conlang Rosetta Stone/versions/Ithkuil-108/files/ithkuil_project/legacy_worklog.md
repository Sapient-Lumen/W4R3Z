## Lexical decisions (current)
- LOVE (as intended in the source text): compound/conflation of
  - -LTW- Stem 1 = fondness/affection (philia-ish)
  - -VÇ- Stem 3 = charitableness/altruism/self-sacrifice (agape-ish)
  Source: New Ithkuil lexicon.
- IMPORTANCE / SALIENCE: -ŠH- (Stem 1 = importance/significance; Stem 3 = salience/prominence). (Corrects earlier placeholder `PŘ`.)
- JOY/HAPPINESS: -ŇV- (jollity / happiness / jubilation)
- FALL (physical falling toward ground): -KTH- CSV gloss includes “to fall toward a planar surface due to gravity; to fall”.
- COMMANDMENT/PRECEPT: -TKH- (rule/procedure/custom/praxis/precept/edict)
- HEART (organ): -ḐL- Stem 1 = heart
- SOUL (as ‘essence of one’s sense of self’): -MN- Stem 3
- MIND (seat of consciousness): -TY- Stem 2
- CARRIER for foreign/proper terms: -S- (carrier root)
