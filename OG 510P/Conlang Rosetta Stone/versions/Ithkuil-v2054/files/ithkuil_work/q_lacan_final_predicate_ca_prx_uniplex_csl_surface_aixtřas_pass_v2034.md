## Why `PRX`, not `DEL`

The tempting `DEL` output would be:

```text
aixtřal
```

That would present the maiming as a whole bounded event considered from beginning to end. It is grammatically clean, and it might be the right form in a narrative sentence such as:

```text
I maimed you yesterday / I performed the maiming act as a completed bounded event.
```

But this Lacan sentence is not mainly narrating a completed physical episode. Its pressure is analytic and structural:

```text
because I love in you something more than you — the objet petit a — I mutilate you
```

The violence is the local logic of desire disclosed by the clause, not a chronological report of a completed action. `PRX` is therefore better than `DEL` because it foregrounds only the contextually relevant portion/aspect/duration of the act. The grammar’s own description of `PRX` with verbs is exactly this: the whole event is not necessarily being considered; only the relevant spatial or durational extent of the event matters in the utterance.

So the reading of `aixtřas` is not:

```text
I complete a maiming from start to finish.
```

but:

```text
I enact the mutilating/maiming violence insofar as it is relevant here.
```

That is closer to Lacan’s theoretical present tense.

## The full `Ca` bundle audit

### 1. Configuration: `UNI`

The maiming is one contextual act-event, not a repetition, swarm, series, composite event, or fuzzy multiplicity.

```text
UNI = one whole contextual unit of the stem concept
```

For this local clause, `UNI` is still right even though the broader sentence contains multiple clauses. The `Ca` belongs only to the `XTŘ₂` predicate, not to the entire sentence.

### 2. Affiliation: `CSL`

`ASO` was tempting because the final action is agentive, and the speaker is marked separately as `lo` ERG. But `ASO` would add too much: it would construe the act as by design or with specific purpose. The source has a causal structure, but it is specifically “because inexplicably...” rather than “in order to...” or “for the purpose of...”

The final mutilation is structurally driven by the preceding desire-clause, not instrumentally planned inside the surface grammar. `CSL` keeps the event neutral with respect to design/purpose while allowing agency to be handled by `lo`.

```text
CSL = neutral/naturally integrated event
ASO = demoted: would over-purpose the act
```

### 3. Perspective: `M`

`M` / MONADIC is right because the maiming is being accessed as the event at hand in the speaker’s present theoretical frame. Ithkuil’s Monadic perspective for verbs can cover an event that takes place in the present context or whose present-context portion is what is relevant.

This strongly cooperates with `PRX`: the event is monadic because it is the accessible local event; it is proximal because the clause does not care about the entire event from beginning to end.

### 4. Extension: `PRX`

This is the main v2034 decision.

```text
PRX = not the whole act-event; only the contextually relevant portion/aspect/duration
```

This preserves the Lacanian present-tense shock: the addressee is mutilated as a function of the speaker’s desire for the object-cause/excess lodged “in” the addressee, not because the line is enumerating a completed criminal act.

### 5. Essence: `NRM`

The maiming is not being presented as a representation, symbol, model, counterfeit, or stand-in. It is the real violence of the desire-structure inside the paraphrase.

```text
NRM = actual/non-representative
RPV = rejected: would make the mutilation symbolic or representational as a grammatical category
```

The sentence may be psychoanalytic and metaphorically charged, but the Ithkuil predicate should not grammatically mark the act as merely representational unless we intend to translate “I symbolically mutilate you.” That is not the wording we are building here.

## Tone/stress policy for this pass

v2033 postponed tone/stress. v2034 now leaves them at the default ordinary main-predicate values unless a later audit discovers that the surrounding frame forces a non-default relation.

Current policy:

```text
tone    = default PROCESSUAL / ordinary event unfolding
stress  = default penultimate = INFORMAL designation + UNFRAMED relation
surface = aixtřas
```

This is not a claim that tone/stress are irrelevant. It is a claim that nothing in the present Lacan clause demands formal designation, framed relation on the final predicate itself, completive version, or an overt marked tone. The situative middle clause is already framed separately; the final maiming predicate remains the unframed main event.

## Rejected / demoted alternatives

### 1. `aixtřal` = `DEL`

Demoted, not impossible. This would be a good form if the translation wanted “I maim/mutilate you” as a whole bounded telic event. It is too narratively complete for this Lacanian clause.

### 2. `aixtřar` = `ASO + DEL` candidate family

Rejected as over-intentionalizing. It would add purposeful/design affiliation where the sentence says “because inexplicably,” not “in order to.”

### 3. `aixtřaš` = `ASO + PRX` candidate family

Also rejected for the same reason: useful if the mutilation were being portrayed as the speaker’s designed project, but that would flatten the paradoxical psychoanalytic causality.

### 4. `aixtřac` / ABSTRACT perspective family

Rejected. The sentence is not defining “mutilation as an abstract concept”; it is predicating a local event/state-act of the speaker and addressee.

### 5. `aixtřas` with `RPV` essence

Rejected. The line is metaphorically and psychoanalytically dense, but grammar should not demote the act to representation unless the source explicitly asks for “symbolically mutilate.”
