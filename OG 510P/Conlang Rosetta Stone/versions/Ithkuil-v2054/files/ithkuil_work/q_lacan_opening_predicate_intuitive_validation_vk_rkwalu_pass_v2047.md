## 1. Scope of this revision

v2046 active line:

```text
rkwal li sëi ; evvralácboa sou li hlušh-otilöehëi sue hlu’u «objet petit a» hü ; alo extřudá lo že
```

v2047 audits the opening predicate after the final predicate was rebuilt with an explicit Slot-IX `VK` value. The active change is:

```text
rkwal li sëi  →  rkwalû li sëi
```

New active line:

```text
rkwalû li sëi ; evvralácboa sou li hlušh-otilöehëi sue hlu’u «objet petit a» hü ; alo extřudá lo že
```

Approximate control reread:

> I assert, on the basis of subjective feeling, that I experience romantic love with you as its affective stimulus; given that, for no reason, through you I undergo infatuated/obsessive attachment toward a salient something more than you, named “objet petit a”; nevertheless, I maim you-to-your-detriment.

This is not a wholesale validation policy for the entire quotation. It is a local repair: the opening affective self-report should not remain less explicit than the final violent predicate.

---

## 2. Why the old `rkwal` is superseded rather than declared wrong

The v2036 opening form was:

```text
rkwal li sëi
```

That was a useful compressed bridge form. It relied on the ordinary economy of a short unframed verbal formative: the word could be read as carrying the default verbal force by relation/stress rather than by an overt final `VK` vowel.

After v2046, however, the final predicate became explicit:

```text
extřudá
```

There is now a cube asymmetry:

```text
opening predicate  rkwal      hidden/default VK
final predicate    extřudá    explicit VK
```

v2047 repairs that asymmetry. The opening predicate now also carries visible Slot-IX verbal force:

```text
rkwalû
```

The old `rkwal` is therefore marked **superseded for active running-line use**, but still valid as a compact diagnostic predecessor.

---

## 3. Why the opening validation is ITU, not OBS

### 3.1 The default candidate would be `á`

If this pass merely mirrored v2046 mechanically, the opener would become:

```text
rkwalá li sëi
```

That would show:

```text
ASR + OBS  →  á
```

But `OBS` is too empiricist for the specific source burden “I love you.” The speaker’s claim is not primarily based on outward observation of an external fact. It is a first-person affective self-report.

### 3.2 The better local validation is `û`

For the opening clause, v2047 chooses:

```text
ASR + ITU  →  û
```

The point is not that the statement is weak, speculative, or merely imagined. It remains an assertion. The change is the evidential/validation lane: the speaker claims the love-state from subjective feeling or internal intuition rather than from external sensory observation.

So:

```text
rkwalû
```

is the active bridge form:

```text
a-rkw-a-l-û  →  rkwalû
```

where the initial Stem-1 PRC `a-` remains elided in the running surface, as in v2036.

---

## 4. Why not make the opener UNSPECIFIED instead

A philosophical quotation could justify deliberate validation non-commitment:

```text
rkwalêi li sëi
```

That remains a live global-quotation alternate. It would say, roughly:

> I assert that I love you, but I leave the validation basis unspecified.

v2047 does not choose it for the active line because the local English is not a detached theoretical paraphrase. It is a first-person avowal:

```text
I love you
```

The avowal is affective and self-reporting. `ITU` therefore says more of the right thing than `USP` at this local layer.

The future global validation pass may still decide that all finite predicates inside the Lacan quotation should use `USP`, but that would be a quotation-wide policy, not a reason to leave the opening clause under-specified now.

---

## 5. Why not use DYN or an agentive opening

The participants remain unchanged:

```text
li   = 1m/AFF, speaker as affective experiencer
sëi  = 2m/STM, addressee as affective stimulus
```

v2047 only changes the predicate’s `VK` value. It does **not** re-open the v2036 decision that the opener is affective-stimulative rather than agent-patient.

Rejected alternatives remain rejected:

```text
rkwil... lo se     dynamic/agentive love-doing, too transitive
rkwalû lo se       static predicate but wrong participant roles
rkwalû li sü       dative-directional addressee, too much like “love sent to you”
```

The source contrast still requires:

```text
rkwalû li sëi      = I experience love; you are its stimulus
extřudá lo že      = I maim; you are the harmed patient
```

That split is the sentence’s ethical and psychoanalytic hinge.

---

## 6. Interaction with the middle framed predicate

The middle predicate remains:

```text
evvralácboa
```

It is not given a `VK` vowel because it is a framed formative. Its Slot IX is already occupied by the Situative case `oa`, and FRAMED relation is carried by antepenultimate stress:

```text
e-vvr-a-l-acb-oa  →  evvralácboa
```

If the project later wants to show illocution/validation inside the framed clause, it should do so with the appropriate illocution/validation affix strategy, not by replacing the `oa` case. For now, the middle clause inherits its discourse force from the surrounding asserted line.


Still active as bridge default. A later pass may choose `USP` if the whole quotation should avoid local evidential commitments.
