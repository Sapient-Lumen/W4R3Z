## 2. Why this pass follows naturally from v2050

v2050 made the “in you” anchor abstract:

```text
sou  →  swou
```

That move said the Lacanian “in you” is not merely spatial or monadic. It is the beloved as a whole desire-bearing field:

```text
swou = through/in all-that-is-you; through everything-about-you
```

But v2050 left the comparison standard as:

```text
sue = you-CMP
```

This created a useful but slightly asymmetrical reading:

```text
swou ... sue
in all-that-is-you ... more than you
```

v2051 regularizes the semantic domain of the two “you” values while preserving the case distinction:

```text
swou  = through all-that-is-you, ITP
swië  = compared to all-that-is-you, CMP
```

So the beloved appears twice as the same expanded personal field, but in two different grammatical roles:

1. the field through which desire interprets the beloved;
2. the standard exceeded by the surplus object-cause.

---

## 3. The grammatical build

The base addressee is:

```text
s = 2m neutral monadic addressee
```

The ABSTRACT increment is:

```text
-w / -y = all that X is; everything about X
```

The Comparative case is:

```text
CMP = ue / ië
```

The `ië` allomorph is required after the abstract `-w-` increment. Therefore the transparent build is:

```text
s + w + ië  →  swië
```

This matters. A naive written form:

```text
*swue
```

would preserve the intended semantics but miss the New-Ithkuil case-vowel alternation. v2051 freezes the corrected form:

```text
swië
```

---

## 4. Why `swië` wins over `sue`

### 4.1 `sue` remains valid but minimal

```text
sue
```

means:

```text
you-CMP
as compared to you
than you
```

That is a correct minimal reading of the surface English. It remains a valid fallback and should not be deleted from the cube.

### 4.2 `swië` better matches the Lacan burden

The Lacan line is not saying:

```text
I love an extra item in addition to your ordinary personhood.
```

It is closer to:

```text
I love, through everything that you are to me, an excess which is still more than everything that you are.
```

That is exactly the difference between:

```text
sue   = than you, flat conversational participant
swië  = than all-that-is-you / everything-about-you
```

### 4.3 The asymmetry of roles remains

v2051 does not collapse `swou` and `swië` into the same meaning. Their referential domain is now parallel, but their cases differ:

```text
swou  ITP  = by/through all-that-is-you as interpretive context
swië  CMP  = as compared to all-that-is-you as comparison standard
```

This preserves the phrase’s internal fracture:

```text
in you             → swou
more than you      → swië
objet petit a      → hlu’u «objet petit a» hü
```

---

## 5. Why not keep the beloved-as-standard monadic?

A plausible argument for `sue` is that “more than you” might contrast the surplus object with the empirical beloved as a discrete person, while `swou` already handles the beloved-as-field. That was v2050’s reading.

v2051 rejects that as slightly too tidy. In Lacan’s sentence, the “you” being exceeded is not merely the body/person standing there. It is the beloved as loved, idealized, fantasized, spoken to, and interpreted. The thing “more than you” exceeds the beloved as an entire subjective field, not merely as a countable participant in dialogue.

So the active line now says:

```text
through all-that-is-you, I fixate on a salient something more-than-all-that-is-you
```

rather than:

```text
through all-that-is-you, I fixate on a salient something more-than-you-as-bare-addressee
```

---

## 6. Why not NOMIC, GENITIVE, or CORRELATIVE here

### 6.1 NOMIC rejected

A NOMIC “you” would drift toward:

```text
you-ness
what-you-are-as-a-type
```

That is too universalizing. Lacan’s addressee remains this beloved, not a general category of belovedness.

### 6.2 GENITIVE rejected

GENITIVE would say the object-cause is “of you” as an inherent possession or association. That risks making *objet petit a* a property owned by the beloved, which is not the intended theoretical burden.

### 6.3 CORRELATIVE rejected for the comparison term

CORRELATIVE would be good for “in relation to you,” but the host already carries a Level comparison. The Y-term of a Level comparison wants CMP, not a looser associative relation.

## 8. Updated tight English bridge

> I love you; given that, inexplicably, through all-that-you-are, I love/fixate on a salient something more than all-that-you-are, named “objet petit a”; nevertheless, I maim you.

This is not an idiomatic final translation. It is a pressure-tested bridge line designed to preserve the grammatical commitments currently frozen in the cube.
