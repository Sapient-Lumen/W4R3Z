## 2. Why `sou` was good but slightly too flat

v2028 froze:

```text
sou = s + ou = 2m/ITP
```

That was already much better than a literal locative. The source does not mean:

```text
inside your body
inside your spatial interior
```

It means something like:

```text
in the beloved as encountered/interpreted by desire
through the beloved as the subjective field in which the object-cause appears
```

So **ITP / Interpretative** remains active. The issue is not the case. The issue is the domain of the referent.

Plain `s` makes the addressee a simple monadic “you.” But Lacan’s phrase is not merely about the addressee as a discrete conversational participant. It is about the way the beloved’s appearance, body, speech, history, symbolic position, and fantasy-bearing surface make the surplus object appear. That pushes the anchor from:

```text
through you
```

into:

```text
through all-that-is-you / everything-about-you
```

---

## 3. Derivation of `swou`

New Ithkuil single-referent referentials use:

```text
C1 + VC
```

The neutral monadic addressee is:

```text
s
```

The Interpretative case is:

```text
ou
```

and New Ithkuil allows an ABSTRACT referential increment:

```text
-w / -y
```

which, when applied to a tangible/personal referential, means roughly:

```text
all that X is
all about X
everything having to do with X
```

For this line, the phonotactically transparent build is:

```text
s + w + ou  →  swou
```

So:

```text
sou   = through/in you as monadic addressee
swou  = through/in all-that-is-you; through everything-about-you
```

---

## 4. Why this fits the Lacan burden

The source line has two separate “you” values:

```text
in you
more than you
```

v2050 now deliberately separates them:

```text
swou  = in/through all-that-is-you, the interpretive field where the object-cause appears
sue   = than you, the addressee as the comparison standard exceeded by the salient surplus
```

This is important. If both were plain `s`, the distinction between the beloved-as-field and the beloved-as-comparison-standard would be too weak.

With v2050:

```text
swou ... hlušh-otiröehëi sue
```

means roughly:

```text
through all-that-is-you, I fixate on a salient something more-than-you
```

That better matches the psychoanalytic reading of *objet petit a* as not the empirical beloved but the object-cause/surplus by which the beloved becomes desired.

---

## 5. Why not GEN, ATT, OGN, or a literal locative-like option

### 5.1 GEN rejected

A possible alternative would be something like:

```text
sei = 2m/GEN
```

This would make the excess “of you” in the sense of an inalienable association/possession. But the Lacanian object is not simply a property of the beloved. It is not “your attribute” in a stable ontological sense.

### 5.2 ATT rejected

A possible ATTRIBUTIVE version would make the object-cause too much like an attribute or characteristic that the beloved has or undergoes. That would domesticate the weird surplus into a quality of the beloved.

### 5.3 OGN rejected

An ORIGINATIVE reading would make the beloved the source/origin/location from which the object-cause comes. This is tempting, but still too literal: the source is the subject’s desire/fantasy structure as much as the encountered beloved.

### 5.4 literal location rejected

A literal locative/interior reading would be the worst active option because it would make `in you` spatial or bodily. The Lacan line needs subjective/desire-structural anchoring, not anatomy.

## 6. Placement decision

The line now places `swou` immediately after the framed `VVR₂` head:

```text
evvralácboa swou li hlušh-otiröehëi sue hlu’u «objet petit a» hü
```

This keeps `swou` as a **frame-level interpretive anchor** for the whole affective fixation:

```text
through all-that-is-you, I fixate on X
```

rather than as a narrowly postnominal modifier of the host packet:

```text
X-through-you
```

That is deliberate. The source says “I love in you something...,” not merely “the something-in-you.” The `in you` relation belongs to the act/state of loving/fixating as much as to the object-cause.
