## 3. Validation-lane split after v2049

The Lacan line now has a deliberate validation contrast:

```text
rkwalû      ASR/ITU: subjective affective self-report
extřudêi    ASR/USP: asserted violent consequence/action, evidential basis withheld
```

This avoids collapsing the line into either:

```text
I feel that I love you; I feel that I mutilate you.
```

or:

```text
I observe that I love you; I observe that I mutilate you.
```

The opening love-state is affectively self-known; the closing maiming is a structurally asserted consequence whose evidential mode remains philosophically unstable.

### 4.2 `literal-violence-overcommitment-risk`

Definition:

> The risk of making Lacan's “I mutilate you” too narrowly observational/sensory, as though the line were a factual witness report rather than a theoretical/dramatic assertion.

### 4.3 `violence-demotion-risk`

Definition:

> The opposite risk: softening the final clause into mere fantasy, metaphor, or feeling and thereby losing the violence of the assertion.

Rejected candidates:

```text

extřudû   ASR/ITU
```

### 4.4 `validation-lane-asymmetry`

Definition:

> A cube rule allowing different predicates in the same sentence to take different validation strategies when their epistemic modes differ.

Applied here:

```text

extřudêi    maiming/assertion = ASR/USP
```
