## 2. New cube axis: `anchor-domain`

The previous cube tracked cases and surface words, but it did not distinguish the domain of the “in you” anchor. v2050 adds:

```text
anchor-domain
```

Allowed values for this local region:

| Value | Candidate | Status | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|---|
| literal-interior | locative-like option | rejected | “inside your body/spatial interior.” Wrong register. |
| monadic-interpretive | `sou` | superseded/minimal | “through/in you” as 2m interpretive context. Good but flat. |
| abstract-interpretive | `swou` | active | “through/in all-that-is-you; everything-about-you.” Best active line. |
| possessive/inalienable | `sei`-type GEN | rejected | Makes objet a too much “of you.” |
| attribute-like | ATT option | rejected | Makes objet a a quality/attribute of the beloved. |
| origin/source | OGN option | rejected but live | Makes the beloved too much the source/origin of object a. Tempting but not active. |
| relational-associative | COR option | live commentary alternate | Could work for a flatter theoretical paraphrase. |

## 3. Why `swou` wins

The source has two “you” functions:

```text
in you
more than you
```

v2049 had:

```text
sou ... sue
```

That distinguished case but not domain. v2050 has:

```text
swou ... sue
```

This gives:

```text
swou = in/through all-that-is-you as the interpretive/desire field
sue  = than you, the comparison standard
```

This matters because the object-cause is not simply inside the beloved, nor simply owned by the beloved, nor simply identical to the beloved. It appears through the beloved as encountered by desire and yet exceeds the beloved as ordinary empirical addressee.

## 4. Placement policy

`swou` remains immediately after the framed predicate:

```text
evvralácboa swou li hlušh-otiröehëi sue ...
```

rather than moving after the host:

```text
* evvralácboa li hlušh-otiröehëi sue swou ...
```

Reason:

```text
source = I love in you something more than you
not    = I love something-more-than-you-in-you
```

So the anchor scopes over the loving/fixating state rather than merely modifying the host noun. This is now an explicit cube rule:

```text
predicate-anchor-before-experiencer
```

The active order remains:

```text
FRAME-HEAD  ANCHOR  EXPERIENCER  STIMULUS-HOST  CMP-STANDARD  NAME
```
