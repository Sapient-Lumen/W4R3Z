# q_lacan_inexplicably_rsn7_suffix_on_framed_vvr2_head_pass_v2041

**Date:** 2026-05-25  
**Project:** Ithkuil translation of the love text — Lacan quotation micro-passage  
**Source span:** “I love you, but, because inexplicably I love in you something more than you—the objet petit a—I mutilate you.”  
**Pass type:** current-public grammar repair of “inexplicably”; RSN/7 suffix on the framed VVR₂ affective-state head; stress recalculation after suffixation

---

## 1. Scope of this revision

v2040 deliberately removed the inherited `hleňfó` prehead because the current public lexicon assigns `-ŇF-` to “sliver-moon shaped,” not to mystery/puzzlement/inexplicability. That left a known debt:

```text
<INEXPLICABLY> = unresolved; do not restore hleňfó.
```

v2041 pays that debt through current-public suffixal morphology rather than by resurrecting a non-current lexical prehead.

The active replacement is:

```text
évvroîl  →  evvróîlošš
```

where:

```text
-ošš = RSN/7, Type-1 Degree-7
     = “for no reason” / reasonlessly, applied to an emotional state or motivation
```

This is a deliberately Lacanian choice: the line does not merely say the speaker is *surprised* by the love, nor that the speaker is consciously *puzzled* about it. It says the love-in-you relation is reason-opaque: its causal/explanatory ground is not morally or rationally legible even though it becomes the situative ground for the mutilating clause.

### v2041 line

```text
rkwal ti kui ; evvróîlošš köi ti hlušh-otilöehui kè upeal «objet petit a» ; iňň aixtřas to ke
```

Approximate back-translation:

> I love you; given the background situation that, for no explicable reason, through/in you I undergo infatuated/obsessive attachment toward the salient something-more-than-you, identified as “objet petit a,” nevertheless I maim you.

The English “because inexplicably I love in you…” is now represented by two interacting layers:

```text
evvróîlošš     = framed situative VVR₂ state + reasonless/emotion-reason suffix
... ; iňň ...  = nevertheless/however adversative bridge to the maiming clause
```

## 3. Why RSN/7 is better than the candidates left open in v2040

v2040 listed three plausible routes: `-ŠX-`, `-ND-`, and `-Xh-`. After checking the current public materials again, I am freezing a fourth route instead: **the RSN suffix**.

### 3.1 Rejecting `-ŠX-` as the main surface

Current `-ŠX-` is:

```text
feeling of puzzlement and curiosity, a desire to solve a mystery
```

That is affectively close but it changes the source. Lacan’s “inexplicably” does not mainly mean:

> I feel puzzled while loving in you something more than you.

It means the love-relation itself is opaque to adequate explanation. Using `-ŠX-` would over-psychologize the speaker’s conscious attitude.

### 3.2 Rejecting a full `-ND-` construction for this pass

Current `-ND-` is extremely tempting because its field includes question, inquiry, answer, analysis, answer-as-cause/reason, solution, resolution, and elucidation.

But an `-ND-` construction would require a heavier paraphrase:

```text
not having / lacking / resisting an answer-or-reason to inquiry
```

That may eventually be useful, especially if the project wants a more philosophical expansion of “inexplicably.” For the running Lacan line, however, it would be too syntactically expensive and would compete with the already dense `objet petit a` packet.

### 3.3 Rejecting `-Xh-` as too epistemic

Current `-Xh-` covers fact, know, learn, understand, and expertise. That would support something like:

```text
unknownly / not-understoodly
```

But “inexplicably” is not merely “I do not know why.” It is stronger: the relation seems to lack a satisfying reason or explanation as such. `-Xh-` undershoots the modal/explanatory opacity.

### 3.4 Rejecting XPT and DLB as neighboring but wrong

Two suffixes are superficially attractive:

```text
XPT/2 = completely unexpectedly
DLB/5 = unforeseeably / unpredictably
```

They are not wrong in ordinary English, but they point at the wrong axis. `XPT` is about expectation of outcome. `DLB` is about deliberateness/agency/foreseeability. The source adverb is not mainly saying the speaker failed to predict the state or acted without deliberateness. It is saying the affective relation lacks an explanatory reason.

### 3.5 Freezing RSN/7

The current suffix table gives:

```text
RSN = Reason for an Emotional State or Motivation for an Action
RSN/7 = for no reason
```

This is almost exactly the needed lane, because the middle predicate is already `VVR₂`, i.e. an affective/obsessive love-state. The suffix therefore applies to the right semantic target without adding a new clause or forcing the speaker to be consciously puzzled.

## 4. Morphological derivation of `evvróîlošš`

### 4.1 Base from v2039–v2040

```text
evvr-oî-l
VVR₂-SIT-Ca.default
```

with FRAMED relation shown by stress:

```text
évvroîl
```

The `oî` sequence remains deliberately split rather than recombined as `oi`, because the frame-head needs enough syllabic material to display FRAMED relation by stress.

### 4.2 Add RSN/7 Type 1

The current suffix system gives Type 1 Degree 7 as:

```text
-oC
```

For RSN, the consonantal suffix is:

```text
-šš
```

Therefore:

```text
RSN/7 Type 1 = -ošš
```

Attach it in Slot XI after `Ca`:

```text
evvroîl + ošš → evvroîlošš
```

### 4.3 Recalculate FRAMED stress

The addition of `-ošš` adds another syllabic nucleus to the word. Because the formative remains the head of a case-frame, it must keep FRAMED relation, i.e. antepenultimate stress for informal designation.

Using the same hiatus-preserving `oî` policy from v2039, the stress now shifts from the initial vowel to the first vowel of the case sequence:

```text
évvroîl      →      evvróîlošš
```

This is not a semantic change; it is the mechanical consequence of adding the suffix while preserving FRAMED relation.

## 6. Why this better matches the source burden

The source line is not simply:

```text
I love you, but I unexpectedly mutilate you.
```

Nor is it simply:

```text
I love you, but I am puzzled by something in you.
```

The line’s cruelty comes from a nested causal fracture:

1. the speaker professes love;
2. the speaker then locates within the beloved a surplus object-cause of desire;
3. this surplus makes the love relation reason-opaque rather than ethically stabilizing;
4. the final mutilation follows under adversative pressure, not as a morally transparent consequence.

`RSN/7` on the middle `VVR₂` frame-head captures exactly the local fracture in step 3. It says the affective/obsessive state lacks a reason, rather than saying the final maiming is accidental, surprising, or confused.

## 8. What remains open

This pass does **not** reopen:

- `rkwal ti kui` as the opening “I love you” packet;
- `köi` as the current 2m Interpretative “in/through you” referential;
- `ti` as the current 1m Affective experiencer;
- `hlušh-otilöehui kè` as the “salient something-more-than-you” comparison packet;
- `upeal «objet petit a»` as the current carrier-root apposition;
- `iňň aixtřas to ke` as the final “nevertheless I mutilate you” packet.

What remains open is now narrower:

```text
Should the final frame boundary after the foreign phrase remain punctuation-only, or should the current grammar’s optional frame-end -t’ be surfaced on the last frame word despite the carrier phrase?
```

That is now the next clean pass.
