## 6. Retained lessons from the quarantined branch

The v2037–v2042 branch is not wasted. It contributed three useful questions that remain active:

1. **Carrier case:** Should `objet petit a` be RLT or ESS? v2038’s ESS argument is conceptually strong, but must be rederived with the New-Ithkuil `-S-` carrier.
2. **Frame boundary:** A visible frame-end marker may be useful, but New-Ithkuil `TPF/1` must be audited as the candidate, not the v2042 `TPF/2` form.
3. **Inexplicability:** RSN belongs on the VVR₂ predicate, but v2043 corrects the suffix to `RSN/1 -acb`.
