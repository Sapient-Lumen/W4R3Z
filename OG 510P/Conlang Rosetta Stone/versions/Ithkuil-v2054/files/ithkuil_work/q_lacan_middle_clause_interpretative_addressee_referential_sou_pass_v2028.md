## What this checkpoint now freezes

### 1. `ITP` here is the official **Interpretative** case, not a spatial “inside” case

The English source says:

```text
I love in you something more than you
```

The phrase **in you** is not being read here as physical containment.
It does not mean:

```text
inside your body
inside your spatial interior
inside a literal locative region named “you”
```

Nor should it be reduced to simple possession:

```text
your something-more-than-you
```

The archive’s earlier `ITP` choice should be retained, but now made explicit:

```text
ITP = INTERPRETATIVE
VC  = ou
```

Officially, ITP marks the party acting as the subjective interpretational context of another noun: the party by or through whom something is subjectively considered, interpreted, or described.
That matches the current Lacan reading better than a literal locative or possessive case.
The addressee is the medium/context through whom the speaker’s obsessive attachment apprehends the excess-object.

So the working English control remains:

```text
through you / in you-as-interpretive-medium
```

not:

```text
inside you as a place
owned by you
as part of your body
```

### 2. the referent is the monadic addressee `2m`, just as in `sue`

The addressee in this line is still the same direct singular **you** established by the opening clause:

```text
I love you
```

The relevant referential consonant is therefore:

```text
2m neutral addressee = s
```

The archive should not use the plural addressee:

```text
n-  = 2p neutral addressee
```

and should not use the animate third-person or obviative lanes.
The sentence is direct address, not narration about someone else.

### 3. the Effect remains neutral: not `šou`, not `žou`

The referential table distinguishes three effects for each referent:

```text
neutral     2m = s
beneficial  2m = š
detrimental 2m = ž
```

For the present slot, the effect stays neutral.

The final clause does harm the addressee:

```text
I mutilate you
```

but that harm belongs to the later `XTŘ₂` clause.
It should not be back-loaded into the middle-clause interpretative medium.
So this pass rejects:

```text
žou = 2m:DET:ITP
```

Likewise, the opening clause says “I love you,” but the love relation itself is not enough to make the **interpretative medium** beneficial in the technical effect-marking sense.
So this pass also rejects:

```text
šou = 2m:BEN:ITP
```

The neutral form is the disciplined one:

```text
sou
```

### 4. the case vowel is `ou`, yielding `s + ou = sou`

The surface derivation is minimal:

```text
C1  = s     2m neutral addressee
VC1 = ou    ITP / Interpretative case
```

Therefore:

```text
<2m:ITP>  →  sou
```

No extra epenthetic `ë` is needed before `s`.
No second referential is present.
No stacked second case is present.
No carrier, quotation, or naming material is present.

The form is simply a single-referent Referential:

```text
sou
```

### 5. stress stays unmarked

`ou` is a permitted diphthongal vowel-form in the New Ithkuil vowel sequence.
Thus `sou` is a monosyllabic Referential.
For Referential stress, monosyllabic or penultimate stress is default.
No acute accent is needed.

So the preferred form is:

```text
sou
```

not:

```text
sóu
soú
söu
sëou
```

### 6. this does not collapse the already-frozen `sue`

The middle clause now contains two overt second-person referentials:

```text
sou  = through/in you, as interpretative medium
sue  = than you, as Comparative standard
```

This is not redundancy.
It is the whole point of the Lacan clause.
The addressee is split across two roles:

```text
through you, I love / am obsessively attached to X;
X is something more than you.
```

In the archive’s current Ithkuil line, that fracture is now visible as:

```text
sou ... sue
```

This validates the older decision not to case-stack the two roles onto one compressed second-person referential.
The two cases belong to different local scopes:

- `sou` belongs to the middle predicate frame,
- `sue` belongs inside the internal comparison of the host packet.

### 7. why `sou` remains immediately after `hleňfó-évvroal-`

Earlier ordering passes already froze the adjacency:

```text
<VVR₂> <2m:ITP>
```

and later replaced the abstract `VVR₂` opening with the overt local packet:

```text
hleňfó-évvroal-
```

So the new overt substitution should preserve the same adjacency:

```text
hleňfó-évvroal- sou
```

not:

```text
hleňfó-évvroal- <1m> sou ...
```

and not:

```text
hleňfó-évvroal- <1m> hlušh-otilöehëi ... sou
```

The reason is scope.
The interpretative addressee is attached to the obsessive/infatuated attachment predicate, not to the host packet alone and not to the whole sentence as an afterthought.
Keeping it adjacent to the predicate preserves:

```text
I love/obsess through you
```

rather than:

```text
something-more-than-you through you
```

or:

```text
I mutilate you through you
```

### 8. specialized personal-reference roots remain demoted

A full specialized personal-reference-root strategy for the addressee is still too heavy here.
It would force a choice between the addressee as:

```text
you-as-self/person
you-as-psyche/essence
you-as-body/embodiment
you-as-person-in-my-life/niche
```

Those readings are Lacan-relevant, but this slot does not need them.
The English `in you` is currently doing relational case work, not lexical personhood analysis.
A plain Referential is still the cleanest official mechanism.

So this pass freezes:

```text
sou
```

and does not promote a personal-reference root.

### 9. locative, genitive, attributive, originative, and transmissive alternatives stay rejected

This pass also rechecks the most tempting non-ITP cases.

#### LOC / spatial containment

Rejected.
The source does not say the excess-object is physically inside the addressee.
The beloved is the interpretive field or medium, not a container.

#### GEN / inalienable association

Rejected.
GEN would over-possessivize the excess-object as an inherent possession/part of the addressee.
The archive has repeatedly avoided saying that `objet petit a` is simply owned by or organically part of the beloved.

#### ATT / attribute-bearing relation

Rejected.
ATT would pull the sentence toward “the excess-object as an attribute of you.”
That is too static and too property-like for the Lacan reading.

#### OGN / source

Rejected.
The addressee is not the origin/source of the obsessive object-cause.
The beloved is the medium through which it is encountered.

#### TRA / intended benefit/detriment recipient

Rejected.
TRA would move this slot toward benefit/harm transfer.
The benefit/harm logic belongs elsewhere in the sentence, especially the final mutilating clause.

ITP remains the best slot because it says exactly what is needed:

```text
through/by you as subjective interpretive context
```

## Rejected or demoted alternatives

### A. leave `<2m:ITP>` abstract

Rejected.
The official referential and case tables provide enough information to surface this placeholder safely.

### B. use `sue` for both second-person roles

Rejected.
`sue` is already frozen as Comparative.
It cannot also mean the interpretative “in/through you” without erasing the internal fracture of the clause.

### C. use one case-stacked second-person Referential

Still rejected.
The two addressee roles belong to different scopes.
Case-stacking would obscure the difference between:

```text
predicate-level through-you
nominal-comparison than-you
```

### D. use `šou` or `žou`

Rejected.
The slot is effect-neutral.
The love and injury are handled by their respective predicates.

### E. replace the Referential with a full personal-reference root

Demoted.
Too semantically committal for this exact local function.
