# Lacan second-person Comparative Referential `sue` pass v2026 — 2026-05-21

## English target span

This checkpoint continues directly from v2025.

Target sentence:

> **I love you, but, because inexplicably I love in you something more than you—the objet petit a—I mutilate you.**

The v2025 local packet was:

```text
hlušh-otilöeh- <2m:CMP> esälu’u «objet petit a» hü
```

where the host-side comparison had just been surfaced as:

```text
hlušh-otil-{LVL:SUR}  →  hlušh-otilöeh-
```

The remaining deliberate placeholder was:

```text
<2m:CMP>
```

v2025 named the next live question explicitly:

> can the than-term now be safely realized as the bare second-person neutral Referential in Comparative case: `sue`?

This checkpoint answers that question.

## What this checkpoint now freezes

### 1. the than-term is a Referential, not a formative

The English segment under audit is small:

```text
something more than you
```

The “you” here is not a property, event, role, social identity, body, psyche, personhood-concept, or discourse-named entity.
It is simply the addressee functioning as the Y-term of a comparison.

So the archive should not make a full personal-reference formative unless additional morphology is needed.
A full formative would be justified if the text needed something like:

```text
you-as-a-person
you-as-your-embodied-self
you-as-your-psychic-essence
you-as-the-entity-in-my-life
```

But the source says only:

```text
than you
```

A bare Referential is therefore the cleanest and most canonical structure for the Y-term.
It carries exactly the needed information:

```text
referent = addressee
case     = Comparative
```

No Specification, Context, Function, CA, VXCS affix, second stacked case, or root semantics are required.

### 2. the addressee is monadic `2m`, not polyadic `2p`

The Lacan quotation is framed as a direct “I love you” address.
Nothing in the passage suggests a plural addressee.
The archive should therefore preserve the intimate singular/addressee reading:

```text
2m = monadic addressee
```

not:

```text
2p = polyadic addressee
```

That makes the neutral Referential consonant:

```text
2m / neutral effect = s
```

A plural reading would instead begin from:

```text
2p / neutral effect = n
```

and would produce a different candidate:

```text
nue = “than you-all / than you-plural”
```

That is not the present line.
The packet should use singular `s-`.

### 3. the referential effect remains Neutral, not Beneficial or Detrimental

A tempting mistake would be to mark the addressee as Detrimental because the whole sentence ends in mutilation:

```text
žue
```

or to mark the addressee as Beneficial because the sentence begins with love:

```text
šue
```

Both would misplace the effect.
The harm belongs to the final mutilating predicate.
The love relation and its puzzling object belong to the framed middle clause.
The comparison term itself merely supplies the standard against which the “something” exceeds the addressee.

Therefore the comparison Y-term stays effect-neutral:

```text
s = 2m / neutral addressee
```

The archive should not load the final injury into the comparative pronoun.

### 4. the case is `CMP`, because `SUR` requires a Y-term in Comparative case

v2025 already froze the host X-term as carrying relative SURPASSIVE Level:

```text
hlušh-otilöeh-
```

The official semantic pattern of relative Level is:

```text
X is more M than Y
```

For the Y-term, the grammar’s explicit instruction is to decline the Y noun into Comparative case.
That means the addressee must be:

```text
2m:CMP
```

The Comparative case vowel-form is:

```text
CMP = ue / ië
```

The `ië` alternant is only the relevant choice in the environment that calls for it; there is no preceding `w` trigger here.
So the default surface for a bare `s` + CMP is:

```text
s + ue = sue
```

This is now the new frozen surface.

### 5. `sue` is a complete single-referent Referential

The single-referent Referential pattern is:

```text
C1 + VC1
```

where:

```text
C1  = Referential A
VC1 = Case of Referential A
```

For this packet:

```text
C1  = s    2m neutral addressee
VC1 = ue   Comparative case
```

Therefore:

```text
sue
```

No additional slot is filled.
There is no Referential B.
There is no second stacked case.
There is no C_P carrier/quotation/naming material inside this referential.
There is no epenthetic `ë`, because the word begins with a simple permitted consonant `s` and the vowel form follows immediately.

So the old placeholder:

```text
<2m:CMP>
```

is now replaced by:

```text
sue
```

### 6. specialized personal-reference roots are rejected for this exact spot

The grammar allows specialized personal-reference roots, and v2025 was right to leave a pass of caution around them.
But this location does not need one.

The specialized-root route would be attractive if the translation wanted to treat “you” as a full lexical/formative head, e.g.:

```text
you as your identity
you as your body
you as your psychic self
you as the place/entity through which I act or associate
```

That kind of interpretation may become relevant elsewhere in the sentence, especially in the “in you” phrase.
Indeed the middle clause already has a separate `<2m:ITP>` placeholder for the in/through-you relation.

But the comparative Y-term is narrower.
It is not “in you.”
It is not “you as embodied object.”
It is just the standard in:

```text
something more than you
```

So the archive should not inflate it.
A bare Referential with Comparative case is the most source-near solution.

### 7. no case-stacking is needed

Another possible overbuild would be a Combination Referential with case-stacking.
That would be useful if the addressee had to bear one case while also carrying an additional relation.
For example, a construction might need something like:

```text
you-as-comparison-standard and as possessor
you-as-comparison-standard and as interpretive frame
you-as-comparison-standard and as named carrier target
```

This line does not require that.
The Y-term has only one overt grammatical job:

```text
CMP
```

Therefore the simple `sue` wins over any stacked or combination form.

### 8. `sue` belongs adjacent to the SUR-marked host, before the carrier label

The local structure after v2025 was:

```text
[host-X carrying SUR] [Y-term in CMP] [carrier label identifying the host]
```

That order should be retained:

```text
hlušh-otilöeh- sue esälu’u «objet petit a» hü
```

The reason is interpretive clarity.
Placing `sue` immediately after the host makes the comparison legible as a unit:

```text
hlušh-otilöeh- sue
= a salient something more-than you
```

The carrier phrase then follows as an appositive or identifying label:

```text
esälu’u «objet petit a» hü
= namely / identified as “objet petit a”
```

If the carrier label were placed between the host and `sue`, the comparison would become harder to parse.
The archive should keep the than-term close to the SUR-bearing X-term.

### 9. `sue` is inside the object packet, but it is not itself the object of love

The readable line uses:

```text
OBJ:[hlušh-otilöeh- sue esälu’u «objet petit a» hü]
```

This is archive notation, not a claim that every internal item is independently the object of the love predicate.
The semantic object is the host:

```text
hlušh-otilöeh-
```

The `sue` term is licensed by the host’s `SUR` Level as the comparison standard.
The carrier phrase names or identifies the host.

So, internally:

```text
host-X      = the salient something-more
CMP-Y       = you
RLT label   = objet petit a
```

Externally, the entire packet remains the local object of the framed “I love in/through you …” clause.

### 10. this pass still does not freeze the final Slot-IX case of `otilöeh-`

The host remains written with a trailing hyphen:

```text
hlušh-otilöeh-
```

That is deliberate.
The host still needs a final Slot-IX case relative to the middle predicate.
The archive has not yet chosen whether the whole object packet should be surfaced via the most direct object-like case, a thematic/appositional strategy, or a locally scoped relation governed by the framed middle predicate.

The gain in this pass is narrower:

```text
<2m:CMP>  →  sue
```

not:

```text
hlušh-otilöeh-  →  final fully cased host word
```

That larger case decision is now the next live frontier.

## Local packet after v2026

The v2025 packet was:

```text
hlušh-otilöeh- <2m:CMP> esälu’u «objet petit a» hü
```

The v2026 packet is:

```text
hlušh-otilöeh- sue esälu’u «objet petit a» hü
```

Glossed in archive-style components:

```text
hlušh-        Type-1 salience/prominence prehead
otilöeh-      Stem-0 OBJ demonstrative parent head + relative SUR Level + natural Case-Scope
sue           2m neutral Referential in Comparative case; “than you”
esälu’u       carrier-root formative in Relative case
«objet petit a» unchanged foreign theoretical label
hü            carrier-end boundary marker
```

Working English control-reread:

> **a salient something-more-than-you, namely “objet petit a”**

or more literally:

> **a salient discourse-specified something surpassing you, with you as the comparison standard, identified by the foreign label “objet petit a.”**

## Rejected or demoted alternatives

### A. leave `<2m:CMP>` semi-overt

Demoted.
That was appropriate in v2025 while the archive checked whether a bare Referential was safe.
It is no longer the preferred running line.

### B. use a specialized personal-reference root

Rejected for this slot.
The than-term does not need full formative semantics.
The “in you” term may still need deeper treatment later, but the comparison standard does not.

### C. use `nue`

Rejected.
That would be plural/polyadic “you,” not the singular direct addressee of the quotation.

### D. use `šue` or `žue`

Rejected.
The comparison standard is neutral in effect.
Love and mutilation are represented elsewhere in the clause.

### E. place `sue` after the carrier phrase

Rejected for now.
The most legible local order keeps the SUR-bearing host and the CMP Y-term adjacent:

```text
hlušh-otilöeh- sue
```

before the RLT carrier-label apposition:

```text
esälu’u «objet petit a» hü
```

### F. treat `objet petit a` itself as the comparative Y-term

Rejected.
The foreign phrase names the “something more than you”; it is not the thing to which the “something” is compared.
The Y-term remains the addressee.

## Provisional takeaway

The archive should now carry the Lacan object packet forward as:

```text
hlušh-otilöeh- sue esälu’u «objet petit a» hü
```

This is the first local version where both halves of the comparison are overt:

```text
X-term with SUR:  hlušh-otilöeh-
Y-term in CMP:   sue
```

The next deep pass should decide the final Slot-IX case of the host/object packet relative to the framed middle predicate, i.e. what replaces the trailing hyphen in:

```text
hlušh-otilöeh-
```
