# q_lacan_current_grammar_hlenfo_retirement_bare_evvroil_pass_v2040

**Date:** 2026-05-25  
**Project:** Ithkuil translation of the love text — Lacan quotation micro-passage  
**Source span:** “I love you, but, because inexplicably I love in you something more than you—the objet petit a—I mutilate you.”  
**Pass type:** current-public grammar audit of inherited `hleňfó`; retirement of non-current ŇF₂ mystery prehead; safe bare frame-head baseline

---

## 1. Scope of this revision

v2039 successfully moved the middle clause from a bracketed analytic wrapper into current-public case-frame morphology:

```text
<FRAMED:SIT[ hleňfó-évvroal- ... ]>
        ↓
hleňfó-évvroîl ...
```

The v2039 note deliberately left `hleňfó` as the highest-risk survivor. v2040 audits that survivor.

The result is a **negative but important freeze**: `hleňfó` must be retired from the current working line. The current public lexicon no longer supports the old `ŇF₂ = mystery / puzzling` interpretation, and the old hyphenated Type-1 concatenated-prehead machinery is no longer the grammar lane I want to compound into the post-v2037 current-public branch.

Main replacement:

```text
hleňfó-évvroîl  →  évvroîl
```

This means the line temporarily loses a fully surfaced equivalent of English “inexplicably.” That is intentional. A known semantic debt is better than carrying a false morpheme that now points to the wrong root family.

## 2. Working bridge line after v2040

### v2039 line being audited

```text
rkwal ti kui ; hleňfó-évvroîl köi ti hlušh-otilöehui kè upeal «objet petit a» ; iňň aixtřas to ke
```

### v2040 current working line

```text
rkwal ti kui ; évvroîl köi ti hlušh-otilöehui kè upeal «objet petit a» ; iňň aixtřas to ke
```

Human-readable back-translation after this pass:

> I love you; given the background situation that through/in you I undergo infatuated/obsessive attachment toward the salient something-more-than-you, identified as “objet petit a,” nevertheless I maim you.

What is missing is the English adverbial burden “inexplicably.” v2040 does not erase it from the target intention; it marks it as **not yet safely surfaced**.

## 3. Why `hleňfó` cannot remain current-canonical

Earlier passes treated `hleňfó` as an old-style local prehead built from an `ŇF₂` “mystery / puzzling / inexplicable” root. That made sense in the older project branch, where the archive was leaning on New-Ithkuil-era lexical material and old concatenation expectations.

Under the current-public grammar/lexicon branch, however, `-ŇF-` is not that root. The current lexicon lists:

```text
-ŇF- : ‘sliver-moon shaped’
```

So if the active line keeps `hleňfó`, it risks saying something like:

> sliver-moon-shapedly / moon-sickle-ishly I love in you ...

rather than:

> inexplicably I love in you ...

That is too semantically catastrophic to leave as a live surface form.

---

## 4. Structural reason to retire, not merely rename

The problem is not only lexical. The old `hleňfó-` packet was also a pre-v2037 structural survivor:

```text
ŇF₂ prehead + VVR₂ parent head
```

The current public verb grammar explicitly frames expansion of a verbal formative through **stem incorporation** and suffixal morphology. It does not give me a reason, in this pass, to keep an old hyphenated Type-1 concatenated-prehead surface in front of `évvroîl` and simply swap the root.

Therefore v2040 does **not** make a speculative replacement such as:

```text
?šx- + évvroîl
?nd- + évvroîl
?xh- + évvroîl
```

Those may become relevant later, but only after a current-public derivation is spelled out through the correct incorporation / suffix / adjunct path.

---

## 5. Candidate lanes for re-surfacing “inexplicably” later

The English adverb “inexplicably” is not a simple lexical adjective. In the Lacan sentence it does at least three things at once:

1. it says the speaker’s loving-in-you is **not transparent to explanation**;
2. it says the relation between “you” and the `objet petit a` excess is **not rationally settled**;
3. it prepares the violent non sequitur: love gives no gentle guarantee, and the causal connection to mutilation is not morally explanatory in the ordinary sense.

Under current-public materials, three routes are plausible but not yet frozen.

### 5.1 `-ŠX-` route: puzzlement/curiosity

Current `-ŠX-` is close in affective tone:

```text
-ŠX- : feeling of puzzlement and curiosity, a desire to solve a mystery
```

Problem: this is a **feeling of puzzlement**, not necessarily the objective property of being inexplicable. If applied naively, it may shift the line toward:

> I, feeling puzzled, love in you ...

That would make the speaker’s conscious puzzlement too prominent.

### 5.2 `-ND-` route: question / inquiry / answer / response

Current `-ND-` offers a more epistemic path:

```text
-ND- : QUESTION / INQUIRY / ANSWER / RESPONSE
```

Its stem field includes inquiry, investigation, analysis, answer/result, problem/solution/resolution-style derivatives. This may be the best future route if we can safely build something like:

> lacking / not-yet-having / resistant-to answer or elucidating reason

Problem: that requires a precise negative / absence / unresolved-answer construction, not a one-word guess.

### 5.3 `-Xh-` route: fact / know / learn / understand

Current `-Xh-` could support a knowledge/understanding construction:

```text
-Xh- : FACT / KNOW / LEARN / UNDERSTAND / EXPERTISE
```

Problem: “inexplicably” is not merely “unknown to me.” It suggests a deeper opacity of explanation or justification. A naïve “unknownly” form would undershoot the Lacanian burden.

## 6. Why the bare `évvroîl` baseline is better than a forced replacement

The source line is already hard enough:

```text
because inexplicably I love in you something more than you—the objet petit a—I mutilate you
```

The middle clause contains:

- the `VVR₂` affective/obsessive love-state;
- `köi`, the addressee as Interpretative “in/through you” locus;
- `ti`, the speaker as Affective experiencer;
- `hlušh-otilöehui kè`, the salient “something more than you” as DER/stimulus plus comparison target;
- `upeal «objet petit a»`, the current carrier apposition;
- `SIT + FRAMED` on the head itself via `évvroîl`.

That is already a high-density Ithkuil packet. Leaving an incorrect inherited prehead on top of it would be worse than leaving “inexplicably” in an explicit debt ledger.

So v2040 freezes this editorial rule:

```text
Never retain a pre-v2037 inherited lexical surface solely because it carried useful English gloss pressure.
If the current-public lexicon reassigns that root, retire the form and rederive.
```

- `köi` as the current 2m Interpretative “in/through you” referential;
- `ti` as the current 1m Affective experiencer;
- `hlušh-otilöehui kè` as the “salient something-more-than-you” comparison packet;
- `upeal «objet petit a»` as the current carrier-root apposition;
- `iňň aixtřas to ke` as the final “nevertheless I mutilate you” packet.

It only removes the now-unsound `hleňfó` burden.
