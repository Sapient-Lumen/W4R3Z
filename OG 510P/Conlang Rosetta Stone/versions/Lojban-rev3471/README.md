# Lojban rev3471: making “the other” explicit

This edition lets a reader follow a philosophical sentence through a concrete translation decision. Its latest revision returns to the Aquinas formulation, “To love is to will the good of the other,” and asks what the translation must say rather than leave to charitable interpretation. The package retains the preceding v3470 compilation, the revised v3471 compilation and their patch, so the decision remains inspectable.

## Three decisions inside one sentence

The first concerns who is being loved. As the [supplied revision note](files/README_rev3471.md) explains, using two different variables, `da` and `de`, does not by itself establish that they refer to different beings. Rev3471 adds an explicit condition, `drata da`, to distinguish the other from the lover. This is not merely a more elaborate spelling: it makes a relationship previously left implicit part of the sentence's stated meaning.

The second concerns the good being chosen. The archive retains `cuxna` for willing or choosing, and `xamgu be de` for what is good or beneficial for the other. That connection keeps the beneficiary in view. “The good” is not presented here as a detached object whose relationship to the beloved the reader must supply. Willing-as-choosing remains the translation project's interpretation, not a claim that one lexical decision exhausts the philosophical question.

The third concerns scope. The new opening, `ro da ro de … zo'u`, presents the sentence as a general equivalence concerning any lover and any distinct other. The work is trying to formulate what loving means, not merely describe one occasion on which someone chooses something beneficial.

## Read the decision in four places

1. Start with the short [revision note](files/README_rev3471.md), then the [v3471 opening](files/lojban_love_translation_master_UPDATED_2026-05-30_v3471.md#L1-L41), which states the repair and its intended meaning.
2. Read the [active phrase glossary](files/lojban_love_translation_master_UPDATED_2026-05-30_v3471.md#L7296-L7315) for “the good of the other” and the whole Aquinas sentence.
3. Compare the [quotation in the translated body](files/lojban_love_translation_master_UPDATED_2026-05-30_v3471.md#L7851-L7856).
4. Inspect the [patch](files/DIFF_rev3470_to_rev3471.patch): it shows the new explanation, glossary additions and changed quotation together. The [preceding compilation](files/lojban_love_translation_master_UPDATED_2026-05-30_v3470.md) preserves the earlier context.

This route matters because the compilation retains a long history of alternative formulations. A search result can lead to an older decision rather than the active passage. The latest explanation, glossary and body should be read together; the preceding draft makes comparison possible.

The neighboring Matthew passage offers a second encounter with the same kind of work. Its retained [v3470 note](files/lojban_love_translation_master_UPDATED_2026-05-30_v3471.md#L45-L99) replaces strict logical entailment with metaphorical support when describing the Law and Prophets as hanging on the commandments. Read that rationale beside the [active passage](files/lojban_love_translation_master_UPDATED_2026-05-30_v3471.md#L7793-L7808). Precision can mean refusing a stronger assertion, not merely making an assertion more explicit.

These are evolving translations with documented interpretations, not an independently certified final Lojban text. The grammatical explanations above describe the project's decisions; this guide adds no translation.
