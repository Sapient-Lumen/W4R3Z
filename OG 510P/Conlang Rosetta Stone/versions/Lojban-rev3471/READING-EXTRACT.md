## Session note v3471
- Continued from rev3470 with an explicit **Aquinas otherness / variable-identity / goodwill-good-of-the-other** cube audit/refactor.
- Repaired the live Aquinas quotation so **the other** is no longer only implied by using `da` and `de`; it is now explicit as `de poi ke'a drata da ku'o`.
- Recast the line under a universal prenex: `ro da ro de poi ... zo'u`, so the quote reads as a general equivalence for any lover and any other beloved/contextual other.
- Preserved the archive's settled lexical spine: `prami` for love, `cuxna` for willing/choosing, and `xamgu be de` for the good/benefit of the other.
- Added active phrase-glossary locks for **the good of the other**, **will the good of the other**, and the full Aquinas sentence so future passes do not recover the line from older unbound-`da/de` notes.

```lojban id="v3471-aquinas-phrase-glossary-refactor"
.i zoi gy. the good of the other .gy cu se smuni

  .i to pe'a zoi gy. what is good or beneficial for a contextual other .gy toi.
.i zoi gy. will the good of the other .gy cu se smuni
  lo si'o zo'e cuxna lo xamgu be lo drata be zo'e

.i zoi gy. To love is to will the good of the other. .gy cu se smuni
  lo du'u ro da ro de poi ke'a drata da ku'o zo'u
    lo nu da prami de cu du lo nu da cuxna lo xamgu be de
  .i to pe'a zoi gy. for any lover and any other, loving is choosing that other's good .gy toi.
```

Best compact reading:

**For any one and any other, for the first to love the second is for the first to choose what is good for the second.**

Why this is better:
- `da/de` alone does not guarantee otherness; the explicit `drata da` condition does.
- `ro da ro de ... zo'u` makes the Aquinas line a general definitional equivalence, not a single local existential event.
- `xamgu be de` keeps **the good of the other** as what benefits the other, not as an abstract good detached from the beneficiary.
- The active glossary now preserves this as a stable mini-cube: **love → will/choice → good → otherness**.

## Session note v3470
- Continued from rev3469 with an explicit **Matthew commandment / whole-self devotion / support-not-entailment** cube audit/refactor.
- Repaired **with all your heart / soul / mind** from a generic `fi'o se srana ...` relation into `sepi'o ro lo pagbu be ...`: love is performed using/involving every part of the emotional, spiritual, and mental self.
- Repaired the active **neighbor** glossary from a literal `do`-anchored entry to a contextual-nearness entry: `lo prenu poi ke'a jibni zo'e`.
- Repaired **On these two commandments hang all...** from `se nibli` to metaphorical support, `pe'a se sarji le re te minde`, so the line reads as dependence/support rather than strict logical entailment.
- Added active phrase-glossary locks for the three whole-self modals, the first commandment line, the Law/Prophets support line, and the whole commandment block.

Best compact reading:

**You are obligated to love The Good with every part of your emotional, spiritual, and mental self; the neighboring-person commandment is like the first; and the Law/Prophets corpus hangs from, or is supported by, those two commanded duties.**

Why this is better:
- `sepi'o` keeps the **with all** phrase in the use/instrument/involvement cube rather than the weaker relatedness cube.
- `ro lo pagbu be ...` makes **all** explicit as every part/aspect of each self-faculty, not a vague totalizing modifier.
- `sarji` keeps the final line closer to the source metaphor of hanging/support/dependence than `nibli`, which over-logicizes the image into strict entailment.
- The active glossary now preserves the commandment block as a coherent cube, so future passes do not have to recover it from older historical phrase notes.
