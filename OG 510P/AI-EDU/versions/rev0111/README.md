# AI-EDU — rev0111

## A deadline should begin when the decision becomes possible

A learner receives a replacement study route on Monday. Financial assistance is still only an estimate; its actual payment arrives on Thursday. A separate review depends on a later formal decision. One notice may describe all three developments, but its mailing date does not make them happen together.

rev0111 asks how an institution can keep a learner moving without turning incomplete information into a deadline trap. Its contribution is a proposed rule for **separate decision clocks**: choosing a route, responding to a money decision and using an external review process can depend on different events. A tidy shared date is warranted only when the underlying decisions really are ready together.

The [original continuity document](files/docs/30-operations/no-fault-transition-cost-absorption-and-fee-waiver-rules.md) develops this inside a broader account of system-caused disruption: the learner should not become the default financier of the repair, and an institutional reimbursement dispute should not automatically produce a new learner invoice. Its final sections follow that practical concern into information, action, notice and timing. These are institutional design proposals, not a deployed deadline service or a statement of present legal deadlines.

## An estimate, a pending answer and a disputed answer are different

A packet of information can help before every question is settled. The proposal distinguishes five states:

- **Fixed for this packet:** sufficiently settled to state as the current answer, not guaranteed never to change.
- **Bounded estimate:** useful for orientation but not an actual charge, payment or approval.
- **Pending confirmation:** another office or event must supply an answer; delay is not itself denial.
- **Disputed:** a position exists, but its challenge must remain visible rather than disappear behind the institution's preferred answer.
- **Unknown:** no honest answer or estimate is available yet; blank space must not silently mean zero, none or ineligible.

The document's `UF0–UF4` states are therefore more than warning labels. Each unresolved item should carry its last confirmed date, last settled fact, responsible party or dependency, next meaningful review point, interim protection and what the learner can presently do. Naming a problem without a next step would leave the learner holding the institution's uncertainty.

## Keep options usable without inventing a final choice

The next distinction is between information and commitment. Comparing destinations, ranking preferences and reserving a reversible place need not surrender existing protection. A temporary arrangement can also be useful when its downside is bounded, correction is possible without learner loss and the unresolved parts remain protected. None of those acts automatically becomes a final transfer, settlement or waiver.

The proposal allows ordinary running of a consequential decision clock only when every fact material to that particular decision is settled enough. Irrelevant unfinished detail does not freeze everything. Conversely, a missing fact about actual cost or support can be decisive even if the route itself is clear.

A harmless reservation timer is a different case: it may release a genuinely reversible hold, but must not conceal lost rights, an undisclosed priority penalty or conversion of silence into commitment. This is how the account avoids two extremes, forcing people to commit on uncertain facts and preventing every useful provisional step.

## Three ways clocks can relate

Read the final `SY0–SY3` section of the [source](files/docs/30-operations/no-fault-transition-cost-absorption-and-fee-waiver-rules.md) through the Monday/Thursday example.

**Separate clocks are the default.** If the money decision depends on actual posting, the route-ready notice cannot backdate that money clock to Monday. If an external process depends on a later final decision or other operative trigger, an internal update does not substitute for that event. Different responsible parties and dependencies stay visible.

**One common start can be justified.** The same refreshed packet must resolve the same material ambiguity for every listed decision; all must be ready on common facts; none may still await a later act, posting or external trigger. Shared presentation is not the reason. A genuinely shared basis is.

**An individualized supplement can require its own start.** If a particular learner cannot make the relevant decision until their specific route, money or rights information arrives, an earlier group notice does not give them that missing opportunity. The proposed clock begins when the necessary supplement reaches that learner, not when a generic message reached everyone else.

The dates here are illustrative, not a source-reported case or a prescribed number of days. The archive supplies conditions for a fair start, not one universal timetable.

## Restart means renewed notice, not resurrection of an old date

Resolving uncertainty does not alone provide a usable chance to act. The preceding sections require a restart notice to identify what changed, which decision is now live, its new start and deadline or review point, who is responsible, and what inaction would mean. Delivery should preserve an accessible channel.

An adverse restart needs a real nonzero buffer; where restart depends on an individualized supplement, newly live external-rights option or material shift in available options, the proposal requires a larger buffer. Late, contradictory or inaccessible notice is the institution's problem to repair, not evidence of learner lateness: the adverse clock pauses again until corrected notice. The text does not fix one numeric grace period for every sector.

A reminder reinforces an unchanged opportunity. If the operative facts, date, responsible party or consequence change again, the proposal calls for a fresh notice rather than disguising the change as a reminder. That distinction gives the temporal scheme its point: time to choose must be time in which the choice can actually be understood and exercised.
