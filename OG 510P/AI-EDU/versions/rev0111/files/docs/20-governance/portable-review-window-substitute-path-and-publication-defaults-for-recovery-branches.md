# Portable review-window, substitute-path, and publication defaults for recovery branches

This document closes the archive's next recovery-governance gap: **which of the named recovery child branches are portable enough to inherit shared review-window bands, substitute-path expectations, and publication floors, and which still remain too office- or regime-specific to travel beyond the floor.**

The archive's current bet is:

> keep the generic packet-and-signal rule, the sector/context starter recovery profiles, and the child-branch map, but add one tiny portability layer so institutions do not say “we use the same recovery branch” while hiding materially different review timing, interim protection, or publication duties in local process notes.

The point is to avoid four predictable failures at once:

- **branch-name fiction** — institutions reuse a recovery branch label even though the real review timing or interim protection has changed in ways learners would actually care about;
- **silent-penalty drift** — recovery remains nominally “supportive” while the learner quietly loses ordinary place, comparable access, or timing position before any named human review;
- **publication-floor collapse** — recovery child branches are named in design discussions but never exposed to teachers, learners, or auditors as live operating defaults;
- **fake universality** — the archive pretends one portable timing or substitute rule fits welfare, queue, readiness, standing, and eligibility treatment even when only a thinner floor really travels.

Current public signals point in the same direction. OECD's 2026 outlook still pushes GenAI toward clear pedagogical purpose rather than generic task completion; RAND's March 2026 student signal still warns that use and concern about critical-thinking harm are rising together; UNESCO's current teacher/student competency frameworks still keep human agency and AI pedagogy central; OpenAI's 2026 learning-outcomes work increases pressure for longitudinal evaluation without turning recovery into learner dossiers; and current ICO guidance keeps minimisation, design-by-default, and child-facing anti-profiling expectations visible. The DfE's product-safety standards reinforce the same direction by treating cognitive, emotional, and developmental effects as product-governance issues rather than optional local cautions. See `B11`, `B17`, `B102`, `B151`, `B154`, `B155`, `B156`.

## Four portable review-window bands

The archive still refuses to publish one universal number-of-days rule. It now names four **event-relative review-window bands** that may travel because they govern sequencing, not local merits.

| Band | Meaning | What it does not decide |
|---|---|---|
| `RW-NEXT-CHECKPOINT` | named human review before the learner would otherwise hit the next ordinary study / support checkpoint while still carrying the same recovery condition | grading standards, welfare judgments, or the content of the instructional reset |
| `RW-BEFORE-DURABLE-SHAPE` | named human review before the recovery branch would otherwise shape a durable record, readiness, queue, eligibility, or standing effect | the substantive standard for readiness, discipline, aid, queue priority, or eligibility |
| `RW-NEXT-EXPOSURE` | named human review before the next supervised live performance, client-facing exposure, or external-practice step when full pause is not already presumed | licensing rules, partner acceptance rules, or legally fixed no-exposure duties |
| `RW-FIRST-STABLE` | ex post named human review in the first stable window after an urgent or support-linked intervention when earlier review is not truthful or possible | safeguarding thresholds, statutory response duties, or emergency calendars |

These are **bands**, not clocks. The archive is still refusing to smuggle local law, school calendars, conduct timetables, or route-service statutes into a supposedly portable template.

## Four substitute-path families that may travel one step further

The archive now treats four substitute-path families as portable enough to name, while still refusing fake universal guarantees.

| Family | Meaning | What it preserves | What it does not promise |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SP-ORDINARY-RETURN` | ordinary study/help remains open with a cooler default, bounded reset, and named handback into normal participation | the learner is not trapped in perpetual special handling | success, mastery, or exemption from ordinary course demands |
| `SP-SUPERVISED-EVIDENCE` | supervised continuation, simulation, extra evidence, or nearby evidence-building path remains available while recovery is reviewed | dignity and forward motion without pretending the original condition has disappeared | exact equivalence to live placement, grading outcome, or readiness sign-off |
| `SP-NO-SILENT-PENALTY` | reserve, protected re-entry, non-worsening treatment, or the nearest comparable path remains visible while review is pending | the learner does not quietly lose ordinary place merely because review is slower than the branch | original timestamp, exact seat, exact ranking, or a legally barred opportunity |
| `SP-MINIMAL-LOSS` | where stronger preservation is impossible, publish the least damaging truthful route that remains open until the first stable review | some humane continuity floor remains visible | a full pause right, full access, or a universal anti-forfeiture rule |

## Two publication floors

The archive now distinguishes between a lighter **support floor** and a hotter **treatment floor**.

### `PF-SUPPORT`

For a recovery branch that remains pedagogical or informational, publish:

1. the child-branch identifier;
2. whether ordinary recovery prefers `RP0` or allows bounded `RP1`;
3. the review-window band in use;
4. the substitute-path family in use;
5. the named owner of review and handback;
6. the prohibited joins or downstream uses;
7. the trigger for returning to ordinary support or for moving onto a hotter rail.

### `PF-TREATMENT`

For a branch that can shape record-bearing, readiness-bearing, queue-bearing, or eligibility-bearing treatment, publish the same seven fields plus:

8. the durable effect family that the branch can shape;
9. the earliest meaningful review point before that durable effect;
10. the strongest interim non-worsening or minimal-loss statement the service is actually prepared to honor.

That is the archive's current publication floor. It is enough to make recovery legible without publishing welfare files, queue formulas, or local legal annexes.

## Which recovery child branches now truly carry shared overlays

The archive can now close the broad form of `OQ-0028`. The question is no longer whether the child branches exist. It is whether their **review-window band**, **substitute-path family**, and **publication floor** can travel unchanged.

| Child branch | Review-window portability | Substitute-path portability | Publication-floor portability | Current archive disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `RD-K12-OPTIONAL-STUDY` | `RW-NEXT-CHECKPOINT` may harden unchanged | `SP-ORDINARY-RETURN` may harden unchanged | `PF-SUPPORT` may harden unchanged | **portable unchanged** |
| `RD-K12-REQUIRED-SUPPORT` | `RW-FIRST-STABLE` may harden unchanged at the floor, and `RW-BEFORE-DURABLE-SHAPE` applies whenever attendance, discipline, or welfare treatment is next | no stronger portable family hardens beyond `SP-MINIMAL-LOSS`, because wellbeing, safeguarding, attendance, and family-treatment regimes differ too much | `PF-TREATMENT` may harden unchanged at the floor | **partial portability; floor plus local residue** |
| `RD-HE-COURSE-BOUND` | `RW-NEXT-CHECKPOINT` may harden unchanged | `SP-ORDINARY-RETURN` may harden unchanged | `PF-SUPPORT` may harden unchanged | **portable unchanged** |
| `RD-HE-RECORD-ADJACENT` | `RW-BEFORE-DURABLE-SHAPE` may harden unchanged | no stronger portable family hardens beyond `SP-NO-SILENT-PENALTY`, because standing, aid, misconduct, and accommodation processes differ too much | `PF-TREATMENT` may harden unchanged | **partial portability; no universal interim package** |
| `RD-PRO-COACH` | `RW-NEXT-EXPOSURE` may harden unchanged where supervised performance continues | `SP-SUPERVISED-EVIDENCE` may harden unchanged | `PF-SUPPORT` may harden unchanged | **portable unchanged** |
| `RD-PRO-READINESS` | `RW-BEFORE-DURABLE-SHAPE` may harden unchanged, and `RW-NEXT-EXPOSURE` still applies where full pause is not presumed | no stronger portable family hardens beyond `SP-SUPERVISED-EVIDENCE` at a very abstract floor, because licensure, remediation, and safety regimes vary too much | `PF-TREATMENT` may harden unchanged | **partial portability; floor plus regime-local residue** |
| `RD-ROUTE-INFO` | `RW-NEXT-CHECKPOINT` may harden unchanged | `SP-ORDINARY-RETURN` may harden unchanged so long as no scarce opportunity is being allocated | `PF-SUPPORT` may harden unchanged | **portable unchanged** |
| `RD-ROUTE-TREATMENT` | `RW-BEFORE-DURABLE-SHAPE` may harden unchanged | no stronger portable family hardens beyond `SP-NO-SILENT-PENALTY` or, where even that is untruthful, `SP-MINIMAL-LOSS` | `PF-TREATMENT` may harden unchanged | **partial portability; queue and eligibility rules remain local** |

Four narrower judgments now follow:

1. **ordinary pedagogical recovery branches really can carry shared overlays** — optional minor study help, course-bound higher-ed recovery, professional coaching, and informational public-route help now deserve portable review-window, substitute-path, and publication expectations because their live problem is re-entry truth rather than consequence-bearing treatment;
2. **consequence-bearing branches only harden one step** — record-adjacent, readiness-adjacent, and route-treatment branches now deserve a shared review-before-durable-effect rule plus a treatment publication floor, but not one universal interim package;
3. **required/support-linked minor recovery stays cooler than institutions may want** — it earns a portable first-stable review floor and a treatment publication floor, but the archive still refuses to harden one universal wellbeing / attendance / safeguarding substitute path;
4. **“same branch” no longer excuses hidden place-loss or hidden delay** — if the service cannot publish either a truthful `SP-NO-SILENT-PENALTY` statement or a truthful `SP-MINIMAL-LOSS` statement where treatment is consequence-bearing, it should not claim to be running a portable recovery branch at all.

## What remains permanently local

Three things still remain local even after this portability layer:

1. **merits criteria** — what counts as sufficient evidence, readiness, welfare risk, attendance concern, aid eligibility, misconduct, or route priority;
2. **regime-bound timing** — exact calendar deadlines, legal notice windows, partner timetables, cycle dates, and statutory clocks;
3. **outcome formulas** — grade rules, conduct sanctions, aid calculations, queue ordering, placement allocation, and formal safeguarding or eligibility effects.

The archive therefore rejects both extremes:

- one universal anti-penalty template for every recovery child branch;
- and one opaque local-code story in which even review timing and interim protection cannot harden one step further.

## What now counts as a rebuttal packet rather than a new shared overlay

Keep a departure local when it is genuinely episodic, partner-specific, or too thin to deserve a portable overlay. The archive's current examples are:

- one school's temporary counselling or attendance staffing collapse;
- one course's short calendar anomaly that shifts the next meaningful checkpoint;
- one professional site's brief placement freeze or safety restriction;
- one route-service cycle disruption that temporarily changes what the nearest comparable slot can mean.

If the same departure reason keeps recurring across sites, programmes, or cycles, the archive should stop treating it as a mere packet and instead split, cool, or tighten the named branch.

## What institutions should publish now

When a recurring official study companion, tutoring surface, coaching surface, or route-help service uses one of these child branches, publish:

1. the child-branch identifier;
2. whether it is operating under `PF-SUPPORT` or `PF-TREATMENT`;
3. the inherited review-window band in use;
4. the inherited substitute-path family in use;
5. the named owner of review;
6. the strongest truthful interim protection statement;
7. the durable effect family, if any;
8. the trigger for handback, hotter rail, or human-only handling.

That is enough to stop recovery from hiding inside vague “student support” language while staying well below a dossier or rulebook dump.

## What counted as a real archive gain

The archive already knew that recovery should stay packet-light, aggregate-first, and earlier-human-owned once dependence stops being ordinary study friction. It then learned that not all recovery contexts share the same parent profile, and that some child branches are already predictable enough to name. It still lacked a compact answer to a harder operational question: **what actually travels with those child branches besides the label?**

This document supplies that missing layer. It keeps the generic packet-and-signal rule, the starter profile layer, and the child-branch map, but now distinguishes among:

- branches whose review timing, interim substitute family, and publication floor can travel unchanged;
- branches that deserve a shared review-before-durable-effect rule and a hotter publication floor but still do **not** justify one universal interim package;
- and branches whose strongest truthful continuity promise still stops at `SP-MINIMAL-LOSS` rather than a stronger anti-penalty default.

That is a real operating gain because it blocks three opposite failures at once:

- child-branch names with no published operating consequences;
- support language masking silent penalty or place-loss;
- and fake universal substitute guarantees where only a thinner humane floor really travels.

## Current archive bet

The archive's current best guess is that **a generic recovery grammar plus starter profiles plus a child-branch map plus a tiny portability layer for review windows, substitute families, and publication floors** will outperform both extremes:

- one universal recovery profile for all official study support;
- and bespoke local recovery writing in which the archive can never harden even modest anti-hidden-penalty expectations.

That claim is now canon and now has a companion mini-code layer.
