# Mini-codes for partially portable recovery treatment branches

This document closes the archive's next recovery-governance gap: **which of the hotter recovery treatment branches deserve one further portable mini-code around non-worsening, minimal-loss continuity, or authority-sensitive handback, and which details should still remain permanently local.**

The archive's current bet is:

> keep the generic packet-and-signal rule, the sector/context starter recovery profiles, the child-branch map, and the portability layer for review windows, substitute paths, and publication floors — but add one tiny mini-code layer so the archive can say a little more than “floor plus local residue” without pretending that full merits criteria, queue formulas, welfare thresholds, or safety rules now travel universally.

The point is to avoid four predictable failures at once:

- **silent treatment drift** — recovery remains nominally supportive while pending review quietly worsens a learner's place, note, access, or route;
- **fake humane promises** — institutions publish a strong anti-penalty or continuity claim that local law, safety, or inventory rules cannot actually honor;
- **wrong-owner decisions** — a study or route-help surface behaves as though it can authorize record, readiness, or eligibility treatment that should belong to a named human owner;
- **local-residue sprawl** — every hotter recovery branch is said to be “special,” so the archive never hardens even a thin shared protection or publication norm.

Current public signals still support this thinner move. OECD's 2026 outlook keeps pushing GenAI toward clear pedagogical purpose rather than generic task completion; UNESCO's rights framing keeps privacy, autonomy, and the right to be heard in view; current product-safety guidance keeps cognitive, emotional, and developmental effects visible for learner-facing systems; current medical-education guidance reinforces that professional readiness remains a human-judgment domain; current learning-outcomes work keeps pressure on longitudinal measurement rather than short-run task success; and current ICO guidance keeps data minimisation, privacy by default, and child-facing profiling constraints visible. See `B11`, `B20`, `B94`, `B102`, `B151`, `B154`, `B155`, `B156`.

## Three mini-codes the archive now permits

The archive now permits only three portable mini-codes for the hotter recovery branches.

| Mini-code | Meaning | What it does | What it does not promise |
|---|---|---|---|
| `MC-NW` — non-worsening hold | pending review should not itself make the learner's durable treatment worse where a truthful comparable hold is still possible | names a bounded no-new-worse-position floor until the first meaningful review | the original seat, the original timestamp, unchanged rank, or a legally barred opportunity |
| `MC-ML` — minimal-loss continuity | where a truthful comparable hold is not possible, publish the least damaging meaningful path that remains open and the strongest non-punitive statement that can still be honored | keeps continuity humane without faking full preservation | full ordinary participation, full anti-forfeiture, or universal non-delay |
| `MC-AH` — authority-sensitive handback | only the named human owner of the consequence-bearing regime may impose, continue, or lift the durable effect | blocks study-support or route-help surfaces from acting as hidden record, readiness, queue, or eligibility authorities | a portable merits test, a universal owner list, or one cross-sector challenge calendar |

These mini-codes travel only one step further than the portability layer. They are not full local procedures.

## What each mini-code requires when published

### `MC-NW`

When an institution claims `MC-NW`, it should publish:

1. the relevant child branch;
2. the durable-effect family that is being held stable;
3. the earliest meaningful review point;
4. the strongest truthful no-worsening statement the service is prepared to honor;
5. the point at which the hold expires or must either convert to `MC-ML` or move onto a hotter rail.

`MC-NW` is truthful only when the same family of participation, record status, or route position can actually be held without lying about legal, safety, or inventory constraints.

### `MC-ML`

When an institution claims `MC-ML`, it should publish:

1. the relevant child branch;
2. why a stronger non-worsening hold would be untruthful;
3. the nearest meaningful path that remains open;
4. the strongest no-added-punishment statement the service can still honor;
5. the earliest meaningful review point or first stable review window.

`MC-ML` is the archive's humane floor when stronger preservation is impossible.

### `MC-AH`

When an institution claims `MC-AH`, it should publish:

1. the relevant child branch;
2. the named human owner or authority family;
3. the boundary between what the AI/service may surface and what only the authority owner may decide;
4. the interim path, if any, that remains open while that owner reviews;
5. the explanation or challenge route.

`MC-AH` is the archive's anti-hidden-authority rule. It prevents ordinary recovery from being quietly mistaken for lawful or professionally accountable treatment.

## The archive's narrower judgments branch by branch

### 1. Required/support-linked minor recovery deserves a humane floor, not a fake universal support package

`RD-K12-REQUIRED-SUPPORT` now earns one small further hardening step:

- **ordinary presumption:** `MC-ML`
- **thin extra floor:** no new attendance, discipline, or family-treatment worsening should occur solely because recovery is pending before the first stable review
- **owner rule:** once welfare, safeguarding, or formal attendance treatment is actually implicated, `MC-AH` should publish the named human owner

What does **not** travel:

- safeguarding thresholds;
- local child-protection process;
- exact attendance law consequences;
- family-notice rules or school-specific support entitlements.

The archive is intentionally refusing a fake universal child-support package.

### 2. Record-adjacent higher-ed recovery deserves a real non-worsening mini-code

`RD-HE-RECORD-ADJACENT` now earns the archive's clearest portable treatment mini-code:

- **ordinary presumption:** `MC-NW`
- **owner rule:** `MC-AH`
- **fallback:** use `MC-ML` only where a truthful hold is impossible

The practical archive rule is:

> pending recovery review should not, by itself, create a new adverse durable note or a loss of ordinary participation before the named record owner reviews the case.

What does **not** travel:

- financial-aid rules;
- misconduct procedures;
- accommodation documentation standards;
- academic-standing formulas;
- appeal calendars or local evidence burdens.

### 3. Professional readiness recovery deserves handback, not a portable promise of unchanged exposure

`RD-PRO-READINESS` now earns one portable gain and one deliberate refusal.

Portable gain:

- **ordinary presumption:** `MC-AH`
- **continuity floor:** nearest safe `SP-SUPERVISED-EVIDENCE` or simulation path where truthful

Deliberate refusal:

- the archive does **not** publish a general `MC-NW` promise for supervised exposure, live practice, or readiness sign-off.

That refusal is part of the design. It keeps the archive from saying “no worse treatment while review is pending” in settings where the truth may instead be “no unsupervised exposure until the accountable supervisor or safety owner reviews.”

What does **not** travel:

- exposure permission rules;
- licensure or regulator obligations;
- remediation content or duration;
- placement-owner constraints;
- safety thresholds and escalation law.

### 4. Public route-treatment recovery deserves a conditional non-worsening mini-code

`RD-ROUTE-TREATMENT` now earns a conditional split in the archive's judgment without creating a full new branch family.

- where a **comparable hold is truthful**, publish `MC-NW`;
- where scarcity, statutory timing, or partner inventory makes that untruthful, publish `MC-ML`;
- in either case, use `MC-AH` because the office that owns the slot, eligibility, or cross-agency case must own the treatment decision.

The archive's practical rule is:

> route-help surfaces may surface the problem and preserve the strongest truthful continuity statement, but they may not quietly act as the slot-owner, benefits-owner, or statutory case owner.

What does **not** travel:

- queue formulas;
- funded-seat inventory rules;
- statutory eligibility tests;
- local partner capacity;
- mandatory-participation or sanction calendars.

## What remains permanently local even after the mini-codes

The archive still refuses portability for four things:

1. **merits standards** — what actually counts as readiness, risk, eligibility, or sufficient evidence;
2. **irreversible-loss rules** — where law, safety, or real scarcity makes some losses impossible to prevent;
3. **exact challenge calendars** — notice windows, hearing steps, regulator clocks, and route-cycle timing;
4. **owner-specific outcome formulas** — standing rules, aid formulas, queue rank logic, safety determinations, and statutory consequences.

That is the archive's current stopping point.

## What institutions should publish now

When a recurring official study companion, coaching surface, or route-help surface uses one of these hotter branches, publish the ordinary publication floor from [`portable-review-window-substitute-path-and-publication-defaults-for-recovery-branches.md`](portable-review-window-substitute-path-and-publication-defaults-for-recovery-branches.md) plus, where applicable:

1. the mini-code in use (`MC-NW`, `MC-ML`, and/or `MC-AH`);
2. the strongest truthful non-worsening or minimal-loss statement;
3. the named authority owner if `MC-AH` applies;
4. the point at which the mini-code expires, converts, or moves to a hotter rail.

That is enough to make hotter recovery legible without publishing a welfare manual, conduct code, or queue formula.

## What counted as a real archive gain

The archive already knew that hotter recovery branches should carry shared review windows, shared substitute-path families, and shared publication floors only one step further than the generic packet rule. It still lacked the next narrower answer: **can any of those hotter branches safely harden a little more without collapsing into fake universality?**

This document answers yes, but only barely.

It now distinguishes among:

- branches that deserve a portable **non-worsening hold** (`MC-NW`);
- branches that deserve only a portable **minimal-loss continuity** floor (`MC-ML`);
- and branches whose real portable gain is not continuity strength but **authority-sensitive handback** (`MC-AH`).

That is a real operating gain because it blocks three opposite mistakes at once:

- pretending all consequence-bearing recovery must stay fully local forever;
- pretending the archive can now publish one thick humane-treatment package for every hotter branch;
- and letting study or route-help surfaces quietly behave like record, readiness, or eligibility authorities.

## Current archive bet

The archive's current best guess is that **a generic recovery grammar plus a portability layer plus three tiny treatment mini-codes** will outperform both extremes:

- one universal humane-recovery package for all hotter branches;
- and one local-residue story in which even thin anti-worsening or handback rules never harden.

That claim is now canon, but still live.
