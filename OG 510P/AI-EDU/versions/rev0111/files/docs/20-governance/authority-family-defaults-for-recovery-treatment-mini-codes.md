# Authority-family defaults for recovery treatment mini-codes

This document closes the archive's next recovery-governance gap: **once hotter recovery branches already have portable mini-codes, which of those mini-codes are stable enough to harden as authority-family defaults, and which still have to remain branch-bound even after repeated local reuse?**

The archive's current bet is:

> keep the generic packet-and-signal rule, the sector/context recovery starter layer, the child-branch map, the portability layer, and the treatment mini-codes — but now add one still smaller authority-family map so the archive can say which commitments usually travel with record owners, supervisor/safety owners, and route-case owners, without pretending that all merits tests, queue formulas, welfare thresholds, regulator rules, or statutory calendars now travel too.

The point is to avoid four predictable failures at once:

- **owner-blind hardening** — a branch-level pattern repeats locally, and the archive mistakes that for a cross-owner rule even though the durable effect is still controlled by very different authority families;
- **fake universality by kindness language** — institutions publish a warm continuity promise that a different owner family cannot actually honor because safety, inventory, or law makes it false;
- **handback without structure** — the archive says “named owner required,” but leaves unclear whether the family default also includes a bounded non-worsening hold, only a nearest-safe alternative, or merely a human-signoff rail;
- **permanent branch sprawl** — the archive names the same owner logic over and over inside separate recovery branches but never hardens even a thin family default.

Current public signals support this thinner move. OECD's 2026 outlook keeps stressing that generative AI in education should be purpose-built around pedagogy rather than generic task completion; UNESCO's current rights framing keeps agency, privacy, and participation visible; DfE's updated product-safety standards keep manipulation, developmental effects, and learner safety in frame; AAMC's principles keep professional readiness under accountable human judgment; OpenAI's current learning-outcomes work keeps pressure on longitudinal learning rather than short-run completion; and ICO guidance keeps data minimisation, privacy by default, and child-profiling constraints visible. See `B11`, `B20`, `B94`, `B102`, `B151`, `B154`, `B155`, `B156`.

- **when does a recovery mini-code stop being merely a branch judgment and become a thin default of the authority family that actually owns the durable effect?**

## What counts as an authority-family default

A mini-code should harden as an authority-family default only when all four tests hold.

1. **Same durable-effect family.** The owner family controls the same kind of durable educational effect across local variants — for example academic record treatment, supervised-readiness exposure, or public route-case disposition.
2. **Same truthful interim shape.** The strongest truthful interim promise is materially the same across those variants: usually a no-new-worse-position hold, only a nearest-safe alternative, or a conditional hold/fallback split.
3. **Same accountability logic.** The same kind of named human owner must authorize continuation, reversal, or escalation across the family.
4. **Local residue stays genuinely residual.** Local formulas, evidence burdens, legal calendars, and thresholds still matter, but they no longer decide whether the mini-code itself is truthful.

If even one test fails, the archive should keep the mini-code at branch level.

## The four family tags the archive now permits

The archive now permits four thin family tags.

| Family tag | Meaning | What hardens | What does not harden |
|---|---|---|---|
| `AF-RECORD` | owner family for durable academic-record treatment | `MC-AH` plus a presumptive `MC-NW` floor | aid rules, misconduct procedure, standing formulas, accommodation evidence standards, visa/sponsor or regulator overlays |
| `AF-SAFETY` | owner family for supervised readiness, exposure, welfare, or safety-governed treatment | `MC-AH` only as the universal floor | any universal `MC-NW`; most continuity promises remain branch-bound or local |
| `AF-ROUTE` | owner family for public route-case, slot, or eligibility treatment | `MC-AH` plus a portable choice rule between `MC-NW` and `MC-ML` | queue formulas, statutory tests, funded inventory, sanction or participation calendars |
| `AF-LOCAL` | no authority-family default yet | only the branch-level mini-code judgment travels | any stronger family inheritance |

These tags are intentionally thin. They are not substitute case codes.

## The archive's current family-level judgments

### `AF-RECORD` — record owners deserve the strongest portable continuity floor

This family covers the authority owners of durable academic record treatment: programme or faculty record owners, registrars, academic-standing offices, and other named humans who can authorize, preserve, or alter official participation/standing consequences.

The archive now hardens:

- **`MC-AH` as universal within the family.** Study or route-help surfaces may not quietly add, continue, or clear durable record treatment.
- **`MC-NW` as the ordinary family presumption.** Pending review should not, by itself, create a new adverse note, standing loss, progression loss, or ordinary participation worsening before the named record owner reviews.
- **`MC-ML` only as a fallback.** Use it when a truthful comparable hold is impossible, not as the ordinary first move.

Why this family hardens more than the others:

- the durable effect family is unusually stable;
- the owner logic is clear and recurrent;
- the truthful interim shape is usually the same: do not worsen the durable record merely because the recovery review is pending;
- and the local residue mostly governs *how* the case is resolved, not *whether* an interim anti-worsening floor is still truthful.

What remains local even inside `AF-RECORD`:

- financial-aid treatment;
- misconduct procedure;
- accommodation documentation burdens;
- local academic-standing formulas;
- immigration, sponsor, or regulator consequences outside the record owner's control.

So the family default is real, but deliberately narrow.

### `AF-SAFETY` — supervisor/safety owners harden only at handback

This family covers named humans whose authority turns on supervised exposure, practice readiness, welfare, safeguarding, or other direct safety-bearing treatment: clinical supervisors, practice educators, site owners, safeguarding leads, attendance/welfare owners where those functions actually carry consequence-bearing authority, and similar roles.

The archive now hardens only one thing at family level:

- **`MC-AH` is universal within the family.** AI recovery surfaces may not act as readiness owners, exposure owners, or safeguarding/welfare authorities.

The archive does **not** harden a family-wide `MC-NW` or `MC-ML` default here.

Why not:

- the durable-effect family is not stable enough at the interim-treatment level;
- the truthful interim shape varies too much between “hold exposure,” “move to nearest-safe simulation,” “pause pending owner review,” and “continue only under a tighter supervision pattern”;
- and local law, safety, child-protection, or regulator duties often decide whether even minimal-loss continuity is truthful.

So `AF-SAFETY` means:

> the authority owner is portable; the continuity promise usually is not.

This is the archive's deliberate refusal of a fake universal humane-safety package.

### `AF-ROUTE` — route-case owners deserve a portable choice rule, not one continuity code

This family covers public-route case owners, slot owners, eligibility owners, and similar named humans who actually control route disposition in adult-route services, funded seats, scarce programme entry, or cross-agency navigation systems.

The archive now hardens two things:

- **`MC-AH` is universal within the family.** Route-help surfaces may not quietly act as the slot owner, benefits owner, or statutory case owner.
- **A portable choice rule is universal within the family.** Where a truthful comparable hold exists, publish `MC-NW`; where scarcity, statutory timing, or partner inventory makes that untruthful, publish `MC-ML`.

That means the family default is not one continuity mini-code but one **conditional split**.

Why this is the right hardening level:

- the owner family is stable;
- the publication logic is stable;
- but the truthful interim shape is highly sensitive to real inventory, funded slots, partner calendars, and statutory gates.

So `AF-ROUTE` hardens the *decision rule* rather than hardening `MC-NW` or `MC-ML` across the whole family.

### `AF-LOCAL` — required/support-linked minor recovery still remains branch-bound

The archive now makes one important refusal explicit. Required/support-linked minor recovery does **not** yet deserve a general authority-family hardening beyond the already-published branch-level mini-codes and thin anti-worsening floor.

Why it stays local for now:

- welfare, attendance, safeguarding, and family-treatment ownership do not collapse neatly into one authority family with one truthful interim shape;
- the difference between supportive continuity and mandated welfare or attendance action is often governed by local child-protection law or school-specific process;
- and even where `MC-AH` is eventually implicated, the branch still carries too much context-sensitive residue to harden further.

So minors/support-linked recovery remains:

- branch-level `MC-ML` as the usual humane floor;
- a very thin anti-worsening statement before first stable review;
- `MC-AH` when a named welfare/safeguarding/attendance owner is actually implicated;
- and otherwise `AF-LOCAL`.

## The family map in one table

| Recovery domain | Family tag | Family-level default | Practical archive meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Record-adjacent higher-ed treatment | `AF-RECORD` | `MC-AH` + presumptive `MC-NW`; `MC-ML` only as fallback | no new adverse durable record treatment merely because review is pending, and only named record owners may decide |
| Professional readiness / exposure / safety treatment | `AF-SAFETY` | `MC-AH` only | named supervisor/safety owner must decide; continuity promises stay branch- or case-bound |
| Public route-case / slot / eligibility treatment | `AF-ROUTE` | `MC-AH` + conditional `MC-NW`/`MC-ML` choice rule | named case owner must decide, and interim continuity depends on whether a truthful comparable hold exists |
| Required/support-linked minor recovery | `AF-LOCAL` | no stronger family inheritance yet | humane branch-level recovery remains local beyond the thin anti-worsening and handback triggers already published |

## What institutions should publish now

When a recurring official study companion, recovery surface, or route-help service reaches a hotter recovery branch, publish:

1. the child branch;
2. the family tag (`AF-RECORD`, `AF-SAFETY`, `AF-ROUTE`, or `AF-LOCAL`);
3. the applicable mini-code or family choice rule;
4. the named human owner where `MC-AH` applies;
5. the strongest truthful interim statement;
6. the first meaningful review point or review-window band;
7. the expiry/conversion point for the interim treatment.

That is enough to make family-level hardening visible without publishing a registrar manual, a safeguarding protocol, or a route-case formula.

## What still remains permanently local

Even after this authority-family map, the archive still refuses portability for:

1. **merits judgments** — readiness, risk, eligibility, and evidence sufficiency;
2. **owner-side evidence thresholds** — what documentation or corroboration a specific owner needs;
3. **legal or regulator clocks** — notice windows, mandatory timelines, hearing steps, statutory deadlines;
4. **real scarcity logic** — seat inventory, queue rank, supervisor capacity, placement safety constraints;
5. **cross-regime spillovers** — consequences owned by aid, immigration, licensing, or safeguarding regimes outside the immediate family default.

That is the archive's stopping point for now.

## Why this counted as a real archive gain

The archive already knew that hotter recovery branches could carry small publication floors and a tiny mini-code layer. But it still lacked the next practical answer:

- when is a branch judgment merely repeated local craft;
- and when has it become stable enough to harden at the level of the authority family that actually owns the durable effect?

This document answers that question narrowly.

It now says:

- record owners usually deserve **handback plus non-worsening**;
- supervisor/safety owners deserve **handback only** as the true portable gain;
- route-case owners deserve **handback plus a conditional continuity split**;
- and minor support-linked treatment still remains **branch-bound**.

That is a real gain because it prevents two opposite mistakes at once:

- pretending every humane interim rule should remain forever local even when the same owner logic keeps recurring;
- and pretending one warm continuity package can truthfully travel across record, safety, and route-case owners.

## Current archive bet

The archive's current best guess is that **a recovery grammar, a portability layer, treatment mini-codes, and now one still smaller authority-family map** will outperform both extremes:

- a universal humane-treatment package for all consequence-bearing recovery;
- and a local-residue story in which even thin owner-family defaults never harden.

That claim is now canon, and now has a companion packet-field rule plus a departure-publication-and-ratchet rule.
