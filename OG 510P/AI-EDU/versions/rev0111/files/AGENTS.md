## Non-negotiables

- Keep the archive small, linked, and cumulative.
- Add evidence only when it changes design judgment.
- Preserve the distinction between what is **canon**, what is **open**, and what is **quarantined**.
- When making policy or procurement claims, name the evidence threshold.
- Preserve a visible pilot-to-scale gate so promising local pilots do not silently become default infrastructure without repeated evidence, durable support, and a live rollback path, preserve a tiny sector-and-function rollout profile layer where minors, formal assessment, professional gatekeeping, or public-route services already need a hotter scale burden or a lower default ceiling than the generic ladder, and do not let those inherited rollout profiles harden by drift instead of an explicit harden / branch / cap decision.
- When making design claims, say what educational failure mode they are meant to prevent.
- Treat teacher agency, student learning, and institutional safety as co-equal constraints.
- Do not let “AI literacy” degenerate into tool tips detached from curriculum, rights, or assessment.
- Prefer mode-tagged subject routines and a light common course grammar over generic exhortation whenever implementation is the real problem.
- Preserve the distinction between `P0-P3` proof-intensity planning and `PX` escalation so bundle-based assessment does not quietly become ambient documentation or surveillance.
- Keep observability cooler than it first seems: prefer aggregate signals, selected excerpts, and case-bound files over routine full-trace capture or cross-function reuse of learner logs, and preserve a tiny sector-and-function profile layer where minors, formal test monitoring, professional gatekeeping, or public-route coordination already require different default rails or stronger modality limits.
- Preserve a visible failure-escalation and manual-fallback rule for recurring services so harmful drift, silent updates, or outages do not become learner-borne disruption or fake human oversight, and preserve a tiny sector-and-function failure profile layer where minors, formal assessment, professional gatekeeping, or public-route services already need faster brakes, stricter change freezes, or hotter reopen floors.

## Command posture

Run `make lint` before packaging a revision. If you add or remove a surface, update `ARCHIVE_INDEX.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, and `REVISION_RECEIPT.json` together.

## What good changes usually do

Good revisions usually do one or more of the following:

- clarify what learning should be measured once AI assistance is common,
- tighten the procurement/evidence bar,
- refine the teacher–student–institution role split,
- turn a vague policy concern into a concrete design constraint,
- preserve a bold idea in quarantine without smuggling it into canon,
- or reduce archive size while improving future recovery.
