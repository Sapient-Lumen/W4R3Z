# Bibliography

This bibliography is intentionally short. Each source earns its place by shaping design judgment rather than merely confirming that AI in education exists.

## B01

**UNESCO (2023, updated 2026). _Guidance for generative AI in education and research_.**

URL: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/guidance-generative-ai-education-and-research

Why it matters: establishes a global, human-centred policy framing for GenAI in education and research.

## B02

**UNESCO (2021, updated 2025). _AI and education: guidance for policy-makers_.**

URL: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ai-and-education-guidance-policy-makers

Why it matters: gives a public-policy frame for balancing opportunity, risk, equity, and system design.

## B03

**UNESCO (2024, updated 2026). _AI competency framework for teachers_.**

URL: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ai-competency-framework-teachers

Why it matters: defines teacher competencies across human-centred mindset, ethics, AI foundations, AI pedagogy, and professional learning.

## B04

**UNESCO (2024, updated 2026). _AI competency framework for students_.**

URL: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ai-competency-framework-students

Why it matters: supports the claim that AI literacy should become part of formal curriculum rather than remain extracurricular.

## B05

**RAND (2024). _Using Artificial Intelligence Tools in K–12 Classrooms_.**

URL: https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA956-21.html

Why it matters: gives early evidence on how teachers were actually using AI and how uneven training support was.

## B06

**RAND (2025). _AI Use in Schools Is Quickly Increasing but Guidance Lags Behind_.**

URL: https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA4180-1.html

Why it matters: shows rapid uptake by students and teachers, paired with policy/training lag and concern about critical thinking and false cheating accusations.

## B07

**U.S. Department of Education / ERIC (2024). _Empowering Education Leaders: A Toolkit for Safe, Ethical, and Equitable AI Integration_.**

URL: https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED661924

## B08

**U.S. Department of Education (2025). _Guidance on Artificial Intelligence Use in Schools_.**

URL: https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/us-department-of-education-issues-guidance-artificial-intelligence-use-schools-proposes-additional-supplemental-priority

## B09
