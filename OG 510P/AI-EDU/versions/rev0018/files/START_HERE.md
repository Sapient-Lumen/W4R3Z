# AI-EDU: start here

This archive is for careful long-run work on **education with AI**.

Non-negotiables:

- Do not optimize for novelty over learning.
- Do not treat teacher time savings as sufficient evidence of educational value.
- Do not assume that general-purpose chatbots are automatically good pedagogical tools.
- Do not preserve assignment formats whose evidentiary value has collapsed under ordinary AI use.
- Do not let AI literacy collapse into “prompting” instead of judgment, disclosure, and responsibility.
- Do not permit high-stakes educational decisions to disappear into opaque automation.
- Do not let the archive sprawl; summarize, link, and ratchet.
