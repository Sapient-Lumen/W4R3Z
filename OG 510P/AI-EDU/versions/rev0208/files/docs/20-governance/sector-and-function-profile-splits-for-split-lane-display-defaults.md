# Sector-and-function profile splits for split-lane display defaults

This document closes the archive's next implementation gap about **where the generic split-lane display grammar already needs sector-shaped starter defaults**.

The archive now has a small `DL0-DL3` / `LD1-LD7` / `PX0-PX3` display layer for learner-facing continuity packets. What it still lacked was a compact answer to a narrower question:

> **which kinds of educational continuity routes should already start from hotter or cooler display assumptions because office family, legal trigger, route stakes, or public-entry role are knowable in advance?**

The archive's current bet is:

> one generic split-lane display rule is not enough; some sectors should already start from stricter packet-separation assumptions because the offices, triggers, and protected channels are visibly different before local evidence accumulates.

Current public signals point the same way. OECD's 2025 lifelong-learning outlook says policy should support non-linear transitions rather than fragmented handoffs. HLC's current teach-out rules keep communications granular by requiring accepted-credit treatment, tuition / fee information, records treatment, and an applicable time period. Federal Student Aid still distinguishes FAFSA estimates from school aid offers and ties Title IV rights to actual aid handling rather than to generic route notices. OIA still runs its external complaint clock from the Completion of Procedures Letter. DOL's current AI-literacy guidance says public workforce systems can combine education, training, and support services, while the core WIOA page still describes employment, education, training, and support services as a joined public system. PLA's public digital-literacy work still functions as low-barrier, multilingual first-contact infrastructure rather than as a benefits or sanctions office. U.S. Department of Education licensure guidance and FSA disclosure rules still treat professional licensure as state-specific and separately disclosed rather than as a simple by-product of ordinary programme completion. See `B177`, `B180`, `B181`, and `B182`.

## Relationship to the generic split-lane display rule

This document does not replace [`../30-operations/split-lane-packet-display-and-dependency-rules.md`](../30-operations/split-lane-packet-display-and-dependency-rules.md).

It adds one thing only:

- a **starter profile layer** for places where the archive already knows the generic `DL0-DL3` floor should begin hotter or cooler.

The generic display document still answers the first question:

- may one packet still be used at all;
- what each displayed lane must show;
- what precedence relation is truthful;
- and when co-display must split into a supplement or separate packet.

This document answers the second question:

- in this sector-and-function context, does the archive already know the honest starting posture is more like `DL1`, `DL2`, or `DL3`, or should the service stay closer to `DL0-DL1` because it is a first-contact or referral surface rather than a consequence-bearing owner lane?

## The starter profile table

The archive keeps this table intentionally small. These are **starter inherited profiles**, not full local office maps.

| Profile | Scope | Function families most affected | Default display adjustments | Never below | Why the split exists |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `DP-HE-TEACHOUT` | higher-education closure, teach-out, transfer, and continuation packets once route, money, and complaint / external-rights lanes are all plausibly live | teach-out routing, transfer / accepted-credit communication, aid / refund / charge lanes, complaint and external-rights timing | route and money may share one packet only at `DL1` or hotter; money should usually split to its own row/card even when route continuity is already visible; complaint / regulator / discharge relevance should usually move to `DL2` once learner-specific trigger status differs; use `PX1` or `PX2` rather than one implied order | `DL3` for protected support facts or for rights-critical lanes whose trigger depends on a final internal decision / COP letter not yet shared by the whole packet | teach-out, aid, and complaint systems usually mature on different owners and different event anchors even when the learner experiences one disruption |
| `DP-WF-PUBLIC-ROUTE` | public workforce, funded-route, and adult-transition packets where route, support, money, or benefits-adjacent timing can all matter | funded training navigation, support-service routing, referral continuity, scarce-slot pathway help, benefits-adjacent timing | default to `DL1` once more than one lane is live; move to `DL2` when support, funding, or learner-specific route rebuilding depends on individualized facts or on a different office family; use `PX3` aggressively where taking a route step should not look like waiving funding, complaint, or other still-live help lanes | `DL3` for sanctions, formal public eligibility, or protected case-management / support channels that should not be buried inside a convenience bundle | workforce systems combine education, training, and support services, so route help, funding status, and protected case handling often sit in one public experience but under different owners |
| `DP-LIB-CIVIC-FIRST` | library, civic, and community first-contact surfaces that introduce AI learning routes or continuity help | orientation, digital-literacy first contact, multilingual referral, appointment / navigator intake, route-family explanation | usually stay at `DL0` or light `DL1`; show route family, owner, and next handoff clearly, but avoid co-displaying downstream benefits, complaint, sanction, or eligibility lanes as if the library owns them; where those lanes exist, point outward rather than simulating a unified packet | `DL3` for any protected support, benefits, or adjudication lane that belongs to another office family | civic first-contact points are valuable precisely because they are low-barrier and trusted; turning them into fake all-in-one adjudication packets would overclaim office authority and bury real ownership |
| `DP-PRO-LICENSURE` | professional-practice, practicum, clinical, apprenticeship, and licensure-sensitive routes where completion, placement, readiness, and external approval can diverge | placement / practicum continuity, readiness help, programme-completion notices, licensure or certification disclosures, supervisor or regulator-facing channels | educational route and local support lanes may share `DL1`; move to `DL2` when placement, host approval, readiness, or licensure facts are learner-specific; move licensure / regulator / safety-sensitive lanes to `DL3` whenever the governing owner, evidence packet, or state-specific rule differs from the teaching route | `DL3` for fitness-to-practice, patient/client-safety, or regulator-owned licensure lanes | professional routes often look like one pathway to the learner, but completion, host placement, and legal permission to practice are not the same lane and should not masquerade as one schedule |
| `DP-PROTECTED-SUPPORT` | any sector where a continuity packet also touches accessibility, safeguarding, welfare, or other protected support channels | accommodation-sensitive support, wellbeing routing, safeguarding-linked follow-up, protected translation/access channels | ordinary route and money lanes may remain in `DL1` or `DL2`, but protected support should usually leave the shared packet at `DL3`; the shared packet may name the existence of a protected channel and the owner, but not collapse its operative details back into the ordinary lane bundle | `DL3` always | once support facts require narrower audience, different access channel, or protected evidence handling, convenience is no longer a truthful reason to keep one packet |

## The archive's default posture shifts

This document now hardens four small posture shifts above the generic display grammar.

### 1. Higher-ed continuity should assume early lane-splitting

Once a teach-out or transfer packet carries both route and money, the archive now assumes `DL1` rather than decorative `DL0`. Once some learners have a live complaint, regulator, or discharge trigger and others do not, the archive now assumes `DL2` rather than one common clock.

### 2. Public-route packets should assume office-family divergence

Once public workforce or adult-route help touches funded access, support-service routing, or benefits-adjacent timing, the archive now assumes route and funding/support lanes may need different owners and different dependencies even if they are still co-displayed.

### 3. Civic first-contact surfaces should stay visibly modest

Libraries and community first-contact points should usually explain route families and handoff owners, not impersonate the downstream packet logic of benefits offices, financial-aid offices, sanction owners, or regulators. Their value is truthful entry, not pseudo-unified adjudication.

### 4. Professional-practice routes should split legal permission from educational progression

Completion, placement, and licensure often touch the same learner journey but they do not share one governing owner or one evidence regime. The archive therefore prefers hotter `DL2-DL3` separation sooner in licensure-sensitive contexts than in ordinary route guidance.

## What institutions should publish

Where a continuity surface inherits one of these profiles, publish one extra line with only six additions beyond the generic split-lane packet:

1. profile identifier (`DP-HE-TEACHOUT` through `DP-PROTECTED-SUPPORT` or local equivalent);
2. whether the profile is inherited unchanged or locally heated/cooled;
3. which lane pairs are presumed co-displayable and which are presumed split;
4. any default precedence relation the packet uses unless a case-specific relation overrides it;
5. any protected-channel or office-family trigger that forces `DL2` or `DL3`;
6. the no-common-countdown rule whenever two displayed lanes do not share one operative anchor.

This keeps the public layer small while blocking a common failure: one institution-wide packet template that silently assumes higher-ed teach-out, workforce routing, library referral, licensure-sensitive progression, and protected support lanes can all inherit the same display posture.

## What counted as a real archive gain

The archive previously had a generic split-lane display grammar, but still left one obvious implementation question unresolved: **which contexts should already start from stricter or cooler packet-display assumptions because the office families and trigger shapes are visible in advance?**

This document answers that with a bounded starter layer. It keeps the generic `DL0-DL3` grammar, but it now makes the archive more deployable across higher education, public workforce routing, civic first-contact points, licensure-sensitive routes, and protected support channels without pretending that all continuity packets begin from the same office map.

## Current archive bet

The archive's current best guess is that a **generic split-lane display grammar plus a tiny sector-and-function starter profile layer** will outperform both extremes:

- one universal packet-display posture for every continuity context;
- and fully bespoke local packet templates with no inherited defaults at all.

That claim is now canon, but still live. The next problem is narrower: which of these starter profiles are stable enough to harden, where they should branch further by after-hours posture, office family, or external-owner timing, and when packet tidiness should yield entirely to office-bound or protected-channel separation. See `OQ-0045`.
