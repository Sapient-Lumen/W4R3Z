# Mission kernel rev0280

The heart of the mission is still institutional truth in education: AI systems
may help learning work, but they must not silently acquire authority over
learning claims, grades, records, protected routes, public language, or service
changes before owner evidence and reversible controls justify that authority.

Rev0280 spends the change budget on the riskiest live seam that can be improved
inside the cloudtainer: the first-contact execution lane. The archive still
cannot send the external owner packet or manufacture SRC2+ evidence. It can make
that next human act cleaner, less stale, and harder to mistake for evidence.

## Current live kernel

1. Ask the router for exactly one executable command.
2. Prepare the bounded `AIEDU-SR-003` owner packet when scratch is empty.
3. If an accountable owner route exists, send/adapt the generated email and
   blank CSV outside the archive.
4. Record a local send log only after that human send/adaptation.
5. Record the bounded contact clock from the send log, not from the packet.
6. Intake a real returned CSV only through the active source contact clock.
7. Seed, review, decide, ticket, card, and read out only through the routed
   non-evidence chain.
8. If the bounded re-ask expires, record `NO_OWNER_PACKET`; do not widen the ask
   or create new governance surfaces to compensate.

## What changed in rev0280

Rev0280 repairs the executable packet/router version seam. The local field-next
docket and generated packet manifest now stamp the current release revision
instead of carrying old hard-coded version labels. The owner-request packet
validator now checks that packet manifests match the current receipt revision,
and the field-next validator checks that dockets do the same.

This pass also removes a duplicate manifest key in packet generation and tightens
the send-now brief around the only pre-send field judgment: whether a real
accountable owner route exists. If the route exists, send. If it does not, record
the block locally instead of writing more doctrine.

## What counts as progress now

- A real external send/adaptation followed by a local send log.
- A bounded source contact clock.
- A real owner-returned CSV tied to that clock.
- A source-clocked intake, seed, review, first-packet decision, post-decision
  ticket, live-window card, and readout that remain below acceptance.
- A bounded `NO_OWNER_PACKET` record after the permitted clocks expire.
- A small executable refactor that makes one of those steps less stale or less
  likely to be skipped.

## What must not happen

Do not convert a fresh version stamp, a packet, a send-now brief, a field-texture
memo, a send log, a contact clock, an intake bundle, a workbench seed, a review,
a decision board, a change ticket, a live-window card, a readout, or this audit
into owner evidence, custody evidence, acceptance, closure, public-summary
support, or a claim that the service improves learning, safety, access,
workload, compliance, scale, or effectiveness.
