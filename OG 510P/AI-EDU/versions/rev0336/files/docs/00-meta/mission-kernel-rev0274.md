## Rev0274 execution kernel

Rev0274 closes the one-step-later bypass after the returned-CSV gate. A `PROCEED-STAGED` intake bundle can no longer create or route a workbench seed unless the bundle still preserves a revalidated active `SENT_AWAITING_REPLY` or `REASK_AWAITING_REPLY` contact-status clock. The router also blocks old or hand-edited seeds that lack that provenance.

The seed still says `NOT_ACCEPTED`. It does not copy owner answers, contact details, row text, raw CSV rows, protected facts, small cells, screenshots, credentials, vendor telemetry, or public-claim language.
