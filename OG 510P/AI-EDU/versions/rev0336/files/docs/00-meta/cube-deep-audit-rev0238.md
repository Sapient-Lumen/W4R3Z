# Cube deep audit rev0238: field request handoff repair

## Bottom line

The cube is not broken at the lint, schema, surface, or release-control layer.
The failure mode is different: `FT-0181` is now over-prepared for a real pilot
packet and under-supplied with a field-facing ask that a real owner could answer
without becoming trapped in the archive's internal control language.

Rev0238 therefore makes a narrow correction. It adds a copy/pasteable owner
field request and micro-packet, and it repairs maintainer handoff drift so the
newer post-readout recheck due-date firebreak cannot be skipped by the operator path.

## What is missing

The missing artifact was not another schema, branch family, or closure control.
It was a small human request that says: send one real owner-reviewed packet for
one service, one owner path, one date range, one source system, and aggregate or
minimized fields only.

The archive already had an owner action kit, owner packet workbench, first-packet
decision board, change ticket, live-window card, readout gate, and post-readout
dispatch. Those are useful once evidence exists. They are less useful if the
first human owner receives an archive-native request that reads like a control
plane instead of a minimal operational ask.

## What should change

The next few turns should prefer field acquisition and pruning over expansion.
Good changes now should do one of three things:

1. get a real SRC2+ packet closer;
2. drop a requested field because it cannot change authority, evidence,
   construct, public language, lifecycle, security, privacy, access, or burden;
3. repair a narrow handoff gap that would cause an operator to skip an existing
   gate.

They should not add broad release-control families, more negative fixtures, or
additional example planes unless a real packet exposes a repeatable failure the
current gates cannot catch.

## Where something went wrong or wasteful

### Control saturation became the dominant smell

The cube now passes many integrity checks, but the operational blocker remains a
real external packet. The existing saturation rule is directionally correct:
`FT-0181` should not receive another broad control layer until the owner packet
path is exercised.

The practical correction is to move from archive completeness to owner
legibility. The new field request makes the first ask smaller, safer, and easier
to answer.

### Handoff drift escaped lint

The rev0237 maintainer handoff included the first-sprint path, owner workbench,
decision board, change ticket, live-window card, and end-window gate, but it did
not force the newer post-readout recheck due-date firebreak into the startup surface list.
That means a human could follow the handoff and miss the release's own newest
gate.

Rev0238 repairs the handoff record. It does not add a new validator yet. If this
pattern recurs, the validator should require every current FT-0181 execution
surface named in the re-entry navigation path to appear in the operator handoff.
One observed drift is enough to patch the record; two would justify tooling.

### Branch history is large but not the current emergency

The hot-exam and other branch families are still large. However, they are indexed
and compressed enough that they are not the main blocker. Pulling them into the
startup path would be wasteful. The better move is to leave them in the branch
family index and only retrieve them when a current decision needs their pattern.

### Research watch needs translation, not another bibliography pass

Current external signals reinforce the cube's posture: AI use in schools is
increasing, governance and training lag behind adoption, cognitive offload is a
real design risk, privacy and minimization obligations matter, and generative-AI
risk frameworks keep emphasizing context-specific risk management. The archive
already knows these themes. The missing step is translation into packet fields
that real owners can answer.

Rev0238 translates those signals into four owner-facing fields: training/use
guidance, public claim ceiling, stop condition, and redaction assertion.

## Speculative working diagnosis

The cube's hidden waste pattern is not sloppy tooling; it is success-path
overfitting. Each prior revision made it harder to falsely close `FT-0181`, but
also made the first real human interaction feel more formal than it needs to be.
Over time, that can create a paradox: the archive becomes excellent at rejecting
fake evidence while becoming slower at obtaining real evidence.

The corrective strategy is a ratchet:

- expand controls only after real packet failures expose a missing repeated
  check;
- otherwise shrink the ask;
- keep the first owner contact outside the internal control dialect;
- keep all public and lifecycle claims below the evidence actually received.
