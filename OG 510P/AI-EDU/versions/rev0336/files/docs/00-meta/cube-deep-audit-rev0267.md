## Heart of the mission

The mission is still human-centered education with AI: increase understanding, agency, access, teacher capacity, and institutional intelligence without increasing shortcutting, surveillance, deskilling, hidden authority, or inequality.

The operational heart is narrower: one real `AIEDU-SR-003` owner-reviewed packet must pass through the field lane without overcollection, protected-route leakage, hidden send/write/penalty authority, or claim laundering.

## Riskiest unfinished work

The riskiest unfinished work is the real owner-contact step. The archive is now well-equipped to prepare a packet, route a sent clock, triage a returned CSV, and refuse unsafe replies. It still cannot manufacture the thing that matters: an accountable owner filling or refusing the eight-row reply from real operational material.

The second risk is that a maintainer treats owner silence as a reason to expand the ask. Rev0267 reinforces the opposite rule: after one bounded clarification and a lapsed clock, record `NO_OWNER_PACKET`, keep `FT-0181` live, and do not widen.

The third risk is that a small accepted packet becomes a public story about learning or safety. The new public outcome kernel makes the first admissible claims deliberately small: process readiness, authority confirmation, fallback naming, field trimming, or a bounded stop/continue decision.

## What was refactored

The branch-history tail was audited as the most obvious waste sink. `BRANCH_FAMILY_INDEX.json` reports 100 branch surfaces in 12 families, with 50 in `hot_exam_recipient_followup_shells`. Rev0267 does not delete them, because they still have retrieval value, but it changes their posture: branch files stay out of first-read paths, and new branch siblings require a live artifact failure plus a named uncovered risk.

The startup path was refactored from a downstream-control tour into a field kernel. The generated `context-pack.json` now emphasizes mission, packet prep, router execution, intake, workbench seed, public outcome boundaries, risk crosswalk, evidence ladder, authority ceiling, branch freeze, and the live followthrough row.

The owner packet prep utility now emits `FIELD-TEXTURE-MEMO.md`. That memo captures local implementation facts such as send date, owner route clarity, confusing rows, refusal reasons, time burden, and whether the bounded ask felt safer than a broad export. It explicitly forbids recipient details, learner facts, protected facts, small cells, screenshots, raw exports, and claim language. It is not evidence.

## What remains missing

The same missing center remains: a real `SRC2+` owner-reviewed packet, accepted workbench review, live-window readout, post-readout dispatch, closeout-board minutes, and safe public summary. Rev0267 makes those steps easier to enter and harder to overclaim, but it does not complete them.

The archive also still lacks field texture from a real owner. The generated memo is only a capture surface; it will matter only after an actual contact attempt or real owner friction exists.

Recent tutoring studies show promise when AI is pedagogically designed and bounded, which reinforces a selective evidence-and-authority posture rather than a blanket anti-AI stance.
