# Mission kernel rev0285

The mission remains institutional truth in education. AI systems may assist
learning work, but they must not silently gain authority over learning claims,
records, protected routes, public language, service changes, or closure before
owner evidence and reversible controls justify that authority.

Rev0285 spends the change budget on the narrowest remaining false-completion
seam: activation must not merely cite a plausible local packet file. It must cite
the same returned owner packet hash that produced the intake bundle, workbench
seed, workbench review, and first-packet decision.

## Current live kernel

1. Ask the router for exactly one executable command.
2. Prepare the bounded `AIEDU-SR-003` owner packet when scratch is empty.
3. If an accountable owner route exists, send/adapt the generated email and
   blank CSV outside the archive.
4. If no accountable owner route exists, record `owner-route-block` locally and
   stop without creating a send log or contact clock.
5. Record a local send log only after an actual human send/adaptation.
6. Record the bounded first contact clock from the send log, not from the packet.
7. Record the one bounded reask log only after a human sends/adapts the bounded
   clarification.
8. Intake a real returned CSV only through the active source contact clock.
9. Treat workbench proceed reviews as `SRC2-CANDIDATE-NOT-ACCEPTED` until a real
   acceptance gate says otherwise.
10. Let `ready_for_real_packet` tickets stop; do not hand-create active tickets.
11. Record an activation receipt only when the source packet hash matches the
    returned CSV hash preserved by the decision chain.
12. Create `active_change` only from that activation receipt.
13. Write a live-window card only from a guarded `active_change` ticket.
14. If clocks expire or owner route/evidence is missing, stop locally and keep
    `FT-0181` live.

## What changed in rev0285

The activation receipt recorder and shared guard now reopen the decision chain
and verify the source CSV hash through seed, review, decision, and packet. An
unrelated local file, stale seed, edited review, edited receipt, archive example,
smoke packet, or weak source class cannot activate a change.

## What counts as progress now

Progress is a real owner route, a real human send/adaptation, a real returned
owner packet, a lineage-matched activation receipt, or a truthful local stop when
any of those are missing. More prose without one of those artifacts is not
progress.
