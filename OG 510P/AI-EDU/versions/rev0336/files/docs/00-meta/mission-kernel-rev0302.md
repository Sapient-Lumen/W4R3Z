# rev0302 mission kernel

The mission remains unchanged: keep the AI-EDU cube answerable to real learning,
agency, access, teacher capacity, source truth, and public-claim humility after AI
becomes ordinary education infrastructure.

The live risk remains `FT-0181`: local tooling can make the archive feel active
while no real owner evidence has been accepted. `rev0302` therefore avoids a new
registry family and shortens the due-date recheck seam after a human post-readout
action dispatch.

## Current field rail

```text
owner-field-work
→ owner-after-human-send after real human send
→ owner-returned-reply-work when a real CSV returns
→ owner-workbench-review-brief
→ owner-workbench-review by a human
→ owner-first-packet-decision-brief
→ owner-first-packet-decision by a human
→ owner-post-decision-change-ticket-brief
→ owner-post-decision-change-ticket by a human
→ owner-activation-live-window-brief
→ activation receipt only from the same real SRC2+ packet, or live-window card only from active_change
→ owner-live-window-terminal-brief after staged/active card
→ owner-live-window-readout-brief after terminal card
→ owner-post-readout-action-brief after terminal readout
→ owner-post-readout-action by a human
→ owner-post-readout-recheck-brief on/after the due date
```

`owner-field-work` may prepare safe local bridges only: first-contact packet,
workbench review brief, first-packet decision brief, post-decision change-ticket
brief, activation/live-window entry brief, live-window terminal-state brief,
terminal readout brief, post-readout action brief, or post-readout recheck brief.
It cannot record a send, route block, contact clock, returned CSV intake, human
review, board decision, change ticket, activation receipt, live-window card,
terminal card, readout, post-readout dispatch, recheck, context receipt, owner
action, custody, acceptance, public support, lifecycle movement, or closure.

## Mission heart

Make the smallest real next field action easier than writing another doctrine
surface. At the due-date seam, the archive should show the human exactly which
bounded recheck choices exist and which firebreaks must hold, then stop before
making the choice.
