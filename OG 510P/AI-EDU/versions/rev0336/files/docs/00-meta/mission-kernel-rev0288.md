# rev0288 mission kernel

The mission remains: protect learning, learner agency, access, teacher capacity, contestability, source provenance, and claim humility before AI systems gain authority in education.

## rev0288 kernel change

The riskiest unfinished work is now the owner-held action lane after a terminal readout and dispatch. `rev0288` makes that lane dated and executable: a dispatch waits until its due/recheck date, then must be rechecked without becoming evidence, service mutation, public language, lifecycle movement, custody, acceptance, or closure.

## Current live rail

1. Prepare the owner packet.
2. Record route block or real human send log.
3. Record contact clock.
4. Intake a real returned owner CSV only from that clock.
5. Seed/review/decide without accepting candidate evidence too early.
6. Record activation receipt only from the same source packet lineage.
7. Record active-change ticket, live-window card, and terminal readout.
8. Record one bounded post-readout recheck due-date firebreak.
9. On or after the dispatch due date, record one bounded post-readout recheck.
10. Stop unless actual new owner context exists, in which case restart through `owner-field-next CSV=...`.

## What stays forbidden

No local artifact may become real evidence, service-record mutation, lifecycle movement, public claim support, custody, acceptance, or closure. `FT-0181` remains live until real owner evidence and closure signoff exist.
