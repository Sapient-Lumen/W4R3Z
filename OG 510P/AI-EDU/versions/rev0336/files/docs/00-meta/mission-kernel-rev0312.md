## rev0312 correction

The riskiest completion seam in this pass was not a missing schema. It was a
bad re-entry contract: the current operator handoff still advertised obsolete
`run_lint_suite.py --mode ...` commands even though the runner accepts `--lane`.
That kind of stale handoff turns a ready field rail into wasted operator time and
encourages another round of doctrine instead of the next owner action.

`rev0312` makes the handoff executable again and turns that mistake into a
validator failure. Operator handoff records now must use the current lint lane
commands, must expose the router/report-first field actions, and must not list
direct downstream owner, readout, import, or closeout actions as allowed next
moves.
