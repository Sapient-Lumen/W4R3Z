# Mission kernel rev0283

The mission remains institutional truth in education. AI systems may assist
learning work, but they must not silently gain authority over learning claims,
records, protected routes, public language, service changes, or closure before
owner evidence and reversible controls justify that authority.

Rev0283 spends the change budget on the source-truth words that make field
progress look safer than it is. A candidate owner aggregate may be useful. A
ready-for-real-packet ticket may be useful. Neither is accepted SRC2 evidence,
and neither may authorize a live-window card.

## Current live kernel

1. Ask the router for exactly one executable command.
2. Prepare the bounded `AIEDU-SR-003` owner packet when scratch is empty.
3. If an accountable owner route exists, send/adapt the generated email and
   blank CSV outside the archive.
4. If no accountable owner route exists, record `owner-route-block` locally and
   stop without creating a send log or contact clock.
5. Record a local send log only after an actual human send/adaptation.
6. Record the bounded first contact clock from the send log, not from the packet.
7. Record the one bounded reask log only after a human sends/adapts the bounded
   clarification.
8. Intake a real returned CSV only through the active source contact clock.
9. Treat workbench proceed reviews as `SRC2-CANDIDATE-NOT-ACCEPTED` until a real
   acceptance gate says otherwise.
10. Let ready-for-real-packet tickets stop; do not write a live-window card from
    readiness alone.
11. Write a live-window card only from an `active_change` ticket with the real
    source boundary still intact.
12. If clocks expire or owner route/evidence is missing, stop locally and keep
    `FT-0181` live.

## What changed in rev0283

The router no longer uses bare `SRC2` for pre-acceptance workbench-review proceed
commands. It uses `SRC2-CANDIDATE-NOT-ACCEPTED`. The router also no longer emits
a live-window-card command from a `ready_for_real_packet` ticket. Live-window-card
recording now requires an `active_change` ticket for live/staged window states.

## What counts as progress now

- A real external send/adaptation followed by a local send log.
- A bounded route-block record when no accountable owner route exists.
- A bounded contact or reask clock sourced from the proper send/reask log.
- A real owner-returned CSV tied to an active clock.
- A candidate workbench review clearly labeled as not accepted.
- A ready-for-real-packet ticket that stops until the real packet is accepted.
- An active-change ticket that alone can source a live-window-card command.
- A small executable refactor that removes source-truth ambiguity.

## What must not happen

Do not convert a candidate source class, readiness ticket, route block, packet,
send log, reask log, contact clock, intake bundle, workbench seed, review,
decision board, change ticket, live-window card, readout, or audit into owner
evidence, custody evidence, acceptance, closure, public-summary support, or a
claim that the service improves learning, safety, access, workload, compliance,
scale, or effectiveness.
