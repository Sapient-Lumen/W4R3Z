# rev0297 mission kernel

The mission remains unchanged: keep the AI-EDU cube answerable to real learning,
agency, access, teacher capacity, source truth, and public-claim humility after
AI becomes ordinary education infrastructure.

The live risk remains `FT-0181`: local tooling can make the archive feel active
while no real owner evidence has been accepted. `rev0297` therefore avoids a new
registry family and shortens one execution seam after the human first-packet
decision.

## Current field rail

```text
owner-field-work
→ owner-after-human-send after real human send
→ owner-returned-reply-work when a real CSV returns
→ owner-workbench-review-brief
→ owner-workbench-review by a human
→ owner-first-packet-decision-brief
→ owner-first-packet-decision by a human
→ owner-post-decision-change-ticket-brief
→ owner-post-decision-change-ticket by a human
→ activation/live-window gates only if real SRC2+ packet rules are met
```

`owner-field-work` may now prepare safe local bridges only: first-contact packet,
workbench review brief, first-packet decision brief, or post-decision
change-ticket brief. It cannot record a send, route block, contact clock,
returned CSV intake, human review, board decision, change ticket, custody,
acceptance, public support, live-window movement, or closure.

## Mission heart

Make the smallest real next field action easier than writing another doctrine
surface. The next archive operator should see the command to run, the boundary it
cannot cross, and the missing human-owned action.
