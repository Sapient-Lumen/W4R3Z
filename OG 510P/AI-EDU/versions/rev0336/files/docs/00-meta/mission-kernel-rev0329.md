# Mission kernel — rev0329

## Heart

The archive exists to protect one human move: a teacher or tutor chooses a real instructional friction
point, tries a teacher-facing AI move coach in a bounded way, and judges whether it helps their own
pedagogical decision-making without weakening learner independence.

## Current risk

The project has enough gates to prevent many false claims. Its weak point is conversion: moving from a
real educator conversation to exactly one local feasibility/usability cycle. If that conversion fails,
the cube returns to its old vice: producing more doctrine while no learner-facing or educator-facing
field event occurs.

## Rev0329 correction

Rev0329 removes a readiness trap. The generated owner plan had a post-cycle attestation placeholder,
while the readiness scorer required the whole owner plan to be placeholder-free before the first
cycle. That made a pre-cycle state depend on a post-cycle field. The correction is to require only a
pre-cycle local attestation route and to put the post-cycle decision in the owner decision memo.

Rev0329 also generates `CYCLE-RUN-SHEET.md`. That sheet is the operational bridge from entry readiness
to a single local cycle. It says what to confirm, how to run, when to stop, and what not to claim.

## Non-claims

No educator was contacted, no local cycle ran, no owner review happened, no result exists, no `SRC2+`
owner packet was accepted, and `FT-0181` remains open.
