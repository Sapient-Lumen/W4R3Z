## Rev0272 field kernel

Rev0272 burns down a clock-drift risk. A local send log and contact-status note
can no longer create an open-ended wait state. The first owner-contact response
clock is capped at seven days from the recorded send date. The one allowed
clarification/re-ask clock is capped at three days. A `NO_OWNER_PACKET` outcome
must preserve the exact source re-ask clock instead of substituting older first-
ask dates or a softer due date.

The current kernel is:

1. prepare the bounded eight-row packet;
2. send or adapt it outside the archive;
3. record a minimal send log with no contact details or owner answers and a
   bounded response clock;
4. record `SENT_AWAITING_REPLY` from that send log;
5. intake a real returned CSV through the router, or send one bounded re-ask, or
   record a bounded `NO_OWNER_PACKET` once the re-ask clock passes;
6. never turn any local artifact in that path into `SRC2+` evidence.

## What must not change

memo, send log, contact status, field-next docket, intake bundle, workbench seed,
or smoke output. `FT-0181` remains live until a real owner-reviewed packet is
received, staged, accepted, reviewed, and closed through the downstream evidence
path.
