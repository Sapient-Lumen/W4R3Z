## Mission sentence

Use AI in education only where it preserves human agency, protects the learning construct, keeps consequential authority contestable, and can support public claims with evidence that is no stronger than the source truth actually received.

For this archive, the live mission is now operationally narrow: get `AIEDU-SR-003` through one real, bounded owner-contact cycle without asking for raw learner records, protected facts, small cells, hidden authority, vendor-only assertions, or learning-effectiveness language.

## Current live risk

The riskiest failure is still not a missing registry. It is that the owner packet never leaves the archive. Rev0268 therefore spends its change budget on send friction, stale scratch friction, and first-read compression rather than on another doctrine family.

The second risk is bad local state. A maintainer may rerun packet or contact-status commands, hit an already-populated output directory, and compensate by hand-editing dates, copying scratch material into the archive, or creating another explanatory surface. Rev0268 exposes `OVERWRITE=1` through the relevant make targets and makes overwrite remove stale packet files.

The third risk is claim creep after a small packet arrives. Even a viable eight-row reply initially supports only bounded process/readiness language, not learning, safety, access, workload, compliance, scale, or effectiveness.

The fourth risk is attention leakage into oversized historical surfaces. Rev0268 adds an explicit compression note to the largest operational surface so that continuity-cost doctrine does not displace the live `FT-0181` field path.

## One-session action bias

A maintainer should spend most of the session on one of these concrete moves:

1. run `make owner-field-next OUT=scratch/ft0181-field-next-action/aiedu-sr-003`;
2. run the emitted packet-prep command in `scratch/`;
3. open `SEND-NOW-BRIEF.md`, copy the generated email, attach only the blank CSV, and send/adapt outside the archive;
4. rerun the router and record the dated sent clock outside the release archive;
5. triage a real returned CSV through the router; or
6. record a bounded `NO_OWNER_PACKET` outcome after the router's one clarification path expires.
