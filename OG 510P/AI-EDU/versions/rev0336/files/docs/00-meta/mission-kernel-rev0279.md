# Mission kernel rev0279

The heart of the mission is not AI adoption. It is institutional truth in
education: every AI use should protect learning, learner agency, access,
teacher capacity, contestability, and evidence discipline before it earns
stronger claims or operational authority.

Rev0279 keeps `FT-0181` live and adds a mission/waste repair pass. The archive
is now good at blocking false field progress, but the next danger is internal
control work becoming a substitute for owner evidence. This revision therefore
refreshes the live queue, adds a freshness check for the active followthrough,
and names the work that should shrink rather than grow.

## Current live kernel

1. Prepare the bounded `AIEDU-SR-003` owner packet.
2. Record a local send log only after the human send/adaptation.
3. Record the bounded contact clock from that send log.
4. Intake a real returned CSV only through the active source contact clock.
5. Create a `NOT_ACCEPTED` workbench seed only from a valid intake bundle.
6. Record a minimized workbench review only from a valid seed.
7. Record a minimized five-slice first-packet decision only from a proceed-capable workbench review.
8. Record a minimized post-decision change ticket only from a valid first-packet decision.
9. Record a bounded live-window stop/rollback card only from a valid change ticket.
10. Use the end-of-window readout gate only after a terminal/readout-ready card
    state; do not treat the card, ticket, readout, or local routing trace as
    closure evidence.

## What changed in rev0279

Rev0279 does not add another doctrine branch. It corrects a live navigation
error: `FOLLOWTHROUGH_QUEUE.json` and the generated `context-pack.json` still
pointed at the older rev0276 first-packet decision gate even though the release
had advanced to the rev0278 live-window card gate. The queue now points to this
revision and the checker now requires queued followthrough text to cite the
current revision and an existing current-revision surface.

Rev0279 also adds `mission-waste-and-repair-audit-rev0279.md`, which records the
online research synthesis and the speculation boundary for this session.

## What still counts as progress

Progress is one of these, in order:

- a real external send/adaptation followed by a local send log;
- a bounded contact clock;
- a real returned owner CSV tied to that clock;
- a source-clock-gated intake bundle;
- a seed, review, first-packet decision, post-decision ticket, and live-window
  card that preserve non-evidence boundaries;
- a bounded no-owner-packet record when the source re-ask clock expires; or
- deletion, compression, or freshness repair that makes the next real field
  action easier without weakening gates.

## What must not change

No local field artifact can close `FT-0181`, prove a learning or service outcome,
upgrade public claims, become custody evidence, or modify service records. The
archive may support a field pilot, but it is still not the pilot.
