# rev0292 mission kernel

The mission remains: protect learning, learner agency, access, teacher capacity,
contestability, source provenance, and claim humility before AI systems gain
quiet authority in education.

## rev0292 kernel change

`rev0292` moves from naming the field-contact bottleneck to reducing it. The
archive now has a safe local field-work mode that can run the router, prepare
the first-contact packet when that is the router's route, and immediately rerun
the router so the next visible fork is human send/adaptation or route block. It
cannot record a send log, contact clock, intake, custody, acceptance, live-window
movement, public support, or closure.

The scarce resource is still accountable owner action. The new target only
removes copy/paste friction before that action.

## Heart of the mission

The heart is not AI adoption, tooling maturity, or a larger control plane. The
heart is an education system where AI can support understanding, access,
teacher capacity, and institutional learning without laundering shortcuts into
proof of learning or letting automated tools acquire hidden authority over
records, placement, grades, accommodations, discipline, eligibility, or public
claims.

Operationally, the cube should act as a small live rail backed by a quiet
archive. It should make the honest field move easier than adding another rule.

## Current live rail

1. Run the safe local field-work target or the underlying router.
2. If no packet exists, prepare the bounded first-contact packet locally.
3. Have a human either send/adapt that request to a real accountable owner route
   or record the no-route block.
4. Record send/reask/contact state only after real human action.
5. Intake only a real returned owner CSV/source packet through the router.
6. Keep candidate evidence non-accepted until the workbench, decision,
   activation, ticket, live-window, readout, post-readout, recheck, and context
   receipt chain actually requires movement.
7. Stop before public claims, service-record edits, lifecycle movement, custody,
   acceptance, or closure.

## What stays forbidden

No field-work session, field-next docket, prepared packet, send-now brief,
field-texture memo, send log, contact-status note, intake bundle, workbench seed
or review, first-packet decision, activation receipt, post-decision ticket,
live-window card, readout, post-readout action, recheck, context receipt, audit,
release example, schema, registry, or lint result may become real evidence,
service-record mutation, lifecycle movement, public-claim support, custody,
acceptance, or closure. `FT-0181` remains live until real owner evidence and
closure signoff exist.
