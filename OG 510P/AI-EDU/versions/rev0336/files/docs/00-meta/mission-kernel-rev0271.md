# Mission kernel rev0271

## Active mission

The mission remains to make AI in education accountable to learning, human
agency, access, safety, workload reality, and honest public claims. The archive
should not reward itself for more control-plane text while the first real owner
packet is still missing.

## Rev0271 field kernel

This revision narrows the active path again. A prepared packet is not enough to
record that contact happened. The operator must create a minimal `send-log.json`
under `scratch/owner-send-logs/` after the human send or adaptation, then rerun
the router for the sent contact clock.

The kernel is now:

1. prepare the bounded eight-row packet;
2. send or adapt it outside the archive;
3. record a minimal send log with no contact details or owner answers;
4. record `SENT_AWAITING_REPLY` from that send log;
5. intake a real returned CSV through the router, or send one bounded re-ask, or
   record a bounded `NO_OWNER_PACKET`;
6. never treat any local artifact in that path as `SRC2+` evidence.

## What must not change

No new public claim can be made from packet prep, send-now brief, field-texture
memo, send log, contact status, field-next docket, intake bundle, workbench seed,
or smoke output. `FT-0181` remains live until a real owner-reviewed packet is
received, staged, accepted, reviewed, and closed through the downstream evidence
path.

## What should change next

The next substantive change should be external, not another internal control:
run the packet path, send or adapt the request, record the send log, and let the
router choose the next field command. If the route fails, record that bounded
failure instead of widening the ask.
