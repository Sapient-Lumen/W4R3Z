# rev0289 mission kernel

The mission remains: protect learning, learner agency, access, teacher capacity, contestability, source provenance, and claim humility before AI systems gain authority in education.

## rev0289 kernel change

The riskiest unfinished work is now late owner context after a terminal readout and recheck. `rev0289` makes that context path executable without treating the recheck as evidence: returned context must be hash-linked by a scratch-local context receipt before intake or workbench seed can proceed.

## Current live rail

1. Prepare the owner packet or record a no-send route block.
2. Record send/reask/contact state only from real human action.
3. Intake a real returned owner CSV from either a valid contact clock or a valid post-readout context receipt.
4. Seed/review/decide without accepting candidate evidence too early.
5. Gate activation by same-source packet lineage.
6. Record active-change ticket, live-window card, terminal readout, post-readout action, and dated recheck.
7. If new owner context exists after recheck, record a context receipt and route the same CSV through intake.
8. Stop before public claims, service-record edits, lifecycle movement, custody, acceptance, or closure.

## What stays forbidden

No local artifact may become real evidence, service-record mutation, lifecycle movement, public-claim support, custody, acceptance, or closure. `FT-0181` remains live until real owner evidence and closure signoff exist.
