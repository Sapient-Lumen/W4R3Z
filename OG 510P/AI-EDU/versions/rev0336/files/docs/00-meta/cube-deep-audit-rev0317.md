# rev0317 cube deep audit: execution over doctrine

## Executive judgment

The riskiest unfinished work is still external and pedagogical, not technical. The cube can now
prepare a safe owner packet and describe a teacher/tutor micro-pilot, but neither matters until a
human owner acts. Rev0317 therefore turns two scratch-level or plan-level surfaces into durable
operator artifacts:

- an `FT-0181` owner contact send pack with the exact router-emitted boundary command;
- a teacher/tutor micro-pilot run card with an owner plan, session log, stop rules, and end-of-cycle
  decision thresholds.

This is forward motion because the next human can now do the work without reading the governance
tail.

## What is at risk of not getting completed

### Owner contact

The field router produced the correct next state in rev0317:

```text
RECORD-SEND-LOG-AFTER-HUMAN-SEND
```

That means local packet preparation is complete enough for a human send decision. The next operator
should either send the bounded owner request and then record the send log, or record the absence of a
safe owner route. The archive must not widen the request, fabricate a send log, or treat a prepared
packet as evidence.

### Pedagogical cycle

The micro-pilot is now executable as a one-concept run. The owner-facing plan asks for a concept,
setting, baseline, no-AI transfer item, fallback route, review-time expectation, stop triggers, and
public claim ceiling. The session log captures only aggregates and incident counts. That is enough
to decide retire, repeat narrower, continue bounded, or escalate to pilot review after owner
attestation.

### Governance burden

The cube's governance markdown now exceeds 350,000 words. The `portable-*` and `sector-and-*`
profile tails alone account for roughly 66,788 words. Those surfaces may still matter as cold
retrieval, but they must not compete with the owner send pack or run card for operator attention.
