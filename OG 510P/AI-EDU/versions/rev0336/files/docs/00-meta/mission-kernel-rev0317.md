# rev0317 mission kernel

AI-EDU's mission remains teacher-led, student-active, evidence-rich, safety-governed education in an
AI-normal world. The current revision treats two tasks as more important than any new registry:

1. cross or honestly block the `FT-0181` owner-contact boundary;
2. make the teacher/tutor augmentation micro-pilot runnable by a real owner.

The archive has enough proof machinery for now. It needs human routing, a bounded learning cycle,
and deletion pressure after evidence arrives.

## Current operating thesis

Use AI first to strengthen human instructional judgment. In the near term, the safest high-value
experiment is a teacher/tutor-facing move coach that helps a human choose probing questions,
misconception checks, hint ladders, and reflection prompts. The coach must not become an autonomous
student answer surface, a grading tool, a record writer, a risk classifier, or a protected-support
inference system.

This direction is consistent with `B11`, `B13`, and `B279`: guided educational AI can support
learning when teaching principles and human judgment remain central, while unmanaged task completion
can mask weak learning.
