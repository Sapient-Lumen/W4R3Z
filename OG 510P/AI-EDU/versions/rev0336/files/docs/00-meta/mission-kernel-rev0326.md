# rev0326 mission kernel

## Heart of the mission

AI-EDU exists to make **human learning and human educational judgment stronger**, especially where
teachers, tutors, and learners need better questions, feedback, access, and transfer. AI is useful
only when it helps a person become more capable without quietly becoming the authority, the record,
or the substitute for thought.

Governance is a constraint on the work. It is not the product. The product is a repeatable learning
interaction in which:

1. the learner can explain or transfer the idea without AI;
2. the teacher or tutor makes a better instructional decision without hidden net burden;
3. the learner can decline, contest, or use a non-AI route without penalty.

## Current sequence

The pedagogical rail is primary. The `FT-0181` owner-contact rail remains useful evidence
infrastructure, but it must not block local learning discovery.

1. A teacher or tutor names a real local problem, construct, learner context, and ordinary fallback.
2. The owner selects the concept. `equality-one-step` remains an optional demonstration kit, not a
   project-selected default.
3. One small cycle tests feasibility and usability only: can the human use the move coach safely,
   is at least one suggestion instructionally useful, does workload remain acceptable, and is there
   no obvious access or transfer harm?
4. The owner records model/tool/version, learner notice and aggregate voice, privacy threshold, and a
   protected local equity-review route.
5. No efficacy or learning-effect claim is made from the micro-cycle. A larger, pre-specified design
   is required for that question.

## Revision gate

After rev0326, a release should exist only for one of four reasons:

- a real field event occurred;
- a reproducible defect was fixed;
- material was deleted, merged, or moved out of the hot path;
- material external evidence changed a decision.

A new recorder, branch, schema, or validator is not progress by itself.

## Boundary

Rev0326 fixes path drift, improves the feasibility protocol, strengthens synthetic-example labeling,
and adds release provenance. It does not contact an owner, run a real pilot, accept `SRC2+` evidence,
prove learning, or close `FT-0181`.
