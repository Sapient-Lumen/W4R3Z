# rev0336 mission kernel

The mission remains to help teachers and tutors choose better next instructional moves while preserving human judgment, learner dignity, fallback access, and unassisted transfer.

rev0336 protects that mission at the point where a first local result could otherwise turn into fake progress. A `repeat-narrower` or `continue-bounded` decision must now create a fresh packet through a hash-linked prior result receipt, while the actual next concept and required change still come from the local owner and stay de-identified.

Success is still not a green validator. Success is one real local educator conversation, one locally approved bounded cycle, and a result that either retires the line or informs one fresh, source-linked local packet without claiming learning impact.
