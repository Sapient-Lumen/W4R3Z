# rev0328 mission kernel

## Heart of the mission

AI-EDU exists to strengthen learner independence and teacher/tutor judgment. The smallest useful unit
is still a human-reviewed next move after a learner attempt: a better question, misconception check, or
smallest-useful hint that preserves no-AI explanation and transfer.

## Current correction

Rev0327 repaired the readiness gate. Rev0328 repairs the next practical bottleneck: the archive still
made it easier to open internal administration than to ask a real educator for one locally selected
problem. The field handoff now leads with a one-page `DISCOVERY-FIRST-CONTACT.md` card and puts the
teacher/tutor discovery rail before the `FT-0181` owner-evidence rail.

This is not a new control family. It is a hot-path inversion: ask the human first, then complete the
owner plan, then score readiness.

## What remains risky

No revision can create the missing relationship. A real teacher/tutor still has to choose the concept,
confirm local participation and fallback rules, name tool/model/version/configuration, define local
privacy and protected-review handling, and decide whether one feasibility cycle is permissible.

`FT-0181` also remains open until a genuine owner-evidence route is used, blocked, or returned. The
pedagogical feasibility rail cannot substitute for `SRC2+` owner evidence.

## Revision gate

The next revision should ship only for a real educator conversation, a real cycle, a real owner send or
route block, a reproducible defect, material deletion/compression, or evidence that changes a decision.

## Boundary

Rev0328 creates no owner contact, no route block, no real pilot, no `SRC2+` packet, no owner review, no
result, no learning proof, no public claim support, and no `FT-0181` closure.
