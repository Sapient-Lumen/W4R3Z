# rev0291 mission kernel

The mission remains: protect learning, learner agency, access, teacher capacity, contestability, source provenance, and claim humility before AI systems gain authority in education.

## rev0291 kernel change

`rev0291` is a mission/interface audit rather than a new gate. The cube is technically healthy, but its live value is now bottlenecked by field contact, not by policy imagination. The right posture is to keep the large archive quiet behind a small executable rail: ask the router for one command, prepare/adapt the bounded owner packet, record only real human action, and refuse every local shortcut into evidence, custody, public claims, or closure.

## Heart of the mission

The heart is not “AI adoption.” The heart is an education system where AI raises understanding, agency, access, teacher capacity, and institutional intelligence without laundering shortcuts into learning evidence or letting automated tools quietly acquire authority over grades, placement, records, eligibility, accommodations, discipline, or public claims.

In operational terms, the cube is a brake and a compass: it permits movement only when the next move is reversible, source-traced, teacher-legible, learner-protective, and honest about what has not been proven.

## Current live rail

1. Prepare the owner packet or record a no-send route block.
2. Record send/reask/contact state only from real human action.
3. Intake a real returned owner CSV from either a valid contact clock or a valid post-readout context receipt.
4. Seed/review/decide without accepting candidate evidence too early.
5. Gate activation by same-source packet lineage.
6. Record active-change ticket, live-window card, terminal readout, post-readout action, and dated recheck.
7. If new owner context exists after recheck, record a context receipt, rerun the router with the same actual CSV, and intake only through `SOURCE_POST_READOUT_CONTEXT_RECEIPT`.
8. Stop before public claims, service-record edits, lifecycle movement, custody, acceptance, or closure.

## What stays forbidden

No field-next docket, prepared packet, send log, contact-status note, intake bundle, workbench seed/review, first-packet decision, activation receipt, post-decision ticket, live-window card, readout, post-readout action, recheck, context receipt, audit, release example, schema, registry, or lint result may become real evidence, service-record mutation, lifecycle movement, public-claim support, custody, acceptance, or closure. `FT-0181` remains live until real owner evidence and closure signoff exist.
