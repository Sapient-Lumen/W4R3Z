# rev0318 mission kernel

AI-EDU's mission remains teacher-led, student-active, evidence-rich, safety-governed education in an
AI-normal world. Rev0318 tightens the working rule: **do not add doctrine when a one-command field
act can reduce the bottleneck.**

The current bottleneck is still not theory. It is the absence of two real-world events:

1. an accountable-owner send, route block, or returned owner packet for `FT-0181`;
2. one bounded teacher/tutor micro-pilot cycle that tests transfer, workload, access, and answer
   leakage with aggregate capture only.

## Current operating thesis

Use AI first to strengthen human instructional judgment, not to automate learner answers or
institutional decisions. The highest-value near-term experiment is a teacher/tutor-facing move coach:
a human asks for probing questions, misconception checks, and smallest-useful hints, then chooses,
edits, or rejects every move. The coach must not become a grading tool, record writer, learner risk
classifier, protected-support inference system, or autonomous student companion.

This stays aligned with `B11`, `B13`, and `B279`: AI can help when instruction and human judgment stay
central, while unmanaged task completion can mask weak learning.

## Priority order

1. Use [`../30-operations/ft0181-owner-contact-send-pack.md`](../30-operations/ft0181-owner-contact-send-pack.md)
   to send the bounded owner request or record a route block.
2. Use `make micro-pilot-pack` to prepare a complete scratch-only teacher/tutor packet for one
   concept, one owner, one fallback, and one no-AI transfer check.
3. Use [`../30-operations/teacher-tutor-micro-pilot-run-card.md`](../30-operations/teacher-tutor-micro-pilot-run-card.md)
   during the run, not the governance tail.
4. Keep raw learner, protected-route, security, staff-surveillance, and small-cell material out of
   the archive.
5. After a real owner packet or real micro-pilot readout, run the deletion test in
   [`governance-tail-consolidation-audit-rev0318.md`](governance-tail-consolidation-audit-rev0318.md).
