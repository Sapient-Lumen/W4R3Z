# rev0303 mission kernel

The mission remains unchanged: keep the AI-EDU cube answerable to real learning,
agency, access, teacher capacity, source truth, and public-claim humility after AI
becomes ordinary education infrastructure.

The live center remains `FT-0181`: import real owner-reviewed pilot records only
when they exist, and prevent local tooling from becoming evidence, public claims,
custody, acceptance, service-record authority, lifecycle movement, or closure.

## Heart of the mission

The cube is not here to produce a prettier governance library. It is here to make
the next truthful field action smaller than the next attractive doctrine surface.
When the archive succeeds, a maintainer can tell exactly what is known, what is not
known, who must act next, which local artifacts are only rehearsal/control state,
and which claim words are still forbidden.

For `FT-0181`, the smallest honest next move is still owner-route work: prepare the
bounded first-contact packet or route the real owner-returned CSV/context through
the guarded intake path. Until a real owner packet exists, the cube can only reduce
operator drop-off and false closure; it cannot prove learning, safety, access,
workload, compliance, scale, or effectiveness.

## rev0303 correction

`rev0303` fixes a cloudtainer-specific waste seam discovered during deep readout:
lint/check helpers write synthetic scratch artifacts under `scratch/check-*`, and
some helper outputs written outside those subtrees can still provenance-link back
to checker scratch. The field router previously scanned default `scratch/` without
distinguishing test/check scratch from field scratch, so a maintainer who ran lints
could be routed as if a synthetic checker artifact were current local field state.

The router now applies a scratch firebreak: it ignores `check-*`, `smoke-*`,
`test-*`, and `fixture-*` scratch subtrees relative to the selected scratch root,
and ignores artifacts whose provenance references point back into those non-field
subtrees. The package already excluded `scratch/`, but the live cloudtainer session
needed the same separation before routing.

## Current field rail

```text
owner-field-work
→ owner-after-human-send after real human send
→ owner-returned-reply-work when a real CSV returns
→ owner-workbench-review-brief
→ owner-workbench-review by a human
→ owner-first-packet-decision-brief
→ owner-first-packet-decision by a human
→ owner-post-decision-change-ticket-brief
→ owner-post-decision-change-ticket by a human
→ owner-activation-live-window-brief
→ activation receipt only from the same real SRC2+ packet, or live-window card only from active_change
→ owner-live-window-terminal-brief after staged/active card
→ owner-live-window-readout-brief after terminal card
→ owner-post-readout-action-brief after terminal readout
→ owner-post-readout-action by a human
→ owner-post-readout-recheck-brief on/after the due date
→ owner-post-readout-recheck by a human
→ owner-post-readout-context-receipt only for actual new context held outside the archive
```

`owner-field-work` may prepare safe local bridges only. It cannot record a send,
route block, contact clock, returned CSV intake, human review, board decision,
change ticket, activation receipt, live-window card, terminal card, readout,
post-readout dispatch, recheck, context receipt, owner action, custody,
acceptance, public support, lifecycle movement, or closure.

## Missing on purpose

The missing thing is still real owner evidence, not another policy layer. The
archive still lacks an accepted owner-reviewed SRC2+ packet, real field readout,
owner-held post-readout action result, accepted context cycle, and closeout. The
right correction is not to invent substitute evidence; it is to keep the local rail
clean enough that a real owner packet can enter without being mixed with checker
state or narrative residue.
