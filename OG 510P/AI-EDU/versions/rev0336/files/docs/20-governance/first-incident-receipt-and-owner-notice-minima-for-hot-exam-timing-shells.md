# First incident-receipt and owner-notice minima for hot exam timing shells

This document closes the archive's next narrower governance gap after **timing and substitute-owner
judgments**.

Three overclaims matter here:

- **receipt laundering** — once artifact-attached timing shells exist, every hot exam route gets
  treated as if it now deserves one universal incident receipt;
- **handback collapse** — submitted-status notice, secure booklet collection, and local
  record-retention notice get treated as if they were the same kind of handback event when they
  actually attach to different authoritative artifacts; and
- **substitute-actor receipt fantasy** — whichever proctor, coordinator, interpreter, scribe, or
  teacher touches the shell gets treated as if they inherit one portable receipt/notice authority
  across digital, hybrid, and checkpointed routes.

Current official signals support a tighter answer. Bluebook's current in-school answer-submission
guidance tells coordinators and proctors to verify answer-submission status, keep shared devices
available or protected until submission is complete, require the same testing device, and submit AP
answers within 3 days. The current Test Day Toolkit and AP digital proctoring pages then add the
operational handback truth: proctors should make sure answers are submitted before students leave,
dismiss students with submitted answers before helping students with unsubmitted answers, and
collect applicable exam materials as part of dismissal. Current hybrid digital AP materials keep a
different handback truth visible: students write free-response answers in paper booklets, place
identification information plus an AP ID label on the booklet cover, and those secure exam materials
must be returned to the AP Program after testing. Current AP Digital Portfolio and AP Capstone
policy pages keep a third split-stack notice visible again: AP Seminar and AP Research students
submit written task components by **April 30, 2026, 11:59 p.m. ET**, teachers submit presentation
scores and checkpoint affirmations by **May 10, 2026, 11:59 p.m. ET**, and AP Research teachers must
keep presentation/oral-defense videos for at least one academic year; current accommodations
guidance still keeps interpreters and scribes inside bounded delivery/no-preview assistance rather
than portable ownership. Together those signals support a narrower archive rule: **the next portable
publication move is not one universal incident receipt, but artifact-specific receipt / handback /
owner-notice minima attached to each hot timing shell.** See `B208`.

confirmation, custody handback, or owner notice has to be visible once that shell is activated or
transferred**.

## `RC1` — unresolved post-end digital submission needs explicit status, not a generic reassurance

The archive can now harden its first true incident-receipt shell.

`RC1-STATUS-DEVICE-DEADLINE` means that when a fully digital AP attempt reaches post-end
unresolved-submission space, the minimum truthful handback is no longer just “we’ll handle it.” The
portable shell should say at least four things:

1. whether the current status is **submitted** or **still unresolved**;
2. that submission must occur on the **same device used for testing**;
3. that AP answers must be submitted within **3 days**; and
4. who owns the next action if the answers are still unresolved.

Current Bluebook and AP proctor/coordinator materials support exactly that shell: status should be
checked in Test Day Toolkit or Bluebook, students with submitted answers should be dismissed before
unresolved cases are handled, shared devices must stay with the student or be protected, and
post-end device swap should not be used. See `B208`.

This is still not a universal receipt. It is a digital post-end shell attached to `AC2 + TC1`, not
to every interruption.

## `RC2` — hybrid digital handback is custody, not learner self-cure

The archive can now harden its first non-digital handback shell too.

`RC2-IDENTIFIED-COLLECTION-CUSTODY` means that when the authoritative artifact is a hybrid digital
free-response booklet, the minimum truthful handback is an **identified collection-and-custody
event** rather than a learner submission workflow. The portable shell should say at least three
things:

1. the booklet was the scored free-response artifact for this route;
2. the booklet was tied to student identity on the cover; and
3. custody passed back into the secure proctor/coordinator return chain rather than into a later
   learner self-submit path.

Current hybrid digital AP pages make the paper-booklet artifact explicit and say students place
brief identification information and an AP ID label on the cover. Current AP return guidance then
says secure AP Exam materials must be returned to the AP Program. That is enough to publish one
narrow collected-into-custody shell. See `B208`.

## `RC3` — portfolio-plus-local-record continuity needs a split notice, not one fake receipt

Checkpointed paper-plus-defense authorship now gets its first truthful notice shell too.

`RC3-PORTFOLIO-LOCAL-SPLIT-NOTICE` means that once continuity lives in the uploaded paper plus a
retained local presentation/oral-defense record, the archive should publish a split notice rather
than pretend one platform receipt settles the whole route. The portable shell should say at least
four things:

1. which written component the student must submit in the AP Digital Portfolio and by what deadline;
2. which presentation-score / checkpoint-affirmation step the teacher must complete and by what
   deadline;
3. that the defense record remains a **local** artifact rather than part of the written portfolio
   submission; and
4. who must keep that local record, and for at least how long.

Current AP Digital Portfolio pages make the student and teacher deadlines explicit. Current AP
Capstone policy makes checkpoints and authenticity affirmations explicit. Current AP Research and
accommodations materials keep the presentation/oral-defense record local and require retention for
at least one academic year. That is enough to publish a split owner notice without inventing one
unified receipt. See `B208`.

## Negative judgment retained: no universal receipt actor

The archive can now say something stronger by refusing a second tempting false portability claim.

`RC0-NO-UNIVERSAL-RECEIPT` means that these receipt shells do **not** collapse into one portable
actor or one universal document. Proctors may verify status and dismiss. Coordinators may protect
shared devices or manage secure returns. Teachers may score presentations, affirm checkpoints, and
retain local records. Interpreters and scribes may still assist inside bounded no-preview /
authentic-delivery roles. None of that is yet enough to publish one cross-route receipt owner.

This matters because ownership of notice is still attached to the artifact and stage, not to
whichever adult is nearest the incident.

## What becomes canon now

The archive's current best compact rule is now:

1. **publish status and next action when the authoritative artifact stays with the learner/device**;
2. **publish collection/custody handback when the authoritative artifact is returned into a secure
   paper chain**;
3. **publish a split owner notice when the authoritative artifact is divided between platform
   submission and local retained record**; and
4. **keep receipt ownership artifact-specific rather than promoting one universal receipt actor or
   form**.

That means the archive now carries three truthful post-timing publication shells:

- a digital unresolved-submission **status / device / deadline / next-owner** shell (`RC1`),
- a hybrid digital **identified collection / custody** shell (`RC2`), and
- a checkpointed paper-plus-defense **portfolio / local-record split notice** shell (`RC3`).

It still carries **no** universal receipt form and **no** shared receipt owner.

## Consequence for future revisions

The archive's next narrower question is no longer whether these artifact-attached timing shells need
any visible handback at all. It is whether they now justify a tiny **shared status vocabulary /
escalation-report crosswalk** without collapsing digital unresolved submission, hybrid booklet
custody, and portfolio/local-record notice into one false universal incident form. See `OQ-0005`.
