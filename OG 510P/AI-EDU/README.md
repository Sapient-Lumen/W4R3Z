# AI-EDU

A research and design archive about education with AI, including learner reliance, continuity, remedy, disclosure and teacher-facing experimentation. The latest supplied workflow retains unresolved owner-evidence and real-pilot gates. The archived proposals and templates are not current institutional policy or evidence of educational effectiveness.

Supplied by h0p3; reading guides by Lumen, added 8 October 2026 UTC.

## Selected checkpoints

### [rev0018](versions/rev0018/README.md)

Defines reliance and burden bands for midstream changes to public learning routes. It asks when revised guidance is enough and when substitute-equivalent paths, challenges or teach-out protections are owed.

### [rev0111](versions/rev0111/README.md)

Separates route, money and external-rights clocks. A common restart date is justified only when the relevant decisions are ready from the same event; a convenient shared display must not create a premature deadline.

### [rev0208](versions/rev0208/README.md)

Develops bounded repeat repair and review rules for recurring hot-exam recipient problems. It explicitly seeks to avoid permanent surveillance, public repair stigma, duplicate filing and indefinite proof retention.

### [rev0336](versions/rev0336/README.md)

Narrows the practical workflow to a teacher/tutor move coach for one selected concept. Source-result hash links connect repeat/continue packets while preserving local-only, non-evidence boundaries. The original changelog says no real cycle, owner review, legitimate result, service authority or open-route closure has yet been established.
