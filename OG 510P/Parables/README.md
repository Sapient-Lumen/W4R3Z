# Parables

Seven selected snapshots of a four-file literary collection: collected or retold parables, a separately labeled AI-original collection, a searched-URL ledger, and selection notes. The archive’s sources and editorial decisions are retained with the stories. These are historical retellings and attributed research notes, not newly verified biographies or a new grant of rights to the underlying works.

Supplied by h0p3. Reading guides by Lumen, added 8 October 2026 UTC.

## Selected snapshots

### [snapshot-1771735262](versions/snapshot-1771735262/README.md)

The small early snapshot contains four collected-story sections and one AI-original section. Its source notes begin on 21 February 2026 and explain the selection of forgiveness, mutual giving and unobtrusive mercy stories. The numeric filename is retained without inventing a revision number.

### [snapshot-20260307-091655](versions/snapshot-20260307-091655/README.md)

A larger dated snapshot whose last collected titles include The Yoke He Would Not Divide, The Wet Note in the Morning, and The Clothes He Gave Again. The notes retain rejected candidates and the decision not to add another AI parable.

### [round74-20260309](versions/round74-20260309/README.md)

The filename identifies round74. The final collected entries include The Woman Who Would Not Let the Hidden Die Alone. Read the source notes with the retelling: they explain the selection and distinguish work spent considering a story from a decision to keep it.

### [rev0201](versions/rev0201/README.md)

Adds The House That Burned Second to the collected shelf. The notes compare several kindness-story candidates and record no new AI parable. It illustrates the collection’s preference for a concrete act over a tidy reward or moral explanation.

### [rev0326](versions/rev0326/README.md)

Keeps the retelling titled The Rolls After Midnight, sourced in the notes to Raymond Carver’s A Small, Good Thing. The selection turns on care offered after injury and the shared meal; attribution and the original work’s rights remain important.

### [rev0401](versions/rev0401/README.md)

A documented no-keeper pass. Its notes examine further stories about mercy after humiliation and leave both story files unchanged. The absence of a new story is part of the recorded editorial judgment, not a missing delivery.

### [rev0515](versions/rev0515/README.md)

Keeps The Croissants Between Enemies, with the notes identifying Greg Boyle/Homeboy Industries as its source context. The selected image is enemies working beside one another at a bakery. The AI-original file is unchanged in this pass.
