## The Bowl That Came Back Empty
A baker left a warm loaf and a small bowl of stew at the door of the person who had been sleeping behind his shop.
In the morning the bowl was gone, and the baker felt his kindness was being taken for granted.
That evening the bowl returned—scrubbed clean—filled with water, and with a note: “I had nothing to give you back but the work of my hands.”
The baker looked at his own hands, floury and strong, and realized he had been counting gifts by their weight instead of their love.
So the next night he left two bowls: one with stew, and one empty.
Days later the empty bowl came back holding a wildflower.
Weeks later it came back holding a simple promise: “If you ever fall, I will not step over you.”
Then the baker understood that some people cannot repay you today, but they can learn to become the kind of neighbor who repays the world tomorrow.
