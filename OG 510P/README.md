# OG 510P

## The writing of sandpeople

A library of prose, research, stories, translations, designs and experiments, supplied by h0p3. Read it for the questions it pursues and the turns its thinking takes.

Some works come with software. Some imagine institutions, languages or tools. Some revisit the meaning of a word, a moral claim or an act of care. The common invitation is to read, not to assume that every project is a product awaiting completion.

## A few places to begin

- [Parables](Parables/README.md): enter through stories and the choices behind their retelling.
- [Conlang Rosetta Stone](Conlang%20Rosetta%20Stone/README.md): follow what happens when passages about love meet Ithkuil and Lojban.
- [The Good](The-Good/README.md), [Righteousness](Righteousness/README.md) and [Goldenrule](Goldenrule/README.md): approach related moral questions without treating the three works as synonyms.
- [MissingKnowledgeHalf](MissingKnowledgeHalf/README.md) and [DelayBasin](DelayBasin/README.md): consider what gets lost, and what deserves to survive.
- [Rust](Rust/README.md): read proposed tools as an exploration of needs, possibilities and reasons an idea may fail.

These are invitations, not a ranking of the collection. [The full catalog](../CATALOG.md#og-510p) includes every shelf and its related projects.

## Reading through time

Each work retains the snapshots h0p3 supplied. They let a reader encounter earlier formulations, later corrections, unresolved questions and changes in emphasis. The newest package is not automatically the clearest introduction, and a revision number does not prove a complete lineage.

Lumen’s guides sit beside the works. Their purpose is to help readers find worthwhile passages and understand their context, while leaving original prose, source attribution and disagreements available to inspect.

## The preserved collection

Original uploads and separately extracted contents remain with their projects. The shared shelves—[nicotine+](nicotine%2B/README.md), [MTG](MTG/README.md), [Conlang Rosetta Stone](Conlang%20Rosetta%20Stone/README.md) and [Rust](Rust/README.md)—keep related works together without conflating their histories.

Historical plans and commands are part of the documents; publication does not restart an experiment. [Preservation, evidence and rights](../PRESERVATION.md) gives the fuller account. Existing licenses and source-specific notices remain applicable.
