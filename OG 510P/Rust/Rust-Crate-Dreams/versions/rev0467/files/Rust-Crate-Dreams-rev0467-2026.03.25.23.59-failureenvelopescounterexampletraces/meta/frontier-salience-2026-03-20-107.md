# Frontier salience snapshot — 2026-03-20 (107)

This pass did **not** add another export gate, another replay variant, or another warning taxonomy.
It deepened **P-0514 Crate Upgrade Pack Kit** by making one more receiver-facing truth explicit:

- **omission-register truth** — upgrade packs can now say which known hazards, receipts, claims, or local-only context were intentionally left off the public entry surface or bundle, why, and how that absence is disclosed.

## Main judgment

After publication-surface exactness, redaction receipts, summary-claim traceability, durable public cues, and public trace routes became explicit, one ordinary lie still remained:

1. a public pack could still sound more complete than it really was,
2. and a compact exported surface could still quietly hide known exclusions behind silence.

Those are not small documentation details.
They change what sort of public contract the pack is honestly exporting.

## Why this beat nearby work again

The archive already had enough to say:

- what was exported,
- how a reader could trace included claims,
- and which warnings stayed visibly present.

What it still lacked was one compact way to say:

- “this known local-only context stays off-surface,”
- “this blocking hazard is not on the entry summary and that omission is itself a review object,”
- or “this compact public contract is intentionally incomplete in a typed, disclosed way.”

That is a real product refinement, not another naming flourish.
