- **P-0285** HL7 v2 + MLLP Interop & Evidence Kit (`*.hl7bundle.zip`)

  - HL7v2 over MLLP concept overview: https://docs.cloud.google.com/healthcare-api/docs/concepts/hl7v2
  - Note on MLLP lacking native encryption + MLLP-over-TLS trend: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/healthcare-industry-lens/interoperability-architectures.html

## Why these belong in the archive

Across these domains, teams repeatedly need the same missing primitives:
- **canonical transcripts** (diffable, minimal, versioned IRs)
- **replayable scenarios** (runner specs + seeds)
- **safe bundles** (redaction policies made explicit and reproducible)
- **“explain mode”** for why two stacks diverge

These proposals are meant to *compose* with existing protocol crates and vendor stacks, not replace them.
