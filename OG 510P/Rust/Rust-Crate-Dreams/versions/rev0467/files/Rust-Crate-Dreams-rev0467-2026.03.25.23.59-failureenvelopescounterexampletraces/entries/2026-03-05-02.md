# 2026-03-05 (02): supervision + sandbox policy doctor + determinism lab

This entry adds three proposals that aim to make Rust systems **more operationally trustworthy**:

1) **Task supervision & restart policy** for async systems (“OTP-ish” resilience, but Rust-native).
2) **Sandbox policy authoring & diagnostics** (capability model + audit feedback loop).
3) **Determinism lab workflow** for schedule-sensitive async bugs (capture/minimize/replay bundles).

## Added proposals

- **P-0095 — Task Supervision & Restart Kit (async “OTP for Rust”)**  
  Evidence: task grouping exists (Tokio `JoinSet`) but restart/supervision policy is still mostly ad-hoc; OTP shows reusable strategies.  
  Sources: https://docs.rs/tokio/latest/tokio/task/struct.JoinSet.html , https://www.erlang.org/doc/apps/stdlib/supervisor.html  
  See: `proposals/task-supervision-restart-kit.md`.

- **P-0096 — Capability Sandbox Policy Kit (“policy doctor”)**  
  Evidence: Landlock enables unprivileged sandboxing; docs and guidance highlight the need for better integrated policy tooling and log-driven iteration.  
  Sources: https://crates.io/crates/landlock , https://landlock.io/rust-landlock/landlock/ , https://github.com/ossf/wg-best-practices-os-developers/issues/631 , https://www.openwall.com/lists/oss-security/2025/05/19/2  
  See: `proposals/capability-sandbox-policy-kit.md`.

- **P-0097 — Determinism Lab Kit (schedule exploration + replay bundles)**  
  Evidence: `loom` provides concurrency interleaving exploration; `sturgeon` and runtime experiments show demand for record/replay tooling and seeded schedules.  
  Sources: https://docs.rs/loom/latest/loom/ , https://docs.rs/sturgeon , https://docs.rs/asupersync  
  See: `proposals/determinism-lab-kit.md`.
