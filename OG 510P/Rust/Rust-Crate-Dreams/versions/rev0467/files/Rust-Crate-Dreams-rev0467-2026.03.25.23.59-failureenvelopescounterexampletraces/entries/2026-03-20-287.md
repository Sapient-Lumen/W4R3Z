# Entry 287 — crate upgrade support sharpened around omission-register truth and public-completeness honesty

This pass came from noticing that the upgrade pack had become exact about exported files, public trace paths, redaction, durable cues, and summary-claim traceability while still leaving one ordinary bluff available:

> a public pack could still look exhaustive even when some known hazards, receipts, or local-only context were intentionally left off the exported entry surface for non-redaction reasons.

## What moved this time

One donor pattern kept surviving scrutiny:

- **Overseer** kept a first-class **Not Included** surface, and **Anonymity** kept treating explicit no-publication / hold reasons as part of the outward contract rather than as silent absence.

## Main planning move

The previous passes already established:

- exact export/publication surfaces,
- exact redaction basis,
- exact summary-claim traceability and trace routes,
- and exact durable public cues.

This pass adds one sharper review object above them:

1. **omission-register truth** — the pack can now say which known claims, hazards, receipts, or local-only context were intentionally left off the public entry surface or bundle, why they were omitted, whether that omission is explicitly disclosed, and where the nearest safe fallback lives.

## Why this matters

Without **omission-register truth**, the lane still permits one ordinary lie:

- a summary can be traceable,
- exported files can be exact,
- redaction can be fully reviewable,
- yet the public contract can still sound more complete than it really is because known exclusions remain silent.

That is not cosmetic.
It is the difference between “this pack is compact” and “this pack is silently incomplete in ways a reader should know about.”

## Main judgment after the pass

A worthy upgrade-support crate should now provide more than exact public files and traceable included claims.
It should also provide:

- one compact **omission register** for known-but-not-exported material,
- one explicit distinction between **redacted away** and **deliberately omitted for another reason**,
- and one consistency failure when decisive known material disappears from the public entry surface without typed disclosure.

That keeps the lane from quietly turning “compact public contract” into “pretend exhaustive public contract.”
