# Entry 111 — future-incompat triage ledgers and artifact sidecar contracts

This pass stayed inside the same general family as the recent Cargo-focused work, but tightened it again:
**the ecosystem increasingly has the raw signals, yet ordinary teams still lack small review artifacts that make those signals boring to operate.**

New proposals:

- P-0478 Cargo Future-Incompat Triage Kit
- P-0479 Cargo Artifact Sidecar Contract Kit

Why these rose:

- **P-0478** rose because Cargo already has real future-incompatibility reporting and even a dedicated `cargo report` surface, but maintainers still lack a durable **owner / waiver / remediation ledger** above that report. The sharper missing layer is triage discipline, not another pretty renderer.
- **P-0479** rose because Cargo is now emitting more artifact-adjacent sidecars, especially around SBOM precursor output, but downstream tools still lack a compact **sidecar attachment contract** saying which companion files belong to which artifact and how schema drift should be reviewed.

What changed in archive judgment:

- The archive now treats **future-incompat management** as distinct from raw diagnostics display. A worthy crate contribution here may be a **dependency-risk ledger**, **waiver file**, **owner map**, or **upgrade-path bundle** rather than another report UI.
- The archive now treats **artifact sidecar contracts** as distinct from broad build manifests or SBOM generation. A worthy crate contribution here may be an **attachment receipt**, **sidecar index**, or **schema drift report** rather than another artifact exporter.
- The repo keeps converging on a sharper heuristic: when Rust/Cargo gains a new output surface, the missing crate may be the **reviewable contract above it**, not another executor below it.

Archive hygiene added in this pass:

- Added an amnesia resistor warning future revisions not to confuse built-in future-incompat reporting with a boring ownership and waiver workflow.
- Added an amnesia resistor warning future revisions not to confuse the existence of SBOM sidecars or sidecar discovery env vars with a ready-made attachment contract for packagers and CI.
