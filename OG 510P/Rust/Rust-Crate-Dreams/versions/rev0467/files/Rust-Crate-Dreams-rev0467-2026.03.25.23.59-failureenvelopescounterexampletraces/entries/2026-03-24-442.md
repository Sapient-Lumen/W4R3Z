# Entry 442 — pair pathfinder with knowledge packs, freeze packet-family discipline, and make review packets a first-class product surface

This pass again chose **deepen over append**.

The archive already had:
- a broad top frontier,
- better receiver and pilot thinking,
- and stronger first-release bundle language.

What it still lacked was a cleaner answer to three next-order questions:

1. how the top practical crates fit together as one **front door**,
2. what a shared **packet family discipline** should look like across those crates,
3. and what a more concrete **review bundle** for crate knowledge should hand other people on day one.

## Main judgment

Three repo moves were worth making now.

### 1. Treat **P-0509 Pathfinder** and **P-0536 Crate Knowledge Pack** as a paired front door

The practical queue already had these two near each other.
This pass makes the relationship explicit.

Pathfinder is strongest when it gives another person a frozen decision packet.
But that packet gets brittle very quickly if it depends on floating docs.rs `latest` pages, target-blind links, or unscoped support claims.

That makes **P-0536** more than an adjacent helper.
It is the substrate that keeps pathfinder packets:
- pinned,
- cited,
- machine-usable,
- and honest about what still needs manual review.

So the repo now treats these as a coupled front-door stack:
- **P-0509** decides and packages,
- **P-0536** supplies the reviewable basis those packets stand on.

### 2. Add packet-family discipline for the leading crates

By now, the archive’s best lanes all emit some mix of:
- receipts,
- reports,
- manifests,
- packs,
- locks,
- notes,
- matrices,
- and imported evidence.

That is good.
But without stronger discipline it is too easy for future passes to blur:
- raw observation versus derived judgment,
- local evidence versus imported upstream evidence,
- replayable frozen basis versus latest-view browsing routes,
- and human review notes versus machine-facing packs.

The new packet-family note gives the repo a smaller contract for how these files should be named, versioned, and scoped.
It uses current Rust substrate as the model rather than fantasy design:
- Cargo’s plumbing work explicitly talks about schema evolution and stable programmatic stages,
- Cargo already lives with strong versioning expectations around `Cargo.lock`,
- docs.rs rustdoc JSON is explicitly format-versioned,
- and the libtest JSON effort is motivated by how much the ecosystem depends on machine-readable output.

### 3. Deepen **P-0536** around review bundles, not just compact export

The archive already had strong work on:
- material basis,
- citation locators,
- answerability,
- item witnesses,
- build surface,
- and conditioned availability.

The next missing layer was:

> what compact packet should another engineer actually receive when a crate maintainer wants to support triage, architecture review, or assistant/tool import?

The new review-bundle plan says a worthy first release should hand other people a small family centered on:
- `review-packet.manifest.json`
- `assistant-context.pack.json`
- `query-support.matrix.json`
- `claim-trace.report.json`
- `citation-locator.receipt.json`
- `answer-boundary.note.md`

That is more operational than “machine-readable export”, but still narrower and more honest than a fake “chat with your crate” product.

## Salience consequence

This pass still does **not** justify a dramatic top-frontier rerank.
The top salience block stays broadly stable:

1. **P-0509 Crate Ecosystem Pathfinder & Decision-Pack Kit**
2. **P-0486 Debuggability Support Contract Kit**
3. **P-0538 Concurrency Contract Kit**
4. **P-0472 Docs.rs Build Parity & Evidence Kit**
5. **P-0536 Crate Knowledge Pack Kit**
6. **P-0496 Cargo Vendor & Source Parity Kit**

What changed is the practical reading of the top two lanes:
- **P-0509** remains the first receiver-facing product,
- but **P-0536** is now treated as the supporting packet substrate that should be developed in lockstep rather than as a later convenience layer.

## Practical queue consequence

The practical queue now reads best as:

1. **P-0509 + P-0536 together** — the front-door stack for decision packets backed by pinned review packets
2. **P-0486** — debug capability packets aligned to the same packet family discipline
3. **P-0496** — restricted-delivery / source-parity bundles
4. **P-0472** — docs.rs hosted/local parity doctor and issue bundles
5. **P-0489** — build-dir dual-support transition
6. **P-0535** — lifecycle / off-ramp packets
7. **P-0484** — target/support truth for hard domains
8. **P-0058** — native prerequisite / ABI provenance

Interpretation:
- this is **not** a claim that pathfinder and knowledge packs should merge into one crate;
- it **is** a claim that the repo should design them together because decision packets without pinned review packets will drift or bluff too easily.

## What this pass says a worthy crate should provide other people

A worthy crate should now survive **six** practical questions, not four:

1. **receiver** — who gets the packet?
2. **artifact family** — is the output a receipt, report, manifest, pack, lock, note, matrix, or import, and why?
3. **basis** — what pinned materials and exact versions support it?
4. **program** — what narrow workflow or first-adopter loop proves the packet is useful?
5. **schema** — how does the packet evolve without silently breaking downstream consumers?
6. **ceiling** — what must the crate still refuse to claim?

If a proposal cannot answer those yet, it may still be important, but it is probably not ready for promotion.
