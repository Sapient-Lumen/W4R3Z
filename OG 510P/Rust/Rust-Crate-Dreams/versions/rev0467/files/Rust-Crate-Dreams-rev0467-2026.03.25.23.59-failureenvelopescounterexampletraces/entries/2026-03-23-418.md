# Entry 418 — pathfinder deepened around explicit candidate elimination, re-entry triggers, and no-silent resurrection

This pass chose to **deepen P-0509 Crate Ecosystem Pathfinder & Decision-Pack Kit** again instead of opening another search tweak, popularity scoreboard, or blessed-starter-set essay.

The sharper question was:

> once a decision pack has ranked and frozen a starter set, how does it preserve *why a plausible candidate was excluded* and what exact evidence would let that candidate back in later, without silently resurrecting it because search order, docs visibility, or popularity changed?

## Why this deepening pass earned a full revision

Fresh sources made the next missing layer concrete:

- the March 2026 challenges write-up still says crate choice is shaped by **choice paralysis**, **undiscoverable crates**, and tacit knowledge;
- the 2024 survey results still say many people learn Rust by reading crate docs and source, which makes public visibility a real influence on choice;
- the January 2026 crates.io update adds **SLOC** and **`pubtime`** to public/index surfaces, which are useful but still not final task-fit authority;
- the active crates.io search discussion still says ordering is unresolved and current relevance ordering uses weighted text ranking plus lexical tie-breaking when ranks match;
- Cargo `info` still gives a human-readable package snapshot and may pick a version based on MSRV when none is specified;
- Cargo `add` is still an ergonomic add command, not a task-lane recommender;
- and the existing pathfinder lane already knows how to freeze a choice and replay it later, but not yet how to publish durable receipts for **why another candidate was kept out** and **what would reopen consideration**.

That means the next worthy move for **P-0509** is not only “show the winner” or “watch the winner later.”
It is to make **candidate exclusion** and **candidate re-entry** reviewable artifacts.

## Main judgment after this pass

The top frontier still begins:

1. **P-0537 Compile Iteration Feedback Kit**
2. **P-0509 Crate Ecosystem Pathfinder & Decision-Pack Kit**
3. **P-0535 Dependency Lifecycle Transition Kit**
4. **P-0536 Crate Knowledge Pack Kit**
5. **P-0538 Concurrency Contract Kit**

But **P-0509** is stronger now because the archive no longer stops at:

- task profiles,
- candidate imports,
- basis receipts,
- support-visibility reports,
- decision packs,
- starter-set locks,
- watch policies,
- and freeze-time replay receipts.

It now has a clearer product shape for:

- **candidate-elimination receipts**,
- **candidate-reentry policies**,
- **blocked-vs-runner-up separation**,
- **no-silent-resurrection doctor rules**,
- and portable bundle inventory that keeps the chosen answer, excluded candidates, and re-entry conditions separate.

## What should count as a worthy crate contribution in this lane now

A worthy crate-ecosystem pathfinder should provide other people:

1. **one candidate-elimination receipt** that says whether a candidate was merely a runner-up, blocked by a hard constraint, vetoed by local policy, or excluded only because evidence was too weak;
2. **one candidate-reentry policy** that says exactly what change would make that candidate eligible again;
3. **one explicit separation between exclusion and replacement** so a winner is not replaced just because a loser became eligible again;
4. **one defense against public-surface overreach** so search order, docs polish, or SLOC visibility cannot silently overrule a task or policy mismatch;
5. **one portable bundle** that preserves why a candidate was out at freeze time even after later ecosystem drift.

That is closer to an ecosystem-worthy crate than another static recommendation list.
