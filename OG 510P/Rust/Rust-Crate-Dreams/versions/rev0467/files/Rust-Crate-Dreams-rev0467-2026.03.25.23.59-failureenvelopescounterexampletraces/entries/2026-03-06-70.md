## Synthesis / pruning notes

- **Promoted** OCPP as a certification/evidence opportunity, not a “types crate” opportunity.
- **Promoted** FHIR with SMART because many real failures cross auth and data boundaries.
- **Merged** RDAP and EPP into one candidate because operators suffer at the provisioning/publication boundary.
- **Framed** IFC as conformance-first and exchange-quality-first rather than rendering-first.
- **Kept** OpenADR focused on reproducible grid-event workflows rather than broad utility-platform ambitions.
