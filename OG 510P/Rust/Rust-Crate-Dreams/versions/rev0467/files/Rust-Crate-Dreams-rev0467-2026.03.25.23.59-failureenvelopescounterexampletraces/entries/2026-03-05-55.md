## Why these are “missing-middle”
Across all five proposals, the unifying shape is: **interoperability depends on shareable evidence**.
The archive keeps steering toward *bundle-first* tooling because it scales: it turns “I saw a weird failure” into a portable artifact that others can replay, diff, and reason about.
