# 2026-03-04 — expanding “epic crate” territory: async replay, sandboxing, provenance, local-first

Added four proposals targeting ecosystem-level pain across reliability, security, and new product categories:

- **P-0073 Async Replay Debugger Kit** (deterministic record/replay + shareable bug bundles). Sources: https://cliffle.com/blog/lildb/ , https://github.com/tokio-rs/console
- **P-0074 Capability Sandbox Kit** (unified capability policy mapping to Landlock/seccomp/macOS/Windows). Sources: https://docs.kernel.org/userspace-api/landlock.html , https://users.rust-lang.org/t/using-rust-programs-inside-seccomp-sandbox/18488 , https://www.chromium.org/developers/design-documents/sandbox/osx-sandboxing-design/
- **P-0075 Cargo Provenance Suite** (SBOM + SLSA provenance + Sigstore verification + policy gates + evidence bundles). Sources: https://mozilla.github.io/cargo-vet/ , https://slsa.dev/spec/v1.1/faq , https://crates.io/crates/sigstore-verification , https://edu.chainguard.dev/open-source/sigstore/cosign/how-to-sign-an-sbom-with-cosign/
- **P-0076 Local-first Sync Kit** (CRDT stack with persistence/transport/E2EE + conformance vectors). Source: https://loro.dev/blog/v1.0

## Repo hygiene / amnesia resistors

- Each new proposal emphasizes **Non-goals** and **conformance artifacts** (TCKs, golden fixtures) as the “ecosystem contract”.
- Security/determinism proposals explicitly name **threat models** or **determinism envelopes** to prevent silent scope drift.
