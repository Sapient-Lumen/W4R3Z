# Scenario — known blocking hazard left out of public summary fails omission consistency

A public pack should not quietly leave a known blocking hazard off the entry summary and exported bundle without one explicit omission record.

This scenario keeps the failure visible:

- the warning register says a blocking hazard exists,
- the public summary does not mention it,
- no omission register explains the absence,
- and the consistency surface fails until the omission is either disclosed or the summary is corrected.
