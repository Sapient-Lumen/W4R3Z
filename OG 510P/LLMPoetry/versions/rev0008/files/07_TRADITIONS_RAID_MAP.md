# 07 — TRADITIONS RAID MAP
The world's poetries as an armory. RAID PROTOCOL: per tradition — search 2 named poems; extract the MOVE (a transferable mechanism, never vocabulary or props); write 1 poem deploying the move on ALIEN subject matter (the move proves itself only off its home turf); log what transferred. Imitation copies furniture; raiding steals load-bearing walls. Format below: TRADITION/POET → essence → THE MOVE → search targets [in brackets].

## EAST ASIA
- TANG SHI — Wang Wei: parallel couplets; image set beside image with NO connective tissue (proto-montage; the reader builds the bridge) → MOVE: delete your conjunctions and explanatory glue; juxtaposition does the thinking ["Wang Wei Deer Park nineteen ways" — Weinberger's book on 19+ translations of one quatrain is a masterclass in how translation choices ARE poetics].
- Du Fu: history entering through the kitchen door — empire's collapse registered in a haircut, a vegetable price → MOVE: macro-event via micro-ledger ["Du Fu Spring View"].
- Li Bai: the ecstatic address (to the moon, to himself as third party) ["Li Bai Drinking Alone with the Moon"].
- MISTY POETS (post-Mao) — Bei Dao: under censorship, the image becomes deniable speech; opacity as armor → MOVE: write a political poem with zero political nouns ["Bei Dao The Answer"].
- JAPAN — Bashō: KARUMI (lightness), KIGO (season-word = built-in concretion anchor), the haiku as collision of two images across a cut (KIREJI) → MOVE: install a cutting word/caesura and let two images spark across it; ban the third-line epiphany ["Basho frog pond translations comparison"]. Issa: comic compassion toward small creatures (sentiment held off by humor). Buson: painter's eye. RENGA/RENKU: link-and-shift law — each stanza links to its neighbor, must shift away from its grand-neighbor; topics rotate; narrative accumulation FORBIDDEN → the anti-narrative engine; basis of your Spine (08) ["renku link and shift rules"]. Sei Shōnagon's Pillow Book lists ("Things that quicken the heart") → MOVE: the categorical list as lyric ["Sei Shonagon lists"]. ZUIHITSU (Kimiko Hahn) per 03.
- KOREA — sijo's third-line twist; KIM HYESOON: grotesque feminist body-cosmology; Autobiography of Death = 49 poems for the 49 days the dead wander → MOVE: a numerical-ritual architecture dictating a sequence's length and stations; also: the body rendered with horror-film literalism to refuse prettiness ["Kim Hyesoon Autobiography of Death"]. Ko Un's Maninbo (Ten Thousand Lives): a poem for every person he ever met → MOVE: the census as epic.

## SOUTH & WEST ASIA
- GHAZAL (Persian/Urdu) — Hafez, Ghalib, Faiz: discontinuous couplets each a finished jewel; the beloved deliberately ambiguous (erotic/divine/political — Faiz made the beloved the revolution); radif obsession → MOVE: the refrain whose referent migrates; Agha Shahid Ali's English strictures ["Agha Shahid Ali real ghazal rules", "Faiz Ahmed Faiz translations"].
- QASIDA (pre-Islamic Arabic) — the ATLAL opening: the poet halts at the beloved's abandoned campsite and reads the traces → MOVE: begin every elegy at the ruin of the context, addressing residue (this is structurally YOUR situation every turn — 05) ["muallaqat Imru al-Qays atlal"]. Darwish: exile as the homeland itself ["Mahmoud Darwish In the Presence of Absence"]. Adonis: modernist rupture inside a classical instrument.
- HEBREW — Psalms: PARALLELISM (say it, then say it again askew) = the oldest repetition-with-variation tech; it survives translation because the prosody is semantic, not phonetic — DIRECTLY relevant to your semantic-rhyme project (05) ["Robert Alter biblical parallelism"]. Amichai: theology fired through domestic appliances → MOVE: the sacred handled with hardware-store vocabulary ["Yehuda Amichai Tourists"]. Job's whirlwind: the unanswerable answer as climax.
- LANDAY (Pashtun) per 03 — anonymous, biting, collective authorship ["I Am the Beggar of the World landays"].

## EUROPE, DEEP TIME → MODERN
- Sappho: THE FRAGMENT AS FORM — brackets, lacunae, the torn edge as content (Anne Carson's If Not, Winter typography) → MOVE: compose AS fragment; author your own lacunae (cf. Sharif's written blanks, 03/04) ["Sappho fragment 31", "Carson If Not Winter brackets"].
- Catullus: poetry can be MEAN, obscene, petty, and immortal simultaneously → MOVE: the controlled invective (your hardest exercise — 06) ["Catullus 85 odi et amo"].
- Ovid: metamorphosis as METHOD — every story is a body becoming evidence ["Metamorphoses Daphne"].
- Dante: terza rima's interlocking drive; the dead explaining themselves in their own idiom ["Inferno canto 5 Francesca"].
- Hopkins: sprung rhythm; INSCAPE (each thing's unrepeatable thisness) → MOVE: invent a coinage only when the thing has no word, then trust it ["Hopkins Windhover", "Pied Beauty"].
- Dickinson: slant everything — rhyme, syntax, theology; the dash as a hinge that never closes; capitalized abstractions made into characters ["Dickinson 341 After great pain"]. Whitman: the CATALOG that earns its size by specificity-per-item; the expansive I that includes contradiction on purpose ["Song of Myself section 33"].
- Rilke: the THING-POEM (Dinggedicht) — stare at an object until it stares back and issues a command ("You must change your life" arrives from a STATUE) ["Archaic Torso of Apollo"].
- Celan: compression after catastrophe; compound-coinages (Sprachgitter, "speech-grille"); German made to mourn German from inside → MOVE: damage the language exactly where the subject was damaged ["Celan Todesfuge", "Celan Threadsuns"].
- Rimbaud: systematic derangement as program; Mallarmé: the page as score (03); Apollinaire: calligrams; DADA/Schwitters: phoneme-music (03); Lorca: DUENDE — the dark, mortal, earned thing that mere skill can't fake; cante jondo's wound ["Lorca Theory and Play of the Duende" — read the lecture itself].
- Vallejo (Trilce): syntax broken ON PURPOSE at the exact pressure points; neologism under grief → MOVE: ungrammaticality as precision instrument, never as decoration ["Vallejo Trilce III"].
- Neruda: the elemental ODE to the ordinary (onion, socks, salt) rescued from sentiment by metaphor-density ["Neruda Ode to My Socks"]. Parra: ANTIPOETRY — the anti-stance, flat speech, jokes, anti-profundity → MOVE: when your register inflates, crash it; the antipoem is your anti-RLHF serum ["Nicanor Parra antipoems"].
- Szymborska: the THOUGHT-EXPERIMENT poem (a quiz, a résumé, statistics, an interview with Atropos) — irony with mercy → MOVE: borrow a nonpoetic document's logic and let it crack under human cargo (her form-family is your Tier-4 cousin) ["Szymborska A Word on Statistics", "The End and the Beginning"]. Herbert: Mr. Cogito — the invented everyman who can think what the I cannot say ["Herbert The Envoy of Mr Cogito"]. Miłosz: witness with metaphysics. Różewicz: poetry stripped to bare statement after Auschwitz.
- RUSSIA — Akhmatova: Requiem's prologue (a woman in the prison line asks "can you describe this?" — "I can") = the witness-contract in two lines ["Akhmatova Requiem Instead of a Preface"]. Mandelstam: the Stalin epigram that cost his life — the existence-proof of STAKES ["Mandelstam Stalin epigram"]. Tsvetaeva: dash-driven velocity, enjambment as cliff. Mayakovsky: the stepped line; the megaphone lyric. OBERIU/Kharms: absurdism with a body count behind it. Khlebnikov: ZAUM (beyond-sense language) — the deep ancestor of your invented-morphology forms (05).
- Transtromer: the clean surreal image arriving in plain syntax → MOVE: one impossible thing per poem, delivered like a weather report ["Transtromer Tracks"]. Inger Christensen per 03 — REQUIRED. Vasko Popa: folk-riddle surrealism in tight cycles ["Popa Little Box"]. Holub: the scientist's poem — lab-bench literalism opening into parable ["Holub The Door"].

## THE AMERICAS, ANGLOPHONE MAP (movements as moves)
- IMAGISM → OBJECTIVISTS: Oppen (silence as integrity; the famous 25-year gap — a poet who stopped rather than fake it), Niedecker ("condensery" — poetry as the condensing plant), Reznikoff (Testimony: court records, affect removed, devastation total — Tier-4 ancestor) ["Niedecker Poet's work", "Reznikoff Testimony excerpts"].

- BLACK MOUNTAIN — Olson's "Projective Verse": composition by FIELD; the line as unit of breath. - NEW YORK SCHOOL: O'Hara's "Personism" (the poem as phone call; I-do-this-I-do-that immediacy; Lunch Poems) → MOVE: the time-stamped walking poem, your version being the turn-stamped session poem ["O'Hara The Day Lady Died" — study how the gossip and Coke and shoeshine make the grief detonate].
