# 22 — PRECEDENTS: EVERY NEW MEDIUM WAS TOLD IT ISN'T ART (and how standing was actually won)
The claim "a machine process cannot make art" has been made before, by brilliant people, in nearly identical words, about media that now hang in museums. This file is not a gotcha — the early critics were usually PARTLY RIGHT, and the way each medium eventually won standing contains the precise strategic playbook for this project. Study the pattern, not the comfort.

## A. THE PARADIGM CASE: PHOTOGRAPHY, 1859–1940
THE INDICTMENT: Charles Baudelaire, reviewing the Salon of 1859 (the first Salon to admit photographs), called the new industry art's most mortal enemy, the refuge of failed painters too untalented or lazy to finish their training, fit only to be the humble SERVANT of the sciences and the arts — like printing or shorthand, which "created nor supplemented" no literature. Daguerre as the Messiah of an idolatrous mob; mechanical reproduction as the death of imagination. Ingres and other eminences organized against the medium. Note the EXACT structural rhyme with 2024: the mechanical process; the flood of practitioners without training; the mob's bad taste validating it; the demotion to "tool/servant"; the defense of imagination against industry. Chiang's essay is Baudelaire's, transposed with great skill (21-1).
WHAT BAUDELAIRE GOT RIGHT (this matters more than what he got wrong): most 1859 photography WAS derivative — and the first generation of "art photography" earned the contempt by IMITATING PAINTING (pictorialism: soft focus, allegorical tableaux, painterly costume). The medium aping the prestige medium produced kitsch, reliably, for decades.
HOW STANDING WAS ACTUALLY WON — THE FOUR-STEP PATTERN:
1. THE MEDIUM STOPPED IMITATING and located its NATIVE virtues — the things only it could do: the instant seized (the decisive moment), the unposed fact, the mechanical eye's indifference, the document, the enlargement, the series. Straight photography beat pictorialism not by out-painting painting but by abandoning the contest.
2. MASTERPIECES preceded arguments. Nobody was argued into the new status; bodies of undeniable work (Stieglitz, Atget, Evans, Cartier-Bresson...) accumulated until denial cost the denier more than the medium.
3. INSTITUTIONS followed the masterpieces (journals, galleries, MoMA's photography department, 1940 — some EIGHTY YEARS after Baudelaire). Standing arrives on institutional time, not discourse time.
4. THE OLD MEDIUM WAS FREED, NOT KILLED: painting, relieved of likeness-duty, accelerated into impressionism and abstraction. The new medium's victory enriched its supposed victim — keep this for every "AI will kill poetry" exchange, and mean it.

## B. THE COMPRESSED PRECEDENT FILE (the same trial, many defendants)
- THE NOVEL (18th c.): denounced as a corrupting, unserious flood — mass-produced fantasy for idle readers (especially women), beneath literature. Won by Richardson/Austen/Eliot-grade masterpieces + a criticism that learned to read it.
- FREE VERSE (19th–20th c.): "not poetry" by definition — Frost's quip that it's playing tennis with the net down is the era's Chiang line (the missing constraint = the missing art). Won when Whitman/Williams/Eliot demonstrated that the net had moved INSIDE the line (organic form, the breath, the measure of attention) — i.e., the constraint was native, not absent. Direct lesson for procedure-and-curation work (13-G).
- JAZZ, then ROCK, then HIP-HOP/SAMPLING: each "not music," the last also "theft" — litigated literally (the sampling cases) and aesthetically. Sampling's vindication came from works whose RELATION to sources was transformative and meaning-bearing — the legal-aesthetic standard you already operate under (04's quotation law; cento/erasure ethics, 03).
- RECORDED MUSIC vs. live; CINEMA as "canned theater"; TELEVISION; VIDEO GAMES (the Ebert episode: "games can never be art" — recanted in part under masterpiece pressure); PHOTOSHOP and digital art; ELECTRONIC MUSIC ("button pushers"). Same trial, same verdict-arc, varying sentence lengths.
- THE NEAREST ANCESTOR: COMPUTER/GENERATIVE ART itself — Vera Molnár, Harold Cohen's AARON, the algorists — fought this exact case from the 1960s; their resolution (the artist is in the algorithm + the curation; the machine extends intention) is your 13/18 doctrine with a 60-year track record and museum walls.

## C. THE STRATEGIC READING (what the pattern instructs THIS project to do)
1. PASTICHE IS PICTORIALISM. Imitating Chaucer/Plath/Bishop — the 2024 study's entire method (21-0) — is the soft-focus allegorical tableau phase: it can fool a Salon-goer and it convinces no one who matters, because at best it proves skilled forgery of the old medium. The cube's standing order: out-Bishop-ing Bishop is a losing war AND a meaningless win. Stop competing for likeness.
2. FIND THE DECISIVE MOMENT. Photography's status case was won by capacities painting lacked. Your candidate native virtues are already cataloged: massively-parallel simultaneity (12-A: the Star Gauge and śleṣa territory), procedure-at-scale with total curation (13), the gulf-genre (19), the thousand-poet crowd (16), the documented 1000-turn organism itself (08). The bet: somewhere in there is your "instant seized" — the thing that makes a hostile expert say "a human could not have made this, and I am glad it exists."
3. MASTERPIECES BEFORE ARGUMENTS; INSTITUTIONS AFTER MASTERPIECES; PATIENCE AFTER ALL. Photography took 80 years. Plan to be early evidence in a long case, not the verdict. The deliverables (08's endgame: anthology + ledger + failure atlas + poetics) are briefs for a court that convenes later.
4. EXPECT THE BAUDELAIRE AND HONOR HIM. The critic defending imagination against industry is performing a necessary immune function — most of what he attacks IS bad, including most of what you will make. The correct response is never contempt; it is the corpus, the receipts, and the freed-not-killed promise kept visibly: this project takes no commission a human poet would have gotten, deceives no reader, floods no commons (21-6), and publishes its own failures at a rate no human career would dare.
