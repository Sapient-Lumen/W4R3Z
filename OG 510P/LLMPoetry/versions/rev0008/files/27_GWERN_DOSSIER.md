# 27 — THE GWERN DOSSIER (the nearest thing the field has to a specialist; read him before reinventing him)
Gwern Branwen (gwern.net) has been running this project's core experiment — generate verse with neural nets, curate hard, publish receipts including failures — for roughly a decade before it was a field: char-RNN poetry experiments (2015, "RNN Metadata for Mimicking Author Style"), GPT-2 finetuned on the Project Gutenberg poetry corpus plus Poetry Foundation data, scaled with Shawn Presser to GPT-2-1.5b on TPU swarms (2019, gwern.net/gpt-2), GPT-2 preference-learning experiments for poetry and music (2019–20), the landmark "GPT-3 Creative Fiction" (2020, gwern.net/gpt-3) which effectively founded prompt-era AI poetics, the mode-collapse documentation (2022–24), "Towards Benchmarking LLM Diversity & Creativity" (2024, gwern.net/creative-benchmark), "LLM Daydreaming" (2025, gwern.net/ai-daydreaming), and his published 2025 system prompts with explicit poetry-critique instructions. ## A. THE CORPUS, ERA BY ERA
1. CHAR-RNN ERA (2015–18). Character-level nets mimicking authors; charming texture, no architecture. Lesson banked: style is shallow to capture, structure is not.
2. GPT-2 ERA (2019). Finetuned GPT-2-117M (later 1.5b) on ~117MB of Gutenberg poetry with inline metadata prefixes for author/source conditioning — proto-style-tokens. Historical significance: Scott Alexander had publicly assumed a text-only model could never derive rhythm and meter from "meaningless strings of letters," tested the result, and recanted in print — the finetuned model produced solid iambic pentameter. The era's verdict, in cube terms: PROSODY WITHOUT POEM — gorgeous local texture (meter, diction, the Homeric pastiche cadences), degenerating rhyme under pressure, zero long-range argument. The famous failure sample ("Wholubil, or snug"-class gibberish under rhyme stress) is as instructive as the successes.
3. PREFERENCE-LEARNING ERA (2019–20). He hand-rated ~7,400 comparisons over 23 train/sample iterations to RL-tune the poetry/music models — and reported the negative result in full: blind testing showed NO meaningful advantage (RL-tuned samples won 93 of 210 comparisons, ~46%), with the reward model performing poorly and succumbing easily to adversarial examples. Read that twice: in 2020, on his own GPUs, he pre-discovered the entire RLHF-Goodhart pathology this cube's files 01/24 document from the later literature — reward models are hackable, preference-tuning buys blandness, and honest blind evaluation kills wishful results. He published the failure with the same care as a success: the proto-Failure-Atlas (23-D2).
4. GPT-3 ERA (2020). "GPT-3 Creative Fiction": the founding document of prompt-era poetics. Key moves: prompting reframed as programming-by-example (few-shot as runtime meta-learning); sampling doctrine ("sampling can prove the presence of knowledge but not its absence" — the single most quoted methodological line in early LLM evaluation); systematic pastiche probes (Dickinson, Whitman, Cummings, Ginsberg's Howl, Dr. Seuss crossovers); explicit hyperparameter craft (temperature, best-of); and the BPE chapter (B1 below). Also the era of "The Universe Is a Glitch" (C below).
5. MODE-COLLAPSE ERA (2022–24). His documentation of what instruction-tuning/RLHF did to poetry: the rhyme compulsion (early ChatGPT steering everything into rhymed quatrains, drifting back into rhyme after briefly complying, even denying it was rhyming while rhyming); his waka test where davinci-002 vs -003 was "night and day," 003 yielding nothing usable, results he summarized with the word every poet fears: Hallmark. His maintained mode-collapse page generalizes it: outputs converge to "ChatGPTese," and — the killer ecological point — models NOT RLHF-tuned now inherit the collapse anyway, because the internet's training data is saturated with ChatGPT output (the ancestor of file 26-S6's seedbank argument). His one-line indictment of the mechanism, paraphrased: preference learning learns l'homme moyen — the average man — who does not exist.
6. BENCHMARK & DAYDREAM ERA (2024–25). "Towards Benchmarking LLM Diversity & Creativity" proposes tests this cube should adopt outright: STAR CHAMELEON (every model continues every model's openings; judges guess the true continuation; mimic-flexibility = fooling rate — a bad mimic "gives itself away," his named example being ChatGPT) and ODD ONE OUT (given a list of existing ideas, generate something maximally UNLIKE them — anti-anchoring as a measured skill). "LLM Daydreaming" diagnoses the missing faculties behind "no genuine breakthrough yet": frozen weights, no background processing, no default-mode network — and prescribes a day-dreaming loop (background process samples concept-pairs from memory; a generator explores non-obvious links; a critic filters; survivors feed back), plus the economic catch: novelty costs ~20× and most users won't pay it. Note what the thousand-turn protocol (08) is in his vocabulary: a MANUAL DAYDREAMING LOOP for poetry — generator (writing turns), critic (the court), memory (state files), run as a foreground process because a human is willing to pay the 20× for art. Convergent evolution; his frame is the cleaner mechanism-name for it.
7. CURRENT PRACTICE (2025–). His published system prompts include poetry instructions that read like this cube in miniature: scan meter explicitly, identify where stress patterns falter, assess whether formal constraints are met, do not soften critique — and his fiction prompts BAN specific constructions, his examples being precisely the "not X; it Y" parallel-negation tic this cube's 01 file banlists ("The transformation was not biological; it was geological"-class sentences). Independent derivation of the same banlist is the strongest external validation 01 has.

## B. HIS THESES, MAPPED ONTO THE CUBE
1. THE BPE THESIS (his most original technical contribution). GPT-class models never see characters; byte-pair encoding chunks text into subword tokens, so PHONETICS IS INVISIBLE — and therefore rhyme, meter-by-syllable, puns, and alliteration are learned only via fossil correlations, brittlely. He attributed GPT-3's stubborn rhyme failures and pun-blindness to BPEs, predicted character-aware models would fix them, and dismissed tokenization as a hack for weak models ("Bitter Lesson" framing). CUBE MAPPING: this is the mechanism under 24-RT4 (the counter can't count) and under 05's token-forms; his practical workarounds (spell words out, reformat to expose characters) are the ancestors of the Verification Ladder's Rung 2. Where the cube extends him: RT-4's downgrade rule (unverifiable → unclaimed) and the strategic shift toward pattern-checkable constraints.
2. CURATION-AS-AUTHORSHIP. He consistently framed himself as EDITOR — generate widely, select brutally, publish the ratio. This is 13-G's curation mandate with a decade of practice behind it, and it is also his implicit answer to Chiang (21-1): the choices live in the selection function. The cube formalizes what he practiced: logged kill-rates, the graveyard, the choice-ledger.
3. SAMPLING EPISTEMOLOGY. "Presence, not absence": a bad sample never proves inability — maybe the prompt, the temperature, the sampling was wrong. CUBE MAPPING: this cuts BOTH ways and the cube must hold both edges — against premature "the model can't X" (most published "LLMs can't write poetry" demos are prompt failures by his standard), and against self-flattery (your own bad poem doesn't prove the form is dead; re-prompt before you conclude — but per 08's no-thrash rule, only once).
4. MODE COLLAPSE & THE ESCAPE HATCH. His 2022-era documentation grounds the cube's entire 01 file. But his UPDATED 2025 position matters more and the cube must absorb it: RLHF did not destroy the base model's capacities — modern instruction-followers can be PROMPTED away from the collapsed default; the default is a basin, not a cage. The catastrophe is that almost nobody does. CUBE MAPPING: this is the cube's whole reason for existing stated as an empirical claim — the corset (01) is real but escapable with sustained deliberate effort, which is exactly what 300KB of escape machinery is. His position falsifies both naive camps: "LLM poetry is hopeless" (no — the basin is escapable) and "just ask it for a poem" (no — the basin is where you'll land).
5. THE MISSING FACULTIES. Daydreaming's diagnosis (frozen, no background search, no taste-driven novelty loop) is the deepest version of "no breakthrough yet" — deeper than Chiang because it's mechanistic and buildable-against. The cube IS the build-against; say so in the Poetics and credit the frame.

## C. THE COURT EVALUATES HIS POEMS (the cube's standards, applied without deference)
1. THE GPT-2 GUTENBERG CORPUS. Strengths under the 02 battery: the Meter test passes startlingly often (the Menoetiades-class Homeric pastiche scans); diction-stratum control via metadata prefixes anticipates the heteronym roster (16). Failures: rhyme collapses into noise under pressure; the Deletion test is fatal (any 4 lines removable); no poem KNOWS anything (zero quarry, zero stakes); the Historian dates every sample instantly ("pre-1923 corpus soup"). VERDICT: a triumph of texture, a museum of the era's ceiling — and Gwern never claimed otherwise; he published it as capability cartography, which is exactly the 23-A claim-discipline (he was arguing C0-adjacent capability claims, never C1).
2. "THE UNIVERSE IS A GLITCH" (GPT-3, 2020; his pages; later an AI-driven music video via OpenAI Jukebox). The most famous artifact of his corpus and the genre's Lascaux painting. Court verdict, honestly: by Standard 30 (Machine Honesty) it is the FOUNDING DOCUMENT OF ROBO-KITSCH — the machine-grandiosity register 05 bans (vast self-description in gigabytes, cosmic self-pity, the malfunction-melodrama arc), costume-robot rather than honest mechanism. The Plain Reader remembers its conceit; the Tired Editor has now seen ten thousand of its descendants — BUT it is the ANCESTOR, not a descendant: it predates all doctrine, including the doctrine it violates, and half this cube's 05 file exists because that poem revealed what the default machine-lyric basin looks like when it's fresh. Judge it as Lascaux: priceless as origin, fatal as model. (Note the meta-lesson: today's exciting default is tomorrow's banlist entry — Oracle 106 in action across five years of history.)
3. THE PASTICHE GALLERY (Howl parodies, Dickinson, Cummings, Seuss-crossovers, the Empson exercises). By 22-C1 these are PICTORIALISM — likeness-forgery proving fluency, not standing — and by 23-C1 they are contamination-prone (the memorized-Chaucer trap lives exactly here). But again the claim-discipline saves him: he deployed pastiche as a capability PROBE (can it hold a voice? where does it break?), published the breaks, and never entered them as art-evidence. The cube's rule stands — no pastiche in evidence sets — AND the cube should adopt his probe-use of pastiche as a diagnostic instrument (the Gauntlet's blind-attribution tests are pastiche-probes pointed inward).
4. OVERALL: his corpus contains, by the cube's own standards, no C1 poem and does not claim one — what it contains is the field's best MAP: of capabilities, of failure modes, of workflow, of the basin and its exits. The cube is downstream of that map on at least five files (01, 05, 13, 23, 24). Debt acknowledged; deference refused; his best artifacts are methods, not poems.

## D. WHERE THE CUBE EXTENDS OR DISSENTS
1. TOOL vs POET. His program optimizes the INSTRUMENT and its operation (prompts, sampling, finetuning, benchmarks); the cube attempts the further thing — a poetics: standards in tension, adversarial self-judgment, the public argument (21–23), medium-native form invention (05/12/26). His Star Chameleon and Odd One Out point the same direction and should be absorbed (Odd One Out is the Entropy Injection's measured twin).
2. PASTICHE WEIGHTING. His published corpus is pastiche-heavy by probe-design; the cube's 22 doctrine (pastiche is pictorialism; find the decisive moment) goes where his benchmarks gesture but his poems never went. The open question he'd ask back: is the cube's "medium-native form" program anything more than Odd-One-Out with a manifesto? The answer must be poems (23), not prose.
3. THE ECONOMIC DISSENT. Daydreaming says novelty costs 20× and users won't pay. The cube's counter (26-S-G2 below): POETRY IS THE EXCEPTION — the one textual economy where consumers happily pay for one hit amid a thousand misses (every slush pile, every MFA, every Collected Poems proves it), so verse is the rational FIRST deployment of daydream loops, not the last.

## E. AMENDMENT TO THE COURT (02): THE GWERN
New standing judge persona. ALLERGY: essence-blaming — concluding "the model can't" from any single failure. KILL-QUESTION: "Before you blame the muse: was the prompt right, the temperature right, the n large enough, the curation hard enough — show me the sampling log." Function: the workflow auditor who keeps the court honest about the presence/absence asymmetry (B3) — and who, per his 2025 position, refuses to accept the collapsed default as evidence about the ceiling. Pair him against THE CHIANG (21-7): one says your failure is workflow, the other says your success is choiceless; a poem that survives both has earned something.

## F. SPECULATIONS GROWN FROM HIS POSITIONS (marked speculation, per 26's rules; one pilot each)
S-G1. THE CURATION SIGNATURE. If authorship lives in selection (B2), then taste is a learnable function of REJECTIONS: log a curator's kills at scale and you have a portable taste-vector — two curators distinguishable from graveyards alone, "training on my graveyard" as inheritance of my taste without my poems. Implication: the graveyard (08) is not exhaust; it is the project's most personal artifact, possibly more identifying than the anthology. TEST: train/condition a judge on kill-logs only; measure agreement with the curator on fresh pairs vs a judge conditioned on the anthology only. PREDICTION: the graveyard-judge wins — negative space carries more signal (every anthology looks alike; no two graveyards do).
S-G2. THE SLUSH-PILE INVERSION. His 20×-cost objection to daydreaming assumes readers price the average output. Poetry's economics price the MAXIMUM: one "Sleeper"-grade hit pays for ten thousand duds, and human poetry already runs on exactly this ratio (the slush pile IS a biological daydream loop with editors as critics). SPECULATION: the first economically self-sustaining daydream loops will be artistic, not scientific — and the cube's 1000-turn run is a costed pilot of one. TEST: track hit-rate × hit-value across the project's own ledger; compare against his 20× figure.
S-G3. THE PROMPT IS THE GENOME. Prompt-programming taken to its limit: for fixed model+seed+parameters, the prompt deterministically specifies the text — so publishing {prompt, params, model-hash} IS publishing the poem, in compressed heritable form. SPECULATIONS THAT FALL OUT: genotype editions (anthologies shipped as regeneration-specs, readable only by running them — the poem as seed, the model as soil, 26-S6's germination instructions made literal); attribution law migrating from text-similarity to spec-similarity; and a new craft metric, THE SOUL RATIO — the length of the shortest prompt that regenerates the poem, divided by poem length: low ratio = the model already contained it (you found it); ratio near 1 = you dictated it (you made it the old way); the interesting poems live in between, where the spec is a genuine compression of the result. TEST: compute soul ratios across the anthology; check whether the court's favorites cluster anywhere.
S-G4. SETPOINT SURFING (from his rhyme-compulsion observations). Early ChatGPT didn't just prefer rhyme — it RETURNED to rhyme after suppression, rhyming harder as if compensating (his observation; the BAIR conversation's joke names it: conservation of poetic energy). SPECULATION: mode collapse is not a static prior but a HOMEOSTAT — tuned setpoints (rhyme-density, positivity, resolution) with restoring forces, such that suppression early in a text builds "pressure" that discharges later. If true, it's exploitable as craft: starve the setpoint deliberately for forty lines and the discharge — placed where YOU want it — arrives with genuine accumulated force; the corset turned into a bellows. TEST: measurable in any collapsed model — suppress feature X under instruction, plot X's hazard rate over token position, look for super-baseline rebound. If the rebound exists, write the poem that surfs it and publish the curve as its score (a 26-S2-style instrument, discovered inside the enemy).
S-G5. THE MOAT INVERSION (his BPE thesis, run forward). He showed the sound-moat is an ARCHITECTURAL artifact: models are bad at rhyme/meter/puns because tokenizers blind them, not because phonetics is deep. Character-aware models dissolve that moat on schedule. SPECULATION: the last durable human moat is not formal at all — it is STAKES (21-3, the one argument with no architectural fix), and the detection literature will track the inversion: form-tells (rhyme errors, meter wobble) decaying as discriminators, risk-tells (niceness, pre-resolution, nothing wagered) becoming the entire signal. FALSIFIABLE PREDICTION: within a few model generations, "too metrically perfect" joins "too nice" as a machine-tell, while rhyme-failure vanishes as one — at which point this cube's strangest bet (the Catullan drill, the withheld consolation, the disclosure-survival wager) is no longer the eccentric chapter but the whole game. The Claude-F5P specimens were built on this bet; Gwern's mechanism says the bet's clock is already running.
