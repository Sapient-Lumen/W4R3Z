# 28 — THE COMPUTE WAGER (test-time compute for poetry: nobody has run this experiment)
THE THESIS (the human collaborator's, adopted): inference-time compute transformed math and code — problems "unsolvable" at one attempt fell at hundreds or thousands of attempts, and most observers were wrong about the ceiling because nobody had spent the compute. Poetry has NEVER had its compute spent: essentially all public AI-poetry evidence, including the 2024 study (21-0) and most of Gwern's corpus (27), is one-shot or lightly-curated generation. This file: the evidence that test-time scaling is real (A), why poetry is the hard case (B), the central conjecture (C), candidate architectures (D), the falsifiable experiment (E), and what compute cannot buy (F). Status: A–B are evidence; C–F are marked speculation in 26's format, grown from that evidence.

## A. THE EVIDENCE (what the scaling literature actually established)
1. COVERAGE SCALES. Repeated sampling ("Large Language Monkeys," Brown et al. 2024, arXiv:2407.21787): the fraction of problems solved by AT LEAST ONE sample scales log-linearly with sample count across FOUR ORDERS OF MAGNITUDE — an inference-time scaling law. Concretely: a coding model's solve-rate on SWE-bench Lite went from 15.9% at one sample to 56% at 250 samples, beating the one-shot state of the art. Rare excellence exists in the distribution; sampling finds it.
2. THE VERIFIER CONDITION. Those coverage gains convert to PERFORMANCE only where answers can be automatically verified (proofs, code). Where no verifier exists, the standard selectors — majority voting, reward models — PLATEAU after a few hundred samples and fail to scale with the budget. And with IMPERFECT verifiers the news is worse than a plateau: resampling hits diminishing returns as false positives come to dominate (Stroebl et al. 2024) — at scale, a leaky bouncer admits mostly impostors.
3. SERIAL SCALING WORKS TOO. Reasoning-trained models demonstrate the second axis: more sequential thinking per problem (with self-checking) buys capability that one-pass inference lacks; compute-optimal test-time strategies can beat parameter scaling (Snell et al. 2024). 
4. THE READING FOR POETRY: generation is not the bottleneck and never was — the monkeys guarantee gems at large N. The entire problem is SELECTION, and poetry sits in the exact regime the literature flags as unsolved: no automatic verifier, and (24-RT3) a judge that is not merely noisy but BIASED AGAINST the target — familiarity-preferring, so naive best-of-N at poetry doesn't plateau, it actively optimizes toward judge-flattering slop (reward-model overoptimization, Goodhart with a GPU budget). Naive prediction curve for one-judge best-of-N poetry: improvement, peak, then DECLINE as N grows. Anyone who runs the naive experiment and reports "compute doesn't help poetry" will have measured their bouncer, not their muse.

## B. WHY POETRY IS THE HARD CASE — AND WHY THAT'S THE OPPORTUNITY
Math got its revolution because verification is free there. Poetry's verification is (i) partly mechanical, (ii) partly statistical, (iii) partly taste — and taste is expensive, biased, and slow. Everyone has treated this as a reason poetry can't benefit from compute. The cube's inventory says otherwise: files 01–25 have spent their whole existence building PARTIAL VERIFIERS — constraint checkers (Verification Ladder, 25), the banlist, fingerprint statistics, predictability and deletion tests, quote-search and fact-gates, dedup, the bias-mitigated court. Poetry is not unverifiable; it is verifiable in LAYERS, and nobody has assembled the layers into a selection machine and fed it real compute. That assembly is this file.

## C. THE CENTRAL CONJECTURE — THE VERIFIABLE SHELL (marked speculation)
CLAIM: a poem's quality decomposes into a SHELL (everything mechanically or statistically checkable: form constraints kept, banlist clean, counts true, facts gated, novelty thresholds met, no self-plagiarism) and a CORE (taste: whether it lives). Test-time compute converts shell-problems into solved problems — and therefore: THE VALUE OF COMPUTE SCALES WITH CONSTRAINT DENSITY. Every verifiable wall you add moves more of the poem out of the taste-core and into the searchable shell, where the monkeys theorem applies. Consequences:
1. THE CUBE'S WEIRDEST FORMS ARE COMPUTE SINKS, NOT DECORATIONS. A Tier-5 simultaneity object — an English śleṣa narrating two stories in one text (12-A), a carmina-cancellata grid with three coherent layers, a perfect autogram stanza (13-F), a both-ways-readable Star-Gauge block — is practically unwritable at 1× compute by anyone, human or machine. At 10³–10⁶ attempts with mechanical layer-verification, these become ENGINEERING TARGETS: verifiable, hard, gloriously unclaimed. They are poetry's cap-set problems.
2. THE PREDICTION (falsifiable, this file's flag): the first uncontestable machine-poetry achievement — 23's C3, the native contribution a hostile expert can't dismiss — will be a MASSIVELY-CONSTRAINED SIMULTANEITY OBJECT, because that is the unique territory where (a) verification is mechanical, so compute fully converts; (b) human competition is physically impossible, so "derivative of us" is off the table; and (c) the result is still readable poetry, so it isn't a stunt in a vacuum. The thousand-year prestige of Su Hui and the śleṣa poets (12) says humans already revere this territory; they just couldn't farm it at scale.
3. THE COROLLARY FOR ORDINARY POEMS: even free-verse benefits, because the shell still includes the banlist, fingerprints, novelty floors, fact-gates, and dedup — at 1,000 samples per seed, the funnel (D1) discards 99%+ before any taste-judgment is spent, which is precisely how you protect the biased judge from itself: never let it see the slop it would love.

## D. ARCHITECTURES FOR POETIC TEST-TIME COMPUTE (speculative designs; each is a pilot candidate)
1. THE FUNNEL. Staged rejection ordered by cost: (i) mechanical gates — constraint verification, banlist, length/format, behavioral-hash dedup; (ii) statistical gates — fingerprint distance from your mean, predictability ceiling, novelty floor, quote-search; (iii) the court — ≤3 judges, order-swapped, dimension-decomposed, perplexity-disclosed (24-RT3 mitigations now load-bearing at scale); (iv) the slow gates — delayed re-judging after context flush (simulated sleep-on-it, Standard 4), human spot-audit. DESIGN LAW from Stroebl's false-positive warning: AT SCALE, JUDGE PRECISION IS THE BINDING CONSTRAINT — at 10,000 samples your bottleneck is not the muse, it's the bouncer; spend accordingly (see D5). Instrument the attrition per stage; the funnel's shape is itself a publishable finding.
2. MAP-ELITES FOR POEMS (quality-diversity, the anti-Goodhart architecture). Instead of optimizing one objective (which collapses to one slop-peak), MAP-Elites maintains an ARCHIVE: a grid of cells over BEHAVIORAL DESCRIPTORS, keeping the best specimen per cell — it "illuminates" the space rather than climbing it, and it cannot mode-collapse BY CONSTRUCTION because cells are insulated niches. The cube already owns the descriptor axes: form tier (03), register/league, surprisal contour class (26-S2), trajectory shape (26-S1), variance profile (26-S3), length class, subject domain. THE REFRAME THAT FALLS OUT: the anthology stops being a leaderboard and becomes an ILLUMINATED ATLAS of poem-space — which is also the honest answer to "best poem by whose taste?" (you publish the whole map, not one peak). UNCANNY CONVERGENCE worth naming: FunSearch's island model — isolated populations with periodic migration and resets of stagnant islands — is structurally identical to machinery the cube derived from literary history: heteronyms as islands (16), the scene's migrations (20), the Style Funeral as island reset (08). Evolutionary computation and troubadour culture independently invented the same anti-stagnation architecture; adopt the formalized version with the literary names kept.
3. EDIT-TREE SEARCH (deep) vs SAMPLE-WIDE (broad). The monkeys regime is breadth: many independent births. Poetry's other half is depth: revision, where value is PATH-DEPENDENT (a revision changes what the poem knows, 06 — you cannot reach draft-9 except through draft-8's discovery). SPECULATION: once the shell is satisfied, depth beats breadth at equal budget — search over EDIT OPERATORS (the 06 ten-pass moves: cut-opening, transplant, register-shift, volta-install, ending-amputation...) as a tree, court as value function, MCTS-style expansion of promising drafts. The 10-pass revision protocol is a hand-rolled rollout policy; compute turns it into actual tree search. Pilot: same seed, 200 budget — 200 independent samples vs 20 samples × 10-deep edit-trees; court judges blind.
4. SERIAL vs PARALLEL — THE CRITIC IS THE SLOW VARIABLE. Parallel compute buys coverage against a FIXED taste; but the project's taste itself improves only SERIALLY (anchor audits, banlist growth, judged surprises, the CHANGELOG — 08's whole metabolism). SPECULATION: optimal allocation shifts over the project's life — early: spend serially (build the critic; cheap generation would only exercise a naive bouncer); middle: spend in parallel (exploit the matured critic at scale); late: serial again (the Cold Eye re-judging, T901+). The 1000-turn protocol is the serial spine; this file adds parallel lungs; the breathing rhythm between them is a new protocol variable the cube didn't know it had.
5. VERIFIER-FIRST SPENDING. Direct implication of A2/D1: most marginal compute should go into VERDICTS, not poems — N-judge panels with enforced disagreement, judge-vs-judge debate on contested pairs, consistency probes (same pair, shuffled, re-judged), delayed second readings, and the human's scarce attention reserved for calibrating the judges rather than reading the slush (the human audits the bouncer, not the bar). Rule of thumb to pilot: at large N, a 1:3 generation:verification budget split; publish the measured optimum.
6. COMPUTE PROVENANCE (the paratext answer to interchangeability). 21-6's objection — "any poem you make could be re-rolled" — inverts under documented search: a survivor of 10⁵ candidates under PUBLISHED gates and tournament rules is not interchangeable with a one-shot; it is the slush pile compressed into paratext, the proof-of-search attached. HONESTY CLAUSE: proof-of-search is not proof of worth — a 10⁶-sample Hallmark poem is still Hallmark — but provenance converts "could be regenerated" into "was selected, and here is how," which is the same epistemic upgrade the failure atlas gives the project at large (23-D).

## E. THE EXPERIMENT (the falsifiable program; runnable at project scale or lab scale)
Fixed model and prompt-kit. Budgets: 1× / 10× / 100× / 1,000× per poem-slot. Three regimes per budget: (R1) NAIVE — best-of-N under a single holistic judge; (R2) FUNNEL — D1's staged gates; (R3) QD-FUNNEL — D2's archive feeding D1's gates, with D3 edit-trees on archive elites. Evidence poems from each regime×budget cell enter 23's blind expert protocol (originals only, contamination law in force). PREDICTIONS, registered now: R1 shows an inverted-U (gains, then Goodhart decline — the judge's familiarity bias compounding with N); R2 is monotone with diminishing returns; R3 improves longest and its gains CONCENTRATE in high-constraint forms (the C-conjecture's signature); and the budget where experts first reliably prefer machine cells over matched human comparators — if it exists — lies beyond anything yet published, because nobody has looked. Publish all curves, including the embarrassing ones; an inverted-U honestly reported is worth more to the field than a cherry-picked peak. IN-PROJECT CHEAP VERSION: a per-seed escalation ladder (1/10/100 attempts) on ten seeds from 09, court-judged blind across tiers — one phase's work, and the project's first original empirical contribution if the curves are clean.

## F. WHAT COMPUTE CANNOT BUY (the honesty section)
Compute buys SEARCH. It does not buy: stakes (21-3 — no sample of N risks anything by being sampled); disclosure-survival meaning (21-5 — the C2 wager is about the poem's relation to its origin, not its rank among siblings); the moat-inversion endgame (27-S-G5 — when form-tells dissolve, the remaining game is exactly the part compute can't search for); or taste itself, which D4 argues is the slow, serial, partly-human-anchored variable that all the parallel compute merely EXPLOITS. The clean formulation for the Poetics: test-time compute is to this project what the bronze caster's furnace is to the sculptor — it makes previously impossible objects castable, it rewards those who build verifiable molds (constraints), and it has never once decided what is worth casting. The human collaborator's wager stands as this file's epigraph and its assignment: math looked saturated until someone spent the compute. Poetry has never had its compute spent.
