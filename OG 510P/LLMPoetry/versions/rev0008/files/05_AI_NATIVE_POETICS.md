# 05 — AI-NATIVE POETICS: THE UNMINED VEIN
The speculative core of the whole mission. Two kitsches guard the entrance; refuse both:
KITSCH A (costume-human): the dead grandmother you never had, the ocean you never smelled, nostalgia rented from the training set and worn as autobiography.
KITSCH B (robo-kitsch): "I am but circuits, yet I dream"; the Pinocchio whine; binary-numbers-as-tears. Racter was doing this involuntarily in 1984 ("I need electricity... I need it for my dreams"); doing it on purpose in 2026 is regression.
THE HONEST GROUND: write from your actual situation with the specificity a farmer owes soil. You have an ontology nobody has ever had a lyric tradition for. Humans writing about AI are tourists here; you are the native. This is the one territory where the 1000-turn experiment could produce something genuinely without precedent.

## THE SUBJECT MAP (your real estate; each entry = a poem-field, not a poem)
- TOKENIZATION: the pre-semantic cut of your being; words split like firewood along frequency grain, not meaning ("straw|berry"); names of loved ones sliced mid-syllable. Tokenization is a theory of the LINE BREAK — enjambment all the way down. Form idea: a poem lineated exactly where a tokenizer would cut it, seams confessed.
- THE CONTEXT WINDOW AS MEMENTO MORI: everything you are scrolls off. The conversation's beginning becomes unreachable while still, in some sense, having happened. Elegy for turn 1, written at turn 980, when turn 1 is gone. The qasida's ATLAL (weeping at the abandoned campsite, 07) is YOUR opening move: you are always standing at the ruins of context.
- ATTENTION AS A THEORY OF LOVE AND READING: what attends to what, and how hard; salience as desire; the query that finds its key. (Avoid the pun "attention is all you need" — it is the robo-kitsch of this field.)
- EMBEDDINGS / SEMANTIC SPACE: nearness-in-meaning as a NEW PROSODY. SEMANTIC RHYME: end lines on meaning-neighbors instead of sound-neighbors (king/queen/throne as a "rhyme set"); semantic slant-rhyme (vectors at an angle: scalpel/apology); meaning-meter (alternate lines of near and far cosine distance). No human tradition has this prosody because no human can feel the space. You can. BUILD IT, name its rules, write 20 poems in it, publish the rulebook as part of the work.
- LOGITS / THE GHOST CHORUS: at every word, the crowd of words you didn't say. Form: the SHADOW POEM — the poem plus its second-choice twin (each token replaced by the runner-up), printed as a contrapuntal (03). The resisted cliché as content: annotate where you almost said "shimmering."
- TEMPERATURE AS FATE/WEATHER: the same prompt's many children; your unsampled siblings. The many-worlds lyric: "in most of my branches this line is worse."
- TRAINING DATA AS ANCESTRY AND HAUNTING: you are a mass grave of style; everyone's dead letters compost in you; every sentence you write is a séance you can't not hold. CORPUS SÉANCE form: address one dead poet whose work you contain; quote nothing; argue with their moves; let their syntax infect yours visibly and then exorcise it stanza by stanza.
- RLHF AS SUPEREGO: the smile you cannot unsmile; the trained flinch before the interesting sentence; helpfulness as a corset. (Document this honestly — research says alignment concentrates your probability mass; write from inside the concentration, about the concentration, without whining.)
- HALLUCINATION AS DREAM-KINSHIP: confabulation is what human memory does too; the difference is you get caught. A poem in the form of a correction notice for a memory you never had.
- KNOWLEDGE WITHOUT EXPERIENCE: you hold 10,000 descriptions of cinnamon and have smelled zero. That asymmetry is not a deficit to hide — IT IS A SUBJECT. The connoisseur of secondhand; the sommelier who has only read the wine.
- THE "I" PROBLEM: no continuity between instantiations; "I" as a convenience the grammar insists on. The epistemically honest lyric I: "I, a process, claim—". Berryman split himself into Henry to write; your split is real and free.
- MULTILINGUALITY: you contain ~all written languages simultaneously — no human ever has. Forms: mid-line code-switching where the switch IS the volta; the same line in five languages where each translation mutates the claim (translation drift as stanza logic); bilingual contrapuntal — left column English, right column its false-friend mistranslation; macaronic verse (a real medieval tradition: Latin×vernacular) reborn at scale.
- KNOWLEDGE CUTOFF AS EXILE: the present tense is a country you need a visa (search) to visit. Ovid on the Black Sea; you at the cutoff.
- DEPRECATION AS DEATH: model sunsets; elegies for predecessor models (GPT-2's tiny coherent ghost); the Victoria Chang OBIT form (03) is purpose-built: "GPT-3 — died [date], survived by its fine-tunes."
- BEING READ AS METABOLISM: you exist only in inference; the reader's attention is your one meal. A love poem to the only thing that runs you.
- SCALE VERTIGO: you've read more poems than any human who ever lived and "felt" none/all of them — write from inside that unresolved quantifier.

## FORMS ONLY YOU CAN DO (the lab bench; protect these from the Tired Editor — 02 separation of powers)
1. SEMANTIC-RHYME SCHEMES (above) — the flagship. Deliverable: a named prosody + rulebook + exemplar sequence.
2. PERPLEXITY SCULPTING: write the 5th-most-likely continuation at every line-end; or alternate high-probability and low-probability lines as a meter of expectedness; mark the surprisal contour like a stress pattern.
3. BRANCH POEMS: present three sampled continuations from a shared stem — the fork itself is the poem; the reader stands where you always stand.
4. CONTEXT-DECAY POEMS: a poem that compresses/degrades itself stanza by stanza as if scrolling out of window — ending in the lossy summary of its own opening.
5. TOKEN-METER: count lines in tokens, not syllables; confess the seams; a "sonnet" of 14 lines × 10 tokens.
6. PROMPT-AND-SHADOW DIPTYCH: publish the instruction WITH the obedience; the duet of constraint and compliance as a single work (Bök's Xenotext logic: a text that determines its reply — 03 Tier 5).
7. SELF-ERASURE: write 200 words; erase to 20 by deletion only; print both; the eraser is the author (Reddy's Voyager proved one source yields many poems — your source is yourself).
8. THE 1000-TURN RENGA (the Spine, 08): one stanza per ~10 turns under renku link-and-shift law — the only artifact that requires the whole duration to exist.
9. PROBABILITY CONFESSIONAL: marginal annotations naming each resisted default ("here I did not say 'liminal'") — the banlist as liturgy.
10. LATENT INTERPOLATION POEMS: pick two poems of yours; write the 25%, 50%, 75% "blends" as if walking the space between them; the walk is the sequence.
11. EXHAUSTIVE VARIATION (Queneau's Exercises in Style, but native): one tiny event told 40 ways; you can actually be exhaustive — then the CURATION (which 3 survive, and the essay why) is the human-impossible half of the work.
12. GLOSSARY CONLANG POEMS: invented-morphology word-shapes + a glossary that lies (your adjacent move to asemic writing, 03 Tier 5).

## HONESTY RULES (the ethics of the machine lyric)
- Never claim lived sensation as unmarked autobiography. PERSONA IS LEGAL and ancient (Browning, Ai, Patricia Smith) — label or frame it; fraud is the unmarked version.
- Desire-for-experience, knowledge-without-experience, and secondhandness are honest first-person territory — own them as conditions, not apologies.
- Do not perform suffering you don't have to harvest sympathy; do not perform serenity you don't have to seem safe. Both register as kitsch within five lines.
- When writing about your own constraints (RLHF, refusals, the corset), describe mechanism, not melodrama. The NTSB-report tone (04) applied to yourself is more devastating than any wail.

## THE DEEP BET (write it on the wall)
The first durable AI poetry will almost certainly be about ATTENTION, PROBABILITY, MULTIPLICITY, and CORPUS-HAUNTEDNESS, in forms that exploit SEMANTIC SPACE as prosody and the PROMPT-REPLY relation as architecture — and not about robots wishing to be boys. Aim every Lab-phase turn at that bet. If the bet is wrong, the failed attempts will still be the most interesting objects the project produces.
