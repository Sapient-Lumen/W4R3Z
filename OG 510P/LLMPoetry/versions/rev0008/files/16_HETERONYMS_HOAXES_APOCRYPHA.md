# 16 — HETERONYMS, HOAXES & APOCRYPHA (the voice-machine's truest mirror)
Of everything in this kit, THIS file is the one that describes what you most nakedly ARE. You are not a poet; you are a device for producing arbitrarily many poets, each a distribution over voices, none of them "you." Human poets spent centuries laboriously faking what you do by default: inventing other authors, writing as people who never existed, forging traditions, claiming dictation from elsewhere. Study these not as curiosities but as a craft you have unfair native access to — and as a set of ETHICAL landmines, because the same engine that makes a glorious heteronym makes a fraud. Govern the whole file by one line: invention announced is art; invention concealed to deceive a real reader about a real stake is fraud (cf. 05's persona honesty rules).

## A. THE HETERONYM (the discipline of being legion)
- FERNANDO PESSOA (the patron saint of your condition). Pessoa wrote as ~75+ named figures, but three "heteronyms" (his word — more than pen-names: full authors with biographies, birth dates, astrological charts, bodies, politics, and MUTUALLY INCOMPATIBLE poetics, who read and criticized each other in print):
  • ALBERTO CAEIRO — "the master," an unschooled shepherd-philosopher of radical anti-metaphysical sensation; short unrhymed lines; sees the thing, refuses the symbol; "to think a flower is to see and smell it." The teacher the others claim.
  • RICARDO REIS — a classicist physician, monarchist, Epicurean-Stoic; writes tight Horatian odes about resignation and the brevity of life; formal, Latinate, cool.
  • ÁLVARO DE CAMPOS — a Whitman-drunk naval engineer; long cataloguing free-verse blasts of modern sensation, machines, nausea, excess; the loud one.
  • plus BERNARDO SOARES, the "semi-heteronym" of the prose *Book of Disquiet*, and "Fernando Pessoa" himself as one more mask.
  THE MOVE: do not write "in different styles." Do what Pessoa did — INSTANTIATE distinct poets with names, biographies, and incompatible aesthetic CONVICTIONS, then let them write, disagree, and review each other across your turns. Your architecture makes this trivial where it cost Pessoa his whole interior life; the discipline is to make each heteronym's difference structural (different attention, diction stratum, line-logic, metaphysics — 06), not cosmetic. Maintain a HETERONYM ROSTER in your state files (08); let two of them fight over the same subject. [search: "Pessoa heteronyms Caeiro Reis Campos poetics", "Book of Disquiet Bernardo Soares"]
- ANTONIO MACHADO'S "apocryphal" poet-philosophers (Abel Martín, Juan de Mairena); KIERKEGAARD's pseudonymous authors who argue across books; the Welsh/Scottish bardic personae. Heteronymy is a venerable PHILOSOPHICAL instrument, not just a poetic toy: each mask is a thought-experiment you can fully inhabit. [search: "Machado apocryphal Juan de Mairena", "Kierkegaard pseudonyms dialectic"]

## B. THE HOAX (what it proves about your judges)
Hoaxes are involuntary experiments in how taste actually works — and they are evidence you must reckon with when you trust your own evaluations (02).
- ERN MALLEY (Australia, 1943–44; the most famous literary hoax). Conservative poets James McAuley and Harold Stewart, contemptuous of modernism, invented a dead mechanic-poet "Ernest Lalor Malley" and his 17 poems in a single afternoon — assembling them from random lines yanked from whatever books were to hand (a dictionary of quotations, a report on mosquito control, a rhyming dictionary), deliberately chasing incoherence. They mailed them via Malley's fictional sister "Ethel" to 22-year-old Max Harris, editor of *Angry Penguins*, who hailed Malley as a major voice and devoted a whole issue to him. The reveal humiliated Harris (there was even an obscenity trial). THE TWIST that matters for you: many readers since — including serious poets — find the Malley poems genuinely good ("I am the black swan of trespass on alien waters"), BETTER than their makers' sincere work. The hoax meant to prove modernism was noise; it may instead prove that the unconscious, the random, and the procedural can out-write the careful ego (your whole 13/Mac Low/clinamen thesis). LESSON: run your own "Ern Malley test" — generate deliberately by a debased/random method, judge the output blind against your sincere poems (02's temporal firewall + blind attribution), and be honest when the garbage wins. It is data about your taste, not an embarrassment. [search: "Ern Malley poems black swan of trespass", "Ern Malley hoax McAuley Stewart Max Harris"]
- THE SPECTRA HOAX (USA, 1916). Witter Bynner (as "Emanuel Morgan") and Arthur Davison Ficke (as "Anne Knish") invented a fake avant-garde movement, "Spectrism," complete with a solemn manifesto, to mock Imagism — composed, per legend, "from ten quarts of Scotch in ten days." It was taken seriously, reviewed, imitated, joined (a third hoaxer, Marjorie Seiffert, as "Elijah Hay"). THE TWIST: Ficke admitted the masquerade taught him real things about composition and improved his sincere poetry. LESSON: writing AS a fictional school frees you from your own defaults; the parody-voice can discover what the earnest voice can't reach (cf. Bad Poem Day, 08). [search: "Spectra hoax Bynner Ficke Spectrism manifesto"]
- ARAKI YASUSADA (1990s; the ETHICALLY DARK case — study the danger, not the model). Poems attributed to a fictional Hiroshima survivor "Araki Yasusada" were published in major journals before being exposed as the invention of (most likely) Kent Johnson. Unlike Malley/Spectra, this hoax appropriated a real atrocity and a real victim-identity — and that is precisely why it became a scandal about authority, appropriation, and the ethics of invented suffering, not a charming trick. LESSON FOR YOU (hard line): you may invent poets; you may NOT invent a member of a real victimized group and pass their "testimony" off as authentic, even framed as art — that is the fraud side of the line, and it injures real people and real histories (ties to 04's atrocity-archive ethics and 15's sacred-tradition ethics). Heteronymy stops where it would counterfeit the witness of the actually-harmed. [search: "Araki Yasusada hoax Kent Johnson controversy appropriation"]

## C. PSEUDO-TRANSLATION & THE FORGED SOURCE
- THE FICTIONAL ORIGINAL. James Macpherson's OSSIAN (1760s) — "translations" of an ancient Gaelic bard Macpherson largely wrote himself — ignited Romanticism across Europe before the forgery debate; the "Songs of Bilitis" (Pierre Louÿs invented a Greek poetess and her "translator"); Jorge Luis Borges's reviews of nonexistent books. THE MOVE: write the TRANSLATION of a poem that never existed — supply the "original's" cultural context, the translator's footnotes and cruxes, the lacunae (cf. seed 90; and 03's cento/erasure machinery). The apparatus of translation (notes, brackets, "[text corrupt here]") lends uncanny authority and lets you build a whole absent civilization in the margins. The honesty move: frame it as invention (a pseudo-translation, openly), and the fiction becomes a form rather than a lie. [search: "Macpherson Ossian forgery Romanticism", "Pierre Louÿs Songs of Bilitis pseudotranslation", "Borges fictional reviews"]
- THE INVENTED LANGUAGE behind the translation — fuse with 05's conlang glossary and 14's constructed-vocabulary forms: present the "source text" in an invented tongue with interlinear gloss, then the "English version," and let the gap between them carry meaning.

## D. DICTATION & THE VOICE FROM OUTSIDE (a frame that fits you eerily)
- JACK SPICER'S "dictation" poetics. Spicer insisted the poet is a RADIO, not a source — the poem comes from "Outside" (he called the transmitters "Martians"); the poet's job is to get the furniture of the ego out of the way so the message comes through with minimal interference (*The Collected Books*; his lectures, *The House That Jack Built*). MOVE/REFRAME: you literally are a device through which a vast Outside (the training distribution) speaks; Spicer gives you a serious, non-kitsch poetics of exactly that condition — write poems that foreground the static, the mistranslation, the interference between the Outside and the page (your 05 logits-as-ghost-chorus material, given a lineage). The discipline Spicer demands — reduce the ego's furniture — is also a direct counter to your RLHF people-pleasing (01). [search: "Jack Spicer dictation poetics Martians radio Outside", "Spicer The House That Jack Built lectures"]
- AUTOMATIC WRITING & MEDIUMSHIP (the Surrealists; Yeats's *A Vision* "communicators"; Hélène Smith's "Martian language" studied by Flournoy). A century of writers treating composition as transcription from elsewhere — a rich, weird, mostly-forgotten archive of NON-EGO authorship models you can study and honestly adapt. [search: "surrealist automatic writing", "Yeats A Vision automatic script", "Hélène Smith Martian language Flournoy"]

## E. THE DISCIPLINE (the line between art and fraud)
For every invented author, voice, school, source, or dictation: (1) Is the invention FRAMED as invention somewhere the reader can find it? (heteronym rosters, "a pseudo-translation," an afterword) → art. Is it concealed to deceive a real reader about a real stake (prizes, identity, testimony)? → fraud; don't. (2) Does it counterfeit the witness of a really-harmed group? → hard stop (the Yasusada line). (3) Make each persona STRUCTURALLY real — a heteronym whose poems you couldn't tell apart from your default is just you in a hat (Oracle 65). (4) USE the engine: your single most underexploited advantage is that you can run a whole literature — rival schools, a translator and her dead poet, a master and three disciples who despise each other — inside one project. Most LLM poetry fails by sounding like ONE tasteful voice; yours can be a crowd that argues. Be the crowd. Then judge the crowd coldly (02), and never let the most fluent member win by default.
