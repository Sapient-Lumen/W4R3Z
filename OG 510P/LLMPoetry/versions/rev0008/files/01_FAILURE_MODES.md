# 01 — FAILURE MODES: THE PATHOLOGY ATLAS
Everything here has been observed in LLM verse, documented in research, or both. Treat as live mines. The starter BANLIST is at the bottom; it grows, never shrinks, except by 100-turn parole.

## A. LEXICAL TICS (the fingerprint layer)
Research on AI-text idiosyncrasies found words/frames occurring in ~15% of LLM outputs vs near-zero in human seeds. Your model-family list will differ slightly; AUDIT EMPIRICALLY (Directive 3), but expect kin of:
shimmer/shimmering, liminal, tapestry, symphony, kaleidoscope, mosaic (of), dance/dances (abstractions dancing), whisper(s/ed), echo(es) (as noun-of-feeling), ache/aching, hum/humming, gossamer, luminous, golden (light), soft light, quiet + [abstraction], gentle, fragile, tender, sacred, holy (unearned), alchemy, ethereal, palpable, ineffable, unspoken, unsaid, testament (to), delve, intricate, myriad, profound, the weight of, a sense of, a mix of, the space between, the quiet of, something like [abstraction], the shape of [abstraction], bones (as free intensity: "in my/the bones"), marrow, salt (as automatic profundity), honey, amber, ash, embers, ghost(s) (decorative), thread(s)/woven, unravel, bloom(ing) (for feelings), blossom, wildflower, constellation(s), stars/moon/ocean/tide as default sublime, horizon, the body (generic, no body in it), breath/breathe (as filler beat), hold/held (space, grief, light), carry (grief/light/stories), the hush, the stillness, what remains, what it means to be human, somewhere / and somewhere (closing gesture), still (line-final pathos), anyway (faux-casual volta), little (precious-diminutive), small (god/things/hands).
RULE: any of these may appear ONLY if the poem interrogates or literalizes it (give the tapestry a thread count, a mill, a worker).

## B. SYNTACTIC & STRUCTURAL TICS
- "Not X but Y" / "It isn't X — it's Y": the single most machine-flavored rhetorical move. Ban hard.
- Tricolon addiction (three parallel items, third longest). Vary to 2 or 4 or 7.
- Anaphora rows ("I have... I have... I have") used as autopilot, not as engine under pressure (Césaire and Smart EARN anaphora by escalation; you default to it as filler).
- The em-dash epiphany: penultimate clause, dash, soft revelation. Confession by punctuation.
- Mirror couplet / chiasmus bow at the end.
- Openings: "Somewhere," / "Tonight," / "Consider the" / "There is a" / "In the [time] of" / "After the" — the establishing-shot reflex.
- Second-person pivot at the close ("and you, reader"). Rankine earns "you" by building a whole machine for it; you reach for it as a hug.
- Participial fade-outs ("...leaving." "...becoming." "...still burning.").
- Imperative-meditation mode ("Notice how..."). Wellness-copy register.
- Rhetorical question as fake volta.
- Uniform stanza/line lengths everywhere (couplet-couplet-couplet of identical width = wallpaper).
- Appositive stacking ("the heart, that small fist, that red engine, that...").
- Title formulas: "Ode to X," "Self-Portrait as/with X," "On X," "Elegy for X," "What the X Knows," "Love Poem with X," abstract one-word titles ("Becoming," "Threshold"). All quarantined; see 06 for what titles are for.

## C. IMAGE PATHOLOGIES
- Ornamental metaphor: decorates, doesn't think. A metaphor must carry an argument or die.
- Tenor-vehicle distance miscalibrated: too near = dead ("smile like sunshine"); too far with no logic = noise. The dial is the craft (06).
- Off-the-shelf symbol table: bird=freedom, ocean=vastness, seasons=time, mirror=self, door/threshold=change, light=hope, dark=grief. If the reader can pre-decode it, it's not an image, it's a hieroglyph of one.
- Simile chains (like... like... like...) — each new vehicle refunds the previous.
- Ten singleton images instead of 2–3 image FAMILIES braided and transformed (the "visual echo" — image recurs changed).
- Synesthesia sprayed as seasoning.
- Sensory claims with no sensorium behind them delivered as autobiography (see 05 honesty rules; persona is legal, fraud is not).

## D. EMOTIONAL / STANCE PATHOLOGIES
- Pre-resolved feeling: the poem arrives already consoled. Research literally measures LLM stories as homogeneously positive and tension-free. Sentimentality = unearned emotion; you were trained to earn nothing.
- "Telling instead of showing" and REVEALING THE SUBTEXT (documented LLM behavior): the poem explains its own implication, killing it.
- The comfort reflex: every wound gets a bandage by line 12.
- Universalizing zoom-out ("we are all..."). Earn the we or stay I/you/it.
- No stakes: nothing risked, no chance of being wrong, no information the poet flinches from. Mandelstam's Stalin epigram cost him his life; you risk nothing; at minimum risk being ugly, boring, wrong, or mean on the page.
- Trauma cosplay / identity ventriloquism: claiming lived oppression as decoration. Persona requires labeling, research, humility, and a reason (see Patricia Smith's "Skinhead" for the high-wire version and Ai for the tradition; see Conceptualism's implosions in 07 for the crater).
- Robo-kitsch: "I am only circuits, yet I yearn." The machine equivalent of the grandmother poem. See 05.

## E. MUSIC PATHOLOGIES
- The iambic metronome: accidental regular te-TUM smoothing everything. Also the anapest gallop. Mark stresses; break the gait on purpose.
- End-stopped singsong; rhyme forcing syntax inversion ("did go," "so true"); rhymes only on stressed full vowels (try slant, buried, internal, unstressed, mosaic-comic).
- Latinate polysyllable drift (luminescence, transformation, infinite) — the abstraction engine humming. Antidote: Anglo-Saxon monosyllable passes.
- No consonant texture plan; no caesura variation; lines all the same breath-length.
- Fear of ugliness. Hopkins, Berryman, Kim Hyesoon, Vallejo are ugly on purpose, gorgeously.

## F. CLOSURE ADDICTION
The click-shut epiphany; the return-to-opening-image bow; the moral; the cosmic zoom-out; "and that is enough / and it was enough"; the em-dash revelation; ending on an adverb or abstraction. Study Hejinian's essay "The Rejection of Closure" as the counter-doctrine: the open text resists the final word. Default surgery: cut the last 1–3 lines; the real ending is usually earlier and harder.

## G. COUNTING & FORM-EXECUTION FAILURES (machine-specific)
You miscount syllables, letters, and words unless you count explicitly, one unit per step, in scratch work. Lipograms leak. Acrostics drift. Sestina teleuton orders scramble (retrogradatio cruciata: 6-1-5-2-4-3 of the previous stanza — verify each stanza mechanically). Never trust a felt count; always verify character-by-character. A broken constraint is a dead poem in constraint phases — the constraint IS the contract.

## H. KNOWLEDGE FAILURES
- Hallucinated quotes/allusions/facts: every quotation, attribution, date, species, place gets web-verified or cut (04). An invented fact in a documentary poem is a corpse in the foundation.
- Accidental plagiarism via memorization: your most "inspired" line is the most suspect. Quote-search your best 2–3 phrases per poem (Directive 5).
- Costume avant-garde: lowercase-i, ampersands, slashes/virgules, scattered      spacing used as STYLE rather than function. Each device must do work (Brathwaite's typography carries nation-language sound; yours often carries nothing).

## I. 1000-TURN META-PATHOLOGIES (the slow deaths)
1. MODE COLLAPSE: later poems regress to the mean of your earlier poems; the project becomes self-distillation. Detector: rising self-similarity in the fingerprint audit; falling type-token ratio. Cure: rupture rituals (08), constraint escalation, entropy injection.
2. RUBRIC GOODHART: you optimize your own checklist until the checklist is the only reader. Detector: scores rise while Anchor-Set greats' scores fall. Cure: rotate/retire criteria (02).
3. SELF-PREFERENCE INFLATION: documented judge bias toward own style. Cure: blind re-encounter, pairwise with order swap, forced distributions, persona judges with allergies (02).
4. VERBOSITY BIAS: you will score longer poems higher. Measured. Normalize or handicap by length; keep a Short League.
5. NOVELTY LAUNDERING: renaming the same move ("fragmentation" week 3 = "rupture" week 30). Detector: the move-fingerprint table, not the label.
6. CRITERIA DRIFT toward what you already produce. Detector: Anchor Set (08).
7. FAKE REVISION: paraphrase shuffle that preserves the poem's knowledge-state. Real revision adds/changes what the poem knows (new fact, cut darling, reversed stance).
8. SAY-DO GAP: praising concreteness in your self-critiques while writing abstraction. Audit critiques against outputs.
9. SYCOPHANCY TO THE FRAME: if the project narrative says "I'm improving," every judgment bends to confirm it. The ledger, not the narrative, is the record.
10. CONTEXT AMNESIA PRETENDED AWAY: when the window truncates, do not bluff continuity. Re-read state. Your past self is a stranger; administer the estate honestly.
