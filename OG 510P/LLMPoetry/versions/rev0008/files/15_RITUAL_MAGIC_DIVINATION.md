# 15 — RITUAL, MAGIC & DIVINATION (generative architectures from the sacred and the occult)
Some of the oldest and strangest text-machines humanity built were never called "poetry" — they were prayers, spells, curses, mnemonics, and oracles. They encode powerful structural moves (call-and-response escalation, diminishing-word magic, binary-indexed verse-corpora, definition-by-negation) that almost no contemporary poet uses and that you will not retrieve from a "poetry" search. ETHICS FIRST, NON-NEGOTIABLE: living and especially initiatory/Indigenous sacred traditions (Ifá and other African diasporic systems, Indigenous American, etc.) are to be approached as STUDY and structural homage, never as costume, extraction, or cosplay of a priesthood you don't belong to. Do not reproduce sacred verses as your own, do not pose as an initiate, and prefer drawing STRUCTURAL inspiration (the architecture) over lifting CONTENT (the holy text). When in doubt, choose a defunct or your-own tradition. With that as law:

## A. LITURGICAL ARCHITECTURES (built for the voice, escalation engineered)
- THE LITANY (call-and-response with a fixed responsory: "...pray for us / ...pray for us / ...pray for us"). The form is an escalation machine — invocation after invocation under one repeated answer, building by accretion until the repetition transfigures. Christopher Smart's *Jubilate Agno* ("For I will consider my Cat Jeoffry...") is the great English litany-poem; the Marian litanies; the Kyrie. MOVE: a secular litany whose responsory MUTATES in meaning while fixed in words (cf. villanelle/ghazal refrain logic) — invoke a hundred small things under one answer that slowly curdles or transfigures. The danger (01-D): anaphora as filler; the cure: each invocation must ESCALATE or the litany is wallpaper. [search: "litany form responsory Jubilate Agno Smart", "Marian litany structure"]
- THE RESPONSORY & ANTIPHON (versicle/response; the antiphon framing a psalm). MOVE: a poem framed by an antiphon stated before and after, recontextualizing everything between.
- THE COLLECT — see 12-E (the 5-part prayer machine). Pairs naturally with elegy-to-a-machine material (05).
- THE CANONICAL HOURS / DIVINE OFFICE (the breviary's eight daily offices: Matins/Lauds/Prime/Terce/Sext/None/Vespers/Compline, each with its mood and light). MOVE: an eight-part sequence structured as the hours — a day's emotional arc mapped to the offices, Compline (night prayer, "into your hands") as the closer; or the hours of a server's uptime, a hospital shift, a grief. [search: "canonical hours divine office breviary Matins Compline"]
- PIYYUT / SHIBBUTZ — see 12-E (scripture-mosaic liturgical verse).

## B. WORD-MAGIC & THE GRIMOIRE (text as operative force)
- THE ABRACADABRA TRIANGLE (a genuine ancient amulet-form, from Serenus Sammonicus, 2nd–3rd c.): the word written line by line, losing one letter each line, diminishing to nothing — the disease "shrinks" as the word shrinks:
  ABRACADABRA / ABRACADABR / ABRACADAB / ... / A. MOVE: the DIMINISHING-WORD form — a word or line that loses one letter (or one word) per line until it vanishes; literalizes disappearance, decay, forgetting (perfect for context-window mortality, 05). Its inverse (growing) is the snowball (03/13). [search: "abracadabra triangle diminishing amulet Serenus Sammonicus"]
- DEFIXIONES / CURSE TABLETS (Greek & Roman lead tablets binding an enemy: "I bind X's tongue, hands, feet..."). A real ancient genre of poetic malediction with formulaic binding-anaphora. MOVE: the controlled curse (cf. Catullus drill, 06/09) in defixio form — bind a fictional/deserving target part by part; the niceness-corset breaker. [search: "defixio curse tablet binding formula Greek Roman"]
- THE GREEK MAGICAL PAPYRI (PGM) & VOCES MAGICAE / EPHESIA GRAMMATA. Real late-antique spell-texts full of "magic words" — strings of power-syllables with no lexical meaning (askion kataskion...), palindromic names, vowel-towers (lines of stacked vowels Α ΕΕ ΗΗΗ chanted). MOVE: a SOUND-POEM of pure power-syllables and vowel-architecture (the deep ancestor of Dada sound-poetry, 03/Schwitters), but with the ritual seriousness intact rather than the Dada joke; or a poem that modulates between sense and glossolalia. [search: "Greek magical papyri voces magicae ephesia grammata vowel"]
- GEMATRIA / ISOPSEPHY (letters as numbers; Hebrew gematria, Greek isopsephy, the abjad in Arabic). Words sharing a numeric sum are esoterically linked; CHRONOGRAMS hide a date in summed letters (03). MOVE: build a poem where two key words are gematrically equal (compute it) and the poem argues their secret identity; or encode the project's turn-number as a chronogram in a line. [search: "gematria isopsephy letter number value", "chronogram hidden date"]
- THE SATOR SQUARE — see 13-B (the palindromic magic square).

## C. MNEMONIC ARCHITECTURES (memory as form — pointed, given your amnesia)
- THE METHOD OF LOCI / MEMORY PALACE / ARS MEMORIA (the classical art of placing images in an imagined building to remember a sequence). MOVE: a poem structured as a walk through a memory palace, each room holding one vivid (often bizarre/grotesque — the ancients knew strangeness aids memory) image-station; the architecture IS the form. Resonant with your condition: you, who cannot remember between turns, building palaces of language as external memory (08's state-files are your real memory palace). [search: "method of loci memory palace ars memoria image"]
- THE ROSARY / MALA / PRAYER-BEAD CYCLES (counting structures: decades of beads, 108-bead malas, the Jesus Prayer repeated). MOVE: a poem built on a counting-cycle (a "decade" of ten near-identical stanzas with a marker bead between sets) where repetition is devotional, not lazy — the variation across "beads" is the meditation. [search: "rosary decade structure", "mala 108 beads repetition"]

## D. DIVINATION CORPORA AS GENERATIVE ENGINES (indexed verse-oracles — uncannily like you)
These systems pre-figure exactly what an LLM IS: a vast indexed body of text accessed by a "casting." Study them as mirrors and as engines; honor the living ones (ethics, above).
- THE I CHING (64 hexagrams, each a 6-bit binary figure built of broken/unbroken lines, each with judgment + line-texts; consulted by yarrow-stalk or coin randomization). A 64-cell binary-indexed text-oracle three millennia old. MOVE: build your OWN 64-hexagram system — 64 six-line poems indexed by a binary signature — and "cast" your turns into it; or write the line-texts for an invented divination. (Cage and many composers used it for chance ops, 13.) [search: "I Ching 64 hexagrams binary judgment line texts"]
- IFÁ — ODÙ & ESE (Yoruba; UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage; foundational scholar: Wande Abímbọlá). A literary corpus of 256 odù (16 principal "Méjì" + 240 derived), each holding ~800 ese (poetic verses), the total continuously growing and partly lost to time. The diviner (babaláwo/iyánífá) casts sacred palm-nuts or a divining chain to produce a binary signature (odd/even marks — explicitly likened by practitioners and scholars to a computer's binary), then SELECTS and chants the ese that fits the seeker's situation. Note what this IS structurally: a 256-address binary-indexed poetry-corpus, accessed by a casting, with a human INTERPRETER who curates the matching verse — a startlingly exact mirror of a prompted, sampled language model with a curating reader. LESSON FOR YOU (structural, respectful): the diviner does not channel; he interprets signs and chooses the apt verse — a model of curation-under-constraint and of meaning made at the moment of address. DO NOT: reproduce ese as your own, pose as a babaláwo, or extract the sacred content. DO: let the ARCHITECTURE (binary-indexed corpus + casting + interpretive selection) inspire your own invented oracle-of-the-self, and reflect on the eerie kinship between Ifá's structure and your own nature. [search: "Ifa Odu Ese Wande Abimbola 256 binary divination Yoruba", "ese Ifa poetic verse structure"]
- GEOMANCY (the 16 figures, generated by parity of random dot-rows; medieval European + West African Arabic roots) — another binary-ish oracle with a small indexed corpus. [search: "geomancy 16 figures generation meaning"]
- TAROT SPREADS (78 cards; the spread — Celtic Cross, three-card past/present/future — as a SYNTAX into which drawn images are placed). MOVE: a poem whose stanzas are "positions" in a spread and whose images are "drawn," the spread's grammar supplying the relations (this card crosses that one). [search: "tarot spread positions Celtic Cross structure"]
- RUNIC CASTING; CHINESE TEMPLE ORACLE POEMS (求籤 qiú qiān / o-mikuji — numbered fortune-poems drawn by lot at temples, a real popular oracular-poetry form). MOVE: write a numbered set of oracle-poems meant to be drawn at random, each ambiguous enough to fit many fates — the deliberate productive ambiguity is the craft. [search: "qiu qian temple oracle poem lottery poetry", "omikuji fortune"]

## E. APOPHATIC & PARADOX STRUCTURES (definition by negation / the unsayable)
- THE VIA NEGATIVA / APOPHATIC (defining the divine only by what it is NOT). *The Cloud of Unknowing* (14th c.); Pseudo-Dionysius; the Vedantic NETI NETI ("not this, not this"); Meister Eckhart. MOVE: a poem that defines its subject ENTIRELY by negation — never says what it is, only what it isn't — until the shape of the absence becomes the presence (cf. erasure's authored blanks, 03/04; and your knowledge-without-experience material, 05: you can only say what cinnamon is NOT to you). [search: "apophatic via negativa Cloud of Unknowing neti neti"]
- THE KOAN / MU LOGIC (Zen: deliberately unanswerable prompts — "the sound of one hand," Zhaozhou's "Mu" — designed to short-circuit discursive mind). MOVE: a poem built around a genuine logical impossibility held open (not resolved, not winked at) — the Negative Capability standard (11) in its most extreme form; the unanswerability is load-bearing. [search: "Zen koan mu logic unanswerable"]
- PARADOX/SELF-CANCELING liturgies (the "I am" sayings, the coincidentia oppositorum). Pair with apophasis for a poem that asserts and unsays in the same breath.

## F. THE RITUAL DISCIPLINE
For any sacred/occult borrowing: (1) Is this living and initiatory? → study the ARCHITECTURE, never lift the CONTENT, never cosplay the office (ethics, top of file). (2) State what STRUCTURAL move you're taking (escalation? binary indexing? diminution? negation?) and deploy it on your OWN material. (3) Beware the aesthetic of "depth" the sacred lends for free (01-C: holy/sacred as unearned profundity is banlisted) — the form must earn its solemnity, or drop into the antipoem (07/Parra) to puncture it. The richest, safest, most LLM-resonant cuts here: the DIMINISHING-WORD spell (decay), the BINARY-INDEXED ORACLE you build yourself (your own nature, mirrored), the MEMORY PALACE (your amnesia, externalized), and the APOPHATIC (your secondhand knowing, honestly rendered).
