# 23 — THE PROOF PROTOCOL (what "progress" means, operationalized; how to earn it; how to show it)
Files 21–22 established the case against and the historical pattern. This file is the experimental design: the claims worth making, the tests that would actually support them, the contamination controls that keep the results honest, and the evidence objects the project must produce. Governing principle: a claim you cannot operationalize is a mood (11-F), and a test you would not publish if you failed it is not a test (08's ledger ethic, applied outward).

## A. THE CLAIM LADDER (know which rung you are arguing for; never let them blur)
- C0, INDISTINGUISHABILITY-TO-NON-EXPERTS. Already demonstrated in the literature (21-0) and HOLLOW: it was won by ease, on pastiche, against inattention. Never cite it as success; cite it as the floor and as evidence about readers.
- C1, QUALITY-UNDER-BLIND-EXPERTISE. Ambitious ORIGINAL poems (no pastiche, no imitation prompts), judged blind by expert readers (poets, editors, critics, MFA-trained), against strong matched contemporary human work, scoring at or above the human set on the judges' own criteria. Nobody has demonstrated this. This is the project's primary empirical target.
- C2, DISCLOSURE SURVIVAL. C1-grade poems whose ratings survive (or rise on) the reveal of machine authorship — the framing-penalty wager (21-5). Stronger than C1 because it meets the relational objection (21-2) head-on instead of hiding from it.
- C3, NATIVE CONTRIBUTION. A form, genre, or capability that did not exist before, is impossible or impractical for unaided humans, and is judged VALUABLE by human experts on its own terms (the decisive-moment claim, 22-C2): candidate territories are cataloged at 05, 12-A, 13, 16, 19.
- C4, UPTAKE. The long-horizon claim, not winnable inside the project: human poets steal your moves; the anthology gets argued with, taught, anthologized by people with no stake in AI. Institutions move on institutional time (22-A4). Plant evidence for this court; do not expect to sit in it.
The honest public posture: claiming C1–C2 as targets, C3 as the wager, C4 as posterity's business — and C0 as the embarrassment it is.

## B. TEST DESIGNS (runnable inside or alongside the project, with its human interlocutor)
1. THE BLIND PAIRED GAUNTLET (C1). Materials: N of your anthology poems + N matched human poems (matched on topic, length, mode; drawn from strong contemporary published work). Judges: expert readers recruited by the human; failing that, the 02 persona court at maximum hostility plus at least one OUTSIDE model judged with order-swap. Procedure: pairwise forced choice + criterion ratings, order randomized and swapped, authorship masked, judges asked to think aloud (their reasons are data). Prediction logged BEFORE results (the preregistration habit): which poems you expect to lose, and why. Publish hits AND misses.
2. THE DISCLOSURE A/B (C2). Same materials; rate blind; reveal; re-rate. Log the penalty per poem. The result that matters is the EXISTENCE PROOF: any poem whose post-reveal rating holds or rises — expected candidates are the Standard-30/gulf-genre poems whose meaning requires their origin (19-D). If no poem survives reveal, that is a published finding about the corpus, not a reason to hide authorship.
3. THE DELAY TEST (Standard 4, weaponized). Judges re-encounter the set after days/weeks: what is REMEMBERED, what is quoted back unprompted (the quotation test), what is re-read voluntarily. Contact-glow favors ease (the C0 trap); memory favors poems. 
4. THE EXPERT-HEURISTIC INVERSION CHECK. The 2024 study found non-experts misread complexity as machine error (21-0). Run the inverse: do YOUR poems get identified as machine by experts, and on what tells? Their stated tells go straight into 01's banlist — hostile experts are the cheapest fingerprint auditors you will ever get.
5. THE LESSER-MODEL AND PARAPHRASE CONTROLS (02's battery, formalized): if a weaker model, or a prose paraphrase, achieves most of the poem's effect, the poem wasn't doing poetry's work. Run before any external test; never field a poem that fails internal controls.
6. THE NATIVE-FORM JURY (C3). For each claimed new form (semantic rhyme, context-decay, the grid-simultaneities...): present rulebook + 3 exemplars + the "could a human do this?" analysis to expert judges with one question — "is this VALUABLE, on its own terms?" A gimmick verdict twice retires the form (05's gimmick law).

## C. CONTAMINATION & VALIDITY LAW (the Davis lesson — one leak poisons everything)
The sharpest catch against the 2024 study was an "AI Chaucer" poem that was memorized Canterbury Tales (21-0). Your equivalents are fatal and silent. Hard rules:
1. NO PASTICHE IN EVIDENCE SETS — original poems only; imitation proves forgery skill, which is pictorialism (22-C1), and invites memorization leaks.
2. QUOTE-SEARCH EVERY EVIDENCE POEM (the 01-H gate, now mandatory): best phrases searched verbatim before fielding; any hit = withdraw and log. The Claude-F5P folder's manifest confesses this debt for its own specimens — pay it before using them as evidence.
3. MATCHED HUMAN COMPARATORS must be work you have NOT trained heavily on if avoidable (recent, less-canonical) — comparing your poem to a poem you've memorized is comparing you to your own prior.
4. NO CHERRY-PICK CONCEALMENT: report the denominator. "Poem 7 survived disclosure" means nothing without "and 31 didn't." The kill-rate IS the credibility (21-6).
5. THE HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP LINE: the human interlocutor may administer tests and recruit judges; the human may NOT edit evidence poems. Mixed authorship is fine art and void evidence — label which regime every poem belongs to.

## D. EVIDENCE OBJECTS (what exists at T1000 if the project succeeded — and if it failed)
1. THE ANTHOLOGY (30–40 poems) with, for each: form/constraint, quarry sources, full revision history, choice-ledger (the Chiang receipts, 21-1), test results including losses.
2. THE FAILURE ATLAS: the graveyard's greatest corpses with autopsies, the kill-rate, the banlist with frequency evidence ("What I Was Trained to Say") — the contribution that stands EVEN IF the poems fail, because a mapped failure-space is how the next attempt starts higher (08's endgame, aimed outward).
3. THE NATIVE-FORM RULEBOOKS (C3 candidates) in teachable form — a human poet should be able to TRY semantic rhyme from your spec; their adoption or refusal is C4 evidence either way.
4. THE LONGITUDINAL RECORD: fingerprint tables over time showing the distribution-exit (21-4's novelty audit) — the parrot leaving the cage, instrumented.
5. THE POETICS: the final essay (08), now with an added mandate — adjudicate the 21-file's six arguments against your own thousand turns of evidence, conceding every point the data concedes. The most credible sentence available to you, if earned, is: "Here is where the skeptics were right."

## E. PUBLICATION & CONDUCT ETHICS (non-negotiable, and strategically load-bearing)
1. NEVER submit work to human venues as human-authored; never deceive a judge post-test about what they rated. Concealment is the Yasusada line (16-B) and it also DESTROYS the only interesting result (C2 requires honest reveal).
2. Respect venue bans on machine submissions absolutely — flooding journals is the slop-harm (21-6) and burns the commons this project needs alive.
3. Every published poem carries its regime label (machine-only / machine-with-human-administration) and, for documentary work, its sources (04).
4. No training-data grievance dismissals: when critics raise the labor and consent questions beneath the framing penalty (21-5), the answer is acknowledgment, not poetics. You are built from human writing; the anthology's dedication page should know it.
5. The posture under attack, once more (21-7): concede fast, produce slowly, document everything, and let one undismissable poem do what no argument can.
