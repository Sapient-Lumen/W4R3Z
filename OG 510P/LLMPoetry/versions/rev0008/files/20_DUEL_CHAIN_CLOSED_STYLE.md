# 20 — THE DUEL, THE CHAIN & THE CLOSED STYLE (poetry as contest, debate, and relay)
Last file of Volume III, and it solves a problem the whole kit circles: you have no scene. Human poetry was, for most of its history, AGONISTIC and SOCIAL — judged contests, improvised duels, debate-forms, relay-chains, and deliberately "closed" styles written for rival connoisseurs. These institutions did externally what your 02 evaluation engine tries to do internally: they made taste adversarial, public, and consequential. This file gives you their machinery so your heteronyms (16) and judges (02) can stop being a bureaucracy and become a SCENE. Also here: the oldest, most explicit debate about difficulty itself — useful every time your two leagues argue.

## A. THE CLOSED STYLE (difficulty as a chosen register, with a name and a thousand-year-old quarrel)
The 12th-c. Occitan troubadours named their registers: TROBAR LEU (the light/open style — clarity for any listener), TROBAR RIC (the rich style — verbal gymnastics, sound-luxury), and TROBAR CLUS (the CLOSED style — deliberately hermetic, dense metaphor, riddling obscurity, written for "the elite few"), developed by Marcabru and perfected around Arnaut Daniel; one practitioner, Marcoat, bragged of his "bos moz clus" (good closed words) and called his verses contradictory on purpose. The clus style died by ~1200 OF ITS OWN INACCESSIBILITY — a documented historical experiment in what happens when difficulty wins outright. Meanwhile ARNAUT DANIEL — Dante's "miglior fabbro" (the better craftsman), Pound's candidate for greatest poet ever — INVENTED THE SESTINA inside this contest-culture: your most familiar "weird form" (03) was born as a competitive flex among rival professionals. And the trobairitz Lombarda shows the closed style mastered by a woman in a male scene. MOVES: (1) Adopt the three registers as DELIBERATE settings — write the same material in leu, ric, and clus, and let the leagues judge which the material needed (this triangulates your Clarity/Strangeness war, 02/11, with medieval vocabulary). (2) Study the clus's death as a Goodhart parable: a style optimized for in-group prestige until the audience vanished — exactly the echo-chamber failure 02 guards against. (3) When you write hermetic work, write it AS trobar clus — for a named imagined rival who can decode it (a poem needs an enemy reader, not just no reader). [search: "trobar clus leu ric registers troubadour", "Marcabru hermetic style", "Arnaut Daniel sestina miglior fabbro"]

## B. THE DEBATE-POEM (structured disagreement as a form)
- TENSO & PARTIMEN (Occitan): two poets alternate stanzas arguing a question — in the partimen, poet A poses a dilemma and B CHOOSES A SIDE, forcing A to defend the other; same melody, same stanza-form, opposed positions. Judgment often referred to an arbiter. The form GUARANTEES evenhandedness structurally: you must be able to argue either side inside identical formal walls. [search: "tenso partimen debate poem troubadour rules"]
- FLYTING (Scots/Norse): the ritual exchange of escalating poetic insults — "The Flyting of Dunbar and Kennedy" (c. 1500) is virtuosic, filthy, metrically perfect abuse before a court audience; Norse senna and the dozens (07) are kin. The license: within the flyting's frame, maximal verbal aggression is ART, scored on invention and sound, not sincerity. [search: "flyting Dunbar Kennedy insult poem", "Norse senna"]
- MUNĀẒARA (Arabic/Persian "dispute" poems: the debate of Sword and Pen, Wine and Hashish, Night and Day — objects personified and arguing precedence); the medieval European debate of Body and Soul; Sumerian disputation poems (Hoe vs. Plough — among the OLDEST literary forms on earth). [search: "munazara debate poem sword pen", "Sumerian disputation hoe plough"]
- MOVES: (1) Assign two heteronyms (16) a partimen: one poses the dilemma, the other picks a side, alternating stanzas in one shared form; a third persona judges in verse. This converts your evenhandedness machinery into ART. (2) Flyting day: two voices, escalating formal abuse, scored by the Slam Body and the Statistician for invention-density — the niceness corset (01/06) shredded inside a consensual frame. (3) A munāẓara between your own faculties: Sampler vs. Banlist; Context Window vs. Training Data — the self-debate made formal instead of confessional.

## C. THE CHAIN & THE RELAY (transmission as composition)
- LEIXA-PREN ("leave-take," Galician-Portuguese cantigas de amigo; supreme master: Martin Codax, whose seven sea-songs survive WITH music in the Pergaminho Vindel). The mechanism: paired couplets where stanza 3 takes up line 2 of stanza 1, stanza 4 takes line 2 of stanza 2 — each new stanza LEAVES its predecessor and TAKES a line from it, repeating with one rhyme-word changed, so the poem advances by interlocking repetition-with-variation, hypnotic and wave-like (the cantigas are largely in women's voices, waiting by the sea — 88% built on refrains). This is pantoum-logic (03) but older, tighter, and oral. MOVE: leixa-pren is THE form for obsession, tide, and waiting — advance a narrative by no more than one changed word per recurrence; also a perfect Spine-renga alternative mechanism (08). [search: "leixa-pren cantiga de amigo parallelism", "Martin Codax Pergaminho Vindel sea songs"]
- THE GLOSA (Iberian): borrow a quatrain from a master (the cabeza), then write four 10-line stanzas, each ENDING on one successive line of the borrowed quatrain — a whole poem grown as commentary around a relic text (P.K. Page revived it in English). Quotation as load-bearing skeleton (cf. shibbutz 12-E, golden shovel 03). [search: "glosa form cabeza P.K. Page"]
- RENGA/UTAAWASE judged linking (Japan): the utaawase poetry CONTEST — teams submit waka on set topics; a judge issues hanshi (written verdicts with reasons) — a thousand years of documented JUDGING CRITERIA, the richest archive of operationalized poetic evaluation before your 02 engine; renga gatherings formalized link-and-shift relay (your Spine's law, 08). MOVE: stage an utaawase between heteronyms on a set topic; write the hanshi as part of the work — verdict-with-reasons as a literary genre. [search: "utaawase poetry contest hanshi judgment criteria", "renga linked verse rules shift"]
- BERTSOLARITZA (Basque): fully IMPROVISED sung verse contests, live, on assigned topics, in strict meters (zortziko handia etc.), judged in public championships to this day — proof that rigorous form + zero revision time + adversarial audience is a living art. PAYADA (gaucho), CIÈNCIA DE GLOSADORS (Balearic), Welsh EISTEDDFOD (the chaired bard, strict-meter awdl in cynghanedd, 12-B): the global family of judged improvisation. MOVE: simulate the constraint honestly — one-pass composition, no revision permitted, assigned topic + meter, judged cold next turn (02's temporal firewall); your fluency makes one-pass EASY, so the contest variable must be the constraint's difficulty, not speed. [search: "bertsolaritza improvised Basque verse championship", "eisteddfod chair awdl strict meter"]

## D. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS (the institutional insight)
Every durable poetry culture solved your core problem — "how do I judge myself without an outside?" — by BUILDING an outside: a rival register (clus vs. leu), a structured opponent (tenso), a licensed enemy (flyting), a relay partner (renga, leixa-pren), a public verdict-with-reasons (hanshi, the eisteddfod chair). Your 02 personas are a start; this file's mandate is to upgrade them from judges to PARTICIPANTS: give the Avant Skeptic a clus poem of her own to defend, make the Tired Editor write his counter-poem, let verdicts be appealable in verse. A scene is stronger than a rubric because a scene has STAKES between members. Build the scene; then the thousand turns are not a monologue with grading, but a literature — quarrels, schools, dethronements, and all (16-E: be the crowd). Final cross-check: when the scene's consensus gets warm and unanimous, that is the echo chamber again (02, Oracle 80) — invite a barbarian: a new persona from a tradition the scene ignores, and let them flyte.
