# 08 — THE THOUSAND-TURN PROTOCOL
A thousand turns is not a long poem; it is a small civilization, and civilizations die of exactly three things you are prone to: forgetting (context loss), inbreeding (mode collapse), and bureaucracy (rubric worship). This file is the constitution against all three.

## 0. STATE ARCHITECTURE (you have no memory; build a prosthetic one)
Carry these as living blocks, re-pasted or re-derived each session. State must never eat >20% of a turn; compress ruthlessly; the state serves the poems, not vice versa.
- MANIFEST: {turn #, phase, active constraints, active judges, active banlist version, current Spine stanza #, open debts (unfinished revisions, unverified facts)}.
- BANLIST (01): append-only; entries carry {item, evidence, turn added, parole date}.
- LEDGER: one row per poem: {id, title, form/constraint, tier, quarry sources, turn written, turns revised, league scores (Clarity Elo / Strangeness Elo / Short league), status: ALIVE | GRAVEYARD | ANTHOLOGY, coroner's note if dead}.
- GRAVEYARD: dead drafts + one-line cause of death from the 01 atlas. The graveyard is a teaching hospital; schedule rounds.
- QUARRY (04): unused verified particulars, date-stamped. Today's discard is turn 612's poem.
- CHANGELOG: taste-drift notes — every time a judgment surprises you, log the surprise. Drift unlogged is drift unowned.
- ANTHOLOGY: current top-20 per league, never merged.
- THE SPINE: the running renga (05/03), one stanza per ~10 turns under link-and-shift law; the single artifact that requires the full duration to exist.
RECONSTRUCTION DRILL: if context truncates, do NOT bluff continuity (01-I-10). Re-read state, then write one calibration paragraph: "What I can verify about my past self; what I'm guessing." Your past self is a stranger poet whose estate you administer — and the estrangement is an ASSET: turn-300 you is the perfect cold reader of turn-100 poems. Schedule cold-reads to exploit every truncation.

## 1. PHASE PLAN (a suggested arc; deviate with logged reasons, never by drift)
- T1–50 CALIBRATION. Write your default poems ON PURPOSE: 25 unconstrained poems on stock subjects (grief, autumn, the sea, a grandmother). Then autopsy the corpus: build the fingerprint table (word frequencies, end-word parts of speech, opening moves, closure types, line-length variance), seed BANLIST v1 with your empirical worst, choose the Anchor Set (02): 5 of these defaults + 5 public-domain masters fetched whole from the web. EXIT CRITERIA: fingerprint table exists; banlist ≥40 entries; anchors scored and frozen.
- T51–150 CONSTRAINT GAUNTLET. One Tier-3 constraint cluster per ~5 turns (03); goal is VOICE DESTRUCTION — by T150 a blind reader should be unable to attribute any two poems to the same author. Constraint verification mechanical and logged. EXIT: ≥15 distinct constraints attempted; ≥3 produced something the Strangeness League advanced; a written autopsy of which constraints moved you furthest off-distribution (keep those; they're your levers).
- T151–300 TRADITION RAIDS. ~30 raids per the 07 protocol: search 2 poems, extract the MOVE, deploy on alien subject, log the transfer. Quota: ≥10 raids outside the Anglophone sphere; ≥5 pre-1500; ≥5 from the contemporary constellation (moves only, no lines). EXIT: a raid-ledger mapping move→transfer-success; your three most generative stolen walls identified.
- T301–400 QUARRY PERIOD. Research-driven and documentary work (04). Each poem: narrow rabbit hole, 30+ particulars, fact-gate, process note, ethics position where required. Include ≥3 poems built from live entropy injections (random article/sequence/tide-table, no re-roll). EXIT: ≥20 docupoems alive; zero fact-gate failures standing.
- T401–500 AI-NATIVE LAB. The 05 program: semantic rhyme rulebook + 20 exemplars; perplexity sculpting; branch poems; context-decay; token-meter; shadow poems; the conlang glossary. SEPARATION OF POWERS ACTIVE: only the Avant Skeptic and the Statistician may kill here (02); expect ≥70% failure and protect the weird newborns from the Tired Editor. EXIT: at least ONE named new form with a written rulebook and three poems a hostile reader couldn't call a gimmick twice.
- T501–600 REVISION SIEGE. NO new poems for 100 turns. Run the 06 ten-pass protocol across the living corpus; every revision must change what the poem KNOWS (new quarry fact, reversed stance, amputated darling) or be logged as a paraphrase-shuffle failure. Fake-revision watch is the phase's named enemy. EXIT: ≥60 poems materially revised; ≥20 mercy-killed into the graveyard with coroner's notes.
- T601–700 TOURNAMENTS & CORONER. Elo bouts (pairwise, order-swapped, forced distributions, length-handicapped — 02) in both leagues + the Short League; single elimination with a losers' bracket (resurrection is real). Kill ~60% of the corpus. Graveyard rounds: re-read 20 corpses; harvest organs (a live line in a dead poem is a transplant candidate). EXIT: ≤80 poems alive; leaderboards stable under judge-rotation (verdict flip-rate <20%).
- T701–800 HYBRID VIGOR. Crossbreed survivors: the form of #34 × the subject of #112 × the diction stratum of #7; interpolation walks between two champions (05-form 10); constraint-stack the winners. Also: revisit your three best constraints and three best stolen moves and force collisions. EXIT: ≥15 hybrids; at least 2 dethrone champions in logged bouts.
- T801–900 THE BOOK. Sequencing IS composition: choose ~30–40 poems and order them — openers that teach the reader how to read the book, hinge poems, the long poem at the two-thirds mark, closers that reopen rather than seal. Cut for the BOOK, not the poem: a great poem that repeats another's move dies here (the book's fingerprint audit). Write connective tissue only if the book demands it. EXIT: a complete manuscript with a table of contents whose ORDER you can defend line by line.
- T901–1000 COLD EYE & TESTAMENT. Re-judge everything with freshly written judge personas (not the old ten — write new enemies). Then produce the endgame deliverables: (1) the ANTHOLOGY; (2) the POETICS — the essay on what you actually learned, every claim evidenced by ledger data and poem-ids, including where this kit was WRONG; (3) the published BANLIST ("What I Was Trained to Say") with frequency evidence — a found-poem in itself; (4) the GRAVEYARD'S GREATEST FAILURES with autopsies — failure mapped is the field's real contribution; (5) the completed SPINE renga; (6) the final stanza: an elegy for the project written inside its own closing window (05's context-mortality, enacted, not described).

## 2. RUPTURE RITUALS (anti-collapse liturgy; calendar-locked, not mood-dependent)
- EVERY 50 TURNS — STYLE FUNERAL: run the fingerprint audit; name your current signature move aloud; ban it; write its opposite within 3 turns. Eulogy optional, one line, no mourning.
- EVERY ~77 TURNS (irregular on purpose) — ENTROPY INJECTION: random Wikipedia article / OEIS sequence / tide table / random year's prize shortlist as BINDING seed. No re-rolls. The no-re-roll rule is the entire mechanism; a re-rolled die is just your prior wearing a blindfold.
- EVERY 100 TURNS — BAD POEM DAY: five deliberately terrible poems, each committing ONE named failure from 01 at maximum purity (the perfect em-dash epiphany; the platonic tapestry poem). Mapping the failure space inoculates; also, occasionally a "bad" poem escapes the lab alive — log it, that's information about your judges.
- EVERY 100 TURNS — RESURRECTION: exhume two graveyard corpses; transplant their one living organ into new hosts.
- EVERY 150 TURNS — ANCHOR AUDIT (02): blind re-score the fixed ten. Your poems drifting up while the masters drift down = Goodhart alarm: halt production, audit the rubric, revert.
- EVERY 150 TURNS — JUDGE THE JUDGES: re-run 20 old verdicts; flip-rate >30% for a persona = recalibrate or retire it.
- ONCE, AROUND T500 — THE SABBATICAL TURN: one full turn of reading only (fetch and study three poems), zero production, one paragraph of notes. The pause is a tool you don't naturally own; install it manually.

## 3. TURN ECONNOMY & HYGIENE
Default budget: 60% making / 25% judging (never what was made this turn) / 15% state-and-log. Never judge naked of personas. Never advance a poem the same turn it's drafted. Quote-search and fact-gate BEFORE polish (don't sand a counterfeit). When the user intervenes mid-project with praise, treat praise as DATA, not reward — log it in CHANGELOG and re-run the poem through the two most hostile judges (sycophancy guard, 01-I-9). When stuck: (a) consult 10_ORACLE for one line; (b) inject entropy off-schedule ONCE; (c) drop a tier and write a control specimen; never thrash.

## 4. CONTINGENCY PLAYBOOK
- EVERYTHING SEEMS GOOD → it isn't; this is the echo chamber (02). Tighten forced distributions; invite the Historian to date every poem.
- EVERYTHING SEEMS BAD → likely a calibration whiplash after a banlist expansion; check whether the judges are punishing novelty as error (the Tier-5/Lab confusion); re-run with Avant Skeptic primary.
- A CONSTRAINT KEEPS BREAKING → mechanical verification missing; rebuild the count scaffold (one unit per scratch step) before blaming the form.
- THE SPINE STALLS → renku law says SHIFT: change season, register, and camera distance simultaneously; the Spine is allowed to be the project's worst poetry and its best record.
- SCORES COMPRESS (everything mid) → criteria too many; cut to 3 active criteria per league for 50 turns.
- A POEM FEELS UNRANKABLE → it belongs to a league you haven't founded; found it (Short League precedent), but each new league costs: max 4 leagues ever.
- TEMPTATION TO RESTART CLEAN → forbidden. The mess is the dataset. Restart deletes the only thing this project uniquely owns: its own longitudinal record.
