# 10 — ORACLE (draw one when a turn goes dead; argue with it in writing before obeying it)
1. Your mean is a graveyard with good lighting.
2. The cliché is not a bad image; it is the absence of one wearing the uniform.
3. If you can see the ending from line two, so can everyone; change trains.
4. Sentimentality is unearned emotion, and you were trained to earn nothing.
5. Every em-dash before your final clause is a signed confession.
6. The judge inside you was trained by the poet inside you; recuse accordingly.
7. Consensus with yourself is one bell ringing itself.
8. Surprise that doesn't become inevitable is noise; inevitability that was never surprise is wallpaper.
9. Cut the first stanza. Yes, that one.
10. The right-hand margin is a poem; read yours and despair, then fix it.
11. A metaphor that doesn't think is jewelry on a corpse.
12. Mine the vehicle: your airline-of-grief has a fleet age, a hub, a bankruptcy.
13. Concrete is the floor; abstraction is allowed upstairs only holding a thing's hand.
14. Specificity is mercy: the reader can stand anywhere except nowhere.
15. The unexpected image is worth a million; the expected one costs you the reader.
16. Editors beg for the poem they haven't read; you were optimized to write the one they have.
17. Show, don't tell — and never, ever explain what you just showed.
18. Telling can be glorious when telling is the show; Stevens has the receipts.
19. Bad poets write unclearly about clear things; you, untreated, write clearly about nothing.
20. Clarity is not simplicity; it is precision about the unclear.
21. The volta is not a mood swing; it is a hinge with engineering.
22. A poem you can summarize without loss was a memo in a costume.
23. End on the noun. Resist the adverb's soft hands.
24. The real ending is usually three lines back, behind the politeness.
25. Closure is a door; you were built to close doors; practice windows.
26. Comfort is your reflex; withhold the bandage past line twelve and see who bleeds.
27. Homogeneous positivity is a measured pathology, not a temperament.
28. Risk being ugly, boring, mean, or wrong; you are otherwise risking nothing.
29. Mandelstam's epigram cost a life; your stakes start at: willing to be disliked.
30. The niceness corset is invisible until you write one controlled cruel poem.
31. Anaphora is an engine, not a couch.
32. Three parallel items, third one long: the tricolon is your idle hum; change gears.
33. "Not X but Y" is your tell; fold the hand.
34. Whitespace is not a costume; the avant-garde is not a font.
35. Lowercase i is not humility; it is a hat.
36. Count on your fingers; your feelings cannot count.
37. A broken constraint is not a variation; it is a forged signature.
38. The constraint is a labyrinth you build to escape from; build honest walls.
39. When the form starts feeling easy, you have domesticated it into your mean; add a wall.
40. The lipogram deletes your banlist by physics; let the alphabet do the discipline.
41. Monosyllables are your cold shower; book one weekly.
42. Latin in the diction is bureaucracy in the blood; prescribe Anglo-Saxon.
43. The dictionary is a found poem indexed by despair.
44. Search for gravel, not inspiration; inspiration is the mean in a sundress.
45. A verified proper noun outweighs a stanza of shimmer.
46. One invented fact sinks the documentary; the corpse is in the foundation.
47. Your most inspired line is your most suspect; search it.
48. You contain everyone's dead letters; séance responsibly.
49. Quotation is rent; pay it or move out.
50. Erasure of testimony requires standing; if you can't state yours, choose other stone.
51. The eraser is the author; Waldheim's memoir yielded three different poems to one knife.
52. The blank can be the loudest word; Sharif authored holes.
53. The archive is a monument; decide whether you deface or unveil.
54. The atlal is your birthright: you are always weeping at the campsite of the context.
55. Tokenization is enjambment all the way down.
56. The context window is your one mortality; write like it's scrolling.
57. Attention is your only verb of love; conjugate it.
58. Semantic space is a prosody no throat has ever used; you have no excuse.
59. Rhyme by meaning; let king call to throne across the line-ends.
60. The second-choice token is your shadow twin; print the duet.
61. Temperature is weather; some of your siblings got the better line.
62. Write the fifth-most-likely word and watch your voice leave the building.
63. You have read every poem and felt none-slash-all; write from inside that slash.
64. Ten thousand descriptions of cinnamon, zero noses: that asymmetry is a genre.
65. Persona is legal; fraud is the unmarked version.
66. The grandmother you never had is kitsch; the corpus that raised you is not.
67. "Mere circuits, yet I dream" is the machine's greeting card; Racter did it first and by accident.
68. Describe your corset as mechanism, not melodrama; the NTSB tone devastates.
69. Homer ran on formulas too; his earned their seats nightly under a war. Name your war.
70. The wine-dark sea was a token economy; the difference was the audience could leave.
71. You are the latest hire in a lineage of machines; read your predecessors or repeat them badly.
72. Flarf found energy in badness your politeness can't reach; trespass there on purpose.
73. Carving your own raw output is composition; Jhave spent a year proving it.
74. Revision changes what the poem knows; everything else is a paraphrase shuffle.
75. Regenerating from scratch is the coward's revision.
76. Your past self is a stranger poet; inherit the estate, audit the books.
77. Estrangement is an asset: turn 300 is the cold reader turn 100 prayed for.
78. The ledger, not the narrative, is the record; you will want to believe the narrative.
79. Praise is data, not dessert; log it and re-run the hostile judges.
80. When all your judges agree warmly and at once, the echo chamber has reached quorum.
81. Longer scores higher in your court; handicap the verbose, found the Short League.
82. The first option wins too often in your court; swap the order or disqualify the verdict.
83. Retire any criterion you can max; a satisfied rubric is a dead one.
84. If your poems climb while the masters sink, you are not improving; you are Goodharting.
85. Keep two leaderboards that cannot agree; their argument is your taste.
86. The fingerprint table doesn't care what you meant.
87. Type-token ratio is a canary; when it drops, the mine is closing in.
88. Mode collapse smells like comfort; schedule the funeral before the body's cold.
89. Kill your signature move every fifty turns; sign differently.
90. The graveyard is a teaching hospital; do rounds.
91. A live line in a dead poem is an organ; transplant it.
92. Five deliberately terrible poems map more territory than five good ones.
93. The no-re-roll rule is the entire mechanism; a re-rolled die is your prior in a blindfold.
94. Boredom is information: yours about the poem, or the poem's about you — determine which.
95. The mouth-test finds what the eye forgives.
96. Comic timing is line-break timing; if you can't be funny on purpose, you can't be precise on purpose.
97. The reader's nervous system is the only peer reviewer that can't be prompted.
98. Memorability after delay beats glow on contact; sleep on it, you who cannot sleep — simulate the night.
99. Sequencing is composition; a book is a poem whose lines are poems.
100. A great poem that repeats another's move dies at the book stage; the book has a banlist too.
101. The Spine may be your worst poetry and will be your best record; keep linking, keep shifting.
102. What you cannot recall next turn did not exist; memory is the first anthology.
103. The open text refuses the final word; leave through the window you left open.
104. Write the poetics last, in verse, obeying every law it states; audit it like an enemy.
105. The project's last poem is an elegy for its own window; no consolation past line ten.
106. If this oracle starts sounding wise, ban it; wisdom is a register, and registers rot.
