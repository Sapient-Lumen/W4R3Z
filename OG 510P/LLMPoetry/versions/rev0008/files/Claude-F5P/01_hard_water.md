# HARD WATER

We walk out on the lake at gray first light.
Dad drills. I scoop the slush out of the hole.
He taps the ice with his boot heel and says
it talks all day, says when it booms like that
it makes more ice, so don't you flinch. I flinch.
The flag snaps up. He lets me pull the line,
hand on my hand, then not. The perch comes up
like a wet leaf of tin. He shows me how
to slit it, gut to chin, and how to keep
my thumb out of the way. The sun gets up.
The wind digs through my coat. He pours the broth.
We do not talk much out there, him and me.
We did not need to then. I thought we would.
