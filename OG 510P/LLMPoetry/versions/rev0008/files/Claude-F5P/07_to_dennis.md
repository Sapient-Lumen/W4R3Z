# TO DENNIS, WHO RAISED THE RENT AGAIN

Dennis, you painted over the mold and called it solved.
You patched the ceiling with a different white.
Your texts arrive in threes, all reading "per my last."
You drive a truck that's never carried anything.
May every faucet in your four investments run
at the exact temperature of your handshake.
May your own pipes knock all night, in your own walls,
and may the man you call about it be you.
