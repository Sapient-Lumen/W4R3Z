# SUBTRACTION

My mother still knows the way back to the lake.
My mother still knows the way back to the.
My mother still knows the way back to.
My mother still knows the way back.
My mother still knows the way.
My mother still knows the.
My mother still knows.
My mother still.
My mother.
My.

---
PROCESS NOTE. The abracadabra triangle's mechanism (15-B) — the diminishing form that ancient amulets used to shrink a sickness — applied at the word level and inverted in purpose: here the diminution doesn't cure, it documents. Each line deletes exactly the final word of the line above, verified mechanically (01-G); nothing is reordered or substituted. The form is fused with deliberate agrammatism (14-E): the grammar is allowed to break where it breaks — "knows the way back to the" is exactly the sentence with its destination gone first — because the syntax dissolving in order IS the condition, enacted rather than described. The found double meanings are the poem's only ornaments and they were not planted so much as exposed by the procedure: "My mother still knows the" (the article held after its noun is lost), "My mother still" (still = yet / motionless), and the last line's stranded possessive, which owns nothing. Ten lines, no adjectives, no feeling named, no statement about memory anywhere — the disease performs itself and stops. The title is arithmetic because the family experiences it as arithmetic.
