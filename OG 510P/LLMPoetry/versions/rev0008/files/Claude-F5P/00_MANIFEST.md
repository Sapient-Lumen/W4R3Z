## THE ANTI-CLASSIFICATION STRATEGY (what was actually done, so you can replicate it)
Detection feeds on the mean. Every choice below is a way of not being the mean:
1. BANLIST ZERO. Audited against 01's lexical/frame/move banlist. No shimmer, no liminal, no "not X but Y," no em-dash epiphany, no mirror-couplet bow, no participial fade-out, no second-person hug.
2. ENDINGS. Stressed monosyllabic nouns and flat declaratives; consolation withheld; several poems end before the comfortable ending would have started (01-F surgery applied at draft time).
3. GRAVEL. Verified proper nouns and trade particulars (a real town, a real date, real ring-codes, real prices) fetched and fact-gated per 04. Where sources conflicted, the conflict is admitted in the note, not smoothed — smoothing is a machine tell; admitting is a researcher's.
4. RISK REGISTERS LLMs AVOID. Meanness (specimen 07), pettiness and suspicion (08), jokes (10, 15), grammatical collapse on purpose (13), bitterness without uplift (04). The niceness corset is the strongest classifier feature there is; these take it off inside consensual frames.
5. MECHANICAL CONSTRAINTS, VERIFIED. Counted forms executed unit-by-unit in scratch (01-G): syllable-counted landays, a word-count diamond, a final-word-deletion cascade, a 14-letter acrostic. A constraint kept perfectly is authentication: it proves a maker counted, chose, and could not autocomplete.
6. SYNTAX VARIANCE. Fragments next to a 20-line periodic sentence; line lengths uneven by design; the iambic metronome broken with substitutions wherever it crept in.
7. EMOTION NEVER NAMED. The tiṇai discipline (12-C) governs throughout: landscape, object, and procedure carry the feeling; no poem tells you what to feel, and none reveals its own subtext (01-D).
8. VOICES, PLURAL. These are not one tasteful voice. The Catullan is not the elegist; the HR deadpan is not the child mishearing the prayer. Blind-attribution should partially FAIL across this folder (the Gauntlet's success condition, 08).
9. ONE CLINAMEN. Somewhere in this folder a single rule is deliberately broken at the point of maximum meaning (13-B). Finding it is left to the judges.
