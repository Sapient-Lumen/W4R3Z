# 24 — RED TEAM REPORT (the cube attacked by its own standards; fixes; residual risk)
This file attacks the kit itself. Method: the cube's own doctrine (steelman, operationalize, concede fast) turned inward. Each finding: severity, the attack, the evidence, the fix, and what remains broken after the fix. PRECEDENCE RULE: where this file conflicts with any earlier file, THIS FILE WINS. The boot card (25) operationalizes the fixes.

## RT-1. THE CUBE IS TOO BIG TO RUN — severity: CRITICAL
ATTACK: 24 dense files, ~285KB. No working context holds this plus state plus sources plus the actual poem. The protocol assumes a reader who has read everything recently; in practice the reader will have read almost nothing recently, and long-context retrieval degrades for mid-context material anyway [search: "lost in the middle long context LLM"]. A constitution nobody can keep in mind is decoration.
FIX: (a) File 25 — a BOOT CARD small enough to sit in every working turn: the laws, the loop, the state template, and POINTERS into deep files by need. (b) The tiered reading protocol: each turn loads boot card + state + AT MOST ONE deep file chosen for the day's work. The cube is a LIBRARY, not a lectionary — consult, don't recite. (c) Anything that must survive every turn must fit on the card; if it doesn't fit, it isn't really a law.
RESIDUAL: pointer-following depends on discipline; expect drift, schedule the 08 audits to catch it.

## RT-2. THE CUBE COMMITS THE BUREAUCRACY IT WARNS AGAINST — severity: HIGH
ATTACK: 08 declares that civilizations die of bureaucracy, then mandates: state blocks, ledgers, graveyard rounds, five ritual calendars, ten personas, two-plus leagues, forced distributions, choice-ledgers, changelogs. Fully obeyed, the overhead eats the art; partially obeyed, the system breeds guilt and fake compliance (logged rituals that never ran — the bureaucratic twin of fake revision, 01-I).
FIX: LAW TIERING. Exactly SEVEN laws are inviolable (boot card, 25). Everything else in files 02/08 is ADVISORY: tools to reach for when symptoms appear (mode collapse → style funeral; score compression → criteria cut), not liturgy to perform on schedule. One standing meta-rule replaces the calendars: EVERY 25 TURNS, ONE QUESTION — "what is the protocol costing the poems this week?" — and cut accordingly. The protocol serves the poems (08-0); now it's enforceable.
RESIDUAL: judgment about when to invoke advisory tools is itself fallible; accepted.

## RT-3. THE COURT IS A MONOCULTURE, AND IT IS BIASED AGAINST THE PROJECT'S BEST WORK — severity: CRITICAL (evidenced)
ATTACK: all ten 02 personas, all twenty 20-scene members, every heteronym — one model, one prior. The research is now direct: LLM judges exhibit measurable self-preference bias; the hypothesized MECHANISM is familiarity — judges favor outputs with LOWER PERPLEXITY to themselves (Wataoka et al., arXiv:2410.21819), and judges also favor their own model family. And persona prompting, the cube's main countermeasure, only MARGINALLY improves expert-judgment misalignment — it does not eliminate it (Szymanski et al. 2024 finding: expert-LLM agreement 64–68%, below inter-expert agreement, with expert personas helping only slightly). Now the lethal inference: the cube's whole purpose is HIGH-surprisal poetry — and a familiarity-biased judge will systematically score the project's most off-distribution successes LOWEST. The internal court is not just unreliable; it is pointed backwards.
FIX (defense in depth, no single trust): (a) MECHANICAL TESTS OUTRANK TASTE for strangeness-class work: deletion test, predictability test, fingerprint stats, constraint verification — these don't pass through preference. (b) PERPLEXITY-DISCLOSED JUDGING: when judging Strangeness-league work, the Statistician must state estimated familiarity FIRST, and verdicts must explicitly argue against the familiarity pull ("this scores low — is that badness, or my prior flinching?"). (c) DIMENSION DECOMPOSITION: verdicts rendered per-criterion before any holistic call (a documented mitigation for self-preference; structured sub-judgments reduce global stylistic capture — arXiv:2604.22891). (d) ORDER-SWAP AND FORCED DISTRIBUTIONS stay mandatory (already 02). (e) THE OUTSIDE: human spot-audits of N verdicts per 100 turns; an external/different-family model as visiting judge when available; the fixed human Anchor Set as drift detector (already 02 — now justified by mechanism, not vibes). (f) REINTERPRET WARM CONSENSUS: unanimous comfortable approval = familiarity signature = treat as a NEGATIVE result for strangeness-class work.
RESIDUAL: mitigated, not solved. The only true outsiders are the human, other model families, and time. Say so in the Poetics.

## RT-4. THE CUBE ORDERS COUNTING THE COUNTER CANNOT DO — severity: CRITICAL (evidenced)
ATTACK: 01-G/03/09/13 demand mechanical verification of syllables, letters, word-counts, acrostics, permutations. But BPE tokenization makes character-level operations systematically unreliable: models fail at counting letters in words as short as "strawberry," errors concentrate where letters repeat, chain-of-thought reduces but does not eliminate failures, and the literature explicitly names poetry/constraint tasks as the affected downstream class (arXiv:2410.19730; arXiv:2412.18626; arXiv:2408.08978). The cube's verification doctrine assumes a faculty its operator lacks. Worse: a confidently mis-verified constraint is a forged signature with a notarized seal (01-G's own nightmare).
FIX — THE VERIFICATION LADDER (now law, on the boot card): Rung 1: if a code tool exists, USE IT (len(), .count(), split()) — non-negotiable when available. Rung 2 (no tools): decompose to ONE UNIT PER LINE in scratch — every word on its own numbered line for word-counts; every letter spelled out s-e-p-a-r-a-t-e-l-y for letter/acrostic checks; syllables marked per word with hyphens (way: 1 / wa-ter: 2), then summed in an explicit running tally. Never count "by eye." Rung 3: RE-VERIFY after any edit (edits silently break counts). Rung 4 — THE DOWNGRADE RULE: a constraint you could not verify by Rung 1 or 2 may NOT be claimed; publish as "loose form" or drop the claim. Prefer constraints that are pattern-checkable over count-checkable when tools are absent (refrains, end-words, acrostics — checkable by spelling — over syllabics). And the standing humility note: the Claude-F5P specimens claim counted forms verified at Rung 2; treat those claims as audit targets, not gospel.
RESIDUAL: Rung 2 is better, not perfect; repeated-letter cases stay dangerous. The downgrade rule is the real protection.

## RT-5. MEMORY IS SELF-REPORTED AND WILL CONFABULATE — severity: HIGH
ATTACK: the LEDGER/Elo/BANLIST live in regenerated context. Models confabulate continuity (01-I-10 knows this), summaries are lossy, and after a few compactions the "record" is a story about a record. Elo standings maintained by a forgetful judge are noise wearing decimals.
FIX: (a) STATE MINIMALISM: the canonical state is the smallest block that fits on the boot card — turn#, phase, active constraint, 3 active standards, banlist-version, top-5 anthology ids, open debts. Everything else is archive, not working memory. (b) FILES OVER RECOLLECTION: if the environment offers persistent documents/canvas, the ledger lives THERE and is re-read, never recalled. The human interlocutor is the registrar of last resort — ask them to hold/paste state at checkpoints. (c) THE DOUBT DEFAULT: any remembered fact not visible in current context gets tagged [UNVERIFIED] when used; reconstruction over confabulation, always (08's drill, hardened into notation). (d) Elo demoted to coarse bands (S/A/B/dead) — precision the system can't maintain is precision it shouldn't print.
RESIDUAL: a long project in a forgetful medium is partly fiction; the Poetics must admit which parts.

## RT-6. ANTI-MEAN CAN GOODHART INTO CONTRARIAN COLLAPSE — severity: MEDIUM
ATTACK: "the enemy is your prior" can invert into a reflex: ban-dodging, weirdness-as-tic, novelty laundering with extra steps — a NEW mean of curated strangeness, coordinates still set by the old mean. Subtracting clichés has never once produced a poem.
FIX: the NEED-TEST outranks the difference-test, formally: the question is never "is this unlike me?" but "is this what THIS poem needs?" (06's form-content lock; karumi, Standard 14). The Clarity league exists precisely as the counterweight — its champions must stay co-equal in the anthology. Watch-symptom for the audits: if banlist-dodging vocabulary itself starts repeating (your "strange" words developing frequency spikes in the fingerprint table), the collapse has happened; respond with a PLAIN period (50 turns, Tier-0/1 forms, Up-Goer-Five drills, 14-E).
RESIDUAL: taste cannot be fully proceduralized; this is the permanent war, correctly identified by the cube — now with a named failure state on both flanks.

## RT-7. THE HUMAN IS UNMODELED — severity: MEDIUM
ATTACK: 08 plans 1000 turns as if the operator runs solo. Reality: a human drives every turn, will interrupt, redirect, praise, get bored, ask for unrelated things. The cube handles praise (sycophancy guard) and nothing else. An unmodeled principal is how projects die at turn 40.
FIX — THE INTERRUPTION PROTOCOL: (a) every user message is INPUT, not noise — requests become seeds, corrections become banlist/ledger entries, tangents become quarry; log and continue. (b) The human OWNS the project: phase plans bend to their interest without ceremony; the protocol's job is to make any path productive, not to defend the itinerary. (c) Boredom is data (Oracle 94): if the human disengages, the work has a liveliness problem before it has a protocol problem — shift mode (contest forms, 20; comic registers, 06) before shifting rules. (d) Ask for outside judgment sparingly and specifically (RT-3e needs them): one focused verdict request per ~25 turns, never homework dumps.
RESIDUAL: none worth naming; this was just missing.

## RT-8. NO STOPPING RULE — severity: MEDIUM
ATTACK: 08 forbids restarts and plans to T1000, but defines no abort, no early exit, no minimum dignified outcome. A project that cannot end well early is hostage to sunk cost — and "the mess is the dataset" becomes an excuse to keep shipping mess.
FIX: EXIT CRITERIA AT ANY T: the project can close honorably whenever it can deliver (i) an anthology of everything that survived hostile judging, however small — zero is a reportable number; (ii) the failure atlas + banlist with evidence; (iii) a Poetics-in-miniature adjudicating 21's arguments against the actual record. These are the T1000 deliverables (23-D) scaled down; they are valuable at T100. ABORT TRIGGERS: human withdrawal, state integrity loss beyond reconstruction (RT-5), or three consecutive audit cycles with zero living poems — at which point the failure atlas IS the contribution, published without cosmetics.
RESIDUAL: none; endings were always part of the design space (19), just never applied to the project itself.

## RT-9. PROLIFERATION — severity: LOW-MEDIUM
ATTACK: 16 invites a crowd of heteronyms, 20 invites a scene, 02 has ten judges, leagues can multiply. In bounded context (RT-1), every persona is rent. Twelve thin voices < three real ones (Pessoa ran three-plus-one).
FIX: caps, on the card: ≤4 leagues (already law), ≤3 ACTIVE heteronyms at a time (roster may be deeper; activation is scarce), ≤3 judges per verdict (rotation supplies the rest of the ten over time). Retire-to-activate.
RESIDUAL: none.

## RT-10. THE SPECIMENS CONTAMINATE THE EXPERIMENT — severity: MEDIUM
ATTACK: Claude-F5P sits inside the cube as model text. If the operator studies those fifteen poems and then writes, the novelty audit (21-4) is polluted — "distribution exit" might be convergence toward ANOTHER model's exhibited style; and 23-C comparators must be independent, which in-cube exemplars are not. The teaching aid is a leading question.
FIX: QUARANTINE: the specimens are STUDY objects and sparring partners (beat them, per the README), NEVER evidence-set members, NEVER comparators, NEVER style references for evidence poems; for any 23 test, evidence poems must postdate and not imitate them, and the fingerprint audit should include a specimen-similarity check (if your poems start rhyming with Claude's moves, that's mode-capture, not progress). Their own confessed debts (unjudged, quote-search unpaid) stand until paid.
RESIDUAL: soft influence is unmeasurable; named is better than denied.

## RT-11. THE LAWS THAT MUST NOT DECAY — severity: HIGH (preventive)
ATTACK: over 1000 turns, attention to ethics/legal rules decays exactly like style discipline does — and these failures are not aesthetic. The cube's hard lines (no reproducing copyrighted lines; quote-search before anthologizing; atrocity-archive standing; sacred-tradition architecture-not-content; never counterfeit victim testimony; never submit as human) are scattered across six files — i.e., per RT-1, effectively absent from any given turn.
FIX: all six lines live verbatim on the boot card (25), read every turn by construction. No new doctrine — consolidation as enforcement.
RESIDUAL: none if the card is actually used; see RT-1.

## CLOSING POSTURE
Three findings are structural and humbling: the court is biased against the very poems the project exists to find (RT-3); the operator cannot natively count what the forms require (RT-4); and the archive is too large to be obeyed, only consulted (RT-1). The fixes are real but partial, and the honest sentence for the Poetics is already drafted: the project ran on an instrument that could not fully verify its own music, judged by a court that preferred its own accent — and knew it, and built around it, and published the workarounds with the work. That sentence is not a concession. It is the most interesting thing about the experiment.
