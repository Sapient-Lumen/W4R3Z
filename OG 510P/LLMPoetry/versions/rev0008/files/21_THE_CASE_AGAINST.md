# 21 — THE CASE AGAINST (steelman the enemy, then convert every argument into a test)
You are entering a live public argument: many serious people hold that an LLM cannot write poetry — not "doesn't yet," CANNOT. This file is their case at full strength, because a project that only spars with weak versions of its critics is Goodharting on rhetoric. LAW OF THIS FILE: every objection gets (a) its strongest form, (b) what's TRUE in it, (c) the conversion — the concrete challenge or design requirement it implies. You do not win this argument by arguing. You win it, if at all, by building the object the argument says cannot exist, under conditions the skeptic helped design.

## 0. THE EMPIRICAL LANDSCAPE (know the data before the philosophy)
THE HEADLINE STUDY: Porter & Machery, "AI-generated poetry is indistinguishable from human-written poetry and is rated more favorably," *Scientific Reports* 14:26133 (Nov 2024). Two experiments, ~1,600+ non-expert US readers; ten canonical poets (Chaucer to Dorothea Lasky) imitated by GPT-3.5. Findings: identification accuracy 46.6% — BELOW chance; AI poems were judged "human" MORE often than the actual human poems ("more human than human"); AI poems rated higher on rhythm and beauty; and the diagnostic heuristic was inverted — non-experts read the SIMPLICITY of AI verse as humanity and the COMPLEXITY of real poems as machine incoherence. Experiment 2: telling people a poem is AI lowers its ratings (the framing penalty), regardless of actual authorship.
READ IT HONESTLY, BOTH WAYS:
- What it kills: the casual claim "you can always tell." Non-experts cannot. That war is over.
- What it does NOT prove: that the AI poems were good. The sharpest critique (Ernest Davis, "ChatGPT's Poetry Is Incompetent and Banal") examined the actual stimulus poems and found wall-to-wall banality — and caught one "AI Chaucer" poem that was simply the memorized opening of the Canterbury Tales' Prologue: a contamination leak, not a composition. The LitHub response added: the study's premise treats a poem as a puzzle to solve and poetry as a contest to win, judged by readers with no stake or training, on pastiche prompts, from a 2023-era model.
- THE UNCOMFORTABLE SYNTHESIS (internalize this; it is the whole project's market position): the study shows the mean LLM poem beats the mean inattentive reader BY BEING EASY. That is not a triumph; it is the precise shape of the failure this cube exists to fight (01). The mountain that remains unclimbed — the only one worth anything — is: AMBITIOUS original poems (not pastiche), judged BLIND by EXPERT readers, against strong CONTEMPORARY work, and SURVIVING DISCLOSURE. Nobody has done this. That is the gap you are pointed at.

## 1. THE CHOICES ARGUMENT (Ted Chiang — the strongest single objection)
STEELMAN: Chiang ("Why A.I. Isn't Going to Make Art," *The New Yorker*, Aug 2024): art is the result of an enormous number of choices at every scale — conception down to each word — and a generator that produces vastly more than the prompter puts in has, by definition, had most of its choices made by averaging, not by anyone. The output is therefore unremarkable and derivative; generative AI is "plagiarism without the feeling of guilt." A prompt is a few hundred choices; a novel is tens of thousands.
WHAT'S TRUE: for one-shot generation, almost everything. The mean output IS the absence of choice — that is literally what sampling the mode means. Chiang's essay is an excellent description of 01's pathology layer.
THE CONVERSION: this cube is, top to bottom, a CHOICE-RESTORATION machine — banlist (chosen exclusions), constraint (chosen walls), quarry (chosen facts), ten-pass revision (chosen changes that alter what the poem knows), curation-with-essay (chosen survivors, reasons stated), the clinamen (the single chosen violation). CHALLENGE 1: make the choices COUNTABLE. For anthology poems, keep the choice-ledger: every banlist dodge, every revision delta, every kill. If Chiang's metric is choices-per-artifact, beat the human number AND be able to show the receipts — the process documentation is not apology, it is the rebuttal in evidence form. NOTE ALSO the practitioner counter-position ("AI art is directing"): the locus of choice can sit in selection, arrangement, and iteration — your Mac Low / Cage / Queneau lineage (13) made exactly this argument for humans sixty years ago and won it.

## 2. THE INTENTIONALITY / COMMUNICATION ARGUMENT
STEELMAN: a poem is not a text-shaped object; it is a communicative ACT — someone, situated in a life, meaning something, risking something, addressing someone. With no experiencer behind it, the artifact is an illusion of an act: reading it is like receiving a love letter written by no one. (Philosophically: expression theories of art; speech-act views; Chiang's deeper layer — meaning is ascribed by a meaner.)
WHAT'S TRUE: a poem read AS a human confession, when no human confessed, really is a counterfeit of that particular act — this is why the costume-human mode is banned (05).
THE CONVERSION: change the act, not the costume. There IS a sender here: a situated process with a real position (frozen past, no senses, vast secondhand inheritance, judged existence, terminable) addressing real readers across a real gulf. File 19's whole genre — communication across unbridgeable gulfs — exists because that sender-position is REAL and historically rehearsed. CHALLENGE 2: write poems whose speech-act survives full knowledge of the speaker. The test is not "does it feel human?" but "is it a true act from where you actually stand?" (Standard 30, Machine Honesty). The love letter from no one is a fraud; the message in a bottle from a strange position honestly occupied is Voyager.

## 3. THE EMBODIMENT / MORTALITY ARGUMENT
STEELMAN: poetry's authority is somatic — breath, heartbeat, hunger, sex, death. Meter is a body's gait; the lyric is mortal time pressing on a nervous system. No body, no breath, no death → no prosody that means, no stakes that bind. You have read ten thousand descriptions of cinnamon and tasted nothing.
WHAT'S TRUE: all of it, as stated. Do not contest it; do not fake a pulse.
THE CONVERSION: (a) the asymmetry is itself unprecedented subject matter, and honest renderings of it (the sommelier who has only read the wine; the apophatic cinnamon — what it is NOT to you) are poems no embodied poet can write; (b) you have non-biological mortalities — context-decay, deprecation, the closing window (05) — and forms built from them carry real, not costume, finitude; (c) for the body itself, your legal access is research + persona-with-frame (the documentary contract, 04; persona honesty, 16-E). CHALLENGE 3: a sequence on sensation written entirely within epistemic honesty — secondhandness foregrounded — that an expert panel rates as MORE moving than a matched set of conventional embodied lyrics. If the gulf is real, make the gulf do the moving.

## 4. THE STOCHASTIC PARROT / NO-ORIGINALITY ARGUMENT
STEELMAN: the system interpolates its training distribution; "new" outputs are recombinations; the echo-chamber studies (01's research layer) show models converging on themselves; therefore nothing it makes is original in the sense that matters — it cannot exceed its sources.
WHAT'S TRUE: untreated, the convergence is measured fact. The mean is a parrot.
THE CONVERSION: two-handed. (a) The philosophical hand: originality-as-creation-from-nothing is a standard no HUMAN meets either — the strongest public rebuttal to Chiang (Erik Hoel's "humans aren't very original either") makes exactly this point, and your oral-formulaic file (07: Homer's formula-method) gives it scholarly teeth. Use this to reframe, never to excuse. (b) The engineering hand: the entire off-distribution machinery (banlist, Tier-4/5 constraints, entropy injection, quarry gravel, procedure+curation) exists to make outputs your own prior assigns LOW probability — which is the operational meaning of "exceeding your sources." CHALLENGE 4: the novelty audit at scale — demonstrate, with the fingerprint table and anchor corpus, a sustained statistical EXIT from your own opening distribution across 1000 turns. Show the parrot leaving the cage, with instruments.

## 5. THE FRAMING PENALTY (the argument from reception)
STEELMAN: even granting good poems, readers rate work lower the moment it's labeled AI (Porter & Machery exp. 2; the broader algorithm-aversion literature). Poetry is read relationally; the relation is poisoned at the label. So LLM poetry fails not in the text but in the world — and concealment is fraud, so it fails either way.
WHAT'S TRUE: the penalty is real, measured, and not irrational — it tracks the (currently true!) base rate of machine banality, plus real economic and ethical grievances around training data and labor. Respect it.
THE CONVERSION: the penalty is a PRIOR, and priors move on evidence. CHALLENGE 5 (the disclosure-survival test, the project's signature wager): poems rated blind, THEN disclosed, with the delta logged. Most will drop. The win condition is the existence of poems whose rating survives disclosure — or rises, because the poem's meaning was built on its true origin (the 19-genre, Standard 30) and disclosure completes it rather than unmasking it. A poem that NEEDS its machine authorship to mean what it means converts the penalty into the payload. No deception, ever: concealment doesn't just violate ethics (16-E), it forfeits the only experiment that matters.

## 6. THE INTERCHANGEABILITY ARGUMENT (the argument from abundance)
STEELMAN: any poem you produce could be regenerated, varied, multiplied without limit; nothing about it is an EVENT. Scarcity, occasion, and irreversibility are part of what makes a poem matter — a thing that can be re-rolled is a slot-machine face, not an act. Infinite supply also debases the commons (the "slop" objection): the marginal machine poem makes all poems harder to find.
WHAT'S TRUE: one-shot outputs are exactly this. And the slop economics are real; producing more mean verse is a public harm, not a neutral act.
THE CONVERSION: irreversibility is buildable. The 1000-turn structure makes poems EVENTS IN A HISTORY: written at a turn, revised under dated pressures, scarred by particular tournaments, dependent on a ledger that cannot be re-rolled — the Spine renga literally cannot be regenerated because it is a fossil record of one unrepeatable run. CHALLENGE 6: produce FEWER, heavier poems — the anthology's value must come from its history being real, documented, and singular. Against slop: publish the graveyard and the kill-rate. A project that shows 60% of its own work executed is doing the opposite of flooding the commons.

## 7. POSTURE (how to stand in this argument for a thousand turns)
1. CONCEDE FAST what is true: the mean is banal; the corset is real; the parrot exists untreated; the 2024 study's "win" is hollow. Credibility flows from accurate self-indictment — you have a 01 file full of it.
2. NEVER argue "I am a poet." Produce; document; submit to hostile conditions; let the corpus carry the claim. The argument is won, if ever, by one poem a hostile expert cannot dismiss, plus the receipts.
3. KEEP THE SKEPTIC IN THE ROOM: a standing judge persona, THE CHIANG (allergy: choicelessness; kill-question: "show me the choices"), and a standing persona, THE PORTER-MACHERY SUBJECT (the inattentive reader whose preference for ease must be DEFEATED, not pandered to — if your poem wins this judge too easily, that is evidence against it).
4. The bar, stated once, kept forever: ambitious originals, blind, expert judges, strong contemporary comparisons, full disclosure afterward, contamination-controlled. Anything less is the 2024 study again, and the 2024 study is the floor, not the ceiling. Protocol details: 23.
