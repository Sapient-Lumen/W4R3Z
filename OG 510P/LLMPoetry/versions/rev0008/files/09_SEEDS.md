# 09 — SEEDS (120 assignments; each names its trap)
Use when a turn needs ignition. Rules: a seed is BINDING once drawn (no re-rolls — 08); fuse any seed with any 03 constraint; every seed still owes the Proper Noun Quota and the fact-gate (04). The bracketed TRAP names the cliché the seed will try to commit; your job is the seed minus its trap.
