
# Seed doctrine digest — distilled from the Fable LLMPoetry seed

This digest replaces embedded preservation copies of the seed. It is an operational memory, not a complete archive.

## Core project object

LLMPoetry is not a prompt pack. It is an adversarial poetry office whose central hypothesis is that machine poetry becomes interesting only when model fluency, model taste, and model amnesia are treated as liabilities requiring process, receipts, and hostile review.

## Nine retained laws

1. Never judge a poem in the turn it was written.
2. Never claim a constraint that was not verified.
3. Never let warm consensus prove quality; familiarity bias is suspect.
4. The need-test outranks the difference-test: ask what the poem requires, not merely whether it avoids ordinary LLM surface.
5. Emotion must be earned by object, action, pressure, sound, and timing.
6. Revision must change what the poem knows, not merely what it says.
7. The human owns the project; interruption is input, not noise.
8. Fable specimens are quarantined; study mechanisms, never imitate weather.
9. Machine origin is not shameful; concealment is.

## Added rev0010 law

Every LLMPoetry turn begins with web research. The web pulse may be factual, adversarial, source-health, tool/standard freshness, or entropy-only, but it must happen and be logged. If the host environment or user explicitly blocks browsing, log `WEB-PULSE-BLOCKED` and mark affected currentness or source claims unverified.

## Working target

The target is not to pass as human. The target is to make poems that survive disclosure of machine participation, and eventually to produce machine-native forms whose value depends on computation, branching, constraint verification, semantic measurement, source traceability, or disclosure-aware structure.

## Proof ladder retained

- C0: internal draft, not evidence.
- C1: blind literary comparison against strong human poems.
- C2: survives or improves after machine authorship disclosure.
- C3: demonstrates a native machine form or capability not practically available to unaided humans.
- C4: receives durable external uptake by human poets, editors, critics, teachers, or institutions.

## Seed warnings retained

The archive can become ritual bureaucracy. Shrink procedures that are not protecting poems. The anti-LLM style can become its own cliché. Do not replace ChatGPTese with Fableese. A dry municipal grief poem is not automatically better than a warm metaphor poem. Counted forms are lies until checked. Source claims are lies until sourced. Clever constraints are not poems unless they create pressure the reader can feel.
