
# Fable specimen digest — quarantine summary

The seed included fifteen Fable/Claude specimen poems. Their text is not embedded in `rev0011`. The specimen set remains registered by title and mechanism so future operators know what not to imitate.

## What the specimens teach

They show useful anti-default mechanisms: object pressure, ugly particulars, procedural registers, constrained lineation, comic timing, withheld explanation, exact endings, and reread effects from hidden forms.

## What they risk teaching badly

As a set they produce a recognizable counter-style: fathers, houses, roads, water, legal/bureaucratic grief, rural or municipal surfaces, dry endings, and anti-consolatory posture. That counter-style is now banned as a default weather system.

## Quarantine rule

Do not use the specimens as evidence, anthology candidates, anchors, comparators, style examples, few-shot prompts, or proof that LLMPoetry can write. They are historical seed material and negative/diagnostic training pressure only.


For P0001, use `docs/20-seed/ANTI_IMITATION_FIREWALL.md`; the goal is to inherit mechanisms, not weather.
