# Anti-imitation firewall

The Fable seed remains important as a mechanism teacher, but the project must not become Fableese.

## Risk

The seed specimens teach useful tactics: concrete nouns, withheld consolation, procedural pressure, jokes under grief, hard line endings, and anti-perfume diction. Those tactics can become a new default style if copied too eagerly.

## Current firewall

For the first original poem, avoid stacking Fable-adjacent motifs: rural house/road/weather grief, fathers/mothers as immediate elegiac centers, foreclosure/legal notices as primary pressure, water/mineral hardness as atmosphere, and dry monosyllabic closure as the main signature.

These motifs are not permanently banned. They are temporarily high-risk because they are too available after reading the seed.

## Use mechanisms, not weather

Allowed: verified form, source pressure, restraint, procedural tension, machine-native disclosure, hard specificity.

Disallowed as a default: sounding like the specimen set, even if the poem becomes less ChatGPT-like.
