# External material pressure form

Introduced as a recommendation in rev0024 and first implemented in `rev0025` with `P0002-D001`.

The form asks the poem to submit to a small external object/source/measurement before adding more internal machinery. At minimum, the packet must declare source IDs, exact fact strings that must survive into the draft, a transformation policy, disclosure text, and a negative diction firewall for known house fog.

Validator: `tools/check_external_material_pressure.py`.

Non-claim: this form checks pressure and traceability only; it does not prove quality.
