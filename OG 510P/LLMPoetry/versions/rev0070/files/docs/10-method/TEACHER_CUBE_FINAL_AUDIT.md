
# Teacher cube final audit

This is the last required teacher-cube pass. Future turns do not need to reopen the four teacher cubes unless the operator has a specific reason.

## DelayBasin

Useful: compact surface bundle, replay capsule, canary protocol, validation toolchain manifest, surface status, and explicit non-claims. Adopted in smaller form as `COMPACT_SURFACE_BUNDLE.json`, `REPLAY_CAPSULE.json`, `CANARY_PROTOCOL.json`, and `VALIDATION_TOOLCHAIN_MANIFEST.json`.

Not adopted: hundreds of project-specific witness ledgers. LLMPoetry is too young for that mass.

## Anonymity

Useful: pruning policy, proof boundary, publication/non-publication separation, archive invariants, transfer-source hashes, and conservative review order. Adopted as pruning policy, archive invariants, and source/quote gates.

Not adopted: TeX/paper publication machinery and large release queues.

## Radical-Governance

Useful: editable metadata spines separated from generated surfaces; matrix registries; source health and review cadence; defeat-test thinking. Adopted as source-health registry and canary/evidence gate framing.

Not adopted: huge generated control surfaces and hundreds of canon notes.

## Micromax

Useful: small command posture, portability concern, bounded/checkpointed test batches, context JSON, and dependency-free tools. Adopted as `make doctor`, small validators, and bounded future test lanes.

Not adopted: full programming-language source tree, pytest suite, editor docs, and implementation bulk.
