## Minimal current test

1. Present the disclosure-before-reading note first.
2. Present the poem before any hostile evaluator rubric.
3. Ask whether disclosure improved, weakened, confounded, or merely explained the poem.
4. Ask for strongest line, weakest line, keep/reject/uncertain, source-packet dependence, documentation-pressure note, and one revision instruction.
5. Record no personal identifiers or demographics.
6. Treat any response as local editorial pressure unless separate ethics/legal review authorizes broader research use.
