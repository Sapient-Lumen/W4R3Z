# High-risk machine-native posture

The human owner selected risk over short-horizon publishability. Treat this as a standing creative preference, not a one-turn whim.

## Operational consequence

P0001 and early pilots should prefer forms where the machine process is structurally necessary. A rough, strange, auditable machine-native object is more useful to the project than a smoother lyric that merely avoids obvious LLM fingerprints.

## Publishability horizon

Publishability is not abandoned; it is deferred. The project assumes that strong public-facing poems may require hundreds or a thousand turns of drafting, killing, measuring, revising, and cold reviewing.

## Guardrail

Risk does not excuse laziness. A high-risk poem still needs receipts for formal claims, source claims, and authorship disclosure. It also remains subject to the same-turn judgment firewall.
