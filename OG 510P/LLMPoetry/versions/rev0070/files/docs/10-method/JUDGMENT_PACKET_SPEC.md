# Judgment packet specification

A judgment packet is what the judge sees. It must exclude halo-producing draft rationale unless the form itself requires process visibility.

## Minimum cold packet

- poem text
- declared form only when needed to evaluate form integrity
- source/claim notes only when factual accuracy is under review
- no same-turn author commentary
- no self-praise

## Pairwise packet

For serious comparisons, create two packets: A/B and B/A. If the verdict flips, the judgment is null until rerun with a better criterion or a human reader.

## Disclosure packet

External-facing tests may begin blind, but the packet must include a planned disclosure step. A poem that only wins by hiding machine participation has failed this project’s central wager.
