# Proof ladder

The project should not claim victory from a pleasing poem.

- **C0 — Draft hygiene**: the poem has a record, constraints are not falsely claimed, and sources are logged.
- **C1 — Blind quality**: expert or serious readers rate the poem well against strong human comparators without knowing authorship.
- **C2 — Disclosure survival**: ratings survive, or the poem becomes more interesting, after machine authorship and process are disclosed.
- **C3 — Machine-native contribution**: the poem uses a form or affordance difficult or impractical for unaided humans, and the form is legible as poetry rather than gimmick.
- **C4 — Uptake**: human poets, editors, critics, or institutions engage with the work after disclosure.

Rev0009 admits no C-level evidence. It creates the surfaces where future evidence can be stored.
