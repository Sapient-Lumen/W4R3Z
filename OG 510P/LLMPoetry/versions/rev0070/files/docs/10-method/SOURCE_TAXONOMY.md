# Source taxonomy

The cube treats research as both fact-gate and entropy. Those are different jobs.

## Source roles

- **authority**: official standard, law, institutional documentation, primary dataset, or direct source. May carry factual claims if cited and current enough.
- **primary**: firsthand record, source text, interview, archive item, original publication, data release. May carry factual claims within scope.
- **peer_reviewed**: article or scholarly venue. May carry claims, with method/scope limits.
- **source_health**: used to check whether an already-cited source, standard, or tool remains current.
- **adversarial**: critique, dissent, failure report, or negative evidence. Carries counterpressure, not necessarily authority.
- **texture**: contributes diction, objects, formats, surfaces, or atmospheric particulars. Must not silently carry factual claims.
- **entropy**: deliberately chosen to disturb the model prior. Must not carry facts unless separately upgraded to authority/primary/etc.
- **receipt**: quote-search, provenance, DOI, URL, or selector verification.

## Rule

A factual claim must bind to an authority, primary, peer-reviewed, or otherwise explicitly justified source. A texture or entropy source can shape language but cannot become a fact witness by accident.
