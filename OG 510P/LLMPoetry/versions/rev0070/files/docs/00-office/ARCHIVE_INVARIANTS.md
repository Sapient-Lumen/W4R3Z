# Archive invariants

The working cube must remain resumable, lightweight, and honest.

Current invariants include: every completed turn has a research pulse or blocked-pulse log; no preservation copy of prior revisions is embedded; no Fable/Claude specimen poem text is embedded; no poem enters evidence flow without receipts; manifests and checksums match shipped bytes; P0001 preflight contains no draft text in rev0011; standards-inspired metadata surfaces do not claim full compliance without validation.
