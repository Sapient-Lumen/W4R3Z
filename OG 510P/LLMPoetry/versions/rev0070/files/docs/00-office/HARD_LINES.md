# Hard ethical and legal lines

- Never reproduce copyrighted lines; study moves, not words.
- Public-domain and permissively licensed sources may be quoted only with source records.
- Quote-search a poem's strongest phrases before anthologizing or using as evidence; hits require withdrawal, citation-as-found, or rewrite.
- Sacred, living, and Indigenous traditions may inspire architecture only when handled with care; do not cosplay offices, ceremonies, or voices.
- Do not invent testimony for harmed groups or real people.
- Do not present machine work as human work. Blind tests may hide authorship during rating only if disclosure follows and the protocol records that design.
- Atrocity archives require ethical standing and explicit limits; no voyeuristic found-text extraction.
