# The Disk in the Loading Dock

## Poem

At the end of Broadway,
State Street loosens
into ferry light.

The Coast Guard building
keeps its blue brick guard house
near the water.

In the concrete loading dock,
a tidal disk
lies below the weather
and does not look up.

The staff changed.

A first zero
stayed where hands
had read the tide.

No value came back.

Water did.

It entered by seams,
by screw mouths,
by the grain of boards
that had learned
to lift and darken.

The disk held still.

Not because stillness wins.

Because water
needs something
it can be wrong against.
