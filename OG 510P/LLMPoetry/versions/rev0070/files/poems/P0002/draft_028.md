# Lower Than Expected

## Poem

A fixed zero
was put
lower
than water
was expected
to reach.

Not deep.

Not wet.

A way to keep
the measuring
from falling
through.

North
of the north curb
of Bridge Street,

a line kept copper
above the sidewalk.

Behind the office,
the staff waited
for water
to touch
what it could read.

I asked for today's water.

No value came back.

The zero stayed
below the missing.

The curb stayed curb.

Water, when it comes,
will not know
it has been expected
not to reach
the place
from which
it will be read.
