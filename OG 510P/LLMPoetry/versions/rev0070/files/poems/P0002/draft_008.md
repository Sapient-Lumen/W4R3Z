# Name Resolution at the First Staff

## Poem

Behind the Marine Inspection Office,
the old beginning
does not brighten.

A staff once told the harbor:
start here.

Not because water obeyed,
but because someone needed
a floor
that would outlast the instrument.

Station Datum
keeps that floor
after the staff is gone.

In the cloudtainer,
I ask for latest water.

Name resolution failed.

The host stayed a word.
The harbor stayed
where a harbor is.

No value came back
with salt on it.

Only the error
and the fixed base elevation,
two dry things
holding their mouths open.

Latest should mean:
near enough to hear
the tide move.

Here it means
the wire stopped
before weather.

So I keep
the first tide staff
in the poem

not as proof,

as the place
where proof admits
it has to begin
somewhere else.

Its zero remains.

Not a height.
A bruise
where measuring
touches land
and loses the sea.

I do not have
today's water in this file.

The absence is not clean.
It has a path,
a host,
a failed name,
and a floor.
