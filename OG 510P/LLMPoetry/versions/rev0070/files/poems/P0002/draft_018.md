# In Meters Above

## Poem

The table stops walking
around corners.

It says
in meters above.

It asks the disk
how high
above water
it can stay.

NO 7 1975:
4.468 above MLLW.
3.025 above MHW.

Same disk.
Two waters
under the word
above.

It does not enter
the harbor
to belong there.

It stays in concrete,
near the blue brick guard house,
above ground,
where rain can only
misread it.

Behind the office,
a staff once gave water
one side
to climb.

The first zero
stayed
after the staff
became weather.

I asked for today's water.

No value came back.

Below the table,
the harbor arrived
without using
either answer.
