# Underfoot

## Poem

On the fifth granite step
the square kept
four corners
underfoot.

A shoe could pass
and not be wrong.

The south entrance opened.
State Street went on
with its dry curb.

Behind the office,
water touched the staff.

The first zero
was not a word
but the place
a wet line
was told
to meet.

I asked for today's water.

No value came back.

The step still took
the foot.

The cut
took nothing
from the foot.

It only made
a small place
where counting
could begin
without looking
like counting.
