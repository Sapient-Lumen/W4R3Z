# Cut in the Fifth Step

## Poem

The blank was not blank
on the step.

A square was cut
into granite.

Not a word.
Not a disk.

Four short sides
where a shoe
could pass
without reading.

At the south entrance
of the U.S. Customs House,
the fifth granite step
kept the mark
above the sidewalk.

State Street
did not stop
to translate it.

Water was elsewhere,
behind the office,
touching the staff
that once taught
the first zero
to stand.

I asked for today's water.

No value came back.

Still the cut
held its corner.

Not speech.
Not tide.

A dry cut
for a wet count.
