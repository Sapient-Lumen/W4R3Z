# The Water Chosen

## Poem

Near the blue brick guard house,
the disk stays in concrete.

NO 7 1975
does not rise
when the table changes
the water underneath.

Mean Lower Low Water
makes a deeper floor.

Mean High Water
lifts the floor
and calls the same disk
less above.

No one moved
the loading dock.

Only the water
chosen for zero
entered the height.

Behind the office,
the staff once gave tide
a rung
and a first mark.

The hand left.
The zero stayed.

I asked for today's water.

No value came back.

So the harbor
used no column.

It found the lower boards
without naming
what it stood on.

Rain crossed the dock
without becoming
a datum.

The disk kept
its dry face.

The water chosen
was not the water
arriving.
