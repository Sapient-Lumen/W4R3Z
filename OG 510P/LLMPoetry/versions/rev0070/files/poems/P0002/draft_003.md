# Staff Behind the Inspection Office

At station 8518750, The Battery, NY,
the tide gage and staff are located
on the pier behind the Inspection Office.

All data values are relative to the MLLW.

MHHW 5.05.
MHW 4.73.
MSL 2.57.
MLW 0.20.
MLLW 0.00.
NAVD88 2.77.
STND -3.29.

The numbers are not decoration.
They are seven ways
to ask the same water
where it stands.

A tide staff is a local answer:
a pier, an office, a kept vertical,
a rule where a rule can be found.

Max Tide 14.04: 10/30/2012 01:12.
Min Tide -4.29: 02/02/1976 21:30.

One record is not a metaphor
until someone has to climb
the fixed object
to read the moving one.

HAT 6.48: 10/27/2011 12:48.
LAT -1.53: 02/12/2032 20:18.

The calendar keeps both
what was observed
and what has not arrived.

The relative sea level trend is 2.95 millimeters/year
with a 95% confidence interval of +/- 0.09 mm/yr,
based on monthly mean sea level data from 1856 to 2025.

Nothing in that sentence floods.
It only lengthens the question:
whose floor, which zero,
what office, what pier,
how many years before the ruler
has to be moved
and still call itself exact?
