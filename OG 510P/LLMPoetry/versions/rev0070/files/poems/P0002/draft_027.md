# North of the North Curb

## Poem

North
of the north curb
of Bridge Street,

the line kept
its small place
in copper.

The buttress held
its face.

The sidewalk went on
below it.

Behind the office,
the staff waited
for water
to touch
what it could read.

The first zero
was elsewhere
and older.

I asked for today's water.

No value came back.

Nothing rose
at the curb.

Nothing rang
in the bolt.

Still the line
stayed above
the sidewalk,

not wet,
not hidden,

ready for a height
that had to arrive
from somewhere else.
