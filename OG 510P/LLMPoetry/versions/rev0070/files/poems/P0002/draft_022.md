# Stamping or Designation

## Poem

The table gives
one mouth:

Stamping or Designation.

The disk answers
from concrete:

NO 7 1975.

The other name
stays elsewhere,
all station
and tide.

Near the blue brick guard house,
the loading dock
keeps the disk
above ground.

The staff behind the office
once let a hand
meet water
at a line.

The hand left.
The first zero
stayed.

I asked for today's water.

No value came back.

Still the table
could call the disk
from two waters
without wetting it.

That is the work
of a chosen name:
to be dry enough
for height.
