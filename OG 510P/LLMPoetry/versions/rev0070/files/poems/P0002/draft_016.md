# The Westernmost Corner

## Poem

To find the mark,
leave the water
out of the question.

Start with the Coast Guard Building.
Take its southwest corner.

Then the blue brick guard house:
its westernmost corner
points without moving.

The south corner
of the building
keeps the last dry turn.

Between these instructions
the disk lies in concrete,
small enough
for rain to overlook.

On the pier behind the office,
a staff once gave water
a side to climb.

The first zero
stayed with the dry work.

I asked for today's water.

No value came back.

Below,
the boards darkened
without reporting.

The harbor kept
its own arrival.

The disk waited
above ground,
found by corners,
not by tide.
