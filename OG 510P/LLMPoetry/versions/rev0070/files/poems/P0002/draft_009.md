# No Height Follows

## Poem

Behind the Marine Inspection Office,
the harbor is not waiting
for my file.

A staff once said:
begin.

Water did not agree.
Water kept arriving.

Station Datum
kept the beginning.

Not the staff,
not the hand,
not the tide mark
whitened by weather.

The first tide staff
is gone
or far enough gone
that only its zero remains.

I asked for today's water.

The name did not resolve.

No value came back.
No wet number.
No minute
with harbor in it.

So this poem has
the floor
and not the tide.

It has a first mark
under later instruments.

It has a dry request
where latest
should have opened.

Outside the failed name,
water takes the Battery
without reading this.

Ferry wash,
piling shadow,
wind against the mouth
of the island.

Inside the file,
only the chosen floor.

Not proof.
Beginning.

A fixed base elevation
is a way of saying:
we cannot hold motion,
so we hold the place
we start from.

Its zero remains.

No height follows.
