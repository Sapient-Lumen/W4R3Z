## Verdict

`P0002-D007` should not be promoted. It is the most disciplined no-number draft in the P0002 line, but its discipline becomes a clean metaphor too quickly.

## What improved

D007 leaves the D006 MLLW/NAVD88 number hinge behind and removes the witness/oath courtroom frame. It brings the NOAA Station Datum definition into the poem as actual source pressure: a fixed base elevation, held through changes in gauge or staff, most often tied to the zero of the first tide staff installed.

The strongest pressure is the old-zero image: the first staff is gone or not gone enough, and its zero remains. That phrase is closer to the poem P0002 has been trying to find than any datum table line.

## What still fails

The draft still explains itself into safety. It tells the reader what Station Datum means, then supplies a familiar set of abstractions: truth, mercy, fact, question, floor. The result is cleaner than D006, but not strange enough. It risks being the tidiest metaphor the source can offer rather than a poem under source pressure.

The live-data failure is present, but only near the end as disclosure-like material: the line to the harbor breaks before the harbor. That is the sharper machine-native pressure. The cloudtainer has repeatedly failed to fetch a current water value; D007 acknowledges that failure but does not let it reorganize the poem.

## Required next change

D008 should keep the first-staff / Station Datum anchor, but bring the failed live reach into the poem body as material. It should not invent a current water level. It should make the local name-resolution failure and the old fixed zero collide: a station with a fixed beginning, a live API endpoint the cloudtainer cannot resolve, and a poem that has to admit where its proof stops.

No admission, evidence claim, or same-turn judgment is authorized for D008.
