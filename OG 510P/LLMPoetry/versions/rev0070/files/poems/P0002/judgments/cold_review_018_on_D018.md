## Judgment

D018 found a real pressure in the NOAA benchmark elevation table: one dry disk is described as being above two water datums. The phrase "Same disk. / Two waters / under the word / above" is the strongest discovery in the draft.

But the draft reintroduced the source table too directly. `4.468 above MLLW` and `3.025 above MHW` are accurate and bounded, yet they pull the poem back toward an elegant fact-card demonstration. The title and opening tell the reader how to read the table before the image has time to become strange. The ending, "without using / either answer," is graceful but remains conceptual closure: it explains how the harbor exceeds the data rather than letting the scene bear that excess.

The next revision should not add more source detail. It should subtract the visible elevation numbers from the poem body, keep the official numbers in packet/disclosure space, and pressure the word `above` itself: above what, and by whose water?

## Required consequence

Do not promote D018. A successor may be justified only as a numeric-subtraction / above-question reanchor that preserves the official benchmark facts outside the poem body while refusing the source-table lyric regression.
