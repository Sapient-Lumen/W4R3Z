## Judgment

D016 did useful work. It moved away from D015's above-ground aphorism and made the NOAA benchmark sheet's dry location language active: Coast Guard Building, southwest corner, blue brick guard house, westernmost corner, south corner, concrete, staff, first zero, no value.

But it still reads too much like a poem made by following directions. The opening command, "To find the mark," tells the reader how to use the source before the poem has made the source strange. "Start with" and "Take" keep the body close to procedural paraphrase. The closing phrase, "found by corners, / not by tide," names the insight before the image can do more work.

The strongest pressure inside D016 is smaller: the disk appears to have no location of its own. It is described by borrowed corners, other walls, and dry objects before it can become a mark for water. A successor should test that borrowed-corner condition, not add more route detail and not return to D015's aphorism.

## Required consequence

Do not promote D016. A successor may be justified if it cuts the instructional opening, keeps the source's corner-offset facts, and makes the borrowed-location problem carry the poem without presenting the benchmark sheet as directions.
