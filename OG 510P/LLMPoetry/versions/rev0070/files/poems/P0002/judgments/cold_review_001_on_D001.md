## Firewall

## Diagnosis

D001 did the important job of escaping P0001's internal codework fog. The station, unit, datum, accepted date, epoch, max/min values, and MLLW definition all survived into the surface. That was real forward motion.

The failure is that the surface mostly recites its packet. It says, correctly, that the zero is not the sea, but it does not yet force the reader to feel why that matters. The strongest lines are the rule-like ones — `The poem may not say “rise” / without leaving a unit behind` and `The gauge refuses beauty / whenever beauty forgets its reference point` — because they turn source pressure into a discipline. The weakest passages are the stacked fact labels, where the poem behaves like a card pinned beside the station page.

D001 also underuses the richer datum table. It has one datum and two extrema, but not enough of the competing reference surfaces: MHHW, MHW, MSL, MLW, MLLW, NAVD88, STND, historical maximum/minimum, astronomical highest/lowest, and relative sea-level trend. Those values are not decoration; they are the poem's actual conflict. A harbor has several floors depending on who is reading it.

## Revision demand

D002 should keep the external-material firewall, but make the datum table create the poem's pressure. It should expose the multiple-zero problem and make the measurement frame alter ordinary words like `water`, `danger`, `future`, and `floor`. No P0001 D011. No promotion. No live/current reading unless actually captured and receipted.

## Non-promotion reason

P0002-D001 proves the fork was worthwhile, but it remains closer to a fact-card poem than a transformed source-bound poem. It is not admitted and not evidence-ready.
