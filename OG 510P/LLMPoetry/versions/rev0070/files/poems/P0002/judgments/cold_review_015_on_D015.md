## Judgment

D015 is stronger than D014 because it abandons the stamped-mark pun and finds a better source contradiction: the mark for water is above ground, set in concrete near the blue brick guard house. That pressure is real.

But D015 still solves itself too quickly. The opening line, "The mark for water / is not in the water," is a thesis before it is an image. "A disk cannot float. / That is its work." turns the source object into an aphorism. The old D010 hinge, "No value came back. / Water did.", remains powerful but is now overfamiliar inside the P0002 sequence. The board-end and screw-mouth ending tries to make wetness arrive, but it feels added after the conceptual object has already closed.

The riskiest next move is not a bigger source packet. It is a narrower pressure from the benchmark sheet's location syntax: the disk is found through dry corners and offsets before it is found through water. That may restore friction without returning to D012 route-directions or D014 stamped-name cleverness.

## Required consequence

Do not promote D015. A successor may be justified only if it subtracts the aphoristic above-ground thesis and tests the dry-corner / offset location pressure against the same no-live-value boundary.
