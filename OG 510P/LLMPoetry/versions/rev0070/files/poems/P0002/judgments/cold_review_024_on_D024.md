Temporal firewall: D024 was created in rev0055; this is a later-turn review.

## Finding

D024 made a real advance over D023. It stopped treating the blank `BENCH MARK STAMPING:` field as the main event and found the stronger material mark: a chiseled square cut into the fifth granite step at the south entrance of the U.S. Customs House.

But D024 still solved itself too cleanly. `A dry cut / for a wet count` is a thesis ending. `Not speech. / Not tide.` categorizes the mark before the reader can stay with it. The best pressure is earlier: `Four short sides / where a shoe / could pass / without reading.` The next draft should let the city walk over the water-mark without turning the mark into doctrine.

## What holds

- The fifth granite step and chiseled square are materially stronger than the blank-stamping field.
- The shoe/passing line gives the mark a public surface and a body-scale encounter.
- The no-live-value line remains concise and non-infrastructural.

## What fails

- The ending converts the object into a neat wet/dry proposition.
- The blank-field lead keeps D023's absence conceit alive.
- The poem tells the reader how to categorize the mark instead of letting underfoot contact carry it.

## Revision instruction

Make one bounded underfoot reanchor. Remove the D024 thesis-ending and blank-field lead. Keep the fifth step / Customs House / State Street / staff / first-zero / no-value pressure, but move the body toward a foot crossing the mark without knowing it has crossed a height.
