## Verdict

`P0002-D005` should not be promoted. It is the strongest P0002 draft so far, but it still explains its own discovery too eagerly.

## What improved

D005 found a real pressure point: the same storm can be reported as different heights when the zero/reference frame changes. The surface-burden cap also helped; the poem no longer printed the whole NOAA table as if validation were the poem.

## What still fails

The opening line announces the station before the poem makes the reader need a station. The fact rows are cleaner than D004, but still arrive as fact rows. The strongest conceptual lines—about the zero chosen after water leaves, the neutral nail, and almost-nothing having a job—are good commentary on the source packet, not yet a sufficiently enacted poem.

The core failure is not missing evidence. The evidence is now strong enough to reveal the literary problem: D005 is a compact explanation of reference frames. It does not yet make reference frames behave as pressure inside the poem.

## Required next change

D006 should stop beginning with station metadata. It should hold the Marine Inspection Office/staff object, keep only the smallest necessary datum contrast, and make the reference frame act like testimony or oath: one storm, two sworn heights, no change in the body of water. Dates, station identity, table rows, API mechanics, and failed-pull detail should stay in the packet and disclosure surfaces.

No admission, evidence claim, or same-turn judgment is authorized for D006.
