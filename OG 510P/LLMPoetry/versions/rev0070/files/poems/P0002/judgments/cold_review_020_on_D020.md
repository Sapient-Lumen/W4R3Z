## Judgment

The draft also preserved a few strong pressures: `No value came back`, the harbor using no column, rain crossing the dock without becoming a datum, and the split between water chosen and water arriving.

The strongest next move is not to add more datum definition. It is to keep the benchmark table in the packet/disclosure layer and make the body face the table's absence: the table has columns for official water, but no column for rain crossing the dock.
