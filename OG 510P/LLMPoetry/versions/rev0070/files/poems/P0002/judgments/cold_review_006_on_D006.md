## Verdict

`P0002-D006` should not be promoted. It is the cleanest P0002 draft so far, but the central witness/oath conceit still explains the reference-frame problem too much.

## What improved

D006 finally stopped opening with station metadata. It began at the Marine Inspection Office, kept the staff/gage object, and reduced the source table to one meaningful conflict: the same observed high entering as different heights under MLLW and NAVD88.

The line pair around nothing draining and the oath changing is the strongest P0002 pressure yet. It makes the source fact behave instead of merely reporting it.

## What still fails

The poem still leans on a courtroom vocabulary—witness, first question, oath—that arrives as a ready-made metaphor. The reader can understand the conceit immediately, which is exactly the problem: the draft becomes a lucid demonstration of datum relativity rather than a poem whose pressure must be encountered.

The numbers are fewer, but they still remain the poem's hinge. D006 is improved compression, not yet transformation.

## Required next change

D007 should stop inheriting D006's courtroom frame. It should move below the MLLW/NAVD88 values into the more material NOAA definition of Station Datum: a fixed base elevation, held constant through changes to the gauge or tide staff, most often at the zero of the first tide staff installed.

The next draft should make that source phrase carry pressure directly. It should use no body numbers, avoid witness/oath language, and let the first staff / old zero / failed live reach produce the poem's action.

No admission, evidence claim, or same-turn judgment is authorized for D007.
