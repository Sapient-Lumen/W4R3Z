## Judgment

D017 is a genuine improvement over D016 because it stops reading like a route sheet. The repeated east / southwest / westernmost / south-corner language turns the NOAA benchmark syntax into a pressure of dependency: the disk can be located only by borrowing walls and corners that are not water.

But D017 still closes too cleanly around its own conceit. "The disk has no room / of its own" and "Every phrase / borrows a wall" explain the discovery before the reader has to discover it. The final turn, where the tide arrives without knowing which corner named it, is graceful but too complete. The poem has learned to metabolize the official directions, yet it still risks being a clever benchmark-location lyric.

The strongest next pressure is vertical rather than horizontal. The same published benchmark sheet does not only locate the disk by corners; it gives the disk official elevations **in meters above** water datums. That table is dangerous because it can become a fact card, but it is also materially stranger than the corner conceit: the dry disk answers two waters without entering either.

## Required consequence

Do not promote D017. A successor may be justified only if it moves from borrowed-corner horizontal location to a tightly bounded meters-above reanchor, preserves the no-live-value boundary, and avoids returning to a table-heavy NOAA display.
