## Judgment

D019 correctly identified the weakness of D018: the visible elevation figures made the poem behave like a refined table demonstration. Moving those figures into packet/disclosure space was the right subtraction.

But D019 over-smoothed the source after subtracting the numbers. `Above what / is left / for water / to argue` is a strong hinge, but it turns the official datum difference into philosophical weather. `One water / makes it taller` and `Another / reaches up / and takes / the tallness back` are legible, but they anthropomorphize the datum shift instead of letting the named reference planes do the pressure. The ending, `above nothing / it could keep`, closes handsomely while loosening the NOAA resistance that made D018 interesting.

The next revision should not restore the elevation numbers to the poem body. It should name the two water datums that create the difference — Mean Lower Low Water and Mean High Water — and make the chosen zero itself the scene. The risk is that this will become a cleaner explanation. The opportunity is that the official names may restore material resistance without reverting to a table dump.

## Required consequence

Do not promote D019. A successor may be justified only as a named-datum difference reanchor that keeps exact elevation numbers in packet/disclosure space, names the water datums in the poem body, and tests whether `chosen for zero` is stranger than `above what`.
