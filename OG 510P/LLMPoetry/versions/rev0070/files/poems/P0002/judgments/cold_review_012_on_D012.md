# Cold Review 012 on P0002-D012 — rev0044

Target: `poems/P0002/draft_012.md`  
Verdict: `revise_not_promote`

## Finding

D012 repairs the worst fault in D011: it stops drifting as freely into generic harbor atmosphere. `Broadway ends`, `State Street`, and the Coast Guard office give the poem a real approach path, and the no-value hinge still has pressure:

```text
No value came back.

Water did.
```

But D012 makes a new mistake. The route begins to behave like a set of better directions, then the poem tries to convert those directions into a finished idea. The most vulnerable lines are:

```text
Broadway ends
before it learns the harbor.
```

and:

```text
Nothing reads the harbor
better than what it ruins
at the same height
again.
```

Those lines are fluent, but they close the source material into an abstract thesis before the object has fully resisted. D012 is more site-specific than D011, yet it still lets a graceful conceptual ending do too much of the work.

The core failure is not traceability and not lack of place. The core failure is **route specificity turning into lyric explanation**.

## Line-level notes

- `Broadway ends` is the right source pressure, but `before it learns the harbor` over-personifies the route.
- `State Street turns / the last small distance` is useful, but it remains a direction rather than a material object.
- `a pier keeps the kind of mark / water cannot keep / for itself` is nearly the old D010 explanatory pressure in softer clothes.
- `A first zero / low in the old arithmetic` preserves datum pressure, but `old arithmetic` is a concept label.
- `No value came back. / Water did.` remains the line pair to preserve.
- The final stanza is too resolved: it tells the reader what the harbor means instead of leaving the mark, disk, staff, or dock to bear the pressure.

## Decision

Do not promote D012. Do not make it a candidate. Create one bounded successor only if it shifts from route/place directions to a smaller resistant benchmark object: the tidal station disk set in the concrete loading dock near the blue brick guard house. Keep the no-live-value boundary intact and keep the D010 reader handoff separate.
