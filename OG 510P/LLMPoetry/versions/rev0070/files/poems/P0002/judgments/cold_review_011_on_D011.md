# Cold Review 011 on P0002-D011 — rev0043

Target: `poems/P0002/draft_011.md`  
Verdict: `revise_not_promote`

## Finding

D011 is a real improvement over D010 in one respect: it stops making the technical term do the poem's work. `Station Datum` leaves the body; the poem no longer closes by explaining its own concept. The useful pressure is in these lines:

```text
No value came back.

Water did.
```

and:

```text
The staff is gone enough
to be floor,
not gone enough
to be past.
```

But the subtraction overshoots. Once the technical pressure is moved into disclosure, the body fills the gap with broadly plausible harbor atmosphere: ferry wash, gulls, green weed, brine, boards, old holes. Some of that is sensuous, but too much of it could belong to any waterfront poem. The source is no longer dominating the poem, but it is also no longer resisting the poem enough.

The core failure is not traceability. The core failure is **generic harbor realism after successful technical subtraction**.

## Line-level notes

- `Behind the Marine Inspection Office` is still the right kind of anchor, but D011 treats it as a backdrop rather than a route or pressure.
- `the pier kept a ruler / for water` remains strong, but it is inherited from D010 and does not by itself justify the fallback.
- `with its first zero / cut low / into the weather` is promising, but it floats free because the poem has removed too much of the surrounding place specificity.
- `Ferry wash slapped the piles`, `Green weed`, and `Gulls walked over it` add atmosphere without enough source pressure.
- `No value came back. / Water did.` should be preserved as the live/runtime gap hinge.
- `It knocks. / It leaves a narrow sound / inside the old holes.` is a decent ending, but still risks generic waterfront closure.

## Decision

Do not promote D011. Do not make it a candidate. Create one bounded successor only if it re-anchors the scene in a more resistant source detail and keeps the no-live-value boundary intact.

D012 should use the NOAA published bench-mark route/place material — Broadway ending at The Battery, State Street, the U.S. Coast Guard Inspection Office, and the pier behind it — rather than adding more unsourced harbor atmosphere.
