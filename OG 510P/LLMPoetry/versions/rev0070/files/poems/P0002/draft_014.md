# NO 7 1975

## Poem

The loading dock
keeps a small stamped disk
in concrete:

NO 7 1975.

A stamped face,
less weather
than weathered.

Near the blue brick guard house,
near the Coast Guard wall,
it sits above ground
like a nail
that learned to be floor.

Behind the building,
a staff once offered
water a numbered side.

Hands read it.
Salt climbed it.
Then the staff changed.

The first zero
stayed.

No value came back.

Water did.

It did not come
to be verified.

It came by the screw mouths,
by the swollen grain,
by the lower boards
going dark
before anyone
could call them high.

The disk held
its small round no.

The dock took
the rest.
