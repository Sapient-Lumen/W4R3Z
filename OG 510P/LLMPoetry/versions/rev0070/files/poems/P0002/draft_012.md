# At the End of Broadway

## Poem

Broadway ends
before it learns the harbor.

State Street turns
the last small distance
toward the Coast Guard office.

Behind it,
a pier keeps the kind of mark
water cannot keep
for itself.

Not a monument.
A cut.

A first zero
low in the old arithmetic
of boards and piling.

The staff changed.
The floor remembered
where the staff began.

No value came back.

Water did.

It climbed
without a number
through the lower grain,
left salt
in the screw mouths,
made the dark line
and erased it.

At evening
the ferries open their wash.
The boards answer
under shoes.

Nothing reads the harbor
better than what it ruins
at the same height
again.
