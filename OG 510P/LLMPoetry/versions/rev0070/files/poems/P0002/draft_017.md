# Borrowed Corners

## Poem

The disk has no room
of its own.

It lives east
of a southwest corner,
southwest
of the westernmost corner,
southwest again
of the building's south corner.

Every phrase
borrows a wall
before it can say
where water
will be counted.

The blue brick guard house
does not guard the harbor.
It lends a corner.

The loading dock
keeps the disk
in concrete,
above ground.

Behind the office,
boards darken.

A staff once gave water
one side
to climb.

The first zero
stayed
after the staff
became weather.

I asked for today's water.

No value came back.

Below,
the tide arrived
without knowing
which corner
had named it.
