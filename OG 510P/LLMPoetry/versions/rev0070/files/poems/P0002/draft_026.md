# Horizontal Line

## Poem

The mark is not the bolt.

It is the line
on the bolt,
a horizontal line
set vertical
in the north face
of the south buttress.

Bridge Street kept
its curb.

The Custom House
kept its stone.

Behind the office,
water touched the staff.

The first zero
sat lower
than anyone
using the door.

I asked for today's water.

No value came back.

Still the line
held level
on something
standing up.

No shoe
had to read it.

No tide
had to be present.

A small crosswise
place
for height
to begin
without water
showing why.
