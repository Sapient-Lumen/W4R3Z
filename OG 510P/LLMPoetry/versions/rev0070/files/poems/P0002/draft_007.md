# Zero of the First Staff

## Poem

Behind the Marine Inspection Office,
someone once gave the harbor
a first staff

and told the water:
begin here.

Later, other gauges came,
other names for ground,
other floors carried into court.

Station Datum
kept the old nail in place.

Not truth,
not mercy,
not the sea's own height—

a place to start
when every wet thing
tries to leave
as weather.

The first tide staff
is gone
or not gone enough.

Its zero remains
like a mark under paint,
asking each number
what it has promised
before it calls itself fact.

I do not have
today's water in this file.

The failure is plain:
the line to the harbor
breaks before the harbor.

Still, the staff
does what a staff can do.

It does not know the storm.
It gives the question
a floor.
