# Blank Stamping

## Poem

One mark
had no stamping.

The table left
a white place
after the colon.

Still it gave
a designation:

851 8750 TIDAL 749.

A chiseled square
in the fifth granite step
kept quiet
under shoes.

Water was elsewhere,
behind the office,
touching the staff
the way a hand
used to.

The square did not say tide.

It waited
above the sidewalk
with no name
for weather
to polish.

I asked for today's water.

No value came back.

No tide
could read it
back
into speech.

The blank
did not become
less official.

Only less able
to pretend
a name
was the mark.
