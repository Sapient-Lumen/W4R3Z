# Above Ground

## Poem

The mark for water
is not in the water.

It is set in concrete
above ground
near the blue brick guard house.

A disk cannot float.
That is its work.

On the pier,
a staff once gave water
a side to climb.

Hands read it.
Salt read the hands.
Then the staff changed.

The first zero
stayed low
in the word for height.

I asked for today's water.

No value came back.

Water did.

Not as a height.

As board ends
going dark.
As screw mouths
taking in the old answer.

The disk stayed
where water had to miss it.

The dock
did not.
