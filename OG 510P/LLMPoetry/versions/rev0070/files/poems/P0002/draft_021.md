# No Column for Rain

## Poem

Near the blue brick guard house,
a disk is set
where the loading dock
can keep it dry.

NO 7 1975.

The table gives it
two columns.

Rain is not
one of them.

Mean water
stays elsewhere,
under official
weather.

The staff behind the office
once let a hand
meet tide
at a mark.

The hand left.
The mark stayed.

I asked for today's water.

No value came back.

On the dock,
rain crossed the disk
without asking
which zero
had been chosen.

It darkened
the stamped no.

Then left
no height
behind.

The harbor kept
its lower boards.

The table kept
its columns.

Between them,
water arrived
without a name
that would hold still.
