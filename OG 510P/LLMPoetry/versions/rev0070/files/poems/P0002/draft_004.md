# Behind the Marine Inspection Office

Station 8518750, The Battery, NY.

NOAA's older bench-mark sheet says
the tide gage and staff are located
on the pier behind the Marine Inspection Office.

So the poem begins
not at the sea,
but at a stick trusted
enough to be left outside.

The zero called MLLW
is not nothing.
It is a habit of lower lows
taught to a ruler
until the ruler sounds objective.

At 10/30/2012 01:12,
the water wrote 14.04
above that habit.
The staff did not become dramatic.
It held still,
which is a form of being implicated.

Somewhere below,
Min Tide -4.29 keeps its old night.
Somewhere ahead,
LAT -1.53 waits in 2032.
The page can say future low
without leaning over the rail.

NAVD88, STND:
two names for where land starts talking
as if water had agreed to the floor.

The relative sea level trend is 2.95 millimeters/year,
based on monthly mean sea level data from 1856 to 2025.

A small number,
until it has had a century
to find the door.

Behind the Marine Inspection Office,
the staff keeps losing the argument
that measurement is neutral.
Every mark is a bargain
between what rises
and what insists
it has not moved.
