# Against Which Zero

## Poem

Station 8518750, The Battery, NY.

The tide gage and staff stand
on the pier behind the Marine Inspection Office.

A storm can be two heights
without changing its body:
8.99 above MHHW,
14.04 above MLLW.

The difference is not water.
It is the zero
chosen after the water leaves.

A ruler does not lie
by standing still.
It lies by letting us forget
who taught the nail to look neutral.

Below the staff,
Min Tide -4.29 keeps
the night it was allowed
to be negative.

Ahead of it,
LAT -1.53 is already
a future appointment
for the bottom.

NAVD88 and STND
argue under the floorboards.

2.95 millimeters/year
is almost nothing
until almost nothing
has a job
and keeps it.

Behind the office,
inspection goes both ways:
the harbor reads the staff,
the staff reads the land,
and the land keeps passing
by refusing to move.
