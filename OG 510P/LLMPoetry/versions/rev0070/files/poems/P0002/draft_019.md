# Above What

## Poem

The table says
above.

Above what
is left
for water
to argue.

Same disk.

One water
makes it taller.

Another
reaches up
and takes
the tallness back.

NO 7 1975
keeps its face
in the loading dock.

It does not go down
to prove
the harbor.

Rain crosses it
near the blue brick guard house,
above ground,
wrong
in no official way.

Behind the office,
the staff once gave tide
a side
to climb.

The first zero
stayed
after the hand
left the reading.

I asked for today's water.

No value came back.

The harbor
did not wait
for the table.

It touched
the lower boards
and went on,
above nothing
it could keep.
