# Zero Has a Witness

## Poem

Behind the Marine Inspection Office,
the staff waits for water
the way a witness waits
for the first question.

The storm says
I was not taller.
You changed the floor.

Under MLLW
it enters as 14.04.

Under NAVD88
it enters as 11.27.

Nothing drains from the room.
Only the oath changes.

A minus tide
is not absence.
It is the harbor
being asked to stand
below a sentence.

Every zero
wants to call itself ground.

The nail in the pier
does not choose a side.
It holds still
while land and water
borrow its seriousness.

Tonight there is no live reading
in this file.

That absence is also a gauge:
not the harbor,
only our reach
coming up dry.
