# Cold Review — P0003-D002 “Specific Items of Disagreement” — rev0064

**Verdict:** `revise_not_promote`  
**Temporal status:** later-turn review of the exact rev0063 draft and artifact hashes.  
**Successor boundary:** D003 may be created, but remains same-turn unjudged.

D002 makes a real advance over D001. The WAL no longer hides a wholesale replacement: it checks one disagreement box and appends particulars against the shared clean inspection. The official heading “SPECIFIC ITEMS OF DISAGREEMENT” imposes a useful obligation, and the vacant-yet-occupied close is sharper than an explanation of the sidecar.

It still does not earn promotion. The database contains numbered, pre-rendered poem lines. It does not know what an inspection item, cost, observation, response, or occupant is. Disclosure therefore exposes a formal mismatch: the poem presents a contested record, while its data model presents only a sequence. The zero costs are promising but passive. “Normal wear,” the hinge between visible trace and chargeable damage, never appears. The Census classification is appended like a sourced coda instead of being derived from persons-present and usual-residence facts. Several traces—the unfaded rectangle, standing pile, darkened outlet, fresh brass—remain familiar enough that the apparatus can still be admired separately from the writing.

Preserve D002 and its exact database/WAL bytes. Do not promote it. D003 should model the record relationally, render it through a SQL view, derive vacancy status with a generated column, keep every item GOOD and zero-cost normal wear, and concentrate the observations. The classification should become the record’s consequence rather than a quoted ending.

This is one internal cold review, not external reader evidence, admission, or proof that D003 succeeds.
