# P0002-D010 adversarial candidate read — rev0041

Target: `P0002-D010` / `A Ruler for Water`  
Status: internal machine adversarial read. This is **not a reader response**, not admission, not evidence, and not a substitute for the disclosed-reader handoff.

## Verdict

**Keep the candidate hold, do not admit.**

The poem has earned pressure, not celebration. The handoff pipeline was beginning to protect it from the only question that matters: after disclosure, does the body itself still make a reading event?

My answer remains provisional: yes enough to keep testing, no enough to admit.

## What survives hostile reading

The opening finally gives the source a body before the source gives the poem a name:

```text
Behind the Marine Inspection Office,
the pier kept a ruler
for water.
```

That is the most important advance over D001-D009. The poem no longer opens like a datum record or like a validator explaining itself. It gives a pier, a ruler, and a body that refuses to stay ruled.

The second surviving hinge is:

```text
No value came back.

Water did.
```

This is the candidate's live nerve. It converts the local runtime failure into absence without pretending the failure produced a measurement. It is also the line most likely to survive disclosure, because it does not need the NOAA packet to be intelligible.

## What still feels protected

The poem still leans on a technical noun at a critical moment:

```text
Station Datum
held the old start
under later metal.
```

This is accurate, but it may ask imported authority to perform what the poem should perform with scene. A reader who does not care about datum language may feel the poem briefly turns into a caption.

The closing is also too eager to summarize its own thought:

```text
Its zero remains.

Height does not.
```

The pair is clean, but maybe too clean. It risks closing the poem as a concept demonstration: reference frames endure, measured states pass. D010 is strongest when water and apparatus touch each other; weakest when it converts that contact into an aphorism.

## Fallback revision brief

No D011 is created in rev0041. If a real disclosed reader rejects D010, or if the project owner explicitly overrides the reader-first hold, the next draft should not add source material. It should **subtract explanation**.

The fallback D011 should try the following constraints:

1. Keep the title pressure around ruler/staff/water, but remove `Station Datum` from the poem body.
2. Keep `No value came back. / Water did.` or a stricter equivalent.
3. Cut the explicit `Not the tide. / The rule / for saying tide.` stanza.
4. Replace the final aphorism with an image or action.
5. Add no new NOAA fact unless the poem loses source truth without it.
6. Keep the disclosure and packet as source authority; do not make the body recite that authority.
7. Treat `height` as a consequence, not a thesis word, unless a reader specifically names the closing as the strength.
