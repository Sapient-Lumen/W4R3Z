## Poem

Behind the Marine Inspection Office,
the pier kept a ruler
for water.

Or kept the place
where a ruler
told water
to begin.

A tide staff is honest
only while wet.

After the staff,
instruments changed.
The first zero
did not.

Station Datum
held the old start
under later metal.

Not the tide.
The rule
for saying tide.

The harbor does not begin there.
It keeps crossing there.

Ferry wash darkens the piles.
Salt lifts
through old bolt holes.
The island opens
its gray mouth
and shuts it.

No value came back.

Water did.

No minute.
No borrowed height.

Only wind
taking the rail's edge,
only brine
on the lower boards.

A beginning was set
lower than water
was expected to reach.

Water found it anyway.

The first tide staff
is gone enough
to have become
a floor.

Gulls step over it
without lowering
their heads.

No wet number.

Only a held zero,
and water passing
without agreement.

Its zero remains.

Height does not.
