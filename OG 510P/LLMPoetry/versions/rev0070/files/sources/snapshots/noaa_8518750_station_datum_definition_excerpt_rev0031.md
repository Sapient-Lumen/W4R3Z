Relevant sourced pressure: NOAA's Tidal Datums page defines Station Datum as a fixed base elevation at a tide station to which water-level measurements are referred. It says the datum is unique to each station, is referenced to the primary bench mark, is held constant regardless of changes to the water level gauge or tide staff, and that the datum of tabulation is most often at the zero of the first tide staff installed.
