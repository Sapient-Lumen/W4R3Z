Bounded observations used in P0003-D004:

- For an occupied unit, people living there must treat it as their usual residence or have no usual residence elsewhere.
- A unit may be classified vacant while entirely occupied by people whose usual residence is elsewhere.
- The D004 transition models only that classification boundary. It does not model a real interview, household, property, duration, tenure, or legal status.

This is a bounded source note, not a complete methodology capture, current housing statistic, or quality judgment.
