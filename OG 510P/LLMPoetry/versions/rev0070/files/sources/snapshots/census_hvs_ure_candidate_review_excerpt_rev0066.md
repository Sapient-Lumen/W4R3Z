# Census HVS usual-residence boundary — bounded excerpt note — rev0066

Official source: U.S. Census Bureau, *Housing Vacancy Survey Definitions and Explanations*.

Bounded source note: the definitions distinguish occupied units by whether the people living there consider the unit their usual residence or have no usual residence elsewhere, and state that a unit may be classified vacant when it is entirely occupied by people whose usual residence is elsewhere. The examples add temporary occupancy and a maintained usual residence elsewhere.
