# Census Housing Vacancy Survey definitions — occupied by persons with usual residence elsewhere (rev0064)

Bounded observations used in P0003-D003:

- An occupied housing unit ordinarily requires people living there to consider it their usual residence or to have no usual residence elsewhere.
- A unit may nevertheless be classified vacant when entirely or temporarily occupied by people whose usual residence is elsewhere.

The generated `VACANT` field in D003 operationalizes only that narrow source condition. This is not a complete survey-methodology capture or a live housing statistic.
