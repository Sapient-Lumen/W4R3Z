# HUD damage-claim guidance — normal wear versus extraordinary repair (rev0064)

Bounded observations used in P0003-D003:

- HUD says only extraordinary repairs or replacements should be claimed in the cited special-claims context.
- A submitted damage claim must not result from normal wear and tear or routine maintenance.
- The guidance requires itemized damage documentation and a repair-cost breakdown.
