# Source snapshot — NOAA Station Datum / first tide staff definition — rev0043

Source: NOAA Tides & Currents datum options page.
Captured as a lightweight excerpt for P0002-D012. This is not a full source capture and not poem quality evidence.

Relevant excerpt/paraphrase:
- Station Datum is a fixed base elevation at a tide station to which water-level measurements are referred.
- It is unique to each station.
- It is established lower than the water is expected to reach.
- It is referenced to the primary bench mark and held constant regardless of changes to the water level gauge or tide staff.
- The datum of tabulation is most often at the zero of the first tide staff installed.
