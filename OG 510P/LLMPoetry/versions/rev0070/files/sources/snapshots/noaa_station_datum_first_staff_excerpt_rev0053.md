# NOAA tidal datum / Station Datum excerpt — rev0053

Source: NOAA CO-OPS tidal datum definitions page. Evidence ref: `turn687311view1`.

Relevant excerpt/paraphrase: NOAA defines a datum as a base elevation used as a reference for heights or depths, says tidal datums are referenced to fixed points known as bench marks, and defines MHW/MLLW as averages over the National Tidal Datum Epoch. The Station Datum definition in the page states that Station Datum is a fixed base elevation at a tide station, held constant regardless of gauge or tide-staff changes, and that the datum of tabulation is most often at the zero of the first tide staff installed.
