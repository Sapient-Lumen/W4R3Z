Relevant NOAA Tides & Currents source pressure: Station Datum is defined as a fixed base elevation at a tide station to which water-level measurements are referred. The NOAA page says it is unique to the station, held constant regardless of changes to the water-level gauge or tide staff, and that the datum of tabulation is most often at the zero of the first tide staff installed.
