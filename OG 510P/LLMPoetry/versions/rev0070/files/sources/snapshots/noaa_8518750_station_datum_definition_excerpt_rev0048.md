Relevant excerpt summary: NOAA defines Station Datum as a fixed base elevation at a tide station to which all water-level measurements are referred; it is unique to each station, held constant regardless of water-level gauge or tide-staff changes, and the datum of tabulation is most often at the zero of the first tide staff installed.
