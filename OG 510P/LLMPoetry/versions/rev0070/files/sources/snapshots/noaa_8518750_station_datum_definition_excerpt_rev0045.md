Relevant excerpt summary: NOAA defines Station Datum as a fixed base elevation at a tide station to which all water-level measurements are referred. It is unique to each station, established lower than the water is expected to reach, referenced to the primary bench mark, held constant regardless of gauge or tide-staff changes, and the datum of tabulation is most often at the zero of the first tide staff installed.
