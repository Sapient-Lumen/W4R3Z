Relevant extracted/paraphrased source pressure:

- NOAA describes a datum as a base elevation used as a reference from which to reckon heights or depths.
- NOAA defines Station Datum as a fixed base elevation at a tide station.
- NOAA says Station Datum is held constant regardless of changes to the water-level gauge or tide staff.
- NOAA says the datum of tabulation is most often at the zero of the first tide staff installed.
