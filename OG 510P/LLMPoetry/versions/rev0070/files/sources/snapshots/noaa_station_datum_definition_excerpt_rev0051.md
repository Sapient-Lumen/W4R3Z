Relevant excerpt summary: NOAA defines Station Datum as a fixed base elevation at a tide station to which water-level measurements are referred. It says this datum is referenced to the primary bench mark, held constant regardless of gauge or tide-staff changes, and that the datum of tabulation is most often at the zero of the first tide staff installed.
