\begin{abstract}
This addendum repairs the evidentiary status of the tables that accompanied the anonymous-DHT dial sheet.
The former packet treated \(p_{\mathsf{obs}}=1-(1-\rho)^m\) as a contact-amplification proxy and then inserted it into an independent-erasure maximal-leakage formula. That chain silently required two separate independence assumptions: independence among contact-compromise events and independence of the reveal gate from the protected secret and revealed transcript.
Neither follows from \((\rho,m)\), a measured contact count, or equal reveal rates.
The revised packet exposes the full marginal correlation range, labels the old numbers as explicit control-model scenarios, and retains dummy-width tables only as deterministic regression checks for the proved independent-erasure model in Boss Fight~B.
No row is a deployment certificate.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Scope and status.}
Boss Fight~B owns the repaired channel boundary and exact erasure-model theorem. This addendum owns only sensitivity calculations under named assumptions. It does not export a public anonymity claim, a deployed observation probability, or an inference from contact telemetry to maximal leakage.\cite{series:bossfightA,series:bossfightB,series:traceifaces}

\paragraph{Fail-closed rule.}
A table row may be reused only with the complete scenario label. Removing the words ``independent contact events'' or ``independent erasure gate'' changes the mathematical claim and invalidates the row. Source-bound channel evidence belongs in Boss Fight~C or a successor deployment certificate, not in this table packet.\cite{series:bossfightC}

\section{Three objects that must not be conflated}\label{sec:objects}
For a lookup contacting \(m\) peers, distinguish:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item the marginal event probabilities \(\Pr(A_j)=\rho\), where \(A_j\) means contact \(j\) is adversarial or exposed;
\item the any-hit event \(H=\mathbf 1\{\cup_j A_j\}\);
\item the complete conditional transcript law \(P_{Y\mid C}\), including which transcript is revealed on \(H=1\), plus timing, retries, aborts, and stopping.
\end{enumerate}
The first object does not determine the second without dependence information. The second does not determine the third even when \(\Pr(H=1\mid C=c)\) is identical for every secret. Boss Fight~B supplies an explicit cyclic-reveal counterexample.

If \(A_1,\ldots,A_m\) are independent with equal marginal \(\rho\), then
\[
p_{\mathsf{hit}}^{\mathsf{ind}}=1-(1-\rho)^m.
\]
With equal marginals but arbitrary dependence, the sharp range is
\[
\rho\le p_{\mathsf{hit}}\le\min(1,m\rho).
\]
The lower endpoint is attained by perfectly coincident events; the upper endpoint is attained by disjoint or space-covering events. Thus the independent value is a scenario point, not a conservative bound.

\section{Representative routing scale is not an observer model}\label{sec:routing}
Kademlia-like systems expose routing parameters and implementation-dependent contact counts. These can help bound the size and shape of a potential transcript, but do not establish who observes it or how observation depends on the target.

The IPFS routing specification describes protocol behavior; implementation measurements describe behavior in a particular experiment. A privacy claim additionally needs a declared observer, public side information, extraction map, and complete conditional channel.\cite{ipfs_kad_spec,cortes2024kadlimits,probelab_rfm17,trautwein2024ipfs}

For uniformly sampling a committee of size \(s\) without replacement from a fixed population of \(N\) nodes containing exactly \(\rho N\) corrupt nodes, the exact hit probability is
\[
p_{\mathsf{hit}}
=1-\frac{\binom{(1-\rho)N}{s}}{\binom{N}{s}}.
\]
The familiar approximation \(1-(1-\rho)^s\) replaces this finite-population model by independent draws. Either expression remains only a committee-hit calculation. It does not prove that a corrupt member learns the declared transcript or that the hit gate is independent of the secret and transcript.

\section{Independent-erasure dummy-width regressions}\label{sec:dummy}
Under the exact control model in Boss Fight~B, a candidate set of size \(w+1\) containing the destination is exposed through an independent all-or-nothing gate with parameter \(p\). The maximal leakage is
\[
\MaxL=\logtwo\!\left((1-p)+p\frac{K}{w+1}\right),
\]
so
\[
w+1\ge\frac{pK}{2^b-(1-p)}.
\]
Every number below is conditional on that complete model.

\paragraph{Why the old chained table was invalid.}
The former packet first computed \(p=1-(1-\rho)^m\) and then treated that scalar as the gate in the erasure formula. Even if contact compromise were independent, the second step would still require the reveal decision to be independent of \((C,V)\) and the non-revealed transcript to be secret-independent. Boss Fight~B shows equal hit rates can instead leak one full bit in a three-secret example. Therefore the composition
\[
(\rho,m)\longmapsto p_{\mathsf{hit}}\longmapsto \MaxL
\]
is non-authorizing without an additional channel theorem.

\section{Takeaways}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item Contact marginals leave a wide dependence interval; the independent any-hit value is not conservative in either direction.
\item An any-hit event still does not define the transcript channel.
\item The dummy-width tables are useful regression fixtures once the erasure model is proved, not before.
\item Routing parameters and contact measurements help enumerate exposure surfaces but do not certify anonymity.
\item This addendum must remain on hold until any public-facing wording carries the complete control-model label.
\end{itemize}
