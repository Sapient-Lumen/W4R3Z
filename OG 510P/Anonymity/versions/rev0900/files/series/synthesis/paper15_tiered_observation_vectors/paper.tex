\begin{abstract}
Replacing one scalar observation probability by a vector of scalars does not repair an underspecified privacy model. Each tier must carry a source-bound channel witness, and the joint release must either satisfy a proved conditional-independence factorization or receive a direct joint-channel bound. This revision keeps the valid maximal-leakage composition theorem but withdraws the former receipt rule based only on pairs \((p_i,b_i)\). A two-tier one-time-pad construction makes the missing joint law explicit: each tier alone has zero maximal leakage, while the pair reveals the secret exactly. The repaired interface records per-tier channel mode and witness digests, a joint-dependence witness, adaptive-history and stopping semantics, and a fail-closed publication status.
\end{abstract}

\section{A tier is a channel object}\label{sec:tier}
Fix a protected secret \(C\) and declared witness projections \(Z^{(1)},\ldots,Z^{(m)}\). A tier record contains more than a name and two scalars:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item the projection/extractor ID and complete output alphabet or schema;
\item the observer, public side information, unit, horizon, retries, stopping, and reset semantics;
\item a channel mode and source-bound witness digest;
\item a channel-level bound \(\MaxL(C\to Z^{(i)})\le \ell_i\), or inputs that deterministically reproduce it;
\item any scalar gate parameter only as a field inside a proved common-mixture or independent-erasure witness;
\item drift invalidators and publication status.
\end{enumerate}

A vector of monitored rates \((p_1,\ldots,p_m)\) does not define the tier channels. A vector of valid marginal channel bounds \((\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_m)\) still does not define their joint law.

\section{Marginally harmless tiers can reveal jointly}\label{sec:synergy}
\begin{theorem}[Two zero-leakage tiers with one bit of joint leakage]\label{thm:synergy}
Let \(C\) and \(R\) be independent uniform bits. Define
\[
Z^{(1)}=R,
\qquad
Z^{(2)}=C\oplus R.
\]
Then
\[
\MaxL(C\to Z^{(1)})=\MaxL(C\to Z^{(2)})=0,
\]
but
\[
\MaxL(C\to (Z^{(1)},Z^{(2)}))=1\ \text{bit}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each marginal is a uniform bit independent of \(C\). The pair determines \(C=Z^{(1)}\oplus Z^{(2)}\), so its channel has two disjoint secret-indexed supports and leaks one bit.
\end{proof}

Therefore summing per-tier bounds without a joint assumption can understate leakage arbitrarily relative to the marginal values. The same issue appears when contact-set, timing, and congestion projections share randomness, state, retries, or path-selection decisions.

\section{The valid factorized composition theorem}\label{sec:composition}
\begin{definition}[Conditional factorization]
The released tiers factor conditionally on \(C\) when, for every secret in the relevant support and every joint output,
\[
P_{Z^{(1)},\ldots,Z^{(m)}\mid C=c}(z_1,\ldots,z_m)
=\prod_{i=1}^m P_{Z^{(i)}\mid C=c}(z_i).
\]
For adaptive use, this equality must hold conditional on every reachable pre-release history, with the remaining secret support and public state made explicit.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[MaxL subadditivity under conditional factorization]\label{lem:maxl-add}
If the factorization above holds and \(\MaxL(C\to Z^{(i)})\le\ell_i\), then
\[
\MaxL(C\to (Z^{(1)},\ldots,Z^{(m)}))\le\sum_{i=1}^m\ell_i.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For each joint output,
\[
\max_c\prod_iP(Z^{(i)}=z_i\mid c)
\le\prod_i\max_cP(Z^{(i)}=z_i\mid c).
\]
Summing factorizes into the product of the per-tier column-max sums; taking \(\log_2\) gives the claim.
\end{proof}

Conditional independence is a theorem input, not a natural default. Separate vantages, independent compromise marginals, or distinct field names do not prove it. Shared routing state, a common path selector, synchronized failures, or a single logging pipeline can violate it.

\section{Combining valid observation wrappers}\label{sec:wrappers}
Suppose tier \(i\) has a source-bound common-mixture witness with gate parameter \(p_i\) and pre-wrapper channel bound \(b_i\). Synthesis~11 then gives
\[
\ell_i\le\log_2\!\left((1-p_i)+p_i2^{b_i}\right).
\]
If, in addition, the released post-wrapper tiers satisfy Lemma~\ref{lem:maxl-add}'s conditional factorization, then
\[
\MaxL(C\to\mathbf Z)
\le\sum_{i=1}^m\log_2\!\left((1-p_i)+p_i2^{b_i}\right).
\]
Both layers are required: a valid wrapper for each tier and a valid joint factorization for the released vector. Independent gates alone do not establish independence of the underlying tier transcripts.

When factorization cannot be proved, the receipt must treat \(\mathbf Z\) as one joint observation and publish a direct joint bound. It may still list marginal health metrics, but they are non-authorizing.

\section{Receipt-facing structure}\label{sec:receipt}
A repaired vector uses a structure like:
\begin{verbatim}
obs_model:
  mode: tiered_channels
  tiers:
    - tier_id
      projection_id
      channel_mode
      channel_witness_id
      channel_witness_sha256
      source_config_sha256
      maxl_bound
      gate_parameter: optional
      assumption_evidence[]
  joint_dependence:
    mode: conditional_factorization | direct_joint_bound
    witness_id
    witness_sha256
    adaptive_history_rule
  combined_bound
  drift_invalidators[]
  publication_eligible
\end{verbatim}

Canonical human labels such as CSET (contacts), TIME (timing/length/abort), and CONG (congestion/loss) remain useful indexes, but the projection IDs and witnesses are binding. Path selection or fallback is another released witness unless proved secret-independent and absorbed into the joint channel.\cite{series:tieredpattern,series:fallbacktax}

\section{Hostile-review checks}\label{sec:audit}
A reviewer should:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item substitute the one-time-pad pair from Theorem~\ref{thm:synergy} and confirm that marginal-zero rows cannot authorize a zero joint bound;
\item delete one tier's channel witness and confirm publication failure;
\item preserve per-tier witnesses but correlate the gates or base transcripts and confirm the factorization witness fails;
\item add a visible fallback or abort bit and confirm it appears in the joint projection;
\item condition on a history that removes secrets and confirm all conditional bounds use the remaining support;
\item change a projection or source digest and confirm recertification rather than arithmetic-only refresh.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Takeaways}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item More scalars do not make a channel model complete.
\item Each tier needs a source-bound channel witness; each vector needs a joint-dependence witness or direct joint bound.
\item Marginal zero leakage can coexist with perfect joint disclosure.
\item Conditional factorization is sufficient for additive MaxL composition but must be proved for the released tiers and adaptive history.
\item Missing tier or joint evidence is publication-blocking.
\end{itemize}
