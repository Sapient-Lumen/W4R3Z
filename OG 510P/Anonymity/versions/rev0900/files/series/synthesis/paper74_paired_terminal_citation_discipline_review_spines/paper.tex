\title{Paired Terminal Citation Discipline for Stable Review Spines\\
\large One Answer-Side Stop and One Request-Side Capstone for Terse Papers}
\author{}
\date{Unified archive note v0.10 (2026-03-28; archive v724)}

\begin{abstract}
Two reusable terminal forms now exist for the worked release spine: an answer-side stop that ends at the review menu and a request-side capstone that ends at the closure verdict for the visible-refresh branch.
A terse later paper often needs both facts together: where the public checked-answer path currently stops, and where the adjacent source-only widening branch currently closes.
This note gives that paired reuse rule one home.
It does not create a new owner.
It only states when the already-owned answer-side and request-side terminal citation normal forms may travel as one stable paired citation discipline, and it packages that ordered pair as one fillable two-clause sentence later terse papers can quote without rephrasing either terminal form, together with one fail-closed first-reopen-owner map for when that paired shortcut has gone stale.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Non-redundancy note.}
Synthesis~54 owns the answer-side terminal citation normal form, Synthesis~73 owns the request-side terminal citation normal form, Synthesis~55 owns the wider theorem-to-review series spine that reaches both of them, Synthesis~17 owns the concrete worked instantiation, and Synthesis~76 owns the wider theorem-to-publication route that stops before this paired tail.\cite{series:challengeanswerreviewmenus,series:publicrequestresponsepacketrefreshresponsepacketclosureverdicts,series:seriesspines,series:workedexample,series:theoremtopublicationspine}
This note owns only the paired reuse rule for later terse papers that need both terminal forms at once.

\paragraph{Scope.}
This is not a new answer companion, not a new source-only packet, not a new closure verdict, not a new bridge stage, and not a theorem-to-publication route.
It is a very small citation-discipline note saying when one answer-side stop and one request-side capstone may be cited together without reopening the intervening owner layers.

\paragraph{Exports.}
This note exports (i) the paired terminal citation discipline, (ii) an answer-first rule, (iii) an adjacency rule, (iv) a paired terminal sentence normal form, (v) a paired-reuse lemma for later terse papers, and (vi) a compact first-reopen-owner map for stale paired-terminal shortcuts.

\paragraph{How later terse papers should cite this note.}
Later terse papers should cite Synthesis~74 only when the live issue is the paired discipline itself: namely that one already-valid answer-side terminal stop and one already-valid adjacent request-side terminal capstone may travel together as one ordered two-part reference, that this ordered pair should be rendered as one stable two-clause sentence rather than rephrased ad hoc, or that one stale paired shortcut should be routed mechanically to the first narrower or wider owner that regains authority. When the live issue is the answer-side terminal form alone, stop at Synthesis~54; when the live issue is the request-side terminal form alone, stop at Synthesis~73; when the live issue is whether a paper may stop, widen, or fail closed, widen to Synthesis~75; when the live issue is still which adjacent owner bucket, packet-local cut, or bounded source-only packet became live after leaving the answer-side archive cut, stop first at Synthesis~55 and then the first sufficient worked cut mirrored by Synthesis~17 rather than invoking the paired shortcut too early; when the live issue is one auditable concrete checked-answer / request-tail seam rather than the abstract paired wrapper, drop to the worked publication-boundary card in Synthesis~17; when the live issue is the wider theorem-to-review bridge or the concrete maintained route, stop at Synthesis~55 or Synthesis~17 instead; and when the live issue has widened back above the publication boundary into theorem/worked/release/notary/claim/release stop selection, fail closed to Synthesis~76 rather than stretching this paired tail into route choice.\cite{series:challengeanswerreviewmenus,series:publicrequestresponsepacketrefreshresponsepacketclosureverdicts,series:pairedterminalcutoverrule,series:seriesspines,series:workedexample,series:theoremtopublicationspine}

\section{Why the pair deserves its own note}
Once the answer-side and request-side terminal forms existed separately, terse papers still faced one repeated judgment call.
Sometimes the paper only needs the answer-side stop: public anchor, checked sentence, answer-side omitted-support cut, and review-menu handoff.
Sometimes it also needs to say what happens if that exact handoff widens one source-only step further and then runs to the visible-refresh closure capstone.
Without one paired citation rule, later notes either restitch both normal forms in prose or silently slide from one to the other without saying why that widening was justified.
So the archive benefits from one narrow note that says: cite the answer-side stop first, widen only when the adjacent source-only branch is genuinely the live question, and when you do widen, cite the already-owned request-side capstone rather than reopening the middle of the branch. When a later paper needs that same seam as one auditable concrete route rather than as an abstract paired wrapper, it should then drop to the worked publication-boundary card in Synthesis~17 instead of restating the pair again.

\begin{definition}[Paired terminal citation discipline]
Fix one audience/question answer whose checked-answer path already exports an answer-side terminal citation normal form in the sense of Synthesis~54, and fix the adjacent source-only widening branch whose visible-refresh successor path already exports a request-side terminal citation normal form in the sense of Synthesis~73.
The \emph{paired terminal citation discipline} for that branch is the ordered pair
\[
\Pi(a,q)=(A(a,q),R(a,q)),
\]
where \(A(a,q)\) is the four-anchor answer-side normal form
\[
\textsf{public-anchor} \to \textsf{checked-answer-card} \to \textsf{answer-disclosure-packet} \to \textsf{answer-review-menu},
\]
and \(R(a,q)\) is the four-anchor request-side normal form
\[
\textsf{public-request-notice} \to \textsf{bounded-request-packet} \to \textsf{successor-facing-request-packet} \to \textsf{closure-verdict}.
\]
So the pair records one stable paper-facing stop and one stable request-side capstone for the same maintained branch, without inventing any intermediate owner.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Answer-first rule]
A paired terminal citation discipline satisfies the \emph{answer-first rule} if a later paper cites the answer-side normal form alone whenever the live question is still answered by the public checked sentence, its answer-side omitted-support cut, or its review-menu handoff, and cites the request-side normal form only once the live question has widened beyond that answer-side archive cut into the adjacent source-only branch.
So the pair is not permission to skip directly to source-only machinery when the answer-side stop is still sufficient.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Adjacency rule]
A paired terminal citation discipline satisfies the \emph{adjacency rule} if the cited request-side normal form begins at the paper-level public request notice that is adjacent to the answer-side review-menu handoff for the same audience/question branch, and if its closure verdict ends on the exact visible-refresh successor-facing packet that still answers that same branch under the shipped follow-up taxonomy.
So the pair may not splice together unrelated answer-side and request-side families merely because each one has a terminal form. In particular, the pair may not be used to skip an earlier adjacent owner-bucket choice once the question has merely left the answer-side archive cut but has not yet reached one fixed request-side capstone.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Paired terminal sentence normal form]
A paired terminal citation discipline \(\Pi(a,q)=(A(a,q),R(a,q))\) exports a \emph{paired terminal sentence normal form} if it supplies exactly two clauses:
\begin{quote}\small
checked-answer clause: stop at \(A(a,q)\).\\
adjacent-request clause: if widened, the adjacent source-only branch closes at \(R(a,q)\).
\end{quote}
The second clause is only a rendering of the already-owned adjacent request-side capstone; it does not itself authorize widening, and it remains bound by the answer-first and adjacency rules.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Later terse papers may cite the paired terminal citation discipline]
Assume one branch has an answer-side terminal citation normal form satisfying the reuse conditions of Synthesis~54, and an adjacent request-side terminal citation normal form satisfying the reuse conditions of Synthesis~73.
Assume further that the pair satisfies the answer-first rule and the adjacency rule.
Then a later terse paper may cite the paired terminal citation discipline to say, in one compact move, where the checked-answer path currently stops and where the adjacent source-only widening branch currently closes, without reopening the imported publication-profile / citation-docket / carrier-slot / audit-trail / escalation-ladder owners on the answer side or the imported notice-family / selector / response-menu / carryforward owners on the request side.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
By the answer-side reuse conditions, the answer-side terminal form already preserves the compared public anchor, the exact checked sentence, the answer-side omitted-support cut, and the terminal referee-handoff owner without reopening its imported middle owners.
By the request-side reuse conditions, the request-side terminal form already preserves the opening source-only lineage, the first bounded omitted-support packet, the exact successor-facing visible-refresh packet, and the closure verdict without reopening its imported middle owners.
The answer-first rule prevents the pair from being used as an excuse to widen prematurely.
The adjacency rule prevents the pair from silently mixing different branches or different visible-refresh families.
So once both constituent normal forms are already valid, the pair adds no new semantic owner; it simply lets a later terse paper cite the stable answer-side stop and the stable request-side capstone together as one disciplined two-part reference, and render that already-owned pair through one stable two-clause sentence instead of restitching it in prose.\qedhere
\end{proof}

\section{Worked example pointer}
The maintained worked bundle now exposes the pair concretely.
Its answer-side terminal citation normal form is:
\begin{quote}\small
\path{example_compare_report.json}\\
$\to$ \path{auditor_compact_diff_summary_answer_card}\\
$\to$ \path{auditor_compact_diff_summary_answer_disclosure_packet}\\
$\to$ \path{auditor_compact_diff_summary_answer_review_menu}.
\end{quote}
Its adjacent request-side terminal citation normal form is:
\begin{quote}\small
\path{example_public_request_notice.json}\\
$\to$ \path{worked_example_receipt_interlock_successor_public_request_response_menu__request_scope_check}\\
$\to$ \path{worked_example_receipt_interlock_successor_public_request_response_menu__request_scope_check__delta__refresh_notice__selection__response_menu__packet_membership_check}\\
$\to$ \path{worked_example_receipt_interlock_successor_public_request_response_menu__request_scope_check__delta__refresh_notice__selection__response_menu__packet_membership_check__closure}.
\end{quote}
The first tuple gives the stable checked-answer stop.
The second gives the adjacent source-only widening capstone.
Later terse papers may therefore cite the pair to say both ``stop here for the checked answer'' and ``if widened, the adjacent public request branch closes here,'' without restitching the whole answer ladder or the whole source-only successor ladder.

\begin{remark}[What the pair does \emph{not} erase]
The paired discipline is intentionally conservative.
It does not erase the exact carrier slot and audit trail on the answer side, and it does not erase the response-menu, refresh-notice, selector, or carryforward owners on the request side.
It only says that later terse papers need not reopen those imported owners unless one of those owners has become the live question again.
\end{remark}
