## Narrow public claim

The paper publishes one optional companion to Certified Menus: a routing-signature manifest may reduce the effective many-testing menu only when the manifest records the transcript projection, contexts, quotient map, representatives, raw menu size, effective menu size, and either exact-equivalence evidence or transfer margins for every downstream SLA coordinate that consumes the reduced menu.

## Risk closed before publication

The risky bridge was approximate merging. Rev0880 made the bridge explicit: hash agreement is only an integrity pointer, not a proof of statistical interchangeability; exact identical tests give zero transfer cost; approximate rows must spend their recorded transfer margins; absent rows fall back to raw multiplicity.

## Known limits

This release does not claim deployed drift handling, wrapper refresh safety, or recertification without rechecking the manifest. Those remain downstream Certified-series work, not hidden prerequisites for this public head.
