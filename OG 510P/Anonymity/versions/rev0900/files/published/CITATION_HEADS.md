## Rev0898 alert-tax correction

The immutable public calibration head remains a citation target only with a second partial-claim withdrawal. Its alert table used one direction of the testing inequality and allowed a necessary utility lower bound to be read as a privacy-spend certificate. Do not reuse that table, the `4.31`-bit row for `(beta,alpha)=(0.01,0.05)`, or any inference that a lower bound below a cap proves the alert fits. For symmetric bit-unit stability, both directions are required; the necessary floor is `max(log2((1-beta-delta)/alpha), log2((1-alpha-delta)/beta))`. This screen may prove infeasibility but never sufficiency. A cap certificate needs a named mechanism upper bound and composition over every public release slot. The mutable State~3 and Eval~3 sources contain the repair; the immutable public snapshot and receipt are intentionally unchanged.
