# Review Order and Family Triage

This is the single review-priority surface. It combines the old order and family-triage notes so operators do not have to reconcile two overlapping documents.

## Main rule

Do **not** start with the paper touched most recently. Start with the smallest theorem-bearing source whose claim is both high-risk and capable of being completed with executable evidence. Prefer one repaired claim over another registry.

## Suggested order

1. **Standalone-series papers first**
   - `series/anonymity_series/`
   - `series/anondht_state_series/`
   - `series/bossfight_series/`
   - `series/certified_series/`
   - `series/congestion_series/`
   - `series/evaluation_series/`
   - `series/operational_series/`
   - `series/release_and_destination/`

   These are lower-regret review targets because they are less entangled with the actively splitting synthesis tail. This is a review-order judgment, not a publishability verdict.

2. **Early synthesis foundations second**
   - roughly `paper0` through `paper30`
   - use them to understand interfaces, schemas, crosswalks, and receipts
   - do not assume internal centrality makes them the best first public entrypoints

3. **Crosswalk and index notes for orientation, not default release units**
   - use them to find owners and dependencies
   - review their citation and dependency discipline before treating them as standalone claims

4. **Late synthesis tail last**
   - especially the `paper65` through `paper75` successor/reissue/challenge-answer zone and repeatedly refreshed worked-example surfaces
   - default posture is defer/hold unless a decision records a concrete stabilization reason

## Existing public backbone

The five Mathematics-era canonical wiki links and seven post-policy Anonymity heads are already public. They are context, not evidence that adjacent unpublished sources are ready.

## Operational takeaway

A productive turn normally does one of three things: closes a theorem/evidence gap in one standalone source, repairs a concrete verifier failure, or shrinks a duplicated hot-path surface while preserving its full source of truth. A doctrine-only note is not forward momentum.
