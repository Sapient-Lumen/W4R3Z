## Subject
Freeze/publication compile-witness depth and rebuild-helper isolation.

## Decision
No publication is authorized. No queue item is moved.

## Reason
rev0829 correctly made the Candidate/Published-ready queue compile gate a three-pass final-clean TeX witness, but the freeze/publication path still accepted a two-pass `pdflatex` witness. That left a risky asymmetry: the broad queue lane was stricter than the actual freeze evidence used by the next-release machinery. The rebuild controller also still ran dozens of helper programs inside one long-lived Python interpreter, which is fragile in this cloudtainer because module state, signal handlers, and child-process behavior can leak across checks.

## Changes accepted
- `publishing/release_preflight.py` now runs three `pdflatex` passes for compile evidence and records `minimum_required_passes`, final unresolved-warning counts, and final rerun-warning counts.
- `publishing/build_freeze_compile_witness.py` now refuses short `pdflatex` witnesses and rebuilt the Certified Menus freeze witness with three passes and zero final-warning debt.
- Freeze packet, publication-decision, and guarded-publisher checks now reject short `pdflatex` witnesses or final rerun-warning debt.
- `publishing/rebuild_archive_surfaces.py` now runs helpers as isolated subprocess process groups with timeout cleanup instead of sharing one in-process `runpy` interpreter.
- No report/schema surface was added; no publication and no queue movement.

## Follow-up
Keep the queue-wide compile smoke and the freeze witness aligned. The next high-value work remains reducing or externalizing large derivable worked-example artifacts rather than adding more registry surfaces.
