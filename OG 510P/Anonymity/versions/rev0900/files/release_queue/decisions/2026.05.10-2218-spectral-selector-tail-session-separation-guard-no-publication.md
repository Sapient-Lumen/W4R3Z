# Decision: spectral selector, tail-coarsening, pairwise/session, and separation-ratio guard; no publication

- Subject: residual spectral witness seams around selector visibility, quantile/tail reuse under coarsening, pairwise/session support conditions, and separation-distance misuse for upper posterior-ratio claims.
- Scope: `published/2026-01-23_spectral_anonymity/paper.tex`, its source/raw supplement, Paper~D, Synthesis~24, compact reentry surfaces, and the queue/citation/operator guardrails that consume exact spectral witnesses.
- State before: rev0772 correctly added observation-channel coarsening monotonicity for expected posterior chi-squared leakage, but four receipt-side ambiguities remained: exact rich-channel quantiles could be mistaken for target-scope quantiles after coarsening, independent hopcount/schedule/retry selectors could be hidden or visible without a serialized selector scope, average posterior chi-squared could still be overread as a pairwise/session support-safe bound, and separation distance could be misread as an upper odds/PML/MaxL cap rather than a lower-coverage diagnostic.
- Decision: add a tail-certificates-under-coarsening guard, serialize hidden-versus-visible selector scope for independent branch/length/schedule choices, require pairwise/session claims to carry pairwise law plus support / absolute-continuity evidence rather than relying on average posterior chi-squared alone, and route upper posterior-ratio claims to max-relative-density, pointwise, tail-converted, or stronger evidence instead of separation distance.
- State after: the spectral paper now states that rich-channel quantiles are not automatically target-scope quantiles after coarsening and must be recomputed or accompanied by a coarsening-contamination certificate. The supplement now includes `--tail-postprocess-sanity`, `--support-sanity`, `--selector-sanity`, `--ratio-separation-sanity`, and `--pairwise-session-sanity` raw anchors. Paper~D and Synthesis~24 now route `chi2-general` cards through `prior_scope`, `observation_scope`, `selector_scope`, endpoint-kind, support policy, pairwise/session evidence, and separation-vs-upper-ratio checks before exporting PML or MaxL semantics.
- Evidence / checks: `spectral_reconstruction.py --tail-postprocess-sanity` constructs a finite counterexample for quantile reuse under coarsening; `--support-sanity` verifies positive-marginal trimming for zero-marginal observation columns; `--selector-sanity` separates hidden selector mixtures from visible selector channels; `--ratio-separation-sanity` shows that small separation distance is not an upper relative-density/MaxL cap; `--pairwise-session-sanity` records support-mismatch and pairwise KL/Pinsker requirements; `--check-raw` and `--archive-guard` enforce the same boundaries across paper, supplement, Paper~D, Synthesis~24, and compact operator surfaces.
- Archive hygiene: to preserve the hard 700-file cap while adding this decision note, this pass prunes the superseded worked-bundle micro-decision note `release_queue/decisions/2026.03.23-2330-oddsa-workedbundle-primarypath-rootcarrier-no-publication.md`; later worked-bundle surfaces and decision history still carry the maintained odds/primary-path routing rationale.
