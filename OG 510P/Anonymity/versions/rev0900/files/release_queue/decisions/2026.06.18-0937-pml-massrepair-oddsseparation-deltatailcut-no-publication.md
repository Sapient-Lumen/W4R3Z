- Concrete correction: if `p=pi(i)` and `m=pi(i|o)/pi(i)`, then the true one-vs-complement odds factor is `m(1-p)/(1-pm)` while `pm<1`. A PML cap `epsilon` yields a uniform finite true-odds cap only with `p <= p_max` and `2^epsilon p_max < 1`. Deterministic revelation of a uniform binary identity has exactly one bit of PML but infinite true odds. For the illustrative `N=4096`, `epsilon=0.75` card, the mass factor is `1.681792830507`, while the guarded true-odds factor is `1.682072885510` (`0.750240220031` bits).
- Delta correction: for `P1=(0.51,0.49)`, `P2=(0.49,0.51)`, and uniform mixture `P=(0.5,0.5)`, the identity-vs-mixture hockey-stick slack at epsilon zero is `0.01`, but the positive-loss event has probability `0.51`. A loss-tail bound implies a hockey-stick bound; the converse does not hold. Multi-step hockey-stick slack is therefore composed with the explicit suffix-amplified formula rather than by silently summing alleged bad-event probabilities.
- TV correction: common-reference TV gives the singleton deviation `|P(o)-Q(o)| <= delta`, so the finite ratio bound is `(Q_min+delta)/(Q_min-delta)` under `delta < Q_min`. The old doubled-deviation expression was unnecessarily loose. At `Q_min=0.10`, `delta=0.01`, the corrected cap is `0.289506617195` bits rather than `0.584962500721` bits.
