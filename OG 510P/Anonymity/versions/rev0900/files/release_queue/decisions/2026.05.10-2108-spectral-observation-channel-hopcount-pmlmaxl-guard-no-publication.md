# Decision: spectral observation-channel/hopcount/PML-MaxL guard; no publication

- Subject: spectral observation-channel scope, visible-versus-hidden hopcount, and PML/MaxL receipt semantics.
- Scope: `published/2026-01-23_spectral_anonymity/paper.tex`, its source/raw supplement, Paper~D, Synthesis~24, compact reentry surfaces, and the queue/citation/operator guardrails that consume exact spectral witnesses.
- State before: rev0770 closed prior-scope, strong-stationary-time visibility, and exact weighted quantile seams, but the exact witness could still be read too narrowly as a kernel/prior object rather than as a witness for the observer's declared channel. Hidden randomized length and visible hopcount also needed a receipt-facing fail-closed boundary, and the PML-versus-channel-level-MaxL split needed to survive the downstream crosswalks.
- Decision: generalize the prior-aware identity to a finite observation-channel identity; state visible-hopcount leakage as the revealed-length average and hidden-hopcount leakage as the mixture-discriminant value; require `chi2-general` receipts to serialize both `prior_scope` and `observation_scope`; and keep high-probability PML envelopes distinct from channel-level MaxL unless the witness is pointwise or the failure-mass fallback is applied.
- State after: the spectral paper now gives a declared-channel posterior/Frobenius proposition, an explicit visible-versus-hidden randomized-length chi-squared proposition, and stronger design guidance for hopcount/stop-time/latency/transcript visibility. The supplement/raw anchors now include observation-channel sanity checks, biased-prior channel checks, and a stop-time example with zero delegate-only leakage but positive visible-stop-time leakage. Paper~D and Synthesis~24 now treat hidden-hopcount, visible-hopcount, delegate-only, prior-aware, and richer transcript witnesses as distinct theorem-side scopes before receipt conversion.
- Evidence / checks: `spectral_reconstruction.py --observation-channel-sanity` verifies hidden versus visible hopcount under stationary and biased priors; `--random-length-sanity` verifies the mixture-discriminant value, the revealed-length average, and the convexity ordering; `--stop-time-sanity` verifies delegate-only perfection can fail when the stop time is visible; `--tail-sanity` preserves PML/MaxL failure-mass semantics; `--check-raw` validates the shipped anchors; `--archive-guard` checks the repaired prose and downstream guardrails.
