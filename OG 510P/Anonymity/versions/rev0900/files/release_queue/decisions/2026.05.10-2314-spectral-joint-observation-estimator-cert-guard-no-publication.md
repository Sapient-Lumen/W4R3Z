# Decision: spectral joint-observation and estimator-certification guard; no publication

- Subject: residual spectral receipt seams around jointly visible observation channels and stochastic trace/SVD estimator reuse.
- Scope: `published/2026-01-23_spectral_anonymity/paper.tex`, the shipped spectral supplement, Paper D's spectral-delegation receipt bridge, Synthesis 24's math-backbone routing note, and the operator/control surfaces that summarize these boundaries.
- State before: rev0774 had closed prior-minimum and adaptive-selector seams, but two reuse hazards remained. First, a certificate for one observation component could still be over-read as a certificate for a jointly visible transcript assembled from delegate, hopcount, selector identity, retry metadata, or another side channel. Second, Hutchinson/randomized-SVD point estimates could still be mistaken for certified upper bounds.
- Decision: keep the spectral repair in no-publication archive-maintenance mode and add two fail-closed guards. A jointly visible transcript is a new observation channel: use the product/full observation channel or an evaluator-certified composition bound, not the maximum or reuse of individual component witnesses. A stochastic trace/SVD output is estimate-only unless an `estimator_certification` field records the estimator family, sample count, seed/replicate policy, target confidence, one-sided UCB or deterministic margin, and the final certified upper bound.
- State after: the spectral paper states the joint-observation channel identity and estimator-certification guard; the supplement has executable sanity checks for both failure modes; Paper D and Synthesis 24 propagate `joint_observation_scope` / product-full-channel and `estimator_certification` requirements; START_HERE, CONTEXT_PACK, patch notes, and receipts carry the same operator warning.
- Evidence / checks: `spectral_reconstruction.py --joint-observation-sanity` demonstrates that two individually modest observation-channel witnesses can have a larger jointly visible expected chi-squared value; `spectral_reconstruction.py --estimator-certification-sanity` demonstrates that a Hutchinson point estimate can understate the exact Frobenius value and therefore understate the derived Markov tail if misused. `--check-raw` and `--archive-guard` require both guards in the shipped anchors and archive surfaces.
- Archive hygiene: to preserve the hard 700-file cap while adding this decision note, prune the superseded worked-bundle micro-decision `release_queue/decisions/2026.03.23-2358-releaseb-workedbundle-fallbackvector-packet-no-publication.md`. The maintained queue/index surfaces retain the relevant historical posture.
