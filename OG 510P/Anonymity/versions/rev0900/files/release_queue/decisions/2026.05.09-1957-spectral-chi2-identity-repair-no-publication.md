# Decision: spectral expected-chi-squared identity repair; no publication

- Subject: `published/2026-01-23_spectral_anonymity/paper.tex`, Anonymity~D, Synthesis~21, Synthesis~24, and the published / queue control surfaces that route spectral witnesses.
- State before: the legacy spectral head stated the exact directed-chain expected-chi-squared leakage as a sum of one-step singular values raised to the `2t` power. That equality is only valid when the discriminant operator is normal, in particular for reversible chains. For directed/non-normal chains the exact quantity is the squared Frobenius norm of the t-step discriminant operator with the stationary direction removed.
- Decision: repair the canonical spectral statement in place; route exact leakage through $\|\mathcal D^t\|_F^2-1$ and the singular values of $\mathcal D^t$; retain $\sum_{i\ge2}\sigma_i(\mathcal D)^{2t}$ only as the normal/reversible specialization; reprove the conservative $(n-1)\sigma_2^{2t}$ bound by contraction on the stationary-orthogonal subspace; update Paper~D and the synthesis/control surfaces so `chi2-general` witnesses require true t-step posterior/Frobenius evidence.
- State after: the five legacy Mathematics public citation heads remain the only approved public heads; Paper~D remains Published-ready because its live `spectral-uniform` route uses the surviving conservative contraction bound; exact `chi2-general` cards must fail closed if they were computed from one-step singular powers without a normal/reversible certificate; Synthesis~24 is the maintained paper-facing crosswalk for the corrected legacy-root semantics.
- Evidence / checks: the repair was motivated by a direct non-normal countercheck on lazy de Bruijn walks and a reversible hypercube sanity check. The local archive repair also searched the active paper/control surfaces for old exact one-step-power usage and replaced it with the t-step Frobenius rule or an explicit normal/reversible side condition.
