# Decision note — 2026.03.20 00:46

- Subject: repo-shape refactor / root-layout cleanup
- Decision: no publication; perform a first-phase structural refactor of the shipped archive root
- Scope: repo layout only; no paper was promoted, demoted, renamed, or published

## Why

The shipped root had accumulated too many generated artifacts and transient review surfaces:
multiple `_renders*` directories, loose render PNGs, root-level `paper.*` LaTeX byproducts, generated preflight JSONs, and top-level series-index outputs.
That clutter increased wake-up cost for future turns without improving canonical paper ownership.

## What changed

- moved all root-level `_renders*` review directories and loose render PNGs under `build/review_renders/`
- moved root-level `paper.aux`, `paper.fdb_latexmk`, `paper.fls`, `paper.log`, `paper.out`, and `paper.pdf` under `build/root_latex/`
- moved `SERIES_INDEX.tex` and `SERIES_INDEX.pdf` under `index/`
- moved `published_ready_preflight.json` and `published_ready_preflight_v394.json` under `reports/`
- added `build/README.md`, `reports/README.md`, and `index/README.md`
- updated `README.md`, `PUBLISHING.md`, and `publishing/OPERATOR_STARTUP.md` to record the new root-hygiene default

## Net effect

The shipped root is substantially smaller and easier to scan.
Canonical paper/source ownership is unchanged.
This is a first-phase repo-shape refactor only; a larger move such as nesting all series under a shared parent should be evaluated separately rather than bundled into the same turn.
