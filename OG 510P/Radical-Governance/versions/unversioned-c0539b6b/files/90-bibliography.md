# Bibliography (Compact Anchors)

This list is intentionally short: it’s an anchor set, not a literature dump.

## Core governance norms
- UN Universal Declaration of Human Rights (rights baseline): https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights
- ICCPR (political participation rights): https://www.ohchr.org/en/instruments-mechanisms/instruments/international-covenant-civil-and-political-rights
- Venice Commission Updated Rule of Law Checklist (2025) (rule-of-law rubric): https://www.venice.coe.int/webforms/documents/default.aspx?pdffile=CDL-AD%282025%29002-e
- World Justice Project rule of law overview (operational definition): https://worldjusticeproject.org/about-us/overview/what-rule-law
- UN Basic Principles on Remedy and Reparation (A/RES/60/147) (rights enforcement baseline): https://www.ohchr.org/en/instruments-mechanisms/instruments/basic-principles-and-guidelines-right-remedy-and-reparation

## Transparency, integrity, and public finance
- OECD Recommendation on Open Government (2017): https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/en/instruments/OECD-LEGAL-0438
- OECD Recommendation on Public Integrity (2017): https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/en/instruments/OECD-LEGAL-0435
- OECD Recommendation on Transparency and Integrity in Lobbying (updated 2024): https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/en/instruments/OECD-LEGAL-0379
- UN Convention Against Corruption (UNCAC): https://www.unodc.org/documents/brussels/UN_Convention_Against_Corruption.pdf
- IMF Fiscal Transparency Code (2019): https://www.imf.org/external/np/fad/trans/Code2019.pdf
- OECD Principles for Independent Fiscal Institutions (2014): https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/public/doc/301/301.en.pdf
- PEFA 2016 Framework (PFM assessment): https://www.pefa.org/sites/default/files/PEFA_2016_Framework_Final_WEB_0.pdf
- World Bank *Intergovernmental Fiscal Transfers: Principles and Practice* (multi-level finance design): https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/entities/publication/141ce28b-a090-5310-9f25-88fe04bde211
- Open Budget Survey (budget transparency/participation/oversight measurement): https://internationalbudget.org/open-budget-survey/

## Regulation, competition, and public ownership

- OECD Guidelines on Corporate Governance of State-Owned Enterprises (2024) (SOE ownership + disclosure standard): https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2024/06/oecd-guidelines-on-corporate-governance-of-state-owned-enterprises-2024_68fa05cd/18a24f43-en.pdf
