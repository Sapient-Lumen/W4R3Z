# National Government (Nation-State)

**Typical scale:** ~1,000,000–1,500,000,000 residents  
**Primary advantage:** rights protection + redistribution capacity + national security + macro policy  
**Primary risk:** authoritarian drift; polarization; capture of courts/oversight

## What the national level must own (and nothing else)
- constitutional rights and the rule-of-law backbone
- macroeconomic stabilization and national fiscal policy
- national defense and foreign affairs (with hard constraints)
- national standards for equal citizenship (anti-discrimination; due process)
- redistribution and equalization across regions where needed

## Minimum Viable Government (MVG)
- national standards + independent oversight for coercive institutions (`SAFE-1/2/4/5`; see `05-public-safety-and-coercion.md`)
- constitutional charter with justiciable rights (`LAW-1`)
- independent courts + constitutional review (`LAW-2`)
- legislature with competitive elections and fair representation (`DEC-1`, `DEC-5`)
- professional civil service with leadership/capability baseline (`CAP-1`, `CAP-6`; see `09-public-service-and-state-capacity.md`)
- readable national accounts + execution transparency (`CAP-2`) and fiscal risk disclosure (`CAP-3`; see `07-fiscal-and-budgetary-governance.md`)
- independent fiscal institution or equivalent costing/forecast office (`CAP-4`)
- predictable intergovernmental transfers/equalisation where multi-level (`CAP-5`)
- SOE governance + consolidated disclosure of state support/guarantees where the state owns operators (`CAP-8`, `CAP-3`; see `13-regulation-utilities-and-soes.md`)
- independent audit + anticorruption capacity (`ACC-1/2/4`)
- open government baseline (FOI, contracting transparency) (`OPEN-1/2`)
- regulatory policy baseline (public rulemaking, RIA/ex-post review) + independent regulators for high-discretion sectors (`CAP-7`; see `13-regulation-utilities-and-soes.md`)
- CRVS + legal identity baseline with non-exclusion design and strong privacy/oversight; publish status decision rules + appeal paths (`IOP-6`, `LAW-5`; see `12-identity-and-recognition.md`)
- national ecological governance baseline: environmental regulator + inventories/registries + SEEA-linked accounts (`OPEN-6`; see `11-commons-and-ecological-governance.md`)
- effective remedy stack (ombuds/tribunals/courts) (`LAW-5`, `LAW-3`; see `08-remedy-and-grievance.md`)
- baseline rights + remedy standards for automated decisions in high-stakes domains (`IOP-5`; see `06-digital-and-algorithmic-governance.md`)
- emergency powers protocol with sunsets and review (`SAFE-3`)
## Ideal institutional stack (bounded)
### A) Bicameralism as a “people + places” compromise
- Chamber 1: proportional representation of people.
- Chamber 2: regional representation to protect federated units.
- Use joint committees for cross-cutting long-horizon issues.

### B) Integrity architecture as a first-class system
- Whole-of-government public integrity strategy (`ACC-4`) aligned with UNCAC (https://www.unodc.org/documents/brussels/UN_Convention_Against_Corruption.pdf).
- Influence transparency (lobbying and political finance disclosure) (`ACC-5`).

### C) Constitutional maintenance without permanent constitutional crisis
- Periodic constitutional review via citizens’ assembly + legislative response (`DEC-2`).
- Rule-of-law health checks using a stable checklist method (`LAW-4`).
- Long-horizon review trigger for high-impact laws/plans (Future Council/Commission) (`DEC-6`).

### D) Fiscal federalism with transparency
- Clear assignment of mandates and revenues (avoid unfunded mandates).
- Predictable transfers and equalization (formula-based) with public reporting.

## Top failure modes + countermeasures
- **Authoritarian drift / emergency normalization:** strict `SAFE-3`, court review (`LAW-2`), open reporting (`OPEN-1`).
- **Capture of oversight:** budget formula protections for auditors; transparent appointments (`ACC-1/4/5`).
- **Epistemic failure:** independent statistics + information integrity + evaluation loops (`OPEN-4/5`, `03-metrics-and-evidence.md`).

## Interfaces upward/downward
- Downward: constitutional protection of local self-organization where feasible.
- Upward: treaty compliance, mutual recognition regimes, and cross-border coordination (see `50-supranational.md`).

## Success metrics (minimal set)
- rights and rule-of-law health (case timelines; compliance with judgments)
- integrity risk measures (procurement competitiveness; audit closure rates)
- fiscal sustainability (debt, contingent liabilities, off-book exposures)
- trust and participation breadth
