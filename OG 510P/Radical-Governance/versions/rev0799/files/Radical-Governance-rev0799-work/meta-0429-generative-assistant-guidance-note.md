# Meta 0429 — Generative-assistant guidance and chatbot case-packet note

This meta note records the rev0728 pass. The revision adds `876` as a general generative public-service assistant / guidance-liability docket, `877` as the applied NYC MyCity Chatbot negative-control case, and `878` as the applied GOV.UK Chat positive-control case.

The reason for the pass is that the archive had just added `874` and `875` for model-mediated decisions, but public-facing AI assistants can shape behavior before they make formal decisions. The new pass therefore separates search, guidance, task triage, compliance instruction, decision support, and legal effect. The core rule is that a chatbot answer cannot become public law, and a disclaimer cannot substitute for source grounding, reliance controls, evaluation, incident correction, accessibility testing, and withdrawal or handoff records.

The new machine-readable layer is `metadata/generative_assistant_tests.json`, rendered into `generated/GENERATIVE_ASSISTANT_TESTS.json` and `generated/GENERATIVE_ASSISTANT_TESTS.md`.
