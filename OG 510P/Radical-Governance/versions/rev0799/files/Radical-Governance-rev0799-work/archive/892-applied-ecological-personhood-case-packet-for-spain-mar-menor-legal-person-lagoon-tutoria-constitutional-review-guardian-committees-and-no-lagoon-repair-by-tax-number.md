# 892 — Applied ecological-personhood case packet for Spain Mar Menor: legal-person lagoon, Tutoría, constitutional review, guardian committees, and no lagoon repair by tax number

## One-line thesis

The Mar Menor arrangement is best classified as a statutory ecological legal-personhood and public-action waist: Law 19/2022 recognizes the lagoon and basin as a rights-bearing legal person, while the implementation proof lies in the Tutoría organs, scientific indicators, citizen and public representation, standing, administrative duties, contract / tax-number capacity, and whether ecological restoration follows rather than merely constitutional survival.

## Why this matters

Mar Menor tests a different route from Atrato and Taranaki Maunga. It is a European statutory legal-personhood model born from citizen initiative, severe lagoon degradation, public-rights-of-nature advocacy, and subsequent constitutional review. It shows how ecological personhood can move from campaign to statute to constitutional doctrine to implementing decree.

That makes it valuable and dangerous. Valuable because the statute gives the lagoon rights, representation, standing, and public duties. Dangerous because a legal person can acquire committees, procedural capacity, and a tax number without proving ecological recovery. The archive must therefore separate recognition, representation, litigation capacity, scientific monitoring, public administration duties, and actual restoration.

Mar Menor is not a mere symbol. It is also not a repaired lagoon.

### 5. Ecological-personhood docket

This packet activates all ecological-personhood tests:

1. **Subject boundary** — lagoon, basin, catchment, aquifers, wetlands, and interacting systems.
2. **Rights catalog** — existence, natural evolution, protection, conservation, maintenance, restoration.
3. **Guardian / Tutoría spine** — Committee of Representatives, Monitoring Commission, Scientific Committee, management support, decision rules.
4. **Citizen and sector representation** — municipalities, economic sectors, environmental groups, fishers, farmers, and residents without capture by one lane.
5. **Scientific indicators** — annual socio-ecological assessment, risk indicators, restoration indicators, publication and independence.
6. **Public-owner boundary** — ordinary state, regional, local, and EU-law duties remain with public bodies.
7. **Public-action route** — standing is broad but must not become litigation theater.
8. **Budget and operating capacity** — fiscal identity, contracting, personnel, and support must be tied to public records.
9. **Restoration proof** — nutrient load, water quality, biodiversity, land-use correction, and enforcement records.
10. **Failure route** — what happens when indicators worsen, committees stall, or public authorities ignore obligations.

### 6. Opposition and rival readings

**Rival 1: legal personhood finally gives the lagoon a voice.** The archive accepts the gain but asks whether the voice can move permits, budgets, land use, enforcement, and restoration.

**Rival 2: personhood is legally incoherent.** The archive does not need to settle the jurisprudential debate. It asks whether the enacted form has usable public-law records and remedies.

**Rival 3: constitutional validation proves the model.** The archive rejects the inference. Constitutional validity is not ecological performance.

**Rival 4: public action and citizen guardians solve enforcement.** The archive treats them as routes that still need funding, standing discipline, scientific evidence, and administrative follow-through.

**Rival 5: the implementing decree completes the governance.** The archive says implementation begins there; it does not end there.

### 7. Capture and theater channels

- **tax-number repair**: fiscal / contracting capacity is treated as ecological progress.
- **committee theater**: organs exist but lack public minutes, indicators, or effect.
- **indicator capture**: scientific indicators are nonbinding, late, or politically softened.
- **citizen-lane capture**: citizen representatives are selected through opaque or elite-dominated processes.
- **public-action overload**: broad standing creates litigation volume without restoration capacity.
- **constitutional-survival laundering**: judicial validation is treated as proof that the lagoon is recovering.
- **ordinary-duty evaporation**: existing environmental regulators hide behind the lagoon's personhood.

### 9. Holding

Mar Menor is a statutory ecological legal-personhood and public-action waist. The rule is: **no lagoon repair by tax number**. Legal personality, constitutional validation, Tutoría organs, and fiscal capacity are real governance surfaces, but ecological success requires indicators, enforcement, restoration budgets, public records, and accountable public authorities.

## Anti-theater tests

This packet fails if legal personality, broad standing, committee creation, fiscal identity, or constitutional survival is scored as ecological repair. It passes only when the lagoon's rights are tied to independent indicators, public duties, restoration money, action records, enforcement, and visible rights-to-remedy routes.
