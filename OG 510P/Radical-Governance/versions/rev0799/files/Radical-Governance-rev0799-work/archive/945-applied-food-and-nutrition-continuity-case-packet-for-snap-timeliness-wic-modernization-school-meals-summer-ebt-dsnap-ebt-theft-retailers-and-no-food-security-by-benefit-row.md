# 945 — Applied food and nutrition continuity case packet for SNAP timeliness, WIC modernization, school meals, Summer EBT, D-SNAP, EBT theft, retailers, and no food security by benefit row

## One-line thesis

USDA/FNA/ERS food-security statistics, SNAP data and timeliness tables, WIC data and modernization pages, child-nutrition tables, Summer EBT/SUN Bucks materials, disaster-assistance/D-SNAP guidance, EBT modernization, stolen-benefit dashboards, and retailer data should be read as separate evidence lanes. They cannot be collapsed into proof that a specific household, infant, student, survivor, older adult, disabled person, or rural/tribal resident received timely, usable, nutritious food.

## Why this matters

This applied packet converts the food/nutrition docket into an operational case surface. The sources are strong enough to show scale, program structure, clocks, and vulnerable transition points, but each source has a narrow lane. SNAP data can show participation and issuance; timeliness materials can show processing standards and state performance; WIC tables can show participation/costs; child-nutrition tables can show meals and reimbursements; Summer EBT materials can show benefit structure and implementation plans; disaster guidance can show waiver authority; EBT pages can show security modernization and stolen-benefit replacement limits. None of those alone proves food security.

The governing repair phrase is **no food security by benefit row**.

## Pattern pack

- **No food security by benefit row.** Program participation and issuance data are denominators, not proof of meals or groceries.
- **No timely food by SNAP data table.** SNAP participation, household, or benefit data must be joined to application and recertification clocks.
- **No WIC nutrition by data table.** WIC participation and cost data must be joined to appointment, eWIC, food package, formula, breastfeeding, and redemption evidence.
- **No child meal by child-nutrition table.** NSLP/SBP/CACFP/SFSP tables must be joined to school/service-day and child-level receipt evidence.
- **No summer nutrition by SUN Bucks headline.** The benefit amount and program plan must be joined to child matching, card delivery, spending, expiration, and appeals.
- **No disaster nutrition by D-SNAP approval.** Disaster authority must be joined to survivor proof, power, retailers, transport, shelter, hot foods, and replacement receipts.
- **No household security by EBT modernization.** Chip cards, dashboards, and theft guidance must be joined to household loss, claim route, replacement authority, and card state.
- **No grocery access by retailer row.** Retailer authorization must be joined to distance, stock, payment uptime, online access, disability access, and price.

## Source-to-duty map

| Source surface | What it proves | What it cannot prove alone |
|---|---|---|
| ERS food-security statistics | National/household food-security denominator and child food-insecurity context. | Which household missed food or was repaired. |
| SNAP participation/data tables | Participation, households, benefits, costs, and program activity. | Timely eligibility, card receipt, retailer access, theft repair, or hunger outcome. |
| SNAP application and recertification timeliness | Processing-clock definitions and state performance lanes. | Household-level food receipt, notice comprehension, or appeal success. |
| EBT modernization and stolen-benefit surfaces | Card-security posture, chip transition, replacement authority, and reported stolen-benefit data. | A secure household account or replacement of a specific stolen benefit. |
| WIC data and modernization | Participation, category/cost data, modernization funding and tools. | Appointment availability, eWIC redemption, formula availability, or nutrition service. |
| Child-nutrition tables | Program participation, meals served, and costs for school/child nutrition programs. | Whether a particular child ate, received special diets, or avoided summer gaps. |
| Summer EBT/SUN Bucks materials | Program benefit structure and implementation/oversight planning. | Child matching, card activation, spending, expiration, or appeal completion. |
| Disaster assistance / D-SNAP | Federal disaster food authority and state waiver route. | Survivor food receipt, open retailers, power, transport, or replacement completion. |

## Applied continuity ladder

1. **Classify the food route.** SNAP, WIC, school meal, CACFP, SFSP, Summer EBT, D-SNAP, replacement, retailer, charitable distribution, or household cooking.
2. **Bind the participant.** Household members, student, infant, pregnant/postpartum participant, older adult, disabled person, homeless/custody/care status, tribal/rural edge, language, and representative/proxy.
3. **Separate source surfaces.** Mark every source as statistic, data table, timeliness report, guidance, plan, dashboard, waiver, retailer data, or live case record.
4. **Prove eligibility and clocks.** Application, expedited service, recertification, verification, direct certification, Summer EBT child match, WIC certification, and appeal dates.
5. **Prove issuance and spendability.** Benefit amount, EBT/eWIC card and PIN, chip/card state, benefit availability date, online purchasing, replacement, stolen-benefit claim, and transaction evidence.
6. **Prove food/meal receipt.** Groceries, formula, WIC food package, meals served to the child, special diet, summer meals, or disaster food actually reached the person.
7. **Prove retailer and delivery access.** Authorized store, stock, distance, transport, disability access, online/delivery, farmers market, rural/tribal route, and POS/processor uptime.
8. **Prove disaster or emergency continuity.** Power loss, destroyed food, hot-food purchase, D-SNAP eligibility, USDA Foods, shelter/mobile distribution, and replacement receipts.
9. **Prove remedy and learning.** Denial correction, card replacement, stolen-benefit replacement if legally available, appeal outcome, complaint, after-action fix, and residual gap.
10. **Close source-currentness.** Identify whether new FNA/USDA reorganization pages, SNAP timeliness data, WIC updates, Summer EBT planning, or replacement authority changed after the packet was created.

## Applied examples

### SNAP data and timeliness

Use SNAP data tables for participation and issuance context, then check application and recertification timeliness, expedited access, verification, notice, EBT card state, retailer access, and appeal route. The data table is a denominator, not proof that the household bought food.

### WIC

Use WIC data tables and modernization materials to identify participation, category, cost, and eWIC/remote-service posture. Then demand clinic appointment, nutrition-risk certification, food package, formula substitution, breastfeeding support, referral, proxy pickup, and redemption evidence.

### Child nutrition and Summer EBT

Use child-nutrition tables for meal/program scale and Summer EBT materials for benefit design. Then check student matching, direct certification, special diets, school closure, meal service days, card delivery, spending, expiration, and appeal records.

### Disaster nutrition

Use FNA disaster/D-SNAP materials to establish authority. Then check declaration, affected geography, destroyed food, power outage, retailer operations, hot-food waiver, shelter distribution, transport, and replacement-benefit receipt.

### EBT security and retailers

Use EBT modernization, stolen-benefit, dashboard, and retailer locator sources as security and access surfaces. Then check household loss date, replacement authority, claim route, card/PIN reset, chip or lock state, retailer distance, stock, and transaction logs.

## Repair surfaces

- household/participant/student/WIC-category identity map;
- application, expedited, recertification, certification, direct-certification, verification, notice, appeal, and correction clocks;
- issuance amount/date, card/eWIC/PIN, replacement, account lock, stolen-benefit claim, and transaction file;
- retailer locator, stock, price, delivery, online purchasing, transport, disability access, and rural/tribal availability file;
- WIC appointment, food package, formula, breastfeeding, nutrition education, referral, and redemption file;
- school meal, CACFP, SFSP, Summer EBT, special-diet, closure, service-day, and child receipt file;
- disaster food, D-SNAP, replacement, USDA Foods, hot-food, shelter/mobile distribution, and power/refrigeration file;
- fraud/integrity/overissuance, trafficking-control, false-positive, complaint, and remedy file;
- food-security outcome and after-action learning record.

## Anti-theater tests

- Do not cite SNAP participation as household food security without timeliness, issuance, redemption, and hunger-outcome evidence.
- Do not cite WIC participation as nutrition continuity without certification, service, food package, formula, and redemption evidence.
- Do not cite school-meal claims as child nutrition without child-level receipt, school-day, summer, and special-diet evidence.
- Do not cite SUN Bucks/Summer EBT as summer food without child matching, card delivery, spending, expiration, and appeal evidence.
- Do not cite D-SNAP or replacement authority as disaster food without survivor access, retailer/power/transport, and receipt evidence.
- Do not cite EBT modernization as household security without actual theft prevention, claim, replacement, and card-state proof.
- Do not cite retailer authorization as grocery access without distance, stock, payment uptime, delivery, and accessibility evidence.
- Do not cite food-security estimates as proof of program performance for a household or locality.

## Source posture

This packet uses official USDA/FNA/ERS source surfaces current enough for rev0755. Use them as national and federal evidence lanes. For live cases, require state eligibility systems, school and WIC clinic records, EBT/eWIC processor records, retailer logs, disaster/shelter records, household notices, and appeal or complaint files.
