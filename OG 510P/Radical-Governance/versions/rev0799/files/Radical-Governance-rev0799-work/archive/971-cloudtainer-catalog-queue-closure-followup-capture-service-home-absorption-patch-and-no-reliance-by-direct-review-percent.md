# 971 — Cloudtainer catalog queue closure, follow-up capture, service-home absorption patch, and no reliance by direct-review percent

## One-line thesis

Closing a catalog-only source queue is maintenance progress, not reliance readiness: a source can be directly refreshed and still require blocked-route, PDF, sparse-page, or search-index follow-up before it can carry quote-level, legal, or outcome-continuity authority.

## Why this matters

Rev0772 left a bounded risk surface: seventy-four source keys still had only catalog-triage posture. Rev0773 closes that queue, but the dangerous mistake would be to announce completion by percentage. The archive has now seen too many public-service failures where a register row, dashboard, guidance page, board packet, project page, or AI transparency record was mistaken for proof that the person, place, account, bridge, benefit, complaint, emergency path, or service home could actually function.

The new maintenance boundary is therefore sharper: **direct review** means a maintainer reached or attempted the official route and recorded posture; **clean reliance** means the source is current, sufficiently captured, and fit for the specific claim being made. Those are not the same thing.

## Pattern pack

### 1. Close catalog-only posture without pretending every source is clean

A source-health row should not remain indefinitely in catalog triage once a maintainer has attempted the route. But a route attempt can land in several non-clean states: blocked official page, sparse page, search-only official result, PDF requiring capture, dashboard requiring current snapshot, or statutory text requiring amendment-context review.

The repair is not to keep the row unclassified. The repair is to move it into explicit follow-up capture posture.

### 2. Preserve a follow-up capture queue after direct refresh

The durable source-health states after this pass are:

- clean direct review for sources that can support ordinary archive routing;
- frequent-check direct review for dashboards, registers, status pages, and living guidance;
- follow-up capture for blocked, sparse, PDF-only, search-indexed, or page-level sources that cannot yet carry quote-level reliance;
- stable historical or statutory anchors where the risk is supersession rather than daily drift.

This prevents the archive from using a perfect percentage to conceal source limits.

### 3. Treat public AI, status, benefit, emergency, and infrastructure routes as pointers, not outcomes

An AI transparency record is not a safe AI system. A status-proof page is not rights continuity. A bridge authority page is not cross-border mobility. A flood plan is not flood protection. A water authority board page is not water continuity. A tax or redress page is not paid remedy. A transport governance page is not the service-home test.

Each source route can start an inquiry. None can end it without outcome, clock, authority, contestability, affected-party, and fallback evidence.

### 4. Partial absorption patch for the service-home merge packet

This note records a partial absorption patch for `MP-003-615-to-849`. It is not deletion authorization for note 615.

The generic service-home elements that must survive any future route consolidation are:

- a public service map distinguishing local entry, shared/regional work, direct operation, state/national retention, and private/operator chain;
- a named authority/operator/regulator/commissioner/contractor split;
- near-to-person entry and complaint handoff even where back-end governance is metropolitan, regional, or cross-border;
- continuity, substitution, failure-takeover, reserve-capacity, and fallback-duty tests;
- a trigger for when repeated shared-service cooperation becomes real service government;
- source/test parity before any older route is retired.

Rev0773 absorbs that protected grammar into the current maintenance route as an explicit requirement, but note 615 remains active because the successor route still needs generated test parity and a surviving generic service-home surface beyond the London transport example.

### 5. Do not retire routes by preservation gesture

A merge packet is not a merge. A protected-elements list is not absorption. An absorption patch is still not deletion unless it moves the protected grammar into a stronger surviving route, preserves source/test parity, updates generated surfaces, and leaves a reader-facing redirect.

## Audit/refactor shipped

Rev0773 changes the audit surface from “catalog triage versus direct review” to a stricter three-way posture:

- direct review;
- clean direct review;
- follow-up capture.

That makes source-health percentages less flattering and more useful. It also keeps the retirement queue honest by recording partial absorption progress separately from deletion readiness.

## Failure modes blocked

- No source reliability by direct-review percentage.
- No AI governance by transparency-record route.
- No immigration, benefit, tax, redress, or border continuity by status page.
- No infrastructure governance by authority homepage.
- No route retirement by similarity score, merge packet, or preservation gesture.
