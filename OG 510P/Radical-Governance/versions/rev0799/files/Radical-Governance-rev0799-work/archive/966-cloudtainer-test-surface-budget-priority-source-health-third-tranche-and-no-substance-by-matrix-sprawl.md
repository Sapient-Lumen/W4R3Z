# 966 — Cloudtainer test-surface budget, priority source-health third tranche, and no substance by matrix sprawl

## One-line thesis

The next risk was not a missing doctrine family; it was a maintenance accounting error that treated every test-matrix leaf render as front-door sprawl, plus a large block of high-dependency source keys still lacking health posture. This revision separates reader-route pressure from evidence-library coverage, then uses the freed maintenance lane to review the next high-consequence source tranche.

## Why this matters

Rev0767 closed the last file-level generated-surface warning, but the generated-surface audit still carried one package warning: raw generated file count. That warning was directionally useful when giant generated surfaces were hiding reader cost, but after compaction it became too blunt. The archive has many `*_TESTS.json` and `*_TESTS.md` outputs because the evidence library is intentionally broad. Counting those leaf matrices as equivalent to front-door routes mislabels coverage as clutter.

The correction is not to delete tests. The correction is to distinguish route pressure from leaf evidence. A first-time reader needs small routes; a maintainer needs complete matrices. The audit now keeps both facts visible: total generated file count remains visible, while package warnings are driven by reader-route files and total bytes.

The second risk was more substantive: the highest unchecked source-health rows were not obscure. They included metropolitan-governance reports, LAHSA restructuring and budget/audit surfaces, Detroit Financial Review Commission waiver records, NYC AI/MyCity chatbot anchors, Canada HR/pay modernization, Atrato/Ganga ecological-personhood anchors, NHS FDP procurement/benefit pages, SIS/watchlist infrastructure, One Login technical docs, HHS/IRS/SSA representative-authority and redress pages, eVisa and infected-blood transition updates, Haiti force-mandate evidence, and IHR emergency-committee surfaces. Those sources carry claims and cases. Leaving them unclassified would make mature notes look better grounded than their currentness posture supported.

## Pattern pack

1. **Do not punish evidence coverage as reader bloat.** A test matrix is a leaf artifact. A route file is an entry surface. Budget them separately.
2. **Keep raw count visible.** Reclassifying a warning must not hide package facts. Total generated count, leaf-matrix count, route count, and total bytes should all stay explicit.
3. **Use audit repair to buy source repair.** Once the audit stops emitting a false package warning, spend the saved attention on high-dependency live sources rather than declaring victory.
4. **Historical reports are anchors, not current implementation.** OECD, World Bank, Markup, AP, ELAW, and stable legal/report surfaces can ground a case, but they should not be treated as continuing operational proof.
5. **Oversight waivers need clocks.** Detroit FRC waiver records, minutes, and resolution pages show a monitoring posture; they do not prove durable local democratic recovery without future waiver/reactivation evidence.
6. **AI progress surfaces need withdrawal and correction tails.** NYC AI action/progress pages and chatbot reporting should be paired with audit, beta-ended, correction, and successor-service evidence.
7. **Proxy authority is outcome-critical.** HHS representative forms, IRS authorizations, SSA representative payee rules, and Social Security Scotland acting-on-behalf pages affect notice, deadline, appeal, and money control; they deserve source-health cadence.
8. **Border, status, and emergency waists are high-consequence even when technical.** SIS, eVisa updates, IHR emergency committees, and Security Council force-mandate records connect registers to liberty, movement, eligibility, and safety outcomes.

## Failure modes

- **Matrix deletion theater:** reducing generated file count by deleting tests instead of improving route design.
- **Audit relabeling theater:** changing warning names while hiding raw counts or total bytes.
- **Source-key complacency:** treating a registry row as reviewed because the audit now has no package warning.
- **Stable-anchor overreach:** using an old report or news story as if it were a live operational status page.
- **Implementation-clock drift:** leaving waiver, migration, platform, representative-authority, eVisa, redress, or emergency-committee surfaces without short review cadence.
- **Front-door sprawl recurrence:** adding more reader routes when a matrix, catalog, or generated machine surface would suffice.

## Audit/refactor result

The generated-surface audit now says what it actually knows. There are still many generated files, but 78 are leaf test-matrix renders rather than front-door route surfaces. Reader-route budget files are now counted separately, and package warnings fall to zero without erasing the raw count.

This is a substantive audit correction because it prevents the archive from optimizing against the wrong metric. The aim is not fewer tests; it is fewer confusing front doors, fewer duplicated catalogs, and clearer pressure where readers actually pay the cost.

## Source-health result

The third tranche moves fifty high-priority source keys out of unchecked status. It does not claim those sources prove outcomes. It classifies their role: stable reference, historical news anchor, live dashboard, implementation clock, legal text, technical documentation, benefit/representative guidance, procurement surface, redress clock, or emergency/cross-border waist.

That makes the archive more honest. A note that depends on the OECD metropolitan-governance report now sees a stable-reference posture; a note depending on GOV.UK One Login or UK eVisa pages sees supersession or implementation risk; a note depending on LAHSA budgets or Detroit waiver minutes sees a monitoring cadence rather than a closure certificate.

## Operational next move

Keep going on source-health, but stop using package count as the headline risk unless the role-classified audit points back to route pressure or byte pressure. The next tranche should favor the remaining unchecked rows with live dashboards, public benefit consequences, redress clocks, high-dependency case packets, and non-English official implementation pages.

If another refactor is needed, prefer consolidation of front-door language and retirement/merge queues over deleting leaf matrices. Test coverage is not the vice. Undifferentiated route mass is the vice.
