# 925 — Utility shutoff, medical baseline, and climate-continuity dockets: arrears, outage, cooling, electricity-dependent equipment, and no life safety by account code

## One-line thesis

A utility account flag, medical-baseline certificate, LIHEAP award, outage map, arrears plan, cooling-center list, or reconnect promise does not prove life-safety continuity; the governing object is the joined docket across account state, health risk, climate hazard, assistance posting, communications, backup power, reconnection, and aftercare.

## Why this matters

Utility service can be both a billing relation and a life-safety dependency. Electricity powers oxygen concentrators, ventilators, dialysis-adjacent routines, refrigerated medicines, cooling, heating, phone charging, elevators, and communications. Heat, wildfire smoke, storms, cold snaps, arrears, billing disputes, and utility de-energization are not interchangeable triggers, but they can converge on the same household.

The dangerous simplification is administrative: call the household protected because the account has a hardship code; call the person safe because a clinician signed a certificate; call the event handled because a map shows the outage; call the bill cured because an assistance award exists; call the public response sufficient because a cooling center is open. Those are fragments. The waist has to join fragments into a time-bounded continuity record.

## Pattern pack

1. **Separate trigger classes before repair.** Nonpayment disconnection, final notice, winter moratorium, summer heat emergency, wildfire public-safety power shutoff, storm outage, fuel-delivery failure, meter / service-line problem, and building-level electrical failure use different authorities and repair paths.
2. **Treat medical baseline as a risk flag, not backup power.** A certificate or allowance can change pricing, notice, or shutoff protection, but it does not itself provide a battery, generator, transport, cooling, medication cold chain, or rapid restoration.
3. **Track assistance at the account-posting layer.** An energy-assistance eligibility row or award letter is not continuity until the relevant utility, fuel vendor, landlord / master-meter arrangement, or arrears plan records the credit and adjusts the disconnection clock.
4. **Put climate hazard clocks in the same docket.** Heat, cold, wildfire smoke, storm restoration, and medically necessary power have different safe-time limits. The docket should show when the hazard began, when the person was contacted, and when service / cooling / backup power was actually restored.
5. **Require contact cascades, not one notice.** The accountable route may need customer, representative, caregiver, clinician, utility, public-health agency, disability office, emergency manager, and community organization contacts, with language and accessibility state preserved.
6. **Measure the tail.** Reconnection date, restoration date, spoilage, medication replacement, device failure, adverse health event, debt rollover, deposit requirement, and re-disconnection risk are part of the public-service outcome.
7. **Publish denominators that reveal harm.** Counts of final notices, shutoffs, reconnections, medically protected accounts, assistance approvals, assistance-posting lag, high-temperature shutoff days, PSPS-affected medical-baseline customers, and after-hours reconnection failures belong in the same evidence frame.

## Failure modes

- **No life safety by account code.** A hardship, medical-baseline, or vulnerable-customer flag can be stale, missing, unrenewed, in the wrong utility account, or ignored during a different event class.
- **No health protection by certificate.** A medical certificate may stop ordinary nonpayment disconnection but not wildfire de-energization, storm outage, building failure, fuel shortage, device battery exhaustion, or medication spoilage.
- **No cooling by center list.** A cooling center matters only if open, accessible, reachable, safe for the person and device, language-accessible, pet / caregiver compatible, and communicated in time.
- **No reconnection by promise.** A call-center commitment or payment arrangement is not repair until service is restored and the customer has a stable route through the next billing cycle.
- **No LIHEAP by award.** Assistance can fail at eligibility, document, vendor, payment-posting, landlord-master-meter, oil-delivery, repair, recertification, or arrears-plan stages.
- **No outage response by map.** An outage map does not show device dependency, indoor temperature, refrigeration state, phone battery, language, transport, or whether a person received help.
- **No equity by aggregate disconnection rate.** County, service-territory, language, disability, age, race, income, renter / owner, master-meter, and medical-device denominators are needed before calling policy fair.

## Minimum joined docket

A usable utility-climate continuity docket contains: account and meter; customer and authorized helper; medical-baseline / serious-illness / disability / age risk state; device and medication dependence; arrears, deposits, and payment-plan state; assistance application and posting; hazard / outage / PSPS event; notice and contact cascade; backup power, cooling, transport, or relocation action; reconnection or restoration receipt; post-event repair; and learning metrics.

## Upgrade triggers

Escalate from ordinary billing governance to life-safety continuity governance when any of these appear: electricity-dependent equipment; refrigerated medication; indoor heat / cold risk; wildfire de-energization; storm outage longer than device-battery duration; household hardship plus disconnection notice; older adult / disability / child vulnerability; language isolation; inaccessible notice; pending energy assistance; or repeated disconnection / reconnection cycling.

## Downshift conditions

Downshift only when the docket proves the account is current or protected, the person has usable backup or relocation, the hazard has passed, assistance has posted, service is reconnected or restored, device / medication continuity is intact, and the next billing / renewal / recertification clock is visible.

## Source posture

Use utility disconnection data, regulator rules, LIHEAP materials, emPOWER electricity-dependence data, medical-baseline programs, emergency-preparedness guidance, and outage-health evidence as complementary source classes. None is authoritative for the whole chain alone.
