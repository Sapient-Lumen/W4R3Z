# 886 — Applied transition-receipt case packet for NYC MyCity Chatbot beta closure: successor gates and no public AI repair by maintenance page

## One-line thesis

The ended NYC MyCity Chatbot beta should be classified as a quiet public-AI withdrawal that still needs a transition receipt: a maintenance or beta-ended page can redirect users, but it cannot prove incident closure, affected-domain learning, source-corpus repair, successor gates, or no public AI repair by maintenance page.

## Why this matters

`877` already classified the MyCity Chatbot as a public generative guidance assistant with failed reliability / reliance controls. This note reopens the same case from a different angle: transition. The public issue is no longer only whether the chatbot gave inaccurate or inconsistent business guidance. The issue is what counts as closure after an official AI assistant vanishes from public use.

That matters because government AI systems will often be retired quietly. A beta will end. A tool will be renamed. A vendor will change. A feature will be removed. A model will be swapped. A public link will become a redirect. Without a transition receipt, neither users nor auditors can know whether a bad public AI experiment produced lessons, changed successor gates, preserved logs, notified affected users, or simply disappeared.

The MyCity beta closure is therefore useful as a negative-control transition case. It does not prove that a city may never test a public assistant. It proves that a public assistant should not leave only a public-facing closure message after audit-level reliability concerns.

## Pattern pack

### 1. Trigger and dispatcher table

| Field | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| trigger | public municipal chatbot beta ended after audit and reliability concerns |
| boundary mismatch | official portal, trusted information claims, business guidance, 311 / service expansion, feedback records, source pages, vendor / model support, and public closure notice diverged |
| first-form question | whether ending the beta is decommission, incident closure, ordinary product iteration, or successor readiness |
| lower-form test | simple redirect / maintenance page is too thin after official guidance failures |
| heavier-form test | individualized legal remediation may be too thick unless reliance harm or adverse action can be traced |
| form verdict | quiet public-AI withdrawal requiring transition receipt and successor launch gates |

### 2. Transition receipt demanded

The closure record should show:

| Receipt field | MyCity-specific question |
| --- | --- |
| terminal event | when the chatbot beta ended, who decided, and whether any part survived inside MyCity, 311, or another assistant |
| affected domains | which business, housing, employment, licensing, tax, or service topics produced wrong, inconsistent, or high-risk answers |
| affected users | whether users who asked high-risk questions could be notified, or why notification was infeasible |
| source corpus | which NYC pages were used, stale, conflicting, excluded, or insufficient |
| answer logs | what query / answer records were retained for audit, red-team, and future design |
| feedback | how negative user feedback, comptroller findings, and city testing drove closure or repair |
| vendor / model | what model, prompt, guardrail, retrieval, and support records were preserved |
| successor gate | what tests must pass before a new public assistant answers comparable questions |
| public route | where users now obtain authoritative business guidance |
| lessons | what public policy changed for future public-facing AI launches |

### 3. Relationship to `876`, `877`, and `884`

Use `876` for the public guidance-liability docket, `877` for the original case diagnosis, and `884` for transition-receipt closure. This packet adds one narrow rule: closure of a defective public assistant must leave a public trail proportionate to the reliance risk.

A public-facing chatbot need not produce individual court-style reasons when it is withdrawn. But after audit-visible accuracy and consistency failures, it should at least leave enough record for source repair, future launch gating, user-channel redirection, and legislative / audit oversight.

### 5. Opposition and rival readings

**Rival 1: a beta can end without a public postmortem.** The archive accepts that not every pilot requires a long public report. It rejects silence when the beta was official, user-facing, service-relevant, and publicly audited for reliability failures.

**Rival 2: no individual legal decisions were made.** The archive accepts that this was not a Robodebt-style legal-effect system. It still requires a public guidance transition receipt because official answers can shape compliance before formal process.

**Rival 3: detailed answer logs may raise privacy or security concerns.** The archive accepts redaction and aggregation. It still requires a versioned incident taxonomy, source-domain analysis, and successor-gate record.

**Rival 4: users can simply use NYC.gov.** The archive accepts redirection as a necessary public route. It rejects redirection as proof that the public AI experiment learned anything.

### 6. Capture and theater channels

- **maintenance-page repair**: a closure page substitutes for incident closure.
- **successor amnesia**: new assistants launch without inheriting old failure categories.
- **feedback evaporation**: negative user feedback disappears into product-management history.
- **source-corpus fog**: no public record shows which content domains were risky.
- **audit-only theater**: comptroller findings are acknowledged but not mapped to launch gates.
- **beta erasure**: experimental status is used to avoid public lesson publication.
- **no-decision minimization**: absence of formal legal effect is used to ignore practical reliance.

### 7. Repair surfaces

The transition should not be marked complete unless the record includes, or explains the absence of:

- beta-end date and public owner;
- successor / replacement / no-replacement statement;
- high-risk topic and failure taxonomy;
- source-corpus correction or exclusion record;
- user-feedback disposition;
- answer-log preservation rule;
- model / vendor / guardrail version record;
- accessibility and language failure analysis;
- public authoritative-route redirect;
- future public-assistant launch gate.

### 8. Holding

The MyCity Chatbot closure should be treated as a quiet public-AI withdrawal, not final repair. The archive should not score the case as closed until a transition receipt shows what failed, what was preserved, what users should do, and what gate any successor assistant must pass.

## Anti-theater tests

This packet fails if the city treats the end of the beta, a maintenance page, or a redirect to ordinary webpages as complete repair. The closure record must prove source-domain learning, affected-user notice rationale, feedback disposition, log preservation, successor gates, and future launch discipline.
