# 926 — Applied utility-shutoff and medical-baseline case packet for EIA disconnection data, LIHEAP, HHS emPOWER, Connecticut winter protection, California PSPS, and no safety by medical certificate

## One-line thesis

The U.S. utility-shutoff / medical-baseline case proves why billing protection, energy assistance, medical certification, outage notification, and public-health mapping must be joined: no household is safe merely because a utility record, benefit award, map, or certificate says so.

## Why this matters

Residential utility disconnection is no longer a dark category: federal reporting now makes final notices, shutoffs, and reconnections visible at national scale. But visibility is not continuity. A household can be counted in a disconnection report, approved for energy assistance, listed as medically vulnerable, placed on a regulator protection route, notified about a planned de-energization, and still lose safe power, cooling, medication refrigeration, communications, or timely reconnection.

This packet uses four complementary official surfaces. EIA supplies the disconnection / reconnection denominator. LIHEAP supplies energy-assistance and crisis-assistance pathways. HHS emPOWER supplies a public-health view of electricity-dependent medical equipment and services. Connecticut shows statutory medical / winter protection logic. California shows medical-baseline and wildfire public-safety shutoff notice logic. Together they force a continuity packet rather than a single-program story.

## Pattern pack

1. **Disconnection data is a denominator, not a remedy.** It shows the scale of nonpayment shutoffs and reconnections, but not which households had device dependence, assistance applications, language barriers, or post-shutoff health harm.
2. **LIHEAP is a bridge only when posted.** The packet must show application, eligibility, award, utility / fuel-vendor posting, arrears effect, disconnection hold, reconnection effect, and remaining balance.
3. **emPOWER is preparedness intelligence, not individual consent or service repair.** Aggregate electricity-dependence mapping helps public-health and emergency managers prepare, but individual outreach and privacy constraints still need an operational route.
4. **Connecticut protects the disconnection category differently from the outage category.** Life-threatening medical protection and winter-protection rules can block or delay ordinary shutoff, but they do not eliminate storms, equipment failure, wildfire de-energization elsewhere, or the need to keep bills and arrangements visible.
5. **California medical baseline and PSPS rules separate cost / notice from energization.** Medical-baseline allowance and advanced notices can reduce cost or improve warning, but a PSPS may still de-energize the area; the packet must track backup power, relocation, contact, and restoration.
6. **Outage health evidence raises the consequence floor.** When long outages are associated with cardiovascular and respiratory hospitalization risk among older adults, continuity evidence must include the medical clock, not just the electric account.
7. **The repair object is a household continuity timeline.** The timeline runs from final notice or hazard forecast through assistance, outreach, shutoff / outage / de-energization, backup action, reconnection / restoration, medication / device repair, and the next recurrence risk.

## Applied state ladder

- **Account and billing state:** utility, meter, master-meter / landlord problem, arrears, final notice, deposit, payment plan, assistance pending, shutoff / reconnection status.
- **Medical and functional state:** medical-baseline or serious-illness certificate, electricity-dependent equipment, refrigerated medication, age / disability / caregiver needs, language and accessibility route.
- **Assistance state:** LIHEAP / state energy assistance application, crisis route, vendor posting, shutoff hold, reconnection fee, weatherization or repair need, residual debt.
- **Hazard and event state:** heat, cold, storm, wildfire PSPS, rolling blackout, building outage, fuel delivery, event start, expected duration, actual restoration.
- **Continuity action:** backup battery / generator, cooling or warming site, transport, relocation, emergency medical route, medication replacement, phone charging, home health contact.
- **Notice and representative state:** customer, authorized helper, caregiver, clinician, utility, public-health authority, emergency manager, and community organization contact receipts.
- **Outcome and learning state:** reconnection / restoration, adverse event, spoilage, debt rollover, appeal / complaint, repeated disconnection, subgroup denominator, policy change.

## Failure modes

- **EIA count without health overlay:** national disconnection data can make scale visible while still missing medically vulnerable household consequences.
- **LIHEAP approval without utility posting:** a household can be administratively approved but remain exposed if the benefit is not posted or does not change the shutoff clock.
- **Medical protection without outage plan:** a certificate may protect against termination but not guarantee energization, transport, cooling, medication cold chain, or device battery life.
- **PSPS notice without life-safety support:** warning before de-energization is not continuity unless the household has workable backup or relocation.
- **emPOWER map without outreach receipt:** preparedness data does not prove that a person was contacted, understood the risk, accepted help, or received power-dependent equipment support.
- **Reconnection without stabilization:** restoration after a shutoff can still leave debt, deposits, equipment damage, food / medication spoilage, and near-term re-disconnection risk.

## Minimum test packet

A complete applied record must show: final notice / shutoff / outage event; medical-baseline or functional-needs state; device / medication dependence; household assistance status; regulator or utility protection route; notification receipts; backup or relocation plan; reconnection / restoration receipt; post-event repair; complaint / appeal route; and public metrics that separate shutoff prevention from restoration and health protection.

## Anti-theater core

No safety by medical certificate. No life safety by account code. No continuity by LIHEAP award. No outage repair by map. No resilience by PSPS notice. No medical-device continuity by discounted rate. No climate protection by cooling-center list. No public-health response by aggregate data alone.

## Source posture

This case should cite EIA for disconnection and reconnection scale, LIHEAP for assistance routes, HHS emPOWER for electricity-dependent medical-equipment preparedness, state utility regulators for shutoff protections, California PSPS / Medical Baseline materials for de-energization and notice, CDC / Ready guidance for household medical-device planning, and health research for outage consequences. Treat each as a partial source that must be joined, not as a standalone proof of safety.
