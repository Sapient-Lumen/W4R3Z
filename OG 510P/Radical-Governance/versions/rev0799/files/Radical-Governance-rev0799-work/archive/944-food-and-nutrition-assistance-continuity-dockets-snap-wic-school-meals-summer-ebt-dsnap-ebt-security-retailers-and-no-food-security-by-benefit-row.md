# 944 — Food and nutrition assistance continuity dockets: SNAP, WIC, school meals, Summer EBT, D-SNAP, EBT security, retailers, and no food security by benefit row

## One-line thesis

Food and nutrition assistance continuity is not a SNAP case row, WIC category count, meal reimbursement table, Summer EBT plan, D-SNAP waiver, retailer authorization, EBT card state, stolen-benefit dashboard, or food-security estimate. A household, infant, pregnant participant, student, older adult, disabled person, disaster survivor, tribal household, unhoused person, or rural family can appear inside a valid program while application clocks, recertification, benefit issuance, card security, retailer access, school meal delivery, summer coverage, replacement, disaster feeding, and remedy are broken. The archive should join eligibility, issuance, access, meal, retailer, device/card, nutrition, emergency, and repair records before treating a benefit row as food security.

## Why this matters

The archive had food systems by scope, entitlement continuity, health-benefit continuity, water, housing, child welfare, and disaster assistance, but it did not yet have a direct operational nutrition-assistance packet. That was a dangerous gap because food assistance is a cash-like benefit, a public-health intervention, a child-development service, a school/care/custody dependency, a disaster lifeline, and a payment-network surface. A program row can be true while the person still cannot buy, receive, prepare, or safely consume food.

The governing repair phrase is **no food security by benefit row**.

## Pattern pack

- **No food security by benefit row.** Eligibility, certification, enrollment, participation, or issuance data is denominator evidence, not proof of actual nutrition access.
- **No SNAP by approval.** A SNAP approval does not prove timely application processing, expedited service, EBT card receipt, retailer access, benefit theft repair, language access, or recertification continuity.
- **No WIC by category count.** A WIC infant, child, pregnancy, or postpartum category row does not prove appointment access, benefit issuance, formula/food availability, breastfeeding support, referrals, or redemption.
- **No school meal by reimbursement.** Meal counts and reimbursement data do not prove that a particular child received breakfast, lunch, afterschool, summer, special-diet, disability-accessible, or stigma-free meals.
- **No Summer EBT by plan.** A state, tribal, or territorial plan and a benefit amount do not prove correct child matching, card delivery, activation, household notice, retailer usability, or expiration awareness.
- **No disaster feeding by waiver.** D-SNAP, replacement benefits, hot-food approval, or USDA Foods authority does not prove survivor access, open retailers, electricity, transport, cooking, or culturally appropriate food.
- **No EBT security by dashboard.** A stolen-benefit dashboard or chip-card transition is not proof that a household avoided theft or received replacement/remedy.
- **No retailer access by locator.** Authorized retailer rows cannot prove distance, stock, online purchasing, disability access, payment uptime, price, or household transportation.

## Continuity docket

| Ledger | What it must preserve | Bad shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Household and participant identity | Household members, children, infant/pregnancy category, disability, language, shelter/custody/school/care setting, and representative authority. | Treating program enrollment as person-level continuity. |
| Eligibility and clocks | Application, expedited SNAP, recertification, verification, direct certification, categorical eligibility, work/immigration/student rules, and appeal deadlines. | Treating an approval or denial reason as final food access. |
| Benefit issuance and card state | Allotment amount, issuance date, EBT card/PIN, replacement card, chip migration, account lock, online purchasing, and point-of-sale usability. | Treating posted issuance as spendable food money. |
| Retailer and food access | Authorized retailers, distance, stock, online delivery, farmers markets, transportation, disability access, rural/tribal access, and outage state. | Treating retailer authorization as reachable groceries. |
| WIC service lane | Certification, nutrition risk, appointment, eWIC, food package, formula, breastfeeding, referrals, proxy pickup, and redemption. | Treating participation category as nutrition service. |
| Child nutrition lane | NSLP, SBP, CACFP, SFSP, special diets, direct certification, community eligibility, meal service days, closures, and claims. | Treating aggregate meals served as a child fed. |
| Summer EBT lane | Child match, school/benefit crosswalk, notice, card issuance, benefit amount, expiration, retailer acceptance, and appeals. | Treating an approved plan as summer nutrition. |
| Disaster/emergency lane | D-SNAP, replacement, hot foods, USDA Foods, mass replacement, power loss, open retailers, shelters, and mobile distribution. | Treating waiver approval as edible food. |
| Theft, fraud, and integrity | Skimming/cloning, claim route, replacement authority, card modernization, trafficking controls, false positive holds, and notices. | Treating fraud control as household security. |
| Equity, remedy, and outcome | Food-insecurity denominator, complaint/appeal, replacement receipt, nutrition outcome, child meal gap, after-action learning, and source-currentness. | Treating participation or spending as reduced hunger. |

## Minimum joined record

A serious nutrition-assistance packet should contain at least:

1. **Participant map.** Household, child, infant, pregnancy/postpartum category, disability, language, homeless/custody/shelter/school/care status, tribal/rural edge, and representative/proxy authority.
2. **Eligibility and clock file.** Application date, expedited status, verification requests, recertification, direct certification, student/work/immigration/categorical rules, and appeal or correction clock.
3. **Issuance and access file.** Benefit amount, availability date, EBT/eWIC card and PIN state, replacement-card route, online-purchasing access, point-of-sale uptime, and stolen-benefit claim state.
4. **Retailer and food file.** Authorized retailers, distance, stock, price, formula, dietary/cultural fit, delivery, transport, disability access, and rural/tribal/farmers-market routes.
5. **Child meal file.** School-breakfast/lunch/CACFP/SFSP/Summer EBT lane, meal-service days, direct certification, special diets, school closure, and child-level receipt.
6. **WIC service file.** Certification appointment, nutrition education, breastfeeding support, food package, formula substitutions, eWIC issue, referral, and redemption.
7. **Disaster food file.** D-SNAP or replacement authority, destroyed food, power outage, retailer operating state, hot foods, USDA Foods, shelters, mobile distribution, and transport.
8. **Integrity and remedy file.** Fraud signal, benefit theft, trafficking control, overissuance, notice, claim/appeal, replacement receipt, and false-positive harm.
9. **Outcome and equity file.** Food insecurity, child/senior/disability/language/geography denominators, culturally appropriate food, missed meals, and residual hunger.
10. **Source-currentness boundary.** Mark whether each source is a program dataset, timeliness table, rule/guidance, plan, dashboard, waiver, report, or live case record.

## Upgrade triggers

Route to the full food/nutrition continuity docket when any of these appear:

- a SNAP, WIC, school meal, Summer EBT, D-SNAP, retailer, or EBT row is cited as proof that people were fed;
- application or recertification delay, expedited SNAP, verification, notice, direct certification, special diet, or appeal clocks can interrupt food;
- card theft, card replacement, chip migration, EBT/eWIC outage, online purchasing, retailer distance, store closure, or formula/food stock affects redemption;
- school breaks, school closures, summer months, disasters, evacuations, power outages, shelters, nursing homes, prisons, or child care settings make ordinary food routes unavailable;
- program-integrity controls, stolen-benefit limits, overissuance, trafficking investigation, or fraud screening can block or fail to repair a household.

## Downshift conditions

A lighter summary is acceptable only when the question is purely national nutrition-program context and no household, child, infant, WIC participant, student, disaster survivor, retailer, card, meal, notice, appeal, or replacement consequence is live. Once any person may miss food, meals, formula, benefits, replacement, or notice, open the full packet.

## Failure modes

- **No food security by benefit row:** a household is eligible or issued benefits but cannot redeem or receive food.
- **No SNAP by approval:** approval exists but application timeliness, expedited access, EBT card, retailer, theft, or recertification fails.
- **No WIC by category count:** a participant category exists but appointment, food package, formula, breastfeeding support, referral, or redemption fails.
- **No meal by reimbursement:** school/program claims count meals but child-level access, special diets, stigma, closure days, or summer gaps remain unresolved.
- **No summer by plan:** a Summer EBT plan exists but child matching, card mailing, activation, spending, expiration, or appeal fails.
- **No disaster food by waiver:** authority exists but power, retailer operations, transport, shelter, hot food, or replacement logistics fail.
- **No security by chip migration:** anti-theft modernization exists but stolen benefits remain unrepaired or households cannot secure cards.
- **No retailer access by locator:** authorized-store data exists but distance, stock, disability access, payment uptime, or price blocks food.

## Audit questions

- Which people and food routes are affected: SNAP, WIC, school meals, Summer EBT, D-SNAP, TEFAP/USDA Foods, retailer, or household self-provisioning?
- What is the application, expedited, recertification, direct-certification, issuance, notice, appeal, and replacement clock?
- Is the benefit actually spendable: card received, PIN usable, account secure, retailer reachable, POS/online transaction working, and foods in stock?
- Are children, infants, pregnant/postpartum participants, older adults, disabled people, people in shelters/custody/care, tribal/rural households, and language communities separately visible?
- What evidence proves a missed benefit, stolen benefit, missed meal, formula gap, disaster food loss, or summer coverage gap was repaired?

## Source posture

This docket uses official USDA/FNA/ERS public sources current enough for a rev0755 packet. They are source surfaces, not live case files. Use state agency systems, school nutrition records, WIC clinic records, EBT/eWIC processor files, retailer logs, shelter/disaster records, and household notices for live determinations.
