# meta-0434 — entitlement continuity, Medicaid unwinding, and Universal Credit managed-migration note

This revision adds the entitlement-continuity annex:

- `894` for renewals, managed migration, ex parte proof, procedural churn, and no benefit loss by process failure.
- `895` for Medicaid / CHIP continuous-enrollment unwinding and renewal procedural-churn governance.
- `896` for UK Universal Credit managed migration, migration notices, transitional protection, and legacy-benefit closures.

It also adds `metadata/entitlement_continuity_tests.json`, `schema/entitlement_continuity_tests.schema.json`, `tools/build_entitlement_continuity_tests.py`, and generated `ENTITLEMENT_CONTINUITY_TESTS.*` surfaces.

The policy reason is that the cube now has enough digital-status, platform-migration, model-decision, transition-receipt, and staff-copilot doctrine to confront the most ordinary public-law harm: a person does not lose a right because a legal rule changes, but because the proof, notice, renewal, deadline, portal, or claim route fails.

The rule added here is: no benefit loss by process failure.
