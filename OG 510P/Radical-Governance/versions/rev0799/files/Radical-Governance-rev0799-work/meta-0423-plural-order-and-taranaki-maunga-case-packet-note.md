# meta-0423 — Plural-order and Taranaki Maunga case-packet revision note

rev0722 adds the archive's first plural-order stress test. The revision responds to the case-matrix warning that the cross-boundary manual had not yet been tested against Indigenous authority, legal personality, settlement mandate, relational jurisdiction, and retained state operating duties.

The revision adds two numbered notes:

- `864` — plural-order authority and Indigenous co-governance lens for legal personality, consent / co-decision labels, settlement mandate, relational jurisdiction, retained state duties, and no sovereignty by consultation theater.
- `865` — Taranaki Maunga / Te Kāhui Tupua applied case packet for legal-person mountain, iwi-Crown conservation waist, public access, Department of Conservation operational responsibility, funding, asset, plan, and rights-boundary records.

The revision also adds a plural-order flattening defeat test, two claim-ledger entries, new source keys for UNDRIP / FPIC and New Zealand legal-person natural-entity materials, and updated generated case / claim / defeat / audit surfaces.

Continuity rule: `864` is a lens beside `856` and `861`, not a replacement for them. A future case that invokes Indigenous authority, legal personhood, customary law, treaty redress, or relational territory should activate `864` before the archive chooses an ordinary level-of-government form.
