# Meta 0427 — Capacity-floor and Haiti GSF case-packet note

This meta note records the rev0726 pass. The revision adds `872` as a general fragile-jurisdiction capacity-floor docket and `873` as the applied Haiti GSF / UNSOH / HNP case packet.

The reason for the pass is that the archive had become strong at classifying authority forms, but still needed a hard test for places where legal mandate, donor pledge, emergency authorization, or support-office architecture can outrun the operating floor. Rev0726 therefore asks whether personnel, money, logistics, access, records, rights routes, justice handoff, humanitarian boundary, and local handback are actually present before a support arrangement is treated as governing capacity.

The new machine-readable layer is `metadata/capacity_tests.json`, rendered into `generated/CAPACITY_TESTS.json` and `generated/CAPACITY_TESTS.md`.
