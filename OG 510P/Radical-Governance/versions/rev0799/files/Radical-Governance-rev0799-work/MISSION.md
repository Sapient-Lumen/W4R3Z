## Heart of the mission

**Do not confuse the government’s record of an event with the public reality it is supposed to prove.**

This archive is an evidence-continuity discipline for public power. It asks whether authority, delivery, contestability, continuity, repair, and source posture join up around the person, community, duty, asset, or right at stake. A portal, register entry, approval, dashboard, account balance, assessment form, generated route, or green lint result can be useful evidence. None is automatically the outcome.

The recurring negative formulations—no government by outcome letter, no service home by portal, no usable money by approval, no archive authority by unnamed proof—are not slogans for their own sake. They are compression rules against administrative theater.

## The proof ladder

A serious case packet should distinguish at least these layers:

1. **Authority** — who may act, under what law, mandate, delegation, budget, or compact.
2. **Decision object** — the authoritative act, class, reasons, effective date, conditions, and lineage.
3. **Delivery chain** — the operators, interfaces, vendors, staff, channels, handoffs, and failure points that must work.
4. **Person or community outcome** — what changed in access, safety, liberty, money, care, mobility, status, burden, or environmental condition.
5. **Contestability** — how an affected person can understand, challenge, pause, correct, or appeal the act.
6. **Repair** — whether the remedy was completed, not merely offered or logged.
7. **Continuity** — what happens through migration, outage, provider exit, emergency, supersession, and institutional handback.
8. **Evidence continuity** — which source supports which claim, how current it is, what it cannot prove, and whether the evidence can be reconstructed later.

The archive fails when it collapses one rung into another.

## What this archive is not

It is not a claim that more schemas, registers, dashboards, standards, or automation necessarily create better government. It is not a substitute for legislation, budgets, staffing, organizing, adjudication, field research, democratic authorization, or direct experience. It is not yet a complete theory of redistribution or structural power.

Its strongest contribution is narrower and still important: it makes public-power claims harder to fake by forcing administrative surfaces to dock to authority, operations, lived outcomes, remedies, continuity, and evidence.

## What counts as a new note

Add a note only when it does at least one of four things:

- introduces a genuinely reusable doctrine not preserved elsewhere;
- applies an existing doctrine to a materially different failure mode or jurisdiction;
- repairs a source, schema, route, or continuity defect that could mislead readers;
- creates a tested successor that permits an older active route to become historical or superseded.

A new noun, institution, platform, or topical example is not enough.

## Rev0786 proof pilot

The current red flag is no longer merely that affected-person proof is missing; it is that two pilot lanes now exist and still must not be called complete. For unemployment insurance, the question is whether a valid claimant can be followed from hold, notice, cure, appeal or waiver, through actual payment and burden. For housing continuity, the question is whether a household can be followed from arrears or filing through assistance, representation, possession, screening, shelter or re-housing, and durable stability. The evidence receipt pilot records the official-source boundary; it does not store private lives or replace field validation.

The maintenance rule for this pass is: **no proof by official surface**.

### Current execution boundary

Rev0793 adds a final non-closing fieldwork boundary: even if an owner later authorizes collection outside the cube, operating logs, incident reports, audit trails, breach response, retention decisions, and disclosure review still do not prove public outcomes. The archive may preserve redacted status and aggregate-safe findings; it must not become the fieldwork operator, incident file, audit log, or private evidence store.



## Rev0794 release-control addendum

Public release is now a separate non-closing gate. A disclosure-reviewed table, de-identified summary, quote, dashboard row, or release packet may support a public-safe finding only after disclosure limitation, re-identification risk, methods, harm review, and errata controls pass outside the cube. It still does not prove claimant or household outcomes by itself.
