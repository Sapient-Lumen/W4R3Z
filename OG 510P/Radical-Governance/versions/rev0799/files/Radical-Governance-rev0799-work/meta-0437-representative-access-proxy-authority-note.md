# meta-0437 — Representative access proxy-authority note

This rebuild adds notes `905`–`909` and the generated `REPRESENTATIVE_ACCESS_TESTS.*` surface.

The point of the pass is to make representative access its own governance lane rather than folding it into login, customer service, professional practice, appeal filing, or informal family help. The new layer separates the person with the underlying right from the person or organization acting for them, the credential used to enter a system, the legal authority to act, the scope of that authority, the duty attached to payment control, the route for notices and deadlines, and the routes for revocation, restoration, misuse correction, and public metrics.

The new anti-theater rule is: no public action by password sharing.
