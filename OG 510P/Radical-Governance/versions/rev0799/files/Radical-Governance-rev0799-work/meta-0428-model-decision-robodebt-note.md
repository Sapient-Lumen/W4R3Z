# Meta 0428 — Model-decision and Robodebt case-packet note

This meta note records the rev0727 pass. The revision adds `874` as a general model-mediated public-decision / evidentiary-debt docket and `875` as the applied Robodebt case packet.

The reason for the pass is that the archive had become strong at classifying authority forms, supplier dependencies, capacity floors, and symbolic mandates, but still needed a hard test for existing public agencies that use data matching, rule engines, risk scores, or models to convert administrative signals into legal effect. Robodebt forces the archive to ask whether the state has lawful evidence, burden allocation, intelligible notice, meaningful review, collection restraint, class remediation, and future automated-decision transparency before it treats efficiency as governance.

The new machine-readable layer is `metadata/model_decision_tests.json`, rendered into `generated/MODEL_DECISION_TESTS.json` and `generated/MODEL_DECISION_TESTS.md`.
