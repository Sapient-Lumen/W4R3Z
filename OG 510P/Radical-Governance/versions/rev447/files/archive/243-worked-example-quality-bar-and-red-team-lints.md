# Worked Example Quality Bar & Red-Team Lints


**Purpose:** keep new examples sharp enough to teach and test institutions rather than drifting into vibes or anecdote.

**Person served:** archive maintainers and memo writers.

---

## Quality bar
A strong worked example:
- has a concrete trigger event;
- names the missing artifact;
- states the immediate protective move;
- makes the smallest believable repair explicit; and
- points to the pattern if the case repeats.

## Red-team lints
Reject or revise examples that:
- describe a wrong but never name the artifact that should have existed;
- diagnose a harm but never name the interim protection;
- rely on “someone should have known” instead of a visible owner or clock; or
- use vendors, provisional states, or review tiers as narrative color without specifying where accountability actually lives.
