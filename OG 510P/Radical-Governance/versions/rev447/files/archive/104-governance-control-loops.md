# Governance Control Loops (Make “Rule of Law” Operable)

**Purpose:** compress the archive’s accountability mechanics into one diagram-free page: how a person can *see* a decision, *contest* it, and force *learning* rather than drift.

**From-below thesis:** a government is “real” when it runs **closed loops** that convert harm signals into corrected action, with receipts, deadlines, and consequences for non-response.

(See: decision-receipt and reason-code surfaces in `115-information-integrity-and-record-interfaces.md` and `52-reason-codes-registry.md`; remedy `08-remedy-and-grievance.md`; program register `28-program-register-and-evaluation-commitments.md`; records/FOI `31-records-foi-and-government-memory.md`; oversight `32-oversight-institutions-and-follow-through.md`; budget levers `07-fiscal-and-budgetary-governance.md`.)

## The minimum loop (per decision, per program, per system)

1) **Observe (make state legible)**
   - Publish the **rules-in-force** + who can change them (`25-legal-legibility-and-rule-inventory.md`, `39-rulebook-and-instruments-registry.md`).
   - Maintain event registers where harm concentrates (e.g., enforcement/custody, inspections, benefits cutoffs) (`43-enforcement-and-custody-event-register.md`, domain memos).

2) **Decide (and leave evidence)**
   - Every materially adverse action yields a **Decision Receipt**: basis, authority, inputs, who approved, portable reason codes, and the path to review (`115-information-integrity-and-record-interfaces.md`, `52-reason-codes-registry.md`).
   - When algorithms mediate: register the system, versions, and human responsibility (`42-automated-decision-systems-and-model-registry.md`).

3) **Act (deliver or explain)**
   - Services must have published standards; delay is a contestable outcome, not “just backlog” (`82-service-standards-and-minimum-service-guarantees.md`, `108-service-standards-and-time-budgets.md`).

4) **Contest (person can force a response)**
   - A remedy lane must exist with **deadlines**, **interim protection** where irreparable harm is plausible, and a clear escalation chain (`08-remedy-and-grievance.md`, `101-claude-rev142-normative-requirements.md` (NR-16, NR-20)).

5) **Audit (independent checking)**
   - Oversight bodies must have powers to compel records, subpoena, publish findings, and trigger corrective orders (`32-oversight-institutions-and-follow-through.md`, `22-public-integrity-and-procurement.md`).

6) **Learn (close the loop)**
   - Programs have pre-committed evaluation questions + stopping rules; rule changes are versioned and attributable (`28-program-register-and-evaluation-commitments.md`, `39-rulebook-and-instruments-registry.md`).
   - Budgets reflect results: conditionality, clawbacks, and reallocations are explicit (`35-transfer-register-and-conditionality.md`, `18-intergovernmental-finance.md`).

## “Loop-completeness” tests (quick checks)

A person should be able to answer, in minutes, for any decision that touches them:
- **What happened to me, exactly, and under what authority?** (receipt + rule inventory)
- **Who is responsible and how do I reach them?** (mandate registry + contactable offices)
- **By when must someone respond, and what if they don’t?** (remedy lane + escalation)
- **Where do similar harms show up system-wide?** (event registers + metrics)
- **What changes because of this signal?** (evaluation + budget levers)

If any answer requires insider knowledge, the loop is not closed.

---

## Common failure modes (and the minimal fix)

- **Opacity-by-default:** “policy” exists but can’t be found. → mandate the rule inventory + decision receipts.
- **Delay-as-control:** people are “resolved” by waiting them out. → enforce response clocks + interim protection triggers.
- **Paper rights, no enforcement:** oversight writes reports; nothing changes. → link oversight findings to budget/funding and corrective orders.
- **Gaming metrics:** targets drive perverse behavior. → publish metric definitions + audit trails; treat dashboards as contestable.
- **Jurisdictional buck-passing:** “not us.” → interop join-keys + named escalation across scopes (`70-interoperability.md`, `14-scope-ladder.md`).
