# Prosecutorial and Disciplinary Integrity Rails (Anti‑capture Enforcement)

**Purpose:** ensure *enforcement* of integrity rules cannot be quietly disabled (by capture, fear, or procedural decay).

**Why this memo exists:** many jurisdictions have “good rules” but **selective non‑enforcement**. This memo defines a minimal set of rails that make enforcement *auditable* and *hard to interfere with*.

## Invariant
Enforcement must be:
- **Independent enough** to investigate powerful actors.
- **Accountable enough** to avoid politicized targeting.
- **Legible enough** that the public can detect under‑enforcement.

## Minimum rails (MR‑Enforce)
### 1) Case intake and routing (no silent discard)
- **Universal intake** (public + referral + protected disclosure).  
- **Receipt‑first:** every intake generates a case ID + clock + scope + next step.
- **Routing rules are published:** which unit handles which allegations.
- **No-drop requirement:** if declined, publish a structured declination reason category.

### 2) Assignment integrity (anti‑favoritism)
- **Randomized assignment** within eligibility constraints.
- **Conflict screens** (recusal triggers) are logged.
- **Overrides require written approval** + publication in aggregate stats.

### 3) Independence safeguards (without “unaccountable prosecutors”)
- **Protected tenure / removal discipline** for key integrity roles.
- **Budget insulation with transparency:** staffing cuts require public explanation.
- **Barrier against “acting” abuse:** limits on indefinite interim leadership.

### 4) Transparency without jeopardizing investigations
Publish *aggregate and procedural* transparency:
- case volumes, clocks, outcomes, declination categories
- investigative stage counts
- plea/settlement counts and restitution totals

(Do not publish sensitive details that enable witness intimidation or evidence tampering; publish *process integrity*.)

### 5) Discipline and ethics (internal integrity)
- **Ethics complaint lane** for enforcement personnel.
- **Independent review** for allegations of bias, retaliation, or interference.
- **Training + clear standards** to reduce discretionary abuse.

### 6) Anti‑interference tripwires
Create **interference event categories** and require logging:
- pressure to drop/slow a case
- reassignment attempts
- record access obstruction
- retaliation against complainants/witnesses

Tripwires trigger:
- automatic escalation to an oversight body
- preservation orders
- publication of a redacted interference receipt

## Oversight model (two-key)
Enforcement integrity is strongest with **two-key control**:
- **Operational key:** prosecutor/disciplinary function.
- **Oversight key:** inspector general / audit body / judicial review lane.

Neither key alone can bury a case *without leaving artifacts*.

## Scope assignment
- **Municipal/regional:** intake, receipts, discipline, and referral rules.
- **National:** independent prosecution capability and cross‑jurisdiction coordination.
- **Supranational/global:** mutual legal assistance, asset recovery coordination, and beneficial ownership interoperability.

## Tests (add to Governance Test Suite)
- **T‑Enforce‑01:** Intake cannot be silent: every allegation gets a receipt + clock.
- **T‑Enforce‑02:** Case assignment is auditable and resistant to favoritism.
- **T‑Enforce‑03:** Declinations are categorized and reported (under‑enforcement becomes visible).
- **T‑Enforce‑04:** Interference events are logged and trigger escalation.
