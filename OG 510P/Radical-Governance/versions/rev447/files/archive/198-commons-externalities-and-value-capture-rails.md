# Commons, Externalities, and Value-Capture Rails (Price power without selling the polity)

**Purpose:** turn diffuse harms/benefits (pollution, congestion, land value uplift, resource depletion) into **legible, appealable, anti-capture** fiscal interfaces—without creating a rent machine.

**Person served:** anyone paying the charge *or* harmed by the externality who needs the system to be explainable, contestable, and fair.

**From-below:** if we price an externality, people must be able to see (1) *why*, (2) *how measured*, (3) *where the money goes*, (4) *how to appeal*, and (5) *how capture is prevented*.

---

## The core doctrine (three obligations)

1. **Polluter / user pays (bounded):** the party causing the external cost pays *up to* a receipted, reviewable schedule. (Anchor: OECD Polluter‑Pays Principle.) citeturn0search4
2. **Beneficiary shares:** when public action increases private value (zoning, transit, public realm), a portion is recaptured for the public (land value uplift, special assessments, development charges).
3. **Return legitimacy:** revenues must not vanish into opacity. They are either:
   - **rebated** (dividend / tax swap), or
   - **earmarked with a sunset** for the harm domain, plus independent audits.

---

## What “ideal” looks like (interface spec)

Every externality/value‑capture instrument must publish a **Pricing Receipt**:

- **Harm statement:** what harm/benefit is priced; which alternatives were considered; why this lever.
- **Boundary & scope:** geography, sectors, exemptions, thresholds.
- **Measurement method:** sensors/proxies, error bounds, update cadence, and known blind spots.
- **Rate schedule:** formula + examples; how rates change over time; escalation/de-escalation triggers.
- **Equity lane:** explicit protections (hardship credits, mobility/service access guarantees, rural/low‑income adjustments).
- **Revenue disposition:** dividend formula, earmarks, and the **anti-siphon** rule (no silent repurposing).
- **Appeals + redress:** contest measurement, classification, or eligibility; deadlines and remedies.
- **Capture tripwires:** concentration of benefit, lobbying intensity, contractor dominance, exemption creep.
- **Sunset & review:** periodic re‑authorization with published evaluation.

(See also: `108-service-standards-and-time-budgets.md`, `110-budget-procurement-integrity.md`, `189-service-level-governance-and-redress-ops.md`.)

## Canonical policy patterns (use as building blocks)

### A) Carbon price + dividend (cap-and-dividend or tax-and-dividend)
- **Strength:** clear signal; dividend protects legitimacy and reduces regressivity.
- **Governance must include:** coverage map, exemptions ledger, price corridor, and revenue recycling ledger.
- Reference baseline: World Bank “State and Trends of Carbon Pricing” (coverage + revenues). citeturn0search1turn0search13

### B) Congestion pricing (time/space as a scarce commons)
- **Strength:** direct congestion/air-quality improvements; funds transit and reliability.
- **Governance must include:** performance dashboard (traffic, travel time, air quality), exemptions ledger, and transit reinforcement plan.
- Empirical anchor: Stockholm congestion charging reduced traffic and emissions in evaluations. citeturn0search3turn0search15

### C) Land value capture (public action → private uplift)
- **Tools:** land value tax, betterment levies, special assessment districts, developer contributions.
- **Governance must include:** appraisal method transparency, anti-displacement protections, and a public “uplift register” tied to projects.

### D) Resource royalties / severance taxes (finite commons depletion)
- **Strength:** converts depletion into public value; can fund intergenerational trusts.
- **Governance must include:** production measurement integrity, price benchmarking, and a leakage/transfer‑pricing audit lane.

## Anti-capture rails (non-negotiables)

- **Exemptions are *receipted***: every exemption has a public rationale, beneficiary estimate, and expiry.
- **No backdoor privatization:** rate-setting cannot be delegated to entities paid by the charged parties.
- **Independent measurement:** sensors/models have an audit path; publish drift/incident logs (see `191-algorithmic-systems-registry-and-audit-rails.md`).
- **Spend joins to outcomes:** budget lines join to contracts and outcome metrics (see `194-fiscal-federalism-open-budgets-and-participation-rails.md`).
- **Deliberation lane for major shifts:** big rate changes require a bounded public docket + response obligations (see `111-deliberation-to-decision-binding.md`).

---

## Minimal evaluation loop (don’t fly blind)

Publish quarterly:
- **Effect metrics:** emissions/congestion/health proxies, distributional impact, compliance burden.
- **Legitimacy metrics:** appeal rate + remedy time, exemption creep, trust indicators.
- **Integrity metrics:** lobbying spend spikes, single‑vendor dependence, model drift incidents.
