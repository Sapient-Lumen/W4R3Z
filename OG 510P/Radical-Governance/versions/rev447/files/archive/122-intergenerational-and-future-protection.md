# Intergenerational & Future Protection (Make long-term harms contestable)

**Goal:** treat “future people” as a **standing class** *without* turning governance into unaccountable technocracy.

This memo specifies minimal, portable primitives for **long‑term stewardship** that can be used from micro‑local to global scope.

---

## Failure modes this fixes

- **Slow violence invisibility:** harms accumulate (pollution, debt, ecosystem loss) without a decision point anyone can contest.
- **Short-term capture:** incumbents externalize costs beyond their term.
- **Emergency drift:** exceptional measures become permanent.
- **Irreversible lock-in:** infrastructure / legal changes that are hard to unwind get pushed through as “just policy”.

---

## Core primitives (portable)

### 1) Future Impact Statement (FIS-*)
A **FIS** is the *minimum long‑term payload* required for:
- major rules (`RCR-*`), major spending (`DRR-TYPE: FUNDING`), and coercive expansions (surveillance, detention capacity).
- **Contents (tight):**
  - *irreversibility score* (0–3) + why
  - *time-horizon* used (e.g., 5/20/50 yrs) + sensitivity bands
  - *known unknowns* + monitoring plan
  - *rights-floor risk* (who bears long-term risk)
  - **sunset/evaluation hook** (see SRR-*)

### 2) Sunset & Review Receipt (SRR-*)
Every high‑irreversibility change MUST have:
- an **automatic review date**
- a public **SRR** (what will be measured; what triggers rollback; who must respond)
- a “no-review → pause” circuit breaker (defaults to *interim continuation with tight bounds*, not silent permanence)

### 3) Intergenerational standing & advocate lanes (IGA / IGO)
Provide a **standing path** for long‑term harms:
- **IGA:** an *Intergenerational Advocate* role with authority to file contests (not to govern)
- **IGO:** an *Intergenerational Ombud* with duty-to-investigate + duty-to-publish findings
- Advocates must emit **Reason/Standing receipts** (tie to `106-legitimacy-protocols.md`)

### 4) Future Risk Register (FRR)
Maintain a short public list of existential / tail risks relevant to the scope:
- each entry has an owner, monitoring signal, and “what we do if signal crosses threshold”
- integrates with `104-governance-control-loops.md` (learn fast, publish updates)

---

## Guardrails (anti‑technocracy)

- **No blank-check veto:** future advocates can *trigger review* and *force response*, but not silently block.
- **Bounded expertise:** expert reports are inputs; decisions remain receipt‑bound and contestable.
- **Equity across time:** treat “future people” protections as **rights-floor constraints**, not permission to sacrifice present vulnerable groups.

---

## Minimal metrics (publishable)

- % of major changes with FIS + SRR present
- % of SRR reviews completed on time
- reversals/rollbacks due to long-term harms detected
- “irreversibility score 3” changes per year (should be rare and heavily justified)
