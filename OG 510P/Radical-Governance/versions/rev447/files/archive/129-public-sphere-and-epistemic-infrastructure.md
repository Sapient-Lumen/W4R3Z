# Public sphere & epistemic infrastructure (make truth-seeking governable)

**Purpose:** treat the *public sphere* (shared attention, shared facts, shared contestation) as critical infrastructure—so power cannot win by controlling what people can see, say, or trust.

**Person served:** a person trying to make a consequential choice (vote, medical decision, safety response, economic choice) without insider access—and who needs *corrections* and *dispute lanes* that do not require heroism.

**See also:** `161-information-commons-and-public-media-governance.md` (ownership + platforms + ads; pluralism without censorship).

**Core failure modes**
- **Censorship capture:** “misinformation control” used to suppress opposition.
- **Attention capture:** incentives reward outrage/fraud, drowning out contestation.
- **Correction asymmetry:** lies travel fast; corrections don’t propagate.
- **Opaque amplification:** recommendation/boosting hides power.
- **Safety collapse:** harassment and doxxing make participation impossible.
- **Emergency drift:** crisis measures become permanent (see `112-exception-control-and-emergency-powers.md`).

## Minimal primitives (portable across scopes)

**EPI-1 Public sphere incident ledger (PSIL)**
- MUST: record major integrity incidents (coordinated manipulation, deepfake campaigns, state-aligned influence operations, critical infrastructure rumor shocks) with *time, scope, affected populations, mitigation actions, and expiry*.
- SHOULD: classify incidents by *harm pathway* (fraud, violence risk, election integrity, public health, market manipulation).
- MUST: link any exceptional measures to the **Exceptions Ledger** (`112`) with a sunset and post-mortem.

**EPI-2 Amplification transparency (what reached whom, and why)**
- MUST: publish a **Reach & Amplification Policy** for any institution that controls public communication channels (platforms, broadcasters, emergency alert systems, official comms).
- SHOULD: maintain an **Amplification Register (AR-*)**: material policy changes and “boosting” programs get a receipt (AR-CHANGE-*), with rationale and risk tier.
- MUST: provide a contest lane for *downranking/takedown/labeling* decisions, with receipts that attach to `DRR-*` / `RCR-*` patterns (`106`, `118`).

**EPI-3 Corrections that propagate**
- MUST: provide **Correction Receipts (ECR-*)** for contested claims:
  - Claim snapshot (hash/pointer), evidence basis, decision (correct/uncertain/false/unsupported), and propagation requirement proportional to original reach.
- SHOULD: “non-reset correction rule” at seams: if a correction exists, transfers must carry it (see continuity patterns in `109`, `114`).
- MUST: protect whistleblowers/reporters of manipulation (see `121`).

**EPI-4 Safety to speak (anti-harassment floor)**
- MUST: anti-retaliation + anti-doxxing enforcement as a *precondition* of open participation; otherwise “transparency” becomes a weapon.
- SHOULD: provide protected participation lanes for high-risk users (journalists, witnesses, public servants, vulnerable groups).

## Cross-links (joins to other primitives)
- **Legitimacy receipts:** if speech limits exist, they MUST be receipt-bound, contestable, and time-bounded (`106`, `112`).
- **Information/records:** evidence and decision logs must be retrievable (`115`).
- **Influence footprint:** political advertising and coordinated influence MUST be legible (`120`).
- **Rulemaking:** policy changes to moderation/amplification are “rule changes” and need change-control (`118`).
- **Data governance:** targeting and profiling must be purpose-bound and logged (`127`).

## Minimal metrics (publishable without bloat)
- Time-to-correction for high-risk false claims (median / p90).
- Correction propagation ratio (correction reach ÷ original reach) for high-impact items.
- Contest-resolution time for takedown/downrank/label disputes.
- Harassment/doxxing incident closure time + retaliation substantiation rate.
- Emergency measures: % with sunsets met + post-mortems published.

**Notes:** This memo does *not* pick a single “speech regime.” It specifies *interfaces* that keep whatever regime you choose visible, contestable, and resistant to capture.
