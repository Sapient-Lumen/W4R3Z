# Worked Example Missing Records, Adverse Inference & Proof Failures


**Purpose:** define what happens when the institution cannot produce the notice, delivery proof, or basis packet it says justifies an adverse outcome.

**Person served:** advocates, hearing officers, auditors, operators, and memo writers.

---

## Protective defaults
- no adverse outcome without the packet the institution says existed;
- shift proof risk back to the institution while records are missing;
- open a missing-record incident rather than treating absence as ordinary business; and
- publish repeated proof failures by office, vendor, language, and channel.
