# Worked Example Quick Reference & Training Drills

**Purpose:** turn the casebook into a fast routing and rehearsal layer so people can move from symptom to the right examples, questions, and first protective moves.

**Person served:** readers, trainers, advocates, operators, organizers, journalists, and auditors who need quick routing prompts rather than a long theoretical tour.

**From-below:** this memo exists so a person-facing problem can be routed to the right example fast, and so examples can double as drills for building, checking, or contesting institutions.

## The five questions that should work in almost every example

1) What happened?
2) Why did it happen?
3) What happens next, by when?
4) Who owns the next step?
5) What protects the person while review runs?

If an institution cannot answer those five without improvising, it probably has not emitted enough accountability artifacts.

## First-contact drills

### Drill A — Live harm in motion
Start with `WX-36`. Ask: what irreversible event is next, who can still stop it today, what proof can the person carry, and what opens automatically if the stop fails?

### Drill B — Relief on paper, harm in fact
Start with `WX-37`, `WX-38`, or `WX-39`. Ask: which downstream systems still think the old state controls, what portable proof exists, and who owns propagation failure?

### Drill C — Vendor seam without a clear owner
Start with `WX-33`, `WX-40`, or `WX-49`. Ask: who can actually stop the harmful action today, what logs count as public records, and which public authority is still accountable even if the vendor touched the screen?

### Drill D — Missing record or missing notice
Start with `WX-43`. Ask: can the institution actually produce the notice-and-basis packet, who bears the burden while proof is missing, and what protective default should apply now?

### Drill E — School or care continuity
Start with `WX-35`, `WX-41`, or `WX-50`. Ask: what support is unsafe to interrupt, what interim flag or continuity bridge should stay live, and which family burdens are the system currently externalizing?

### Drill F — Binding hearing without continuity of counsel
Start with `WX-42`. Ask: who is supposed to appear, what transfer or withdrawal broke continuity, and what automatic stay or continuance should fire before the hearing can proceed?

### Drill G — Temporary state that hardened by silence
Start with `WX-44` or `WX-46`. Ask: what review or handoff was supposed to happen, what should have auto-extended, and who owned the overdue temporary state?

### Drill H — Impossible deadline / fake waiver
Start with `WX-45`. Ask: when did the notice become practically usable, what outage or accessibility barrier stole time, and what reopen or good-cause default should fire now?

### Drill I — Renewal seam erased a protection
Start with `WX-47`. Ask: what fact was already verified, what should have carried forward automatically, and where did the review seam wipe the prior protection?

### Drill J — Signature or acting authority looks invalid
Start with `WX-48`. Ask: what role signed, what delegation or acting order made that role valid on that date, and what should pause automatically if the authority chain cannot be proved?

### Drill K — Front-door layer became the hidden decider
Start with `WX-49` or `WX-51`. Ask: which step was called administrative or preliminary, what public owner should still have been visible, and what downstream action fired before a binding decision really existed?

### Drill L — Support-state vanished in a handoff
Start with `WX-50`. Ask: which accommodation, safety, or representative state had already been established, what should have traveled automatically, and what adverse clock should have been blocked until the receiving side acknowledged it?

### Drill M — Same person, different rendering
Start with `WX-52` or `WX-53`. Ask: which fields actually diverged, what stays continuous while the mismatch is reviewed, and who can approve a safe merge or split without inventing fraud?

### Drill N — Household edge is wrong
Start with `WX-54`. Ask: which relationship edge is disputed, what harm is spilling to dependents or represented people, and what should stay live until the graph is corrected?

### Drill O — Severe false-positive match
Start with `WX-55`. Ask: what source asserted the severe status, what second source or human verification should have been required, and who can fire the same-day kill switch now?

### Drill P — Address role confusion
Start with `WX-56`. Ask: which address role is wrong, what should still route safely while the dispute is corrected, and what unit-level ambiguity makes the wrong door dangerous?

### Drill Q — Boundary seam
Start with `WX-57`. Ask: which map or territory rule controlled, what continuity should stay live while the seam is reviewed, and who owns the wrong-door handoff?

### Drill R — Wrong-place action
Start with `WX-58`. Ask: what parcel, geocode, frontage, or unit evidence is actually being relied on, what is its confidence class, and what action should pause until place resolution is strong enough?

### Drill S — No-fixed-address continuity
Start with `WX-59`. Ask: what reachable contact path exists, what can count as a visitable or safe-contact substitute, and which rights are being wrongly tied to one stable residential field?
