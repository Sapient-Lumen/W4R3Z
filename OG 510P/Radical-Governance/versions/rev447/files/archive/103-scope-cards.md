# Scope Cards (Ideal Government by Scale)

**Purpose:** provide a **one-page** “ideal government by scope” view that stays consistent with the archive’s cross-scope interfaces, without repeating the whole theory.
**Person served:** someone being bounced across jurisdictions who needs to know **who owes what**, what must be published, and where escalation goes.

**From-below:** every card names (1) what this scope *must* do, (2) what it must *publish*, and (3) how a person *forces follow‑through*.

See the map memo `14-scope-ladder.md` (assignment logic) and `71-interface-obligations-by-scope.md` (minimum publications).

---

## Card format

- **Owns (primary jobs):** what this scope should hold as default responsibility.
- **MVG (minimum institutions):** the smallest legitimacy + capability set that makes “government” real here.
- **Must publish (interfaces):** the few artifacts needed so people can *find* power, money, rules, and remedies.
- **Escalation / coordination:** what happens when this scope fails or the problem crosses boundaries.

(Shared anchors: person path `98-persons-path-and-accessibility-invariants.md`; remedy `08-remedy-and-grievance.md`; interop/join-keys `70-interoperability.md`.)

## Micro-local (building / block / neighborhood)

- **Owns:** immediate place-based order and care: small commons, neighbor disputes, micro-safety norms, visible maintenance, rapid coordination (`10-micro-local.md`, `24-mutual-aid-and-serious-incident-protocol.md`).
- **MVG:** a convening body with rotation + anti-capture rules; a small budget + transparent receipts; a named steward for urgent issues; a basic grievance lane with time bounds (see legitimacy patterns `21-legitimacy-architecture.md`).
- **Must publish:** (a) who is responsible *today*; (b) meeting / decision receipts; (c) spending ledger; (d) how to file a grievance or request a review.
- **Escalation / coordination:** when harm exceeds local capacity or rights floors are threatened, escalation must be **legible**: a posted path to municipal/county ombuds + emergency contacts.

## Municipal (city / town)

- **Owns:** baseline local services + local rights enforcement: streets, sanitation, housing-code enforcement, local permitting, local public health delivery, local safety partnerships; “front door” for most daily-life interactions (`20-municipal.md`, `09-public-service-and-state-capacity.md`).
- **MVG:** elected council/executive; professional admin; ombuds + audit; procurement discipline; usable local court/tribunal access; published rule inventory (`22-public-integrity-and-procurement.md`, `32-oversight-institutions-and-follow-through.md`, `25-legal-legibility-and-rule-inventory.md`).
- **Must publish:** rulebook + instruments registry (`39-rulebook-and-instruments-registry.md`); budgets/contracts (`38-contracting-and-procurement-register.md`); service standards + queues; complaint/appeal lanes (`36-appeal-lanes-and-redress-registry.md`).
- **Escalation / coordination:** mutual aid compacts and cross-border cooperation are default for spillovers (`19-compacts-and-cooperative-governance.md`); rights-floor failures route to regional/national review.

## Intermediate local administration (county / prefecture pattern)

- **Owns:** “boring but critical” admin capacity and cross-municipal services: courts/admin services, public health, land records, child/family services, regional transit coordination where applicable (`87-intermediate-local-administration.md`).
- **MVG:** stable civil service; standardized service access across municipalities; a boundary-spanning grievance escalator; intergovernmental finance interface (`18-intergovernmental-finance.md`).
- **Must publish:** coverage maps (what is handled here vs municipal); transfer rules; performance + equity dashboards (tight, person-relevant) (`03-metrics-and-evidence.md`).
- **Escalation / coordination:** acts as the default “backstop” when micro-local/municipal units fail capacity tests; must keep escalation **fast**.

## Metropolitan + functional authorities (overlays)

- **Owns:** multi-jurisdiction systems with strong spillovers: transit, watershed/air, housing markets, major emergency coordination, regional labor-market infrastructure (`16-metropolitan-governance.md`, `15-functional-authorities.md`).
- **MVG:** a mandate registry + competence ledger to prevent buck‑passing (`34-competence-ledger-and-mandate-registry.md`); joint budgeting; conflict-of-interest discipline; specialized oversight with follow-through.
- **Must publish:** who owns which failure mode (service breakpoints); cross-jurisdiction compacts; shared procurement contracts; incident registers when coercive powers are used (`43-enforcement-and-custody-event-register.md` when relevant).
- **Escalation / coordination:** needs strong “exit to higher scope” for rights and safety (regional/national), plus hard interoperability requirements (`70-interoperability.md`).

## Regional (state / province / land)

- **Owns:** rights floors within the region; education and health system design; major infrastructure; environmental stewardship at regional ecological scales; coordination across cities (`30-regional.md`, `11-commons-and-ecological-governance.md`).
- **MVG:** regional legislature/executive; constitutional/rights review capacity; equalisation and standards-setting; independent integrity institutions.
- **Must publish:** standards + waivers; transfer formulas; enforcement guidelines; emergency powers and sunsets (`45-emergency-measures-register.md`, `74-sunsetting-and-deprecation-discipline.md`).
- **Escalation / coordination:** acts as the “rights backstop” for municipalities; escalates up to national when cross-regional markets/externalities dominate.

## National (nation-state)

- **Owns:** national rights floor; fiscal/monetary backbone (or macro-fiscal stance); national security with strict accountability; immigration/status; nationwide standards when needed for equity and scale (`40-national.md`).
- **MVG:** constitutional rule-of-law and independent courts; professional administration; national audit; election integrity; public knowledge infrastructure (`26-epistemic-infrastructure-and-public-knowledge.md`).
- **Must publish:** national rule inventory and delegations; budget + procurement; enforcement and custody accountability; automated decision system registry where used (`42-automated-decision-systems-and-model-registry.md`).
- **Escalation / coordination:** must accept petitions from below on systemic failures, and must maintain lawful pathways for sub-national experimentation *without* rights erosion.

## Supranational (treaty/union level)

- **Owns:** cross-border coordination problems: trade and standards where needed, shared environmental constraints, migration compacts, cross-border competition policy, conflict prevention and dispute settlement (`50-supranational.md`).
- **MVG:** limited enumerated powers; strong transparency + review; representation that prevents domination by large members (`21-legitimacy-architecture.md`).
- **Must publish:** competence boundaries; dispute processes; enforcement limits; cross-border impact assessments; a public docket for scope contests (`54-subsidiarity-and-scope-assignment-test.md`).
- **Escalation / coordination:** must be able to bind members on narrowly-scoped collective-action failures, while preserving local democratic legitimacy through clear exit/voice mechanisms.

## Global (planetary)

- **Owns:** problems that are truly planetary: climate/ozone/biodiversity constraints, pandemic coordination, extreme-risk technology coordination, global financial integrity, humanitarian standards (`60-global.md`).
- **MVG:** legitimacy through plural representation + constrained mandates; independent verification capacity; funding mechanisms not captured by a few donors.
- **Must publish:** global commitments registry; verification/audit results; exceptions and waivers; remedy channels for affected populations (including non-citizens).
- **Escalation / coordination:** relies on nested implementation (regional/national/local). The “global” layer should mostly set **constraints + verifications**, not micromanage delivery.

## Non-territorial & indigenous polities (cross-cutting)

Some legitimate governments are not “tiers” inside a single territorial ladder (e.g., indigenous nations, diaspora institutions, platform/community governance, professional bodies). Treat them as **first-class units** in the competence ledger with explicit coercion ceilings and interfaces.

- **Owns:** membership-based norms, internal services, and (where recognized) land/commons stewardship.
- **MVG:** clear membership and representation rules; remedy and appeal; fiscal transparency for shared funds; anti-retaliation.
- **Must publish:** enforceable norms + decision receipts; how to contest status/discipline; interfaces to municipal/regional/national services.
- **Escalation / coordination:** nation-to-nation compacts and shared stewardship agreements should be registered as `CMP-*` and remain contestable (see `12-identity-and-recognition.md`, `19-compacts-and-cooperative-governance.md`, and `197-polycentric-federalism-and-overlapping-sovereignty.md`).

## Two non-negotiable cross-scope moves (keep small)

1. **Anti–buck‑passing circuit:** every scope must publish a “who-owes-what” map for the top failure modes *and* an escalation path (`71-interface-obligations-by-scope.md`, `34-competence-ledger-and-mandate-registry.md`).
2. **Rights floors + functional equivalents:** allow diverse institutional forms so long as the **person’s path** and **remedy** are equivalent (`98-persons-path-and-accessibility-invariants.md`, `08-remedy-and-grievance.md`).
Common upgrades that apply at every scope: deliberation-as-infrastructure (`143-…`), data stewardship + assurance (`144-…`), and allocation mechanisms threat-modeling (`145-…`).

## Scope Card: Future Stakeholders (Non-present standing)

**Use when:** decisions impose **long-tail harms** (irreversibility, debt, ecological loss) across terms.

**Minimum:** standing + trigger list + contest window + option-preserving remedies.  
See: `122-intergenerational-and-future-protection.md`, `196-future-guardianship-and-standing.md`.
