# Worked Example Reversal Propagation, Reinstatement & Restoration Proof


**Purpose:** define what it means to unwind harm for real rather than only centralize a “fixed” note.

**Person served:** implementers, supervisors, auditors, field operators, and advocates.

---

## Restoration proof
A system has not restored a person until it can show the reversal reached every live edge that was acting on the old state: route, roster, provider, payment, terminal, watchlist, queue, or benefit file. The person should carry a portable restoration or override proof while stragglers clear.
