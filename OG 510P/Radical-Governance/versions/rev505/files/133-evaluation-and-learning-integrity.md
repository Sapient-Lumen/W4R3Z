# Evaluation & Learning Integrity (Make reforms reversible and provable)

**Problem:** governance fails when decisions never get *re-tested*; policies become permanent regardless of outcomes, and evidence is “optional.”

**Goal:** turn evaluation into a **joinable interface**: every major program/rule/budget line can be traced to (a) a *testable claim*, (b) an evaluation plan, (c) publishable results, and (d) a recorded decision to continue, change, or stop.

## Core primitives (receipts and registers)

**Evaluation Plan Receipt (EPR-*)**
- Links: `DRR-*` (decision), `CLM-*` (claim), affected scopes, metrics, baseline, target band, time horizon, and “stop/adjust” thresholds.
- Includes: pre-analysis plan (what counts as evidence), data sources, and a publication date (or justified delay).
- MUST: name what would falsify the claim.

**Evaluation Finding Receipt (EFR-*)**
- Links: `EPR-*`, data provenance, model/analysis version, confidence/uncertainty, and key subgroup results.
- MUST: separate *what was observed* from interpretation; include “known limits” and missingness.

**Post‑Implementation Review Receipt (PIRR-*)**
- A short operational review: delivery reality vs design, failure modes, backlog/time-budget impacts, and grievance/contest statistics.
- Links: service promises/time budgets (`108`), complaint/remedy lanes (`08`), and audit follow‑through (`130`).

**Decision Update Receipt (DUR-*)**
- The binding loop-closure artifact: “continue / modify / pause / retire,” with reasons, effective date, and revised claims (`CLM-*`) if continuing.
- MUST: cite `EFR-*`/`PIRR-*` (or explain why evidence is unavailable).

**Learning Register (LR-*)**
- A lightweight index that maps `DRR-* → (CLM/EPR/EFR/PIRR/DUR)` so reforms are replayable and decisions are auditable.

## Minimal rules (defaults)

1. **No “forever without review”:** material programs/powers/rules MUST have a review clock (pair with `118` rule change control and `345` future protection).
2. **Publication is the default:** findings publish unless a concrete, bounded harm case is stated (tie to record interfaces `115` and data governance `127`).
3. **Stop thresholds are real:** if pre-declared thresholds are triggered, an automatic escalation/circuit-breaker should fire (see `104`/`105`).
4. **Counter‑capture:** evaluators disclose conflicts and influence footprints (see `120`); major evaluations get independent review.
5. **Seam continuity:** transfers across jurisdictions do not reset evaluation commitments (see `109`, `114`, `132`).

## Metrics (small but powerful)
- % of material `DRR-*` with a linked `CLM-*` and `EPR-*`
- % of `EPR-*` with published `EFR-*` by deadline
- Median time from `EFR-*` to `DUR-*`
- % of programs changed/retired following negative findings (anti-theater signal)

## Notes
This is intentionally small. It is a *protocol layer* that can wrap any evaluation method (RCT, quasi‑experimental, audit, expert review, participatory monitoring) as long as it produces joinable receipts.
