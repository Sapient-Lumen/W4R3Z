# Worked Example Harm Accounting, Back Pay & Aftercare


**Purpose:** make the archive explicit about what happens after a wrongful extraction, suspension, gap, or coercive error has been reversed.

**Person served:** finance teams, remedy owners, auditors, and advocates.

---

## Aftercare rule
Closure is false if the file says “fixed” but the person still absorbed unrepaired rent loss, missed medication, travel cost, missed work, replacement childcare, or other downstream harm. Wrongful extraction, stale-state lag, and stop-rule breaches should feed make-good and aftercare review.
