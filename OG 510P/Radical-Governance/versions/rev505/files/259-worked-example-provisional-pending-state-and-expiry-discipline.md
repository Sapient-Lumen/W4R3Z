# Worked Example Provisional / Pending State & Expiry Discipline


**Purpose:** prevent temporary states, bridges, or pending labels from becoming silent instruments of punishment when review, handoff, or receiving capacity fails to catch up.

**Person served:** implementers, supervisors, auditors, advocates, and operators dealing with “still pending,” “temporary extension,” or “bridge ended” cases.

---

## Design rules
Temporary power still needs a receipt. Expiry must test reality, not only calendar time. Review due dates need consequences. Temporary-state inventory is publishable.
