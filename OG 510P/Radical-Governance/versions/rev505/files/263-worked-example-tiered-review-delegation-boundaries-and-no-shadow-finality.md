# Worked Example Tiered Review, Delegation Boundaries & No-Shadow-Finality


**Purpose:** make multi-stage review legible so screening, recommendation, vendor-side triage, or delegated intake cannot quietly become the real final decision.

**Person served:** supervisors, hearing officers, quality-review teams, program managers, vendors under public contracts, advocates, and auditors.

---

## Stage discipline
Every stage should say whether it is advisory, screening-only, ratifying, or binding; who can still change the outcome; and what protections remain live until that binding stage is visible. If a stage can cause seat loss, extraction, enforcement, or queue drop, it is not merely advisory.
