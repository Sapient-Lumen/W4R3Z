# 289 — Public Integrity Stack and Enforcement Routing Guide

**Purpose:** turn the archive’s public integrity / anti-capture cluster into a readable stack so procurement integrity, compact anti-corruption rails, influence transparency, system architecture, and enforcement stop competing for the same conceptual role.

**Why this memo exists:** `22`, `163`, `181`, `187`, `226`, and `227` all surface for “corruption”, “capture”, or “integrity system” queries because they describe one family from different layers. Nearby `120` and `130` are narrower subsystem memos inside the same family. The archive should name those handoffs explicitly rather than invite flattening.

**Evidence anchors:** public-integrity system standards and implementation measurement [BIB-OECD-PI] [BIB-OECD-PII] [BIB-OECD-ACI-OUTLOOK-2024]; lobbying/influence transparency update [BIB-OECD-LOB-2024]; prevention, strategy, and risk-management obligations [BIB-UNCAC] [BIB-UNODC-NACS-RISK].

## Confusion boundaries

### `226` vs `187`
- `226` is the **current architecture anchor** for interfaces, institutions, and observability.
- `187` is the **shorter blueprint** for quick orientation and minimum viable system design.

### `22` vs `163`
- `22` is about **procurement, spending channels, and anti-capture around public money flows**.
- `163` is about the **portable anti-corruption rails packet** across influence, money, detection, protection, and sanctions.

### `181` vs `120`
- `181` is the **public influence-transparency interface**.
- `120` is the **broader conflicts / disclosure / recusal subsystem** that plugs into more than lobbying alone.

### `226` / `187` vs `227`
- `226` and `187` explain **how the integrity system is structured**.
- `227` explains **how enforcement stays auditable and hard to disable** once wrongdoing is alleged.

### `22` / `163` vs `130`
- `22` and `163` specify **what anti-capture controls should exist**.
- `130` specifies **how oversight, audit, inspection, and follow-through are receipted and made operational**.

## Safe merge rule for this cluster

Do **not** flatten this family into one general memo on “anti-corruption”.
The safer pattern is:
1. keep `226` as the architecture anchor,
2. keep `187` as the compact blueprint,
3. keep `22` as the procurement / spending / capture front door,
4. keep `163` as the compact anti-corruption rails packet,
5. keep `181` as the influence-transparency subsystem,
6. keep `227` as the enforcement rails memo,
7. keep `120` and `130` as narrower component hooks,
8. and use this memo as the bridge that names the roles.

That preserves distinct retrieval hooks while sharply reducing reader confusion.
