# 297 — State Capacity / Appointments / Agency Independence Routing Guide

**Purpose:** turn the archive’s state-capacity / appointments / agency-independence cluster into a readable stack so workforce capability, merit recruitment, appointment integrity, board governance, and regulator insulation stop competing for the same conceptual role.

**Why this memo exists:** `09`, `113`, `199`, `228`, and `229` all surface for queries like “merit civil service”, “depoliticisation”, “agency independence”, “appointment integrity”, or “how to stop government from being run as patronage”. They describe one family from different layers. The archive should name those handoffs explicitly rather than invite flattening.

**Evidence anchors:** merit, professionalism, impartial leadership, and people-management authority [BIB-OECD-PSLC] [BIB-OECD-PSLC-REPORT-2025] [BIB-SIGMA-PA-PRINCIPLES-2023] [BIB-SIGMA-PSHRM-HUB]; civil-service and regulator-governance measurement [BIB-OECD-GAAG-CIVILSERVICE-2025] [BIB-OECD-GAAG-REGULATORS-2025]; board-professionalism, independence, and nomination discipline [BIB-OECD-SOE-2024] [BIB-OECD-SOE-ACI-2025].

## Canonical reading order

### 1. Start with `09-public-service-and-state-capacity.md`
Use `09` when the question is:
- whether the state can actually execute decisions without patronage, chaos, or vendor capture,
- what minimum public-service capability looks like,
- how staffing, continuity, training, and service dignity fit together,
- or why institutional capacity is a governance obligation rather than an administrative afterthought.

`09` is the **state-capacity and execution front door**.

### 2. Move to `199-civil-service-merit-and-capacity-rails.md`
Use `199` when you need:
- a tighter merit-recruitment and workforce-governance packet,
- depoliticisation boundaries,
- workforce metrics, hiring ledgers, and training/certification rails,
- or a compact implementation memo for recruitment, promotion, and reporting culture.

`199` is the **merit / workforce / depoliticisation rails memo**.

### 3. Use `113-appointments-and-tenure-integrity.md`
Use `113` when the real issue is:
- appointments, acting roles, and removals as a capture surface,
- whether authority-bearing roles can be silently swapped,
- how to receipt appointment decisions,
- and how removal standards, term discipline, and board stacking become contestable.

`113` is the **personnel-power integrity memo**.

### 4. Use `228-public-appointments-and-board-governance-rails.md`
Use `228` when you need:
- a public-body or board appointment packet,
- role cards, competition notices, panel charters, override receipts, and conflict packets,
- quorum stability and vacancy manipulation safeguards,
- or a specialization for commissions, oversight bodies, agency heads, and SOE boards.

`228` is the **board and high-discretion appointments specialization**.

### 5. Use `229-independent-regulators-and-agency-independence-rails.md`
Use `229` when the question becomes:
- how independence is engineered across appointments, budget, information, method, and case assignment,
- how agencies are insulated from informal direction without becoming unaccountable,
- how regulator contacts and ministerial pressure become visible,
- or how budget retaliation, revolving doors, and interference are turned into tripwires.

`229` is the **agency / regulator independence specialization**.

### 6. Use `308-peaceful-transfer-of-power-caretaker-government-and-continuity-of-office-rails.md` when authority is changing hands
Use `308` when the issue is:
- peaceful transfer of power after an election, no-confidence vote, resignation, incapacity, or coalition deadlock,
- caretaker limits on appointments, contracts, grants, and major discretionary decisions,
- acting-authority bounds and vacancy continuity,
- incoming-government briefing discipline and transition records,
- or how to preserve service continuity while political authority is in flux.

`308` is the **transfer / caretaker / continuity seam memo**.

### 7. Use `310-cabinet-government-coalition-agreements-portfolio-architecture-and-centre-of-government-rails.md` when the issue is the political-executive operating layer
Use `310` when the issue is:
- how cabinet should be formed and coordinated once confidence exists,
- how coalition / confidence-and-supply terms, consultation rights, or “agree to disagree” rules should be published,
- how ministerial portfolios should be mapped to departments, agencies, votes, and legislation,
- how cabinet committees and the centre of government should work,
- or how to keep the political executive from corroding the professional state through opaque portfolio and coordination arrangements.

`310` is the **cabinet / portfolio / CoG seam memo**.

## Confusion boundaries

### `09` vs `199`
- `09` is the **broad state-capacity and execution frame**.
- `199` is the **tighter workforce-governance and merit-rails packet**.

### `113` vs `199`
- `113` is about **appointments, acting authority, removals, and tenure as integrity events**.
- `199` is about **recruitment systems, promotion, workforce capability, and reporting culture**.

### `113` vs `228`
- `113` is the **general appointment / tenure integrity layer** across authority-bearing roles.
- `228` is the **specialized public-appointments / board-governance packet** for commissions, boards, oversight bodies, and similar roles with structured selection artifacts.

### `228` vs `229`
- `228` is about **how the appointment and board process itself is structured and receipted**.
- `229` is about **how independence is sustained after appointment** through funding, case integrity, method controls, and anti-interference design.

### `09` / `199` vs `229`
- `09` and `199` focus on **public-service capability and merit systems across the administration**.
- `229` focuses on **narrower institutions that require heightened insulation from political or regulated-party pressure**.

## Safe merge rule for this cluster

Do **not** flatten this family into one general memo on “merit civil service” or “agency independence”.
The safer pattern is:
1. keep `09` as the state-capacity front door,
2. keep `199` as the merit / workforce / depoliticisation rails packet,
3. keep `113` as the appointment / acting / removal integrity memo,
4. keep `228` as the board and high-discretion appointments specialization,
5. keep `229` as the agency / regulator independence specialization,
6. keep `310` as the cabinet / portfolio / CoG neighbor,
7. and use this memo as the bridge that names the roles.

That preserves distinct retrieval hooks while sharply reducing reader confusion.
