# Public Integrity System Blueprint (Anti-capture infrastructure)

**Stack relation:** use `289-public-integrity-stack-and-enforcement-routing-guide.md` for the canonical route across the public integrity / anti-capture cluster. This memo is the compact blueprint and orientation companion; `226` is the architecture anchor; `22` handles procurement/capture surfaces; `181` handles influence transparency; `227` handles enforcement integrity.

**Purpose:** specify the *minimum viable integrity system* a government must have to prevent capture and corruption from becoming “normal operations”.

**Person served:** a person who suspects public power is being traded, who needs the system to produce contestable evidence and enforceable consequences without insider access.

**From-below floor:** integrity failures must be **detectable, receiptable, and sanctionable**.

---

## 1) Integrity is a system, not an ethics poster

An integrity program that relies on individual virtue fails under pressure. A system must combine:

- **Clear standards** (conduct + conflicts + gifts + outside employment)
- **Transparency registers** (influence, contracts, assets/interests)
- **Prevention** (risk assessment, controls, separation of duties)
- **Detection** (audits, analytics, reporting channels)
- **Enforcement** (credible sanctions + follow-through)
- **Learning** (post-incident reforms)

OECD treats public integrity as a **whole-of-society system** with risk-based strategy and culture of integrity. `[BIB-OECD-PI-HANDBOOK-2020]`

---

## 2) Minimum viable integrity stack (MVIS)

### A. Rules + records
- Code of conduct + enforceable rules for conflicts of interest, revolving doors, gifts/hospitality, procurement integrity.
- A **Rulebook & Instruments Registry** so standards are versioned and discoverable (`39`).

### B. Registers (public-by-default where safe)
- **Influence & Interests Register** (`46`, `181`): meetings, lobbying, donations where relevant, beneficial ownership links.
- **Contracts & Procurement Register** (`38`, `179`): tenders, awards, change orders, performance.
- **Oversight Findings & Response Register** (`55`, `183`): findings, deadlines, completion evidence.

### C. Controls that resist “workarounds”
- separation of duties on spend decisions
- controlled exceptions with receipts (`85`)
- procurement gates (competition default, justification for emergency sole-source) (`179`)
- staffing rules that preserve merit and prevent patronage slippage

### D. Detection + reporting
- internal audit + inspector general style functions (`183`)
- protected whistleblowing channels with anti-retaliation enforcement
- anomaly detection on procurement and benefits adjudication (but with contestability)

### E. Enforcement + remedies
- sanctions ladder with due process
- clawbacks and contract remedies for vendor misconduct
- person-facing remedy lanes for those harmed by corrupt decisions (`08`, `76`)

---

## 3) Anti-capture “tripwire package”

When capture risk is high (procurement, land, policing, licensing, extractives, large subsidies):

- **Mandatory disclosure joins** (beneficial ownership + related-party detection)
- **Cooling-off periods** and post-employment limits (`79`)
- **Rotating assignment / dual sign-off** on high-value decisions
- **Independent review trigger** when:
 - single bidder,
 - repeated change orders,
 - concentration of awards,
 - whistleblower report with credibility markers,
 - large variance from market benchmarks

---

## 4) Integrity metrics (don’t game yourself)

Track a small set of metrics that expose drift:

- % of spend with full competition
- change-order rate + “scope creep” variance
- time to resolve whistleblower reports
- audit findings closure rate within deadlines
- conflict disclosures filed vs expected by role type

All integrity metrics that affect enforcement must be versioned and contestable (`142`).

---

## 5) Adoption guidance (make it real)

- Start with **registers + receipts** (visibility makes enforcement possible).
- Add controls that **remove discretion** where it does not create value.
- Protect investigators and auditors from retaliation and budget capture.
- Publish annual integrity system assurance case (join to `73`).

---

## Minimal tests (hook into `107-governance-test-suite.md`)

- **T — Evidence path:** Can a member of the public trace a high-stakes decision to money/interests/contracts?
- **T — Enforcement reality:** Do findings reliably produce consequences (not just reports)?
- **T — Capture resistance:** Are the most capture-prone interfaces pre‑wired with tripwires?
