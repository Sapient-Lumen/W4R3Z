# 302 — Fiscal State / Budget / Revenue / Transfers Routing Guide

**Purpose:** turn the archive’s fiscal-state / budget / revenue / transfers family into a readable stack so broad fiscal governance, budget allocation, revenue integrity, collection administration, intergovernmental finance, open-budget participation, and equalization design stop competing for the same conceptual role.

**Why this memo exists:** `07`, `110`, `135`, `93`, `18`, `194`, and `217` all surface for queries like “how money power should be governed”, “what a fair tax state looks like”, “how budgets stay legible”, “how transfers should work across levels”, “how to prevent fiscal blame-shifting”, or “how to fund services without dark extraction”. They describe one family from different layers. The archive should name those handoffs explicitly rather than invite flattening.

**Evidence anchors:** OECD’s current public-finance work frames today’s problem as high fiscal pressure requiring higher-quality budget institutions and processes rather than ad hoc austerity [BIB-OECD-PUBLIC-FINANCE-BUDGETS-HUB-2026] [BIB-OECD-QBI-INFORMED-SPENDING-2025]; OECD’s 2025 fiscal-federalism work keeps transfer and equalization design centered on efficiency, equity, stability, and adaptive institutional design rather than opaque bargaining [BIB-OECD-IGFT-2025]; IMF’s fiscal-transparency hub and code still treat disclosure of reporting, budgeting, and fiscal-risk information as the international baseline for public-finance legibility [BIB-IMF-FISCAL-TRANSPARENCY-HUB-2026] [BIB-IMF-FTC-2019]; and the International Budget Partnership’s Open Budget Survey continues to treat transparency, oversight, and public participation as measurable features of budget accountability rather than civic decoration [BIB-IBP-OBS-2023].

## Canonical reading order

### 1. Start with `07-fiscal-and-budgetary-governance.md`
Use `07` when the question is:
- how the fiscal state should be governed in the first place,
- what minimum legibility, reporting, and rights floors money power owes the governed,
- how budgets, deficits, and public accounts stay contestable,
- or how fiscal choices fit the archive’s broader person-facing governance model.

`07` is the **broad fiscal-governance front door**.

### 2. Move to `110-budget-procurement-integrity.md`
Use `110` when you need:
- budget readability and reason receipts for material allocations,
- procurement anti-capture mechanics and open-contracting defaults,
- variance discipline and off-cycle spending controls,
- or a way to treat budget allocation and procurement as visible power rather than back-office procedure.

`110` is the **budget-readability / spending-allocation / procurement-capture front door**.

### 3. Use `135-taxation-and-revenue-integrity.md`
Use `135` when the real issue is:
- the integrity of revenue rules themselves,
- replayable assessments, refund clocks, and bounded discretion,
- tax expenditures, complexity budgets, and no-surprise liability,
- or how revenue power becomes legible before it becomes coercive.

`135` is the **revenue-rules / assessment-replay / refund-clock front door**.

### 4. Move to `93-tax-and-revenue-administration.md`
Use `93` when you need:
- the operational pipeline for assessment, dispute, collection, and hardship handling,
- service standards and anti-retaliation in revenue administration,
- receipting for penalties, audits, payment plans, and collections,
- or a way to govern the actual administrative coercion layer instead of only the tax code.

`93` is the **operational revenue-administration / dispute / collection specialization**.

### 5. Use `18-intergovernmental-finance.md`
Use `18` when the question becomes:
- how the money map should align with the competence map across levels,
- how to avoid unfunded mandates, soft budget constraints, and bailout expectations,
- how vertical and horizontal imbalances should be named and governed,
- or how cross-scope fiscal dependence becomes legible and contestable.

`18` is the **broad intergovernmental-finance / vertical-and-horizontal-imbalance front door**.

### 6. Use `194-fiscal-federalism-open-budgets-and-participation-rails.md`
Use `194` when you need:
- open-budget bundles and participatory-budgeting rails,
- independent fiscal scrutiny and fiscal-risk statements,
- transfer and mandate receipts rendered legible to the public,
- or a bounded public-participation lane in fiscal governance.

`194` is the **budget-openness / participation / fiscal-scrutiny specialization**.

### 7. Use `217-intergovernmental-fiscal-transfers-and-equalization-rails.md`
Use `217` when you need:
- formula-first transfer design,
- equalization rules aimed at service capability rather than patronage,
- indicator governance, review cycles, and Transfer Commission design,
- or a sharper answer to how transfer systems avoid bargaining chaos and perverse incentives.

`217` is the **formula-transfer / equalization / Transfer Commission specialization**.

### 8. Use `97-public-investment-and-capital-project-governance.md` and `138-public-investment-and-capital-projects-integrity.md` when the issue is really capital allocation
Use `97` when the real issue is:
- capital selection, project appraisal, stage gates, and lifecycle fit inside the fiscal system,
- how maintenance-first and portfolio discipline shape infrastructure commitments,
- or how major projects should be chosen and paused before they become sunk-cost traps.

Use `138` when the real issue is:
- large-project integrity, change-order capture, benefit drift, and benefits-realization discipline,
- how megaproject promises stay bounded after approval,
- or how capital-project governance becomes auditable through delivery and change control.

`97` and `138` are the **capital-allocation and major-project neighbors** rather than the main fiscal routing spine.

## Confusion boundaries

### `07` vs `110`
- `07` is the **broad fiscal-governance front door** for budgets, public accounts, deficits, and fiscal rights floors.
- `110` is the **budget-allocation / procurement-capture memo** for readable allocations, variance discipline, and open-contracting mechanics.

### `135` vs `93`
- `135` governs **revenue rules, replayable assessments, refund clocks, and bounded discretion**.
- `93` governs **the operational pipeline** for assessment, dispute, payment, collection, hardship, and anti-retaliation.

### `18` vs `217`
- `18` is the **broad intergovernmental-finance front door** for money-map / competence-map fit, vertical and horizontal imbalance, and soft-budget-constraint design.
- `217` is the **transfer-formula and equalization specialization** for indicators, smoothing, review cycles, and Transfer Commission design.

### `18` vs `194`
- `18` is the **structural money-between-levels memo**.
- `194` is the **public-legibility / participation / scrutiny memo** for how those structures are published, inspected, and opened to bounded participation.

### `110` vs `22`
- `110` treats **budget and procurement decisions as money power** and emphasizes readability, reason receipts, and anti-capture mechanics inside the fiscal stack.
- `22-public-integrity-and-procurement.md` remains the **broader public-integrity / procurement-capture neighbor** when the issue is really integrity-system architecture, corruption risk, or procurement enforcement across sectors.

### `07` / `18` / `217` vs `157`
- `07`, `18`, and `217` govern **public budgeting, revenue, and transfer design**.
- `157-monetary-and-financial-stability-governance.md` remains the right neighbor when the issue is **monetary policy, financial-stability architecture, payments-system resilience, or lender-of-last-resort design**.

### `97` vs `138`
- `97` is the **capital-allocation / stage-gate / lifecycle-governance memo**.
- `138` is the **large-project integrity / change-order / benefits-realization specialization**.

## Safe merge rule for this cluster

Do **not** flatten this family into one general memo on “public finance”, “budgeting”, or “tax governance”.
The safer pattern is:
1. keep `07` as the broad fiscal-governance front door,
2. keep `110` as the budget-readability / procurement-capture front door,
3. keep `135` as the revenue-rules / replayable-assessment front door,
4. keep `93` as the operational revenue-administration specialization,
5. keep `18` as the broad intergovernmental-finance front door,
6. keep `194` as the open-budget / participation / scrutiny specialization,
7. keep `217` as the formula-transfer / equalization specialization,
8. keep `97` and `138` as the capital-allocation and major-project neighbors,
9. keep `22` and `157` as named integrity-system and monetary/financial-stability neighbors,
10. and use this memo as the bridge that names the roles.

That preserves distinct retrieval hooks while sharply reducing reader confusion.
