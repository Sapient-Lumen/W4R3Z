# Worked Example Intake Triage & First-10-Minute Questions


**Purpose:** help first-contact workers ask the few questions that reveal which example and protective move should fire first.

**Person served:** intake workers, navigators, advocates, ombuds staff, and supervisors.

---

## First 10-minute questions

1) What is the next concrete harm if nothing changes today?
2) What artifact did you receive, if any?
3) Who, if anyone, told you what happens next and by when?
4) Is there a live deadline, hearing, extraction, shutoff, removal, or service interruption?
5) Did the problem start at a transfer seam, vendor seam, access seam, or review seam?
6) Is the institution claiming a record, notice, or signature that it cannot actually show you?
7) What must remain live while review happens — money, housing, route, medication, counsel, safety, or accommodation?
