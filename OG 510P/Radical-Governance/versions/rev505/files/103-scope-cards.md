## Card format

- **Owns (primary jobs):** what this scope should hold as default responsibility.
- **MVG (minimum institutions):** the smallest legitimacy + capability set that makes “government” real here.
- **Dominant legitimacy:** which channel should mainly authorize this scope.
- **Decision-rule default:** how this scale should usually decide: consensus-seeking, ordinary majority, supermajority, or concurrent / double majority.
- **Selection default:** how offices at this scale should usually be filled: rotation, direct election, delegated representation, appointment, or a mixed selection path.
- **Tenure default:** how long authority at this scale should usually last: short-term rotation, medium fixed cycle, staggered tenure, bounded renewal, or non-renewable / anti-entrenchment design.
- **Office-status default:** whether the office should usually be volunteer, expense-reimbursed, lightly stipended, delegated / dual-hatted, salaried part-time, or salaried full-time.
- **Remedy default:** what review path should usually fit the scale: visible challenge, complaint handling, ombuds review, administrative appeal, tribunal / court, or treaty / compliance lane.
- **Geometry default:** what kind of map or governing geometry should usually fit the scale: membership map, walkshed, addressable municipal territory, county catchment, functional urban area, territorial region, constitutional territory, member-territory overlay, or planetary constraint surface.
- **Participation default:** how people should usually get into the process at this scale between elections and court cases: open meeting, public comment, participatory budgeting, representative deliberative process, petition or initiative, stakeholder-observer lane, or some explicit mix.
- **Artifact default / must publish (interfaces):** the few artifacts this scale should usually expose so people can *find* power, money, rules, and remedies.
- **Constituency default:** who should mainly count here by default: members, residents, regular users, commuters, territorial populations, citizens, member states, or future generations.
- **Finance default:** what revenue model should mainly fund this scale.
- **Default tempo:** which clock this scope should mainly run on.
- **Epistemic default:** what kind of knowledge and evidence this scope should mainly govern with.
- **Coercion default:** what level of direct force, detention, inspection, or emergency power should usually sit here, if any.
- **Escalation / coordination:** what happens when this scope fails or the problem crosses boundaries.

(Shared anchors: person path `98-persons-path-and-accessibility-invariants.md`; remedy `08-remedy-and-grievance.md`; interop/join-keys `70-interoperability.md`.)

## Micro-local (building / block / neighborhood)

- **Constituency default:** people in the place — residents, occupants, regular users, and immediately affected neighbors — rather than owners or informal insiders alone (`336-scope-constituency-defaults-members-residents-users-commuters-citizens-member-states-and-affected-publics.md`).

- **Owns:** immediate place-based order and care: small commons, neighbor disputes, micro-safety norms, visible maintenance, rapid coordination (`10-micro-local.md`, `24-mutual-aid-and-serious-incident-protocol.md`).
- **MVG:** a convening body with rotation + anti-capture rules; a small budget + transparent receipts; a named steward for urgent issues; a basic grievance lane with time bounds (see legitimacy patterns `21-legitimacy-architecture.md`).
- **Dominant legitimacy:** open participation, visible voice, and short-loop recallability rather than thick electoral personalization (`328-scope-legitimacy-defaults-voice-elections-delegation-dual-legitimacy-and-verification.md`).
- **Decision-rule default:** consensus-seeking plus simple-majority fallback for reversible neighborhood choices, with higher thresholds for exclusion, durable rules, or common-asset disposal (`337-scope-decision-rule-defaults-consensus-majorities-supermajorities-double-majorities-and-no-false-consensus.md`).
- **Selection default:** short-term election, rotation, or lot for steward roles; no permanent neighborhood bosses (`338-scope-selection-defaults-rotation-elections-delegates-appointments-and-no-prestige-elections.md`).
- **Tenure default:** short, interruptible, and rotation-biased — often about a year or one visible work cycle, with no open-ended stewardship (`339-scope-tenure-defaults-short-terms-medium-cycles-staggering-and-no-openended-office.md`).
- **Office-status default:** volunteer or lightly stipended stewardship; paid part-time only where the burden is clearly continuous and no longer occasional civic duty (`340-scope-office-status-defaults-volunteer-stipends-salaries-full-time-and-no-unpaid-government.md`).
- **Finance default:** micro-budgets, common maintenance contributions, and delegated neighborhood grants — not sanctions, exclusion rents, or unpaid labor masquerading as finance (`333-scope-finance-defaults-dues-fees-equalisation-shared-taxes-and-burden-sharing.md`).
- **Capacity default:** a named steward plus rota / convening support and a posted municipal backup path, not a freestanding local bureaucracy (`334-scope-capacity-defaults-stewards-managers-cadres-secretariats-and-no-hollow-shells.md`).
- **Default tempo:** continuous sensing, rapid repair, and frequent visible convening; escalate upward quickly when local capacity breaks (`329-scope-tempo-defaults-fast-loops-slow-commitments-and-review-clocks.md`).
- **Epistemic default:** situated observation, steward logs, and visible place-level issue tracking; local harms should become reviewable records before they become distant statistics (`330-scope-epistemic-defaults-local-observation-service-data-territorial-indicators-official-statistics-and-mrv.md`).
- **Accountability default:** visible challenge, short-term recall or rotation, and a posted upward complaint path to the municipal layer (`331-scope-accountability-defaults-recall-ombuds-audit-courts-peer-review-and-compliance.md`).
- **Remedy default:** visible challenge and quick correction first, with automatic upward routing to a municipal or parent-institution complaint lane when rights, safety, housing, or exclusion are touched (`341-scope-remedy-defaults-complaints-ombuds-tribunals-courts-and-no-dead-end-review.md`).
- **Geometry default:** building / block / campus / obvious neighborhood catchment with porous coordination edges — clear enough for responsibility, but not treated as a hard sovereign border (`342-scope-geometry-defaults-membership-maps-walksheds-service-areas-functional-urban-areas-and-no-cartographic-theater.md`).
- **Participation default:** open assembly, posted agenda, steward availability, and low-friction issue logging — voice should be face-to-face and immediate before it hardens into formal complaint (`343-scope-participation-defaults-open-meetings-public-comment-participatory-budgeting-deliberative-panels-and-no-spectator-governance.md`).
- **Coercion default:** no direct public coercive authority; rely on prevention, steward presence, and immediate escalation to municipal emergency/safety services rather than local armed power (`332-scope-coercion-defaults-policing-detention-emergency-force-and-no-shadow-militias.md`).
- **Must publish:** (a) who is responsible *today*; (b) meeting / decision receipts; (c) spending ledger; (d) how to file a grievance or request a review.
- **Escalation / coordination:** when harm exceeds local capacity or rights floors are threatened, escalation must be **legible**: a posted path to municipal/county ombuds + emergency contacts.

## Municipal (city / town)

- **Constituency default:** residents as the main political public, with regular users and many cross-boundary service claimants retaining standing where city systems materially govern them (`336-scope-constituency-defaults-members-residents-users-commuters-citizens-member-states-and-affected-publics.md`).

- **Owns:** baseline local services + local rights enforcement: streets, sanitation, housing-code enforcement, local permitting, local public health delivery, local safety partnerships; “front door” for most daily-life interactions (`20-municipal.md`, `09-public-service-and-state-capacity.md`).
- **MVG:** elected council/executive; professional admin; ombuds + audit; procurement discipline; usable local court/tribunal access; published rule inventory (`22-public-integrity-and-procurement.md`, `32-oversight-institutions-and-follow-through.md`, `25-legal-legibility-and-rule-inventory.md`).
- **Dominant legitimacy:** representative elections plus administrative fairness, with bounded participatory and deliberative supplements for place-based trade-offs (`328-scope-legitimacy-defaults-voice-elections-delegation-dual-legitimacy-and-verification.md`).
- **Decision-rule default:** ordinary council majorities for ordinary law and service operations; higher thresholds only for charters, boundaries, exceptional debt, or disposal of irreplaceable public assets (`337-scope-decision-rule-defaults-consensus-majorities-supermajorities-double-majorities-and-no-false-consensus.md`).
- **Selection default:** directly elected council by default; executive selection matched to the real city model; professional administration appointed on merit rather than elected for theater (`338-scope-selection-defaults-rotation-elections-delegates-appointments-and-no-prestige-elections.md`).
- **Tenure default:** medium fixed electoral cycles for representative offices; indefinite merit tenure for administration; staggered terms for continuity-sensitive boards (`339-scope-tenure-defaults-short-terms-medium-cycles-staggering-and-no-openended-office.md`).
- **Office-status default:** mixed — representative roles may be part-time in smaller places, but the operating core should be salaried, and thick city leadership often becomes functionally full-time (`340-scope-office-status-defaults-volunteer-stipends-salaries-full-time-and-no-unpaid-government.md`).
- **Finance default:** immobile local bases plus predictable transfers and equalisation: property/land taxes, rights-compatible service charges, and formula grants rather than fine-funded government or grant-chasing (`333-scope-finance-defaults-dues-fees-equalisation-shared-taxes-and-burden-sharing.md`).
- **Capacity default:** a professional manager plus frontline service staff, records/case continuity, and basic in-house procurement/finance competence rather than heroic improvisation (`334-scope-capacity-defaults-stewards-managers-cadres-secretariats-and-no-hollow-shells.md`).
- **Default tempo:** fast daily service loops, annual budget cycles, and multi-year maintenance / capital planning rather than one undifferentiated municipal clock (`329-scope-tempo-defaults-fast-loops-slow-commitments-and-review-clocks.md`).
- **Epistemic default:** frontline service data, inspections, parcel/address records, asset condition logs, and neighborhood observation rather than citywide averages alone (`330-scope-epistemic-defaults-local-observation-service-data-territorial-indicators-official-statistics-and-mrv.md`).
- **Accountability default:** complaints handling, ombuds, administrative review, local audit, and council scrutiny rather than elections alone (`331-scope-accountability-defaults-recall-ombuds-audit-courts-peer-review-and-compliance.md`).
- **Remedy default:** one city front door for complaints, fast frontline correction, ombuds or administrative review for unfairness and delay, and tribunal / court escalation where sanctions, housing, benefits, or rights are in play (`341-scope-remedy-defaults-complaints-ombuds-tribunals-courts-and-no-dead-end-review.md`).
- **Geometry default:** contiguous local territory anchored in parcels, addresses, streets, utilities, and service obligations, with annexation or split handled through explicit consultation and continuity rules (`342-scope-geometry-defaults-membership-maps-walksheds-service-areas-functional-urban-areas-and-no-cartographic-theater.md`).
- **Participation default:** public comment plus service co-design plus participatory budgeting where discretionary local spending is real, with citizens’ panels for dense tradeoffs such as housing, streets, siting, or resilience (`343-scope-participation-defaults-open-meetings-public-comment-participatory-budgeting-deliberative-panels-and-no-spectator-governance.md`).
- **Coercion default:** this is the usual frontline holder of ordinary administrative enforcement and, where assigned, civilian policing; it should prefer civil/code enforcement where possible and keep custody tightly receipted, bounded, and independently reviewed (`332-scope-coercion-defaults-policing-detention-emergency-force-and-no-shadow-militias.md`).
- **Must publish:** rulebook + instruments registry (`39-rulebook-and-instruments-registry.md`); budgets/contracts (`38-contracting-and-procurement-register.md`); service standards + queues; complaint/appeal lanes (`36-appeal-lanes-and-redress-registry.md`).
- **Escalation / coordination:** mutual aid compacts and cross-border cooperation are default for spillovers (`19-compacts-and-cooperative-governance.md`); rights-floor failures route to regional/national review.

## Intermediate local administration (county / prefecture pattern)

- **Constituency default:** residents across the covered localities, seam-crossing service users, and member municipalities as represented units in cross-boundary administration (`336-scope-constituency-defaults-members-residents-users-commuters-citizens-member-states-and-affected-publics.md`).

- **Owns:** “boring but critical” admin capacity and cross-municipal services: courts/admin services, public health, land records, child/family services, regional transit coordination where applicable (`87-intermediate-local-administration.md`).
- **MVG:** stable civil service; standardized service access across municipalities; a boundary-spanning grievance escalator; intergovernmental finance interface (`18-intergovernmental-finance.md`).
- **Dominant legitimacy:** delegated or indirectly authorized backstop legitimacy, upgraded to elections only where the unit holds broad general-purpose political power (`328-scope-legitimacy-defaults-voice-elections-delegation-dual-legitimacy-and-verification.md`).
- **Decision-rule default:** ordinary majority for shared administration and coordination, with qualified thresholds for forced local mergers, competence reallocation, or redistribution-rule redesign (`337-scope-decision-rule-defaults-consensus-majorities-supermajorities-double-majorities-and-no-false-consensus.md`).
- **Selection default:** delegated or indirectly selected leadership for narrow backstop units, upgraded to direct election only where the unit holds broad political authority (`338-scope-selection-defaults-rotation-elections-delegates-appointments-and-no-prestige-elections.md`).
- **Tenure default:** medium coordination terms, with delegated tenure often expiring with the parent office and staggered renewal where shared administration needs continuity (`339-scope-tenure-defaults-short-terms-medium-cycles-staggering-and-no-openended-office.md`).
- **Office-status default:** delegated or office-linked political roles plus salaried full-time administrative backstop; use stipends or per diems for bounded committee burdens rather than inventing a prestige political caste (`340-scope-office-status-defaults-volunteer-stipends-salaries-full-time-and-no-unpaid-government.md`).
- **Finance default:** shared taxes, delegated-administration funding, and formula backstop transfers that make cross-municipal services legible rather than discretionary (`333-scope-finance-defaults-dues-fees-equalisation-shared-taxes-and-burden-sharing.md`).
- **Capacity default:** standardized administrative cadres and shared-service backstops for courts, records, public health, family services, and cross-municipal continuity (`334-scope-capacity-defaults-stewards-managers-cadres-secretariats-and-no-hollow-shells.md`).
- **Default tempo:** steady administrative backstop work with fast escalation clocks and less dependence on theatrical electoral churn (`329-scope-tempo-defaults-fast-loops-slow-commitments-and-review-clocks.md`).
- **Epistemic default:** standardized administrative records, coverage maps, and equity backstop indicators that can compare municipalities without losing case-level traceability (`330-scope-epistemic-defaults-local-observation-service-data-territorial-indicators-official-statistics-and-mrv.md`).
- **Accountability default:** legality supervision, ombuds / administrative review, standardized audit, and comparator scrutiny across the service bundle (`331-scope-accountability-defaults-recall-ombuds-audit-courts-peer-review-and-compliance.md`).
- **Remedy default:** single intake with mandated routing across county-scale services, ombuds or administrative appeal for service determinations, and judicial review where legality, detention, or rights floors are at stake (`341-scope-remedy-defaults-complaints-ombuds-tribunals-courts-and-no-dead-end-review.md`).
- **Geometry default:** contiguous backstop catchment spanning several municipalities so records, health, welfare, emergency coordination, and courts do not bounce people across small local seams (`342-scope-geometry-defaults-membership-maps-walksheds-service-areas-functional-urban-areas-and-no-cartographic-theater.md`).
- **Participation default:** county-scale hearings, service-user advisory channels, and routed participation across fragmented municipal systems so people do not have to guess which town owns the issue (`343-scope-participation-defaults-open-meetings-public-comment-participatory-budgeting-deliberative-panels-and-no-spectator-governance.md`).
- **Coercion default:** may host courts, jail/custody administration, inspection, or emergency-support roles where law assigns them, but should avoid proliferating separate prestige police forces or shadow militias (`332-scope-coercion-defaults-policing-detention-emergency-force-and-no-shadow-militias.md`).
- **Must publish:** coverage maps (what is handled here vs municipal); transfer rules; performance + equity dashboards (tight, person-relevant) (`03-metrics-and-evidence.md`).
- **Escalation / coordination:** acts as the default “backstop” when micro-local/municipal units fail capacity tests; must keep escalation **fast**.

## Metropolitan + functional authorities (overlays)

- **Constituency default:** residents plus commuters, network users, and affected member jurisdictions / communities, because the relevant public here follows systems and flows rather than one city line (`336-scope-constituency-defaults-members-residents-users-commuters-citizens-member-states-and-affected-publics.md`).

- **Owns:** multi-jurisdiction systems with strong spillovers: transit, watershed/air, housing markets, major emergency coordination, regional labor-market infrastructure (`16-metropolitan-governance.md`, `15-functional-authorities.md`).
- **MVG:** a mandate registry + competence ledger to prevent buck‑passing (`34-competence-ledger-and-mandate-registry.md`); joint budgeting; conflict-of-interest discipline; specialized oversight with follow-through.
- **Dominant legitimacy:** delegated and compound legitimacy from member jurisdictions, thickened only when metro powers become strong enough to justify direct election (`328-scope-legitimacy-defaults-voice-elections-delegation-dual-legitimacy-and-verification.md`).
- **Decision-rule default:** concurrent or double majorities for mandate, budget, and revenue-power choices; weighted or ordinary majorities only for already-delegated routine operations (`337-scope-decision-rule-defaults-consensus-majorities-supermajorities-double-majorities-and-no-false-consensus.md`).
- **Selection default:** member-jurisdiction delegates as the normal case, with mixed or direct election only when metro powers become thick enough to justify a distinct mandate (`338-scope-selection-defaults-rotation-elections-delegates-appointments-and-no-prestige-elections.md`).
- **Tenure default:** delegated seats usually track originating local offices, while technical / oversight seats should be staggered rather than wholly replaced at once (`339-scope-tenure-defaults-short-terms-medium-cycles-staggering-and-no-openended-office.md`).
- **Office-status default:** delegated / dual-hatted political seats plus salaried operator staff; pay the shared-system work, not a separate metropolitan political class (`340-scope-office-status-defaults-volunteer-stipends-salaries-full-time-and-no-unpaid-government.md`).
- **Finance default:** formula contributions, shared tax slices, and network/service charges for genuine shared systems, backed by multi-year capital plans rather than one-off bargaining (`333-scope-finance-defaults-dues-fees-equalisation-shared-taxes-and-burden-sharing.md`).
- **Capacity default:** a thin but professional network operator and compact secretariat, not a duplicated all-purpose bureaucracy (`334-scope-capacity-defaults-stewards-managers-cadres-secretariats-and-no-hollow-shells.md`).
- **Default tempo:** slow planning, steady operator cadence, and fixed compact-review windows for shared systems (`329-scope-tempo-defaults-fast-loops-slow-commitments-and-review-clocks.md`).
- **Epistemic default:** harmonized cross-jurisdiction operational data, territorial indicators, and shared network models rather than isolated municipal dashboards (`330-scope-epistemic-defaults-local-observation-service-data-territorial-indicators-official-statistics-and-mrv.md`).
- **Accountability default:** member-jurisdiction scrutiny, joint audit, bounded appeal, and competence review over shared authorities (`331-scope-accountability-defaults-recall-ombuds-audit-courts-peer-review-and-compliance.md`).
- **Remedy default:** no-wrong-door intake, lead-case ownership across member jurisdictions, operator complaint review, and a bounded venue for competence disputes so the person is not forced to litigate the map first (`341-scope-remedy-defaults-complaints-ombuds-tribunals-courts-and-no-dead-end-review.md`).
- **Geometry default:** functional overlay — city plus commuting zone, corridor, basin, airshed, grid, or similar spillover footprint — layered across municipal lines rather than collapsed into them by default (`342-scope-geometry-defaults-membership-maps-walksheds-service-areas-functional-urban-areas-and-no-cartographic-theater.md`).
- **Participation default:** cross-jurisdiction participation — representative metro panels, user boards, and published consultation routes across the real footprint, not one-core-city town-hall theater (`343-scope-participation-defaults-open-meetings-public-comment-participatory-budgeting-deliberative-panels-and-no-spectator-governance.md`).
- **Coercion default:** direct general policing should usually remain narrow or absent here; metro/functional bodies should prefer coordination, inspection, and incident-support roles over stand-alone armed power (`332-scope-coercion-defaults-policing-detention-emergency-force-and-no-shadow-militias.md`).
- **Must publish:** who owns which failure mode (service breakpoints); cross-jurisdiction compacts; shared procurement contracts; incident registers when coercive powers are used (`43-enforcement-and-custody-event-register.md` when relevant).
- **Escalation / coordination:** needs strong “exit to higher scope” for rights and safety (regional/national), plus hard interoperability requirements (`70-interoperability.md`).

## Regional (state / province / land)

- **Constituency default:** territorial residents plus constituent municipalities / communities, with thicker plural-territorial recognition where the region is asymmetrical, islanded, linguistic, or plurinational (`336-scope-constituency-defaults-members-residents-users-commuters-citizens-member-states-and-affected-publics.md`).

- **Owns:** rights floors within the region; education and health system design; major infrastructure; environmental stewardship at regional ecological scales; coordination across cities (`30-regional.md`, `11-commons-and-ecological-governance.md`).
- **MVG:** regional legislature/executive; constitutional/rights review capacity; equalisation and standards-setting; independent integrity institutions.
- **Dominant legitimacy:** representative elections for a legislature plus territorially aware executive accountability and deliberative support for uneven spatial trade-offs (`328-scope-legitimacy-defaults-voice-elections-delegation-dual-legitimacy-and-verification.md`).
- **Decision-rule default:** ordinary legislative majorities for ordinary law, with stronger thresholds for autonomy settlement, electoral redesign, or municipal-order restructuring at scale (`337-scope-decision-rule-defaults-consensus-majorities-supermajorities-double-majorities-and-no-false-consensus.md`).
- **Selection default:** directly elected representative chamber, constitutionally typed executive selection, and merit appointment for sectoral execution and oversight roles (`338-scope-selection-defaults-rotation-elections-delegates-appointments-and-no-prestige-elections.md`).
- **Tenure default:** medium fixed terms for representative politics, confidence-linked tenure for parliamentary executives, and longer or staggered terms for review / oversight bodies (`339-scope-tenure-defaults-short-terms-medium-cycles-staggering-and-no-openended-office.md`).
- **Office-status default:** salaried representative and executive politics, with full-time professional administration and incompatibility rules strong enough that office is not quietly subsidized by outside patrons (`340-scope-office-status-defaults-volunteer-stipends-salaries-full-time-and-no-unpaid-government.md`).
- **Finance default:** broader tax handles, intergovernmental transfers, and equalisation strong enough to backstop weaker municipalities while preserving bounded local discretion (`333-scope-finance-defaults-dues-fees-equalisation-shared-taxes-and-burden-sharing.md`).
- **Capacity default:** sector backstop cadres, inspectorates, territorial planning teams, and municipal-support capacity rather than symbolic regional sovereignty without operators (`334-scope-capacity-defaults-stewards-managers-cadres-secretariats-and-no-hollow-shells.md`).
- **Default tempo:** electoral and legislative cycles at the surface, medium-term implementation below them, and long-horizon territorial review above them (`329-scope-tempo-defaults-fast-loops-slow-commitments-and-review-clocks.md`).
- **Epistemic default:** territorial statistics, spatial-equity mapping, sector indicators, and evaluation-backed planning rather than national averages or one-city proxies (`330-scope-epistemic-defaults-local-observation-service-data-territorial-indicators-official-statistics-and-mrv.md`).
- **Accountability default:** legislative scrutiny, regional ombuds/audit, legality review, and territorial-equity monitoring (`331-scope-accountability-defaults-recall-ombuds-audit-courts-peer-review-and-compliance.md`).
- **Remedy default:** regional ombuds, sector appeals, legality review, and administrative courts with interim protection where regional rules or transfers can cut off essential services or territorial rights (`341-scope-remedy-defaults-complaints-ombuds-tribunals-courts-and-no-dead-end-review.md`).
- **Geometry default:** durable subnational territorial region, sometimes supplemented by ecological, corridor, island, or development overlays where the base map cannot by itself carry the real planning or redistribution burden (`342-scope-geometry-defaults-membership-maps-walksheds-service-areas-functional-urban-areas-and-no-cartographic-theater.md`).
- **Participation default:** committee hearings, territorial consultation, and representative deliberation for long-horizon allocation questions where several municipalities or publics must share burdens and benefits (`343-scope-participation-defaults-open-meetings-public-comment-participatory-budgeting-deliberative-panels-and-no-spectator-governance.md`).
- **Coercion default:** can hold inspectorates, emergency command, major corrections or specialized enforcement where constitutionally assigned, but should not absorb every ordinary frontline coercive task by default (`332-scope-coercion-defaults-policing-detention-emergency-force-and-no-shadow-militias.md`).
- **Must publish:** standards + waivers; transfer formulas; enforcement guidelines; emergency powers and sunsets (`45-emergency-measures-register.md`, `74-sunsetting-and-deprecation-discipline.md`).
- **Escalation / coordination:** acts as the “rights backstop” for municipalities; escalates up to national when cross-regional markets/externalities dominate.

## National (nation-state)

- **Constituency default:** citizens for the main constitutional authorization story, but all persons / residents remain the basic rights-and-service public for ordinary administration, due process, and remedy (`336-scope-constituency-defaults-members-residents-users-commuters-citizens-member-states-and-affected-publics.md`).

- **Owns:** national rights floor; fiscal/monetary backbone (or macro-fiscal stance); national security with strict accountability; immigration/status; nationwide standards when needed for equity and scale (`40-national.md`).
- **MVG:** constitutional rule-of-law and independent courts; professional administration; national audit; election integrity; public knowledge infrastructure (`26-epistemic-infrastructure-and-public-knowledge.md`).
- **Dominant legitimacy:** general elections and party competition, constrained by rights review, public reason, and remedy rather than by one plebiscitary office alone (`328-scope-legitimacy-defaults-voice-elections-delegation-dual-legitimacy-and-verification.md`).
- **Decision-rule default:** ordinary majorities for ordinary governing; supermajorities, delays, or dual decisions for constitutional, regime-shaping, emergency-renewal, and similarly entrenched moves (`337-scope-decision-rule-defaults-consensus-majorities-supermajorities-double-majorities-and-no-false-consensus.md`).
- **Selection default:** competitive election for representative institutions, constitutionally typed executive formation, and appointment for bounded expert or neutral offices (`338-scope-selection-defaults-rotation-elections-delegates-appointments-and-no-prestige-elections.md`).
- **Tenure default:** medium legislative cycles, no default cap for confidence-dependent parliamentary leadership, stronger anti-entrenchment for singular executives, and long staggered terms for independence-sensitive bodies (`339-scope-tenure-defaults-short-terms-medium-cycles-staggering-and-no-openended-office.md`).
- **Office-status default:** salaried, functionally full-time representative and executive office; salaried professional administration; no thick national public office dependent on private wealth (`340-scope-office-status-defaults-volunteer-stipends-salaries-full-time-and-no-unpaid-government.md`).
- **Finance default:** broad taxation, pooled insurance, macro-stabilisation, sovereign borrowing, and explicit equalisation duties rather than offloading national floors onto local wealth (`333-scope-finance-defaults-dues-fees-equalisation-shared-taxes-and-burden-sharing.md`).
- **Capacity default:** a merit civil service plus specialist backbone corps for treasury, statistics, regulation, digital infrastructure, and crisis continuity (`334-scope-capacity-defaults-stewards-managers-cadres-secretariats-and-no-hollow-shells.md`).
- **Default tempo:** stacked clocks — fast administration and correction, annual budgets, medium-term planning, and slower law / constitutional maintenance (`329-scope-tempo-defaults-fast-loops-slow-commitments-and-review-clocks.md`).
- **Epistemic default:** independent official statistics, census and registry backbones, administrative data integration, and reproducible evaluation rather than ministerial performance theater (`330-scope-epistemic-defaults-local-observation-service-data-territorial-indicators-official-statistics-and-mrv.md`).
- **Accountability default:** elections plus legislature, courts, supreme audit, administrative justice, and integrity enforcement as a mesh rather than a single checker (`331-scope-accountability-defaults-recall-ombuds-audit-courts-peer-review-and-compliance.md`).
- **Remedy default:** ombuds plus administrative justice plus courts, with interim protection and restoration where national systems suspend liberty, livelihood, status, or access to essential services (`341-scope-remedy-defaults-complaints-ombuds-tribunals-courts-and-no-dead-end-review.md`).
- **Geometry default:** recognized constitutional territory with stable internal administrative and statistical geography, standardized geocoding, and explicit procedures for territorial or local-boundary change (`342-scope-geometry-defaults-membership-maps-walksheds-service-areas-functional-urban-areas-and-no-cartographic-theater.md`).
- **Participation default:** public consultations and petitions as routine, with representative deliberation or citizens’ assemblies for constitutional, electoral, climate, bioethics, or other long-horizon questions that party competition alone cannot metabolize well (`343-scope-participation-defaults-open-meetings-public-comment-participatory-budgeting-deliberative-panels-and-no-spectator-governance.md`).
- **Coercion default:** national government is the usual holder of the highest coercive powers — criminal-law framework, border force, intelligence, armed forces, and national emergency authority — while still publishing tight limits and independent checks (`332-scope-coercion-defaults-policing-detention-emergency-force-and-no-shadow-militias.md`).
- **Must publish:** national rule inventory and delegations; budget + procurement; enforcement and custody accountability; automated decision system registry where used (`42-automated-decision-systems-and-model-registry.md`).
- **Escalation / coordination:** must accept petitions from below on systemic failures, and must maintain lawful pathways for sub-national experimentation *without* rights erosion.

## Supranational (treaty/union level)

- **Constituency default:** a compound public of citizens / peoples and member states, with standing for affected persons even where franchise remains nationally mediated (`336-scope-constituency-defaults-members-residents-users-commuters-citizens-member-states-and-affected-publics.md`).

- **Owns:** cross-border coordination problems: trade and standards where needed, shared environmental constraints, migration compacts, cross-border competition policy, conflict prevention and dispute settlement (`50-supranational.md`).
- **MVG:** limited enumerated powers; strong transparency + review; representation that prevents domination by large members (`21-legitimacy-architecture.md`).
- **Dominant legitimacy:** dual legitimacy — member-state consent plus citizen/people representation, backed by conferral, subsidiarity, and review (`328-scope-legitimacy-defaults-voice-elections-delegation-dual-legitimacy-and-verification.md`).
- **Decision-rule default:** qualified or double majorities for ordinary shared-rule decisions, with unanimity reserved for accession, withdrawal, competence expansion, and other conferral-sensitive core changes (`337-scope-decision-rule-defaults-consensus-majorities-supermajorities-double-majorities-and-no-false-consensus.md`).
- **Selection default:** compound selection — direct election where citizens are directly represented, delegated state representation where member polities remain principals, and appointment / approval for bounded executive and review offices (`338-scope-selection-defaults-rotation-elections-delegates-appointments-and-no-prestige-elections.md`).
- **Tenure default:** fixed medium cycles for directly representative institutions, bounded multi-year terms for commissions / secretariats, and staggered tenure for courts, regulators, and review bodies (`339-scope-tenure-defaults-short-terms-medium-cycles-staggering-and-no-openended-office.md`).
- **Office-status default:** directly representative and executive roles are usually salaried and full-time; delegated member-state seats remain office-linked; courts, agencies, and secretariats are salaried professional work (`340-scope-office-status-defaults-volunteer-stipends-salaries-full-time-and-no-unpaid-government.md`).
- **Finance default:** assessed contributions plus narrow own resources where conferred, with bounded common borrowing only for genuinely shared goods and crises (`333-scope-finance-defaults-dues-fees-equalisation-shared-taxes-and-burden-sharing.md`).
- **Capacity default:** a bounded expert secretariat plus legal, review, standards, and implementation teams rather than a generic full-service state apparatus (`334-scope-capacity-defaults-stewards-managers-cadres-secretariats-and-no-hollow-shells.md`).
- **Default tempo:** slow conferral and competence change, scheduled implementation windows, and visible review / compliance cycles (`329-scope-tempo-defaults-fast-loops-slow-commitments-and-review-clocks.md`).
- **Epistemic default:** harmonized cross-national indicators, peer review, and disclosed comparability limits rather than scoreboard theater (`330-scope-epistemic-defaults-local-observation-service-data-territorial-indicators-official-statistics-and-mrv.md`).
- **Accountability default:** dual scrutiny by member states and common institutions, court/tribunal review where conferred, peer monitoring, and competence-bounded compliance (`331-scope-accountability-defaults-recall-ombuds-audit-courts-peer-review-and-compliance.md`).
- **Remedy default:** maladministration complaint lanes, petition or review channels where conferred, tribunal / court remedies where justiciable, and clear distinction between supranational acts and member-state implementation failures (`341-scope-remedy-defaults-complaints-ombuds-tribunals-courts-and-no-dead-end-review.md`).
- **Geometry default:** member territories plus competence overlays — customs, market, migration, environment, transport, funds, or judicial space where conferred — rather than one fake fused territory (`342-scope-geometry-defaults-membership-maps-walksheds-service-areas-functional-urban-areas-and-no-cartographic-theater.md`).
- **Participation default:** formal initiative, petition, consultation, and selected representative panels under dual-legitimacy conditions, with explicit publication of what is truly decided jointly and what remains with member states (`343-scope-participation-defaults-open-meetings-public-comment-participatory-budgeting-deliberative-panels-and-no-spectator-governance.md`).
- **Coercion default:** direct coercive power should usually stay narrow, treaty-confined, and judicially reviewable here; shared-rule spaces are not default general police states (`332-scope-coercion-defaults-policing-detention-emergency-force-and-no-shadow-militias.md`).
- **Must publish:** competence boundaries; dispute processes; enforcement limits; cross-border impact assessments; a public docket for scope contests (`54-subsidiarity-and-scope-assignment-test.md`).
- **Escalation / coordination:** must be able to bind members on narrowly-scoped collective-action failures, while preserving local democratic legitimacy through clear exit/voice mechanisms.

## Global (planetary)

- **Constituency default:** states, peoples, affected humanity, and future generations — not a fake singular world electorate, but also not a no-public technocracy (`336-scope-constituency-defaults-members-residents-users-commuters-citizens-member-states-and-affected-publics.md`).

- **Owns:** problems that are truly planetary: climate/ozone/biodiversity constraints, pandemic coordination, extreme-risk technology coordination, global financial integrity, humanitarian standards (`60-global.md`).
- **MVG:** legitimacy through plural representation + constrained mandates; independent verification capacity; funding mechanisms not captured by a few donors.
- **Dominant legitimacy:** treaty consent plus verification, finance, public reason, and affected-public voice rather than a theatrical global plebiscite (`328-scope-legitimacy-defaults-voice-elections-delegation-dual-legitimacy-and-verification.md`).
- **Decision-rule default:** consensus-seeking for universal framework commitments, with treaty-specified majorities or club pathways where universal unanimity would otherwise block urgent shared-risk action (`337-scope-decision-rule-defaults-consensus-majorities-supermajorities-double-majorities-and-no-false-consensus.md`).
- **Selection default:** state delegations for negotiating bodies, appointment for secretariats and expert panels, and advisory / review-facing participation channels rather than fake global executive elections (`338-scope-selection-defaults-rotation-elections-delegates-appointments-and-no-prestige-elections.md`).
- **Tenure default:** bounded multi-year terms for secretariat leadership, rotation or staggered renewal for committees and panels, and no open-ended diplomatic permanence where legitimacy would otherwise freeze (`339-scope-tenure-defaults-short-terms-medium-cycles-staggering-and-no-openended-office.md`).
- **Office-status default:** state-funded or office-linked delegations for political negotiation; salaried secretariat and expert-support work for ongoing administration; stipend or honorarium only for bounded advisory participation (`340-scope-office-status-defaults-volunteer-stipends-salaries-full-time-and-no-unpaid-government.md`).
- **Finance default:** assessed contributions, burden-sharing formulas, and pooled funds for global public goods and under-capacity jurisdictions rather than donor whim or summit theater (`333-scope-finance-defaults-dues-fees-equalisation-shared-taxes-and-burden-sharing.md`).
- **Capacity default:** a bounded secretariat with science, MRV, finance, and technical-assistance depth rather than a fantasy world bureaucracy or an empty summit shell (`334-scope-capacity-defaults-stewards-managers-cadres-secretariats-and-no-hollow-shells.md`).
- **Default tempo:** slow commitment formation, recurring stocktake / verification, and fast response only for tightly bounded emergencies (`329-scope-tempo-defaults-fast-loops-slow-commitments-and-review-clocks.md`).
- **Epistemic default:** treaty reporting, MRV, scientific assessment, Earth-system observation, and bounded early-warning systems rather than unverifiable summit claims (`330-scope-epistemic-defaults-local-observation-service-data-territorial-indicators-official-statistics-and-mrv.md`).
- **Accountability default:** reporting, verification, peer review, complaint/adjudication where accepted, and visible domestic follow-through mechanisms (`331-scope-accountability-defaults-recall-ombuds-audit-courts-peer-review-and-compliance.md`).
- **Remedy default:** domestic implementation remedy first, then treaty complaint, inspection, inquiry, or adjudication where jurisdiction exists; never promise a fake direct world-government appeal (`341-scope-remedy-defaults-complaints-ombuds-tribunals-courts-and-no-dead-end-review.md`).
- **Geometry default:** planetary constraint surfaces plus treaty-membership and implementation maps — atmosphere, oceans, biodiversity, orbital, epidemiological, or financial-network space — not a faux world district map (`342-scope-geometry-defaults-membership-maps-walksheds-service-areas-functional-urban-areas-and-no-cartographic-theater.md`).
- **Participation default:** stakeholder-observer, affected-public, and expert-supported input around state-led negotiations — visible and structured, but honest that this is not a directly elected world legislature (`343-scope-participation-defaults-open-meetings-public-comment-participatory-budgeting-deliberative-panels-and-no-spectator-governance.md`).
- **Coercion default:** global governance should almost never default to direct general policing; it should rely mainly on standards, verification, sanctions, consent-based peacekeeping, and domestic implementation (`332-scope-coercion-defaults-policing-detention-emergency-force-and-no-shadow-militias.md`).
- **Must publish:** global commitments registry; verification/audit results; exceptions and waivers; remedy channels for affected populations (including non-citizens).
- **Escalation / coordination:** relies on nested implementation (regional/national/local). The “global” layer should mostly set **constraints + verifications**, not micromanage delivery.

## Non-territorial & indigenous polities (cross-cutting)

- **Constituency default:** members and recognized community/public counterparts, with thicker external standing as delegated public power, service dependence, or coercive consequences grow (`336-scope-constituency-defaults-members-residents-users-commuters-citizens-member-states-and-affected-publics.md`).

Some legitimate governments are not “tiers” inside a single territorial ladder (e.g., indigenous nations, diaspora institutions, platform/community governance, professional bodies). Treat them as **first-class units** in the competence ledger with explicit coercion ceilings and interfaces.

- **Owns:** membership-based norms, internal services, and (where recognized) land/commons stewardship.
- **MVG:** clear membership and representation rules; remedy and appeal; fiscal transparency for shared funds; anti-retaliation.
- **Dominant legitimacy:** membership-based consent and recognized compact interfaces, bounded by coercion ceilings and external rights floors (`328-scope-legitimacy-defaults-voice-elections-delegation-dual-legitimacy-and-verification.md`).
- **Decision-rule default:** membership-fit majorities bounded by compact obligations, minority protections, and stronger thresholds for membership, land, or delegated-public-power changes (`337-scope-decision-rule-defaults-consensus-majorities-supermajorities-double-majorities-and-no-false-consensus.md`).
- **Selection default:** selection rooted in the polity’s own legitimate traditions, with more formalization where delegated public power, service dependence, or coercive effects grow (`338-scope-selection-defaults-rotation-elections-delegates-appointments-and-no-prestige-elections.md`).
- **Tenure default:** tenure should follow legitimate internal traditions, but duration, renewal, and replacement must become explicit as delegated public power or coercive consequence grows (`339-scope-tenure-defaults-short-terms-medium-cycles-staggering-and-no-openended-office.md`).
- **Office-status default:** follow legitimate internal traditions where the burden is genuinely communal, but do not rely on unpaid insider labor once the office carries delegated public power, essential services, coercion, or major public funds (`340-scope-office-status-defaults-volunteer-stipends-salaries-full-time-and-no-unpaid-government.md`).
- **Finance default:** membership dues, common funds, and compact-facing transfer clarity; no opaque quasi-taxation or exclusion rents for delegated public power (`333-scope-finance-defaults-dues-fees-equalisation-shared-taxes-and-burden-sharing.md`).
- **Capacity default:** steward / registrar / administrator depth should scale with the delegated public power actually held, with stronger records and external review as coercion or service dependence grows (`334-scope-capacity-defaults-stewards-managers-cadres-secretariats-and-no-hollow-shells.md`).
- **Default tempo:** internal clocks should stay short and reviewable unless the polity holds durable delegated authority or long-lived assets (`329-scope-tempo-defaults-fast-loops-slow-commitments-and-review-clocks.md`).
- **Epistemic default:** membership records, decision receipts, and compact-facing jurisdiction claims, with stronger external verification as delegated coercive power grows (`330-scope-epistemic-defaults-local-observation-service-data-territorial-indicators-official-statistics-and-mrv.md`).
- **Accountability default:** membership challenge and compact-facing review internally, with stronger external review as delegated coercive or public power grows (`331-scope-accountability-defaults-recall-ombuds-audit-courts-peer-review-and-compliance.md`).
- **Remedy default:** internal challenge first, but compact-facing external review must become explicit once delegated public power, essential services, coercion, or major public funds are involved (`341-scope-remedy-defaults-complaints-ombuds-tribunals-courts-and-no-dead-end-review.md`).
- **Geometry default:** membership map plus explicit territorial interfaces for land, service, mobility, and compact relations; do not force the polity into a standard territorial tier when it is not one (`342-scope-geometry-defaults-membership-maps-walksheds-service-areas-functional-urban-areas-and-no-cartographic-theater.md`).
- **Participation default:** membership-rooted participation with explicit consultation interfaces for affected non-members and neighboring governments once shared land, services, or delegated public power are in play (`343-scope-participation-defaults-open-meetings-public-comment-participatory-budgeting-deliberative-panels-and-no-spectator-governance.md`).
- **Coercion default:** any delegated policing, detention, discipline, or exclusion from essentials must be explicitly bounded, rights-floored, and reviewable from outside the polity (`332-scope-coercion-defaults-policing-detention-emergency-force-and-no-shadow-militias.md`).
- **Must publish:** enforceable norms + decision receipts; how to contest status/discipline; interfaces to municipal/regional/national services.
- **Escalation / coordination:** nation-to-nation compacts and shared stewardship agreements should be registered as `CMP-*` and remain contestable (see `12-identity-and-recognition.md`, `19-compacts-and-cooperative-governance.md`, and `197-polycentric-federalism-and-overlapping-sovereignty.md`).

## Two non-negotiable cross-scope moves (keep small)

1. **Anti–buck‑passing circuit:** every scope must publish a “who-owes-what” map for the top failure modes *and* an escalation path (`71-interface-obligations-by-scope.md`, `34-competence-ledger-and-mandate-registry.md`).
2. **Rights floors + functional equivalents:** allow diverse institutional forms so long as the **person’s path** and **remedy** are equivalent (`98-persons-path-and-accessibility-invariants.md`, `08-remedy-and-grievance.md`).
Common upgrades that apply at every scope: deliberation-as-infrastructure (`143-…`), data stewardship + assurance (`144-…`), and allocation mechanisms threat-modeling (`145-…`).
