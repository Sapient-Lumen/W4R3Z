# 291 — Public Health, Preparedness, and Biosecurity Routing Guide

**Purpose:** turn the archive’s public-health / preparedness / biosecurity cluster into a readable stack so cross-scope outbreak governance, public-health operating rails, research biosecurity, emergency-powers adjacencies, and communication/correction duties stop looking like accidental duplicates.

**Why this memo exists:** `57`, `234`, and `192` all surface for “pandemic governance”, “biosecurity”, “outbreak response”, or “preparedness” queries because they describe one family from different layers. Nearby `165` and `61` are adjacent cross-cluster memos that often become part of the same operational problem. The archive should name those handoffs explicitly rather than invite flattening.

### 1. Start with `57-public-health-and-biosecurity-governance.md`
Use `57` when the question is:
- what public-health governance is for across scopes,
- how surveillance, legitimacy, contestable restrictions, and care continuity fit together,
- and how outbreak response stays bounded enough to remain publicly trusted.

`57` is the **canonical public-health governance front door**.

### 2. Move to `234-public-health-preparedness-and-response-rails.md`
Use `234` when you need:
- the public-health operating packet,
- threat registers, triggers, evidence packets, scarcity allocation, and mutual-aid logistics,
- or a tighter preparedness / response artifact set for actual operations.

`234` is the **preparedness-and-response operating specialization**.

### 3. Use `192-dualuse-research-and-biosecurity-oversight.md`
Use `192` when the real issue is:
- risky research,
- laboratory and supplier controls,
- funder / publisher / facility oversight,
- or pre-event biosecurity governance rather than live outbreak operations.

`192` is the **research-ecosystem and laboratory biosecurity extension**.

### 4. Use `165-emergency-powers-derogation-sunsets-rails.md` when the health question becomes a general emergency-powers problem
Use `165` when you need:
- proclamation packets,
- derogation and renewal discipline,
- bounded emergency procurement,
- or a general rights-limiting emergency lane that is not specific to health alone.

`165` is the **general emergency-powers rails neighbor** for this cluster.

### 5. Use `61-public-communication-and-information-integrity.md` when the issue is trust, corrections, or official messaging discipline
Use `61` when you need:
- correction discipline,
- communication provenance,
- anti-propaganda and no-silent-edit rules,
- or stronger boundaries between public-health guidance and rights-affecting orders.

`61` is the **public-communication and correction-integrity neighbor** for this cluster.

## Confusion boundaries

### `57` vs `234`
- `57` is the **cross-scope governance front door** for surveillance, legitimacy, restrictions, and continuity.
- `234` is the **tighter operating pack** for preparedness, activation, logistics, and response artifacts.

### `57` / `234` vs `192`
- `57` and `234` govern **public-health operations and outbreak response**.
- `192` governs **research, laboratory, supplier, and publication-side biosecurity oversight** before or around an event.

### `234` vs `165`
- `234` is the **public-health specialization** with threat registers, evidence packets, and scarcity/allocation operations.
- `165` is the **general emergency-powers rails memo** for declarations, renewals, derogations, and exit discipline across domains.

### `57` / `234` vs `61`
- `57` and `234` govern **what public-health authorities do**.
- `61` governs **how official communication stays truthful, versioned, and corrigible** when those actions are explained or announced.

### `192` vs `168`
- `192` is about **biosecurity oversight in research and laboratory ecosystems**.
- `168-intelligence-and-secrecy-governance.md` remains the memo for **classified capability, covert authorities, and secrecy-governance architecture**.

## Safe merge rule for this cluster

Do **not** flatten this family into one mega-memo on “pandemic governance”.
The safer pattern is:
1. keep `57` as the cross-scope governance front door,
2. keep `234` as the preparedness / response operating specialization,
3. keep `192` as the research / laboratory biosecurity extension,
4. keep `165` nearby as the general emergency-powers neighbor,
5. keep `61` nearby as the communication / correction-integrity neighbor,
6. and use this memo as the bridge that names the roles.

That preserves distinct retrieval hooks while sharply reducing reader confusion.
