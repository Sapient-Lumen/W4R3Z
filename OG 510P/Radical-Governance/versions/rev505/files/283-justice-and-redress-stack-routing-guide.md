# 283 — Justice & Redress Stack Routing Guide

**Purpose:** make the archive's justice / grievance / redress cluster behave like a single navigable stack instead of four adjacent memos that readers can mistake for duplicates.

**Why this memo exists:** `08`, `36`, `76`, and `195` all sit on the same person-path. They are close in vocabulary because they are supposed to compose. The archive should therefore name the handoffs explicitly rather than force readers to infer them.

---

## The canonical no-wrong-door route

### 1. Start with `08-remedy-and-grievance.md`
Use `08` when the question is:
- what does a harmed person deserve,
- what makes remedy real rather than theatrical,
- and what person-facing floors must every contestation path satisfy?

`08` is the **normative and architectural front door**.

### 2. Move to `36-appeal-lanes-and-redress-registry.md`
Use `36` when the question becomes:
- where do I file,
- by when,
- what can this lane actually do,
- and how does routing avoid clock reset or forum ping-pong?

`36` is the **lane-discovery, receipting, and route-comparison layer**.

### 3. Use `195-dispute-resolution-escalation-and-odr-rails.md` when the path forks or escalates
Use `195` when the issue is:
- escalation order,
- channel choice,
- online dispute resolution,
- cross-boundary or multi-forum conflict,
- or how a case moves when the first lane is insufficient.

`195` is the **routing, escalation, and dispute-resolution operations layer**.

### 4. Use `76-systemic-redress-and-pattern-remediation.md` when the harm is patterned
Use `76` when repeated cases reveal:
- a recurring illegal denial,
- a discriminatory pattern,
- a design bug,
- a queue / delay pattern,
- or a need for rule / system change rather than one-off relief.

`76` is the **pattern-detection and structural-correction layer**.

---

## The person-path in one line

**Single harm** → `08` for remedy floors → `36` for the usable lane → `195` if the case must escalate or cross forums → `76` if many similar cases reveal a patterned failure.

That is the safe reading order for most people.

---

## Confusion boundaries

### `08` vs `36`
- `08` answers **what remedy must feel like from below**.
- `36` answers **how the remedy lane is published, named, versioned, and found**.

### `36` vs `195`
- `36` is about **discoverability and legibility of lanes**.
- `195` is about **movement between lanes, escalation thresholds, and dispute-routing logic**.

### `08` / `36` vs `76`
- `08` and `36` correct **individual cases**.
- `76` turns repeated individual cases into **pattern remediation and system change**.

---

## Retrieval guidance (what to cite when)

- Cite `08` for **principles, person-facing invariants, interim protection, and the meaning of effective remedy**.
- Cite `36` for **lane fields, route design, deadlines, receipting, and no-wrong-door routing**.
- Cite `195` for **escalation ladders, forum routing, ODR, and multi-lane case progression**.
- Cite `76` for **pattern recognition, recurring-harm correction, and structural remediation**.

If a memo or future addition tries to cover all four roles at once, split it back along this stack unless there is a compelling reason not to.

---

## Safe merge rule for this cluster

Do **not** destructively merge these four files into one long super-memo.
The safer move is:
1. keep `08` as the front door,
2. keep `36` as the implementation registry,
3. keep `195` as escalation logic,
4. keep `76` as pattern-remediation logic,
5. and use this memo as the canonical bridge.

That preserves salience while removing duplicate-reader confusion.
