# Transitional Justice & Reconciliation Governance (After Mass Harm)

**Purpose:** define *person-facing* governance interfaces for societies transitioning after systemic or widespread human rights abuse—so truth, accountability, repair, and reform are **legible, safe, and enforceable**.

**Person served:** the victim/survivor (and communities) who need recognition, safety, and repair **without** having to trust the institutions that harmed them.

**From-below:** you can learn what you’re owed (truth, repair, guarantees), file safely, see timelines and decision receipts, and prevent “reconciliation” from becoming a slogan that blocks accountability.

**Anchor set:** UN approach + rule-of-law toolkits on truth commissions and transitional justice: see [BIB-UN-TJ-GUIDANCE-2010], [BIB-UNSC-S2004-616], [BIB-OHCHR-TRUTHCOMM-2006], [BIB-OHCHR-TJ-2023], [BIB-ICTJ-TJ-PRIMER-2009].

---

## Scope fit (micro-local → global)

Transitional justice is **polycentric** by necessity: harms cross families, neighborhoods, institutions, borders. Design MUST specify *which scope owns which duty* and publish an inter-scope **handoff + dispute lane** (no ping‑pong). (See `197-polycentric-federalism-and-overlapping-sovereignty.md`, `114-interjurisdictional-dispute-and-coordination.md`.)

- **Micro‑local / community:** truth-telling and repair where victims can safely participate; memorialization; local service restitution; survivor support; community monitoring of retaliation.
- **Municipal / regional:** records access + custody, safe reporting, local reparation delivery, housing/land restitution operations, vetting of local security/justice personnel.
- **National:** legal frameworks, budget authority, prosecution capacity, national reparations program, institutional reform, guarantees of non‑recurrence, national archives/memory.
- **International / regional bodies:** support, monitoring, technical assistance, and (where applicable) complementary accountability/rights protection; safe refuge pathways for witnesses.

**Design rule:** every scope MUST publish (a) its obligations, (b) what it cannot do, and (c) the “next lane up” with a receiptable escalation path.

---

## The transitional justice “bundle” (minimum components)

A society can sequence components, but MUST not substitute one for another. UN guidance treats transitional justice as a set of complementary measures (truth, justice/accountability, reparations, and guarantees of non‑recurrence / institutional reform). [BIB-UN-TJ-GUIDANCE-2010] [BIB-UNSC-S2004-616]

Minimum bundle to publish (even if phased):
1. **Truth-seeking** (records + testimony + findings)
2. **Accountability** (criminal, administrative, professional, and/or other lawful processes)
3. **Reparations** (material + symbolic; individual + collective)
4. **Institutional reform / non‑recurrence** (vetting, oversight, training, doctrine, and structural constraints)
5. **Memory & education** (archives + memorialization + curriculum commitments)

## Interface obligations (MUST / SHOULD)

### TJ-1 A Survivor Front Door (safe, assisted, offline)
- MUST provide **non-reading** and **offline** intake (in-person/phone/paper) with interpretation and trauma-aware options (`98-persons-path-and-accessibility-invariants.md`).
- MUST publish retaliation protections, confidentiality limits, and witness support services; treat fear as a demand-suppression defect (`83-whistleblowing-and-protected-disclosures.md`, `03-metrics-and-evidence.md`).

### TJ-2 Evidence & Records Custody (no disappear-the-files)
- MUST create a **Records & Evidence Custody Protocol** with: chain-of-custody, redaction rules, access tiers, and retention commitments; publish a minimal public register of record sets without revealing sensitive contents (`31-records-foi-and-government-memory.md`, `77-sensitive-information-and-secrecy-governance.md`).
- SHOULD establish an independent archival custodian with tamper-evident publication logs where safe (see `51-release-registry.md`).

### TJ-3 Decision Receipts for “truth” outputs
Truth processes MUST not be vibes: they generate findings that affect rights, legitimacy, and policy.
- MUST issue **Decision Receipts** for: scope, mandate, methods, inclusion/exclusion rules, and final findings (fields per `31`).
- MUST include a **duty-to-respond** lane for implicated institutions (bounded time, public response register where safe). (See `111-deliberation-to-decision-binding.md`.)

### TJ-4 Reparations as an enforceable service, not a promise
- MUST define eligibility, evidence burdens, timelines, and appeal lanes (`36-appeal-lanes-and-redress-registry.md`).
- MUST publish a **reparations catalog** (cash/health/education/housing/land restitution/symbolic) and a delivery dashboard with privacy protection. (See `108-service-standards-and-time-budgets.md`, `99-protective-legibility-and-adoption-dynamics.md`.)

### TJ-5 Vetting & institutional reform with due process
OHCHR toolkits emphasize vetting as part of broader rule-of-law work; design MUST protect against both impunity and arbitrary purges. [BIB-OHCHR-TRUTHCOMM-2006]
- MUST publish standards for removal/discipline, evidentiary thresholds, and appeal lanes; include anti-retaliation monitoring.
- SHOULD separate **professional disqualification** (fitness for authority) from criminal guilt (courts), while enabling referral paths between them.

### TJ-6 Sequencing & “no fake closure” safeguards
- MUST publish a **sequencing plan** (phases, dependencies, budget) and a **closure test**: what it means to end a commission/program and what remains open.
- MUST define a **no-silent-swap** rule: you cannot replace accountability with “dialogue” without an explicit, appealable decision and published rationale (see `106-legitimacy-protocols.md`).

## Common failure modes (treat as design defects)

- **“Reconciliation” used to suppress claims:** programs that promote social harmony while blocking remedy, records, or safety. Counter: bind to TJ-1..TJ-6.
- **Elite capture / narrative laundering:** truth processes controlled by implicated elites. Counter: independent selection + transparency + duty-to-respond registers; integrity tests (`107-governance-test-suite.md`).
- **Victim burden shift:** making survivors prove what the state destroyed. Counter: state-held evidence retrieval duty + least-burdensome proof (`08-remedy-and-grievance.md`).
- **Retaliation & intimidation:** participation collapses because filing is unsafe. Counter: protected channels, relocation support where necessary, and active monitoring (`83`).
- **Archive loss / tampering:** records disappear during transition. Counter: custody protocol + external mirrors + public register of record sets (`31`, `51`).

## Minimal “TJ Test Card” (for program acceptance)

A transitional justice program SHOULD be considered **non-credible** unless it can answer, in one page:

1. What harms are in scope? Who decides? What’s excluded (and why)?
2. How can a survivor file *offline* and get a receipt today?
3. Where are records kept? Who can access them? What cannot be destroyed?
4. What are the enforceable timelines for truth outputs and reparations delivery?
5. What accountability/referral lanes exist (criminal, administrative, professional)?
6. What reforms prevent recurrence (and how are they verified)?
7. What is the escalation lane if the program stalls or is captured?

(Implementers can map this to the archive’s legitimacy protocol + test suite: `106`, `107`.)
