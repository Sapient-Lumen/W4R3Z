# Independent Regulators & Agency Independence Rails (Insulation Without Unaccountability)

**Stack relation:** use `297-state-capacity-appointments-and-agency-independence-routing-guide.md` for the canonical route across the state-capacity / appointments / agency-independence cluster. This memo is the agency / regulator-independence specialization; `09` is the broader state-capacity front door; `199` is the merit / workforce rails layer; `113` handles appointment / acting / removal integrity as a general power surface; `228` handles the appointment and board-governance packet itself.

**Purpose:** design independence so agencies can act objectively and consistently, *while remaining auditable and contestable*.

**Core claim:** “independence” is an engineering choice across **appointments**, **budget**, **information access**, **case assignment**, and **removal**. If any one is missing, independence can be performative.

## A. Independence surfaces
### 1) Appointment insulation
- fixed terms with staggered appointments
- explicit statutory removal grounds + published removal receipts
- conflict constraints + cooling‑off rules

### 2) Budget insulation
- multi‑year baseline funding rule or protected fee model
- transparent budget requests + denial reasons
- prohibition on targeted budget retaliation for specific decisions

### 3) Decision & case integrity
- assignment integrity (no “pick your judge/regulator”)
- record completeness requirements and publication integrity
- protection from informal direction; contact logs for regulated entities and ministries

### 4) Evidence and expertise integrity
- published methodologies and update discipline
- scientific/technical integrity boundary (no silent edits)

### 5) Accountability without capture
- ex post oversight with access to records
- performance auditing focused on *outputs and fairness*, not political compliance

## B. Minimal artifact set
- **Regulator Charter Card (`RCC-*`)**: mandate, independence grade, powers, prohibited interference, and review lane.
- **Appointment Rails (`AIR-*` join)**: link to `228-public-appointments-and-board-governance-rails.md`.
- **Budget & Funding Card (`BFC-*`)**: funding sources, formula, and retaliation safeguards.
- **Contact & Direction Ledger (`CDL-*`)**: logged contacts/requests from ministries, elected officials, and regulated parties (with limited exceptions for safety/security).
- **Method Change Receipt (`MCR-*`)**: when methods/thresholds change, publish delta + reason + monitoring.
- **Case Assignment Receipt (`CAR-*`)** (when discretion exists): why this case went to this team and how conflicts were handled.

## C. Anti-capture tripwires
Trigger mandatory review if any of the following occurs:
- removal or threatened removal outside enumerated grounds
- budget cut/disbursement hold correlated with an adverse decision
- repeated “informal direction” events without logged receipts
- high revolving-door rate into regulated entities
- unexplained method changes near politically sensitive deadlines

## D. Compatibility with democratic control
**Principle:** elected bodies set *policy goals and constraints*; independent agencies apply rules consistently and publish evidence.

Use:
- **Rulemaking & change control** for policy direction (`118-rulemaking-and-change-control.md`).
- **Observability & audits** for accountability (`183-governance-observability-and-public-audits.md`).
- **Appeals & redress** so independence doesn’t become unreviewability (`195-dispute-resolution-escalation-and-odr-rails.md`).

## E. External anchors (for grounding, not import)
- OECD framing on regulator independence (independence as a means to objective, expert decision‑making).
- OECD corporate governance guidance for state-owned enterprises (board professionalism, transparency, integrity).
