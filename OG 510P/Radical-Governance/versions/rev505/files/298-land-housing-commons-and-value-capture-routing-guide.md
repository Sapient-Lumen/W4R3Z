# 298 — Land / Housing / Commons / Value-Capture Routing Guide

**Purpose:** turn the archive’s land / housing / commons / value-capture cluster into a readable stack so zoning and permitting, parcel-level integrity, anti-speculation stewardship, and land-uplift recapture stop competing for the same conceptual role.

**Why this memo exists:** `62`, `136`, `150`, and `198` all surface for queries like “housing governance”, “land reform”, “anti-speculation”, “community land trust”, “land value capture”, or “how to keep public action from becoming private windfall”. They describe one family from different layers. The archive should name those handoffs explicitly rather than invite flattening.

**Evidence anchors:** responsible tenure governance and tenure-security implementation pressure [BIB-FAO-VGGT-2012] [BIB-FAO-SLTG-2026]; housing-policy integration across taxation, spending, regulation, and planning [BIB-OECD-HOUSING-TOPIC-2026] [BIB-OECD-HOUSING-REFORM-2024] [BIB-UNHABITAT-HOUSING-ROADMAP-2025]; land-based finance and land value capture as public-action recapture tools [BIB-OECD-LAND-BASED-FINANCE-2026] [BIB-OECD-LVC-COMPENDIUM-2022] [BIB-WORLDBANK-LVC-MODULE-2019].

## Canonical reading order

### 1. Start with `62-land-and-housing-governance.md`
Use `62` when the question is:
- how housing allocation, zoning, permitting, code enforcement, eviction-linked decisions, and land-use changes become auditable,
- what a person-facing planning / permissioning pipeline should look like,
- how displacement risk changes the design of permits, hearings, and review lanes,
- or how housing and land decisions become contestable instead of back-room bargaining.

`62` is the **land-use / housing / planning front door**.

### 2. Move to `136-land-housing-and-commons-integrity.md`
Use `136` when you need:
- the tighter operational artifact layer for parcel, housing, and commons decisions,
- joinable land-use / housing receipts, parcel or commons records, and displacement-continuity rules,
- complexity and legibility controls around land administration,
- or a compact integrity packet that turns land power into auditable interfaces.

`136` is the **parcel / housing / commons integrity rails memo**.

### 3. Use `150-land-commons-stewardship-anti-speculation.md`
Use `150` when the real issue is:
- which institutional carrier should hold land outside ordinary speculative markets,
- how community land trusts, shared-equity structures, stewardship entities, or other non-market carriers stay accountable,
- how anti-speculation rules, steward governance, resale formulas, and exit/fork discipline should work,
- or how to preserve long-horizon affordability and community control instead of only auditing transactions.

`150` is the **stewardship / anti-speculation institutional-carrier memo**.

### 4. Use `198-commons-externalities-and-value-capture-rails.md`
Use `198` when the question becomes:
- how public action that creates private land uplift is partially recaptured for public use,
- how land value capture, congestion pricing, depletion charges, and related externality tools stay legible and appealable,
- how rate-setting, exemptions, and earmarks avoid becoming a rent machine,
- or how “beneficiary shares” and “polluter / user pays” logic get turned into public fiscal interfaces.

`198` is the **externalities / land-uplift / value-capture fiscal memo**.

## Confusion boundaries

### `62` vs `136`
- `62` is the **broad planning / permitting / housing-governance front door**.
- `136` is the **tighter artifact-and-integrity layer** for parcel, housing, and commons interfaces.

### `136` vs `150`
- `136` tells you **how land and housing decisions must be receipted, joined, and made contestable**.
- `150` tells you **which stewarding institution can actually hold land outside speculative logics and how that steward stays accountable**.

### `150` vs `198`
- `150` is about **non-market stewardship and anti-speculation carriers**.
- `198` is about **pricing externalities and recapturing value uplifts when markets and public action interact**.

### `62` vs `198`
- `62` is about **who gets approval, allocation, enforcement, or review in land/housing pipelines**.
- `198` is about **how the fiscal consequences of scarcity, externality, and publicly created uplift are structured and receipted**.

### `62` / `136` / `150` vs `11`
- `62`, `136`, and `150` focus on **land and housing as person-facing governance surfaces and stewardship problems**.
- `11-commons-and-ecological-governance.md` remains the **ecological ceilings / biophysical commons neighbor** when the issue is watershed, habitat, airshed, or shared-threshold governance rather than parcel / housing administration.

### `62` / `136` / `150` vs `294`
- `62`, `136`, and `150` govern **ordinary land, housing, and stewardship power**.
- `294-climate-resilience-infrastructure-retreat-and-risk-finance-routing-guide.md` is the right neighbor when the real issue is **adaptation, retreat, relocation, resilient infrastructure, or catastrophe finance**.

## Retrieval guidance (what to cite when)

- Cite `62` for **zoning, plan changes, permitting, housing allocation, code enforcement, eviction-linked government decisions, displacement-risk handling, and appealable planning pipelines**.
- Cite `136` for **parcel and commons cards, housing receipts, displacement continuity, legibility budgets, and the operational integrity packet for land / housing interfaces**.
- Cite `150` for **community land trusts, shared-equity models, steward governance, anti-speculation rules, exit/fork discipline, and other institutional carriers for durable affordability / commons stewardship**.
- Cite `198` for **land value capture, special assessments, congestion or depletion charges, earmarks/dividends, exemption discipline, and anti-capture rate-setting around land uplift or externalities**.

## Safe merge rule for this cluster

Do **not** flatten this family into one general memo on “housing policy”, “land reform”, or “commons”.
The safer pattern is:
1. keep `62` as the housing / planning front door,
2. keep `136` as the parcel / housing / commons integrity layer,
3. keep `150` as the stewardship / anti-speculation institutional-carrier specialization,
4. keep `198` as the externalities / value-capture fiscal specialization,
5. keep `11` and `294` as named ecological and climate / retreat neighbors,
6. and use this memo as the bridge that names the roles.

That preserves distinct retrieval hooks while sharply reducing reader confusion.
