# 290 — Evidence, Statistics, and Publication-Integrity Routing Guide

**Purpose:** turn the archive’s evidence / statistics / publication-integrity cluster into a readable stack so minimal measurement loops, institutional evidence governance, program-level evaluation commitments, official statistics, experimentation, evidence commons, and publication integrity stop competing for the same conceptual role.

**Why this memo exists:** `03`, `28`, `142`, `184`, `190`, `202`, and `214` all surface for “evidence”, “metrics”, “statistics”, “evaluation”, or “learning” queries because they describe one family from different layers. Nearby `37`, `51`, and `53` are narrower component memos inside the same family. The archive should name those handoffs explicitly rather than invite flattening.

**Evidence anchors:** official-statistics independence and public trust [BIB-UNFPOS] [BIB-UNSD-HMONSS]; evidence-building systems and evaluation governance [BIB-US-EVIDENCEACT-EVALGOV] [BIB-OECD-GAAG2025-EXPOST] [BIB-OECD-EIPM-2025]; open-science and evidence-commons framing [BIB-UNESCO-OPENSCI-2021].

## Canonical reading order

### 1. Start with `03-metrics-and-evidence.md`
Use `03` when the question is:
- what a minimal public evidence loop should do,
- how measurement stays tied to correction rather than theater,
- and how waiting, omission, error, and retaliation become visible enough to act on.

`03` is the **person-facing measurement front door**.

### 2. Move to `214-evaluation-learning-agendas-and-evidence-governance-rails.md`
Use `214` when you need:
- a durable evidence function,
- learning agendas and annual evaluation plans,
- publication guarantees for findings,
- and evidence-to-decision joins that survive political turnover.

`214` is the **institutional evidence-system anchor**.

### 3. Use `28-program-register-and-evaluation-commitments.md`
Use `28` when the real issue is:
- a stable inventory of programs,
- testable claims attached to named programs,
- evaluation commitments and stop/adjust triggers,
- or how “forever programs” are prevented from drifting without proof.

`28` is the **program-inventory and evaluation-commitment layer**.

### 4. Use `190-policy-experimentation-and-evidence-rails.md`
Use `190` when you need:
- reversible policy experimentation,
- stronger causal designs,
- guardrail metrics and kill-switches,
- or ethics rails for piloting and staged rollout.

`190` is the **experimentation and causal-learning memo**.

### 5. Use `184-official-statistics-and-census-integrity.md`
Use `184` when the key issue is:
- professional independence of official statistics,
- census integrity,
- release discipline and anti-interference,
- or confidentiality as a precondition for trusted public measurement.

`184` is the **official-statistics and census-integrity memo**.

### 6. Use `202-open-knowledge-evidence-commons-and-scientific-integrity-rails.md`
Use `202` when the question becomes:
- how evidence used by government is indexed and made reusable,
- how open science and safe access ladders fit together,
- or how scientific integrity is protected against suppression or paywall capture.

`202` is the **evidence-commons and scientific-integrity memo**.

### 7. Use `142-metrics-and-indicators-integrity.md`
Use `142` when you need:
- Metric Cards,
- metric change and use receipts,
- anti-gaming design for indicators that allocate money, sanctions, queue priority, or reputation,
- or stronger governance for metrics as instruments of power.

`142` is the **indicator-governance subsystem memo**.

### 8. Use `37`, `51`, and `53` when the issue is narrower
- Use `37-claims-evidence-and-update-discipline.md` for the **claim, falsifiability, correction, and retraction layer**.
- Use `51-release-registry.md` for the **versioned release, method-note, and join-key layer**.
- Use `53-publication-integrity-and-tamper-evident-logs.md` for the **anti-silent-edit and as-of retrieval layer**.

These are the **narrower component memos** inside the same family.

## Confusion boundaries

### `03` vs `142`
- `03` is the **front door for minimal measurement loops from below**.
- `142` is the **stronger metric-governance memo** for indicators that actively shape allocation, sanctions, queues, and incentives.

### `214` vs `28`
- `214` is about the **durable evidence function**: roles, agendas, annual plans, publication guarantees, and decision joins.
- `28` is about the **program-level register and commitment object** attached to a specific program or policy.

### `214` vs `190`
- `214` structures the **overall evidence-building system**.
- `190` governs **specific experiments and causal tests** inside that system.

### `184` vs `202`
- `184` is about **official statistics and census integrity**: independence, confidentiality, methods, and release discipline.
- `202` is about the **wider evidence commons**: access, reproducibility, scientific integrity, and evidence reusability across the policy ecosystem.

### `37` / `51` / `53`
- `37` is about **whether claims stay falsifiable and correctable**.
- `51` is about **stable release objects and method/version joins**.
- `53` is about **tamper evidence, change history, and as-of retrieval**.

## Safe merge rule for this cluster

Do **not** flatten this family into one general memo on “evidence” or “statistics”.
The safer pattern is:
1. keep `03` as the person-facing measurement front door,
2. keep `214` as the institutional evidence-system anchor,
3. keep `28` as the program register / evaluation-commitment layer,
4. keep `190` as the experimentation memo,
5. keep `184` as the official-statistics integrity memo,
6. keep `202` as the evidence-commons and scientific-integrity memo,
7. keep `142` as the indicator-governance subsystem,
8. keep `37`, `51`, and `53` as narrower component hooks,
9. and use this memo as the bridge that names the roles.

That preserves distinct retrieval hooks while sharply reducing reader confusion.
