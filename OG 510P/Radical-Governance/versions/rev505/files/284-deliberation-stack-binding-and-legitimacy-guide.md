# 284 — Deliberation Stack, Binding, and Legitimacy Guide

**Family neighbor:** use `307-legitimacy-representation-elections-and-selection-guide.md` when the question spans elections, representation, election administration, legitimacy engines, selection integrity, and mandate design rather than the internal deliberation family alone.

**Purpose:** turn the archive's deliberation cluster into a readable stack so assemblies, civic lotteries, binding duties, and legitimacy arguments stop competing for the same conceptual space.

**Why this memo exists:** `88`, `111`, `143`, `159`, `180`, and `224` often surface together. They overlap because they describe one family of institutions from different layers: legitimacy theory, institutional design, selection infrastructure, binding-response rails, and operational artifacts.

---

## Canonical reading order

### 1. Start with `143-deliberative-systems-and-citizens-assemblies.md`
Use `143` when the question is:
- what deliberative systems are for,
- where citizens' assemblies fit,
- when representative deliberation is appropriate,
- and what the core failure modes are.

`143` is the **best front door for the deliberation family**.

### 2. Move to `159-civic-lottery-and-deliberation-infrastructure.md`
Use `159` when the issue is:
- representative selection,
- participant support,
- institutionalization,
- and the minimum viable infrastructure that keeps deliberation from becoming consultation theater.

`159` is the **infrastructure and institutionalization layer**.

### 3. Use `111-deliberation-to-decision-binding.md`
Use `111` when the key question is:
- how recommendations bite,
- what a response duty looks like,
- and how deliberation connects to an actual decision rather than a symbolic event.

`111` is the **binding and anti-theater kernel**.

### 4. Use `224-deliberative-processes-and-citizens-assemblies-rails.md`
Use `224` when you need:
- the minimal artifact set,
- concrete process rails,
- charter / draw / witness / recommendation / response / evaluation packets,
- or an operational blueprint for running a process.

`224` is the **process-rails and artifact layer**.

### 5. Use `88-deliberative-institutions-and-sortition.md`
Use `88` when you need a tighter memo on:
- sortition-heavy institution design,
- assemblies/juries/mini-publics as governed institutions,
- and design invariants for representative deliberation as a standing component of a regime.

`88` is the **institution-design specialization**.

### 6. Use `180-legitimacy-engines-elections-sortition-deliberation-recall.md`
Use `180` when the real question is comparative:
- how deliberation fits alongside elections, recall, and other legitimacy engines,
- and when a polity should compose multiple legitimacy mechanisms rather than rely on one.

`180` is the **legitimacy-stack comparison memo**.
