# Worked Example Recurring Review, Reopen Clocks & Grace Bridges


**Purpose:** make periodic review, renewal, recertification, and scheduled re-checks safe by default so previously validated protections do not vanish at the seam.

**Person served:** program operators, queue managers, engineers, case reviewers, advocates, and auditors.

---

## Review-seam checklist
Carry forward previously validated protections, preserve queue/priority facts, keep protections live while review runs, and audit drop-off rates at renewal, migration, and transfer seams.
