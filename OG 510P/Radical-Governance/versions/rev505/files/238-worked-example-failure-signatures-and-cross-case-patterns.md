# Worked Example Failure Signatures & Cross-Case Patterns


**Purpose:** name recurring institutional failure moves so the casebook can be used diagnostically across domains.

**Person served:** organizers, auditors, operators, advocates, and memo writers who can describe the symptom before they know the right domain memo.

**From-below:** this memo exists so lived harms can be translated into institutional patterns without requiring insider fluency.

---

## Failure-signature catalog

| ID | Pattern | It often sounds like… | Start with | Smallest believable repair |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `FS-01` | Hidden threshold / silent cutoff | “It just says ineligible / under review.” | `WX-01`, `WX-09` | require a person-usable receipt with consequence, owner, and next step |
| `FS-02` | Queue without owner or clock | “I’m waiting, but nobody can tell me who owns it.” | `WX-02`, `WX-28`, `WX-30` | publish owner + clocks and make missed clocks trigger escalation |
| `FS-03` | Boundary transfer reset | “They moved me and now I’m back at zero.” | `WX-02`, `WX-30`, `WX-32` | non-reset transfer receipt with preserved evidence/priority |
| `FS-04` | Participation without binding response | “We commented, but nothing shows what changed.” | `WX-07` | recommendation-level duty-to-respond |
| `FS-05` | Delay as hidden sanction | “Nobody said no, but waiting is ruining me.” | `WX-01`, `WX-10`, `WX-28`, `WX-44` | define missed-clock consequences and interim protection |
| `FS-06` | Access failure miscast as refusal | “I missed it because the path was inaccessible.” | `WX-28`, `WX-30`, `WX-31`, `WX-45` | no loss or sanction unless the institution can prove the path was usable |
| `FS-07` | Low-dollar debt becomes coercion pipeline | “It started tiny, and now I’m facing collections or levy.” | `WX-29`, `WX-37` | warning + hardship/integrity review before punitive escalation |
| `FS-08` | Account lockout manufactures multi-system noncompliance | “The portal broke, and other systems treated that as refusal.” | `WX-31` | freeze derivative penalties and provide an offline recovery lane |
| `FS-09` | Vendor seam privatizes public accountability | “Every vendor tells me to call someone else.” | `WX-33`, `WX-40`, `WX-49` | keep one accountable public or regulated owner across the chain |
| `FS-10` | Impossible conditions manufacture violation | “They punished me for a path that was impossible to use.” | `WX-34`, `WX-45` | prove feasibility before punishment can attach |
| `FS-11` | Reassessment logic silently cuts essential support | “The hours dropped, but nobody can show what changed.” | `WX-35` | preserve baseline support while the basis is challenged |
| `FS-12` | Halt authority is diffused or missing | “Everyone can explain the process, but nobody can stop the harm.” | `WX-36` | define who can pause the harmful action before final merits review |
| `FS-13` | Relief on paper does not propagate | “The judge or agency fixed it, but the old status still controls.” | `WX-37`, `WX-38`, `WX-39` | same-day propagation and proof of restoration across joined systems |
| `FS-14` | Representation continuity breaks at the decisive moment | “The hearing is here, but the lawyer/file continuity disappeared.” | `WX-42` | stop the binding event when representation continuity is unresolved |
| `FS-15` | Missing-record proof failure | “They say they sent it / had it / used it, but they cannot produce it.” | `WX-43` | no adverse outcome without state-produced proof or a protective default |
| `FS-16` | Temporary state hardens by silence | “It is still pending or provisional, but I’m already living the final consequences.” | `WX-44`, `WX-46` | force temporary states to escalate, continue, or decide |
| `FS-17` | Renewal seam wipes an existing protection | “I already proved the hardship / priority / accommodation, but review made me start over.” | `WX-47` | keep the last verified protection live until a fresh receipt changes it |
| `FS-18` | Authority chain cannot be proved | “I can’t tell whether the person who signed this was actually allowed to.” | `WX-48` | require every rights-affecting act to cite a valid authority chain or stop the act |
| `FS-19` | Delegated front door becomes hidden adjudicator | “They say the vendor only documented the interaction, but that entry already closed the case.” | `WX-49` | no abandonment / withdrawal closure without accountable public ownership |
| `FS-20` | Handoff drops support-state invariants | “The file moved and my interpreter / accommodation / safety flags disappeared.” | `WX-50` | treat high-risk support states as lossless handoff invariants |
| `FS-21` | Preliminary layer becomes shadow finality | “They call it screening or recommendation, but nothing higher ever really reopens it.” | `WX-51` | make stage boundaries legible and forbid execution before binding sign-off |
| `FS-22` | Same person, different rendering becomes no-match | “The script, transliteration, or identifier format changed and now I’m administratively unreal.” | `WX-52` | emit a mismatch receipt and preserve continuity while alias/transliteration review runs |
| `FS-23` | Duplicate resolution manufactures fraud or split harm | “One person got split across records and now the split itself is being treated as suspicious.” | `WX-53` | treat duplicate resolution as its own governed case and block punitive escalation |
| `FS-24` | Household graph spillover harms the wrong person | “The system linked the wrong people and now someone else is losing support or carrying the penalty.” | `WX-54` | publish household-edge and spillover rules, then keep dependents safe while review runs |
| `FS-25` | Severe false-positive match unpersons a live person | “A system matched me to death, detention, departure, or sanctions and everything shut off at once.” | `WX-55` | require a two-source severe-match rule and a same-day override |
| `FS-26` | Mailing/residence/safe-contact conflation routes action to the wrong place | “They had me, but they used the wrong address role or wrong unit.” | `WX-56` | separate address roles and keep the last safe contact path live |
| `FS-27` | Hidden boundary file becomes the real eligibility gate | “They say I’m out of district or coverage, but nobody can show the controlling map.” | `WX-57` | cite the controlling map/rule and preserve continuity while the seam is reviewed |
| `FS-28` | Wrong-place action from parcel/geocode ambiguity | “They acted on the wrong parcel, frontage, structure, or unit.” | `WX-58` | no high-consequence action on low-confidence place resolution |
| `FS-29` | No-fixed-address status is recoded as no-person or no-contact | “Because I do not have one stable residence, they act like I can’t be reached or served.” | `WX-59` | define reachability and location substitutes that do not require one fixed residential field |
