# Public Appointments & Board Governance Rails (Merit, Independence, and No-Crony Receipts)

**Stack relation:** use `297-state-capacity-appointments-and-agency-independence-routing-guide.md` for the canonical route across the state-capacity / appointments / agency-independence cluster. This memo is the board and high-discretion appointments specialization; `09` is the state-capacity front door; `199` is the merit / workforce rails layer; `113` is the broader appointment / acting / removal integrity memo; `229` is the agency / regulator-independence specialization when insulation after appointment is the central question.
Use `308-peaceful-transfer-of-power-caretaker-government-and-continuity-of-office-rails.md` when the central issue is not ordinary appointment integrity but appointments, acting authority, vacancy handling, or override risk during a transition / caretaker period.

**Purpose:** make appointments to public bodies *auditable*, *merit‑based*, and *capture‑resistant*—without requiring trust in insiders.

**Core claim:** if an appointment cannot be reconstructed from public artifacts (criteria, process, conflicts, reasons, and challenge path), it is an integrity incident.

## A. When to use
Use these rails for:
- statutory boards, commissions, oversight bodies, regulators, SOE boards
- senior civil service & agency heads where appointment discretion is high
- any role with budget authority, enforcement discretion, gatekeeping power, or agenda control

## B. Failure modes this prevents
- **Crony capture:** donor/adviser/party network appointments with thin justification
- **Silent conflict:** undisclosed financial/relational interests; revolving door
- **Opaque selection:** unknown criteria; panel overridden with no reasons
- **Independence theatre:** “fixed term” but removable at will; budget strangulation
- **Dead‑man boards:** members missing, unfilled seats, quorum games

## C. Minimal public artifact set
Keep this *small* and joinable.

### 1) Role & scope
- **Role Card (`RLC-*`)**: mandate, statutory basis, key powers, decision types, term length, removal grounds, disclosure duties, and required independence level.
- **Selection Profile (`SPF-*`)**: essential competencies, disqualifiers, conflict constraints, and evaluation rubric (weighting allowed, but must be stated).

### 2) Process & reasons
- **Competition Notice (`CAN-*`)**: role, criteria, pay/benefits, deadlines, accessibility supports.
- **Panel Charter (`PCH-*`)**: panel composition, independence constraints, recusal rules, scoring method.
- **Panel Outcome Receipt (`POR-*`)**: shortlist, top-ranked recommendation(s), summary reasons.
- **Minister/Decider Override Receipt (`MOR-*`)** (only if override happens): explicit statutory authority, reasons, and why the panel recommendation was not followed.

### 3) Conflicts & integrity
- **Conflict & Interest Packet (`CIP-*`)** per candidate: disclosures, recusals, managed interests, and cooling‑off constraints.
- **Appointment Integrity Receipt (`AIR-*`)**: final decision record linking to `RLC/SPF/CAN/PCH/POR` and `CIP`, with start date, term end date, removal constraints, and appeal/complaint lane.

### 4) Performance & removal
- **Board Performance Snapshot (`BPS-*`)** (annual): attendance, quorum stability, decision throughput, declared conflicts, and performance against the board’s own published objectives.
- **Removal / Non‑renewal Receipt (`RNR-*`)**: statutory basis, factual reasons, evidence pointers, and review lane.

## D. Independence grades (small taxonomy)
Use a simple grade to stop “independent” from meaning nothing:

- **I0 Advisory:** can be ignored; publishable reasons required when ignored.
- **I1 Operational:** day‑to‑day independence; policy set by elected authority.
- **I2 Quasi‑judicial / enforcement:** fixed terms, enumerated removal grounds, protected case assignment.
- **I3 Constitutional / rights guardian:** strongest insulation; heightened appointment + removal constraints.

**Rule:** higher independence grade ⇒ *stronger* appointment artifact requirements and *tighter* removal grounds.

## E. Integrity gates (non‑negotiable)
1) **Merit gate:** criteria + rubric must exist before applications open.
2) **No dark conflicts:** all conflicts are disclosed or the appointment is invalid.
3) **Override legibility:** overrides require a `MOR-*` with a challenge lane.
4) **Seat continuity:** prolonged vacancies or quorum manipulation triggers automatic review.
5) **Exit discipline:** no “quiet resignation”; departures generate a receipt with continuity plan.
