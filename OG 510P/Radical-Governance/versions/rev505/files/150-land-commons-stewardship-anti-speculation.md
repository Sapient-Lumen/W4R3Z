# Land & Commons Stewardship (Anti‑Speculation Rails)

**Stack relation:** use `298-land-housing-commons-and-value-capture-routing-guide.md` for the canonical route across the land / housing / commons / value-capture cluster. This memo is the stewardship / anti-speculation institutional-carrier specialization; `62` is the broad housing, planning, and permitting front door; `136` is the parcel / housing / commons integrity layer; `198` is the land-uplift / externalities / value-capture fiscal specialization.

**Purpose:** provide implementable *non‑market rails* that keep land and essential space **use‑first** (home, work, ecology, culture) rather than **asset‑first** (speculation). This memo is a companion to `136-land-housing-and-commons-integrity.md`: it describes *institutional carriers* (stewards) and the minimal records/controls that make them accountable and forkable.

**Core claim:** land is a *governance substrate*. When it is treated as a purely financial asset, scarcity + opacity + delay become domination.

**Person served:** a resident, tenant, worker, or neighbor who needs stability and voice against displacement, rent shocks, absentee ownership, and enclosure.

**From-below:** if you can’t understand who controls land, how decisions are made, and how to contest them, you don’t have rights—you have hopes.

---

## 1) Steward types (a small menu)

Use these as **carriers** for the protocol layer in `136` (PCR/LDR/HAR/DRP). Pick by scope and asset class.

1. **Community Land Trust (CLT)** — community steward owns land; residents/owners hold long‑term ground leases; resale restrictions preserve affordability and prevent windfalls.
2. **Shared‑equity / limited‑equity cooperative** — ownership form that encodes affordability and shared governance.
3. **Commons steward unit (CSU)** — chartered steward for commons assets (parks, forests, watersheds, cultural sites) with explicit duties, monitoring, and remedy.
4. **Public land bank / right‑of‑first‑refusal operator** — acquisition + holding + disposition under transparent rules (often municipal/regional).
5. **Indigenous / non‑territorial polities as first‑class stewards** — stewardship authority recognized through compacts (`136`, `103`, `19`, `136`).

**Rule:** treat these as *competing implementers*. People must be able to **exit / voice / fork** (`117-exit-voice-fork-federation.md`) and carry their rights/records across stewards (`109-portability-and-cross-jurisdiction-continuity.md`).

## 2) Minimal governance artifacts for any steward

A steward is legitimate only if it publishes a compact set of records that allow contestability.

### 2.1 Steward Charter Card (SCC-*)
A one‑page charter record:
- **Asset scope:** what parcels/commons are stewarded and why (link to `PCR-*`).
- **Duties:** affordability, ecological integrity, maintenance, anti‑enclosure, non‑discrimination.
- **Decision rights:** who decides what (board/assembly/lottery seats), quorum, and recall/removal.
- **Benefit rule:** who benefits and how allocation happens (links to `HAR-*`, `LDR-*`).
- **Exit & fork terms:** how a community can reconstitute the steward if captured or failing.
- **Audit & remedy hooks:** how complaints are filed, deadlines, and enforcement (links to `08`, `76`).

### 2.2 Steward Allocation Register (SAR-*)
A joinable table:
- asset → current use → allocation decision → responsible role → review date
- **Required:** the *reason code* for each allocation (`52-reason-codes-registry.md`) and the appeal path.

### 2.3 Steward Finance & Anti‑Capture Ledger (SFAL-*)
- budgets, loans, grants, related‑party transactions
- procurement links (`22`, `38`, `110`)
- donor / influence disclosures (`46-influence-and-interests-register.md`)
- conflicts and recusals (`79-conflict-of-interest-and-revolving-door-discipline.md`)

### 2.4 Steward Performance & Risk Bundle (SPRB-*)
A small bundle of *leading indicators* with Goodhart protection (use MIIP `142`):
- displacement risk flags; vacancy; rent stress
- repair backlog / safety
- governance participation and grievance throughput
- ecological indicators for commons (where relevant)

## 3) Anti‑speculation primitives (the “rails”)

Pick a few primitives and bind them with receipts and remedies; do not rely on “good intentions”.

### A) Permanent affordability via ground‑lease / resale constraints
- CLT ground leases + resale formulas prevent windfall capture while allowing equity building for residents.

### B) Land value capture (LVC) for public benefit
Capture uplifts created by public action (zoning, infrastructure) and reinvest in housing/commons:
- betterment levies, impact fees, inclusionary tools, land readjustment/pooling (context‑fit).

### C) Land value taxation / property tax reform (use‑first incentives)
Land value taxation is often argued to be efficient and can reduce incentives to hold land idle; distributional design still matters.

### D) Vacancy/anti‑hoarding tools
- vacancy taxes, progressive surtaxes for long‑vacant or under‑used property
- “use it or lose it” for developable land (with equity safeguards)

### E) Acquisition priority + right of first refusal
- tenant / community purchase rights, municipal ROFR, public land banking
- pair with financing and time‑budgeted process clocks (`108`, `135`, `136`)

### F) Zoning as a rights interface (not a permit lottery)
- treat zoning changes as rights changes with **reason codes**, **notice**, **appeal**, and **change-control receipts** (`118`, `52`)

**Implementation discipline:** every primitive must be bound to:
- **a registry** (what assets, what constraints),
- **a receipt** (why this decision, who decided, what evidence),
- **a time budget** (decision clocks),
- **a remedy** (appeal + reversal + compensation rules).

## 4) Failure modes & circuit breakers

Common failure modes (and required breakers):
- **Capture** (developer/elite takeover) → board composition rules + recusal enforcement + fork clause + external audits.
- **Token participation** → deliberation pipeline with binding path (`143`) and clear “what changed because of you” receipts.
- **Under‑maintenance** → asset integrity registers + restoration clocks (`137`) and budget floors.
- **Exclusion / discrimination** → allocation rules published + audit sampling + fast remedy.
- **Complexity as a moat** → legibility/complexity budgets (`134`) and plain‑language requirements.

---

## 5) Scope fit and compacts

Stewardship is often *polycentric*:
- micro‑local stewards manage use and allocation;
- municipal/regional authorities manage finance, infrastructure, equalisation, and dispute resolution;
- national level sets floors (anti‑displacement rights, tax rules, transparency);
- international level provides finance and norms (housing, land governance).

Use `197-polycentric-federalism-and-overlapping-sovereignty.md` and `19-compacts-and-cooperative-governance.md` to bind this.
