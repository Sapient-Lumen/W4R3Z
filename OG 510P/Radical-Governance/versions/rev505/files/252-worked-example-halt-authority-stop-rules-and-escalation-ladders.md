# Worked Example Halt Authority, Stop Rules & Escalation Ladders


**Purpose:** answer the hardest operational question: when the machine is already hurting someone, who can actually halt it and on what rule?

**Person served:** supervisors, intake teams, field operators, advocates, judges, and ombuds staff.

---

## Stop-rule ladder

| Harm in motion | Minimum halt owner | Portable proof |
|---|---|---|
| eviction / lockout / shutoff | office with power over last harmful edge | halt-state receipt |
| levy / garnishment / offset | collections owner with channel-wide stop authority | extraction-hold receipt |
| binding hearing with broken counsel continuity | hearing owner / court operations | continuance or stay receipt |
| service suspension after reversal | service owner or provider-facing override desk | restoration or override proof |
| invalid signatory or shadow finality | supervisor or binding owner above the defective stage | authority/stage defect receipt |
