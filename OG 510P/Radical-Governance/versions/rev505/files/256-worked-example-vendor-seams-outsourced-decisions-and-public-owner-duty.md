# Worked Example Vendor Seams, Outsourced Decisions & Public Owner Duty


**Purpose:** prevent outsourcing from erasing public accountability.

**Person served:** contract managers, regulators, advocates, auditors, engineers, and operators.

---

## Public-owner duty
If a vendor portal, call center, subcontractor, or PBM materially shapes a rights-affecting outcome, one public or regulated owner must still be visible in the person-facing trace. “Call the vendor” is never the complete answer.
