# 295 — Public Information, Media, and Access Routing Guide

**Purpose:** turn the archive’s public-information cluster into a readable stack so public-sphere integrity, media/pluralism governance, official communication discipline, requester-side access-to-documents, and secrecy boundaries stop competing for the same conceptual role.

**Why this memo exists:** `129`, `161`, `61`, and `200` all surface for “misinformation governance”, “media freedom”, “public communication”, “freedom of information”, or “access to government information” queries because they describe one family from different layers. Nearby `77` is the secrecy / withholding boundary when the question becomes exception design rather than openness by default. Nearby `115`, `31`, and `53` are the record / provenance / publication-integrity substrate that the whole cluster depends on. The archive should name those handoffs explicitly rather than invite flattening.

**Evidence anchors:** current freedom-of-expression and media-freedom framing [BIB-OHCHR-FOE-HUB-2026] [BIB-COE-FOE-HUB-2026] [BIB-OSCE-MEDIA-FREEDOM-HUB-2025]; current access-to-information framing [BIB-COE-TROMSO-HUB-2026] [BIB-UNESCO-ATI-HUB-2025]; current public-good / media-pluralism framing [BIB-UNESCO-MEDIA-PLURALISM-HUB-2026] [BIB-UNESCO-INTERNET-SHUTDOWNS-2026].

## Canonical reading order

### 1. Start with `129-public-sphere-and-epistemic-infrastructure.md`
Use `129` when the question is:
- how a society keeps shared facts, corrections, amplification, and safety-to-speak governable,
- how manipulation, harassment, and opaque ranking distort democratic choice,
- or how the public sphere itself should be treated as critical infrastructure.

`129` is the **canonical public-sphere and epistemic-integrity front door**.

### 2. Move to `161-information-commons-and-public-media-governance.md`
Use `161` when you need:
- media-pluralism and ownership-transparency design,
- platform systemic-risk duties, ad-market transparency, and public-interest-media capacity,
- or the governance problem is ecosystem structure rather than one official message or one ATI request.

`161` is the **media-pluralism / platform-governance specialization**.

### 3. Use `61-public-communication-and-information-integrity.md`
Use `61` when the real issue is:
- official guidance provenance,
- correction discipline and no-silent-edit rules for public statements,
- crisis communication integrity,
- or the boundary between advisory communication and rights-affecting orders.

`61` is the **official-communication and correction-integrity specialization**.

### 4. Use `200-freedom-of-information-and-access-to-official-documents-rails.md`
Use `200` when you need:
- ATI / FOI intake, routing, clocks, refusals, and appeals,
- proactive publication duties,
- or a person-facing lane to obtain and contest access to official documents.

`200` is the **requester-side openness and access-to-documents specialization**.

### 5. Use `77-sensitive-information-and-secrecy-governance.md` when the problem becomes secrecy-boundary design
Use `77` when you need:
- withholding/classification receipts,
- narrow exceptions, declassification review, and oversight access to unredacted material,
- or the issue is not “how should information be public?” but “when may it lawfully stay secret?”

`77` is the **secrecy and withholding boundary neighbor**.

### 6. Use `115-information-integrity-and-record-interfaces.md`, `31-records-foi-and-government-memory.md`, and `53-publication-integrity-and-tamper-evident-logs.md` when the issue is records, provenance, or tamper-evident publication
Use `115` / `31` / `53` when you need:
- stable record pointers,
- retention and disclosure clocks,
- or no-silent-edit / as-of / publication-integrity guarantees that support the cluster without replacing it.

These memos are the **record / provenance / publication-integrity substrate** for the whole family.

## Confusion boundaries

### `129` vs `161`
- `129` is the **public-sphere integrity front door** for amplification, corrections, manipulation, and safety-to-speak.
- `161` is the **ecosystem-structure specialization** for media pluralism, ownership transparency, platform systemic risk, ads, and public-interest-media capacity.

### `61` vs `129`
- `61` governs **what official communicators must do**: provenance, updates, corrections, uncertainty, and the no-decision-by-press-release rule.
- `129` governs **the broader information environment** in which claims propagate, are challenged, or are amplified.

### `61` vs `161`
- `61` is about **official communication integrity**.
- `161` is about **the wider media/platform/publisher ecosystem and its incentives**.

### `200` vs `77`
- `200` is about **how a requester asks for official documents, receives a refusal, and contests it**.
- `77` is about **how secrecy and withholding are bounded, reviewed, and made legible even when payloads cannot be public**.

### `200` vs `115` / `31`
- `200` is the **person-facing access lane**.
- `115` / `31` are the **recordkeeping and disclosure substrate** that make that lane usable at all.

### `129` / `161` / `61` vs `53`
- `129`, `161`, and `61` govern **speech, communication, and information-environment structure**.
- `53` governs **tamper-evident publication and no-silent-edit integrity**, which supports those domains without replacing them.

- Cite `200` for **ATI / FOI requests, refusals, proactive disclosure, processing clocks, and requester-side review lanes**.
- Cite `77` for **withholding receipts, secrecy scope/duration, declassification review, and oversight access to unredacted materials**.
- Cite `115` / `31` / `53` when the real issue is **record continuity, stable pointers, retention, as-of access, and tamper-evident publication guarantees**.

## Safe merge rule for this cluster

Do **not** flatten this family into one memo on “information integrity governance”.
The safer pattern is:
1. keep `129` as the public-sphere integrity front door,
2. keep `161` as the media / platform / pluralism specialization,
3. keep `61` as the official-communication and correction-integrity specialization,
4. keep `200` as the requester-side access-to-documents specialization,
5. keep `77` nearby as the secrecy-boundary memo,
6. keep `115` / `31` / `53` nearby as the records and provenance substrate,
7. and use this memo as the bridge that names the roles.

That preserves distinct retrieval hooks while making the cluster substantially easier to enter.
