# Public Service & State Capacity (Merit, Skills, Continuity)

Many “ideal constitutions” fail because the state cannot **execute** decisions without patronage, chaos, or vendor capture. This memo defines a minimal *public service capability* baseline.

**Anchor set:** see [BIB-OECD-PSLC].

---

## A. Minimum viable public service (MVPS)
1) **Merit and non-partisan administration**
- MUST: merit-based recruitment and promotion; clear job classifications; transparent pay bands.
- MUST: limits on political appointments and “acting” roles in core delivery/oversight functions.

2) **Leadership and capability (fit-for-purpose)**
- SHOULD: workforce planning; competency framework; training pipeline; mobility across departments.
- SHOULD: “skills core” for procurement, digital, data, evaluation, and frontline management.

3) **Ethics and protection**
- MUST: conflict-of-interest rules; asset disclosure for high-risk roles; procurement integrity controls (see `22-public-integrity-and-procurement.md`).
- MUST: whistleblower protection and retaliation monitoring (UNCAC supports this baseline).
- Optional comparative reference: EU whistleblower directive text (Directive (EU) 2019/1937) [BIB-EU-WHISTLE-2019]

4) **Operational continuity**
- MUST: records management; continuity plans for critical services; clear delegation and decision logs.
- SHOULD: post-incident learning loop (after-action review) integrated into training.

5) **Contracting without “hollow state”**
- MUST: capacity to specify, audit, and exit vendor contracts (no dependency on a single supplier for core sovereignty functions).
- SHOULD: open standards + portability clauses; public system register for high-stakes systems (`IOP-5`).

6) **Service legibility (reduce procedural cruelty)**
- MUST: publish and maintain a Service Catalog & Access Journeys Register keyed by `SRV-*` for core public services; keep versions/as-of aligned with `RULE-*` (see `47-...`, `39-...`).
- MUST: service-facing denials and restrictions issue `DRR` receipts citing `SRV-*` + `RULE-*` (as-of) + `AL-*` (see `47-...`, `08-...`, `31-...`).
- SHOULD: measure and reduce administrative burden (fold into CAD-2 burden indicators) as a first-class capacity metric; treat burden as a design choice, not an accident. See [BIB-RSF-ADMINBURDEN-2018], [BIB-OECD-GPP-SERVICE-2022], [BIB-UK-SERVICESTANDARD].

---

## B. Failure modes (and the smallest countermeasure)
- **Patronage / politicization:** hard boundary for political appointments + transparent hiring + independent appeals.
- **Churn and brain drain:** pay transparency, career pathways, and training; protect technical roles from political cycles.
- **Vendor capture:** procurement competence + auditability + exit plans (`OPEN-2`, `IOP-3/5`).
- **Administrative burden:** simplify forms and eligibility rules; measure “time tax” on residents.

---

## C. Minimal metrics (portable)
Prefer metric IDs from `03-metrics-and-evidence.md` packs; keep ≤10 total.
- time-to-hire / critical vacancy rate [CAD-4] + churn/attrition in key functions [CAD-5]
- training completion for procurement/digital/frontline management [CAD-8]
- procurement competitiveness (single-bid share) [IPM-2] (+ vendor concentration where feasible) [IPM-3]
- service delivery reliability (uptime/outage) [CAD-1] + response/queue times [CAD-2]
