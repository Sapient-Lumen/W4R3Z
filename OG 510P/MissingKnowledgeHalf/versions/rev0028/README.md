## When a lesson reaches the next decision

A warning can survive in an archive and still fail to reach the person making the next consequential choice. This edition carries a useful way to read that distance: follow a correction from its first durable record to the practice it was supposed to change.

Begin with [Correction memory and correction infusion](files/MissingKnowledgeHalf-rev0028-2026.05.28.16.48-maritimecasualties-watertightrails-transportstateaudit/docs/60-synthesis/correction-memory-infusion-crosscase-memo-rev0017.md). Its seven stages separate capture, discovery, interpretation, authority, entry into practice, verification and eventual decay. Imagine a recommendation that is easy to find but never enters a procurement requirement. Its visibility is a real achievement; the design change remains unestablished. Conversely, a checklist can incorporate a lesson while gradually becoming a ritual whose users no longer understand the conditions that made it useful.

[Practice lag and de-adoption](files/MissingKnowledgeHalf-rev0028-2026.05.28.16.48-maritimecasualties-watertightrails-transportstateaudit/docs/10-method/practice-lag-and-de-adoption.md) makes the distinction concrete in research uptake. It separates the claim tested and the outcome measured from later guidelines, reimbursement, conversations and evidence of actual use. A negative result in the literature does not show that the practice disappeared. Nor does a broad reversal settle every narrower indication: the method keeps exceptions visible rather than converting a correction into a new universal slogan.

Read the two short texts together. The first asks where a lesson can be lost; the second shows why evidence against a claim and evidence of changed behavior need separate records. The cross-case memo also keeps its own proposed pattern at candidate status because its evidence in that lane is thin. That restraint is part of the inquiry: a framework for asking better questions has not already answered them.

Continue through the [full project guide](../../README.md) for the marine cases, replication methods and genealogy work carried beside this question.
