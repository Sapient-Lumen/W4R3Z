## Non-negotiables

- Do not promote a seed mention into a factual record without source acquisition.
- Do not collapse null finding, underpowered null, failed method, failed replication, engineering failure, fraud, and genealogy into one undifferentiated “failure” bucket.
- Do not frame the archive as a wall of shame. The object is learning, correction, recurrence detection, and epistemic humility.
- Keep source permanence visible: DOI/registry/official archive/retrieval/hash/copyability/sensitivity.
- Separate claim status from source existence. A source can exist and the claim can still be contested, superseded, or poorly supported.
- Keep practice/literature divergence explicit when a disproven or narrowed finding persists in practice.
- Keep genealogy evidence-graded: advisor, citation, co-presence, correspondence, reaction-against, translation, and deep influence are different edges.
- Pattern records remain candidates until earned by multiple cases, negative controls, counterexamples, and rollback triggers.
- Run `make lint` before packaging.
- Each revision must update `REVISION-RECEIPT.json`, `RELEASE-MANIFEST.json`, `SURFACE-STATUS.json`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `ARCHIVE_INDEX.md`, and `context-pack.json` when relevant.

## Choose a resume mode before acting

- **Accrete** — add source-backed records.
- **Review** — reopen earlier records for claim/source/status precision.
- **Dream** — expand telos, query primitives, or future capabilities.
- **Consolidate** — link existing records into useful cross-surfaces.
- **Red-team** — add controls, counterexamples, and rollback triggers.
- **Source-acquire** — improve provenance and permanence.
- **Handoff** — improve restart surfaces.

## Good changes usually do one of seven things

1. Acquire and stabilize sources.
2. Extract a record without overclaiming.
3. Link records into patterns, claims, status histories, practice-lag timelines, or genealogy.
4. Improve archive controls that prevent drift, bloat, or ethical harm.
5. Review earlier records and make them more precise.
6. Add controls that make patterns less anecdotal.
7. Make future-session resumption easier.
