# Crosscase memo — candidate plausibility and denominator expansion

The three promoted scientific/audit records share a correction shape:

1. A plausible biological mechanism narrows attention to a small set of variants or genes.
2. Small studies and heterogeneous analyses allow striking claims to travel.
3. Later denominator expansion changes the evidentiary surface: many variants, larger samples, stricter thresholds, consortium methods, and reusable summary statistics.
4. The resulting status is not “nothing genetic matters”; it is “the early large-effect candidate claim did not survive the newer denominator.”

This is why `MKH-INF-0030` and `MKH-INF-0031` matter. They are not just databases. They are correction rails: the infrastructure that lets a field see more of the hypothesis space.

`MKH-PAT-0015` remains candidate-level. It cannot mature until the cube adds positive controls where candidate-gene reasoning worked, plus ancestry/phenotype caveat records and a split/merge decision against broader publication-bias and denominator-visibility patterns.
