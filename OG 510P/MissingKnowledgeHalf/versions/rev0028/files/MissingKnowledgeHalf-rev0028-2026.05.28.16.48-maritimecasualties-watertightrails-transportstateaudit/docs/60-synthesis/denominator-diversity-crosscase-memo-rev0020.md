# Denominator diversity cross-case memo — rev0020

The cube now has enough genomics records to see a new candidate pattern: evidence scope follows denominator diversity, portability, and governance.

Candidate-gene nulls show that plausible mechanisms can outrun denominator-scale association evidence. Pharmacogenomic and PCSK9 controls show that strong genetic relations can be real and useful. PGS portability work and GWAS diversity monitoring show that even real association and prediction surfaces may fail to travel if the training/discovery denominator is narrow. All of Us and H3Africa show that repair requires infrastructure and governance, not only more publications.

No synthesis is mature. The next hard task is controls: cases where narrow denominator evidence transferred robustly, cases where diversity expansion did not repair portability, and non-genomics analogues.
