# Crosscase memo: denominator visibility rails

Rev0008 adds a pattern candidate, `MKH-PAT-0008`, whose core claim is modest: multiple infrastructures make parts of the invisible denominator queryable.

The infrastructures are heterogeneous. ClinicalTrials.gov and WHO ICTRP make trial existence and status more visible. OSF and AsPredicted preserve research plans, with different choices about privacy and public release. AEA RCT Registry narrows this to social-science field experiments and versioned trial records. Registered Reports change the publication decision point so outcomes are not known at acceptance. Retraction Watch/Crossref encode formal post-publication status changes. PubPeer exposes community critique signals that may or may not mature into formal correction.

The common mistake would be to call all of this “transparency” and stop. The cube should do the opposite: preserve the differences. A private preregistration and a public NCT result table are not the same kind of evidence. A PubPeer thread and a retraction notice are not the same kind of status change. A registered protocol and an adhered-to protocol are not the same event.

The next mature move is one complete lifecycle record: plan/registry → execution/completion → results → publication/nonpublication → later correction or critique, if any.
