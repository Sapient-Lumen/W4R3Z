# Crosscase memo: from replication failure to boundary map

Revision: rev0012

The strongest new pattern is not that effects fail. The stronger pattern is that a single effect claim becomes scientifically useful only after it is re-expressed as a boundary map: protocol, materials, population, sample, lab ecology, endpoint, effect size, uncertainty, and visibility constraints.

## Crosscase contrasts

- Ego depletion and facial feedback are RRR-style single-target protocol tests.
- Many Labs 1 and Many Labs 2 are multi-effect maps with mixed outcomes and effect-size comparisons.
- Reproducibility Project: Cancer Biology adds a second axis: even designing a replication can fail when protocols, data, reagents, or cooperation are missing.
- ManyBabies 1 is a successful-control case: robust effects still need magnitude and moderator maps.

## Candidate pattern

`MKH-PAT-0012` should remain candidate-level until the cube adds child records, author-response histories, and non-psychology controls.

## User-facing warning

A replication record should not be written as “X was wrong.” It should be written as “Under this protocol and source route, here is the effect estimate and what remains unsettled.”
