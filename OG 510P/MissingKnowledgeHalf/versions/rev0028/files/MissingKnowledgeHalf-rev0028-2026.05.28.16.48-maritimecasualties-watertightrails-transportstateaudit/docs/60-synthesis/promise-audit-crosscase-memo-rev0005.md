# Promise-audit cross-case memo, rev0005

Rev0005 adds a new synthesis possibility: a research field can have a visible **promise route** before it has a reliable **capability route**. Program audits are moments when that gap becomes explicit.

## ALPAC

The ALPAC report is a negative result for near-term useful machine translation of general scientific text without human editing. It is also a preservation record: the committee recommended computational-linguistics work, objective quality evaluation, machine aids, and translation-process improvements.

## Lighthill

The Lighthill Report is an evaluator scene around AI research-policy expectations. It criticized inflated promises and disappointment, but must be read with its response symposium and later historiography. The report should not become a monocausal “AI winter” slogan.

## Dartmouth

The Dartmouth record is not negative evidence. It is an upstream founding scene whose proposal conjecture helps explain why broad AI promises became legible as promises at all.

## Pattern status

`MKH-PAT-0006` is admitted as a candidate because the record design now has at least two audit/evaluator cases and one upstream scene. It remains weak until the cube adds:

- successful program audits;
- prematurely pessimistic audits;
- downstream funding/practice records;
- later revival records, especially for machine translation.
