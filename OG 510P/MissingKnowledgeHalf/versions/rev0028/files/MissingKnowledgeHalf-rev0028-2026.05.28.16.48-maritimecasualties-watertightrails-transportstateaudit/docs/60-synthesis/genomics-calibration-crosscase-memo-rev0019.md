# Cross-case memo: genomics calibration after candidate-gene failures

Revision: rev0019

Rev0018 opened the candidate-gene denominator lane with psychiatric genetics records: depression candidate genes, 5-HTTLPR×stress, and schizophrenia candidate-gene audit. Those records could accidentally teach a crude lesson: candidate genes failed.

Rev0019 prevents that.

The control records force a richer grammar:

- APOE ε4 shows a durable gene/risk relation that is important but non-deterministic.
- HLA-B*5701/abacavir shows gene-drug actionability when the phenotype, intervention, and guideline rail are tight.
- ADH1B/ALDH2 show biochemical-pathway variants with population-scoped signals and observable intermediate phenotype.
- ClinGen/CPIC/PharmGKB show that association, validity, and actionability require curation rails.

The candidate pattern is therefore narrower and more useful: plausible mechanisms can outrun denominator-scale evidence **under some conditions**, especially when phenotypes are broad, effects are small, populations are heterogeneous, and multiple testing is under-governed. Under other conditions, candidate mechanisms can survive and become useful.
