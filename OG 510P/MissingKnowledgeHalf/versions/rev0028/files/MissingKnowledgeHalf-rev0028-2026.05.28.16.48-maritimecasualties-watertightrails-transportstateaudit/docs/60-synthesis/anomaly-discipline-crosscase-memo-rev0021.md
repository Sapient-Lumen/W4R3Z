# Cross-case memo: anomaly discipline

OPERA, BICEP2, and cold fusion should not be collapsed into the same story. They are useful precisely because they differ:

- OPERA is a careful public anomaly followed by instrument-chain correction and independent checks.
- BICEP2 is an extraordinary interpretation narrowed by foreground/control information.
- Cold fusion is a replication/research-program trajectory with strong historical nonconfirmation and residual materials questions.

The synthesis candidate is not “extraordinary claims are false.” The synthesis candidate is that anomaly claims become knowledge only through discipline: independent retest, foreground or instrument controls, versioned provenance, public correction, and preserved scope.

Open danger: the lane could become debunking theater. Pattern controls must prevent that.
