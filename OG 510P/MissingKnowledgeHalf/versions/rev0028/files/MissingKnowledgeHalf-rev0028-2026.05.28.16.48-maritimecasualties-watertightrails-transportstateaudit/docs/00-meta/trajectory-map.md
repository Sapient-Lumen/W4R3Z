# Trajectory map

## Phase 0 — Seed grammar

Status: active in rev0001.

Goal: make the archive reopenable, honest, and safe before content expansion. This phase creates reentry surfaces, schema, candidate queues, source rules, and ethics posture.

## Phase 1 — First sourced records

Goal: promote a tiny number of pilot records with primary sources. Recommended order:

1. One engineering-failure record from an official report.
2. One failed-replication record with original and replication sources.
3. One negative-result / medical-reversal record with trial or registry sources.
4. One genealogy scene/event record with attendance/advisor/correspondence sources.

## Phase 2 — Source permanence and extraction tooling

Goal: build consistent source records, capture hashes/retrieval/archive metadata, and implement extraction templates.

## Phase 3 — Cross-links and pattern records

Goal: make the corpus more than a list. Add pattern records only after at least two or three case records justify the pattern.

## Phase 4 — Claim and practice lag graph

Goal: connect claims to literature status, textbook status, policy/practice status, and time-to-update.

## Phase 5 — Field expansions

Goal: expand by bounded lanes: psychology replication, clinical reversals, official engineering investigations, software incidents, drug trial graveyards, advisor/genealogy infrastructure, and lost lineages.

## Phase 6 — Queryable corpus

Goal: expose stable query surfaces: by record type, domain, source class, status, failure pattern, ethical sensitivity, time, place, and genealogy relation.
