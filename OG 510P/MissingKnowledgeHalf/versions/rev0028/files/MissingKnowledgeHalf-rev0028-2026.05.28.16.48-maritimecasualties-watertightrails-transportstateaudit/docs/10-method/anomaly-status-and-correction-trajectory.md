# Anomaly status and correction trajectory

Physical-science anomalies should not be entered as one-word verdicts. A mature record should preserve the trajectory:

1. Claim surface: what was announced, with what caveats and uncertainty.
2. Instrument or foreground surface: what could make the signal appear without the claimed phenomenon.
3. Independent retest or replication: who checked, with what independence.
4. Versioned provenance: how the record changed over time.
5. Status narrowing: what is now unsupported, what remains possible, and what new limit was learned.
6. Residual learning: methods, materials, instruments, or models improved even when the headline claim failed.

The lane should avoid two opposite errors: treating every anomaly as likely truth suppressed by orthodoxy, and treating every anomaly as foolishness. The cube wants correction discipline, not ridicule.

`MKH-PAT-0017` is candidate-only. It should not mature until positive controls, negative controls, and counterexamples are promoted.
