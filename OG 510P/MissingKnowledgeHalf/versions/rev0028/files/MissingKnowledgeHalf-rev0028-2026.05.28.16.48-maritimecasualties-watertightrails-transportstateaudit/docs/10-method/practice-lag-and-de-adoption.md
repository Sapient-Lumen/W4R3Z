# Practice lag and de-adoption

A negative result can be true in the literature and still not be true in practice. Rev0013 introduces a separate practice-lag lane for cases where a practice was adopted, then narrowed or reversed by stronger evidence, but removal from care required further correction surfaces.

## Separate these things

1. The tested claim.
2. The comparator and endpoint.
3. The evidence turning surface: randomized trial, sham control, hard endpoint, pooled analysis, systematic review.
4. The practice surface: guideline, coverage, reimbursement, low-value-care list, patient/clinician conversation, utilization data.
5. The exception surface: subgroups or indications where the practice may remain valid.

The cube must not encode “negative trial published” as “practice disappeared.” De-adoption is an additional empirical claim.
