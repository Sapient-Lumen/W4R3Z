# Denominator scope and portability

A genomics claim inherits the scope of its denominator. That denominator is not only sample size. It includes ancestry distribution, phenotype definition, environment, measurement, recruitment, consent, governance, data access, and the training/target relationship of any predictive model.

Rev0020 adds a method rule: whenever a genomic record is used in a pattern, future sessions should ask whether the association is being treated as portable beyond the population, phenotype, or governance surface that made it visible.

This is not anti-genetics. It is anti-unscoped-generalization. Strong pharmacogenomic and human-genetic controls can coexist with candidate-gene failures and PGS portability caveats.
