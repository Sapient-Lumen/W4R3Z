# Impoundment failure and material-state legibility

Rev0026 treats water dams and tailings dams as adjacent, not identical. The shared object is a stored-material impoundment whose danger depends on state variables that are often hidden from casual observation: hydraulic gradient, seepage, pore pressure, saturation, stratigraphy, foundation behavior, construction history, raise method, drainage, material brittleness, monitoring sensitivity, and downstream exposure.

The method rule is separation:

- mechanism is not consequence;
- monitoring is not actionability;
- a standard is not conformance;
- disclosure is not verification;
- a case-study rail is not a primary investigation report.

The lane should grow through controls, not just more catastrophes. Future work should add near-misses where risk was detected and corrected, stable high-consequence facilities with robust controls, and failures whose dominant mechanism is not hidden material/hydraulic state.
