# Record review checklist

Before a record can be treated as corpus content, check:

1. Does the record type match the evidence?
2. Is the claim/incident/influence stated precisely?
3. Is at least one source primary or high-grade synthesis?
4. Are dates and places separated from interpretation?
5. Are null, underpowered null, method failure, counter-finding, and boundary condition distinguished?
6. Is current status separated from historical status?
7. Are source permanence and retrieval metadata present?
8. Are ethical sensitivity and naming choices justified?
9. Are cross-links present or explicitly pending?
10. Is there a stated defeater or update trigger?
11. Does the revision receipt name the promotion?
