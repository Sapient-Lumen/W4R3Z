# Corpus 3D map: genealogy and anti-genealogy of ideas

## Why this belongs with failures

Genealogy explains why some errors persisted, why some warnings were ignored, which scenes transmitted methods, and which intellectual reactions reshaped fields.

## Initial source families

- advisor-tree infrastructure,
- biographies and autobiographies,
- oral histories,
- acknowledgments and prefaces,
- correspondence archives,
- conference attendee lists,
- festschrifts and memorial issues,
- citation and coauthorship graphs,
- translation/reception histories.

## Immediate danger

Influence is interpretive. A genealogy record should distinguish formal relation, cited relation, self-declared influence, historian-attributed influence, and reaction-against.
