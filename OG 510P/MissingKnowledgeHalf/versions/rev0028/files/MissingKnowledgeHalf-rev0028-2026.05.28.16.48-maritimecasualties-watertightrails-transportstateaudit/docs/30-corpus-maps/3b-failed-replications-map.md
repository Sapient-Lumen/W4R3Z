# Corpus 3B map: failed replications

## Record structure

Every failed-replication record should pair at least two objects: original study and replication attempt. A good record also includes author response, subsequent attempts, and field status.

## Replication types

- direct,
- conceptual,
- meta-analytic disconfirmation,
- adversarial collaboration,
- mega-study,
- Many Labs style,
- reanalysis disconfirmation,
- forensic disconfirmation.

## Immediate danger

A failed replication may reveal method sensitivity or boundary conditions rather than total falsity. Avoid verdict language until the comparison is explicit.
