# Corpus 3C map: engineering failures and near-misses

## Why this lane is a strong pilot

Engineering failures often have structured public reports, timelines, causal analysis, recommendations, and regulatory follow-up. This makes them ideal for testing the schema.

## Initial source families

- transportation accident boards,
- chemical safety boards,
- nuclear regulators,
- space agency reports,
- court/inquiry records,
- official lessons-learned systems,
- confidential near-miss reporting systems where public summaries exist.

## Immediate danger

Do not reduce records to dramatic anecdotes. The value is the causal chain, latent conditions, recommendations, and recurrence links.
