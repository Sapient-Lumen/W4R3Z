# Pilot lane: official engineering-failure reports

## Why first

Official engineering reports are public, structured, and rich in timeline/causal fields. They also test the ethics posture because many involve death, injury, institutional blame, and survivor/family dignity.

## Candidate record types

- incident record (`ENG`),
- source records (`SRC`),
- pattern records (`PAT`) once multiple incidents exist,
- claim records (`CLA`) for recurring causal claims.

## Suggested first workflow

1. Choose one incident.
2. Locate official report.
3. Create source record with permanence metadata.
4. Extract incident fields.
5. Assign ethics sensitivity.
6. Add status and update triggers.
7. Link one or two candidate patterns, but keep patterns candidate until recurrence is sourced.
8. Update receipt and changelog.
