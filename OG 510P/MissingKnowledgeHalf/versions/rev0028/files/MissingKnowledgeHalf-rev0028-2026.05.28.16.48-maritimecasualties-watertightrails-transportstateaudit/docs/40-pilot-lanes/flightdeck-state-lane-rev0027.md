# Flight-deck state lane — rev0027

Promoted records: United 173, AF447, Asiana 214, Colgan 3407, United 232 positive control, FAA Lessons Learned, FAA CRM guidance, and BEA/Annex 13 safety-investigation rail.

The lane asks how aircraft state becomes or fails to become shared operational knowledge. The answer is not always “automation,” not always “training,” and not always “crew communication.” The record must preserve the official investigator grain.

United 232 is intentionally present as a control: flight-deck coordination can be a resilience surface, not merely a failure surface.
