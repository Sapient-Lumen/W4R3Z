# Rev0008 pilot lane: denominator visibility rails

Rev0008 promotes eight source-infrastructure records:

- `MKH-INF-0006` ClinicalTrials.gov
- `MKH-INF-0007` WHO ICTRP
- `MKH-INF-0008` OSF Registries
- `MKH-INF-0009` AsPredicted
- `MKH-INF-0010` AEA RCT Registry
- `MKH-INF-0011` Registered Reports
- `MKH-INF-0012` Retraction Watch / Crossref
- `MKH-INF-0013` PubPeer

The purpose is to make the file-drawer lane operational. Before the cube can record unpublished nulls and failed studies at scale, it must know how to represent the infrastructures that expose study existence, analysis plans, result-posting obligations, publication-before-results acceptance, and post-publication correction signals.

No child study records were promoted. This revision is a map of rails, not a scrape of traffic on the rails.
