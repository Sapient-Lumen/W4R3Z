# Pilot lane: medical reversal / clinical null or harm

## Why useful

Medical reversals test the archive’s ability to connect literature status to practice change and patient harm without sensationalism.

## Minimum structure

- initial claim or practice,
- observational/RCT evidence split if relevant,
- trial or registry sources,
- result and uncertainty,
- guideline/practice update,
- persistence or reversal lag,
- patient-safety ethical handling.
