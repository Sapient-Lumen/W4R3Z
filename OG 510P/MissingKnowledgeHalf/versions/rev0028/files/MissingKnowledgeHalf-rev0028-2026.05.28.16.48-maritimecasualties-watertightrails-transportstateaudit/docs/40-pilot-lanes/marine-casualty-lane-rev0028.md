# Marine casualty lane — rev0028

Promoted records: Herald of Free Enterprise, MV Estonia, Costa Concordia, El Faro, Viking Sky near-miss control, MAIB, IMO Casualty Investigation Code, EMSA EMCIP, NTSB Office of Marine Safety, and pattern candidate MKH-PAT-0023.

The pilot purpose is not to make a complete maritime disaster archive. It is to test whether the cube can preserve multiple vessel-state surfaces without collapsing into a single moral: open bow doors, bow visor/ramp behavior, close-approach navigation, hurricane routing, lube-oil/sump-tank design, downflooding, evacuation, and report/recommendation rails are different objects.
