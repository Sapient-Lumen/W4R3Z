# Genomics portability and controls — rev0020

Rev0020 adds two control records and two audit records.

The controls are HLA-B*15:02/carbamazepine and PCSK9 loss-of-function. They prevent the candidate-gene denominator pattern from becoming anti-genetics or anti-candidate-mechanism.

The audit records are polygenic-score portability across the genetic ancestry continuum and a GWAS Diversity Monitor snapshot. They prevent the controls from becoming naive universality claims.

The lesson is not contradiction. The lesson is factorization: effect size, phenotype tightness, actionability, population scope, portability, and governance are separate axes.
