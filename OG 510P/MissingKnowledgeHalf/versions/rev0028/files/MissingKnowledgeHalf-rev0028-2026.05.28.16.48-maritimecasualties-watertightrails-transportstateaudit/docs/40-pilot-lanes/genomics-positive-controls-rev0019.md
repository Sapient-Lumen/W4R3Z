# Rev0019 pilot lane: genomics positive controls

Revision: rev0019

Promoted records:

- `MKH-CTL-0001` — APOE ε4 / late-onset Alzheimer disease risk, with non-determinism preserved.
- `MKH-CTL-0002` — HLA-B*5701 / abacavir hypersensitivity, with screening/actionability and clinical-judgment limits.
- `MKH-CTL-0003` — ADH1B/ALDH2 alcohol-metabolism variants, with population and phenotype scope preserved.
- `MKH-INF-0032` — ClinGen / CPIC / PharmGKB as curation/actionability rails.

These records calibrate `MKH-PAT-0015`; they do not mature it. The intended lesson is not “candidate genes fail” or “GWAS wins.” The intended lesson is that genetic evidence needs axes: phenotype tightness, mechanism/effect size, population scope, denominator breadth, evidence curation, and clinical actionability.
