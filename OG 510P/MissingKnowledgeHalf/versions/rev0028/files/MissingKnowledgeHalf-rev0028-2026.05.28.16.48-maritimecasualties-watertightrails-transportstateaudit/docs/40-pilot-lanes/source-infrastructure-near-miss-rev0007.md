# Rev0007 pilot lane: near-miss source infrastructure

Why this lane now: rev0006 created warning-governance cases from fatal or severe failures. That is necessary but biased. To understand warning knowledge, the cube needs institutions that catch signals before catastrophe.

Promoted infrastructure records:

- `MKH-INF-0001` — NASA ASRS.
- `MKH-INF-0002` — CHIRP.
- `MKH-INF-0003` — CIRAS.
- `MKH-INF-0004` — ATSB / REPCON.
- `MKH-INF-0005` — NTSB safety recommendations.

These records are deliberately about source systems rather than individual incidents. They establish vocabulary for confidentiality, de-identification, voluntary reporting, no-blame investigation, action routing, and recommendation closure.

What was deferred:

- NASA LLIS as a full infrastructure record.
- CSB animations as multimedia source infrastructure.
- MAIB safety digests and marine near-miss sources.
- Patient-safety reporting systems.
- Nuclear operating-experience databases.
- Individual ASRS/REPCON/CIRAS/CHIRP report narratives.

The new pattern candidate, `MKH-PAT-0007`, must remain a candidate until report-to-action exemplars, negative controls, and privacy-safe sampling are added.
