# Nuclear safety / design-basis pilot lane — rev0023

Rev0023 is the first nuclear safety lane. It promotes three severe-accident records and two infrastructure records:

- `MKH-ENG-0021` — Three Mile Island Unit 2.
- `MKH-ENG-0022` — Chornobyl/Chernobyl Unit 4.
- `MKH-ENG-0023` — Fukushima Daiichi.
- `MKH-INF-0038` — IAEA/NEA incident reporting systems.
- `MKH-INF-0039` — IAEA emergency notification, USIE/NEWS, and Early Notification Convention.

The lane is intentionally non-ideological. It is not an argument for or against nuclear power. It is an engineering-failure/correction-memory lane about hazard-envelope assumptions, instrumentation, control-room legibility, common-mode exposure, safety culture, emergency information, and the difficult transition from lessons recorded to lessons infused.

The new pattern candidate, `MKH-PAT-0018`, is not mature. It needs positive controls, negative controls, recommendation-closure records, and regulator/industry follow-through before synthesis claims are allowed.
