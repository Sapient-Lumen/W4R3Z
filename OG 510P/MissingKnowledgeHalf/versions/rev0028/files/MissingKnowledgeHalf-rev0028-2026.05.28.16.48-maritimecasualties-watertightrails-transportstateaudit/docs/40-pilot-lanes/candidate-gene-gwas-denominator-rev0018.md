# Candidate-gene / GWAS denominator lane — rev0018

Rev0018 opens a high-sensitivity genetics lane. The goal is not to mock candidate-gene research. The goal is to encode a historically important correction dynamic: biologically plausible, low-dimensional targets can accumulate claims before the field has enough denominator-scale testing, power, and multiple-comparison control.

- `MKH-NEG-0018` — 5-HTTLPR × stress/depression collaborative meta-analysis.
