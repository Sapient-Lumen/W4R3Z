# Rev0004 non-engineering first-records pass

Revision: `rev0004`

Rev0003 proved that engineering failures can be admitted as source-backed incident records. Rev0004 tests whether the same discipline can cross into the harder lanes: failed replications and medical negative results.

## What was promoted

- `MKH-REP-0005` — Reproducibility Project: Psychology as a **program-level replication record**.
- `MKH-NEG-0009` — WHI estrogen-plus-progestin trial as a **medical reversal / risk-benefit negative result**.
- `MKH-NEG-0010` — CAST antiarrhythmic trial as a **surrogate-endpoint reversal with excess mortality**.
- `MKH-NEG-0011` — SELECT vitamin E/selenium prostate-cancer-prevention trial as a **null/harm prevention trial**.
- `MKH-NEG-0012` — CARET beta-carotene/retinol lung-cancer-prevention trial as a **null/harm prevention trial**.
- `MKH-PAT-0005` — provisional pattern candidate: plausible positive proxy routes failed under hard endpoints.

## Why these records

The goal was not to add famous examples. The goal was to test whether the cube can preserve *evidentiary motion*:

1. A positive route existed before the hard test.
2. The positive route was not stupid in context.
3. A harder endpoint or replication program changed the status.
4. The archive preserves benefits, harms, uncertainty, and scope instead of replacing history with a punchline.

The records therefore include `prior_evidentiary_route`, `hard_endpoint_result`, `scope_cautions`, `unknowns`, and `next_actions` fields. This is the start of the denominator graph in medicine and metascience.

## What this revision deliberately does not do

- It does not claim that all psychology failed to replicate.
- It does not claim all hormone therapy, antiarrhythmic therapy, vitamin E, beta-carotene, or dietary nutrients are harmful.
- It does not claim surrogate endpoints or observational evidence are useless.
- It does not finish practice-lag history.
- It does not embed full PDFs or replace DOI/PubMed/official archive capture.

## Immediate follow-through

The next good moves are: second-review these records; add practice-lag subrecords; extract direct primary papers where only professional summaries were used; add negative controls where plausible proxy routes correctly predicted hard endpoints.
