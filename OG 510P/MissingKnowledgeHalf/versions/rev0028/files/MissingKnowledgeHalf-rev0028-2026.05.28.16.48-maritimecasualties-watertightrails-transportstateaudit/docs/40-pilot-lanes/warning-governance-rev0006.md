# Rev0006 pilot lane: warning governance

Rev0006 opens a cross-domain engineering lane for cases where warning signals existed before failure but did not become authoritative action.

Promoted records:

- `MKH-ENG-0003` — Challenger STS-51L.
- `MKH-ENG-0009` — Columbia STS-107.
- `MKH-ENG-0005` — Boeing 737 MAX.
- `MKH-ENG-0014` — BP Texas City refinery explosion.
- `MKH-PAT-0002` — safety warnings lose force when proof burden flips under schedule, production, certification, or metric pressure.

Why this lane now: the seed document emphasizes that engineering failures are a mature negative-result subgenre and that recurring patterns across industries are one of the unified corpus’s distinctive powers. This revision begins that pattern work with official reports rather than secondary lore.

The lane deliberately avoids “human error” summaries. Each record now carries warning signals, landing zones, proof-burden observations, counterpressures, and next actions.

The biggest danger is hindsight bias. The cube must not treat every pre-event hint as a decisive warning. Future positive controls and false-alarm records are required before this becomes mature synthesis.
