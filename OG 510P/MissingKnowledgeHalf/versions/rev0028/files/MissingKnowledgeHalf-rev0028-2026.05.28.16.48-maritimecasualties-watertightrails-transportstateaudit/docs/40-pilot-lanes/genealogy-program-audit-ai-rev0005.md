# Rev0005 pilot lane: genealogy + program audit around early AI

Rev0005 deliberately opens the fourth seed lane, genealogy of ideas, while preserving the source/claim/status discipline established in engineering, replication, and medical negative-result records.

## Why this lane

The seed says the unified corpus needs not only failed studies and engineering disasters, but also the transmission structures that explain why promises spread and why corrections do or do not travel. Early AI is a good first test because it has:

- a source-rich founding scene (`MKH-GEN-0004`, Dartmouth 1956);
- a mature genealogy-infrastructure model (`MKH-GEN-0001`, Mathematics Genealogy Project);
- a program-audit negative result (`MKH-NEG-0013`, ALPAC 1966);
- an anti-genealogy/evaluator scene (`MKH-GEN-0005`, Lighthill 1972/1973).

## What was avoided

This revision does **not** promote broad AI-winter claims, does **not** import a person graph, and does **not** treat either ALPAC or Lighthill as final truth about artificial intelligence. It creates bounded records with next actions.

## New pattern candidate

`MKH-PAT-0006` is a candidate only: program audits can convert broad field promises into accountable scope. It requires negative controls, funding-consequence records, and revival records before it can become a synthesis.
