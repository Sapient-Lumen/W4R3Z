# Rev0012 pilot lane: multi-lab replication

This revision adds six replication records and four infrastructure records. It deliberately includes successes, failures, shrinkage, and feasibility barriers in the same lane.

## Why this lane now

Before rev0012, the cube had one replication parent: the Reproducibility Project: Psychology. That was too thin for a project whose seed gives failed replications a distinct corpus. Rev0012 creates enough records to start comparing record grains.

## Main lesson

The word replication hides several operations. Sometimes the field wants a direct retest of one finding. Sometimes it wants a multi-effect map. Sometimes it wants a feasibility audit. Sometimes it needs a robust-control record.
