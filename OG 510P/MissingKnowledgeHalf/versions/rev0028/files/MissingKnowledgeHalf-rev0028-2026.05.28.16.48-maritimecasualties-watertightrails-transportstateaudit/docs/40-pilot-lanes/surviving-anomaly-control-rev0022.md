# rev0022 pilot lane: surviving anomaly control

rev0022 adds a positive-control surface to the physical-science anomaly lane:

- `MKH-CTL-0006` — GW150914 / first direct gravitational-wave detection.
- `MKH-INF-0037` — GWOSC open-data rail.
- `MKH-MET-0011` — reproduction/reanalysis audit surface.

The purpose is not to make the anomaly lane pro- or anti-extraordinary claims. The purpose is to make the status trajectory complete enough to distinguish at least four things:

1. anomaly corrected as instrument error;
2. anomaly corrected as foreground/model error;
3. anomaly not reproduced or research-program failure;
4. anomaly survives and becomes consolidated discovery, but with inspectable reproducibility limits.

GW150914 enters as the fourth kind.
