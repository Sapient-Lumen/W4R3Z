# Practice-lag fields

New and future practice-lag records should include:

- adoption surface: why the practice spread before hard evidence;
- evidence turning surface: the specific RCT/sham/hard-endpoint/synthesis that changed status;
- de-adoption surface: how practice changed or failed to change;
- exception surface: where the practice remains indicated, contested, or revived;
- utilization surface: measured change in use over time;
- guideline surface: dated recommendations and jurisdictions;
- ethics surface: not medical advice, no hindsight contempt, patient dignity.
