# Factor axes

Record types are not enough. The cube needs factor axes so that cases from medicine, engineering, software, replication, meta-research, and genealogy can be compared without being flattened.

See `FACTORING-LEDGER.json` for the machine-readable form.

## Required habit

When a new record is added, future sessions should ask:

- What is its object role?
- What evidence surface supports it?
- What status motion is being captured?
- What denominator is visible or hidden?
- What correction surface made it visible?
- What transmission edge explains spread or correction?
- What is the practice status?
- What harm/ethics class governs handling?
- What pattern maturity level applies?
- What review depth has been earned?

## Why this matters

The seed cube began as four interrelated corpora. The current cube has more than four containers. Factor axes keep that expansion from turning into uncontrolled taxonomy growth.
