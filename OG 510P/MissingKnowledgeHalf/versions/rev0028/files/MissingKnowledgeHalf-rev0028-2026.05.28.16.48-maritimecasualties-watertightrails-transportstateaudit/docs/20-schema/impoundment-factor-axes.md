# Impoundment factor axes

The rev0026 overlay uses these axes for water dams, tailings dams, levees, waste impoundments, and adjacent stored-material systems:

1. `impoundment_class` — water reservoir, tailings facility, levee, ash pond, waste pond, etc.
2. `stored_material` — water, saturated tailings, slimes/sands, coal ash, process waste.
3. `hydraulic_or_pore_pressure_state` — seepage, pore pressure, saturation, water level, drainage, suction.
4. `foundation_or_stratigraphy_state` — abutment, key trench, weak layer, foundation material, geological history.
5. `construction_or_raise_history` — first filling, upstream raising, design changes, conduit or starter-dam damage, slope history.
6. `monitoring_legibility` — what instruments could or could not see; whether signals were actionable.
7. `warning_or_emergency_response` — alarm, evacuation, arrival time, failure speed, public communication.
8. `downstream_consequence_exposure` — people, ecosystems, infrastructure, workers, emergency access.
9. `regulatory_or_standard_rail` — investigation, standard, review board, regulator, legal obligation.
10. `public_disclosure_or_denominator_rail` — portal, registry, database, inventory, case memory.
11. `correction_infusion_status` — whether lessons changed design, operation, monitoring, land use, emergency planning, or conformance.
