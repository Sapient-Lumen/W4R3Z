# Human-factors factor axes

rev0027 adds a factor overlay rather than a hard schema migration. Future aviation and non-aviation human-factors records should annotate at least these axes when source-supported: actual system state, displayed/interpreted state, automation/warning state, crew/team coordination, training/procedure/memory surface, fatigue/supervision/oversight, survivability, and legal/safety status.

These axes are deliberately orthogonal. A record can have automation ambiguity without fatigue, training debt without legal conflict, and excellent crew coordination under unsalvageable physical damage.
