# MissingKnowledgeHalf

## Keep the routes that did not lead to success

The seed proposes a history of knowledge that makes room for null results, failed replications, engineering failures and the genealogy—and opposition—through which ideas move. Those are related, but they are not one undifferentiated bin of mistakes.

Its attraction is practical as well as historical. A future researcher can repeat a dead end because it was never written down; a successful theory can look inevitable because the competing paths vanished from the story. The archive wants the missing routes to remain available for judgment.

## Failure needs its own evidential discipline

An accident is not automatically a simple lesson. A failed replication is not automatically fraud or proof that an entire theory is false. A historical influence claim is not established by two similar ideas. A corpus of failure can acquire its own hindsight and blame biases unless it preserves causal scale, source quality and what remained unknown.

The supplied revision expressly says no pattern is mature and that the new marine records need further extraction and closure work. That is a useful instruction for reading their status, not an invitation to continue the project’s fieldwork.

The retained older README heading and manifest self-entry discrepancy are described below. Their preservation keeps the artifact honest without changing the intellectual value of the question it asks.
