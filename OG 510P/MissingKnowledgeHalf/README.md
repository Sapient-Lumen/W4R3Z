# MissingKnowledgeHalf

## Make failed attempts useful to the next inquiry

MissingKnowledgeHalf asks what research and engineering could become if they inherited more than their successes. A published finding can survive after the attempts that qualified it disappear. A disaster can become a familiar story while the warnings, design constraints and unfinished repairs that explain it remain scattered. An idea can acquire a celebrated author while its teachers, translators, opponents and abandoned alternatives fall out of view.

The project builds a linked record of that missing material. Its unit is not a cautionary tale but a record whose claims, sources, uncertainty, development and relations can be inspected. The purpose is constructive: help someone choose a better experiment, recognize a recurring design problem, distinguish a genuine correction from a change in rhetoric, or carry a difficult inquiry forward without beginning from the same ignorance.

The [charter][charter] expressly rejects replacing a success canon with a failure canon. The interesting ambition is a less distorted account of how knowledge develops, including findings that survive scrutiny and failures that turn out to have been misclassified. Its [account of earned capabilities][earn] measures progress by what a reader can now discover or check, not by how many famous cases have been accumulated.

## Four corpora, four kinds of question

The [seed][seed] proposes four connected bodies of material. Their connections matter because a finding, its reception, its practical use and its correction are often stored in different places.

- **Negative results and the file drawer:** what was attempted, measured, abandoned or left unpublished? A small effect, a method that failed, a study unable to discriminate the hypothesis, and a result found only under narrower conditions are different outcomes. Recording only “worked” or “failed” destroys information.
- **Replications:** what precisely was repeated, with which materials, populations and uncertainty? The [multi-lab method][replication] distinguishes a robust control, a smaller effect, an interval compatible with no effect, failure under a specified protocol, and an attempt limited by unavailable materials or data. A program-level result is not automatically a verdict on every constituent effect or an entire discipline.
- **Engineering failures and near-misses:** what broke, what made it vulnerable, what warning could have become action, and what changed afterward? A physical sequence, a management condition and a recommendation belong in the same account without becoming interchangeable explanations.
- **Genealogy and anti-genealogy:** what travelled through teaching, reading, collaboration, translation or opposition? The inquiry includes the transmission of methods and corrections, not merely a family tree of prestigious names.

The seed is an expansive research agenda, not the completed corpus or a source validating every example it names. The later records and methods are where particular claims acquire support, narrower scope and a history of revision.

## Recover the denominator, not just another dramatic example

Suppose a literature search finds twenty published studies. That number says little about studies begun but not published, outcomes left out of papers, or results framed differently from their original protocols. Simply adding some famous null results cannot establish how selective the visible literature is.

The [audit-pairing method][audit-pairing] therefore asks for two distinguishable bodies of evidence: a record of what existed before publication, such as a protocol cohort or registry, and the literature that ordinary readers can actually find. Their comparison can distinguish never-published work from delay, an unlinked publication, omitted outcomes or changed outcome definitions. A mismatch becomes a question with traceable evidence rather than a general suspicion about science.

The [registration method][denominator] keeps the intermediate states separate. Registration does not establish completion; completion does not establish results reporting; a preregistration does not establish adherence; a public criticism does not establish its own verdict. Infrastructure records describe what a reporting system can reveal and what it misses. They do not pretend that naming a registry imports its whole contents into this cube.

That distinction makes the project useful beyond collecting negative results. It asks how one could know what is missing at all.

## A claim graph that distinguishes evidence from its interpretation

The [record lifecycle][lifecycle] separates a remembered example, a candidate, a located source, extraction, review, promotion and synthesis. These are intended evidential transitions, not a promise that every carried record has completed them: the current marine entries explicitly retain one-pass status and further review work. A recognizable case name is a starting point, not a credential.

Each substantive claim can point to support with a target identifier, target kind, support role, locator and confidence description. The [rev0016 evidence-reference redesign][evidence] fixes a small-looking but consequential error: an engineering case used to support a proposed pattern is not itself an original source. Calling every target a source would make the graph misleading even if all its identifiers resolved.

The retained rev0016 receipt explicitly describes this as structural repair, not fresh source validation or a second review. Better representation makes a claim easier to challenge; it does not retroactively improve its evidence.

A direct passage in a report, a conclusion derived through another record, a description of reporting infrastructure and a pattern's own statement of scope now have different roles. Locator confidence describes the state of retrieval or extraction, not a probability that the claim is true. A pattern citing its own limits documents those limits; it does not independently confirm its causal explanation.

The resulting structure lets a reader follow a proposed generalization back through a case and then into its evidence, while seeing where interpretation entered. It also makes unfinished work specific: repair a locator, split an overbroad claim, retrieve an opposing source, or establish what would change a status.

## A concrete case: the ship, the warning and the repair

The carried [Herald of Free Enterprise record][herald] illustrates the intended grain. Its account separates the open-door and flooding sequence from vehicle-deck vulnerability, procedural enforcement and management conditions. The claim-level references point to different parts of the investigation rather than using one report citation to support an undifferentiated story of “human error.” Unknowns include casualty-count conventions and unfinished recommendation history; they are not quietly resolved by a polished summary.

The [marine method][marine] expands that separation across watertight integrity, stability, routing, propulsion, team organization, displays, evacuation and investigation status. Its companion [cross-case memo][marine-memo] does not assume that ferries, cruise ships and cargo ships fail in the same way merely because all are vessels.

The [Viking Sky control record][viking] adds a different outcome: a severe near-miss, not another fatal loss. Its role is to test a candidate pattern against cases that do not end the same way. The record explicitly does not treat survival as proof that the system was well designed, that every recommendation worked, or that the relevant industry is generally safe.

A further distinction gives this research its practical force: an investigation report preserves a lesson; it does not show that a design changed, a recommendation closed, a crew's practice changed, or the hazard stopped recurring. Those require their own evidence. The same separation appears in the [practice-lag method][practice]: publication of a negative result and abandonment of a practice are different events. An archive that records only the first may preserve correction while missing its failure to take effect.

The [correction-memory synthesis][uptake] names the full chain: capture a signal, make it findable, interpret it with enough context, give it a route to authority, incorporate it into practice, check the result, and notice when the lesson decays into stale ritual. A searchable archive can succeed at the first two and fail at the rest. That is also a self-criticism of this cube: a better record of failure still has to reach the future decision that needs it.

## Test patterns against what does not fit

A collection of memorable failures can train its reader to see the same failure everywhere. The [pattern-control method][controls] counters that tendency with cases outside the original supporting set, superficially similar cases that should not fit, hazards caught before harm, and conditions for splitting or retiring a pattern.

Its unit/representation-boundary example adds the Gimli Glider as an explicit control outside the original supporting cases. That addition tests a candidate; it does not certify a universal law. The [surviving-anomaly method][anomalies] goes further: a collection of corrected anomalies also needs a case where an extraordinary result survives scrutiny. The cube introduces GW150914 in that role so that failure literacy does not become reflexive debunking.

The distinction is between “this resembles the story” and “this proposed explanation has survived a meaningful contrast.” In rev0028, no pattern is declared mature. That is a substantive state of the research, not a reason to ignore the comparisons already made.

The [warning-governance method][warnings] provides another useful contrast: information can exist without a route by which it acquires authority to stop or change an operation. It records where a warning landed, who could act, which proof burden applied, and which pressures competed with it. Its initial severe-failure cases are explicitly a biased sample requiring successful stops, near-misses and false alarms. The question is not simply whether someone was right in hindsight, but what could have made the warning actionable at the time.

## Trace influence without inventing ancestry

The [genealogy confidence ladder][genealogy] distinguishes formal supervision, shared institutional presence, explicit influence and reaction against an idea. Attendance creates an opportunity for contact; it does not prove intellectual influence. A formal advisor relation does not capture everything a thinker learned, and a missing database entry does not establish that a relation never existed.

The carried [Mathematics Genealogy Project record][mgp] is accordingly a study of an infrastructure model, not a bulk import of its database or a promoted graph of individual relationships. The broader ambition includes informal, undercredited and non-doctoral transmission as well as formal lineage. This matters to the other corpora: methods of recognizing error, habits of dismissing warnings and ways of correcting practice also have histories of transmission.

## A portable research workshop, not an automatic verdict machine

This older cube is a collection of Markdown, JSON records, schemas, indexes and Python tooling. Its [resume contract][resume] lets a later session choose among adding material, reviewing, consolidating, challenging a pattern, obtaining sources, imagining useful capabilities and improving the handoff. Continuing the work need not mean extending the latest subject lane or adding another case. A revision that repairs evidence or abandons a misleading pattern can be the more valuable continuation.

The actual [linter][lint] checks revision identities, expected counts, schemas, reference fields, indexes, ledger consistency and file hashes. It requires candidate status for patterns and checks that the control matrix permits none to mature in this snapshot. The [audit script][audit-code] inventories references and weak locators, using text patterns to identify some extraction debts. These are implemented structural checks, not a semantic judge of whether an official report warrants a claim or a cross-domain explanation is sound. The source methods supply the harder questions those checks cannot answer.

Read beside [DelayBasin](../DelayBasin/README.md) for a different inquiry into resumption, and [EvidenceVault](../EvidenceVault/README.md) for the obligations of a visibly incomplete recovery. [Parables](../Parables/README.md) preserves rejected candidates in a different genre: refusal and negative knowledge can matter to artistic selection as well as empirical research. None is merely a component of the others.
