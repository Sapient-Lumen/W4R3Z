# Ward

[SlopOS](../README.md) · [Complete editions](../DOWNLOADS.md#ward)

Ward gives trust a structure that can be examined, revised, and limited. It separates the custody of a credential, the claims made about a subject, the evidence supporting them, local appraisal, and the permission to do something consequential. Within the SlopOS companions, it is the place where “what do we know?” meets “what does that entitle us to do?” without collapsing the two questions.

## Signing in is the beginning

An early specimen starts with a synced passkey. A person signs into a website; a device-bound session mechanism then addresses the risk of stolen session credentials. A local service is considering a higher-value action. Ward records credential class, where the person approved, the account relation established, and the session’s protection. A separate spend gate can allow the action, demand a stronger credential, request local reapproval, or deny it. This proposed workflow makes a familiar security problem intelligible: successful authentication is useful evidence, but not permission for everything that follows. [Sign-in and local-spend specimen, rev0020](../sources/Ward/rev0020/docs/30-architecture/specimen-flow-03-recovery-grade-signin-and-local-spend.md).

The relationship continues after admission. A later signed signal may say that a session was revoked or device compliance changed. Ward’s receiver must map the subject, decide how the signal applies locally, and preserve whether access was reduced, suspended, refreshed, or left unchanged. A valid signature cannot resolve an ambiguous identity mapping by itself. The proposed “receiver stance” makes that local interpretation visible instead of treating a remote status message as an executable command. [Continuous-signal specimen](../sources/Ward/rev0020/docs/30-architecture/specimen-flow-17-attenuation-event-receiver-stance-and-subject-drift.md).

## Trust can become a landscape

Ward later extends this attention to analytical work. A reviewer pins a specific evidence basis, compares a suspicious approval cohort with a background population, and discovers differences that a fixed dashboard might have concealed. A useful query becomes a saved recipe. If repeated use justifies maintaining it, a “promotion bay” adds an owner, refresh cadence, and lineage before it becomes a shared derivative. The result helps people orient and investigate while remaining weaker than the underlying evidence and appraisal. [Basis-pin and cohort-contrast specimen, rev0099](../sources/Ward/rev0099/docs/30-architecture/specimen-flow-39-basis-pin-cohort-contrast-and-promotion-bay.md).

This is part of Ward’s artistic character. Trust is rendered as a changing relationship with history, scope, and unfinished obligations, rather than a badge painted onto an object. Even the vocabulary has an admission test: a proposed noun must answer outside pressure, help a neighbor, improve a concrete specimen, remain smaller than the consequence it describes, and retain an exit if it proves wrong. The names are meant to earn their place. [Noun-promotion rubric](../sources/Ward/rev0099/docs/00-meta/noun-promotion-rubric.md).

## Proof arrives, and quarantine remains

The final verification-intake scene shows the idea at its most precise. A public governance surface previously displayed a mismatch. A remediation review sustained quarantine, and a handoff was acknowledged. Harbor now reports an implementation or recheck proof. Ward binds that intake to the source closure receipt, target, audience, purpose, and proof subject. It can record verification, failure, absence, or conflict. Even the verified outcome keeps quarantine in place until a separately authorized transition. The proposed presentation asks Helm to show the state with a no-authority watermark; SlopOS may carry the receipt as context without treating it as a grant. [Verification-intake specimen](../sources/Ward/rev0099/docs/30-architecture/specimen-flow-96-verification-intake-proof-drift-guards-and-metadata-lineage-audit.md).

There is a meaningful implementation distinction inside this scene. Ward’s common reference validator resolves repository-bounded paths, reads referenced receipt bytes, recomputes SHA-256, checks schema and receipt identity, and can call the source receipt’s validator. Local lineage is checked, not merely asserted. [Governance reference helpers](../sources/Ward/rev0099/tools/governance_common.py). The intake checker then compares mirrored fields and computes structured scope and evidence digests, rejecting inconsistent bindings and forbidden authority upgrades. [Verification-intake checker](../sources/Ward/rev0099/tools/check_governance_verification_intakes.py).

The external Harbor proof is different. In the positive fixture, its digest is a descriptive placeholder and its trusted-issuer and validation-success fields are supplied assertions. The checker constrains those declarations; it does not fetch and cryptographically verify an actual Harbor artifact or establish that remediation occurred. The same fixture explicitly forbids clearing quarantine, completing the remedy, exporting evidence, or mutating the status pointer on this basis. [Verified-proof fixture](../sources/Ward/rev0099/fixtures/governance-verification-intake/external-remedy-proof-verified-quarantine-sustained.valid.json).

Ward therefore offers executable local refusal and receipt models alongside proposed trust workflows, not a deployed authorization service. Its distinctive achievement is the carefully preserved interval between receiving better evidence and acquiring permission to act on it.
