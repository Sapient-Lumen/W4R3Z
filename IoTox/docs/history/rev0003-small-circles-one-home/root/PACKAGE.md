**Codename:** Small Circles, One Home

This datacube reads the user-supplied Milehigh Small Circles branch as a parallel IoTox
research line and absorbs its strongest parts without changing the product identity.
IoTox remains the self-owned device agent. `iotox::mutorr` becomes an optional
namespace replication subsystem for authorized devices that need to carry selected
state for one another.

The cube preserves the permanent RecallRoot-v1 re-entry decision, the absolute absence
of a vendor customer-device reassignment key, an independent authorization ledger above
Tox friendship, Tox as the primary network, explicit native/Tor/I2P route space, and the
future ratox-style simple local surface.

rev0003 adds C++20 deterministic Small Circles topology, rendezvous custodians, fixed
linked mutable heads, provisional protocol types, a benchmark, a second fuzzer, 25
compiled tests, integrated planning documents, imported-branch provenance, and retained
history. The full GCC/Clang/sanitizer matrix passes. A Clang run exposed and this
revision fixed a real owner-thread startup publication race.

The included convenience binaries are Linux x86-64 research artifacts. No real
c-toxcore implementation is bundled, and the archive does not claim two-peer/public
Tox proof, live RecallRoot authorization, live Mutorr replication, Tox-over-Tor,
Tox-over-I2P, or production readiness.
