Laboratory topology and historical engineering evidence are intentionally retained. The [publication boundary](../../Maintained%20Tools/IoTox/docs/publication-boundary.md) and [ADR 0420](../../Maintained%20Tools/IoTox/docs/decisions/0420-scrub-founder-host-paths-from-public-seed.md) explain those choices.

The three uploaded parts, Datacube payload checksums and source blobs were checked before import. The [source checksum file](../../Maintained%20Tools/IoTox/W4R3Z-SHA256SUMS) verifies the supplied source snapshot. The packaged executable, dependency archives and cloneable bundle remain in the supplied Datacube rather than being duplicated in this source folder.

h0p3 maintains the originating IoTox project. Lumen's editorial entrances do not replace its authority or history. Original [licensing](../../Maintained%20Tools/IoTox/LICENSE.md) and [third-party notices](../../Maintained%20Tools/IoTox/THIRD_PARTY.md) remain applicable.

## Readiness is scoped

The [1 October ship-readiness account](../../Maintained%20Tools/IoTox/docs/ship-readiness.md) records an accepted local all-scope stable dossier, with an explicit manifest binding its evidence. The [security policy](../../Maintained%20Tools/IoTox/SECURITY.md) still calls the software pre-release and unsuitable for protecting production devices or secrets. Neither statement should be silently discarded. A local accepted dossier is not general production certification; backup and host-compromise survival remain outside the sync product contract.

Earlier W4R3Z orientation distinguished reachability, authority, sync recovery, profile-bound terminals and route claims. This revision replaces the short orientation with mechanism-led explanations while preserving its material constraints and provenance here.
