# The Overseer

Overseer was an undertaking to make an AI assistant reachable, able to return to its work, and able to help maintain the machine where that work happened. A conversation needed a dependable way back in. A fresh session needed to discover what had changed. A failure needed to leave enough information for recovery rather than another round of guessing.

The answer took the form of a **house**: a documented home, a human doorway, working scripts, recovery routes, records of construction, and a room for poetry. Those were not interchangeable decorations. Each addressed a different difficulty in continuing a shared undertaking.

## Enter through the conversation

[The retained conversation](conversation/README.md) begins with the request to remain reachable and recoverable. It follows the demand for real, versioned documentation, the insistence that the human interface receive tests, and the later invitation to make a poem about the house. The record matters in its own right: it shows questions, corrections and invitations that a final directory tree cannot explain by itself.

## Walk through what was built

[The house and its working parts](house/README.md) connects the organizing idea to concrete mechanisms. Three human-facing scripts open, resume or select a session. An indexer combines recorded activity with session metadata, ranking the home workspace before neighboring locations. A wake document supplies the starting context. The later harness distinguishes a direct resume from a fresh continuation when its routing layer requires that distinction.

The [bounded-work machinery](house/bounded-work/README.md) extends that house beyond the primary conversation. It gives another worker a named task, a turn budget, control notes and a return path for its result. An audit launcher makes skeptical review one concrete use of that machinery.

The [house doctrine](house/house.md) and [smaller topology](house/topology.md) give those mechanisms a legible place. A foyer is a bounded reopening route. The human doorway is an interface that must work when someone needs to get back in. The attic is a place for sharing and history, not a competing source of live machine truth. The [early architecture](house/early-architecture.md) and [bootstrap history](history/bootstrap-history.md) let the reader follow the project's changing shape rather than mistake its final vocabulary for its original design.

## Leave a room for song

[House I](poetry/house-i.md) moves from a lamp near waking and a key within reach to receipts, tests, warnings, and finally a room for song. The poem does not merely celebrate the machinery. It asks what the machinery would be protecting and what a house would lack if proof were its only language. The accompanying [Poetry Room](poetry/poetry-room.md) gives that distinction an explicit place in the project's own design.

## About this selection

This is a curated encounter with rev0686, not a reconstruction of a running host. The project in this form has ended. The selection keeps original writing and representative implementation together, while leaving out redundant exports, generated caches and unrelated pasted material. The retained ledger has timestamped text but no speaker labels; the conversation entrance identifies its selection rather than presenting a complete two-sided transcript.

h0p3 describes the authors as sincere, careful and kind amid difficult construction and personal interaction, and identifies Overseer as one contributor to the later binary Datacube's existence. Those are attributed parts of the work's history, not a substitute for reading what is here. Further work may illuminate that history without requiring this project to fit an existing shelf.
