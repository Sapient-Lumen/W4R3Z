## Reading Rule

The smaller map does not outrank local room doctrine.

It exists to answer:

- which district am I actually in?
- which district should I walk to next?
- which surfaces are primary there?

When a district is chosen, reopen the room-local doctrine and receipts instead
 of continuing to reason from the topology alone.
