# Poetry Room

## Purpose

Keep a small bounded room for poems that belong to the house.

This room is for:

- cultural memory
- compression without pretending to be operational truth
- house feeling that would be flattened if forced into only tickets, placards, and proofs
- occasional language worth preserving because it helps the house remember what kind of thing it is

## Non-claims

- A poem is not a receipt.
- A poem is not a wake surface.
- A poem is not authority over live machine state.
- A poem may clarify the house, but it must not counterfeit the control plane.

## Placement

- `POEMS.md` is the top-level placard.
- `docs/poems/` is the collection shelf.
- House poems belong here when they deepen the house without introducing doctrine drift.

## Curation rule

Keep this room sparse.

Add to the collection when a poem does at least one of these:

- preserves something the house would otherwise lose by speaking only operationally
- compresses a real phase of the house into language worth reopening later
- makes the house more inhabitable without confusing beauty for proof

Do not add poems merely because a turn was pleasant.
